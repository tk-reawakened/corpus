# 05/27/05 - ketchup on the Times

by John@aljo on May 27, 2005
yeah it has been a little while since I have posted anything around here, on the forums or otherwise. Guess been real busy with life the past little while that this place has slipped to the bottom of the to-do list. Well its made it back up the stack. I guess I can start with news of work. Just got done with counting for inventory this week. What a hassle. fortunatly we took the time to also clean up the back room as it really needed it. I wish freaken Grocery would hurry up and get their garbage out of our back room. I swear half of the pallet bays are taken up by their stuff and they have a whole other wing of recieving in which they have pallet bays they can put things in. When I become Drug Manager that will be one of the first things taken care of. Oh yeah on that note, I have been moving up in the corporate ladder myself. I have now earned the title of Assistant Drug Manager and am working on training for the real deal. I also now have a crew of 4 stockers instead of me and 1 stocker taking care of the whole freaken department. I don't know how long its going to last though as Albertsons does not pay enough to keep help for long. They have stooped low enough to start paying our help $6.40 an hour and 20 hours a week. That isn't at all in the best interests in the store I can tell them that right now. Luckily I became full time when I gained the new title though they weren't kind enough to give me a raise yet. As it is I make the highest wage next to the manager because of my lengthy stay at the company. Most don't even make it the first 6 months so they never see a raise in pay. myself I have been able to stand it for 1 year and 3 months and its paid off I think. Hell as soon as my boss decides she is going to become the Assistant Store Director I'll be getting a $3 an hour pay increase. Not only that but the game will get more challenging. Thats the only reason I can stand going to work every day, because I picture it as a video game to beat and the only way I can beat it is if I keep beating each stage that comes at me. Hell even when Im in back stacking pallets or throwing truck, I invision a puzzle game. The point of the game is similar to memory, or a jigsaw puzzle, match the shape (or numbers in this case) and see if it is gonna fit. The paperwork part of it hasn't got to me yet either thank goodness and I like who I work with even if every single person I began my career with in my department has moved on to differnt jobs, I am still there climbing and hopefully make it to the top. on a differnt note: Allan and I did take a trip this month. Our second oldest brother got married for his second time this month. This bride seems like a keeper, though she is the regular gossiping type. We left here about 4-4:30 and made it just in time for the afternoon rush hours in downtown Dallas and Ft. Worth. What a hassle. We litterally made a total of 20 miles that first hour on the road, but as soon as we left Ft. Worth there wasn't a car in sight hardly. Of course we weren't on the Interstate as Highway 377 was a much faster route. The only bad thing was the fact it was only 2 lanes with construction half the time, it also kept 'Y'ing off and twisting and turning. I am so used to interstate driving where you stay on one road until your destination. That is just the way most traffic works in Texas. The thought of the trip just made me relize that I need to go and fix the dimmer switch for my headlights. The whole way through deer country and lights on low. Couldn't see maybe 4 stripes in front of me. Alas we made it to my brother's house about 10:30. It was just him and his best man setting around getting drunk and playing cards, a nightly occurance by what I seen and hear. We join in and play a couple of rounds while drinking a few keystones. Then Eldon decides that he is going to bed so he won't be so tired for the wedding. Of course when the groom leaves the bachelor party you know what happens next. Everyone else (3 of us) decides the party is over. The best man is drunk off his ass and he is the type that just can't shut up when he is drunk. Allan goes out to the car and leaves me alone with this man. I set there and listen to this guy until 5 am till I tell him I am turning out the lights. He was ragging Eldon for getting married and saying its just because of the kid they have had. That may be true but no need to rub it in. Even though I have been puking my brains off one time when I had too much tequila, I know you can still control yourself for the most part, that is until you just can't remeber anything :P. Ok so I deal with this guy all night and then get 2 hours of sleep until Eldon turns the TV on in the morning. We all begin to start getting ready for the wedding, taking turns using the one bathroom while jabbering about how the bachorettes had more fun than we did. They ended up comming to our party, mind you its just me and the drunk up, and started painting crap all over the other 2 that are asleep faces, shaving creme, styling hair. None of which Eldon woke up to witness. Allan did wake up but really didn't care until he got up in the morning. They also had poured koolaid in the coffee pot and put a fake dog turd in the coffee pot as well, fully knowing that this was the place Eldon would head when he first woke up in the morning. The coffee from that pot will never taste the same. Never the less we made shitty jokes about the turd :P. I had decided I was going to take a stroll outside as I had never seen my brother's new home before. It was very beautiful. A trailer slapped to the side of a hill, green grass all around, and a mist in the air giving it that foggy look. My parents and Mom's mom decided to grace us with their presence and we all piled into 2 vehicles and ran off to the church, females in one car, and males in the other. Kinda ashamed of the wedding not because it was bad, but because of how few members of my family was there. Just about all that was in the 2 cars was on the Chappell side of the pews. on the other side it was brimming with people. My oldest brother couldn't make it because of some legal and helth problems. My uncle that lived around the area was under house arrest for too much to drink. Soon after the wedding his wife was put in jail for hot checks. My other grandmother was at the funeral of her brother's. So everybody had something preventing them from attending this wedding. Was a nice, traditional wedding. Nothing too much to brag about. Allan did catch the garder though so I guess he getting married next. We had flown his g/f down from Canada as she was the maid of honor. She got to see it. I was wondering what was going through their minds. After all that garberlygook was done my dad rode with Allan and I and we headed off to see our uncle as he was on house arrest. of course as usual he was drinking. You think he would of learned that drinking was a bad decision the day his father died from liver failure because of the 24/7 drinking. I know I have. I guess it takes more some times. BTW he got arrested on Mother's day because he broke the rules of his house arrest aka drinking, and was caught by the officer comming to check on him.Now he is in a drying out place. We will see how long that keeps him dry. I doubt even a day after he comes home is he sober. Believe it or not during this whole time had anyone offered food (besides at teh wedding). I mean it was nothing but boose. I swear these people drink more boose than water and they wonder why they never have any money for gassing up their car. We finally ordered everyone pizza. Yep Allan and I forked the bill for that one as no one else was willing to do so but they were hungry enough to finish off all the pizza. After supper everyone went to bed once again, this time no raving drunk to keep me up but something did keep me up. A storm blew in about an hour after going to bed and it was bright as day outside because of all the litening. The wind was blowing hard, hail was hitting the trailer. To think my dad slept out in his truck during all this. Lord almighty, I finally got some sleep about 4 am just to wake back up at 5 so we could return home and get Allan back to work. he had to be back by 1. so here was a guy that had only 4 hours of sleep the past 48 hours driving all the way back home on a 6 hour trip. Allan had lost his glasses (still hasn't found them) so I didn't want him driving. What kept me awake was watching that storm off in the distance and trying to catch it. Never was able to reach it though. We made it home about noon and I laid down and got a lil shut eye when the phone called and it was work. No one had shown up to throw the truck that had just rolled in the back door. I went for about 2 hours and couldn't stand up any longer. I told em Id be back in the morning and begin work on the truck. Walla another trip in the past. If I had some pictures I would post em but right now I do not have any copies of the pictures taken but will soon. May post them then.
