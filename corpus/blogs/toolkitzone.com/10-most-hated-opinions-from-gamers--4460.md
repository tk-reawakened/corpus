# 10 Most Hated Opinions From Gamers

by Isolocis on May 31, 2006
I hate it when players: 10. Are highly dependant on game ratings from websites like Gamespot or Game Informer. 9. Think that games should be made for casual gamers instead of hardcore gamers. 8. Like the idea of humanoid races, including elves, dwarves, and gnomes. I've already seen these races in every fantasy genre. 7. Like the idea of "permanent death" in MMOGs. 6. Think that average games (which receive ratings from gaming websites like 5/10 or 2/5) are total garbage. 5. Think that PvP should be removed or excluded from MMOGs. Don't even get me started on this. 4. Believe that games should be easy. Players who want easy games may think that anything harder than easy is too hard. Apparently, games are suppose to be challenging. 3. Put down a game just by its graphics. Therefore, they put down games with dated or old graphics despite the gameplay, story, etc. I'm sure you heard of "Never judge a book by its cover." 2. Hype or praise a game that they did not play. Or even worst: when the game has not been released yet. I believe that this happens to games that show promotional screenshots and promise great features. I'm sorry, but Crysis is my example. 1. Put down another game and then suggest a "better" game. Like for example: "Red Orchestra sucks. Play Battlefield 2 instead because it's better."
