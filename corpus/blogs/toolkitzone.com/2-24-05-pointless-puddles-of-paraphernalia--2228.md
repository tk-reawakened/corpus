# 2/24/05 Pointless Puddles of Paraphernalia

by John@aljo on February 24, 2005
Been a long while since I have updated my blog but wanted to say a couple of things. This may be a long one so if you don't like long blogs just pass this one by. Took a trip to my parents house this weekend. A drive all the way across the state of Texas, from east to west. Was a great feeling seeing my home town and noticing that a few new buisnesses have came into town. The Wal-Mart now stays open 24 hours and has some type of grociery section. They built a new AutoZone right next to the Wal-Mart. Was also nice being able to go to a burrito shop as in east Texas they don't have em on every corner. Matter of fact, I have yet to see one. Was even good to see some old friends as long as they didn't ask that stupid and age old question "Why are you not in School?". By gosh I hate when they ask that. Its as if they think that everyone in the world has to go to college and right after high school no less! What if your not sure what you want to do for the rest of your life? What good would a college education do for you then? I have met so many people that have a degree in something like Chemistry and yet been working at Wal-Mart for 10-15 years of there life because they didn't want to do anything in Chemistry again. Why waste all that money and be paying for loans on something that you are not going to use or not sure you are going to use. I am pretty sure though that in the near future I may go back for buisness classes though. I got it into my head at the moment that I can work on computers. Maybe join the Geek Squad at Best Buy and find out if I really know as much as I think I do. The visit with the parents was wonderful. Also took the small trip to the mountains while I was down there and did some rock climbing. Mostly it was just good to be around the parents and shot golf balls. As for other things in life such as this community, Everything has been put on hold until March just about. Allan's girlfriend has been down from Canada. Of course you prolly know all the details if you been keeping up with him. Myself I been enjoying the time to get out of the house and do something with someone. I guess March 2nd Ill be back on track and beable to work and such things as the chat. BTW I noticed that it always says Offline/Unknown all the time now days. Why is that? Oh well I shall soon have the new one done and running. Little Man I been doing odds and ends on as I find out about them. I wonder if Little Man will be able to enter the Summer Expo? Considering it was actually created several years ago, I am not sure, but I have been updating it so would that be considered a current release of a game? Something I will have to ask around about that. Thats a good run down of what has been going on.

I couldn't agree with you more about the college immidiately after high school. I made that mistake, but luckily I got out after the first semester, and only owed about six grand in school loans, rather than the $55,000+ it would've cost for the entire degree in Computer Engineering Technology, something that sounds interesting, but is completely different than what they initially told me. I notice a of people getting BS (bullshit) degrees in communications, including my best friend Mike, who recently graduated from ASU a few months back. It only took him an entire seven years to get a four year degree, because he switched majors half-way through... My friend Stefanie is getting the same degree. I give them shit about it all the time, and when I confronted Stefanie about why she was getting a communications degree, and not something worthwhile, she replied; "Because I don't know what I want to do yet". O_O. At least she's getting hers from a junior college and not a university, so it doesn't cost nearly as much, but its still a huge waste of money. A communications degree is sort of like the "A+ Certification" of college degrees; where you can tell people that you have a piece of paper that says you know something, but it won't actually get you anywhere. It also lets you save face when your friends/relatives ask you if your going to school. I wouldn't be surprised if some of those friends that ask you why your not going to school are getting communications degrees themselves =D. BTW, my friend Mike now works at a car lot, selling cars, where his communications degree is pretty much worthless, just as I told him. Now you might say that it could help him sell cars, but believe me, he was a good salesman before his degree; and he definately had no problems communicating with people. Don't join the Geek Squad at Best Buy. I wouldn't anyways. Your bound to make more money working for a genuine computer repair shop, and if you'll have the comfort of knowing you didn't work for an underhanded company that charged $40 to install a CD-ROM. If you want to know if you know what your doing with computer hardware, build a PC. If you can build a PC from scratch, you probably know what your doing. As far as your game goes, there's no reason you shouldn't be able to enter it into the Expo. I could enter the four year old demo of Godiva:Alpha if I wanted. Granted, I probably wouldn't win any awards; but lets face it, with the current competition, that's not likely to happen no matter how much I work on my game. **lot**
