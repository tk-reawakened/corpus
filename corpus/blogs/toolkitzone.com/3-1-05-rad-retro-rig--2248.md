# 3/1/05 Rad Retro Rig

by John@aljo on March 1, 2005
Well since I have not much more to talk about and since I owe some pictures to Spyder, I am going to talk about my newest PC. It can also be considered my oldest system because Ive actually had it for going on 17 years. May I introduce my Nintendo. Might be asking what the hell is that tape running out the front. Its a temporary IDE cable until I can get an IDE to USB convertor. My NES is actually my PC. NESPC as http://junkmachine.com puts it. Basically what I did is gutted my NES and put my PC parts in there. Of course taking shorcuts means you will not beable to stuff everything into that tiny little box. Never the less even after shortcut taking and not being able to put the cdrom drive in there I have a plan for that and will reveal it at the end of this blog. This is the inside of my NES. A little more crowded than the standard model NES-001 eh? :P At the top of the picture there is the top of the NESPC it contains a 4 cm fan and the motherboard velcroed to the top. This is one of those Mini-ITX boards made by VIA and has an EPIA 1 Ghz Processor on it. Granted this is not the fastested PC in the world but it does everthing I could ever ask of it. It has one sting of DDR 2100 512 MB RAM in it to help it excel in nearly any torture I put it through. That would be all that is in the top. The bottom half of the NES, however, is crawling with several things. In the bottom right of the picture there is the 80 GB 7200 RPM hard drive. These Samsung hard drives are very cheap at Microcenter ($56) and I got 3 of them floating around in differnt computers around the house. On the bottom left hand side of the picture we have my gutted and minimalized 300W Power Supply that I got at Microcenter for $30. If I would of waited until my Income Tax return would of came in I could of put a laptop power coord on the NES and fit the cd drive into the NESPC but felt it was too far away and wanted to get on with the building of the computer. in the top right of the bottom portion we have the original power and reset buttons and LED that came with the NES. They are all functional. An added twist I added was a blue LED for the hard drive light. I taped it to the top of the plastic LED light chenneling device that comes standard in the NES to give the red light an eirie blue halo when the hard drive is active. On the top left of the bottom section we have the controller ports. Player one port is rigged up so that a modified keyboard can be hooked up to it. I plan to change this to where I have a special adapted instead of having to modify every keyboard I ever use on this thing. Just sauter me a Ps/2 port to the end of a controller plug and I will have it made. The player two port is for a real NES Controller. I found a while back a kit to turn your original NES controllers into a USB Controller for your PC. I used that kit on the port in the inside of the NES and hooked it up to an internal USB jack so now any time you wish you can hook up a NES Controller to use on original games and as I got it set up right now with Joystick to Mouse 3, I can use the Controller as a mouse when Im not playing games with it. All of that is inside this NES. This is what the Desktop looks like: And that would be a tour of my Computer :). BTW it hooks up to the TV too :p by the by, the CD drive is going to look like a nintendo cartridge have I get done with it.
