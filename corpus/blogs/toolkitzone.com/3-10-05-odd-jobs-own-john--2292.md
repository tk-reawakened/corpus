# 3/10/05 Odd Jobs own John

by John@aljo on March 11, 2005
Today was my day of from real work. Found out I got another raise so now Im up to that wonderful $7.65 an hour. Not bad living at all though Im only part time :(. Got a $30 fee stopped on my bank account because I went over $.77 and tried to correct it by putting in $1 before my paycheck came in. I am just glad I got friends at the bank. Still no cable guy to change the sockets on the wall even though they said it would of been yesterday that they would of done it. Had an odd job to do tonight with someones computer. The lady had Windows ME on it and wanted it wiped and put Windows XP on it. We got most of it done for her. She only has to put a few software titles on there, otherwise got internet set back up and everything. Got paid $100 for 4 hours of work on the puter. I say not bad living at all. Also worked on the chat for most the morning and afternoon. We managed to get 54 users on the chat. we had one or 2 that had problems staying on but otherwise it was solid as a rock. I got the side to have icons and color now, fixed a bug or two, added /me, added support for the client to know who was admin or not, partially added abuse. Now I am working on it notifying the administration. Then I need to make it create a file with all the users online, then I need to add in the admin panel, and got to add the differnt ban systems. More stuff just seems to keep popping up the more I think about it. finally it seems all I got left to do until its ready to be used regularly by the public is making it where multiple chat rooms could be ran by the same server, some small tweaks to configurability, and possibly Private messaging. Oh and of course the emoticons. Its getting closer and closer to the end each day and I praise the lord that it is because I need to clean the damn house. Stupid cat has pissed on everything. No matter what I do I can't get the damn thing to stop unless I keep the cat box changed every other day. I mean this guy is like Peewee the ultra pisser. Seems like he uses the bathroom half his life and the other half he running like crazy. Oh well nothing I can't handle. Tommarrow going to take a lil break from the chat and clean house then at 3 go to work. I hope this what happens tommarrow anyway. we will see.
