# 3/11/05 Typical TwentyFour Tings and Dings of Time

by John@aljo on March 11, 2005
got up bout 8:30 this morning to the cat meowing at the window. Apparently a female has been passing by frequently. He sees that I rise and rapidly makes his way to my lap and to rub on my face where he stands and meows for 5 minutes until I finally awake all the way. I go and feed him some breakfast and fill up his water then come back to the computer where I set to check my email and see what has happened over night on the server, email, and TKZ. The cat then hops in my lap wanting to be petted, I graciously do his will then toss him out of my lap to the other end of the room. He comes back running full speed. Typical morning for me. After Allan left for work I started picking up on the house a bit. Trying to make sure the living room was at least clean so when our room mate, Fabian, desided to come home from Galviston, it would be nice and clean for his arrival and he wouldn't write one of those nasty letters that the house wasn't clean. Next time I get one of those I'm gonna say "if you don't like it then clean it." In any case the shared rooms are clean now. Doing my fill of cleaning, I decided to head off to Wal-Mart. Stopped at the stop light just down the road and checked out the gas prices. $1.94, the highest I have ever seen the cheapest gas station ever go sense I have lived here in Dallas. Mind you I have seen it that high in Pecos. I think they just raise and lower the gas when they want to and make some bull corn excuse of why it is the way it is. I mean $.24 over night just isn't right. Might as well be putting gallons of milk in my car. Next paycheck Im gonna spend my spending money on getting a new wheel for my bike so Im not spending all my money on my car. made it to Wal-Mart to get a few things, AAA batteries, Fragrance oil simmer basons, and a wireless keyboard for the NES. My shorts kept trying to fall down so I decided to get me a belt while I was there. Stopped by work before I came home to show the manager that I had bought $10 worth of Shamrocks for the Muscular Distrophy thing then mozied on home. Got back home to notice my cable channel lineup had changed some but they never did change the sockets on the wall. I will complain to them about that tommarrow when I get home from work. After resetting my TV channels I found the Weather Channel and watched it for a while as I set up that new wireless keyboard. Damn thing don't like being near the TV. I guess it gives off too much interference. Having completed fiddling with the keyboard I got ready for work and checked the mail. Got our first piece of the new server in today, a 512 MB 2100 DDR chip. Upon my arrival to work I had several tasks to do. The first one was to try and get as many of those stupid plastic 15" inflatable balls out of the backroom as possible. I then got some coolers down and displayed them up on top of the Meat Deli freezers where all the rest of the coolers went. Next on the TODO: list was to make signs for all the stuff on top of these freezers so maybe some lost soul would buy our highly over priced coolers. Hehe when making the signs I got caught up talking to the bakery lady and starring and a wonderful looking Strawberry Pudding cake she had whipped up. This cake looked so wonderfully delicouse that I swear it called out to me "Eat me!" Needless to say all my signs printed wrong and I had to go back into the database of non-used signs to find them. The rest of the night was spent outside trying to display Potting Soil and other gardening soils & dungs to make them look nice. Can you believe a 40 lb bag of dirt will cost you a properly sanctioned price of $1.49? I was really expecting higher. What was left of the night was spent throwing away the garbage the other departments left at the trash compacter. The company now locks the trash compacter so no one puts sellable product in there then later that night from the outside grab it. Lets just say I got me a nice meal from produce. Prolly threw away 100 lb worth of bananas tonight. After that I decided there wasn't much more I wanted to stick around and do so I came home 30 minutes early from work. When I got home I started the routine cycle of Laundry, checking server, email and TKZ. Listened to the new Hedghog heaven on http://ocremix.org. Not so much as remixes as they are the same ole songs played with better instruments. Now I set here typing my blog. Hehe now you have read a typical day in my shoes... not much goes on does it?
