# 3/6/05 Restless After Asimir Resignation

by John@aljo on March 7, 2005
Damn I should go into Paper title making. anyhow I know this is a stupid thing to loose sleep over but it keeps on bugging me. Of course if your and active member that reads the General Chat you know by now Asimir has resigned from being Coding manager. If you been here for about 6 months you probably even knew that I was coding manager at the time. I had to resign because of Life issues. I also felt that I wasn't doing a very good job despite what Xavier would say. I mean I would miss days to check the damn thing in frequent spurts, my spelling was bad, yet I enjoyed every minute of it. I am just that way. I feel that I am not necessarily good at anything but that what makes me strive to be better. I guess I put a high standard on myself to keep going for and each time I reach it I raise the bar a lil higher and if I can't reach it I get upset and feel I am not doing a good job at it. Any way I been looking for a chance to get this position back. Though I felt that Asimir earned it far and square I figured I would just wait until he desided he didn't want to do it any more and I would reapply to get the job again. This time I got unexpected competition and reasons it could go ways other than for me. Attention to those who read this that these are my views and solely can be contributed to me so do not take it out on anybody else that may be mentioned here. PM me if questions arise. So far publicly announced as interested is me, Colin, and Double E. Notice Xavier didn't ask for applications this time around but we just said we are interested in feeling the position so it could go that niether one of the 3 of us will get it. some times I wish this thing wasn't public but I must type this somewhere so that I feel statisfied and get some sleep. What is bothering me is Colin has expressed his interest in the position. We all know how Colin has a way of taking over things and calling it his own. well what Im afraid of is that if he got the position that the peacefulness of the coding section will be well, disturbed. Also with staffy comes Modship wich in turn means he can edit & delete posts. My concern is what I know of colin that posts and stuff could just *dissapear* or be edited. Who knows maybe I don't know him well enough to make such and accusation but it bothers me that the balance will be upset. I see this going several differnt ways. If I know Xavier right he has had a small storming session with this, got fed up with it, and left it for later to decide. He has probably thought about how Colin might be affected if he was/was not hired. I know I have and that is where it has been bothering me tonight. I almost want him to get the part so that what might happen won't happen. But then again I don't because of the above paragraph. Never the less writing this blog probably ruins my chances but let pass if it may. I just got to get it off my mind so I have peace of mind and able to sleep and get rested before 5 AM when I have to be up to get ready for work. Why something like this bothering me? I don't got a clue. It would be nice if it wasn't. I think what is going to happen is we will see some new blood into the Code Manager spot. Possibly Double E. Me and Colin had a debate and as you guessed it he powns me on such qualifications as coding experience and knowing the TK. I mean he coded part of the freaken thing though I disagree with him calling it his project. Well now that I got this out of my system maybe I won't be worried about it and go back to sleep. I don't see how in the world. I fell asleep for about an hour then my eyes pop wide open thinking about this. Maybe I am once again to involved in something I enjoy? It has happened before and it came back to haunt me and ruin my life. will it happen again this time with the TK? I love being involved do not get me wrong about that but maybe I already got the involvment I need. I got the Chat I am working on. Maybe thats the only thing I should involve myself in now? Though I remeber enjoying doing the Code Section as well too. Testing out the code and rating it good or not. Well in any case it is Xaviers decision on who gets put in the spot. No one should question his word because it is his own that he has made for the community and frankly he has the right to let anybody he wants to run what section he wants based off what ever reasoning he has for the person running it. Now Im just rambling. Ill talk at yall tommarra. Now im going to get some sleep now that I have calmed down.
