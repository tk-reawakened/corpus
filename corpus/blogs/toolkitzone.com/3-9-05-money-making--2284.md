# 3/9/05 Money Making?

by John@aljo on March 9, 2005
Today they were supposed to of came and changed out our cable sockets on the wall. So far it has not been done. I just want them to hurry up and get it over with so we don't have to worry about our cable going out for a while. Woke up about 8:45 AM this morning and cleaned on the house for a moment or 2 trying to make it looks somewhat presentable when they do come into our house. Then I got me some breakfast then worked a little bit on the chat before I made a needless trip to work. I thought I went to work at noon today but is seems it was at 3. In any case I told them to call me when the truck got there so I could spend all my time doing that instead of wasting it by setting there with nothing but Zoning to do. After getting all that wonderful work buisness out of the way I come home to find a private message from Dan. Woowee was I suprised by the words that came from his keyboard. He is actually interested in my chat and wants to use it as part of the service he sales on the internet. How could I refuse an offer like that. Just to know my software was wanted somewhere was enough but he took it a lil farther by offering money as an inscentive. How cool is that!?! Now that I have accepted the offer there will have to be a few changes to the chat that normally I would of let slide as I would of been the only one to of used the thing. Instead of just allowing one chat room per server I need to now allow for multiple chatrooms to spawn from teh one server. Not a hard feat at all. It just means a little more extra checking when the server recieves a message and sends a message. As I mentioned below I have tested the Max capacity (or lack of) and found it well suited for its uses. For now on we shouldn't have any problems with people not getting on. I have it, and everything that previously ran on my server computer including the other chat server, and I got the damn thing up to 17 people before I said there wasn't any limit. We shall see now if that is true with this multiple chat per server. If I can manage it, if it doesn't work well on my server, I may ask Dan to allow for a spot for TK for free for maybe even a reduction in my pay. Yes I would do that for yall :).
