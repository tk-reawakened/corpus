# 5/25/05 From Dawn Till Dusk

by John@aljo on April 25, 2005
wow.... thats the only word I can think of at the events thats been going on the past couple of days. It starts off earlier this week at a party for one of the long time employees of Albertsons. She was in our department and did all of the technical rigamaroe that none of us have ever been trained to do. Welp now she is gone, and I had another guy quit on me (seems to be a trend here). Now it is I, I am now the "Drug Second" so I did get a promotion :). This is my first week of being in this new position and boy it has been tough. Not so much because of the position but because of the events that have been going on. As I had said one of my workers quit on me and another one might as well have retired :P so there was only 3 of us in the department the past couple of days. The one that quit left a pallet of unstocked merchanidise in the back room for me on top of 2 other truck loads of merchandise I had to do that very same day. So naturally, yesterday, I was at work for 12 hours from 8 to 8. Today it was spent doing much the same thing still trying to catch up from the loss, and also getting ready for inventory. I stayed from 7:45 to 9 today then clocked out waiting for my brother to get off work. While setting there waiting I here this roaring sound like you stuck a bunch of marbles in a box of fixed pegs and shook it except this was 2 octaves lower. Then I hear my boss scream. I run to see what happened and was amazed to find that one of the pallets she was putting on the top layer of the pallet docks was toppled over, we were trying to do something with all the stuff grocery had left in our backroom, well she did :P. we had steak sauce, liquid smoke, soda, molasses, and salsa all over the floor and all the pallets between the toppled one and the floor. I climbed up there (being the monkey that I am) and salvaged 2 out of the 3 crates still hanging on to the pallet. by this time you probably relized I clocked back in because I felt sorry for my boss and as a friend knew she needed help. There was no way I was gonna leave it all to herself to do it. Maybe if it was somebody I hate :P. In any case it took us 3 hours with 3 people cleaning up the mess that was caused by a wobbly pallet. I worked from 7:45 to 11:40 today. So I worked a total of 15 hours today, 11 hours yesterday... hmmm seems like Im gonna have a good paycheck :P nearly 30 hours over 2 days :P
