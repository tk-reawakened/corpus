# 9/10/04 The Real World

by John@aljo on September 10, 2004
Bah too bad the real world isn't like it is in Static Shock or Batman and Robin where the theives always get caught and prossicuted. Nope the real world is where they get caught and get away. I said this leading up to a story that happened at work today I was setting up an end cap full of merchandise today when I hear a "Pop!" then a "SQeeeeeesh!". I turn the corner to find 2 little kids spraying glow in the dark silly string all over the place. I tell em "you gonna have to pay for that." and their words was "uh oh". I ask Where is your parents? they take me to this woman and I splain to her that her kids were doing this and that them or her are going to have to pay for the can of silly string. She says "I ain gonna pay for that thing there!" I say fine then let me call one of my managers. When I finally get one over there (None of em wanted to mess witht he situation) The lil girl said "I just sprayed it." making it sound liek she just sprayed a lil bit to see what color it was. The Lady says I aint gonna pay for this thing and here is this guy here making it out like my niece had stole something. All the manager says is yes ma'am. and the woman walks off. I was so damn pissed because not only had they not paid for it or anything but I found out that we can't really do too much about it besides what I did. So I went back and cleaned up the 4 foot section that was covered in silly string. I just know now someone is gonna get a 1/4 empty can of silly string now and there was nothing I could do about it besides what I did do. That just steams my clams. Well now ya know about my work day. The good news is that tonight Im going to a classical music concert. This is gonna be awesome!
