# 9/11/04 I'm Hot!

by John@aljo on September 11, 2004
Oh yes Im just the hottest thing on this earth. *cough* More like maybe the ugliest. Not really. Actually My AC went out in my apartment last night. What in the hell! Just wasn't a good day yesterday at all. I got fans all over the freaken place today. Today, 9/11. gonna always remeber that day too well. I figure we gonna here everyone's story today so why not I begin it. I had woke up that morning bout 7:45. All my family was over because my grandpa had died 2 days earlier. His liver finally went out after 5 years of 24/7 drinking. Not the best man in the world but he did earn a decent funeral at least. Any how My mom had just drove out of the drive way to make a run to town. We were watching Cartoon Network. Think it was Bugs Bunny or something when my mom calls on the Telephone and yells,"We're under attack! Turn on the tv quickly!" We thought our mom had gone nuts! So we just try to calm her down over the phone and calmly turn the channel to CNN. Sure enough you see this big gaping hole in a building. I figured at first maybe it was an accidental plane crash. That was the case till bout 5 minutes later when live on TV I saw a second plane crash into the other building close by. We were glued to the tv for and hour or 2 then we decided to go to our grandma's (bout 15 acres down the road). There I was with our uncles and we just kinda carried on as usual with a dred in the back of our minds. That wasn't gonna ruin the fun we had planned for the day though. Our grandpa had left a bunch of old gun powder just laying around. Hehe we found us some copper tubing and then set my grandma's trash burning pit ablaze. Poped a piece of that copper tubing in the door of the pit and ran for cover. Bout 3 seconds afterwards you here a big BOOOM! Ha Ha was so funny. Grandma and Aunt comes out of the house screaming, thinking we were getting bombed. My uncles just set there and laugh there heads off but I kinda simpathized with my grandma and aunt. I believe it was also that day that we had the funeral. It was more wierd than it was sad. To see this man with makeup and all that stuff on him. Wasn't natural looking at all. One of my uncles that disowned the family was their at the funeral. We thought it a perfect oppurtunity to change his mind. He left the family because he felt his brothers was stealing my grandpa's guns while he was drunk when actually it was my grandpas drinking buddies wich mainly was a bunch of wetbacks. That being said he agreed to come around and not hide his face. I haven't seen hide of hair of him since then. Afterwards we had a big ole dinner and watched the news. Saw that the Pentagon was hit also. Really pissed us all off and wished Clinton would of left some of the old rednecks in the army so we could of had whoever done this to us. It was a Heated conversation for sure. Although the day's actions didnt' reflect it. We were all afraid of what was gonna start happening. Oh hell! there was just a wreck outside. I think Im gonna go check it out in just a second. Wierd time is 9:11 AM. Welp this gonna be a wierd day after all eh?
