# 9/7/04 Isn't Everything Wonderful now?

by John@aljo on September 8, 2004
Well today I wake up bout 9 am (wow why so late?). Nothing interesting to do. Get up and get some ceral. Just as soon as I pour a bowl my friend Richard walks in the door. He had a program that was due for his comp sci class and he doesn't have a puter to do the work at his house until he gets his financial aid in. So he was using mine. I helped him with his programming. Boy Im glad my programming style has changed drastically. Methods and classes are sure much prettier than top down design. He does a somewhat top down design that our High School comp sci teacher taught us. It is horrible. Well after adjusting to that style I was actually able to help him once again. I take a stroll outside today and it feels so wonderful outside. The weather was awesome. A meer 83 degrees F. outside and only 51% humidity today. I take off for work about 2 pm. I get there in a happy go lucky mood and every one else seems to be about ready to chop each others heads off. Today Inventory had to be finished. The whole day was a sinch for me. I just had to stock stray items they found and did not want to count. Also got to put together a barboque grill today as well. Not a bad day at all I feel. Called my parents today. Found out my car right now is not running. The spark plugs weren't fireing. Oh well, like I got the money to go take an 8 hour trip to Pecos to get it. Right now all I really have is my bike. It needs a tune up itself. I read about Tk 3.0.4. If it really is as good as its claimed to be I will be impressed, but there have been promises before about this kinda stuff before. wasn't 3.0.3 supposed to be a speed up in RPGCode? and what we get? a disfunctional version that was bloated as hell. Seemed the only things that did work was the trans3 actually closed (My fix) and the board editor had a few pluses to it. I was so satisified actually with 3.0.2 and the unofficial fix to it but stupid me had to install 3.0.3 over it and afterwards couldn't get the fix again so everything was crap again. I mean come one Little Man was actually getting done and now freaken methods wouldn't even work. But like they say It always could get worse. No but Im confident that this one will be better. I figured the testers would leak out versions of 3.0.4. seeing those comments and junk about it really made me see how childish this community could be. If I was one of those testers I would keep the info between me and the developement team. I wouldn't be out giving it out to little wieners that think they are special just because they know one of the testers. Believe it or not I take the work here very seriously. I keep telling myself I can do better than what I have been doing. Xavier can tell ya how I feel about my performance here. I feel that I could be lots better and am always afraid I wasn't doing a good enough job for the community and for Xavier himself. He keeps saying Hell you do a great job but I don't feel I have done that great. Maybe my low self esteem getting in the way or what a counselor once said to me "high expectations of myself", either way I feel like I have failed alot of the times in the job that I was hired to do here. I see Asimir do it and its just neat seeing how well he does it and yet I feel I can't do it well at all. Blah, Ill keep trying is all I can say. Well think I preached enough tonight. Talk at ya laters.
