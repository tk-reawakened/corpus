# A touch of class

by Ryukenshen on June 2, 2006
I have decided to make my blod entries hotter. Yay for HTML. The expo's a lot of fun. I got some recognition, too. But for those who haven't...visit my showcase, download the demo! Earlier today, my sister went to her piano lesson. I stayed behind. Doesn't it figure that she saw a friend of mine at the shop? Anyway, I pretty much just sat around and did nothing. Sounds like fun, eh? I got in a little Playstation, but the sounds was all messed up, so it was a little dull. It's working now, but I'm not sure if that's because I gave it a good whack or it just started...working. Whatever. Did a bit more on the forest background. Taking a lot of time, that one. For the most part, I've been busy at the expo, or going through the first draft of my revision of the story I submitted for the Writing Contest so long ago, but I've also been brainstorming some ideas for the dungeons for the Story Demo. There will be two. One will be more of a tutorial dungeon, the other an actual obstacle. So, yeah, I haven't been busy - you've heard that before, if you read my other recent entries - but I haven't been doing nothing at all, either. *whistles Zelda theme*
