# aaahhhh....

by spoopryme on July 6, 2006
still recovering from the madness that was the 4th of july get together. got burnt as per usual, but this time nothing serious. a few people went a tad overboard with the bottle rockets this year...for about 6 solid hours there were 8 people setting the damn things off and we still had a pile left over at the end of the night! heh. still groggy as my sleep schedule got altered but that is slowly falling back into place as well, though that would be easier if the cats werent all frisky now that the weather here is cooling off. just finished reading the article about the potential changes in just the tk3's board editor. with the inclusion of vectors, tiled boards will no longer be of any particular use except for us old timers who make heavy use of em. just like with the tile bitmaps, things are progressing away from the simplicity of the earlier versions of the tk into somthing a tad more complex. dont know how i feel about it yet. im sure it will be cool, just makes me hesitant to go any further with my game knowing that somthing like the board editor and the very nature of making boards is changing like that. i would prefer to do my project with all native bits, rather than converting them from the old into the new, if that makes any sense. ill have to wait and see.
