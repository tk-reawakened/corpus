# After effects of a hard day of practice.....

by Ivan on August 16, 2005
Wow. All I can say is I am beat like never before. I always thought football to be a "middle" sport but heck, its one of the hardest out there if not the hardest. 2 days of practice gone by and I feel like I'm going to die. I can hardly sit on my butt right no typing this. But you gotta do what you gotta do, and visiting this community is one of them things :). The more I think about it , the more it looks like Football is based on the military of old. Where you had right flanks, left flanks, and if someone broke throughthe first line of defense , there would be a backup line etc. its all about formations, strategys etc. Watching football games and not ever playing real ones, I thought it was really boring. Now I can actually watch the games and pick someting up. It is very difficult though. Of course there are people who already know all the positions plays etc. and it makes it a lot easier on their asses, but that , regrettibaly (sp?) does not hold true for me. I have picked a lot though, in a mere 2 days. My mind is filled with all the hooks, curls, flat, hook-to-curls,hookey dookey 38. It is all confusing and new to me. Like a new programming language( i dont know one good) except its probably harder to learn a whole nw proggramming language than the football formations. But all in all its about strategy. Having one of the hardest positions doesnt help any in giving me any slack at all. I play DB (Defensive Back, Corners) and shit, you gotta run backwards, as fast as the opposing guy can run forward. And you're not running straight backward, you are holding a squatlike position in which you run backward. Makes things difficult/ It all pays off though in the end , hopefully we can get our 5th League Championship (Varsity team) and when I become Varsity, my team can win too. But now, its all work work and work. And Zyr , if you're reading this (doubt it) I hope us, JV guys and the Varsity guys will win against your Tonawanda teams.
