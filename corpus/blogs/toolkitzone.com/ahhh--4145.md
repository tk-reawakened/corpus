# AHHH!!!

by Alex on March 29, 2006
I've mentioned things about my roommate before. He drives me crazy sometimes but he's generally a good guy. Yesterday, I almost took the liberty of putting him in the hospital. Its a short story, he was talking about his trip to Washington DC and the only thing he had said anything about was the Smithsonian Air and Space Museum. I simply told him that there were way better things to see in DC and he laughed at me like I was some idiot. My jerk reaction was to stand up and ask him what his problem was, but I stopped myself while at the same time wishing I had helped him see the birdies flying in circles over his head. I instead told him that the Lincoln Memorial was better than the entire Museum (as I had been to both), but it wasn't true and I didn't care because I was so pissed. Last night I only got one hour of sleep. I could not go to sleep, until a little over an hour before my class was supposed to start. Now I have some throbbing headache that won't go away since I'm exhausted.
