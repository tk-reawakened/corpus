# Alcohol & Antidepressants - Farewell My School!

by Darwin on April 29, 2006
Note #1: All names are anglicised for the sake of easier reading. Yesterday we had an official "goodbye" at the school, on our final day before the exams. It started with a mass - in fact, the last church mass in my whole damn life :P That thought made it almost enjoyable. Then the pirncipal gave out the awards (I got one for getting to the 2nd part of the English "school olympics") and then was the main fun part - the younger classes, traditionally, organised a farewell with awards given to people for their particular features / personality and some scenes illustrating events connected with us. The first part was the awards - each had a music theme. Some were great, some were straightly rude (one girl got an award for "lung capacity" - ie. big breasts; another one got one for "ligh behaviour" - ie. being a slut, literally; both of these features would be hard to deny, but they could think of something nicer). I got one with a comment "The school never had such an individual before": large female briefs for comedic effect (an allusion to me showing a bare ass :P) and a knife (an allosion to several my behaviours involving knives). My theme song was Korn's "Twist", which I used to sing vocally in the middle school. The next part was "The calss in 40 years", showing people doing strange things or performing jobs in the supposed future. Not bad, though my pal Chris got a rather vague mentioning in a cabaret though he's one of the most "iconic" people of our class and certainly the most charismatic one. I got mentioned in the news (run by Mozell, another guy who led some school events and apparently got a fame of being a good journal) as performing unsuccesful attempts to prove gravity doesn't exist and pregnacy as a myth (I mentioned stuff like that in the middle school due to my overall skepticism :P). Overally, another lacking feature was that not everyone got mentioned - for God's sake, honestly, when people watch such things, they expect to have something about them, too, and since that's the last encounter with our class, that should be done at lseat briefly. Other than that' the thing was great because of one simple fundamental reason: it was fun! Some references were obvious, other "fit on force", but you cou/d overally have a laugh. Then our whole class went to Alhambra restaurant, ate a pizza, drank beer, discussed the bad old days and sweared at the teachers (who succesfully weren't there). One of the greatest moments of the day, then everyone said a ritual goodbye to everyone else, there were hugs, number exchanges, talks about future parties and meetings, and obviously all despite the fact that half of the people, boys and girls, includng me, then happily went to the park together (despite all the farewells ;)). At this point I should mentioned my pal Richard got drunk as hell and offered a comedic effect on the way to the park, especially in his "Johny Knoxville" glasses borrowed from another guy, Fiddler. So, we went to the aforementioned park. We sat down, waited for Kate who was supposed to come there, got sworn at my a bunch of drunk guys which outnumbered us, so we changed the place and headed to the bridge, despite Fiddler claiming he knows Aikido and Dziewiur* wanting to show him the finger and run away. Yeah, easy to talk, they were both drunk as hell :P At the bridge, we talked more about future parties, Dziewiur made some photos, we hugged ourselves goodbye and me, Richard, Dziewiur, Fiddler and Oxy* went to Richard's house, on the way Dziewiur took a photo of Richard pissing and we discussed aggressive behaviour of people. Ah, yes, we bought some more beer on the way. We arrived at Richard's, drank the beer and began watching "Green Street Holligans" on the computer after a debate of what to watch. "Watching" may be a bad term - we all watched the movie before so we knew it, beside Dziewiur (who watched it, too, but didn't remember because he was drunk or high or something), so the more sober** part of us (me, Oxy, Dziewiur) tried learning to speak the London slang as in the movie ("ya, may'"), "for fuck's sake, stop sayin' socca" - with the perfect British accent, beats all the American vernacular English ). The less sober part of us****... errr. Talking nonsense, repeating nonsensical sayings, and at one point Richard just laid down on the floor next to a part of a devastated wardrobe Then, Exy and Dziewiur left, and we went toour pal Nigger's***** place, where he and his girlfriend Ann joined at the pizza place. Of cournse, the way to Niggert's was long, zigzag routed and full of attractions which distracted us, including meeting with our pal Johhny who watche an amateur concert where his pal, "Satan Metal", played the drums. So we stood under the scene and shouted "Satan! Satan!" to get the guy's attention though the music was something of a christian rock and a priest was playing the keyboard. Must have looked fun... Either way, we miracully got to Nigger's just to go back to Alhambra. Richard "ran across" some pal of his and organised marijuana smoking for himself (he has talent ;)), then went to the toilet to kiss the porcelain god, Fiddler was lying like wasted, and we waited long for a pizza, so Richard at one point said "That damn slut says 10 more minutes" while the girl working at Ashambra which he asked about the time until pizza arrives was just standing behind. :P She looked weirdly, and we wondered if she gets pissed and asks the cook to put some rape pill into our pizza and we'll wake up in the kitchen with sore asses the next day. Either way, it got dark, the climate got bad, no serious parties were around and I didn't really want to go back to Richard's, so I went home.
