# Also Sprach Zarathustra

by Darwin on April 5, 2006
Today morning I woke up, did some push-ups, listened to Asfalt Tango by Fanfare Ciocarlia (what I listen to over and over again now, gotta download more songs by the band but just that one rocks) and I felt a WILL TO POWER, the first characteristic of having the predispositions to become an ÜBERMENSCH. A long time ago have I discarded the religious utopianism and the submissive, conformist illusions of pantheism. Since I have been abandoned by hope and am aware of the bare truth as it is, in order to survive in this fucked-up world, I can't be a decadent fool oscillating between bare hatred for this reality and the attempts to justify it. I must develop inner strength and instead of following blind pseudo-moral rules, I must be the maker of morality myself. My favourite German word: Sauber Fun fact: Since we're all bound within physical organisms, we're never free. But since we aren't conscious either, we can't be imprisoned either. We are not - we just aren't, and that's one fucking word, "to aren't" is the infinitive form, I make the rules here, thus spoke Darwin.
