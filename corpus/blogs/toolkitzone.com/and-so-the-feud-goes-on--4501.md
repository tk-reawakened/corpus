# And so the feud goes on-

by Icbim on June 10, 2006
Man, I just went to go get a new chord to my amp and some chinese food and I had to embarrass some body out in public. So, wait, lemme tell this. So me and my mom are going out to get a new chord to my amp, right? Afterwards we're all like "Hey, i'm hungry...How about some chinese food" and i'm all like "Yeah, sure, why not." So we go to the mall to get some chinese food to go 'cause the mall's chinese food is always the best at a low price...And here comes an old "pal" of mine, goes by the name of Jake, from the seventh grade out of nowhere acting all big and badass. See, but Jake wants to get me back so bad because I always picked on the kid since we met...I mean I gave him a little slap into reality in 7th grade infront of his friends and they all made fun of him...I shanksed (Dunno how to spell it...The one where you pull down shorts to embarass people) more than a few times and stuff....But today ousted it. Out in the public, out at the fucking mall...He came up trying to be badass and said in that punk tone of voice "Hey, look, it's mr. music"...Which I dont mind that name...but the way he said it kinda pissed me off like he thinks i'm a fake or something...so he tries being a punk saying stuff like "Why are you hanging around with your mommy" and shit like that...So i'm silent....and I look around...then I look down...And I look at him with a smirk on my face and I yell out "Good night Jake! When's the last time you brushed your fucking teeth!". Right then everybody in the area looked over and some laughed, other were like "Wtf". I looked at Jakes face and it was red as hell...and then I left the area to enjoy some electric guitar and chinese food. So i'm satisfied. I dont think it ousted the pulling down the shorts...but i'm satisfied. I did get in trouble with my mom...but it was worth it. Well, anyways, I was supposed to go swimming today but we ended up cancelling it off because my two cousins wanted to stay home 'cause last night they bought the new Prince of persia...Kinda ruined it for the rest of us who wanted to go...Ruined my day... I mean I lost all energy to do anything :P. So this saturday blows... Music-wise.....Since I got a brand spanking new chord to my amp, I can finally get back on my feet with rock instead of the soft acoustic shit (not saying that i'm never gonna make anymore acoustic stuff, I just need some rock in my life). Like I said a blog or two ago, my main influence is the band N*E*R*D (initials mean "No one ever really dies) as they play some of the more pop-rock with a hip-hop beat just to get you up and moving (i.e. "She wants to move" and "Fly-or-die")...So i'm gonna post more blogs about the progress on any new songs. I think I also may have a drummer...he's a good friend of mine and is always busy with music 'cause he's like the only drummer (in our grade atleast...We dont have that many drummers at our school...nor bassist)... He said he might be able to do some tracks with me. Well...that's about it...my mom found someone in the yellow books to be my driving instructor for the next two weeks 'cause my mom never took me driving since I got my permit. She was to scared. So hopefully I can get back into driving with this driving instructor and get my drivers liscense by the time this summer ends. I guess i'm outta here. Maybe play a game or go outside and brush up on some soccer skills.
