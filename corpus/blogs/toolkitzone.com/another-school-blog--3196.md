# Another school blog....

by DarkMoon on August 29, 2005
Yep, you read it right. But I'm not going to repeat everyone by saying "School started up again...blah blah blah...so now I won't be able to come around here...blah blah blah...I won't be able to work on my game(s)...blah blah blah...I'll have a ton of homework...blah blah blah. I hate school. I hate my teachers, school is stupid...blah blah blah blah blah..." and so forth. I think you get the idea. Anyway, it is true school has started again around here. But my main concern is about football season, also known as soccer. Practices are right after school, so that puts me home pretty late. Gah, I broke my finger again, which is not cool at all, especially when you consider that I'm goal keeper. At least this isn't the first time that I've broken my finger. In fact, it's the third time! My father said that he wanted to give me a stupidity award because I managed to break my finger three times in only 18 months! Well, at any rate, I won't be able to do much work starting now. I just may be getting my job back, so that'll throw off my time for TK stuff. I've got quite a few things going on ranging from what I've mentioned to writing my two stories to working on my music. Working on my game and hanging around here just doesn't have as much time as it did in the summer. I'll probably only be around in the evening...which I know doesn't really matter to anyone too much because we're all pretty far spread across the globe. I think you get the idea though, it's the same for everyone: school = less time for TKZ. Beh, at least there isn't too much left...for me anyway.
