Well, my first 2 exams out of the four are tommorrow, also the day that the expo begins....I'll get home early though, so that means I get to enjoy the expo soon and not have to sit through classes thinking about it. I'm planning out what i'm gonna do after my exams :P...Kinda sad but i'm looking forward to it. Gonna bring in a Musicians Friend catalog and stuff... Back on the exams....Tommorrow I have to take two. English and Computer Applications. English is gonna be hella long but hella easy. My teacher's a total bitch though and wont let us listen to our cd players. After that we have what our school calls "Brunch" or as I like to call it "let all the kids run damn wild across the school grounds and watch total destruction and choas uproar in bloody hell"....But we'll keep it around the brunch area. Just to keep things simple. After that it's Computer Applications which is the coolest class ever 'cause I own that class :o. After that i'm gonna hitch a ride from geoff and off to home. Geoff's staying for about a hour after that..... I'm gonna show him Antique 'cause that was a comic I was making for weeks that he seemed to like...Now he gets to see the game. Friday is my last day at school...Finishing it off with the biology exam and art exam. The biology exam is what i'm stressing about cause i'm barely failing biology with a 68 out of 100..... I need to make a 70 or above on the exam in order to pass though. Easy enough....I'm just failing that class cause i'm against homework. I have that sort of rebelious side of me that just doesnt like take my free time to do shit I dont wanna do. I'm gonna pick up my act next year though.... The art exam's pretty easy. It's a phsycological self-portrait...Mine is, ofcourse, my portrait and then guitar tabs oozing out of my ears into a guitar off in the corner and then music notes coming from my mouth as if i'm whistling. I've gotten a bunch of good comments about the sketch so i'm confident. After that, life is all clear and set for relaxation and one hell of a crazy summer. I bet i'll be bored by next week though :p Away from all that mess, some things that are happening in my life besides the exams. A local rap artist and his group want to do a track with me featuring in it o_O... I usually dont like rap, well, not anymore. This is the good rap though....The kind that i'd listen to a bunch. First off it has nothing to do with drugs or hoes. It's about skating and rebelious acts over "no skating" zones. This is cool, though, cause I love skating. I'm better with roller blades(in-line, street stuff...not the gay kind :P) over skate boards but none of this even really matters though. They said that I would be a cool guy to do this kind of track with. The second reason why I like this because the beat is just plain out awsome. None of that banging plain beat that you'd catch in the other kind of rap....Guess you could seperate this into pure hip-hop. I'm pretty excited about this though, even though I never tried rapping...I can do it though ;P. I almost forgot this one...Kinda funny....A freshman on the JV Basketball team said today that I should be on the soccer team after I started hackey sacking with a piece of paper when I went to throw it away but it bounced off the wall....Appearantly he doesnt know me being on the soccer team last year :P. I didn't say anything....The guy's cool though :P My birthday is coming up this sunday!!!!! I could get my drivers license this sunday...BUt wont, sadly. My mom was too paranoid to let me practice driving for a year so now i've completely lost it all, and now have to gain it back. I honestly wanna drive, but not on the freeway...even though it's the safest place to drive. I'm just so freaked out right now XD. Well...I get two things for my birthday anyways...A car...Actually three things :P.... A car, and two things of my choice which I said this cause I already chose what I wanted. One being a new chord to my amp cause the old one fell apart. The other being a mp3 player. You guys can go ahead and say "about time!"...I'll join you. I always spend money on other things and say "I'll get a mp3 player some other time!". I dunno what kind it's gonna be just yet...I just want something really small, simple, looks cool, and has atleast 1gig. The one i'm looking at right now is the 1gig Sandisc mp3 player... good deal for I believe $75. Shweeeet. The car, if you're wondering, is a 1997 kia sophia. It's been my mom's forever which is in a gurly colour so i'm gonna get a new paint job on it and then maybe get a cd player in there er something. Maybe a black paint job :P. It'll go good with the interior. Well...That's it....I'm off to record some of my music over at one of my friends recording studios and practice areas. I dont even know if his stuff is hooked up. Oh yeah, if you were wondering what was with the title...It was because today I was asking tons of people for a ride home tommorrow and friday...Which, ofcourse, I waited long enough so out of the bunchs of people I asked they were already giving rides home...or just plain out not doing it....or charging money. But fuck that :P. Alright, i'm outta here guys. Well.....in a little while I will be. I might work on my game some more.
