# Arabus Drive-Over

by Darwin on June 16, 2006
Yesterday night I checked diving schools online. I spent some time doing that, afraid to play FF again because the shit'd crash again and piss me off, with an intend of today waking up and reminding my father that I should find some driving course. Instead, I wake up, and hear, "Oh, and Philip, I've enlisted you for a driving course on monday in Country Defense League, on 16:00." Well... nice, I guess. Lately, since my mother is gone to visit her mother (my grandmother, for those who don't get it yet) I'm sepdning some time with my father who's lately - through for a long time enough already - obssessed with one thing. "Arabusses." According to his beliefs, shaped mostly by Rita Fallaci and anti-Arabic press, Arabs, "barbarians" and "dirty scum", are invading the "high European culture". They try to get "Quran into schools" and they "shit inside cathedrals". They also "sit on their backs and want European governments to give them money". He goes to the point where he pretty much negates the principle of hating everyone equally. He even started supporting Jews and Bush, partially, after hearing some interview with a Jewish origin American professor on TV. "We can't negotiate with terrorists". And here we go to the scariest fact - he pretty much convinced me. I mean, as Vampz would say for political correctness, terrorists constitute a minor part of Arabic society. Which is, for the most part, bullshit. Because growing up in the religious environment like this, it doesn't take a determined terrorist to kill - I may hate catholics, for example, but when someone offends them, they don't scream about death penalties in the name of Shari'a. And, due to a marvellous nature of reigion, one day you think you're a good person, praying, believing in God, giving to charity and defending values, next day people scream about death, burn flags and you do it along with them. To quote Dawkins, "To get good people to do evil things, you need religion." Because religion, quite honestly, especially religion such as Islam, is a great tool to turn people's aspirations for idealism into mass hatred. Plus, not shaving and goat sacrifices once a year? Give me a break.
