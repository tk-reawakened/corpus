# Argh!!!!

by DarkMoon on September 2, 2005
Yesterday sucked beyond all comprehension of the word! As if being stuck at school for around 7 hours wasn't bad enough, I was stuck there yesterday for a full twelve hours!! Football practice is after school, so that's two hours. Of course, after that I was all sweaty, tired, and especially dirty from diving and such other actions of a goal keeper. I had to wait around at the school until the annual board meeting was over. It didn't start until 7!! I could have gone home, but I didn't drive into school yesterday...I just had to ride the bus. We couldn't think that far ahead now could we, no, we could not. I didn't get home until after 8:30 P.M. and that just sucks. I was really mad. Man, I really did not need to be at school for twleve hours! Oh well, at least I got to play mindless computer games for like two hours. I also slept on the couch in the library for a bit because I was so tired from practice. I didn't even get to drink tea yesterday and that's just sick. I didn't even go and see what was happening during the meeting. I stayed in the computer room and played some games. At least we got our uniforms today for football, so that cheered me up some from my traumatic experience. Of course, my jersey is nice and colourful, after all, I am the goal keeper! I had some trouble finding a number, however, as my jersey from last year was in disrepair...the numbers were coming off. I probably won't be wearing a regular jersey very much anyway but at least I got to keep my number. Argh, I hope I never have to be at school for twelve hours again! School is of the devil! At least this is my last year in the bloody place. Blah.
