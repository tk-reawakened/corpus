# atv accident

by Sir Pimpalot on July 5, 2006
Today, about 6:30, me and some friends went for a ride down some old field paths on our 4-wheelers. all was going well, at first, that is. then i decided to be a show off and go as fast as i could go, wich on mine was about 65-70 mph. unluckily for me, something happened to one of my front tires, cuasing me to loose control. on the other side of my was a canal. it probably would have been best for me to just jump off or run straight into it, but i tried to turn the other way and cuased it to flip over, on top of me in the canal. i'm surprised i didn't shit on myself..heh heh...anyways, while flipping oveer, somehow my hand got on the engine and burnt the hell out of it. well, after it was all over and they helped get me and my 4-wheeler out of the ditch i had a sprained wrist and a burnt palm of my hand...and my other arm is kind of sore and shaking. My left leg got scratched up pretty bad, too. now I'm wearing a stint-thing and a bunch of bandages... moral learned: dont go friggin' 70 mph on an atv cuase you'll be sore as hell.
