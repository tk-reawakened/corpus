# Back from Colorado

by Alex on June 17, 2006
I was going to do a blog on this before I left but I never got the chance. Anyway, I just got back from my fourth hiking trip in Colorado. Me and some other people from my church hiked up Mt Elbert, the highest mountain in Colorado (14,433 ft), in four days (we went the long way). On Sunday, before we hiked to Low Camp, we went rappelling off of a small cliff about 200 ft high. Since this was going to be my fourth time to do this I decided that I was going to go Aussie style, that is rapel face first. When I got back from climbing the mountain me and some of my friends decided to swim in a pond made of melted snow behind base camp. I think the water was around 35-40 F when I jumped in. I got to understand what its like to be a girl for about 15 mins due to the shrinkage. Out of the four trips I had done like this, I have to say that this was the best one.
