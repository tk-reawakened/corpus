# Bowling.

by Trent on September 17, 2005
Listening to by In an mood. Well, the official first day of league bowling was today. This is my first year, and I had to start a new team with a friend of mine, because the captain of my original team had a buttload of people coming, so I ended up, like, out. I do have a team now, so it's okay. I got real good right before the end of last year, and now I don't have it this year. Here are my team's scores, of three games. Trent McPheron (moi, of course): 99, 102, 93 Mike Morris (friend in new team): 86, 99, 117 Cody McPheron (little brother, bleh): 94, 90, 105 I also went on a hunt for a pair of black wristbands. There's a wristband machine at the bowling alley. There are so many types of them. Some with one solid color, some with three colors, I saw one with checkers, and even little finger bands! I ended up getting over eight wristbands and two fingerbands before I had both of my black ones. I like them quite a bit, a nice change from my old blue ones. Hm, I should (not) do little reports of my Tales of Symphonia party at the end of each blog. Useless, but I'm doing it anyway. These four are inside my active party, and the top character is the one I'm controlling. Heh, it's really easy to get huge combos with multiplayer, but I haven't beat the 68 hits I set last playthrough, using a 30-hit Unison Attack. Best I've done this playthrough is 47, no 30-hit U-Attack.
