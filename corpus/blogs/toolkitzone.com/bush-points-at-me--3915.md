# Bush Points At Me

by Ersuis on January 27, 2006
http://www.cnn.com/2006/POLITICS/01/26/bush.poll/index.html "President Bush defended his job performance Thursday, pointing to an improved economy." That's the caption of the picture in the article, and frankly it looks like Bush is pointing at me. If that's the case, though, he doesn't quite get the idea of an improved economy; I'm still just as poor as ever, and while during my senior year of high school a few years ago I didn't have as many worries about paying for university, I have many more now, due at least in part to the cuts his Republicans are making to financial aid programs. There's nothing like starting a war to convince the American people that government social programs are evil. At least there is a little good news; 58% of Americans are considering his second term a failure, and 53% have accepted he deliberately misled us to go to war. Maybe this fall there will be a real coup in Congress, with Democrats gaining seats to become the majority. If you live in a state with Congressional elections being held this fall, consider voting for a Democrat. Republicans just don't have your interests in mind.
