# by Penguin on October 17, 2005

A policeman enters a library. "Is it raining?" the librarian asks. Yes, apparently Polish policemen are really that stupid. The haunted priest sometime ago spoke about a situation where his car was about to be stolen, and the policeman was writing a protocol about things of any worth in the car. He found a radio, too bad it was some old radio bought in the middle of nowhere, so it had not a single string of text on it saying anything about the company that made it, so the policeman along with the priest tried finding any signs on the radio about the producer. "I found it!" finally the policeman said in a moment of revelation. "This radio is made by Tuneseek!" (he pronounced it the Polish way, obviously - "toonseck") In other news, we were going to some job skills determining office today with the school, it came out I have skills to become a sociologist, and absolutely no skills to be an urbanist, so the woman there said something that sounded like I'm going to waste my whole future life because I have scientific and sociological skills and not artistic and organisational, which are required for spatial economy. I could ask why the fuck is it then that spatial economy requires you to write advanced math (scientific) and geography (sociological), but I didn't, so now I get more life-resorting troubling thoughts and depression is upon me. While on the topic of jokes, my bad transliteration of a leetest of Polish jokes: Stasheck Spear the master of brilliant riposte travels by a train. Suddenly, John Doe enters. 'Howdy' he says. 'I'm John Doe.' 'Stasheck Spear.' Suddenly, John Doe pulls out two beers and gives Stasheck one. They sit, and drink. 'Say what,' says John. 'I made a lyric about you.' 'Really? Let me hear it.' 'Stasheck Spear / Drinks a beer.' 'Oh.' answers Stasheck, master of brilliant riposte, 'I made a lyric about you, too.' 'Let me hear it.' 'John Doe / You fucking dick.' LOLOLMAOLOLOROTFLOLOLOROTFLOSORE. Of course, in the original version it was Staszek Paliwko and "Staszek Paliwko / Pije sobie piwko" and "Marian Gula / Ty stary chuju". But whatever.
