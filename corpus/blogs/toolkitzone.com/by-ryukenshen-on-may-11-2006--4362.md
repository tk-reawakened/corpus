# by Ryukenshen on May 11, 2006

**...Oh...**
by Ryukenshen on May 11, 2006
I just realized something: even though signups begin May 15th, the actual expo starts on June 1st. Meaning? I blew all my energy on nothing. Me! My urgency to finish the demo as quickly as possible seems to have gotten me very involved in Toolkitzone. I've been posting about four or five times a day. It sounds odd, as that's what most people do, but until now I've been averaging once or twice a day, mostly. In other news, I haven't really been doing much. Algebra's annoying the heck out of me. I've always hated Math with a passion, mostly because I suck at it, but thinking Algebraically's...tough. Actually, the book I'm taking says 'Algebra 1/2', which is more like an intro to Algebra, but that's still not cool. Now that I'm getting into the actual Algebra half of the book, I've gone from about 5 out of 30 wrong per lesson to 8 or 9. Of course, the past few days I haven't gotten as much sleep, so that might be a leading factor... But listen to me, rambling on about Math. There are more important things. Rave Larrin ...Like this, for example. So. You're probably wondering where, exactly, the demo stands right now. I am proud to say that it's roughly 95% complete. I'm waiting for UltimaWizard to hand me the code for the Mwin. Speaking of Ultima, I composed an ultra-crappy piece of music the other day and handed it to him for uberifying. I didn't really care how bad I had done, as long as the theme was there. He just passed the Overworld theme back to me, and I have to say, it's awesome. Of course, iFrog's music donations are what I'm using for the Walkaround. Both of them are great, but the styles clash a bit, so I'm just to take a moment to thank iFrog and let Ultima, who was originally given the position of music-man, do his thing. *looks around* Where's Part Two of Saphyra's journals, eh, Lisha?
