# Careless Whisper

by Slasher on May 9, 2005
The true embodiment of the 80's; a tribute to that glorious decade in which scantily-clad Madonna posters and mullets ruled the life of Man. The smoky saxophones, the delicate melodies --- what part of "Wham!"'s top-charting song wasn't enchanting? Only the dirty, heartless bastards of today could listen to this fantastic tune and say, with a straight face, that they did not really enjoy it. I did not really enjoy it. It's nice to listen to for a few times, though, so if you've Windows Media Player, go search for "Careless Whisper" in the Media Guide. It shouldn't be difficult to find, since only 2-3 results come up. Have fun. (Puts on mullet-wig)
