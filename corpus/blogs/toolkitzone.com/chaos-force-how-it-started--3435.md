# Chaos Force - How it started

by Ryan on October 4, 2005
Hi. I'm sure most TKers who have posted in the forums since 2004, remember the planning of Chaos Force. Well, do to complications, the project had to shut down temporarily. It's since then been a year. This is what has happened to Chaos Force. Chaos Force. That's how it started out. Me and a Programmer named Marc began to collaborate fundamentals of game making. Then we knew what we had to. A fighting Game Maker. We were newbies at the time, not too much sense in what we were doing, or why we were doing it. We need an Easy formatted syntax to start off with a good programming language. And what other than the horrible Visual Basic! We didn't have the money for Visual Basic 6, or VB.Net 2003. We then found out Microsoft had a Beta for free. Visual Basic 2005. We downloaded it and got to development. We started by just making layouts with VB's form designer. I thought at the time(as well as Marc) it was a good idea to make the layouts, then program the forms. The Prototype of Chaos Force's IDE was  Not successful. There were bugs, and we didn't know enough VB script Chris came into the picture because he posted on the TKZ forums that he was a VB.NET programmer. This Caught me and Marc's eye. With an MSN conversation, a little fast talk, Chris became part of the project. We used Windows' Remote Desktop to use Chris' VB.Net 2003. Word of selling CF comes out(from me). Everyone is interested in selling CF for profits. What's wrong? There were 3 of us and one CF. Well, Needless to say, the project split because Chris did most of the work, and Money issues broke us as well. It's been A year since these events. Me and Marc are much more experienced programmers. We still sometimes talk to Chris, but he left the project for the most part. We're now recoding CF in C++. C++ is a far more powerful language than VB.NET and we now have a (literally) limitless things we can do with a language like C++. CF's new features are really remarkable. The Engine as of now is 80 to 95% complete. Now, This is the blog to keep you updated on the CF news. Chaos Force is becoming a great 2d 32 bit Fighting Game Engine that will have great abilities. Check Marc's blog and he'll tell you More about Chaos Force as well!
