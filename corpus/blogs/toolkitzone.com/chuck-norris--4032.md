# Chuck Norris

by DarkMoon on March 1, 2006
It was thanksgiving. Chuck Norris's wife accidentally burnt the turkey. I mean, she really burnt the bird to a crisp. She was upset as she told her husband, Chuck Norris. "It's okay, honey." Chuck Norris replied. He promptly went outside. Several minutes later he returned with a live turkey. He swallowed the bird whole and after a few moments puked it back up. The bird was fully cooked, completely with cranberry sauce and all. Chuck Norris's wife couldn't believe it. "How did you do that?!" She asked. Chuck Norris immediately gave her a roundhouse kick. "Don't question Chuck Norris."
