# "Come on! You said just a quick look!"

by Ryukenshen on May 1, 2006
... Parents ...Are so wierd. My sis had a friend over, and together we...erm...had some fun with our dad's computer. It involved changing his desktop to a screenshot of Barbie.com, which became his homepage, and setting up some dollies in his cabinet. When he got home he was half amused, half irritated. So, why do I appear upset with my peers? The way they treat me. My mom takes everything ultra-seriously; for example, my dad bought Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire the day it came out. My mom told me. I said I didn't believe it. She givesd me a look of total shock and says, 'why not?'. She also saw me typing up a particular Farodin news article about me having zero composers, and she tried to get me to have dad do music. (Admittedly, he's quite the music man.) But that's just wierd. When I tried to say that I don't want my father involved, she got all upset. I just don't feel like getting my parents into my hobbies. I want to keep them...I dunno. It's wierd. But the worst part happened just a moment ago. First, dad was into my e-mail account with everyone hanging over his shoulder. I told him to get out, as it really wasn't his business in there. Again, my mom steps in and asks why. Clearly, she doesn't trust me. Yeah, mom. I guess you can read my mail, bug my phone calls and stalk me on forums, too. Oh yes, and dad returned Spirited Away and Angel Eyes. I asked him if we could rent Advent Children and offered to pay. He said no. I asked why. 'We are not renting tonight. Tommorrow.' I pointed out that it'd just be a waste of gas, and he said he wanted to watch TV. (It's eight thirty now, and he's playing the piano.) I reminded him we have two TVs, but he countered, 'they're both mine.' I folded my arms and told him he was being mean. Then mom gets all upset again. Rave Larrin Flower tiles're looking much nicer, but I've hit a speedbump - battle graphics. Animating is one thing I simply cannot do - what now? A Tale of Two Hearts Yep. Check out Cy's Writing Forum for more details; I've decided to write a story. And now I'm going to start. Good-bye! Lisha...keep up the awesome work with Saphyra's journal. If you're reading this, mom and dad, I don't hate you or anything. Just promise to take me to see Tales From Earthsea when it comes to America.
