**The Mysterious Allan@Aljo...**
06 December, 2004
Nobody much knows me here anymore. I am the Aljo Brother that subtlly dissappeard from the face of the TK Community. I am Allan Chappell, a 19 year old Meat Counter Supervisor that looks for the peace and comfort of my one true love. Most of yall proably thinking... Oh no... not another one of these... Oh but it gets worse :P. There isn't much to me on a daily basis. I have the ole eat sleep poop routine. I wake up in the morning, goto work from 9-5, come home, do some random thing, goto sleep. But enough about that. Lets go back a bit. I joined this community in August of 2000, my 10th grade year when I was still using the library computer on tuesday and thursday nights. Me and my brother would browse the forums, mess with the TK a bit, so on. Finally that Christmas we got our first PC. We started the game The Quest of Friends. At first it seemed so great because we were making a game about all our friends and thier idea of themselves in fantacy. Of course we were very unknowlegable and the fact we couldn't make tiles very good was obvious. We stuck with a ripped tileset, an intresting but made up along the way story line, and some iffy programming. The game in a whole was horrible compaired to most. Was a typical noob game. During the development of The Quest of Friends, we also completed another game called Elves in Charge which was a FPS about some snowmen stealing Santa away from the elves and the elves obtain a tank that shot snowballs to get him back. Not much story, but a fun game to blow up stuff on. Used a FPS game maker for that. Ray Game developer I think is what it was called. Then I started to slow on my visits but still visited often... My brother and I entered contests for MrG. Bus Driver, Little Man With A Pea Shooter... All both of our work. Together we made a couple of tools and plugins for the tk which were later revised by my brother on his own. Before CBM left to start his life, I had done just that. I had found Amanda. I promise you I won't go on for hours about her. I could easily. Simply said, she is the love of my life and we been together for 1 1/2 years. We met on Furcadia, back when it wasn't those D&D regects that just want to make cookies and drink at the pub. Me and John had made a Mario Bros. dream. She came in intrested in the patches. I didn't know that 3 years later we would be engaged. Things happened... I finally decided I would go see her. She lives in Calgary Cananda. I live in Dallas, Texas. You see it's a long way and not many could keep a relationship like this and I admit it hasn't been without problems. But I will save that for another blog about my love life. Intresting women steal intrest from other things. Since I have went to school for Computer Science, decided I liked it as a hobby and then stopped programming due to lack of intrest and time. November of 2003 was the last time I programmed till recently in November of this year. I did miscellanous web pages but I don't call that programming. Now I watch my brother who knows an entire world of knowledge of programing more than I do. I sometimes wish I had the intrest to start again but I am working on another project for life. I am a beginner meat cutter. I am working on making it a career and my personal business. This is Allan. Not much really.. want to see someone intresting behind the scenes, check out Xavier aka Vampz. Have fun.
