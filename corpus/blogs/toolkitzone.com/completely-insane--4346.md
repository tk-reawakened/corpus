# Completely insane.

by Ryukenshen on May 9, 2006
Yes, that's me. Rave Larrin Why bother with anything else right now? Until the expo's gone, this is me, summed up in two words. It's really quite incredible. When I drew a sketch of the outside of Rima Castle, it looked great. When I compiled it in the TK Editor, it looked great. Five hours later, I squinted, and said, 'Well, it looked good on paper.' Complete. Lack. Of. Sense. Not that I'm changing anything at this stage, of course. These graphics are still good, even in my critical eyes, and I'm going to see them through, no matter what. Ah, the stress of making a game... You know, I've always read that those big game directors have their lives sucked away with every project they start, but I always thought it was a bit of an exaggeration, but if this is what making an indie game is like, then I feel for you, Miyamoto. Typing this is almost like syphoning off all of my worries as I listen to music samples to be used in the game/demo. Being a game maker is all about dedicating yourself to something, then sticking to it. I think it's about time I learned to do that. I couldn't with Farodin, Zelda: Seeds of Sorrow, The Last Warrior, one of my many pathetic Pokemon remakes/fan games, another version of The Last Warrior, or Vennar. (Yeah, those are all things I've started, and I think that there are more.) Rave is pretty much my first big project that I've gotten from design to developement. Gotta think positive, gotta keep going. I don't even have all that much more to do. Just a few day's, Ryu, and you can take a little break...
