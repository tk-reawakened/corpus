Anyway, to the topic: : The English language developed naturally as a Germanic tongue from the Proto-Indo-European language as it was spoken and written. : Nonsense! How could a language develop from Proto-Indo-European to English? Minor changes are possible (let's call it glossal microdevelopment) like centre > center, but there is no chance English would develop "sh" and "th" sounds if Proto-Indo-European didn't have it! There's also not enough written textual evidence - the missing link between Proto-Indo-European and Proto-Germanic has never been found! Also note the irreducible complexity: if you remove letters from the word "book" it becomes "ook", "bok" or "boo", none of which make sense; how could "book" develop from something else, then?
