# Cross-Purposes

by Slasher on June 7, 2005
Throughout my rather short life up to this point, I remember having wished to become a successful neurologist, possibly get married if it was in my financial (and emotional) capacity to do so, and live a happy, long life in security until I age and wrinkle and return to dust. However, I have recently realised that this is not my destiny, not what I am meant to do. There is no true happiness in pursuing the "American Dream" (embodiment of Capitalism) --- those who believe so are, unconciously, insignificant to "The Big Picture". This world is pedaling on a collision-course with a brick wall, and many are unaware of it. There are those, however, who believe they can become the "brake pedal" for Earth and it's people; those who believe that they can bring on the revolution needed to alter this corrupt World into a utopia of happiness in which Man can pursue his true purpose (whatever that may be). Of course, one cannot undertake such an arduous task without extensive research and planning into the matter. Does a revolutionary faction such as this currently exist? If so, where is it? If it does not exist, how would one go about establishing it? Such are the questions I hope to devote my life to answering, and if I were to come to such an answer (which I believe I have), I would invest my very soul into seeing that faction establish itself as a State recognised by the World, and soon to dominate it. No, I am not looking towards failing systems such as Fascism, Communism, or a Totalitarian regime --- it is a system that sets a specific ideology as a form of "government" (for lack of a better word). However, this ideology is neither oppressive nor all-powerful. Belief in it is solely based on conviction alone, and all those under it's wing that are not convinced of it could simply live in peace with their own beliefs, provided they work in unison with the laws established by the State. Such is the World I hunger for. Heh. Defying the current system usually means death. I won't be surprised if an uprising occurs soon in which I will take part and perish. If I live, then all the better --- I would hope that I could see the true system of life established, making sure that all would live peacefully until the sun rises from the West.
