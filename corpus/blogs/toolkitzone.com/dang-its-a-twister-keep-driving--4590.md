# dang its a twister! keep driving.

by Hamster on July 6, 2006
no seriously. My dad, my grandmother and me were all driving back from a town called el campo back to college station. we had just come out of whataburger and were on highway 71 headed north. The sky was really dark but the sun wasnt out so you knew it was really a big storm. As we went down the road some people where out of their car and had digital cameras and we were wondering what they were taking pictures of. Then we looked up in the sky and there was a funnel cloud reaching halfway to the ground. My dad as usual just kept driving and we actually went right under it and i could see almost into it. after we passed it it got a bit lower and then went back up into the clouds. The storm was pretty big though, i wonder if it ever came back and touched down? even though it never got bigger it was pretty awesome, something you dont see everyday. Whats strange is that just looking at the sky there was no warning that a big storm like this would bubble up. I guess its a good thing that it touch down because then people on the road wouldnt be prepared and if it crossed 59 itd be interstate 35@OKC all over again and that wouldnt be good. Anyways nothing else really interesting happened. My mom bought these guacamole pringles that are teh shit.
