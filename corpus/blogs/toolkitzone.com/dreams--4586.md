# Dreams...

by Isolocis on July 6, 2006
Well, I am near the end of the vacation at the Philippines and am currently typing this blog at an internet cafe. I will be getting up early tomorrow to make it back to Manila and will take a plane back home. Earlier, I did not feel like spending a few minutes explaining that I am currently on vacation for some reason. Actually, it was because I have spend most of my time playing Warcraft 3. A group of people introduced me to something called DOTA Allstars and I was absorbed into the game as I played. Now I regret putting down WoW with an awkward point of view. At that time, I was angry that I wasted hours just to download the client. Though that is apparently not a good excuse. Though I felt like talking about a dream I had last night since it involved the TKZ community. I was sitting at my bed, reviewing the progress I made so far on my "design notebook". I flipped through the pages and came to a page that was like some sort of comment page. Seven, Horizon, taco, and Mark wrote comments on the pages with colored markers. When they wanted to say their part, they first wrote their usernames with a colon, then wrote what was on their mind. It seems like that they were criticising my designs. I remember when Seven, Horizon, and taco drew some random pictures. edit: I simply hate this keyboard. The keys are slightly hard to push down, though it is still enough to cause with a few typos here and here.
