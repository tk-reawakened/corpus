No, it's alright, I'll share it. It's a pretty crappy poem, but I had to come up with something for class. It symbolizes the struggle of man, and someone who is to come to relieve us. We're to believe that we will experience salvation some day. No, it's not Jesus. Just some ominous figure who'll relieve us of our sorrow. Like I said, it's pretty craptastic, and probably doesn't make any sense. As soon as I said "Pains your heart", she burst. Tears flowed down her cheeks like rivers, and suddenly, they turned red. Maybe it was something else...in any case, I'm going to the Hospital today to check on her. If the doctors say there isn't a problem with her eyes, I will officially be scared.
