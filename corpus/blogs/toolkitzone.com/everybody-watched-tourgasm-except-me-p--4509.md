# Everybody watched Tourgasm except me :P

by Icbim on June 12, 2006
Man, even my mom watched it last night over at her that one dude's place. She said it was awsome...Geoff called today ranting about it...I had two or three more phone calls talking about it. Gah i'm like the biggest Dane Cook fanatic and I dont even have HBO :P. Geoff, being the best pal that his, taped it for me and is gonna bring it over sometime. Well, to continue on with my blog from yesterday.... I didn't get the game. I'm happy, though. I'll get it soon enough. It was greed that killed it for me...but whatever. I still hate my moms boy friend and I wouldn't have approved anyways. Fuck that, I dont want a new father. I'm kinda pissed over it though, mainly 'cause I waited up till about 1am in the morning for her to get home. Not only that but it was storming really bad and the power flickered on and off every now and then. I did get to catch Aqua teen though....havn't seen that show in a long time. And the venture bros. Well....other things that happened today.....My mom wants to get a kitten so we went to petsmart...whatever the place is called. Either pets mart or pet smart....I'm voting on Pets Mart, though. Anyways, we went there and checked out the kittens, and I gotta admit it brightened the rainy day. I'm serious, the sun came out not to long after. Those little kittens wanted a home so freaking bad and it made me feel really bad for them. It's sad...and now I have a soft spot for little kittens. But that wasn't the main story here, though. Well, on the way out...Which I gotta say is a long ass walk...felt like it was a half of a mile. It wasn't, though. But walking out I ran past this chick that I used to go out with....but see, it was one of those girls you never call back. Her problem was eating with her mouth full and spitting food all over the fucking place. That's always nice, right? Well, anyways.... Thank god she just sort of walked past and didn't say anything 'cause that would've made some bad talk. I walked out of there shaking a little :P. In music....Last night I messed around on the guitar for awhile and came up with a song. Thinking about it now, I forgot the whole thing. It was pretty good, too. I need to start writing stuff down. I now need a good amp complete with effects in order to get what I want for my music...'cause my little amp sucks. Geoff's step dad said that when geoff comes over he can bring his line 6 amp...I call it THE line 6 mainly 'cause that sounds like some kind of cool speed racer, mock 5 stuff. Well..... I'm out.
