# Final Fantasy VII

by Darwin on June 17, 2006
The only game where grinding is more fun than playing the storyline parts! No, really. The game plays like this: Enter some storyline sequence, watch it all, pray that you can save after it happens, then realise you can't, and that you're forced to go through a long dungeon, where you run around, collecting rare items, level-up two or three times, then face the boss, run out of potions, and die. Then you get pissed, get over it, launch the old save, get through the boring storyline sequence again, get into the dungeon, rush straight to the boss avoiding random battles not to waste MP and items, win, watch another storyline sequence, the dungeon gets locked, and you realise you've just lost access to unique items and materia that won't appear later in game but you're too pissed to play the third time through the same mess. The above example is from Cosmo Canyon. I apparently also skipped Ramuh and Gongaga town altogether, in fact, before I read about them in some FAQ, I've never know they existed so early in the game. Right now, I'm near Rocket Town. I ran around the plains, fight the same monsters all over again, and it's FUN! I can save anywhere, no stress involved, I can switch party members and enjoy the game. I don't have to run around some fucking Nibel mountains, backtrack two or three times, pray for a save point but instead die from the hands of some boss or regular enemy. So, humbug. Final Fantasy series, especially Final Fantasy VII, have officially the worst save system I've seen in any computer game. Ocarina of Time had the best idea for one, I have to say. There goes my rant. And one more thing: If I ever see anyone with an avatar of Sephiroth standing in the flames, I'll first kill them, and then write reasons for this in their blood.
