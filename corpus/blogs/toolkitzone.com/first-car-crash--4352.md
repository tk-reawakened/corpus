# First car crash

by DrakeLord on May 10, 2006
Well, today I had my first car crash. It was rather minor, but a crash nonetheless. We were going down a hill, which was right before an intersection, and we saw a truck with a flatbed trailor right in the middle of the intersection, backing up because he was lost and started going the wrong way. Anyway, my brother slammed on the brakes and stopped about 5 meters from it. Then a Ford Mercury convertable comes over the hill. She didn't see us in time, and hit us in the back. Just a little jolt. She was probably only going about 20-30 mph. But you know what the truck driver does? Drives off! Pathetic. Luckily nobody got hurt. Our van just has a cracked bumber. The convertable had a broken light, a dented hood, and a beat up fender. Not too much. It isn't nearly as bad as it sounds, which isn't saying much. The girl in the Ford was cute, BTW. ;) But yeah, my first car crash. :D
