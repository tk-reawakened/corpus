# Gamer's Club.

by Trent on September 13, 2005
I'm listening to by I'm in a mood Updating your blog regularly isn't that hard, at least when you do stuff. I should update just to prove I'm still alive. Well, I did do stuff today. There was a Gamer's Club meeting today. It is a branch of STLP, Student Technlology Leadership Program. This club was centered around getting together and creating computer games. One dude showed a bunch of stuff he made in C++, instantly causing me to feel like a novice. His friend made a maze generator program, that would create a maze of any size, and would also solve it. Not only that, but the dude himself, who was there showing it off, made a program using OpenGL to wander the maze. If that's not cool enough, this guy's doing a port of Skyroads. Flippin' awesome. I did get up there and show off a little bit of Fighter's Fury, but I'd nothing to show for my work on it, 'specially since the battle system's not nearly done yet. What I did show more of, was Eternity Star, a game I made for a Humanities project last year. It was amusing to play again. I do believe I'm done here. Sayonara. **Invoke** **T.M. Revolution**
