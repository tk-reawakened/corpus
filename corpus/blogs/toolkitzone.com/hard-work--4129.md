# Hard Work

by Alex on March 26, 2006
On Spring Break (last week), I went to my church building with my friend to get a hundred bucks or so of work. Anyway, the main project for us to do was clean this backyard of a house that my church bought. We went in the back and I kid you not, it looked like Hurricane Katrina hit it. Turns out, a shed (or two) in the back was filled with a bunch of crap and got torn down. Anyway, here are the pictures. (I have to give a link 'cause they're way too wide.)
