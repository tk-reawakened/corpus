# hatred

by Asimir on December 15, 2005
The auction sucked. It didn't happen. They'd sold most of the stuff through private bids beforehand. The only thing they had left which we were interested in was the chairs. We left our address and number and they're going to get back to us (the manager wasn't in when we went there) Work's gonna be really shit for a while. We've got multiple parties booked every day from now until Christmas Eve. That means we'll have the party hosts in, and they'll do their usual thing of spending half the time chatting in the kitchen, leaving more work for the rest of us as if we're not already understaffed as it is. Work's really pissing me off recently. My dad's just letting the other staff have days off when they want, and getting me to cover for them whether I want the extra work or not (which I don't) I would quit, but I'm the only person who knows how to do a lot of stuff with the computer system and the laser packs, and I don't want to see the family business fail. Bleh. My foot's all swollen. It suddenly happened one day for no apparent reason, so I went to the doctors about it. I was told to take a week off work to let it heal, and they were going to give me a letter so I could, but I stupidly thought "My dad won't need a letter to let me have time off work due to illness." So I got two days off then had to go back in before the swelling had got down much, then it got worse from me standing on it for hours. I should've just taken the letter, and left my dad with the problem he's caused by refusing to hire enough staff. Bleh. Bloody pissed off with work.
