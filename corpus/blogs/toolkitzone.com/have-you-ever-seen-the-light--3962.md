# "Have you ever seen the light?"

by Talyn on February 11, 2006
: The Subways - Oh Yeah : Rant-i-licious Oooo I've got some complaining to do today. Firstly, one of my favourite ranting topics at the moment, and that is kiddie hackers. Hacking for me has always been a pointless thing to do, it's much more fun to try and keep the bastards out rather than try to get in. The reason? Ultimately, I get more statisfaction frustrating the hell out of someone rather than getting into some accountants granny-porn collection. Anyway, I digress. Kiddie hackers. There's a couple in my Design class who are pillocks. They spend the entire period huddled together on a PC with the command prompt open and typing away at it. Problem is, these guys know shit about the command prompt. Since they discovered my interest in programming, they have decided that I should help them try to "hack" the school network. Of course, they get no further than being able to find "phatom" drives that turn out to be some younger kids pen drive that they left in the PC before these clowns. It fucks me right off when these guys try to do stuff like that, and yet, if the system goes down, they're the first to moan. They're always like "The computers here are so shit" etc. Fucks me off, as the machines are actually pretty nice, it's just fuckers like you tie up so much of the technicians time that they can't get the system working more efficiently. Phew, that rant lasted quite a bit longer than I anticipated. Apologies =) It does bring us on nicely to my next item though. I had the fright of my life on Monday, when I powered up a school computer and was greeted with the following image on the schools intranet homepage: Yep, that gorgeous bloke on the left is yours truly. I'm considering filing a lawsuit against the school though for using my picture without permission. On the day that was taken we were assured that we would be recieving release forms so that they could use them. However, no such form arrived. Therefore, the use of that image on the intranet, and indeed in the schools prospectus, is a violation of privacy surely. Anyway, that's me done for the moment. Be prepared for another possible rant about HMV in the future. They're very close to getting a very abusive email from me :P **Music**
