# Hmm. bmp warning.

by Charlie on April 24, 2006
More or less a progress blog. I've recently acquired Bryce and am having fun with it, but sometimes i find the terrain editor limiting so I ran a test. :D I decided terrgan creates some bad ass terrains, lets try importing those. I tinker in terragen and export to Lightwave Object format. (.lwo). I then go back into Bryce, blah blah, File>Import Object and I import the terrain. Note, at this point, im well aware of the fact that the .lwo format carries just polygons and shape, no mats or textures. So, I plop down the object on a blank scene and select a nice material/texture that varys with object height. Great. My end product: http://sarge.devpoint.net/~charlie/render/terrarender.bmp As you can see, I have to work out some series polygonal issues, but if you look in the top right cornor you'll see a very nicely created ridge. My thoughts on this stray to the idea that perhaps these terrains would be great far background backdrops, rather than settings themselves. Thoughts and ideas are welcome. :P Edit: Apparently vampzy does not like bmps. Ill make a png later today.

I always found that whenever you create a terrain though, that the textures seem to not scale very well, or are at least very difficult to manually scale. Whenever I've tried to make a very large scene, with mountains, etc, the textures seem much too pixelized to look very nice. Not sure if it's me or the program.... probably me though.
