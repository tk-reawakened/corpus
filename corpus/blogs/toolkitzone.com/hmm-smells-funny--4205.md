# "Hmm..smells funny."

by Charlie on April 12, 2006
Haha...just...haha.. Today was freaking hilarious. Okay, okay. 7th hour. Literature. Boring sub. RING! Fire drill, freaking awesome! We haven't had one in a while so I figured that this is prolly just another routine drill. As we all pile down the stairs laughing at the homework about to not be assigned, we smell: smoke. Burning plastic kinda smoke. Needless to say, the little kids in front of us decided it was best to hurry a little bit. We all got outside into the parking lot and looked up at the school, some kids crying, some laughing, some in awe. No reports, just teachers standing around. Odd. A few rumours from the HS kids says that a high schooler lit a can on fire. Odder. The klaxon-like alarms go off and we are allowed back into the building. Just settling into our Lit story again, the alarm beeps, just once. We think it's a false alarm and start reading again. Almost immediatly a person comes running down the hall yelling through classroom doors. "Why the hell are you still in here? Get out, get out!" And the alarms commenced yet again. For the second time in ten minutes we piled down the stairs. This time we eventually all sat down and played Duck Duck Goose (albeit with tackling and making out) while we waited. More rumours, kids say that theres three cans burning in all three wings of the school. After nearly 20 minutes we are allowed back in, with emergency smoke fans set in every hallway pumping out smoke. By now kids are worried. No teacher has said anything so far. Eventually we grab our stuff as the homeroom bell rings and pile back into Lit, where our homeroom is located. The sub tells us to shutup and talk quietly (what a moronic statement), and we do. Eventually the math teacher walks in and says quietly and carefully leave the school, you are dismissed. We still have no freaking idea what happened. Oh, yeah, I got my Les Paul Special II pack today. The amp BLOWS, but the guitar is actually pretty cool.
