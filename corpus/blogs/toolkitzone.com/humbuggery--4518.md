# Humbuggery.

by Darwin on June 15, 2006
I had the argument with my mother at 3 am. "Bla bla bla, what are you doing nightly?" "Sitting online." "Doing what?" "Browsing a forum." "A forum about what?" "Everything." "Bla, bla, bla, you're asocial, you won't be able to talk to real life people, bla, bla, bla." Today she left to visit her mother (my grandmother). I didn't go, because I don't really like the family from my mother's side so without a good reason, I'm not going to meet them. An interesting conception struck me. Anyone of you heard about Trementina base? If not... Teachings of L. Ron Hubbard, immortalised and indestructible. Now imagine a humanity faces some catastrophe, a collapse of science, and is left in a dark universe it doesn't understand again. And then it finds Trementina base. The indestructible tablets surely must have got created by some higher force. Soon, I bet, teachings of L. Ron Hubbard, and scientology, would become a basis for social moral code and scientific doctrine. Why, or why, doesn't anyone make a giant indestructible monument with a message about evolution? I could add an emoticon to mark this as a humorous remark, but considering the above a real possibility... WHY OH WHY?! True to my word, here's some Bible for you again. This time a bit more seriously. Let's take a look at good old doctor Luke: - Luke, 22:44 The above, quite naturally and straightforwardly, describes blood sweating. It is an occurence under extreme stress which was pretty much known to Lucas, who was a medic. What interests me is that a man who understood such strictly medical conceptions also believed epilepsy or diseases were caused by evil spirits. - Luke, 9:39 - Luke, 13:11 And a fun part about what God thinks about medicine: 2 Chronicles, 16:12 Sadly, as the example of my school priest shows, there are indeed people who take it literally and believe they'd be better if they pray to God or go to Lourdes than visit the doctor. As Dawkins remarked on "Root of All Evil?", he strangely never heard about wonders like a severed limb growing back... And here we get back to poor Jesus: - Matthew 13:31-32 And his poor, poor scientific knowledge. I guess epiphytic orchids, in reality the smallest seeds, were not well known in Israel. But apparently mustard trees were, even though they don't exist (anymore?). And finally, another fun conception I'd adress if I were to rewrite my story "Kemal", which I began, but it doesn't come up that well. Was the language Jesus used, including his metaphors and religious definitions, a part of his bringing up in human incarnation? If Jesus was locked up in a building, and never heard the words "God", "Abraham", "creation", "kingdom", "wonder", "sin" and "Devil", how would he convey his religious message (assuming he would then be let out into the world)?
