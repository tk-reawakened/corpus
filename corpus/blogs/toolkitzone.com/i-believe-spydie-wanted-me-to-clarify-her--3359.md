# I believe Spydie wanted me to clarify 'her'...

by Trent on September 19, 2005
Listening to by In a mood. Well, as per request by Spyder, I have a story to tell, about who "her" is. First, I will lay down the list of alienated girls. She is #1 and #3 on it. These names mean nothing to you people here at Toolkitzone, but when I repost this at MySpace, some people may go "Oh, I know her." And tell her that she's on my list. That would be bad. Bold AND italics. I hope I'm the only one on MySpace who uses Firefox, 'cos for some reason, blog formatting doesn't work on it. 1. Ashley Dixon 2. Kelsey Fritsch 3. Ashley Dixon 4. Stacey Smith 5. Samantha Kampsen Before I get started, I will provide quick little synopsis...es... on the other ones... 2: A silent girl who reminded me of myself from 7th grade. Not even Craig Reeves could save me from this one. 4: I'm not even sure why I tried this. She's not my type at all. This one doesn't bother me really as much. 5: And I knew something was REALLY wrong when I creeped out the freshman nerd girl last year. Okay, it's time for me to start. Ashley Dixon was an unbelievably cute (to me, at least. Not sure how others viewed her) girl in my homeroom, with a pleasant sounding voice, too. You can imagine I was really screwed over here, seeing as how she's #1 on my list. The first one. I became obsessed with her in 8th grade. I didn't do anything until near the end of the year, when I had my friend try to ask her out for me. Now that I'm older, the flaws are obvious. One, I didn't know her. Two, a friend's doing it for me. My friends ended up finding out who I was interested in, and that was really bad. Enter Kyle Jostmeyer, and he cannot keep his mouth shut. Let's tell everyone in the school about it! Luckily, I didn't kill him. Actual progress started to be made during the summer, when she IMed me. We became acquainted, and became friends, and she knew how I felt about her. Yet she always refused to go out with me. I tried desperately to find anything common between us, and failed miserably. I made it into the fresman year of high school, and we're still talking. However, about 9 weeks in, I find that she's blocked me. Click to find out just how badly that owns me. I hate her for weeks, and become interested in #2 and meet Craig Reeves at the same time. He does well to clear out the hate, but fails badly to get me the girl. After that failure, it seems as if Ashley has unblocked me, but I'm hesitant to talk to her. Luckily, she sends the first IM. We continue talking again, and I get listed in her buddyprofile. Yay. Enter her sister. She removes me from the buddyprofile. I think Ashley did it, and I go off on her. Her sister takes over, and commences to degrading me, refusing to send my apologies to Ashley. Thus seals #3 as she blocks both of my screen names. I found her to be unblocked a while ago, but I dared not talk to her. She's off my buddy list completely, and I don't want a three-peat. I'm not even gonna bother. Now, as for today's events... I ripped my French assignment today. Watching two friends of mine (they claim to be my friends), one female, one male. The female and her friend cling to the male all the time, and yet neither of them want to even touch me. I don't understand human interaction at all, honestly. Anyways, I get pissed off when my female friend gives the guy a nice, big hug. A surge of anger, and oh look. I've ripped the paper to the center. Luckily, no one over there noticed. They don't much care for me anyways, I'm sure. I'm pretty sure they won't be reading this blog. That was a huge entry. Haven't played ToS since last blog entry.
