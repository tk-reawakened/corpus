# I could be a philosopher...

by Darwin on March 28, 2006
But there's no point because Dawkins explained everything that was worthy to be explained. All I can do now is fight in the name of truth that I don't even want to be true. One particular tragedy of humans comes from the fact that when confronted with a desire, they seek to obey it rather than get rid of it. How many males, when seeing a hot female, don't think "I'd do her" but rather "I wish it didn't turn me on"? The weather started to change. The snow melted and sunk into the ground. The air feels fresh after the minimal rains that passed through here. The world returns to life, and it's not that I like it. If you ask me, I could have winter all the time. I need to print out my bibliography for the oral part of Polish exams today and maybe, this week or so, try to write an essay and send it for some university contest - they accept stuff in English. I don't have MS Office, though, and sending an essay in Wordpad would be lame... meh. I need to ponder on this a bit. The perspective of finishing school and needing to go on studies, get work and live on my own slowly crawls to my mind. Scary.
