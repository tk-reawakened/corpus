# I feel different...

by Lisha on April 30, 2006
This is only part one of Saph's entries today. I'll post another blog entry with part 2 later. It'll be a very interesting one. Can't wait to write it. **** April 30 I was taken again this morning. To the roof. They did the same thing that they did before, I think, but I cant be sure. Like last time, I cant remember much of it. But there does seem to be something different now. I cant quite put my finger on it. I feel different. Only slightly, but enough to be noticed. Sometimes I just lose focus of what Im doing and start doing something totally different, but not really be paying attention to it. Almost as if my body leaves my mind behind and does something different. And I feel more frustrated, and angrier. Like I want to do something, but I cant, and I dont know what that something is. My muscles constantly feel strained, and I cant make myself relax. And then there are times when Ill turn and see something, and it will remind me of a memory. But then the memory will be gone before I can really tell what it was, and Ill be left standing there trying to remember it, but not being able to. It probably has nothing to do with what they did, though. But then why would I need to stop. Right now. This is getting out of hand. Arguing with myself. I really am going insane. And besides, wasnt I always the one who told Lianne and that friend of hers that arguing gets nobody anywhere? I should Just heard someone scream. Write later.
