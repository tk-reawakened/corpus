# I feel like ranting again.

by Ryukenshen on May 16, 2006
As far as my life's concerned, it's still raining, I wrote an article and submitted it to the library, and I drew pictures of Rave, Yuuvin and Suna. Still dying over Latin. XD Rave Larrin As I said, I drew some concept art. I wanna showcase them at the expo, but my lines are rather jagged. If I could ink well, I'd uberify all three of them, but it'll come out even worse than before if I try. Sigh. Oh, and signups are supposed to start today! Rant Society has reached an all time low. Yesterday, I got on Runescape 'cause Chuck's always begging me to play. Of course, he was on. He's always on. Thus, I text him nerd. He laughs and says 'This coming from a guy that's never had a girlfriend...ironic...' If people are measured by their ability to seduce more than willing girls, then too bad for me. In my free time, I browse the internet, write stories, make games, and play outside. He runs track and plays Runescape. Who has a life? It's incredible how stupid kids are. I could easily get a girlfriend, I just never pursued. The idea never really appealed to me. Once you break up, it really hurts your relationship; I stick to being friends with people, and avoid intimacy until I'm older. For being smart, I'm branded a loser. Sigh.
