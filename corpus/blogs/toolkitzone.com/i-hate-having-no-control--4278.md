# I hate having no control.

by Ryukenshen on April 30, 2006
Today wasn't the best of days. I've seen worse, of course. :P Crappiness. Well, my mom managed to get me out of going to Confirmation classes, as we were doing the most retarted things, like establishing the fact that 'we can choose our friends'. However, I couldn't get out of going to a retreat. It sucked. I didn't know anyone. We did the most idiotic activities, and the lady running the frickin' thing didn't have a clue what she was talking about. Four hours of my life, utterly wasted. And dad won't let me rent Advent Children. 'Maybe another time.' Yeah, right. Rave Larrin Things are looking good, so long as your moniter's brightness is relatively high. I gave the tree a shadow and made a flower tile, which honestly blows. Still having trouble with that east running animation. Expect a demo for winter.
