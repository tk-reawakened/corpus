# I like being one of the only people at school.

by Icbim on June 6, 2006
Well, today I had to go back and re-take my biology exam. I wasn't the only one thank god. About 25 of us out of 2nd and 4th period biology. It was really boring... and there were alot of freshmans in there I didn't know. All I did was sit in the back with a amigo of mine and answered some questions..... Flirted with these two girls too....Then I fell asleep for a few but the teacher came and got me up. It was soon lunchtime and my friend split off to go eat some lunch and I just went to go enjoy nature. I walked around school and it was very calm and quite...as well as it was one of those days where it feels good outside and the sky was clear. I never noticed how beautiful our schools campus was until I got to explore it myself. I popped on my mp3 player too and started listening to some relaxing dj beats. It was a great 30 minutes walk around the school....well....laps around the school. Walking, though :P. Well, after lunch it was time to put the remediation aside and get cracking on the exam. It was the same exam, but different form. I found it to be pretty hard. Didn't feel to confident i'd pass afterwards. I headed off to the circle waiting for my ride, and here comes my teacher out of no where! She asked "how do I feel I did on the exam" and I was all like "Not too hot..." which another class member was there too and she said the same thing...And then the teacher walked away and looked back and said "well, it doesnt matter 'cause i'm passing you two anyways."...er something like that. Just reworded. I'm happy, but still pissed that I had to spend half of my day at school studying for no reason. I doubt I would've passed if I hadn't had shown up, though. I'm definitly gonna try harder next year. I've learnt my lesson.... Plus I think my depression had something to do with my slacking in school. It's all over with now though. Happy as can be :P. So.... i'm officially on summer break now. Not that happy right now though.... I have nothing planned. I mean I might go swimming this weekend with some friends, but I havn't really made any plans for this summer or started any projects i'm tickled about...Besides music. But my microphone sucks and my chord to the amp still doesnt work. Which lead to another problem that I dunno wether or not this happens to begginers or not! I cant sing or talk while I play the guitar that good! I mean i'm able to sometimes... But not too often. I either jump in with the strumming or my strumming falls back. It pisses me off knowing that it's something that I have to conquer before I can really get out there with the music. I do like teh challenge though... Makes me feel like i'm going for something big, which I am. And when i'm done I can show it off and it's like "Wow, he improved". Well... Since i'm talking about my music. As much as I love my acoustic stuff.... i'm not feeling it right now :P. I wanna do rock! Well...not rock rock....But somewhere between Fall out boy(for example XD) and N*E*R*D. N.e.r.d mostly....I love their stuff. It has that hip-hop vibe mixed in with rock.... If you listen to some more of their "up" stuff like "She wants to move" or "Jump"....It's sorta what i'm after. Just something I can have fun with and just get up and move around instead of being this emotional city boy, john mayer stuff :P. Nothing against my hero, though. I still love my acoustics. Well, in order to have a band like N.e.r.d....I gotta have other members. Drum and bass, i'm still after you. I think I might have someone providing background stuff like the effects in N.e.r.d songs. Well, enough of that. I'm done right now. I guess i'm just gonna go lay down on the love seat and listen to some music. Take it all in. Also, if you have any tips to help me get into talking/singing while playing the guitar, please do share :P. See you guys.
