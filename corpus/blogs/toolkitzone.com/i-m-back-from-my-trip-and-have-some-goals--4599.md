# I'm back from my trip, and have some goals...

by Isolocis on July 10, 2006
I have three goals in my mind: to expand my game collection, to finish the design notebook for The Daylight Project, and to upgrade my computer. The latest computer game I bought was System Shock 2. Like what everyone said about it, SS2 is very scary. Other than the fear factor, the gameplay was pretty good. If I could change the game in any way, I would make the wrench swing faster. I was also thinking about purchasing a motherboard with PCI Express and a PCI video card, or save some money and try getting a high-end AGP video card. I currently have $400 at the bank, so I might go ahead and buy a Radeon X1600. And I don't believe that recovering jet lag should be a goal. :P
