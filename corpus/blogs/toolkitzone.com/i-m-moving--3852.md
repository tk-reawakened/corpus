# I'm moving

by DrakeLord on January 13, 2006
After I read ICBIM's blog and that it said he was moving, I thought I should right my first blog: Well, as you've probably guessed, I'm moving. The new house is located about 20 minutes from our current house. We've been in this house about 7 years, I had my 7th birthday on the plywood boards that were on our living room floor while it was still being built, and we've finally decided to move on. The new house is going to be cool. Our current one doesn't have a basement, which for some reason I find annoying. I like basements. But the new one will have a fairly big one, along with my brother and my bedrooms. My sisters are going to be on the top level, or second floor. The parents are obviously on the main floor. But all in all, it's going to be a huge and awesome difference. There. I'm done ranting, or what was sort of short and rantish, and I finished my first blog. Go me!
