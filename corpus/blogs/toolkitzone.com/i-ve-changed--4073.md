# I've changed

by Alex on March 13, 2006
Yeah, whenever I say something "I chaged" everyone knows I mean something like my political beliefs. I remember when I posted something saying that I didn't consider myself a Republican because of the stupid border problems. That didn't last very long. Now I think I can honsetly call myself an independent. Let me explain... It all started when I got my new roommate. He's a very decisive guy. He hates liberals and loves to argue and basically embaress people when they're wrong. I don't like that and I started not liking it even more recently. I wouldn't say I've moved to the left or the right, but that I've gotten more...reasonable...I can't find the word. Anyways, I have several problems with people who call themselves 'conservative'. I am sick of them spouting off this stupid "I hate gays" crap. I've decided that voters should just decide it and let it be. No-one is going to say "Oh yeah, you're right" if all you're doing is trying to make yourself feel better. Also, I'm tired of these 'conservatives' not caring about the environment. How many Republicans run on the platform of cleaning up the environment? Almost none. However, I am quick to say that people should have their needs taken care of first and if possible keep everything clean while doing it. No longer do I want to consider myself affiliated with the Republican Party. I guess I could still call myself a 'conservative', but I have other beliefs to add on. I think 'compassionate conservative' works best because I am concerned about things that most conservatives don't care about. Anyway, I'm done with my little rant.
