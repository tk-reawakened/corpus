# I've lost my virginity to a marshmallow.

by Trent on May 27, 2005
good "Spirit Dreams Inside" - L'Arc~en~Ciel "OW!!!!!" Today and yesterday were my last days and my exam days. In my county, we have block scheduling, meaning 4 classes a day. I took 2nd and 4th block exams yesterday and 1st and 3rd block exams today. A lot of today, I was going around singing a Strong Bad scroll rap-style song utilizing Xorlak's popular phrase, which I used repeatedly on its own. L to the A-S-T D-A-Y! The end is nigh Oh good friend of mine! 2nd block was Biology. Oh my, this exam was interesting. My teacher wrote the topics that were going to be on the exam on the board, on the day of the exam, and at least 5 people in the class were flipping out. "I'm gonna fail!!!" The teacher even reviewed the material on the exam and gave time to study right before the exam. Needless to say, I got a 100%. 4th block was English II. A 116 question scantron exam, it was. I got a 97%. That night, I was flipping out over the exam for 1st block -- Geometry. I hate Geometry. I could blame it on the teacher, but it's not her fault; I can't memorize every theorem and postulate, and be ready to utilize any one at any time. Luckily, I'm in Special Ed. >D 1st block comes around the next day (today), and a lot of the questions I had been uncertain on were X-ed out, and we were not to do them. Everyone got to slide out of doing the only proof on the test. I hate proofs more than rap. 2nd block today was fun. I played cards the entire block, really. I learned to play Speed, and I played Rummy. Yay. 3rd block. Français, mon cours favori! The exam was an essay where we had to get as many grammar points as possible while talking about a vacation. I already know one part where I messed up -- using avoir instead of être in the passé composé of reflexive verbs. I figured that out today reading the book he decided to go ahead and give us for French III, which I've signed up for (My first semester this year was French I, and the next was French II). I've already learned the future and conditional tenses. Hah. After the exam and lunch, there was a pizza party. I'd been carrying a digital camera with me the entire week, and was able to get a buttload of pictures. 4th block, and more cards. The rest of the class was watching A Series Of Unfortunate Events, but I had resolved to play cards with some of my friends. 5 Card Draw Poker with deuces wild, and I repeatedly get four of a kind. My friends wish to kill me. My english teacher then came over and taught me how to play Euchre. Me, her, and two other students played Euchre and I got owned. YAY! School ends! And I get off the bus at my friend's house like usual (An earlier blog entry details why), and it is here that I lose my virginity to a marshmallow. Mike Morris has made a marshmallow gun out of pipes. I'm playing on his air hockey table against my brother, and he's shooting me in the frickin' butt, landing quite often between the buttcheeks. I'm being butt-screwed by marshmallows. It stings, bringing a loud and funny sounding "OWWW!!!!!" from me. So, of course, I get shot more, and plenty more "OWWW!!!!!"s. And more. And more, causing me to lock myself up in his bathroom for 15 minutes. He even got me so bad, it put a mark on my chest and gave me a third nipple. A marshmallow. A flippin' marshmallow. It was an awesome day today. w00t, I'm a junior now! **I feel**
