# It all sucks. I fucking mean it, all year screwed!

by Penguin on September 2, 2005
And no, I'm not just mad because of some shitty feelings. Let's face it, today was first real day in school. 8 lessons. Eight. I have just returned, and it's 16:04 (4:00 pm). Marching 4 kilometers in sunlight exhausted me pretty much, too. And say what, whole year's gonna be like that. The scheme of subjects looks like macabre. We don't have lessons at the swimming pool, so we get 3 sport lessons at the hall. 2 one day, 1 another. Thank God it's all at the end of week (thursday, friday), else I would not be able to walk to school because of the tire and ache. Speaking of God, the new priest who's gonna teach us religion is some cleric just returned from America. So he's a Westerner. He doesn't even sound like a cathole, more like some protestant guy. He puts impact on reading Bible and faith, while for the last 2 years of religion with the former priest we haven't opened a Bible once. That is obviously bad news, honestly, I don't know what cleric is worse, the new one may turn out to look like an Opus Dei fanatic on a longer scale. Or talk shit about how you have to invite Jesus into your heart et cetera. English. Instead of good old 2-group division, there's now a 6-group division. To make it even more senseless, the groups look like "Group 3 - girls from naturistic and mathematical profile and boys from proeuropean and classical profile". I'm in groups 1, 3 and 6, and additionaly at the standard proeuropean profile additional English. Uh. What is beyond me is why in the bloody hell my profile get's one more class of German and one of Social Knowledge. Uh. At least we got 3 geography lessons in the end. We've already gotten a chart with the stuff to choose to make a presentation about for matural exam on Polish. All suck, I guess I'm gonna make a speech about Jews in literature or shit like that. I was gonna write that the Logsan page is finished and doing well, but I guess I won't have much time to care for it anyway, thanks to the God damn fucked-up matural class school. Sheesh.
