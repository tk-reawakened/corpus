# It's been too long.

by Trent on September 4, 2005
Listening to Mood is . Mkay, here I am... A futile attempt to update you on the current happenings of my life. I've got lots to go over, so let's begin, shall we? We got an XBox, and Halo. Amazing game. I rented Dead or Alive Ultimate Collection. Amazing game (at least DoA2U is). I needed DoA 3 to unlock one of the characters, so I tried to rent it. Couldn't. So I bought it. A lot later, we ended up with CSI, DoA3, DoAUC, and Halo 2 in addition to that. Actually, the last two we got yesterday. Halo 2 has an AWESOME glitch that causes you to fly right at an opponent and kill them with the energy sword. >D Onto something else. Criss Angel. He is inconcievably awesome. Hmm... Tales of Symphonia. I beat it again, and did 2x better than before. Colette is cute in her Maid outfi, and Presea's cute in the Klonoa outfit. When I finish a couple more playthroughs, I'll buy an enhancement for a new game to carry through every technique, and switch everyone from T-type to S-type, and vice versa. I'll have all the techs, maybe. >D Indignation Judgment KICKS ASS. Hm, what next? Our financial situation. Since the incident with my mom last christmas (I've a blog about it), I have been living with my Dad. We live on retirement checks, since Dad's unemployed, and our grandparents live there too. 5 people in one house. Anyways, we use that up fairly quickly, even treating ourselves to a few little goodies with it, such as the games we bought yesterday. We have been waiting for a check since May, from an insurance company called Wyeth. While the retirement checks come to $1600, the check from Wyeth's looking to be over $3000. We were looking to upgrade my computer and get a new MP3 player with it, as well as have extremely overdue birthday parties with it. We live in a mobile home (not a trailer, next step up), we can't have a birthday party in it. I now have a Celeron 1.7ghz processor in my computer thanks to a free motherboard from my friend, but that's not gonna cut it, sadly... -_- Now, on to School. It started. I am in my Junior year. My schedule goes like so: Art I French III US History Algebra II. Also, since we live in a different county than the school, I have to pay $150 every quarter for Tuition. Fun. I'm really enjoying my classes. My US History teacher is brilliant. To demonstrate how the colonists felt right before the American Revolution, he pulled a hoax on us to invoke indignation (Best word ever). It involved recieving a memo from the principal that said that all photocopies used in Social Studies classes would cost 10 cents for every student. It would eventually expand to the whole school. Needless to say, we were all very indignant. I almost busted his scheme when I pointed out to him how similar this was to what England did to the colonists. Everyone had to pay their 10 cents right there and then, and they took a quiz. At the end, the hoax was revealed, and the money paid was returned. The WEBN fireworks are tonight. w00t. Oh, my game. *sigh* I haven't touched it all summer. Please forgive me. If anyone knows of any way to get me working on it again, LET ME KNOW. Now I end on an even worse (to some of you) note. www.myspace.com/eternal_eon All my best friends have one, so I figured it wouldn't hurt to try it. It doesn't seem too bad, so far. I just wish I could use Fighting of The Spirit in it. **Fighting Of The Spirit, ToP SNES Version**
