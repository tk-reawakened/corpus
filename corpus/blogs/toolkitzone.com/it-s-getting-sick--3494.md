# It's getting sick.

by Penguin on October 14, 2005
Did I ever brag about how several dead newborns were found in jars with lettuce in Poland once? Well, I am against abortion and all, but after the entire thing previously mentioned chilled out, now similar case appears. A dead newborn was found flowing in one of the rivers. Some kid was. And from the other barrel (to use a Polish idiom), two nurses were lately photographic themselves happily with an early-born taken out from the incubator. My God and my all - has my entire already sick nation turned into a bunch of infant-murderers? Blegh. In other news, I'm going to geography tutor tommorow, my favourite white T-shirt is missing, we had some celebration at school yesterday, and the bishop asked to talk about Solidarność (Solidarity) began talking shit about how teaching Greek is necessary to understand where the word 'geology' comes from (he got it wrong, by the way), and then made a detailed historical speech on his view on communism. It's kind of getting me pissed. Nuns weren't allowed to walk outside of monesteries in nun-clothes, and some guy who started a religious organisation was convincted to 8 years, and the Church calls that "persecution". Let me compare it to Hitler. Hitler persecuted the Jews. He gassed them, murdered, lynched, put to death. And the results? No Jews. Once judaism in Poland was religion of 25% people, not it's one of the lesser denominations. Christian hold tight with 95% (which is biased, as the statistics originate in number of baptisms - and because catholics baptise young children, even I am technically baptised), so can't they just shut the fuck up about the persecutions? The sad truth is, communism in Poland tried eliminate religion by way too humane means. They tried negotiations, minor repressions. If they began sending bishops to ags chambers, then christianity would slowly fade away, and no fat bishop would scream about persecutions, because he would represent some insignificant denominational minority of a fully operating socialist country. Sad but true. The only true way of destroying an ideal is by biological genocide. It's not that I want to genocide the catholics anyway. Another fun fact: When christians try converting atheists, it is consider a "mission", a "repention of sinners", a "good deed"; when atheists try convince christians it is considered "offending religion", "bible-bashing", a generally vile, bad, and wrong thing. Yet another fun fact: Most of Europe was christianised through force by Charlemagne. Why's the Church attacking communists for trying to apply force to stop the religion, then? Meh. All the hypocrisy. And newborn-killing. Scary shit.
