# It's not just a theory, it's the only possibility.

by Darwin on April 24, 2006
6 more days of high school, assuming I'm going to go to school on them all. I had to dress up and go to the photoagrapher because my school demanded 3 photos for the papers they're going to give me after finishing the school, whatever their name is. Certificate type of thing. After the oral exams from Polish, which are the last thing, most probably there's gonna be a school party or whatever. In short, an opportunity to get wasted and look at all these silly losers dancing with girlfriends and whatnot. I began to write another story. So far two short chapters of bad quality. The title is "Invasion Force" and it's my attempt at writing some xenofiction. Earth is attacked by the titular Invasion Force, but the story is seen almost cmolptelety from the invaders' view. Through destructive, they are one of the most advanced races in the Universe and transverse it, conquering vast worlds, hoping that some of the planets or civilisation they encounter will give them insight into what the seek: explanatio;on of the purpose of the existence of the Universe and Life's condition. Overally, one of the main heroes are the alien currently named Aleph (I need to find better names), who is an idealistic optimist, and the one currently called Beth, who just tries to get along and do his job in the Invasion Force. There's also a human Chinese general captured by the inavders, with whom later Aleph tries to estabilish a system of communication. One of the main aims of my story is an attempt to imagine a realistic relationship between humans and a non-human race. In the story, though the ivaders look rather humanoid, they have a completely different form of a society, don't have the sense of smell or hearing, see a different scale of light and communicate by sending electromagnetic impulses directly to the mind, a system of communication that also derives from their method of combat (causing direct pain with magnetic blasts). The aliens have also originated from a completely different planet with varying gravity, thus they are extremely agile and swim perfectly. Other features are being able to swap objects with feet (the aliens are usually biepedal, but can walk on all four just as easily) and using the mouth hole for both eating and excrementing. I still have no idea how to handle their breathing. :P The major theme of the story is evolution, not just because I tried to make the aliens evolutionary probable, as it is seen from the aliens' side as the universal force that shapes life and the condition of individual, which is responsible for the morality, good, evil, way of thinking and logic. The aliens, Beth especially, generally regard humans as lower in cluture due to their alliegnace to religions and nonensical philosophies, referring to them as "animals". If I manage to pull it off, I'll hopefully post it on Cy's board and no one will ever check it out. :)
