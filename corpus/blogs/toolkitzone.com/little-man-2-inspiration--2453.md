# Little Man 2 Inspiration

by John@aljo on April 16, 2005
Well Cesque there is responsible for the inspiration of the enemies for Little Man 2. I was analyzing his drawings earlier and it got me into a drawing mood. I began to think about how I used to be a faithful Reader of TKGB's rants (still would be too if they were in the forums or on the front page of tkz) I thought about how he drew Vampz with the vampire like style with bat ears. I tried coming up with a realistic version of it (will post pictures later) but turned out rather ugly. Then R# with a heart necklace, inspired by the wizard of oz :P I thought about how Cesque managed to throw in that picture of my snail and relized I hadn't drawn a snail for me in a while. This one has a don't mess with me attitude. Then again thought of the rants and drew a version of Asimir. All of these I relized fit in the roles of differnt enemies on Lolo! (given Asimir can throw projectiles and Vampz has some kind of death ray vision). So I believe folks I found our enemies for Little Man 2
