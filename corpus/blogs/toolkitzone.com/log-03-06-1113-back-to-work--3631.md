Actually, leet is way more complex, and its morphological and lexican aspects are more interesting that just changing letters into numbers. There's whole set of popular rules of using '-z' instead of '-s' suffix ('skillz'), adding '-or-' as an interfix to strenghten the meaning of word ('haxor', 'haxored'), changing f into ph ('phear') and letter order ('pron', 'teh'). There's also a thing for 'xxx' instead of 'k phoneme ('haxxxor') or 'ee' instead of 'ea' ('pheer'). There's also a huge vocabulary of custom words.
