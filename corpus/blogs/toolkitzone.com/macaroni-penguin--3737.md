# Macaroni Penguin

by Penguin on December 11, 2005
So, there's this food sold in bags. It's like noodles, but instead of a linear shape it looks like tiny bits or pieces. It's damn cheap. It's normally used in soup or to feed geese, but I'm eating that mit Geschmack without anything else. I'm reading "Inny świat" (different / another world). Fun book. It's pretty long, about Russian work camps. Stalinism, as it seems, is not a doctrine. Stalinism is a religion. Communism gives the right to purify one's errors in gulag camps, the purgatories of the new faith. Unlike nazi camps, Russian lagers look more like monasteries, and apart from hard work, rapes and starvation, they are a place of mystic atmosphere, usually connected with thoughts about dying. In other news, I am - or so the teachers say - the first one to reach the second level of some pan-Polish English contest, so during the winter holidays I'll have to spend 2 days in Lodz (as far as I remember) attempting to pass the second level. It looks crazy. The teacher gave me shitload of materials to study (history of USA / UK). Food and accomodation is said to be provided on the contest place. Overally, it's going mediocre. I need to chill out and stop being such an ass on TKZ, oh well.
