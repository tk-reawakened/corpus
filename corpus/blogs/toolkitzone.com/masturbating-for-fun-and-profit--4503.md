# Masturbating for fun and profit

by Darwin on June 11, 2006
I was at the wedding, and the wedding party of my cousin. It was tolerable in that it was bearable. Almost. Of course, the whole grand familia gathered for the occassion. Not much to say about my crazed family (Slavs are probably the only ethnicity so fucked up to wish people health with a custom of drinking vodka and eating shitloads of red meat - it's like wishing long life by stabbing with knives). Not to mention the average age of the population was over 60. The church part was, well, a typical catholic wedding. "Do you take this man as your husband? Do you want to have as many kids as God wants and raise them in catholic spirit, thus proving the Church exists for nothing else but biological reproduction of its followers? Good." The idea that kids are "being given by God" is the funniest way of saying there's no "family planning" involved I've ever heard. Then, there was the wedding. Vodka, vodka, vodka. 40% vodka, 100% vodka, vodka in a small glass, vodka in a big glass, vodka everywhere. Amazingly, people didn't get drunk and still had time to dance to the typical wedding music. I've met some of the cousins my age, which were discussing street life and I've learned about a new subculture group akin to nazis: Praetorians. Apparently, there's to do with police or militia or something, but considering that I've also learned that some people in akin subcultures can't even read, I doubt ideology would matter much. What pisses me off is the over-politeness of my grandparents. It's as lame and as fake as hell. "Bla bla bla, please eat! You sure you don't want more ugly fat ham? You're young and you need to eat ugly fat ham to be healthy! Bla bla bla, bla bla bla, is the bed comfortable? You need to fix it else you won't sleep well. Bla. Bla. Bla." Whatever. Memorable quotes by my family: My father (in the church): "Pompancy of little midgets! One wants to stand up and say, 'People! What the hell are you doing? Pour vodka and celebrate like people!'" My father (about the wedding party): "Majority of people at the party were of premortal age." Grandma: "Why are you sitting in a cap, like a Jew?" Grandpa: "I am a Jew. Nowadays anyone the government is uncomfortable with is a Jew." Granpa: "People weren't too happy..." Grandma: "What do you mean? They had fun." Grandpa: "I mean in the sense: Drunk." Some old female family member of mine reading some 'devout' book: "Oooh! How many churches have they built during the last 16 years!" My father: "Oooh! And how many factories have they built so that people would have work! NONE!" And of course, nothing beats my paternal uncle talking with some female family member from Sweden, who's a vet, about taking sperm from a bull.
