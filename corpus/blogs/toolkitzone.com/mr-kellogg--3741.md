# Mr. Kellogg

by Euix on December 13, 2005
Methodical. That describes me; sums me up. Procedural, methodical, whatever you wish to call it. It seems to be the perfect description, yet it's so far from the truth. Can you really describe a person with a single word? Music, reality. Sometimes it's hard to tell the difference. Does music create this illusion or do we create it ourselves? The human mind, so complex, methodical, yet seemingly random at times. Which one is it? The universe deals us many questions. How is everything in existince right now? How did it come to be? Is there some form of thought after death? Is there some form of life before birth? Is everything seperate and real, or are we all tied together? Religion attempts to answer these questions, tells us that a god, or many, had created our universe and it's laws and properties. How did such gods come to be? How did any of this begin? Some are answered with a fact that these beings are simply there, they have always been there. Is this really true? How do you know for sure? Yes, this truly is me writing this blog entry, Euix, Chris, whatever you call me. Just trying to keep your interest and keep you thinking and wondering. Some of these questions tie back to religion. So i'm going to attempt to describe my feelings towards religion. These are my opinions, you don't have to accept or agree with them, so I would greatly appreciate it if you did not try to flame me or attempt at proving me 'wrong'. I do not believe there is a god, or any form of one. I do not have all of the answers, all I can say is that we are just here. It's not any different than saying a god put everything here. I feel that religion is just used as an excuse at times, and hinders important developments. But I don't believe religion is a total waste, as it has helped us develop many great, important, moral values. Things would definately be much different without religion, but not necessarily for the worst. I would write more, but i'm feeling a little sick and tired, so I want to go. I'll think of more to write for next time.
