# My eyes hurt.

by Ryukenshen on May 9, 2006
Yeah...this screen's too bright. My Life Life? What life? Rave Larrin I've been working constantly. The expo is looming ever closer, and I'm determined to make a single board that will, indeed, be used in the game for the player to walk around on. What does this mean? It means that I get about an hour outside every day until the signups begin. How hard can one board be, right? That depends on how much I strip it down. For all I know, I'll be leaving out the MWin, which'll take a small amount of time to program and draw. Time is time, though, and I don't have much to waste. Heck, Rave's sprite isn't even finished. He can run south and east, but I still need to finish north and west (neither of which wil take long, but, as I said, time is time). This little walkaround demo is about 70% complete, but that's 30% too little; I have, like, six days before the expo signups begin, and I've been working on this for over a month. Of course, in the past four days I've filled in about 20% of the demo, but I'm also thinking about changing the grass tile, etc. Will I make it? No idea. The board will include a lot of hedges, a castle you can't enter, some flowers, trees, patches of dirt, cliffs, banners, and a fully-animated Rave. I still need to finish the castle, the banners, Rave, and possibly the grass tile. Then I have to compile it all, get a quick musical piece, and throw it at you guys in disgust. This is gonna be so awesome! A quick shout-out to Lisha: Do you plan on entering the Horse Smell? You probably said something sometime, but I honestly can't remember.
