# New PC, Anyone?

by Charlie on September 13, 2005
Yes, I'm going to buy a new pc. In like....a while. But I need suggestions. I have a Dell Celeron, which I HATE TO NO AVAIL, with 312mb of ram, and integrated graphics. MY price range is 0-$600. I feel an alternate OS would be nice... So, I would like a pc with a linux hd and a windows one. Or, a Mac mini. What would you say...? My purposes: Gaming, Media and Music Studio PC, programming. And yes, I REALLLY REALLLY want a mac.
September 13 2005, 7:54 pm
Well, if you're gaming, I would suggest probably not getting a Mac. I love Macs, I use my Mac daily, but I don't game, besides the occasional round of Civilization III or a MUD. Games just aren't as available for Macs as they are with cheapo x86 PCs with Windows. Lots of good games get ported, but you expect to see them at least a year after they're released, and some ports are just terrible, with badly ported graphics code slowing the game down tremendously. Macs are great for programming, graphics work, and just about any other work as well, but games just aren't so great.
