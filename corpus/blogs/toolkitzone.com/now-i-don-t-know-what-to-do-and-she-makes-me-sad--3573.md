# Now I don't know what to do...and she makes me sad

by nova11 on October 31, 2005
Now Playing: Slipknot - Vermillion Pt.2 Feeling: Disdainful Halloween, Blah. We bought like 5 bags of candy for the little greedy bastards, and we didn't get a single one. All the more for me, bitches. I guess I haven't left many good blog entries recently, and I can begin to see how my public image of being a noobish ass is coming out. Oh well, right now, I could care less. I've begun to realize that I have more feelings for Anne than I thought. She's a really nice girl, with a great attitude. She recently broke up with another guy, and apparently is pretending to be infatuated with a 9th grader, (we are in 8th), in order to make her ex feel bad. Or so some other girls say...oh well. I suppose I have a chance, we hang out often. My music creation has taken a definite second to my homework and social life. See, I'm in a dilema, however. Because I live in a different district then the school I am attending, (long story, parents work in EB district, we found an awesome house in Starmont district, so blah), and no matter how much I like this house, I can't believe how boring it is. Since I live in Starmont district, I often get odd looks, because, like most 8th graders at my school, I have quite a collection of EB propoganda that I wear often. It goes without saying that I have no friends here. It's amazing how lonely you can feel in the middle of a small town, surrounded by children that turn their head when you politely say hello. The only greetings I get is from a young cashier girl, who works at the local grocery store. This is due to the fact that she sometimes works in the EB district, and recognizes my lonliness. A girl at school has noticed how I tend to be on MSN alot, and asked why. Telling her my lonliness, she wants to visit me often. Always a silver lining.
