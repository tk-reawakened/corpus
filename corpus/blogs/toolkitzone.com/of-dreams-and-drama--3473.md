# Of Dreams and Drama

by Penguin on October 11, 2005
I had a nice dream 2 days ago, that I remember amost completely. It was about the Smurves (or Smurfs). Of course, it was a complete alteration of the child tale. At the smurf village, there was a well-like openable thing, where the Black Hole was said to appear. And there came a time that the Black Hole appeared. The Black Hole was something that appeared after Papa Smurf opened the well. It was like a black hexagon that looked like "pasted" over the well when one opened it, and its position was absolute, ie. didn't change when camera view changed. The appearing of Black Hole has signified a great time for the Smurfs, it was in a way reference to the Jews and the Messianic Age. Unlike the cartoon, there were two bad guys. One was Gargamel (or maybe was it that dragon from one lame Polish cartoon?), I could not really tell, the one other was a cunning Bishop in Red, who I remember I kept refering to "bishop" the whole time. He looked like first form of Ballos, at least from the face (he had a red robe, but without the eye symbols that Ballos had). He was making wierd facial expressions. Anyway, there was a giant fest at night at the smurf village (which was a giantic place with some sort of dam, looked pretty spectacular). Papa Smurf was going to say what waits the Smurfs with the appearing of Black Hole now, and he didn't really know, but he said in a clear voice, in Polish, what he predicted (I remember the quote perfectly): "It will be nothing else, but a Wonder of the World." Wonder of the World was not a building, or a place, but an event, a new Messianic Age of the Smurfs. Anyway, Gargamel and the Bishop tried spoling the celebration, but they got caught, and tied to some chair. But they freed themselves by running around in circular motion. Then the Bishop said something, made a weird expression and broke the glass of the window of the room they were held in. It came out they were in a sort of, hmm, waste dumb factory, with green ooze rivers running. The whole place looked like from Duke Nukem 3d. Around that I woke up. I guess the celebration went on in a dream after that, maybe even the Wonder of the World thing was after that, not before. A kick-ass dream. Today I also had a dream, but it was more scary. First it was some running around a park / hill /jungle area where people from my family and school appeared, but then it went on to watching a "Japanese scary movie." In the movie, a guy was walking with some servant behind, because he was called to inspect a prison or something. On a way, he didn't care the way he was walking, some car stopped right in front of him having to use a hand brake but he and his servant didn't care, they went on. It was revealed that suddenly, all the prisoners have dissappeared... They got into the place. There was the guy, the servant, the guard and some other guy from the prison. They began talking, then suddenly, the guy looked at the cells (the camera was looking at his face, the cells were never actually shown) and got a scary facial expression. I remember thinking, "So now they'll show the cells bathed in blood or something", and waited for that effect. But instead, the guard got struck with a dart in his neck and fell on the ground, convulsing, then the faces of all the people were shown and they began exploding, disfiguring, mutating, blood and flesh was twisting and, meh, other ugly stuff and I woke up. I don't remember whenever all four guys got lethally face-distorted, maybe one of the them was a traitor or murderer or someone. To add to the drama, Polish investigation with the "corpse trade" goes on. As it comes out, burrial service providers were offering gifts, also Christmas gifts for kids, for information dealing with the deaths of hospital patients. As someone called them, they were "people without emotions". Oh well. The entire thing was obviously a giant mafia system in Polish hospitals, where the doctors "finished off" the patients to get cash and gifts from funeral houses. I forgot to take my school shoes today, but I escaped the angry and evil boot-gazing 'portieress' or someone (no idea how the profession is called in English, the woman responsible for taking our boots and giving us tag numbers to later get the boots back, in every school and university of Poland people performing these professions are ugly, old, screaming all the time, hating everyone and making black humor jokes).
