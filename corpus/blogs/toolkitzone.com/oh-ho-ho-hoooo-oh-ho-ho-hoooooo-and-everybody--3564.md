# Oh ho ho hoooo, oh ho ho hoooooo, and everybody...

by Charlie on October 29, 2005
..was kung foo fighting.... Yeah, anyways. I was a REAL DJ for the first freaking time last night. It wasnt that special, just a 2 deck CD player, a preamp, two huge speakers, a mic, and a gym-full of middle schoolers. I didn't do that bad, I was one of 6 on the music committee, and Vice DJ. :P Taylor and I were main DJs, but we had 3 groups that were supposed to take turns. In the end, as im the only single guy right now, I was DJ for most of it. Whenever the other group got up, they just killed the preamp and started yelling for me. Embarassing. :/ But when I got it fixed, and my CDs running again, the kids went wild. Note to self: Good songs for snobby MS chicks - My Humps and Play That Funky Music. ;)
