# Oh, Joy! (and the story of multiple authors)

by Chaos on May 9, 2004
I hate biology. Last lesson we learned the population of snails in a small area of muddy ground. GREAT JOY! HOW USEFULL!.....ok now that little rants over allow me to give you the story of multiple authors, epizode 2: And so the mighty cult of the scandanavian moose did meet in the shadows of Mt. Spoon for their anual meeting. The scandanavian moose did grace us all with his mighty presence. The serman began: "Give me beef, alot of beef" and so they brought beef. It continued "Now, declare holy war against our mortal enemies, the spanish golfers," there was much uproar and rejoicing, for all had been looking forward to a good few weeks of ritualistically pelting golfers with shrimp, and now they could. What they didnt consider was the french. They had set up a large chain of fat sea food fanatics all along the border. Amoung them was their mighty leader "Timmy Byles" who was famous for saying the words "I shall use your legs to wipe my arse on a warm summers evening." So saying, Timmy byles (Or TB for short) sat down to comb the shrimp out of his muff. Without warning the cult of the scandanavian moose attacked! For they had received a message from their God, Via "moose mail"(tm) declaring all out war on everything non-moosey EVEN TB'S MUFF! They had TB pinned down and were about to insert an oddly shaped root vegedable into his various orifices when suddenly the battlefield was invaded by a gay rights march. "Gay people are people too!" quoted a bizar bearded women who was handing out leaflets. At that moment something very strange happened. A passing herd of blue recycling bins (who were going south for the winter) set upon them and used "this phrase" to give the french a complete breakdown. And everyone had a huge, drug fueled rave to celebrate. Becaus they all hated the french. Exept of course the scandanavian moose himself who had always loved seafish. He walked away weeping whilst the dustbins were debating whether to throw pins at him. The moral of this tale is: Dont do drugs.
