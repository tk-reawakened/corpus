@ Darwin, I don't think I have thousands of dollars to spend on painting Lenins face ;) plus the car has to sell so I can get a winter driver. @Spyder , yes he is. Those aren't the only ones he has either. Right now he owns that red Eclipse, the Maxima, the Mustang, a 97 BMW 740 IS, and that black 318 IS. Not too long ago he sold a 97 Eclipse and a 03 Tiburon to his sister.He started out when he was 14, working for another guy. Dropped out at 16, sold 3 of his cars that year, and has been going on since then. He only started the Collision shop IN Collision inc. a year or two ago. For the paintjob, it depends what it is. If it was just to repaint the car without taking care of any dents etc. it would be around $1000, that is non-street price. If it was some random person who came in, it would be more expensive. If it was you , I know you (somewhat lol) so it would be different. If you wanted a full paintjob with all dents removed etc. it would be around $1500. Then again, it all depends on cost of paint etc. Some paint is $70 a gallon, other paint like chameleon is $1k a gallon so you can see that price would vary. So basically what happens if you want something done is you show the car, say what you want done, we give the price. There have been too many times before where some russian people would come in and say "I need my fender painted, there is a small dent". The price would be given, they would bring the car in, and the fender is not a little dent, but needs to be replaced. Now we like to look at the car first by either having the customer come by the shop, or come by their house. That way it's easier on both the customer and our ends. I'm sure looking at pictures of a car would work, as that is how Ilya buys wrecked cars from different auction sites.
