# "Punch me I bleed!"

by Ryukenshen on September 2, 2005
If your curious about the new format check out my last blog. General. Yesterday could've been better. Basically I sat around pixelling, talked on the phone, and played soccer. The soccer bit was moronic - it was the 'Kick off' of the season, and pretty much we just got up on the bleachers to have our picture taken. At the very least, I got to kick my brother's ball (to all perverts: not THAT kind of ball) into the girls' practice field. And he hates girls. I also got to finish watching Spider-Man 2 - or, rather, watch it over from the beginning. It got me into a Spider-Man mood, so I googled 'Spider-Man' and, to my delight, found the official site. Even more to my delight, there was a panel that read, 'Spider-Man 3 countdown: 609 days left! May 4, 2007'. So, today, there are only 608! I'm so happy. Speaking of today, I plan to take a trip to the library and lay my dirty hands on Artemis Fowl 4: The Opal Deception. Hopefully they'll have it - or else my friend Chuck will read it before me. I also want to get this book I saw about Harry Potter, but I don't know if I can do it without mom noticing - some of the illustrations of mythical creatures are - how shall I ut this? - nude. Farodin. Yesterday was basically re-coloring trees for the Demo to break up the monotony. I'm redoing boards 1 and 2, because I just HATE them, and I'm also changing the colors on Will slightly to match my avatar, which is much better. Video Games - Rayman 2. This is an excellent game that I recomend to anyone looking for an adventure. The graphics are good, and the story, while not being exactly innovative, is fun. Music is nearly flawless, and the gameplay is top notch - just don't get the GBC version. Rating: 8/10
