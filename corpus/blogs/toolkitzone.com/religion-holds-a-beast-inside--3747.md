# religion holds a beast inside

by P-WURM on December 16, 2005
Long time no blog. Let's get back to bloggin' and tell about what happened in the month of no bloggingness. Well, first of all, I got myself an accustic guitar. A Santos y Major. It's a lightwood guitar with a pattern around the soundbox and redwood on the sides and back. Nylon and Metal strings (5 sets that come with it) and a free tuning machine... which I don't need but it takes less time and it looks really awesome. It came all the way from Spain to Kapelle where my parents picked it up. It sounds really clear and pure. Wrote some songs on it and play it everyday. For the rest I was simply really bussy with school and the Expo. I drew some awesome stuff which I didn't get a chance to upload for yet. Be aware Art Forum, because I'm on my way. I have a small demo out for my game and that's about it, for the rest still a lot of stuff which is personal and none of your business..
