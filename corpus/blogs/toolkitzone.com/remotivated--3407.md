by Trent on September 28, 2005
Listening to from . In a mood. I've recieved new motivation to work on my game, thanks to STLP. However, I'm welcomed by a REALLY hard bug. I've posted it in the Advanced Coding forum. I want to finish the BS completely, and get all characters, techs, and spells done before I release another demo.
