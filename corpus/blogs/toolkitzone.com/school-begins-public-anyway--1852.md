# ...School Begins. Public, Anyway.

by Slasher on December 9, 2004
Posted by Amin on Thu Dec 9 15:42:35 2004: ...Just for anyone who wanted to hear about it. My schedule is as follows: Anatomy/Physiology - (redacted: address) Chemistry - (redacted: address) Geometry - (redacted: address) English - (redacted: address) Yes, only 4 classes. The high school runs by quite a strange system every year. The year is divided into 2 semesters, and each semester has 3 marking periods within it. During the first semester, you are given 4 courses, and each class takes about an hour and a half (instead of the usual 45 minute class). Lunch is strangely stuck into 3rd period, no matter what it is, so when the bell rings, class is interrupted as you go to lunch for 20 minutes, and come back (Mr. Reed, a guidance counselor, says this is the "flaw" in the school. He then scratched his chin and said that basically everything in the school system is a flaw. How lovely.). In any case, the school has a whole history of strange occurances, but I'll go into detail another time. The school is flooded with over 3000 students (!), all of them EXTREMELY loud and outgoing. I shouldn't wonder if I'm the only student who likes to stay reserved. In any case, classes aren't so bad. Chemistry is being replaced by Computer Lab, so I'm a tad bit happy about tomorrow on. Ah, well. I bet you're bored by now, so I apologize ^_^
**Post Scriptum: I dissect a cat tomorrow in Anatomy. Hurrah.**
