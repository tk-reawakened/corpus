# School Pilgrimage

by Darwin on April 8, 2006
Yes, we were at the school pilgrimage to Częstochowa. But Częstochowa sounds lame, so I'll use the name Varanasi. First we drove 3 or so hours to Varansi with a bus. Thes we got out, got some free time, and were supposed to meet at the start of the "cross road". I hanged around with 3-4 other guys, went up some tower, whatever :P. Then we got to the place. "Cross road" is a catholic march through 14 stations, commemorating the path of Christ to the crucificiton. At every station, we heard some catholic propaganda - henotly, I expected something more sensical. Even the march itself was short. At the start of it, I stood cloe to the bishop, so I even thought about grabbing his hat and running away. :P But I didn't do it (and honestly, I had a moment of thought as for whether I should). After that, we got to a conference. At this part I should note that religious celebrations like these generally make me depressed and I took no pills today. Either way, at the conference, my thoughts were oscillating between how the bi-hop isn't such an idiot as I expected him to be and how he makes sense, but is on the wrong side, and between the usual: commiting suicide (which in my mind became a secular way to restore honour) or just opening the window and running away to some wilderness area. In such cases, I am probably the one who takes most suffering. Other people just either half-listen and half-don't-give-a-fuck. The bishop spoke about the question of existence of God, various moments of his life when he thought he believed in God but really it was just because he was so romantic or his parents told him to do that. He ended up the story with a talk like "People don't disbelieve in God because of the reason. They disbelieve because of the sin they have. Anyone who has a reason and who has a heart never questions the existence of God." At this point I just wanted to grab a sharp object and stab myself repeteadly. But I jind of got through that, too. Then we got to the mass. 5 thousand people in a chapel... I could almost imagine why people get trampled during the Hajj. The mass was long, dull, boring, and I virtually sat on some "side altar" (no idea what these are) by the wall because there was no place. After that, we got out, and it turned out that putting a skull cap despite the scorching sun is a great way to attract unknown females' attention. Then we got some more free time and were supposed to meet again at the monastery for a blessing but I didn't come. I hanged with some other bunch of guys and gals, ate some ice-cream and trash food, then needed to take a piss but there were no bushes around where I wouldn't be seen and the toilets were commercial. We got back to the bus, I slept through the way, and we got back. It's easier living in this sad world when you don't realise it's run by idiots.
