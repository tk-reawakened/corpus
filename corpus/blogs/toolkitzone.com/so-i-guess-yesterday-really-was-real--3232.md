# So I guess yesterday really was real.

by QuickCat on September 2, 2005
I woke up, and my mom was crying. That's what I fell asleep to. Crying. I cried a lot myself, but I still expect him to show up from work. He was ten minutes away from home. Half an hour late, and my mom was already in tears. He told my mom once, that if she had died, us kids would never visit him. He was a fool. We all loved him. It just took his death to make us all realize it. I went to the wreck yard to get what we could of his from the wreckage of the car. There was come device used for CPR, with blood on the mouth piece. Blood on the seats. We found his cell phone. 20 missed calls from home.
