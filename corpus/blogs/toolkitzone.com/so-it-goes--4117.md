# So it goes...

by Darwin on March 23, 2006
I didn't return to the chat after walking the dog because I was watching the last episode of "Stones of death" series (that's the literal translation of the Polish title, but the series are French, and I don't know the original name or its English translation, which may be totally different). I saw just one pisode before (my parents watch that), but it's an amazing thing if you like thrillers. Other that that, I am making the bibliography (or at least am supposed to) for the oral presentation. And I am kind of short on resources. Wouldn't it be nice if I could list 2 books instead of 1? Of course, but how to do it? And here, I employ a brilliant trick: I make 1 book... into 2. In the bibliography, that is. It turns out the book with a satire poem I will talk about has some descriptive material as an intro... which I'm going to list separately. Brilliant. Did you ever notice how human figure, when crouched, resembles a frog? In fact, I guess that'd be true for all land-based predators.
