# Spells

by Lisha on May 20, 2006
Not as good - or as long - as previous entries, but be leaniant please. =) Because I have: a) Done TWO entries today, one for yesterday (so my creative juices are kinda dry) b) Written this under the effects of antihistamine (I have a REALLY bad cold) I think that this is pretty good considering those two things. =) *** May 20 I am so frustrated! Ive tried , and nothing helps. I cant figure out what the hell that stupid spell means. At first I thought that maybe if I did the opposite of everything in the spell then I could reverse it - there are spell like that, sometimes - but that did nothing. Then I tried what can only be explained as every other damn possibility out there. Hanev was patient as I performed all of these spells on him. I give him credit for that. I couldnt have been as patient as he was. But even though he was patient, he had his limits, too. It was my seventh hour of spell casting, and both of our fortitudes were wearing thin. I nearly shot a hole through the wall be accident, just then, and that was when Hanev sighed and said, Maybe you should call it a day? His voice was barely audible. He was getting weaker. I slumped down into a chair. I should, but I feel like I have to keep going. You cant help me if youre exhausted. I bit my lip as I stared back at him. It looked like was the one who was exhausted. I couldnt blame him. Its more than likely I ended up hurting - rather than helping - him with some of those spells. He wouldnt have said so if I had, but I had a feeling that I did. Im so sorry about all of this, I said apologetically. He looked surprised. Why? Youve done nothing wrong. This cant be very fun for you. Thats what youre sorry about? He smiled, weakly, but sincerely. I cant imagine that its horribly fun for you either. I snickered. Not particularly. Then, more seriously, But its worth it. Instead of making him feel better, this appeared to upset him. He shot me a troubled look. Saphyra dont dont get your hopes up. If you cant if cant He seemed to be unsure of his words. What Im trying to say is, dont blame yourself if I die. You arent going to die, I said with false confidence. The confidence was false because I wasnt as sure as I wanted to sound, but still, I really dont think that Hanev is going to die. If I continue working as hard as I am now, I find a way to lift this spell hes under. I have to. He frowned at that, but only for a moment before it was replaced with an expression that somehow was both sad and grateful at the same time. I appreciate all of this, he said quietly. I wouldnt be able to do this for someone whodid what I did to you. You got me into it, and you got me out, I answered. At great expense to yourself, too. I owe it to you. You dont owe me anything, he said with finality. I stood up and crossed the room. Hanev, you arent as bad a person as you think you are. Nowhere near. You have to give yourself at least a little bit of credit. As I spoke, I glanced out the window to see the horse grazing peacefully in the field. It was strangely comforting. I turned around to see him slowly shaking his head, a smile on his face. Its strange that you should be the one to tell me this, he said. You, of all people. I raised one eyebrow questionably. Why is that? Because The smile disappeared from his face instantly. Because what you went through thats all my fault. Yet its who is telling me that I dont give myself enough credit. Its amazing, he went on, smile returning, voice getting softer, the way you can do that. Amazing. It isnt amazing, I said, very quietly, but I couldnt restrain a small smile. Then he said, hesitantly, I wasnt going to tell you this, because I didnt want you to worry. Right as he said those words, I became worried. But now I think that you have to know, or else you wont be prepared. My nervousness heightened as he paused for a moment, as if collecting his words. I wrote to Brayan. I blanched. You ?! I was horrified. Just after I saw that picture that he had painted, too did Hanev know about that picture? I assumed not, because it was obviously painted after Hanev left. If he knew, would he still have wrote to Brayan? Why did he even write to Brayan in the first place? Just a small section of the questions swarming through my head at that moment. I wanted to see him again, Hanev said, avoiding eye contact, in case I die. But but I was at a loss for words. Hanev, he still could be with you. He could I didnt finish that sentence, because pictures of all of the way I could finish that sentence flashed through my mind. I shuddered. He wont. He sounded so sure. You sound too sure. He wont, Hanev persisted. He wont hurt me. How do you know?! I cried. For Gods sake, Hanev, the man painted a pictureof I stopped too late. Hanev stared at my questioningly. When I didnt continue, he said, Go one. He painted a picture of what? There was a slight note of fear in his voice. I didnt want to try and explain it, so instead I ran downstairs and took the painting from the wall. I brought it back to Hanevs room and showed it to him. I am sure that I must have been visibly cringing. Then it was his turn to blanch. For a split second panic passed over his face, then he calmed amazingly quickly. His voice was even and composed when he spoke. I will just have to be careful, then. Are you sure that he will come? I asked desperately. Hanev nodded once, slowly. Its definitely more than likely. I buried my face in my hands. My God, Hanev, what possessed you to something like that? The many ways I could have finished that sentence, He could, were still running through my mind. He didnt answer, so I went on, When do you think hell come? Tomorrow, or the day after Im not sure where he was, I just sent it by a simple spell. His voice was calm and even, but his face betrayed underlying fear. I groaned and went on asking questions, and Hanev went on answering them. I didnt learn much more. Finally, we decided it best to just call it a day. We were both exhausted, from the spell casting earlier and then the worrying later. Im hoping that Brayan doesnt come. I dont know what well do if he does. Its more than likely that hes still mad at Hanev more than mad, even. I dont know what I could do if he tried to hurt him. I dont know. Oh, please God, please, please, please let him not come. -Saphyra
