# Sticky controller buttons...

by DrakeLord on June 12, 2006
Ok. My brother took my gamecube over to a friends house, so they could all play Super Smash Bros. Melee and Double Dash. Now, today I opened up the gamecube cover, and it felt like the Open button was somewhat sticky. I thought it was just me, so I popped in Double Dash to play it. Everything loaded when I pressed the A button. It was sticky. I pressed the B button. It was sticky. Luckily every other button was fine, but as most of you should know, Double Dash is quite hard to play when the B button is pressed all the way down, and the A button doesn't work half the time. -_-; That isn't my only controller, but I'd sure like to know how to fix it. So, all I'm asking, is if someone out there knows how to "unstick" controller buttons.
