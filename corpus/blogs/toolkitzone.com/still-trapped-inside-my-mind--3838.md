# Still trapped inside my mind

by Penguin on January 10, 2006
I have become... indifferent. Physically, mentally, morally, politically, socially, sexually and ideologically. I have also noticed that our school radiators have been made in USSR. They were protected by some wooden fence, but someone devastated the fence around one radiator and it reveals "sdelano v sssr" written on it in Cyrillic. The tragism of my world can be presented in 5 simple sentences of the world truth that I can't get out of my mind. 1. The only goal of genes is reproduction. 2. We are just survival machines created by our genes to fulfill their task efficiently. 3. All altruism is induced by the genetical design of us - the survival machines - to increase chances of survival of the same genes as ours in other individuals, the fresh copies of our genes, or our genes' means of reproduction. 4. In order to reproduce, genes can sacrifice the organism. 5. Death is not a biological necessity, but a genetical mechanism to free space for the survival machines bearing fresh copies of our genes. Slowly, through this genetic darkness, light tries to cling me and give hope to my tormented mind; that doesn't change the fact that I have abandoned most past ideals and live on cynism, masturbation, drinking coke and eating meat.
