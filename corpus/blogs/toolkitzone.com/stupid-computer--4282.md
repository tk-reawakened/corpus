# Stupid Computer...

by Alex on May 1, 2006
Well, my stupid computer stopped working, or the hard drive rather. This is incrediblly unlucky because it lands on final exam week and I make my study guides on my computer. It all started when I was trying to attach my makeshift 'headset' to the computer, but it just seemed like it was taking a little too long to load. All of the sudden I hear this scratching sound coming from where my harddrive was. Crap. I turned off my computer and turned it back on to see what would happen and a message comes up saying "Internal HDD Hard Error". I know that pretty much tells me the arm in the hard drive is jacked up or something. I've got no clue how or why is happened.
