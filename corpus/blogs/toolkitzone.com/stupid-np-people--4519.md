# Stupid NP people...

by Ryukenshen on June 15, 2006
So a couple days ago my brother had his birthday party. His friends came over and they went out for a while. Conveniently, his friend's brother's my friend. We hung out for a while. Dad also rented Hoodwinked, which we promptly watched. It was surprisingly funny, if not laugh-out-loud funny. The animation sucked worse than Veggietales, though. And yesterday Mom dragged us on this six-hour road trip. The only plus was that I got half way through another level in Sacred Stones and got her to let me buy an issue of Nintendo Power and Shonen Jump at Wal-Mart. Then I got hooked on Buso Renkin, but a headache, put it down, and picked up the Nintendo Power. Now those stupid NPs made me want to go out and buy a DS Lite. I've gotta admit, the games look absolutely hot. Even - though I don't know why - Brain Age. I found myself writing a list of games I'd want the other day. It's about ten items too long. I'm not sure about my Overworld background. Might make the colors darker. Too damn bright at the moment. Also thinking about character placement. Drew a few more enemies. Not sure about them. Might need to be bigger. Getting started on Castle Dungeon crap. Hooray. Life sucks right now. Don't know why. I guess I've got some kind of developer's block going on right now with Rave. In any case, see you later. **Main**
