# That was a close one...

by DarkMoon on January 15, 2006
Well, it's winter, right? Guess again. There hasn't been too much snow of anything around here like normal. Understand now that by now there's normally about a half a metre of snow. There is no where near that amount. Well, it was snowing and cold and everything, just like winter should be. But no, it just couldn't stay like that. For probably a week or two the weather was nearly spring. Temperatures ranged in the low 50s and all of the snow melted. It was really beautiful. However, it was very strange. The weather was great, everyone was happy, yeah. You know what? It snowed again. It got really cold again and it is now like winter should be. Well, last night my friend had a piano recital. Okay, well, it snowed and the roads got icy and it was windy. So, I'm driving out to where the recital is because I made a special guest appearance that no one knew about. Well, I'm going along and when I least expect it-BAM! The car is sliding on the road and I can't do anything. Whirl whirl whirl and I'm sliding all over the road. Before I knew it I'm sliding around and coming to a stop near a small downward bank. I managed to do a 180 and come within centimetres of fall over the bank. As I sat there I couldn't help but think how close that was to me probably getting flipped over. Well, no damage was down so I continued on. As I drove I realized that I quite enjoyed spinning around like that and I even thought of trying to do it again. I figured I could probably do a full 360 since the roads were so icy. Yeah, I almost got in a wreck but it was worth it to crash the piano recital. It was fun, though. That's the scary thing. I actually enjoyed doing the 180, although I was a little shaken at first. But, WOW!, was it ever close.
