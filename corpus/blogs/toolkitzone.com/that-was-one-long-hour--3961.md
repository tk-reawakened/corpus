# That was one long hour

by TK Game Boy on February 11, 2006
Yesterday a package came to me and I had to pick it up. But apparently I didn't knew where the UPS was located so I went to downtown. I though it was there but apparently there was no UPS. I asked around and got nothing. I then saw a UPS worker delivering and he said that the UPS I'm looking far up north. It appears I was searching south. Without knowing where to go, I took a bus but it failed to take me anywhere. I had to take a couple of busses till I decided to just walk. OI! I was like on the S. 300 and had to go to N. 1800. So that was like 21 blocks. Apparently I walked a while and saw the bus I had to take. I got on it. I got to see some stuff from Los Angeles that I never would of thought of. I saw the Los Angeles river, which is hardly impressive, the historical route 99, which looks like the same crummy neighborhood I live in, and chinatown, which was anything but impressive. I also saw a transvestite, which is something I didn't want to. Eventually I make it to S. 1800 but I don't know where Main street was. I walked around in circles then this half drunken guy tells me where it is. So I walk to the directions he said and there it was. I got my package and went to the bus stop. OI! I told my sister I was only gonna take an hour and I spent 4 hours trying to get here and had I known that the number 45 bus would of taken me there, I probably would of only tooken an hour cuz the 45 is right were I live. So anyways, in the package was my order of Black Jack anime. Very good anime. I may get another disc. TKGB ***************************
