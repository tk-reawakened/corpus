# The Incredible Ghibli-a-thon!

by Ryukenshen on May 6, 2006
Joe Hisaishi r0x0rs. My Stuff We recorded Castle In the Sky! It reeks awesome. We watched it three times; it never got old. Dad saw it too. He liked it, but thought that the ending was weak. It felt a bit...open. Nothing wrong with that, but hey, you know dads. I was worried about the score, seeing as Nausicaa's was...erm...strange...but it turned out great. The animation leaves a little to be desired, but I watch too much Princess Mononoke. (Which I've seen about seven or eight times.) Other than that, the expo's a great way to motivate me. I've been rather busy. Oh, and my cold's going fast, which's pretty nice, too. Rave Larrin What have I done in the past two days? - A battle sprite that could very possibly be scrapped, after some critical thinking - Animations galore: finally a good East running animation, and an update of the South running animation. Both are looking good. - Castle Garden graphics, including dirt, hedges and some flowers that are currently in the works. The grass is questionable, but I'll see what I can do. So, yeah, I've been quite the busy bee. I have, what, twenty days before people start flooding in for the expo, and, like, ten before signups start? Ah, time restraints. A Tale of Two Hearts Nothing to say. Rave has consumed me.
