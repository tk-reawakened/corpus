# This blows banana chunks!

by DarkMoon on October 6, 2005
Okay, so on Tuesday we had another football game. We played the team we went into a shootout with the first time. Well, guess what happened...go ahead and guess. We went into another freakin' shootout! The score was the same at the end! It was tied at 4 - 4. We won the shootout again, however, so it's all good. Anyway, whilst playing the game, I got really beat up. I even messed up my right knee pretty badly. I went down to try and stop the ball right at the end of the first half and when I came up my knee was hurt. I mean it was hurt. I had to get helped off of the field. So, when the second half came around, I slapped on a knee-brace and hobbled onto the field. My defense did an awesome job at stopping the other team. I had to do nothing the entire second half and the extra five minutes of overtime! Which was good because I couldn't walk, run, dive, or do anything. Well, we switch to the secondary goal keeper for the shootout. It worked fine and, like I said, we won. So, I've been limping since then. Well, my knee and calf was swollen, I couldn't really bend or straighten my leg, and I couldn't put any pressure on my foot. I didn't think I hurt my knee that badly but it was swollen even more yesterday. It still hasn't improved much today. I went in to the doctor's office and got some x-rays done. Well, nothing is wrong with the bone but I need to go and get an MRI and I have another appointment. This really blows banana chunks! It puts me off the rest of football season. Well, I just got done icing my knee again and I can actually bend it pretty comfortably; also, some of the swelling has gone done and the bruising is starting to go away. I'm on crutches at the moment and the doctor said I should continue to use them. I started using them on my own because I didn't even plan on going to the doctor. Like I said, I didn't think it was that bad. Anyway, it does seem to be healing so maybe I'll be able to sneek in another game or something, completely against the doctor's orders.
