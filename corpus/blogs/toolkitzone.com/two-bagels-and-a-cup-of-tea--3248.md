# Two bagels and a cup of tea

by DarkMoon on September 5, 2005
Yeah, now that's a fine meal. I had a very nice (and much needed) three day weekend. Only problem is that it's already over! Didn't get to do too much either, which is not cool. At least I got to run over to the movies and with some friends. Well, tomorrow we start school back up after the nice weekend and tomorrow is also our first football game. I'm actually quite looking forward to it, save the fact that I have a broken finger. It is still really fun to be goal keeper and get to bash people around almost the entire game. It's bloody well awesome that's what it is! I also took some time off this weekend from working on my new project. But I will probably do some work tonight and tomorrow night and hopefully by Wednesday I'll have something to show. I think I'll need to eat some more food, like perhaps a bagel, whilst I code programs. I said it before but I do think it helps. Food calms me. I was just worked up a bit from having fun on a trampoline when I came home and ate the two bagels and had a nice cup of tea. It soothed me and now I am more focused and ready to do things. Perhaps it was just the tea. I would say that my drink of choice is tea. In any case, this was just an excuse for me the have another blog. What's the point you ask? Well, this isn't really one. But then again, isn't that the point of most blogs anyway? Oh, and did I mention that I did get quite a bit more of my one story written this weekend?
