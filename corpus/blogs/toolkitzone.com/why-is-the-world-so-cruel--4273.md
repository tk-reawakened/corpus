# Why is the world so cruel?!

by Ryukenshen on April 29, 2006
I really, really, don't get my dad. Recent Events Huh. Thinking up titles indicating the past few days of the story of my life is imcomprehensibly difficult, especially as I know that I'll exaust my glut of plausible ideas after a very short amount of time. Speaking of exhaustion, limiting the amount of words in my sentences to, like, ten, which I just exceeded, would be wise. Well, we rented Spirited Away the other night. Dad also got Angel Eyes. He said that it's a really good film that got bad reviews. Well, whatever you say, father. Watch your hot action on mute once I'm asleep. Today was rather boring. I had a friend over last night. We pretty much stayed up and watched Princess Mononoke, (which I'm...erm...'borrowing' for an extended period of time,) and then played Smash Brothers. Go figure. I'm really starting to like that game. I already watched it, but, as it was leaking awesome all over the living room, I popped Spirited Away in the DVD player. Like, three hours ago. Despite my sister's dire warning that we'd rapidly lose brain cells as the film progressed, dad sat down to watch it too. It took a bit of convincing, actually. We got as far as the Stink God and the disk froze up, so we had to stop. It wouldn't be so bad, but dad isn't exactly pleased with it so far. Mother, father, sister...why is the world so cruel? I thought I had these peoples' genes! Rave Larrin Tree perfected. Perfecto. It took about a week, but now I can now move on to twenty variations of rock formations. Hoorah for crappy jokes. For those of you following iFrog's blog: I still can't get that photo of him out of my head... His nature contradicts his image. Seriously. *really*
