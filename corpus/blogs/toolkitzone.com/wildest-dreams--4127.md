# Wildest Dreams

by Darwin on March 26, 2006
The firist part of my dream was about the guy who played in that movie about a man who fell in love with the female brain. In my dream, he found some way to ressurect dead people and was doing that in his office using a strange orb, but then he got some problems or whatever. Either way, it later turned out (the dream totally shifted) that the guy's office was in Poland. South of it, there was an unfinished temple, which was missing 2 final levels, at which pinnac/le was supposed to be a satined glass orb representing the world (but there wasn't, because it was unfinished). In order to secure the temple and that aforementioned guy's office, Latvians and Lithuanians have invaded Poland and took over some territories (like in San Andreas) - amazingly, Latvia was to the south of Poland, but that's not important. Because I was so noble that I wanted people to be able to be ressurected, I wanted to get back these territories. The attackers drew some comics on the captured territiories and I drew mine, so that my comics could persuade them to leav. Either way, that part of the dream made little sense. I also had some crazy feeling that my mouth feels with saliva and I can't spit it (ugly). Then the dream shifted to show some events from World War Two - a regular explosion followed by some blue flame explosion. In that event, as it was lather revealed, some Alien Queen took over Latvia. I got put into prison - presumably for drawing the comics - and I've lost my sight. The prison was white (I knew it because in the dream, I could see myself in the third person ;) though the view sometimes changed to mine), filled with some techonology (it looked more like an inside of a submarine, but all white) and I was put in a small cell. There, there was a bed and some machine. A guy came, some friendly one, and told me to do something with the machine, put some crystal in it and look at it - either way, there was a sudden flash and I could see. But my vision was bizzare, filled with strange lights. Something was strange about me... Then, suddenly, a Latvian (who was some evil doctor, I thought) broke nto the prison and, I guess, killed the personel and the friendly guy who helped me regain my sight. I had to run away. Then, I saw a vision - the unfinished temple that I mentioned before turned into a giant, blue organical fleshy mound that was the incarnation of the Alien Queen (it looked like some Zerd building, except blue, some saliva-like substance was dripping from it). The Queen demanded to give her the orb (I guess the same as the guy used previously to ressurect people, but it may have been a later-added retrospection), and as the Latvians returned to her with it, she demandedthat they stop building imperfect structures and instead allow the pure form to take place (the blue organical mass). I knew I had to stop the Queen, but the final part of the dream turned into just a sequence of scenes, like in a movie commercial. There was me, with my strange vision, sminning underwater over some gaps or explosive mines, and I suddenly turned into a blue, muscular alien spawn to gain strength to smin through the danger. I realised that I have this evil power that I need to use, but I can't use it all the time because it is so evil. Whatever. Then, there were some commercials of "Dinosaur Erotica" which I won't describe because you'll get bad mental pictures. The end.
