# Wow

by DarkMoon on September 23, 2005
Okay, so we had our third football game, also out first home game. It was a doozy! Last year this team was pretty weak and we completely destroyed them. However, things were not so this time around. They gained more members and some skill. We did not have an easy fight! Our team completely fell apart at the end and they scored a few goals and tied the game. Now, while I'm the goal keeper there were a few that should have been stopped by defence. They scored one goal because the bottom defender on our team got a hand-ball in the box! It was nerve-wracking! I nearly saved it but it was just out of my reach. Bleh, the game was tied at the end. We normally would just go into overtime but this case was different. One of our officials had to leave, meaning we did not have time to go into overtime. We had to do a shootout! Now, anyone that knows anything about football knows about a shootout. It was pretty scary. It was the first shoot out that I've ever been in and, boy, was it exciting. It was kinda scary too. Five guys, five shots, which means I had five chances to save our game. It also came down to the other guys shooting on our team. Yeah, that's right, I'm counted in the five best kickers of our team. Well, they went over the rules and we got set up. I was up first, meaning they shot first. The guy got a good hard kicked off to the side. I somehow managed to dive and save it! Wow, everyone was cheering and excited. It was awesome. I was surprised I got it, but I guess that's why I'm the goal keeper. Anyway, we switch and our first guy scored. The next shot from the opposing team got in because the guy placed it perfectly. It went right into the upper right corner. I dove but couldn't reach it. Our next shot went in, putting us at a lead of 2 - 1. There next man stood to shoot. I prepared. Bam! He sent it hard right at me. I fumbled some but recovered and had another save! The next shot came and almost missed. We now had a lead of 3 - 1. But things weren't over yet. Their next player came up and put the ball in the far left corner, on the ground. I couldn't reach it if I tried. It was a beautiful shot. Our next shot went in and we won it with a shootout score of 4 - 2. There was no reason for our last players to shoot because we would win either way. So, wow, things were pretty intense.
