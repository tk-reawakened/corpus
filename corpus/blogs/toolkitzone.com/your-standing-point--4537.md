# "Your standing point"

by Icbim on June 18, 2006
I feel good. I feel like doing my blogs like how I used to. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Well, today I did nothing much. I got back from spending the weekend over at a old friends house. I'll talk about that later, though. When I got home I was tired and felt horrible...But I had to go driving with my instructor at 3pm to 5pm. When we got to the place to meet him at he never showed up. He's old so maybe he forgot today was fathers day. Or we heard the date wrong. So we left and went out to eat at Ruby Tuesdays... Well, not too long after we got home from all that my friend called me and told me to go to church tonight...even though i've only been to church twice. Not that much of a religeous fellow. So I went...and I was dragged into being in a fucking play is what it turnt out. So we had skits...I was in my friends group and we made little to no progress... My friend eventually brought out his guitar and decided to use it for a skit when we had to rush up a idea. My friend knows I play guitar and he does too which he always says he "owns" at playing guitar....so I felt like showing him up. I told him to hand me the guitar when we got on stage and he said "Wait, don't you need a pick?". Right then I let out a sarcastic "Pfffft". So I got on stage in front of a bundle of people and played green day's "Good Riddens"...finger picking and all. My friend was in awe and the crowd was clapping and stuff. He said he wants to play without using a pick now. That left me with the greatest feeling in the world. My legs were shaking all to hell though. I was suprised I didn't mess up...My hand was really shakey. Well. That was my day. I feel good. I want to make music. And I did. I'm putting periods everywhere. Well, I went over an old friends house friday and stayed till sunday morning. It was pretty awsome for staying in the house all weekend... I sort of invited myself over there friday since geoff was going over and there's a pool over there. I got there at about 7pm and started playing Full Auto on Xbox live. After that we ate some pizza and went to go swim till about 9pm or something like that. Saturday we just played Full Auto and Call Of Duty 2 online...and I started singing some Eagle Eye Cherry and it sounded . I wish I had the guitar with me :P. We also went swimming for a couple of hours again... Just pizza and stuff again. After awhile, geoff choked on water and puked. It was nasty...but it happens. Sunday, you know. Whatever :P. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- It's been awhile since i've done a serious music blog. Now I can finally do them again 'cause i'm doing serious music work. Back then it was just lyrics...now it's both lyrics and the guitar. "Your Standing Point" is one of my more serious songs that I put some work into...I'd say either the first or second in place of my most-worked-on songs. It has to do with wondering where this girl went since I havn't seen her in awhile. It was dedicated to this neighbor of mine years ago that I fell for...but she eventually moved away. The songs rules...it's very soft but sounds more professional against my other songs both lyric and guitar-wise. I'm hoping to record a live acoustic version here soon and share it soon. Which will also be the day I open up my official Pure volume account...not a myspace though. I just don't like myspace anymore for some reason. Havn't been on in ages. I'm hoping to continue my musical blogs much more from this point on. Marking my 70th blog :P ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ I'm out amigos. I'm pretty whiped. It's been another day...I need some sleep. See ya.
