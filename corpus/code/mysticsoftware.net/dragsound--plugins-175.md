# DragSound

Plugins
DragSound
I dont know why I never submited this plugin. Its been sitting on my computer and website for atleast 9 months or so. Anyway this plugin gives you access to DirectX's sound resources. You can play a midi and set it to loop easily, it loops very nicely(no pauses like TK :P). While the midi is playing you can play several wave files(all at the same time, unlike TK :P). You can set the volume and pan of EACH!! wave. For those who don't know, basically, pan the volume/level of a sound on a left or right speaker :S. If you wanted to create the illision that a car was comming from the right side of the screen, you would set the pan of a 'vroom' wave to the right speaker. Most 3D games use this technique. Download this plugin if you would like to improve the sound in your Game.
