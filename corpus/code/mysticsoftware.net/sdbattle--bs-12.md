# SDBattle

Battle Systems
SDBattle
This an original, well done battle system by Dragcash. It is less extensive than DragBattle2, but it has a stellar interface and a new stat orientation. Requires you turn off paralaxing. In the future, with a polished package and some documentation, this will surely be the one of the best battle systems out there. Until then, check it out if you think you can understand pretty difficult code, or you just want to see the beginnings of a great BS!
