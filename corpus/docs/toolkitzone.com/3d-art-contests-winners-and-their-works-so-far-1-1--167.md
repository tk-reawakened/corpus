[ 3D Art Contest 1.1 took place in October 2005, and started off slow with just a few contestants. Indeed, the Contest had to be bumped a few times to keep it at the top of the list...until I submitted the article , which not only gave the contest the additional shout-out it oh-so-needed, but helped to show the entire community this is every bit as much of an iron-clad contest as Khin's Pixel Art Contests. Anyways, the theme of that contest was weapons modeling...all contenders placed in this one, as there were only 5 contestants, and a 3-way-tie for 3rd Place. Here's their works: [3rd Place]: Caliber Mengsk, Notsosuperhero, McAden [2nd Place]: Venstor [1st Place]: Greg

[ 3D Art Contest 1.2, this time in November, was a lot stronger from the git-go. Armed with the advice from my critiques of their weapons renderings, and with a few critiques of their own to streamline the contest in general, the contestants dived headlong into a...well, not rush...to render some wonderful landscape scenes. There was much modeling, and updating, until the Contest came to an end on November 30th...and here's the winners and their works: [Honorable Mentions]: I R A Ch00b, Caliber Mengsk [3rd Place]: Notsosuperhero [2nd Place]: Spyder [1st Place]: taco
