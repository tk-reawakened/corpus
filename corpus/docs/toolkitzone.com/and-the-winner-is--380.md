# And the winner is...

Browse
Best Game Graphics
So the first award this year is for Best Game Graphics in a game released as a demo or full game during 2008. Before we go any further, I'll talk a bit about how the awards themselves. I'd like to point out that all game related awards, with the exception of one, are solely for games with playable releases. We want to recognise these projects and curb the usual trend of games that never see a release dominating awards such as graphics. Plus, it's a helluva lot easier to determine if a game qualifies for a calendar year if we go by an actual release date. Also, there are no second and third place awards. Just a winner and, where appropiate, some honorable mentions. Finally, each award comes with 50 ranking points on the official contest rankings.![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1236.png)

Over the past few years we've slowly seen Brad emerge from his niche of creating super smooth, professional looking user interfaces to branch out into creating custom tilesets with great aplomb. His graphics may not blow you away like some of the "will they ever be released?" projects out there, but they're slick, consistant and above all easy on the eye. The humorous Three Guys No Beer is further evidence of his hard work in this field and if you take a look at the , in the "Graphics" category you may be surprised to know it's in 2nd position for full games and 5th overall. The main criticism raised was a lack in variation, but otherwise it's glowing reviews. Here's a sample of what people had to say in their reviews; [official game rankings](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?say&mode=ranking)
[TraceTheHybrid](http://www.toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=10444) said *"* *Brad really is good at graphics. All the boards, NPCs, interfaces, etc, really did fit well, and it was all just the perfect fit."* [more](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=587)
[Bagel](http://www.toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=4798) said *"Brad's graphics have always been a main focal point in his games. The menu is streamlined and the board tiles and character tiles mesh well together." [more](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=556)*
[UltimaWizard](http://www.toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=5278) said *"* *Excellent graphic design! The UI was clean, simple, and stylized; the tiles were convincing, even if the trees weren't quite so. Still - excellent pretty graphics!"*
[Mr.Troublesome](http://www.toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=7910) said *"The interfaces were professional and clean looking. The overall quality of the graphics could easily be in the 9-10 range, the reason why I give it only a 8 is that it gets a bit repetitive visually." [more](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=409)*
So, congratulations to Brad as he picks up the Best Game Graphics award for Three Guys No Beer!
