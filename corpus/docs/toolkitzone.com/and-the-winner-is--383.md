# And the winner is...

Browse
Outstanding Forum Contribution
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1238.png) Onto day three of our awards marathon and today it's all about services to the community. The forums are a hub of discussion and activity, it's the first place people head to get help or discuss their game ideas and efforts. And while the internet isn't always the greatest at constructive or to the point feedback, there is always the valiant few who do their upmost to come to the aid of other users. Whether it's solving an RPG Code bug, explaining classes, giving feedback on a screenshot... these guys are the unsung heroes of any community. So... the Outstanding Forum Contribution award is all about recognising a member from the previous year who did just this.
**And the winner is...**
He's only been around for 18 months and yet is known to most regulars in the community. Not because he gets into flame wars, or exhibits acts of stupendous noobness, but because he's constantly and more importantly helpfully replying to the questions and troubles of newbies and oldbies alike. Whilst never proclaiming to be always having the right opinion or answer, jackboy can be seen spending most of his Toolkit life on the Code Debugging and Newbie Haven. It's testament to the guy that as soon as these awards were announced people were already name-dropping him as someone who deserves to win something. If he has one flaw, it's that the silly bugger refuses to get an avatar which makes this article look real plain and boring. Damn you!
I am, of course, talking about...
**jackyboy**
I'm sure many of you can give him a good pat on the back as a response to this award. And when you do, tell him to get an avatar for next year's awards!!!

The truth is there are many people who, in their own way, made good impressions on the forums over the past year. So f eel free to name drop in the discussion that follows, I'm sure they'd appreciate it.
