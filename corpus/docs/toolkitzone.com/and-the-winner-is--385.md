# And the winner is...

Browse
Demo of the Year
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1243.png)
Before I begin, I'd like to congratulate Brad on picking up his second award of the month as he won the vote for with First Death on Mars. Bravo, sir. Despite the Toolkit community being one that generally produces more demo than full games, this year has been something of an oddity with just five demo releases to eight full games. To be honest, the demo field is extremely weak this year both in numbers and quality. But hey ho, we need a winner so let's get on with it... [Most Promising Game](http://toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=384)
**And the winner is...**
[![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1244.png)](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?game=2658)

With its surreal take on the Toolkit community and fun dialogue, The Truth Revealed has the chance to be an instant favourite. The solid if not spectacular demo sets the tone for the story and has glimmers of promising feature ideas such as character transformation. Marz has stated that the full game will do away with the ripped stuff, so it's already a step in the right direction. Here's a sample of what people had to say in their reviews;
said [Casimir](http://toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=3770) *"* *It was fairly fun, with the given ability to transform and do a quest or two, as well as interact with some of the members of TKZ in sprite form. It was very humorous, too, which is always a plus in my book!".* [more](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=555)
[Slasher](http://toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=3969) said *"...the demo is overall enjoyable, and I'd encourage at least one go to anyone slightly curious about our "rich history" and Marz's coding talents. I'll be watching this one." [http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=480](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=480)* more
Well done Marz for your award of Demo of the Year! Keep plugging away and we all hope to see more in 2009!
