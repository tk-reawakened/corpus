# And the winner is...

Browse
Best Game Music
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1247.png)
After yesterday's article, I still can't quite believe Jackyboy has 99% of his posts in the Toolkit related forums, while Sir Pimpalot has 94% in off-topic forums. It's almost tailor made for some kind of Toolkit hero/nemesis comic entitled Topic Wars or something. Anyhow, onwards we go and onto the Best Game Music category, remember it's from any released game in either demo or full game form. Will Brad be in for his third award of the opening week, or will someone else spring a surprise? Well... what you waiting for? The answer is a few inches way, stupid!
**And the winner is...**
[![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1248.png)](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?game=3599)
**User:**
**Released:**
November 4th
**Score:**
72% (6 says)
**Ranking:**
Full Games - 7th All Games - 10th
It's another project that has decided to go against the stereotype of what type of game the Toolkit is supposed to produce, receiving a good response during its limited release period thus far. The game needed music and sound effects to match the unique setting and style, and it delivered the goods. Can't ask for much more!
