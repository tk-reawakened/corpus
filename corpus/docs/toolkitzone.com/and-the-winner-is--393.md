# And the winner is...

Browse
Plugger of the Year
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1252.png)
As well as the 20ish awards and additional articles, we're handing out a few jokey awards. They don't carry any contest ranking points nor are they recorded in the winner's profile... just a bit of fun. Later in the month some will also be handed over to community voting, although some like today are given outright to the "winner". Plus, considering today's award, it's pretty darn obvious who the winner will be! Still, we have procedure to adhere to. What's a plugger? It's someone who manages to mention their game despite there being little to know context to do so. They'll crowbar a reference to their project at any cost with no shame whatsoever.
**And the winner is...**
Here's a few quick examples I found by this user, I replaced his game name (for sake of needless suspense) to "[plug]";
- In a review of someone elses game, this plugger opens the very first sentence with; So not only has he plugged his game is a review for someone else and implied it is potentially great, but he also takes a jab at those who he perceived as having judged his demo harshly for bugs and yet were more leniant on a (superior) peer (Mr. **"This is a potentially great game marred by stupid little bugs (and I'm not talking about [plug], though apparently these games are held to different standards..."** Chip, meet Mr.Shoulder). It's a beautiful one-two plug punch!!
- In another of his reviews, a discussion in the comments ensues and someone mentions the reviewed game is one of the best demos. Did the user give a reasoned, objective response to this claim? Of course not, he simply said; . He'll claim it was a self-deprecating joke, but even so a shameless plugger will sell their mother if it means mentioning their game. In all, he mentioned it three times in the topic... and his mother is now being shipped to the Dominican Republic. **"[plug]'s demo was better than this"**
- One developer posts a town map for their project. This plugger's insightful feedback? . I guess it's a compliment? **"Good good... reminds me of my layout for [plug]"**
But the following is my particular all-time favourite and it was posted with a straight face, that you can have no doubt. This guy is genius at plugging...
- A user posts how he has been hit by a car and will be absent for a while. This good plugger's heartfelt, concerned response? I mean seriously, you got to applaud him for that! **"Wow, this reminds me of a scene in [plug]. Good luck to you!"**
The ultimate irony is this whole joke award is yet another plug for his game. We are, of course, talking of the community's own red-headed step child that we all wish we could beat...
**Seafroggys**
And his game [plug]!
Congrats, you shameless bastard. I challenge you to not plug your game name in the comments to this...
