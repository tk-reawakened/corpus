# And the winner is...

Browse
Best Game Writing
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1253.png)
Back on the award trail today and it's all about game releases once more. The Best Game Writing award is handed out to not just a game with a good story but also engaging dialogue, interesting character interaction and all-round fluent exposition. Afterall, it's possible to have a premise of a good story but execute it terribly through a badly written script. It was a tough choice deciding where to award this, it really was, I'm sure there will be a bit of debate about it since the field is a bit weak this year. So let's not beat about the bush any further...
**And the winner is...**

It got mixed reactions, in truth. While many said it was lacking in depth when it came to plot, which even the developer wouldn't argue with, the majority praised it for its comedic premise and dialogue that made the game fun to play and the characters engaging. Plus, as far as the Toolkit goes, the departure from your usual fantasy based setting is a refreshing and original change. The writing compliments the gameplay and keeps things moving and interesting, so despite the lack of a strong storytelling backbone the overall composition and exposition does just what you want it to.
