# And the winner is...

Browse
Outstanding Say Contribution
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1260.png)
After our Hall of Fame weekend break, as it were, we're back with three consecutive awards starting today with Outstanding Say Contribution. When I re-coded the website the Have Your Say feature was the one big idea I really wanted to implement, since reviews were slow to come back in the day and we all know feedback is a major motivational tool. In the two years since then we've had over 600 reviews, nearly one every day, which is a pretty massive total. Of course, what became apparent was that some were better at reviews than others. What makes a good review? It has nothing to do with the deemed fairness of the scoring but the reasoning and constructive feedback that accompanies it. So who this year wrote not only a healthy number of reviews, but also wrote quality feedback?
**And the winner is...**
It was actually a tough one to decide, but eventually we settled on a name who wrote six reviews each of great quality in their feedback and reasoning, but also an informative read for fellow gamers. This year he reviewed (72%), (50%), (92%), (67%), (50%) and (83%). [Galaxy Hero](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=406) [RPGADVENTUUUURE](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=446) [Three Guys No Beer](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=493) [Anytown](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=569) [Sprite Wars](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=588) [The Dragoon](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?say=599)

Congrats man, keep up your fine reviewing next year... no doubt people will return to favour when you next release something.
