# And the winner is...

Browse
Best Game Programming
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1261.png)
I'm absolutely shattered after a long day of work, so forgive me for a quick write-up. We've had Graphics, Music and Writing, so now it's unsurprisingly programming that gets shoved under the award spotlight today! The award is given to the release that most pushed the RPG Toolkit to provide a gaming experience with many new and exciting features. So without wasting any time...
**And the winner is...**
[![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1262.png)](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?game=3317)
**User:**
**Released:**
May 28th
**Score:**
80% (13 says)
**Ranking:**
Full Games - 3rd All Games - 4th
What we have here is an arcade style space-ship shooter. You might ask, so what? Well... it's made with Code! Grindalf continued his excellent Galaxy series with the popular Defender edition that catapulted to 4th in the overall gaming charts. Another fine example of his programming abilities and there is plenty in the mechanics that can lend itself to features for more traditional RPG games. *RPG*
