# And the winner is...

Browse
Newcomer of the Year
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1280.png)
It's no longer December, but I'm just about sobered up after Christmas and New Year so it's time to finish this beast of an awards ceremony. The final awards are the most prestigious and difficult to choose. In the coming days we will announce the user and game of the year, but today we have the award for newcomer of the year. Essentially, it is given to a new user who became noticed within the community during 2008 for their efforts and contributions. It's acceptable if they actually registered in the second half of 2007 as long as it was mostly during 2008 they came to prominence. This was a very difficult one to decide, there hasn't been any standout newcomers this year like there was in 2007 when the likes of jackyboy arrived. The award was given to someone that, love him or hate him, actually caused a mini-sensation in the world of game reviews and kick-started a wave of reviews from a large number of users. In all he reviewed 51 games in less than two months, prompting a combined 50+ reviews from other users.
**And the winner is...**
So, the Toolkit Zone Newcomer of the Year is...
**Field Marshal Shockwave**
Congratulations! I'm not sure if you're someone who'll be sticking around the community, but you certainly made an impression and created a bit of debate.
