# And the winner is...

Browse
User of the Year
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1287.png)
Our penultimate award is a big one as we recognise the user who has accomplished and contributed the most in community during the calendar year. Alongside the final award, it's the most prestigious of the awards ceremony. It takes into account every aspect of life in the community, from game development to providing feedback, from contributing to forum discussions and submitting resources. So who will be our first ever User of the Year?
**And the winner is...**
Although Jackboy makes a strong case with his award winning contributions on the forums and Toolkit contributions, we couldn't ignore another user who has raised the bar when it comes to pushing the flexibility of the software to the max and as a result producing games that are not just complete but fun to play. This community is all about making games and right now no one is doing it better and more innovatively than this guy. Not only that, but he takes the time to write the occasional review and comment feedback for other developers too.
So, the award for User of the Year goes to...
**Grindalf**
Well done to you, I can't wait to see what else you develop during 2009... I'm already looking forward to the Toolkit version of the classic Worms game!
