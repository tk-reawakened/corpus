# And the winner is...

Browse
Newcomer of the Year
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1434.png)
The final awards are the most prestigious and difficult to choose. In the coming days we will announce the user and game of the year, but today it's rewarding a new user to the website that became noticed within the community during 2009 for their efforts and contributions. As with last year it's acceptable if they actually registered towards the end of the previous year as long as it was mostly during 2009 they came to prominence. Last year it was a controversial choice as prolific review writer, some say spammer, Field Marshall Shockwave walked away with the prize. Like or dislike what he did, it made an impact and causes many people to write their own reviews in response. So who wins this year?
This was a very difficult one to decide but unlike last time we had a lot of strong newbies that have coming into the community, posted a lot, got involved in discussions, worked on their game and contributed for the benefit of the community in one way or another. Indeed, 2009 was one of our healthiest years yet for generating new active members.
A strong contender at the start of the year was who came ready-equipped with maturity and intelligence, but he tried too much and disappeared under needless self-imposed pressure such as his magazine project. Other newbies such as , and have joined the community, got involved in topics and contributed reviews and/or resources. [Deathworks](http://toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=25459) [RPGWarriorX](http://toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=27392) [aurum34](http://toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=25910) [Rjedi](http://toolkitzone.com/community.php?user=25939)
**And the winner is...**
The award was given to someone that got involved with various aspects of the community and despite his young age is making a name for himself. A global member of the community, hailing from Lebanon, he has submitted quite a lot resources that helped him pickup the Outstanding Resource Contribution award this year, written five game reviews and has his hand in several game projects including working for the promising Ander's Journey animating sprites.
So, the Toolkit Zone Newcomer of the Year is...
