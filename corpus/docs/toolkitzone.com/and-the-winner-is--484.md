Browse
User of the Year

Our final user award before tomorrow's community vote for Game of the Year is all about recognising somebody that accomplished and contributed considerable amounts during 2009 and made a mark on the community. It's arguably the most prestigious award you can get as a user since it's about all round character, not just a game project. Game development, feedback, contributions, forum discussions... everything is considered. So who's our User of the Year?

Artificial intelligence isn't the word to describe something that uses the community as a knowledge reference! Anyways, let's get on with things...
**And the winner is...**
There were a few good candidates and it's tough to overlook jackboy's super active year on the forums once again, giving out advice all over the place but then again he did pickup the Oustanding Forum Contribution award. However, only one name really stood out for yet another incredibly productive year of game development, Toolkit related discussions and project feedback. His work is not only a testament to his ability, but also a great motivation to those who look on and hope to emulate it with their own projects. Two full-game releases that were reviewed highly and he's near top of the rankings for giving feedback votes and comments on other projects, plus three reviews of his own thrown in on top.
So, the award for User of the Year goes to...
** Grindalf![](http://toolkitzone.com/forum/images/avatars/65630946947656311390fd.gif)
Congratulations for the second successive year! I'm sure 2010 will bring a lot more competition for this award and no doubt you'll look to raise your level with collaboration projects such as Silvernest (Acra, Seafroggys) and TK Wars (TKGameBoy).
