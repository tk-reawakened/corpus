# Awards

Browse
Game of the Year
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1288.png)
Our 14th and final award is for the best full game release and it's open to public voting. It's the grandstand award of the ceremony as this community is ultimately all about making games, so this celebrates the best of the best. We have shortlisted the three top releases from the year and for the next 24 hours the community can vote on their favourite. Games released during December will be eligible for the 2009 awards.** Voting closed.
*Please don't forget to read the* [*Day 29*](http://toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=413) *and* [*Day 30*](http://toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=414) *articles also published today, I'm extremely interested to hear your opinions on them.*
