# Book 1: Freddy Gets His Name

Last time, on Freddy Ball Z...
Freddy and his TK-Mon Seven Eternal head out to face R#, the Robotic TK-Mon in the local gym at Mystic City. Freddy can't wait to get his hands on his first badge, and gets a few pointers from William, a shadowy figure who was apparently sent by Mr. Picso. The battle begins, today!
**Chapter 6: Fry 'em circuits!**
"Enter, feeble beings!" A computerized voice from the distant dark side of the gym called forth Freddy and Seven Eternal.
"What makes you so confident that you can beat me, the great R#!" The robot trainer steps forth, showing himself dressed in some sort of weird robot halloween costumed picked up from the local K-Mart.
"I am Freddy, and I challenge you to a d-d-d-d-d-d-d-duel!", Freddy was ready, and had obviously watched too much TK-Gi-Oh.
"Then let the battle begin! But just remember, only I know what lies in your future... future... future..."
"Um, what?" Freddy was a little confused at the last sentence.
"Do you want to fight or not?! You're allowed two TK-Mon, send out your first and hurry up, I have a republic meeting to attend".
Freddy turned to Seven Eternal, "Okay Seven, let's show him how it's done!"
Seven Eternal nodded its head and made a weird giberrish sound, and then stepped forward.
"Ha! Pathetic!" R# was being ignorant and pompous, just like his usual self, "I choose you, Android zzz!"
"Hey! You can't play humans!" Freddy was confused as to why there was a human in play on the battle field.
"You idiot, that's an android! He's a regular TK-Mon. What's the matter, afraid your going to lose?"
"Afraid?! Ha! Far frome it! Seven, Fry that android's circuits!" Freddy yelled the order and Seven Eternal quickly complied. The result was a fried piece of circuit, with the smell of burnt electricity in the air.
"Alright! We got him!" Freddy was ecstatic, he just beat his first TK-Mon in a gym!
"Don't be so happy, foolish Toolkitzone Citizen! My first TK-mon is always the weakest! Now I shall choose my ultimate, super-duper, crazy ultimate android. Go, Android Word2!" And with that order, another android appeared, but didn't seem to look much different or tougher.
"Ha! That looks just like the other android! You're really gonna lose now. Seven, fry this one too!" Seven was about to launch a massive thundershock attack, but was interrupted.
"Word2, quick, use your ultimate-super-duper-spam attack!"
All of a sudden, Word2 spammed Seven Eternal faster than perhaps any TK-Mon imaginable. Seven Eternal was losing health fast, and then lost conciousness. Even Freddy got dizzy!
"What sort of attack is that? That's just cruel!" Freddy protested against the immoral attack. Who would've thought Spam was a TK-Mon move.
"Sometimes you have to play dirty in these games, now hurry up and play your next TK-Mon!"
Freddy was annoyed that his Seven Eternal was taken out by such a cheap and annoying attack. But then he proceeded to send out his next and only TK-Mon, "Go Spydie!" Spydie looked extremely small compared to Android Word2, and that gave Freddy an idea.
"Spydie! Quick! Use your gooey attack on the android's eyes!" A thick white gooey substance was squirted from the TK-Mon's ass onto Android Word2's ass, blinding him momentarily.
"Alright Spydie, now crall in through his ears and mess up his circuits!" Freddy's plan was ingenius, and Spydie couldn't wait to try it. It launced itself using its strong legs into the ear of the Android, and then began using its fang attack on its inner circuits. Android Word2 Was going down, similar to that of a horrible migrane a human might encounter.
"What! No, this cannot be!" R# bellowed out a cry of disbelief, and the android fell onto the floor. "You've won! That's impossible! But alas, I must bestow on you the Racoon Badge."
"Racoon badge?"
"Budget cuts. The TK-Mon League is trying to save money. Anyway, here you go. And remember, I'll be seeing you in the future... future... future..."
"What?"
"This is where you dissapear, now get the hell outta hear!"
And so, another adventure had come to an end. And Freddy had one more badge than before! His first badge ever, the racoon badge! What a joyous occasion! Stay tuned next time, for the next Freddy Ball Z!
