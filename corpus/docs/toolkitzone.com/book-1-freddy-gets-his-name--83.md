# Book 1: Freddy Gets His Name

Last time, on Freddy Ball Z
After deciding to cut through General Chit-Chat Forest, Freddy came across a TK-mon that he decided to catch, without the help of 7E. After several attempts, Freddy finally caught the bug-type TK-mon and was then attacked by several of its relatives. While under attack, Freddy insulted Seven Eternal, and was also attacked by a large jolt of electricity. This jolt scared off Spydies relatives, but left Freddy unconscious on the floor.
**Chapter 3: Freddy's First Fight!**
Freddy awoke slowly, moaning as he got up from his face-planted self in the middle of the forest. Aw what happened? Seven Eternal just shook its head, holding back a huge grin. Hey wait a minute, I remember! Freddy reached back to his lower back and plucked off a TK-Ball, I caught a Spydie! Yes! That means I can finally do something TK-mon related! No thanks to you, you little rat Seven Eternal just gave him a big fake smile, and continued on. Hey, where are you going? You cant just leave me here! Freddy ran after Seven Eternal, and followed the way it was walking.
After walking for about 3 hours, Freddy finally realized hed walked by the same tree three times, Hey, do you ever know where were going? Seven Eternal just shrugged, and Freddy collapsed. Oh my god, you mean weve just been walking in circles this whole time! Seven Eternal just shrugged again. Damnit Seven, just follow me this time! And so just as Freddy began to take a step, he heard a loud car motor nearby. He quickly ran to take a look, and there was his rival, Dont_think_so.
Hey! When did you get a car! yelled Freddy at his fellow rival who he strongly disliked. When you became a loser! DTS replied with a pompous tone and Freddy wasnt going to stand and take it. Oh yeah! I bet I can beat you in a TK-Battle! Freddy was eager to get his first match underway.
Dream on kiddo, bring it!
Fine! Okay Seven Eternal, lets show him what were made of. Seven Eternal just looked at Freddy with a puzzled look, and then began pointing and laughing its ass off. Whats so funny, you are going to help me arent you? Seven Eternal just continued laughing. Damnit, you suck! Fine, I dont need you. If I can catch a TK-mon by myself, then I can win a TK-battle by myself too!
Go Spydie! Freddy through the TK-Ball that contained Spydie, and when it hit the floor a very tired and warn out Spydie showed up. Dont_think_so began laughing, Hey Freddy! Dont you know that TK-mon get tired, numb-nuts? You cant just use them if they havent been healed at a TK Centre!
Freddy felt like such a complete idiot, but then he pulled himself back together, Of course I know that, Im just giving you a head start! Freddy smiled at the great comeback he thought of on the spot. Unfortunately, Dont_think_so didnt find it so funny, Oh, you think so eh? Well dont expect any head starts from me! Go Asimir!
Freddy knew instantly that this TK-mon must have been the one that DTS got from Professor Randy. He quickly pulled out his DEXTER which told him, Asimir, a fiery salamander-like TK-mon. Tends to be lazy at times, and doesnt accomplish much. But when he does, his attacks are extremely powerful
Alright Spydie, use your Fang Attack! Spydie heard the order, and attempted to use what little energy he had to attack Asimir.
Asimir, counter quickly with a fireball attack before he gets you! Asimir agreed almost instantly, and opened its mouth, hurling a ball of fire straight at Spydie.
Watch out Spydie! Freddy tried to warn Spydie of the attack, but it was too late. The fireball attack took out Spydie in one hit, and Spydie just fainted onto the ground. No! Get up Spydie! Come-on!
Its over wimp, I win DTS smiled again, and flicked his blond hair back. Im outta hear fool! Asimir, return! DTS returned Asimir to the TK-Ball and road off in his TKW.
Aww man, nothings ever gonna go right for me!
And so, after getting defeated in his first TK-Battle with his most disliked rival, Freddy continued walking, looking down as he did, kicking the dirt. Ill show them, Ill show the world one day! I just need to train!
Despite a horrible beginners luck, Freddy stayed determined to become the best TK-Master in TK Land. But will he really achieve such a great feat? The odds are very much against him, but one things for certain, Seven Eternal is a major pain in the ass!
