# Book 1: Freddy Gets His Name

Last time, on Freddy Ball Z...
Freddy awoke from a deep sleep only to remember he had caught a pokemon, and after circling the forest many times in a lost manner, Freddy came across his despised rival Dont_think_so. And with that a battle began between Freddy's spydie and Dont_think_so's Asimir, but the battle was short-lived as Freddy's Spydie was taken out in one attack. Now, Freddy continues on his journey as he's about to embark on an exciting quest....
**Chapter 4: Mystic City**
"Damnit, I'l never get anywhere at this pace. I'm going to have to think of away to get Seven Eternal to fight for me" Freddy was thinking to himself, trying to determine away for him to become one of the best TK-Mon trainers in TK Land. "Hey, what's that ahead?!" Freddy could see the top of a building above the trees of the forest, it appeared he had finally reached civilized land. "That must be Mystic City! Yes, I made it!"
"AHEM!" Seven Eternal 'cleared its throat' to get Freddy's attention. "Oh right, we made it" Freddy acknowledged Seven Eternal, despite it not making any contributions whatsoever. Freddy felt if he was nice enough to Seven Eternal, it might actually listen to him in the near future. "Let's go!" Freddy ran towards Mystic City and Seven Eternal quickly followed.
"Wow, it's huge" Freddy was standing in the middle of the city, looking up at all the apparetment complexes and commercial buildings that surrounded him. Then he saw a large TK-Ball, "Hey, that must be a TK-Centre! That means I can finally heal my Spydie!"
Freddy entered the TK-Centre and found Nurse Ersuis arguing with a boy and girl of similar age, and what appeared to be a talking cat. "I keep telling you, I cannot permit you access to the healing room, there are hurt TK-mon in there" Nurse Ersuis ruffled her breasts and tired to remain a firm face, "Now if you'll excuse me, I have other people to attend to".
"Oh no! I don't think you understand Nurse! You see..." The boy began speaking
"... we're Team Spaceship! And we demand you stand by while we snatch those hurt pokemon..." The girl continued the boys sentence without hesitancy
"... Or else we'll use force!" The cat actually spoke.
Freddy finally decided it was time for him to intervene. "Hey, you guys can't talk to the nurse like that!"
"And who are you twirp?"
"I could ask the same of you!"
"Hehe, I thought you'd never ask. My names Eternal, Eternal Blue!"
"And mines Zap!"
"And I'm Meowy!" The cat spoke again, and Freddy realized that meowy had the most annoying voice ever - sort of like spammers he met online at home.
"Well what difference does it make what your names are, you have no right talking to Nurse Ersuis like that!"
"What're you gonna do about it, twirp?" Eternal Blue seemed to be the semi-leader of the group, aforementioned as Team Spaceship.
"I..." Freddy just remembered that his spydie had fainted, and he had no TK-mon unless you want to count Seven Eternal. He looked down at Seven, and Seven stepped infornt of him, so as to step forward and say, "Then you'll have to deal with me". It spoke in jibberish, but only meowy was able to understand.
"Ha! You think you can take all three of us?! Comeon guys, let's show this little rodent how it's done." And with that, Zap and Eternal blue released their to TK-Mon; Moo of Doom and Zyrshnikashnu.
"What the? I know what the cow is, but what the hell is that?!"
"How dare you!" Said Zap, "That's an elegant and rare TK-mon! And it's gonna take out your rodent, zyr, use your Suck Attack!"
Seven Eternal was suddenly confronted by a semi-retarted TK-Mon that was about to suck on it, so Seven Eternal quickly used its thundershock attack, and fried zyrshnikashnu. "Yeah! Alright Seven!" Freddy didn't want to yell out any orders just yet, because he wasn't sure if Seven Eternal had warmed up to him yet.
"You can't just do that to my friend!" Eternal Blue then instructed Moo of Doom to do a pound attack on Seven Eternal. But at the same time Meowy charged at Seven Eternal for a double team. Freddy noticed that Seven was introuble, and jumped in to take the pound attack from Moo of Doom.
"Ahhh! Get off me, you fat cow!"
"Mooo!"
Seven Eternal couldn't believe what Freddy just did, and then quickly dodged Meowy's attack. It then released a split thunder attack which attacked both TK-mon, taking them out almost instantly. Team Spaceship quickly retrieved their TK-Mon and ran out yelling, "This isn't the end twirp!"
Freddy was still on the floor, the wind was knocked out of him after having that fat cow sit on him. But he was happy. Happy because it seemed things had finally turned around for him as Seven Eternal now seemed to be on his side.
