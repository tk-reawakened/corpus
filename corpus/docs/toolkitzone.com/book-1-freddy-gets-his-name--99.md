# Book 1: Freddy Gets His Name

Last time, on Freddy Ball Z...
Freddy and Seven finally made it their unknown destination, Mystic City! Upon arrival, Freddy head straight to the TK-Centre to heal his little spydie, but as is the case with freddy's luck, Team Spaceship was already there trying to steal poor and defenseless TK-Mon. Not to fear though, as Freddy and Seven took care of Team Spaceship in a less than diplomatic manner.
**Chapter 5: Freddy at the Gym!**
"Are you guys alright?" Nurse Ersuis ran over to Freddy who was lieing on the floor hardly able to breath. She ben't over to help Freddy up, but all Freddy could notice were Ersuis's nice plumb breasts that hung out under the baggy white lab coat the nurse was wearing. Ersuis was also sporting a mini-skirt, and one could imagine perhaps a thong underneath.
"Thanks Nurse Ersuis!" Freddy said with a grin.
"Oh please..." said the nurse, "Please call me Curt."
Freddy was a little puzzled, but decided to let it slide and continued to call Curt by Nurse Ersuis.
"Is there anything I can help you too with?"
"Huh?" Freddy was still staring at Curt's vulumptuous breasts, unknowing of his surroundings.
"You guys did come into the TK Center for a reason, right? Do you have an injured TK-Mon?"
"Oh right right! Yeah, I have my hurt spydie, do you think you could help it out?"
"Of course!"
Nurse Ersuis retired to the back of the TK-Centre to put Spydie to rest, and when Freddy looked down at Seven Eternal, Seven was looking back at him with an angry look. "What? It's not my fault Cur... I mean, Nurse Ersuis is hot". Seven Eternal folded its arms and turned its back on Freddy in disagreement. "Alright alright, I'll stop. Jeez, so testy".
The nurse returned, "Alright, your spydie has been put to rest. It'll be a few hours before his health is fully restored."
Freddy looked directly at the floor, "Thanks Nurse Ersuis, you're the best." Freddy kept looking at the floor so that he did not look directly at the nurse's breasts. If he wanted Seven Eternal to battle with him, he'd have to make sacrifices.
"So what brings you two to the city? Are you here to battle Gym Leader R#?"
"There's a gym leader here?!" Freddy completely forgot that almost every city has a gym with a gym leader where he can earn badges. "Where's the gym?! Where's the gym?!" Freddy was ecstatic and couldn't wait to get his first badge.
"Oh, it's just down main street, not too far from here actually."
"Thanks Nurse! Comeon, let's go Seven!"
This time, Seven obeyed and followed Freddy to the gym.
After about 20 minutes of walking down Mainstreet, Freddy finally found the gym. "Alright, there it is!" Freddy ran up to the front and opened up the doors. "Wow, my first gym".
"What was that?" A voice from behind a statue confronted Freddy.
"Huh? Who's there?"
A shadow emerged from behind the statue, and then stepped into the light. The man standing there definately wasn't the gym leader, so who was he?
"Hi, my name's William. I'm here to aid travellers in their quest to become the ultimate master in TK-Mon. I believe you said this is your first time, correct?"
"Er... yes. But do you mind if I ask a question? Why do you lurk there behind the statue? It's kind of freaky, sort of like Mr. Picso-pedaphile freaky, you know?"
"Hush now, child. This gym's leader is R#, and he uses TK-mon of the Robot type. Electricity type pokemon will easily be able to take him out and fry his circuits!"
"Thanks William, I'll remember that!"
William then murmured to himself, "Everything's going according to plan Mr. Picso... mwahahahahaha"
"What was that?"
"Ahem... erm... nothing, I was just... um... whistling, yeah that's it"
"Whatever, comeon Seven let's go face R#!"
