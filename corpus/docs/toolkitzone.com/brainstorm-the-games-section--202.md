# Brainstorm - The Games Section

By Xavier
The games section was a late addition to the Toolkit Zone when the site first appeared but fast proved the most viewed and active area outside of the forums. Such popularity lead to a re-code of the entire games system at the start of last year, and we think it's time to think about improving it some more. Forget the general layout and look of TKZ, just think about what you'd like in the games section from both the perspective of using it for your game and browsing it for other games. Ask yourself questions such as; What do I dislike about browsing other profiles? What is hard about maintaining my own profile? How could the content and games be better displayed to the community and linked with other aspects of the site? What do I like about the profile system? Feel free to take a lead from other replies by users and discuss their comments if they take your interest, I want this to be a community discussion. However seemingly unrealistic or trivial, however broad or nitpicky, it'll be great to find out exactly what you guys like and dislike about the games section and how it could be better tailored to your needs. The more detailed you are, the better. Although comments here are welcome, we'd prefer responses to go in the forum topic.... Thanks
