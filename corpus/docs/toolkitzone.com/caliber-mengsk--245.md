# Caliber Mengsk

TKZ Pixel Art Competition - June Edition
Pixel art has undoubtedly become a part of just about every game maker, and the people of Toolkitzone is no exception to this. For this reason, the goal of perfecting the has been a primary mindset of all aspiring pixel artist. Any method that could become a tool for improvement will have to suffice; and for that reason, along with having fun, the monthly Pixel Art Competition has been conceptualized. *Way of the Pixel*
The original pixel art competition was hosted by joenorton, a very skilled (and young) pixel artist. The first competition, although proved itself to be fun, was quite disorganized and members called for another one. I siply reinvented what joenorton has started.
This document shall share with you the synopsis of the said event. For a more detailed reading, head to the -- assuming it's not yet deleted. [original thread](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/viewtopic.php?t=20216)
The contestants were given a template, more so like this: All they have do is create their sprite out of the template. They may give it skin color, dress it up and whatever, but they're not allowed to change the pose, canvas size, and the overall appeal. I gave a sample output, which looks like this: Fortunately, a lot of members took notice in the competition, so I received a lot of entries. Some entries are of beginner's quality, but that is not really in question. The contest is open to everyone who would be willing to share what they know about pixel art and at the same time grab the chance to study other's works so they could better their own.![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/spritecontest_template1.png)
![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/spritecontest_sample.gif)
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/157/stage.png)
The winners were determined by vote. All TKZ members were invited to vote on the said contest. Each voter was given the right to vote for three entries, each grabing their respective 1st, 2nd, and 3rd places. There were some disagreement with the system later on and the host promised to improve the system for future contests.
