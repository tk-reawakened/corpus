# Chapter 3

For of those of you just joining the story... In the previous 2 chapters, Frap and Tano find each other and head on over to the House of the Jewel where they find it STOLEN! Now comes the fun part!
Chapter 3
Frap glanced at the sign as he left the city and sighed. He was going to miss that sign he felt. Welcome to Shal, it said. Frap remembered when he first had moved to Shal, he read that same sign. There was always a Ms. Walker out greeting people as they came into the town. Welcome to Shal. She even did that when you had lived in the village for ages. Frap thought to himself, Why did she always tell me welcome?
Frap come on, are you going to come to the castle or not? yelled out Tano. Frap turned and walked away from the village and looked forward to the grand adventure they must be fixing to have.
Sometime later, Frap turned to Tano. You know, when people go off on these adventures, do you think they think of everything?
Tano walked on steadily, raised an eyebrow, and then shrugged.
I was just thinking. We must not be the brightest turnip in the crop.
Tano was fed up with being left in the dark. What are you getting at? Are you saying we forgot something?
Fraps face brightened up as if a bright candle had been lit next to him in the dark. Yes! Thats exactly what Im saying. Look at us. We are halfway there and I finally think that we come ill prepared. We have no food, no extra clothes, and no money besides the spare coins I had left in my pocket. Best of all we left my front door unlocked.
Tano just smirked. He read a sign as they passed it. Hey Frap you ever went to the cave before?
No. I Heard there was some a water demon there.
Hah! You are so full of superstition its not even worth to laugh at. Why do you believe all this stuff for? I mean look. We are probably going to get to the castle and no one will care if the Jewel is gone. Wisemen, why do people always put wisemen in legends? Its because they need something to look up to. For all you know it could be some small town attraction like Port Citys biggest bowl of potato soup.
Frap was getting really annoyed, Tano, I didnt ask you to come along. Are you going to help me or not?
Frap, Im coming because Im bored of sitting around Shal. There is nothing there encase you havent noticed. We have the tavern and the club. Thats it.
Well you could always go explore that town well.
What do you mean by that?
Frap and Tano glared at each other for a bit as they kept walking. The silence seemed to spread all around them. It was the kind that when you walk by a bird and it wont sing, the crickets stop their chirping, and the air doesnt move.
I know what I believe in Tano. I believe I am the one. I feel I need to protect that Jewel. I believe the legends. Once we get to the castle you do what ever you want. Im going to talk to the King, Frap finally responded after he cooled down a bit.
Fine with me, I just dont like getting laughed at.
They walked on for a couple of hours, occasionally stopping at streams to get a drink and chasing rats and bugs to pass time. Finally, the entrance to the castle gate came into view. Both were excited but for different reasons. They hurried along because the sun was low in the sky.
Chapter 4
Frap and Tano walk in the classic gate. It was made from the gray stone, and rusting metal just like you would imagine in a fairytale. Hylanka City itself was busy and bustling. Traders come in and out. Woman roam the streets as the men stand staring in excitement. The buildings were tall. The inn, known as The BirdsNest, was the first thing anyone seen as they walked in. This was a great place for it too. The walk to the castle was always a long one. The BirdsNest was also three stories tall. It had to be big to house so many people.
Across the straight brick road was a pub. The Watering Hole was it's name. The owner of the inn and the owner of the pub had a great referring business between one another. If someone got drunk, the bar tender would send him over to the innkeeper. If someone asked for something to do at The BirdsNest, the innkeeper would send them over to The Watering Hole.
Just down that straight brick road was the entrance to the castle, grand it was too. A big rock arch lingered over your head as you walked for the door and it was the incentive to look up right when the castle came into view. gallant it was. The castle seemed to stretch high. Some say it loomed over mountains. Others spoke of a legend where the castle threw away the sea with it's bold look and it's courageous stance. The castle had two towers. One was where the king's chief adviser stayed and the other was where royal guests roomed. It was fantasized that you could see the sea from that tower, that it was the eye still keeping it at bay.
The two excited boys looked around because it had been some time since either had been in the city. Frap's main concern though was still the Jewel. A heavyset guard was standing next to an inn keeping one eye on the gate and the other on girls walking into the inn. Frap looked at this man curiously because he was standing still but doing the march routine. Frap thought to himself, "That's one funny way to exercise." Frap walked up to man just about the time he started yelling out, "One! Two! Three! Four! One..."
"uh, excuse me?" inquired Frap trying to get the guards attention from the woman coming out of the inn to go to the pub.
"What is it!?! I'm busy!" yelled back the guard rather annoyed by Fraps attempts to get his attention.
"Well, it's about the Jewel..."
"Don't bother me about the Jewel boy. I've got more important business. Now run along." snapped the guard because Frap had ruined everything. Frap's mouth twisted in disgust because he had never met someone quite so rude.
"How do people get jobs with importance as high as the guard of the gate when you act like that?"
Tano had not been paying attention to Fraps ramblings, but rather had been watching a big breasted blond that has walked by and ran her hand across his thigh to tease him. Tano was about ready to ditch Frap when he turned around.
"Tano we got to go see the king."
"You were just downed by a guard. What makes you think that the King will listen to you?"
"I don't know but I got to try because I know the Shal is in trouble... if not the world."
Tano shook his head at the nonsense, but decided to follow just encase Frap happened to get thrown in the dungeon for insanity or something along the lines. The boys followed the brick road. It was amazing that through all the chariots, horses, and people walking on that road, not a single brick was cracked. The Royal Road was what the signs called it. It was the red carpet that the king would follow straight out of the city in the event he had to leave or enter. Parades were held on the same road. The Watering Hole and The BirdsNest both profited from this. Frap looked up at the arch as they passed under. The sudden ending of the arch and the appearance of the castle seemed to beat down the skinny 17 year old. Tano began to lead the way because he wanted to hurry up, get in, get thrown in jail, and get out as quickly as possible. There was still a gorgeous blond waiting for him at The Watering Hole. Tano just drooled over the thought.
Once inside the castle, there was a sanctuary hall. The castle was decorated in blues, whites, and grays. The tile had elaborate zigs and zags that made you walk crooked if you watched them as you walked. The walls were whitewashed and had elaborate wall hangings of the kings flag. An elaborate silver chandelier hung from the ceiling like a galaxy of stars. All around was people, too many to ask really. Tano shrunk back and let Frap walk ahead. There were farmers and peasants in the same room. This wasn't odd for this particular castle, but for most, a riot, food fight, or something worse would had happened. There was word that the king was to hold a council for the payment of taxes by the farmers in the form of produce instead of money this year because the city was suffering from the trade route through the caves being closed. Apparently twenty people had died in the past month and no one else wanted to brave the mission.
Near an open hall door were two of the kings advisers. Frap thought that would be a good way to start asking for the Kings audience. Frap approached the men, slightly bowed and asked politely, "Excuse me Lords, We need to talk to the King."
"Well, well, young man. You are going to have to talk to Argat about that," responds the adviser chuckling at the preposterous request.
"Could you tell me where to find this Argat?" The second adviser answered, "Well he's not here is he?"
Frap, fed up with his share of rude people turns and walks through the door. Tano tapped Frap on the shoulder. "Are you joking Frap? This is the way to the throne room."
Frap looks around, hoping to see a sign but decides to take Tano's word for it. "Yes that's exactly where I want to go. I must talk to the King." Tano backs off and walks slower than Frap. He thinks to himself, "I knew it, we are going to die. I want to say bye to you brain before you depart from my body."
Frap notices two guards up ahead. One of them is leaning on a statue of a knight, drinking his big cup of evening coffee to stay awake. Frap this time wastes no time being polite. He yells out desperately trying to get someone to listen to him, "I really must talk to someone about the Jewel of Destiny!" The guards straighten up and act more professional. "Look, kid, I really can't help you there. You're going to have to talk to the king and that means getting Argat's permission."
Frap relieved that no one is upset at his sudden outburst then in a more calm voice asks, "Who is Argat?"
The second guard took a sip of his coffee and nearly choked at the question Frap so diligently asked. "Where have you been kid? Argat is the king's Steward and is the descended of the second of the Wisemen."
Frap raised an eyebrow in curiosity because this was the most interesting thing he had heard all night. Tano came up behind him when he heard about the "Wisemen." "Well that gives us something in common," Frap said to himself but loud enough for the guards to hear.
The one drinking his coffee, spit it out when he laughed and the other pounded on the second's metal armor trying to pat his back to help him from choking.
"You're descendant of the wisemen too, are you? You crack me up kid. That may work on the ladies but you ain't fooling me," laughed the first guard still pounding on the other's armor.
Annoyed Tano pulls Frap away knowing he couldn't fight an armored guard to save Frap's behind.
"Did you see those two. They mocked me!" Frap cried out angrily. Tano smiled, "You know that would happen Frap. Any time you tell anyone you are decedents of wisemen, they laugh at you. Why don't you give it a rest already? Seriously, you came from a common family, a common house in a common town. You cannot be a wiseman. Look at this Argat. He's got a fancy title, a castle for a home, and has probably never touched a manual labor tool in his life."
Frap walked away, more annoyed than before. Down the hall, walked out a very nicely dressed old man. He was a short figure. He had a head full of hair that was snow white, a pointy goatee style beard, and a full mustache both the same color as his hair. His clothes were gold and red. He walked swiftly. He must have been in his fifties.
Frap ran up to him and stops him, "Excuse me sir. We must speak to a man named Argat. We must see his majesty promptly concerning the Jewel of Destiny!"
The man looks at Frap surprised and respectfully says, "I am Argat, whom you seek."
