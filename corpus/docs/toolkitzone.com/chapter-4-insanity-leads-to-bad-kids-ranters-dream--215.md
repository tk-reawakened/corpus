# Chapter 4 : Insanity leads to Bad Kids Ranters Dreams

Our hero Mr. Whatamunchkins has evaded a close call against an evil monster. Even though he did not attack the monster even once, he atleast tried. One does not achieve greatness by just letting others do the work and Mr. Whatamunchkins recieved the message. Now he and his new found friend continue to find a way out of that dark, dreary forest and continue to each of their paths.

"I'm tired." Mr. Whatmunchkins said. "We've been walking for a million hours. Can't we rest?" "We can't rest now." Said Roc. "I want to get the heck out of here now. This place gives me the creeps." "I want to get out of here too but my feet hurt. I want to rest". "Dang kid, I..." "I WANNA REST!!!" Mr. Whatamunchkins pouted. He then sat down disgruntled. "What a annoying kid. Suddenly a sound of rustling was heard. Roc and Mr. Whatamunchkins turned around. Footsteps were then heard treading away. "What the?" exclaimed Roc. "What do you think it was?" "An EXIT!? Well then come on!" "I thought you were tired." "Who cares about that, I don't want to stay here anymore. Come on Mr. Roc." They both darted towards the white dot.
