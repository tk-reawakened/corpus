# Chapter 5

So far we have started an adventure that seems to grow with excitement and word count as we are now to chapter 5 and 6. Frap has Left his home town of Shal to meet with the king about the missing Jewel of Destiny. In the problems of getting around the kings court he meets Argat. What will happen next?
Chapter 5
Frap, surprised that he actually found the man he was looking for before Tano persuaded him to go home, was without words. Argat stared at Frap for a second and then went on, "I am a descendant of the second wiseman. I am sworn to protect the Jewel and the land of Hylanka from any evil."
Frap was in awe. The man seemed to have a new glow to him. Argat's golden colored clothes seemed to glisten in the torches' light as if it was in the sun.
Argat read the young boy. "Hmmm," he mumbled.
Tano looking from behind starting to second guess Frap's stories for the first time in his life blurts out, "We need to see the King!"
Argat elegantly looks up to Tano. "The King? Ah yes. We must make haste to talk to His Majesty. I will grant you an audience. Now come with me."
Argat motioned his hand and to follow. Frap followed excited because for the first time in his life he felt as if he was wanted, believed, and respected. He thought to himself how not even Tano had ever believed his stories of the Wisemen and their oaths to protect the new land. Argat opened a grand door. Inside was the throne room. There stood six knights all dressed in shining silver armor. Red carpet lead from the door to the kings thrown which was upon a pedestal of steps. The room was more beautiful than the sanctuary where the masses had joined, though it did have matching chandeliers and and wall hangings. The walls shined golden in this room though. Not that the walls were made of gold but the globes around the lights casted a beautiful golden light that was steadier than your normal torch. There were no windows in the thrown room as the kings of old had once said that the moon light would impose on the sun's rays that radiated from inside the room
One of the King's scribes had been standing next to him but retired when Argat opened the door. The king looked expectantly upon Argat to have some interesting news since he disturbed his preparation for speaking to the masses. Argat bowed, as did Frap and then Tano respectively. Argot announced, "Your Majesty, I bring a young man from he village of Shal. He has news concerning the Jewel of Destiny."
The King looked at Frap, "Step forward young man."
Frap bowed again before he spoke. He spoke nervously, "Your Majesty, something horrible has happened. I woke up this morning with a strange feeling about the Jewel for I had had a dark dream in the night. My companion, Tano, and I walked to the House of the Jewel and inside there was nothing."
Suddenly there was a gasp. Every knight, the king, and Argat did it all at the same time. The air in the room seemed to be sucked out with the united gasp. Frap continued, "The guards were all gone and there was a strange darkness that resided about the lower level. When we went upstairs to the room in which the Jewel was kept, for the door was open, it was gone!"
At the word "gone", everyone gasped again. The king stood up and yelled it again, "Gone!?!"
The guards started to mumble about how impossible it was that the Jewel was gone. By all means there is no reason it should be gone. Why would someone go that far to pull a prank?
Tano jumped in, "Frap show them what you found in while we were there."
Frap pulled out the key shaped rock, "The Key Stone."
Argat had recently already started to pace and mumble as well, "The jewel is gone...," He then looked up at everyone and announced, "This is serious. Without the Jewel, the Giants can rise out of the underworld and destroy us all. There is one hope, however."
Argat waked over to Frap and put his hand on his should, "You."
Frap curious as to where this was going look cockeyed at Argat.
"Frap, I sense wise blood in you. you and I are kinsmen, I can feel it!" Argat extended his hand to Frap, "Let me see the Key Stone."
Frap handed the keystone to Argat in a gentle manner as if giving something to a king.
"Look how it shimmers!" Argat stared at the rock for seconds. "This key was forged many years ago by the First Wiseman, Hubra. It has a power over the Jewel that allows a person to control the Jewel."
Argat seemed to grow wearier with each breath, "And yet I am not strong enough to carry it. Even as I hold it now, I can feel it burning my hands... Please take it back." Argat kneeled in exhaustion and offered it back to Frap.
Frap accepted it back the same way he gave it away and acknowledged, "Alright," and nods.
"Frap you are descendant of Habra, the first and most powerful of the wisemen. You alone have the power to bear the keystone," explained the old man.
Tano in disbelief of what his ears make him listen to finally yells out, "Whoa! what does this mean?"
Argat walked to the door and spoke wisely as he explained to not just Tano but everyone, "Frap alone has the power to get the Jewel of Destiny back!"
Frap was overwhelmed with excitement. He quickly ran over to Argat nearly yelling squeamishly, "I knew it!" He then turned to Tano, "See I knew it all along. I am a descendant of the wisemen! But this..." Frap took a deep breathe and gave a nearly silent sigh that was apparent to the whole room, "this, I had no idea."
The King was quite confused and not sure he followed. He quickly announced, "It is a lot to digest. Perhaps you should sleep now and we will discuss this more tomorrow morning." The King motioned to Argat.
"Very good. I shall show you to a room," promptly spoke Argat obeying the king.
Frap and Tano followed Argat to a big beautiful guest room which happened to be in that second tower. Frap and Tano were now royal guests.
Chapter 6
Frap couldn't sleep. He laid there looking around. The very beds the were laying on were nearly fit for the King, himself. Everything seemed to glitter with gold. The room was grand. There was a fireplace burning with a big log to keep out the drafty cold that still persisted to blow in through the beautiful blue drapes that hung over the window. The walls had a wood trim. But this wasn't just any type of wood, it was cherry wood. There was only one place cherry wood grew on the island. It was in the Forest of Heknuff. It was located by the city so imaginatively named after it that was about a day's walk south of the castle. The King was very fond of chandeliers. He had one put in every room that had any importance. This one put off a golden light to match the interior of the room. The ceiling had a mural of the wisemen on it. One was holding a jewel in one hand and the keystone in the other. Frap thought to himself, "That is me. I am the one to hold the future of this land."
Frap took his gaze off the ceiling and looked over at Tano who too was looking at the ceiling. "Tano," Frap said looking back at the ceiling, "Did you imagine anything like this happening to you?"
Tano spoke at the ceiling as well, "No. My imagination never gets past women. I'm stuck on breasts, legs, and sex."
Frap comes back with a quick wit for once, "You may be imagining that because you will never see the day you get to ACTUALLY see and do those things."
Tano smirks and then drops into a frown, "You may be right. If you are the wisemen's descendant and the story happens like it is said... " there was a long silence.
Frap nodded agreeing.
"I mean, this means we are going to have to fight. Who is going to think about sex in a time when our lives may be at stake?" continued Tano.
Frap agreed again and sighed, "That puts a gloomy look for use doesn't it. I mean, this is all very strange."
Tano looked over at him not sure if Frap was agreeing just to agree or doing it because he understood. "Do you know what it means for the Jewel to be gone?"
"Yes, Yes I do. The Giants are afoot once again."
"Do you think the giants know that the Jewel is gone?" Tano seemed to start to panic a bit at more of a realization than he had before.
They both laid there looking up at the ceiling. Suddenly the whole room shook and bits of dust and ceiling hit them in the face. Both the boys were startled and stood up. They yelled nearly in unison, "What was that!?!"
Tano panicked and yelled out, "It sounds like the castle is under attack!"
Argat bursts through the massive door and yelled, "The giants have risen! They are attacking the castle! We must leave at once!"
Frap flapping his arms up and down like a girlish chicken squeals out, "But where are we to go?"
Argat motioned with his hand for the two freaked out boys to follow him. "There are secret ways. Follow me." The boys sit there blankly. "Now!" screamed out Argat as bits of ceiling started to hit them in the head.
The noises of the castle taking a beating grew as the group ran through a secret passage way reveled by Argat. It was behind a banner with a picture of the king on it. In this country it was illegal to touch such decorations but Argat had permission to do what he must in the case of an emergency. Frap's breath was short as they ran through the narrow dark passage ways. Tano was last in line following close to Frap. Turn after turn Argat would lead and occasionally bark out "This way!" For one of his age and royal position, this man was fit. Argat never worked out, but he did have a special magic about him that seemed to boost his physical shape. When Argat wasn't serving the king he was serving his desire to learn new helpful magics. Now he used it to help them run for their lives.
Finally, moonlight showed at the end of the tunnel. The group was tattered with spider webs, falling stones, and dust. The run seemed all worth while until they stopped at the door and looked up at what was staring back.
Frap shivered in his shoes and asked, "What is that?" even though he knew exactly what it was.
It was a giant. Not one of the leaders, but just a lowly henchman. The guards were sacraficing their lives just to be slaughtered by the hideous being. This particular one had green skin. If it had lived in a river full of algae, it would be camouflaged. The skin was cracked like a dry lake bed after 2 years of drought. It was about 5 stories tall. It's eyes were as red as blood and reflected light. The touches of the city and the chaos that the giant had created with fires shown his eyes as brightly as the moon. This wasn't the fat troll you would expect. It was kinda impish with without the wings. It had a slender face, pointed nose, thick eyebrows, just the kind of evil skinny villain you would expect, just a few times larger. The evil wore a purple tunic and purple draping shirt as well as a matching dwarf hat. Very stupid, but very intimidating if you are only five to six foot tall.
The giant had been trashing about at the guards in the archery zones and that is why the castle had been attacked. But now he had his gaze on the Key Stone holder. Frap was getting very nervous. Argat noticed a couple of knights already beyond the help of magic or medicine alike so he quickly picked up three swords.
Tano yells back to Argat, "No way! I'm not going to fight that thing."
Frap's knees are knocking now and the giant has started his pursuit. He finally picked up his sword from Argat.
Argat boldly and calmly said, "Follow my lead and you will be safe."
Argat ran straight for the demon screaming like in a charge. Frap followed close behind, but Tano was still at the entrance way. Argats sword started to spark and turn blue as he raised it to swing at the beast. Frap in a state of awe, did the same with a very normal sword.
"Stab your sword straight down into the dirt through the foot!" yelled Tano for "moral support" as he cowarded in the entrance way to the secret passages.
Argat had that already in mind and had murmured a small incantation that would make Fraps thrust a bit stronger.
The monster swooped down trying to swat the insect sized humans trying their best to harm him. Argat's goal was to get so close that the giant would have a hard time hitting them.
The purple draped monstrosity kicked at Frap with his right leg. Frap dove out of the way which just left Argat standing. Argat ran striaght for the middle of the giants stance. Knowing he couldn't do his normal plan by himself, he quickly came up with an encantation that he yelled out. The moon dissappeared as quickly as the words came out of Argat's mouth as the clouds formed, and as the last word rolled of his tounge, a giant lightning bolt fell from the sky and struck the beast through the temple, and traveled through his body to Argat's sword which he had risen.
Frap layed there on the ground and watched as this mircle happened. Argat was Zeus, himself. The giant was brain fried. His eyes rolled into his head and he started to fall.
Tano yelled, "Frap watch out!" and scooped him up right as the giant's head was about to fall on the boy.
"It's a good thing I hung back here to save you isn't it Frap?"
Frap answered, "You coward, how dare you leave us like that! We could have..."
Argat walked up beside the two staggering from his loss of energy during the bolt. The smell of cooking giant brain lingered in the air.
Frap couldn't help but to ask again, "What was that?"
Argat had now started to sip on a syrup that he carried in his shirts inside pocket. "It was one of the ancient creatures of the Giant Dominion. Now there can be no doubt." Argat pauses and looked up at the sky as it is started to turn the light grey color of approaching dawn. "It is almost morning. Lets head for the safety of the mountains ahead."
Argat motioned his hand to follow again and Frap did. Tano looked blankly. After being left behind a bit he yelled out, "Wait up."
"Come on Tano!" yelled back Frap.
Tano caught up and talked lightly to Frap as they fell behind a bit, "Why are we following him? Hasn't he heard of the people dying in the mountains."
Argat over hearing this said out loud, "I don't see that you have many options Tano. It's die here later, or if you wish die in the mountains later. The chance in the mountains is considerably less because you have me."
Tano shrugged and shook his head. "What have I gotten into."
So Argat, Tano, and the at the time courageous, powerless, Frap walked for the mountains.
