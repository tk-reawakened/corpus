Community > The Meridian Weekly
After a two week absence, R043482 is back with his Meridian 59 column. This week he details the Brax Lever Puzzle, helping you solve the problem that has baffled many a gamer.
Sorry for the lack of articles. If you have noticed, I have not written any articles for the past two weeks. That was because of the examination I had to study for, and write. Now that it is over, I will promise you the much anticipated, Brax Lever Puzzle. This article is very detailed, and extensive. It is probably the longest article I have written. Well, enjoy this weeks addition. Even to those who do not play Meridian, you may follow along, and solve the puzzle yourself found under the sub-heading Method two. he Brax Lever Puzzle is complicated, and has frustrated many players. With this article you will be able to easily solve this perplexing puzzle. Now, to reveal as to where you should head to the map called, An ancient place, its origin forgotten: Red line  How to get on the platform. Blue circle  Where the levers are located. You can get to An ancient place, its origin forgotten by using this map: . To get on the platform, you will require a lot of running, and jumping. If you have failed getting on the platform about a dozen time, here are some words of advice: Try, and Try, and Try again. After trying so many times, you will have mastered the ability to get on the platform. Why would someone attempt the Brax Lever Puzzle? Simple. It is to gain access to Brax, a dangerous ruin where undead creatures exist. There are two ways to gain access to Brax. This method requires two people, one who has the ability to cast, Lightning Wall, a level six, Faren spell, and another person to stand on the platform. The following picture will give you a visual illustration: First off, both players should get on the platform. Refer to the map above for instructions on how to get on the platform. The player without the Lightning Wall spell should remain idle on the platform throughout the entire process. Now for a simple three step-by-step process: 1. The player with the Lightning Wall spell should cast Lightning Wall on the center brown pillar as shown in the picture above. The center pillar should now turn green, and four pillars around the platform should rise. Each of these four pillars should be brown. Red Circle  The four indicated brown pillars that should rise. 2. Now, have the player with the Lightning Wall spell cast Lightning Wall on each of the four surrounding brown pillars. Upon casting lightning wall on each of the brown pillars, they should turn green. 3. After all pillars on the screen are green, the platform should rise. Once the platform has rose to the top and stopped, you will require to jump northward. If you fail to jump across, you will need to repeat the process, by using method one, or two. Now, only the person who has been idle the entire time would be able to gain access to Brax. You may be asking, How does the player with the Lightning Wall spell who has done all the work get to Brax?. Well, this is where the idle player uses method two in order to help his buddy in return. This method is the most popular among Meridian players. However, this method is also the most complicated. This is where you will require to use your puzzle skills to solve this one. Luckily, I will describe to you the finer details of the puzzle that will help you in solving this perplexing riddle. Requirements: One player who is already at the Brax Levers (Refer to Method one), and another player who is to now remain idle at the platform throughout this entire procedure. The player who is at the Brax Levers are presented with nine levers as seen below: The indicated nine levers are circled above. For our convenience, we shall refer to each lever from left to right, lever one to lever nine. What would happen if you pulled on one of the many levers? The five pillars (four on each corner, one in the center) would rise, and lower depending on which lever you have pulled. They would also change colour, from brown to green, or green to brown. Goal: Your goal is have all five pillars , and . If any of the pillars are down, or brown, then the center platform will not rise. Lever one has the following description: Now, when you pull on that lever, this is what happens: 1. The Center Pillar would turn brown to green, or green to brown. 2. Each of the four pillars would raise or lower. If they were up, they would go down. If they were down, they would go up. None of these four pillars would change colour. Lever Two to Five has the following description: Lever two to five, each, activates a corner. They raise or lower the pillars, and when any pillars are up, certain levers will lower, and change colour (from green to brown, or from brown to green). These levers are conveniently set up at the center, and each one of these levers act similarly to one another. When you pull on Lever Two, the following occurs: 1. It raises/lowers the top-left, bottom-right, and bottom-left pillars. (Pillars that were initially up, will go down. Pillars that were initially down, will go up) However, it does not effect the top-right pillar in terms of raising, or lowering. 2. If the top-right pillar is up before you pull on lever two, then the pillar would change colour. (From brown to green, or green to brown). The pillar would remain standing. When you pull on Lever Three, the following occurs: 1. It raises/lowers the top-left, top-right, and bottom-right pillars. (Pillars that were initially up, will go down. Pillars that were initially down, will go up) However, it does not effect the bottom-left pillar in terms of raising, or lowering. 2. If the bottom-left pillar is up before you pull on lever three, then the pillar would change colour. (From brown to green, or green to brown). The pillar would remain standing. Lever Three acts nearly identical to Lever Two. When you pull on Lever Four, the following occurs: 1. It raises/lowers the top-left, bottom-right, and bottom-left pillars. (Pillars that were initially up, will go down. Pillars that were initially down, will go up) 2. If the top-left pillar was you pulled on Lever Four, the top-left pillar would change colour (from brown to green, or green to brown), lower. 3. If top-right pillar is unaffected. The top-right pillar does not change colour, nor does it raise or lower when you pull on lever four. 1. Only the bottom-left pillar rises/lowers. The bottom-left pillar does not change colour. 2. If the bottom-right pillar was you pilled on Lever Five, the bottom-right pillar would change colour (from brown to green, or green to brown), but does lower. 3. The top-right, and bottom-left pillars are unaffected. They do not change colour, nor do they raise or lower when you pull on lever five. Lever Six to Nine has the following description: Lever Six to Nine are the third, and final set of levers we will be looking at. These levers only raises, or lower a particular corner. I will now describe as to what each of the remaining lever does. - It raises/lowers the top-right, bottom-left pillar. - It raises/lowers the top-left, bottom-right pillar. - It raises/lowers the top-right, and bottom-right pillar. - It raises/lowers the top-left, and bottom-left pillar. Note: Top-left refers to North-west. Top-right refers to North-east. Bottom-left refers to South-west. Bottom-right refers to South-east. Refer to in-game map. With the following information at your disposal, you should now be able to understand the function of each lever, and be able to solve the Brax Lever Puzzle by making all the five pillars When all the pillars are up, and green, the center platform should rise: Make sure to jump north where I have indicated with the red arrow, and land where I have indicated on the red circle. Failure to do so will result in you, re-doing the entire process again. I have always had a system for solving this. I divided the nine pillars into three sets. Set one has lever one, Set two has lever two, three, four, and five, whereas the Set three has lever six, seven, eight, and nine. First off, I would make the center pillar brown. If it is not brown, I simply refer to Set one, and pull lever one to make it brown. Then, I would try to make all the pillars green. To accomplish this, I refer to Set two (lever two-five). I would fiddle around with only the Set Two levers until I have made them all green. At this point, it does not matter whether they are up or down, as long as all four pillars (not the center one) are green. After all of them are green, I would only use Set three (lever six-nine). At this point, I want all the green levers lowered. After pulling a couple of switches, you should have all the pillars green and lowered into the ground, except the center one, where it is brown. Now, make sure all of your friends are on the platform, and when you are ready, pull on Lever One (Set One). By doing this, you are solving one aspect of the puzzle at a time, and I hope that this hint helps you as much as it helped me. Too lazy? Not enough time to figure it all out? Luckily, I have the solutions to the Brax Lever puzzles. However, this short-cut can only work if no one has tampered with the levers since the last time the platform raised. (This means all pillars are brown, and the four corner pillars are down) If the situation meets that requirement, then you may use the following combination: 1,2,3,4,5,2,3,4,5. (Solution from R043482 AKA Fenix). 7,6,5,4,3,1,2. (Solution from Asimir). If anyone has their own solutions, then you have fully understood the Brax Lever puzzle, and I commend you for that. Very few fully understand the puzzle, and many rely on those who understand it to gain access to Brax. Feel free to tell me of your solutions if you come up with any. ------------------------------------------------ There you have it, every single detailed aspect of the Brax Lever Puzzle. I hope you find it informative. If you have any questions, or require further help, try to contact me in-game. Thank you all.
