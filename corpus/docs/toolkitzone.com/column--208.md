# Column

Boredom A Weekly Word By Cyrus Martin
Welcome to the first weekly column that I will be writing for toolkitzone.com Yeah I know * y a w n * Woopdeedoo Another article. Wow. How exciting
Whats that? Bored by this already? Good. Glad to hear it.
You have just enthusiastically volunteered to take part in my first topic of interest Boredom.
Thats right, I said boredom.
You see (or at least you might if you werent already drooling all over the side of your own face from the near coma that your boredom has manifested) we all have one major thing in common when it comes to participation as an audience. We are all hard to entertain.
Why am I talking about this? I mean so what? Who cares? Well, if you are planning on being involved in the entertainment industry in any way then you had better figure out a way to straighten up and pay attentionbecause if you dont care then NOBODY is going to.
Ok, so I think that you probably understand by now what I am getting at. People want to be entertained not lectured to not toyed with not talked down to They want you to present something that will speak to them with a level of intellect that at least appears comprehendible but is also amusing in some significant way.
Huh
That sounds kind of like a contradictory statement
Well yes it does because it is... but it is also the truth. Holding an audiences interest is sort of like juggling a fireball, a water balloon and a hammer. A miscalculation at any point in the process can result in a wild variety of embarrassing results.
It is vitally important to maintain your own interest if you wish to gain that of anyone else. If you become bored with your own project then guess what? Yep The audience will be too.
So despite the obvious peril of this juggling act it is essential otherwise you are just wasting your time.
So what do I do about this? How do I make something that ISNT boring? The answer to that is pretty obvious. Entertain yourself before you try to entertain others. Dont expect anybody else to care if you dont.
Talk to your friends or family about your ideas. Even if you think that they are morons, you might find the quality of their advice surprising. Try it. It cant hurt.
You are more than capable of producing something that will amaze and captivate an audience. All you have to do is stay focused on your project, committed to your goal and interested in your work. You can do it.
So rather than bore you any longer I will end this article by thanking you for reading all the way to the bottom of it without falling asleep. Hopefully these words will be of some help to you or at least of interest.
Best of luck.
-Cy
