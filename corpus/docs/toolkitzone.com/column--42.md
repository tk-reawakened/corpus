But I'm not going to talk about that. Instead I would like to introduce myself to all of you by means of my past, present, and future works. Hopefully I will inspire some of you in your endeavor with whatever you are most interested in.
So let's get started.
Lorie-Jay Comiso Gutierrez, a Philippine national, was born in April 12, 1983. He was the eldest of the three siblings. Born of Julita Gutierrez, a plain housewife; hardworking and capable of many things, and Lorenzo Gutierrez, a Civil Engineering undergraduate; considered by many as a genius in many fields of studies. Lorie-Jay grew and learned to lead a simple life--not too much to spend, and only buys what is really needed. Part of the simple joy of entertainment was his interest in drawing. At a youg age of 4 he began to draw images of a deer and other animals. Although he had not seen a deer his entire life, he managed to draw one in the image of the famous cartoon from Walt Disney: Bambi. He has found his place in the company of papers and pencils. He kept on drawing more and more images of his favorite animals. Soon, he started taking interest in a different theme of artwork. He started watching Japanese shows like Voltes 5, Bioman, Shaider, and the likes. Naturally he adjusted his drawing capability to match those of which he had been watching on a daily basis. At the age of 9 he was making his own comic books.
