# column

Community > The Adventures of Porno Fred
From the surreal and often far too perverse mind of Marz comes the start of a new community based story revolving around his alter-ego, Fred (for the purposes of the column image, he happens to look just like a rather popular cartoon character also called Fred *cough*).
**Book 1: Freddy Gets his Name**
**Chapter 1: A Shocking Beginning**
Freddy sat upright on his bed, leaning slightly forward with his fists clenched as he watched the TK-mon competition. As he absent-mindedly looked at the 13-inch television plopped in front of him, all he could think about was the next day, when he would be able to embark on his own TK-mon quest and become master of all TK-mon. Suddenly, his mother began yelling, “Freddy! Shut that damn TV off before I have to go over there and give you a spanking”. Freddy quickly turned off the television and jumped under the covers of his racing car modelled bed. Throughout the night Freddy tossed and turned as he dreamt about becoming the ultimate TK-mon master, capturing all the TK-mon in Tkland.
“ Freddy! Get your ass out of bed, your late!” Freddy woke up to perhaps the most obnoxious yell he'd heard in his life, and in the act he fell off his bed in the excitement. Freddy changed as quickly as possible and barely had his pants on as he ran out the door. Grabbing his bike, he peddled as quickly as possible to Professor Randy's lab so that he could get his TK-mon, guessing frantically at which TK-mon was left for him to take, or if everyone else was also late. As he biked up the hill, he was able to see the top of Professor Randy's lab, and it became more and more visible as he approached it. Unfortunately, to his shock, Dont_think_so was already there accepting congratulations on his newly gotten TK-mon. Freddy couldn't believe it, and he brushed Dont_think_so aside as he ran up the steps to seek Professor Randy.
When Freddy entered the lab, he found the professor riding his freakish animal around the lab while performing some sort of experiment.
“ Professor Randy! Professor! Look at me!”
“ Hmm, eh? What? Why Freddy! Your late, don't you think.”
“ Yeah, sorry about that. Do you still have a TK-mon for me to choose from?”
“ Well, actually you were the last one to arrive, so I only have this one...” the professor outstreched his hand with a TK-ball that contained Freddy's new TK-mon. Freddy snatched it right out of the professor's hands and was about to release it from the ball so he could see which one he got.
“ But I must warn you,” said the professor, “this particular TK-mon isn't very friendly.”
“ Aw shucks, don't worry professor, me and my TK-mon will be the best of friends!”
Suddenly, Freddy released the TK-mon from the TK-ball and out came the cutest little creature he had ever seen. It was a Seven Eternal. It had big blue eyes, yellow fur, and a tail to match! Freddy was ecstatic that he was still able to get such a rare TK-mon despite being late. He approcahed the Seven Eternal to shake its hand, and at that very instant was surged with a high wattage of electricity.
“ AHH! You little piece of...”
“ Now now Freddy, I told you this one was tough to handle”
“ But it just shocked me, isn't there some disciplinary action I can do to like... you know?”
“ I'm afraid not Freddy, you're going to have to get it to like you all on your own.”
“ Oh man! What if I sent it to a Homosexual TK-Bar?”
“ That wouldn't work too well Freddy, TK-mon only have one gender.”
And so Freddy tried returning the Seven Eternal to the TK-Ball, but the stubborn creature refused to remain confined to a small ball, and insisted it stay outside. Professor Randy offered Freddy some rubber gloves and a leesh, and so Freddy set out. His adventure started a little bit on the rough side, with his rival Dont_think_so already way ahead of him, but that didn't discourage him. No, Freddy kept his eye set on being the best TK-Master there is!
