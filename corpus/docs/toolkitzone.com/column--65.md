Community > The Adventures of Porno Fred
Second chapter of Marz's community alter-ego introduces some more familiar names into the fold in this Pokemon/Dragon Ball Z styled adventure.
**Book 1: Freddy Gets His Name**
**Chapter 2: Freddy Gets Bugged**
We last left our less-than stellar dynamic duo back in the little village of Kaffienated. Freddy and Seven Eternal weren't and still aren't on very good terms, and as they walk through General Chit-Chat forest, one has to wonder, what is to become of these two 'partners' as Freddy tries to become a TK-Master.
"You know, you've gotta help me out sooner or later." Freddy was trying to persuade 7E to be a good TK-Mon, and help him out in his personal quest. Seven Eternal just ignored Freddy and kept whisteling the tune from Kill Bill. "Fine! You know what, I don't need you. I'll catch another pokemon all by myself without your help!" Seven Eternal just kept whisteling.
Just at that moment, a bug TK-Mon crawled out of the bushes. Freddy quickly pulled out DEXTER, a little gizmo that he managed to steal from Professor Randy's lab. DEXTER identifies the bug TK-Mon and presents the information to Freddy, "Spydie, a bug pokemon. Uses its anus to squirt out a sticky substance and trap its foes, and uses its ninja-blade-esque legs to chop up its prey."
"Well those don't look very sharp," exclaimed Freddy, "this is going to be an easy one to catch! Ya hear that Seven Eternal you bastard?! I'm gonna get a Tk-mon!" And with that, Freddy picked up a nearby rock and tried whipping it at the spydie. Spydie used its spydie senses and quickly dodged the attack. "Huh? Piece of shit, I'll just use a bigger rock." Freddy then went for the biggest rock he could fine, and hauled it over to within range of spydie. He then launched it at the little spydie. It hit! "Yes! Now all I need is my pokeball!" Freddy whipped out his pokeball and threw it at the spydie, but the spydie wasn't content on getting caught yet. Spydie bided its time, and then launched a long string of white gooey substance from its ass, stopping the ball dead in its tracks.
"What the hell?! That's it!" Freddy broke a twig off a tree, picked up the pokeball, and attacked the spydie with the twig. Spydie quickly dodged the first poke attack by Freddy, but Freddy wasn't gonna let himself fail again. He quickly stabbed spydie again, and then launched the TK Ball at him. Spydie was caught inside the TK Ball, and began frantically struggling. The TK Ball wobbled twice, but then stabalized. Spydie had been caught! "Yes! That's right 7E! Who's your daddy!" Seven Eternal looked back at him, smiled, and then pointed behind Freddy.
"Wha-?" Freddy turned around, and all he could see behind him were a swarm of Spydies, getting ready to attack him. "Aw, crap!" Freddy began running his ass of down the forest, getting his ass pinched by the the little spydie's as he ran. All Seven Eternal could do was sit in a tree and laugh. "Aren't you going to do anything!" Yelled Freddy as he ran around frantically in desperation. "What are you, useless?! Don't you have any attacks!" Seven Eternal quickly took offense to that, and stood upright on the ground. Suddenly, a large jolt of electricity came out of nowhere, directed at Freddy. Freddy saw the jolt coming at him, "I'm gonna shit myself"
And with that, Freddy and every little spydie around him was jolted with a huge surge of electricity. Freddy collapsed onto the floor, and all the Spydie scurried away. Seven Eternal went over to Freddy and looked down with a puzzled look. Freddy was out cold, so 7E tried poking him with a stick. It was official, Freddy wasn't going to get up for a while. Seven Eternal then proceeded to walk ontop of Freddy, sit down on his head, and a big grin appeared on his face. It was obvious that 7E had no remorse. Did this TK-Mon have any concious? Will Seven Eternal ever team up with Freddy in a TK Duel? Find out next time, on Freddy Ball Z!
