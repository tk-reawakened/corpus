# Deadline Extended: April 15th, 10pm GMT

Browse
Short Story Contest - 2007
**Deadline Extended: April 15th, 10pm GMT**
It's been around 18 months since Blob Dude picked up the first place award in the [inaugural short story contest](http://www.toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=50) back in 2005 and after various delays the contest is back for a second run. The original was certainly a popular event attracting 20 entries and a lot of non-participating community interest, so hopefully this will prove just as successful.
The contest structure is a simple one and won't change from last time; you have four weeks to submit a short story that in some form revolves around three specified plot points, the entries are published on the site, the community votes on their favourite three stories and an invited judge gives a critique on the final shortlist and decides the winner. This year's judge is former moderator and staff member MrG. Since leaving the community four years ago he has gone on to manage a bookstore and currently teaches English in Portland.
Anyone can enter and the rules are as follows;
- Story must revolve around these three plot points in no specific order; a re-discovered message, an untimely end, and a journey.
- How you interpret the three plot points, if and how they relate to eachother, and the part they play in the story is completely down to you.
- All other story elements are for you to decide.
- Can be any writing style.
- Between 500 and 2500 words.
- Must be your own work and in the English language.
- Submit entries via e-mail to xavier@toolkitzone.com
- **Dea dline is April 15th, 10pm GMT**
If you have any questions then feel free to post them as a comment here or message me, I look forward to receiving your entries.
Happy writing!
