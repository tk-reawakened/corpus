# Deadline Extended: September 28th, 10pm GMT

Browse
Short Story Contest - 2008
**Deadline Extended: September 28th, 10pm GMT**
Sharpen your pencils and put those thinking caps on because it's time for the third short story contest at the Toolkit Zone! Around sixteen months ago Talyn picked up the gold medal in the with his tale "Solution 9". The past two contests have proved popular with high quality entries from a diverse range of community members, so I hope this will prove just as popular. [second short story contest](http://toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=301)
The contest structure is a simple one and won't change from last time, in fact I'm copy and pasting most of this text; you have three weeks to submit a short story that in some form revolves around three specified plot points, the entries are published on the site, the community votes on their favourite three stories and an invited judge gives a critique on the final shortlist and decides the winner. This year's judge is yet to be decided, I'll have more news on that at a later date.
Anyone can enter and the rules are as follows;
- Story must revolve around these three plot points in no specific order; a pact between foes, a catch twenty-two and a forgotten promise.
- How you interpret the three plot points, if and how they relate to eachother, and the part they play in the story is completely down to you.
- All other story elements are for you to decide.
- Can be any writing style.
- Between 500 and 5000 words.
- Must be your own work and in the English language.
- in plaintext or rtf/doc format Submit entries via e-mail to (redacted: email)
- **Dea dline is September 28th, 10pm GMT**
If you have any questions then feel free to post them as a comment here or message me, I look forward to receiving your entries.
Happy writing!
