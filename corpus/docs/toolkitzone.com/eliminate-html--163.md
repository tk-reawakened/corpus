# Eliminate HTML

Browse
The Day I Administer TKZone... By...um...Yammy? Some weird day back during Summer Expo of 2006, with an Evangelion episode playing in the background on my computer, I was doing some typing on that odd ShoutBox thingy. You know, that contraption where you type stuff that will then appear. It's amazing. And I mean amazing! I was so suprised by that ShoutBox that... Okay, I'm babbling so let me get to my point. Okay, I was chatting on the ShoutBox with Xavier, or Vampz to all the newbies who want to sound cool. I asked him if I could Administer TKZone. Expecting an imminent yes, I was proud of the answer I received. He said I could be Administer the day he wanted Toolkitzone to die! I was so excited. I couldn't wait until that day. Well, the day still hasn't come so I thought I'd write an article about it. But don't expect this on anytime soon because Xavier never really publishes my stuff. Okay, now you're probably wondering why you're reading it. Well let me explain. I thought I'd make a list of stuff I'd do if I took hold of TKZone. I know after Xavier reads this, he'll surely surrender his position as Administrator and let me have a try. So enough chit-chat. I'm going to type something pseudo-important. Belive this or not, everytime I have to write an article and stuff, I have to use HTML. It's very complex. So I thought I'd get an HTML expert here to make a drag-and-drop typing editor. Instead of worrying about fancy coding like that tag, we could take a letter from a sandbox and drag it onto the page. After about an hour, you could complete a few paragraphs. What a brilliant idea I have. Of course, I'd take the credit while not working at all on the project. The RPG Toolkit really sucks. No doubt about it. I'll find some other program to have this site about like Game Maker or FPSCreator. Maybe even the 3D Gamemaker System. Okay, moving on. The Toolkit just really is awful. Period. .DoireP I apologize, but I just don't like anyone here. Your presence annoys me. Annoys me greatly. And I don't like to be annoyed. Users like Whist and Ivan are what keep me up all night, plotting to kill my neighbors while I chew on my $75 beret. I even really hate it when people post. Posts usually include information that is either too complicated for me or not superior enough for me. So in other words, bye-bye if I'm ever Administrator, which I will be. I've only been in a single TK Rant so far. Only one! That's unheard of! I deserve more respect than that. So I will punish TKGameboy by making all his Rants focus on me. I can even have him to propaganda Rants to make me look good. Following that, I will proceed to eliminate the word "Oi" from his vocabulary. I'm going to build myself the biggest, bad-ass castle in the world using the TKZone donation money. You people will think you're giving me money to branch off on the creation of the Toolkit, but boy will you be suprised. I'll embezzle all the dough and have myself a nice pad to reside in. Ah, the sweet life of an Administrator... Using what is left of the donation money, I'll hire some of TKZone's most pathetic noobs to create the finest TK Online game ever. This will be so awesome, it will even use the Mwin function. I'll be the director and should post the job positions soon. I'll do the amazingly diffucult and complex job of writing the plot. What time-consuming job I'll have. Be lucky you'll just have to do simple stuff like programing and sprite design. When I hit the reign as Administrator of TKZone, I shall rename Blog to Yammy. You will now posts Yammys, and active participants shall be called Yammyers. Be proud my name can be used when ever you rant about your petty life. I shall reduce the required Article size to have one published. One sentence is sufficient enough to have your Article put in the royal TK Zone Library. You know, I don't really feel like reading an amazingly long story or guide on how to use MS Paint. All I want to do is increase the size of the Library so people will think TKZone is full of literary geniuses. This blue layout really bugs me. I want to make it yellow. I'm sure someone here can transform the layout to a more yellowish color. The template in itself is perfectly fine. Also, the gray buttons are weird too. I'll make them yellow also. Then I'll remove the game section so no one will feel it necessary to create a video game. Jack Thompson will be so excited I'll win the Nobel Peace Prize and have that shiny medal to wear around my neck.
