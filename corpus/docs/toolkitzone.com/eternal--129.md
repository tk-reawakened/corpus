# Eternal

As the series of explosions faded away, Jared Christianssen got to his feet and started sprinting again. The corrugated metal deck beneath his feet rang as his boots pounded against it. Another huge explosion rocked the ship, and Jared was thrown headlong into the corridor wall. The vibrations rang through the dark metal walls, deafening him for a split second. Then he was back on his feet and running. Jared had served on this ship for 10 years now, and at 26, it was more of a home than any other place in the United Systems. He'd been in many a battle in his time, but this was truly the worst. was possibly the most heavily armed Gunship in the Armada, and one of the most agile, but it wasn't invincible. As this battle might turn out to prove. Jared hurtled round a corner and entered the Bridge corridor. Suddenly, he was jerked sharply back by the waist and slammed into the ground. Looking over his shoulder he saw his red Tag caught on an jagged piece of debris. "Bloody thing" he muttered whilst untangling it. Within the Armada, the separate ranks were indicated by coloured Tags worn on the waist. A Tag was a three inch long strap of nylon that hung down to about the knee. Operationals wore blue Tags; Troopers wore red, Lieutenants brown and Commanders black. Finally getting loose, he got back up and burst through the alloy double doors onto the Bridge of the , known by the crew as Valhalla, an ancient word found buried among documents meaning Hell. The bridge itself was fairly small, and crewed by only four people. On the right stood a large hologram, showing all the ships in this Battle Arena and their current status. One Lieutenant manned this section, every now and again calling out position vectors and trajectories. Over to the left was a hologram of the showing areas of damage and weaknesses in the shields. At the front of Valhalla was the pilots seat, slightly sunk into the deck. Seated in here was the ships Commander, now twisting and turning the control yoke rapidly, sending spiralling through the battle masterfully. In the centre of the room, the gunner sat on a raised seat, his chair spinning left and right, as the cannon above tracked enemy targets. Jared rushed into the room and stood behind the pilots seat. Sir, engine bays three and six are completely shot, and were losing four and eight! We dont have enough power left to maintain this! he blurted out to the man sitting in front of him. How long have four and eight got? the man replied. 5 minutes, at absolute most. After that, were at half power. Jared replied. Then we keep going. Five more minutes and we can wrap this up. Sir, well cook the engines! Shell hold. The pilot replied as another explosion rocked the ship. Dammit Chase! Take out those tracking ships! he yelled up at the gunner. Im on it. The gunner, Lance Chase called back. His chair swung round violently and four silent shots were fired. This was the strange truth about battles in a Starfield Arena, as it was a vacuum out there; all the explosions and shots were totally silent. It was only when the ship was hit did it make noise. Good shooting. The Commander called and pulled Eternal into an impossibly steep climb. The engines screamed under the exertion, and Jared shifted uncomfortably. Easy Jared, theyll take it. Now moving onto an escape vector, said the pilot. The ship thundered upward further until finally the Battle Arena Display hologram showed no other Gunships. The pilot brought the ship calmly back to true orientation and retracted the control yoke. At the same time, Chase descended from the gun turret, grinning like a kid. Jared stayed respectfully behind the Commander while he got up from the pilots seat and turned around. Commander Jake Vaysonn was probably the best pilot in the Armada. Admitted into the training program at just thirteen years old, he showed immense potential from an early age. Vaysonn was one of the rare few Commanders who rose through the ranks, instead of being granted it as an honorary merit. He had been born in one of the cramped living quarters of the Gunship itself and no-one was more at home in its metal walls than him. Excessively tall, good looking and charming, Vaysonn was the model Commander, and in many propaganda leaflets he was described as the Peoples Commander. However, Vaysonn wasnt as squeaky clean as many would believe. Two years ago when he was twenty eight and still a Lieutenant he was charged with assaulting an enemy prisoner of war so severely that the man nearly died. Vaysonn has come close to being thrown out of the Armada, and if it werent for his exceptional skills as a pilot, he would have been. After the hearing, Vaysonn had returned to active duty on the Gunship and kept his head down for eighteen months. It wasnt until he was awarded three bravery in battle medals that there was an outcry for his promotion to Commander. Vaysonns secret charges were never made public, and so the Commodores who lead this fragment of the Armada had no option but to promote him. And to add insult to injury, the only Gunship in need of a commander was , the pride of the Armada. Now Jake Vaysonn stood beside the pilots seat of the most feared Gunship in the United Systems in the gunmetal grey bomber jacket and jeans of a Commander. The long black Tag hung from his waist, accompanied by the three smaller white straps that indicated his bravery medals. The black boots that he wore were strapped and buckled in every direction, an addition to the standard uniform that Vaysonn had added himself. Brushing the long, stylishly dishevelled blonde hair from his eyes, he grinned at his crew. Good job guys, I think we struck a worthwhile blow there. Gill, set a course for home, he added to the Lieutenant stationed at the Battle Arena Display, Gill McKenzie. Im on it boss, the impossibly thin figure of Gill replied as he punched the command into the navigation computer. Right Jared, lets go see those engines of yours, Vaysonn said and strode off into the Bridge Corridor with Jared following behind. Walking beside Vaysonn, Jared felt slightly uncomfortable. The guy was a war hero and in charge of the best ship in the Armada. Plus he was irritatingly good looking, something which Jared was amazed to find himself resenting. Some guys just get all the breaks he thought darkly. As they walked, Vaysonn made small talk. So you reckon three and six are gone? he asked. Yeah, they both overloaded. And four and eight? They were on the verge of burning out, took glancing hits from one of those pursuit Eeyayohs. Im amazed they held out as long as they did. You keep them in good order Jared. Without you and your engineers work, Eternal would be useless. Thank you sir. He also supports his crew well, thought Jared. If were four engines down, well have to spend a couple of days landlocked. Yeah, thats if the techs have four spare ex-kays kicking around. If not we could be there for a lot longer. Well deal with that if it arises. They walked though the pressurised hatch into the engine bay where the eight XK-6 generators were housed, now humming quietly. Vaysonn was always amazed at how small they were. A single XK-6 was far smaller than one of the ancient automobiles that used to trundle around everywhere on Earth. Arranged in a perfect circle, the generators propelled a stream of highly negative particles into space, providing 20% of the thrust. The other 80% came from using the rear plate of the ship itself. By also charging this negatively, the entire rear hull was repelled from the stream that the engines produced. Essentially it was just very large magnets at work. It was plain to see that two of the ex-kays were completely wrecked, their casing had cracked open and the insides were hanging out. Another two had clear scorch marks on them and were crackling slightly as they worked. Not good thought Vaysonn, these things cost a bomb. The Commodore will not be pleased. There again, they had struck a vital blow. Sir? We have a choice here. I can leave those damaged engines running and run the risk of completely totalling them, or I can shut them down and ramp the others up to compensate, said Jared. Are the two damaged ones salvageable? Highly unlikely Id say. Theyre probably write-offs now. Then keep them running. If they blow, they blow. No point straining the others though if we can help it. Aye Sir. Well you seem in control here. Ill return to Valhalla. Comm if you need me. Vaysonn said. Aye Sir, Jared replied and went off to supervise the two other engineers on the damaged XK engines. As Jake Vaysonn walked back to the Bridge, he ran his hands along the cool metal of s walls. He could never quite believe he was in charge of this ship after all his years growing up and training on her. truly was a beautiful ship. From her razor sharp front end, all the way through to the swept back wings, she was truly beautiful. Even now, when the note of the engines was decidedly more strained than normal and there was debris along the corridors, Vaysonn still had eyes for no other ship. As he re-entered Valhalla, he let out a sigh of contentment. This was his home. The huge, glowing mass of Jupiter hung in the background as swept onto one of Europas floating landing decks, its four remaining engines howling in agony. The ship struggled to maintain its entry path as it slowly manoeuvred onto the deck. Julia Reynolds watched in dread as dipped suddenly at the last moment, but then the ship was down and she relaxed. Wearing the armour-like suit of a ground technician, or GT, she strode across the deck towards the now descending access ramp. Reynolds was Europas best trooper. Period. Her active ranking was higher than any serving soldier, she had been awarded seven medals for gallantry above and beyond the call of duty, and she was madly in love with Jake Vaysonn. As she approached the ramp leading to the her heart skipped a beat as Vaysonn walked down from inside the ship. He was characteristically in his custom made Ground Kit, a remarkably Spartan amount of armour, with a headset that had a jaw piece on the left side, but not the right. He jogged down the ramp and embraced Reynolds briefly. Julia had first met Vaysonn on a mission aboard the Gunship when he was a trooper and she was an Operational. They had been part of the specialist team that had succeeded in destroying one of the enemies Gunships. Both had been awarded medals for this mission and Vaysonn had been promoted to Lieutenant. In the celebrations following the promotion ceremony, Julia had made a pass at Vaysonn. He had replied in a way that she would never forget. Julia, if I wasnt gay I would love to take you up on that offer. Im sorry. She could never decide if it was his flat out honesty that had been so weird, or if it as the fact he had apologised for it. Vaysonn let go of Julia and looked her straight in the eye. How you been then? he asked. Good thanks. I hear youve toasted Eternal. Yeah, well you know. Cant go leaving a battle with a healthy ship can you? Do you know if the techs have any ex-kays kicking around? Nope, no idea. Im not a bloody tech Jake. Could have fooled me, Vaysonn nodding at the suit she was wearing. Cheeky sod, she retorted and punched him playfully. Easy trooper, Lance Chase called from behind them as he disembarked, followed by Gill McKenzie, Jared Christianssen and the techs. Hey Lance, Julia called back. Hey babe, kept your bed warm for me? Lance said while pinching the armour plate that covered Julias bum. Sod off Lance, Julia laughed and half-heartedly swung at him. Cmon children. Lets head in, Vaysonn interjected. The three soldiers headed towards the elevator that would take them down to the base, as ground crews began strapping down onto the deck. The last limping engines of the ship whined to a grateful silence. Vaysonn wandered into his quarters in the Europa base and threw his kit bag onto the bed pushed up against one wall. The quarters on Europa werent bad, but they werent exactly great either. Everyone had the same size room, regardless of rank. This wasnt anything to do with equality; it was the fact that it wasnt practical to have large quarters in an underwater base. Vaysonn wandered over to the bed, pushed the kit bag aside and dropped down onto his back, facing the ceiling. Tiredness coursed through him, making his eyelids heavy. Sod it, he though. Five minutes. Vaysonn awoke suddenly to someone banging loudly on his door and yelling something. What? What is it? he shouted. The Commodore wants to see you, replied someone from the other side of the door. Vaysonn stood in front of the commodores desk watching the slightly portly figure wander up and down behind it. Eternal will need to remain grounded for at least three days. We have three ex-kays here and another two on order, but they wont arrive until then, the Commodore said. Understood sir, Vaysonn replied. In the meantime, Eternal will have a full refit. Shes been a bit neglected just recently, so well upgrade all her systems and armoury. Thank you sir. Which reminds me, Eternal will have a new crew member after the refit. Oh? Vaysonn was troubled by this. was full, there were no places open. Yes. I asked her to come here to meet you. What position will she be filling? That will become clear when you meet her, the Commodore replied with a sideways glance. There was a soft knock at the door, and a Lieutenant entered. Commodore, shes has arrived, he said. Good, send her in. The Lieutenant went outside for a moment, and then opened the door wide. Through the door walked a tall, incredibly beautiful woman who looked in her mid twenties. Her black hair was pulled back into a high ponytail, revealing small, hooped earrings. Her deep brown eyes twinkled as she strode into the centre of the room. Her high heeled boots clunked on the iron floor and the chains at her waist clanked together with every stride. Vaysonn stared at her in amazement, his eyes tracking down from her head, taking in the tight green tank top, then the black cargo trousers and the black boots she wore. Commander Vaysonn, Id like you to meet Jenni, the Commodore said. The Jenni woman shot Vaysonn a smile and held out a hand to shake. As her hips moved with her arm, Vaysonn noticed the strap hanging at her waist. A single, black strap.

Part I - Arena
* Early in the 24th Century, Space, as it had once been called, had become a battlefield. Stars and constellations that had once been given the names of God's were now half whispered, the whisperers terrified of the battles that accompanied their names. A century previously, a new technology, the XK-6 engine had allowed for in and out of atmosphere craft, known as IAO (pronounced ee-yay-oh) to be created, and soon Earth had its very first Galactic Armada. The 'Solar System', as the old binary records called it, quickly became colonised throughout its twelve planets and their moons. However, Earth was dying. The XK-6 ran on pure hydrogen which was easily fabricated using new atomic engineering technology, however the processes of making the engine required a vast amount of Earths natural resources. Earth's already dwindling fossil fuels became completely drained, and eventually vanished. Earth began a period of twenty year convulsions that would eventually become known as "The Shift". The weather turned, the seas rose and fell by dozens of meters every hour. Earthquakes rocked the centre's of the tectonic plates. The planet became irradiated as the Ozone layer broke down as gravity weakened. Eventually, the governments of Earth decided that the planet had become too dangerous to inhabit. By 2280, Earth had been evacuated. However, the issue of what to do with the hulk of Earth became the topic of heated argument. In the end, three clear groups emerged. The "Guardello", the "Phaynix" and the "Kyran". The Guardians wanted to maintain Earth as a memorial to mankinds upbringing. The Phaynix wanted Earth to remain, but for it to become simply a central port for the colonisation of the rest of the galaxy. The Kyran wanted Earth to be destroyed, to wipe away the memory of humankinds destructive misuse of what didnt belong to it. The Armada split between these three groups, each one taking roughly the same number of the XK-6 Gunships, and with the armament came the beginning of the war. Planet Earth had become the pivot point in a deadly three way conflict. The Armada ships fought their own kin, crews fighting against those who they had grown up and trained with. Armada ships locked each other in deadly combat, each one's firepower matched identically to the other. One such ship, was the Gunship Eternal...
