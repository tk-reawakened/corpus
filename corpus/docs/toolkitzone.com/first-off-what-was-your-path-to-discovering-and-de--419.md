# First off, what was your path to discovering and deciding to use the RPG Toolkit?

Browse
![Gabbling With Grindalf Banner](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1313.png)
Recently, I had the pleasure to ask a few questions to the user who shined in 2008. Not only did one of his *FIVE* released games in 2008 take both the award and award, this user also brought home the title of . This user goes by the alias of Grindalf. *Game of the Year* *Best Game Programming* *2008 User of the Year*
**First off, what was your path to discovering and deciding to use the RPG Toolkit?**
I actually started programing with Yabasic on the Playstation 2 but it was really limited in what you could do. Plus, it would take about five minutes to parse a large program. When I saw RPGMaker on the Playstation, I was very excited but I couldn't get hold of a copy here in Tenerife (we normally have to get certain games sent here from England). I was then given a really old crappy computer with windows 3.1 and Qbasic on it which had great help files and helped me learn basic programing. When I eventually bought a modern computer, I got a load of discs with some programs on it and one of them was a demo of blitz basic which I found quite easy to use. I bought Blitz3D and was quite happy with it. I tried making some things but with little success of actually completing anything. Then I started making my own rpgmaker, at which point I decided it would be best to do some research and downloaded every rpgmaker/gamemaker I could find and test them all. I thought it would be best if I made a game with at least one of those rpgmakers for research. That was where I started Darkened Worlds, I soon lost interest in the rpgmaker I was making and just stuck with the best rpgmaker/gamemaker I had found, which was the RPG Toolkit. I still do some work on my rpgmaker every now and then, but at this rate it will never get done.
Very Happy
**What influenced you to make the type of games you did in 2008?**
I guess I make non RPGs because I get bored of projects quickly and RPGs take the longest to make. I am working on a JRPG calledThe Paladin but I get bored of making tiles and boards really easily and find myself wanting to code something.
**What kind of mindset/expectations did you have going into the Zone Awards?**
I actually expected to win because I thought it would be done according to ranking and was the highest ranked game of 2008. When I saw it was going to a vote I thought Brad would take the award because his game was funny with great graphics and I knew a lot of people loved it. I knew I would be considered for the award but I still think SuperIsoRpg should have taken it for his game *Game of the Year* *Galaxy Defender*
*Best Game Programming*Of Rage and War . Still, I'm not complaining. Wining the was a shock. I really expected jackyboy to take it. I'm really happy with the way it went. I really didn't expect to do so well. *(editor - it wasn't released in '08)*Very Happy
*User of the Year*
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/vault/games/images/10886.png)
***Galaxy Defender captured the 2008 Game of the Year award.***
**After five game releases in 2008 and one already in 2009, what keeps you going?**
I guess mostly the community. We have a great group of people here at Toolkitzone and the encouragement and feedback that we get from everyone should help us to keep going and get our games finished. I know it does for me.
**Speaking of 2009, do you have any insight to what the community should be expecting from you?**
Hopefully some good stuff.Galaxy FinalMad Golf , and the one I'm expecting to be the most fun;TK Wars . But don't expect that to be all.
Very Happy
**What TK games are you anticipating?**
Ander's JourneySoldat RPG and slipknot_hellraiser'sWrestling game and also hisshooting game . I've kinda lost hope that
[Heraldry: Freija](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?game=69)The Lost Epic will ever be finished.
**Are there any ambitions in getting into the gaming industry or is game making strictly a hobby?**
I would like to make games where you pay to download them but I don't think I'm good enough to get into the mainstream game industry.
**Finally, what advice would you give to those who have a hard time sticking with a project until the end?**
Follow Brad's example and keep your games short. He has made two great games and the longest is about three hours. Also, if you hate making graphics, use ripped graphics. If you hate making music (like me), use ripped music. Nothing is going to kill your love for your project more than forcing yourself to do hours worth of work that you hate doing. - - - - - - - - - - I'd like to thank Grindalf for taking the time in doing this interview. It was an honor to conduct it with him.
