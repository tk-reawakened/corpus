# For Your Own Safety

Browse
Some game projects are doomed from the start, they'll just never happen. Not because it's too hard, or will take too long, but because the user has no real clue how to do anything apart from eat, sleep, and can just about urinate without splashing some on themselves. But do not fear young developer, FYOS is here to outline some of the most common scenarios such users fall into - so avoid at all costs! And if worst comes to worst, just say it was all a bad dream and start again. You have this idea, a REALLY great idea. Aliens invading New York with super dooper ultra violet ray guns and some big fat blobby bastard of a bad guy as the final boss - OMFG it's perfect!!!!!!!!!!1111. So what next? Well, you need a team. You need some art guy, music guy, coding guy, website guy, board guy, another coding guy, tester guy, food guy, motivational guy, another coding guy, another art guy, maybe a further music guy...and not to forget, a script guy. Perfect! So with the speed of a very fast thing who is really excited and going faster than it's normal fast, fast thingy speed...you post your requirements on the job board. Bingo! All you gotta do is wait for all the talented people to sign up and do all the work for you. Just remember to mention they'll only get salaries if the game eventually goes retail!!!! Aint game creation great. Sadly, three weeks later no one has signed up apart from equally untalented penguins who goof around with really wacky colors on their MSN font and think Hillary Duff is the next best thing since the inflatable dart board. Alas, your dreams are abandoned. You've just played Final Fantasy, and man does that game rock. Wow. And you know what, it's an RPG. And guess what? You have an RPG Maker! (cue: OMFG!!!!111 style revelations to pet cat). You can now make a whole new Final Fantasy game yourself! This afternoon! And still have time to watch pokemon!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! What's even better is that all the Final Fantasy tiles, music and backgrounds are all available to download! How great is this? Within one hour you have fifteen repetitive boards using the same ripped FF tiles everyone uses - it looks so fricking good! Just like professionals! You're getting confident, feeling good about this so called game making lark. You might become a professional, afterall - you do know HTML really, really well....you can make scrolling text and everything!!!! In fact, you're so happy with things that you decide to announce a demo release date. Next month! No, no...wait...that's not good enough. Next week! YES! Next week you will release Final Fantasy: Death Wish to the world! The joy! But wait...you can't do anything in the game yet....nothing happens! Oh wait, you need some of that rpg code stuff! But what the...there's no Final Fantasy code to download!!! WHAT'S THE POINT HAVING ALL THOSE FF TILES AND MUSIC, BUT NO CODE! You need someone to help you make your game, so you post a reasonable topic on the most unrelated forum you can find, probably web design, and ask someone to send you a battle system. To your horror, no one gives you any code! How dare they! All this effort you did and no one wants to help. Bastards. What now? Well, you damn well can't spend time learning to code - pokemon starts in a minute! And then your day is brightened once more, you find the RPG Maker software - you don't need to do anything to make a game in that! PERFECT! You always knew you were good at this game making stuff. Now to find some FF tiles for it... You've started making a game. It's got a really cool plot where your guy has to fight an evil guy with a super cool ending that will shock anyone who has been under a rock and never played a cliche ridden game before! You've made a start to the game and it's going well, you've even created a game profile at TKZ and put up screenshots. People like it! Sweet! You settle down to create some tiles, but you suddenly have a flash of brilliance - a new idea. The main character is actually the evil guys son, and this can be put into a sequal! So you create a second game profile and put up the info for how you will reveal him to be Evil Junior and that his brother is actually his sister from the future! Sweeeeeeet. Happy that you have a clear course to follow for the future, you sit right back down to work on some code. But wait...another lump of inspired thought lands on your head. Before, Evil Senior was actually also a good guy...and he was shockingly married to your sister from the future, who was actually the older version of your sister from another future, if you follow. And not only that, but the world is at war because of some basic split between two peoples for no real reason - causing Evil Senior, now Good Senior, although then he wasn't technically Senior because his son wasn't born yet, to go fight the big bad. Now's the time for a third game profile as the originals prequal! Sweeeeeeeeeeeeeeeet. Right. Great. The entire trilogy...OMG you have a trilogy!!!!!111...is laid out. Back to work on the game, now where were you? Hmm. Look at all the coding you have to do, and those tiles arn't very good. Maybe you should simplify the plot...or do the prequal first! Great! Lets do that.... Four months later, in a puddle sweat, it's no good. You can't decide what to do first, and you keep getting these really good ideas about the story of a vampire slayer who fights evil in some typical little town. How do you keep getting this fantastic ideas? Maybe it's time to find a team to help make your game...(see first scenario).
How Not To Start A Game Project

1. I Have A Really, Really, Really Good Idea That Is Really, Really Good and I Need Some Really, Really Good People To Help Me!
2. I Love That Game So Much I Want To Remake It Right Now, And It'll Be Even Better Because I'm Going To Try Really Hard And Make It Better!!
3. I Have So Many Ideas My Head Might Expand To The Size Of The Moon And Cast An Everlasting Shadow Over Europe Causing A Whole New Dark Age And Some Other Cool Stuff!
