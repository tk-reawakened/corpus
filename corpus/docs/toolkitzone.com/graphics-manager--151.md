# Graphics Manager

By Xavier
After several months with us, Indy has stepped down as graphics manager for the website due to ever decreasing time to dedicate to the community. We are all thankful of his service with us, and I am glad to say he'll remain as moderator of the art board and he will be introducing some initiatives there over coming weeks and months. So, what about a replacement? I always prefer a staff member who is talented in the area and enthusiastic in relation to the community. I accepting applications now, this procedure will be announced in due time. For now, I will be stand-in manager for the graphics section.
