# Have Something To Say?

By Xavier
After the busy months of November, December and January - the frontpage articles are beginning to wind down as can be expected. The likes of Khin, R043482, Colin and Occasionally_Correct have help provide over 40 articles over the weeks for the frontpage on a range of Toolkit related and other topics. So we're after some new blood to help entertain and inform the community via the frontpage. If you feel like writing about RPG Code help, community activities, commercial gaming, music creation, comic strip or story - we'd like to hear from you! As long as your topic has an audience in this community, we're happy to give it a read. For the more motivated and talented among you, we've also got weekly column/article slots open for resident writers. With this comes a forum rank and general kudos, as well as the chance to be a fixture on the frontpage of the official Toolkit site. So if you're interested, feel free to send a private message to me and we'll take it from there. Regardless of if you want to write one article or a series, get in touch!
