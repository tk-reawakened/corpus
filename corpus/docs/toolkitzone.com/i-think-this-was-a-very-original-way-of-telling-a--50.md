# I think this was a very original way of telling a story

Short Story Contest
Summer 2005
So we've come to the end of the road for our first try at a writing contest here at TKZ, twenty users took up the challenge of writing a short story, those entries were whittled down to a shortlist of five and now we have the final results. Thanks to everyone who entered, we hope to see you again in the next contest this winter. But without further delay, lets get into the results...
Please understand that these reviews contain the opinions of me, Cyrus Martin and if you do not agree with what is said then I apologize and welcome your comments at cyrusmartin@yahoo.com or talk generally about the contest at the here at TKZ. I rated each entry on a set of categories to determine an average score out of 100. The categories are as follows; [writing forum](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/viewforum.php?f=181)
Scoring
0-100 - Originality 0-100 - Character Design and Development 0-100 - Entertainment Value 0-100 - Technical Composition 0-100 - Artistic Composition and Description Overall Score: Average %
5th - UltimaWizard
60 - Originality 60 - Character Design and Development 80 - Entertainment Value 95 - Technical Composition 75 - Artistic Composition and Description
The details included in this story were very well thought out and put together. In fact, this entry has perhaps the most details of any in the competition. I found the substance of this to be very expansive and somewhat daunting. It almost seemed as though this writer was actually recounting a Dungeons and Dragons match or merely describing events that took place in a video game of some sort... which leads me to the problems. First, I can not overlook the usage of so many borrowed themes and details from pre-established doctrines and works. Much of the specifics used in this story were in fact created by someone else. Though the implementation of these was done in an original way, the substance of these was not even given the pretense of originality. Artistically, I felt that the prose could have been crafted better. There did not seem to be a clear expression of style which made it difficult to maintain interest throughout the entire piece. It would have been nice to have a moment or two in the middle of things where we were told how the characters felt and why or what their motivations were beyond the obvious. I felt as though this story could have been much better if some time had been taken to craft it a little more. It certainly has potential and I look forward to more from this author.
Overall Score: 74
4th - Ersuis
75 - Originality 50 - Character Design and Development 75 - Entertainment Value 90 - Technical Composition 90 - Artistic Composition and Description
First off, I want to say that I thought this piece was very interesting. It recieved an average score under "Entertainment Value" because the reasons that I thought this story was entertaining had little to do with what was happening in the plot. The artistic composition of this story made what is actually a very vague and unsophisticated plot into a fairly captivating narrative. There really was not a lot of substance behind the pretty words. Don't get me wrong, I love a story told with careful prose just as much as the next guy but I expect to have some sort of compelling events and characters behind that. The main character and his nemesis are each described in very aloof ways that never tell us much about who they are or why we should care about them. All that we really know is, one of them is probably a good guy and one is probably a bad guy though even this is uncertain. In the end, I was sort of left wondering why I should care what happens to the main character, the stranger or the magic item (which is equally vague). I would like to say that I liked the presentation of this story very much and I thought that it was very well worded. I think that there was much more to this particular entry than what was posted, but I can only judge this by what I have been given... and so I must conceed that I was probably not as impressed by Ersy's story as I expected to be. I think that he is capable of some very good work and I look forward to reading more of it.
Overall Score: 76
3rd - Ryukenshen
80 - Originality 80 - Character Design and Development 85 - Entertainment Value 90 - Technical Composition 90 - Artistic Composition and Description
As the first chapter in a longer story, I am limited in how I can review this work but I can only base this review on what I am given so please bear that in mind. This story was well written and seemes to be leading into an extremely interesting one. I liked it very much and thought that it was both compelling and well paced. The characters were intersting and seemed to carry the plot along well. The words were chosen carefully and used effectively. There were only a few grammatical problems which did not detract from the whole and were mostly inconsequential. There were a lot of details included in this story and the author had obviously taken the time to work out a system of organization behind the operations of his world as well as the psychologies of the people in it. It seemed believable and credible. I thought that some of the story was perhaps a little bland though I do understand that this is only a chapter of a larger work. I would very much like to read more of this and see where things go. Overall, I was impressed and intrigued.
Overall Score: 85
2nd - Moonews
90 - Originality 85 - Character Design and Development 80 - Entertainment Value 80 - Technical Composition 95 - Artistic Composition and Description
This was a very fun story to read. I would have scored it higher if there was more to it but as it is, this is a very nice piece. The plot was good though a little simplistic. It moved very quickly (too quickly perhaps) and I felt myself a little sad when it was over. I wanted more... a lot more... perhaps an entire novel... which means that I really enjoyed reading this one and would have gladly kept reading had it continued on longer. I felt that the ending was a little rushed and a little harsh ( a la Frank Miller) but I appreciated the "Frankness" of it and the intent of the author to create a gritty story in a gritty world. The choices used in the composition of the prose were also very fitting for the style and helped to shape a tale crafted to express a grimy sentementality for an older era. I admit that I am a sucker for this kind of story. I can relate to this very easilly (escpecially with the Texas references) and I am quite fascinated with the "Pulp" style. This story oozes with elements that I truly adore yet havent managed to find the inspiration to apply in my own stories yet. Basically, I am a little jealous. Very good stuff. I want to read more of this... Badly.
Overall Score: 86
1st - Blob Dude
85 - Originality 85 - Character Design and Development 95 - Entertainment Value 80 - Technical Composition 95 - Artistic Composition and Description
I found this story to be quite entertaining and very well composed. The characters were clear if only slightly 2 dimensional but they moved the story along at an effective pace that held my interest. The author obviously took his time crafting the details of this narrative and the result was a story that I enjoyed very much even if some of the techical aspects were a little distracting (grammar mainly). I think this was a very original way of telling a story that could have seemed a little bit silly in the hands of a lesser writer. Fortunately, this is not the case as he is both talented and experienced enough to understand how to entertain. Overall, I felt gripped by this story throughout and remained intriguied until the very end. "The Three" was a nice surprise and I definitely look forward to reading more of this author's work.
Overall Score: 88
Well that wraps up our contest, congratulations to Blob Dude and a huge thanks to all the writers for their great efforts. The short stories will remain on the forum for a short while longer before being moved to a special sub-site for archiving these contests.
Thanks again, Cyrus & Xavier.
