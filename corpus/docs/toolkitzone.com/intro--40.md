# INTRO:

By:I_Cant_Beleive_Its_Me I hate to have to always see beautiful project ideas be thrown away for knewer ideas. The purpose in this short little document is to get your mind set to create a RPG using some tips that might be helpful for the people with growing ideas. Think of this as an excersise. You may have to sacrifice a couple of things, but its for the sake of your project. Plus its not like what your sacrifising will disappear of the face of the earth when your all good and done with your project. ------------------------------------------- During the creation of a RPG you want to make sure you dont get side-tracked. I found the easiest way to end up side-tracked to another project is playing other video games. Video games are the things that inspire us the most to make games. If your playing two or three different games at a time it will be really hard for you because your game ideas will switch between games. What I suggest is to limit it down to one game or no game at all. If your current project that you are all hyped about is created in the shadow of another game just limit it to that game only because the second you play a second game and get addicted, you've got a whole other project on your hands. -Console games can be the cause for your continues change in project ideas. Playing multiple console games can sometimes lead to 15 other game projects with nothing but a single news post. I suggest limiting your games played to one a day(which is the one that inspired your current project if one) or no games at all just so your addicted to your game only. ------------------------------------------- If you ever end up with free time on your hands and you might wanna start planning things for your RPG. Ofcourse by free time i mean by your away from your computer, you have free time during classes, basically when you have free time! Plan out in sections your boards, characters, programs, storyline ect. Surely planning out the future storyline of your RPG will give you good ideas of your games and just thinking about the exciting events to come in your game will surely have you eager to share your ideas with everybody else to enjoy in a RPG. -during free time, if your not doing anything else, you can plan future events and creations of your game. Surely it will have you excited and rearing to work on your project. ------------------------------------------- Planning your game is one thing but when you get around to testing it you relize the hard work that you put into your project. Once you relize that, you know how dedicated you really are to working on your project which also leads to you working on your project more and more. Problems with your codes can sometimes be a problem and may eventually leave you fed up but thats what the code forum is for. Post your problems there and they're sure to be replies answering your question so you can keep moving forward. --Testing your game is also another way to get hyped about the future of your project. The more you test, the more you get attached to your game. --Flaws can sometimes leave you to a stop, but there are always people willing to help at the forums. ------------------------------------------- One sure fire way to get your mind set to work on your project is to gather a crowd. Very good way to attrack crowds is: - Every now and then, a competition between toolkit games will be held for awards. Every summer and winter is one of the best ways to show of your games by competing in the RPG Toolkit expos, led by In-Dream Studios. Who knows, if your lucky you may just when recognition for your game like a trophy to be placed in your games profile. - New ideas are posted everyday surely to attract a crowd in the project disscussion forums located in toolkitzones very own forums. People are always there to check out whats new in the games section and there to point out anything that may need to be fixed up a bit before its good to go. - The neighboring toolkitzone site known for its wide variety of rescources can be found at http://mysticsoftware.net. This is also a great place to get a game hosted as well. - The hotpicks section located in the left panel in the games section is there to advertise games, but the ones that have the popular votes. This is a great way to attract people just strolling along the games section. This should help attract more people to bookmark your game to check on its progress every now and then. Who knows, you may even end up with some fans! -By showing your game arround you may pick up a few followers along the way. This will surely make you want to work knowing that you have people waiting for your game to be released. ------------------------------------------- Enough of hearing my ways, lets ask the guys with the demos out. Khin, Mrb and Talyn are all devoted game designers for the toolkit and took time to answer my question I had for them...What makes them work so hard on their projects? What makes me work so hard on my project? Many, many different reasons... I could literally write a 500 word essay on it easily, hehe. But I guess the best answer to the question that would relate to your article would be: I work hard on my project because I love the game. I've taken everything I enjoyed about different RPGs I've played, from Final Fantasy to Morrowind, and combined them into basically my dream game. I think you should create a game for yourself around a concept you love. No one's seriously trying to make a profit off their game, it's just a hobby - and so your first priority should be to have fun making the game. And with that, you'll find it automatically easier to work and keep working on your game. I work so hard because I truly believe that what I'm doing is going to become the next big thing. It's also really satisfying when you play back your game and think "Yeah, I did that". You have to have a passion for what you're doing, and my passion lies firmly with making video games. Hope that helps! hmm... because it's fun. Now, hopefully, you kept my teachings in mind and started working on your project. Just remember a project is your creation, your imagination basically. Filling your mind up with different project ideas will, ofcourse, lead to other project creations thus leading to no work at all. I look forward to seeing demos of all your games! **Sticking with your Project**
