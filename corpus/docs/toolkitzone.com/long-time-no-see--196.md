# Long time, no see...

By Xavier
Well, it's been near four months since the last news item which is something of an oddity. It seems the community in general is in a slight consolidation phase where people are getting to grips with the now much preferred TK3 and leaving the memories, and projects, of TK2 behind. As a result, game production, in terms of actual releases, has dropped off but there is a new level of quality being seen in games currently under development. One project that particularly caught my eye is by 7LegionArmy, and it's indicative of the new level that games are being taken to by many of our new users. Speaking of the Toolkit and games, make sure you had down to the at the next available opportunity and support our fellow community members by checking out their games. I know many of you, if not all active users of the software, are very eager to get the latest on the next release of the Toolkit and feel somewhat clueless as to what's going on. Well, not to worry because during the winter holiday I'm going to write a comprehensive article covering what's been done, what's going to be done, and also get some thoughts from the two developers who devote their time to working on our software; Colin and Delano. As for the site, there's a slight backlog of resources that I'll wade through over the weekend. I've just finished by first term of my final University year, which was laden with coursework recently, and so only now am I finding the time to spend at TKZ more. I think I've only been able to post a handful of times in recent weeks! I have to admit another reason for my quiet nature is because what time I do have for the community I've spent working on a side project that will hopefully come to fruition in the early months of next year, I'll reveal more closer to the time when I know for sure if lazy ass has a chance of completing it :). I should also say a thank you to all the uber members who stayed with us as donators for a second year, as well as newcomer Doc_Wellsy. You guys take all the financial stress off my shoulders of keeping this site up and now I'm even looking for a better server, especially in terms of reliability and speed, since we have a little more breathing room in budget. Thanks for all your patience regarding the Toolkit and site news, look out for not only the TK3 article but also a look back at the year, run-down of the expo results and a look ahead at the most promising games for 2006.
