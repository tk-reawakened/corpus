# Magic Roundabout

By Xavier
There's been a few changes behind the scenes recently at the site. As some of you may know, I asked for a volunteer to help moderate the game section itself and the screenshots board. Also, Asimir resigned from his position of code manager for the resources section, so that vacancy was also put up for grabs on the forums.
So, without delaying any further we are pleased to announce two well known community and IDS members in Oblivious_Casualty and Mike to the staff.
>
> **Oblivious_Casualty** *(Game Section Moderator)*
>
> OBCT is the developer behind the promising game Bounty. His duties will be keeping the content of the game section in shape as well as working on any additional material he sees fits such as FAQs and getting in touch with me on improvements for the section itself. He will also be granted moderator powers on the screenshots and sprites boards.
>
> **Mike** *(Code Manager)*
>
> Also part of the IDS team, who run the popular Expo, Mike will be now reviewing and managing any RPG Code resources sent in to the site. He will also be granted global moderator powers for the entire forums.
>
Both users will be fully upgraded to their new status within a few days.
I'd also like to take this opportunity to thank Asimir for the past six months of staff work he has put in. He has proved invaluable as a global moderator and will now continue in that capacity.
It is likely in the near future a Graphics Manager will also be appointed. If you're interested in that position, shoot me a PM stating so.
