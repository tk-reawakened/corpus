# Marz's New Years Resolution

*The Toolkitzone Broadcasting System would like to apologize, but our regularly scheduled program (Freddy Ball Z) will not be played today. Instead, the TBS would like to introduce to you our annual presentation, "Marz's New Years Resolution". Please don't change the channel, as we'll be right back.*
**Marz's New Years Resolution**
Well, it's almost that time of year. You know, the end? At this point in time, some of us look back to see what we've accomplished, to reminisce in fun memories, and to just plain remember the good ol' summer. Well, while those people do that, Marz is busy trying to figure out what he's going to accomplish in the upcoming year. Having failed miserabley at New Years resolutions of 2004, Marz is setting his goals even higher for 2005. "There's no where to go but up" Says Marz, who has had a pitiful year as far as doing useful things goes. So here are Marz's new years resolutions for 2005. You can use them as well as your own goals, cause lord knows Marz isn't going to get them done.
Resolution #1: Get Girl, Get Laid
As surprising as it may be, the all-sexy Marz does not currently have a girlfriend. And, for a bigger shock, Marz is still a virgin. But that is all about to change. No longer will Marz have to fantasize about the skanky Nurse Ersuis, or download porn from "The EB Gaymer" and Moonews to satisfy himself. No, this year Marz will get a girlfriend, and he will go far. Farther than any Marz has gone before. You better believe it, this is gonna be Marz's break out year.
Steps to be taken: 1) Shower Regularly 2) Wear Cologne 3) Turn on the "Marz Charm" 4) Refrain from frequent Masturbation 5) Cancell subscription to Vampzino's GDP Monthly
Resolution #2: Code the Custom Systems for Marz's Game
Yes, my game has been in production since 1998. No, I don't have much to show for it. Yes, I am taking strides in the right direction. Yes, I do finally have an artist. Yes, I will be in the next TK Expo. No, I will not be releasing a demo. And yes, my game will be the "uber-bestest-greatest-RPG ever made".
Steps to be taken: 1) Get off my lazy ass and do something 2) Get motivation from getting laid (see above)
Resoultion #3: Get Better Grades
If this isn't at the top of everyone's new years resolutions this year, then those who don't have it as a resolution are obviously nerds. Grades, alas, are not Marz's friend. They used to be, being very leniant and allowing him not to study to get grades. But now, the work has gotten harder and Marz is shocked to find he actually needs to do work. Work!? Who the hell does work?!
Steps to be taken: 1) Listen to tutor 2) Do more homework 3) Get motivation from getting laid (see above)
Resolution #4: Quit McDonalds
Marz has been working at McDonalds for too long now. The clown is getting on Marz's nerves, and the hamburgler just isn't as entertaining to catch anymore as he used to be. By the end of 2005, Marz will no longer be a McDonald's manager. No, he will be something much greater. Marz is thinking big folks. Perhaps a bar tender when he becomes of legal age? Or professional man-whore? Time will tell where Marz's career path will take a turn next.
Steps to be taken: 1) Grow some balls 2) Make fun of store 3) Exclaim boss is lazy and does no work 4) Get motivation from getting laid (see above)
And so, Marz has yet again set up resolutions that obviously cannot be reached by himself. Setting yourself up for failure is considered unhealthy by many, but Marz has been doing it for so long it would be unhealthy if he didn't do it. Marz urges you to come up with your very own New Years Resolutions and stick to them, so that when you fail at least you know you tried - making it even worse.
