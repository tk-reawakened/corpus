Pixel Art Competition - July Edition
The second Pixel Art Competition was held late July, 2005. Due to the success of the June edition, members requested for yet another competition. This month's edition promises firmer rules and more polished voting system. Here is the compilation of the activities in the said event:
Welcome to the (late) opening of July Edition's Pixel Art Competition. Last months contest proved to be fun, and educational too. No doubt every participant have enjoyed it, and learned from it. We continue the trend with this month's competition. It may be too late for July, but it doesn't matter. July and August competitions may overlap anyway.
Guidelines
This month's competition, let's take our hands at creating our own figures. Last month you were given a shot at how to dress up a template, but this time around, you get to draw them from scratch. Don't let your fear of anatomy get to you To narrow it down, let's create chibis. Yes, chibi. Lately I've noticed the growing popularity of chibi art around here, especially based on the fightclub, so the challenge is this: create a chibi representation of any TKZ/Fightclub character.

The voting system was changed to accommodate the new rules.
Voting Rules
- All TKZ members are invited to cast their votes
- Each voter shall be given six(6) points. He/she has the prerogative of distributing those points among the entries.
- The first three entries with the highest scores/points shall be the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd respectively
- In case of a tie, subsequent rounds shall be made to break such ties.
