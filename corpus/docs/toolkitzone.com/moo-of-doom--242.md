# Moo Of Doom

Last time, on Freddy Ball Z... Freddy took on R# in his first ever gym battle. After frying R#'s first android TK-Mon with Seven Eternal's electricity attacks, 7E was quickly knocked out by an immoral and heartless spam attack by the Word2 android. Freddy quickly recovered with his Spydie TK-Mon, and was able to get his first badge, the Raccoon badge! Now after a long vacation of being lost in the Bermuda Triangle, Freddy finds himself back in Mystic City ready to continue his adventure! Chapter 7: Professor Randy Checks In Seven Eternal walked alongside Freddy on the Mystic City streets, as Freddy held up the Raccoon badge in front of him. "Look at that, isn't that the most beautiful badge you've ever seen!" Seven Eternal sighed and nodded. It had been hearing about the badge for over 3 hours now as Freddy triumphed around the streets of Mystic City explaining to all those who were willing to listen how he defeated the cruel gym leader R#. Then, all of a sudden, Freddy heard a loud beeping noise coming from right behind him. "What was that?!" Freddy quickly jumped a 180 and looked behind him. The beeping noise continued, again, right behind him. "What the!" Freddy did another 180. Seven Eternal finally pointed out to him that the beeping was coming from his backpack, which was ideally "right behind him." Blushing, Freddy took off his knapsack and took out the beeping device. It was DEXTER, the device he had stolen from Professor Randy's lab. He opened it up, and low and behold there was Professor Randy holding a video conference with him. "So it was you who stole my precious DEXTER!" Professor Randy yelled at Freddy, who quickly put on the innocent look. "Uh - uh... no, no of course not Professor. Uh... Seven Eternal here, yeah, he had it with him and I had to take it away from him and told him it wasn't a toy." Seven Eternal resented that statement and quickly zapped Freddy. "Mmhmm, Seven Eternal took it eh? Do you really believe me to be that stupid" "Well... you know... after watching you ride that freakish creature around I..." "Oh shut up Freddy. It was going to be yours anyway. Now, the reason I'm calling you is..." "Wait Professor! Look!" Freddy quickly pulled out his Raccoon badge. v "See, I beat a gym leader! I bet you Dont_Think_So is off somewhere partying and I'm on my way to becoming the greatest TK-Master ever!" "Actually Freddy, Dont_Think_So is miles ahead of you. He's caught 14 TK-Mon and already has two badges." "What?! You're lying!" "Oh no Freddy, quite a bit happened while you were lost in the Bermuda Triangle for those many months. Which is why I'm calling you." "You have a job for me?" "Yes indeed. I want you and Dont_Think_So to begin a quest to capture all the TK-Mon in TK Land." "Oh, of course Professor Randy, I was planning-" "And, I'm going to need you to pick up a parcel for me." "Sure thing professor, where is it?" "It's in one of the stores in the OT-Republic" "What?! You mean... you want me to go there?!" Freddy was scarred. Very few people went to the OTR anymore. After Dictator Vampzino cut off their clean water supply and electricity, and basically excommunicated the republic altogether, people all assumed the republic was dead. "It's the least you could do, after stealing DEXTER. Anyway, I have to go now, so make sure I get that parcel soon!" *Click* And so, Freddy had two new quests to add to his becoming a TK-Master. And he had to complete the parcel quest soon, before Professor Randy gets madder at him!
