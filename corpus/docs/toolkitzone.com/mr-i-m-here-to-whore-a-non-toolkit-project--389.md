# Mr."I'm Here to Whore a Non-Toolkit Project"

Browse
They All Came Crawling Back
Some things are as sure as anything in the world of the Toolkit; Seafroggys will always plug his game in the most inappropiate topic he can find, Marz will get stabbed at least once a day and every month an oldbie will come crawling back to the community. Today it's all about the latter of those certainties, the phenomonan that nothing short of death or a sex-change can prevent a user coming back with cap in hand and an ego driven "do you remember me?". I'm one of these pathetic creatures. I left in April 2002, closing down Toolkit Zone and doing a runner. "Onto a better life!" I thought. I got the better life, I entered University, had a lot of fun, and yet 18 months later I felt the inevitable draw back in to the murky waters of the RPG Toolkit. You know, Al Pacino wasn't talking about the mafia when he said "Just when I think I'm out, they pull me back in"... he was also an avid TK user back in the early day. Defeated by life, searching for the one place where they know their standing, knowing that even people they hardly knew "back then" will congratulate their return in the greatest reverence because if they are seen to know an oldbie, it must make them an oldbie too!
So who came crawling back this year, even if just for a quick hello? I've picked three so it's by no means comprehensive. If you remember some crawl backs from 2008, why not share it with us in the comments.

White Divine
**Mr."I'm Here to Whore a Non-Toolkit Project"**
It's not the most notable comeback this year since he also appeared for a few months in 2007. However, he did have an extended stay away for over two years from 2005 to 2007. Mostly seems to disappear in the winter and spring months, must be some kind of delayed hibernation. Maybe he's a squirral. A user of true years gone by with his game Never Ending Scroll, but these days he can be seen whoring off his latest off-topic projects to our community every so often. Abusing his legacy here to get feedback - if anything, a worthy reason to return other than just sheer "boredom".
