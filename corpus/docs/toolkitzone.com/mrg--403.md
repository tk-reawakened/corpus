# MrG

Browse
Hall of Fame
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1265.png)
I'm running a day behind everything, which I'll rectify with a double publishing of articles tomorrow. For now, it's our second Hall of Fame inductee this week, after CBM became our first ever member last weekend. So who's in this week? He's arguably the only former member to go down in the memories of so many members without a single negative thought attributed to him. A league above most of us in both intellect and maturity, he was always the daddy of the community guiding us mere wild kids in the right direction. Famous for his wit, he managed to make coding cool and fun... an image that has been hard to re-establish since he left.
**MrG**
When I first arrived in 1999, MrG and Triuken were the forum kingpins, passing out knowledge to the masses. I quickly snapped them up to work with me on TKZ and it was an absolute joy to get to know them. Over the months the plugins, custom battle systems, tutorials and competitions flowed. When TKZ closed in 2002, MrG took the code resources he had collected and created the famous Code Shack. An oddball, purple, rabbit-infested website that brought out his quirky personality, something previously restricted under the uniform TKZ system.
After MrG left I was able to keep in touch with him occasionally, even twisting his arm to judge the second short story contest. He had turned his talents to teaching English and, at the time, was even running a bookshop. As with CBM, I will have to use my super secret underworld contacts and get in touch with the man.
Anyway, a deserved second entry into the hall I think!
