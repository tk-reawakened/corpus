# Music Competition

By Doc_Wellsy
There's been a drop in the number of submissions to the sound resource section recently, so, in order to add at least one new and interesting music pack, I have organised a little competition for the more musical amongst you. The task is simple, you have to compose a midi track. At the end of the competition, all the entries will be compiled into a pack which will be uploaded to the music section for the community to use. There are naturally a few simple rules, these are as follows: Please e-mail your entries to: (redacted: email), you can discuss the competition .
- You must create a music track for an RPG- this can be pretty much anything, from an opening title track, to battle music or menu music. The RPG doesnt have to be an existing one, or even one you are working on- just that type of music.
- There is no minimum or maximum length of track, however I would advise something between 1 and 3 minutes.
- There is no minimum or maximum number or instruments or tracks you can use, however, I would advise that you use more than one, and not too many
- The track must be in midi (.mid) format. This is mainly due to file size, but also it makes things a little fairer for everyone if theyre all the same.
- Any music submitted must be entirely original. You may work on it in a group if you see fit, but you must not re-mix or simply alter an existing midi track.
- You can submit any original piece of music- this can be a piece you intend to put into your game or just a piece you have created for fun or for this competition- but you shouldnt use a piece that you have put in a game which you have already released  we want to keep these tracks new and exciting please people ;)
- You may use any program you like to create the track  if you dont already have a suitable program, Anvil Studio is free and can be downloaded here (http://www.anvilstudio.com/)
- You may submit more than one entry if you like, up to a maximum of 3 entries- but each entry will be judged on its own merits. Quality, rather than quantity is whats required.
- Finally, the deadline for entries is Friday 3rd March 06.
