# Music Competition - Results

By Doc_Wellsy
Well done to all who entered, I hope you enjoyed making these and I'm sure anyone who downloads and listens to them will, and a big thank you for contributing to the resource section. We hope to hold similar competitions in the future, so watch this space over the course of the year.
This news piece if a little late, but better than never...right? As many of you will already know from the news item, we held a music competition to help boost entries to the site. We received a reasonably healthy 14 entries of original compositions, which you can now download . As part of the competition all entries were given a short review, which can also be found in the download also. [previous](http://www.toolkitzone.com/library.php?mode=read&doc=130) [here](http://www.toolkitzone.com/fileman.php?id=3&file=1256)
Honourable Mentions
>
> **Ballroom Waltz (João Nunes)**
>
> A very quiet little piano number here - hard to really describe - some bits sounds like there might be a piece of excellent music in there trying to get out, but then other bits seem to dash those hopes again. I can imagine a large mansion setting to this piece, which flows along smoothly enough.
>
> **Drama (Anatomy)**
>
> Not the title I would have chosen to describe this piece - it reminds me of an observatory looking at the stars or something. Nice tune which provides a thoughful background as well as a little light relief.
>
> **Emotional (Caliber Mengsk)**
>
> A tune for on of those moments where you end up sat on a rock talking to the main female character in the game about what the hell just happened. Slow and sorrowfull (in an intentional way), but not depressing, with a very basic harmony about half way through just to make you cry at the sad news you may just have been given. A fitting piece.
>
> **Fight 1 (Caliber Mengsk)**
>
> A battle midi here which reminded me strangely of James Bond. Good most of the way through, but a little lacking at the end. It does loop round pretty well which is always good in a battle midi though. Tempo is nice and fast and the instruments chosen fit a battle scene quite nicely.
>
> **First Midi (Kevin Chau)**
>
> Not quite sure what this midi is supposed to accompany, but the melody wanders around a little aimlessly, with the odd note which doesn't quite fit. The melody and harmony go together ok, but it doesnt flow as well as it might. Not bad, but could do with a little extra attention in places.
>
> **FFIII Remake Theme (Shayne Valencia)**
>
> Not really a supprise this didn't actually get put in a FF game - only one instrument with no harmony. Also, the pitch keep changing like its on acid. It doesn't make your ears hurt too much, but it doesn't make any musical sense either.
>
> **FFIII CS Town Theme (Shayne Valencia)**
>
> Much better than the Theme remake, but still lacking a harmony. The 'twiddly' bits at the beginning and at various points throughout are strangely compelling. I don't love it, but at the same time it would be hard to hate this one...
>
> **Magic Shop (Perry Swain)**
>
> Typical toe-tappingly happy shop music here. And it sounds like a great shop - really makes you picture all the magical instruments ticking away. Superb melody which keeps you listening all the way through - sounds a little like an old 'Dizzy' game for those that remember. Not sure about the ending, but superb stuff.
>
> **Midi 2 (Kevin Chau)**
>
> A dissapointing lack of any sort of harmnony until half way through, then a bit of a feeble effort. The tune is lacklustre and somewhat doleful. Could fit somewhere at the beginning of a game as the initial story is told - but it wouldn't really inspire much.
>
> **Pirates Theme (DwD)**
>
> Another entry from DarkwinterDreams, again in a tropical style - this time set on the open seas. It does convey a strong sense of the wind whistling through your hair, as if you were on a real pirate ship. Well maybe not quite, but it does set the scene. The melody does start to drag, then all of a sudden just about 2 minutes into the track another interesting harmony comes in which is great. Good stuff.
>
> **Raging Battle (Ultima Wizard)**
>
> A good battle midi - nice drums and nice tempo to get the adrenaline pumping. The melody sometimes fells as though its lacking in direction - its not catchy enough to tap along to, but some solid stuff here all the same. Considering its a short battle midi, it doesn't lend itself to looping very well, but is fitting for a fight scene.
>
> **Runaway - Let the Fire Begin (Shayne Valencia)**
>
> I very RPG-esque midi, which I guess has to be a good thing. All the instruments suddenly stop except the horn about half way through which seems to disrupt the flow a little, and the melody get a little dreary after a while - makes it feel as though it might be raining :P Not too shabby.
>
> **Templatic Misery 2 (RDX)**
>
> This song really makes you want to slit your wrists - but in a good way. Its so dark and brooding it feels just as the title suggests - like a temple of misery. The melody will haunt you for days, although it is a little basic - nothing changes after the first few bars melody-wise for the duration, but theres some great extra parts from other instruments.
>
> **Tropical Town Theme (DwD)**
>
> This song is written in the typical carribean swing style, with some lovely pan-pipes kicking in half way through if you couldn't already picture the coconuts. A little flatter and less lively than you might expect from a tropical town, but some good piraty stuff all the same.
>
Runner-Ups
>
> **Templatic Misery 2 (RdemusX)**
>
> A great entry - very moving. It does lack perhaps a little variation, and it would be nice if it were a touch longer, but it gave me nightmares which is a very good thing.
>
> **Pirates Theme (DarkwinterDreams)**
>
> Another excellent entry - a little repetitive in places, but you can alomst taste the salty air when you listen to it. Excellent.
>
Winner
>
> **Magic Shop (Perry Swain)**
>
> This entry contained some excellently toe-tapping beats, with a nice mix of instruments at all the appropriate places, and would really lend itself to being in a magic shop as its title suggests. My only complaints would be that it maybe takes a little too long to really get going (about 43 seconds to be precise), and the ending could use some work, but its very magical indeed.
