Library Launched!
The official site finally gets the much promised and talked about community frontpage and an entire new library system online. Along with it, five weekly columns by community members will be featured.
Well, after months of saying I'd actually do it and several false starts - the new frontpage and library system are finally here! A bloody relief. The whole new library section, totally re-coded, is now available via the menu button and the coding, graphics and sounds sections are now within the resources button. The ability to submit documents, whether it is a column, news item or single-page article, are only available to the official writers who have already been chosen. Each of these writers have a weekly column running on different days covering various aspects of the Toolkit and community - look out for them as featured pieces on the frontpage. Colin (aka KSNiloc) will be writing columns on Monday about rpg code and the development of the Toolkit. Tuesday belongs to R043482 and his Meridian 59 column. On Wednesday you'll have Khin talking about Heraldy as well as other things and Thursday is the obscure sounding "The Adventures of Porno Fred", an on-going story by Marz. And last not be least, I will be writing a Friday column looking back at some of the famous names from Toolkit history. Over the coming weeks and months I will begin to move over all the old documents to the new library format as well as any code, graphics and sound tutorials. By Christmas the backend should be fully up and running, so you guys will be able to compose and submit documents too. Enjoy, and I ask you to send in any suggestions, ideas and bug reports you have for the new library section on the forums.
