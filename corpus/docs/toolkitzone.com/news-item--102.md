3.0.5 Release
After months of anticipation, the nicely timed release of 3.0.5 is here to add that extra sparkle to your Christmas day. Along with the new object-orientated capabilities and revamped movement system, there is a host of fixes, improvements and additions to occupy several releases! Contributors for this release are Colin, Delano, Faero, Occasionally_Correct and Shao.
I am pleased to announce the long awaited TK3 release, packed with bug fixes, improvements and a bunch of new and exciting features. And to top it all off, it's bundled into a new and much more professional setup program. So what can you expect from this release? Delano put together a more complete list , but here are of some of the more anticipated aspects; The impact of the above list alone would ensure a new release for any software, but over the last four months an almighty number of changes have been made. The word stability has been thrown around a lot in previous times, but from my perspective it is never more deserving than now (don't expect the disaster that was the initial 3.0.4 release). A huge thanks to Colin and Delano who did the clear majority of work with this release, without them the Toolkit would be no where near its current state. Also thanks should go to Occasionally Correct for his great new help files, and Shao and Faero for their own small, yet nontheless impressive, input into the release. I should also thank the following users, among others, who helped test this release; Dont_Think_So, Khin, Serge, Woozy, and akura_thedragonblader. I won't delay any further, you can get the brand new Toolkit release by clicking . Have a great Christmas guys, happy game making! [here](http://www.toolkitzone.com/library.php?mode=read&doc=100)
- All audio issues such as MIDIs and WAVs not working in Windows 95/98 and modular music not working have been resolved.
- Completely re-written movement system, which includes the ability to set individual speeds of items and characters.
- New object-orientated system with full functionality, further increasing the power of RPG Code.
