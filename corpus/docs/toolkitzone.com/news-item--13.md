Developers get together to crush bugs in 3.0.4.
On the back of the release, the dev team have set about fixing up any bugs that have been reported by the community. As a result, we're pleased to say a patch will be released sometime today or early tomorrow for those of you who have already downloaded the Toolkit. Users yet to download will have the full TK3 installation updated too. Fixes include support for gif images, and various bugs in the tileset and animation tools. If you find any bugs while using 3.0.4, click and report them. We are also keeping an up-to-date log of the latest fixes that will be definately in the patch .
