frontpage thingy
Frontpage ideas and initiatives back to the fore.
Yes, yes - I know. I totally abandoned the frontpage idea and left a few e-mails hanging, but I've been exploring the idea again this week for no particular reason. I've come to the conclusion that I want to first, before I accept public submissions, a set weekly rotation. - let me explain. Each day there is a "featured piece" that will get a whole lot of space on the front page, alongside the non-featured. This feature will written weekly by a specific user, for example - every Monday one of the TK3 Devers will have a column talking about TK3s progress, future, issues etc, every Tuesday there will be a game review and so forth. Around those established pieces will be user submitted articles which will get onto the frontpage but in a less prominent way. The likelyhood is if any users impress me with their writing I will promote them to an official writer and find them a weekly "slot", rank image and fame galore. Other ideas for the frontpage is more coverage to demo releases to encourage feedback and downloading, instead of just listing them maybe go with a little image and description (ala GameSpot), and a few other tidbits. So - what does all this mean? Nothing right now, since I come up with about 10 ideas a week for this site. Just thought I'd let you in on the possible outcome of the initial frontpage ideas.
