The Dragoon
The long awaited demo from MrB arrived in the late summer and, even with ripped graphics, is a treat for the eyes. You start the game as Ervin, an officer, on a quest to find a Dragoon in the Tivian Caves with your friend.
| One of the more impressive and professional demo releases to come out of the community for some time, and that's with ripped graphics taken into account. Shame MrB has left the community, but there still may be a chance this game will see it through to the end. You are Ervin, following your late father's footsteps as you climb through the ranks of the Davian Force. You start the game on an important mission with your life-long companion, Jacobs, to find and rescue a Dragoon in the Tivian Caves - and so the adventure begins. As mentioned, the game uses ripped graphics but the boards are designed with the upmost care and intelligence, often lacking in games with such tilesets, and as a result compensate for the now familiar tiles.
