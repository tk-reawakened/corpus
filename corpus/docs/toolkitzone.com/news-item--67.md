Justice Lost
A tale of a normal man with dreams of being a hero, to one day slay some evil force and stand tall among his people.
| It's an Expo timed released for Akura_TheDragonBlader and his tale of dreams and heroes. The demo is a little short-lived, but as described by the creator it's merely a project to help him learn more about the Toolkit. Although the game uses familiar default and ripped tiles, you certainly see some often overlooked features in relation to role-playing games. The game opens with a tutorial to teach you the basics, and is weaved nicely into the game with some fun banter. Within this mini-tutorial you can choose how to react to certain possibilities. Simple idea, but is one of those little extras that help set apart games from the rest of the pack. Unfortunately the game crashed after a short while into the tutorial, and beware of selecting "configuration" on the menu as it exits the game for some reason Even so, a promising start with some nice extras.
