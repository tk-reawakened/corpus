Forever Tides
An unlikely alliance between an outcast sorcerer, a war veteran and a young priestess forms the basis of this impressive demo from Avathar.
| From Avathar comes this well put together story of an outcast sorcerer who joins forces with a war veteran and a priestess to fight evil and embark on an adventure to stop a slowly unfolding conspiracy. From the introduction sequence, which uses a simple but effective technique of zooming in on the sunset menu background and then merging it into a campsite fire to go to the next cut scene and then onto a flashback, you know and appreciate the fact that a lot of time and effort has been put into the project. Although the game uses a mixture of ripped tilesets, the character graphics are original and well-designed. You get the impression of them actually being human, as opposed to the usual midget style characters you get in most games. A very promising project, I recommend you try it out as soon as you can. | [GAME PROFILE](games.php?game=681) [DOWNLOAD](games.php?view=file&mode=comments&game=681&file=70)
