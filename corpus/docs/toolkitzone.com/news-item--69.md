Winter Expo
The two week long game showcase is here once more for its Winter edition. Check it out for the latest news, images and downloads from the top community projects.
Just informing those of you still unaware that the Winter Expo is now open for business. Check out the site for a huge array of screenshots, demo releases, and information of a long list of game projects from around the community. It's a great chance to help support your fellow developers and provide feedback and encouragement on their work. At the end of the two weeks, awards will be given to the best projects in fields such as originality and graphics. You can find the official Expo site by clicking and the Expo showcase forums .
