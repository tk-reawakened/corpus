TK Land
The beautifully crafted graphics demo for the community based adventure featuring Spyder, EB, Nick and various landmarks such as the old RPG Code Shack.
| The long awaited demo, albeit just a graphics release, was destined to cause jaws to drop with the likes of Indy and Woozy working behind it - and it doesn't fail. It's a community based game, with Spyder in the lead role and backed up by the familiar figures of Xorlak, EB and others. Due to the fact it takes so long to create a quality game, it is a little out-dated in places with community members a lot of you might not know and also misses some you do such as Asimir and Colin. But even so, it's still got plenty of current members to keep you interested. By graphics release, we mean there are no battles, or quests and the like - just boards to wander around, but that is easily enough. The beautifully crafted characters represent their community personas remarkably and when you explore the nearby town the various websites are re-created as bars, shops and hotels to great effect. Needless to say, try this demo now! Not only is this looking like the first ever community game to be finished, but has a good chance to become one of the best ever. | [GAME SITE](http://www.thanian-nation.com/tkland/) [DOWNLOAD](http://thanian-nation.com/expo/viewtopic.php?t=161)
