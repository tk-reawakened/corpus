Future Stuff
Now the task of the new library and frontpage is pretty much complete, details of a new Toolkit section are being drawn up. Also includes info on public submissions to the library.
The blogs, games and llibrary sections have all been renovated over the last 3 months to great effect, leaving one last area still looking a little sparse and lonely - Toolkit. So what can you expect? I'm looking to expand it into more of a development zone for TK3 as well as supporting older versions. Online histories, dev team biogs, bug tracker, wishlist, periodic builds, news, technical articles and so forth. I have also inked Delano to head up the new section and help keep it ticking over, which is a great bonus. Just like the library/frontpage before - I have no idea when it'll come. I'll be suprised if it's before Christmas or the 3.0.5 release (coming very soon!). Time will tell. As for those of you interested in contributing to the library, I hope to have that ready to go before Christmas. However, unlike resources, we will be taking a much more tough stance on submissions. As opposed to accepting sometimes poor efforts and giving it a low score, all articles/tutorials will have to be of a good standard and a worthwhile addition.
