Waste
The deserts inhabited by desert tribes are under threat from the Government looking to take over the land, and it's your task to help protect these lands.
| Waste centers on a world which has been ravaged by a large change in the climate 250 years ago, and from the resultant wastelands warriors have prospered in such harsh times. It's up to you to help protect these lands and people from those looking to conquer it. Zyrshnikashnu's game has a unique look to it and a lot of time has been given to developing the plot and character classes which always help provide a solid base for a project to be built upon. It also sports some nice feature ideas in terms of the interface including a tips window and custom cursor. It's amazing how such a small detail can add an extra degree of professionalism to a game. There is also a day/night system in place. There are a few bugs littering the game, but nothing dramatic. However, beware that the custom cursor actually remains even after the game is closed.
