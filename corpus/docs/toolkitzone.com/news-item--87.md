Incredible Cock Fighter
Fight with the best of 'em around the world, including Russia, Mexico, Bolivia and India. A crazy slice of insane animal brutality - go play!
| It's a pretty crazy concept that is about as far away from a role-playing game as you can get, but that's credit to the Toolkits flexibility and the warped mind of TKGB. The premise is simple, you and your cock (quiet at the back!) fight against said animal opponent in a variety of locations and styles of play. Frankly, I love this game...mainly because it's so strange. A lot of attention has been paid to the little things that make it so unique, such as the ability to fight with themed backgrounds and music such as Russia and Japan. The humour behind it all means the graphics don't have to be relatively cutting edge, it's all part of the comedy (see South Park).
