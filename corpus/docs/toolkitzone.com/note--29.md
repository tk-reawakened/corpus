# Note

: Anyone who wants to learn the keyboard should probably own one to start with or have access to one. Ok, Here We Go... I will start with the basic notes, C through to C going up the octave (I will tell you about octaves later on). First I will show you the keyboard. It looks like this, . All the notes I am about to show you will be the white ones, I will show you the black ones later on. I will start with the C note. You should be able to see this on the image. The C Note is to the left of the D The D Note On the left of the E The E Note On the left of the F The F Note On the left of the G The G Note On the left of the A The A Note On the left of the B The B Note On the left of the C The C Note On the left of the D This Is Called An octave. Oct Meaning 8. So You Must Go Up 8 white notes in One Octave. Low C, D, E, F, G, A, B and up to the high C. An octave can be From A Low C To A High C or A Low D To A High D or A Low E to A High E etc with 8 white notes in. These are slightly different than the basic white notes. If you look at the lower C in this image, , Y ou can see a black note above it and to the right. This is C# or Db. This sign, '#', means sharp, and this sign 'b' means flat. The sharp is above and to the right of most notes. E.g. C# is above and to the right of C, (C is the base note of C#) The reason C# Is also Db is because it is above and to the left of D aswell as above and to the right of C. These are the black sharp and flat notes. C#/Db is between C and D D#/Eb is between D and E E#/Fb. Well, It doesn't exist. If you look at the image there is no black note between the notes of E and F. F#/Gb is between F and G G#/Ab is Between G and A A#/Bb is between A and B B#/Cb. This note also doesn't exist. Look at the image again. There is also no black note between the notes B and C That pretty much sums Up An octave and all the notes in it. Remember, an octave is low note to a high note that is the same as the starting note with 8 White notes inbetween and the 5 black notes making sure the start and finish notes are the same, e.g Start: C, End: C, Start: G#, End: G# In The Next lesson, I Will Show You The Chords And The Major And Minors Of Them.
