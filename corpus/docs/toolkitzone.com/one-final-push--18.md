# one final push...

By Xavier
Well, I have decided to consider doing one final new feature for the site before my holidays expire and I go to Uni with no 'net access where I live. I want to give this frontpage a more community orientated feeling like, say, has. I will be willing to code and integrate a new frontpage that supports articles and columns written by community members about games, the community - anything TK or TK community related. Now, I'm not going to make a fanfare out of this, all I will say is the following; E-mail me at xavier@toolkitzone.com if you consider yourself articulate and willing to contribute to the official Toolkit site on a reasonably regular basis. I'll discuss the idea further to those, if anyone, who e-mail. And if possible, contact me as soon as possible so I can get this thing done before I leave to University. If some people do make it into the new feature, you'll get the TKZ: Writer rank title but you won't be a staffy, this is more of a community initiative. Remember to check out the new poll as well.
