# Others Interviews

Blob Dude Interview
By MrB
MrB:
When did you join the TK Community?
Blob Dude:
About October '01 I think. Feels so long ago now! Yeah that must be right date, cuz I was 16 I remember when I found the TK.
MrB:
Hehe, I know how you feel on that one. You're best known for the Blob games... did you ever attempt or plan on creating games without this famous theme?
Blob Dude:
Nah, I was looking for a game making program specifically for creating a Blobs game.
MrB:
What exactly inspired you to create all the Blobs games?
Blob Dude:
Well, I had been drawing them as comic strips for 5 years previous to finding the TK and always wanted to make them into a game so I could walk in the world I had created.
MrB:
Ah, cool... if you had a professional team that you were head of, what would be the dream game you would create?
Blob Dude:
Id like to make my Blobs world an online game, MMORPG style. I might do it one day, if I ever bother to learn C++ or something.
MrB:
You've released three Blobs games now for the TK... do you have a favorite? If so, why?
Blob Dude:
Well.. Xmas Catastrophe is one I really like, although I like Annihilation as well... so I dunno really.
MrB:
The community is highly anticipating the next Blobs game, Blobs:Annihilation - what's unique about this game from the past Blobs games?
Blob Dude:
Well it's the biggest one yet, the last two games were mainly time fillers cuz Annihilation was taking so long to finish. Its nearing the end of completion. I can get 3 hours 30 minutes game play from Annihilation. Plus Annihilation has got plenty more places to see and minigames and the like that the tow other games didnt really have much of.
MrB:
Wow... so it sounds like this will be the biggest and best Blobs yet. How do you predict the community will react to such a big release?
Blob Dude:
Well I just hope that people will take time to download and play it all through! The way it's going though, it might be bit big for those people still on dial ups so I plan to mail CDs to people if they want it and ask me to.
MrB:
Wow, that's quite generous of you. Do you have an estimated file size for the game?
Blob Dude:
Not really, Im hoping to try reduce file size as much as possible once I've completed it. I have loads of crap in all the folders that has piled up over the years!
MrB:
Hehe yeah... now that TKZ is also releasing games right along with MS, there may be some competition between the two for exclusive releases between the two sites... do you plan on exclusively releasing Blobs:Annihilation to one of the sites, or will you release the game on both sites?
Blob Dude:
I don't want to cause divisions in the community, so I'll probably give it to all that want it.
MrB:
Sounds fair. Recently there have been quite a few new game releases in the community. Have you played any of the new games? If so, do you think these games have been raising, maintaining, or lowering the bar for quality in TK games?
Blob Dude:
I haven't actually played many TK games in a while, but I did get that Heraldry Freija demo to see what it was like, but it gave loads of errors so I couldnt really play that much, but it did look really good!
MrB:
Hehe... I agree. In my opinion, the rest of the community is similar to you when it comes to playing TK games - they just don't play many TK games. Do you agree? Explain your answer too please.
Blob Dude:
Yeah I don't think enough of us play the community games, unless it seems, they look or have 'professional' quality to them [like Heraldry Freija or Delano's game with the funky 3D/iso graphics], which is a shame really for those that can't make great graphics [like me ] but may have really good storylines and game play.
MrB:
I agree. To wrap this interview up... a few people in the community have said that the TKZ staff and especially Vampz have obtained too much control over the community. This expression could also have been the motivation for the recent hacking into the TKZ database. What is your opinion on all of this? Do you think Vampz and the TKZ staff are making too many decisions on their own?
Blob Dude:
I think it's a load of rubbish really, Vampz was put in charge as the official site and as far as I can see, he is just doing his job to keep the community going and happy and to keep the TK alive. If anything, others may even be just jealous of how popular TKZ has become since being the official site, but then again it was always popular way back when it was just another resource site.
MrB:
Sounds good. Well Blob Dude, thanks for the interview, and good luck with Blobs:Annihilation!
Blob Dude:
Thanky :D.
