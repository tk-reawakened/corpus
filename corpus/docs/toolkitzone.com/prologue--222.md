# Prologue

First I want to introduce this to you all. This is based off the Jewel of Destiny demo released with the tk 2.1 games. The original concept held by CBM is still intact. I feel I have embelished and improved the story to make it readable like a novel. It's still a work in progress and I hope to actually "complete" the story by making an end to it rather than stopping somewhere in the middle like the game does. Enjoy. (More to be released later....)
Prologue
The Jewel of Destiny
Chapter1
War drives man to desperation. Blood is spilled, tears are shed, and sadness embellished the lands. Long ago the men of Bushhole discovered and colonized a beautiful valley they called Hylanka. Peace reigned for ages. Wisemen counseled the kingdom, but soon war shattered their peaceful lives. Giants arose from their imprisonment in the underworld caves of Hylanka and cruelly destroyed the colonies. Death awaits the innocents in the new land if desperate measures are not taken. And so the people may yet die in their desperation.
Frap, a boy raised up with a dream in his head, was confident that he was the heir of the wisemen. No one had to tell him, he just could feel it. He is now 17 years of age, a young man. He had his own house that he inherited from his family that lived in the big city that was the capital of the Hylanka kingdom, small yet delightful.
Though he lived in a little community, Shal, it had a great importance. This town was the bearer of the great House of the Jewel. The House of the Jewel held a precious stone called the Jewel of Destiny. The wisemen back in the day of old had a great battle. The Giants demolished and devastated every living thing in the valley. The only solution for the wisemen was to forge a jewel with the very same powers Zeus used to lock the Titans away in their eternal imprisonment. They named it appropriately because it was their destiny to live on. It was their destiny to live in peace. The Jewel was stored in a secure house where everyone idolized it and protected it for years.
Frap felt he was its protector. He would come to remember his forefathers, the wisemen, even after every soul in the small town of Shal condemned him for his blasphemy in thinking he was a descendent of the wisemen. Frap would have dreams about the Jewel, and his destiny to keep it safe. The Jewel seemed to talk to him.
Frap had one friend though, Tano, a boy of very common background. Tano as well didnt believe in Fraps mumbling and ranting about how he was the protector. Tano stuck with him anyway because they had had very fun times together and been there for each other when stuff was rough. Tonight though was different. Frap had a very bad feeling when he went to bed.
Chapter 2
Frap was in the House of the Jewel late. He felt the compelling need to stay. The Jewel seemed to hang on to him like some kind of magical grappling hook. Suddenly all the lights went out in the sanctuary as no one was allowed up stairs in the actual room of the Jewel. Frap stood afraid of what was about to happen because he had been tense all night long. Suddenly a dark figure seemed to hit him in the head.
Frap woke up. It had all been a dream. His stomach was sick from what had happened but it was bright outside. The day was young. Frap looked out the window and the birds were singing. Nothing could have happened. The birds never lie do they? Frap quickly got dressed and ran out the front door. There stood Tano.
Tano! Its good that you are here. I had a strange dream just now and I have a strange feeling that something is wrong at the House of the Jewel.
Tano replies with a chuckle, Youre crazy. Something is always wrong with the Jewel; the Jewel said this, the Jewel, the Jewel. Tano let out a sigh, But Ill come for the ride.
Tano was in disbelief but Frap was really worried this time. He hadnt ever had a dream that realistic or that disturbing about the Jewel. He had to see for himself, if anything, to calm his nerves.
Tano and Frap then set off to the House of the Jewel which conviniantly was located right in front of Fraps house. It was a temple like building. In the front it had steps leading up to a wide grand door. Anyone could walk in. The sancuary was grand. Marble tile lined the floor and when looked from above formed a moral of the wisemen holding a dimond shaped green stone.
Once in the House of the Jewel, Frap suddenly remembered his dream and picked out the similarities in the sanctuary. He mumbled, Something is wrong here.
That same darkness and strange glow stood about in the room and the door to the room where the Jewel resided was open. Lets go upstairs, Frap said frantically worried about the Jewel. Quickly, Frap ran up the stairs as Tano followed closely behind.
Frap sorrowfully says to himself, Something is wrong.
Tano puts his hand on Fraps shoulder to give him some grip on the situation. Frap then pushed Tano aside and yells, Get out of the way!
 The Jewel! The Jewel is gone! THE JEWEL IS GONE! exalted Frap repeatedly.
Tano, half looking around, half worried for his friend, was hoping that it was just missed places or the Jewel of Destiny was just a myth put in peoples heads to make them feel safe. While looking around he found an odd shaped stone. What is this? he asked as he analyzed the key shaped figure.
Frap turned quickly to see what Tano was talking about and yells out, Its the Keystone! Frap ran to him quickly and picks it up.
Frap and Tano set there analyzing the stone and on occasion wondering if the stone could actually be The Jewel of Destiny and assuming that they could have gotten the legends mixed up. Maybe the Jewel and Stone were the same? After a bit of debate they dismiss the argument and start planning their trip to the castle of the King. The journey to find the Jewel of Destiny had started with two friends on their way to inform the King.
