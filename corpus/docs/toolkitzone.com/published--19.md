# Published

Browse
It's been 5 weeks since ?ber membership was launched and I'd like to publicly thank the following community members for helping me out and kindly donating - the community owe you guys a big thank you. So in alphabetical order... Asimir, Dragonmaster, Cyrus, Kyra, MrB, Spyder and Twin Keyblade. I should also thank Dan for his donation a few months earlier, way before ?ber membership was around. He actually gave money with no incentive, which was nice. In my last news item of the holidays, I'd like to mention I am still pushing for a new frontpage with more items of interest written by community members, but I've decided to give it a little more time as I decide not only how to go about it but who to take on-board. I guess this can be my running "spare time" project over the next week or two when I have nothing better to do.
