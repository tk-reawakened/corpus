# Published

Browse
As part of the new games section coming at the end of the month, we're in need of a new iconset. All site icons in the past have been done by me, Xavier, even though I have little artistic ability and now we're looking for someone who actually has talent! The important aspect of this is that you must be prepared to do a relatively large amount of work, as there's a lot of icons, in a relatively short timeframe (two to three weeks) and to do this all for free. Afterall, this is a site developed and moderated by volunteers.
You'll be doing a great service to the entire community, not just me, and maybe the chosen member can stay on as the official community artist or something else with an equally fancy rank and colour. And, of course, the user will get the chance to use, and give feedback on, the new section before it's made public.
Those who are genuinly interested and capable of putting in the work, please respond and I'll get in touch as soon as possible and we can talk more. I'll probably ask those interested to produce, say, two sample icons and from there we'll see who's most suited.
Thanks for reading guys, Xavier.
