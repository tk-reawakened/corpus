Just to let you know in advance that the site will be down for a reasonably long period, two days at the most but hopefully no more than one day, sometime next week. As I'm sure you would have experienced recently yourself, the servers have been very slow at times and occasionally the site goes offline. As a result, in the best interest of the community, we're moving servers in the next week or two and due to the lengthy task of moving a large database and file base, the site will have to be taken offline during the entire process. We cannot give you a specific date yet since it depends on when I can find time to dedicate to the move but it will not happen earlier than the 28th of November. I apologise for any inconvenience this downtime may cause and I will give you guys 24 hours notice before it actually happens. Thanks.
