Staff Wanted
It's been a while since we've had staff here at the Zone and there's a long line of talented community members that have helped us out down the years when it comes to reviewing user submitted files. The likes of Asimir, Indy and Doc Wellsy have all generously contributed their time in the past and done great jobs, and since the new site has finally arrived and settled down, I think it's time to get file reviewing rolling once more (especially since there's an extensive backlog, heh) and so we need some new faces.
Basically, I'm interested in people who have a passion for their particular area of expertise (audio/coding/graphics), enjoy giving feedback/help to other users, have at least a reasonable reputation in that area of expertise within the community and visit TKZ often. People who simply want the status need not apply and to that end let me remind you that being a staff member does not mean you're a forum moderator. The new staff members will be reviewing files submitted to the site, which involves writing descriptions, giving an appropiate rating and so on. You'll also be fielding reports for files in your category, working with me on related contests (e.g. Midi Music contest) and the new coding staffy will also be helping me get rid of all files that aren't TK3 compatible over the coming weeks.
So, how to apply? Just send a private message to me detailing which category you're interested in (audio/coding/graphics), why you're interested in becoming a staff member and possibly a link or example to illustrate your skills in that area. There's no deadline on this but hopefully I'll be able to get some great new staffies in by the end of the year.
Thanks in advance to all those who take the time to contact me!
Xavier.
