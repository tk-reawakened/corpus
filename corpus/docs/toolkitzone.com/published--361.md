Staff & Mod Changes
I'm happy to announce that I've finally got round to appointing two new staff members to help keep the site ticking over. Bagel will take charge of all graphic file submissions and Brad will be keeping the game section in order and contributing game related articles to the frontpage. Many thanks to the twenty or so applicants, I'm glad there are so many out there willing to help. The two new staff members have a lot of passion for what they do and I know they'll be a great asset to the site.
In less happy news, Spyder is stepping down from his moderator position after a good eight or so years of outstanding service to the community. I'm sure he'll better explain things at a later point, but essentially the site has taken a backseat to his life in recent months and he feels he isn't active enough to moderate. Spyder has been a great part the moderation team over the years and is someone I trust completely when it comes to running the community, it'll be sad to no longer have his name in green. Nonetheless, he promises he'll still visit, so it's not as if he's leaving for good. For the record, it is unlikely at this time we'll be appointing a replacement.
Thanks for reading, Xavier.
