It's no longer christmas, so merry week-of-christmas! Bah. Before I begin, just want to remind you that because we've been playing catch-up on the awards you may have missed the three articles published in the last few days or so; , and . Give them a read and a comment :) [Zone By Numbers](http://toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=405) [Facial Hair of the Year](http://toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=406) [Outstanding Resource Contribution](http://toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=407)
Now, a few of us have put some cash together and got you guys, the community, a present. No really, we have... well, without the cash part but plenty of sweat and tears. Let me explain...
Ever since the last release of the Toolkit in 2007 the community has complained, and rightly so, about the awful function reference that confuses more than it helps. Newbies don't know what a function does and oldbies discover the functions they're looking for don't even appear in the reference. Frankly, it's a bit of an embarrasment for the software and no doubt turns away many new users.
So a couple of months ago I started a new project called "The Code Bible". Simply put, it's an alternative function reference that revives the old style of descriptions and code samples, but in a much more organised and professional layout. With the outstanding help of Asimir, FuryX639, Marz and zyrshnikashnu, the majority of functions have been catalogued and we hope to finish it off over the next couple of weeks. Although it uses a wiki system it will not be open to public editing and before anyone explodes with ideas please remember that it's merely a function reference and not a tutorial website. We just want it to be place to go whenever you want to reference something. *the*
At time of writing we have maybe 75% of the functions entered, so be aware it's not completed, but even now it's infinitely more useful than the official reference. After the Zone Awards are finished I will take the time to style the reference to something more TKZish.
So, take a look and if you have any feedback or opinions about the reference and the way it's shaping up then please do share with us!
Hope you had a great Christmas day!
Xavier.
