The Trouble With Boards
I'm going to show you some examples of boards and what makes them work or not. What we often see is that many people tend to make huge boards and then don't put enough stuff in them to entertain the eye, or often put way too much in there. These examples I found from various games from a variety of editors. Most of them use standard RPG Maker tiles.
. See how the following is almost completely empty? Pretty much nothing here to make me not want to kill myself. So avoid doing this as it's one of the most common problems in games. Even if a location is a baron desert, addlittle variance in the shades of san or rocks. Reptition kills.
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1362.png)
The next board is an improvement, although too many trees in my opinion and no objects in the water so it's pretty plain. However, I do really like the landscape. Not all blocky or square, pretty natural looking with contours.
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1361.png)
Another board and this one is very open but still has tons of scenery and objects to look at, which is really nice in setting the tone. If you look closely you will see random shading all over the ground which I dont really like but it does adds to the overall effect.
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1364.png)
The following possibly goes too far with the trees, which all look the same. A solution would be to have treesof varying sizes and colours to give it a little life.
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1366.png)
The classic cliff mania, a little too much again with lots of scenery but it just doesnt mesh well together
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1365.jpg)
This on is really nice with not too much but just enough that fits together really well. Makes you want to just walk around and look at stuff, doesn't it? Notice that there is even cloud shadows on the board. Whether they are moving or not I dont know but it's a nice touch.
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1367.png)
Another great example, not to enclosed but not too spaced out. The plants are wonderfully unique and colourful, really giving the game a distinct feel. In a game you should be more open to unique visuals as it helps earn an identity... not just another clone
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1360.jpg)
This is my personal favorite. The lighting and colors just go perfect together. To do this you would have to go outside of the tileeditor and use photoshop to shadow and what not.
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1369.png)
Well, I hope I raised a few questions in your mind and motivate you to work on your game or post some of your own boards. It may not be an in-depth look at boards but I'm just trying to help inspire others and also myself!
