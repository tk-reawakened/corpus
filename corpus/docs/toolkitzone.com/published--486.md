A Final Word
Well, that's it for the Zone Awards and our celebration of all things 2009. It's great to be able to remind eachother of some of the fun games and contributions over the last year, and I'm sure many new and old users alike played some games for the first time after they were covered during the awards. If you hadn't read already, check out which game won the community vote for now! [Game of the Year](http://toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=485)
There were plenty of different names picking up awards this year beyond the usual Grindalf/Jackyboy tag team, such as TK Game Boy, Asimir and Lotusware. I hope to see the likes of necromancer13 and close runner-up in the Newcomer award aurum34 push on in 2010 and continue to do good things.
From a personal point of view, I had great fun writing the various articles and awards. In true TK community style I ran late a few times with articles, but I managed to finish pretty much on time - a vast improvement after last year finishing 11 days late! I also used the Awards this year to reveal that a "refreshed" Toolkit Zone will be coming this year. I don't want to raise false hopes or mislead expectations so I'm keeping most details under-wraps and next time you'll hear from me on that will be the day it's launched.
Thanks again to all those that replied and discussed the awards, without your contributions it wouldn't be the same. Any suggestions and questions regarding the awards, please do feel free to post them as a reply to this.
Good luck to all in 2010! Next time I do any big article it'll likely be to announce the new TKZ site :)
Xavier
