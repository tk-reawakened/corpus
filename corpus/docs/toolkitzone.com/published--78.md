Browse
I got an e-mail today which has forced my hand on a few issues relating to the site. My hosts are closing their services on the 2nd February, which means another huge server change which causes several headaches. The move itself probably won't take place until early January at the earliest, and I'll give a good weeks notice before the site goes down, which again will take at least 24 hours to be completed succesfully. However, I have decided to probably take advantage of the move to switch forum software from PHPBB to IPB, a far superior system. This does have many knock on effects, such as requiring a total revision of all the code across the entire site in relation to user data and the fact a new TKZ skin will be required for the new forum. I don't know how much IPB will cost, unlike PHPBB it is not free, and I may setup a community "piggy bank" where we can all donate and once we hit the target I'll buy the software (since current Uber Memberships "run out" after two more months hosting). More on the server and forums over coming weeks.
