# Raises eyebrow

Browse
The Zone Awards
“Oooooh”, “Aaaaaah”, etc, etc. We couldn’t get Jon Stewart or Billy Crystal to do the presenting, they still haven’t forgiven seveneternal for abandoning Vii, and we couldn't bring ourselves to go with Whoopi Goldberg, so you’re stuck with me. Ho hum. Yeppers, welcome to the first ever official Toolkit Zone awards thingamabob. About time considering since the site was first launched over eight and a half years ago, but what would the Toolkit community be if not masterful at procrastination?
The Zone Awards will be held once a year and, with the exception of one particular award, be solely for achievements in the previous calendar year. Although some awards will have shortlisted voting for you guys to participate, many will be awarded outright by the Secret Award Deciding Council at Toolkit Zone (SADCATZ for short.. um) So it’s not like an Expo, there is no mass community voting. We just dole out awards and throw the spotlight on some deserving folk, and we can all argue about it afterwards.
The other thing to note is that the awards run over the entire month of December, with each day being a new award or article or some other super exciting thing. Think of it as our very own advent calendar (actually, literally.. check the front page tomorrow) just without the free chocolate and.. any relation to Christmas.. but um, yeah, advent calendar. During the award month the front page will be a dedicated hub of award activity and discussion, plus the forums will be tweaked somewhat to promote it.
Although the awards themselves will be clouded in a mist of unnecessary mystery until they’re revealed, I’ll throw out some randomly to keep you guys guessing; best demo game, outstanding forum contributions, “stupidest” quote of the year, and member hall of fame. There’s plenty more, although I’m still a bit pissed the SADCATZ rejected my “Gayest Non-Gay Who Is Probably Gay Anyway” award idea (they said Lotusware has enough awards as it is).
So it’s time to celebrate the best (and sometimes worst) that the community had to offer during 2008. I hope it’ll be a fun ride. It’s December 1 here in South Korea, tomorrow I’ll be starting the awards ceremony proper. So check back to the front page and do your best not to wet yourself with the pure ecstasy that comes with ultimately unimportant, trivial website awards! Woop. st
Xavier
