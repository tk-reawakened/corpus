# RANTERS DREAMS: MR. WHATAMUNCHKINS CRUSADE

Our hero seeking for new skills and experience was fooled into being a chore boy but upon convincing Colin that he was able to become a great warrior, Colin accepted him as his apprentice. Now he gets to live with Colin and learn from him all he know about RPGCode.
** RANTERS DREAMS: MR. WHATAMUNCHKINS CRUSADE
Chapter 7 : Stranger in the night
![](library/images/docs/622.gif)
"It's late. I gotta get some rest." Exclaimed Colin. "Come on. I want to learn something before the night ends." replied Mr. Whatamunchkins. "Look kid, I know you're excited but I'm sleepy and I get cranky easily so go to sleep." "HMPH! Fine. Go to bed but I'm not sleepy." "Good." Colin went to his room. "Don't stay up too late." "I wont." Mr. Whatamunchkins stepped outside. He was really excited about learning from Colin that he couldn't sleep. He went for a walk on the road outside. The night was an eery, beutiful thing. There was sounds of chirping and wind rustling. The sky was filled with stars. "Wow! This place is great. I wonder if MS is like this?" His train of thought was then disrupted by the sounds of someone moaning. "What the hell is wrong with you, spraying me with a water hose? I asked for water to drink you idiot." Mr. Whatamunchkins didn't know what to do. "Should I call Mr. Colin?" The cloaked man got up. "Who are you mister?" "My name...is not important." "Not important?" "Yeah." "I don't get it but okay. Hi Mr. Not Important." "HUH?" "What?" "Why did you just say that?" "Well you told me that was your name." "NO! You idiot." "Hey don't yell at me, your the liar." The cloaked man then collapsed again. "Do you need some water?" asked Mr. Whatamunchkins. "Oh GOD NO! Take me inside." "Okay." Mr. Whatamunchkins helped the cloaked man inside. "I should call Mr. Colin." "No! DON'T!" "Why not?" "I'm fine." "So?" "There is no need for him." "Hey, you never answered me. Who are you?" "I'll tell you later. That is if I can survive." "Survive what?" "I...have a virus inside me." "You're sick?" "In a way. This virus is eating me up. The virus is called #DePixel() that was constructed in a plugin called lightBattery.dll." "Light Battery?" "Yes. That plugin was to help me charge up my inner RPGCode and upgrade it but somehow that command, #DePixel(), was inside and it affected me." "Oh no. Can you heal it?" "No. I cannot. I need to debug it. *cough* This...thing is messing me up. I look hideous." "But...but...how did you get this plugin?" "I...I...ARGH...I don't have much time. Take this and plugin and give it to Colin." He handed Mr. Whatamunchkins an envelope. Mr. Whatatmunhckins grabbed the envelope then fell to the ground. "UHH! This thing is heavy." "Of course it is, it's 32MB." "Well what does it do?" "That is a plugin called clockwork_champion.dll. I don't know how it works. I wanted Cyranoid to look at it but Colin will have to do because I...can't...stay." He suddenly got up and ran as fast as he could to the outside. "HEY! WAIT!" shouted Mr. Whatamunchkins. He looked in the envelope. Inside was a plugin with a hote which he read:
![](library/images/docs/623.gif)
Xavier: I had returned to TKZ once again for a brief to see CBM. Had I known that he was absent, I wouldn't have come. But apparently one man came to me. He did not gave a screename, he was a guest, possibly a lurker. He claimed to have made a time altering plugin of the name clockwork_champion.dll. This plugin is remarkable, you must see it. I enclose commands that are associated with it. But I warn you. Do not take this plugin lightly. It is possible to have your RPGCode rearranged in which debuggin cannot handle. I trust you to take care.
MrG
"MrG!" screamed Mr. Whatamunchkins. "Hey!" Colin screamed. "What are you yelling about?" "Oh...uhh...nothing." "Go to bed." Colin went back to sleep. Mr. Whatamunchkins raced to his room. He was so excited that he managed to bring in the heavy plugin with him (well drag it at least). "Wow! A real plugin. And if MrG likes it, then it's gotta be good." He grabbed the plugin. He looked inside the envelope for something that could help. He then found another sheet of paper. "What's this?"

"Concentrate? How?" "Well first blank out your mind. Then just say this program outloud." "Okay." Mr. Whatamunchkins tried to blank his mind but he had so many thoughts, it was hard for him to stop at all. "I can't. It's too hard." "Try closing your eyes first." Mr. Whatamunchkins closed his eyes. He then tried again. This time it was working but not completely. "Try breathing and exhaling and just concentrate on the program I gave you." Mr. Whatamunchkins only concentrated on the commands that Colin gave him. He started to concentrate again and again. he closed his eyes tight and concentrated really hard. "Hey." said Colin. "Where is the dummy I had set up?" Mr. Whatamunchkins invisioned the program inside his head. He could see it clearly now. Colin looked around. "Did someone steal them? Where are...oh no!" Mr. Whatamunchkins saw it clearly and felt a strange sensation. Suddenly the ground shook. It shook harder and harder. Colin couldn't balance himself. Suddenly he was knocked back and he fell to the ground with a feeling in his gut like someone hit him. Mr. Whatamunchkins opened his eyes and was the ground torn. "ALRIGHT! I DID IT!" he exclaimed. "Err...Mr. Colin, are you alright?" Colin got up. "Yeah. I forgot ot put up the enemy dummies." He dusted himself and looked around. "What the? Kid, what was the value of the #Earthquake you used?" "One." "That can't be. A value of one couldn't possibly have done this." He then saw his house. It was realigned and broken. It had become almost delapitated. "MY HOUSE! You destroyed my house!" "I'm sorry, I'm sorry." "SORRY isn't going to get my house back. You were only suppose to add a value of one, you probably increased it to 32." "I...I..." Mr. Whatamunchkins couldn't say anything in his defense. He didn't knew what to say. "Well is there anyhting...I can do?" "NO! There isn't. Get out of here. Find your stuff and leave. I don't want to see you anymore." Colin walked to the debris and was feeling miserable. Mr. Whatamunchkins just stood. Luckily, his room was outside the house but apperenlty he would have to leave it. "Oh man. I wish I didn't do it. I wish I didn't destroy his house." Mr. Whatamunchkins felt compelled. He decided that he'd learn about RPGCode by himself with no help. He put his bag on his back only to have him drop down to his face. He fell to the ground and hit his nose. "Ow. My nose. Why is my bag so heavy?" He thought for a moment. "They Plugin!" He looked into his bag and saw the plugin the stranger gave him. "Clockwork Champion. Hey!" he had an idea. "I can use this to go back in time." Mr. Whatamunchkins thought that he could use the plugin to go back in time but Mr. Whatamunchkins didn't had a single idea of how a plugin worked. He needed to ask someone and Colin was out of the question. "Wait, I promised myself that I was going to teach myself everything from now on." Mr. Whatamunchkins started thinking. "No good. I'm going to go and find a tutorial. Oh man. That means I'll have to carry this heavy plugin. Well I'll just have to leave it somewhere safe until I find a tutorial." He went behind a tree and burried it in the ground. He got his bag and headed towards the next town.
