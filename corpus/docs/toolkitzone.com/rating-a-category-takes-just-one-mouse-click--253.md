# Rating a category takes just one mouse click.

Browse
The Race to Sixty
***The new Have Your Say feature is a great way to give feedback on games and rank them, but it will need a community effort to really get it going.***
One of my favourite things about the new site is the feature. On the old site we used to rate game downloads or a profile out of five or we could post a comment or feedback. None of these allowed for feedback of any substance, afterall what's the difference between a rating of 3 or 4 and why did the user rate it as such? *Have Your Say*
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1119.png)
*Rating a category takes just one mouse click.*
The Have Your Say feature is for games with an actual playable download and while remaining a quick and simple way to give feedback, it's also more in-depth. Just go to any game profile with a demo or full game and you'll notice the "Have Your Say" menu item as well as the bubble box above it. You have the ability to rate the game from 1 (awful) to 10 (perfect) in six categories; graphics, audio, story, fun, stability and length. You end your "say" by entering a general summary of your thoughts about the game, so in effect it mixes the previous rating, comment and feedback features into one easy tool. A score out of 100 is calculated from your ratings and the game's total score is the average Have Your Say score, plus once it reaches five says it can be ranked with other games.
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1120.png)
*Games are ranked after five says.*
But we have a problem, the feature is new so there is only one game at the time of writing that is eligible to be ranked - *so we need your help!*
Presuming there's at least 20 users who like to encourage fellow members, at least 20 users who want to see Toolkit games get better, at least 20 users who would like to have feedback after their hard work creates a demo or a full game, then we're asking these 20 users to have their say on a game at least three times in the next couple of weeks. Pick a game you've played, or maybe be and pick one you haven't, then play it, rate it and say it! adventurous
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1121.png)
*Play it, rate it, say it!*
If we can work together we could create a fantastic starting point of around 60 says which will hopefully allow for at least a half-dozen games to be officially ranked! So please take three ten minute breaks sometime over the next few days and have your say!
Thank you.
