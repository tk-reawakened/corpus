# regardless of whether you win or lose

There are many people who use 3D in games...myself, JinW, and RDemusX, just to name a few. Of course, using prerendered 3D in a game is a bit on the risky side--if an image isn't rendered correctly or with quality, well, the 3D will be as much a turnoff as tiles from the original Zelda for the NES. That's where my 3D Art contests come in. Just as Khin's Pixel Art Contests have helped many people, again myself included, become better pixelists, this set of contests will help those who want to expand the quality of their 3D modeling and rendering jobs. Further, as a detail only present in Khin's Pixel Art Contest by request, as part of the package deal with this one, you will recieve a crtique from myself or one of the leading contenders in the contest about how you can improve your model, what you could do with the rendering process to make it better, even tips on post-production.
