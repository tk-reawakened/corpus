# Robots

The TKZ Daily Sketch Events
When the TK community migrated from rpgtoolkit.com to TKZone, they were introduced, or at least the new ones, to a new style of environment. While some disliked the migration from threaded to phpbb style, it promised better experience and long term enjoyment.
Initially the three main forums and three unofficial forums were kept. There were the General, Help, and Suggestion Boards, accompanied by the Debate, Off-topic, and another board that I regretfully could not recall. Since then the community has learned to adjust, and adapt to the new home they're in.
A series of improvements and overhauls has amiled upon our webmaster's door, which he always welcome and entertains. Soon after other forums were created, like Screenshots, TKZ Support, and not long after the Art Section. Everyone was excited about the change. Finally things can get more organized so the battle for attention and who's topic will be on top has been lessened. The Art Section paved the way for community artists to improve and share their skills in a common ground to which most members are fond of. Not long after the forum became filled with art enthusiasts and the comminuty saw a various mixture of colors as well as share a multitude of styles.
Of course evolution does not stop, and now, with the help of the former Graphics Manager and the current moderator of the Art Forum, Indy has conceptualized a Daily Sketch Group subforum. This was accompanied by other subforums as well. I encourage you, . Eventually, Indy adapted from another community to which he is a member of and brought the idea in our beloved home. The event has seen a somewhat slow start but as people become familiar of the environment, entries flooded even more than it did at the official Art Section. People who do not normally post works and are not normally open to art talks became involved and are surprisingly good. The roundabouts of the event is simple: everyday a topic will be selected by Indy from member suggestions. All members are then encourage to post their drawings that relate to the topic. The time it took them to finish and the program they used are also required for they could be of comparison factor if one wants to know where he/she is lacking. It has become very effective too. [just check them out](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/viewforum.php?selected_id=f16)
The original goal is self improvement-- to better yourself at what you do through practice.
