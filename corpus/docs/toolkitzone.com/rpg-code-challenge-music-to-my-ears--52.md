# RPG Code Challenge - Music to My Ears

It has been an awfully long time since a RPG Code Challenge has been out. Don't fret though as John@aljo is back with a new and fresh idea that will get that RPG Coder part of your mind roaring on high octane. This time around we are going to keep to the music theme that has been ruling the competitions. Your job is to make a Media player. Nothing less. Now I know someone is out there thinking that this is going to be the easiest 2 lines of code they ever wrote. I tell you now that it is the media player that can do the most and looks the coolest that wins the competition. Now here are the rules to this competition: Submitted pieces need to be emailed to , any problems can be discussed with me by IM which is conveniently on my . You can discuss the competition on the forums in topic.
**RPG Code Challenge - Music to My Ears**
By John@aljo
- Everything goes as far as using pictures and plug-ins. (May I suggest 2 plug-ins that would be most helpful. by Oyerth and the by yours truly) [Fmod Audio Plug-in](http://toolkitzone.com/fileman.php?id=3&file=1244) [Shell Plug-in](http://toolkitzone.com/fileman.php?id=3&file=169)
- There is no file size limit. If you can't attach the file to an e-mail then we will find another way for you to submit.
- Please do not add music to your submitted piece. We all like our own kinds of music and frankly I do not want to listen to yours.
- The submitted piece has to be a pakfile or exe. I want everything, not just the RPG Code files.
- You may work in teams if you wish.
- Your allowed one submitted piece. Updates to the submitted piece is fine but creating a new submitted piece is not.
- Entries must be received no later than **May 5th, 2006**
