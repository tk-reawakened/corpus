# Scheduled Downtime

By Xavier
Later this month we will have to move the entire site to a new server, which is a lot of file moving, database transferring and domain propogation. The move is necessary as my current host is closing down. Subject to change, below are the key dates for the move. Expect the site and forums not to be functional at all on the dates specified. 17th January, 6pm GMT - 18th January, Evening. I'll keep you guys up to date on the progress on the day, hopefully it will go smoothly. During the move the domain will also be updated to point to the new server, and this may take an extra day or two. In other news the games section is currently going under a total overhaul. Check out the latest images, news and provide your own ideas by clicking for the development topic.
A date is finally set for the move to the new server which will see the site and forums offline for a day or more, and also some news on the game section.
