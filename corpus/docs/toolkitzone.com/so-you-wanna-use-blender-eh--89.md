# So, you wanna use Blender, eh?

**Well, Good news, this tutorial will teach you the basics.**
This tutorial is for users who are not familiar with Blender programming and have wondered to try and make 3d games, with Toolkit as well as Blender. I will teach basic fundamental skills, and we will build a room with this tutorial as well as incorporate the default character to move around in the house.
Let us start. To draw a box, open up blender. There, you will immediately have a box... Now press Tab on the keyboard to enter edit mode.
![Picture 1](http://i18.photobucket.com/albums/b104/Jinw91/Blender%20Tutorial/bt1.bmp)
After that, all of the vertices should be yellow. If not, press "A". Now press "S" button and scale it and move the mouse away from the box. On the lower left hand corner, above the toolbar, there should be "Size x: 1.0000 y: 1.0000 z: 1.0000". Press the Ctrl button (and hold it) on the keyboard and move the mouse away from the box. When the Lower left hand corner says "Size x: 3.0000 y: 3.0000 z: 3.0000", then you left click with the mouse.
![Picture 2](http://i18.photobucket.com/albums/b104/Jinw91/Blender%20Tutorial/bt2.bmp)
Now press F12 on the keyboard and it will show you the picture of your room. Right now, it's a box.
![Picture 3](http://i18.photobucket.com/albums/b104/Jinw91/Blender%20Tutorial/bt3.bmp)
Now, it's kinda dull, looking black and gray. And also, there's no windows! You'll feel depressed, and gloomy. Now press F5 on the keyboard and you will see a circle under preview on the bottom of Blender. Now, on the right, you should see a grayish color called Col, then under is Spe, then Mir. Now the Col should be highlighted, and right of it is R 0.8000, then G 0.8000, then B 0.8000. Let's make a brown box. Change the R to 0.4000, G to 0.4000, and B to 0.2000. Press Tab again.
![Picture, do not steal](http://i18.photobucket.com/albums/b104/Jinw91/Blender%20Tutorial/bt4.bmp)
Now, rotate the box by pressing "R". Then type 45. It will rotate 45 degrees. Now press "G" and move the box a little NE, keep pressing F12 until the box is centered. (I don't have exact numbers) Now find the camera. It looks like a pyramid with an arrow. It is not on the box however. Right in front of it should be your box. Now press Tab again to enter edit mode. Select the four vertices that make the face that faces the camera. (Hold Shift to select multiple vertices) Press "X" and select Faces. Then highlight the top four vertices and press "X" and go to Faces. We have made a house. Now you can put as much stuff in it. However, we are just going to make it into Toolkit. Go to Files, then Save Image..., then go under the address, and type in "room.jpg".
Now after that, go open the file. It should open. Now, copy it and paste it in your Toolkit directory under your game\bitmap. Now open Toolkit. Go to New, then Tiles, then go to Image, then Grab Tiles. Go open the file called room.jpg. Now, go to Batch Grab 32x32. Save it under room. Now go to a new board, and use those tiles!
