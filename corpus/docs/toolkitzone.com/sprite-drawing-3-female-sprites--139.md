# Sprite Drawing 3: Female Sprites

Browse
***Sprite Drawing 3: Female Sprites***
This time around we'll tackle creating female sprites. It shouldn't be too hard. We'll just have to modify our male sprite. Alright, let's get it started.
Since we are not starting from scratch, just refer to the two images above. It shows direct comparison between male and female sprites. Notice the encircled areas.
One thing that separates males from females are eyelashes. Females have that extra pixel on the side of the eyes to indicate eyelashes. See the red circle.
Next is the shoulder level. If you trace the grid you'll notice that we lowered the shoulder level. Females have less broad shoulders and less stiff (typically). Notice a few adjustments in pixels here and there. Simply count the grids.
And there goes the breasts (Orange). For some people it might be too wide. You'll just have to adjust accordingly depending on your preference.
The violet circle is the hip area. Of course, since females have that ever so attractive body curve, especially on anime types, our female sprite should still possess that important feature. The hip is just a pixel and to achieve the body curve we just make the area narrower than the chest area and the torso area. Feel free to customize the width of the hips as you found pleasing.
The blue circle shows that the elbows of females need not be bent inwards. Such pose denotes masculinity and therefore should be avoided when drawing females. Also, take the time to adjust the size of the arms so that it'll be thinner than a male's arm.
The females hips has got to be the most difficult part, because you'll only deal with a couple of pixels. Even this output I have made does not satisfy me that much, so you'll have to adjust the pixels in order to get the right that you want. Also the legs of females should be less fat than males. *sexiness*
I have taken the liberty of drawing all the walking animations. Just study them and observe carefully the placement of the pixels. Should you find anything undesirable please make the necessary modifications.
Let me dress her up a bit. It looks way better than keeping her nude.
South walking frames. Take note that the elbows still do not bend as much as male elbows do.
North walking frames. The butt sure looks big especially on the standing frame. Adjust the torso width to reduce the effect and try raising the butt area one pixel or two.
Side walking frames. Take note of the placement of the breasts. You may adjust it if it looks awkward for you.
This tutorial is relatively short, although I personally dislike it the way it is, It'll only be a waste of effort if we start from scratch when we have a useable template. The basics have been laid down, that's why the rest seems to come in a breeze. I hope you still learned from it though.
