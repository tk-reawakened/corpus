# Spyder

Browse
Hall of Fame
Make sure you read the Day 26 article I published earlier today - .![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1265.png) [Newcomer of the Year](http://toolkitzone.com/library.php?doc=409) After inducting the creator of the Toolkit and a pioneer of RPG Code, our third and final oldbie to go into the Hall of Fame is a community leader and nice guy. As if you hadn't guessed already...
**Spyder**
He effectively stopped being an active part of the community earlier this year when he stepped down from his moderating duties, although he is by no means dead and can be seen posting in the Other forum from time to time.
A prolific poster, his name is one of the best known in the community and his laid back attitude made him one of the most popular we've ever had. He made his biggest splash back in the early days when he took over from Lance as the head of Mystic Software (correct me if I'm wrong guys, my memory is hazy) and with a re-design (the days when bevel was the go-to design feature of any good site) helped make it the leading resource site. Indeed, the reason I created TKZ was because I was so inspired and impressed by the work Spyder did... I kinda ripped off his design too. Ahem.
He also released a popular game, Godiva: Alpha, many years ago that sported some features that at the time were pretty revolutionary such as a custom menu system that had an overworld map showing you where you were. Spyder was also a moderator for many years, as reliable and trustworthy as they come. That said, he was also pretty prone to saying dumb things from time to time (my personal favourite was when he said to me in the infamous bravenet chat - "If humans evolved from monkeys, why aren't monkeys turning into humans now?")
Congratulations Spydie! Have fun in the Hall... don't make too much of a mess.
