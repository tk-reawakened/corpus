# Step 1: Your Drawbridge

As a gamer, I've participated in the downfall of many a fellow villain. It's almost too easy these days just to control a bunch of heavily-armed 10-year-olds, some of whom are possessed of magic capabilities and raging hormones, and kill a perfectly powerful and evil villain like Sephiroth, Mithos Yggdrasill, the Sorceress Ultimecia, or any number of others. 'What can I do about it?', you villains ask. Because you're such badasses, you have use of forbidden powers, legions of monstrous minions, and of course your castle that you base your world domination/ascension above all mortal life actions from. Well, my friend...that's what this guide is here to help you with! A step-by-step detail of ways you can make your dominion hero-proof! Pictured above: A properly defended evil castle repelling heroic attacks from all directions. Your drawbridge is your first line of defense against heroic invasions--so treat it like it! These days, your generic do-goods can simply walk in to your castle and start slaying your minions! There are obvious steps you can take against this, like leaving your drawbridge up when you're not using it, or if you're looking for amusement...make your drawbridge (see Getting Into Your Keep step, as traps are more fully outlined there.) If you don't use a drawbridge, but prefer magitechnology transporters (see Step 4: Useful Investments), make sure you have some kind of a dud set up with the real transporter carefully hidden elsewhere. The reason you should do this is because heroes these days really aren't that bright, and it takes them lots of experimentation to discern traps from honest-to-goodness passages into your sanctum of darkness, most of which they have to fall into first. Sadly, heroes are very annoying about finding ways into your keep that you either designed for your minions...or other, more inventive ways like drops from their airships (see the step about Useful Investments). Now, in addition to reinforcing security on the entrances to your stronghold, there are YET MORE things you can do to stop heroic incursions. First--make your keep hard to get into. Most villains (even myself) employ floating keeps, as those are notoriously hard to invade unless said heroes have somekind of way to get to your entrance. Of course, you need to consider your various minions' welfare (see Step 3: Your Minions, as well as Step 5: Useful Investments), so some NOT OBVIOUS way for your minions to enter their sleeping quarters is a must. Also effective: distractions. Considering your everyday hero's avaricious nature, putting out some nice, apparently useful equipment (see Step 4: Chests) help you be safer...provided you use traps to help slow or possibly even halt said heroes' progress. A castle where you base your magical research on immortality as well as studies of hyper-powerful forbidden magic are good...but as military organizations that even the militarily-retarded good guys have have observed, a castle is nothing without proper support staff. Now, bad guys I've seen have managed to keep their castles in tip-top visual shape despite heroic incursions that slay most of the monster population...if you can spare man/mon/netherbeing-power on home decor, maybe you should up your defense spending a little? Now, since we're on the topic of defense spending (see Step 4: Chests to supplement this), we come to the issue of the random equipment secreted around your castle from the previous owner who got whacked by a past group of goodies. There are useful things you can do with it--first, you have magic powers, thus allowing you to make your servant appear human for a bit. Give the money to the monsters/netherbeings so they can go to a nearby human town and buy some GOOD equipment. When they comeback in legendary armor with powerful weapons, they'll be much more effective warriors for your cause. Your elemental equipment that comes with your castle should have one of two things done with it--either give it to your division commanders, so they're harder than normal to beat, hide it deep in your castle as goodie-bait (yes, you keep a hero's avaricious nature in mind...) where you can ring it with traps, or just break it. Less is more, right? Support staff is nothing if they can't work your castle gates and help to stop goodies from invading. However, before you put your cannon-fodder minions on gate duty, take a look at them, as there are several things you need to do before you can even allow them to start keeping watch. First, Employee Education is a worthwhile investment. Your average netherbeing/monster/evil human isn't that bright. They've got OK combat skills, but don't ever use them effectively. Some martial arts training, as well as teaching them to search for wierd and/or altered objects around them are helpful, as a heroic invasion can be spotted fairly early. Next, don't allow sleeping on the job! You have magic powers that let you keep watch over the heroes! Instead of watching their Days of our Lives -type...well, Lives, make sure your minions are staying awake...any who aren't, well, you have magic to solve that. A small fireball on the rump is usually enough to rouse them from a potentially crippling slumber. Further, make sure your minions are well-insured! Heroes have a bad habit of activating your base's oh-so-necessary Self-Destruct mechanism, so make sure your minions have carefully concealed, yet (to them, anyways) well-known escape routes. With any luck, your minions will be safely away from the castle while the heroes are halfway out of the caslte...with 3 seconds until detonation. Trust me, they'll thank you later--they may be bad guys, but they have their feelings too. ...Just don't feel bad about neglecting the feelings of any traitors. These tend to abound in a just-purchased Evil Castle, and contain things that usually aid heroes in their quest to whack you. In the above step, I gave some good ideas about how to deal with the gear that comes with your castle (when you distribute the money and equipment, drop junk in there by the way, like thongs, panties, old '80s T-Shirts, even some gumdrops!), but there are even possibilities with chests! Here's a fun idea: Heroes need healing potions. They can't rely upon their restorative mage's abilities indefinently, as their magic power runs out...also, that same restorative mage to be a very pretty girl whom you've just kidnapped from said party so you can get advice on how to get a date. However, aside from my malfunctioning social and/or romantic life, heroes do need healy potions...which offers you this great idea. Step 1: remove the healing potion. I don't care what you do with it, but I recommend you distribute it to your forces, and make sure they know to use healing potions. Step 2: replace said potions with any or all of the following alternatives: Extra-strength Ny-Quil, cow droppings, granny-panties...if you're really hardcore, some extra-strength laxatives. Anything that can deter heroic progress is fair game! Next, I notice my fellow villains like using monsters called 'Mimics'. They look like chests..but are in fact hard-as-hell-to-beat monsters unless the heroes have access to their best skills and abilities. Don't use these! Chests that are traps are FAR preferable to Mimics. If you want to involve monsters with chests...make monster that a chest. Heroes will NEVER be able to figure it out! Yes, you are a villain. Yes, you are most likely a silver-haired prettyboy who gaming women in real life want to bang. That mean your only investment to your castle should be a mirror so you can marvel at how you look in a Speedo. Of course, there are so many investments ABSOLUTELY NECESSARY to an Evil Castle, it's better simply to list them for your perusal. Be sure to read this if nothing else. Firstly, you may be a hyper-powerful badass who has near 0 casting time, over 80000 HP, and resistances to all elements as well as ungodly high stats. However, goodies can still hurt you quite easily for thousands of damage per hit. The prudent thing to do is to use effective physical and magical shielding, as these to prolong the battle...which isn't good for heroes. Also, if you can find yourself a 'weakness' that involves the hero(s) sacrificing themselves, it's good for use--10 year old kids (in other words, your generic RPG hero) are dickless, hormone-ridden suckers. Now, there is a step to immortality that involve completing your plans, and is thus very convenient. It's a simple spell villains have used in the past, which is what allows them to menace the world every 1000 years on schedule: the 'You Can't Kill Me, Bitch!' Spell. It's so common, all you have to do is Google it. Seriously, it's the reason I'm over 10,000 years old! Further, it's easy to use. Whenever a potential hero's about to kill you, cast it. Mission complete. You'll be fully revived within a few hundred years, and can restart your plans. But before you do that, get a date first. Seriously. Even bad guys can be losers. Well, my fellow villains, I hope you've found these tips helpful. If all goes right, the generic bands of early-teenage heroes who used to cut bloody swaths across your expansive mon-controlled dominions will be threats no more when they come to your stronghold! Further, if I've left out anything, PM me so I can add it with Xavier (aka Vampz)'s approval. You're welcome in advance.

- Traps: Here's the meat of any castle, and ways to delay and stop a hero's progress. Heroes love falling into traps, and apparently can't get enough of doing so. I've had too many vogues as various heroes, and through the course of every game, they fall into a minimum of 10 traps. Now, my fellow villains, I have one thing to ask you: I think I know the answer--therefore, you should make those spikes more than 5 mm tall (try increasing the size 100, or even 10,000-fold), ditches should be MILES deep with power locks that are properly shielded and insulated, and it's always effective to make sure any falling weights are in excess of five pounds, even 5,000 tons, as heroes capable of lifting (and holding) that much as well as jumping multiple times their body height, even if the various NPCs who accompany them aren't and swiftly die trying to do so.
***Are you kittens or are you evil super-powerful badasses?*** *are*
- Block Puzzles: Most villains use these dubious pieces of rat butt to lock away and/or hide parts of their castle and some treasure. Assuming you took my pointers about treasure, that should be no concern, but the 'locking away parts of your Castle' part is what should be worrying you. Fortunately, you can do multiple things. First, you can have a null block puzzle (meaning it's a dud you can use to get heroes to waste their time while your badass hunter/killer squadrons take up positions and their magic-users can begin casting offensive magic), which is effective...but there's more you can do than just that--make the blocks explosive. Nothing says 'Merry Christmas' quite like a pressure-sensitive, medieval-version-of-C4-plastique, 6 foot by 6 foot, 9999-ton block of whatever.
- Anti-Aircraft Weaponry: As mentioned above, heroes occasionally tend to forgo well-guarded gates in favor of parasail or just normal drops into the very top of your dominion. Don't allow a weakness of this magnitude! The best way to stop an aerial threat is some kind of Anti-aircraft weapon. Airships abound in every world on every plane of existence, so these . - Transporters: Your minions live in your keep where they bathe, eat captured soldiers from the world's various villages and kingdoms, play video games (yeah, even monsters and netherbeings can be gamers), and help you to fulfill your plans, whatever they may be. If you're a traditional villain, you employ a floating keep that no one can get to...thus why transporter devices are necessary. Of course, heroes love using these no matter how confusing you make the teleport configuration. There is a solution. Pay a few more dollars for the 'confusing version', where the heroes have to operate a terminal to figure out where to teleport to...only if they press any button a hyper-powerful laser will shoot them in the head. Take that, suckers!

That said, heroes are out to destroy this before, during, or after they take you out of commission. There are several things you can do: either only have ONE Doomsday device, and make it well defended, rig the doomsday device so that any dumb heroes who try to tamper with it wind up activating it...or simply have two so that the poor idiots destroy one...but buy you time to complete the second one!
