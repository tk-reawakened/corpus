# Summer Expo

By Xavier
The Expo opens to the community on the 1st of June, but until then calling all developers to register at the site and start putting together your game showcase with any images, files and plot info you have. For those who don't know, the Expo comes around every 6 months and lets any Toolkit developer show off their project in an event lasting two weeks. A large percentage of the community will be checking out the games, so it's a great chance to get feedback. At the end, awards are handed out after public voting. You can see the past award winners . I'd like to also note you hotlink to TKZ game profile images at the forums if you so wish :). http://expo.indreams-studios.com [official expo](http://expo.indreams-studios.com/)
