# That Night...

I felt someone shaking my shoulder roughly, and I heard a lot of unfamiliar voices. "Amin...Amin.... I heard a familiar voice call. It was my mother's voice. "Amin...wake up.... I opened my eyes slowly, and lifted the blanket off of my head. I rubbed my eyes, and then looked around. My jaw literally dropped open at the display of horror before me: The room was crammed with girls. **ENTIRE**
![](library/images/docs/126.jpg)
***Heaven...Or Hell?***
"Wha...?" I said, half to myself, half to my Mother. "I tried to wake you up, Amin...I tried.... My Mother said to me. "What? Why? What's going on?" I asked jumbled questions as I looked around the room again at all the unfamiliar faces...the faces of females. My Mother shook her head, and said, "Your father and brother left already to your Aunt's house, and I tried to wake you up to go with them, but you wouldn't wake." "Why?!" I asked, my voice panic-struck. Why had they gone? What was happening? And why were all these girls in my room?! "Well, Amin, your sisters decided that we were going to invite all the girls in the neighborhood for one huge sleep-over!" My mother said cheerfully.
I sat there, utterly nonplussed, staring at her. I had always thought my sisters had a bit of a vacancy in the region between their ears, but how could they have possibly been? And how could my actually let a thing of this sort carry on?! "Ma...wha...?" I said as I searched for the right words to express myself. This was . . It had to be a joke. "Don't worry, Amin, at least we have a man in the house to help out with any heavy or dirty stuff, right?" My Mother said, with that cheerful smile still on her face. "No...no, this isn't happening!" I said as I got up and pushed through the masses of girls. **STUPID** **MOTHER** **STUPID** **CRAZY**
I ran upstairs...only to find the entire TV room was filled with girls as well! My mind was racing with questions, and new ones kept popping up so fast I couldn't even keep up with the other ones fast enough to think about their solution. I plowed through the mass of females, and all of a sudden, I heard Indian music blasting from the Living Room. With curiosity newly added to the list of emotions I was experiencing, I squeezed through the crowd of girls and went to the source of the blaring sound. There was a circle of girls that were clapping while one girl held a Boom Box, and they were all laughing at something they were observing within the circle. I stood on the tips of my toes to look over the circle, and was appalled at what I saw.
My was dancing in the middle of the circle! "Hee yee!!!" My Grandmother was making some sort of sound as she raised her arms and hopped slowly up and down on one foot. "What the devil...?!" I exclaimed, backing away from the circle, but only to be pushed forward as a wave of girls moved into the room. This was completely insane. I decided I was going to leave the house in order to escape the chaos and go stay over at my cousin's house for the night. I let the wave of girls push me through the Living Room into the main hall, where the front door was located. I reached out my hand and placed it on the knob, but as I started to turn it, it wouldn't open. "Wha...?" I tugged at the knob once more, but in vain. I knew I was locked inside. **GRANDMOTHER**
![](library/images/docs/127.jpg)
***Didn't she have back problems?***
Just as I had lost all hope for escape, a girl casually walked up to the door. "Alright, guys! I would love to sleep over, but I have to go late night shopping! See you later!" She turned the doorknob, but as I was about to tell her it was locked, the door miraculously opened. After she stepped out, she quickly closed the door behind her. "Funny thing...I mustn't have turned all the way," I said to myself as I grasped the doorknob once more, sighing with relief. To my utter disbelief, the door refused to open. What was wrong with it? Just then, another girl came up to the door, and said, "Alright, guys, I gotta catch up wtih my sis. Bye!" She then turned the doorknob, and opened the door. Before she closed it behind her, I caught it with my finger. Freedom at last.
I shoved the girl out of my way, and ran down the front steps. I almost made it to the driveway, when..."Amin! I need you!" I heard my Mother call from the window. "Ma! I need to go to my cousin's house! I can't stay with all those girls!" I called back. "AMIN! I need you inside RIGHT NOW! Come in!" She repeated with an angry tone in her voice. I reluctantly walked up the steps, and went back inside.
I closed the door behind me stupidly, then noticed my mistake, and pulled at the knob again. It wouldn't open. "ARGH!!!" I yelled as I slammed the door with my hand. "AMIN!" My mother called. No need to try to get out anymore, I thought. I decided to just go see what my mother needed. I pushed through the masses of girls once more and walked downstairs. While I was on the bottom step, I heard my Grandmother let out a sound of glee. She was still dancing. I rolled my eyes and walked into the basement.
It was really hot in there, especially since all the girls were packed together, and it was hard to make it through to get to my mother. When I had gotten to her, she handed me a vacuum cleaner. "Here you go, Amin. I need you to patrol around the house and vacuum up the mess the girls make, alright?" "C'mon, Ma! Don't I have sisters who are hosting this party? Why don't they clean it up?" I inquired in a whiny tone. "They're too busy having fun. Now go clean!" As she said the word 'clean', she smacked my head lightly with the top of the broom she was holding, and I ran (if you could call squeezing through a crowd running) upstairs, holding the vacuum cleaner above my head.
![](library/images/docs/128.jpg)
***Suddenly, I pity hotel maids.***
It's funny how someone notices a mess much more if they're the ones cleaning it. That was my night of realization, and sudden remorse for all of those who clean messes for a living. There was a rubbish pile of food, napkins, plates, tissues, anything you could think of that would be at a party. I then started to clean. I ran through the different rooms, vacuuming multiple times in one room due to the constant flow of trash falling to the ground. Until after what seemed like forever, the gross, littering masses started to finally go to sleep. The thing was that it was harder to move around while they were asleep as opposed to them awake. The girls were sleeping everywhere! In the kitchen, living room, on the barbecue grill outside; I couldn't think of a place where they wouldnt be sleeping. Hell, there were even some in the bathroom and closets. I was relieved that I had cleaned up the mess, but I found that there was nowhere I could go to sleep. **HUGE**
I went downstairs to the basement to find that some girls had slept on my bed. I sighed, and ran up two floors, to my Grandmother's room. She was sound asleep, and there were four girls also deep in slumber on her bed. I stepped over them to reach the window. I opened it, and went outside. The window leads to a slanted part of the roof. I thought that I could at least sit there until morning. The roof was much too far from the ground to go down and run to my cousin's house, so even though I was outside, I was still trapped within my own home. But, alas, girls in sleeping bags were outside on the roof, so I had nowhere to retreat to.
I went back inside, and stomped down the stairs. I was irritated and sleepy, so I just sat down on the couch, right on top of a girl. At that point, I was rather indifferent to how rude it might have seemed. The girl grunted, and got up. I had just noticed that she was and intimidating. "What chyo sit on meh fo, foo?!" The giant of a girl said, cracking her knuckles. "Aw, shut up, will ya?!" I said, my irritability getting the better of logic. She growled, picked me up by the collar, and tossed me into the next room. **HUGE**
![](library/images/docs/129.jpg)
***Why must blind rage always get the better of me?***
I yelled, half with surprise and half with fear, and landed on a girl sleeping on the floor by the fireplace. "OW!" She said, and got up. "I'm sleeping outside." She walked over to a sliding door that leads to the backyard, and she opened it. Before she closed it, I yelled, "WAIT!" She looked back at me. "How are you able to open the door, but I can't?!" I inquired. She giggled, and replied, "Because, silly, this is a girl's sleep over, so girls can open the doors, but boys can't!" She then walked out, and I ran after her, but she closed the door too fast, and I heard her laughing as she walked on.
I tried to open the door, but it wouldn't budge. The final bit of sanity I was hanging on to finally left me, and out of frustration, I punched the couch. The couch weirdly sunk in, more deeply than it usually does. Then, it hit me - it wasn't the couch. "RWARR!!!" The big girl yelled as she grabbed my outstretched arm, and pulled my face towards hers. "Yous goin DOWN, FOO!!!!" She picked me up by my collar, and slammed my back into the wall. Just then, a girl woke up. Then another. And another. All the girls in the room woke up.
I heard the distant sound of Indian music from upstairs, and girls clapping while my Grandmother was making those Hee yee sounds again. Then, one girl said, "Boy." Another girl then said, "Boy." All the girls in the room started to chant "Boy, boy, boy, boy." They then stopped, and the girl beast said, "Boys aint no 'lowed in sleep-ova's, FOO!" Just then, one of the girls in the room approached me, and ripped my shirt in the area around my collar with her bare hand. "What the Hell're you doing?" It came out as a whisper instead of the shout I had intended it to be. She then raised her head, bared her teeth, which were unusually sharp, and sunk them into my shoulder.
![](library/images/docs/130.jpg)
***...A strange twist of events...***
It was pain beyond pain. It hurt too much for me to even make a sound. I was frozen there, staring into nothing. The worst day of my life had turned into the last day of my life. Slowly, everything around me swirled around into blackness. The last thing I recall hearing was a distant Hee yee from my Grandmother. And then...nothing.
