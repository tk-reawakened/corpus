# The Doc Departs

By Xavier
It's with sadness we announce that our longest serving staff member, Doc_Wellsy, is leaving the team as he embarks on his new career with the RAF (Royal Air Force) at the start of May. The Doc has been an outstanding staff member and leaves just short of his two year anniversary of reviewing your audio submissions and also running any related contests, and it's quite safe for me to say he was easily the most dedicated and consistant staffy we've seen at the Zone since the days of Triuken and MrG. He's been a relatively quiet figure on the forums of late and so may not be the most renowned member, but I know many of the older members will be sad to see him leave, albeit to bigger and better things, and he does promise to check in from time to time. So good luck to the Doc and his new career, and thanks for being such a great sport! Xavier. P.S Any details regarding open staff positions will be announced another time.
