# The Good and the Bad

By Xavier
Well, lets start with the bad and get it out of the way. As most of you will have noticed the site has been extremely slow at times recently, sometimes leading to short downtime periods. The cause is unknown, and is not because of the site itself. Seems our hosts are having various server issues, and on behalf of the community I have already complained. Hopefully things will get better over coming days. Now for the "good". The continued interface tweaks and feature enhancements continue to go on in spurts, with the most significent change being the much needed introduction of a search tool for the resources. Click the "search" link to the top of the resources page and you can sort, filter, and apply keywords to your search. You can also execute a quick search by entering a keyword into the form on the right handside bar and click the search button. This new feature has also allowed the addition of a "files" button to join the "games" one at the bottom of forum posts, so fellow users can now click and instantly have listed the resources you've submitted. Similar buttons are also present now for blogs and documents, and all of this was sparked by a simple suggestion from Brad for a blog button. So remember to keep those ideas coming in. There's been lots of other small changes and additions, such as the full integration of the My Files tool into the latest layout, and no doubt more will be on the way.
