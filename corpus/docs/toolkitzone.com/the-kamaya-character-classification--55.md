# THE KAMAYA CHARACTER CLASSIFICATION

Browse
***THE KAMAYA CHARACTER CLASSIFICATION***
Below are the instructions for my Character Classification system. This is best done on a spreadsheet document in the order that follows:
**1.** *NAME*
**2.** *RACE*
*CLASS* (OPTIONAL)
**4.** *ELEMENT*
*HEIGHT*
**6.** *WEIGHT*
*BODY STRUCTURE*
**8.** *BODY TYPE*
**9.** *DEMEANOR*
**10.** *PHYSICAL DETAILS*
So create a chart with ten(nine) rows across and as many down as you need. After you've added the correct number of cells, you should start filling in spaces. **DO NOT FORGET TO ADD SPECIAL NPC'S!!!**
Here's an example:
*NAME RACE CLASS ELEMENT HEIGHT WEIGHT BODY STRUCTURE BODY TYPE DEMEANOR*
Edward
Human
Warrior
Fire
6' 2''
124 lbs
Bulky-
Humanoid
Serious
Tall, Wears lots of armor. Rather Slow. **PHYSICAL DETAILS:**
This gives you some perspective on Edward. You can now create a picture of him in your mind. (I recommend drawing a picture of all your characters) I will now go into greater detail on each category:
***1.NAME***
**A: Everyone needs a name!**
Every character (save most ) must have a name. Your character could have a player set name, or a set one. It doesn't matter. EVERY CHARACTER NEEDS A NAME. A name sets a character apart from everyone else. NPCs
**B: Bosses are people too!**
Bosses deserve a name as well. I created special names for my bosses and I think you should too. As I stated earlier, EVERYONE NEEDS A NAME!
**C: Creative Ideas**
For added creativity, if your game takes place in a different world, try creating names that you would not see in our world. (I did this with all of my characters, and I think it really adds to the story!)
***2.RACE***
Your character's race is very important. If you don't put down a race, you might get a picture of a monkey or a Dryad or something, when you were going for a Human. I cannot give you any ideas for different races; but they are not necessary either. It is entirely up to you whether to use different races or not. I believe that a just human populated world is rather boring, and other races adds some flair.
A good way to start creating races is to make human/animal combinations. These can add to an otherwise dull story, and can add some unexpected story twists.
***3.CLASS***
Character Class is also a helpful, but non-vital part of a classification. A Class determines what special moves a character can use, their attack, defense, speed, magic points, etc.
Examples of class:
, Wizard, Swordsman, Gladiator, Archer, etc. Mage
Some characters can actually change classes throughout the game, but most characters just keep one class.
***4.ELEMENT***
A character's element also determines usable magic, and even some other things, such as body structure and class. Here's a clich?d elemental statistic:
*NAME RACE CLASS ELEMENT HEIGHT WEIGHT BODY STRUCTURE BODY TYPE DEMEANOR*
Melvin
Human
Mage
Wind
4' 9''
86 lbs
Medium-
Humanoid
Shy
Seen this kind of character before? Like I said: a clich?d elemental statistic. Wind characters are usually high on the magic scale, but low on the strength and/or HP scale.
***5.HEIGHT***
How tall the character is, plain and simple. This could help you while drawing tiles. Here's my scale:
***Less than 6 feet***
Keep it on one tile.
***Greater than 6 feet, Less than 12 feet***
Feel free to branch up to two tiles.
Etc.
Etc.
Etc.
More physically powerful characters are usually taller, while less physically powerful characters are usually shorter.
***6.WEIGHT***
This is very similar to Height. Weight can contribute to muscular mass, or it could contribute to an It's your choice. However, weight means little unless you put down Body Structure (next category) overweight character.
***7.BODY STRUCTURE***
Body structure relates heavily on a character's physical power. Small characters will do little damage, while large characters will do lots of damage. This is a ?Physical Law of Video Games?. I've created a chart of almost every Body Structure type you'll need to use. There is an ?Overweight? category, but I couldn't figure out where to place it. At the end of the Body Structure Spectrum, you will see some categories marked ? ?. This is just for the sake of keeping the Male/Female anatomy intact. **Male Only**
***THE BODY STRUCTURE SPECTRUM***
***SLIM--***
Very small character. Virtually no muscular structure. Usu. High magic.
*Good for: Fairies(Faeries) and Sprites(the creature)*
***SLIM-***
Teeny, tiny character. Very low physical power.
*Good for: Gnomes and Elves*
***SLIM***
Small character, low physical power, usu. High Magic.
*Good for: Dryads(Forest Spirits), Nymphs, and Naiads(Water spirits)*
***SLIM+***
small character. Low physical power. Relatively
*Good for: Mages and young characters.*
***MEDIUM-***
Low physical power. All-around character.
*Good for: Normal players*
***MEDIUM***
Basic muscular structure. All-around character.
*Good for: Main character*
***MEDIUM+***
Good physical power. All-around character.
*Good for: Warrior/Gladiator*
***SINEWY-***
Good physical power. High speed.
*Good for: Archer, Thief*
***SINEWY***
Great physical power. High speed.
*Good for: Speed Character*
***SINEWY+***
Great physical power. Very high speed.
*Good for: Strong Speed Character*
***BULKY-***
Super physical power. ?Pumped? muscular structure.
*Good for: Warrior/Gladiator and Dragons*
***BULKY***
Super physical power. ?Pumped? muscular structure. Low speed.
*Good for: Dragons, Power Characters*
***MALE ONLY!***
***BULKY+***
Amazing physical power. High HP, defense. Very low speed.
*Good for: Dragons, Power Characters*
***MALE ONLY!***
***BULKY++***
Practically 1-hit-KO attack. Large muscular structure. **EXTREMELY LOW SPEED!**
*Good for: Dragons*
***MALE ONLY!***
***8.BODY TYPE***
Different characters will have different body types. This is another ?Physical Law of Video Games?. For example: A human character will have an upright posture. Whereas a Zombie would have a hunched-over posture. It all depends on the type of character.
***Humanoid***
Upright posture. Walks in the normal style.
***Undead***
Hunched over with head upright. Almost like a limping walk.
***Primate***
The clich?d monkey position. Arms drag on the ground, almost runs like an animal.
***Fairy***
Small wings protrude from the back. Full time use of these wings.
***Dragon***
Large Wings come from the back. Walks upright, or flies.
***Wyvern***
Medium-sized wings replace the hands. These are used in basic motion.
P.S. If you think of a new body type I should add, please
contact me through the private message section of
ToolkitZone.com
***9.DEMEANOR***
A character's demeanor determines all of their lines throughout an entire game. Some Demeanors are:
Shy, Boisterous, , Bold, Prideful, etc. mischievous
***10.PHYSICAL DETAILS***
In the Physical Details section you are basically writing a ?Summary? on your character. Going back to Edward from earlier, we see that his Physical Details were as follows:
.......
Tall, Wears lots of armor. Rather Slow. **PHYSICAL DETAILS:**
See? You don't need to write a novel about your character, just a short, sweet, and to the point bit of information.
***CONCLUSION***
I hope I've taught you how to create an easy way to create detailed characters. I highly recommend you draw up a character before you pixelate, so you have an Idea of what It's supposed to look like.
If you wish to send me a setup, I will gladly draw up a character for you. **NOTE: I will not draw demons, devils, evil entities, or anything of that nature. I also will not let you paste your name on one of my drawings. I keep the originals, so don't try anything!**
