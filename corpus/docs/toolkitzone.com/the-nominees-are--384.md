# The nominees are...

Browse
Most Promising Game
![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1239.png) Although the main focus of the game related awards are projects with releases, we couldn't go a whole month without taking a little time to tip our cap to those games currently in development. The Most Promising Game category is also where you guys have the chance to decide the winner for once as you vote on our shortlist of games. The games listed are those we feel are the most promising in quality and also have a slight chance of actually seeing a completed release. Also, the project must have been started or come into prominence during 2008. I'm sure there will be a bit of debate about this one but voting isn't hidden, so feel free to discuss it all you want. **Voting closed, scroll down to see winner.**
**The nominees are...**
[![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1240.png)](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?game=3482)
After his successful outing with Three Guys No Beer, which recently picked up Best Game Graphics here at the Zone Awards, Brad surprisingly turned to a Sci-Fi setting for his new project.
With his track record in releases, which is impressive as far as the Toolkit community goes, ability to put together professional and play tested games, fun dialogue and ever improving graphics, we say First Death On Mars is one to keep your eye on over the coming months.
[![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1241.png)](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?game=3602)
The Galaxy series from Grindalf has revolutionised how people perceive the capabilities of the RPG Toolkit and the type of games that can be made with it. The Defender, Fighter and Hero installments attracted a bundle of hype and positive reviews, each lying in the top fifteen ranked games and Defender sitting in fourth position just one percent behind the leader Dark Age 2.
The appropiately named final game of the series promises to push RPG Code that little bit further, adding that extra dose of fun and replayability to an already hit game.
[![](http://toolkitzone.com/vault/documents/1242.png)](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?game=3578)
Most of us already know all about EraFlux, the graphically orgasmic project that died and has since been resurrected by Lotusware. However, a retro-styled prequel has sneaked in on the quiet and has been garnering a lot of attention.
Although still in early stages and Lotusware is by no means renowned for seeing through projects, the simplistic look and feel to the game will hopefully liberate the developer from heavy graphic duties to actually see this one out. The game has already taken presidence over EraFlux and we hope the promise shown can be realised in 2009.
**And the winner is...**
Picking up 56% of the vote and winning the Most Promising Game award is Brad with ! Congratulations! Galaxy Final finished second with 34% and third is EraFlux: Dawn 9%. **First Death on Mars**
