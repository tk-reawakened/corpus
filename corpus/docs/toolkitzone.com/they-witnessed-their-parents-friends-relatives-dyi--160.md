# - They witnessed their parents/friends/relatives dying.

HOW TO MAKE AN EVIL GUY IN YOUR GAME 101: BOSSES! BY: YAMMY We all know, in most Role Playing Games, Evil Bosses are imperative. This document shall help you make the most 1337357 evil guy for your game. Whether it is some traitor knight who formed an army of donuts to destroy the main character's village, or a webcam that just got tired of taking pictures. This document focuses mainly on evil bosses, other ones will do lesser evil guys. LAW NUMBER 1: CAPES; Okay, this is a fact. ALL EVIL GUYS MUST WEAR CAPES!!!! If they're not wearing a cape, then they aren't evil and probably will join your side later in the game. The more eviller and poweful the person, the fancier the cape. Evil footsoldiers should have simple straight capes, preferably red. When you get to generals have a more triangular cape and possibly some emblem printed on it. The big final boss must have the humongous cape that is so starched it floated in midair and have the coat of arms of the antagonist printed on the inside. Capes are a big deal for villains. And furthermore, all good guys CANNOT wear capes, or they wouldn't be good, would they? ;) LAW NUMBER 2: LOOKS; There are many different evil guys out there. I'll list the many types and descripe the looks: *Some punk weirdo who grew up in the ghetto and now wants revenge against the Government for no good reason: The character needs lots of tatoos, body piercings, and black clothing. Add a few skulls and chains. These guys are pretty bland, but I've seen them in some games so shall include them. *The fancy rich guy who wants world domination (Young): This guy should have very gelled black hair pulled back. They need to wear long coats, but sometimes sleek suits can do. These guys needs blue eyes of any brightness and a glass of wine in their hand. *The fancy rich guy who wants world domination (Old): This is the senior citizen who wants to rule the world and somehow made a fortune by selling fake rolexes on the streets of New York. Like the younger version, they need highly gelled hair, but grayish. The can get away with any kind of suit, and once again wine is a necessity. *The strong general of an army that wants more power: This guy will have very long hair that strangely stands up. It can be of any color. Dress them up in red or black. These characters will have to have a sword at their waist that should have a golden hilt encrusted with jewels finer that less-fine jewels. A metal cape-holder on their shoulders would be a good thing too, it adds pizzazz! *The super muscular lady who wants to, as you guessed it, rule the world (Will people STOP ruling the world now?!) : This lady should be a former warrior who never got enough respect. In turn, she'll go beserk and try to kill everyone on a mad quest for power. She can wear almost anything but a 60's dress with a lace collar. Short blades and blunt weapons are a must for these evil bosses. *The young flirtacious girl who...wants to rule the world... : This will be the pretty girl of the game, who manipulates men to do her bidding. Strangely, these are probably the strongest bad guys ever. Not only do they have the most troops, but when you battle her in that final room atop the mountain library, she'll transform into her gigantic angel/demon form with a loud harp music and shoot very bright lasers down at you. *The ugly king who wants to extend his empire everywhere for...NO!!!! WORLD DOMINATION!!!! STOP YOU CLICHED VILLAINS!!!! : There once lived a mean old tyrant who dressed in long royal coats and jeweled crowns. He had gray hair and a short beard. Then he took his bright yellow or green eyes and glared at his cronies and commanded them to rule the world like everyone else. *The nefarious Pen that sprays ink on everyone: Nevermind this, few people use that idea. LAW NUMBER 3: Evil laughs; Thinner and more cunning villains should have a fufufufufu laugh. The most powerful and crazy should go mwahahahahahaha. The ugly and strong should go gwahahahahahahahaha. The rich and smooth-talking should go ahhahahahahaha. The annoying lady who you thought was good should go hehehehehe. The disruptive general who is like evil, you know, should go hehhehhehheh. Evil laughs are imperative to evil guys. Along with capes, they are evil without evil laughs. REASONS VILLAINS ARE LIKE EVIL:
- *They witnessed their parents/friends/relatives dying.
- *They are just psycotic in the head.
- *They were treated unfairly by their boss.
- *Their iPod was stolen.
- *They found an easy way for power.
- *They couldn't learn RPGcode.
- *Some eviller than them from the above reasons convinced them to be evil.
- *They are really good but were pocessed to be evil.
- *They were really evil and pocessed to be eviller.
- *They drank too much bleach as a child.
- *Their brain blew up from reading my article. I hope this was helpful to you. I shall be writing more for your enjoyment. Remember, YOU NEED CAPES AND LAUGHS TO BE EVIL!!!!
