Welcome to the first edition of our new newsletter here at TKZ which gives you a monthly round-up of the latest goings on in the game section. Each month I will detail all the releases in the last four weeks, list the top five rated images in that time and also have a special spotlight feature. It's not meant to be an official review magazine or whatever, but simply my look at the months gaming releases. So lets get started with our look back at the month of July... In total there were six new game downloads added to the game section, five of which were brand new demos and the other has the guys at InDream Studios finally adding the TK Land demo. It's good to see a lot of new faces jumping into the tough world of game production, unfortunately most of the games are pretty poor efforts and have little to no actual gameplay. And although it's to be expected with a user's first effort, maybe if the thought of releasing a demo didn't seem to supercede the actual creation of a demo it wouldn't be so bad. My verdict on July? Lots of "look at my first try at using the Toolkit" releases and nothing that can pass as a playable game. Hopefully these developers will use these initial explorations of the Toolkit to create bigger and better things in the future. *personal*

[![](games/phpThumb.php?src=../library/images/docs/668.png&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](games.php?game=978) (Kiren, 6th July) It's an all too common case of someone working on a new game and rushing it to a demo release to simply show off a few boards and cutscenes - no actual substance. There's an artificial loading screen which annoys, but there is a decent enough use of board and sprites to help the opening plot blurb along as opposed to just an MWin. Ripped tiles used, but the high quality sort and the boards arn't completely sparse. The reason it gets a 2 rating is because of what's to come in comparison. Has potential. [Age of Dragons](games.php?game=978)
*For some unexplained reason, but hey it is an RPG, the world is ruled by dragons and the humans are looking to rebel. Five warriors give up their lives to trap these dragons, somehow...magic no doubt, in an unbreakable "seal". And so the game begins, with you being awoken from your sleep by an angel.*
[![](games/phpThumb.php?src=../library/images/docs/669.png&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](games.php?game=1322) (Treefrog_programmer, 14th July) The developer describes it as a tiny demo and, well, it's just that. Default start menu, which lets be honest is normally the first thing you expect to be changed, and probably one of the longest MWin plot detailing ever. The boards fall into about every newbie trap, with huge areas of grass, walls or flooring with little or no detail. No apparent ingame plot either, just lots of wandering around. [Belgariad, The](games.php?game=1322)
*I have no idea, I gave up reading after the third paragraph - you probably will too. Lots of stuff about mother nature creating seven gods, who then create acid rain and something or other. Appears to be copied straight out of another book or game?*
[![](games/phpThumb.php?src=../library/images/docs/670.png&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](games.php?game=1360) (eric11045, 24th July) Okay, so maybe the plot summary was a tad harsh but that really is it. The game consists of two boards, which are seemingly the two demo boards edited a bit, and that's it. Nothing else unless I missed some super secret thingy. I can understand someone wanting to use the demo to further their knowledge and practice with the Toolkit, but why release it as an actual demo? [Dark Stories Zero](games.php?game=1360)
*"Listen to this, this is a story ur mind probably wont process" is how the game begins and boy, is it right! Afterall, how can your mind process a non-existant plot? You're on some planet, you wake up in bed, apparently you want to look for Daisy. You get to the frontdoor and King Matthew, your father, wants to see you because the "Elvish" war is getting ugly...and that's it. Watch out that mind doesn't explode all over the carpet!*
[![](games/phpThumb.php?src=../library/images/docs/671.png&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](games.php?game=1351) (THeanimeguy, 14th July) The opening story is a tad long winded, especially when it's just a message window full of text. Wouldn't be amiss for a little concept art changing in the background or something just to liven it up a tad. Unfortunately, that's as far as I got since the opening board was empty and continually freezing. The game must have worked previously as there are screenshots available and I ask the developer to maybe get around to fixing whatever bug it is. The typo in the game title doesn't help either. [Emerald's Of Earth](games.php?game=1351)
*Before the universe formed, two emeralds were somehow created to represent light and its balance; darkness. Fast forward to the time of man and there are now several emeralds representing such things as water and strength. The final one is magic, which is left unbalanced and is causing chaos among the humans. A man named Max comes across a text that talks of a final emerald that when put with the others creates the much needed balanced for magic, combat (?).*
[![](games/phpThumb.php?src=../library/images/docs/672.png&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](games.php?game=1400) (corim123, 29th July) Default tiles, huge sparse boards and so you begin to think "here we go again". But there are a couple of programming aspects that you don't normally see from new users. First off the use of passwords, given to you upon the completion of tasks, to enter areas (maybe keys are a better concept) and a unique maze task whereby you cannot see the maze and have to guess the next step to take. If you're wrong, you're returned to the start, although this does become tedious quite quickly. There seems to be no real explanation why you have to do these tasks, but although they're not that much of an RPG Code feat, it's still more than what the other demos have to offer! [Jewel Quest](games.php?game=1400)
*In the land of Kiya there is a magical jewel that grants the wishes of those who use it, especially useful in a land with little natural produce to eat or use. However, seven theives attempt to steal the jewel and end up shattering it into a convenient seven pieces. They end up taking one segment each and so the story begins with hero Keachi looking to get that jewel back together again.*
[![](games/phpThumb.php?src=../library/images/docs/673.png&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](games.php?game=1383) (Sander et al, 22nd July) The developers openly say it's just a graphics demo, showing off the various boards and sprites that make up a large part of the TK world, therefore I won't talk about the complete lack of anything to do. As to be expected from Indy and friends the graphics are of a high quality, and even though the game is heavily dated it still brings a smile to the face with the various community figures that are present. Great potential. [TK Land](games.php?game=1383)
*You play a strangely articulate and poetic Spyder who is suffering from nightmarish dreams. You awake with visions of a robot and seek out MrG. And so far that's as much as it discloses, featuring the super cheesy line; "My ninja training allows me to be calm".*
