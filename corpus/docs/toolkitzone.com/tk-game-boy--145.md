# TK Game Boy

HOW TO MAKE AN EVIL GUY IN YOUR GAME 101: TROOPS! BY: YAMMY We all know evil guys are imperative in your games...or they just wouldn't be fun, would they? What good is it fighting a bunch of pink pony princesses or nonetheless...no one? That is why this article covers how to make the lowly evil troops, infamous for their ironic stupidity. Incase you read my other article in this category, I explained the overly cliched ways to make evil bosses. Now we'll cover evil troops in the same format! Yay! Now don't touch that mouse! This article will actually almost be interesting and your game will almost beneift from it. Okay...you game will not benefit under any circumstances, so be reading...or be square. Wait, I think it was triangle. Or circle! Ack! Let's get on with the show...or document...whatever. Olden Thyme Evil Troops: Back in the good old days, the golden years, the mideval times where witches were hung, their lived a rich evil king. He had poor evil troops as you would suspect. At least I hope you would... Nevertheless, when making an old troops, like a low-class knight or something, make them stupid. And weak of course. I'm sorry to sound timist, but low knights of the 1500s are just stupid and weak. No question! So you (Robin Hood, Sir Cumfrence, King Arthur, Samus Aran, Luigi Mario, Zasta Someone) can easily come and cream these guys despite they have thick armor. Well, they fall down in mud puddles, what can you expect? When you beat mideval troops, they just have to give out the big evil guy's plan who was stupid enough to tell it to a guy who was stupid enough to tell you who was stupid enough to fall into their trap. Loop-de-loop! It just wouldn't be an RPG, of course, if there wasn't a stupid mideval guard who told you Evil Guy Anonymous was going to rob the Pink Pony Princess Market...or the bank. Nowadays Evil Troops: Imagine yourself. You're an idiotic lazy guy who spends their whole life sitting on a computer reading my high quality articles. Why thank you. Unforuntately, that would make you a guard. You're just like the old troops except you have an iPod...which you listen to as Wonderwoman comes in and saves the Pink Pony Princess Market from being robbed. Strangely, this happened about 500 years ago. Odd, isn't it? Futuristic Evil Troops: You may be thinking this is another short paragraph about stupid guards. You are highly mistaken. I hate to say this, but these guys are the Uber 1337s of RPGs. They are intelligent, quick (They have light Hypo-Titanium armor, you know!), and unforuntately powerful. Worst of all, there's no Ultima-Mintril armor for you to put on, and no long-named sword and wooden buckler for you to fight with either! How ashamed. Fortunately, you are from a class of magicians 1000 years ago who saved the no bankrupt Pink Pony Princess Market. You can use your powers to short-circuit the iPod Micros of the guards and punch them in the face old-school style! In other words, have fun making a bunch of stupid guys in your games for villains. And if you're making a futuristic RPG, just still make stupid troops so I can beat it! Thank you very much! ;)

Hi there. I am the person creating Astroseries (though not with RPG Toolkit, but Flash, but maybe one day...) I might give you all some tips on making bad guys, but ultimately, it is good to let imagination flow. The evil guys I normally go for are not the old traditional type. In fact, some are not extremely evil, they seek more the practical things. They do not seek to destroy the World, nor the universe. Just to conquer and be Emperors,but almost never destroying the very thing they conquered. Point? Here is where the tips comes: Don't make lame evil guys. See the actual real life. Evil people seldom are stupid. Evil people are ussually hard to defeat, if at all. And often, it is another evil person that beats them. Make your characters realistic. Sure, use all the spells and whistles you like and add some more, but psychologically, make them pretty much normal. Use this king who is massing troops to invade all other kingdoms to expand his own, or this mad scientist who is trying to go back 20 years in time to save a loved one who died (causing a lot of mess through, unwillingly). Make them have some substance. They can be even elegant, fine, with good manners. Evil ones dont always have to be ugly monsters, or laughing psychos. Take a lot from real life, and add some sparkle to them. Let your imagination flow. In Astroseries, there is an evil boss, who will ultimately be betrayed by his master, and will get evil vs evil. Havent made that part yet, but feel free to benefit from the tid bit ideas I gave you. Gee, you can even benefit from Anakin Skywalker!
