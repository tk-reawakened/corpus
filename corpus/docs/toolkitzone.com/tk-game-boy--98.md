# TK Game Boy

This is my first document for the Toolkitzone so do not expect me to be master of the library right away. I will teach you how to make your own toga. All the information I got here is from my Latin class teacher and myself, If you have any remarks, post them as a reply in the reply box in the bottom. It is all explained by the hand of simple paint made drawings that might not be very clear but this is all I can do to make it clear to you. I hope you enjoy this tutorial and that you have a lot of fun making your own toga. first you need a piece of cloth with the size in length of 480 cm and with of 360 cm like shown in this image: Make a circle in this piece of cloth that exactly hits the edges of the cloth. If you want to make it look even more professional you can add a purple stroke around the circle. its all shown in this image: Fold the piece of cloth trough the length so you have half a circle like shown in this image: Now you have the piece of cloth ready for your toga. Now stand in this position with your arms straight to the left and right. Now let two people hold the cloth behind you and you stand right in the middle. Hold your arms above the cloth. In this image I left the 2 helping people away to make it clearer. Let the helping person on your right hand take the end of the piece of cloth and throw it over your right shoulder and pull it to the ground till the corner reaches your feet. Now let the helping person on your left hand take his end of the piece of cloth and let it go under your arm to your neck and then throw it over your right shoulder and pull it to the ground (note: Dont pull to far else it will look stupid.) you now have your toga finished. I hope you have fun with it and make lots of other people laugh at you. ~ P-WURM
