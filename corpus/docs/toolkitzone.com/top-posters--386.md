# Top Posters

Browse
Zone by Numbers
This month isn’t all about handing out awards, it’s also a look back at the previous year in the world of the Toolkit. In addition to the awards they’ll be a few articles, including a short series that spits out a load of trivial, unimportant but somewhat interesting statistical facts about the last 12 months. It’s a number crazed stocking filler, so to speak. It’s pretty easy for me to produce these stats by running database queries, so it’s relatively quick (and kind of fun.. in a geeky way) to calculate these numbers. It’s a statistical overdose, but it doesn’t harm anybody.. I think. Today, we’re looking at the Community section of the website and all statistics are from January 1 to December 5 (a 48 week period for sake of averages). st th
