# Uber Membership Relaunch

By Xavier
The popular uber member scheme is now officially open again nearly a year after its original launch. The scheme rewards anyone who donates to the site with webspace, forum space, special rank and e-mail accounts. After a succesful first run, some of the current batch of uber members are now leaving so we have some spaces to fill. Once again there are two types of donation, monthly and annual, and both at the same amount as before. To entice interest, we're also launching a competition. Anyone who signs up to become an annual uber member before September 6th will be entered into a prize draw for a free domain which can be used as their website and for any e-mail accounts they create.
