# Uber Signups Closed

By Xavier
Last year a new initiative was launched to help pay for the website and its high bandwidth usage called uber membership. This scheme rewarded anyone who donated to the site anually with webspace, forum space, special rank, nd e-mail for the duration of the year. The scheme was succesful and eventually led to monthly scheme which has since had a many signups. The aim was to be at a point whereby the donations paid, in full, the costs of the server both currently and at least the next couple of months, and also the renewal of the two domains. I am happy to state the current batch of monthy donators and several annual members have meant this goal has been reached. As a result, no more uber memberships will be accepted until circumstances change whereby server costs have increased or donations dropped off. Of course, all current uber members will continue to be fully supported and catered for. Thank you to all those who have supported the site with donations during the last few months, it has taken a huge load of my shoulders as webmaster and has meant features such as game hosting can be continually supported. Thanks again!
