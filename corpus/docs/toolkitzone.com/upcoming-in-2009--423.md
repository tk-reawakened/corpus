# Upcoming in 2009

Browse
**Upcoming in 2009**
It's almost March as yet another year gets underway and it's time to lay down the upcoming schedule for the community as far as events go. This list is by no means final, but what's listed here will definitely happen. Hopefully some other things will be added along the way, whether it be organised all-nighters or rpgcode chellenges or other smaller contests.
** Poster Challenge
Past winners; Devastation (2008)
*March 1st - 31st*
The challenge is simple; pick any game from the official TKZ rankings and design a game poster for it. The community votes to decide the winner on the forums.
** Midi Music
**Past winners; Palladium (2007), Pezzer (2006)**
*May 1st - 31st*
The ability to create new and interesting music is a great asset to have and every year we seek to seperate the best from the rest in our annual midi music contest.
** Sprite Off V
Past winners; Khin (IV), Sophia (III), Montai (II), JaK (I)
*July 1st - 31st*
The titans of pixel art clash heads in the fifth edition of our knockout competition; Sprite Off. Contestants are randomly drawn against eachother and given a sprite concept, the contestant with the most votes advances to the next round.
** Short Stories
Past winners; Acra (2008), Talyn (2007), Blob Dude (2005)
*September 1st - 30th*
Every year the community is invited to write their own short story that revolves around three specified plot points. The stories are then voted upon by the community to form a shortlist for an invited judge to decide a winner from. Past winners;
**Zone Awards**
*December 1st - 31st*
The official Toolkit Zone awards event, where the best games, contributions and users of the past year are all recognised.
