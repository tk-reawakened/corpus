This community ultimately exists for the sake of making our own games and so in many ways this is the marquee award. We have shortlisted the three top releases from the year and for the next 24 hours the community can vote on their favourite.

And the winner is...
Grindalf took 57% of the vote with his two games, but it was Eek! that came out on top with 34% and narrowly beating out Asimir's Pancake stack which came in with 31%. Behind were Fishbate the Worm 23%, The Blobs 5% and Garquest 5%.
So, congrats to Grindalf and Lotusware on winning the Game of the Year award!!
