# Webmaster.

Browse
Recent Downtime
First off, huge apologies for the recent downtime! Basically my domain registrar disappeared, leaving me out of pocket and, crucially, unable to renew or transfer my domains. As it happens, it coincided with ToolkitZone.com expiring. Here's a timeline of what happened during the downtime taken from postings made by me on Mystic Software and these forums;
Friday, 7.41pm GMT: "Anyway, the problem right now is my dear registrar keeps telling me I don't have enough funds to renew, despite there being twice as much shown on my remaining domain funds. It deducts the cost then tells me I don't have enough and refunds it. I've sent e-mails off but these guys are notoriously bad at customer support (i.e. they never respond). But I presume this is something they'd want to fix, heh."
Saturday, 2.51pm GMT - Support E-Mail Reply: "Sadly [company] is pretty much defunct now, the original operators having vanished off into the sunset, support has been non-existant for almost a year. If you have recently made a deposit into your account, you should contact paypal/your card issuer and organise claiming it back, as it is unlikely you will be able to spend it."
Saturday, 12.42pm GMT: "While I fight to get the .com back, tell everyone the .net alternative will be working by the end of the day."
Saturday, 5.53pm GMT: "I've now e-mailed enom.com, appears my company are (were) a reseller for them, and asked them to give me the EPP codes required to transfer. See what happens."
Monday, 9.31pm GMT: "Luckily, I tracked down the company my registrar resells for and sent a letter to their legal deparment. Three days later they sent an apology for the registrar's behaviour and sent me the EPP codes I need to transfer my domains out. The frustration doesn't end there though, since it'll take a few days for the transfers to clear and I have to pay a small fee. But there's now light at the end of the tunnel."
Wednesday, 8.29pm GMT: " I managed to expediate the domain transfer since the registrar that apparently now owns my domain (the registrar that, bizarrely, wanted me to transfer the domains *to* them at the start of the week) allowed me to confirm the transfers before the 5-day deadline. Also eNom gave me an extra year's free renewal, which is nice. So .com should be back, depending where you are in the world, within 12 hours. As a result I have to switch over the cookie settings, so you may not be able to stay logged in using the .net address."
