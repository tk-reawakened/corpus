# What is the Expo?

Yep, the damn foolishness of the Summer Expo has come again, and the developing members of the comunity are starting to look ashen faced. This article is a quick little intro to the event with a few pointers to getting the most out of the experience. In the Summer and Winter of every year, for two weeks (three this time round) the community plays host to the biggest game show in the RPG Toolkit calendar! The Expo's! Hosted by the guys at Indreams Studio's, the Expo is a hive of frantic developing, promoting, winning and losing. Basically, it's a two week event, whereby people developing games with the Toolkit can have a showcase to flaunt their game to the rest of the community. In the second week (or third in this years case), the games are put to the test and the public (that's us) can vote for our favourites in several catagories spanning all aspects of development, from Music to Originality. This Summers Expo can be found . This expo's shaping up to have some massive names rolling out showcases. To name but a few: TKLand, Talona, Dark Age EX, Quintessance... the list goes on. As far as the awards go, Dark AgeEX is definately one to look out for. Xorlak is renouned for his ability to take almost every award in sight. However Dogs Diary stands a pretty good chance in the graphics department and you cannot ignore the talents of Talona for storytelling. At the last Expo certainly, there was a lot of talk about the big names dwarfing the smaller developers and many people found this unfair. Unfortunately, I don't sypathise with these moaners. The Expo is a popularity contest yes, but so is the real gaming world. However if you create something astounding, people will notice you. How do you think the big names got to be big? They did different, better, stronger, faster, smarter. Small developers need to do the same. There are quite a few "smaller" name showcases which I'd be looking out for this year. I wont name names 'cos that's unfair, but take a look around everything and you'll see that this year's shaping up to be a corker. That leads me nicely into this next section. Get into the spirit! Last expo everyone complaining of the lack of activity didn't bother visiting anyone elses showcases. Dont do that! Visit everything! The Expo is running for 3 weeks this year, so make the most of it! Play everything, comment on as much as possible and let the developer know what you thought. Another big problem was cheating in the voting. The guys at IDS are assuring us this year that there are more measures in place to stop it, but I personally think it's sad that we need them. The awards are just a bit of fun at the end, and not live or die. It's the two weeks before the awards which matter! Get as much feedback as possible and give others the same. The Expo is full of , not competitors. That said, walking away with an award is certainly a nice bonus! Think of this expo as the Toolkit communities answer to E3! You know in my articles I like to relate things to big business, so here it is. E3 is the biggest game show in the world (second perhaps only to the Tokyo Game Show), and the Expos are our biggest event. Therefore, get as much out of it as you can. It's also a good opportunity to sneak a look at what your rival developers are doing... Know your enemy. Well if that hasn't got you into the Expo mood, nothing will. Enjoy the three week event, send lots of praise and thanks to the guys as IDS for hosting it, vote fairly, visit everything, and check out the Another Side Studio's showcase! [/shameless self publicity]. See you on the boards.
