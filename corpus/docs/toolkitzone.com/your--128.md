I'm DROWNING!!!!!! SOMEONE HELP!!!!!!!!!!" "This is just great!" exclaimed Ifreet. "Wait, I got it!" "Yeah?" "Well...", said Ifreet,"Not what I was thinking but whatever works." Ifreet resisted but Nico gave a hard push and down Ifreet went. Ifreet started falling, not knowing when he would stop, until he landed on a bin full of trash. "AH! GARBAGE!!!!" "Hey!" Nico was outside the bin. "You alright?" "Nico! How'd you get outside so fast?" "I took the stairs." "Stairs? Why didn't you tell me instead of pushing me into that damn chute?" "Quit complaining. Cao responded "There's dirt everywhere. Eat up." "CAO! Don't worry kid, come with us to the village. The Wobbit will take care of everything." "Wobbit? What's a wobbit?" "What?" said Cao. "Mr. Whatamunchkins." Cao looked dumbfounded. "I'm calling you Bob." "Are you going to turn yourselves in?" "No!" "Why not?" "Because we don't want to." Ifreet stepped in, "Come on. You're the ones who robbed the casino and were the ones paying the price." "Yeah well that's life. The good guys finish last. Good things happen to bad people. You get the idea." "That's right." Said Frei. Khin slapped Frei upside the head. "SHADUP!" "Why did you did that for?" "Cuz your no bad ass like us you wuss." "Yes I am." "Then why are you starting to cry?" Frei was starting to tear up. "NO I'M NOT!!!" Frei ran off crying. "So that's it?" asked Nico. "Were just gonna have to take the blame for actions?" "That's right." "Come on Nico." said Ifreet. "Were just waisting our breaths." "Well what do you propose we do now?" "Well, let's appeal this to Xavier." Nico started blankly. "Like I said, what do you propose we do now?" "Let's just go." "Not so fast." said Ashe as Khin and she drew out their weapons. "What is this?" said Ifreet. Ashe continued. "You know our secret hiding place, now we can't allow you leave." "Secret hiding place? But this is just an alley." "Correction, our alley." "That's right." said Khin. "Now get on the floor." Mr. Whatamunchkins looked in awe as he saw the town, which was made of great stones and gems. "Wow. This is just like an RPG game." "That's right." said Danger. "With inns and everything." "When can we see the Wabbit?" "That's Wobbit your little punk." "Cao, calm down." said Danger. "Go get something to drink will ya?" "Fine. Give me GP." "No. You got plenty of money." "That's not the point." Danger walked over to Mr. Whatamunchkins. "Why don't you explore a bit.
