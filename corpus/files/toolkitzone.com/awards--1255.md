# Awards

![](games/phpThumb.php?src=../fileman/images/490.PNG&iar=on&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)
**Awards**
A rather unique pack of three tilesets that contains thousands of colour variations for medals and ribbons. Quality isn't high but they are just medals, and if you're making an RPG about athletics then you've just hit gold (pardon the pun). (4617 tiles)
