# CBS IV: Battle Phase

Yet another installment of the CBS series. This time, Cedrick gets into the nitty gritty of managing the battle phase. ** Updated **
17 March, 2005
Version 1.1 Updates 1. Changes the line # ("You Win.") To #If(battle!==0) {# ("You Win.")} #If(battle!==2) {#BMwin("Annihilated...")} I used the wrong Method to call (in bold to show emphasis). The Battle Message that says "You Win" will still be displayed even if your characters are all dead, so an #If statement is necessary. I also add another conditional statement to display "Annihilated..." when all players died. 2. I also added a randomizer at method #BattleLoop. Based from the lines of the method,player 0 will always go first,then 1,2,3,4,10,11,12. The randomizer is necessary so that this order of turn will be quashed. 3. Added some more notes and explanations. 4. And I logged in using my former account. Darn
