# Classic Songs and Sounds

I moved this to the effects section as there aren't really many songs in here - more sound effects - they are however all MIDIs. Whilst there are a few sounds that may be useful, there are some where it sound like someone has had a midi keyboard, got cross with it and thrown it down the stairs, whilst recording from it. When the saints go marching in is particularly badly played. A strange pack - the mario doorbell tune is interesting, thats about it
09 December, 2004
