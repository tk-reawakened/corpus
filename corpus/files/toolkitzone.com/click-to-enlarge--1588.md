Fury's Custom Systems
Fury's Custom Systems includes sets of classes for a custom item system and for a custom menu system. Each comes with a sample to demonstrate the features of the custom systems, as well as, how you use them. The samples cannot be used as full-fledged systems, but are merely tech-demos for what you may be able to accomplish when you use these in your own game. Requires .
