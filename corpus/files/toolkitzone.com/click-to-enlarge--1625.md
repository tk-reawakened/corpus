There's this problem in that the program assumes that you have all your slots filled up. Same goes for players, for some parts it assumes you have a full party rather than checking. This also causes crashes. This menu is in no way a complete menu, but it's a nice start. Complete menu examples are ones that can be plopped into a game, and immediately you can use it. From there it's up to the coder to customize it to suit his needs.
