# Click to enlarge

Stitch Tiles (based on Lilo & Stitch)
Neat collection of 4 original (Not Ripped) tile sets based on the movie Lilo and Stich. Includes sprites, effect animations, pod icons, DNA tiles and building tiles for Jumba's ship. The sprites are animated for walking and include Stitch(626), 625, 627, Angel (624) and more. UPDATE: Current update is now live, and download link is working.
[![TBD](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/57800253540c5b4544e21f.png&w=60&h=60&iar=false&bw=1)](forum/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=207)
My tileset contains (as stated above) walking graphics for Stitch (626), 625, Angel (624), and 627. The tileset for Jumba's spaceship also contains graphics for a background; but, because they are blown up 3x, the characters may stand on the wall. In the items tileset, I have some tiles for a smaller version of a pod, the Experiment Computer, and a nuclear reactor (on & off). I am currently working on an update for the character graphics that will include Splodyhead (619), Plasmoid (617), Yaarp (613), and Heat (609). I plan to update the file untill I have the entire experiment set complete (Note for future reference: currently, there are no experiments in the 400 series.)
