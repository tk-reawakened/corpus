TSTExtract
Robust tileset editor, allowing you to manage your sets. Main features include auto-resizing to fit window, tile movement/deletion, export/batch export, merge multiple sets and other nice things. Definately the most complete external tileset editor out there - and getting better every release! Good work, Andreas!
