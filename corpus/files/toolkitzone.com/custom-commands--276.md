# Custom Commands

Nameless when I recieved it I just couldn't leave well enough alone. Custom commands give an interesting twist of originality to the idea of method calls. Two thumbs way up to Dont_Think_So
posted by on 2004-03-15 15:02:46 [Son Gotsu](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=702)
Cons: NOT for the newbie programmer. Perhaps it would have looked nicer (and more ordered) as HTML or MSWord DOC or RTF even. Dont_Think_So neglected to mention that #Redirect is not a standard RPGCode Command. Pros: Without a doubt the BEST Tutorial written for custom commands. Methods displayed can be Cut and Pasted directly into code. Adds a very useful look at a General Programming Concept that many advanced programmer aren't even aware of. #Redirect Method actually saves memory!
