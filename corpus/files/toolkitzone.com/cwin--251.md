# Cwin

Very perty Mwin Clone. Includes a place to put your characters profile, characters name, and 7 lines of text. Defenatly not the most robust Mwin clone ever but if you looking on how to make your own this would be a nice place to start. needs 800X600 fullscreen or higher for this example to work correctly. version 1.1 sports a spiffy new mwin background.
