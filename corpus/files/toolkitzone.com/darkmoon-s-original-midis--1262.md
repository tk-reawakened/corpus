# DarkMoon's Original Midis

A set of 14 original midis which, on the whole, are very good. Most are quite catchy, and although in general they do tend to lack a little variation in the melody, they are nevertheless well put together and nice to listen to. Well worth a download, and excellent if you want to use some midis in you game which not everyone has heard a thousand times before
