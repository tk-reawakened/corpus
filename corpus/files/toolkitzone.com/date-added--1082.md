Word Wrap
A word wrap program. ** Updated **
[![TBD](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/870024045451d5586001.png&w=60&h=60&iar=false&bw=1)](forum/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=1188)
And Word Wrap means...?
[![TBD](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/854920603444e76d660439.png&w=60&h=60&iar=false&bw=1)](forum/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=76)
what else? it is used for custom Message windows to wrap text around lines so you don't have to make new #mwin commands every time you want a new line of text. Lets say you got a line of text of this nature: "My sources tell me that your are going to be a wonderful leader to the rebels in the future." Well on a message window this would go off the end and a certain font. so what this method lets you do is make the words wrap to the next line. lets say you have a limit of 20 characters on one line well then the output of that text would be like this My sources tell me that your are going to be a wonderful leader to the rebels in the future.
[![TBD](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/854920603444e76d660439.png&w=60&h=60&iar=false&bw=1)](forum/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=76)
The updated version allows for text to be word wrapped by width specified in pixels. This was included because of a so called "bug" Colin reported. Though I am greatful for the suggestion it was not actually a bug yet the method worked as intended. Now the .prg can be considered a "Method Pack" as it contains 2 methods one that includes the old form of the word wrap method specified by length of the words used and now the new and improved method that allows for wrapping achieved by pixel size of the text.TBD
