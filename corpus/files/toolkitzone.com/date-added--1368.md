# Date Added

Menu Backgrounds Pack 1
Pack of ten backgrounds for your menu that differ in colour and texture. If creating custom backgrounds isn't your thing, then one of these may just add that extra bit of gloss to your menu system.
[![TBD](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/660976929452886ea89607.gif&w=60&h=60&iar=false&bw=1)](forum/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=1384)
Here's the instructons on how to replace the default menu graphic with one of the graphics included in the Pack: 1. Rename the graphic file that you want use as 'layout.gif'. 2. Copy the graphic titled 'layout.gif' that you just renamed. 3. Navigate to your Toolkit3 folder (C:\Program Files\Toolkit3\) 4. Enter to 'game' folder. (C:\Program Files\Toolkit3\game\) 5. Enter your own game folder. (C:\Program Files\Toolkit3\game\MyGame\) 6. Enter the Bitmap folder. (C:\Program Files\Toolkit3\game\MyGame\Bitmap\) 7. Paste the copied 'layout.gif' in the Bitmap folder, overwriting the default 'layout.gif'. Also, if you have any requests for a background with a specific color or theme in mind, just post a comment here or PM me and I'll get it in the next Pack.
