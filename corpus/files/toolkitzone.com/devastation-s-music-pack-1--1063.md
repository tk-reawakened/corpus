# Devastation's Music Pack 1

A small pack of 3 mp3's. Not incredible, and not really that much better than you could do with a midi. Don't get me wrong, theyre pretty damn good, especially the second two, but a bit too large files really. I'd recommend these to people with high speed internet access, as theyre better than most non-pro made tunes. Look forward to the next pack.
