![](games/phpThumb.php?src=../fileman/images/211.gif&iar=on&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)
**Doc Wellsy's Isometric Tutorial**
A great tutorial explaining the technique used to create his great looking Iso board designs. Create an isometric house with textures and other features. The tutorial gives steps for both PSP and MS Paint, but could be applied to virtualy any graphic editor.
