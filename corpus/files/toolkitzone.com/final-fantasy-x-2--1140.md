# Final Fantasy X-2

A nice set of 22 midis - my favourite is the band version of Eternity - very nice indeed. Shuyin's theme is wonderfully dark and ominous- excellent haunted house stuff. not really much else to say about these except that they're not the best FF midis ever, but still very good.
