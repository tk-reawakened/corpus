# How to Make Vehicles

One of the weirder formats for a tutorial I have seen but if it inspires you, go for it. It's over a rather complicated subject of using a vehicle that you can get in and out of and only be able to use it on certain terrain. Does an alright job of explaining it but could of done without all the crossthroughs and underlines.
