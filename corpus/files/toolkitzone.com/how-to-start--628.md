# How To Start

This fourteen page document takes you through, step by step, the initial creation of your game. From setting the project up, to creating your character graphics, to putting your first board together - it's all there. William's native tongue isn't English and at times it shows, but aside from the few grammatical short comings he puts his view across well and includes images to help guide you through the process. A recommended read for those new to the Toolkit.
