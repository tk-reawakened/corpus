# Huge Collection

As the title suggests - a rather large selection of midis - a comprehensive collection if you are just starting a game and would like a wide variety for any of your musical needs - there are 318 in here, meaning that theres something for everyone, including tunes from FF, Castlevania, Zelda, and many more...
