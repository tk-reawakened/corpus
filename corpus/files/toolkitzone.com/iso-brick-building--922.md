# Iso - Brick Building

First set on the site to make use of the new isometric tileset file type introduced in the 3.0.4 release, providing the basic tiles to construct a brick building. The reason for the low rating is because, on its own, it's not the most expansive or useful set - but it is good quality, and a nice place to start for those looking to explore isometric tiles. (17 tiles)
09 December, 2004
Just to let people know, I do plan on expanding this out to have windows, doors, and so on. As well as maybe even more buildings that aren't so.... square... just thought I would let you know, that this isn't dead, but it may take some time for me to do stuff to it.
