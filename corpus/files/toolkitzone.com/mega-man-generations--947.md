# Mega Man Generations

45 midis in this pack - some, as always, better than others, but on the whole pretty good. A slightly Bomber-man-esque techno feel to them - I guess that's what you'd expect from mega man though right ;) Well worth a look if you're after battle music or just some fairly upbeat background music etc.
21 December, 2004
Personally I think the Wily ones and the Intro and Intro Stages are the best.
