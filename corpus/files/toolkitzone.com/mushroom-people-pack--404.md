![](games/phpThumb.php?src=../fileman/images/221.gif&iar=on&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)
**Mushroom People Pack**
Surprisingly small tileset from Linkin. In this set he's ripped 7 mushroom people sprites from one of the Super Mario Bros games. I think it's SMB2, but I could be wrong. (108 tiles)
