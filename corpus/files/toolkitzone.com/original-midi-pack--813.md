# Original MIDI Pack

A small selection (6) of good quality tracks - all original, which makes a nice change, and I'm pleased to say that some of them (I particularly liked 'Dungeon') are better than a lot of the professionally made tunes we get. Well worth the download - if only for a taste of Aaron's musical style. As with a lot of packs, pity there are so few tracks - oh well, good stuff, and please credit the authors name if you use thes in your game.
