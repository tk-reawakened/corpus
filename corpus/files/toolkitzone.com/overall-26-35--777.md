# Overall: 26/35

-Zs Game Review for Just Another Day-
: All graphics are original. The sprite is a little messed up in the waist and the other graphics dont have much detail but theyre okay. **Graphics 4/5**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/fileman/images/339.png)
: Great Menu, Umm I cant say anything about a battle system cause there was none, The board were also nice and proportioned so It was kind of easy to get that score. **Design 5/5**
: Most of the music was ripped from Queen and other bands but mostly Queen, but the songs Thing did rocked, also nice sound effects too. **Music 3/5**
: The story is kind of stupid and well thats it. **Story 2/5**
: The story ends really soon all you have to do is go to a sewage plant and do a maze, I still dont know how a ring can go down a drain and end up caught in a lift door. The Nifty little programs made this game special. **Game play 3/5**
: All I can say is AWSOME! Everything was cool and nifty, like the etch-a-sketch program that he made. **Programming 5/5**
: The only bug I found was in the maze game bought at the store, otherwise everything was perfect! **Debugging 4/5**
This game was fun. Period. **Zs Fun-O-Meter: 86%**
**Overall: 26/35**
