**The Dragon of Zadran**
** Developed by bradsequin Review by Brad Forbes (MrB)
**Introductory Impressions**
My very first impression of this game was the 8.43 (14 votes) rating on Mystic Software. Obviously anyone would consider that a high score, and according to the Mystic Software rating descriptions, that means the game is between Excellent and Very Good. Lets just say, thanks to me, the game is now has a 7.93 rating after I gave it a 1. Why so harsh? Well, it says a rating of 1 would describe the game as Awful, and that is exactly what this game is.
**Graphics :: 2**
The worst-looking TK game I have ever played. All default tiles, with a few miserable attempts at original tiles. The battle animations were randomly different every battle, ranging from a static leaf to a huge (yet distorted) dragon/bat creature breathing fire. Those animations were just for your basic attack command. The magic animations were the same. Also, none of the animations or sprites had a set transparency color, so an ugly white background stuck out somewhere on the screen during nearly the entire game.
**Gameplay :: 2**
The worst gameplay in a TK game I have ever played. For the entire game I killed the enemies in one, maybe two hits. They would counter my attacks by hitting me by 1 every time. Then I get to the second or third boss and I hit him by 1, then he hits me by 1. This goes on until he uses a special move that hits by over 100 (killing whatever character he decides to hit). The sequence repeats until all my characters are dead. Of course, theres no way to kill the boss, so thats where the game ended for me, which was about 10 minutes into the game.
**Music :: 4**
Though some of the music seemed original and decent, the developer obviously saved all the songs as .wav (they sounded originally recorded and possibly played, so he was probably forced to save as .wav), so this game that consist of default everything is a 20 MB download.
**Story :: 2**
The game begins with a text introduction to the confusing and shallow story, which, of course, is filled with typos and grammar errors against a black message window screen. Then the rest of the story comes in randomly that you accidentally miss because it pops up suddenly, and youre still quickly pressing the keyboard arrows in the intention of walking.
**Tilt :: 2**
Except for a French game consisting of one board, no fighting, and no story I played about two years ago, this was the worst TK game I have ever played. I wont ramble on even more about why.
**Overall Rating :: 2.4**
** Worth the download? Nope.
