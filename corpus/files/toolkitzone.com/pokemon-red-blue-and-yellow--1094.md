# Pokemon- Red, Blue, and Yellow

119 midis in here from the red, blue and yellow versions of the games. Not the first pokemon pack we've had, but if you're really dying for a totally complete colection then get them quick. Not the most technically brilliant tunes out there, and perhaps a little too well known to be included in a game, but a nice mix here.
