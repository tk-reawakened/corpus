# Rock's Backround Music 1

21 wonderful midis in this pack. A bit different to the average pack - probably because they were composed by Bjorn Lynne - presumably either just for fun or for some game - either way, they not the usual rips, and have a lot more imagination in them than many packs even from professional games. Good stuff
