# Rock's Sound Effects 1

Not to be misread as 'rock sound effects' - this is an excellent and varied pack, containing nature effects such as storms and birds, weapon effects - that is guns (no swords Im affraid), and miscellaneous effect, some of which would be ideal for a mini-game game show (as in banjo kazooie). All in all, one of the best packs in this section - 76 files in all.
22 May, 2005
I was in need for sound effects, but these are Great!
