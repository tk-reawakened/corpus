# SFII: Screams and Grunts

Awesome collection of SF2 midis - all the ones I can think of, although there dont seem to as many as I would have thought. These are the character voices by the way - including the comentator, with not only 'you' 'win', but also the names of all the different countries. Could be handy, although no matter what you used it for, it would obviously (as in not subtle) be ripped. A classic collection though
