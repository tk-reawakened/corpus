# Shining Force 1

10 midis here - very similar to the other pack, and equally worth downloading. Nothing groundbreaking here, but a nice alternative to some of the more common tunes flying around the place. I'd recommend downloading both packs if you want to use them in a game, just to give you a bit more to work with.
