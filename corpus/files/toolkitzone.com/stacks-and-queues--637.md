Ive taken Computer Systems in college, so this is how it works: Stack: Picture a pile of plates set on top of one another. When you take the bottom plate everything shifts down one. Now because of this you have one less item in the stack which makes room to add onto. Basically, the first one in is the first one out. Queue: Picture a line at the bank or something. The first person to leave the queue lets everyone else move up one. Its almost the same idea as a stack where you can pop, add, and push objects in the queue. They are all sorting algorithms in essence.
