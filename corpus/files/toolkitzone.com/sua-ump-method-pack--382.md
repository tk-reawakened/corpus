# Sua-ump Method Pack

Several misc methods. Including but not limited to a bank and a custom Mwin. Also has a slope function that will get the slope of a line between 2 points. I would improve on this function by either making a method pack that handled fractions or changing it to a decimal. the later is the easiest :).
