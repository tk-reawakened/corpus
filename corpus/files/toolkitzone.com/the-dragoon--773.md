# The Dragoon

Even though I should, I don't play very many TK games that often. This is my first one I've reviewed in six months, and I'm quite happy with the way Toolkit games are being produced these days. When I first started they were nothing more then a compilation of tiles (more often than not crappy tiles) with dialouge and warp programs. Now decent work is being put into everything these days, and The Dragoon is one of those examples. Argh, your usual ripped RPGMaker graphics. A fairly adequate job at making the overal game look pretty. Moreover, the menu graphics and custom bitmaps put into this game are quite astonishing. Very well done there. If another ripped tileset was used (perhaps a creation of another TK artist) a 4 would be seen here, and decent original tiles will see a perfect 5. Good job.
Graphics: 3/5
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/fileman/images/336.jpg)
The design in Toolkit games have come a long way since when I first started reviewing games. The Dragoon sports a beautiful menu system and a nice battle engine that beats the internal one in the anti-crap contest. Good variety in terms of attacks and spells, and the whole choice between stamina and MP added whole new vaults of strategy. I was also quite pleased with the fact that the house I went into did not fill the screen up! More and more people are listening to me! Yay! The cave design seemed to get confusing at times, but it was alright. Keep up with the work. I would also like to add that I liked the keyboard-based interface. Hard to pull off, but this game did. Again, excellent! I do not know if the music was ripped or if it was original. However, the fact that I couldn't tell means that the creator did a good job at choosing music that people did not know about if it was ripped. I liked the music, though this one song (don't recall where it was) sorta got on my nerves. The annoying thing about this game was the bleep when you changed your selection in the menu or in battles. That sound effect needs to be EQued so it isn't so high pitched (but not removed, as such a bleep, albeit less annoying, is in FF games). Only problem I found in this catagory. I am not the biggest fan of stories. However, I didn't quite find this story engaging enough. At the start of the game, the player is thrown into this mission that he knows very little about (and how did the two characters start in the middle of the cave?) and small tidbits were added here and there. Possibly can be worked out in future versions, or more revealed as the story progresses. A key factor to work on in future demos (but I'm sure that further releases will naturally fix this). Because of the lack of the internal battle system, I knew this game was going to be engaging battle-wise. And it was. Almost too much however. The enemies in the beginning seemed a bit too hard (grey rats wrecking havoc on a full grown human. Oh no!) until Dragoon abilities started coming in and the third character was added into the party. Lots of strategy within the battles, and the maze-like labrynths of the cave and the hill were sorta fun to go through. However, after talking to the general, the game just kinda stops....... Might have been my system, but I encountered a lot of graphical glitches. For example I would be in a battle, then all of a sudden the board that I was originally on would appear, and the battle would keep going. There was also a problem with the Return button on the menu, so in order to leave I'd have to hit escape. I recalled another glaring bug, but don't remember what it was. A little help here is needed as well. At least no typos!
Design: 5/5
