# The Legend of Zelda: FSA

I'd not heard the Four Swords midi's before, and to be honest there's nothing ground breaking in these 9 midis. The overworld theme is a remix of the classic tune. Some are a little darker and spookier than I would usually expect, but still fairly standard stuff.
