# Various

Hmmm- well, a bit lacking in the melody department - some good drum beats once some of the tracks get going though. The neo-classical tracks made my ears bleed - particularly the piano- rather too many notes and not enough structure. 'O O' was the nicest by far, and for the most part, sounded rather good - there was the odd note here and there which didn't seem to fit in, giving the tune a slightly flat sound. All in all, some interesting ideas- keep it up.
