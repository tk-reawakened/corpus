# Various

A pack of 5 original midis - very varied selection. The tunes aren't the greatest I've ever heard, but aren't bad for originals. Walk In The Park is probably the best, and has quite a nice rythm to it. not bad, but not a huge amount to put into a game if thats what you would be downloading them for. Just missed a three star rating by a whisker due to the fact that there aren't a huge number of files in the pack.
