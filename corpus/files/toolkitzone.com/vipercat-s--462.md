**If you have the #Prompt cheat system these are some good cheats for yah,**
**#If(You==don't have cheat system)Then I'll give it to you here-**
**#Prompt("Enter your cheat-",cheat$)**
**#if(cheat$="potioninf")**
**#giveitem("potion.itm")**
