# Zelda- Links Awakening

An excellent set of midis from LoZ:LA, arguably one of the best Zelda games of all time. The music is typical of Zelda games and has gone on to be somewhat of a standard in RPGs. Excellent dungeon music, outdoor music and boss music. Well worth downloading if you dont already have it, although probably not the most original tunes you could put in a game.
