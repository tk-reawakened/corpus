# Zelda - Majora's Mask

One of the few missing sets we were missing of the Zelda series, and, I'm pleased to say a very high quality selection of 31 midis it is too. I always thought Majora's Mask had a very different kind of music to the other games, but still very Zelda-esque. If you haven't heard these before then they're well worth listening to- an excellent pack
