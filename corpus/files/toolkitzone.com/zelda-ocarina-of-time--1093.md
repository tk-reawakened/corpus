# Zelda- Ocarina of Time

A massive 194 midis in this comprehensive pack. If you like the music from OoT then you'll love this pack - everythings in here - even the little fanfare it plays when you learn something new, and a couple of remixed tunes too. Something for all Zelda fans in here, and probably others as well.
15 June, 2005
Not that anyone cares, (:P) but I spent about two months scouring the internet for these. A lot of credit goes to VGmusic.com, so be sure to go there if you ever need a hard-to-find midi.
19 June, 2005
The time and effort shows - I think this is probably the most complete midi set I've seen.
