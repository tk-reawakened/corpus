### Ersuis · 2001-12-08 16:00 · #A1002-1429

**Feedback...(more)**

**1) The character movement is way too choppy. All too unprofessional. Been said before, I know, but it makes RPGMaker 2000 look a lot better.**
Agreed. I don't seem to have that many problems, but there are instances of lag and stuff, though. I'm willing to bet that CBM will have that fixed in a couple releases, but for now (unfortunately), we'll just have to deal with it. :\
**2) There aren't enough fighting sprites for characters, and though I suppose programming could fix that, it'd take just way to long. Speaking of which. . .**
There has been cries for more slots for graphics lately, so more graphics slots is another thing I'm willing to bet will be in a future release. Maybe CBM could have it so that you could choose how many slots you want for each thing, from between 4 and 10 or so...
**3) How about a point and click programming feature for Toolkit, like in RPGM2k? It could also have all sorts of neat things, like multiple codes at the same time for neat effects that take a while to program otherwise, and the like.**
The internal editor on the Toolkit has something somewhat like that, where you double click commands to add them into your code, but it doesn't exactly generate your stuff. I'm sure that in the new crop of RPGCode editors, though, there will be some generators and ways to make programming easier.
