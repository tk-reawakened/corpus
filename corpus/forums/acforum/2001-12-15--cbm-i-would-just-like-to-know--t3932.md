### GAME GUY · 2001-12-15 17:37 · #A1001-3946

**WELL.....I KNOW HOW TTTO MAKE 3D CHARACTERS.....**

Here's how. Download the ANIM8TOR at WWW.anim8tor.com.
Then after downloading it at the top of the screen it should say object mode. OK now By using the shpes on the side of the screen(sphere, cylinder etc.) draw your character. Then after drawing your character you can give it some color by clicking the options menu, and then clicking materials. Next, just chose your colors and you're done! SIMPLE ENOUGH? Then go to the settings option and click object. Give your object a name, and then go to the figure mode and click build. A little menu should pop up and press charater. Then just select the character/object you made. The character should appear on the screen, and just zoom the screen in a whole bunch. The only thing left to do is render it to a file. save it as a .bmp. Open paint on your computer, and select the character you just made in anim8tor. You can add some extra features to it if you want. Save it. After saving it go to your start menu and click explore. Find the Bmp you made, and select it.
Next, click edit and paste it. Now open up microsoft word
and paste your graphic there. After doing that resize it so it's 25x25. Click on what you just resized, and copy it into paint. Then save it again. Now open up the toolkit ,and
click edit tile. Once you're in the tile editor select import. A menu should pop up, and then you click import bmp.
Import the bmp, and hit redraw and you're done. Just be sure to save it. There is an article at the wasteland, if you still need more help.... HOPE THIS HELPS! =D
