# My thorough review on D&C Chapter 1.1(more)

### Chris Reeves · 2001-12-15 17:48 · #A1000-7542

Okay people, here's my review on Deceit And Conquest Chapter 1.1
As most of you know, D&C Chapter 1.1 is not a completley new game, it is just a complete makeover of it's original game, Chapter 1. Although D&C falls to be my 3rd most favorite game (which is damn good) falling behind Godiva Alpha and DarkAge, it is definatley the most well put together game I've seen made in the Toolkit. The maker of this project obviously knows what he's doing.
The new makeover consists of a new intro, 800x600 resolution, a brand new message window with True Type font, a new mouse driven menu system created by one of the best, Spyder, a brand new mouse driven, turn based battle system made my MrG, larger characters and npc's, new tiles and new profile pics, old file formats done away with to improve efficiency, and some of the cutscenes have been changed.
Let me know if I'm missing anything...
While reviewing this game, I was mainly only looking at how the game had improved from it's old predicessor, and I was impressed.
**New Graphics**
Cyrus made a huge improvement with the graphics, making them look more realistic and more impressive. The 800x600 conversion was my favorite feature though, it just made everything "cleaner". The game being brought to full-screen mode also made the game look more proffesional and less RM2kish. Good Job Cy!
New Graphics Rating: 10/10
**New Features**
The new message window was very sleek and nice looking. However, it would have helped that it didn't cover some of the players sometimes. It should appear in different places when there is a possibility of it covering anyone. Also, the menu system was nice, but that style is starting to become overused for me to be impressed with it. The battle system, though nearly bug-free and stable, was not impressive at all. Although the buttons and fight menu looked nice, it really wasn't any different from the old battle system. I think what would make this battle system better would be to just spice things up a bit. Add a cursor pointing to your target of whom you're going to attack. Battle animations sure wouldn't hurt, even sound effects would make it better, just that alone would improve it tremendously. You can then go in and add other things like making a seperate message window for battle stats, adding in a "Win Battle" song, critical hits, misses, blocks, another message window to notify the player of what spell is being casted, like it saying "Cross Slash" in a seperate kind of message window before he does the spell, and special move animations and sound effects.
New Features Rating: 6/10
Basically the improvements are worth the download, it has better graphics, and offers new things and suprises, but D&C still suffers from being a little on the dull side. Still on of the best games around.

**Thanks for the review =)**

I'm sorry you werent happier with it.
There have been a lot of subtle little improvements that I never bothered to mention. My hope was that the player would just enjoy the game more and not even really notice how it was better.
I dunno if you remember but the old Ch1 had some major gameplay flaws.
I did a lot of testing and reworking to improve that...and even added an entire new village and 3 sub quests...though they are very small.
The battle system is now turn based so its a little different. I think it makes the game play a lot differently since you dont have to hurry up and make a decision about what to do...and every player has an indiviual turn.
Both MrG and myself are very interested in suggestions though so we are paying attention to what everyone has to say.
I'll be putting together a final patch for Ch1.1 as soon as I get Chris to send me an updated Trans2.exe that fixed the WinNT crashing bug. In that I'll fix a few other small things that I have noticed while playing...such as that mesage window covering the player thing you talked about.
Anyway, Thanks a million for the review.
Hope you'll enjoy this when all is said and done.
I realize that its just a improved version of the old Ch1 but heh...that was my intention.
