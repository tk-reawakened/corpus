### Daedalus · 2002-02-04 15:41 · #A1000-15985

**Not too bad...**

...But I must say I resent the stereotypical Brittish character:
"Holikin, England-
He is a very wealthy man with amazing fire powers. He was known to be an intelligent man too but he did not seem to pass many subjects in school. He left England so he could get a better education in America but had no success here either. He later found out he was a very good fighter when he joined a martial arts class."
Not everybody in Britain is wealthy and lives in cavernous mansions with cherry and gargoyles and hounds. We're ordinary guys, not all rich or posh.
But other than these little beefs with that character, I think this looks like a pretty good game. A nice concept.
You might want to add a bit more to the site, and maybe lose the italics too.
