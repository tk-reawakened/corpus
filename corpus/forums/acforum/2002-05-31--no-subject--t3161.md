### vagrant · 2002-06-03 09:33 · #A1002-3179

**I don't think so... &gt;more&lt;**

It's a poem in the Lord of the Rings (more specifically The Fellowship of the Rings). Gandalf leaves a letter for Frodo at the Prancing Pony. In it he tells Frodo of Strider (aka Aragorn, Elfstone, Longshanks, etc.) and to make sure it's the real Strider. He also writes that poem in the letter. It's about Strider and he recites part of that poem to Frodo to make him believe he is the real Strider.

---

*All that is gold does not glitter,
Not all those who wander are lost;
The old that is strong does not wither,
Deep roots are not reached by the frost.
From the ashes a fire shall be woken,
A light from the shadows shall spring;
Renewed shall be blade that was broken,
The crownless again shall be king.
- From The Lord of The Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring
