# small drag and drop board on the big board

### grafxguy · 2002-06-01 27:07 · #A1002-3170

To avoid having to create each board tile by tile, have a drag and drop function that will allow you to place a small board (perhaps a house or item shop that is continually used) into the larger board and replace the tiles currently there. I have many houses and item shops that I use more than once. Having to retile them every time can be tiring and tedious.

### vagrant · 2002-06-02 07:51 · #A1002-3172

**Or you could just do this &gt;more&lt;**

Open the one shop you have saved and redo it like you want it and save it as a new board.
