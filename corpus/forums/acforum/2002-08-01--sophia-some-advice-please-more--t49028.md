### sophia · 2002-08-01 31:06 · #A1000-49050

**humm... although I've use bitmap backgrounds..**

The only thing I think you should notice is the size of your final image will depend on what resolution your game runs in, like mine is 640x480, and the image I use is 608x352 pixel.(not sure about other resolution, I think it's in the help files somehwere)
As for color and stuff like what resolution to scann your drawing, I'll say whatever looks good on your screen will probably look good on others... ( not very helpful, I know... but every artist works quite differently and you should test things out just to see what works best for you)
Note: bitmap background is nice, but animated tile that has transparentcy in it will look very bad on a bitmap board... and end up with result similar to #put commands.
