### gamerzero · 2002-08-17 22:19 · #A1002-3997

I don't have all the answers, but here are a few.
Board Contants: A unchangeable variable that exists on that board. Can be used for custom battle system or menus or anything else you can think of.
Default Battle/Menu customization: Not possible because the are written in VB. If you want different BS/menus, write one.
Tile Editor: It has a color selector. And gradient tools are not a good idea, read any article on sprite or image creation to find out why.
