### noadi · 2003-06-09 22:12 · #A1002-995

**(more)**

I think you can choose to make an entire board isometric not just single tiles. Also, importing normal boards an making them iso would look bad, take a sceenshot of a normal board, put it a graphics program and skew it by 30 degrees you'll see the problem.
As for the vanishing point there is a slight problem with that. In order for there to be a horizon (which is what a vanishing point is, the angle has to be small and the view very big (like several miles if it actual size). Really it's too specialized a feature for most people to want to use it the best idea is try to find a trick to fake it.
