**DirectX doesn't necessarily make...(more)**

It any easier to implement multiplayer. There's a ton of issues to deal with when you have multiplayer graphical games, like synchronizing the graphics for all the players, character management, making sure data gets to a player, making sure that data that's sent to a player isn't so big that the next packet isn't ready to send by the time that it's 1/2 way there... the list goes on...
