### noadi · 2003-07-08 13:25 · #A990-2782

**Any image format the TK supports can be used but (more)**

I'd suggest using bmp, png, or gif formats. Jpg loses quality due to compression so your transparency would be off using jpg. Png or gif are the best choices because their file sizes are much smaller than bmps and they don't have lossy problems like jpg.
