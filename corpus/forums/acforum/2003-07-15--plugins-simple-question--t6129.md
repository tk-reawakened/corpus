### soc · 2003-07-15 20:35 · #A1001-6135

**WAVs....**

Are natively supported by the TK, however, I remember having some problems with them at one point, as well..
My solution, since there are multiple commands to play media, such as #wav, #mediaplay, #midiplay, etc, was to experiment with different commands.
if #wav doesn't work, try #mediaplay, or even #midiplay.
Plugins must be downloaded, since the TK doesn't come with any initially. Download them at [The Shack](http://mrgoldsoft.mysticsoftware.net)
Hope this helps.
