### bluewizard · 2003-09-26 18:24 · #A1002-11129

There should be an option to change the amount of tiles you lay down at once in the board editor. Like say you had a 100 x 100 tile board and needed to fill it entirely with grass or whatnot.
From personal expierience I know it takes a long time to do that. So my suggestion for the board editor is that you can specify the length and width of the tile "brush" and lay down multiple tiles at once.
So you would have 2 boxes and in those boxes (sorry can't think of a better word) you would have the width and height of the "brush".
So say I entered 5 and 5. I would be laying down 25 tiles at a time thus decreasing my time immensely. Or even simpler, I could set it to 100 and 100 and cover my board with grass entirely without the work. This concept could also be applied to things like roads and walls.
An alternative to this would be to have a "tile" option in the board background dialogue. As well as a stretch and a center option. Kind of like you would have for desktop wallpaper. This is easier than having to make a big grass scene for a specific board and then only using it once. It would also save space because you would have less images included.
So tell me what you think, but I'm sure somebody has suggested this before without my knowledge.
