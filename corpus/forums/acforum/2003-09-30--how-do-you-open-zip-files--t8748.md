### ersuis · 2003-09-30 21:46 · #A1001-8750

**Well...(more)**

After you've downloaded the file, you're going to need something to uncompress it. I believe WinXP can open a zip file like a folder. Really, though, I'd recommend a good decompression program, like WinRar (Google it). WinRar supports zips, along with a myriad of other formats, like .tar.gz (if you get things from UNIX and Linux people, you'll run into this).
