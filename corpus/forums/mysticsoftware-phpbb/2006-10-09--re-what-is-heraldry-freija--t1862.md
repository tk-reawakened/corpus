**What Is Heraldry:Freija?**

What is Heraldry:Freija
Heraldry: Freija is a standard RPG(roleplaying game) being developed using the [RPGToolkit3](http://toolkitzone.com) platform. It features a female 18 year-old highschool kickout who ventured the world in search of truth and to prove her worth.
The game offers a realworld clock system, to which quests and game events depend upon. It also features two modes of battle, both realtime and time-based. A host of minigames are also under development to enhance the gaming experience and give it the standard RPG appeal. Furthermore, it has a lot of in-game creation systems like chemistry, blacksmithy, engineering, cooking, writing, and composing. All these are aimed to satisfy especially those who want their game fully customized and have the feeling of being in control above things.
Graphics are handrawn, taking its premise from more popular games like Legend of Mana, SaGa Frontier, etc.
The game also features some 17 characters, and a number of NPCs. Aside from the storyline the player has the luxury of engaging into sidequests, to decrease the linear nature of the game.
Currently, this game has undergone 2 years in development, and so far two demos have been released, accompanied by 2 trailers. Get the files [here](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?mode=files&game=69).
Synopsis
It has been fifty years after the Great Division. The world is at peace but still uncoordinated. No world map has been established, since no explorer dared venture the vast ocean of the land. In the huge continent known as Imperia, there exists a peaceful and pleasant kingdom of Skyra -- thrived by the descendants of the once highly appraised race of the Croissans.
During such era, the people of Skyra believed that there exists no more masses of land than the one they're on. The only outer towns that lies within the continent are Ahrid Khan, Aurea, Soleil, New Lucero, Kojima, and Kojima Port. Beyond the seashores of Imperia is a vast mass of blue waves -- and so believed to be the boundary of the planet.
Apart from their beliefs, there is an outstanding Increstian, who, at her youthful age, questioned these "facts" and "historical records". Her name was Freija Ilden Moonault. Freija was dissatisfied with the details of these records and therefore continuously challenged the knowledge of her professors at her alma mater, Aryx Academy. Due to her insistent behavior, driven by her search for truth. she was dismissed from the academy.
Not a single ounce of discouragement overcame her heart. Instead, she became more determined to continue her quest for the truth-- so that the world would know. And thus begins the tale of Increstia's most revered poet, historian, cartographer--Frei Highflyer.
