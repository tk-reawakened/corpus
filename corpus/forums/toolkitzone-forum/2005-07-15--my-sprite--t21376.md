### Khin · 2005-07-16 09:32 · #K232948

Your paint program is missing because someone removed it. If you want to bring it back just go to your Control Panel > Add/Remove Programs > Windows Components > Accessories & Utilities > Accessories > Details > You should see Paint there unchecked. Check it.
For Windows 98, it's also the same, but not exactly. Just keep on going through the Accessories item then hit details. At the end of the road you'll find Paint amongst other default Windows program.
