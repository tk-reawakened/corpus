### taco · 2007-02-12 02:53 · #K379165

The problem with having a layout based on a single image is that everything gets stuck to one side in higher resolutions. The story pages are inconsistent with the rest of your design and at higher resolutions, the text stretches out to both sides of the screen, which isn't very nice when it comes to reading.
Try using divs with relative width (i.e., 80%) and centering the layout.
And perhaps use Georgia as the default font instead of Times New Roman.
