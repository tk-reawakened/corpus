# Doors...

### Final-Knight · 2007-02-12 23:41 · #K379351

How do you make doors lead to another map?

### Mephare · 2007-02-12 23:50 · #K379353

Warp tiles. On the board editor, click "RPGCode" at the top, then click "Set Warp Tile". After that, it's pretty self explanatory.
Of course, you could do more, like create a door opening animation, etc. but thats the basic way to do it.

### k-hos · 2007-02-12 23:51 · #K379354
