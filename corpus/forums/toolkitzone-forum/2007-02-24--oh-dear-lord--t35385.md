### jpsmtm · 2007-02-25 00:11 · #K382395

Huh?
Growth fluctuates?
Cells multiply and then cells die. Evolution has nothing to do with that. Evolution is the theory that creatures adapt to there environment and therefore develop new characteristics.
The Big bang theory is proved by using Redshift. Redshift is what happens when something moves away from you very fast. It causes the wavelength of light to be stretched, meaning it moves towards the red end of the light spectrum. As very, very distant stars appear red, when they are in fact giving off white light, it shows that they are moving away from us very fast. If everything in the universe is moving away from everything else it shows that the universe HAS to be expanding.
That wasn't the best explanation, but there is a very interesting televised lecture on the subject, although you need 3D glasses to watch it. It explains it in very simple terms, and very convincingly. If I can find it online I shall post it here.
