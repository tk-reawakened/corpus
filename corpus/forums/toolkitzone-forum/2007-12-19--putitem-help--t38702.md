if its not the items multitask program then you need to put the items slot number on the board, such as 1.

### k-hos · 2007-12-19 02:59 · #K425252

like:

**Code:**

```
createItem("MrMan.itm",MrManVAR)

putitem(MrManVAR, 5,5, 1)

Or:

createItem("MrMan.itm",0)

putitem(0, 5,5, 1)
```
