### cuthalion · 2009-09-22 20:39 · #K482385

**Power Supply{ SOLVED-Thank you all]**

Could be a virus, a bad RAM stick, a bad power cord, bad power supply, overheating (try carefully vacuuming out your computer if there's much dust in it), a keyboard with a power-off button that's being stupid and activating even when it's not pressed...
Start with the easy stuff (swapping power cable, vacuuming comp and running it with the case open for a while). If none of that works, try virus checking and if you've got multiple RAM sticks, try each one on its own (if you know how to do that without hurting anything).
