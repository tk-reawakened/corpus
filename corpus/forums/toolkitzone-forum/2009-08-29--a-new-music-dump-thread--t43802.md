Ultima - I listened to your works again to take note of specific suggestions of mine. Each of my suggestions is meerly that, so don't feel obliged to appease my sense of what could be done to make your songs sound better (to me).
I started back at Heroic Moment - Valient:
- The first trumpets (as I will call them) are playing at the rythym 1& &a2& or something close to that. In the last four notes there, the stuccato style makes it stuck out almost in a negative way. If it were possible to soften the release of the trumpets, I think that would make that sound better throughout the whole peice.
-The whole piece is fairly short. Just as it seems to be building up in the climax, it ends. Perhaps it is supposed to be like this for Jagen's Treasure, but as for the song standing alone, I feel there should be some reslove to the climax. Some down play so it doesn't feel quite so much like your dropped at the end of the song.
-Since it is called Heroic Moment, this song should make the listen feel this sense of heroism. I think that making the song more full will help further this accomplishment. As of now, you have, at the climax, two trumpet sets, some strings, and piano, and possibly a couple other intruments. All of these are treble related melodic instruments. If you add in a bass it will feel more full. The bass wouldn't even have to stick out, but just as long as it is there. I would suggest having the bass following the second trumpets (the ones that go high at the climax). JJust hum it in your head and I think you will see how it fits.
Astral Malady:
- At 0:22 when the style change occurs, I would say put more emphasis on the kick drum. You drop the melody at this point (it is less emphasized) so to compliment it, a stronger beat will give it a nice feel.
- As well as at this point, see if you can take the attack of the 16th notes being played down. When they come in, they are a little sudden/loud. This isn't that big of an issue though.
