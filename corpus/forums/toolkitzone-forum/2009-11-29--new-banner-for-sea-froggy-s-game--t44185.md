The Colony is so much worse than Colony RPG. :O You have to understand Seafroggy's original intention with Colony RPG.
See, he wanted to create a lunar base where people would endlessly simulate the interactions of a JRPG, only with half of the gravity. However, when NASA decided it was a bad/technologically implausible idea, this pushed Seafroggys to the limit. After two weeks of depression and watching "My Little Pony" while listening to "One Winged Angel", Seafroggys turned to his computer, and after a two-hour cursing tirade about the ways NASA sucks, set to work. While his original effort lasted only a month(ish), his resolve galvanized further, and now whenever he plugs Colony RPG in random forum posts (i.e. Colony RPG is the best TK game ever! Colony RPG is great! Colony RPG is the best thing since waffles,) he rests steady in knowing that every shameless ad is sticking it to NASA for rejecting his plans.
