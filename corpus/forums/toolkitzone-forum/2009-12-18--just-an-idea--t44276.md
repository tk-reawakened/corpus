Excellent drawing! Although, the goddess' right breast looks considerably malformed, it doesn't seem to have the kind of buoyancy or volume that the other breast seems to have. I think clothes would actually improve the boss, because naked people are generally not very intimidating.
Also, I am not sure what emotion this boss is suppose to convey, but none of the expressions on any of the characters make me feel intimidated or challenged.
