### StarRaven · 2010-03-10 21:26 · #K492006

**Johnstein wrote:**

> I often (not always) try to build a character in a manner that isn't really typical and even possibly in a way that makes it impossible for me to win, but I like the challenge to see if I can create character builds that were never intended. Magician that can melee or a fighter who can use magic. Not all games lend themselves to this sort of building, especially the ones that don't have a lot of specialized options.
>

-- Pretty much this, haha. It also really depends on the game. In a single-player game with one character (or a single main character and party members you can't really change), I just usually go for whatever random character concept is in my head, and roleplay it, picking and choosing stats and skills as I feel the character would want or whatnot. In tabletops, I'll make a character, then tweak him/her as I go according to my group and my character's role in the party, though I favor healer/support type classes when convenient. (Although I have played some strange ones, from a blind melee fighter to a wimpy mage who unexpectedly got turned into a giant halfway through the game....) In MMOs I like to try to do the weirdest possible things. Swordsman/Summoner, Archer/Healer, etc. I'll play anything that isn't popular, or I'll play something popular (like Perfect World's summoner/beast trainer/support character werefox) in a strange way (as a melee fighter with no magic.) I have the most fun when I join a group and people can't tell what kind of fighter I am, especially if using that character in an odd way actually works.
