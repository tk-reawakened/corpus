# Screenshot FAQ - How To's

### Hablethorn · 2004-02-18 04:47 · #B2014

( Q. ) How do I post a screenshot?
( A. ) Theres many diffrent steps but read on and we'll describe some simple and commonly used methods.
1. Take/make a screenshot.
2. Upload the screenshot to a webserver or file space.
3. Use BBCode to link to the screenshot that was uploaded.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
( Q. ) How do I take/make a screenshot?
( A. ) Press the printscreen(Prt Scr) key on your keyboard. This takes a picture of what ever is on your screen and puts it in your [clipboard.](http://msdn.microsoft.com/library/en-us/winui/winui/windowsuserinterface/dataexchange/clipboard/abouttheclipboard.asp)
Now open your graphic editor or MS paint and click "Edit" then "Paste" or "Ctrl+V". Save the image as a gif or jpg.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
( Q. ) Where can I upload my screenshots?
Well, if it's about your game - which is should be - why not create a game profile in "My Games" and use that as your image host? Simple.
If you want a general host...
( A. )
<http://www.walagata.com/> - Free Subscription (Images, Files and even HTML Pages)
<http://www.villagephotos.com> - Free Subscription (Images)
<http://www.uploadit.org/-> Free Subscription
<http://www.imageshack.us/>
<http://photobucket.com/>
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
( Q. ) How do I use BBCode to post my screenshots?
( A. ) Click [here](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/faq.php?mode=bbcode#9) to read the BBCode FAQ entry.
