### Xavier · 2004-03-09 12:26 · #B8508

Post your recommended programming books here, with your own description and maybe a link to the amazon page for it.
[Programming Visual Basic.NET 2003](http://www.amazon.co.uk/exec/obidos/ASIN/0735620598/qid=1078834973/sr=2-1/ref=sr_2_11_1/026-0590174-5880449)
I recommend any VB or VB.NET book by Francisco Balena, his books are intelligent and don't pretend the reader is a complete idiot. Nor do the books lead you by the hand, which I find limit learning. Instead, he takes you through all the concepts such as Inheritence, Encapsulation and so forth as well as going through the basics like using the textbox object etc.
Recommened for those who find "Programming for Dummies" or "Learn in 24 Hours" patronising and a waste of time.
