### Tenala · 2004-07-30 20:08 · #B67537

I'll add in a bunch of good art links, ones that cover more than anime.
[Art Technique - Epilogue.net](http://www.epilogue.net/art/tech/)
A few tutorials covering digital painting and a few other topics, like dynamic compositions and color theory. The site assumes that you have at least some basic drawing knowledge, so it's not the best place for a beginner.
[Elfwood FARP](http://elfwood.lysator.liu.se/farp/)
A huge grab bag of tutorials and articles about art and writing. A wide variety of topics and media are covered, including basic drawing skills. Well worth checking out.
[Sumaleth Link Archive](http://www.sumaleth.com/links/)
Another huge collection of tutorials and articles all about graphic arts. If you can't find what you need at Elfwood check here.
[Human Anatomy for Artists Online](http://www.fineart.sk/)
WARNING: THIS SITE CONTAINS NUDE PHOTOS
Nothing provacative, but there are naked men and women. This is what you would see during a college level figure drawing course.
[Altavista Image Search](http://www.altavista.com/image/default)
I use this to look up reference photos when I don't know how to draw something. Never underestimate the value of a good photo reference, it can really pop your art to the next level.
