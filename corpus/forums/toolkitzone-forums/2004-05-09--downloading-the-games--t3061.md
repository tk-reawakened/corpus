### Xavier · 2004-05-09 16:08 · #B30647

Well, when people start uploading the games you can click on the "files" link and select download.
But unless they upload the file or tell you where to get the file...they're missing out on you playing their game!
The two Dark Ages games, for example, the creator tells you in his news that the files are available from the official site. So click the "official site" link and go there.
