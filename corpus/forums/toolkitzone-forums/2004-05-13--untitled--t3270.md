### Vampz · 2004-05-13 15:17 · #B32634

Use an FTP program such as [www.smartftp.com](http://www.smartftp.com) and enter the details.
It will connect to your file host.
Once you uploaded your file, go to My Games and click file then add file.
You will be able to see the file you just uploaded listed in a drop-down select box.
Select it and add it to your profile.
