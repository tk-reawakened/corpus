You stop your degradation of the French RIGHT NOW. This is not a forum to express hatred and bigotry of innocent people who have little control over what their government does.
In all fairness, before the recent events post-9/11 the French had been strong trade partners and we'd established a good bond between us. All the trouble started with the formation of the European Union, and when president Bush went into office. I'm not here to discuss the numerous errors and lies his administration has conjured over the past 3 and a half years, but decisions they've made have influenced France's uncooperative nature. The problem is Bush's vision of Pax Americana, where America becomes the police of the world.
Here's my question to everyone....Is it really fair to the world for one world power to assume control of all law and order on the planet? I think not personally. The current state of the world has its problems, but that doesn't mean that everyone else has to give up their time to assist in building up nations that aren't as economically advantaged as the power in question. Why should America have this power? Why not the UK? Why not Canada? Because we have more nukes? That's utter bullshit.
Why is it the responsibility of America, and only America to eliminate world terrorism? Yes, we were attacked. We don't want to be attacked again. There are better, more efficient ways of eliminating terrorist breaches than the crusades that have taken place over the course of Bush's term in office. The focus should be on DEFENSE, not offense. Bureacracies such as the TSA for airlines, and tighter borders is not cutting it. Instead of wasting resources looking for Arabs (no offense intended, I realize not all terrorists are Arabic, and I don't mean to portray racism in this way) in the sands and hidden caves of Iraq and Afghanistan, we should be beefing up security at home.
Imagine this analogy... The current terrorist situation is like a scabbed wound. Generally, there are two solutions to treat the scab. The first, you can pick at it and pick at it until it comes clean off, obliterated as it becomes crushed by trampling feet which in the process causes slight pain, and a sudden rush of blood that comes oozing from the wound. This is what the Bush administration has done, and continues to do. By irritating the wound, they have opened the possibility for infection which will lead to even further problems down the road if left untreated. The second option is much less painful and helps prevent any complications that might occur. By simply cleaning the wound lightly and applying neosporin to the damaged area, a wound that might take over a week to heal may only take days. Anyway, I think the point has been made. The current solution to preventing terrorist attacks on American soil is not effective, and will only lead to bigger conflicts.
Back to Iraq and the French for a moment. As has been said, the French did not support the decision to invade Iraq because they felt Iraq was no immediate threat to French security, and they had strong trade relations with them. I don't see anything wrong with protecting their country's own interests. The US sure enough protects its interests with the oil tycoons getting rich off of selling gas to poor Iraqi civilians who make as much in a year as the typical American makes in a day's work, sometimes HALF a day's work. Germany wasn't exactly cooperative with us either, but no one seems to care about Germany anymore. Why must the French be targeted when multiple nations disagreed with our decision to invade Iraq? You conservatives need to learn to stop scapegoating innocent people.
My $.02
