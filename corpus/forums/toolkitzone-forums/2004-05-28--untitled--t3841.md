How do you actually make battle animation i dont need a code i need to know how to put a picture and make them move. And also i need help because when i open my tileset there is nothing there please help me. Evil or Very Mad

### Gladius · 2004-05-28 06:06 · #B38873

You have to create your graphics outside of the animation editor itself. After you have made the frames outside of the animation editor (me personally, I rip my graphics from an emulator and then edit them in PaintShop Pro), you then create a new animation.
Once you have the animation editor open, click on the blank white spot and it will ask you to load in a graphic for that frame. Just find the graphic you made and click it in there. Click the arrows on the toolbar to go to the next frame and repeat the above process to load that frame. Rinse and repeat until you have that animation finished and then save the animation with a good name (I save the files using this format so I always know what animation is what: charactername_direction_action.anm. For example, an animation of me fighting a monster would be saved as gladius_left_attack.anm, because the heros always face left in the default battle system.)
If you want to use the animation for battle, open up your character/enemy editor, choose the Attack/Rest/Die/Defend and then browse to the animation you just made.
Hopefully I didn't give you too much info there. If this didn't help, let me know and I'll try to provide you with more details.
