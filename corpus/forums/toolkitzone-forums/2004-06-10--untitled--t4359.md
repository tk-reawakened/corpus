### Dan · 2004-06-10 20:36 · #B43802

I thought it was about time one of the staff made some slightly more official guidelines for this forum, so here we go:
The big ones:
- Follow the general community rules [(here)](http://www.toolkitzone.com/rules.php).
- Do not flame newbies for posting questions that could have been answered by reading the help files. Try and be helpful to everyone.
- Dont ask people to make entire programs, they wont do it, trust me.
Guidelines for those asking questions:
- Try and be detailed in your questions, dont just post questions like My RPG Code doesnt work.
- Use an appropriate title for your topic, "help plz!!" does not count.
- Dont bump your posts, if people know how to help you, they will do it. If your post goes un-answered, it usually means they dont know the answer.
- Try and remember to use the [ code ] tag when posting code, it keeps your post a little bit more tidy.
- For quicker answers to your questions, try searching the forum first (someone may have already encountered the same problem as you.
- If your question has been fully answered and you are happy with the response you received, edit the topic title to include *answered* or *resolved* (or something similar)
Guidelines for those answering questions:
- Dont post in a thread simply to say read the help files or do the tutorial.
- If you know that the question has already been answered, dont post to say this question has been answered before, post a link to the existing thread for the user to view.
- Dont post in any thread in this forum unless your response is useful to the thread, responses like I dont know or Why dont you learn RPG Code first are not welcome.
Please, try and stick by these, it's not hard.
