### Spyder · 2004-06-16 16:40 · #B46296

Technically there are at least seven, but there are only two...base (for lack of a better word) albums. Hybrid Theory and Meteora. There is also the Hybrid Theory EP, which was Linkin Park's first album, released under their original band name, Hybrid Theory, under independant development, as well as Reanimation, which is a remix CD of Hybrid Theory, along with a couple of new songs added to it; then Meteora; and their latest album Live In Texas, which is live recordings of tracks off both Hybrid Theory, Reanimation, and Meteora. Then there is the 2nd LP Underground CD, which I'm not sure what exactly it contains, and the 3rd LP Underground CD, which has the rest of the tracks from the Texas concert that were not included in Live In Texas.
Then there's a bunch of un-official mix CD's on Ebay that people try and pass off as unreleased LP albums. I can't believe people actually buy those...
