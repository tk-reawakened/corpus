### ArchGaden · 2004-11-06 05:24 · #B121833

Reason 2.5, Its very expensive and highly pirated
Comes with about 1.5gb of extremely high quality samples. Its set up to look like standard audio hardware with an amazing interface. The sound quality is truly professional...just importing a crappy midi, and mapping it to some of the instruments will sound amazing. The software is extroidinarily flexible, but a bit complicated.
One of the features I love the most is the distortion unit. It looks exactly like something you would buy in a store...comes with about 15 different distortion modes, and a slew of settings for each. You can reproduce about any distorted guitar sound with true professional level quality....it doesn't sound like a 'sample' in the least because it doesn't 'loop' Instead, there is an advanced set of algorithms to generate the sound to give a sound that...well..sounds real. Its made even some of my guitar playing friends jealous.
Later, I might upload some samples of work with Reason 2.5.
