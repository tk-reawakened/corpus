About language
Language is a tool of humanity, used for communication. As this, it should be as simple as possible. The commonly used words like "I", "yes", "no", "he" and such should be short. The words should also give some telling about what they are supposed to mean, like the sound-based words like "hmm", "ooh" and such. The word for "silence" should sound silent, and the word for "powerful" should sound solemn.
About clothing
The clothing should not be a burden to wear and serve it's purpose. It should not lower ability to move around freely, and should be mainly practical. Of course it can be decorative. Through clothings, much things can be told - like social status, job you do, and such. Using different clothing for different jobs, for example, can provide ability to find some truth about people by sight. Of course they shouldn't be bound to these like slaves, but when you're searching for someone in a place, someone important, having important job, you need to find - like some boss, guard officer or medic - they should wear specific forms of cloths while on-duty, at least, to be easily distinquishable. Also, people should wear clothes that suit their work. Like soldiers or police/militia members not wearing too heavy clothes, but also they need to be endure to resist eventual attacks and such. Clothing should not be needed for special occasions, like enforcing people to look "good" for some party by wearing proper white shirt and other such stuff. People should be able to wear what they like, just not look too dirty / weird for such situations.
Crime
Crime should be considered a direct damage, physical, material or psychical, to anyone. Thus, these are things like direct stealing, murder, rape, pillaging, threatening, molesting, and similar. Whatever item a man has, he should be able to do anything he likes with it if it's legal - thus, copying items or software to sell for profit should not be considered a crime, as drug dealing should be, at least with minor drugs, less harmful for the community.
About crime punishment
Eye for an eye is good method, also law is to ensure that people will be safe. Elimination by death sentencing the most dangerous criminals is a very good method, as there is no chance they will do what they do again. When people can ensure they will change they can be given second chance, but if they break the oath - they should be sentenced twice or trice as they should be previously for that. Beside death penalty, isolation or exile should be good way of ridding the community of the dangerous element, propably with ability to return/be released after certain amount of time passes. Putting criminals to labor is another method, forcing them to work with no gains for the community they betrayed by commiting crime.

### Levi · 2004-06-25 05:04 · #B49959

I agree, the painter can and usually does (at least the ones where you can feel something from) paint solely for the love of painting. And only after they have finished and poured emotion and feeling into ti sell it. It's real art. The selling allows for the free time to do what they love. To feel what they paint. To obsess and change, and change, and change, and change. Until they just have to compromise and deal with what they have created knowing that the image in their mind can only truly be realized in the mind. And the painting is marvelous because of that alone. Now an artist who paints solely to sell is simply someone who can draw or paint or whatever art they do. And that isn't true art. Art is an emotion. Even if the end result is sellign it, if it has that obsession to it, that raw emotion. It's art no matter the end result.
