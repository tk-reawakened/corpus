Please do not take this the wrong way Caliber...
...but I'd like to tell a little anecdote...
There's this little kid.
He's about 8 years old.
He's been sitting in the kitchen on a wooden stool pressed up against the counter top all day drawing a picture. He hopes he can be an artist some day because he thinks that is really awesome.
His mommy is in the living room waiting for him to show her his picture that he's been working on all day.
He's just about finished.
There.
He holds up the crayon image and looks at his yellow lion with a joyous gaze.
He is really nervous because he's never tried to draw a lion before... especially not one so big and strong.
His mommy calls for him and the little boy jumps down from the stool.
He starts running towards the living room when suddenly in front of him the outside door opens. Two teenage boys come in stepping between the little boy and the living room blocking his way.
One of the older boys is his brother, the other is one of his friends.
As the little boy tries to nudge past them, his brother's friend notices the drawing and grabs it.
"Oh a lion huh?" the teenage says.
The little boy replies, "It's mine. Give it back."
"This is yours?" asks the older boy.
The little boy pauses a second and then slowly nods his head.
The teenager looks over the lion quickly and smirks a little.
"Well... The ears are too big, the nose is crooked, the maine is the wrong color, his face is not porportionate, his whiskers are too long, he has too few toes, his teeth are too white, his eyes aren't straight, and his tail is too long."
The teenage boy looks at the little boy with a blank expression as the child begins crying.
He shruggs and walks away.
His voice can be heard fading off as he says, "What dude? I was just being honest. What?"
The little boy looks down at his ugly stupid lion... crumples it up and watches TV.

There are such things as bad art. NOw you're trying to debate over the very meaning of the word art. This is graphical/visual art. Anything that has unintended errors, is generally no longer "good". As much as Caliber (or Razor, or w/e) hates me now, it was an honest crit (rude, yes, and I apologize for the rudeness), pointing out mistakes in the image. The fact he was attempting a realitic pic means that mistakes in proportions are bad. Had this been a surreal, comic, cariacture, or chibi pic, then by all means, anotomy issues are perfectly fine.
an attempt at a realistic picture, is like building a bicycle. If there is a crooked seat and the handlebars are too small to use properly, its a bad bicycle.
When you get into the ground of "no good or bad" you are dealing with abstract or impressionist works. For example, I enjoy Jackson Pollock's work. He dribbled paint all over a canvas for most works, but they were not bad. Now, the guy who cut the head off a cow and left it in formaldahyde/blood borders on the line of "no longer art", but if considered art that is not bad, it accomplishes exactly what it intended with no errors.
To reiterate, this pic, is meant to be a realistic rendition of hand. There are too many mistakes for it to be realistic, therefore it is not good. Now, it does have enough good qualities about it to not be considered bad, however it is not good. I would say above average at best, and appearently the Gaia people agree.
