### Randy · 2004-07-06 05:21 · #B54599

I own 4 guitars. I've been playing almost 8 years now and have never had any formal teaching. I've learned almost completely by ear with the exception of some tabulature and a book on modern rock guitar. What can I play? Too many things to list. I usually can pick up any song pretty quickly if I play along with it. Anyway, here's my babies...
Olympia OMC1CE (MisChief) Acoustic/Electric
[www.olympiaguitars.com](http://www.olympiaguitars.com) (click the Mischief link on the front page)
Just look at the pic. This is my pride and joy. Possibly the best guitar I've ever played with the exception of my uncle's 1960 (or 59, he can't remember) Les Paul Custom (first electric guitar to feature a floating tremolo) or my aunt's boyfriend's Martin and Co. Acoustic. Even still, this guitar is very versatile and creates a multitude of tones. Plug it into an amp, and you've just taken one step closer into musical heaven. Smile
Fender 1962 Telecaster Reissue (Japanese) Electric
No photo available, but it's candy-apple red with a black pickguard along with some other slight modifications from stock...I bought this at Guitar Center about 5 years ago. Right now it needs some work done with the electronics so I don't play it.
Washburn WR150 Rocker Series Electric
Also no photo, but Washburn's website "might" have a pic of one...Mine is A metallic purple color with no pickguard and stickers all over it. Looks very punky. I bought this at a pawn shop for 199 dollars.
Samick 5-String bass (I don't know the model right now).
This bass is a beauty. It's black with gold hardware, and the inlays on the fret board are little sparkly Saturns. In addition, I replaced the pickups with some EMG pickups (250 dollars worth of work including parts) so it sounds mean. I bought it at a pawn shop for under 200 dollars. Worth every penny.
