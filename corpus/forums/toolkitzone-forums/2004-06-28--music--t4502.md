Swearing in songs does anything but bother me. About 80% of the CDs that I purchase have a parental advisory on them for language, it's hard to avoid things like that. When you look at it, there is ALOT more swearing in music in modern music then from, say, 30 years ago. I think that a great deal of that is rap, one of the few types of music I cannot stand. Swearing is also used as a way to express stronger emotion in words. If it is over used, it looses it's meaning and may get repetitive, annoying to listen to, or even funny in some cases.
On a side note about Eminem:
I generally do not like his music. I have heard a few songs though that I fairly like the lyrics to (Loose Yourself, for example) that I think have a good meaning to them. I simply do not like the way they are presented, in rap.
