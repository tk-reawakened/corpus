### Skitchy · 2004-06-29 14:16 · #B51953

Dark selphine does not exist to my knowledge but I had about 100 of just about every item and I also discovered every secret...
And I got every GF (including Eden, Bahamut, Doomtrain, Siren, cerebus, alexander, diablos, brothers, pandemona, levithan, carbuncle, odin and every other gf in the game but I can't remember the names)
If you need any help post onto my FF help topic...
Defeating Omega & Ultima weapons
Ultima weapon
the first thing you should do is get squall and the rest of your most used party to level 100 and junction high-level spells to their HP (high spells like ultima) you should also junction speed spells eg haste to their speed.
when you encounter Ultima you should always try to draw Eden(the strongest GF) and if you have meltdown use it, meltdown reduces the enemy's defence so it makes your attacks stronger, then if you have the aura spell use it on squall (it will make him do a limit break move if you keep pressing circle then activate it in the usual way-make sure you practice at using the limit break because if you do a perfect hit it can take off more than 80,000 HP from your opponent) keep with that and the fight should be over in no time, just make sure that you heal every few times ultima attacks and renew squall's aura every few turns.Also try to use spells like ultima as they can take off 9,999 HP.
Omega Weapon
Use the same tactics as you did for ultima weapon(except you don't need to draw), should that fail just keep trying.I heard a rumor that selphie has a spell in her limit break called holy war(I think that is the name of an item too), holy war supposidly kills omega in One hit.Anyway just keep to the Ultima tactics and also use items that can make you invincible or use rinoa's limit break Invincible moon(if you have this on Omega can't hurt your party for about 10-20 turns.
