# What are game pages?

### Dark Paladin · 2004-06-29 19:18 · #B52050

Maybe I'm just plain stupid, but I don't know what game pages are and I am working on a game. Could someone tell me what they are?

### Xavier · 2004-06-29 19:24 · #B52052

Game pages allow you to create a page about anything to do with your game. The editor allows you to set the fonts, images, and other html aspects for the page.
Users can then read your page and also add comments.
Basically a feature for you to add more info on your project.
