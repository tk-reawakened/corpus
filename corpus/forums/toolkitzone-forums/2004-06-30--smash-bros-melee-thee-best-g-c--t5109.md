Too bad we can't play these games online.
I think Mitsurugi is the best though.
His weapon, "Damascus" is absurdly powerful.
Even though you cannot defend while using that weapon, a person who has mastered the "Guard Impact" will make Mitsurugi wielding that sword the ultimate player. That weapon is the most powerful, because it adds an insane amount of attack and defense. (More attack power than all weapon)
Even without the weapon, he is still deadly in the hands of a skilled player. Then again, Yoshimitsu is also just as strong (mainly because they both have the same amount of moves)
A lot of players (from GameFaqs) find Yoshimitsu as the preferred player though.
PS: Link is by no means useless. His sword jab moves are very quick, and he has the over all best long range on Soul Calibur 2. His Great Fairy Sword is also one of the best weapons available too.
