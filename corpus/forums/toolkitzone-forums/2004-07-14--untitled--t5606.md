### Marz · 2004-07-16 00:26 · #B59696

What is all this clean business anyway? The toolkit would be clean with or without the online functionality, all that's being added are a couple of lines of code (okay, a little more than that, but you understand).
As for Vampz,
"The TK isn't suited to online gaming, so to integrate it as a main feature gives off the wrong impression. "
What makes you so sure the TK isn't suited to online gaming? The TK can work for online gaming with a little bit of elbow grease on the game makers part, but this isn't what the built-in functionality is for anyway. Usually, a plugin is made because there are a large number of people who want that particular feature but it's not programmed in the toolkit. Why not just transfer much-wanted features directly into the toolkit?
I realize this makes plugins obsolete, but cbm made them obsolete when he made the toolkit 3 itself Open-source. Now the only legitimate use for plugins is if I wanted to program my battle-system in C++ and insert it into my game.
