### Levi · 2004-07-31 04:27 · #B67688

Honestly, it stinks. It's bad in a sense, I can see some of what you are trying to get at but you just word it so venimously that I can't believe it was written at all. I love the fact that for this piece you tried to include the "thy, thou, art, etc." But There's too much, it just sounds like you meant to write one thing but the words got jumbled in your mind. I know you say you wrote it to simply put something up but be serious, reread it, look at all of the wording errors and review it, rewrite it, and see what you come up with. Don't write to simply write, write to simply exist. Writing is something all can do but without emotion, or true love of what you are doing the story dies. It's just words on a page. Think about that.
