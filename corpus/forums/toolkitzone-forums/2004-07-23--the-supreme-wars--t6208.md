# The Supreme Wars

### Chrono · 2004-07-23 17:25 · #B64036

One day there was a man who was bored with everyday life...
He had done everything, he lived a life of luxory and had done
everything a man could possibly do in his life that was exciting
or thrilling.
He got bored. Nothing he heard or saw was ever new to him.
"Been there, done that," he would always say.
He soon got depressed, he survived shootings, he grew up in
a household of drug addicts, and overcame odds. He had
competed in competitions of every kind you could imagine.
One day he decided to choose a different lifestyle..he would
use his immense wealth to discover the secret of life.
After several years of research rumors said he hid in his
mansion's basement and never came out for weeks at a time.
"Something is not right," his friends would say, "He no longer
comes to discuss business or see his stockbroker even."
Then it happened. He dialed up several people and set up a
show for his entire city to witness: The unveiling of the fruits
of his research.
But the man was dirty, tired, weak, and had a raspy voice.
He barely did so much to nourish himself, he was so engrossed
with what he found. Usually he was brawny and muscular but
his body had gotten several warts and his eyes bloodshot.
He revealed to the world the first clone of a human that was
ever successful. It was in embryonic state, but the most amazing
part was that he could control its DNA with a small computer. This
computer was hooked to the glass jar of green glowing fluid and
life support equipment.
The people were amazed and funded his research, which was
a mistake they may live to regret..for he started becoming power
hungry. "I am perfect," he would say. "Someone who has all this
wealth and popularity could bring peace to the world. And since I
am so perfect, my word should be law..."
He then hacked into several government super computers.
He started collecting information from everywhere, and was
attempting to put this knowledge within this embryo.
"Anyone who opposes me will have to deal with my precious child,"
he said to himself.
He collected info on the supernatural, the laws of science...
and encoded them into strands of DNA. The embryo grew he found
as he fed it information.
One day the entire planet's power was shut off for 30 minutes.
No light, no electricity. Dark clouds shrouded over the daylight, leaving
no light to cover any part of the earth.
People panicked, scream, ran, slit throats trying to save theirselves
from the darkness. For some reason not even battery operated light would work. There were strange buzzing noises all over and earthquakes.
Then it all stopped. The lights came back on, but with horrible sights.
The people eventually calmed down when they saw the mad scientist on televisions and radios everywhere across earth. How was he doing this?
"Attention, you have no need to panick, people of the Earth. I will be your
new world leader, and my assisstant here which I created with my own hands will help me establish peace."
This clone knew everything technically, it also had ability to learn just as any human would. It had powers because it knew the laws of the unverse, and its mind was powerful. It could move objects with its mind at any speed or velocity simply by thinking about it.
The clone could create matter from his mind. It could go at speeds faster than a jet, and had strength beyond measure.
Then one day the clone went berserk. It usually never talked much because it followed orders a lot. But it too became arrogant just as its father did.
"Foolish old man, I grow tired of your orders." The clone suddenly blurted out. Its eyes glowed green and its skin was tough like leather, yet looked exactly like a normal caucasian man, even wearing street clothes.
"I will use your body to make myself complete. Thank you for releasing me, you old fool.."
"What are you doing?! I created you!" He said.
The clone did something it was not even meant to do: it created a cord from its belly button somewhat like an umblical cord and envelloped the man inside a placenta-like organ, and reversed the aging process to the point he was a tiny seed.
"Hahaha," it laughed, "it looks like you got a lot of growing up to do," it said, smirking evilly.
It zoomed off and started taking control of governments by force if they did not agree with his beliefs.
Soon the world was in his hands, and things actually peaceful.
It is now the year 2700 AD. and the clone is still alive, almost as if it were immortal...300 years since that fateful day.
The clone ran governments, people asked it for help everyday. Eventually the clone sought more power, and people simply called him Supreme Leader.
He then said anyone who does not worship him is killed, and many religious people actually turned against him...
A small rebel group was formed of different religions and cultures, and planned to unnearth the mansion of the famous scientist that created the mad creature rampaging and killing all opposition.
They trained themselves in military combat, the martial arts, and weaponry, since the Supreme has still many followers that remain loyal.
The forces against Supreme then called themselves United Human Causes.

A few dozen other main characters
Somebody to be the evil Supreme clone
Somebody to be a villain or a random badguy that pops up to fight
