### KSNiloc · 2004-07-23 18:25 · #B64067

Many of you have stated that you would like to not to forced to use animations for sprites. You've also said you'd like to use tiles for fight backgrounds. This is, and always has been, possible.
Let's make a fight background.
Choose New-->File-->Tile Bitmap. At the bottom, of the bar, make the size 19x11 and press OK. Make your background like you would have in TK2. Now, in the background editor, set the tbm you just created as the image.
You can also make animations (ie character sprites) with tile bitmaps.
Hope this helps lots of you.
