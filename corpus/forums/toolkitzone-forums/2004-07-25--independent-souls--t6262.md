Brief Summary

**Code:**

```
It starts out with a youngster, aged thirteen, named Cardew Theodore, or Cardew in short. He has to choose his own path, his own destiny. However the Runic Transcript (the Legend) allows a person to only have a choice from only four. A wrong decision made will be a horrible mistake, the path which his parents chose... What will happen to him in the end? How can he accomplish his task? Will his parents be of any help with some relationship, or just set to destroy Cardew? Find out the answer when the short preview is announced.
```
