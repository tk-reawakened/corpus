Character animations have one big flaw in it. You can't choose the walking boundries. What I mean by walking boundries is the area on the image that stops the image from walking through solid tiles. Another problem is not all games are 3/4th perspective. Some may be top down view. The best way to fix the problem is in the player or item editor, have another option to set the walking boundry. Which could be a square drawn onto the image, and when you play the game it'll be invisible.
