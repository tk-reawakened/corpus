### seveneternal · 2004-07-26 10:07 · #B65235

I will write a nice long list of what is wrong with your board, mainly because im so awsome, and because I have too much time on my hands plus the fact that I am an isomniac.
So, what sucks about the board?
First of all, the age of gaming where systems had a knee high limitations are over. Because of these limitations, developers were forced to make some things to look right only with the help of the players imagination. But these limitations are gone. And your graphics are free to be what they really represent. So unless your game revolves around a world where everything is deformed and chunky, I suggest you set those graphics to look like what they should look like.
I can tell that:
1. You only have access to sucky paint programs
2. I can't really tell because of such bad quality, but you put almost no effort into any of these because I see no signs of texture or shading.
3. Obviously, proportions are of no importance to you. Those colorful bars which I assume are books, are bigger then the door.
Unless this is just some crappy 2-day project that you will abandon within a few tries, I suggest you put some effort into it. Most games are crap and just waste space because some morons tend to think inside their own little world and pretend that their game is great.
On more specific help... Lets start with that big white rectangle which I assume is a bed. Take a look. Its a freaking rectangle with several lines going across. Noone can tell exactly what it is because there are a million possibilities of what it could be. It needs details. It needs texture. It needs shading. Im not even saying that it is a bed, im just saying theres no way of telling what that is. Of course, if you are a lazy bastard who never finishes their work, and that the blank white space is a part which has not yet been completed, I completely understand.
Lets look at your Door next. What is that? A pet door? Even if you are making small sprites, its just too small. If you have a 32x32 character, a 48x32 will look a bit better then that dingy tiled crap. Your colorful bars which I assume is a bookcase... full of books, also needs some serious improvement. You can see the top of the walls, yet the bookcase has a flat front view. And small circle things... if they happen to be windows, do better then a paint-made circle with a pixel cross over it. And doesnt light come from windows? Thats what the light-dark tile thing is for. So you dont have to make a billion tiles all varying in tint. Its effective for shadow and light effects.
Now, down in our cozy little livingroom, again, I can't tell what that small square thing is. Is it a TV? A Stove? Which is another bad sign, because I cannot tell straight away what that is. If it is a stove, then you know you have a serious problem with your bookshelf... or "colorful bars"...
About your chair... come on now! Pixel legs? And your talbe has them as well... Say it ain't so. The white square on the "kitchen" or whatever that is, I assume it is a product of total lazyness which I can understand. But the brown ones... What are they... Pads? Counters? Tables? Again I have failed to detect exactly what that thing is... therefore, meaning you need to start working on your graphic because that board of yours right now isn't much of a board.

### KasiTL · 2004-07-26 16:55 · #B65330

Wow, have you ever studdied perspective at all? When I look at the room, I feel like I'm looking at the chairs and counter from a bird's point of view, the bookshelf from a cat's point of view, and the tables from a clock on the wall's point of view. That's not good. They should all be from the same point of view (typically in an rpg you want to go for that "as seen from a clock on the wall" perspective). You really need to get a book on perspective and study it. See if your local library has one and if not, buy one from a book or comic book store (some comic book stores have how-to-draw books). (Personally I have How to Draw Manga: Putting Things in Perspective on my shelf and it's pretty good.)
