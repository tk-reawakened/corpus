### Indy · 2004-07-31 01:54 · #B67647

I don't think you need to make your houses bigger at all. Is got an old school rpg style to it. The scale is just fine...just don't oversize the interior. If you check out games like Lunar: Silver Star Story, you'll notice that even the interiors don't have the same shape as the outside. It's not that big a deal. The tk can't accurately reproduce a professional game like Lunar. So far its meant to create old style rpgs that are tile based. Some people try to do more fancy stuff with the tk, but honestly the results aren't very good. The best games made are those that don't try to do what the tk isn't capable of. Games like Sophia's Rudolphs Adventure and Dustball are great examples. Just stick to the style you have now, it will be much better.

### KasiTL · 2004-07-31 16:19 · #B67844

As others have said, a little bit of texture in the grass and dirt would look nice.
Also, you might want to add some highlights to the tree and toy with dithered shading. Highlights would help bring it up from the grass and provide the illusion that it's a bit lighter in colour than the grass is, while dithering some of the shading carefully could make it appear a bit more realistic (however, if you're going for a cartoonie style, then don't worry about the dithering).
