### Moonews · 2004-07-31 01:46 · #B67646

Just like a parent is a parent, a friend is nothing more then a friend. But parents often die before their children, that's the law of nature. Friends will be there for you even after parents die, so treasure your friends, and a friend you can trust is a true friend indeed.
Too many people question their relationships with their friends, they ask too much. I personally have three friends who I know will be there for me for the rest of my life, but do I look down apon those who I may not follow me to the grave? Of course not. If somebody betrays you, constantly treats you like shit or uses you, they're not a friend and they don't deserve anything from you. However, there may be decent people who respect you and who you have respect for that won't be there forever. Too many people depend on commitment, which makes the world not such a great place. Treasure your friends in the moment, even if they may not be there forever, and somebody will be there for you in the end.
Can't you tell I've been doing some thinking?

### Eternal EoN · 2004-08-04 01:52 · #B69356

From my experience in my 15-year old life... A friend is not a person who talks to you sometimes and seems to like you. That is a person.
What about someone who you talk with a lot, who you really seem to trust, and you know you like each other? There are two words in that last sentence I want to question. "trust" and "know." You may trust them now, but it can and probably will break at the drop of a dime. And you don't know crap about you liking each other unless you can read their minds. They may just be pretending to like you. And there will be those times where you will find out for sure whether you like them or not.
So my definition of "friend" is this: A person who you know you can actually trust, without that trust breaking so easily, and you like them and you have proven that they like you.

### Moonews · 2004-08-04 04:33 · #B69411

I have "friends" who aren't into having conversations about personal problems of theirs and who I don't discuss personal problems with, but I still hang out with them and have a good time. The few friends that I will love and cherish for the rest of my life are a great thing to have, but I still don't share your point of view when it comes to "people". Thinking like that isn't a good way to start and maintain a friendship. At your age, even a slightly negative mind like that will be nothing but trouble. Trust me, you're just starting to enter the real world, which is no wonderland. You need to treasure what you have, without questioning it.
Trust me on this, when I was your age I thought the same way as you did. The end result was that I became depressed, suicidal, even slightly homicidal because of the way I chose to view things. When I admitted the way I was I had to leave school because I couldn't take all of these "people" that taunted me so, and when these "people" found out that I thought like that they were deeply hurt that I didn't trust them as much as they trusted me. I was very close to killing myself, I could practically feel the devil biting my ass. All of these "people" shared the same feelings about this of course, and I ended up pulling through. Some friends are there for emotional support all of the time, but you need to balance that out with good ole' mindless teenager fun in order to stay sane. I may not be sane, ever, but I'm about as close as I'll ever be and I thank these "people" every day for not letting me make a horrible mistake.
There you have it, I have finally shared with the community something really personal. New first for me.
