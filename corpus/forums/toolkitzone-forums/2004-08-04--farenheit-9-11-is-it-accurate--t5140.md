### ForeverNoobish · 2004-08-25 01:59 · #B81075

Not lies, it's just he presents the factual information in a way that would support his argmument. A Bush-supporter could take the same information he got and interpret it in an entirely different way. There will be bias in any movie/documentary like this. And usually it's just using the information you have to your advantage as much as you can, and not focusing so much on the info that degrades your argument. And to say democratic propaganda should be illegal means you would have to say ANY propaganda should be illegal. All propaganda does is try to get you to agree with what they're saying, basically; whether it be a Swifter-Jet commercial that is trying to get you to believe that their product is the best, or Michael Moore trying to get you to hate Bush. You won't see a commercial flat-out lying very often; you will see the commercial present a very select group of people who did like the product to vouch for it while never showing anyone who didn't like the product, even though there are bound to be some. And Michael Moore is only going to show you the stuff that makes you hate Bush, and not the stuff that could make you like him. It shouldn't be illegal by any means. It's just a person with a biased viewpoint on a subject (and I'm sure almost everyone has a biased viewpoint on the issue one way or the other) trying to get you to side with him.
