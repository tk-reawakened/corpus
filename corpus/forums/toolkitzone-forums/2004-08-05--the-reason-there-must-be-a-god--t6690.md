Okay, about the Big Bang theory:
The Big Bang theory is many, many years old; founded way before we knew how the universe(s) work (to the extent that we do now). Through observation, scientists deduced that the whole universe came from a big explosion, and from that came forth all matter.
Of course, we are now trying to refine that theory, by combining it with other theories. For example, according to String theory, there are multiple universes existing within an 11-dimensional space. As one theory goes, two universes (ours and another) collided (scraped edges), and it caused a huge explosion. What does this mean? Perhaps there was life before the big bang. We honestly don't know. That's just one theory.
The widely-accepted theory is currently the Big Bang theory, and until there is evidence otherwise, it will stay that way.
