# Making a CD

### Rueben · 2004-08-06 15:24 · #B70806

I am considering making a CD of a majority of my best works, mostly tracks from Dark Age 2 and the upcoming Dark Age EX and a little from The Fourth Dimension. Although this is in early stages of planning and I'm not sure what will exactly be on the CD yet, I would like to gauge interest and see who would be willing to perhaps buy it.
The content is likely to include a large number of tracks, including possibly unreleased material I have hanging around which I could bring up to date. I am thinking along the lines of maybe booking an orchestra to record the music ... although this might prove to costly. If not, it will be high-quality synth sounds, like what you got on Dark Age 2, only better.
I might even include some bonus material, maybe some extracts from my forthcoming Fourth Dimension novel.
But as I said, very early stages of planning.
