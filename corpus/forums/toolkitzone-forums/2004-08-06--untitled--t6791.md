### Rueben · 2004-08-06 15:30 · #B70808

As much as they are very good games, the Dark Age series is notorious for being very difficult games to the more casual gamer. The jumps in enemy difficulty are usually quite large, you are heavily penalised for running from battles, and rarely do you get a straight-forward one-hit battle over.
The number of people I have heard of who have gotten stuck on Mortis in Dark Age early on is quite high, and I agree, it's a hard battle. And it took me the best part of a year to finish Dark Age 2 (in-between other tasks) thanks to the incredibly difficult battle against Raze.
