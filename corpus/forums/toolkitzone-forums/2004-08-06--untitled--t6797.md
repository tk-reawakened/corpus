Heyo, I just wanted to let you guys know that I added some phpBB forums to tk_gaming. I know forums are hard to get off the ground, but I thought I'd give it a shot. The purpose of the forums is just to talk about TK gaming and not much else, so I'm not just hopelessly trying to duplicate TKZ here. I hope for it to be a pretty relaxing place just to chill and talk about TK games. Personally, I think the actual games of TK seem to get a little ignored once and awhile, and I think if we just talked about games a little more, people would get more motivated. I've talked to a few people or have seen them say that they're probably not going to release anything past the demo they recently released because there was no feedback or talk about the game. I've even had a couple people say thanks for the tk_gaming review simply because it was SOMETHING about their game, and that was enough to keep them working on it. So anyway, sorry to drag on, but please do take a look at the forums.
