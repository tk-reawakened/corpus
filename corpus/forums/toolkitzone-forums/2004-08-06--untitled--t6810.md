### Dont_Think_So · 2004-08-10 04:47 · #B72542

I use Blender, and it can make photo realistic pictures/animation if used right. Now, while I'm nowhere near that quality, plenty of other people that use it more often are. The physics engine is pretty nice, though it only applies to particles; that's pretty useful, however, since DupliVerts allows you to copy mesh data to a particle, thus allowing you to have 10,000 individually calculated fully animated swarming bugs in about 2 seconds, including calculation time. It just takes a little getting used to.
