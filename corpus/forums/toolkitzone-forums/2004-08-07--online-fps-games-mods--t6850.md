### Akura_TheDragonBlader · 2004-08-08 13:31 · #B71667

Ok, I think I figured it out somewhat. There are several servers for multiplayer, what you do is type "connect xxx" without quotes and where x is the ip of the server. [Here](http://nemo3383.werewolfsmg.net/4thReich/downloads/serverQueryDday.zip) is a program that shows what servers are there, as well as players, ip address, what map, and how many kills each player has. Copy the ip, and then connect to it in the Quake 2 console(bring it up with the tilde key ~). Or at least, this is what I think you do.
