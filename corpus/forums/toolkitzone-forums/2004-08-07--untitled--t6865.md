### Dont_Think_So · 2004-08-10 04:31 · #B72535

#x!=x!/32+1
#castint(x!,x!)
#y!=y!/32+1
#castint(y!,y!)
That converts pixels into tiles, if your board doesn't scroll. If it does, you will need to add the corner tile numbers to the x! and y! values (I think the command to get the location is #cornertile or something like that. Look it up).
Also, you can use #run(mouseclick.prg) at the end of your history method in your start program to have that mouse program loop; though I don't recommend it -- there are several problems with it, such as your NPCs wont move, and I'm not sure that step-on activation tiles will work (like warp tiles).
