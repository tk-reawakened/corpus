### Rueben · 2004-08-11 13:54 · #B73177

Let's be honest Master Tamer, you're jumping into making a commercial game. If you make a commercial game then people expect serious quality for what they buy. Graphics may be subsidary to gameplay in real terms, but with graphics like that, you'll struggle to get anyone to even download the thing.
Think of the Toolkit's commercial successes. I know of two TK commercial games, one of which I am not sure how well it fared (but to my knowledge not good) and the other is Dark Age 2. Dark Age 2 sells alright, but hardly by the bucketload! Xorlak gets 2-3 orders a month. Hardly smash-hit eh?
So, you have to ask yourself firstly if you can make a game that can equal or exceed the quality of Dark Age 2 (which was 2 years in the making ... solid work! That's a very large commitment). Secocondly, can you market the game effectively and then distribute the game through secure and professional means after receiving money - not by cash - but by secure methods into a proper current account?
In my opinion the Toolkit is a piece of hobby software. We make games so other people will play and enjoy them, not to become rich. There's a very small market out there willing to buy games created with the Toolkit, and a very small amount of games have made it into the realms of what I would call 'professional' (and most of those are free!).
As a final snag, ever heard of the game Fenix Blade? ( <http://www.fenixblade.com>) ... look at the screenshots, then perhaps play the demo. Then realise it's going to be a free game.
Forget commercial, don't even try unless you already have a good portfolio under your belt of one or two 'good completed' games under your belt.
