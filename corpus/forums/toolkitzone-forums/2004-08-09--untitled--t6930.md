### Xavier · 2004-08-09 16:39 · #B72196

Just so you know, Big D, the problem was that you put the # in front of the variable in the #if statements.
The # sign is used for commands and assigning values to variables (such as #cold!=1) but not for use with variables at any other time.
So it then becomes; #if(cold!=1)
