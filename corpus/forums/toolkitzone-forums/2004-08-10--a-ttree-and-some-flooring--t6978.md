### KasiTL · 2004-08-10 20:23 · #B72857

Just wanted to see if I'm going in the right direction with my trees and see which rugs are prefered....
Cherry Blossom Tree - Parts of my game take place in spring, so here's a flowering tree in full bloom; hence the pink. I realise that the flowers don't cast a shadow on the trunk at this point; that will be added later. Right now I just want to know what people think of the trunk.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/371/957.jpg)
Cherry Blossom Petals Moving in Wind - Thought it might be nice to show breezes in the trees. It's animated at 2, .1, and 5 second intervals for the sample. Cool or no?
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/371/956.gif)
Flooring - Just random flooring such as boards, tiles, and linolium. There's some rugs too. I was wondering what was prefered, the smooth rugs or the scruffy rugs? Personally I'm leaning toward ditching the smooth ones for scruffy ones.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/371/958.png)
