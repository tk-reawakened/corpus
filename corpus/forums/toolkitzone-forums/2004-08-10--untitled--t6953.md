### Dont_Think_So · 2004-08-10 04:26 · #B72532

A couple problems:
1. You can't have more than one parameter in the #if command, so #if(a$=="SPC","ENTER") won't work. You will have to separate them into more than one If.
2. "SPC" isn't a valid key. you will have to set a variable like spc$=" " at the beginning of the program, then check if a$==spc$.
3. In some versions of the Toolkit, Left and Right are switched; so pressing left returns RIGHT, and vise-versa. Other than that, left and right should work.
4. If you are using TK3 (which I assume you aren't, but I'll say this anyway), you could use teh #cursormap functions to do most of that for you.
5. In the middle of your program, #BinternalMenu(2) should be #InternalMenu(2). Don't you hate typos?
I think that's it...
