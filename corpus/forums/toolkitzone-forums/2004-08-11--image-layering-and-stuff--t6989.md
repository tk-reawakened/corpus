# Image layering... and stuff..

### seveneternal · 2004-08-11 02:18 · #B73023

I think images should be able to be put in slots (variables) where you can choose the order of the layers.
Also, when you have lots of images places on the screen, delete a current image or a image in a slot without having to delete everything else on the screen.
Like...
#setimage("blah.jpg", 1, 1, 1, 1, slotnumber1!)
#imagedelete(slotnumber1!)
Something like that.... I think it would be useful for menu or battle systems that use lots of images that needs to be changed often without killing everything else...
Also enables you to have images on screen when moving around... maybe useful for a timer or a special menu...
