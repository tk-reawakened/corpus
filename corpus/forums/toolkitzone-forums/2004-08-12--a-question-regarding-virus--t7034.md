### Dont_Think_So · 2004-08-12 03:08 · #B73733

Yes, they can get through all sorts of things; networks, floppies from other computers, and the like. My unnetworked, no-internet computer got the casper virus about 5 years back; back then, I didn't know of any ways to check where this virus had come from, but judging by the name, I'd say it spawned from "ghosts" on the computer (pieces of undeleted unfragmented data coming together for a completely unforseen effect), or from a floppy on another computer. Probably the latter Smile.
