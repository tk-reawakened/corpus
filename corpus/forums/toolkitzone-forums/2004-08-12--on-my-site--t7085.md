### Dan · 2004-08-13 18:37 · #B74772

TKZ itself offered an individual forum for each game profile at the site, it didn't take off and barely any of the forums were ever used.
So no, I don't think it would.
For an example of the difference between a well written introduction and your introduction:

**You wrote:**

> Well I'm going to do something like if you want a forum about ur game, and just ur game only. Ppl can join, and become a MOD of the game forum, just sigin up and tell me ur company and the game(s) ur making.
>

**I wrote:**

> I am going to offer you the ability to set up a board for your game on my forums, which will allow people to talk about your game and will allow you to update them on it's progress. You will also be able to moderate this forum.
> To get a forum for your game, all you need to do is tell me your company name and the games you are making.
>
