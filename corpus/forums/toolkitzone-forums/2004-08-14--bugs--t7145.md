### Delano · 2004-08-14 13:28 · #B75231

What's happening exactly? Is this a Tk3 problem?
Are you trying to run a Tk3 game on Tk2? If so, then that's not possible - Tk3 games aren't backwards compatible.
If you're trying to run a Tk2 game on Tk3, it *should* work, but you may need to open your files in Tk3 first and resave them, which will convert them to Tk3's format.
What files are missing exactly, and what errors do you get?
