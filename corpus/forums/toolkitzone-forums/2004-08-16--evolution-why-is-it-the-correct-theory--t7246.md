### Ersuis · 2004-08-17 03:13 · #B76926

78% of all statistics are made up on the spot! Can you believe it?
I assure you, 10 out of 10 credible scientists agree: evolution by natural selection is the mechanism by which species evolve. There is no debate about evolution versus anything else in the scientific community anymore; the only debates that happen are based on specifics, and not the mechanism in general. Go to any credible university's biology department: evolution is what's being taught, not junk creation "science," because there is no science in creation.
What's this recent study, though? You can't just give a statistic like that without giving a source. Here's one we can trace, though: in 1997 95% of scientists in the United States believed in evolution (http://www.religioustolerance.org/ev_publi.htm).
A world wide flood wouldn't be a good explanation for sediment layers and fossils, because if there was a world wide flood, all deceased animals would have died at the same time. Multiple dating techniques, including radioactive isotope dating, rock strata dating and even chemical composition of gas pockets trapped in rocks all conclude that every animal did not die at the same time.
Any type of event where an animal is quickly covered in sediments before it could decompose in the open or get scavanged is a good way to create a fossil, however.
As for "real" science observing things directly, when is the last time a scientist observed a quark or watched a probability wave? We don't directly see any of those things; rather, we see the effects and run obscure tests to learn about those things, much like scientists run models and examine fossil evidence, genetic evidence, morphological evidence, geological evidence, atmospheric evidence and more to conclude that the earth is billions of years old and that lifeforms on this planet have changed over that time.
So what if Newton and other scientists were creationists (not to mention Newton couldn't exactly be an evolutionist since evolution wasn't postulated while he lived, but that's a fact you chose to ignore)? Their level of achievement in other areas makes their religious beliefs moot.
Of course there are intermediate forms, but they're not so cut and dry as you would wish. The English language has gone through several different stages, like Old, Middle, and Modern English, not to mention being descended from a Germanic language and taking cues from other languages. So, what happened to those intermediate languages, like Middle English and a half? Using your primary school reasoning, they must not have existed, since you have never heard them, and nobody bothered to write them down. How else could the language have evolved, then, if there weren't intermediate forms? Intermediate forms aren't halfways, or part of the way theres; they're more like variations of a language, and as long as you can still communicate with people, you're still speaking the same language. Once you cross that barrier and your language has changed so much that communication is impossible, you have a different language.
In math, there is something called the Intermediate Value Theorm. Basically, it says that for any continuous function, you will reach every value between a and b (think of driving a car... you don't just suddenly start at 55mph, you have to accelerate to that speed, hitting 15mph, 25mph, and so on). For various creatures, we can deduce what they looked like in a similiar way, if we know what they looked like at point a and point b. Of course it's going to be approximate, but that's part of the game. We accept it, revise drawings as new evidence appears, and continue to move forward.
It's unlikely that any intermediate forms would be fossilized. There are millions of fossils we have found, and still millions more we haven't, but they're just a tiny fraction of the trillions of organisms that ever lived on earth. Getting fossilized is seriously all about being in the right place at the right time, and there's no reason to expect that a single organism with a single change would ever become fossilized, would survive to present day, and would be in a position for us to find it.
