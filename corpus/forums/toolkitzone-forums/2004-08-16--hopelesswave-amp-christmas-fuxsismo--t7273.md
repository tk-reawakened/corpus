### Nih · 2004-08-16 17:50 · #B76570

Well, it seems like just one view of the page uses all your bandwidth. Try uploading them to your game profile.
Anyway...
In the Christmas one, I noticed that all of your inside boards are a single room that fits the entire screen. That's one of the most common problems I see in TK games. People make huge rooms and put maybe 3 things in it. Instead, you should try making the rooms smaller, giving them interesting shapes, and little stuff like rugs. If you look at any professional RPG you'll see what I'm talking about. In fact, I'm going to use the same example from Chrono Trigger that I posted in someone else's posts.
<http://nih.geahost.com/cthouse.bmp>
Notice how they're not huge square rooms with nothing in them.
