# UT vs UT2003/UT2004

### Cesque · 2004-08-16 14:41 · #B76519

I made a short comparision + poll... for all unreal fans Very Happy Again, a sequel does not necessarily must be better. Notice I've never played UT2004, the demo is downloading right now on my puter so I can spend some time waiting for it, writing this thing.
Graphics
Obviously, UT 2003 dominates. Water reflects surroundings. Weapons on level get stained by blood if you kill someone nearby. Player, in opposition to UT die/gib can lose body parts or burn as he dies.
UT has one better things, gaps from bullets. It added to the effect. Also, blood in UT is more tolerable during gameplay... in UT 2003 it sprays straight in your face and effectively disables your sight.
UT2003 also has lame display of armor/medikits, for example, the quakish-symbol style. I like when in games armor is armor, not rotating shield symbol.
Weapons
Both games, first of all, have this totally fucked-up idiotic weapon known as "GES bio rifle". Whoever came up with idea of weapon which spits green shit at enemies is a perverted bastard and deserves crucifiction.
UT had better basic weapon - enforcer pistol. In UT2003 it has been changed to grenader for some part. In UT, you could get two and play Matrix style by jumping and shooting 'em John Woo/gangsta style, in UT2003 there is no such cool effect. (it wasn't well done in UT anyway, I mean, it was cool when you had it, but it displayed as 1 pistol when enemy had this weapon).
Some weapons' effects are generally greatly improved in UT2003. For example, shock rifle's "third attack" (shooting orb with beam). Why in both games beam costs 1 ammo, orb costs 1 ammo, and shooting beam at orb costs 3 ammo, I have no idea. Strange logic behind this. Even redeemer looks sort of better in UT2003.
The thing I also hate in UT2003, though, is lightning gun rather than sniper rifle. I have no idea how headshot of lightning should be better than any other part of body (electricity normally goes through nerve system and main deaths are caused by shock caused to heart, not head)... The lightning looks like crap anyway, sniper rifle is far better idea. Propably they added lightning so that seeing the lightning's path you can see where sniper is, God knows...
Players
First question, what the hell? Why the bloody hell in UT2003 players fight in some space robo suits and are basicaly twice as big as in UT? They look like mechs, no people, now. Why? Because they're easier to hit now? Pardon me, in UT there was a battle between people, in UT2003 and it's armor-suits we have battle between some fat-ugly-mechanised-pseudo-humans. Also, as you walk, you wave on left and right and you're basicaly awfully slow. All dynamics of game are gone, if they wanted to slow people, they could, but why make them giant waving left and right ugly metaltechs? I see no point.
Gameplay
UT2003 had some new innovate gameplay modes... But the player robo-look screws everything up anyway, he he he.
Personally, thus... I prefer classical UT. Yes, graphics are worse, but gameplay is more fun, in my opinion. I'm yet to see UT2004 demo but it propably doesn't differ from UT2003 much. They added vehicles, some new mode and other imporvements.
But... what are your opinions? (for all those who played UT/UT2003 or even demos of those)
