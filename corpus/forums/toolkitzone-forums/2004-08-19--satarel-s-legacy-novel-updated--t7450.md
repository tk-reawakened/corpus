# Satarel's Legacy novel, updated...

### Cesque · 2004-08-19 18:32 · #B78476

Note: I haven't wrote anything for this for long and left this uncontinoued at some point... And thus, having no better things to do with this, I put it up here. If you want more, I can sacrifice some time and finish it Smile
Chapter 1: Price of Loyalty
Gerard went up the staircase into the giant cathedral. The catherine's wheel above him depicted an antropomorphic male god with a book and a sword, with halo of light around his head. Cathedral of Light was the greatest building in whole Campana, altrough still most townsfolk preferred to pilgrimise to the Rock Cathedral at Petronica.
Gerard was one of best paladins in service of the old master of order, or so they said. He was charismatic, always appearing with his brown bear, long hair and brown cape with a golden symbol of the sun. But the old master of order died, and the new master wasn't liking Gerard at all. No one truly knew, propably beside the king, how a man like him could gain control over the order of paladins. But now the new master, Kane Casull, had something to discuss with Gerard. "Another stupid task..." - thought Gerard, and he knew he was right.
- "Your late, boy." - Kane greeted Gerard who entered the chapel room and saluted. - "2 minutes late. Don't think I will tolerate this, just because I'm new here. I will put a limit of my tolerance to 5 minutes... Be later than this and be fired. We don't need disobeying the discipline here."
- "Yes, master." - answered Gerard, at the same time thinking "Shut up, your idiot, I had things to check."
- "The necromancers still look for their missing blade. Like little children who lost a toy and have trouble because mommy wants it back. They have 3 months to find it and deliver it to king of Ascalon, then we will start the war. I don't care where the blade is and what power does it contain. They broke the oath and they will have to pay. The previous master of order was wrong to trust these sorcerors from Twillight Citadel."
Gerard obviously knew it was impossible for necromancers to retrieve the blade. The one who stole it was propably back-uped by forces beyond master of order's comprehension. But this logic propably was too hard to understand by Kane, so Gerard didn't even try to argue.
- "You will deliver this letter to the chancellor of necromancers, Zarget." - Kane said, making a sign to one of the paladins in the room, the black-skinned one (most loyal of Kane's advisors), which handed Gerard the letter. - "Then you will return here instantly. Anything goes wrong and you're fired. Am I clear?"
- "Yes, master." - answered Gerard, thinking "What the hell is with this man?! Majority complex?"
He left. On his way, he noticed a king coming. He instantly bowed on his knees and lowered his head, then stood up and saluted.
- "Watch out for the new master, Gerard." - said the king Gregory XIII.
- "I am, my liege, I am."
- "Good. I don't trust him. I'll get rid of him when it becomes possible."
- "Thank you for advice, my liege."
- "One more thing... Also watch out for his black advisor. He comes from Antioch, he might plot something against you."
- "Yes, my liege."
- "And remember, if war will break, it is not because we hate necromancers, it is because they allowed the Exitium Blade to be lost. Fear not, the Blade propably has no real power at all, after all these years. Follow the Code and fear not, all you do is rightous."
- "I know, my liege."
- "Good. Now I have to talk with this 'Kane'..."
- "Yes, my liege." - said Gerard, saluted and continue walking through the hall of the Cathedral of Light. The reflective floor below him mirrored the fresques above his head. The same god as on the catherine's wheel - god of light, Mithra, main deity worshipped by the paladins, sitting among the stars in heaven, on the right side of the Supreme Lord, Mazda.
- "If he's so all-powerful, why doesn't he come down on the Earth he forsaken and do something with this madness?" - Gerard whispered to himself.
As he was about to leave to the city to get some supplies, a young boy ran toward him, holding a rifle and sack of bullets in his hands.
- "Sir Gerard!" - he said, breathing loud after getting to the paladin. - "King ordered me to deliver you this."
- "A rifle?" - he looked at the long musket. - "He doesn't trust my sword skills?"
- "He doesn't want to lose our best paladin..."
- "Lose? I'm going just on diplomatic mission..."
- "Forget I said anything, sir. That's just my assumption. Our liege must has his reasons. Take it, sir."
Gerard took the rifle and put it behind the belt over his back. He opened the ammo sack and moved all the bullets into the leather openable sack he had on the right side of his belt.
- "Thank you, Johnatan."
- "No problem, sir."
- "How much you got for this...?"
- "Sir, I am a volounteer, I want to become a paladin one day-..."
- "All right, all right. How much?"
- "20 GP."
- "20? Our liege must have trust in you, usually he pays 15. Here," - he grabbed some coins from another of his sacks, this time to the left of his belt. - "5 more for you. Good luck on becoming one of us."
- "Thank you, sir!" - said the boy, took the money and ran back through the hall. - "Your horse waits in the stables!" - he added on the run.
Chapter 2: Flight of the Swan
Gerard was moving fast through the stone roads of Ascalon. He headed west, traveled for about 4 hours and hoped to stop at one of small towns in the middle of his way to give his horse something to drink and eat something himself. As he approached marketplace of one of such villages - Viemont - he noticed crowds gathering before the church of Mithra. Someone was there, at the top of the belltower, visible through the gap in between the columns connecting bottom of bell tower's top with it's roof, with a dagger, in a grey robe with hood cast over hishead and, on his chest, an ankh symbol. Necromancer.
- "Sir!" - one of town women hurried toward Gerard. - "Maybe you can do something! He propably wants to defile our church by killing himself by jumping from it!"
Gerard looked at the necromancer with dislike, jumped from his horse, handed woman 25 GP to bring his steed by the local inn and order innkeeper to feed it. As she hurried to do as he asked her, paladin ordered crowd to move away and stood right before the bell tower, raising his head above to see the necromancer. He wasn't too old, in fact, he was quite young. Compared to 35 year old Gerard, at least. He was bold, without beard and his skin was pale, almost white. "Who deals with the dead, looks like the dead." - paladin thought.
- "You there!" - he said to the necromancer. - "What do you want?!"
- "You all live me alone! I want just to kill myself and no longer listen to your stupid arguments! No hatred, no persecutions, sure! My friend Phoebel was going to one of your hospitals, they diagnosed him with mental disease and put into insane asylum! My friend Ceres was on her way to Ventus as you stopped her, accused for using necromancy in Campana territory and put into prison for 15 years. And I was looking forward to estabilishing closer relationship with her!"
- "Listen..."
- "SHUT UP! My brother Urvile. He lived in Ascalon, he protested openly against war, he was thrown away from his village, now he lives in some dark tower like an outcast. I am sick of this. You took my friends and future and you ruined my life, now that you closed the borders I am trapped in your Hod forsaken country of intolerance and hatred!"
- "What the hell are you talking about?! Closed borders?"
- "Don't you know, paladin? Your good master Kane ordered to close borders this morning. Only representatives of Ascalon government or military, including paladins or people with diplomatic visas close border freely now!"
- "No way... No way! He couldn't-..."
- "As you see, he did, and now I am going to get some rest from this sickness. May Hod allow me to feast with him in the land of the dead!"
- "Stop it! What is your name?"
- "Who cares?"
- "I care! Answer me, or-..."
- "Or what?"
Gerard preppared his rifle, loaded it and pointed at the gap in bell tower. - "Or I will shoot you down."
- "Come on, shoot! I fear no death! I am used to it! Death is only thing you offer willingly to necromancers!"
- "Fine." - Gerard shot few centimeters from the necromancer, striking the bell, which began to ring from the impact. The sound of fire mixed with ringing of the bell totally shocked necromancer, who made a step to the left and slipped, falling down directly on Gerard, before he could take a step back.
- "Thank you so much!" - the necromancer said, standing up, brushing the dust from his arms. - "You ruined my suicide! But who cares? Where were you heading? To make some sneak assasinations in Ventus? To ensure necromancer repressions are going all right?"
- "Shut... up..." - said Gerard, coughing and standing up. - "I am only going to give a letter to your chancellor."
- "Blackmail?"
- "Shut up!" - Gerard grabbed the necromancer by his neck. - "One more word and..."
- "No way!" - answered the wizard. - "I am going with you!"
Gerard left the grasp.
- "Listen, we both see something is wrong with this country, but I can't let you-..."
- "You will, you will. I am coming with you. You think you're that safe in my land? No way, we necromancers know your plans. My nation can easily kill or imprison you. Paladins are our sworn enemies."
- "I know..."
- "I'll go with you to ensure your safety, but you have to do one thing for me. You need to let me get back home."
Gerard wasn't really paying much attention, knowing necromancers will not even touch him. But having necromancer guide in Ventus, country generally ruled by necromancers, was always a good idea. At least... Why not? - "All right."
- "Excellent! Now let's head into some tavern and drink a bit. If I failed to free my soul by killing my flesh, I can always free it by getting drunk."
- "Whatever... You pay."
- "I?! I have to conserve my money in case your people will want to tax us necromancers!"
- "I won't buy you anything, beggar."
- "Oh, come on! I am on your side. Stop being as racist as all you paladins."
- "Seeing you, I began to realise we are right to hate your kind."
- "So you agree? Some strong vodka for me."
Chapter 3: Colors of the Chessboard
Gerard ordered a strong vodka imported from eastern lands of Ki'Tan and 2 mugs of beer for himself. They sat by the table.
- "So what's your name?" - Gerard returned to the question.
- "Cygnus. And you?"
- "Gerard."
- "Oh well. I would never suspect you Ascalonians for being imaginative, even when it comes to giving names."
- "One more word against the Ascalonians and I'll smack your vodka on your empty head."
- "Hmm... head always hurts a bit after vodka."
- "Stop this and tell me something more about what Kane managed to do during his short rule so far?"
- "What a strange country. Not even master of paladin order has greater rule than the king, but also foreign traveller has greater knowledge of your government than a high-ranking military officer?"
- "What are you doing here anyway?"
- "Vodka is cheaper on Ascalon side. Plus, I'm on a way back from visiting Ceres in prison."
- "Oh well. Not let's get back to Kane and his reign."
- "He closed the borders and disabled all necromancy licenses within borders of Ascalon. He increased sentence for being caught using necromancy within town borders to 15 years from 3, ensured death penalties for repeatitive necromancy users, same for communistic rebellion activists support and gnostic support. Simply magnificent." - said Cygnus and began to drink his vodka.
- "How could he-...? He's here only since 4 months, and king wouldn't possibly agree with all this..."
Suddenly, a man rushed into the tavern, his spiky hair black, lower part of his head covered with dark violet scarf.
- "This system will collapse." - he said, his voice was slightly muffled through the scarf. - "You all heard the tales? The demon influence is powerful enough. It corrupted even the mind of master of paladin order!"
- "We don't want more you gnostics here!" - said the innkeeper.
- "Stop it!" - said Cygnus, standing up. - "Let him explain this all to us!"
- "And you necromancers, too! Get out of here to the horse parking place and talk there!"
- "My friend said stop it, didn't you hear?" - now said Gerard, standing up. - "If you won't, I can easily ensure you will be accused for intolerance toward the foreign visitors and your license of running this tavern forgotten."
- "Oh well. I'm sorry, gentlemen." - said the innkeeper and began to rub empty bottles to clear them.
- "Sit with us." - said Gerard.
- "Your mind is unsure, paladin." - said the gnostic to Gerard, sitting.
- "Stop telling me about my mind and tell me about the crap that is going on!"
- "Yeah." - added Cygnus.
- "Everything is going on as it should be." - said the gnostic. - "The changes we are witnessing are regular. The demons have awaken and their existence is like a shadow upon the minds of rulers of Ascalon. Your master of order is merely a pawn of the one whom demons sent here. Yes, your predictions are right."
- "Stop reading my mind!" - said Gerard as he felt strange feeling in his head. - "It is the new advisor from Antioch."
- "You surely know what resides within the jungle land of Antioch."
- "A powerful empire, controlled by military, famous of it's undead slayers, best soldiers trained in eradication of undead and purging influence of necromancy in the world." - hearing this words, Cygnus made a resigned face - "In times past, the empire of Antioch was at war with Ascalon and island kingdom Nifle, as we tried to colonise these lands. But since then, we're at peace with it and it's citisens."
- "Good. You know the history from paladin academy. But not only, my friend. Think... think, and you'll know the answer..."
- "A cult... dark cult, which in ages past worshipped Leviathan and made sacrifices by drowning people in the sea for the giant demon lord - which they called Kanaloa - to feed. But the physical existence of Leviathan has been disproved... The sea became a safe place now, and the cult was forgotten among the sands of time."
- "Now, why would a member of demon-worshipping cult want to take control over paladins?"
- "I don't know. To make us worship the demon?"'
- "You know the answer, as it is already answered."
- "To... wage war on necromancers."
- "Yes. And by the way, what does necromancer land hold that is connected with demons?"
- "The Exitium Blade?"
- "The Devil Spine mountain rage. You propably know also this tale."
- "At ages past, some cult opened a portal to Nether Realm there, using the dark energy that propably gathered there after a starfall accident ages past. Ancient books speak of this. But the early order of paladins managed to destroy the demonic presence and close the portal."
- "Excellent. You propably know why it all happens, and you might even know what happens."
- "I know the puzzle pieces... But I can't see the image."
Gnostic lowered his voice tone to a mere whisper.
- "I will tell you, shortly and easily to understand. Kane knows that necromancers will not return the Exitium Blade because the one who has it already invoked the power sleeping in the ancient weapon. He only wants to wage war on Ventus to turn paladins' heads from Devil Spine and what is really happening. When all continent of Umber will be at war, the demons will approach and sow their destruction. You still have time to warn people responsible to prevent the conflict, but can you prevent the inevitable?"
The gnostic, ending his reponse, stood up and wanted to live.
- "Wait!" - now said Cygnus after him, standing up, drunk already. - "How do you know this all?"
- "The advisor's name is Haman and my name is Urquell. We will meet again." - said the gnostic and left the tavern.
Gerard thinked for a while over his beer. - "Good god." - he said finally, loudly. - "GOOD GOD!"
- "I totally agree." - said Cygnus. - "This vodka is Hod damn strong and the people here are Hod damn weirdos."

He raised his arms and began to chant an incantation.

- "And I'm afraid I'll have to arrest you." - added Ascalon militiaman.

A man at first sight looking like a mage, with rune-covered red and violet robe, came toward them. He had pale skin and long, blond hair, as well as shining blue eyes.

- "All right..."

Gerard looked stunned, as was also Cygnus. - "Umm..." - the first began. - "The light god surely watches over us. I'm Gerard, and this is Cygnus. What can we do for you?"
- "Not much, really. I'm Albert. I'm from town of Almirante, you know, the greatest port in whole Ascalon. It used to be greatest port in whole Navarre, until Navarre became part of Ascalon. Oh well. I will need a guide through Ventus, I have some friends who migrated there. They told me I might find a job or even place to live there and I plan to move out, as the new Ascalon law apparently wants to cripple mages' laws and persecute all magic-users. I sold all my earthly possessions so far and I'm free to go, I just thought a necromancer knowing the region would be nice! And I noticed one here, strangely with a paladin, too. A rare pair, especially in Ascalon."
- "Strange that you can bring luck occasionaly, and not only trouble." - said Gerard to Cygnus as the gate was opened and all three passed through. - "I have to deliver a letter to chancellor Zarget from master of order."
- "Whoa! You must be one of the most loyal paladins, if they chose you for such a task!"
- "Actually, I have purpose to question the sense of my loyalty..."
Chapter 5: Place of Rest

- "For the first one, there are two reasons..." - said Cygnus. - "It's getting late and Ventus is well known for harsh weather. You should be proud you didn't see one of our famous hurricanes which strike the shores one or two times in year."

- "Very good idea." - said Cygnus. - "As far as I know, there should be a town of Tetra somewhere near the border."
Cygnus wasn't wrong, and after half of our during which the cold areas began to change into land of eternal fall, with heaps of fallen leaves lying everywhere, painting earth red and brown, and bare trees the travellers arrived at Tetra. - "What do you mean?" - asked Gerard.
- "Do we head with the letter or to my friends?"
- "Can your friends wait a bit?"
- "Sure, they don't even know I'm visiting them. They just told me I can visit them anytime and they'll help me get some work in Ventus."
- "As a gravedigger?"
- "Shut up, Gerard." - said Cygnus.
- "Oh well, sir undead wizard got offended."
- "I am NOT undead!"

- "You're coming with us?" - asked Cygnus.
- "If you let me, why not?"
- "Of course. I know chancellor pretty well, I was studying in necromancer academy with his son Moebius. Unfortunately now the poor freak is a undead ghoul, as his father ordered his ressurection after some fanatical peoples' crusade from Ascalon began to attack border villages - including this one - and Moebius died, hurrying for help. Oh well. Ascalonian mentality will never change."
- "That's a piece of damn romantic story." - said Gerard who, after his fourth beer, was already getting a bit drunk.
- "Let's go tell the innkeeper we'll rent some rooms before he goes insane and begins to chant exorcisms here." - said Albert.
- "Another good point. I see you understand life pretty well." - agreed Cygnus.
During the night, strong wind was blowing. Altough in Ascalon, it was early spring, snow just melted and first flowers were blooming, weather in Ventus was always different. Most inner parts of country including the capital Nitor with it's Twillight Citadel were always covered with snow. Snowfalls were regular in the middle of spring. As the folklore said, in the land of the dead, mother earth was dead as well.
The party stoop up a bit early. Gerard ordered meeting before the inn and all three men debated over type of transport they should choose. There was a train coursing from Campana through Nitor to enterance to underground Devil Spine mining complex, the only problem was that the train's closest station was at Nitor itself, as necromancers somehow avoided technology and the railway was mainly built for use of paladins who visited Nitor or Devil Spine for diplomatic purposes. The railway was closed anyway now, by orders of master of order Kane, who wanted to isolate Ventus from the rest of world before declaring the war to prevent it from gaining supplies.
- "What a remote civilisation. We'll take another coach and Albert pays this time." - said Gerard, and they done as he said.
Chapter 6: World Forsaken City
Nitor was covered with snow. It was always covered with snow. Second largest city in whole Umber, after Ascalon capital Campana and before Nifle capital Albion, Nitor was the proud monument of still standing necromancer power on western continent, with it's Twillight Citadel, center of whole Ventus government.
Albert paid the coach and all three got out of wagon.
- "Ah, crap!" - Gerard said, getting out into the snow. - "I should have taken my winter clothes!"
- "What should I say?" - asked Albert, whose robe was light as silk. - "Oh well."
- "Home, sweet home." - said Cygnus, and pointed at the dark citadel somewhere in the distance, rising above the other buildings. Ventus architecture was a specific one, buildings were dark, constructed from mud brick or - in cases of more rich families - obsidian. Temples and palaces were easily distinguishable from the rest for it were surrounded by high pylons (usually crowned with some ornamental design), connected with the main building so that they looked like legs around spider's main body. Twillight Citadel had a line of such pylons built before the front wall.
- "We're in the maw of the beast..." - Gerard told himself, followed by necromancer's "Shut up, Gerard".
- "This land is crawling with undead." - noticed Albert as they walked toward the ctiadel through snowy streets. - "It's common in this land, I guess?"
- "Well, yes." - answered Cygnus. - "They're good type of workers. Cheap and effective."
- "And they don't have to eat and sleep."
- "Not exactly. There are 2 types of undead, controlled and sustained by magic and free-willed ressurected dead. The second type is just like living. They're simply revived from dead by healing the wounds of their body and bringing soul back to it.
