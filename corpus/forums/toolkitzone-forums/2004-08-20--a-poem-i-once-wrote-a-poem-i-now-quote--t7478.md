# A poem I once wrote, a poem I now quote...

### Levi · 2004-08-20 05:43 · #B78798

Who I am
By
Levi
Who I am, does it matter
Who I am, do you care
In my life I stumble and faulter
In my life I lie, I live in fear
Darkness hides the light
Darkness keeps me from it
Darkness is my life
Darkness I am in it
Light pours down on me
Light shines with glory
Light Shines, it shines so bright
Light light, I love the light
Light and Darkness my life is filled with thee
Light and Darkness my life my destiny
Light and Darkness both my life they share
Light and Darkness does no one care
I Am, he told me that fateful day
I Am, he said as love came to stay
I am not without darkness
Yet I am full of light
To the great I AM
I simply am
I am me
A child of the light

### Levi · 2004-08-20 06:04 · #B78803

It's just a general description of life and who I am in man's eyes and God's eyes. The difference between the two. "I AM" is God, coming from a few different verses in the bible.
"Who I am, does it matter" refers to who I am in the world. SImply another human in the billions of others that exist.
Darkness is how I view my life, how evil I see myself as.
The light is my escape from that. I have the escape and yet I am still evil, light and darkness.
"To the Great I AM, I simply am"
To God I don't need to be something more than who he made me to be. I simply need to be me. I simply am.
