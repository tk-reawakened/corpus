# One Last Poem

### Levi · 2004-08-20 05:48 · #B78800

Mystery Angel
By
Levi
Dark corner of the sky
I watch you sit and sigh
Your wings folded under your chin
I see that face and I grin
5,6,7 years gone by
I remember you, I laugh and fly
On formless wings of space and time
I soar to you, friend of mine
Darkness hides you from my eyes
Darkness cold and fleeting
Covers you from feet to eyes
In hopes to stop our meeting
It's been too long, I must confess
since you and I have known happiness
I know you yet know you not
A friend I had, a friend I lost
Covers of Darkness I yank from your eyes
I stare at you across the skies
Your hair, your face, those deep blue eyes
Stare back at me across these dark deep skies
I see you, yet I know you not
A friend I had, a friend I lost
Distant are you my angelic friend
Distant are you remembering life from end to end
Mystery angel hidden away in the dark
Mystery angel lost to the dark
You could have lived
You could have stayed
Yet gone were you seven years today
I've lost you, you're never to return
I shall never forget you, I say and then I turn
You reach and grab me by the arm
You turn me back and hold me close
A friend I lost, the one I miss the most
Life is different without you
It will never be the same
So long as I am without you
I am not the same
You oh angel were myself
My friend, my childhood dream
I left you far behind one day
For luscious grass so green
Never thinking, never turning back
I left you that day
Never looking back
never wondering what became
Of my friend so dear to me
I wander now alone
My life is empty without truth
Like you I'm left alone
