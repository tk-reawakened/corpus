# Sinnovations, Inc

### Dragonmaster · 2004-08-24 22:32 · #B80938

Well I thought I would start off by putting some information about the company, Sinnovations, Inc.
1st: The name- Well my company name use to be called Switzer Innovations. I thought that cutting the s and putting it on innovations had a nice ring to it. I think it also works well for my game "Of Darkness and Light".
2nd: About the founder-My name is Ben Switzer. I am currently 26 and working as a Software Engineer for the navy. I recently got married in February.
3rd: The team-Well as of right now, its just me. I usually contract out things like graphics, due to my lack of skills in that area. I have already contracted out the graphics for ODNL to Tenala. She is doing a great job, and I would reccomend her. Im currently trying to find someone to do some music for the game.
4th: Why should you play my games?-Well I have been playing video games for about 22 years now. I have been playing RPGs for probably 16 or so years. Now this doesnt make me an expert at making games. I believe that I have some unique ideas for games, and I know what cliches to avoid.
Thats about all I can think of right now. If you have any questions, just post them here. I will be putting some info on the games that I have in mind here, along with updated screen shots.
I hope to have an alpha version of ODNL by August 3rd. This will be a teaser, hopefully it will get some people interested in the game.
