# Stix and the Stones

### Dragonmaster · 2004-08-25 13:24 · #B81363

Intro:
I started thinking of what might be a funny game, and be fun at the same time. This idea of a "stick man" stuck in a 3D world, trying to become normal came to me. At first, I wasn't sure about it, but I ran the idea across several people and it was generally well received, so I decided to go ahead with it.
The game:
Stix and the Stones will be about a 1 or 2 dimensional stick figure searching throughout his world for a way to become 3 dimensional. Stix will come across a witch that tells him about the 6 stones which have the power to turn a peice of his body to 3D. THis means that at any time Stix may have a 1D head and a 3D Left leg. Many of the peices have special abilities that will be neccesary to acquiring other peices. Im not quite sure how this would work out in the TK, but I may give it a try. Stix will have some unique abilites and enemies as a stick figure as well. Some of his abilities may be something like turning into a broom and sweeping the enemy, turning into a baseball bat and hitting a baseball at the enemy, orplaying fetch with dogs.
Some of the unique enemies will be dogs that want to use him as a pissing post, cats that want to use him as a scratching post, etc.
The main character:
The main character, Stix, is well a stick figure. He's tired of being the only person in his world that isnt 3D.
