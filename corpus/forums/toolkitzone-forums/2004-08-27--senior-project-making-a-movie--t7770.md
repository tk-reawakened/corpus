Don't worry, I came up with an incredibally cliched idea (don't worry, it was more cliched).
High school chic has a great life...best friend, okay that stuff. She goes home, her parents leave, later that night she finds the suicide note. Her parents are dead, but nobody but her knows about it (before, they got in a car accident, but thats been done thousands of times, so I decided to spin things up a bit)
Dealing with all this pain (it will be a bitch to compose painful music) she grows distant from everybody. A guy moves into the school district, and comforts her to a point (they do not get romantically involved...again, steering away from cliches).
Since nobody knows her parents are dead, she can stay at her house. Eventually she tells her best friend, and they have a good cheer at her house. But the cops and the bank comes to evict her (now knowing of the diseased parents); they flee, and have a fun time. Until that one dude is drunk and hits them with his car. Cynical way of ending it.
I'm debating whether after that I should add her parents coming home and laughing about how many people they fooled with their cruel joke. Then they say "Hey daughter, we're home!" That would be too surreal though, so I don't know.
