# Prose - Enlightenment

### Psionic · 2004-08-28 01:30 · #B83141

I saw this forum and I thought I'd post some prose and poetry from my journal...
Enlightenment
A reflected authentic spirit calls me forth from a series of faces.
I detach my tongue,
locking it away for a time when they can comprehend
its true nature and meaning.
My eyes and ears now open wide, so widely opened
that they have begun to understand even more
than ever before.
But the organ of sense that has seemed to have the strongest
reaction to this painfully beautiful call is my third eye;
the purest gateway to my mind.
It seems to have been held forth by this spirit allowing its
supernatural vision to penetrate all the artificial armor
these soulless human shells wear, and have worn for
every year of my existence.
In vane these shells are now to me,
only one certain energy matters.
Yet, it is still oblivious to my master sense, my third eye...
It's masked by a certain fog, until I can see through
this deceiving mist, I shall continue to store these thoughts.
I will no longer allow these hollow apparitions to murder
the spawn of my mind. I am what I create.
