# Western and Eastern RPGs

### Khin · 2004-08-28 01:19 · #B83128

List as many Eastern(mostly Jap) and Western(mostly American) rpgs you are familiar with(probably beaten). Let's try to compare their strong and weak points.
I just find this interesting...you see, we, as rpg developers(or future developers), should do some sort of surveys or studies like this. If Western rpgs prove more popular(or better, in essence) than eastern style, then our development(or mine at least) should be aimed at the more profitable side, don't you think?
So start listing. this may come out as a sort of games review and may evetually help out our fellow rpg makers to have their guidelines set in terms of story, gameplay, etc.
Eastern RPG:
-Final Fantasy Series
FF is undoubtedly the most popular RPG hailing from the Land of the Rising Sun. Wonderful characters and superb storyline, not to mention, since the advent of ff7...breathtaking graphics. Mini games are entertaining..although in latter versions, they seem to have concentrated on single(but elaborate) mini-games such as cards and blitzball. Apart from that, battle is in Turn-Based. very sleep-inducing.
BTW, FFonline is not included here...never seen or played it x_x
Western RPG:
-NeverwinerNights (A D&D game)
My personal favorite. In terms of graphics, it easily beats any other game. Very gorgeous 3D..realtime 3D and realtime battle kicked a million asses! Story is good too. Very revealing and mysterious at the same time. Although not as cinematic as FF(or any Jap RPG). Online is a plus..but not really my concern.
Ithink I may have been unfair..FF is for a console, while NWN is for the PC...can't think of a decent Jap rpg for the pc, or an english rpg for the console...

### ForeverNoobish · 2004-09-01 01:19 · #B85772

I'll compare a western ACTION RPG with an eastern ACTION RPG series... and yet, both were made by Squaresoft! Very Happy Its just that the western one, obviously, was made by Square of America...
Eastern: Seiken Densetsu series
The games I include in this series are FFA, Secret of Mana, SD3, and Sword of Mana, which is really just FFA with better graphics. The strong points on FFA and Secret of Mana are the gameplay, as well as decent plotlines (though, quite frankly, I didnt get too involved in the Secret of Mana story). The strong part about SD3 is the music: although it is actually quite crappy to listen to outside of gameplay, when you are playing the game, it keeps you involved and feeling it. The plot in that game has so many holes though... if they would fix that up, that game would be near perfect. Also, what I don't like about that game is that, like FFA and Sword of Mana, it is a hackfest. Where in Secret of Mana you could only get maximum damage after conserving your energy for x number of seconds, you can just continually press the attack button here and do considerable damage. But the graphics in SD3 are downright gorgeous. I mean, look at the GRASS in that game and compare it to the grass of any other 2D RPG. It's way better. The graphics are fantastic for that game, and not too shabby for Sword of Mana either. There weren't too many hard puzzles in this series, which I didn't like either. I usually like to work my brain a little. But the games actually turned out to be rather involving and keep you in the game until you finish it, which is always a plus (except for Sword of Mana, but that only didn't keep me involved for too much at one time cause I'd already played and beaten FFA).
Western: Secret of Evermore
When I tell people that Square of America developed this game, usually they are surprised. Hopefully this crowd was a bit more knowledgable though. Confused The plotline... well... it wasn't too impressive, IMO. Neither was the music. The graphics weren't half bad though. And even though the gameplay was a direct rip of Secret of Mana's basically, at least the gameplay of Secret of Mana was quite good. Very Happy Why change something that doesn't need to be changed? Also, there were a few good dungeons in this one that made my mind work a little harder than it had to in any of the Seiken Densetsu games. That is a plus. This game was very much like Secret of Mana. Maybe a step below it in some areas, and then a step above it in others. Not too bad of an RPG if you like Action RPGs. I recommend it to anyone who does. But then again, I would also recommend the Seiken Densetsu games even more. Neither the western or eastern versions of these action RPGs are too shabby. But the eastern wins my vote in this case.

### equel · 2004-09-02 05:37 · #B86553

Generally when I compare Eastern and Western RPGs....most of the time, Eastern chooses Turn system (not sure how you exactly call it) but you have to wait for your turn but "most" not "all" -_- or probably little more than half. And most of the Western RPGs...they choose real time.
But again, maybe it's because I've only played small number of RPGs.
As in storyline...for Eastern, compared to Western RPGs, has a small story, I'm talking about the scale it covers, not the quality. I dunno how to explain this clearly but there's something very detailed, cute, small, pretty about Eastern RPGs. For Western RPGs, it covers a lot, very detailed but not as Eastern, something heavy and big. What I mean here...is that when I play Eastern's...there's something very Asian (well obviously) like....water (-_-) but when I play Western's...it's very heavy like a fire or...a rock? hmmm....
Ok I'll try to explain more clearly...y'know how asian soldiers like Samurai or Ninjas wore simple clothes and light armour? And how european soldiers like in medieval time wore a huge heavy metallic armour? 0_0 It's basically the same. -_- Hope I'm explaining better. Both Eastern and Western RPGs are very good and deep and has unique storyline also depicts their culture well. However, while there's something light about Eastern, there's something heavy about Western.
Well in any case, I love both RPGs like Diablo and NWN for Western and FF series, Ys, Arcturus for Eastern. ^^
