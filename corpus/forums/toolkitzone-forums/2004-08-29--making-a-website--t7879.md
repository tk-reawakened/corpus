# Making a website

### TheBrainchild · 2004-08-29 01:12 · #B83913

All right, people keep asking about different aspects of making websites. I'm clearing a few questions. Here's my step- by- step guide:
1)Learn HTML or use premade templates
2)Find a good host (Bravenet, Geocities, Angelfire, etc.)
3)Make your basic site
4)Add stuff, like counters, etc.
5)Shorten your url, using free url masking such as .co.nr, .dk, and .cjb.net
6)List your site in search engines
7)Keep updating your site
