# a poem to my ex

### matt72186 · 2004-08-31 18:16 · #B85501

A poem I wrote to this chick that cheated on me...
I am dead
and born again
now I realize
you were never really my friend
for all that you have put me through
I can never forgive you
I hope someday you pay the cost
and remember the love you lost
you failed to kill me, though you tried
you hurt me badly, and I cried
you said you loved me, but you lied
now my bond to you is untied.
When I needed you, you weren't there
I almost died and you didn't care
IF YOU EVER SEE ME, DON'T EVEN SAY HI, BECAUSE ALL MY CARE FOR YOU DID DIE... GOODBYE.
