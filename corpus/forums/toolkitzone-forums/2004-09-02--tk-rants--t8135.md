Happy Anniversary TK Rants
Oi a small history of my online comic TK Rants.
The date is February 14 2004. During this time, the threaded forums were on the brink of destruction. There were only a couple of members left. During this time people were making TK comics and I wanted a piece of it.
I first wanted to do one called "Cy vs. Cy" but it was a stupid idea and I'm glad I didn't go with it. Oi. My second was one were the TK members would get angry with each other for no reason at all. This was the original TK Rants but that idea didn't go well so I modified it to just create simple humour comics. I kept the name though (although I don't believe that is how it started but I said it so many times, I'm starting to believe it). I showed my first TK Rant which starred Chris Reeves and Zap! (that archive is now lost forever) and didn't get much replies. My second TK Rant was a valentines day special. That one did get replies and they loved it (although only 3 people replied to it, including Blob Dude).
I moved to TKZ and did my first TK Rant package (what I call alot of rants at once). Soon everyone wanted to be in my TK Rants. I kept drawing and making more characters. Soon I developed personalities to my characters and gave them character.
One day Razor stopped his image host so I had nowhere to go. Xavier lend me a hand and gave me some webhosting so I would like to give a giant thanks to him by making more rants about him Laughing .
And the rest is history. What I mean is that the rest is pretty much the same. I can go in depth but you wouldn't want to.
So ya. Happy anniversary TK Rants.
