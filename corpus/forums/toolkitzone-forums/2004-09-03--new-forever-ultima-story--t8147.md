### Levi · 2004-09-04 00:38 · #B86940

Well, it's interesting...I haven't finished reading it because I have alot of work I need to do as well but where I am they just got done destroying Zion's old friend. I think I have 12 pages to go. It's playing out like a regular anime, did you research the word or have personal experience with them because you use alot of japanese/oriental type names and wording for characters, magic, and weapons.
I also noticed that though you explain a lot of the past to your audience you don't explain everything. I was suprised when it first happened to read about energy waves and deflecting weapons. And why wouldn't Xenai be in the Zero Weapon?
I also noticed a reference to Sephiroth's blade. Since this is more of an introduction/fiction I can let alot of these things go because its not a short story meant solely to be a short story. Its a game plot. But normally I'd refer to the length in a different way, like longer than the his arm span from side to side sort of idea or the actual length.
I also noticed alot of "ands" To describe action. SOmetimes simply continuing and going straight to the action without the and works better. One example is

**Quote:**

> He shoved his sword into the ground beside him and used both of his hands to fire an immense energy wave at Zion, bringing a painful scream from him.
>

maybe "...ground beside him THEN using both of his hands fired an immense energy wave at Zion...."
Also its good grammar, or at least prefered grammar to refer to the subject of a sentence in the sentence itself. Not refer back. Though in that one Xenai is the "duh" subject of HE at the beginning it is prefered to write "Xenai shoved his sword into the ground..."
And you do little things like that, that in truth I suppose don't really matter. But I do like the story, it will make an interesting game. And despite the things above it does make me wish to continue reading.
