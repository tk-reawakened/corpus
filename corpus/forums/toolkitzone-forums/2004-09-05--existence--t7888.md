### Dont_Think_So · 2004-09-07 03:58 · #B89261

Actually, if you wanted to go even further, those atoms are made up of a series of strings of electricity, and those strings just seem to float around, making up the rules as they go (not literally, but they certainly seem that way). How, how do THOSE breed conscienseness?
Well, it all depends how you put it together fundamentally, in organization.
Those strings of electricity organized themselves (somehow) into three types; electron, proton, and neutron. These happened to have certain qualities that had them attract to eachother in different ways, and taking different forms. These atoms just happened to have characteristics that allowed them to organize into molecules, which also each adapted their own set of rules for transforming, combining, and such. These molecules just happened to come together to form (along with other things) living cells, which were capable of running a set of instructions that allowed it to live.
These sets of instructions were able to mutate into other sets, which meant that some cells didn't split off when they duplicated. These just happened to have the right instructions that happened to set off some chemical reactions that eventually caused the conveniently formed cells to grow into plants/creatures. These creatures also had the ability to mutate, and when one of them did the cause was usually something that couldn't live past birth. However, on occasion, it would develop some sort of freak set of atoms that came together in some sort of freak set of molecules, which just happened to shape some sort of useful organ, which happened to make the creature better suited to survive.
This organization continued to grow until eventually, bam! Humans. So, atoms can't see, but they can come together in such a way that they will blast electrons at eachother which will happen to change with the light that contacts them, and some other atoms will happen to have formed in such a way that they can manipulate the data.
Of course, all of this stuff takes billions of years, with all that random stuff going on Smile
As for the whole achieving nothingness thing, that would require somehow dissipating all of the energy in the universe, and according to the law of conservation of matter, energy is never created nor destroyed. So, that means that all of that matter will have to go somewhere; just somewhere away from this universe. There is only one thing that can escape the universe, and that is a graviton; an open string. So, you would need to somehow break the bonds of the strings apart so they become gravitons, then blast them at a parallel universe. That would, of course, cause complications in the other universes; you can't just have a suddenly blast of gravity increase a trillionfold and not notice it.
