# toolkitzone.com - site logo

### Xavier · 2004-09-05 12:06 · #B87891

Hey Guys,
Okay, with the redesign going on I want to open up the site logo to you talented guys.
The basics are:
+ Has to be simple, yet elegant.
+ Has to keep the to blue background scheme (100 pixels in height)
+ Design has to be centralised, since the logo is in the middle of the site (current one is lob sided).
+ Must include "toolkitzone", the ".com" and "official home.." are optional.
+ Can include a sword, or sprite or anything...or just plain text.
+ File size not too big (try to keep to 20/30kb say...but we're fliexible).
Reward: Well, I have no idea. How about $10 off über membership? Or if you're not interested in that - rank title "tkz : artist". You'll also get kudos on the site news.
Closing Date For Entries: 8th September
Make as many logos and variations as you want, post entries in this topic. Yes it does mean other people will see it - but hopefully that will inspire better entries too Wink.
Thanks,
Xavier.
