# über membership - competition

### Xavier · 2004-09-05 11:53 · #B87889

Heya Guys,
Okay, to try and increase interest in donating to the site, I'm going to try what may prove to be a success or failure of a competition.
Prize:
One new über member will get a free domain of their choice, worth £9/~$17, and have it hosted with us.
What does this mean? You use your 200mb space and 5gb bandwidth and instead of getting a subdomain with us, just use your domain name.
You'll have your very own domain control panel so you can manage your own e-mails, sub-domains, databases without having to go through me or anyone else - something normal über members don't have the luxury of.
It's basically an entire hosting package - and is hosted on a seperate server to that of TKZ...so even if TKZ is down, your site won't be.
How to Enter:
Simple enough - become an über member. For a period of seven days, ending 12th September, I will log all new donations. Then I will pick at random one of the donators, probably by sticking the names into a box and getting my friend to pick, to get the prize.
If no one donates - alas, nevermind. If one person donates, then that one person is guarenteed the prize.
If you have any questions about the domain, or hosting or the über membership in general, feel free to ask them here.
P.S You can also get a further $10 off the membership if you create a new site logo, vist the topic [here](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/viewtopic.php?p=87891).
