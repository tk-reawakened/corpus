The Valley Of The Dead
A long time ago,
In the valley of the dead,
There lived the girl of ashes
And a boy with a big head.
The valley was dry,
Crusted and brown.
It was void of most life
And it was void of most sound.
No things good,
Grew in this place.
No happy thoughts,
No smile on face.
The girl of ashes was born
Then she came to this land
Where the sun was always burning
The dead valley sand.
She wasnt ashes then,
She was quite whole
But that didnt last long,
In this dead valley bowl.
No one quite liked her
They thought she was wrong
With her laughter and smile
That didnt belong.
They yelled and threw stones
So she would feel sad
They yelled and they told her
That she makes them mad
Its all your fault
They would chant in her ear
And she thought it was true
So she began to fear
And the voices didnt stop
All through the land
And soon the words burned though
Like the dead valley sand
Big head boy
Was a quiet fellow
His parents always thought
That he was quite mellow.
He sat all alone
Content by himself
Never in need
Of anyone else.
Until one day
A red girl said hi,
She was quite pretty
And made big head boy shy
He had never known
What it was to be lonely
He lived his whole life
As his one and only.
But now that she came,
And kissed him from above,
Bighead boy knew
What it was to have love.
The girl of ashes saw this,
And a great many things
She knew the red girl
Did not have white wings
Bighead boy found
It was all just a lie
Red did not love him
And left him to die
In the valley of the dead,
Where the whispered lies stand
And burn in your heart
Like the sun to the sand.
The girl of ashes sat down
And wanted to cry
She had no tears left
She had become much too dry.
Bighead boy sat
And had tears to spare
Now he knew what it was
To not have someone there.
The girl of ashes reached over
To hold her new friend
And they said someday,
Life in this valley, will come to an end.
-End

:TERGER:
I used to be happy
Though slightly strange
Then TERGER came,
And all things changed
In a heart shaped box
I herd something scratching
So foolish I was
To mess with the latching.
I unhooked the lock
And raised the lid slightly
Inside was a creature
Looking out brightly.
I thought for a moment
To set this thing free
It seemed to be trapped
And needed help from me.
Being quite trusting
And a very helpful guy
I decided to open it
And give him a try.
He rose out quite slowly
And gave me alarm
This thing was quite big
And could do me much harm.
He was not as I thought
A helpless cute creature
But some kind of monster
With quite dangerous features.
Its body was huge
And it was quite hairy
With a star on its chest
And claws that were quite scary.
Its eyes glowed blood red
With malice and doom
And bright razor sharp teeth
I was sure would eat me soon.
But he did not attack
With those teeth of such size
He just stood there and stared
With malicious red eyes
Wherever I go
TERGER is there
Never attacking
Just that same evil stair
I wish he would just eat me
But then again, no
But life is hard when he follows
Wherever I go

* * Fire Cracker Bob * *
Fire cracker Bob
was an unusual boy
when he was a small baby
He discovered an unusual toy.
His parents awoke
Late one cold night
And rushed to his crib
To find his pillow alight.
They could not imagine
How the fire had begun
But were surprised that there baby
Had thought it was quite fun.
They put out the flame,
And put him to sleep
But not five minutes later
A flame caught his sheets
His parent ran back
When they heard the smoke alarm
And were quite surprised to see
Little Bob free from harm
He sat the giggling
And they herd little pops
Then with a burst of sparks
He blew off his socks
His parents quite worried
About there flame emitting baby
Asked the family doctor
Is there something wrong maybe?
No, said the doctor
I do not see why
The flames do not hurt him,
But Id keep a close eye
Bob kept on burning
All the wood he could feel
Finally his parents invested
On a room full of steel
And this was Bobs room
Where he grew up alone
Because of his parents fear
That hed burn down their home
Bob started school
When he got a bit older
Though his parents were worried
The building would smolder
He soon earned his nick name
Though it wasnt a hard job
The kids were soon calling him
Fire cracker Bob.
He blew up erasers
And bugs on the ground
Shot sparks at the girls
And chased them around
Until one day
An exchange student came
He thought she was cute
But did not know her name
He shot off some sparks
To break the ice
Maybe she would run
Yes that would be nice
But the girl did not move
And looked quite un-impressed
So fire cracker Bob
Sent sparks at her dress
The sparks never touched her
Because with a grit of her teeth
There was an explosion
That sent Bob off his feet
Lying on the ground
And shaking his head
Bob looked up
And heard what she said
Im Dynamite Jill
Let me tell you something
Shoot sparks at me again
And well see who will be running
With his eyebrows still smoking
He looked at her above
And from the fire in her eyes
He knew it must be love
Now Bob was quite convinced
But the other kids thought the same
That all Jill wanted to do
Was put fire cracker Bob into flame
Bob chased her all over the grounds
Shooting sparks and desperately trying
To get her to notice him
But she just sent him flying
Every time he got close
There would be a defining boom
And send fire cracker bob
Right out of the room
He never gave up
Because it was quite fun
To have a girl push him back
Instead of just run
Then one sad day
The year came to an end
And that meant Dynamite Jill
Would be sent home again
Firecracker Bob
was so scared and sad
To get her to stay
He would give all that he had
But the moving van came
And she said her goodbyes
And gave firecracker bob
One last glance of her flaming eyes
Bob could feel tears
And with all of his power
He created with sparks
A white hot rose flower
Stunned, Jill took the gift
And for once they herd her say
Your not so bad Bob
Then she turned and drove away
Bob stopped making sparks
Some say he just never tried
But others assumed
That the fire just died
Many years later
Bob was turning twenty nine
And was haply working
Deep in a mine
His boss came to him
And told him to train
A new worked he found
Though he forgot her name
The trainee came in
And much to Bobs surprise
The new employee was a woman
With fire in her eyes
The end
