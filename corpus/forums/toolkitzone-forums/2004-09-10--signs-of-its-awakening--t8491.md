"When you take a closer look at the painting, you'll notice that the artist had a taste for light, natural colors. His love for red is also very apparent. The red of the apples on the tree, the red lips of that young woman seeking shade, and the setting of the sky is painted in an absolutely bright pallete of red..." The guide paused to place his finger on the painting to outline the sunset color, only to get some of the color come off on his fingertip. "My dear sir, you told me that this painting was 15th century, not 15 minutes ago!" One of the guests proclaimed. "I assure you it's not that young, sir! It's just...this paint..." The guide took a whiff of his hand and gapsed. "So it IS fresh paint, is it not?" the guest said in an airy tone. The guide gave a stony gaze. "No...it's fresh blood."
