# Condemnation

### seveneternal · 2004-09-11 06:15 · #B91610

There was once a time, where people lived in the sky. A time where there were no evil found in the hearts of men. Entities and Humans coexisted, and the world was powered by peace and harmony. The ground produced crops without any effort whatsoever, and people lived an eternity. Free to explore and discover the universe around them. All this was possible with the use of a force in the form of condensed energy, called the "Eternity star". This star was composed of many diffrent energy stars. When these stars were alone, they could only provide little energy, but together, the star could provide endless life and power, as well as complete control over any elements and force. It was also what kept the demons down on earth. They used this force, only for the benefit of all, and the times continued.
Until one day.
An small seed of evil was found in one's heart. He longed to gain this power for himself. This desire grew, day after day. Until the evil in his heart bloomed, and it made a small opening within the force what kept the demons on earth. This force was powered by the pureness in one's heart, and when one human failed to contribute, demons were sure to get though.
There were seven master demons on earth. Their names were Gluttony, Greed, Sloth, Lust, Pride, Envy, and Wrath. The demon Greed used this chance to slip through and deceive the human to undo the barrier which kept the demons from these flying continents. He did so, and all hell broke loose. Evil spirits gripped the humans and infested the land with impurity. The Master Demons spirits formed to become Sin. While the humans started to fight amongst each other, Sin went for the Eternity star himself. Soon, he would gain limitless power, then instead of a spirit form, they would be able to generate their full physical distortion. With Sin feeding off of the Evil found in the hearts of men, The Angel guardians were helpless against it. Now the star was his. But then, the Entities entered.
Before the demon could take the eternity star, the Entities seperated all of it's stars and spread them across space and time. They held the time star, for it was the only way for non-entity beings to control time. Then without the power of the eternity star, the continents decended onto earth, people lost immortality. Humans were always decieved by demons, causing them to do evil deeds. They fought with each other, and the entities, who gained power off of the purity in the hearts of men soon had very litle energy and had to forfeit their physical form in order to conserve energy. Before they were lost to the world, they sed their last bit of energy to chain the Master demons so their physical beings could not move, and they could not merge together to form "sin".
Humans started their lives over. They worked the ground to bear fruit, and hatred caused them to wage war against each other. thousands of years passed, and in struggle to survive, the past turned into myths and legends.
Humans depended on technology, and worked to know more about their surroundings, for the lack of knowledge was their greatest fear. But on earth, evil grew. Their minds were twisted, and was found to be impure. When this evil got to it's limits, the entities who just watched the last few thousand years took action.
They started the world over with the power of nature. All kinds of disasters destroyed buildings, and fires raged. But while the entities were unaware, some humans were able to use technology to escape with the full knowledge of entities and demons. They descended into the darkness of space.
The world once again started over. But this time, much of the evil was purged from the humans. In the process, their memories were lost. But then hundreds of years passed. The entities grip on the demons were weakening, and the survivors of the disasteres lurked somewhere out in space...
