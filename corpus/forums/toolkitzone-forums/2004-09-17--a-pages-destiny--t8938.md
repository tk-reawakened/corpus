A Pages Destiny
Chapter 1
Kidnap
Maam yelled Josh.
Shhh, son. The guards might here you. What do you need? asked Martha.
 Wheres the kings armour? Replied Josh.
 Oh, darn kid, no wonder you have no friends and everyone hates you. Even I do! If you lose the kings amour you will be punished with DEATH! Youre in luck this time. but its your last warning. Your armour is outside in front of the door where you left it you silly kid! yelled Martha hatefully.
 Sorry, Maam, Ill take more care of it now. And thanks for telling me where it is. said Josh.
Josh was a page; he was 12 years of age and was not trustworthy at all. But he was improving by a bit. He had dark brown hair, long arms and legs that were very strong, blue eyes, a skinny nose and he was skinny because his lack of food and you could see the bone around his cheeks because his cheeks arent puffy. Josh had a blue ripped up shirt and light brown shorts that were ripped but when he was doing his job as a page he wore a purple beret, a shirt that had yellow and red stripes and purple baggy pants.
Now go! The king is waiting for his armour! Do you want to be killed? If not then hurry up and go you lazy kid!.
No I dont want to be killed, and I am going as fast as I can Maam. Replied josh.
Josh left the small wooden house in silence not saying a word to his mother. The streets of Norman were dirty and muddy; the small kingdom was a depressing place for most people. Anyone who was traveling usually just passed the city. King Hoterk wasnt a good king either, instead of helping his people he would be cruel to them and would kill anyone that got in his way, disobeyed him or if they did something wrong like losing his armour. Josh was a page for the king and he hated it, the king would spit on him in disgust on how skinny he was. Along with a few other pages they had made a plan to escape but sadly they were caught and some were killed but luckily Josh wasnt and he was just punished to cleaning the kings armour for days and days until it was very shiny.
He didnt have a father either, it was said he escaped from the kingdom after he was sentenced to death. He had committed a crime of stealing bread for his family as it was said. Most doubt that he is still alive but Josh thinks his father is alive and people would laugh at him for this. They would though dirt at him because he was in a poor mean family and only the rich were liked in this city. The king said that it is a disgrace to have poor families in the kingdom so all the rich followed his theory and threw dirt or rocks at any poor people or families.
Josh just reached the castle, he could see blood marks on it from the wars the city has had and survived, the kings army was fierce and evil, the evil filled their hearts with greed and the need to have war and kill. He entered the castle after the guards inspected him and then he walked to the through the castle looking at the pictures of the king and the pictures of war. The castle was actually quite nice inside with golden statues and red drapes on the walls and beautiful grey marble floors and walls. It took him about ten minutes to walk through all the passages, corridors and up the stairs to get to the king. When he got to the king he bowed to the king and then gave his armour in a grateful way so he would not upset the king.
Ah, I see you have polished it, I will be back in five minutes after I inspect the armour and if it is good you live if it is bad you die. Said King Hoterk evilly.
Josh stood in shock he didnt know this would decide his fate, if he did know he would polish the armour a lot more. He had only touched it up a little. Those five minutes were very long to Josh; it was like waiting his whole life to josh. The king arrived; Joshs heart was beating madly. What will happen to me? Will I still live? Should I run?
Well, I am surprised. you did the worst job in history! said King Hoterk calmly. Then the king made a signal and the guards jumped at him but he dodged the guards and ran through the castle with the guards chasing him from behind. He saw a bow and some arrows on the wall; he went and grabbed them quickly. His six-teen year old friend had taught him how to use a bow and arrows. Josh had gone through days of practising because his six-teen year old friend was once an archer for one of the greatest kingdoms ever. Although a few weeks ago he had escaped from the city.
Josh ran as fast as he could and then he got a head very far then turned around and shot one of the three guards in the head with an arrow and he fell to the ground with a THUMP. Josh ran some more and then turned around and shot another guard in the chest and he staggered to the ground begging for air. Then at last he shot the last guard in the leg and he couldnt run anymore. Josh was free from the castle! Then just as he was running back home all went black.
A few days later Josh woke up to a dark dirty mud filled cave. There were some odd markings on the wall that were drawn with blood and there were some chewed up bones in the cave. It wasnt a very big either; it was about ten metres long and five metres wide.
