### Dan · 2004-09-20 21:47 · #B97772

You don't have to pay at all, don't give advice if you don't know what you are talking about.
It's not really a great idea to host off of your own computer, but if you wish to, the quickest way to do it is to download a server program like [Xitami](http://www.xitami.com) or [Apache](http://httpd.apache.org). That software will let you host files on your own computer.
Then you'll need to find out your IP address from somewhere like [What Is My IP](http://www.whatismyip.com), which users would use to access your computer. Smile
