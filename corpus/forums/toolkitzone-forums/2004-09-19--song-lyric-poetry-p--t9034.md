# Song/lyric/poetry :P

### Cesque · 2004-09-19 11:30 · #B96884

Sitting in the silent chamber
State of divine meditation
Interior homeostasis
Wait for physical salvation
Impurity flows within thee
Streams of poison break belief
Concentration and awaiting
All the blessing of relief
Down the abyss what's imperfect
Void your body and your soul
Physical purification
Release of the liquid foul
Emptiness inside your flesh
All your burden is now gone
Flushing acids out of system
Sitting on porcelain throne
What now remains sure as fact
You drank bit too much last time
Now so pure your flesh and mind
With silence you end thy rhyme
As you might have realised, this is a perfect song to sing while sitting on toilet after the party.
