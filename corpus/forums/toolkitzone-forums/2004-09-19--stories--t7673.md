### Kitsokai · 2004-09-20 21:33 · #B97762

A large, vast, lush-green forest sorrounded the radius of about four miles, and centered in the forest was a large lake. Pines covered the area thickly, and aspens were just as thick. It was just hitting late morning, the dew's last drops falling off the leaves of the forest.
A small smoke stack was drifting slowly and snake-like towards the sky.
There he sat, his cloak wrapped tightly against him, and his sword set down before him. The markings on the hilt of the sword signified his importance of existence. In matter of fact, if he wasn't here, this world would be a living hell.
His scar upon his forehead also told many things of importance. In his forehead lay a small, green crest. The Dragon's Crest. This crest was a symbol of the Dragon Master, which is what also the sword was for.
As he stared at the markings, Kitsokai tightened his grip on his cloak, his knuckles turning a pale white from the squeezing. He wondered, why him. Out of all six hundred seventy-three people, he was picked to carry the burden of the Dragon Master.
When Kitsokai was young, he was fasinated by dragons. He drew them all the time. He loved the sword his father had in his bedroom too, and when his father and mother wasn't there, he would grab the sword and play with it for a bit. Once or twice he would get caught, and then complain to his parents that he wanted a sword.
Here he is though, a legendary Dragon's Sword and the Dragon's Crest, all in his childhood dreams. Yet, he was actually dissatisfied with it all. The possibilities were endless. He could easily summon up storms of dragons to assist him in any needs.
His small fire was burning weaker now. He got up and grabbed a couple blocks of wood and threw them in, poking them with a stick to stir the fire around.
Kitsokai was growing deeply drowsy. He set up a blanket on the ground and used another one for his warming cloth. His dreams stirred him in his sleep, dreaming of the murder of his family, screaming his name, "Kai! Save us! Kai!!!"But at his young age of five, he was pretty defenseless. He attempted to save his sister first, but got burned in the fire that was slowly whiping away his town. Innocents being slaughtered by the dozen. Twelve years later, fury burned inside him, searching for the people that killed his parents, sister, and his village.
