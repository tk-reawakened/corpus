### Seafroggys · 2004-09-26 05:27 · #B100803

Hmm, odd that you think that. Especially when every list in history has put Alien and/or Aliens in the top 10 sci-fi movies of all time.
Alien is very artistically well done. It is dark and suspensful, in contrast to Aliens which is fast paced action (paralell to Alien 3 and Resurrection I might add). Alien was even made by Ridley Scott, the same dude who filmed Gladiator.
I mean, seriously, the guy going through the ducts with the flamethrower, then turning around when he thought he was away from the thing....one of the greatest moments in motion picture history, even though it scared the shit out of me.
I find no corniness honestly. In fact, I found Resurrection to be the corniest one, and really pointless (even though I felt sad when I saw the new alien's face as it was being spaced.....)
This has led me to thinking...action films of the 80s tend to be better then those of the past 6 or so years. I guess there was more emphasise on plot and character development, and because of the lack of advanced cg technology, the director had to rely more on cinematagraphic ingenuity then just the click of a button. After seeing Alien (okay, late 70s, whatever), Aliens, Terminator, Predator, T2 (okay early 90s, but the mean is still intact), I can draw a safe conclusion to my statement.
Btw, I found Alien 3 to be kinda dull, and as said above, A:R was pretty pointless.
