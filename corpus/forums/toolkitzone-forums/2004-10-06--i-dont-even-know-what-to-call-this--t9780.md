# I dont even know what to call this

### Ivan · 2004-10-06 00:44 · #B105658

This is just something out of my life:
What Happend
We used to be like best buddies,
walking talking and laughing together,
you made me laugh like I never laughed before,
you made me feel like I never felt before,
you were my Godsent Angel,
then it all changed,
what happend?
First you were my gift,
then you were taken,
what happend?
I used to dream about us together.
Now I dream of what can never be.
You were like a dream.
I stay up and cry,
my heart ripped open.
Am I not good enough?
What happend?
We used to go out with our friends,
you were never embarrassed to show that you liked me,
then it all ended.
You never talk to me,
never look at me.
You make me feel like I am unworthy.
What happend?
My friends tell me I am good enough,
but I dont believe them.
Why, wouldnt I be worthy if I was good enough?
They tell me you dont deserve me.
But no, I think otherwise.
I thought we had something,
I guess it's not true.
I thought we were close,
yet we wern't i know with remorse.
I wish we were back at the beggining,
maybe I could change me.
I only wish,
yet I know it's not possible.
Why? I wish you would tell me,
yet why I know you won't tell me.
I am unworthy but why.
What is wrong with me?
What happend?
----Johnny J Panasyuk.-------
