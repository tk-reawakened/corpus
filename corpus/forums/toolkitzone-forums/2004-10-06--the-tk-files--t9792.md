The TK Files
Chapter I
I woke up this morning...and to my surprise, a strange monkey was sitting on my desk dancing...I was rather disturbed by this image, so I threw a can at that monkey; the monkey dogged the can, and started speaking. I am the great messenger god, Asimir, and I have been asked to take you to the king. Astonished, and wondering why a monkey would be talking to me, I agreed.
Just as I began to ask this Asimir figure how we were going to get to this king there was a great puff of smoke, and I blacked out.
When I awoke, I was on a cold, dark chamber floor. I could hear a noise of in the distance, but I couldnt identify what or where it was coming from. Luckily, I carried a small flashlight with me at all times, and I clicked it on. I shown the light around the chamber, looking for the source of the sound. In the far right corner, I spotted a small child.
I walked over to the child wearily, and asked him who he was. He simply replied Guru doesnt want to talk to you now. I wondered who the Guru was was... I asked him who he was again, and he replied I said... Guru doesnt want to talk to you!
Just then, a beam of light shot through the darkness. I looked over, and I noticed a short stubby figure. The figure said The king has summoned you. I got up, and walked over to the light. About halfway, I noticed it was a tall door, with handles 25 feet in the air. How had that short thing opened the door if the handles were so high up? I shrugged, and moved on.
As I walked through the door, I looked over. The figure was...as strangely as this is a condom llama! The llama said, Walk down the hall to the right, and keep on walking till you get to the end. I nodded, and walked out. I turned slightly, and I saw the condom llama stretch out and grab the handle with its mouth.
When I was walking down that long hallway, I noticed all of the strange paintings. If you looked at them indirectly, they would have wonderers images on them, but as your eyes became fixated, they would disappear.
It had felt like I was walking down this hallway for more than an hour now, and it hadnt felt like I had went anywhere, I looked forward, and I could see no end to the hallway, I looked back, and could not see the great door. I turned around, and SMACK! I crashed into something.
