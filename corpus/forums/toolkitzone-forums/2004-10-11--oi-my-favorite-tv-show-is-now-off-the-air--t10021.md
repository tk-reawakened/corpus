# OI! My favorite TV show is now off the air

### TK Game Boy · 2004-10-11 19:33 · #B108919

My fav TV (which is a korean show subtitled in english) "Age of Warriors", the one show that I auctually was looking forward every week, the one show I actually watch with eagerness, the one show that give reason to even own a TV besides my video games has aired its final episode on saturday. ITS OVER!
The showed aired the 157th episode making its final show and I for one am sad to see it go. They wont be making no more cause the age of warriors ended. What I mean by this is the show was based on a era in korea's history named the Age of Warriors where ruthless warriors removed emperors from their thrones and added new ones but they were the ones in control for these new emperors were puppet kings.
This show teaches the viewers that absolute power corrupts absolute. Power cannot be shared by others as was seen on this program. There were many awesome characters that died because of power. On particular character named Yi Ui-Bang was a very powerful warrior but was killed by another named Chung Jung-bu who was killed by his son who was killed by Kyung Dae-Sung, quite possibly the best character of them all. As you can see alot of ppl died in the show which could make great protagonist and were based on real ppl.
The show had everything. Suspense, drama, action, bloody fights, war, big buff guys and pretty girls, and great historical figures.
Oh man. Now I am awaiting what the channel will make next. Hopefully its just as good as this show. Oi now I have no reason to get off my gamecube.
