### kahless · 2004-10-13 14:11 · #B109739

I think games are art in the same sense that advertising is art. And actually I think both forms of art have a similar problem, namely when their creators hyperfocus on the "art" portion of the project they forget the reason that they exist in the first place.
My grandfather points this out all the time in advertising. WE'll be watching tv and there'll be a really artistic, creative ad on. Then he'll ask me -- what product were they selling. HAlf the time I won't know. The point being that for an ad, the reason you make that ad is to sell something like insurance or cheetos. If you don't know the product being sold, the ad is a failure. The same thing with games. If you make a game that isn't fun to play, it's a crap game, even if it's the most artisticly beautiful and profound art in the world.
