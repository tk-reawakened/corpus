# CLANS TAKE YOUR POSITION!!!

### Frei · 2004-10-20 12:27 · #B113563

Welcome to the land of Nahcram
![](http://www.icestormcity.com/piarts/heraldry/GD/GDmap_view1.jpg)
As we see, the game will revolve around this epic world. Civilizations must flourish and civilizations must fall.
The game will be chess-based, and updates are made every week. Full game mechanics are to be announced. For now, here are the rules to get things started.
Clan Placement Mechanics:
![](http://www.icestormcity.com/piarts/heraldry/GD/GDmap_top_grid1.jpg)
1. Clans are to choose their placement in the land of Nahcram to be able to participate in various Great Divide activities. Even if the clan is registered, they will not be able to participate if they still haven't chosen a location to build their base. To place the base, declare the coordinates of the chosen block {horizontal,vertical}. Make a 16x16 flag. The background is your race color (look into the GD site for the race colors).
NOTE: Only Leaders can choose. We will not entertain non-Leaders, unless they are given permission. Please PM your Leaders about this.
2. A clan base initially takes up one (1) block in the map. This expands as the clan researches technologies that require usage of other blocks (e.g. marketplace, research towers, etc). Land requirements must first be met (e.g. must be in water, forest, etc.). For expansion buildings, a total of 4 structures are allowed per block.
![](http://www.icestormcity.com/piarts/heraldry/GD/GDmap_legend.gif)
![](http://www.icestormcity.com/piarts/heraldry/GD/GD_bases.jpg)
HUMANS can place their base on LAND. When research is complete, they can place their base ounder WATER and on MAGICK CONTINENT. They can never place their base on FOREST.
ERTHKEEPERS can place their base on FOREST. When research is complete, they can also place their base on LAND with WATER. This gives AIs and NAIs access to Water-borne plants and creatures. When Erthkeepers place a base on Mountains, they gain access to Air-borne creatures.
RUECIANS can place their base on LAND. When research is complete, they can place their base under WATER. When Ruecians place their base on Mountain, their mining skills are greatly increased (money gained is doubled, research time is halved, HP and MP is doubled). When they leave the base, their stats are normalized. Ruecians can never place their base on FOREST and MAGICK CONTINENT.
JINNEES can only place their base on and under WATER.
CROISSANS can only place their base on MAGICK CONTINENT. When research is complete, they can conjure more MAGICK CONTINENTs to live in. Unlike other races, CROISSANS do not need extra blocks for infrastructural add-ons.
3. When a clan decides to transfer his/her base, A fee of 500 dymnos is required. The transfer target must be within the clan's movement range. If a clan declared a transfer without the required 500 dymnos, his/her base will be forfeited and the clan will be unable to participate until the 500 dymnos is met.
4. A castle is built once the race completes the research. This takes up four (4) blocks. land requirements should still be met. Only one castle can be built per race. The castle gives the race additional rewards. It can also accomodate the whole population of the race at any given time.
5. The base of the clan who chooses to reside in the castle will not be forfeited and can still function, this time as an encampment for traveling clans.
6. If the block has more than 1/2 of the needed land-type (e.g. more than 1/2 is water, forest, etc.), the land requirement is met.
For 3dsmax users with version 5.1 or lower or any 3d modeller that can export *.3DS files, you can submit your own base design. Send the 3dsmax file (*.max) or *.3DS to (redacted: email), or give me a link.
That is all...for now...

For now, you may exchange your turns to dymnos. This is valid only to those who have established their location.
Express your turn-selling by declaring a valid activity for you. Here are the list of agricultural/industrial activities:
Mining(+10 dymnos,+2 EXP,+1 Mineral Stock), Fishing(+7 dymnos,+3 EXP), Hunting(+5 dymnos,+5 EXP), Woodcutting(+3 dymnos,+2 EXP,+5 Wood Stock), Quarrying(+2 dymnos,+2 EXP,+5 Stones Stock)
Your action should be logical to your location. Those in forested areas can either hunt or cut woods, those at sea shores or at the sea itself can fish, those in mountains can mine, and those in lowlands(drylands) can stone-quarry.
That should give everyone additional income. This is an individual effort.
