# Emoticon Vs. Smiley

### TK Game Boy · 2004-10-20 05:46 · #B113512

New RP by me ya.
This RP is about the war against emoticons and smileys. The internet is your world and the home country to try to take over is a message board called Fantasy_board.gov. In Fantasy_board.gov, there lives smileys and emoticons but each day they struggle for control and are violent agaisnt each other.
Decide which side you are on and take the roll on that side.
Rules:
-If you are on the side of Emoticons (represented with Em) then you must use emoticons (in fact disable the smileys in each post). If you are on the side of Smileys (represented with Sm) then you must use smileys
-You can make as many chars as you want but plz dont say "I'm going to make a million ppl." They gotta have the following:
Name:
Side: (Sm or Em)
Special Abilities: (healing, attacking, etc.)
- You may use smileys from other website but only if you are on the side of the Sm. Emoticons are the same rule. There are some websites with emoticons.
-Plz take it seriosly. No useless crud.
-Conversations go like this:
The name of the char goes first. Enter two brackets [] and put your emotion there. I.E:
Person of Sm [![](http://www.ous.edu/images/smiley.gif)] - Yo! Hello im Person
Person of Em [ D-) ] - Im a weirdo
