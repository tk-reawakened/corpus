# Its finally time, TBBS

### Alex0Xander · 2004-10-20 19:20 · #B113777

It's time to start on my Turn Based Battle System. I'll call it endless battle in rememberance of my games true name lol. Okay what I want to know is If this is a feasible idea, and something that would be fun.
*3 vs Max[6enemies]
*Players can hide behind each other and recieve no damage, but accuracy is hindered. IE; Marc hides behind Marz. Marz gets stabbed. Marc then lunges out from behind Marz and swings his sword, but because he was behind Marz was unsure of the enemies position and misses. Marz dies. +note, the enemy can use this tactic as well.
*Turn based, like Breath of Fire 4, where the entire party gets a turn, then the enemies turn. This would be easier to code, and allows for combos.
*Experience is distributed to the player that defeats a mosnter, it is not devided among the entire party. This allows for more stragedy. Also players can level up during battle, which may turn the tides when in a tight spot.
*There will be a scapegoat when running away from battle. In order to not divulge too much information, I'll just say that instead of just simply flipping a coin, the entire party runs aay and a "escaoegoat" comes out and takes on the enemy solo. This "escapegoat" also levels up and learns new abilities, just like blue magic. This will make sense when and If finish the battle system.

What do you guys think ?

### Angel of Death · 2004-10-31 16:50 · #B119535

I like the concepts, but as for one person defeating an enemy to get EXP.. what if you have a white mage or something in your party? Then, unless you time it for them to get the last hit after everyone else fights, they'd never level up. So, if you think it's a good idea you might want to measure how much effort each character puts into fighting the enemy and give them EXP accordingly. Like, if two people fought together with equal strength they'd get about the same amount of EXP.
