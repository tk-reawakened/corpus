# A poem by me...

### gcn_zelda · 2004-10-21 01:43 · #B114102

**Stephen Cope wrote:**

> The dullness of the darkened sky
> In the icy silence of that night
> Broken, shattered, and
> Destroyed like his life.
> This dusty vase of wilted flowers
> Was lonely and forgotten
> If only somebody had cared.
> If only somebody had talked with him.
