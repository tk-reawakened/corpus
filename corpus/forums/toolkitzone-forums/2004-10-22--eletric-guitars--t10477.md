### ArchGaden · 2004-10-25 22:35 · #B116951

The mac comment was a joke I hope. For the price of a mac, you could buy true professional grade recording equipment that would blow the socks off the mac...and a pc to interface with.
Besides, anything a mac can do, you can find a pc equivalent for a fraction of the cost (joys of a non-proprietary platform). I took a computer music class my freshmen year. The professor was a mac elitest, so I was forced to use a mac in the class. The G5 wasn't out yet, so we were using G4s. The interface was friendly enough I guess, but I didn't think it was so amazing as most mac elitests think. I was able to visit a recording studio here in Nashville for a field trip..it was good fun. They were working on a song for SheDaisy. The mastering engineer boasted about making $200 an hour with the skill to outmaster everyone else in Nashville. Guess what ? They used PCs ! The professor was surprised and asked them why. They responded that their hardware didn't interface with the mac for lack of supporting software and that their PCs worked just fine. Just goes to show that the mac isn't the end-all to audio hardware...of course, any audiophile could tell you that. I feel no loyalty to any platform...I just go where I can get the best bang for the buck.
