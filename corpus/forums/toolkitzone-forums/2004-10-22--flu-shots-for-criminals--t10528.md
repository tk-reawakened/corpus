### Ersuis · 2004-10-22 22:54 · #B115417

In the long run, it would probably be cheaper to give flu shots to at risk prisoners than neglect them. By innoculating at risk prisoners, the state or federal government might not have to pay not only medical costs for those prisoners, but for the whole prison. If you make the at risk people safe, you're effectively making everyone in close proximity to them safer.
What gets me, though, is how the drug companies continually have a flu vaccine shortage. Could they not see a flu shortage coming, even though we've had one almost every year we've had a vaccine now? Or are they really just deliberately creating a shortage so that they can "discover" or make more vaccine at higher prices?
