Sacred
The Story:
Something sacred was descovered in the depths of the ground, no one knew what it was. It was brought to the king for examining and for safe-keeping. For days they observed it with perplexed looks, until the king got so frustrating that he just picked the sacred item up and threw it against the wall with all his might. Then.... it shattered, all lights went out. All was silent, until the world started to shake, in a few more moments all were dead. Or were they all dead? All the survivors had to live in a dark world now. There was no sun light, all was dusty and most of all, no food. After all the survivors met with each other to decide who would rule the world from 1255 (this day) until their death. The reason they wanted to have a king of the world was because they thought it was a blessing that they lived and that they had to use it wisely. They had been talking for hours but they still could not decide so they all split up into tribes, all with a leader trying to become the king of the world!

How Land Works:
Every tribe starts of with 5 miles of land. This will not be enough to become king of the world so you must buy more land. In total there are 500 miles. Each Mile costs $1000. Every team starts off with $500.
Tribe Land is where all the tribes stay (their base). Un-owned Land is just land that no one owns and has shops in these cities. War Land is places that wars will be held against each other, there can only be two wars at once and there can be more than 2 tribes in each war. Also the land is cordinated by all the letters in the alphabet and the numbers up to 5, example: H4 Each letter stands for a row and each number stands for a mile. So in total if the city is a maximum 155 miles then that will tell you how many letters there will be. So if a city is 124 miles then the city will go up to the letter U with 2 miles in it. How Food Was Brought To The World:
Each tribe starts off with no food what so ever and if you die then you will have to reset all your stats or get another tribe member to try and heal you. To get food every tribe has their own sacred valley that has to be found in one of the Un-owned Lands. At first if another tribe finds your valley before you do and they go in it, they will be teleported out. Once you have found your valley you must always have some tribe members guarding it or the other tribes can come and steal your tribe and you will always be dying and all your stats will keep going back to 1.
