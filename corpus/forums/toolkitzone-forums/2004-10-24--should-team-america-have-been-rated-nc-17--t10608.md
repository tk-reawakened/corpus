# Should Team America have been rated NC-17?

### ForeverNoobish · 2004-10-24 21:47 · #B116364

The movie is rated R, which means that an adult would need to buy a ticket for someone under 17 to go and see the movie... now, if the movie was NC-17, that means that no one 17 and under, even with a ticket, could be admitted into the movie. No one. Why was this an issue with this movie? The main controversy was the scene where the two main characters go on a fuck-fest. Also, there was excessive violence.
Here is why the movie was rated R:
Rated R for graphic crude and sexual humor, violent images and strong language - all involving puppets.
Here are some NC-17 movie titles, and why they were rated NC-17:
High Tension (2004)
Lions Gate Films
Rated NC-17 for strong graphic violence.
Young Adam (2004)
Sony Pictures Classics
Rated NC-17 for some explicit sexual content.
Dirty Shame, A (2004)
Fine Line Features
Rated NC-17 for pervasive sexual content.
Wadd: The Life & Times Of John C. Holmes (2003)
Vca Labs, Inc.
Rated NC-17 for explicit sexual content.
As is evident, sexual content is the biggest factor in making a movie NC-17. However, as you can also see, one movie is rated primarily based on violence. Team America was loaded with violence. And, of course, it had that one sex scene... and what was funny was that in the theater, I kid you not, at least half of the audience left the movie during the scene and never returned to watch the rest of the movie.
For those who have seen it, do you think that the movie deserved a rating of NC-17?
In my opinion, no, because during the sex scenes, the puppets didn't even have genitals. Also, the violence was more humorous (as was the sex scene) than anything. However, anyone think it should have been rated that? I expect right wingers like Paladin to say yes Razz However, there might be some others too...
