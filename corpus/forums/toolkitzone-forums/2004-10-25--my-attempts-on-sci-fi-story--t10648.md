# My attempts on sci-fi story ;)

### Willy Wonka · 2004-10-25 21:40 · #B116893

This is the beginning - mainly containing explanations as for the world it happens in, it's something like in Song of Azrael novel but a lots of time later, it happens in New India after the so-called "downfall of democracy", in a caste-divided society where among higher castes, logic and science are main ideals. It's about one of so-called "priests", which... oh well, it's just the beginning anyway.
It was about dusk. Priest Muni was going through the streets of Delhi toward the Brotherhood building with an escort of two soldiers. The contact with the exterior world was usually a great shock after spending many weaks, if not months, studying, training and perfecting self in the Brotherhood headquarters in the center of Delhi. The city's borders were totally imperfect in physical sense of this word: they were occupied by lowest labor castes, to which hygiene and sanitatation weren't meaning much. The roads were painted in cow extrements, animals were running across the walls of the houses and the streets were like tunnels between rows of buildings, with doors or gate here or there.
But a true, logicaly thinking person like a priest or scientist or any other representative of the higher castes would not think about such insignificant facts, but would rather ask the basic question, standing at the bottom of logic: Why?
Everything had it's reason in universalism, which was the official religion among higher castes. The true meaning of this word has long been forgotten, during enlighted ages (or, ages which happen after Great Revolution, before it there were dark ages full of nonsense, stupidity, unequality and imperfection) universalism was a religion of logic: the only true religion which could not be denied, religion based on science and facts, religion evolving along with science and being one with science - priests and scientists were almost the same in the new caste system, altough main difference was that a priest had to think globaly, not over things important to certain man or whole humanity, but about things important to the working of whole universe and all of it's sub-systems. Universalism was a cross between perfect pantheistic monotheism (voided of all sub-deities and powers acting in the world beside God, be they called gods, saints, angels and such) and atheism: there was one, nameless god, which was known as the Cosmos, which was all.
The job of a priest during enlighted ages in New India was to simply destroy every other religion. Because the level of education was very low among lower castes and the possesion of any religious books was forbidden, it wasn't a hard job. Priest had to first break basic concepts of every religion, believers left without their books like Quoran or Torah could be easily convinced that basic beliefs of their faith are symbolical, then all the visions/prophecies were claimed to be subjective teachings and in the end, any believer could easily understand that all religions are simply personal alterations of the only true religion: universalism.
It was assumed that the religion concepts other than universalism would slowly rot away and in 15-20 years (in englighted ages, one year had always perfectly 1461 days and was divided into 4 "terti-years", according to Solar Calendar, there was also a Lunar Calendar but it wasn't used as commonly) all religions in India but universalism will be forgotten, among all castes.
The priest and his escort could be easily distinguishable among the crowds: he was dressed in white, bold (his body was shaved of all hair, including eyebrows and all the hair on the non-facial parts of body), his skin was perfectly white and the soldiers looked similarily, except they were wearing military uniforms rather than white cotton robe.
They were talking, talking in Logsan, the language of the higher castes, which could not be understood by lower classes, which sticked to using english, sanskrit or hindi. Unlike all other languages spoken in New India, Logsan was logical and perfectly designed: it could be used for computer communication and it's sole construction which was based more on mathematical functions rather than grammar somehow forced the speaker to think logicaly. THere weren't any nouns, or adjectives, or cases, genders and any things like these - there were instead actions, properties and subject types like object, idea, person. The laws of the language were almost primitive, yet it served it's purpose perfectly. The pronounciation was similar to a cross between native languages and english, there weren't many letters and the proper name of the language was "di csha pi loga", which translated into english, should mean something like "Idea language of-property logic".
During enlighted ages, the hgihest ideal was not knowledge, good or prosperity, but logic: the perfect, immortal force upon which the universe is built, which only manifestation in human soul is perfect, logical way of thinking which achieving should be easier with belief of universalism and language of Logsan. At least among higher castes.
- Have long ago did you perform your last 'conversion' before this one? - one of soldiers began in Logsan, altough properly the sentence was built like "person you action convert time question in-relation-to time last-happened-time-of action of-property mentioned-previously", but translating every single sentence to Logsan is almost futile. - How many time passes between them? 32 days?
- Don't you understand the primal rule of scientific advance? Past is insignificant. What matters is present and future. Next time I will have to convert someone - or, better, clear someone's thoughts and infuse his mind with logic - will be in about 32 days.
- "Logic, logic... you scientists and priests see logic everywhere! You claim world is built upon logic, right?"
- Yes.
- Then tell me, why do we have to shave our hair? Why doesn't it simply cease to grow? The hair has to be shaved because it's only trouble - it falls on your eyes, and during combat or rapid movement, hair is unrequired cause of problems. Why doesn't Cosmos stop us from growing hair?
- Because it doesn't know it's insignificant for us.
- It's perfect, isn't it? - The form "isn't it?" is added to imply expression and accent soldier put on words "question is idea Cosmos not of-property truth", in logical language like Logsan illogical, purely expressional form like "isn't it?" has no right of existence.
- It is. But something can be more or less perfect, like infinity. A 4 meter line is built from infinite amount of points, as is 6 meter line, but the second one is more infinite than the previous.
- Isn't Cosmos ultimately perfect?
- There is no ultimate perfection, as there is no practical end of infinity.
- It doesn't seem much sense to me.
- It will be explained better one day, brother, all will be explained. Once we will understand Cosmos perfectly, we shall know all answers for all questions.
- Your belief in science is great. - added second soldier, previously silent.
- I have no reason to disbelieve it.
Slowly, behind the short series of concrete or stone huts running in rows across the city, a giant building constructed entirely of white bricks began to arise - the Brotherhood headquarters. The building itself was merely a heavily guarded enterance, as the main areas were situated underground, separated from the outside world. Buildings like that existed in most big cities in India, holding different scientific and religious organisations where higher castes lived.
Higher - but not the highest. Higher classes stood above the farmers, industrial workers and low class scientists and logics, but still there was a force above them, a caste which was consistent of most wise scientists and priests, best soldiers and the rulers which steered this all, altough the good part of the caste system was that it didn't need too much control: left alone, it could sustain itself, without major revolts or such. The highest classes had their headquarters somewhere, altough no one knew where, they were told to live in isolation from the rest of the world like spirits watching earth from beyond, not appearing physicaly, they were told to have great pwoer in their hand and be perfect - they were told to have achieved the state of perfectly logical thinking, be avatars of the perfect Cosmic balance in their minds.
But higher classes were different story. They were on their way toward advancement. The process of "perfectising" the people creating higher class was mostly based on genetics and unnatural selection: whenever a child was born (and higher classes were voided of any "family life", sex was fullfiled as natural desire of organism like breathing, eating or living in safety) it was geneticaly investigated and if it possessed genes who were generaly considered as "bad" and it wouldn't fit any of the Forms (Forms were terms used to describe most proper set of genes and physical build for certain profession, for example soldiers should be well built while scientists should be white, preferably with straight hair - it was easier to cut and could be left growing without problems for a bit more time - and genes which determined lack of beard as well as some other things), the child was radially sterilized or it was degradated to lower caste.
Such was the case of Muni - unfortunately, he seemed to have a few "bad" genes - who had curly hair and a beard - so he had to be sterilised. The only real difference that the new, safe sterilisation technique was making was unability to give life to further generations, other than that, sterile and non-sterile men looked all the same.
Yes, men - the scientific societies were divided into genders, and beside times of mating - usually, a partner of a male scientist was female scientist from female scientist society - people with same gender didn't work one with another. Such was the plan, and it prevented lots of unexpected accidents usually.

### Levi · 2004-11-04 17:34 · #B121218

Guess what, I read it. Go figure, anyway, it does sound like an interesting story. Though I've never read Song of Azrael I have read such books as The White Mountains and Dusk of Demons which also deal with earth after a major downfall of civilization as we know it. Some through an apocolyptic alien or human war. Which is what part of this reminds me of. Its like re-writing the world to suit your needs. Kind of fun actually.
I did notice a few times where your wording wasn't exactly fluid. But that happens, especially in a rough draft which is what I assume this is. In fact there were times where it appeared as though you were translating from another language, the wording was haulting as you read it. Much like the literal translations you gave for the soldiers to demonstarate Logsan. There were also a few, not many, spelling errors. I'm no king on grammar so I won't make a big deal out of it. I've always been more about content than technical writing. I belive that should be considered last.
Now as for the story writing itself, there are times you appear struggleing with whom this story is meant to be read by. Who your audience is. In the fouth paragraph you said, and I quote:
"Universalism was a cross between perfect pantheistic monotheism (voided of all sub-deities and powers acting in the world beside God, bey they called gods, saints, angels and such) and atheism..."
Just using the words pantheistic monotheism shows you are aiming for an audience of a certain level, probably undergraduates and graduates. So the need to explain yourself is rather redundant. They should know or look up the meaning themselves, its good not to explain everything.
In fact when you get into the conversations its obvious you're discussing and going to discuss throughout the story a sort of higher logic. So it seems a lot like yoru babying your readers. Its like saying "Here's something slightly higher than normal so I'll explain it in detail for all of you who aren't me." I know that isn't your intention but that is definatly how it can come off.
Also this seems border line Hard-Science Fiction. Where you put a lot of emphasis on the facts and what is (or what is considered to be true anyway), now this is not a bad way to go but be weary. Many Hard-sci-fi stories have an extreme lack of story to them. Its all the science and no story, its like they are writing an article for the Scientific American magazine without all the data and proof to go along with it. But yours seems to be following along a certain story path so I don't think I have to worry about you.
Also this seems to start a little slowly, well for me anyway, it doens't immedeatly capture my attention. I sort of have to force myself to read through the facts of the world. I will admit that that is more of a personal preference rather than a critique. I have personally prefered to learn about the world slowly and over time. As something happens I explain a bit of the world. Like in White Mountains the "Vagrants" were only explained when they came into the story. That sort of thing.
All in all its not bad, but like all things made by the flawed minds of man it needs improvement. I can't wait so see how this progresses and what happens to the priest. Good job my frined, good job.

And for those interested, here's a short chronicle of future events which lead to shaping the world situation as it is now:
Moral Rot [2000-2030]
With the series of political improvements countries of western Europe entered age of prosperity. Ideas of equality, tolerance and fast economical growth did not appeal to american corporations investing in Europe as well as many religious and political organisations. This caused huge economical problems, effectively leading to an economical crash at the beginning of year 2030, also known as the Global Discharge. In the meantime, political revolution known as "Moral liberation" in Europe almost toyally eliminated politics' ethics, changing political relations from solemn to friendly and liberate (which did not appeal to everyone).
First Indian War [2031-2067]
With Pakistan growing in power and threatening nearby countries with nuclear warfare and forces of Persia prepparing for war with India, USA decided to interfere and sent forces to southern Asia. Americans managed to overthrow governments of India and Pakistan (forming a country known as New Indias) but Persia, country created when Iran conquered and united nearby arabian territories, defended itself against american invasion.
The war truly ended when indian government initiated a rebellion which led to destruction of american military in the country, theoreticaly breaking free from american occupation (but americans still controlled most of country's institutions and economy).
Light Ages [2068-2077]
First man sent to Mars and new technologies and philosophic ideas brought fast technologic advance to America, Europe and Asia.
Second Indian War [2078-2090]
Government of New India with help of american scientists residing within borders of it's country began to develop the so-called "nexus technology", a brand new way of gaining energy based on hypothetical construction of galactical objects - quasars. The engine producing energy, residing deep in underground city of Zin, soon became number one source of energy for whole country and surrounding ones (provided for a price, of course) decreasing influence of american corporations and increasing power of New India itself.
American government, angered at the situation, accused New India of developing a potential weapon and sent it's forces to conquer Zin. The underground complex, which over time grew into a powerful fortress (the engine was a top secret invention, guarded like nothing else), fended off the attack. After few years of unsuccessful attempts of conquering Zin, USA used nuclear weapons. The explosion of the engine caused an unbelievable ecological catastrophe, which intoxicated almost whole New India territory with extreme amounts of space radiation, ending the country's ages of prosperity for a longer time.
American president was sentenced to death for genocide crimes, which began to lead to downfall of America and democracy.
Downfall of Democracy [2091-2107]
With Mexico attacking America using the chaotic situation and american loss of several southern states, USA ceased to become world power.
Revolutionary ideas began to spread throughout Europe, with new conceptions of country constructions and political ruling system based on military power, which led to return of fascism in Italy and Spain.
Blood Revolution [2108-2119]
Revolution in Czech Republic and it's conquest of almost whole eastern Europe created conflict with western Europe and Russia. After years of bloody battles, Czech Republic - now known as Bohemia - became a powerful federal country controlling western Europe with a new controversial political system known as patrianism, based on full control of citizens and defending ideas of tolerance and freedom at the same time.
After the conflict, due to weakened power and peoples' desire for breaking off the mainland, Russia became divided into several minor autocratic countries.
Last Stand [2120-2123]
Spain declared war on France, which led to more fighting in Europe.
At the end of the Last Stand, countries in Europe became far more isolated one from another, each having totally unique ruling system, ranging from french "perfect democracy" to italian fascism or bohemian patrianism.
Downfall of Tolerance [2124-2130]
Fall of Vatican and spreading of antisemitic ideas throughout Italy and Spain brought new wave of hatred throughout Europe, about which other isolated countries did not really care.
With ended relationships between one another, european, american and asian powers began to invest on neutral territory (poor world countries, mainly Africa).
The world, heading toward second half of XXII century, became a constelation of single, isolated countries each growing in power in separation from others, where international relations and public opinion, so common during previous century, almost ceased to exist...
