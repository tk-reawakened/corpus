# TREASURE HUNTING SCENARIO #1!

### Frei · 2004-10-25 12:41 · #B116696

Hello Guys! This is the first official scenario we're going to give for HERALDRY: Great Divide...
Before great Adventurer Mackenzie Powell retired, she declared that hidden in the world of Nahcram are precious artifacts vital for civilizations. Why did Mack not take them? Well, apparently... she does not want the Humans to have an edge! Eccentricity is one of the words that describe her.
TREASURE HUNTING MECHANICS:
Artifacts are items that aid races in different ways. These items are useful for every race.
Mackenzie Powell left hints and riddles for more fun in searching for these treasures. Sometimes, the clue is pretty obvious, sometimes a brain wringer. Either way, hunters will have to look for them!
Treasure Hunting is done individually. To hunt, simply declare the point you want to search. When you get there, the mod will tell you if the item is there or not. If it's there, you will receive the artifact. If not, you must search for other points.
Standard movement rules apply here.
When artifacts are found, the owner will be the one who found it, not the entire race. The owner will be the only one who can use or even sell it.
Artifacts can only be found once. When they are retrieved, there will be no more artifacts in the said location. After regular intervals, there will be a redistribution of artifacts.
Artifacts are not the only items that can be found during Treasure Hunt. Some rewards include additional EXP, additional dymnos among others.
Let's get started!
GD: TH Scenario #1---------------------------------------
![](http://www.icestormcity.com/piarts/heraldry/GD/GDmap_top_grid1.jpg)
![](http://www.icestormcity.com/piarts/heraldry/GD/GD_map_view.jpg)
The first item is the Wisdom Ore.
This artifact grants the Hunter instant completion of any research. Even if the research is newly proposed, when a Wisdom Ore is used, it is completed instantly.
Normally, Items are open for hunting only one at a time....but as it seems, the races have not yet progressed to move on all land types...so there will be 4 Wisdom Ores!
Hints:
Item1: "Wild hearts run free..."
Item2: "To the ends of the earth!...err land..."
Item3: "Breathe....it won't be long now..."
Item4: "Fly, little wing..."
Happy hunting!
