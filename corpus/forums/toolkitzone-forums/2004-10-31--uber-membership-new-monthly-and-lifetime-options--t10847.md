# über membership - new monthly and lifetime options

### Xavier · 2004-10-31 18:38 · #B119567

After using the results of the latest poll and a few conversations with some users, I have introduced two new ways to become an über member and get your grubby hands on web space, e-mail accounts and your own board here at TKZ.
The first is a monthly payment option, which still offers 200mb space, 4000mb bandwidth, up to 3 ftp accounts, php, cgi, mysql database, up to 3 e-mail accounts, board on the forums and so forth. All at just £2.50 ($4.59) a month!
The second is an option that might never be used, but I thought it would be interesting to throw it out there just in case. It's a lifetime, one-off, donation that will give you 1000mb of space, 15000mb bandwidth and various other extras including your own forum usergroups if you so wish.
For more information on über membership and the changes to the plans such as the new bonus features then click [here](http://www.toolkitzone.com/uber.php?mode=about) or über panel link on the text menu. The entire section has been re-coded to make it much easier to follow and understand.
I will be making a few changes to the über panel tool itself over the day, so anyone who donates to become an über member today will be confirmed and upgraded this evening.
Thanks guys,
Xavi.
