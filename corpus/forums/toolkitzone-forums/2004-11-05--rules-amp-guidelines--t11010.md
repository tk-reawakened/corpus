# Rules &amp; Guidelines

### Randy · 2004-11-05 06:13 · #B121553

Howdy doody. I'm your lovely moderator. Welcome to our little slice of heaven. Intellectual debate and discussion is highly encouraged here, so feel free to post anything that's on your mind, as long as IT CAN BE DEBATED. Smile
To ensure everybody plays nice here, I've got to lay down a few ground rules. Here we go.
1. No topics that cannot be debated. What is debatable will be regulated by me. For instance, no "hi my name is tom" threads.
2. No "Which *insert video game here" is the best?" type threads. All gaming opinion threads belong on the gaming forum.
3. Similar to rule 2, no "Which *insert movie, tv show, etc* is the best threads". Keep those on the film forum. Same goes for writers, musicians, etc...Keep them all on the appropriate forums.
4. No insults. We're all supposed to be intellectuals here. Feel free to criticize and even tear apart your opponent's arguments, but you must be constructive, have a point, and not directly attack them. So, no "you're a dumb bitch, here's why" type comments. Play nice.
5. Don't start a debate and not voice your position on the issue, and you should have reasons behind your position. Exceptions will only be granted for me, because as a moderator I have to be impartial and cannot express my opinion.
6. Try not to take the debate off-topic. Feel free to start a new thread if a particular issue that can be debated comes up during the initial discussion. If a debate goes completely off-track, I may decide to close it.
7. No excessive silliness. Occassional humor is fine, but please keep the wackiness we all know and love on the off-topic forumS (note the S, always visit OT Republic for the real fun! /shameful plug). Smile
8. Use of sources is HIGHLY encouraged.
9. Do not hijack threads, unless specifically humorous. If you don't like a thread, you can say so with arguments, but don't start about "OMG stupid leftist/rightist apologist crap" every time a certain position is defended or shown in a thread. If you don't want to know, don't read it.
10. Do not post threads for the sole purpose of flaming one country, group, side or position. Biased articles, fine; editorial pieces aimed at certain targets, fine; but DO NOT make threads that only consist of "haha, stupid hippies LOL" or "see, France sucks". They will be gassed. Also known as the "France sucks clause".
11. Search before you post. Nobody likes redundant threads.
Though I'm not certain whether it will happen on a sub-forum or just stickies, starting soon there will be a topic of the week thread, where I (or any other mod, admin, etc.) pick a topic for debate. It could be anything, so check back often and be sure to participate.
