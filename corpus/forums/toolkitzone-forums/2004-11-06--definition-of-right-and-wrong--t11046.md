# Definition of Right And Wrong...

### Slasher · 2004-11-06 07:52 · #B121883

**Quote:**

> "No, Timmy, you can't steal, it's wrong."
> "But Mama, other people steal stuff. So how come it's wrong?"
> "Because they're naughty, and you're not."
> "If I steal, who said I am naughty?"
>

...Good point there, Timmy. Now I'm not referring to Laws and what's illegal; this question encompasses our entire lives. I want to know, who defines what is right and wrong for us, as human beings, not as citizens of a country, or anything. Take for instance, stealing.
One person can say it's wrong. Another person may say that there's nothing wrong with stealing. So who's correct? What about something like sexuality, which is a very debated topic? In the instincts of Man, if there is a difference in opinion between the two, they'd go to a person they think is higher than them/able to make such a decision.
But can they go to a man for an answer? Of course not. A man, again, has an opinion, like the two with this dispute between them. The person they ask for help has to be knowledgable of these things, of course, but cannot be someone who owns their own opinion, because it is just that --- an opinion.
Huh? How is it possible to find a Man without an opinion, but a definitive answer? It's not. Most people leave it at that...but how could the world work? Like it is now --- chaos in terms of crime, death, etc. If everyone could come to a definitive answer about something, and have a definition for the world for "Right" and "Wrong", we know that then and only then, we'd all be at peace.
Only one. There can only be one being to make the right and the wrong for us. If we were to appoint a King of the World, again, we'd fail at setting the right and wrong; he, as a human being, is subject to his own desires and opinions, and because of conflicting opinions from his followers, the world would still remain in chaos. So we have to turn to someone or something that is HIGHER than that, and can set the right and wrong because he/she doesn't have opinions; or rather, because this being is not of this world, it doesn't have it's own thoughts on the matter...it just sets something.
And as human nature would have it, we are attracted to the idea of something in charge, or governing people. Currently, the only thing Man can fathom of is some being called God. If this God were to set rules, and Man was to follow, we'd live in peace, because there are no opinions floating around; just a hard rule that has been set by a being we believe is greater than us.
Whether God exists or not, the idea sounds mildly comforting. I just hope that's not what it's all for --- simply a dream of comfort.
