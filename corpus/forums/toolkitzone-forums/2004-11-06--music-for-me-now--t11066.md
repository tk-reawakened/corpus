### Night_Elf · 2004-11-07 20:21 · #B122430

Most of the really good ones cost money.
The one i use (Music Masterworks) is pretty good and it cost me £15 but there is a 30 day free trial. The only problem with the trial (excepting that it only lasts 30 days) is that every time you try to close the program down a timer comes up, which gets longer every day
Full version is much better. It accepts microfone or keyboard (possibly e-guitar as well but i dont know about that one) and it also lets you use the mouse.
It costs money thats the only problem.
Free and good very rarley are compatible factors im afraid... (with the obvious exception of the RPG Toolkit)
