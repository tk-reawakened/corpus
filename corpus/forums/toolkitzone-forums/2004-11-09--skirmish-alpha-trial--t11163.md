# Skirmish alpha trial

### khin · 2004-11-09 05:51 · #B123035

We should begin our alpha test.
This is the skirmish mode. Ig you visit the site, you'll notice in the map some new reddish icons. These icons signify monsters spawning in. It's up to you if you want to fight these monsters. To do so, simply move to the block where they are, then the mods will declare the start of skirmish mode.
Skirmish ranges from RPG-like battles to puzzle-solving minigames. You'll find out the mechanics as the mods give them to you upon skirmish starts. Some skirmishes are also team-based, while some are individual. Rewards of skirmishes also vary from EXP, dymnos, resources, equipments, items, or even valuable artifacts.
For this alpha version, skirmishes will be held in turn-based, text-based. We are going to start a new thread for skirmish events. This is a group/individual activity.
Please beware that before you fight, make sure you have the appropriate technology(basic weapon, basic medicine, etc.) If you die, you will stay dead until somebody revives you, or you can ask a mod to revive you, deducting 100 dymnos from your treasury, plus suffering a 7-day penalty.
