# STARTOPIA: Raveoul Galaxy (Unfinished Ill edit when it is)

### Sevat Syvalluis · 2004-11-09 14:39 · #B123099

Im starting a STARTOPIA based rpg.
Overview: Set in the aftermath of a huge intergalatic war between poweful and aggressive alien races. Planets, Moons, and even Suns have been rendered lifeless. Little has survived, and the war has scattered the remnants of civilization to the far corners of sapce. a once thriving network of space stations and planets have been left lifless, Rebundant scars of the apocalypse. Into this age of darkness comes light. You will lead the way in rebuilding the network of space stations to a standard accepted by the individual alien races, attracting them to harness thier expertise and research new technologies.
Thus begins the hilarious battle of wits and cunning, full of daring escapades. Do you squeeze out your enemies with financial ruin or simply blast them back into space?
Features
* 9 Individual races with own dietary and Enviromental needs.
* Over 40 differnt buildings.
* Extensive Gameplay requiring city planning terra forming, resoursce management and diplomacy.
* Face Genocidal space pirates, plagues, parasitical infestations and letters of complant.
*Make friends and enemys with up to 4 leaders and 20 followers per game
Races
Thyorian (Grekka-Targ)
These are the data and communication specialists of the galaxy. attracting these little fellows will allow you to enhance your sensors, collate useful data on the running of the station, and spy on your opponets. However, if they find themselves with little to do they can start to "fiddle" with computer systems, and pry into the details of residents, causing no end of embarrasment.
Targs generally are an easy-going race, and have few rivals or enemies among the other races.
Polvakian (Gem Slug)
The "Gem SLugs" as they are known are the aristocracy of the galaxy, and are one of the most difficult alien races to keep happy and contented aboard a station. they have no specific skills that improve the stations efficiently, but are extremly wealthy and like to enjoy themselves to excess, as a by-product of their enjoyment, they produce a hard, energy rich substance known as turdite, which they eventually excrete in a crystalline form. These gems can then be collected and converted into pure energy- ample reward for the effort in keeping these visitors happy. Being very Aristocratic in nature, gem slugs disdain anything lowerclass, and are easily offended by the likes of groulian salt hogs. Keep these races apart if you want to keep your slugs happy.
Kasgorvian
Kasgorvian are a proud race of noble warriors, who seek self improvment through battle and confilct. they have a strict code of honour, and will only fight for a just cause is another matter however...
This race make perfect sercurity operatives, and are used to staff your station defensives. Unfortunately, if not given enough outlet for their aggression, they can become somewhat rowdy and are prone to start fights just for the fun of it. This is not always welcomed by the other station inhabitants, Kasgorvians have little respect for races that abhor violence, and do not mix well with Karmaramans
