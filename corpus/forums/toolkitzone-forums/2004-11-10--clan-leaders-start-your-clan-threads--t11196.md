# Clan leaders start your clan threads

### khin · 2004-11-10 05:47 · #B123346

Or any clan members with permission from your leader may do so. Please start your own clan threads. We are going to have a more systematic game now that we have a forum of our own.
The first post of your clan thread will display the day-to-day summary of your activities. I think this is fair enough. You get to compute your own actions. this will reduce errors, but hopefully nobody cheats. I'll check them anyway if your computations are correct. For the report format, please refer to the updates section an dobserve how i make the daily reports. Also, please keep them in the first post. I don't want to scroll through all posts in the thread just to find the report that was buried. That way, even if your clan thread has 100 posts, i'll still check the first page only because that's where the report is.
When your thread reaches about 20+ pages, you can ask me to delete it. You may then start a new clan thread.
