# My Story, rather new so dont mind the poor mechanics.

### darkshine kn · 2004-11-10 01:11 · #B123260

Read the story before you answer the questions, else you will probably not understand the question.
Fahad
Age:16
Weapon of Choice: Handgun and Laser-tipped Lance
Info: Fahad is the protagonist of the story, he handles his weapons very well and is a member of the "Silver Skies" Mercenary group. He acts as a tough fact-based character, but still holds strong emotional feelings. He has never known his parents, yet he holds a strong fondness of a dream he had which depicts a man and a woman, whom he believes are his parents.
Ryan
Age: 18
Weapon of Choice: ~No Weapon of Choice~
Info: Ryan is the brains of the group, he sends messages to Fahad through a communication system in Fahad's earpiece. The two of them had known each other since they were young, and show a great bond of trust and friendship. Ryan became a mercenary mainly because it was an easy way to gain profit for his upcoming "investment" which nobody seems to know.
Girl
Age: Looks 15 or 16
Weapon of Choice: Unknown
Info: A girl that Fahad rescued from a burning piece of lumber, not else is known about her aside from that she is the only survivor of the incident.
Fahad crouched against a large trash bin. The smell of rotting meat and dead rats drove deep into is nostrils. Ryan, what am I doing here again? he asked angrily.
Im sorry for the bad location Fahad, but we need to stay as discreet as possible. Ryan replied.
Im not discreet? Fahad laughed sarcastically.
Well now that you are here, I might as well tell you. You know that there is a big cash prize to the winner of that clue mission right? The one that the girl yaps about every morning on the radio?
Are you serious? Wasnt that just a hoax to increase user feedback to the station?
Afraid not, so the prize money is actually going to be given away
Where do I come in?
Your job is to take out the winner and snatch the prize money.
So I have to kill an innocent civilian?
Not really, you see the contest has been rigged by the producers, the winner is supposed to be Larry Smith, a local businessman.
Still my question remains, I have to kill an innocent civilian?
Innocent isnt really a good term for it, you see our friend Larry has been the mastermind behind quite a few scandals, and I mean big scandals.
Like?
Well you know how the entire citys power went out right? Then the next morning, the records of all the banks billing accounts went missing?
Wow, this guy is hardcore
Indeed, this guy should have been executed seven times already
Alright, so how much am I getting paid?
Well, if you get the prize money, you get to keep it right?
Hmm this job doesnt seem right, they need a mercenary to crash a little radio thing?
I know, but this is one large radio station, so I wouldnt be surprised if there are a bunch of guards and security personnel.
Fahad looked up from the trash bin and over at a ladder placed on the side of the building. He got up and walked over slowly. Suddenly he heard a gunshot, it sounded as if it was coming from the left of him. Fahad ran towards the ladder and pulled himself up it with ease. Another gunshot fired, it sounded as if whoever was firing was getting more and more accurate. Little help? Fahad asked his friends.
On it! Ryan said hurriedly.
After a few moments Fahads Wristband began to flash slowly, This is Pauls invention, some kind of energy barrier. Its only going to be active for a few seconds, so hurry your ass up!
Fahad didnt need to be told twice. He ran as fast as he could across the top of the building towards a large skylight. He looked down to see what looked like an award ceremony. Target reached, how do I know who this guy is? Fahad asked.
After a few moments of waiting Ryan finally responded, Hurry up, hes about to go onstage! He is the one with the silver hair! Fahad pulled out a long pole from the large holster held at his back. With a flick of a switch, a small blue energy ray in the shape of a blade spurted out from the front of it. Fahad stabbed the, Lance, into the skylight and smashed it. Fahad fell to the ground and, amazingly, he managed to land on his feet. That must have looked so cool! he thought to himself with a happy smile. The crowd stared at him, as the host simply looked shocked. Fahad knocked out the host with the dull end of the spear and grabbed the envelope that was clutched in his hand. This must be the prize money, he thought. After a few seconds, he spotted a man with silver hair wearing a business suit. Larry Smith? Fahad asked. The man smiled callously as he put his hand in his pocket. Fahad aimed his handgun and readied a shot. Do you believe in magic? the man laughed as a single shot fired from the handgun. As soon as it reached Larry, the bolt swerved away from him and hit a chair. What the? Fahad asked as he fired round after round of bullets. All of them swerved and hit something aside from the man. A gleam came to Fahads eye, some kind of lead-proof vest no doubt, but lets see how you fare against these! He said while inserting a magazine of shimmering silver bullets. Still, the bullets had no effect. Fahad stood in awe, This shouldnt have happened he stammered. Larry simply laughed and reached into his pocket.  Let me explain it to you, Larry has been dead for a while now, Now all that remains is me! he said as he vanished into thin air, I do hope we meet again Fahad.
Replacing his image was a torrent of flames. The flames blew threw the room, expanding with every passing second. The crowd scattered off to the doors, but all Fahad could think about was Larry, and his magical abilities. Fahad! Fahad! Ryan shouted into his ear, Get out of there! Fahad shook his head and looked up. The escape route was sound, if only he could avoid the flames Fahad, the internal temperatures in there are rising steadily, hurry up and bolt unless you want to die! Ordered Ryan. Fahad ran toward a stairwell in order to get out, but stopped to see a girl getting crushed by a large piece of lumber. With a mighty slash of his lance, the lumber was torn into pieces and the girl got up. She wore what seemed like a blue dress, that is until the fire got through with it. Her also shining blonde hair looked ashy and unclean, she must have been very attractive before the fire had its way with her. . Do you know whats going on? she asked weakly. Fahad shook his head, Any other survivors? he asked. She looked at him sadly, and without even having to say anything, he knew what had happened. Come, lets get out of here quick! he told her. They ran up the stairwell, avoiding flaming wreckage, until they came to the skylight. You, youre the one who started this? she asked with a tint of anger in her voice. Fahad didnt reply as he hoisted himself through the skylight, and let down his hand so she could come up. As soon as they were both up, Fahad, there Is someone close to you, watch out! Ryan screamed.
Calm down, this is one of the few survivors. Were coming back to base. He replied.
You cant do THAT!
Watch me. Over and out.
Fahad and the girl walked along silently until they came to the ladder. So you were the one who attacked us she said under her breath.
Listen! I didnt attack you, it was that magical guy who I was after! Fahad fumed.
Oh, and I suppose those bullets you fired everywhere were supposed to be aimed at him right?
Ive never had to use more than one bullet on one target ever, this guy has, some kind of power
Suddenly a floating robot flew up the ladder and slammed down on the ledge that they stood on, Watch out! Fahad yelled as he struck the robot with his lance. The machine recovered from the blow and continued its attack, this time it succeeded in making the girl fall off the ledge and down the side of the building. Fahad didnt have to look, her scream was enough proof. The machine readied for its next strike, this time his target was Fahad. Bastard! He yelled as he stabbed deep into the robotic shell. The robot went lifeless as he removed it from his lance. He looked down to the ground, and a single tear rolled down his face. This wasnt supposed to happen he muttered, Fahad wiped his face and climbed down the ladder. As soon as he reached the bottom, someone grappled him around the neck, and disarmed his gun and lance.
