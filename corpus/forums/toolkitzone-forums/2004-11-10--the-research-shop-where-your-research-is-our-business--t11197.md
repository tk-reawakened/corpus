# The Research Shop - where your research is our business ^^

### Ross L. · 2004-11-10 06:28 · #B123351

Ok since I seem to have more researches than I can use, I am going to start selling some off. ^^

**Code:**

```
Name: Aquatic Training

Category: Transport

Race: Jinnee

Research Cost: 100

Duration (days): 5

Usage Cost: 1MP/1

HISTORY: The Jinnee Have lived in water all their lives and love it. A time came in each Jinnee's life that they were trained how to move faster to defend themselves better.

Desc: Allows a Jinnee to move 2 squares per turn while underwater (no diagonals).

Name: Whip Training

Category: Offensive

Race: Earthkeeper

Research Cost: 100

Duration (days): 10

Usage Cost: 50 d/3

HISTORY: After the noble invention of the Thorn Rose, the Earthkeepers were better off than they were before, but the Humans had training to back their weapons. So the Earthkeepers organized and trained with the Thorn Rose to give them their edge back.

Desc: Specialized training for combat using a Thorn Rose. Gives +3 ATK to any Nai that fight with a Thorn Rose equipped.

Name: Cactus Bomb

Category: Offesive

Race: Earthkeeper

Research Cost: 250

Duration (days): 21

Usage Cost: 50 d/5

HISTORY: After many border skirmishes and witnessing many Ai and Nai fall to the Human technology of Explosives (something the Humans are also a Master of technically), the Nai began to grow new breeds of plants as bioweapons to make a similar item. After many months of long research, the Sai finally found the correct gene sequence and the Nai grew the Cactus Bombs.

Desc: The equivalent of a Human Shrapnel gernade, the Cactus Bomb explodes upon contact of anything other than a plant/Earthkeeper. Once the outer shell breaks, an acidic fluid spills out. Humans have deemed the acid to be the equivalent of Hydrocloric Acid, and rightfully so. The acid is capable of dissolving all armor currently researched. After the acid pours out, the needles of the cactus launch themselves at any sentient lifeform nearby, dealing -10 dmg a hit.

Name: Cactus Bomb: Poison Needles

Category: Offensive

Race: Earthkeeper

Research Cost: 150

Duration (days): 8

Usage Cost: 20 d/6

HISTORY: After the development of the Cactus Bomb, the Earthkeepers saw less casualties, but the Humans kept coming. The Sai finally made a few changes to the genetic structure of the Cactus Bomb and grew a new breed of the deadly flora.

Desc: Same as Cactus Bomb, except the needles now contain venom, poisoning the victim. Poison does -5 dmg/turn.

Name: Basic Weapon: Martial Arts

Category: Offensive

Race: All

Research Cost: 75

Duration (days): 10

Usage Cost: 50 d/2

HISTORY: Martial arts originated from a fighting style developed by a Chinese monk around 347 BC. It evolved from there into the most widely known and practiced unarmed style of fighting in existance to this day.

Desc: Basics of martial arts. The equivalent of a Yellow belt (next step up from beginner). Adds +2 ATK to all hits dealt by an unarmed warrior, +3 ATK if the warrior is Human (as they know it best).

Name: Robotic Engine: Hover Coil

Category: Economic

Race: Human

Research Cost: 50

Duration (days): 15

Usage Cost:10d/2

HISTORY: After the invention of the Robotic Engine, Humans could traverse the land more freely and set up their own outposts in various places. However, they still desired to be able to visit distant continents. Researchers teamed up with Machinists and made the Hover Coil.

Desc: Hover Coil allows any craft using a Robotic Engine to hover over water. IT CANNOT GO UNDERWATER! The craft will still travel at 1 square per turn.
```

Post here if you see something you want to buy. If you buy it, I will remove it from the above list. I will add all new technologies for sale here. Enjoy!
