# The Jinnek Language

### Sevat Syvalluis · 2004-11-12 15:26 · #B124314

I am planning on writting out the Jinnek language (to give Khin and Frei some history for their game) and to help with the PLACE OF SOLACE V1 puzzel. If I dont have the right to do this then let me know, I dont want to impede on your creation.
but the way I worked it out. The language consists of 5 types
(jy)Jinnek Hyro
(hj)High Jinnek
(jc)Jinnek Common (also known as Levant code)
(dl)Djinn Lok
(dh)Djinn Harnik
on a starwheel JY would be at the top then at the top right (facing it) HJ
then bottom right DL bottom left DH top left. the two types beside any dialect would reflect it and the two across would be confused with it.
