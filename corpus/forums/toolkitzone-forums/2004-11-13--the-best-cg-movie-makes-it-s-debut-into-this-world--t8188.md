### Spyder · 2004-11-13 09:45 · #B124668

For a standard movie, it wasn't that bad, but it wasn't supposed to be a "standard movie", it was supposed to be a Final Fantasy movie. It didn't have the same "captivation" that games like FFVI or VII had, which is what a lot of people were expecting. This is probably because you don't have enough time to get to know the characters like you do in a game that can potentially take 100+ hours to complete. It wasn't really an FF movie either. It didn't have anything that related to Final Fantasy other than the name Cid, or at least not that I remember. There were no chocobos, no crystals, no airships, no magic, very few monsters, no summoned creatures (Ifrit, Shiva, and friends) and no moogles. How can they even call it "Final Fantasy" when it doesn't incorporate any of the basics for a Final Fantasy story?
Advent Children will contain characters that everyone's already familiar with, and hopefully a number of the things listed above.
