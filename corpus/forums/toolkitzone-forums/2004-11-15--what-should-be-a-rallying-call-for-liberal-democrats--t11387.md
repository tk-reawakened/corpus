I find it very informative and interesting... I think that the Democrats need to do a LOT of things to "become more electable" again.
Here are some interesting LA Times articles to read on the matter:
[Blimey, that could save the Democrats!](http://www.latimes.com/news/printedition/opinion/la-oe-mickle14nov14,1,2826434.story)
[The Democrats' Self-Created Hell](http://www.latimes.com/news/printedition/opinion/la-oe-viguerie15nov15,1,6457409.story)
I think that the first article is a little better than the latter. The Democrats seem to lose the edge in the "moral issues." However, this could partly be because they don't create any "moral issues." Could they not have argued the war in a moral sense? How about the death penalty? How about giving people a choice on things like abortion? They all could be argued in a moral sense, but the arguments are not portrayed in such a way. And I also agree with the first article in that "you can never be too close to the middle [of the political spectrum]."
