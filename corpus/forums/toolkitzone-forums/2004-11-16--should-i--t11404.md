Should I swich from RPG Tool Kit 2 to TK3???

### tacofernandez · 2004-11-16 08:40 · #B125820

TK2
Pros:
Editor is easier to use IMO
Tracked music
Cons:
32x32 solid tiles
Tile based movement
Limited sprite sizes
Slow
TK3
Pros:
Actually being developed
Pixel movement
Unlimited sprite size
Isometric boards/tiles
Canvas commands
Not as slow as TK2
Cons:
32x32 solid tiles
Still not very fast
Editor not as intuitive
No tracked music (which apparently has been fixed for the unreleased 3.0.5)
