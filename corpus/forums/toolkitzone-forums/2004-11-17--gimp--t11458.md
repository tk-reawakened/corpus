### Ersuis · 2004-11-19 23:42 · #B127323

It's comparable, but still not quite there. For anybody here, it's not going to matter, but GIMP doesn't have quite the support for CMYK that Photoshop has (which is important to probably not anyone here). And, some of Photoshop's builtin filters for things like blurs, sharpens, and interoperlating are quite a bit more powerful and faster than GIMP's.
Don't get me wrong, GIMP is still a great piece of software, especially for being no cost, and I love using it (on Linux and Windows... it's a pain in the ass to use on Mac because it has to be run under X11), but it's not quite Photoshop.
