# Sin City, Frank Miller's back in Hollywood baby!

### Moonews · 2004-11-17 03:16 · #B126148

![](http://aintitcool.com/image/sincity1.jpg) ![](http://aintitcool.com/image/sincity2.jpg)
![](http://www.joblo.com/big-poster-images/postersincity3.jpg)![](http://www.joblo.com/big-poster-images/postersincity1.jpg)
I'm not a big fan of Frank Miller's work when it comes to movies, but a movie adaptation of his popular comic book is soon to hit the big screen. This is all possible due to a favorite director of mine, Robert Rodriguez, dropping out of the Directors Guild of America just to make this film happen. He did it because of the "one film, one director" policy. Since he is using the same angles and colors (yes, the movie is mainly B&W with splashes of color) as Miller did in his comic book, he wanted to give credit where credit is due. Everything is just as it was in the novel, exept for certain scenes with Jessica Alba due to her no nude policy. I've seen raw preproduction footage and it's quite amazing. The opening scene was already rendered (Yes, rendered. The backgrounds are done in CG and acting is done on a greenscreen) for a preview video. It looked fantastic. The trailer isn't online as far as I know, but if anybody can find it that'd be great.
