### Spyder · 2004-11-18 15:50 · #B126745

Why not have a Radeon 9600 on an 800MHz system? It's only as odd as you having a low-end graphics card with a mid range processor. The IMO, graphics cards determine performance more than processors do. Buying a $150 processor over a $50 one isn't going to jump your performance as much as going from a $50 graphics card to a $150 graphics card. And as he said, his computer is made of parts people were throwing out. It wasn't his choice what he got for free.
