# LMAO, listen to this

### Spyder · 2004-11-19 21:33 · #B127270

I posted about my friends laptop having problems earlier. Well, after I found out it was the motherboard, and it needed replaced, my friend called IBM, who also tells us it's the motherboard, and needs to be replaced, and will cost a minimum of $425 for them to replace it. Obviously my friend doesn't feel like paying a total of $900 for a used laptop that can barely play DVD's, let alone games, so he gets the Post Office to agree to pay for the repair by saying it was their fault that it didn't work (damaged during shipping). They won't pay for it unless he gets a quote saying how much it will cost, and even when he gets the quote, they'll only pay up to $400. IBM won't give quotes, god knows why, so my friend has to take it to a PC repair shop that will work on IBM laptops to get a quote on how much it would cost them to repair it. There apparently arn't many that will even work on IBM laptops, in fact, only 2 in the Phoenix Metro area. One of them will charge $35 to run a diagnostic to find out what is wrong with the laptop and write up a quote on how much they would charge to fix it. The other will charge $50. So we drive like 15-20 miles to the $35 one and tell them the problem, and that we don't need them to run a diagnostic, we just need them to write up a quote that says how much it would cost to fix the problem.
Here's where the fun begins. They say that they can't just write up a quote, they have to run the diagnostic to find out what is wrong with the notebook. What do you mean what's wrong, we already know it's the motherboard. I know this, IBM told us this, even the notebook itself states an error code that corresponds to a motherboard error. The notebook won't even complete a POST, and the only thing that would cause that is the motherboard.
Doesn't matter. They insist that they have to run their diagnostic software to determine the problem, which will cost us $35. Run software? They can't run software, the computer doesn't even complete the POST. It wouldn't boot a CD, regardless of what was on the CD. Doesn't matter, they say. They have to run a diagnostic if we want a quote. So finally my friend gives in. They say it will take 30-40 minutes, so we leave and go eat. When we come back 30 minutes later, they're already done. Guess what's listed on the quote.
Wouldn't even complete POST. Recommend mainboard replacement
Mother F*ckers! We drove 20 miles, argued for 15 minutes, waited 30 more, and paid $35 for them to write down on a piece of paper what we told them was the problem right when we walked in the f'ing door? The diagnostic didn't take 30-40 minutes, it took the 5 seconds that it takes to hit the power button, boot, and freeze at POST. $35 to write on a piece of paper, which is exactly what we were asking them to do when we walked in, but they said they couldn't without doing the $35, five second diagnostic.
I seriously wanted to punch their manager....
