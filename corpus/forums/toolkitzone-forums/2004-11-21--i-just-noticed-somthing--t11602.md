Speak not of that which you do not understand unless in the form of a sincere question (A little rule I wrote that I tend not to follow anyway. lol.)
The main theme of Dragonball is that there are seven balls, called the Dragonballs, that are scattered across the world. When all seven of the mystical Dragonballs are collected, whoever possesses them is able to summon forth a dragon with the power to grant them one single wish. Afterward, the balls are once again scattered and thrown to random places across the Earth. Each planet has their own set of Dragonballs. The Dragonballs on planet Namek are able to grant three wishes. The original Dragonballs of Earth were able to grant only one, and they had many limitations. The second set was able to grant two wishes, with many of the old limitations removed, such as not being able to revive the same person twice and only being able to revive one person per wish, but new ones were added. I forgot what it was though. ^_^' Anyway, yes they can die, and do quite often, but they can be wished back to life through the power of the Dragonballs. However, when too many wishes are made, negative energy builds up within the balls which creates new, evil dragons within each of them, which is what happens in Dragonball GT during the Shadow Dragon saga.
As for the original question, maybe Vegeta no longer wanted the Saya-jins to be revived because of the damage they could cause to the universe. If you remember correctly, not all the old Saya-jins were as valiant and heroic as King Vegeta and Bardock. Remember Turles? Brolli? Nappa? Raditz? Vegeta (back during the Freeza saga)? All the new Saya-jins seem to be heroes though. lol. (Vegeta, Kakkorot/Goku, Gohan, Goten, Trunks, Pan, Goku jr. and Vegeta jr.)
