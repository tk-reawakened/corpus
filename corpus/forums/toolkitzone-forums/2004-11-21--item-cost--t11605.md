### Dont_Think_So · 2004-11-21 18:08 · #B128012

ItemCost[1]! = GetItemCost(Item1$)
ItemCost[2]! = GetItemCost(Item2$)
ItemCost[3]! = GetItemCost(Item3$)
ItemCost[4]! = GetItemCost(Item4$)
ItemCost[5]! = GetItemCost(Item5$)
Add that in there, and Itemcost[x!]! will have the costs of your items.
