### Dont_Think_So · 2004-11-23 00:03 · #B128681

1) You need to make sure all of the animation slots in your character are filled in with frames. Open up your character, then go to graphics, and make sure all movement slots are filled.
2) Open up the board editor, click the "Type Lock" button (it looks like a padlock). Select Solid (the blue one), then make all of the solid tiles solid.
3) Open up the board editor, place a wall there instead of a painting. Then go to layer two, and click "Draw all layers". Then, add the picture on top. Save.
