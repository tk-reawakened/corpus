### khin · 2004-11-27 04:40 · #B130804

yeah people..i invite you to join.
H:GD sets itself apart from all other RP topics in the RP forum mainly because it is not a continue-someoneselse's-story RP
In H:GD, you play the role of your selected character, with your own race, clan, skills, and job. Through the course of the game you(along with your clan peers) should help each other out and mine stone or minerals, cut woods, go hunting, fishing to gain resources. These resources shall be required for you to build buildings to produce weapons, items, spells, etc. You'll also need money to research technologies. On top of that, you have your own character sheet with ATK, DEF, HP/MP, EXP, LV...just like any RPG has. Gain EXP by fighting monsters or doing activities.
Come one, give it a shot..just 3 turns a week...all of which you could use in a mere 5 minutes or so...you don't have to be glued in to play.
Go to the Heraldry:Great Divide Sub Forum under the RP section...
You can also check the Official Site, please click my H:GD banner to proceed there.
We'll be waiting for you Smile
