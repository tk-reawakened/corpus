# Mozilla increases market share from 2.1 to 7.4 in six months

### Spyder · 2004-11-24 16:29 · #B129544

**eweek.com wrote:**

> Mozilla's share is rising at the expense of Internet Explorer.
> Global usage share numbers released Monday by OneStat.com showed Mozilla Foundation browsers increasing to a 7.4 percent share from 2.1 percent in May. Meanwhile, Microsoft Corp.'s Internet Explorer dropped 5 percentage points to 88.9 percent during the same six-month period.
> OneStat.com specifically cited Mozilla's Firefox browser as one reason for the usage shake-up. The Mozilla open-source project released Firefox 1.0 earlier this month. Firefox held a 5.6 percent share in the latest OneStat.com data, putting it halfway toward reaching a Mozilla goal of having a 10 percent Firefox share in 2005. Over the weekend, Mozilla release manager Asa Dotzler wrote in his Weblog that Firefox 1.0 had reached 5 million downloads since its launch.
