### Spyder · 2004-11-26 06:08 · #B130366

Low profiles cards are also known as "half height". They're probably 60% the height of normal video cards. They're mostly used in SFF systems and manufactured PC's from companies like eMachines, etc, who use super small cases because "smaller=better". None of them are going to be particularly powerful, since the more electronics used on a board, the more space you'll need to put them. If you go to Newegg.com, and do a search for "low profile" in the video cards section, it will pull up about four cards, ranging from the Radeon 9250, to the GeForceFX 5200, to the GeForce 5700LE, which is probably the best of the four, and yet, still not that great, especially because it's a PCI version. In fact, you may actually be better getting one of the AGP GeForceFX 5200's, especially because they're half the cost of the 5700LE. Unfortunately the 5200's only have 64-bit memory...so it's pretty much a lose-lose situation no matter what you pick.
