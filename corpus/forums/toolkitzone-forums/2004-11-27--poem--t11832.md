# Poem

### DistantCitySilence · 2004-11-27 10:36 · #B130880

The distance of her heart,
So firmly kept apart, from my own.
I never felt so alone.
The feelings that I had, could only be bad,
As the pain would carry on.
I couldn't say goodbye, as when I tried I'd only cry.
And now the city is gone, bombed by a monsterous one,
Silence makes my ears bleed.
She was mine, but now is freed.
The one who killed her though, honestly didn't know,
She was there that day.
So all I can say, is stop the awful bomb,
My once one love is gone.
I thought I'd add something to this section, being new and not knowing where to start.
