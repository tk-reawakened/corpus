### Talyn · 2004-11-28 09:07 · #B131137

Ok, here's a quick runthru of what you need to do.
Unzip The Dragoons folder using Winzip, or the equivalent.
Inside the unzipped folder there should be some sort of installer. Double click that and install the game following the steps.
There will probably be an icon appear on the Desktop or in your Start menu now which is called The Dragoon. Clcik that, and you should play the game.
If this doesn't work, contact the guy who made it ( [MrB](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=1384)) and ask them.
