### Spyder · 2004-11-28 08:59 · #B131134

There are plenty of games that would benefit from multiplayer, specifically racing and FPS titles, and the option would be nice for fighting and RPG titles as well. Just because they're games for Nintendo doesn't mean they shouldn't have multiplayer, and just because there's a multiplayer option, doesn't mean that the single player modes would start lacking in appeal. In fact, many would gain more appeal to play the single player mode, because it might unlock secrets that could help you in multiplayer mode.
And split screen is horrible. Not only does it make your screen smaller, but you get a far less detailed game as well, because of the lower resolution and smaller screen. Any split-screen game would be better played on four full size screens. Especially because you wouldn't be able to look at someone elses screen, which is a huge problem, especially with FPS titles, like Goldeneye.
