# The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion

### Spyder · 2004-11-28 12:16 · #B131146

This game is going to kick ass. If you liked TES III: Morrowind, this game is going to be even better. Far better. The world is bigger, the graphics are stunning, and there's all sorts of advances to the AI and battle systems. Check out [GameSpy's Pre-view](http://pc.gamespy.com/pc/the-elder-scrolls-iv-oblivion/558955p1.html?fromint=1) of the game, which reveals tons of info on the game, and quite a few screenshots that are amazing. Just to show you how much Bethesda is putting into this game, it's been in development since 2002, and won't be out for some time, 2005 at the earliest, possibly 2006. It'll be worth the wait for sure.
