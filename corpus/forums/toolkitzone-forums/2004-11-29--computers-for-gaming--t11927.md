### Spyder · 2004-12-01 03:29 · #B132621

The 9600XT or 9800 Pro would definately give you better frame rate, and probably allow you to turn on extra quality options like anti-aliasing and ansiotropic filtering, while still running in higher resolutions. It'll also let you play more recent games like HL2 and Doom3 in medium-high res, which your Radeon 9000 surely will not. BHD is a fairly old game, so you shouldn't be surprised that it runs on your comp.
PS, make sure you don't buy any graphics cards with the term "SE" in them. They're underclocked, low memory bandwidth versions of the actual card. The XT and Pro versions are the ones you should look at for ATI cards, which I assume you don't want to buy nVidia.
