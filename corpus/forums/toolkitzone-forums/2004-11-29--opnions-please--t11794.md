### BahamutG · 2004-11-30 14:05 · #B132067

The banner and buttons are too busy. It distracts too easily from what you really want your viewers to see; the content.
I personally do not like how the buttons, and navigation are shoved over to the side.
I think too many new web designers are obsessed with the whole roll over button thing. Learn some CSS, just use simple links, but create effects with the CSS for when they rollover.
Also, the banner; I would suggest making a banner with the same color as your background, with just the title of your site having some cool effect to it. Don't place that ugly background behind it, especially when it stops so ubruptly. Heck, using just the text of the banner would look pretty nice with the background color of your page.
For some final suggestions, I would focus on learning the raw code. It makes it easier to see where you went wrong. Also, learn how to use div tags, they are a lot of fun when mixed with CSS.
Well, hope that helped. Probably not ^^, but I try.
