# warptiles

### Jimmydean · 2004-11-29 01:01 · #B131346

I am having trouble with warp tiles. I'll set a tile and type in the right coordinates, but my character will still appear on the top upperleft tile. I have saved after i place a warp, but it still does the same thing. Please tell me what I am doing wrong and what I should do. Thanks Confused

### Delano · 2004-11-30 21:16 · #B132214

Hmm, sounds like you're doing it right! Have you checked the warp program (it will be something like warp00000003.prg) to make sure the #Send() command has the right information? (Send("board.brd", x!, y!, layer!)
If that's set, then it could be a couple of other things. Check that there isn't more than one warp prg on the same tile, and check that the target location isn't obstructed (although that shouldn't stop the #Send occuring normally).
And if all else fails, check your details again; check you've not exceeded the target board's dimensions.
