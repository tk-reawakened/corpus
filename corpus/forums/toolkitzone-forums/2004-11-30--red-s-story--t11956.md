# Red's Story

### rpgmaster24 · 2004-11-30 19:54 · #B132181

A part of my game's story, when you get a char named Red.
Deep within a dark, green, forest who's trees swayed in the wind like a beautiful an ocean, stood a small village. A rebel village, filled with people who wanted nothing to do with the current state of their menacing kingdom's government. Now since this village lives in the deep forest their homes were not made of red brick, and cement, but of shining, yellow hay and strong, greenish-yellow bamboo. There were many types of people lived in this small village, young and old, small and large, and strong and weak. You would think that this would mean they lived in despair and unhappiness, but no. They live happy peaceful lives in this small village of Porre. An example of how happy these people are was a boy, a boy named Red. He was very strong for a boy his age, at the young age of 13. He wore the hides of animals for clothes, and had, long, spiky, fiery red hair.
One cold, night, he and his Father, that went by the name of Alex, entered the forest to hunt animals for clothing and meat, together for the first time in Red's life. Alex was very tall and strong, he had, like his son, deep red hair. Alex carried with him, attached to a belt, a long sword in which to carve up a kill. Red looked to his Father and said, Really, Father? Am I really really ready to hunt? His Father stopped his steady pace, looked down, then to his son, and then let out a light-hearted chuckle, Ha ha, of course you're ready, you're already the strongest boy in the entire village! He knelt down and took the bow and quiver off of his back, then he gently pushed them in Red's direction. Red's his shined with pure excitement. He smiled the largest smile and quickly accepted his Father's generous gift. He mounted the Quiver around his back and took the bow in his hands and said, Thank you! Thank you so much! Alex stood up and said, I knew you would like it! Why don't you try it out? Red took an arrow out of the quiver on his back he took aim with his bow at the nearest thing he could. Huh? What's that over there? said Red, as he pointed out a horse in the distance.
The horse walked out from behind the trees to reveal that a person was mounted on it! The person mounted on the horse wore pure silver armor, everything, a helmet, gauntlets, boots, even a breastplate! Alex pushed his son back and muttered silently, What's an imperial knight doing here? Red, worried that his Father seems to be silently said, Father, who is that? Alex quickly grasped the hilt of his sword and said, Red, go back to the village, now, tell the others that we have they have found us... Red hesitantly obeyed his Father and rushed off into the distance. Alex looked at the figure and shouted, What do you have business being here?! The armored man looked in his direction and moved his horse closer to him. He glared at Alex and said, Why, we can't allow people like you to cause anarchy in the kingdom now can we? the knight drew his sword and said, You can't be allowed to live...
As Red continued his Father's mission of giving the villagers his message, he stopped hearing the faint sound of metal clashing and the horse's whinny, he turned back around and ran faster than he ever had before. What's happening? Red asked to himself, What does Father's message mean? Red had finally finished his journey back, when he looked at the village and he fell to his knees. The village was aflame! The huts were being reduced to nothing but black ash. The fires were dancing as if demon's controlled their every move. Suddenly, out from behind one of the huts came a woman, she had a sword! She was fighting... a knight! They had reached the village before he could!
The woman was holding her own against the knight, until, she caught a glimpse of a boy, on his knees, by the entrance to the woods! It was Red! She slashed the knight, knocking him to the ground and screamed at Red, Run, Red, get out of here! Red took a closer look, it was his Mother, Cynthia! Her, yellow and brown patterned dress has tattered and charred, her brown hair has messy, and her skin showed signs of cuts and burns! Red stared and saw a knight right behind his Mother, he screamed, Mother! Watch out!! It was too late, the knight's sword pierced through his mother's back. The knight retracted his silver, now blood-stained sword from her body. Her sword fell to the ground, then she soon followed.
No, no... Mother!!!! Red screamed in anguish and in pain. He wept for his fallen Mother, then he grasped his bow tighter and tighter... he stood up with a bow in one hand and the bow in the other. His face filled with rage, and sadness, he took aim at the knight with it and shot, before the knight could even turn around, it pierced his steel armor and through him all the way into a tree almost 50 feet away from him! The knight wasn't even allowed a gasp of air, he was instantly killed.
All of a sudden out from behind him came clapping... the sounds of footsteps came ever so closer... Red turned around. Out of the darkness a man came clapping his hands with a menacing gaze. He exited the shadows, it was the same man from before! The one on the horse! Except now his breastplate was gone along with his helmet, revealing his face, he had a brown pointed beard with wavy brown hair that flowed along with the winds, he looked at Red and said, The man before, you were with him? I have a feeling you won't be again... Red's tortured face turned even worse, What?he said, with a broken voice. He was no match for me, he couldn't handle the edge of my sword. he unsheathed his sword and stared at the blade. Red charged at the armored man with a mind full of rage, and confusion. The man grabbed Red by the neck and lifted him upwards above him, looked at him and said, Boy, you are one of them? Red hacked and coughed for he could not breathe. The man looked around the village and saw the sight of one of his knights felled, with the arrow in the tree, and said in a soul crushing voice, Impressive, a primitive arrow made by you rebels, could never pierce my men's armor! This arrow was not shot by any normal person, I won't kill you yet. We will meet again, and when we do, I want to recognize you. he lifted his sword and slashed Red's face, then threw him to the ground. He began to laugh, the last thing that Red heard.
The next morning, Red awoke screaming, Gahhhhhhhhhh!! A man came rushing into the hut, wearing merchant's clothes, where Red was sleeping, Are you ok, Red? he asked. Red realized he was bandaged, and in a bed, he replied, Are you? The man laid Red back down and said, We have buried the fallen, including your Mother and Father. We were outside the village when this happened, we came back to this. Red's eyes closed and tears began to flow down his cheeks. The man felt sorrow for Red and said, We will bring you some food later, you can visit your friends and family's graves when you are better he then walked out of the hut. A few days later, Red's wounds were healed and the cut the Knight gave to him left a scar across his left eye. He was visiting his Parent's graves and said, I swear... your death's will not be in vain, I will make your dreams come true, and I will avenge your lives. He stood up bowed to the graves and walked into the forest.
About four years later, Red was still fulfilling his promise. He wielded both his Mother's sword and his Father's Bow. He was in the kingdom town of Kino. He now wore a bandanna around his forehead, and a green gi and white training leggings. He was looking for help, so he was in a tavern at a bar. He sat down at the counter and asked the barkeep, his question, for he knew he was a person that was also a rebel. He told him of a group called The End and where they could be located. He went on his way, thanking the barkeep, before so. He had heard that they were located in this town of all places, in a rundown, old building that used to be a harbor.
He made his way and finally arrived. He made it past the security and to the main boss's office. Well, well, you seem to have very good skills at stealth A man in a chair swiveled around revealing himself to Red. He wore traditional Chinese clothing, that was blue, and he had black hair, with a ponytail. You are the leader of The End, I presume?, Red asked the man. He replied in a sly voice, Correct. And you are? Red took a seat in a wooden chair before him and told him of his story, and that, I need your help. He jointed his hands together and laughed, then said, Well, I think we can help you there. They shook hands and then they left the office. Where to now, mercenary? Red spoke. Well, where do you think, He said as he smirked, To the castle, to kill Epoch. Red laughed for the first time in years. Alright, let's hope it's that easy, he spoke.
