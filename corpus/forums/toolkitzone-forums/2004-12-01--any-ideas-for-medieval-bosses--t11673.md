### kahless · 2004-12-01 14:48 · #B132830

What about this -- what is you created a person or a monster on whom all spells have the opposite effect. So you cast a healing spell and it hurts him, or a protective spell and it lowers his stats (shell lowers MD, protect lowers def, haste slows him down).
As far as naming -- depending on the world you're creating, sometimes it works to find an online translator like babelfish and translate a discription of the creature.
Just an example
opposite man (English)
Przeciwłegły człowiek (Polish)
if it's hard to say like this one is, you can play around with spelling and shorten the word a bit.
The polish pronounciation would be something like:
"P'zhechiv-wegwy chwveek" a little long and hard for most people to say.
So maybe you could shorten it a bit:
"Weggy-Cheveek"
a little better right.
keep doing that until you find a name you think fits.
Mine would be "Wecheveek", but if all your names sound japanese or something, then do the same thing with japanese. I think there are online japanese translators but you have to be able to read katakana.
