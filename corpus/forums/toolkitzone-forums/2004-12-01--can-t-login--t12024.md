### Xavier · 2004-12-01 22:04 · #B133058

When changing your e-mail or creating an account, you now have to confirm that e-mail address via an e-mail that is sent to you.
Often this e-mail gets stuck in spam filters by your e-mail service provider. Make sure this hasn't happened in both cases, as it has to other users who didn't noticed it was filtered.
You cannot login until it has been confirmed.
