you never know with some people though... they'll do it just to say they did if your game is popular enough... Anyway, back to the main topic of the subject!
No one can really say where to begin with a battle system. You have to figure out what are the largest bits of code, and start from either the small bits or large bits. You could start in the middle, but it would be alot harder. For instance, Setting up the graphics is the first thing I did for MONO-Max. It wasn't the FULL graphics, but the basic outline of it. Then from there, I made the wait system (you can use a wait system like I did, where your speed moves your bar, or just instant to whomever has the highest speed.) From there, you go to what happens when the person is chosen to fight (enemy or ally, whichever you want to do first) and so on. After that, you just keep adding in section by section over and over till you get the full BS. It is a time consuming process, I will admit that. With testing and so on, I have put in WELL over 10 hours of programing, and I just have 2 allies (well.. main charactor, and one ally) and 3 enemies, but you can kill, and be killed. Now, I am almost done with my items system... actually... I am done with the item menu's and such, I just have to program the items to work. Next, I will work on the Magic system, but I haven't started yet. I would suggest using loops (while or branch loops) and methods. If you need ANY help, PM me, or aim me.
