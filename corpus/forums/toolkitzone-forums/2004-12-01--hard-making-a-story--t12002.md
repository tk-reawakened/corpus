My first game is going to be more story driven than anything else since I can't program it that well Sad
In the ancient land of Atrela, an 18 year old named Tre (after Green Day's drummer) who was trained as an elite soldier during the Great War. An evil empire started this war, and it was ended by the leader of the elites. A few months later that leader was found dead, and Tre was made the new leader because the high council saw a mystical power (how predictable) in him. He starts a journey with the military commander of the elites, McNyrea, to find out why their previous leader was murdered. The evil empire had regrouped, and are launching an even bigger attack on the lands.
6 Characters:
Tre - Protaganist. Parents were killed in the Great War when he was young, then trained as an elite. Has a dark secret about himself which only one other knows of, and he can never have it revealed...
McNyrea - Basic high ranking soldier. Nothing special about him except for amazing battlefield skills. He keeps to himself as much as he can because of mistakes he made in the past.
Theo - He knows Tre's secret, and this scares Tre...and worse than that Theo's parents were part of the evil empire, and were the ones who killed Tre's parents. Theo joins the group in attempts to correct the mistakes of the past, but Tre doesn't want him around because of what Theo knows...
Princess Kaylee - Princess of Atrela. She is supposed to stay indoors and obey her mother, but she doesn't. After an attack is launched against her palace, she is rescued by Tre and company, and, in realizing she has nowhere to go, she joins them. She is secretly a high council member of the elites.
Kalin - Another elite. He is faster and much more agile than most others. The story really has nothing much to do with him, he's just a "filler" character, a bit like Irvine or Selphie in FF8
Stonefire - An old man who claims to have forgotten his name, his past, and his identity. He was a forest hunter who saw the company wandering around the woods one day, then decided they would need a guide to find their way. He guides them through the woods, then joins them. He remains shady, and is not trusted by Tre, but Tre has every right not to trust him for a crime Stonefire commited...
Blah Blah Blah, many plot twists and such...go ahead and steal something like it if u urself is having trouble with a story.

### st_jimmy · 2004-12-12 21:01 · #B138598

Tips: sure...
Make the characters convincing and different. Give them different weapons and a wide array of looks. Good characters are the base of every good story.
Setting: Make the world as imaginative as possible. Most RPG style games start off in some sort of light haven in an otherwise dark world. Make it a big world with many different terrains: Desert, mountain, aquatic, forest...blah blah blah.
Bosses/bad guys/villains: very important. Of course, like in all games, there's gotta be an overall bad guy, but make 'sub-villains'. Henchmen, mercenaries, and convincing rivals make for a good story. The commonly encountered monsters also must be convincing, and it's good to add 'rare' monsters that might give you key items that could help in the quest.
Story: Now for the story itself. A good RPG (imo) has an overall story and a deep story. The overall story is usually the journey faced by all characters, which is usually killing the bad guy and saving the world, but then the deep story should be the main characters personal struggle to become a hero. He may have to deal with mistakes made in the past, a prophecy about him, personal fear, anger, or depression, a loved one, or just being a very dark personality and trying to escape from that. Plot twists and sub-stories are good. Such as: make another character return to his/her hometown and blah blah blah. Have characters leave for short persiods of time then return to your team after a story-driven event happens.
That's all the advise I can give you. I like coming up with LotR and FF style stories, and us gaming nerds always love that crap so there.
