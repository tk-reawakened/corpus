# SCENARIO: Erm meets Hairull

### khin · 2004-12-01 13:24 · #B132810

Sai-Erm has been watching the large creature that has been hovering on the skies of Nachram. Driven by his natural curiosity and lust for knowledge, he tracked down the creature and finally managed to find it resting on the woods.
![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/hairull.gif)
Hiding behind the bushes, he eagerly observed the creature. though painstakingly as to determine where it is. And there he found near cleraberries two Hairulls--aerial creatures with translucent bodies, perfectly blending with their surrounding. Sai-Erm stares at the creatures, and becomes aware that the Hairulls are bobbing their heads, scouting for possible danger. As it seems, Hairulls are very territorial.
