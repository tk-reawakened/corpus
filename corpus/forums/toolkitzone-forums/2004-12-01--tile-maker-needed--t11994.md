# Tile Maker Needed

### danny_boi · 2004-12-01 08:05 · #B132767

I am making a Mario game with the knowledge I have of the games (which is a lot, trust me). I am planning on the game being like Super Mario RPG, but in more of a Super Nintendo fashion with the tiles. I will have enemies from throughout Mario's history, most of which I have already attained the tiles from the previous games. I am relatively good at doing modifications and recolorations to tiles to make new ones, but I seem to be having trouble on 2 perticular tilesets: One for Wario, and one for Waluigi. I need tiles to be made for all the basics, i.e. motion (Front, Back, Left, Right) and fighting (one for a basic attack, and one for special attacks). Try as I might, I cannot seem to come up with a design that I like, therefore I am requesting help. The only guidelines I have are 1, the tiles need to look like Super Nintendo graphics as far as size and shape. I tried modifying the Mario and Luigi graphics from Super Mario World, but couldn't come up with anything good, maybe you'll have better luck... And two, they must be correct color wise and size wise. Waluigi is taller than Wario, that's all I need there, and Wario is fatter than Mario, so that needs to be taken into consideration too. Wario's colors are yellow hat and shirt, with purple overalls. Waluigi's colors are dark blue, almost purple hat and shirt (This Color) and black overalls. Wario's mustache makes a W and Waluigi's makes an L to each side. For reference, simply look at pictures of Wario and Waluigi on the internet. Any who are interested reply here, and begin work as soon as possible. Thank You in advance.
Danny
