# Nermoria: Newfound Land Chapter 1: Shadow awaiting

### Ivan · 2004-12-02 19:40 · #B133623

Here's the first chapter of Nermoria Book One "Newfound Land" Crits would be appreciated, i have some areas i have to clear up, i am oping to make this into a 3D game.
Chapter 1- Shadow awaiting.
What a beautiful morning thought Roland to himself. Perfect for the Mid-Summer Feast Roland imagined it, the best Day of summer, filled with dancing, singing and of course the games.
There were the games for the children, the ones where you run a dash, jump farthest, jump highest and other such games. Roland being seventeen, would only participate in the sports like sword fighting, wrestling and boxing.
The women did not sword fight or wrestle but they had competitions like who could weave the most beautiful scarf the fastest or who could bake the best delicious pie. Rolands village consisted of about maybe eighteen to twenty families.
Many of these people were growing old which often told of stories about the past of their village Koriand, and he never did manage to find what or how it came to be named that way. Better do my part in the Mid-Summer Feast thought Roland as he started walking towards the hills to the East of the village.
His part in the Feast was to gather a lot of firewood which would be needed for the bonfire always held after the Feast very late at night. Of course with him would go his friends Brandwin and Elaynor.
Brandwin was a middle sized, broad shouldered fellow with brown hair just stopping short of where the eyebrows started which had blond streaks in the front, his eyes were like a fresh field green. He was one of the people who could always find something to complain about as if nothing was the way he wanted it to be. Brandwin was one year older than Roland but even so he respected Roland and let Roland be the leader of their trio group.
His other friend Elaynor, was a very beautiful woman, she too like him, was seventeen years old, younger by only a couple of months. Her braided hair fell over her shoulders, her face always lit up like the sun with her beautiful smile, her eyes a deep darkish blue made you feel like you were going to drown in them once you looked at them. She was one of the most graceful women Roland had ever seen! Is she something like those men of the far off places described as a Goddess? wondered Roland.
He thought her as no more than a girl, his sister, although she did have the grace, elegence and mind as a fully grown woman would. She was one of those women who would look at a guy and make him obey as though he was a very pliant man, which in most cases was not true when Roland tried it!
I wonder how she does it Roland wondered. As for Roland himself, he was one of the taller people, strong but not heavily muscled like Brandwin.
Good with a sword unlike Brandwin who could not do anything with a sword but when given an axe or a nice long bow could do wonders in Rolands opinion.
Roland was pretty fast too, as a youngster he won a countless amount of races, could climb any tree around too! His eyes, unlike Elaynors, were a light blue as you would see if you looked at a lagoon in an ocean.
His hair, falling half way down his ears, was black. He too had a very good looking face. He was one of the guys who every girl would die to talk to let alone be on his number one list which he actually didnt have as of yet.
The village might have been pretty small but it was a sybarite village. The houses where most villagers lived were on the outside, with exotic statues, fountains all over the streets. Almost every house had a statue in front of it, always symbolizing something, whether it be a family crest or something like House of Peace or The Dragon Hearts.
Along the paved streets were beautiful fountains with dolphins or birds pouring water out of their mouths. The houses themselves were of different stone and sizes. Whoever could afford a better and bigger house lived in a bigger one whoever couldnt lived in a smaller one. Roland did not remember the last time any one built a house, people always bought them off of each other. From these houses led stone streets farther in the village with arches marking a new area which was the second inner area. This is where the Smiths was, the Tannery, the Bakery, the Mill, the shops which sold things like scarves clothing, home utensils.
Going more inward led streets with arches too but this was the Innermost area where the Healers ground was, the Barracks and the High council building where everything was carried out. The High council building was where merchants came to trade, thats were disputes were settled, the Grand library was within too! This building was probably the most ancient of them all. Nobody knew when it was built and how. It was just too big for Roland to imagine anyone building.
He had heard rumors of countless passages and tunnels going beneath the mansions cellars which held the greatest of wines! This building of course was occupied by the librarians and the High council. The village itself was located in a valley with a river running through it.
Not a very large river but a good source of water. The river came out of the mountains which surrounded the valley on the north, west, and south side. From the north and the south the mountains stretched east and slowly came together but never actually did leaving a gap where a forest now lay.
The forest covered the gap in the east between the mountains and came up to the hills which started just east of the valley. All the other sides of the valley were hard to get up out of and no one ever tried. It would be pointless since the mountains barricaded the way anyway. \
The east was the only way to get out of the valley because the hills made up a step like ground out of the valley and to the forest which ran to the gap and beyond. That was the only way to the outside world and no one left the village to go beyond, everyone was happy in the village of Koriand.
The whole piece of land looked like it was carved out by some force or other, it was an isolated little village with a river running through the valley on the north side of which lay the village.
Going south over the bridge, which too was a work beyond imagination were the fields on which grew the wheat, barley and grape vines needed to support the village. Nothing could have been better. Roland thought.
There ran a path through the forest and through the gap and over from where came merchants, jesters, knights people with bright clothes or people with dull clothes.
Some of the clothes styles were unlike any Roland has ever seen before with women showing a little more bare skin than Roland thought was appropriate. All of them came in time for the Mid-Summer Feast, how they knew about the Feast Roland never did know.
The people always brought something to show or tell. Either it was some kind of sword trick, maybe a spinning trick that a jester would perform or a storyteller would tell of all the far off places, the different people the myths and legends most of which sounded too good to be true!
By this time Roland had reached the hills on the east side of the valley. He had told Elaynor and Brandwin to meet him here in the morning.
Sure enough as he turned around to look at the village he saw Elaynor and Brandwin running up towards him, their faces red from the running. Elaynor beautiful as ever and Brandwin this time did not look like the world was falling around his shoulders.  Cant wait til the Feast this afternoon starts Brandwin said in an exhausted tone as he ran up to where Roland was standing.
Elaynor now standing next to Brandwin said excitedly Yeah, Me too, think of all the people were going to meet, all the stories of the gods and goddesses we will hear You mean all the guys you will see Elaynor said Brandwin jokingly! Yeah but remember we still need to bring in a whole bunch of wood, enough to make that huge bonfire we always make. It should take us way out of this morning. If we start now we should be done bringing all the wood to the site.? Roland said while tapping his foot impatiently.
Well you are right about that, why does the council always gotta give us the hard job? groaned Brandwin. That was good ol Brandwin just there  thought Roland smiling to himself!
Cmon cheer up Bran at least we can look forward to the feast while going away at it! Plus its part of our job as everyone else has a job too! said Elaynor already starting to walk towards the forest.
She was one of those women who never complained about what the village High council said. The villages High council was the head of the village, similar to that of a mayor of a village. The leader of the High council was Rolands dad Borland. The High council was there for as long as anyone could remember. It was just there to oversee the wellness of the village.
The High councils job was to make sure the kids went to school from when they were 6 years old and could be released from school at the age of eighteen.
Brandwin of course was done with school but Roland and Elaynor still had one more year of school left. The High council also oversaw that everyone had a job to do every person in the community had to do some kind of work to help the village.
Even the oldest of the village worked as librarians and teachers in the school and the Grand library. Brandwin was the son of the blacksmith Drakewin they both lived in the Smithy of the village.
Thats what made Brandwin such a muscular and broad shouldered man, all the hours of working on the bellows, the pounding of the hammer and the shaping.
Elaynor on the other hand was the daughter of the Healers family. Her mother, Belaynor was just like the daughter if not more beautiful. Her father, Vahn was a good looking man. He always wore a smile, either it be a bad situation or a good one, this helped him raise the mood of the sick he was tending to! Mother and daughters beauty helped too when they were taking care of the sick.
Everyone trusted the Herdin family which was Elaynor, her mother Belaynor and her father Vahn!
The three friends were now walking along the path which led to the forest and through it. They carried baskets on their backs into which they could put the firewood into.
They walked along in silence, Elaynor, beside Roland was walking in a spring like manner. The sound of this quiet morning is soothing, maybe a little too quiet thought Roland but that thought went away as soon as it came.
When they reached the forest Roland noticed some animal tracks that he had never seen before. He forgot about that too because they had now stepped into the forest and Elaynor said  Cmon lets get started the faster we finish the more fun we can have!
They went deeper into the forest and for some reason they did not see any good sized branches to break into good firewood, all that was around was twigs and brush.
Hmm, thats unusual, most of the time our baskets our filled to the top and we can hardly carry any more by the time we get this deep into the forest Brandwin said wonderingly.
They kept walking looking around, glancing here and there trying to find some good fire wood. Then off to the side Roland saw a pile of branches.
Thats weird, its like someone had gathered wood for us into a pile thought Roland. Hey Bran and Elaynor, theres a good sized pile of wood just waiting for us to pick it up! said Roland pointing in the direction of the pile of wood.
They went to the pile and filled up each others baskets to the brims, and after grabbing a bunch in their arms there was still wood enough to get as much as they just did maybe two times! Isnt it awfully quiet here? asked Elaynor. Now that you mention it, I cant hear the usual birds chirping, the insects buzzing Replied Brandwin.
As he said that a screech arose from the direction of the village and Roland could just make out a large black bird fly away from where the screech came from.
He looked up and thought  Its way past noon, this took more time than I thought he then said Cmon lets go get this over with and started walking faster Brandwin and Elaynor stayed a little behind but close enough for him to see them, he could hear the singing and the laughter rising from the valley now, the tree line was just ahead.
As he came out he got the shock of his life. He quickly motioned his friends to come over in silence to where he was just at the edge of the forest. There at the bottom of the hills behind a bunch of shrubs stood an army of black men.
They were all black with only their swords, daggers, scimitars and maces showing silver. Their faces were covered in hoods and black veils covered their mouths. There were four groups of these men, one hundred in each probably thought Roland.
And those four men, one at each group on those wolf like things are probably the commanders He thought. Do the villagers know those men are there? asked Elaynor.  Probably not since they are all laughing and singing down there, except somehow those black things didnt get noticed somehow yet answered Brandwin.
Well why would anyone notice, they wouldnt go looking for us since the bonfire is supposed to be about where those black beings are now, and the bonfire only happens late at night. replied Roland. Shouldnt we do something like tell somebody? asked Elaynor.
Well what do you expect those things to just let us marching through the middle of them? And its not like we can do anything, we dont even have weapons with us other than these sticks. replied Roland. So what do we do? said Brandwin.
We wait and see what happens Roland answered. They sat the rest of the day and most of the evening in silence. Roland studied the army from where he sat quietly, the laughter and singing was still coming up and it was already becoming dark so only his young eyes let him still see the army.
He looked over to where Elaynor was sitting against a tree stump, cradling her head in her arms. Probably going over all this in her head, cant blame her, shes not supposed to be used to seeing this kind of stuff. Neither am I but I am the son of the Leader of the High council, bound to be the leader myself someday so I cant just break down thought Roland.
He then looked over to where Brandwin was leaning against a rather large tree his eyes closed. Probably sleeping like he always does when he doesnt want to think about something Roland thought.
He then went back to studying the army. In the past few hours he had noticed a different shaped. A black shape but this one revealing a middle aged man with a hard face and reddish eyes.
He sat on the same type of wolf like thing as the four commanders sat on but his was at least three times larger than theirs. He seemed to be the leader of this army since he went back and forth between the four commanders conversing.
Then the leader started moving forward towards the brush and motioned the others to follow. Roland called to Brandwin and Elaynor in an urgent hushed tone, when they came over to where he sat he told them what was happening. As he looked back they had started creeping forward like a cat would on a mouse.
The Shadow had Arisen.

Well I wouldn't say it was unclear as much as I would say a bit hard to follow. Simply because the narrative seems to jump between simply giving facts and Roland thinking or seeing something. If you know what I mean.
When you went on to describe his friends, which you did a good job of by the way with lots of good imagery, at the end you popped in a thought "Is she something like those men of the far off places described as a Goddess? like all the description that was displayed earlier had actually been what he was thinking. We need to see that while he was waiting at that hill to go toward the forest he thought about his friends. Or else the thought just comes out of nowhere. You do this once or twice so look for that when you come back to edit the story.
Also his thoughts should be sperated from normal dialogue format. Make it italicised or put a ' instead of " around his thoughts.
All in all this was a good chapter, and I belive that whatever you think you left out is good to leave for later. This introduces the characters nicely and sets up the town and ideas of the town and their way of life so well and it ends with a great rising action for the scene. Well done.
