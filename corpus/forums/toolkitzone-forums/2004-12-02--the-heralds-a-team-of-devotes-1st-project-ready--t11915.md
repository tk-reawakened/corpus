what you do is you put down a coin (from -9 to 9, including 0). the goal is for you to put down a coin that adds up to [30]. So say you put down a 9, and i put down a 9, and then ross puts down a 9...after the first round we will have reached 27 of 30; so, if you put down a 3, you'd reach 30
now, there's the 0 rule...as long as i have a 0 i can put it down (30+0=30), so i'd reach the 30 mark...now ross does the same, and you have a 0 so you do too. Now, since 30 has been reached and we can't add to the goal...however, we can -'s.
