### machsprinter · 2004-12-03 00:48 · #B133892

no, you draw just as badly.
I can see you're gonig for a cartoon look. First bit of advice, don't start off drawing cartoons...until you know what a person looks like, you can't distort.
Seriously, I say this WAY too much. People have to listen..."Before distorting a human you have to know what they look like...the actual curves of the body, not just what you think they look like. Go out and observe some people; take a number of sketches, sketching only what you see, not what you think exists or what you think something should look like. Only after you can draw a human [somewhat] realistic can you distort their body in such a way that it becomes a well-drawn cartoon."
