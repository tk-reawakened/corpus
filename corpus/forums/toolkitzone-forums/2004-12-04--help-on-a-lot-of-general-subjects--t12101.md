### Akura_TheDragonBlader · 2004-12-04 03:05 · #B134386

**GreKaosAngel wrote:**

> 1. Our games can be distributed non-commercially either finished or a demo correct?
>

With TK3, games can be distributed commercially if you want, provided that all the content and resources are legal(you originally made all music and graphics and such).

**GreKaosAngel wrote:**

> 2. Is it possible to either upload or make it so that "somebody's" game can be played for non-commercially, online?
>

As in, distribute someone else's game? Contact the person who created the game.

**GreKaosAngel wrote:**

> 3. What can't we do as a game idea.
>

There isn't much in the way of limitations. Just don't try 3D or MMORPGs, since there isn't a real support for them in the engine.
