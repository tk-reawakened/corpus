# Boktai Solar Sensor: Gimmick or Gaming Revolution?

### Slasher · 2004-12-05 23:35 · #B135275

I'm sure everyone has heard of the game named Bokura No Taiyou (Boktai) that came out for the GBA. It was supposed to be a game that would revolutionize the market using this nifty little mechanism that would sense actual sunlight and use it within the game.
There were mixed feelings about the game from players. Some people said that the gameplay was great, and was only furthered by the addition of the solar sensor. Others said that the sensor not only made the game inaccessible to many people, but could actually be a lead cause for cancer in little children (not sure why little kids are playing a game about a vampire slayer...). And some said it flat-out stank, with or without the solar sensor.
Now, my question to you is: Do you think the addition of the solar sensor was only a cheap, ill-used idea to earn more money from people who wanted any sort of gaming that was away from the norm, or something that could possibly be the most original and well-thought out game system ever --- not just from Konami, but for Gaming Companies all the world over?
