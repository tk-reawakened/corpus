# Heraldry:Freija Music

### khin · 2004-12-05 05:57 · #B134884

This file contains a couple of soundtracks for the game. Although we already have a number of available music, the others are still subject to final editing, and most of them were already released during the alpha demo. Comments and suggestions are most welcome.
<http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/expo_bgm.zip>
The files are in midi, though I personally would want it in mp3. If someone knows a link to a midi-to-mp3 converter (free) then please do give me a link, I'll thank you for that and credits will go to you on the game dedication.
The first file is entitled Silent Hills. It was composed with the tranquil state in mind. It's not related in any way with the horror game Silent Hill.
The other one is entitled Perils of Imperial Woods and will be aptly used in battle systems on specific area. I hope it's pumped up enough.
Again, comments are most welcome. Any dissatisfying tone? Or perhaps wrong tempo?
Both music were composed and arranged by yours truly.

### khin · 2004-12-05 14:04 · #B135054

gee, thanks Smile
I look up to you as the Indy of music department Wink
Well I use two software, SoundForge 6 and Sibelius 2. I find Sibelius easier to compose with because you are composing from real manuscripts and you get to lay down notes and other musical notations on a bar...I'm not too technical with music though as I have not taken any music related courses, so I'm fortunate enough that I know what a G-clef is and a fermata..but as for the others, well I don't know them Sad
SoundForge is good for mixing existing music...which I don't do because I prefer starting from scratch.
Unfortunately Sibelius lets you save them only as MIDI file..although SoundForge has that ability to export to mp3's it comes with a limited feature..and only a maximum of 10 exports and it'll no longer be useable(we have an unregistered version an dI can't find a serial number anywhere).
My soundcard is an onboard Avance '97 from Roland Inc.
