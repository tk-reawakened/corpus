### eternal gladiator · 2004-12-05 03:09 · #B134805

basically its gonna be like this game but a few improvised rules will be added to make it able to be played <http://www.cartoonnetwork.com/games/action/toonami/igpx/index.html>
I need a graphics desighner (preferably rossL. or khin) to make the maps and portraits
I will enter in the improvised rules in a little while
BTW I know this may just become a bad attempt at another systematic RP but oh well
EDIT: here is the complete registration info. You need to fill out
Name: choose a character name
Armor type: choose either tank, attacker, light, scout, or balanced this is explained on 4.
Team name: choose the team you are going to be in
Special move: choose 1 of the team sazuka moves (or weapon in the katanas case) more shall be added later but you cant master them all
History: this isnt mandatory but we would like a short history of your char.
*Team color: choose the color your team suits are going to be
*starting artillery: choose the weapons (some weapons cant be chosen) your team has (you can get your first 5 free then you have to pay)
*starting turrets: basically like above except you only get 3 free
And here Are a few I shall enter them as I go along (I'm writing in word first so...)
1. You make your own team and 4 players can join in thus allowing 5 people to be in 1 team (leader and 4 other guys)
2. For a while when we don't have many weapons the players shall propose weapons
3. each player shall have a base other players have the option of storming the base storming a base will be like the guided tutorial you go through a long hallway or into a wide room but it will have walls and guard turrets (for a while players shall also be able to propose turrets as well)
4. Tank has a high HP and DEF. But little action points
Attacker can Carry 5 weapons and has a high atk. But low def. And low action points
Light has most low stats however it does hit more often than other suites and has high action points
Scouts were not meant for fighting they have all low stats and can only carry 1 weapon and dont hit often but they have the most, by far, action points
Balanced suites are even in all aspects
Referee is used only by me it is restricted from actual battle its weapons are only used when absolutely necessary it has max all attributes learns all special abilities it is basically a law enforcer
5. The info. Marked with a * can only be entered by team leaders
6. I know it isnt like the show but here is the story line
We (humans) have not made touch with other (intelligent) life yet but we just made it to a new planet the gravity makes us unable to play normal sports (baseball, soccer, basketball, ect.) however we are able to play the newest created sport, IGPX, (if anyone knows what its really called please tell) and the suites were used for exploration of the planet and after a few cities were created also for playing IGPX so then we had entertainers and workers in one package however since they were altered to handle the harsh creatures of this planet they did have the possibility of killing other guys so referees got a bigger role in the game
7. You cant really die you just get launched out of your suite when health gos to 0 but your suite does get beat-up so it does lose strength, speed, max. health, ect. However if you are in an enemy base or exploring and get down to 0 your suite is left behind and you have to wait till your team can buy a new suite
8. You may only make 1 character
9. You may not choose to be a ref.
10. You may go exploring for more money (via selling treasure), supplies, and information on the planet
11. if your suite gets beat up enough that its not usable anymore a game mod (me or a major helper) shall inform you and you must get it repaired you can either take it to a repair man or repair it yourself with supplys
More to come
