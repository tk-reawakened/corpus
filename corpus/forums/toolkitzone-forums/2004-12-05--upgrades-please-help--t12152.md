### Spyder · 2004-12-06 08:38 · #B135465

**Randy wrote:**

> With Windows XP, you will have to reinstall your OS with a motherboard and CPU upgrade...It's just unavoidable unfortunately.
>

Really? Why? Shouldn't it just redetect the new motherboard and processor and ask to install new drivers for it? Or are things different (and thus, more difficult) with a legal version of XP.
Ivan, I doubt your long boot times are being caused mainly by your hard drive. Most computers purchased from a retailer have 5400rpm drives in them. The problem probably exists because you've got a lot of stuff loading at boot. Stuff you may not even know about. Simply re-formatting should clear all of that up, or if you don't want to re-format, deleting everything in the Start-->Programs-->Startup folder should help. You can also go to Start-->Run and type in msconfig. Then go to the last tab and uncheck anything you don't need to run at boot. Nothing there should be critical to running your computer, and if it is, you can always reboot in safe mode and check it back.
