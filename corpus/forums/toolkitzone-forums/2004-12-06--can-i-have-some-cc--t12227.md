### angelis · 2004-12-07 01:04 · #B135771

i have to agree that the technique is good. since you asked to critique on the technique and not on the art itself Razz, here it goes:
i think the part you did "wrong" was in the first and second steps.
in the first step, you framed the character rather well, but you could have made the frame more "detailed" (i.e. frame the joints, use another shape to frame the arms, like a cylinder maybe, and you can use a cone under the circle for the frame of the head). if you intend your character to have a rather large head (with respect to his body), i can understand. but if you took proportions into consideration, you would notice that the frame you used in the head was too big for the body frame. also, you placed the eyes too close to each other. i see that you're going for a 3/4 view of the face. considering the angle, the left eye (his right) should be smaller. the frame you used for the neck was also a bit off. i also noticed that you included the hair in your frame, that would explain why the hat was off.
in the second step, you draw the character over the the frame. if you have had a more proportionate and quite detailed frame, you would have come up with a better sketch. the only thing i've noticed is that the hair didn't go beyond the edge of the circle in your frame. the circle should frame the character's head. in this case, his hair would be short. it doesn't look short, but basing it from your frame, it is.
your shading is good, but if had more detail, you can improve the shading as well.
i hope that helped.Very Happy please post it when you decide to make revisions.
