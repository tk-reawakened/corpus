### zyrshnikashnu · 2004-12-06 20:54 · #B135647

It's a long shot, but try this:
1. Go to the control panel and open "Sounds and Audio Devices."
2. In the "Volume" tab, under "Speaker Settings," click "Advanced."
3. Select "Moniter Stereo Speakers."
Then again, this may only exist on Windows XP Home, so I really can't guaruntee that this will even be available to you. Confused
