Here is a small exerpt from the pages of " Deceit and Conquest: Volume 1 - Seor "
...........From "Prologue: An End to Elder Things".................

**Quote:**

> Geran carefully steps up the hill looking out towards the men. His eyes are fixed iron and his face is characteristically blank. His brow is meshed into a permanent scowl. His flowing green cap appears announcing the presence of a General on the field and inspiring a sudden gasp in the hearts of many of these men now searching his profile with their eyes. His legs move confidently as he slowly walks towards the edge of the high cliff and stops just beside his old friend.
> For a moment, they both stand in a silent affirmation of each others presence Kels eyes open shyly as the two begin staring outward into the vast waters. A calm seems to comfort them both now that they are once again united. Even without words, these two great warriors feel a sentiment of the same accord and a peace of the same kind. The soldiers and officers look on almost without belief that such a man exists but for Kel it is only his friend by his side that old comrade from days long passed of battles fought yet not forgotten.
> His head turns toward Geran as a slight smile creases his aged face. Geran stares emptily ahead as Ruset begins to speak, So here we are
> Hal nods a little as he replies, Here we are
> Kel smiles more widely as his face turns forward once again. He says in a confident tone, All those years of fighting in The Field of Ashes The losses The victories within miles of the ocean yet no sea in sight makes me realize how far weve come.
> Hal remains silent as Kel continues, I wonder if those hard years might have been easier with such a morning view referring to the ocean before them.
> Hals eyes loosen as he speaks, Perhaps Who can say what might have beenand as we said leaving room open for a comment from his compatriot.
> Kel smiles as he politely finishes the thought.
> Here we are.
> Kel and Hal seem to enjoy a moment of reflection that lingers on for only a fraction of the time it should until at last a tone of tension arises. The echoes of distant explosions ring out once more reminding these men of the task at hand. Kels eyes tighten as his mind shifts into his natural mode of battle strategist. The quiet is now gone from his thoughts as his brain races with the complicated elements of warfare. Outside of thought, there is a still silence that remains for a few seconds and then is abruptly interrupted by Gerans aged voice, Are the Battalions aligned?
> Ruset carefully replies, Ive conglomerated all of First, Second and Third Corp. The horses really would have only slowed us down here. Good decision leaving them behind.
> What about Archery and Support? Hal lowly asks.
> Kel continues quickly, Were in the process of re-outfitting Corp 5 with provisions and tallying out the remainder to the troops. Archery is still setting up their formations but should be conglomerated in the next few minutes.
> Hal nods as he says with a slight sigh, Youve been busy.
> Kel smiles a little as he responds, Well not everyone has the time to listen to music by the sea. Really Hal You should put that old thing away somewhere.
> Hal turns toward Kel and grins a little saying, Im surprised you still remember.
> Kel laughs a little as he shakes his head, No youre not. Every time we stopped moving youd pull that trinket out and wander off somewhere lost in whatever song that thing plays besides your men told me what you were doing down there.
> Hals eyes turn down for a moment and then back up towards Kel. Their faces become somber once more as they pause for an instant then The General speaks in a low tone, Come on Kel Lets prepare the briefing.
> Kel turns toward the troops behind them, looking out into the horizon. Hal looks across the gathered troops with interest as Kel motions for one of the officers to come towards him.
> Zemal and The Captain are conversing as they see the Commanders signal. Ill go this time, The Captain quickly says. Zemal smiles a little as he speaks, Try not to fall down if The General speaks to ya. He laughs a little as The Captain walks away with a long stare towards Hal and Kel. Zemal steps over to the other officers and begins speaking lightly, Hes quite a character.
> One of the older officers scoffs as he replies, Hes quite a character? Ha!
> What is that supposed to mean? Zemal inquires quickly.
> Another of the officers looks at Zemal and smirks a little, Hey Kid we all know youre supposed to be some kind of wonder boy or at least you were at the academy but do us all a favor and keep your opinions about your captain to yourself.
> Zemal stares back at the officer with an unexpected confusion. His eyes seem to tighten as he stands solidly glaring at the officer. Made a little uncomfortable by this, the officer responds, Hey What?
> My Captain? Zemal asks with a whisper.
> Zemal looks concerned and quickly turns around towards Kel and Hal as he watches The Captain approach them. Suddenly Zemal sprints off towards them. The officers look on in confusion as Zemal dashes away. The soldiers are also surprised by this sudden outburst and quickly begin rising up. One of them shouts Gerans name just as Zemal is about to reach them.
> As Lieutenant Zemals name echoes through the air, Hal and Kel watch on with a curious look on their faces. The shout calls the attention of The Captain who quickly turns to see Zemal running at full stride towards him. As he turns, The Captain reveals a long silver dagger in his hand. Seeing this, Kel quickly steps back losing his footing at the edge of the cliff. Hal reaches out with a jerk and pulls Kel back onto the rocks with a quick motion and then using that momentum, spins around.
> As Hal turns, The Captain makes a sudden dash towards him and flies through Hals long cape. The cape spreads out obscuring all vision for a single instant and when it flows back the men are witnesses to an amazing sight. Hal is standing before them majestically with his famous platinum long sword sternly gripped in his hand. His cape is resting behind him and to his side the body of The Captain lay flat on the ground.
>
