### RDemusX · 2004-12-07 00:27 · #B135755

These are rendered in Bryce 5.
To get the illusion of 3D in TK you must use background pics as boards rather than tiles. I render the scene then compress the image. Next just set it as the bkg pic in the board editor, set the tile properties and wala ... 3D illusion.
It is actually alot more involved then that but you will see in two days.
