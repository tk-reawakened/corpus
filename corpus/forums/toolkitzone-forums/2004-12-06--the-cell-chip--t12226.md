### Michael J. Hill · 2004-12-07 01:20 · #B135786

No, cell technology is something that's going to be introduced with the PS3, but is planned to be utilized in many things. It allows multiple components to share unused processor time with each other, so a toaster with the chip (which will have them at some point) can share it's processing capability with a regrigerator 10 ft. away. You may laugh at the examples i chose, but this is truely what IBM believes will become of cell processing technology.
