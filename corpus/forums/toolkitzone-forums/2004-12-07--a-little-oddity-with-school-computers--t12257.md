### Nih · 2004-12-09 03:02 · #B136811

Hmm... In my computer science class, we have a similar thing. Every person uses their account to log into the machine and they can access their "H:" drive from anywhere on the network. The "H:" drive is basically your personal folder to keep whatever crap you want. Check to see if you have those at your school. However, at my school, your custom settings are system-specific and are not restored to an original setting when you log on.
