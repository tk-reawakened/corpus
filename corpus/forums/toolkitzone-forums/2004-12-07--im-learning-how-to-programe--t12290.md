### Levi · 2004-12-07 21:44 · #B136155

Indeed. I recommend, Basic, Pascal, Python, as the languages you should start off with. You won't have to of course but they are some of the easiest and they quickly and easily teach you many of programming's basics. Things that can be related to most any language. Sometimes only syntax(the way things are worded or put together) changes, the basic ideas are still the exact same way. Here' an example of basic If statements and then C++ if statements. You'll notice the similarity.
If someVariable = 5 then
Print "You entered 5"
End if
if(someVariable==5)
{
std::cout << "You entered 5"
}
That C++ isn't exact but you get the picture. Very similar and once you understand the language, very easy to understand.
