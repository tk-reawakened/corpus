# Mercy for the mercyless

### andrew133 · 2004-12-07 17:29 · #B136037

can you read over it and tell me what you think its not realy gripping reading but its more of telling programmers the story
Mercy for the Merciless
You are a mercenary paid along with hundreds of others to capture a Tui castle. You start off attacking the gate lots of people die then you break through kill the archers that are on the battlements shooting at your forces that are going through the gate. You finish it then go for the throne room your orders are to kill Tui the entire army attacks at once and almost instantly you are all defeated by Tui. You lay on the floor unable to get up all the other solders are dead. People come in and take the dead bodies away and take you with them thinking you are dead they load you on a cart and go a few miles out of town and dump the bodies an leave. You wait hiding amongst the bodies and when your are certain that you are alone you emerge and make your way back to the town on foot.
You return to the clients H.Q and explain to him what has happened he is furious and says you should have given your life for the mission you try to explain what happened but he does not listen and sends you away without payment be gone you coward he screams at you. You leave and wander several miles again on foot until you reach a house in the desert a storm approaches so you seek shelter in the hut. You knock on the door and a old short man comes to greet you come in you must be tiered after the journey he says in a kind of mystic voice, he continues your journey has just begun young one you look puzzled at him he offers to train you in the art of ko-jan you accept he trains you knight and day for the next 2 years and finally he says you are ready I have given you the knowledge but it is up to you how you use it you exit his house.
You return to Tui castle and immediately you are attacked by soldiers you kill all of them without too much difficulty then you break down the doors of the throne room die you traitor Tui shouts, You after a long fight are victorious you pick up Tui and he screams for mercy then you stop and drop him to the ground then you leave the castle.
You walk to the clients H.Q the same client that had infuriated you and when you go in he stands and you decapitate him with no hesitation and leave. You return to the mans house the man who had taught you all that you know wise choice grasshopper you bow.
The end?
