Fallen Empire story:
In the year 1236 the great empire of Oshalanis ruled over most of the world but corrupted leaders brought on its ruin. Taxes increased heavily and the empire began to have uprisings. Most were put down but one held on. With help from an unknown faction the Kre'ishen rebels overthrew the empire. But in the years to follow they failed to set up a strong government. Plagued by starvation and monsters the new empire fell apart into the current 5 territories. A hero was believed to one day come and unite the land but so far that is just a myth. --Danis 1839
The towers in the picture are the remnant of the capital city of Oshalanis.
