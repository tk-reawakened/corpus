### Slasher · 2004-12-08 20:03 · #B136552

FF2 did come out on the NES --- but it wasn't released in America. I would say that you probably got the GBA Version, but since you mentioned Magnetism Cave, that's actually in FF4, which was released as FF2 here in America. Don't ask.
In any case, you're probably referring to the "Dark Elf". I don't recall him doing 9999, but he IS supossed to kill you. Don't shut off afterwards.
