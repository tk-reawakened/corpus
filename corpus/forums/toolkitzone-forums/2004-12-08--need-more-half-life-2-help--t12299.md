### Colosseus · 2004-12-09 13:56 · #B136965

**Spyder wrote:**

> That room seems to have no purpose. There is no exit, and no items other than some health or something.
>

That room has the very purpose you're looking for to get to that ledge. On the screenshot, you're looking straight at a valve, down with the pipes. Jump from pipe to pipe until you're standing in front of that valve. Turn it, and the water will rise. No, go back into the room with the barrels, and dive into the water. Swim through the pipe which is now filled with water, and get up on the other side. Now, the water should be high enough for you to reach the ledge somehow.
