# Alpha Test of Tactical Battle

### khin · 2004-12-09 05:11 · #B136880

Okay, let's start testing the concept here. I am very open to suggestions.
Tactical battles occur between players only. Number of players may vary. Whoever is in the same block in the worldmap may be involved in tactical battles. Those who are not in the said block may not interfere at any time.
Tactical Battles work like chess, or FFTactics. We give you grids. Actually the real plan is to make an image of a map and put your sprites in them. For now only a couple of sprites are done(Taknu and Morpheus) so bear with me for a while.
Now choose your location. There are grid labels to help you decide. Also, the sprites are in 32x64, so when placing sprites, reference from the sprite's feet, not the head. The grid is 32x32.
Movement range of your characters are determined by the MOV attribute(Blue Boxes). Attack range by your weapons(Red Boxes). Spell range will be explained later. The order of turns are sequential, and determined by the AT attribute. In case players have similar AT, they just have to agree who goes first, or we could conduct a bidding. The highest bidder shall go first and so on.
![](http://www.icestormcity.com/piarts/heraldry/GD/BSSnap1.png)
Available actions are:
Arrow Move(specify destination block)
Arrow Attack(specify destination opponent)
Arrow Defend
Arrow Cast Spell(specify destination opponent)
Arrow Use Item(specify receipient)
Arrow Withdraw(100% success)
Order of Game
Arrow Morpheus
Arrow Taknu
Arrow Boaz
In case a players makes invalid moves, it shall be edited and be Deleted. Please compute ahead your character's abilities and limitations to avoid conflicts.
Players in a Tactical Battle cannot perform other activities such as research, agriculture, etc. The battle must be finished before proceeding.
Ok, so start laying down and I'll update the image. It may be a bit of discomfort, but i'll keep updating only the first post, so you gotta keep tracking back.
I'll only display HP and MP. For other stats refer to the character sheet.

**Quote:**

> Morpheus HP: 15/15 -- MP: 25/25
> Taknu HP: 25/25 -- MP: 25/25
> Boaz HP: 10/10 -- MP: 10/10
>

If a player dies, it'll be like he's on HOLD. We'll change his status to KO'd.
Medic types always has the +50% bonus when healing; whether it be items or magic.
Researchers cannot fight here.
