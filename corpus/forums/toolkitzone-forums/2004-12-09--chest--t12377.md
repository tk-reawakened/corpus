### Brad · 2004-12-14 21:23 · #B139628

Uh.... Okay make a program like this:

**Code:**

```
***If you want to get money***

#mwin(" Bastard recieved 100gp!")

#wait(@$)

#givegp(100)

***If you want to give player an item***

#mwin(" Bastard recieved a , hm... items... Ah! A poop sword!")***

#wait(@$)

#giveitem("poopsword.itm")**name of item

Then go to the main board editor, make two tiles that look like a chest opened, and one closed. Put the open chest on the board. Then place the program on the closed chest, go to the program editor, and make the graphics of the program a closed chest. Then, it should work.
```
