# My first computer-made original graphics.

### Chamgorie Chanbear · 2004-12-09 05:54 · #B136912

These are two screens from the first and second day of design for my new project, How the Church Stole Christmas. I'm rushing because I have way too little time (I want to get done by Christmas Eve), so they're not the greatest quality and my game won't be either. But I want to know what you think about my tiles, all of which are original except for the sprite and grass tile.
![](http://www.geocities.com/pkmndrgnbll/SS1.gif)
My brother did the pews for me. I intended to just use his image as reference to make my own, but I am low on time. This is the Church where the villains came from.
![](http://www.geocities.com/pkmndrgnbll/SS2.gif)
Any Pokémon fan will notice something about the house. It was based on the houses from Pokémon Fire Red and Leaf Green in terms of shape. I have yet to see any other games use this style.
