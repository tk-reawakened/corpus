### zyrshnikashnu · 2004-12-10 21:08 · #B137653

More clearly, open the tile bitmap editor and create the image that you used for your enemies in TK2. Then save the bitmap and open the animation editor. Place your tile bitmap in an animation frame, then put that animation in the enemy's graphics.
