### seveneternal · 2004-12-10 01:28 · #B137227

Ooook...
First of all, this isn't really game material. Unless you like a crappy flash game, no.
Before even attempting something like this you need to practice photoshopping itself. (Or whatever you're using)
Assuming you used PS, first of all, you relied too much on Dodge/Burn. Try using actual colors, or adjusting highlight/midtone/shadows to the approporiate situation.
Your outlines... let's see... suck? It's not constant, and looks like an amateur drew it with hardedge brushtool. But looking at the picture, guess that's quite fitting. This time, make a basic outline with a thinner brush, and keep perspectives and location in mind.
You won't really learn by me telling you whatever. You need serious improvement. Unless you're 6 years old, I'm not really impressed. You should be able to get much more done using backgrounds. Background are lots of work, but it pays off. Only if you do it right.
Try looking at Khin's or Juank corp's screens and try to make similar duplicate. If you're smart, you'll figure the basic techniques from that practice. I'm sure they won't hunt you down for using their screens as reference.
