### Xavier · 2004-12-10 13:43 · #B137544

I'm not definately going to change, but since you all want to know what IPB offers over PHPBB from my perspective as a webmaster then here you go...
Firstly, if the forum software were to change - you'd wouldn't lose a thing. All private messages, posts, users, topics, forums would be kept exactly as they are now. I would also ensure we have a skin that matches to the current one as best possible in terms of color scheme.
As for features, from a moderators point of view it is far, far easier to manage such as inline moderation and topic multi-moderation . It also has a warning/ban system far superior. I can also track moderator actions to ensure everything is being done as it should.
From my point of view, it is far easier to integrate and use with the site (you don't know how messy PHPBB is from the daa storage perspective, see user groups) and supplies some amazing add-ons and upgrades. Sub-forums, categories, et al are simple to manage (right now I have to use a weird PHPBB hack).
Additionally, it also has built in chat room capabilities (real time chat) that uses your forum (thus TKZ) user account to log in.
What else? Well, users can quote multi-sources without constant copy and pasting, you can ignore/block people's private messages, add multiple attatchments to posts (say if you want to share a tileset), images can be resized to fit the screen and then expanded when you click on them, custom profile fields....
There are also a wealth of add-ons and upgrades such as what OBCT mentioned, an arcade feature - which I have seen and is quite cool. You play various arcad games via the forums and it keeps tracks of community high scores, best player of the day and so forth.
Anyways...
Over the past 10 months I have ran this community, and surely by now you must have realised when I make decisions it's for the greater good (especially when my money is involved Wink).
So rest assured, we will only switch forum software IF it holds significent advantages to both users and moderators, and we have a skin that means it fits in with TKZ just like the current forum.
