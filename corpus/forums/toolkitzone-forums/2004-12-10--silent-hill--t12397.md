# Silent Hill

### Moonews · 2004-12-10 03:28 · #B137360

Based on the best selling survival horror series to date, Silent Hill: The Movie is due for release in 2006. I'm really looking forward to this, as I am a huge fan of the series and also of Roger Avary (director: Killing Zoe, co-writer: Pulp Fiction) who will be doing the screenplay. It's not confirmed if the storyline of the movie will follow that of the games, but I'd much rather see a Jacob's Ladder-esque storyline like in Silent Hill 2 that focuses on the charaters more then anything. The other games were good, but 1 and 3 focused mainly on the story itself and failed to dig deep into the characters as well thought out as they were. 4 wasn't much of anything, an average survival horror.
