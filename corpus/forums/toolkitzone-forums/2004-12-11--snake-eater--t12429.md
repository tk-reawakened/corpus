I just beat the game last night/this morning. Amazing game, though the Volgin was really fucking lame. I still loved the last battle with The Boss and that final showdown with Otocon in the end. The End is such a crazy boss. You've got to track him using all of your equipment in order to find him. I actually managed to crawl up behind him and hold him up, he gave me moss colored camo.
Volgin and Raikov are lovers, the Metal Gear Solid series tends to have a lot of messed up romances. When Volgin found Snake in disguise he was waiting for Raikov in his room, the disguise didn't fool Volgin because Snake isn't as thin as Raikov, and the final give-away was the size of his member. Interesting...
Funny thing though: If you let Raikov escape before you can take his clothes and then call Zero he will scream at you to take him out and hide the body because he can't stand his face. This is a reference the the hate that MGS fans showed for Raiden's look in MGS2, Raikov looks just like him.
