# .Dll

### MrMe · 2004-12-12 01:13 · #B138230

I'm using TK2.2, and I am trying to load in some .dll files(plugins). How can I get them to attach???

### Dont_Think_So · 2004-12-12 01:40 · #B138248

Put them in your plugins folder. You then have three options:
1) Download MultiPlug.dll, put it in your plugins folder. Rename it to TKPlug0.dll
2) Rename all of your plugins to TKPlugX.dll, where X is a number from 0-4.
3) Set up the TK to use Visual Basic plugins, using a TK2 SDK, and follow the instructions from there.
No matter what route you chose above, you do the following:
Open up your main file.
Enable TKPlug0.dll (And all of the other plugins if you chose the second one).
Enjoy.
Oh, and before you ask, there is no way to get rid of the "Disable Plugins" dialog box when you start the game. There are, however, some ways to see whether or not the player disabled them and yell at him accordingly.
