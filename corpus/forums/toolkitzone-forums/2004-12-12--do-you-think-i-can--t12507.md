### Caliber Mengsk · 2004-12-12 20:27 · #B138577

Q: Do you think I can do it?
A: Yes... I think you CAN do it... do I think you will? no. Simply because, the TK3 may be able to do it, probably even half way decently, but you would have to create a specified plugin for your system. Why? because if you do one for general, then it will be slow for certain. If you don't tweek stuff for the systems you would have specifically, then you won't have it running at it's max. It would be quite bothersome and time consuming, and I don't belive many people here at the TK community would work for long periods of time without getting much done.
Q: Can you post a link to a server host/plugin?
A: No... sorry. I don't even know what kind of server you would need. Probably one that you could install your own software, which are normally costly. Now, if you have broadband net, you can set up your PC to be a net server, but you would be opening yourself wide open to hackers and virus' if you didn't plan for them with major firewalls, and virus protection. If you were to set up a pc for it with a broadband connection, I would suggest you get another pc specifially for the server, and use it as the runing server, then network to it to update files and such, as well as use the regular(not server) pc for development.
