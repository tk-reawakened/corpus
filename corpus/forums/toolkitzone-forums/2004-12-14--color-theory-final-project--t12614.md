# Color Theory_final project

### Alex0Xander · 2004-12-14 19:39 · #B139577

This is my final project for Color Theory class. In a group effort of 2, we were to use all we learned in class to draw anything we wanted. Only catch was that it had to be mounted on a circular board and each team member had to fill half of the circle. Me and my partner decided on a Ying-Yang symbol, we also decided to do dragons, cuz dragons are cool. Mine is the Red dragon. My partner was lucky, he got to use the black paper board, it allows for some great use of color pencils. Yes his dragon is twice the size of mine, but as you can see my dragon is brolic! ;]
