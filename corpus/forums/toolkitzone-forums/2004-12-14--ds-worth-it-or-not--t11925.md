### Spyder · 2004-12-15 10:26 · #B140080

**tacofernandez wrote:**

> Now if all these technological enhancements make games so great, why would anyone in their right mind play and highly enjoy this?
>

Because it's addicting. The fact is, if there was a version of the game that used vertex shaders, bump mapping, etc; most of those same people would rather play that one than the original, because it has all the fun and adddicting gameplay, but better graphics. I agree that some game companies try to sell games purely based off graphics, but that doesn't mean we can't have good gameplay, story, and good graphics as well. Believe me, if we were still in the 16-bit era, that doesn't mean that there would be more "quality" games. There would still be a few great games, some good ones, and a whole lot of not so good games; just like there is today.
Think of it like a well written books. A good book will describe locations and objects so well that you can picture them in your mind as if it were right in front of you. Video games don't do this as well, so better graphics are the substitute. If a console/computer can produce better graphics, it has a better chance of immersing you into the game. Good graphics are just as important as any other aspect of the game. Take Morrowind for example. There are a thousand games that are similar to Morrowind, but it's above-par graphics set it appart, and help make it more enjoyable. Better graphics also usually mean that there is more to see, which makes replayability higher.
