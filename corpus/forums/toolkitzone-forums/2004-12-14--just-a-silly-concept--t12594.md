# Just a silly concept

### kahless · 2004-12-14 15:36 · #B139515

Just as a beginning of my story:
There was a raid on a villiage and in a rage, a boy uses magic, which a large empire wants so that it can conquer the rest of the continent. They discover that the magic skills come from a legendary species known as the Amikei, who are revered by the locals as gods.
Officially the Empire wants to prevent the use of Magic against their own people, but unofficially the Empire just wants the power for itself to unite the continent with themselves as head. Under the Emperor, there is an advisor who wants to not only rule the contient as Emperors and Overlords but use that magic power to become 'gods' (or maybe Titans, I'm going for a modern greek like setting, in other words ancient greece w/ modern technology).
Charecters so far
A wannabe Gladiator,
The hero
A prisoner from another kingdom
A white mage
A black mage
Father and son pair who's village was destroyed by the Empire
I dunno, this seems a bit off. Any ideas?

### kahless · 2004-12-15 14:33 · #B140095

First off the anger (thus far) is caused by the actual attack on the village.
The reason for the invasion is that it is part of a larger conflict. I'm thinking something similar to WW1 between two alliances, and the kingdom in the middle being wanted by both sides as a stepping stone to the enemy kingdom.
One thing I'm still working on is why magic should exist at all.
Edit: New Idea -- What if the two sides are actually heirs to the throne of the empire trying to prove their worth by invading kingdom B where the people can use magic. They've learned to use magic -- which is more or less like a psychic power requiring concentration.
I'm also concidering Alliance points. Basicly the party will choose sides based on who they help or hurt.
So using an example:
Say the two kids are George and Jack.
During a quest you have to help one side or the other. Helping George raises your Alliance with George. Helping Jack hurts your alliance with George.
I'm also thinking of a sort of Karma system where stealing from villagers houses and so on will cause villagers to think less of you and therefore maybe not give you items before a major boss fight, or charge more for inns or something.
