# Enemy picture question

can you have a "boss" character that is ....say 155x129 pixels?

### Serge · 2004-12-15 06:15 · #B140036

Take an image of an enemy that is whatever size you want, and then import it in as tiles, through the tile grabber. Then piece it together in the enemy editor.

### rpgmaster24 · 2004-12-15 17:11 · #B140124

In tk3 just click the new animation box, then when looking for the tile size at the bottom, check the "other" box and put x[155] and y[129], then import the picture as part of the animation.
