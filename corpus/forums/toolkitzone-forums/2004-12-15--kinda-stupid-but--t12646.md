# kinda stupid but.........

### Ivan · 2004-12-15 05:09 · #B140015

I just found out something new and very simple this sunday. I went to my friends house and he got a new computer, he set it up and all that but when you would turn it on it would just make a beeping sound 5-6 seconds long 3-4 seconds apart. I really didnt know what to do so i was like "let me take a look inside" so he's like "ok" since he thinks i'm a great computer guy which is not exactly true as proven by spyder and randy, anyway i take the side metal sheet off and just go over the wires going to the motherboard, the hddrive and find everything ok. THen i just for no reason take out the ram because i remember reading in a computer shopper something about cleaning ram, i did this just as a routine if you know what i mean, so i just got a white clean piece of cloth and wiped the ram put it back in, look around more, poked around. Then i turn the computer on and whaddya know it starts. I bet people like spyder and randy know this but people who dont know that if your computer doesnt start,your monitor doesnt even get a sygnal and it is making beeping noises, one of the problems might be that you have to wipe the ram with a piece of clean cloth or a clean paper towel.

### matt72186 · 2004-12-15 05:38 · #B140027

Yeah, and also the best way to fix a computer if you've already checked over everything is by starting with bare components (motherboard+processor, video card (unless on-board), RAM, HD) and checking if it starts up, if so then you can add in a piece at a time until you find the one messing it all up, if it doesn't start to begin with then you may have a more serious problem try switching out HDs or RAM first otherwise your motherboard may be fried.
