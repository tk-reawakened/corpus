### Dont_Think_So · 2004-12-15 02:36 · #B139886

Sort of. I spent a long time trying to figure out the language from trial and error, and here's what I got:

**Code:**

```
PROMPT X

IF X>1

THEN

DISP "YAY!"

STOP

END

DISP "AWW!"
```

Razz
It took me a while to find the > sign, you have to press 2nd, then one of the buttons towards the left will be "Test". It's in there, along with a few others.
Oh, and = is the same as == in a computer language; X=1 won't set X to one, it will return whether or not X==1.
