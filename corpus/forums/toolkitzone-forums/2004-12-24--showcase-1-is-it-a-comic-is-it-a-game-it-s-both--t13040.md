# Showcase #1: Is it a Comic? Is it a Game? It's both!

### matt72186 · 2004-12-24 07:20 · #B145054

For the first RPG Makers Anonymous showcase to be posted on these forums, I present to you all the Comic RPG (for lack of a better title).
Right now it is in it's very early baby-stages, but hopefully this will grow up to be a big badass web game!
Item #1: A template design for the game.
[Link](http://comics.toolkitzone.com/tkcomics/template.html)
I designed this simple html page (as well as a few images) for the game. From this you can somewhat see what the gameplay will be like.
The first thing you should notice is the layout, similiar to popular webcomics, with the comic as the center of attention, and links to first/previous/next/last pages.
Next you can see the main links, titled "Home," "Archive," "Inventory," "Stats," "Clan," and "Forums."
Item #2: Playing the game.
As far as gameplay goes, the idea here is that you are a typical RPG Character complete with a class, a level, experience, and miscellaneous statistics.
The game takes the form of a comic, and the gameplay takes the form of a forum. The forum is like any other forum, in that you can make topics, and replies to that topic, except the only difference is that it shows up as a comic instead of a regular forum post. A topic is a new adventure that you can take your character on, by replying you have put yourself in the adventure and you indirectly can affect it's outcome.
Throughout the game you can earn varous extras, this includes extra items, special moves, gold, experience, extra actions in the forums, and many more.
Item #3: Rewards for the good, and death to the bad.
Every reply you make in an adventure will earn your character some experience. Now, before you spammers start celebrating, every reply that has to be deleted will cause your character to lose 1000 experience or more!
You can earn gold by battling other players, enemies, or NPCs. You can also setup a shop to sell items that you have found or won. The last way to earn gold will be through link-clicks.
Items are randomly scattered all throughout the world. You may find them randomly, or when you battle and win it may be dropped by your opponent. The game is expansive so items will constantly be added!
Item #4: Oh the places you shall go.
Every forum offers different opportunities to go on adventures. So far the forums I have planned are...
General Chat - a forum that doesn't affect your character stats at all, but allows you to chat with other members (and shows up as a comic as it does in gameplay)
Training Grounds - Beginners forum, in here you are safe from being attacked; however, it is only available to players lower than level 5.
The World - The open area for everyone and anyone. This place has the most opportunities for you to go on adventures, as well as the most risks.
Bazaar - An area for players to open up shops (special forum with extra features specifically for shops)
Role-Playing - A role-playing forum where any user can start an adventure (does not affect the game stats, but is just for fun)
More to Come: All this and a bag of radioactive chips.
I have so many ideas for this game I couldn't possibly write it all out on one sitting. I'll be working on this game non-stop until I see some good progress being made, and hopefully soon I can release a BETA test run of it for everyone to check it out. Until then as always any comments/suggestions (based on what you know so far) are very welcome here.
