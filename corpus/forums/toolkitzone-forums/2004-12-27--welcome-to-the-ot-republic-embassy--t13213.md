# Welcome to the OT Republic embassy

### Asimir · 2004-12-27 17:09 · #B146446

This is the embassy for the OT Republic. Hence the forum name.
Visit the [OT Republic](http://www.cywh.com/ot/)
This forum is to talk about the OT Republic, or to talk about the type of topics the OT Republic likes to discuss. This includes everything, including but not limited to, anything.
Unfortunately though, due to TKZ's rules, we have to have some restrictions. Spam is not allowed. Games such as number games, word association games and insult the previous poster games aren't allowed here, but they are fully welcome at the [OT Republic](http://www.cywh.com/ot/). They may even get their own forums there if you ask nicely enough.
However, feel free to be as stupid as you like, within TKZ's rules. This is a forum for stupidity, not for spam.
Remember, visit the [OT Republic](http://www.cywh.com/ot/).
We're threaded!
