**Fight Club| Co-op Aetherian Steel [rules evalu.]**

![](http://img.photobucket.com/albums/v661/Alex0Xander/club.png)
These are the rules and regulations of the Fight Club.
1. There are 3 modes of play. These are Co-op story, 1 on 1 match, and Deathmatch.
2. As long as the original creator of the fight has no objections, you may join any fight.
3. You must follow the order of the fight. Pages must be submitted in the order that you joined the fight. i.e
*alex0xander creates fight.*
||Marc joins fight|
||Thany joins fight||
*fight commences*
[alex0xander post first 3 pages]
||Marz joins fight||
[marz posts 3 pages]
[marz gets stabbed for not following the order]
*it was marcs turn*
4. You are not to join a fight then dissapear for more than a week. After a week you can be skipped (up the the participants).
5. You can post a maximum of 3 pages at a time. It would probably be a disadvantage to you to post just 1.
6. No "HAHahaha you deaded!" pages. If you automatically kill off your opponent before atleast 9 pages have been reached, you automatically lose.
._1 on 1_.
Challenge someone (from TKZ or other) to a 1 on 1 battle. Be sure to PM who you want to challenge and let him/her know when the battle will begin and who goes first.
._Co-op_.
Invite someone (as many as you want) to cooperating in an adventure.
It can be a story, or a battle, or anything on your mind.
._Deathmatch_.
Kind of explains itself. As many members as possible may join in a fight, til the last man is standing.
Heres an example of a Fight Club:
<http://www.polykarbonbbs.com/showthread.php?t=593>
Remember, be respectful and keep an open mind. Have FUN.
