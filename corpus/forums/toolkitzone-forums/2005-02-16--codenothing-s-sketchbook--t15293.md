### codenothing · 2005-02-18 03:56 · #B168621

thanks, but to be honest i am not much of a digital color guy. I can mix paint prity good, and make decisions in the physical world, but for some reason my brain still doesnt grasp good digital color mixing. Most of my sketches have "Borrowed" color schemes from other paintings or reference photos.
Like my "Romance" sketch. I took a reference picture of myself with a digital camera, and then i did a pencil sketch of the image. After i scanned the sketch i put it next to the actual photograph and used the eye droper tool to select the correct color for the correct part of the face.
its kind of like cheating, but im learning alot by doing it and hopefully I will get to the point where i wont have to do that anymore. Or at least not rely on it so much.
