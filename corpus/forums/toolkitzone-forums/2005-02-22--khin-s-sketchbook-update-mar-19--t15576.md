# Khin's Sketchbook(Update Mar 19)

### khin · 2005-02-22 09:34 · #B170801

I've finally decided to open up my own sketchbook. After seeing wonderful and improving works around here, I feel that I should share my self-learned methods of doing things...they just might help, although there are plenty of better methods out there Smile
I'll organize my sketchbook by categories, and I'll keep it to the first post, so be sure to check back periodically. I may update it once or twice a week.
Facial Expressions
Here I shall try to practice on a wide range of facial expressions
![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/ross_and_mel.jpg)
This picture should suggest a story. C'mon, make up your own based on it! That's how I can test the success of their faces Wink
Hands and Feet
Drawing hands and feet are always a challenge for me, so I must practice drawing them in every imaginable angle.
Dynamic Posing
The human body is very difficult to render, especially on very complex positions.
![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/mon.jpg)
I drew him out of my imagination. No reference, sadly.
I tried the pose myself and it seems it could be done, though my whole body couldn't hold it still..maybe because i'm not that athletic to begin with...do you think this pose is possible?
Mecha
One of my untouched area of anime/manga is drawing mecha...I'll try my best to practice them.
![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/dynamo_mobile.jpg) ![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/Phanterum_XL.jpg)
I'll start with drawing mobiles first, then I'll move to big time machines.
Color Study
Different style of coloring. I ultimately aim for realism of course.
![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/portrait_euix.jpg)
Photoshop 7, 2 hours, pen tablet.
Meh, still not as good as I had hoped...
Concept/Miscellaneous
Anything that comes to my mind at the moment
Furry
![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/furry.jpg)
Female practice drawing. I chose a furry(animal/human) style. Unfinished. She's supposed to be holding something but I'm still thinking what...
Instrument: mouse
Program: Photoshop
Time: 30 minutes
