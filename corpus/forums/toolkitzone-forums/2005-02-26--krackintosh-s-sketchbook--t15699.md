# Krackintosh's Sketchbook

### Krackintosh · 2005-02-26 03:15 · #B172352

Hello. I'm new here, but I've seen some of the work here. My friend is a member here (known as Forsaken) and he has shown me some of your amazing artwork. He suggested that I join, and seeing how nice you people are I decided to do just that. There's a lot of talent here, and developing talent. This looks to be a very friendly artist community that I think I will enjoy. Hello to you all, I'm looking forward to meeting you. I thought I'd start things off with a sketchbook with a few small pieces of mine. They're not much but I hope you like them.
![](http://oekaki.direstratis.com/pictures/OP_2.png)
A small drawing I made on our oekaki board using Shi-Painter Pro. It was my first oekaki. I drew it on a laptop trackpad, which took pretty long, an hour or so.
![](http://oekaki.direstratis.com/pictures/OP_4.png)
My second oekaki drawing, I used Shi-Painter I don't really like the way this one came out. I'm not so proud of it. I used a mouse and took a hella long time.
![](http://oekaki.direstratis.com/pictures/OP_14.png)
Another oekaki piece, using Shi-Painter. Mouse, took a few hours. It came out okay.
![](http://oekaki.direstratis.com/pictures/OP_20.png)
And here's the oekaki I'm currently working on, using Shi-Painter Pro. I'd say it's looking pretty good so far. I'm using a mouse on it, so far it's taken about 240 minutes.
That's all I have for now. And hello to you all once again.
