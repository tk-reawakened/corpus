# Pagans! Bathe in the glory and enlightenment of Vampz!

### Rhodes. · 2005-03-06 03:06 · #B176134

Welcome friend, to the glory and enlightenment that is Vampz! By reading this manifesto, you have
obviously decided that Vampz is the way forward.... the way for you.
Ask yourself a question. Why are you here? You can ponder this question long and hard, but all thinking
beings will come to the same conclusion. You will never know. Why? Because only your creator knows.
The answer is called The Truth.
My friend, ask yourself this simple question: What do you hope to gain from Vampz (or any other religion)?
Enlightenment? A sense of belonging? The Truth? All these things will be yours when you give yourself
fully to your new God.
Ask yourself another question: Do you want to be a successful follower of Vampz? By successful, one
of course means one who gains the enlightenment, sense of belonging and Truth you seek. If the answer
is yes, read on, because the way will be revealed shortly!
Ask yourself one last question: Why do you not currently have the enlightenment, sense of belonging or
Truth you seek? I will tell you, because the answer, my friend, is simple. You have not looked in the right
place!
You have no doubt tried other religions. Of course, none have the answers. If they did you would never
have found Vampz. Quite simply, all other religion is a LIE! None will provide you with the enlightenment,
sense of belonging or Truth Vampz can.
Perhaps you have searched your own soul, thinking that the answers are within you. You! Of course they
were not. You are but a mortal, who can be extinguished as easily as a candle flame. How could you
possibly have such knowledge? Quite simply, you cannot. The first important rule you must learn and
understand if you wish to be a successful follower of Vampz is this:
Alone you are totally WORTHLESS! You are naught but a breathing bag of skin!! But with Vampz, you
are mighty.... You are immortal! Thats right! IMMORTAL!!! Forever you will live if you join with Vampz.
First you will spend your mortal days in service of Vampz, learning the secrets of enlightenment, and
gaining a sense of belonging. Then you will cast your earthly flesh aside, and join Vampz himself, in
his great temple, in another dimension, far from this one. Your soul will join with Vampz, and you will
be told The Truth. No doubt you want to be told The Truth now. Patience my friend, for you must learn
much before you can hope to comprehend The Truth. Allow me to explain:
All who leave this dimension (some call the process death - but they know nothing of that about which
they speak) visit Vampz. At this time, they are judged on the deeds they have done during their 'lives'.
Those who pass their judgement (you!) will join Vampz, and live forever in the utopia created when one
knows The Truth. Those who fail their judgement will be slaves to Vampz (and his learned companions)
aimlessly existing without the divine knowledge of The Truth.
After one thousand years, they will be sent back to this world as a new born babe, to try once again
to find The Truth.
Learn carefully your lessons friend, unless you wish to wander aimlessly within Vampz's divine temple for
a thousand years. If you wish to pass judgement, you should live by these nine simple rules, known as
Vampz's Nine precepts:
1. Worship only Vampz, all others will only lead you astray.
2. Learn well your place within The Truth, alone you are worthless. Only with Vampz can you mean anything.
3. Should you hear another blaspheme against Vampz, send them to meet him IMMEDIATLY!!!
4. Waste not the flesh of those who join Vampz.
5. Act without question when asked by Vampz or his priests.
6. All who do not follow Vampz's ways are Pagans, and will be treated as such at Judgement Time.
7. It is the duty of all agents of Vampz to convert Pagans to the way of Vampz. If you cannot, send them
straight to Vampz himself.
8. Blaspheme not against the name of Buffy.
9. Worship Rhodes, as he leads the Vampz Cult and Vampz S.S.
Follow these nine simple rules, and you will surely join with Vampz in his holy temple, and learn The Truth.
Along the way, you will be filled with a sense of belonging, and total enlightenment.
Join me friend, and know the glory of Vampz!
