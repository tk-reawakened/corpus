Listening to it now...
INTRO:
It's not long at all...I'm not exactly sure how your boss fight starts out...but, one the beat starts up...it's good =)
...must agree, sounds too cheery to be Boss Music, sounds more like an Advance Factory tune...but you can do the following to make it better =)
MIDDLE-Beginning:
Once the main solo hormony start out about 30 sec in...I like that harmony desceind part...the repetition guitar beat is VERY good...makes it good. Main melody is okay, but if you lower the octive and give it more of a bass punch to it...will sound better.
...The Squaretooth solo with the repetition guitar is a really good one, don't change that =) This part can be used to a good climax part...ever thought about mixing in a flute/clarinet/oboe in there? Give it a good Cringe/Clash of notes...a Discord...but not a long one, few here and their adds uniqueness to it.
Okay...we just finished the first MAIN part and repeating...now we're starting back the Harmony solo again (part 30 sec from begining)...DON"T DO THAT! At this specific point...you need to add a new variety to the section to make the music Lots more interesting and get rid of that repetition out of it.
REST:
What I just stated above...once you finish that secton, you can COTA back to your intro theme (30 sec part) ...maybe on this final section, add more bass harmony to reinforce the tune to make it a strong final end to it. Use more lower BRASS insturments...battle music need BRASS and good loud bass drums.
