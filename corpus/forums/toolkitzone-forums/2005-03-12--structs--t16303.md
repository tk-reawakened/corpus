# Structs

### Pests · 2005-03-12 22:58 · #B179683

I remember reading an article that stated structs can't have member functions. Why not? The only difference between them and classes is the scope of default access.

### Colin · 2005-03-12 23:02 · #B179684

Unfortunately, the goal of structs was not to provide C++ structs. The reason why structs were introduced was to have a 'simpler' way to collect pieces of data. Perhaps a better name for them would be 'records'.
They're more comparable to C's structs, or the VB6 'Type'.

### AlienDude · 2005-03-12 23:12 · #B179687
