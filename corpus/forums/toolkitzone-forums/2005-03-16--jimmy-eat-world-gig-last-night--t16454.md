# Jimmy Eat World Gig Last Night!

### Pearljammin · 2005-03-16 10:00 · #B181219

An amazing Gig. It could have been one of my best if the crowd wasnt so boring. Seriously, the crowd ruined it, Half of em where just standing there watching, and during a good song to jump around too the majority of them would only jump for maybe the first verse, and then stop for the rest of the song. As any veteran "gigger" would know, at least 50% of the show and how kick ass it is, depends how fun and active the crowd around you are.
I didn't stop at all I was going for the whole show, and I could see into the pit, NO ONE in the pit was jumping around or nothing, they where all standing there watching like it was some sort of Britney Spears concert. They didn't deserve to be in there. They should have a rule that if you do not go nuts in the pit, you do not deserve to be in there! Even on the last encore, when you knew it was your last chance to go nuts (they played sweetness, I mean come on... perfect chance "Are you listening? A woo oh oh oooh oh oh *mosh like fuck!*" but barely anyone was throughout the show, I'll admit there where some on the last song, but only a few.
That was really the only flaw of the gig in my opinion. Great performance, Great choice of songs, shit audience. It's just not as fun when you appear to be the only one enjoying the music (everyone else was just like zombies). Anyone else have bad experiences with shit crowds?
It was also Smu's (Sarah Murphys) Birthday and that was her b-day present from her sis (dammit, Janet) she was having a good time too, as was the whole bunch of us that went (Boyler, Haymer, Smu, Sinead, Janet, Sarah Mac, Jenny M, Bocca, Aoife D, Michael, Mark, Lisa etc). So Happy B-day Smu and Hope you like the card I made.
