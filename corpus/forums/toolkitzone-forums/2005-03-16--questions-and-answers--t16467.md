# Questions and Answers!

### Ross L. · 2005-03-16 20:34 · #B181374

Ask questions here.
Complains, suggestions, reactions shall be entertained.
But of course before asking, you might want to read the rules:
[Rules](http://www.icestormcity.com/piarts/heraldry/GD/GDRules.htm)
You are free to suggest additional rules, and comment on them.
For your convience, I have compiled a running list of the most freqently asked questions and have posted them below:
Q: Is this game text-based or do I have to download a game client?
A: Unfortunately neither Khin, Ashe or myself have the time or resources to make a game client and host it on a server for your enjoyment. I could only wish we did. In due time we will, so watch out! Razz For now the game is forum based with some elements in Java.
Q: I still don't understand everything. Is there a tutorial page that can help me out?
A: Yeah there is, although it's really crude. <http://piarts.mysticsoftware.net/HGD/hgdguide.htm> I will try to update that and make it look semidecent sometime soon. Razz
Q: So with magic, do we automatically know the spells (our race can learn) when we get enough exp, or do we need to train/buy them or anything?
A: No one starts out with any magic. In order to be able to use magic, you must build a Magic School and then you can use a turn to learn the requested spell.
Q: What class has the most powerful magic?
A: Croissans are Masters of Heraldric Arts..that's elemental magic, plus summoning. THe downside here is that by Nature, Croissans are dependent with their innate element. That is, If you're a Fire elemental, you can learn other spells(but not Ice)...but you only get to specialize in one field. This means that you can learn most spells from Basic to the Intermmediate level, but when in the advanced and ultima level they can only learn their elemntal innate. Jinees don't have elemental innates and may learn any elemental/time/space/status magic. Jinees and EK aren't as complex. What they see is what they get from the spell table. There are no active Croissans yet so we couldn't make them choose their innate.
Reucians have access to Time and Space(as stated). But they are not Basic Magic. They are intermediate...but we're not there yet...
That was Khin's response anyways. Razz
Q: Can I use Labor Agreement on a clan mate who is on hold?
A: Sorry, but no. Members placed on Hold are like non-members. They're just posted at the clan status for hope that they would return, since they did not formally declare their resignation. Consider them not there. Labor Agreement can only be enjoyed by active players.
Q: If we're in a scenario, can we still perform actions (besides moving) without disrupting it. Also, what about buying supplies?
A: Yes, to train, buy, or transact anything, one must be at the base to do so. And while in scenarios you can't consume your turns...that's one drawback of staying in a scenario for too long..although you may ask your mates to use Labor agreement. You're still earning stuff from the scenario anyway..and they don't have turn limits. If you are still under the scenario(you have not chosen to quit), you can't use your turns until you quit the scenario. All actions you make on the field will not be considered. This may change later however.
Q: How do I get weapons? Once weapons are researched and finished, is it automatically equipped or what? What do you do with it?
A: Once weapons are researched (Basic Weapon), you build a weaponry(look at the buildings page). Once a weaponry is built you may start buying/producing weapons(look at the items page). When you have a weapon it shall be added to your inventory.
When you want to equip something or use any item, just include it in your clan report, or PM me in case your clan leader is unable to update your request. It does not cost any turn when using/equipping/removing items.
Q: What do you mean Researchers cannot fight? What if NPC's attack our base?
A: Researchers cannot fight another player. They may however, lay down traps and whatnot. But whatever the circumstances may be, researchers are not allowed to participate in a Tactical Battle Thread, which is against other players.
Q: What exactly can Croissans do (agriculturally, etc.). On the rules section, it doesn't state anything about wandering on magick continents.
A: That's because they can't do anything up there Smile
They have to get down to perform agricultural. That's a disadvantage. Advantage? Well they are not easily attacked because they are floating in the air.
Q: YAY I LEVELED UP!! How do I distribute my stats?
A: PM me or post them in your clan thread.
Q: My friend in clan X wants to trade me something. Do I need anything to do this?
A: At least one of you needs a marketplace. Whoever owns the marketplace might get a profit (depending on what the mods decide for this issue).
Q: My friend wants to join my clan but he wants to be a Reucian not a Jinnee. Can he?
A: No. A clan must be comprised 100% of it's native race. Mercenaries are excluded from this rule, and each clan can have up to 3 mercenaries. However, there are a lot of requirements to becoming a Mercenary. So eventually yes. Right away no. Razz
