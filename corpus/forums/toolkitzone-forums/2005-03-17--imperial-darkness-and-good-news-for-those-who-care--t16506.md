# Imperial Darkness and Good news(for those who care)

### Sevat Syvalluis · 2005-03-17 15:30 · #B181775

Ok im starting another section for my band, this is the fanpart of course.
Imperial Darkness is on the rise from the ashes once again, we are complete. we have worked hard and have a contract with a record deal. And a cool website (links later) Imperial Darkness has some deep origins which you can read about on the website but the recent good news is this.
Imperial Darkness member Matthias and member Gilbert was all that was left of the local legendary death metal group from 2000. At the verge of destruction into the blackness that claims everything one small prayer was heard. Gilberts newest girlfriend Sammy was a very adept drum player who wanted some action in the local scene. Having now enough people to get started everything was going great. Soon Matthias (me) devolped a problem, he was envolved in an accident that caused him not to be able to play lead guitar no more. But with some work Matthias
was able to get his hand in shape to pick up his bass guitar and work some lines out. that's when he found his calling, he was born to play bass. With the need for some melody and rythm, the band endlessly searched for a guitar player and a keyboard player. thats when Blake showed up. Having amazing abilites on the keyboard and guitar, he originaly signed to play keyboard, but with a little talking and looking on the internet, the darkness was lifted and we found a way to merge both talents. We found a keytar. Now we have somthing original and unique to the heavy/black/death metal scene.
Members
Matthias Newton- Vocals and Bass
Blake Lenon- Keytar, Keyboard, Guitar
Gilbert- Bass
Sammy - Drums
