# Restructuring of Sketch?

### Xavier · 2005-03-23 23:09 · #B184875

When Indy originally brought this daily sketch idea up back in January, we discussed further site related ideas. I'm proposing this next to some changes to boost acitivity.
Although you lose the initial concept, I think you should drop daily and move to weekly.
Have one "Weekly Artists' Sketch" which users can enter any number of times, with the usual sketch phrase. This is an entire forum where entries are posted in a new topic, allowing members to give critical feedback on all entries individually. Users (aka artists) will then vote on their favourite entry.
Secondly, have a "Weekly Golden Sketch" which has a more defined phrase and you can only enter for once. Not only this, but you it's more of a competition whereby no one else can view others entries and the winner is decided by Indy, or another respected person(s).
Here's where I can help. I'll be happy to integrate such a sketch system into TKZ and the forums (which Indy has asked for before) plus allow the winners to be announced via the frontpage. Winners and runners-up of the Golden Sketch will have a trophy added to their TKZ profile, plus a link to the image.
Furthermore, maybe, at the end of each month users or specific people can vote on the most improved artists and oustanding talents. No user can win a monthly award twice, so everyone has a shot.
Just a few ideas to boost acitivty and actual artistic learning which is what Indy strived for.
