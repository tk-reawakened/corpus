### gcn_zelda · 2005-03-23 20:23 · #B184796

In case there are any other Weezer fans out there, radio stations are supposed to start playing Beverly Hills on March 29th, but you can get the song from <http://www.weezer.com(streaming).>
Somebody at <http://torrentspy.com> has a radio rip of it. I don't know if it's legal or not.
Anyway, the review.
The song is way un-Weezer-like. The verses are like a slow, musical rap and the chorus is just a chorus of the band members singing.
The song is pretty good, but the thing that is going to make this single a hit is not the song, but the music video. Again, Weezer has come up with an original idea. But I don't want to ruin it, so you'll have to watch it when it becomes available, again on March 29th, on MTV.
