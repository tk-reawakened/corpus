# Sprite Insertion

### Metallic · 2005-04-03 22:17 · #B189955

I created my own sprite for the main character of my RPG, but how can I actually upload the sprites to the game and animate them? Do I need to separate the sheet into individual sprites or does Tk3 do that?

### Delano · 2005-04-03 22:32 · #B189963

You need to separate it, unfortunately. If you've made it outside of the tk, you can either convert it into tiles using the Tile Grabber (Tile editor > Image > Grab Tiles) or split them into individual image files (e.g. gif), then create animations for each direction.

### Delano · 2005-04-05 09:53 · #B190585

### Metallic · 2005-04-05 23:29 · #B190809

I'm having a problem. You see, when I tile the sheet it comes out currupted with a small section of his chest and part of his shoe as 1 tile, and so on. How can I just create an even sprite-per-tile tileset?

### Delano · 2005-04-06 10:13 · #B191096

You need to align the grid of the tile grabber with the regions you want - right click on the image to move the grid (make sure it's turned on). If your image isn't the right scale, you'll need to resize it outside of the Toolkit (it doesn't have that functionality at the moment).
