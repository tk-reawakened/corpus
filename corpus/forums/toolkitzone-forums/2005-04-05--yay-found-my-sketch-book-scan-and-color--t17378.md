EDIT:
Oh yeah...ZZZ, few tips for you to start drawing little better.
1) Start with the face, eyes most, because that's what gives the character's personality.
2) Various hair styles will give you good examples (Japanese Anime) is a great place for good looking hair styles.
3) USE A SHARPER TIP PENCIL! Dull tips give you very wide, ugly looking lines.
4) Draw light lines first, and learn to draw..."fading lines/brush lines"? Kinda mean by at the end of lines (especially hair) let the pencil lift off the page. DON'T FORCE YOUR HANDS! Let them glide across the page like a brush.
