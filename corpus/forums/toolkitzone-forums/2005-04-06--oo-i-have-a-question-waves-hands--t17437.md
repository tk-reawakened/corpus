### Spyder · 2005-04-06 02:21 · #B190979

Yes, but save your money, the PS2 (Two/Slim) has a built in modem. If you have the older PS2, the network adapter has a built in modem. The network adapter for the PS2 costs around $40, though it might end up getting a lot cheaper now that the PS2 slim is out, and has it built in. I'd personally check Ebay, or see if a GameStop or EB Games might have a used one for cheap. If you get a used one, make sure it comes with the Network Setup disc, otherwise you won't be able to configure it.

### zzz · 2005-04-06 20:43 · #B191272

I wanna get FFXI...because it comes w/a modem as well.
does it just connect to thier server, or does it go thru your ISP

### Spyder · 2005-04-07 03:59 · #B191458

It connects to their server using your ISP. Using the Network Setup disc, you create a network connection much like you would in Windows, by inputing the phone number, username, and password to your ISP. After saving the connection on your memory card, you load up whatever game your going to play on the net (say, SOCOM, or THPS) and it will use the network connection you setup to dial up your ISP, and access the games server, exactly the same way as a PC would.
BTW, you might want to make sure that FFXI comes with a modem. It may just come with the HDD itself, which connects to the Sony Network Adapter, which can be bought seperately. That said, your much, much, much better off buying the PC version of FFXI. Besides being half the cost, it runs fine on a dial-up connection. Of course if your PC's a bit on the older side, then maybe the PS2 version is the only way to go. Remember that you have to pay $13 a month for FFXI as well though, and you must have access to a credit card. You'll also have to update the game every so often when new content is available. The first update will be the longest, and on dial-up, could take days to finish.
