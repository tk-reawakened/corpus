# Using This Forum

### Xavier · 2005-04-09 20:14 · #B192409

Intro
This forum allows you to showcase the latest shots from your Toolkit project. It is also integrated into the games section to provide some extra tools for you to use.
Taking Screenshots
Press the printscreen(Prt Scr) key on your keyboard. This takes a picture of what ever is on your screen and puts it in your clipboard.
The clipboard is just where the image is stored temperarily, in the background so to speak. If you really want to see what's in the clipboard, you can click "Start Menu" then "Run". Type the command "clipbrd" with out the quotes and hit enter.
To make the screenshot accessible for editing and saving you have to paste it into a graphic editor. Open your graphic editor or MS paint and click "Edit" then "Paste" or "Ctrl+V". Save the image as a gif or jpg.
Uploading Screenshots
You should create a game profile in the games section. This gives you free and unlimited space for your screenshots, it also showcases your screenshots in a gallery and allows users to rate and comment on them.
To do this go to the My Account drop down menu and select Games. Create a new profile and then click Images to begin uploading.
Displaying Screenshots
Once uploaded, note the id number of the image. You can get the id number by looking in the url bar when viewing the image, eg: image=100, or on any link to the image.
Then place the number in between the [shot] bbcode tags like so;

**Code:**

```
[shot]100[/shot]
```

This has two advantages. Firstly it automatically displays a thumbnail of the image, you don't need to enter a link to it, and also makes the image clickable so anyone can click it and go to your game profile to rate it.
Secondly, the use of the [shot] tag means thumbnails of the images are included under the topic subject when browsing the forum. This helps attract users to your post.
