# Newbie Haven - Home of the Newb - Forum Rules

### Allan@Aljo · 2005-05-02 12:29 · #B202614

The [rules in the TKZone](http://www.toolkitzone.com/site.php?mode=rules) still apply here in Newbie Haven as well. Here are a few additional rules.
Newbie Haven Rules:
1. There will be no hateful remarks reguarding newbies. Jokes are sometimes hard to distinguished online so most will be removed as well all for the good of keeping the peace.
2. Not only newbies are allowed but if you are not a newbie make sure your conversation is contructive, welcoming, and/or helpful. The goal of this forum is to make Newbies feel welcome.
3. There will be a distguishing party (the Moderators) that will decide if you are a "newbie hater". A poll would most likely be taken on the at the time of decision but a poll does not have to be taken. You can be removed from the forum without the concent of the community. See the definitions at the bottom of this post.
4. Do not ask to be moderators. A proposition will be made if/when the case we need more moderators. Repeatitive asking only lowers your chance.
Definitions:
Newb and Newbie in this forum will be defined by your experiance with the Toolkit. It can be argued (and has been frequently) when you are actually not a newb anymore. So here are a few basic guidlines.
A. You have been declared so by numerous TKers in other forums besides this one.
B. Your experiance with the toolkit does not go beyond using most of the default settings in the editor.
C. You are young (framing a new mind so to speak) and are still learning the ropes. NOTE: alot of people declare immatureness to go with this one. Age and Matureness CAN go hand in hand but doesn't mean you have to be immature to be a newb. Age is suggested here because most young people are still trying to build a relationship with the community and getting experiance with the Toolkit at the same time.
"Newbie Hater" will be defined on this forum as someone who insults the newbies. Breaking rules 1 or 2, or any of the suggested spamming rules in conjunction with newbie rules 1 and 2 will be grounds for taking offence. Don't get on the Newbie community's bad side.
If this isn't clear enough or if you have any suggestions, please PM me. Do not reply to this post because we do not need a long thread of non-sense for the rules page.
-Allan@Aljo (The Newb Keeper)
