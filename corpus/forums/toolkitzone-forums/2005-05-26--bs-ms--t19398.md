### Walker-san · 2005-10-26 08:54 · #B266755

Custom menu systems are somewhat easy once you have the hang of RPGCode. Last I knew, there were several tutorials on this subject...2 of them were good.
Battle systems. I'm not sure how much times have changed, but back when Chris first put in the option to allow us custom battle systems, no one would touch it. I blame Chris only be cause he put the option in and said "there. Have fun." We had been so used to him explaining to us what each new thing can do with good examples that just dropping the option upon us outright was kinda harsh.
In his defense, we had been pestering him for a full 5 version releases to add in the option. I think we had annoyed him to the breaking point.
Anyways, back to story hour...I forget which brave soul first ventured into the world of custom battle systems. Many entered...few returned. G made the first working "zelda" system I believe.
Point is....those of us who had a good extensive knowledge in RPGCode still had a very rough time piecing together custom battle systems. I never did finish mine. I should see if I can't find the disc that has the program on it.
Custom Battle systems are hard. You have to know all the ins and outs of RPGCode. All the work arounds, all the loop holes, and even then you'll never get it "perfect." That's what plugins are for.
