Couple of things to remember about items:
1: they must be created
2: they must be on the board
3: You have to be able to see them!
(this may sound like noobVille but if you are testing a program that involves items, do you really want to go to the board and place each one?)
Now for your code, quite simply, you have written the #if statements wrong.
you are using the assignment operator '=', when you should be using the equality operator '=='. That is whats wrong, not to mention if you didn't place your items right.
