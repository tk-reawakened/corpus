### I_Cant_Believe_Its_Me · 2005-06-17 19:10 · #B220553

To make other walking people on the board, create them as a item and their graphics be, well them. On your board put the items that you just created, which are really your people, down on the board down at random spots. To edit them to where you can buy stuff or make them walk around heres how:
To walk around and lay down a shop:
-first of all open board editor-
1.)Pull down the rpgcode bar thing at the top and set down a item(your walking person).
2.)When you click on "Set Item", it pulls up a info box thing. Browse for your item(person) and there you go. After that, then look around for activation program and Multi-task.
3.)activation:this is the place where you shop program goes so browse for it. If they do not sell anything just simply put down a talking program for them to say something when you talk to them.
4.)multi-tast:this is optional. Here browse for a program called wander and set it with them. They should move if you add this. If you want them to just sit there, just leave it blank.
There you go...
