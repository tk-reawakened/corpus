### Walker-san · 2005-10-26 08:23 · #B266754

I have a copy of 2.02 (where I came in at) on a disc somewhere. before the time of tst.
For the guy who asked what the difference between version was....most of them were just bug fixes, but i'll give a list best to my memory
2.02 was bugfixes and nothing else.
2.06 I think introduced the .tst format
2.07 fixed the many many bugs that .tst introduced, as well as a new interface design
I fell outta the loop for the first time about here. I checked back to see that between 07 and 12 mostly bugfixes occured
2.16ish or so introduced the animation editor I think. The powerhouse edition was also released around here...few version before I think.
2.18 was bugfixes and something else that was really important...the integrated mp3 player maybe.
I fell outta the loop again, coming back only to celebrate the release of 3.0 and have only recently returned.
I'll also add that a number of versions also brought about new website designs. believe it or not but until what....2.08 chris didn't even had the RPGToolkit.com domain. it was runnin off a crappy webserver that I cannot remember for the life of me what it was.
-_- i feel so old remembering all that....
EDIT (cause it won't let me post again ><):
Downloaded 2.0 on a whim, and for archival purposes. Which brings up something I had forgotten. ARCHIVES. Pre-tst gph storage. Phased out around 2.07 and removed completely in 2.08 I THINK (I'm workin off almost 7 years of backhistory here.)
but man....this brings back memories. It's 2 version before me, but the layout is the same. Look at how few commands there were back then. Back when it was easy to learn RPGCode in a week, if that. Back when prg files were limited to 2001 lines too....i remember I had prgs linked 6 ways for one thing.
A difference though is that in 2.00 the enemy editor is disabled. It was usable in 2.02. So he probably put it in 2.01 or something. Still...god....This brings back so many memories.
You made an oldbie come near tears. Congrats.
