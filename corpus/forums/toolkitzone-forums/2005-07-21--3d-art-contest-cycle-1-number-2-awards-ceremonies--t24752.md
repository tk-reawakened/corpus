# 3D Art Contest-Cycle 1, Number 2 [Awards Ceremonies]

### UltimaWizard · 2005-07-21 04:26 · #B235512

**Ander's Journey-Ch.1**

The hunter stalked through the woods silently, searching all quarters of his vision for his prey. He would be very lucky if he spotted his quarry first, as hunting a boar would usually be work for about seven to eight people...not one lone swordsman who was also packing a compact crossbow.
With a fluid and noiseless speed, Ander Geldgion, the hunter from Silvo Village on the southern edge of the Aldin Continent, ducked behind a nearby bush. Things had gotten very quiet, very quickly. That meant one of two things: the boar was either ambling by searching for some chow, or it had smelt him and was heading to find the lone human who had so stupidly entered it's domain.
With a crashing of bramble, wood, stone, and parts of plants flying everywhere, the giant boar burst through the woods near Ander. Already too close for comfort, Ander thought sullenly as the great boar charged. He discharged the arbalest into the boar's poor eyes, and while the animal was thrashing in pain, vaulted onto the gigantic creature's snout and straddled it as a horse. He began to reload the arbalest, ready to make the easy kill while the animal bucked and thrashed.
Of course, the great boar was in pain, and incredibly angry...but not stupid. It turned over on it's side to try to crush the human who had injured it and came so willingly to his maw...yet only crushed the crossbow. Ander had rolled free, and aside from some bumps, bruises, and minor wounds, was unharmed. You bastard, Ander thought, that'll make this so much harder. Ander drew his sword, his most prized possession, with a bitter pain stabbing into his left arm. The boar had righted itself, and was turning about for another charge, which would either trample the insignificant hunter in front of him, or would miss and open him up to further minor injury.
Of course, Ander was prepared as only a seasoned hunter could be. He rolled to the side, and emerged in a low guard near the boar's forward left leg, from it's perspective. He had little time before the boar realized where he was, as even though one eye had been horribly gouged, and blood was seeping from where the bolt had penetrated the skin beneath the other eye, it could still smell with an acuteness greater than most animals, and was clearly more intelligent than most of it's less fortunate, late cousins.
Ander violently cut at the creature's leg, then rolled underneath the beast, cut the other leg, then, calling on some of his magic talents, covered his sword in arcane force and stabbed the great bewildered creature's head where it's brain should be. As a gratifying result, it dropped in the death throes that were already taking it. Getting an unused, special bottle from his belt, he took some of the creatures blood, and dipped a brush in it. This blood would serve the harvesting party as a path to the downed animal, and would be the proof of his kill.
Ander picked back through the forest cautiously. He was covered in boar blood from where he had stabbed past some veins and arteries in the behemoth's leg, and his own wounds were bleeding, and animals in the forest could smell blood easily...possibly including another boar. Not eager for such an encounter, he sidestepped around a tree, his sword still drawn...right into a waiting group of wolves.
Ander, despite being a seasoned hunter, who had survived this sort of thing time and time again, was beginning to be afraid. He had barely gone a mile from the boar, and still had a journey ahead of him to negotiate. He was already weakened, and 3 wolves were far more of a threat than even he would have cared to have engaged after a harrowing battle with a Lord of the Forest, as the great animals were called.
With more channeling of arcane forces, Ander used the same keenness charm on his blade, and began to slowly back away from the wolves while keeping his back to the tree. The wolves, believing their foe near death, yet wise enough to know him as a threat, began to circle him, feinting viciously, trying to provoke the hunter into a foolhardy move.
With a sudden violence, Ander came to life from his calm guarded stance. The large wolf, whom Ander rightly assumed was the Alpha male, fell quickly. The wolves attacked despite their leader's fall, but they had vaulted headlong into a lateral sweep of Ander's enhanced blade. With the beasts howling in their death throes, Ander decided to run for it. If one group could find him that easily, stealth should be damned--he wasn't in the condition to handle that kind of fight every few paces.
-----------------------------------
Ander arrived at the fenced village of Silvo. It was by no means an imposing hamlet; rather, it was something of a woodsman's idea of a fortress. In addition to a robust population of 25 people, there was a small market where traders came to buy dried meats and vegetables grown in and near the town, magitechnology, newly developed by the Althiman Empire, which was currently 'protecting' the town (meaning they weren't attacking it) was being oohed and aahed at by the few who had enough wealth to buy such items.
Of course, the civilian aspect of life wasn't what made the city imposing. There were 5 guard towers around the village, 2 at each entrance, and one in the center, and the small militia's barracks were near the center post. Silvan Archers were revered around the empire, in some slight cases around the world, and were renowned for accuracy at distances. Considering how every tower was manned by 2 archers, Ander would certainly have been marked the second he emerged from the forest.
Ander strode up to the left-hand tower of the Silvo forest entrance. 'Hey, Harrison! Get the harvesting party ready! I downed one hell of a big boar back there! That's enough food for you wusses for a month!', shouted Ander, taunting his good friend who was currently on guard. 'Shut up, you sword-wielding twit! If you're so good, why can I kill a pack of wolves 30 seconds before you strike the first blow,' shouted back the sentry, the sound of the boast mingling with the laughing of the other guard. 'Quit your whining. If it weren't for an intrepid, daring...'
'Idiotic, suicidal...'
'Dashing rogue such as myself, we wouldn't be eating as well as we do!' shouted Ander, completing the time-honored rant with his friend. 'Aah, Harrison, how've things been?' Ander finally asked. 'Good, but boring without you as entertainment,' the sentry replied with a smile so large the sun was hard-put to compete. 'Go get some rest. You look like you've been with the Imperial Army or something.' 'Okay. Just watch out, the creatures are getting wise. I'll tell you tomorrow,' Ander said, eager to heal his wounds, eat some nice food, and take a long-desired nap.
Ander entered his house near the center tower. It was sparsely decorated, only a closet for his clothes, his table, some glasses, a small chest that held vital things like healing herbs and crossbow ammo, and his bed. By the Great Redeemer, Ander thought tiredly, I've never been so happy to be home.
Ander quickly dressed his wounds with Kubtak herbs wrapped around the abrasions, their healing oils stinging slightly, as was to be expected. He moved slowly, as his movements made the stinging worse, on the way to his chest, where he sought his spare candles...to find his spares were the ones on the table and dresser, burnt to the base. Damn, Ander thought, and I was going to buy a magitech oven...just one more reason to go to the bazaar tomorrow. Screw it. With more slow, ginger movements, and pulled out some day-old bread, and a mass of boar jerky, made and ate a modest meal, then somehow fell asleep.
The dream tonight was one he hadn't had for at least 10 years. He saw himself as a child with his mother and father, them proud of their son's good grades. Their faces quickly contorted with rage and pointed at the town square...and the youths who were being tended by Medics. Before he could understand their incomprehensible words, he was stalking through the woods, not with his usual, measured, silent tread, but with a child's fearful footsteps. A Wood Tiger burst from it's cover. Ready, Ander pulled the dagger he had been given, and strove to cast the sharpening charm on it...but the magic didn't manifest. His dream self helplessly slashed as the Tiger, unburdened by the wounds that didn't even show, opened its great, toothed, raw smelling mouth...
...And Ander awoke. He knew it had been a dream, but the terror wasn't so easily dismissed, despite his experience with the creatures of the wood and his hunter's training. Remembering the bottle of boar blood that was proof of the kill, he got up, his wounds fully healed thanks to the restorative virtues of the Kubtak herbs, he discarded the slightly blooded dressings, and changed into some fresh clothes.
It took Ander no time to get to the east part of the hamlet. One would have thought the town's true population to be over a hundred from the bustle, but the itinerant traders were simply vieing for goods, as villagers themselves were. Ander took the familiar route to the Hunter's stall, where hunters recieved money for their kills. 'You took down a good one. The harvesters are still bringing back the pieces, son.' the burly kiosk operator, a former hunter himself, said through a lion's grin. 'It wasn't easy', said Ander, not a little mollified at the praise, 'the little bastard cut me up like a boar chop. I have to buy candles, a full set of healing herbs...and I wanted a dang oven! Everyone else has one by now!' Ander said, some minor frustration rising at the thought of the past day's events. 'Hey, the old ways are good. This !@$Smile magitech bullcrap is too damn easy, and when people realize they don't know how to do jack crap, we hunters will be providing for them. Now, I'll be guessin' you want your money?' the standowner said, his demeanor changed from a friendly swagger, to a more nervous, less secure swagger. 'The drattable headman's getting more stingy by the day. He said from the harvester's report it's 1000 Xua, and much as I tried to bargain with him, he wouldn't budge.'
Of course, 1000 Xua for a larger boar, let alone one that had done some grievous damage to its hunter, was a rip-off at best. Usually, Ander's kills had rewarded him about 2500 to 4000 Xua, not counting the other animals which could be also consumed or their corpses turned to some useful purpose. Of course, rumor had it the Empire was watching the town more carefully, and was always eager to increase their coffers...and would thus attack any overly prosperous, powerful area. So far, Silvo's relative poverty had actually saved them.
Ander accepted the bag of copper coins with thanks, and moved off to buy the goods he needed. He would have pressed the point if it would have helped, but the headman liked to rule Silvo with a wooden hand, and his word was rule.
Mulling these thoughts over, Ander walked up to the Apothecary, and had no sooner bought the new herbs than he heard the tower's bells clang chaotic notes on the horizon. Ander snatched his herbs, and was headed to his house when he saw them.
Apparently, the Empire did want this area. They had dispatched 10 Dark Knights to level the city, and they took no time pulling their dark, evil-looking swords on trader, sentry, and villager alike. A party was harassing more villagers with a cruel, methodical strategy based on herding the poor woodsmen into groups and slaughtering them, and another party was entering cots, no doubt raping the women who lived there.
Ander, who had survived some of the most dangerous animals in this section of the world, had never seen, and never wanted to see, anything like this. With no effort he had turned tail, and hidden behind a broad oak tree near the edge of the city. Of course, that didn't stop him from seeing his acquaintances and friends slaughtered like animals before him, his own things looted and his home torched. Blinded by the smoke and by his own tears, Ander did not see the Dark Knights get the survivors, and read their charge of treason against the Empire who had been protecting them, and summarily slaughter them as they had the others. The Knights made a pass, making sure every villager was dead, then they departed.
-------------------------------------
It was late night by the time his tears had finally stopped.
The bodies of his fallen friends and villagers were still lying on the open earth, some consumed by fire partially, some with body parts feet away from their body, the terror of death still in their eyes, their blood mirroring the waxing crescent with a preternatual clarity. But Ander did not notice any of this; his mind was in turmoil, his sould in upheaval.
My friends are dead.
I have no home.
I should be with them.
Ander felt less anger at the Empire's troops than he did at himself. The Knights, while their methods were inhuman, cruel, and sadistic beyond belief, they were fulfilling their orders. Ander however, had ran from his comrades, watched their slaughter, and didn't even raise his sword to protect them, as he should have done.
I should have.
Ander's tears came yet again. The smoke still bellowing, he slept.
When the new dawn came, Ander was still furious with himself. However, his thoughts weren't of self-destruction. He had more immediate concerns.
Any minute the beasts will come.
I have no home now.
What the hell am I supposed to do?
Ander surveyed the former town. Some fires still burnt, and the bodies were beginning to exude a stench of decay in unison. Ander had smelt horrible, horrible smells from the maws of the forest's beasts, but never the smell of humans decaying in the rising morning sun. Something glinted dully at the corner of Ander's vision, and he turned.
A Dark Knight had fallen in the battle, his helmet shorn where a sentry had buried his sword in the warrior's head. Of course, the villager who did that lay in two, his organs spilling out onto the ground, yet, Ander had the beginnings of an idea.
I need to survive, he began to himself. To do that, I need to survive the forest creatures, and the reach of the Empire, for if they ever find that I am from Silvo, I will be slaughtered like my friends. Those Dark Swords are much stronger than my sword ever was, even with the enchantment, and the armor is equally as strong.
Try as Ander could, he also couldn't think of a better disguise than 45 pounds of Imperial Dark Steel covering him. With an effort, he unharnessed the fallen Imperial, and put on the armor. It was a little large, but that was okay. He picked up the sword from the ground, where it had fallen the day before. It quivered with malevolence, then stilled, as if accepting it's new master.
Ander could feel the sword's innate magic powers. He raised the sword in a high guard, and called on the power while slashing. Sure enough, a wave of dark energy surged from the blade and cut part of a burnt cottage in two, where the blast dispersed into a dark mist. Knowing there was no going back, and pondering the tactical options his new weapon afforded him, Ander sheathed the sword, salvaged some untouched jerky from the vendor's stand, and walked off to the forest, his abandoned friends and fallen past in his wake.

### UltimaWizard · 2005-11-03 22:45 · #B268666

Alright, Greg, I'll answer each of your questions.
1)Small Renderings
This is for functional reasons not only with these webpages, but also with your storage host and for YOUR rendering convenience--a small picture renders more quickly, loads more quickly, and takes up less disk space.
2)Rendering Software
You don't like extruding and stuff like in Blender? Almost too bad. Check out Notsosuperhero's suggestions for modeling software, but I say Blenders BY FAR the best. You have to get used to it, but it can work really good. Check out the Newb to Pro tutorial on Blender3D.org. They guide you through the GUI, it's what I got started with.
Blender dosen't quite suck, just don't be intimidated by it if you don't want to rely on trial editions. Wink
3)Possible City Entry
As a matter of fact, it would. But a city's not exactly easy to model...let alone render, let alone render well. You've got too many shadows to take into account...not to mention necessary imperfections like cracks in concrete, messed up garbage bins...*sigh* If you want to, you'll be my hero.
If you want to participate, please do. If your last victory was any indicator, you're pretty good. Bring it on the contestants in this contest.
