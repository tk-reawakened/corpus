### Spyder · 2005-07-21 17:41 · #B235758

I'd recommend saving for a bit longer if you can deal with what you have for now. The [GeForce 6200](http://www.newegg.com/Product/Product.asp?Item=N82E16814127174) would be a much better choice for a budget card, or even better, wait for the 6600LE, which should be coming out fairly soon.
Also, make sure you get the best card for your motherboard. If you have an AGP port, get an AGP card, if you have PCI Express, get a PCI Express card, if you only have standard PCI slots, there isn't a whole lot that's better than this card. I think the best PCI video card available is the GeForce 5700, which is probably severely limited by PCI's bandwidth.
