### Colin · 2005-07-22 01:38 · #B235933

Physical punishment (and indeed, the death penalty) is utterly pointless. Crimes are either executed situationally with no prior planning, or with an organized plan. In the former case, the spur of the moment nature of the crime means that deterrent is not even considered. In the latter case, the criminals do not intend to be caught.
Telling a neighbourhood where a criminal lives is another dreadfully bad idea. It gives the poor person no chance ever to be regarded as a decent citizen again, even if he intends to try. It will cause the ex-convict to be harassed endlessly and pestered about things he didn't do. It sets him up to lose from the start.
