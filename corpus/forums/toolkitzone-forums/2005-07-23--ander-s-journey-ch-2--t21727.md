# Ander's Journey-Ch.2

### UltimaWizard · 2005-07-23 18:37 · #B236910

Ander's trek through the forest lasted about two days. Granted, the forest was relatively small, but he had only frequented the parts where large and bountiful game abounded, and was therefore unfamiliar with the bulk of the forest. Never mind that, Ander thought tiredly in the noon heat of his second day in the forest, I'll not be coming back.
The journey had wrought many discoveries for the ex-hunter, large numbers of ferocious creatures, colonies of Wood Tigers, and the corpses of fallen hunters and warriors whose camping implements still survived. The most startling discovery, however, wasn't native to the forest--it was what hung at Ander's side.
Ander had been attacked by a large group of animals earlier. There were ten wolves and about that number of Wood Tigers. He'd climbed a tree, and let the naturally hostile animals attack each other. He disposed of the creatures using the Dark Sword's Shadow Wave repeatedly, as Ander called the power, and felt sharp stabbing pains at the sites of wounds he'd thought were healed. When Ander removed the sections of his armor after the battle to tend the wounds he found that many of his wounds--not only ones from four days ago, but also some weeks past--were reopened with what appeared to be very light sword cuts.
Ander was enthralled, yet disgusted with this new sword's power. Unlike traditional weapons, where enchantments the maker had used would draw on the metaphysical power of the user and the environment around him, the Dark Sword actually drew it's power from the user's physical condition. Of course, this meant if Ander used the power too much, it could kill him.
Ander's dream from two days ago came back to him in a flash of clarity. He'd not remembered in his dreaming why children couldn't use magic. Usually, the Dark Sword's power being quite the exception, a human trained to use magic would channel arcane spirit force using a talisman or item of some sort--rather rarely from inner strength or skill, though as there were plenty of mages in the Empire, it was certainly possible. The reason adults could use magic was because as their bodies became more massive and had greater volume, the arcane force could be dissipated more efficiently and the unused force reabsorbed into the body, and therefore spells would manifest and work more accurately. For children, either spells just didn't manifest in any physical form at all, or would manifest in an extremely chaotic, uncontrollable form that was destructive to any entity within range--the caster included.
Ander continued on, not only remembering the mechanics of magic he'd learned in his early years, but formulating his plan for running, as it were, into the Althiman Empire's arms. He was aware of some prowling near him, probably wolves or Wood Tigers, but if Ander used his blade to cut the foliage, he'd not only likely be wasting time which he dearly needed, he'd be wasting his own health as well. Halting, Ander drew into a low guard, his armor harsly clattering against itself in a discordant chord. Nothing came out, and Ander listened, and slowly turned, remaining on guard. Nothing, Ander thought. Am I sure? With another rotation, the ex-hunter surveyed the area. Nope. Nothing. Screw this, I don't have time for this anymore. Ander jogged into the tree bank ahead, around a bend, and fell upon the plains ahead.
-------------------------------------------
The Castle City of Leva was a large, sprawling castle that dominated 2 square miles of the Levan Plains. Housing some 100,000 souls, and being an ally, and a trusted one at that, of the Althiman Empire, it was likely one of the most impregnable structures built and maintained by human hands in the world, likely second to the Imperial Capital farther north in Damarkand. Patrols guarded the outside of the plains from the bandits and ferocious creatures that infested the land, and protected the farmers who provided the food for most of the continent.
Ander was taken aback at the grey stone walls with the blue supports that looked like the held up the walls all by themselves, the tall, graceful watch towers also patterned with blue where Ander knew anti-siege weapons were housed and waiting to repel any would-be invader, and the pennants of the Republic of Leva and the Althiman Empire--For the Levans, a blue field with a gold falcon holding 3 arrows in his left talon, and a laurel branch in the right with a lion on the falcon's chest, the Imperial standard bearing a dark, somber green field with a Golden sword bisecting a buckler with a lion on the right-hand side, and a crane grasping a laurel on the left. Still taken by the fortress city's majesty, Ander didn't notice the four-man patrol approaching him.
'You there! Dark Knight!,' the leader called, suprised that such a high ranking officer of his city's allies had just stepped out of the northern fringe of the Silvo Woods. Ander tore his eyes away from the graceful city, and immediately began enacting the near-flawless plan he had formulated in the forest. 'Good soldiers, where am I?' Ander asked with a confused tone. 'Dark Knight, we saw you and the company you were with ride forth five days ago from our city!' The speaker, a great, brown-haired man with blue eyes and a greatsword strapped across his back, thus wrinkling his slightly weather-stained blue Levan uniform, assessed the warrior in front of him. The Knight was missing his helmet, his armor was beaten in places, and he saw the field treatments this warrior had doubtless administered on himself, his red hair shining in the sunlight, some blood on his forehead with what appeared to be a partially healed, yet slightly opened sword cut. Of course, he was a minor Dark Knight, and in the capital at Damarkand they might know him, but the Knights who had returned reported one of their comrades had fallen. Clearly, that was not the case.
Ander noted their study of him, and made his next move. 'I don't remember anything but waking up two days ago, and something telling me to head north. I woke in a burning city with the inhabitants all slain--I think I killed a couple.' Ander's feelings of shame not only at lying that he had killed them, for he of course felt he played a part in it by not helping his comrades, but the guilt for actually not helping his comrades welled up, and he let the remorse and sadness show. 'I don't remember by name or past. I woke in my armor, my helmet shorn, with this cut. I fled into the woods and used some healing herbs I had with me. I've been attacked by forest creatures for days.'
The guards had listened, and knew at once his recent past. The Dark Knights were sent to punish a traitor village in the south, and they'd heard the mission was successful, of course, and they could see the dressings still staunching the flow of blood from some of the more serious wounds. They didn't quite trust this newcomer, however; he felt...odd. Like a deserter or a liar. They doubted that greatly, as Dark Knights were renowned for their morale and ferocity in battle...and they noticed the exhaustion in the warrior's body language. Doubtful, but bound by their honor to their allies, the leader made the decision.
'We'll escort you into the city, and get you healed. You'll take shelter in the city for the night. We'll report this to our commander.' Said the warrior, his greatsword glinting with the sun's ascension. 'Follow us.'
With carefully veiled pleasure, Ander did.
------------------------------------------------
Ander was taken to a rather nice house in the wealthier part of Leva. On the way there, Ander noticed, to his chagrin, how magitechnology abounded in the city, with the stoves being considered an entitlement, and some other curious devices like magitech lighting and voice message boxes being present even on the carefully maintained dirt streets.
Magitechnology was created by the Althiman Empire as a way to ease the lives of their citizens, and was still being advanced for that same end. The Empire wasn't any more evil than any other man-made institution, mankind in general easily included; it simply grew more paranoid of traitors and spies every day. The current emperor was hard-put to maintain his predecessor's glory and prestige (mostly wrought by magitechnology), and was rather harsh on his retainers, though he maintained his father's desire to benefit the masses, though many believed that was to quell any uprisings or rebellions that were always common with any dictatorship.
Later that night, his wounds bandaged, lying in bed, his armor and sword on a holding rack near him, Ander was reading the manuals for Imperial Military protocol he had asked for, and ordered the servants to not tell about on pain of death. He had learned the rules of the Imperial military rather quickly, and was already planning his next moves for his plan's next phase--showing loyalty to the Empire and it's allies.
The first part had been to get them to believe he was an amnesiac, his forgetfulness brought on by an enemy attack, not only covering his lack of knowledge of military protocol, but also his past. Of course Ander knew this was a thin veil at best, but it was the most he could do until he was sent for as he certainly would be. The second part would be more difficult.
He would have to convince whoever his interrogator was that his memories were slowly trickling back, and that he was loyal to the Empire and it's allies--he would even go so far as to volunteer for a dangerous mission. He still didn't know the fallen Knight's name, but that didn't matter right now. His survival did.
--------------------------------------
That night, Ander dreamt again.
He had woken up in his house in Silvo, just as it had been when he had woken up on that fateful day 3 days past, and had gone out to get his pay and some new candles and healing herbs. He had hardly taken five paces before the Knights attacked again, slaughtering from their horses, just as they had that fateful day.
Ander wasn't going to betray his village again! He drew his sword, only once he did, the villager's wounds became more grievous, and they started pleading for him not only to save them, but to spare them. Apparently, they saw him both as Ander, their friend and hunter, as well as the Dark Knight and traitor that he now was. Ander ran from the scene behind the tree that had hidden him, again in the depths of his despair and self-resentment, and he felt a sudden great weight on his belt. His Dark Sword had become the great Wood Tiger, and he had become a child yet again, fleeing from his other past, and as before, the Tiger sprang forward to devour him...
'Master! I come with a message from Grand General Algonia!'
Ander woke up, recovering from his terror and self-hatred, refocusing on the immediate concern of his survival. 'What did he say?' Ander asked, wondering if he'd cried anything out in his sleep that betrayed his identity. 'You are summoned to the keep immediately, where you will be given your next mission. Also, he congratulates you on your recovery, and hopes your memory will return soon.' The messenger bowed and left in haste, perhaps to return to his master and report that the deed was done.
Ander again donned his mail and sword, had a shield from the house added to the ensemble by one of the servant, and he strode forth from the house to go to the Keep.
-------------------------------
Ander had no trouble finding the keep of Leva Castle. Climbing a staggering ten stories, a remarkable feat without magic or magitechnology to help, and some 3000 years old, the keep was one of the oldest structures remaining in the world. Ander was slightly daunted by the greatness of the keep, but knew there was no turning back, and so continued to find a higher-ranking soldier from whom he could solicit directions.
Ander found a Imperial soldier who, from the emblems on his chest and the sides of his arms, was a Captain, also striding towards the keep, his great bow and quiver slung across his right shoulder. 'Captian, I was summoned by the Grand General forthwith. Where is his office?' Ander asked with a careful authority, hopefully convincing the other he was really a Dark Knight. 'I've been summoned as well, Lord. Let's go there together.' Falling into the role of clearly being superior to the archer, he grunted an acknowledgement and climbed with the soldier. 'Lord,' asked the archer, 'do you have any idea what this mission is to be about?' 'No. I assume we'll find that out when he briefs us,' Ander replied, trying not to enter a conversation that could reveal his temporary unfamiliarity with protocol, yet trying to appear to be a good commander who was accompanying an inferior.
Eventually, they reached the Grand General's office. He wasn't called 'Grand General' for no reason. In addition to a myriad of weapons on his wall, he also had some beautiful artwork, some hundreds of years old, flaking and yellowing with their age, many elegant furniture pieces made of glistening, rich mahogany bearing inlaid patterns of this general's noble House, and a red rug with Leva's crest at each corner.
They didn't wait for the general alone. Two more imperials, one a man bearing a Lieutenant Senior Grade's insignia as well as the Erlenmeyer flask insignia of the Imperial Medic Corps, another a shockingly attractive woman in the Imperial somber green dress that a female mage would wear. Clearly, she was a mage of some sort, and Ander wondered what exactly their mission would be.
Withing a couple of minutes, the General himself came through the door with a nobleman's quicksilver grace, as well as endlessly refined military precision. His slightly greying dirty blonde hair, deep brown eyes, squared jaw and broad shouldered-frame dominated and surveyed the imperials waiting with all of the ease and skill of the combat general he was. The Imperials snapped to, and Ander snapped his most perfect salute, practiced while enjoying a most wonderful magitech cleaning tool called a 'shower.'
'Well met, allies of Leva. At ease, and please seat yourselves. I have an urgent mission for you, and this may take some time.' Eager to hear the ploy that would help his survival along, Ander took a chair, and began to listen.
