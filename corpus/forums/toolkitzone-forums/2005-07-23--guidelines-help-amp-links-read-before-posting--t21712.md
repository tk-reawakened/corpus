# Guidelines, Help &amp; Links - Read Before Posting

### Xavier · 2005-07-23 12:03 · #B236768

Guidelines
To make the best experience out of the art forum for all, we ask you to please follow these simple guidelines alongside the forum rules.
+ Use a descriptive title such as "Robot Sketch" as opposed to less descriptive ones such as "Look at this!".
+ If you're commenting on another's artwork, constructive criticism is always expected over outright insults. Instead of saying "It's bad", say why it's bad and give possible solutions. Same applies when complimenting art, give reasons.
+ Nudity is tolerated to a degree, and in accordance with forum rules we ask you to consult a moderator before posting if you feel your artwork may be suspect.
+ If you want to post updates of the progress of a single piece, a work in progress (WIP), put WIP in the subject and use that one topic - don't use several.
Help
Where can I upload an image?
There are several free hosts for images that you can use, but we recommend when possible to use your [url=../myfiles.php?mode=stuff]stuff folder[/url] which provides free space to all registered members. It also enables you to update images whereas external free hosts require you to upload an update to a new "address".
