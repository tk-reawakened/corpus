### venstor · 2005-07-29 02:25 · #B239304

Nice character art. Always good to have when making a sprite.
The character's size in relation to the tree seems to be about right by RPG standards. I might extend the trunk a bit so that the leaves start at about the sprite's eye level, though.
As for the face, you've given yourself plenty of room to work there. It really depends on what facial features you want to show. Do you want just eyes, eyes and a mouth, or a full face with eyes, mouth, and nose? Here's one way you could easily fit a whole face into that space:
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/marz1.gif)
As for the rest of the body, it looks a bit boxy to me. The bigger a sprite is, the less it looks good for it to be completely symmetrical. Round the sprite out a bit and try to make the pose a little less stiff.
Heh. I meant to just make a small edit, but I got carried away and ended up just doing the whole sprite. Here's how I'd do the sprite:
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/marz2.gif)
Obviously you'll need to do the sprite in your own style to fit the rest of your game, but these are my suggestions anyway.

### venstor · 2005-08-30 02:10 · #B252025

I don't really think any new colors are needed. Anti-aliasing is more used for making outlines look smooth than it is for blending between shading colors. The lines are just a little jagged between shades in some places.
Was the hair reduced in size from a larger image? It looks kind of blurry and not as crisp as the rest of the sprite. All in all, it looks pretty good. He looks a bit funny to me without ears, but that's just following the concept art.

![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/marz3.gif)
