# RPG Toolkit 2 - Powerhouse Version

### Xavier · 2005-07-30 15:15 · #B239805

The Powerhouse version, TK2 with some small additions, has not been available to purchase for two years and will never be available. Due to the open source nature of the far superior TK3, it was not logical to sell a far older version which is not longer supported nor developed.
So please do not post asking how or where to get the old Powerhouse versio as it is not available and hasn't been for two years now.
