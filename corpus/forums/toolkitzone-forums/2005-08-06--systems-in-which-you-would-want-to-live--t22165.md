# Systems in which you would want to live

### Haraldur · 2005-08-06 15:56 · #B242308

I am going to give a list of ideological systems, with a little explanation where necessary, and then people say what systems they would like to be in, or not, preferably with explanation.
Absalute capitalism: No taxes, no nothing. No state in fact - no national army, no national road system. If you want to get a road built then you pay for the labour and materials costs yourself etc.. Libertarians would love this. The police force would be funded by donations by individuals and companies. You get the idea. Could be called anarcho-capitalism.
Anarchy: No rules except mob rule! Absalute economic equalisty, a gift economy. No government, no state, no police, no army, just rule by the majority.
Anarcho-individualism: Every man for himself! This is probably the popular image of anarchy. No state, no rules, not even rule by the mob.
Anarcho-syndicalism: Government of factories and means of production by unions and worker's councils, similar for other rules. Living in communes around factories, otherwise as anarchy.
Communism (Marxism): This is 'true' communism, not communism as appeared in USSR, DPRK or China. Class struggle and revolution, control of factories by worker's councils, preceded by a socialist state that slowly disappears etc.. Read Das Kapital and Communist Manifesto.
Anarcho-communism: Basically the same as communism but skipping the socialist stage.
Maoism: China under Chairman Mao. Little Red Book. Etc..
Stalinism: USSR under Stalin, DPRK under Kim Il Sung and Kim Jong Il. Repressive, totalitarian, gulags.
Democratic Socialism: Absalute economic and legal equality. Democratic rule (whether direct or representative). No corporations, no rich, no poor. Can have esatblished religion, can be fundamentalist. Can be secular.
Dictatorial socialism: Warsaw Pact countries and USSR, for the most part. Absalute economic equality (except that the leaders are usually quite well off thankyou). Absalute power invested in either a person or a political party. Totalitarian. Can have established religion, can be fundamentalist. Can be secular.
National Socialism: Nazi Germany.
Fascism: Italy under Mussolini, Spain under Franco, Japan in 1930s and 1940s. Militaristic. Totalitarian. Nationalistic, possibly racist. Dictatorial. Can have established religion, can be fundamentalist. Can be secular.
Social democracy: Most of Europe. Capitalism, welfare state etc.. Can have established religion, can be fundamentalist. Can be secular.
Capitalistic democracy: USA. Similar to social democracy, but even more capitalistic.
Corporatism: Rule by corporations. Can have established religion etc..
Technocracy: Control of services and production by those who are experts in it (education controlled by an experienced teacher, say). Replacement of money with metric energy credits. Income equality. Direct democracy, but mostly libertarian in that majority rule would not affect such things as whether you are allowed to be a homosexual. [www.technocracy.ca](http://www.technocracy.ca) [www.technocracy.org](http://www.technocracy.org) Scientific governance.
____________________________________________________
Myself, I would want to live in a technocratic system, because it is sustainable, and equal, with the bonus of no politicians!
Second I would have a democratic socialist (secular) state. For the equality, and freedom.
States I would not want to live in:
Any fundamentalist ones. (Atheists like me would have trouble).
Any ones with established religion. (Unfortunately I live in one. Thankfully, people generally do not care a bit about religion anyway.)
National Socialism. (Should be self evident).
Fascism. (Should be self evident.)
Corporatism. (Hell, no!)
Absalute capitalism (Not likely).
Anarcho-individualism (I would fear for my life).
Maoism. (Cultural revolution, anyone?)
Stalinism. (No gulag for me thanks).
Dictatorial socialism. (I like freedom of speech).
Social democracy. (A failing system!)
Capitalistic democracy. (Faling even more!)
The rest would be OK.
You?
