# Just something I cooked up for fun -more-

### Zap! · 2005-08-11 20:39 · #B244434

A Walk in My Shoes
Imagine, 1950, Chicago, on a bus, without a coat. The goose-bumps look like millions of little peebles etched into your skin as you sit there shivering. You used your last bit of money to pay for the bus-fair and now are pondering what you will do for dinner. Then out of providence's grace, you spy a man collecting money for a childrens hospital.
Ok ok, so you might not agree with me on this being out of anythings grace when I tell you what to imagine next, but hey, everyone's gotta eat.
Now imagine, you get off the bus at the next bus stop, shove your way through the masses of people huddling around some newspaper stand and make your way to the collector. Without realizing it (or at least faking you didn't realize this) you slip on ice and fall into the man. On your way past you grab a twenty out of the money plate. The guy doesn't even notice, you just apologize and he helps you up and goes on with his business. You wonder why there are so many theives these days.
Now like I said, the cause for the money is a nice idea and all, but hey the childrens hospitals only get like a quarter on the dollar anyway. Ok ok, back to the story.
You put the money in your pocket and turn the next corner and follow the distant glow to the next hotdog stand, buy one and just pour the ketchup on, you don't mean to of course, the ice has frozen the tip of the ketchup bottle though and you have to get it out somehow. Whats a dog without ketchup you think? ( A dry one.. your mind responds ) You stumble through the crowds of people looking for a nice place to get warm, sensing the glow of a distant fire you travel in that direction. As you arrive, you join the huddle and retreive the hotdog from under your shirt and start to eat. The ketchup feels cold on your stomach as you wish the hotdog stand was closer. Then you look up and see the faces of the people standing there beside you, they smell the savory hotdog and you feel just enough pity for them to toss them a some money to go buy some. Afterall, more fire for you.
Now, let's take a break from this. This guy seems to be pretty greedy eh? Maybe, maybe not. Anyhow, he was my grandfather. And he left my grandmother before my mom was born to go off to chase some crazy dream that didn't work out. And ended up being a homeless person who ended up stealing from the wrong people, which did get him a roof over his head. Well, more like a floor, the floor of the jail seeing as he had a basement cell.
Anyhow, so this story isn't about him, he made his decisions and no one likes him now becuase of it, he tried to commit suicide shortly after and got put in an Asylum, the entirity of the news hit my grandmother hard, but he was always going off and spending all his money to get drunk and then gambling. In some ways he did my grandma a favor by leaving, now she could care for her kid and herself without that goat spending all the money, but it still hurt, deep-down knowing it was a hard time for him. You see, he started doing this crazy shit when his dad died, he just couldn't take it, the only friend he had in the whole world passed away. ( His wife was his lover - not his friend )
Well, the only family she had left happened to live in Kentucky, which is where she ended up moving in with her cousin. Got a job, and took steady care of her son and newborn girl. ( Who happens to be my mom )
- 54 years later -
In the present day, I have a good life. My grandpa is living in Flordia happily with his dog and trailor park friends, and my grandma has re-married. She has 2 more kids now too, who knew huh? She's doing great, despite the abuse from her husband so long ago.
I guess my entire family is a survivor family, my mom had to endure many nights sleeping in the open without food becuase well, her aunt was a very very mean person. And her mom did the best she could, but had no way to get their own house so they had to depend on her cousin. Anyhow, the amount of neglect and abuse she had to go through was much worse then I could write about in this school report, and the stories my dad have told me, I still shiver about. Thankfully my parents arn't those kinds of people. And I'm pretty thankful to have such parents, even though I think they are wrong many-a time in punishing me!
-- END --
