# Short Story Contest - 1st Place - Blob Dude

### Xavier · 2005-08-12 13:02 · #B244764

Although the story topics themselves are locked, feel free to discuss entries with other members on this forum.
The Three
On what was supposed to be a nice, summers day turned out to be a day of fog and drizzle. Typical English weather, especially when a nice day was what Katie Jones wanted. Katie; a pretty, bright, friendly 21 year old blonde girl who was moving house that day to a new area. After completing her college course in dance, she had decided to move out of Plymouth city centre and go south to a small rural town in Cornwall, where there was a calling for dance and drama instructors.
Arriving in the new town, Katie couldnt really see much of what the area looked like from all the thick fog that was drifting through the narrow streets, weaving in an out of the small alleyways. Sightseeing was something she had planned for later on anyway. Driving carefully through the deserted streets, she finally found her new home; (redacted: address) in the main street. Pulling up outside the building, Katie got out of the car and took a look at the old stone building. The building and the whole area looked really old, at least 200 years old for some of the buildings nearby. Moving quickly to the back of her car, Katie opened the boot and grabbed as many boxes as she could carry in her arms and proceeded to take them to her new home. Struggling to unlock the door without dropping everything, she climbed the staircase to her top floor flat in the two-story building. Getting the last of her stuff, Katie locked her car and got inside before the drizzle turned into a downpour. After about an hour of unpacking, she finally had most of the essentials set up and now she just wanted to sit down with a nice cup of hot chocolate and listen to the rain getting heavier and heavier outside. Being on the top floor, the sound of the rain hitting the skylights in the attic above made an interesting sound echo through the flat. Something she would have to get used to if she was going to sleep well that night!
The sound of a car alarm ringing through the early morning streets gave Katie an unwanted awakening the next day. Stretching an arm out of her bed, she felt around for her watch on the bedside table and took a look at it. 8.30am. Groaning and weary eyed, Katie reluctantly got out of bed and opened the curtains to be greeted by grey skys. There was no point in going back to sleep, she thought, since there was a whole new town to explore - which most likely had clothes shops in it! After getting washed and dressed, Katie quickly made some toast and tea before going out. Putting on her long brown coat and scarf, Katie headed out into town. Walking down the street, she could tell that it was a very close-knit community since people kept looking at her as if to say Who is she and what is she doing in our town? Ignoring them, Katie continued her walk to do some window shopping and possibly buy some food for lunch. Walking down the street she smiled to herself and thought that at least it wasnt raining today, but no sooner had the thought crossed her mind, the clouds opened up and it tipped it down. It was like the sky was throwing buckets of water on the world! Looking around quickly, Katie noticed a small alleyway between one of the shops. The two buildings were so close together that their roofs enclosed the alleyway and kept the rain out, so Katie decided to run into there to try and escape the rain.
Cold and a bit wet, Katie looked around at this strange little alleyway and wondered why they didnt just join the two buildings together since they were so close. Either way, it added to the character of the whole area and gave the town a certain feel to it. It suddenly occurred to her that she didnt know where the alley went, if anywhere, and being the curious type Katie decided to take a wander a bit further down the passageway. Not expecting to find anything of interest, she was surprised to see another small alleyway that led to a small out-of-the-way second-hand shop. Peering through the dirty, old shaped glass to see if it was open, Katie noticed a light on inside. Opening the small wooden door, she was greeted with a face full of dust and musty old smells from times long ago.
Glancing around her to see what was in the there, it appeared to be some sort of antiques shop selling things from rot-ridden wooden chairs to full suits of armour. In the far corner was a counter and there stood behind it, a small old lady, hunched over slightly and wearing clothes that looked like antiques themselves. Uninterested, Katie went to leave, but something in the shop caught her eye  a jewelled red egg no bigger than her hand, seated on a little gold stand in the window. Reaching to pick it up, the egg gave her what felt like an electric shock through her hands, yet didnt hurt. Dismissing it, Katie called out to the old lady; How much is this egg? The woman looked up sharply, as though she had been disturbed from something important and frowned. Five pounds fifty the old woman croaked.
That isnt very expensive, Katie thought, but didnt complain since she thought it would look nice on her fireplace. Upon getting home later, Katie set the jewelled, shiny red metallic looking egg on her mantle piece and thought nothing more of it.
That night though, Katie was awoken by the sound of a humming coming from her lounge. Getting out of bed, she shuffled across her room and rubbed her eyes, and opened the door to find that the egg had an eerie red glow around it and it was the source of the humming sound! Creeping over to have a closer look at it, she heard a sound come from behind her. Turning around, Katie saw three men in black outfits and night vision goggles standing there in the middle of the room. She noticed that the man in the middle of the three has a glowing blue gem attached to the back of his left hand. But before Katie knew what was happening, he thrust his hand out and a bolt of blue electricity flews towards her! Panic and adrenaline flowed through her whole body and just as it was about to hit her, a big red glow surrounded Katie and deflected the bolt back at the three men, electrocuting and killing them instantly.
Her heart pounded against her chest, shaking and in shock, Katie ran out of her flat and down into the street, barefoot in the rain, wearing nothing but a long t-shirt and a dressing gown. Scared and confused, she fell to her knees in the road crying, when a man appeared out of the darkness and approached her.
Dont be afraid he said in a deep and hushed voice. Taking Katie by the hand, he helped her to her feet and put his coat around her. Come. Lets get out of the rain; I have a safe place to stay in. Unsure of what was going on, Katie followed the man to a small bed sit nearby. The bed sit was dark and damp room with one dirty window looking out into the street and peeling wallpaper. An old broken door led to a small bathroom at the back of the room. The sofa was ripped and the bed had the rain leaking through the roof onto it, it looked as if no one had lived here for years. Who... Who are you? Katie asked nervously. Dont worry yourself with that. Go into the other room, theres a change of clothes and a shower you can use. Ill explain more later on. Reluctantly walking into the small bathroom, Katie pulled a cord to turn on the light. The bulb flickered into life to reveal a toilet, sink, cracked tiles on the wall and a small chair with clothes on it. The shower was a mess; it looked as if it would make you dirty rather than clean you, but what choice did she have? Katie darent go home in case some more of those men came back and for all she knew the man in the other room could be some maniac wanting to rape her! Despite all that, she still couldnt work out what was going on or how she survived the bolt of electricity the men shot at her.
After cleaning herself up and putting on the new clothes, that were strangely her size, Katie went into the main room with lots of questions on her mind.
Sit down. The man said suddenly, There isnt much time and a lot of information to take in! Katie quickly sat down a small wooden chair, What kind of infor-- The man interrupted suddenly, Shh! he said wide eyed and frowning. A noise was heard outside; pulling out a gun from under his coat they waited in silence for a few minutes, looking around he put his gun back under his coat. Shrugging it off, he reached down over the side of his chair and opened a small box producing Katies jewelled egg.
How did you get that! Katie exclaimed suddenly, It was in my flat! Carefully putting the egg on the table nearby, the man leaned forward. How I got it is not the matter at hand, but how you got it. Now Katie was even more confused, What do you mean? I bought it from a strange little antique shop in town. Anyone could have got it. The man leaned back and tapped his chin, looking deep in thought. What you have bought is the once thought mythical magic item. This egg isnt supposed to exist, yet here it is.
Wait, magical item? Katie asked confused. Are you crazy? The man gave her a look that could have killed her. Do you not remember what happened to those three men in your flat? How you survived and what they tried to kill you with? He asked angrily, getting impatient. Katie thought back, Yes But I thought they had some kind of electronic gun! The man shook his head. The blue crystal in one of their hands is a fragment of a bigger object like your egg. Those fragments are dangerous and unreliable though because they are forcefully bonded to the person with a new type of surgery.
Stop, Stop! Katie yelled, getting up and walking around holding her head. I dont understand whats going on!
Then sit down and listen! The man said sternly. And keep an open mind, for what Im about to tell you will not sound logically possible. Katie nervously sat back down and listened intently to what the man had to say.
There are The Three. They are three of the richest men in the country, and also the main collectors of magical items. One of The Three has six items; the other, four; and the last have three. All of these join together perfectly to make a pyramid shape, but there is still space for one more on the top which they have all been searching after for many years now. They have come to believe that it no longer exists or never did.
What happens when they all join together? Is there a point to it? Katie asked inquisitively.
They believe that once all the items are joined, the ultimate power force will be created from all the different aspects of the single Magical Item properties. But when The Three got together to try this, nothing happened so they all took their own items and went their separate ways. On the way out there was an ambush and some of the items were stolen from The Three. Each one of them thought it was the other that set them up and so started an age old feud between them all that has been going for at least 65 years, causing much bloodshed and misery.
64 years! Katie interrupted, How old are the three men now? How can they possibly fight each other they would all have to be at least 80 odd, surely?
The man cleared his throat, Yes they are all in their 80s theoretically, but somehow one or more of the Items has caused them to age extremely slowly, so they all look about late 30s to early 40s.
Thats just crazy! All of this sounds insane! Just how do you know so much about all of this anyway? Katie asked, afraid of the answer she might get.
I know because I was the person that led the ambush on them! I used to be the main confidante of one of The Three and so I knew everything that was going on. But upon stealing the Items, some of them bonded to us, something that The Three could never get them to do. Items only bond to certain people of their choosing and none of The Three were ever bonded, until recent advances in their medical magic research allowed them to forcefully bond people to fragments of Items  like that man that attacked you.
So why didnt I die? Katie asked, I remember seeing a red glow around me, then a bright flash and suddenly they were all dead!
Thats because you must be bonded to your Item, the jewelled egg. Subconsciously you did something that made the Item protect you; bonded Items are under your control once you learn how to use them.
Katie still looked puzzled by all this, I still dont understand what I have to do with all this, though.
Isnt it obvious? You have the last piece to the puzzle, the Item that doesnt exist! Some one in one of The Threes groups must have spotted you with it and now they are all after it. Its every man for himself now; each of The Three will be fighting each other to get all the Items for themselves, and at the same time they will be hunting you down for the final piece.
A look of horror came over Katies face as all the information sunk in and dawned on her. Staring blankly at the man in front, she slowly started to try and speak, But what if I destroy my egg! Then no one can come after me! She suddenly made a grab for the egg on the table, but the man was too quick and grabbed it. That wouldnt be a good idea. He said coldly.
GIVE IT TO ME! Katie screamed, making a dash for the man. Just as she came towards him, he pulled out his gun and pointed it at her face.
Sit down! The man said sternly, If you destroy an Item youre bonded to it can kill you! The backlash of energy from the Item goes straight to the bonded person and destabilises them on a molecular level  in other words, they explode. Ive seen it happen to people that have undergone forceful bonding, its not a pretty sight. Katie slumped to the floor exhausted from all this strange new information her mind was trying to understand and comprehend.
Suddenly the window smashed and through it came a metal tube and landed on the floor in front of Katie. The man grabbed her arm and quickly pulled her to her feet yelling, We have to get out of here NOW! But it was too late, the tube suddenly burst into life spraying gas everywhere in the room and before they could get to the door, they collapsed unconscious.
A few hours had passed when Katie finally awoke to find herself tied to a chair in what looked like a room in a stately manor. Cautiously looking around the room at all the antique collectable furniture, suits of armour and big oil paintings that adorned the walls; she could see that she was alone. She noticed a clock on a mantelpiece; it read 7.30am. Katie tried to wriggle herself out of the ropes on her wrists and ankles, but they were too tight. Just then the door swung open with the man she met earlier being pushed through, all cut up and bruised. He fell to the floor in a heap of torn clothes and blood, followed by two large men who each gave him a kick as they passed by. Following them was a middle aged man in an expensive looking suit, Have you made yourself at home, Miss Jones? said the man in a well spoken voice. I do hope so, because you may just be spending a long time here in my wonderfully isolated house! With that, he laughed and left with the other two men, locking the door behind them.
Looking around in panic, Katie desperately tried to get out of the ropes that bound her limbs again, but to no avail. Then her eyes landed on the man slumped by the door, Hey! She yelled, Wake up! You better not be dead!
Shuffling her body in the chair, Katie tried to get her chair to move across the room towards the man. Getting about halfway there, a chair leg got snagged on a rug and Katie lost her balance, falling over. Screaming angrily, she squirmed around trying to get back up, but ended up rolling over on her side.
The man groaned suddenly and tried to move, but just rolled over murmuring something. Katie, who was lying on the floor facing away from him, tried to shuffle closer again. Did you say something? She asked desperately.
Mmmff Came the reply. The The bag
Katie looked around for a bag, but couldnt see anything properly from the floor. Shuffling around some more, Katie notices a dark grey bag on one of the old sideboard cupboards at the side of the room. Looking towards the man on the floor, she says, I cant reach the bag! What has it got in it anyway?
The man just lay there. Katie stared at him for what felt like a lifetime, tears starting to roll down her face, as the realisation that she may die here dawned on her. All of a sudden the man rolled over, and using all of his strength, pushed himself up into a sitting position and in a croaky voice said, The bag, it has the egg in Use it! Then he collapsed to the floor again. Looking up at the bag, Katie remembered what the man had told her earlier about it being bonded and under her control. What he hadnt said was how to actually use it. But before she had the chance to try and attempt to control the Item, a click was heard in the lock of the door. The door creaked open and in walked the well spoken suited man that came in earlier, but this time he was alone. Stepping over the man slumped on the floor, with a distasteful look on his face; he walked over to where Katie was lying and stood in front of her and looked down.
So, He started, youre the young woman that has found the Item that grandparents tell their childrens children about. He stared at Katie, and looked almost as if he wanted to laugh at what he had just said. He continued, Now, you do realise that this has put you in some-what of a predicament, dont you? What with Mr. Luther over there, He paused for a moment and glanced across at the man lying on the floor in his own blood. Continuing, he said, With him telling you all the intricate details of our establishment, Im afraid we cant actually let you leave here with that knowledge.
Reaching for his inside pocket, he pulled out a small, black oblong box. Flipping it open, he took out a syringe and tapped it a couple of times.
Dont worry. He said calmly, This wont hurt a bit, and as far as the world is concerned, you will have had an unexpected heart attack.
Katies eyes widened with horror as she looked up at the man, the needle tip glistening in the early morning sunlight coming through the window. Fear took a hold of her entire body as her death sentence came closer. She closed her eyes tight, tears streaming down her face, and waited for the end. Someone screamed, a thud and a crash was heard. Katie opened her eyes to see that her friend Mr. Luther had found the energy to jump on the man with the syringe, knocking him to the floor. Crawling across to Katie, he started to undo the ropes binding her wrists. Katie screamed suddenly as the other man smashed an old vase over Mr. Luthers head, rendering him unconscious.
Fine! The man said, Well have to do it the old-fashioned way! Grabbing a sharp knife-like letter opener that had fell on the floor, he went to stab Katie. Inside Katie felt anger and adrenaline build up quickly and powerfully, and just as the knife was about to rip through her, that big red glow surrounded her again knocking the knife from her captors hand. He stood up quickly, looking confused. You Youre bonded to the last Item! It cant be! He yelled angrily, turning to run away. The glow that surrounded Katie suddenly started to spark like it had electricity running though it. Frozen and scared, the man just watched in horror as the glow became more electrified. Unexpectedly it shrunk, looking almost as if it had disappeared, then from nowhere it expanded at an incredible rate hitting the man, thrusting him backwards across the room and through the window. The man screamed loudly as the glass smashed around him, shredding his back open as he fell three floors to his death on the hard concrete pathway below.
Back inside the room, Katie saw the letter opener near her on the floor. Thankfully Mr. Luther has loosened the ropes on one of her arms enough to squeeze her hand out from. Reaching for the letter opener, she started to cut away at the other ropes that held her in the chair. After eventually freeing herself, Katie tried to wake up her unconscious friend. Looking around the room, she noticed a cabinet of drinks. Running over to it, she found a bottle of soda water that was used for making cocktails. Taking it back over to the man, she squirted it in his face to bring him around. He eventually woke up, unsure of what had happened.
Welcome back, Mr. Luther. Katie smiled at him. He looked at her shocked.
Puzzled he said, How do you know my name? Katie glanced at the broken window, The man out there mentioned it, before all this crazy stuff started to happen! Mr. Luther was now even more confused than before. Struggling to his feet, he made his way to the window and looked out through it. He turned around suddenly and stared at Katie.
What is it? She asked, Are more people coming?
Do you realise who that was! Mr. Luther exclaimed, He was Edward Banks  one of The Three! How did this happen?
Katie looked around at what was left of the bag on the sideboard; the Items energy has burned most of it away.
You managed to control it? Mr. Luther said shocked. Thats impressive!
Well, Katie started, I think Im starting to understand it Just then, a large amount of running footsteps were heard coming down the corridor towards their room. They both stared at the door, then hurriedly tried to find a way they could escape. Theres no way out other than that door! Mr. Luther yelled. Katie stopped, walked over to the sideboard and picked up the egg and just stood facing the door. Before Mr. Luther had chance to question her, the room flooded with about 20 armed men. Katie just stood there and glared at them.
Get behind me! She called across to Mr. Luther, who was by the window still. He dashed across and stood behind her. The group of men just laughed and jeered at the two of them, until Katie held up the egg Item which had now started to glow. Baffled, the men raised their guns towards Katie, ready to shoot. Katie let out an angry scream suddenly, and the glow surrounded her and Mr. Luther too this time. Bullets began to fly towards them both rapidly, but got bounced off the glow protecting them, spraying shrapnel and bullets everywhere around the room and back towards the men, killing some of them.
When they had depleted all of their bullets, the men looked at one another and their fallen comrades, unsure of what to do now. Before they had another moment to think, Katie held the tip of the egg at them and out shot a concentrated beam of red energy, slicing though all who were left standing.
The glow surrounding them faded away, and Katie fell to her knees, drained of energy. Mr. Luther just stood and looked at her for a moment, taking a deep breath, he said, In all my years of working with Items, Ive never seen anything quite like that! Katie was just kneeling down on the floor shaking. Just then she groaned and through up, Those Those men just, they just split open everywhere..! I killed them! She screamed, I killed them!
Mr. Luther helped Katie to her feet and hugged her tightly, Dont worry. Dont think about it, it was you or them. Now we must get out of here before any more people come to find us! Picking up the Item, Mr. Luther took Katie by the hand and led her past the pile of bodies that lay in front of the doorway.
They ran down lots of long corridors, desperately looking for a way out of the mansion they were trapped in.
They came to a big staircase and cautiously looked down it and around to make sure there was no one else around. Creeping down the stairs, they saw the main entrance ahead of them. Quickly making their way to it, they went to open the door. As they opened it to leave, Mr. Luther saw a car outside with some men getting out of it. He shut the door quickly and tried to find a way to block it.
Whats going on? Katie asked puzzled, Isnt that the way out? Mr. Luther pressed his back against the door to try and stop anyone getting in. Yes it is, but we have another problem now  the other two men of The Three are here and have just got out of a car - together! It seems they have all been working together to get this last Item, so they must desperately want it whatever the cost! Katies face turned white; leaning against the post at the bottom of the stairs hand rail, she slid to the floor.
Dont sit down! Mr. Luther yelled at her, We have to get out of here!
Just then the door started banging. People were shouting on the other side of it. I cant hold this door closed all on my own! Mr. Luther shouted. Suddenly a gunshot was heard and Mr. Luther fell to the floor grasping his thigh, blood gushing out everywhere. Katie screamed and ran across pulling him away from the door. A few more gunshots were heard as the people on the other side shot the lock off. The door was kicked open and in walked two smartly dressed men holding guns.
Well, well, well, if it isnt my old confidante, Mr. Luther! The first man said, looking at him and Katie huddled together on the floor. This is a pleasant surprise indeed! He looked back out through the door and clicked his fingers. Two butch men came in. Take these two to the study. The two butch men picked up Mr. Luther and Katie and ushered them off to a study just down the hallway and locked them in. The study was a small room with an expensive looking desk and big leather chair in it. A small filing cabinet was pushed into one corner. Mr. Luther looked around, examining everything in the room, then he tore a sleeve off his jacket and tied it tightly around his thigh to stop the blood and ease the pain of the bullet wound.
So what do we do now? Katie asked nervously looking around.
Mr. Luther thought for a moment. Have you still got the Item with you? He asked Katie. She felt her pockets quickly and pulled it out.
Concentrate and try to get that beam again, to blow open the door. Mr. Luther said to Katie. She looked at him like she was going to protest, but then just tried to do as he said. Aiming the Item towards the door, she concentrated hard on getting it to shoot out a beam of energy again. After a few minutes of trying, she was about to give up when the egg started to glow again. When she saw this, it gave her the confidence that she could do it! Katie concentrated harder this time and the glow got bigger and bigger until it was almost humming.
Just then, a key clicked in the lock and the door handle started to turn, but it was too late; the Item shot out a concentrated beam of energy at the door, blowing it and part of the surrounding wall to pieces! As the dust cleared, they both saw that there was a man lying under the rubble, with blood on his face. Upon closer inspection, they could see it was another of The Three. As they stepped over the rubble and broken wood, the last of The Three, Mr. Luthers old boss, met them in the hallway. In his hand he held a slick black cane with a purple crystal on top as the handle. Dusting down his suit and narrowing his eyes, he said, So you want to play it like that do you? He raised his cane, the crystal handle glowing, and slammed it to the floor causing a shockwave of energy to come off in all directions, throwing Mr. Luther and Katie back a few feet to the floor. Then, waving his cane around some more, a strange looking purple mist came from the crystal and snaked towards Mr. Luther, surrounding his waist like rope. Slamming the cane to the floor again, an electrical shock was sent through the mist and into Mr. Luther causing him to scream a lot. The man suddenly pulled the cane up and towards him, raising Mr. Luther off the ground, suspending him in the air by the purple mist. The man then started to reel him in like a fish on a line.
Now its time to pay the consequences of your actions against us, Mr. Luther! The man yelled at him, pulling out another Item from inside his jacket. This one was a sharp jagged fragment of another Item. Katie went to get up, but the man yelled at her, Dont you move anywhere, or Ill make Mr. Luthers death slower and more painful! But before he could say anything more, Mr. Luther smashed the man in the head with a brick he had grabbed just before he was lifted off the ground. Everyone fell to the floor in a heap. Katie quickly got up and grabbed her Item and pulled Mr. Luther to his feet. They ran down the hallway and into a big kitchen.
Quickly looking around them, they saw a back door leading outside. Rushing through it together, they found themselves in what looked like the old servants entrance to the manor home.
We need a faster way to travel! Katie screamed at Mr. Luther. Desperately looking around, Mr. Luther saw some stables, In there! He urged, taking Katies arm and running towards the stable block. They pulled open the big wooden door with a loud creak that echoed through the surrounding fields. Inside were two horses, tied up. Katie ran up to the strongest looking horse and started to look for a saddle.
We havent got time for that! Mr. Luther shouted, untying the horse. Jump on with me! They both got on the horse and rode it bareback out of the stable as fast as it could run. They galloped off across the fields for what seemed like 15 minutes, until they came to a forest. Stopping the horse, they both examined the dark, dank forest ahead of them. Then out of no where, a big flash of light flew by them and crashed into a tree trunk, exploding and throwing bits of bark everywhere. Looking behind them, to their horror, was the man with the cane they had left in the mansion, riding towards them on the other horse!
It looks like we have no choice but to enter the forest now! Mr. Luther yelled, kicking the horse to get it moving faster. As they rode through the forest, weaving in and out of trees, jumping over fallen branches and small bushes, the man behind them kept shooting balls of energy at them from his Item on the cane. Bits of tree and bark were exploding and flying everywhere from the near misses; scaring the horse making it difficult to ride properly.
Katie lent forward to Mr. Luthers ear and shouted, Im going to try and fight back! Keep the horse steady if you can! He glanced around quickly at Katie with an unsure look on his face, but could do nothing about her decision. With one arm clasped tightly around Mr. Luthers waist, she carefully tried to get her Item out of her pocket without dropping it. Holding it tightly in her hand, she concentrated really hard and the beam of energy shot out of the tip of the egg again in the wrong direction, blowing a hole through some trees. Focussing her aim this time, she directed the egg towards the man on the horse behind her. The man raised his cane to let out another bolt of energy, but just as he did, Katie sent a beam of energy his way and it collided with his shot. The two energy sources mixing reacted and went back into the closest Item to them. The closest was the mans purple crystal on the cane! The cane started to vibrate rapidly and the man couldnt let go of it. His horse got scared and bucked, throwing the man to the ground. He screamed as he tried prising his own hand from the cane, but it was like he had been welded to it.
Katie and Mr. Luther didnt stop and just carried on riding until they got to the edge of the forest. They slowed down once they were out and stopped the horse so they could see what was happening.
A big purple and red glow came from within the forest, casting shadows of the trees across the grassy meadows. A huge beam of light shone upwards to the sky from the centre of the forest, causing the clouds in the sky to darken. Lightning suddenly struck down the centre of the beam of light a few times and white light flashed brightly. Everything stopped as quickly as it had started and fell silent. Katie and Mr. Luther looked at each other in wonder. Then from nowhere it seemed, the whole forest exploded with a sound like thunder, and fell about 20 feet down into a fiery crater.
Mr. Luther and Katie just stared in shock at what had just happened. Dismounting from the horse, they slowly walked towards where the forest once was, only moments ago. Looking into the crater, all they could see was broken, burnt trees and fire everywhere. They decided to leave quickly in case it decided to explode again.
So now we will never know what all the Items do together, will we? Katie asked. Mr. Luther just shook his head and walked back towards the horse.
Its probably better than we never do, considering what just happened. He said solemnly. Katie agreed and jumped back up on the horse with Mr. Luther. As they rode off together, Katie looked at her Item and quietly put it back in her pocket unsure of what she would do with it now.
As the day turned to evening, they eventually found a main road and made their way back into the town, hoping to carry on with life as before.
But they both knew it would never be the same again...
