Well, here's a few tips: spread the arms out a bit as they go down, it looks more natural. Don't make the legs pop out of his waist like that, it makes it look like he has an abnormally large pelvis. This is a bad thing.
Other than that, they're a bit small - but maybe I've just gotten used to big sprites. The character design is a bit bland, he could use a bit more personality - use Golden Sun characters for inspiration - you won't have such detailed clothes, but you can tell just by looking at a GS char that they have distinct personalities. This guy looks a bit NPC-ish, albiet a soldier NPC.
Otherwise, excellent for a first attempt. I'd dig up my first sprite but it's on my old (and occupied) computer. Some other time. Smile
