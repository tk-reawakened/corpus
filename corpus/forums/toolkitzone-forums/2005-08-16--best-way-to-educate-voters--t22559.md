# best way to educate voters...

### US Patriot · 2005-08-16 06:57 · #B246398

I know this sounds like I mean propaganda, but that's not what I'm talking about. If I give an example it might be a bit easier to understand.
At my old job just before the elections I asked my mangers who they would vote for. The answers were astounding. Two of them said they would vote for Kerry because they liked Democrats. Sounds reasonable right? Well, I asked them why they liked Democrats and they couldn't give me an answer. I started naming things on the Democratic platform and asked them if they were for them, but they were against just about everything on the platform. Another of my managers hated both candidates. When I asked her why she had a fairly common reason why she didn't like Kerry (wasn't trustworthy or something like that), but she didn't like Bush because she couldn't get any 2x4 boards at Home Depot during the Iraq War.
I'm not picking on just ignorant Democratic voters. There are idiots on all sides (Republican, Libertarian, Green, Socialist, and Independent).
I guess my question is, "How do we get voters to understand what they are voting for without giving any bais?" I don't think the Democratic process works properly when people vote for someone and don't know why.
