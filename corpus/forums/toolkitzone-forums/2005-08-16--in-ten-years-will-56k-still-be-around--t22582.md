Satellite is just far too expensive, and too slow, to ever become popular. It can compete with Cable TV only because speed doesn't matter a whole lot when your talking TV. Most Satellite connections are relatively slow, and yet cost anywhere between $60 and $100+ per month, plus equipment costs ranging from $100 to $500+. Since the majority can get broadband through much cheaper and faster means like Cable and DSL, Satellite is only for those that can't get either, and yet still have enough reason to spend the extra $ for more speed. Unless Satellite internet providers like DirectTV can bring down the overall price, and increase the speed, Satellite will remain an extremely small market; and considering how fast the various wired internets are picking up in speed, it's pretty unlikely that it will ever be a major competitor.

### Talyn · 2005-08-17 14:56 · #B247028

We here in the UK are resorting more to capped Broadband services. See [here](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/viewtopic.php?t=22524) for an explanation of capped services (my second post).
