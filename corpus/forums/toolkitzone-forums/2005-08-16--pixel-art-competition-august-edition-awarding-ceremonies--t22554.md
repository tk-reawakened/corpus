# Pixel Art Competition(August Edition - Awarding Ceremonies)

### Khin · 2005-08-16 02:43 · #B246333

Welcome to the August Edition of this year's Pixel Art Competition.
In our previous contests, participiants were challenged by dealing with humanoid figures. While it may have been a learning experience, it has proven quite difficult for some TK artist to perfect the human anatomy.
********************GUIDELINES*************************
This month, we'll take a break at bionics and move to an easier theme: weaponry.
All you have to do is draw a weapon of your choice; may it be medieval, contemporary, or futuristic. If the weapon you submitted isn't very popular, please do explain what it is so we would have a better understanding of it.
A good example would be this:
![](http://www.geocities.com/piartsco/sword.gif)
Type: melee/sword
Name: Ragdavene
Colors: 25
Size: 200x200
Title: Weapon of Choice
Color Limit: 32
Canvas Size: min - 100x100, max 300x300
Deadline:September 1
Each contestant is entitled one(1) entry only.
***************Stage is Set********************
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/157/stage-august.png)
Sorry. This isn't really a "stage", but I didn't expect anyone would be making non-linear perspective so I couldn't make a stage that could accommodate all styles together. Sad
Same voting rule:
*********Voting Rules**************
1.) All TKZ members can vote
2.) Each voter is given 9 points to be distributed among the entries
3.) Edited votes will not be counted.
4.) No represented votes(e.g. "Hey, XXX asked me to vote for him so here they are...")
Start voting!
******************Awarding Ceremonies****************
Congratulations to this month's winners. Actually there are many great and improving entries...but don't feel sorry! You all deserve recognitions for your efforts. Just practice more Wink
And now...
1st Place - Gurgan
![](http://www.geocities.com/brgy_sec/trophy_gurgan.png)
No doubt about this work. Pure pixel work dithered and textured to a tee. It got the texture nailed down; what with its worn-out and arabian impression. Very nice indeed. I wonder how it could have been better though.
2nd Place - Venstor
![](http://www.geocities.com/brgy_sec/trophy_venstor.png)
An equally deserving piece of work. Although people may claim this one's better, and IMO it looks the part(more polished to say the least), I'm sure the creator would completely understand why its put on second place by the majority.
3rd Place - seveneternal
![](http://www.geocities.com/brgy_sec/trophy_7e.png)
His first time to join, yet he managed to grab the bronze. Surreal imagination. I really like the combination and the weapons design. Very creative...and he's not (yet) a pixeler so he says.
Congratulations everyone, and to those who joined, don't get discouraged. Practice harder.
For those who are still not joining...you don't have anything to lose. Razz
