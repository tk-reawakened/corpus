# Rough Draft, 1st paragraph

### Kirbykid · 2005-08-17 04:55 · #B246883

Fagus Sukai could only lie there, blood blossoming from his arm as he watched the mysterious hooded man fade away into the forest deep. He had a combonation feeling of nausea, pain and shock, while the red liquid poured more and more, tainting the grass.
"Is this, what it feels like... to die?" questioned Fagus, his voice cracking. He rolled over, causing the wound to spill more and more, as blood was drained from his limpless body. Fagus shot a prayer of hope at god, and then things went fuzzy.
