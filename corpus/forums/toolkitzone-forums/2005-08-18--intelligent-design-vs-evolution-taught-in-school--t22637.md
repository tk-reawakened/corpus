# Intelligent design vs Evolution taught in school?

### SidZero · 2005-08-18 20:17 · #B247606

[President Bush seems to think that the education system should be teaching intelligent design as an alternative to evolution.](http://news.yahoo.com/s/space/20050818/sc_space/intelligentdesignandevolutionatthewhitehouse)
While I'll agree that different schools of thought should be taught to kids to allow them to make their own decisions as to what to believe in, I don't think intelligent design should be taught as an ALTERNATIVE to evolution, because I don't think that it IS an alternative to evolution. I think intelligent design should be taught as a possible means of spurning evolution: IE: the idea that "god" made animals evolve into other animals. But it also shouldn't be taught as the only one. Science itself has no explantion for why evolution happens other than random mutations and natural selection... something I also happen to disagree with, I don't see why it shouldn't be taught as a possible means to an end.
I have a feeling that this is going to turn into a debate as to which one is right, but that's not what I'm intending. I'd like this to be a debate as to whether or not it should be taught in school, and how it should be taught in school if so.
