### EB · 2005-08-20 03:04 · #B248172

![](http://www.cdrinfo.com/Sections/Articles/Sources/MSI_915G_Combo/Images/pci_express.jpg)
The top one is a PCIe x16 slot for graphics cards. The two below that are PCIe x1 slots, which are supposed to be replacing standard PCI slots.
The rest below that are obviously standard PCI slots.
The easiest way to tell whether your motherboard is PCI-Express compliant is to either look up your board on the manufacturer website, or download a program such as [Everest](http://www.lavalys.com), which gives you a very detailed system summary.
