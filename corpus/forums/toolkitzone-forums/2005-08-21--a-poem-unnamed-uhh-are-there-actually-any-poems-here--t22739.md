# A poem, unnamed.. uhh.. are there actually any poems here?

### Merli · 2005-08-21 22:27 · #B248713

Ah well, this is one I wrote about one year ago.. Some parts might not make sense, but heh, this is one of the few poems I've written which I actually like.
---
This artwork we create,
it has never been the same,
constanly changing,
since I were eight.
From the moment you came,
My skins the paper, can you see what we drew?
Chaotic lines, and dots, but just a few,
Some deep some shallow, some old some new,
Let's renew some,
with a cut from me,
or a bruise from you.
The way you hit me,
Akward, in some way.
The way you touch me,
Surprising me again, everyday.
No matter what I do,
You still me hit me harder than I can,
Thats something I can't deny,
but does that mean that I can't try?
Your words cut me, deeper than I can,
But just you wait, cause I've just begun,
I'll reach that spot, where only you
seem to have acces to.
I'll cut my soul out, and hand it over,
Seems it's not where it does belong,
It seemed yours all along.
Maybe one day, i'll be able to,
make all of it run away,
so i could look at you and say:
Now that the life flew,
the blood flowed through the floor,
now that it's gone, you can't reach me anymore.
