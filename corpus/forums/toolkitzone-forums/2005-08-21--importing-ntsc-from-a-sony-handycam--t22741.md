# Importing NTSC from a Sony Handycam

### zyrshnikashnu · 2005-08-21 22:37 · #B248719

### Spyder · 2005-08-22 22:00 · #B249175

There is a firewire output on your camera which will let you transfer the video to your computer. This means you have to buy a firewire cable and PCI card if you don't have firewire built into your motherboard (which is probably the case). You can get a Firewire PCI card at [NewEgg.com](http://www.newegg.com/Product/Product.asp?Item=N82E16815124005) for under $15. The cables are around $10-12 at NewEgg, but you might be able to get them cheaper at a local computer hardware store. Make sure you get the right one for your camera.

### Michael J. Hill · 2005-08-23 21:16 · #B249503

Firewire is also known as IEEE1394 and i.Link (the latter is a Sony term)
After doing a little research, i believe you do have i.Link, and you're probably better off using it, even if it costs a little more. Data trasnfer will be faster than a video capture card, and you don't have to worry about recompression - it'll already be in whatever your camera records it as.
