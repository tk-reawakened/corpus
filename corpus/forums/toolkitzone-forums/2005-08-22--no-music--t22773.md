### Tao-Kun · 2005-08-29 00:23 · #B251481

Whenever you play MIDI on media player and either press stop or pause it, it turns down you SW Synth in your volume control. Next time you play a game made with TK2, make sure that that bar is raised all the way. I noticed in toolkit 3, I do not have the problem. I can play MIDIs in media player, stop it, then run my game, and my bar returns to normal. So check that out.
