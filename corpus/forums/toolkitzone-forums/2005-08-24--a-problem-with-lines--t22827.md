### JuanK_CorP · 2005-08-25 00:32 · #B249827

ok...this is what i do!....first scann the image with a resolution of 300dpi....at 100% of the size....(FULL COLOR...but you can use Greyscale if you want)....then just adjust the bright and the contrast of the image so te black is pure black...that works for me.....or you cand do what Z said

### angelis · 2005-08-25 00:45 · #B249830

as mentioned above. ^-^
i do both. sometimes, my lines are too messy because of excessive erasures. if i have the time, i do what Z said. make a new layer, then trace over the lines. i don't have a tablet, though, so it takes me quite sometime to finish inking (especially if the image has a lot of detailed lines).
if i don't have time, or am too lazy to ink, i do what JuanK_CorP does. sometimes adjusting the levels can get rid of unwanted lines and can darken the lines you want. Razz defining the thickness of the lines at pencil stage can give depth to the image when the levels are tweaked.

### P-WURM · 2005-08-25 10:03 · #B249941

scan at highest resolution and size and color then go to photoshop and use curves to get the right dark color, then use brightness and contrast to adjust your black and white and then do lineart over it. thats the way I do it and it always turns out ok.
