# TK3.0.7 suggestion...

### Caliber Mengsk · 2005-08-26 14:37 · #B250387

I have a suggestion.... "A Few" people may just say it is a stupid idea, but oh well.
How about Real time tile rotation?
What I mean is, how about instead of needing to make 3 copies of one tile just rotated 90 degrees, why not have a rotate button or two (90 degree) in the board editor just below the tile select?
This would lower the amount of files for some people that use a lot of rotated tiles, as well, may speed up construction of some boards. (maybe have two shortcut keys like [ and ] to rotate without clicking)
I know that you can simply rotate inside of the tile editor and save it out, but it is always better to take up less space if you can, and this would save a lot of hassle if you "forget" one of the 3 copies and so on.
Also, maybe just a canvas rotate, or image rotate option. You know, like in the setimage, have an extra option for degrees. 0 being flat, then rotate clockwise or something.
Just some ideas. (maybe the canvas rotate would be better in the draw canvas option.)

### Colin · 2005-08-26 18:40 · #B250474

Rotation is ridiculously slow stuff. It's so slow that DirectX isn't even capable of doing it without a graphics card that does it, i.e., top-notch quality cards. And even with a card that does it it's bloody slow. Rotating tiles for drawing boards, images, canvases, and whatnot is impracticably slow.
