### RDemusX · 2005-08-27 00:20 · #B250641

You need to make all the frames for the animation in an image editing suite ... eg. Photoshop ... then save all frames as .gif or .png ... then bring them into the animation editor in the toolkit ... then make your animation and save it as .anm ... thats it.
RDX
