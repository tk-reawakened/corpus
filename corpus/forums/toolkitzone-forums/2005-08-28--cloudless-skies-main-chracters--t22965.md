### venstor · 2005-09-07 05:31 · #B254879

The side view isn't bad by any means, but it doesn't match the style of the front view at all.
For one thing, the sprite's a lot shorter from the side view. From the front, the iris takes up the whole eye and the eyes look like big black beads. From the side, the iris is much smaller and you can see white part of the eye. He also has eyebrows, which he doesn't have from the front. He has no ears on the side view. The feet are smaller and less prominent from the side.
There are also changes to the hairstyle and costume from the side. His hair's messier instead of parted down the middle, he's now wearing gloves, he's missing his belt, and his pants are a different color.
It's all right if you've changed the character's design, but if that's the case, you need to go back and change the front view to match.
