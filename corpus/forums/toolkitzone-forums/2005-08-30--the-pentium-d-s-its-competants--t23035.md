### Michael J. Hill · 2005-08-30 21:20 · #B252332

A dual core processor doesn't need Hyperthreading capabilities considering each core can handle individual processes. Hyperthreading was designed to streamline the processor and make it more efficient at handling multiple processes, but 1 core means 1 process at a time.
