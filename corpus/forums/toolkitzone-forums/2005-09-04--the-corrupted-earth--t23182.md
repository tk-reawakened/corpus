# The Corrupted Earth

### Shapechanger · 2005-09-04 03:47 · #B253683

I'm not much of a religious man, but I do keep up on bits of world affairs. The problem I'm seeing now is one of two things:
Diminishing Resources
Lack of Conscience
Diminishing Resources:
The world is run through money, it's true. Resources have become the core of the American economy and now that they dwindle, we are beginning to collapse. We now official inport half of what we export, and are in debt to practically every first-world country. What has happen? We used to produce the most steel and coal in the world, and grew a surplus of food for ourselves to be exported. With the exhaustion of our coal and steel industry, we lost half our export products. The country began to turn towards the Computer Age, and entire industries arose that produced luxuries too fragile or expensive to export in mass quantities. These industries stole workers from the farms of America, and we downspiraled into our current Six-Trillion Dollar debt. Notice how thirty years ago the American Dollar was worth 2 Euros? Now it's almost the other way around. I laugh at the Chinese who boast one of the few economies that are in growth, for when Petroleum becomes too expensive, their entire Plastic Industry falls to shreds. It is because our country is dependant now on resources we no longer produce enough of that we must import so much. If we were like China, where Bicycles are the main form of Transportation, we would not need to import the vast amounts of Petroleum we do. Yet the entire world is affect by the same problem. All of mankind has become dependant on these resources, when we have known for a century they would eventually run dry. Unless we receive a truly brilliant President next election, I can say that America will begin to collapse. At current, we are just like Rome when it fell, our armies scattered across the world, our economy faltering. I can see nothing good on the Horizon.
Lack of Conscience:
Modernly, most people prefer to stand out from the rest. By doing this, we have created scores of new age stereotypes. This also results in the unorganization of entire communities, occasionally countries. In World War Two, the Germans, with the exception of the Concentration Camps, were very polite. A truce you say? They would lay down their guns and get drunk with the Allies across the river. Axis troops never shot Allied medics and Allied troops never shot Axis medics (For the most part). When town were invaded, troops died over hesitation because they wanted to make sure they were not shooting a Civilian. And yet there was no UN to moderate this war, no rules to be followed. Nowadays, we have both and war has been forever tarnished. Modern day war is terrorism and rebellion, both vile, evil, backstabbing forms of war. Was Operation Iraqi Freedom a war against a country? No, it was a war against an unorganized group of drunk Arabs with guns. Except amongst these Arabs are the Civilians, the innocents. But since we are so heavily stereotyped nowadays, an Arab is just an Arab. An American soldier just as readily shoots an Arab complaining about his home being shelled as one with an Ak47.
The only way to save America is to stop being the world police, recall our troops, recover our productive industry, and stay nuetral in all world issues. Just because we are militarily superior, does not mean we are responsible for keeping the world in line. Why worry about Iraq when our people are about to face a serious crisis. Right now, the right man in the right place within the US could topple the whole damn Union. So why are we in Iraq? Rome fell because it was strung thin across the world, do we wish to repeat history?
Open to debate, I realize I can be wrong, too, and will not flame anybody who makes a thoughtful and serious argument.
