### Colin · 2005-09-10 17:26 · #B256027

We have listed this question on the help files' technical issues page.
<http://tk3.toolkitzone.com/help/?page=25#tech_7>
Enabling vertical sync makes your monitor refresh at the same rate as your display adapter. The only reason this would ever be disabled is when a programmer is testing the raw rendering speed of an engine. For most users, vertical sync should always be enabled.
To the people who request that we enable vertical sync for you, the simple answer is that we can't. Every graphic card stores the setting in a different way.
