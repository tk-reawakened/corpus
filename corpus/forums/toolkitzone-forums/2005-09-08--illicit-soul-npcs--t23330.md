### venstor · 2005-09-09 14:44 · #B255618

Thanks, guys.

**CyberWolf wrote:**

> Had you not told us you used a template, I would've guessed you pixeled every one of them from scratch.
>

A template just gives me the proper proportions, and I do still have to do quite a bit of work to get finished sprites. Here's what I have when I start out:
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/templatefemale.gif) ![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/templatemale.gif)
Until just now, I only had front view templates, but I've whipped up some side views to work with. Now I just need some back views...

**Quote:**

> I must say though, the children look... well... Strange. They have the hugest mouths I've ever seen. The adults look great though.
>

HA HA! I figured it'd be fun to get over the top cartooney at both extremes of the age scale. The old man's just about as weird-looking as the kids. It kind of hearkens back to my roots, since I've only been honing a more anatomically correct style for the past couple of years.

**phishit wrote:**

> what exactly do you mean by not having to make walking animations for them? Will they all be stationary?
>

I decided early on that I was going to have very few wandering NPCs in my game. I played too many other toolkit games where the game slowed down almost to the point of unplayability once you entered a town due to the toolkit having to move so many objects around. I figured it'd just be easier to lay the NPCs down as tiles in order to make exploring towns less of a chore. (It also fixed the issue of scrolling up onto the screen with an NPC and finding that the sprite's top and bottom tiles were reversed. That was silly looking)
Of course, this was a TK2 issue. I haven't actually played around with wander programs in TK3 to see if it still slows the game to a crawl. If things have gotten better, I might decide to make more people move.

**Quote:**

> By the way, Illicit Soul is my most anticipated TK game. Keep it coming!
>

Thanks. Let's hope I can get it into a playable state at some point. I'm sort of hovering between TK2 and TK3 at the moment, where version 2 doesn't have all of the features I want, (along with some pretty bad glitches) but version 3 has unusable default systems. I'm hoping the next release will fix some of the problems with the defaults so that I can finally get a demo out.
