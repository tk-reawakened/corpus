# The City Life Begining Story

### joenorton · 2005-09-08 03:04 · #B255234

Well here is The story for my game The City Life.Please tell me what you think.
Well the story starts out in the present day. You wake up with a killer headache and a sharp pain in your back. So you get up and youre lying on a rock. Then you realize that you are in an alley. But you dont know how you got there. Matter fact you dont even know your name. So you brush off and you fill your wallet in your back pocket. So you take it out and you look at your driver license. Name: Eric Walter. Date of birth: August fifth 1972. But before you could read your address someone comes up and slugs you in the back of the head. You fall to the ground. Then you here three men laughing. One of them says you are trespassing in are territory. So the first thang thats going to happen is were going to beat the hell out of ya. The second thang thats going to happen is you going to give us all your money. So you get up off the ground and get ready to fight. You take a couple a punches to the head. Then you hear someone running towards you. After that you hear three loud shots.Two men fall but the leader of them did not get hit. So he gets away. The person who had shot the gun came upto you and asked if you were alright. You say just a little banged up. He says that was some of the members of the Hairy Nuts Gang. They think they own every thang. He says the Man that got away was there leader. His name is Barry Grondo. Then he said they stay on Fangsion Ave. In a trashed apartment building. You nod along like you know where thats at. He says you must be new in town I havent seen you around here before. Then you tell him that you dont Know where your at. He says youre in Grandhood City. You tell him that you just woke up in this alley without a clue why youre here. He says you must have gotten really hammered last night. Then he says I better get back to my house if you ever want to se me I live on IDS road. Then he walks off. Faintly you hear him say by the way names Cort, Cort sheifeild.In the distance you here sirens so you hurry and get out of there.
