It's about a kid getting some green goo for his old man. Compare that to the story in Talona (which you can get in the games section). It's full of mystery, originality, intriguing characters, etc. Look at games on the market, like The Minish Cap. This is the perfect example, because the story is a lot like yours - the princess has been solidified! Find the Minish to save her! However, the story is like that, not because the creators thought it would be a good story, but because Zelda games are, in essence, old school - running from dungeon to dungeon, solving a few puzzles and slicing baddies as you go. It's a game focused on gameplay, and a complicated story would just get in the way of things. Even so, there are things elaborated upon, and the story changes subtley as you go. So think about your own story, and ask yourself - If I saw a game like this on the market, would I buy it?
