# Interior Tiles

### venstor · 2005-09-12 17:17 · #B256554

It's been months since I last worked on tiles, so I set to work on an interior tileset. Until now, I've been using mostly ripped tiles as stand-ins for interiors, but I now have the start of an original interior tileset. I can make rooms with it, but I need to make a lot of furniture and hopefully some different types of wallpaper and carpets. Here's a shot put together with what I have now:
[![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games/phpThumb.php?src=../mygames/images/58/4397.gif&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?image=4397)
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/4397.gif)
I'm not great with pixeling man made stuff, so these tiles could probably use some improvement. I'm really unsure what to do about the staircase. It looks weird that the bannister just stops when it hits that board representing the ceiling. Should I put a floor and some railing up there? Or should I kind of fade the bannister into blackness?
I'm also beginning to wonder if I need to stick some shadows underneath my sprites. Does Cerna look like she's actually standing in the room?

### Rydath · 2005-09-12 17:39 · #B256559

You definately need to put more shadow on the staircase to give it some depth. You don't neccessarily need to make it taper off into darkness, but you should definately make it darker near the top (Shadows from the ceiling and whatnot.) Just stay consistent with your shadows (Light from top right, correct?) and you should be able to make it work. Other than that, I envy you with all of my heart. Very pretty.
