Sorry, have only read the first page, but I just wanted to contribute my half cent to this thread.
The high school life varies from person to person and place to place, but I can say that the most notable aspects of life in my high school were the types of people and the gossip.
The types of people were "categories" that any one person could fit into one or more of:
You have your smarties who get straight As and take on extra-curricular activities.
There were the slackers who didn't really care much about doing well in school.
Then there's always the smart people with potential, who are lazy.
Under these "categories" were sub-categories that defined a person by their appearance, much of which seems to be divided by their financial situations and music choice.
Today (in my area) most of the straight A motivated kids ended up going to universities, and have a high chance of following the "set path" of college -> job -> house -> married -> kids.
A lot of the "don't care" kids are still here, working at the local retail jobs, quite a bit of them have their own apartments and cars, they are self-sufficient, but aren't looking forward to any major change anytime soon.
Most of the "lazy" kids are now attending the community college, some working, some live with their parents or some got apartments, a lot of my friends (and myself) fit into this group.
I think to an extent how people are in high school can predict how they end up for a few years after, but that's not to say that it won't change.
