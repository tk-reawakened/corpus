### Moo Of Doom · 2005-09-16 05:35 · #B257675

Very nice tiles! Very Happy I love the grass and the sand especially.
One suggestion is to make the grass blades stick out over the sand and the dirt at the water's edge rather than just casually fade to the other texture. The grass has defined blades, and looks very bushy, so it should stick out over the sand rather than be covered by it. Put a little shadow by the transition under the blades to add to that effect. I think it would look spiffy Smile.
Other than that, it's practically perfect.
EDIT: And just a note... the TK arranges its tilesets in rows of 9 in the default view, so I suggest you plan out your tiles in that layout rather than in rows of 10 (unless you want to resize the tileset window every time Razz).
