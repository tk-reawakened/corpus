### SonodroidStar · 2005-09-22 19:48 · #B259414

**pieman wrote:**

> 1) how do i make things on the ground pick up able
> 2) how do i make stores
> 3) in what script am i supposed to put the code for adding team members
> 4) my enemies dont appear, even though i tried everything u veterans say
> 5) and finally my special move system doesnt work

1. A simple command. #eraseitem. make the thing an item that can't walk.
2. #Callshop(item1$,item2,item3$,item4$) and so on
3. #AddPlayer("playername.tem") replace playername with the character that joins your team's name.
4. Go to the enemy editor, go to graphics, and put in the graphics, then SAVE.
5. Special move system? You mean your special moves? Uh... make sure that you have enough SMP or MP to use it, then use it... put for the Special Move editor, go to the Special Move editor and do the following:
1. Enter the name of the Special Move.
2. Enter in the amount of SMP or MP it will take away from you and the enemy. Most specials don't take away SMP or MP from the enemy.
3. Put in the amount of damage it will do.
4. Put in desciption.
5. Put in effects and animations.
6. Do you want it to be used on boards? If so, go to save as and save it. If not, then erase the check mark in the box where it says that. Then Save it. I repeat. SAVE
