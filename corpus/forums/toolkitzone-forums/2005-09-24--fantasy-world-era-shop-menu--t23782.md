# Fantasy World Era:Shop menu

### I_Cant_Believe_Its_Me · 2005-09-24 15:57 · #B259950

Tis the morning I felt like jumping up and making the menu. Using Photoshop 7 and pixia, I made a very artsy menu to go along with the water theme of this game surely to fit in pretty well I guesse. How it was made:
1.)Pixia
-Added blues and greys
-Blur2 tool to blend the backdrop all together
2.)Photoshop7
-Added a clouds effect
-Added a ocean ripples effect
-Placed 50% transparent rectangles
-Put all the text on there
-For the finish I added 50% transparent blue boxes for another cool little effect.
-How the menu is to work:
When you click on either shop or sell your menu should pop-up (scroll menu) with either yours or the shops equiptment in it.
