# New Goon Battle System

### MrB · 2005-09-27 00:44 · #B260473

Hey guys, been a few months since I posted those new original tiles, so I figured I post some screenshots of what I've been up to lately.
Basically, I'm redoing the entire BS. I was sick of the old one - I was pouring hours of work just to get it stable, and it wasn't worth it. So I'm starting over, and it's been going great and surprisingly fast progress-wise.
Here's some screenshots:
[![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games/phpThumb.php?src=../mygames/images/70/4580.gif&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?image=4580)
[![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games/phpThumb.php?src=../mygames/images/70/4579.gif&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?image=4579)
First off, everything you see is original. It's all pretty traditional and straigh forward, except for maybe how the command menu drops down in the middle. And whenever it doesn't need to be there, it indeed gets out of the way as you can see in the second screenshots. You'll pick what command you want to execute in the middle menu, but you'll also select your spells, items, techniques, etc. there when you need to also.
Keep in mind that everything is subject to change (especially the enemy/character sprites), but I'm willing to consider any criticism you guys have to offer.
And real quick, here are the old mock-ups of the original tiles I posted a few months back:
[![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games/phpThumb.php?src=../mygames/images/70/3622.gif&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?image=3622)
[![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games/phpThumb.php?src=../mygames/images/70/3621.gif&w=200&bw=2&bg=000000)](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?image=3621)
EDIT: Oh, and I am planning to redo the menu and shop systems to fit this new style and get them more stable and whatnot also.
Also, The Dragoon is now a Starflare Productions game! Serge finally let me join his cool little company, so yeah. Hopefully you'll get to know more about Starflare at the Winter Expo, hehe. Wink
