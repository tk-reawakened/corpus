### Moo Of Doom · 2005-10-01 14:57 · #B261618

I like the tiles, man. They're awesome.
Board design-wise, though, I think it could use some work. I'll assume the cliffs aren't vertical (or else the top would line up with the bottom, and the stairs would need to extend out). The front of the mountain in screenshot 2 should probably not be a completely straight line... I'd like it better if it jutted out in some places.
Concerning the clouds, I think they're a big contrast with the otherwise rough, grainy textures of the rest of the board, but this really seperates them from the ground. I'd give them some very light dithered shading (like maybe 25%) just to make them seem round and fluffy.
Overall, nice work. The game looks great. Keep it up.
