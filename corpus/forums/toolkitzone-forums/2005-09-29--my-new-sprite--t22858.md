### CG-I · 2005-09-29 06:37 · #B261086

The guy looks kind of pillow-shaded, and the proportions are pretty off.
I know you don't like Pokemon-style sprites, but you could try and study how proportions work for sprites regardless, because truthfully it looks a bit amateurish. I'm not trying to criticize just to be critical, but it doesn't strike me as a bona-fide 'RPG sprite'.
I would recommend that you try and look up a spriting tutorial on something like Castlevania style sprites if you want a more uber-realistic approach than the so-called 'pokemon style', which is really just super-deformed, because right now he reminds me of a Sesame Street character.
I'm not trying to be harsh, just trying to give constructive advice. If that's the look you're going for, then by all means go ahead, it just might be me and the style. There's a lot of potential there.
