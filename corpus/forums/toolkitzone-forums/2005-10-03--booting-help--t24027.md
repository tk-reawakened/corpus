### SidZero · 2005-10-04 05:30 · #B262212

Here's where I can help. First off, if you're insisting on chatting on yahoo chat, do the wise thing.
DO NOT USE MESSENGER CHAT. [Use yahelite](http://www.yahelite.org/).
The majority of people who I know that use yahoo chat (including myself, that's a lot of them... I'm actually a pretty well known regular in one of the rooms on there) use yahelite. It's got pretty good spam filters (gets rid of the biggest problem on yahoo, having to sit around and stare at bots advertising their sex cams all the time), and it's got pretty good boot protection depending on which protocols you use (ychat and ymsg are the best, depending on if you're using it to just chat, or as a replacement for messenger, respectively).
If you still insist on using messenger, there's still a way to prevent booters (they're a problem all over yahoo, second biggest one after all the bots):
Go into file>preferences.
Under the "Chat" category, tell it to ignore chat invites (one of the ways to boot people is to flood them with invites).
Under the "Ignore List" category, tell it to ignore all users not on your friends list (though this may cause an unwanted effect of not seeing anyone but your friends in a chatroom, I've never tested it before).
Personally, I just recommend yahelite, it may be a bit difficult to understand at first, but once you start to understand the way it works (usually takes 15-20 minutes of playing with it) it's a LOT better than any other way of using yahoo chat.
