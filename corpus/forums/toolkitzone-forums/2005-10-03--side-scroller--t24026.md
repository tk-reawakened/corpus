### Colin · 2005-10-10 16:37 · #B263796

I think it's time someone clarified what we mean when we say no for online games, side scrollers, and other things. Yes, it's theoretically possible to make these things in RPGCode, but that's not what the Toolkit is designed for, so you'll be doing considerably more work and having a considerably poorer result than if you picked a tool that is designed to do what you want to do.
People who have made side scrollers and rudimentary online games have done so for contests and to prove it is possible! They have not made these games because the TK is suited for making them.
The original poster would be better off using a program like [Game Maker](http://www.gamemaker.nl/).
