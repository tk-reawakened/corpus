# TK3 needs a BS Editor...

### Ryukenshen · 2005-10-04 01:58 · #B262166

It has a Tile, Board, and Character Editor, along with many more. But what about a BS editor, for people that don't intend to make CBS's? That way we don't have to deal with the same thing over and over again. If the designer can control whether or not the BS is trade-turn, what the interface looks looks like, how many options the interface has, where the characters are positioned on the battle background....
The ideas are endless. People wouldn't have to spend 10 hours slaving over a CBS in the program editor. I've been daydreaming of something like this since I first played a TK game, before I downloaded the thing. Toolkit could really use something like that.

### Colin · 2005-10-04 02:09 · #B262169

Ten hours isn't actually that long. I coded that current TK3 default battle system in just one sitting, probably about six hours. When you dedicate 6-10 hours of your time making a battle system, you get a battle system that fits your needs exactly, and you learn something along the way. A battle system editor is altogether impractical. There is no way to allow enough options to quench the thirst of anyone who needs the power coding your own battle system offers. And the people who are just looking to tweak the default battle system slightly will find it superfluous.
At best, it would make mediocrity easier to achieve, enabling clueless people to churn out a continual stream of poor, ill-thought-out games. Having a simple default system (or indeed, no default system as the case is in some engines) helps up the quality of the typical game. Sure, it may cost you a few hours of planning and coding, but in the end the system is good for the whole community.
The current battle system plugin setup means that you can theoretically change the battle system greatly simply by changing the battle system plugin. There isn't exactly a library of battle system plugins, but there might be in the future, and someone's making a plugin takes less time and appeases a specific audience more than my making a general battle system editor could.
All these things  online games, three dimensional graphics, extreme customisation of the default battle system, and so on  seem great when you think about them, but bringing them into practice is either not easy or not even good. It may seem unfortunate, I agree, but I just don't think a battle system editor is a good idea.
