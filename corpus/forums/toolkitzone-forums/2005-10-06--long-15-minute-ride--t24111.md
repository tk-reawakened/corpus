# Long 15 Minute Ride

### RachelEvil · 2005-10-06 05:24 · #B262807

Fifteen minutes to home.
We get out of work, and into your car.
You ask me what's up,
And I shrug, like I always do.
We talk, for a while.
I'll be damned if I can remember what about.
You ask me about a Greek Goddess
Who ran really fast.
I say that's Artemis.
You say you're glad to have me around,
As we approach my street.
You know right where I live.
I shift uncomfortably in my seat,
Eyes glued to your face;
The gentle curvature of your cheeks,
The lustre of your soft hair.
I ask, finally, if you knew how I feel about you.
Felt. How I felt.
I meant felt.
You say "Kinda."
I say "Kinda?"
You say you figured it out
When I mentioned I was in the process of getting over a crush on someone.
It's a longer process than I could ever have imagined,
But I don't tell you that.
I remember the night I said that,
When you were dropping me off at home,
(Is that all we ever do?)
After we hung out at your boyfriend's house.
Now, I try not to be too overly disgusted
By heterosexual acts,
But seeing you all over that guy...
I felt something rising in my throat, and I just wanted to run.
But I couldn't have done that.
That would have been too obvious.
So I thought, "This is it.
This is the shock.
That finally gets me out of love."
So I told you I was over you,
Even though I wouldn't tell you it was you.
But I guess you knew anyway.
So I tell you again that I don't feel that anymore.
God I wish I really could stop.
Or at least tell you...
Tell you how much I admire your beauty.
And I don't mean that in a purely physical sense.
You just have this inner essence of beauty
That radiates out in everything you do
And everything you are,
And makes me believe that there cannot be a person out there
Who could interact with you for more than thirty secends
And not fall
Instantly
Deeply
In love with you.
But if I say that, you'll never speak to me again.
I don't know how other people are,
But I tend to remember minute details of conversations,
Sometimes,
And I remember that once you told me you had a friend,
And he had feelings for you,
And he made them known,
And said some things
(You weren't really clear on what)
That you didn't want to hear from a justafriend,
So you started avoiding him.
I don't want you to avoid me.
Even though I can never be with you,
I'd at least like to be around you.
So I say I'm over you,
And that I'm glad we're friends,
And I say good night,
And I get out of the car,
And I get in my house,
And I wish I could just
Obliterate
Me.
Another fifteen minutes.
