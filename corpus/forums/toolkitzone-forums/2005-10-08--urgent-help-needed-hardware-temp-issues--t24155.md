### EB · 2005-10-08 01:00 · #B263220

Pentium 4.
65 Degrees.
Nope, sounds about right.
But seriously, make sure you are applying the paste properly otherwise you will just swamp the cpu. You'll want a very small drop, spread out into a thin layer. Make sure the airflow in the case is correct too, fan on the back should be blowing out, fans on the front blowing in, possibly a PCI exhaust and side fan if its possible.
