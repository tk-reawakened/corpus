# First boards of Yumiko and Venn's Game Spirit Reign....

### Venn · 2005-10-11 00:41 · #B263904

Well, these are the first few boards I did for Spirit Reign. The board #1: Remera Sky Dock - Crimson Demon was my first attempt at a non-tile board ever. I usually pixel my tiles then use the tiles to make a board in a game editor. I drew the basic image in MS Paint and then painted it in Adobe Photo Shop 7.0 for a total of about five hours. Also, yes I did use a few filters and dynamic brushes. The lighting is a little off and I been told the perspective is off as well. Particularly when it comes to the ship. Hmm... I may consider correcting that at a later date, but for now it works. Lastly, I made the front of the ship squared off to make it a little more orient then a common rounded front. Needs work but came pretty good and well work.
Board #2 Remera Sky Dock - Bridge Walkway Intersection is my second attempt. This one is the connecting bridge to board #1, its rather plain but serves it purpose. I added allot of clouds to try a show the distance. I also wanted allot of sunlight. Because of this the board came out a little bright. The builders being of an advance race, they are using an invisible force field to keep people from just being blown off the walkway. I added a video screen showing board #1, for... umm.. I not really sure why.. well whatever it works. This board was made in the very fashion as board 1. Also, I think the perspective came out okay on this one, but I could be wrong. Drawing perspective is not my strong point.Well that about it; so what do you think? - Venn
P.s. This boards are make in 800 by 600, but to save room, I lower the board size down.
