# Bouncing Ball Game

### William · 2005-10-12 14:23 · #B264197

Introduction
A small one player game made by William called Bouncing Ball scripted in VB 6.0. You are supposed to keep the ball from falling below the brick. And to take items that helps you to complete the game. But make sure you dont take the wrong because your brick can increase/decrease in size, and the speed of the ball can increase/decrease as well. And some other once too Wink
How to play
Just use the arrow keys Left and Right to move it. And restart the game with the mouse.
