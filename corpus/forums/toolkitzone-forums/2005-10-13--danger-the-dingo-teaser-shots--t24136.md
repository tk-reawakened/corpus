### Moonews · 2005-10-15 15:14 · #B264728

I first made all the objects I needed like houses, trees and tall grass. Then I made the terrain under it by first making grass and dirt textures and then putting the grass a layer above the dirt, using a smooth eraser at 25% opaque and erasing the grass bit by bit to expose the dirt. I merged those layers and then drew out a shadow for each object based on a 2 tile skew and merged those. Then I selected that layer and switched to the terrain layer to darken it and give it a blue hue. I was going to draw every board by hand, but that'd be a waste of time considering how fast I got the entire town done using this method. As you can see, the houses and indoors are hand drawn.
There are cliffs on the other side of town that I'm having troubles with. I was actually considering PMing you to ask how you got the cliffs in your game looking so nice. Laughing
