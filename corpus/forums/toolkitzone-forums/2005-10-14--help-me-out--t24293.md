### venstor · 2005-10-14 06:45 · #B264503

It looks like the water and the grass are the same texture, only palette swapped. Too many random dots. Grass should be a bunch of spikey blades, and water should be smooth and reflective with little ripples. (Unless it's very shallow, in which case you should see a rocky/sandy bottom)
You could do with more transition tiles between the grass and water. Some curves would look nicer than the river shifting at right angles.
The sign and rocks look decent. Just desaturate the colors a little bit. I think the bushes and flowers would look better with colored outlines instead of black ones.

### venstor · 2005-10-14 23:03 · #B264600

If you're going for a cartoony feel, then the grass and water don't match everything else. Try to mimic SNES Zelda by making the grass mostly just one solid color with little detail tiles scattered throughout. The water would be solid blue with little ripples near the edges.
You'd also want to get rid of most of the grainy detail on the rocks and bushes. Keep things clean and simple, like this:
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/nicktiles.gif)

### venstor · 2005-10-20 00:07 · #B265627

You can use anything you want, and I can try to show you how I did it. You said you wanted a cartoony style, and Link to the Past is what came immediately to mind for me, so I just converted your tiles to that style. It's really a pretty easy style to do, if you choose to use it.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/nickblacklines.gif)
I started off with just clean, one pixel thick black outlines against the green field.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/nickfill.gif)
I then filled the outlines with flat colors: a yellowish green for the leaves, and a reddish brown for the trunk.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/nickoutlines.gif)
Next, I started defining the individual leaves with a darker shade of green. I chose a slightly darker shade of green with a little more blue in it for this. I also added another shade of brown to put a bit of shadow on the trunk.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/nickhighlights.gif)
Next, I added a lighter, slightly more yellowish shade of green to do highlights. I'm assuming the lightsource is from straight above, so I just kind of stuck light parts in the center of the leaves.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/stuff/files/1710/nickfinish.gif)
And then it was just a bit of tweaking to get things done. I added another darker and more bluish shade of green for the deep shadows directly underneath the leaves. I also added a shadow cast beneath the bush on the grass. Finally, I did a bit of anti-aliasing by laying pixels of the second darkest green up against some of the black outlines.
If you keep things simplistic, it should be fairly easy to draw objects this way.
