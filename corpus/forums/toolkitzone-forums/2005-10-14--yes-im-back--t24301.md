### venstor · 2005-10-14 22:55 · #B264598

I can see how the sprites look like the rather beak-nosed characters in the concept art, yes.
It's hard to pull off a game that uses characters from other universes. If you're going for a Kingdom Hearts type of game with Nintendo characters, good luck pulling all the disparate elements into a sensical universe. If not, it's just lazy to use other people's characters instead of making your own.
The color palettes on the hero sprites need tweaking. They look like they were ripped from a Game Boy Color game, but the low contrast doesn't look good on a computer monitor. There are too many shades of blue and green that are virtually indistinguishable. Just start off with basic black and white when you're making sprites, and you can tweak the colors as you go.
I think all the sprites should have dark outlines to keep the style consistent. Sparky, Mario, and Suicune are fully outlined, while Ben and Bob just have their colors bleed into the background.
Looking at the sprites individually, Ben just looks too generic to me. He's supposed to be the main hero of the game, right? And yet he's just a guy in a red long-sleeved shirt and blue pants. The main character of the game should be the most distinctive looking one. I know that some NES games did just make generic "guy in pants and shirt" heroes, but the ones you actually remember, like Link and Mario, have their own distinctive looks.
Bob's spear seems to be glued to his shoulder. Make it look like he's holding it.
Sparky seems to have been poorly resized. Some of his outline is made of two vertical pixels, while other parts are four pixel squares like everyone else.
Maybe you should use the Mario sprite as a base for your other sprites. He at least looks like he's in a battle, while the others are just kind of standing stiff and frowning at the enemy.
