# Long- Episode 1 - Original Story by ViperCat

### ViperCat · 2005-11-23 09:09 · #B273922

LONG - EPISODE I
"You know what I do by now right?"
"But, did you have to choose him..?"
"'I' didn't choose anyone..'They' chose him for me.."
"Who are 'They'?"
"I don't know..."
Episode I
Long stepped silently down the hallway. The lights of the run-down hotel flickered. He slowly loaded his gun, rolled the revolver, and concealed it under his overcoat. He stepped to room number 54 and knocked lightly. A short man answered and looked at him with curiousity.
"May I help you, Sir?" The small man said.
"Yes," Long replied,"I believe you are harboring a witness to a case against a Franky Maggio, that might have potentially critical information that might cinge the case, and mister Maggio's innocence, correct?"
"I'm sorry, Sir. That is strictly confidential information, Good day to you."
The small man shut the door in Long's face. He turned his back, pulled his gun, and took a breath. He swiftly turned, kicking the door in. He turned quickly pulling the trigger, shooting him between the eyes. He turned a full 180 to a guy behind him shotting him in the chest. Jumping beckwards, he flipped a table and reloaded, peeking over he shot another man armed with a shotgun, but hit his leg. The man continued firing at the table, barely missing Long. Long threw a grenade over the table. He heard another man jump out of the door. An explosion followed by a scream approved Long's safety. He looked around, not to see his target. He ran out the door and saw a man sprinting towards a window. He ran in a full sprint after him. The man dove from the window twoards a murky pool in the lobby, but before he could hit the water, Long pulled a 45 Calibur and shot him through the right lung. Concealing all of his weaponry, he casually walked out of the building and called a cab.
"A local hotel was ransacked by an unknown assailant, killing four men including a vital witness in a case against a Franky Maggio, a well known mob...."
Long turned off the T.V. "Hmm.."
The phone rang. He answered the phone, on the other side was a gruff sounding man.
"'They' have a job for you...'"
"Go on..."
"They need you to assassinate the mayor's daughter, make it clean and quick."
"WHAT! You've got to be kidding me. I'm sorry, but I have to refuse."
"I'm sorry, Mr. Long, but I don't think you understood me, let me rephrase that...You have a job, do it, or you'll be on the other side of the bullet.."
The man hung up. Long looked at the floor for a while then put on his jacket. He walked down Main Street to the City Hall. Long walked in the front doors, security guards lined the halls. He smirked. He ran and threw off his jacket exposing his arsenal underneath. He threw two grenades in opposite directions down the two main halls. An explosion alarmed everyone. He pulled two 45 Calibur pistols out and readied them. Guards came pouring from the main stairway. He raised his guns and fired, striking four guards first, then two others. He holstered his guns and slipped on some brass knuckles. The guards rushed him at all angles. He reached into a pouch and threw a ball onto the ground. When the guards neared the ball expanded and released a mustard gas. Long ran through the smog up the stairs and into the main office. The mayor sat at his chair, filing some papers.
"Where's your daughter..She's supposed to be here."
"Well, I had a source tell me of your coming here."
"Huh..?"
The mayor reached under his desk and pulled a 9 millimeter handgun.
"Oh, so you wanna play that way?"
"No, but she does..!"
He opened a door to reveal a girl strapped down to a chair, unconcious.
"You fiend, doing this to your own daughter!"
"Well, if you want to save her, put your weapons on the ground."
"I was here to kill her, you stupid bastard."
"Oh, you were? Then why not get the job done?"
"Because, I don't date girls under twenty-one."
Long pulled out his gun and shot the mayor repeatedly in the chest. He then grabbed the girl and took a back corridor to an alley.
'What were they going to do to her? What kind of sick bastard would want to kill a thirteen year-old kid?!' Long thought. He ran home with the girl and laid her on his bed. Hours later she awoke, startled, she screamed and cried for help. Long ran into the room urging her to quiet down. The girl had obliged.
"Where's my dad?" She asked.
"Dead.."
"Dead? Dead, how!?"
"I killed him.."
"What!? You did what!?"
"Yes, it's true, but if I didn't, he would've killed you...."
"........No..No.."
The girl ran out of the room and into the street, and disappeared.
"She was too much of a burden, anyway."
The phone rang, it was the man again.
"What now?"
"They have another job..Although you failed the last one, 'They' are willing to give you another chance."
"Fine, speak."
"Find a man named Augustus Von Streudel, he has something we want. By any means necessary, take it form him."
"Understood."
A dial tone.
Long walked up to a brick building, about three stories high. Looked like a half-way house. Long entered the doors and was shocked to see the mayor's daughter crying in a man's lap. Long walked up to the man.
"Are you Augustus Von Streudel?"
"Yes, I'm him."
The mayor's daughter looked up and walked towards the door.
"You have something, someone wants this someting. You don't by any chance have this with you do you?"
"What are you talking about?"
"I hate it when they're stubborn.."
Long pulled his gun and aimed it at his temple.
"Tell me now!! Where is it and what it is. NOW!!"
"Fine, it is a micro-chip, used to store all of the citizens files and all the files from the IRS. It was used by some mobsters to hack the country's bank account systems of the citizens twenty years ago. They used the money to make a massive biological weapon, aimed at China."
"Ahh, the Red-Kill bomb.."
"Yes.."
"So tell me where it is!"
"No!"
"Think twice..FAST!"
"You'll have to pry it out of my cold dead hands."
"Ok.."
Long pulled the trigger, and the chip fell to the ground. The mayor's daughter screamed.
"Shut up!"
"Who are you!"
"Long."
"Why do you keep killing the ones I love!?"
"First my dad, now my uncle!"
"Because.."
"Because why!?"
"You know what I do by now, right?"
"But, did you have to choose him..?"
"'I' didn't choose anyone..'They' chose him for me.."
"Who are 'They'?"
"I don't know..."
