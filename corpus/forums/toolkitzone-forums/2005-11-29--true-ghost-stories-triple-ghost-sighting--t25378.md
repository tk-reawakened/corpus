# True Ghost Stories: Triple Ghost Sighting

### Squoo · 2005-11-29 20:54 · #B275472

Triple Ghost Sighting: From Squoo
It was the night after Thanksgiving. It was 8:12 and I was getting ready to hit the hay. When I fell asleep, I heard strange breathing noises, like Darth Vader. For one thing, there was no Darth Vader toy, costume or any TV on. I ignored it, and fell asleep.
A Couple of Hours Later...
It was 12:34 AM. I didn't know why I woke up... "Hey, wait," I thought aloud. I thought I felt something brush me. When I looked up, I saw this peaceful-looking man sitting with me, brilliant streams of light bursting from his torso. I remember this man as my biological grandfather, who, sadly died from a heart attack during a barbeque, and died on this very property. I felt relaxed, knowing that this man loved me, and I loved him.
But it gets scarier.
This house has been in my family for years, from the 1890's. Teenagers died of all causes here... And suddenly, my grandfather vanished. I looked up, and I saw this man with no lower body, dripping blood, pointing his finger and his teeth bared. His teeth were gushing pus, and was hanging from the edge of my bed.
Naturally, I was freaked.
Closely listening, I heard him chanting "Bloody Mary, Bloody Mary" 12 times, when I saw a face appear in the mirror in my bedroom. My door opened slightly, and this man who seemed aged floated into my bedroom. He walked over to me, and screamed a scream so loud that he practically destroyed my ear-drums. A put all my faith into God and all the ghosts vanished. I walked over to the mirror, seeing no face, I was relieved, when suddenly, a bloody hand reaches out and scratches my face rapidly, when I breakaway from her grasp and turn on the lights. Clutching my face, I fainted for the rest of the morning...
