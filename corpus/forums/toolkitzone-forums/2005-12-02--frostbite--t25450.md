# Frostbite

### Penguin · 2005-12-02 15:37 · #B276225

The man was free now.
He stood now with a bag near a desolated railway station in the middle of Siberia. It was cold. It was almost always cold. Around him was just a crystal white wasteland of snow and ice, some trees of the taiga in the distance were probably the only offer of a variety in the landscape.
For the last two years, at least he had something to do. He had a goal. His goal was freedom. Now he has achieved it, after two years of slave work beyond human strength. He survived and reached his freedom. As if it mattered... free, and left several thousand kilometers from his homeland, which he couldn't even fully remember. Free, and left with nothing but a cruel cold fate of the area. Free, no longer cared about.
He moved on. He decided to follow the rails. Somewhere on the other end, his homeland was supposed to be located - he began the journey.
He walked for days, sleeped wherever he could, passed no people other than suffering, seen no cities other than desolated. But a time came that he took a path where for whole three days he could not see a human being, nor a building.
It began to snow. It was very cold early in the morning, on the fourth day since he last saw a human. In the darkness and in the middle of a snowstorm, he could see little.
And suddenly, by the railroad, he saw in the distance a group of three figures. People.
He tried to run towards them, but he was falling in the snow to his knees. He couldn't hold the balance and finally dropped on his face. He tried to stand up, wipe his face and cap clean of the snow. Slowly, he began to crawl towards the figures. He wanted to scream, call the people, but his mouth let only a soft noise that resembled nothing of a human voice.
As he was shivering from cold and aching from the frostburns (an overall fatigue and cold made him notice them less), he finally reached the place where the people stood. Snow was falling all around, but he could see the figures more clearly now, as he stood more than about a meter from them.
A mother, holding a child, dressed in a fur jacket, too big to be hers, probably given to her by another man that stood nearby - a father, with nothing but a shirt on, and a third person, sitting on the snow by the railway, its legs covered by the snow - a grandfather, eyes closed.
All pale white and frozen solid.
The man fell on his face again. It was getting even colder.
For the last two years, at least he had something to do. He had a goal. His goal was freedom. Now he has achieved it. As if it mattered...
