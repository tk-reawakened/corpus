# The Plugin

### UltimaWizard · 2005-12-05 17:31 · #B276905

"Now, let me see you cast your programs!"
Colin had just taught the 3 students how to use a PushItem() command in spoken RPGCode programs. While the three youths had trained in RPGCode from a young age, making spoken RPGCode programs manifest in the real world was proving much more difficult than in the virtual plane they had used the magical commands on for so long.
Geneva, a slender, red-headed, and attractive girl of 17, and easily the best in the class, stepped forward, raised a slender arm, extended her hand, and spoke:

**Code:**

```
CreateItem("FlowerVase", "the Table", 1)

PushItem(1, 1, "N")
```

The instant she finished speaking the words of power, a vase instantly appeared, and moved slightly to the northern end of the table on which it rested.
Colin applauded and immediately doused her with praise of her great grasp of physical RPGCode. Thomas and Genis simply looked bored; this happened all the time. While Thomas, short and somewhat stocky, looked to be perpetually bored even at the tender age of 12, he had a sharp mind and wit, and never left an opportunity to show his charm unused...quite to the contrary of Genis, a 15-year, blond, strapping teen who most girls of his age couldn't quit looking at, who was easily at the bottom of the class due to the fact he only used RPGCode commands he liked...strangely, the Earthquake() command wasn't one of those.
Colin had noticed the bored looks, but wasn't about to let his less-skilled charges off so easily; he turned on them. "Okay...Thomas, you're up next."
Thomas stepped forward, closed his eyes, and was silent for a few seconds. Colin began tapping his foot, impatient to see whether the young Codeuser had mastered physical use of the command. Just as Colin was about to tell his student to hurry up, Thomas' voice rang out with a set of RPGCode quite diffrent from what Geneva had used:

**Code:**

```
CreateItem("Iron Sword", "Floor in front of me", 2)

PushItem(2, 5 feet, "Up")

EquipItem("Me", "Iron Sword")

If (Me.Level>=5)

{

  CreateItem("Iron Plate", "Floor in front of me", 3)

  EquipItem("Me", "Iron Plate")

}
```

When Thomas had quit speaking there was a pause. Colin was about to tell Thomas he had not focused enough on his RPGCode, when suddenly, a 3-foot-long iron-bladed and iron-hilted sword in it's sheath appeared on the floor, rose up to Thomas' chest level, and attached itself to Thomas' school robes. Further, a cuirass of Iron Plate mail appeared on the floor where the sword had been only seconds before, and it strapped itself onto Thomas.
Colin was amazed...not only had Thomas used some advanced RPGCode commands, he had also used a command not usually used in physical RPGCode. "Thomas...where did you learn to use If() statements in physical RPGCode? They usually don't manifest, even when the criteria you state is true!"
Thomas held out in his hand a small iridescent purple ball. Even Genis, the worst in the class, recognized it immediately--the plugin he had lost days ago.
"Thomas, that's mine. I thought I lost that days ago!" Genis exclaimed, suprised by the magical relic's sudden appearance. "Not anymore it isn't," Thomas replied suddenly aggressive, "It once was yours and you didn't know how to use it. Now, it's mine. I think the true ownership is obvious."
Genis and Thomas had been best friends...something had to be wrong. "Instructor...run a diagnostic program on him now!!"
Colin, despite being an instructor and not one to take orders from students at all, had seen this change...and knew that Thomas and Genis had been best friends from an early age. Colin moved his arms into his characteristic RPGCoding pose and began speaking the following:

**Code:**

```
Include("Diagnostic Commands")

Include("Focal Commands")

FocusOnPerson("Thomas")

InitializeDiagnostic()

DiagnoseCode("Thomas")
```

When Colin had spoken this, the plugin Thomas was holding grew in size, began emitting an eerie glow, and wrote the following code in midair:

**Code:**

```
DisruptCommand("Colin", "DiagnoseCode()")

AttackAll()
```

Instantly, the text that was scrolling over Thomas' head disappeared, and wounds appeared in Geneva and Genis' robes, where blood began weeping from. Colin, however, was unharmed--he held up a piece of parchment with the following RPGCode commands:

**Code:**

```
ArcanisShield("All allies")
```

Colin hadn't spoken the code...yet the fact that the code was scribed onto the parchment was enough to shield Colin and the other students from harm.
Genis was now extremely angry. His eyes turned jet black, as did his hair, as he began casting his own RPGCode commands:

**Code:**

```
Include("Combat Library")

Include("Focal Commands")

Include("Colin's Parchment")

ArcanisShield("Geneva")

ArcanisShield("Thomas")

ArcanisShield("Me")

FocusOnPerson("Thomas")

Earthquake(10)

CreateItem("Fireball","Air in front of me", 1)

CreateItem("Icebolt","Air in front of me, to the left of the fireball", 2)

CreateItem("Plasma bolt", Air in front of me, to the right of the fireball, 3)

PushItem(1, "Thomas' Plugin", "Seek")

PushItem(2, "Thomas' Plugin", "Seek")

PushItem(3, "Thomas' Plugin", "Seek")
```

Aquamarine domes appeared around everyone but Colin, who was now awestruck that the class dunce had harnessed so much RPGCode so quickly. A ball of fire, a jagged blade of ice, and a luminescent bar appeared in the air in front of Genis and moved toward Thomas. The Plugin pulsed with eerie energy again and began writing...but the Arcanis shield altered every character so it looked like the following:

**Code:**

```
I|\|(1|_||)3 ("5|>3(7rum libr4|2'/")

13224578378

01109136167
```

The text dissipated into a red haze--the Plugin's code was so jumbled by the Arcanis shield that the code couldn't retain a coherent, manifestable form. The three objects of destructive forces that Genis had created plummeted towards the plugin. Thomas moved to take the hits...but the Arcanis shield around him held, and refracted the bolts. The fireballs rebounded off Geneva's shield, and knocked the plugin out of Thomas' hand. The Plasma bolt redirected two feet away from Colin, where it then hit the Plugin and enveloped it in electrical force. The Ice bolt destroyed the plugin, and Thomas immediately collapsed, unconcious, with labored breathing.
"Thomas!" Geneva and Genis cried at once. "Hold on," Colin told his charges. "We need to dissipate the Arcanis shields first, or our code will be jumbled. Further...his personal code was corrupted by that plugin. I'll need to use my special libraries to heal him completely...and even then I can't promise anything."
Geneva broke into tears, for she had really liked Thomas. Genis was silent. He had finally mastered using RPGCode...but the cost was far too great at the moment.[/code]
