### SidZero · 2005-12-15 08:18 · #B279615

I don't really know a lot about the Patriot Act (I haven't actually read it... I probably should have though), but what I do know about it, I don't really like. I'm not fond of the idea of exchanging my privacy for protection. As far as I'm concerned, privacy is part of both the first and fifth ammendments to the constition. You choose what of your life you wish to share (by choosing whether to speak of it or not), and the government cannot (or at least should not) force you to admit to criminal acts under oath. Those two add up to protected privacy to me, so personally, I hold privacy to be sacred. The idea of going around a persons privacy to find out whether or not they're a criminal... it's like fishing for people to accuse in some sick government sanctioned witchhunt.
Unless there's anything good that the Patriot Act does that DOESN'T invade my privacy, it's something that needs to go. But then again, it's not like it's something they're actually letting the people have a say on, only the politicians. Goes with the whole republic system of government I guess.
