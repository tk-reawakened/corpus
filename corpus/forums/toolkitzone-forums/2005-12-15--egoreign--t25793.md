# EgoReign

### GibbZ · 2005-12-15 18:42 · #B279700

I'm back again after... I don't know how long. I've decided to revive EgoReign. I'm sure that a lot has happened in my absence, so if people could answer some questions...
1) What all improvements have been made to the TK since 3.0.4?
2) Has anyone taken the name EgoReign?
3) Does anybody want to work on EgoReign with me as team lead? Note that I have very slow Internet access.
About the project: EgoReign is a large, non-linear RPG. Pretty classic stuff, but I'm trying to bring in some new ideas about magic and fighting. I gave up before becuase I had too many commitments and I just didn't have the skill. I believe that I now have the creative and technical ability to create a decent game, provided I have a little help from a good team. The plot is a loose chain of events that might not even effect the character. I've eliminated the Good VS Evil concept, and there are only different ideas and theologies fighting over stuff. So you can bring your religion along with you into the game world. Thank you for reading about my project.
