Ugh...
Haven't coded with RPGToolkit in like 6 months, but here I go.

Anyways there is a #shop() command that you can use to buy and sell items in a program... Just enter #shop("item1","item 2","item 3","item 4") etc.
Correct me if I'm wrong anybody.
And the mwin is easy... Just type #mwin("") and type the message inside the quotations. Another command you may want to get used to is #wait().
Anyways just keep practicing with the toolkit and eventually you'll figure everything out.
