# Battle Ratio

### Sunil · 2005-12-29 11:12 · #B283941

How do you lower the battle ratio, in the game
I am using TK2 and the default battle system for now.

### Talyn · 2005-12-29 12:05 · #B283943

Go to Edit Main File, then choose Fighting, then Edit.
In that window there's a box that says "Chances of getting in a fight 1 in x", change the value in the box to your desired number.
