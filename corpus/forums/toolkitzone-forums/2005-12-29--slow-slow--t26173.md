### DrakeLord · 2005-12-29 23:34 · #B284071

For Items:
When you create the item, click Graphics and adjust the Seconds Between Each Step. That should make an NPC walk slower.
For Characters:
Do the same. Edit the Seconds Between Each Step in Graphics.
Hope that helped. Very Happy
