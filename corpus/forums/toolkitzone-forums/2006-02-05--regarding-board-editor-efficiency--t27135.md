# Regarding Board Editor Efficiency

### WiseElben · 2006-02-05 04:43 · #B295226

Hi, I just downloaded the newest version of the toolkit. From the first few hours of playing with it, I can see that it has made a huge jump from the original TK2, including the movement to a C++ engine, open source, and OO programming. Sure, the internal parts of the TK3 is far more advanced than the TK2, along with new editor features, but the TK3, in my opinion, still lags behind in the board editor.
I do not see why the board editor still requires you to set every tile's type (normal, solid, etc). Why not have a function that makes every certian tile a certian type? This would obviously speed up the tedious process of board creation. Also, why must the users always have to do press "L" (when it actually works..) or make 3 button clicks just to change a tile? Why not make the loaded tileset dockable like the Project List and Board Toolbar? There are still many primitive faults in the editor, such as the lack of a larger undo list and sticky tabs. Sure Ctrl + I works, but I'm sure many programmers such as myself cannot stand programming like this. In my opinion, I think 3.0.7 should concentrate first not on vectors and fancy things such as those, but rather on the basic, must-need features.
To be honest, every time I open the toolkit, I cringe from the lack of basic neccessities. I wished I could feel at home with the toolkit, but these shortages makes usage uncomfortable.
I hope this helps the development of the toolkit.

### Delano · 2006-02-05 10:23 · #B295278

You might have noticed in the frontpage article that we are going to work on the board editor - probably a full rewrite - so some of these issues will be resolved. A few of them already exist: there's a dockable tileset right under the project list! We are not neglecting the board editor, quite the opposite.

### WiseElben · 2006-02-07 03:54 · #B295717

Another useful board tool would be brushes. Developers can save these brushes, composed of tiles, and load them whenever needed. This will save developers lots of time on objects containing multiple tiles such as trees, buildings, cliff/water edges, etc.
One simple thing that I think should be implanted is Board Editor Pref save. I mean, what's the reason for us to having to click on the "Autodraw Layers" button every time?
Seeing that the dev team might be rewriting the board editor, I also suggest making layer switching easier. Using a dropbox to switch layers is pretty annoying. It should be made into little buttons like the tile type buttons. Simple and effective.
Adding a Program, Item, or Warp should also be made easier. Pressing Ctrl + P and clicking on the desired tile is a two-step process that can be cut into one: right click -> Set Program.
I know my suggestions seem not important, but I'm certian that these small tweaks will better benifit the developers and the TK community.
