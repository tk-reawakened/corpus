### Stephen · 2006-02-13 00:48 · #B297353

That's actually not bad. However, it's quite plain.
Some suggestions:
1. Don't save pictures as .jpgs or .bmps. .gifs and .pngs are best image quality with unnoticeable compression.
2. Texturize. Your shapes are good, but the tiles need style. They need life. Make the green actual grass with discernable grass stems. Give the water ripples and darken it as the water gets deeper and farther from shore.
3. Lower the saturation. Right now it looks like it's a 3-year old kid game since the colors are so bright and flambouyant. Move the color closer to the grey so the saturation is from 60 to 90 or so(I haven't colored in a while, so I can't remember what I normally use).
4. Shading. Pick a definite light source(top right is my normal source) and make that the light source for everything. For example, you have a top down light source for the tree, and yet there is no shading on the house.
5. Drawing. Your trees and bushes are somewhat distorted. Try fixing the shapes a bit.
You're obviously new at drawing, but practice will help that. The Art forum is there for constructive criticism on non-screenshot artwork.
