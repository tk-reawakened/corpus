### Dont_Think_So · 2006-02-15 05:39 · #B298359

I agree that there should be a personal filter that one can implement; it could be activated by default for guests that are 'browsing' the forums, and when one signs up they can choose to turn the filter on/off. This will protect the youngest demographic from offensive language, as well as blocking it for those of us that would simply prefer not to have to read it as we're wading through the posts.
As for parental guidance, don't forget that some parents use controls on their children's internet; a web page that contains "b*tch" might be inaccessible, just because the electronic filter suspects questionable material.
