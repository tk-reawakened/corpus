### Dont_Think_So · 2006-02-15 01:09 · #B298245

I think I see what you did. You misunderstand layers; they are not like having separate rooms on top of eachother that you can travel between, they are more like layers of graphics set on the screen. Your character appears "transparent" because you have placed layers over him; also, the top layer always shows.
Although, it wouldn't be a bad idea to have RPGCode commands to turn layers on/off...
