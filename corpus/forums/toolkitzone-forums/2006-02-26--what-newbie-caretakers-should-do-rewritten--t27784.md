# What newbie caretakers should do (rewritten)

### Scott92002000 · 2006-02-26 05:28 · #B301776

(Original topic by Renegade, rewritten by Scott#)
What a good caretaker should do:
-Send a personal welcome message to every newbie who post a "I'm new" topic.
(This is really just a reccomendation...its not really required because if every caretaker sent a newbie a welcome message the number of messages could overwhelm them! Still you should try to make them feel as welcome as possible.)
-Protect newbies from newbie bashers and people who call them n00bs.
(There is not much that normal caretakers can do to protect the newbies from the bashers. If the incedent occurs here in the newbie haven and I don't see it right away you can PM me and I'll handle it as best I can...otherwise inform a global mod such as spyder, asimir, etc.)
-Give tips/warnings to newbies.
(Let them know if they get off-topic or anything like that and make sure that they are informed of the rules plus any other important things. Make sure that they know that if they break those rules there will be consequences.)
-Be tolerant of the newbies.
(I know that caretaking can sometimes be tiresome. The newbies asking the same questions over and over can and will get annoying. But you have to be tolerant of them...remember that once long ago, you too were a newbie and most likely asked these same questions. If you still can't be tolerant, then caretaking is not for you.)
If you have any questions or anything that you think should be added to this please PM me.
