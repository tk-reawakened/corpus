![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_1.gif)
Deceit and Conquest
Creator: Alpha and Omega
Author: Cyrus
If you've been in the TK community for sometime, you'd probably know about Mystic Software by now. If you don't then where do you think these games I'm reviewing are coming from? In the front page, there's a small ad for a book called "Seor". Now why would a community based on game making be making ad space for a book? Because before there was Seor, there was Deceit and Conquest, one the best TK games back then and still today. Though after 5 years, many other TK games were created and have risen up to the top of the TK pinacle like Dark Age and Heraldry, but regardless, D&C has remaines one of the best TK games to date because of it's use of programming, imaging, and music.
Plot
![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_2.gif)
In the world of Seor, a terrible and violent war rages on. Through centuries, battles have come and gone. The story centers around a land called Dianese. There, a final battle is set to take place.
When the game starts, we see a man on the cliffs overlooking the ocean with a locket in his hand. He is Hal Gerand, the char you play. A young man comes for him and says that another general is calling for him. Hal is now given a new mission.
The game is a bit complicated than that actually. You go from doing a mission to changing it, to retreating and teleporting to a new location. So much is put in this game, you'll never know what happens next.
Rating: 10/10 for a very intriuging story.
Graphics
![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_4.gif)
Not the greatest pieces of art but they are sure good. The people are created using the gradient tool. Not appeasing to the eye but at the same time they got effort to them. Look at the cut scenes. They are great to watch. The chars show emotions in them. Something that I don't mind at all. Would giving them a better touch up work? Maybe but I wouldn't change these graphics because they are great.
I don't know how Cyrus drew the rest of the stuff but apparently he did an excellent job of drawing so many files. They make the game all the more enjoyble. OI! If you ever tried to draw anything good, you know how hard it is.
Rating: 9/10 for making great graphics.
Media
The music is the highlight of this game. Composed by Randy, Chris Chiu, and Craig Reeves, the music is phenominal. You'll play the game just for the music, or you can always take a peek in the Media folder and listen to them whenever you want. The music is suspensful, sad, and even sometimes happy in such a dark game. The battle theme is my favorite. It's upbeat and it gets you ready to take some enemies down. You'll get into a battle just for the tune.
The SFX in the game are very good but unfortunetaly cannot be heard in the game because of the music. The music is in MP3 format so the sounds are overlapped. You can only hear them when the game is silent. Whats in the game in the sound department are voice overs, including the names of the characters in the game when you go to the options menu. There are also sound effects for certain areas of the game like when your teleporting or when a person becomes mush when he melts in front of the portal thingy.
Rating:
Music- 5/5 for having one the best soundtracks for the TK.
Sound- 4/5 for making great sounds and voice overs but not being able to hear most of them.
Programming
![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_9.gif)
This game is finely programmed. With the help of Spyder and MrG, the Mwin, the battles, and every other deal is great, but the programming isn't so well to make you forget it's a TK game. You still got default menus and shops. Regardless, these are minor things. You wont care because everything else is programmed excellently.
Rating: 10/10 for having solid programming.
Enviroment
![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_7.gif)
The overworld of the game is big. I said big though, not huge. You'll get around in no time. But the overworld is just a small part of the game. You go to towns and other intersting places like the monestary. They look splendid. From the inn's to the monestary, you'll enjoy the way they are made. The whole theme is also congruent with the game. In a war themed setting, everything is dark and gloomy. Though I don't think anyone would notice if Cyrus were to use RPGMaker or the default tilesets but apparently he goes all out to make sure the game looks like its in a state of war.
Rating: 9/10 for making a great envirotment with the theme attached to it.
Characters
![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_3.gif)
So many chars, so little of a game. It's a shame. I wanted to see the rest. I'll just explain the four main chars of the game. Hal Gerand is your char. One of the best Generals in Seor with a mysterious past. Kel Russet is a commander and Hal's best friend. He and Hal fight hard together. Zemal is a liuetenant who quckly becomes friends with Hal and Russet. He is young and full of spunk. Jenna, a mysterious girl. There is nothing on her so she remains a mystery. There are other chars but some of them are not seen in the demo. Maybe in the book?
Rating: 7/10 for a good cast of protagonist but for not showing the rest.
Menu
![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_10.gif)
Yup it's the default but it's not fully terrible. The menu was programmed by Spyder. It features a nifty background of a sword and a swirlf of green. Everything is the same. Items, equipment, stats are all the same. There is an option menu where you can customize the message window to a different color, change the speed of certain aspects of the game, and change the color of the font. Not groundbreaking but it's a nice touch.
Rating: 6/10 for making a good effort of trying to make the default menu look spiffy.
Battles
![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_5.gif)
Where the menu fails, the BS makes up for it. It is one of the best I've seen. Programmed by MrG, it looks quiet impressive. If you look closely thought, it's nothing more than the same ol thing. Attack, Defend, Magic, Item, Run. Basic stuff, no real strategy is needed other than keeping yourself from using all your magic power and items.
There is one thing that I dont like. Why are the enemies too easy. Each enemy has a puny ammount, even the boss. You got like 5x the ammount of power the enemy has so you can easily pummel the enemy with one hit. No challange at all. The first boss you encounter has only 25HP while Hal and Kel have like 150. The battles are truly unbalanced to your advantage.
Rating: 7/10 for having an excellent BS but unbalanced gameplay.
Gameplay
![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_8.gif)
No problems here. The char moves at a decent speed. Enemies come in a while, like every 30 steps or so. The conversations are at a smooth pace. Some things are 1 second laggy but apparently that's not so bad.
Rating: 9/10 for having almost flawless gameplay.
Fun Factor
![](http://i19.photobucket.com/albums/b187/TKGB/dac_6.gif)
I LOVE THIS GAME!!! The story is intruiging. The programming is excellent. The music is out of this world. The art is not bad but not distracting. This game pleases every part of a game. I can occasionally pick up this game again and again.
Rating: 10/10 for being very enjoyable.
Conclusion
This game is one of the first succesful TK games of it's time (not to mention was the biggest of it's time). With Cyrus creativity and help from some fellows, a game was made that was so spectacular, that it still holds up in it's own. Even in today's TK community with TK3 and what not, I bet this game can still entertain those who never heard of it.
Recomendation?
A big YES! This is TK history right here. You gotta check it out.
Overall Rating: 86/100. This game gets a Awesome rating.
