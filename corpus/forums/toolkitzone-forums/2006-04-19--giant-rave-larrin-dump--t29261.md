Now, for a few features...
I know that there's a popular demand for visible enemy encounters, but, due to the somewhat cramped board designs in Rave, random encounters'll do just fine. My main priority is dungeon design for this project, so that works out well for me, anyways.
Also, Special Attacks (or Techs) require you to explore. You'll need to find raw materials and people to teach you new moves, which doesn't take an extremely long time. Nor is it required. Still, boss fights won't be easy if you just swing a sword wildly. The keyword here is balance. Finding ingredients is simple, but if you're using Techs continuously, you'll have to restock often.
While we're on the subject of battles, fights will typically be difficult, but you'll be healed substantiallyafter each one. Why? You're life'll be in danger every fight, but it'll be over quick, and you'll be ready for the next onslaught. Again, balance.
Remember the giant castle of Dark Age 2? Well, my baby won't be so much of a monster, but there'll be areas you'll have to come back to to unlock.
The story won't be as slow as Golden Sun's, but I want to keep cutscenes relatively short. My focus is gameplay.
The graphics are pretty cartoony. What else should I say?
As far as length is concerned, that depends a lot on the way you play. For Tech freaks, it could be a lot longer, due to all the item hunting required. The linear quest probably won't be that lengthy, but a glut of areas to explore could pump up the amount of hours, too. There're a lot of variables to consider.
Speaking of which, UltimaWizard agreed to do some coding, but some extra help is much appreciated.
One last thing... The grass tile might hurt your eyes. I'm not really good at grass.
Total demo completion: 5%. Bear with me. Some things might be unfinished, though, so it won't neccesarily be too far in the future.[/b]
