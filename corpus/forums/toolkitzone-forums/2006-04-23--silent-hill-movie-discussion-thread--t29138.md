### Moonews · 2006-04-24 03:14 · #B315929

Even if you are a fan of the games and you get it, I don't understand why you would like it. Here we have...
1) 1 good monster in the entire movie, the Janitor, who is explained as being a rapist in Alessa's past.
2) 3 monsters from Silent Hill 2 (Patient, Nurses and Pyramid Head) that are put in the movie for the hell of it, despite being directly tied to SH2's storyline. They make no sense in this movie.
3) The siren, which signals the end of the Otherworld in all of the games now signals the beginning and it's a warning siren on the top of the damn church instead of being a product of the otherworld.
4) The "cult" is now a bunch of witch hunters trying to fight the darkness instead of trying to feed the darkness to birth a god.
**SPOILERS, AS IF IT MATTERS**
5)The real Kentucky-Fried-Alessa coming back and barbedwire raping Christabella was just too much.

I just saw it, and I thought it was quite good.
I've only played Silent Hill 1, and that was years ago, so I can barely remember the storyline. I thought some of the gory parts were just silly, like the policewoman's face burning and that woman getting ripped in half, and Anna's death was as stupid as Anna herself (Why couldn't Pyramid Head have killed Sharon too? She was the only person more annoying than Anna. Meow.)

All in all, I give it 1.6 thumbs up. A good movie.
