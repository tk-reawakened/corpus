# Game of the Week (4/25/06): Lazarus Mist : Frozen Sun

### Kabuki · 2006-04-26 01:58 · #B316284

Game of the Week | April 25th, 2006
[![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/games/img/1764.JPG)](http://toolkitzone.com/games.php?game=1764)
"In ancient times, they called today armageddon. The time has come"
Developed and in the process of being created by [Red Tree Developers](http://www.freewebs.com/superisorpg) Lazarus Mist: Frozen Sun has major potential. Though no demo is avaliable as of yet SuperIso and his team of two, forensixman and daman, have generate a small trailer that displays quite the awesomeness of the graphics that this game consists of. With a bundle of picture avaliable to view, you'll never know what they'll be working on next.
With a rating of 4.5/5 and 19 people already casting their decision, this game seems to be up there.
This game spans through 2000 or so years of work, battles and all around hardship. You will take part in fierce massacres, rescues and assassinations, and thievery. Plan on calling some sick days, because this looks like it will be a long game. Though not much about the storyline has been talked about amongst the Developers and the Tk Community, this game is sure to draw eyes by its amazing-ness.
Good job to SuperIso, Forensixman, and Daman for all their hard work on such a marvelous looking game.
