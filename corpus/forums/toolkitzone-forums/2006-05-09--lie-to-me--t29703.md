# Lie to me

### Cyrus · 2006-05-09 01:53 · #B319480

------------------------
Lie to me
By: (redacted: name)
------------------------
Beautiful disease of virtue...
looking for me in halls of always...
Will you find me in that lonely place?
Will you corrupt me there?
I've forgotten who the mirror knows.
I've mistaken him for me.
If I could see myself then I might remember...
might remember... to believe.
Dust and ashes fill me veins...
they billow in the air and through my heart...
but they do not know the mirror man...
or risk a glance to see.
Rusted eyes and salted lips...
Remind me I am grieving.
...but why hides in blotches...
...hides in smears of secluded imagery.
Lie to me and love me.
Lie to me and stay.
Laugh inside if you need to...
but please... dont go.. away.
Tell me of days I wont remember.
Tell me of times without regret.
Speak silly promises into my ear.
Tell me what I want to hear.
Somewhere in this absence called life...
boys and girls think love is real.
I'll try to smile when I see them.
I wont be the one to say.
The hurt lives inside the space...
inside the place I call secret.
It never opens any more...
though it has no treasures left to sell.
Lie to me and love me.
Lie to me and stay.
Laugh inside if you need to...
but please... dont go.. away.
The rain is cold upon the ground.
The wind is lonesome in the sky.
Somewhere in between we linger...
waiting eagerly to die.
The sun is a cruel reminder...
of a warmth the moon forgot.
Yet it rises every evening...
just to glimpse the day.
Time has left me in its shadow...
forever in the dark.
but I am happy to be something...
even if disguised.
Lie to me and love me.
Lie to me and stay.
Laugh inside if you need to...
but please... dont go.. away.
dont... go... away...
