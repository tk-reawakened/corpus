Talk It Up! Week of May 18th, 2006
Talk it Up! is a weekly thread to brag about your game and anything you've done in the development process. Perhaps you created a massive 150x150 dungeon or pixeled a wicked enemy sprite. Or you think your custom battle system is gonna be great and you wanna say why. It's loose discussion on your own personal game (and only your own game).
Since OBCT is working on getting the Expo set up, I've gone ahead and decided to give the mate a hand with his TKGaming duties. He should be posting these again in no time.

Graphics:
--So far things are going good on this angle. I'm rerendering the title screen and keeping it pretty much the same, but I've also made a great amount of headway on the Silvo Woods in general. The Expo demo will feature the story up to Ander leaving the Silvo Woods, which though it isn't much should be packed with plenty of gameplay.
Programming:
--Things are also going well on this angle. Along with making my code more reworkable should I ever need to, I'm also reworking my Item system and getting that prepared so that it looks spiffier.
