### Tooks · 2006-06-07 15:11 · #B325583

Thanks for the constructive crit guys, Smile now to answer some questions thrown in by Rydath in some greater detail.
I know that in the original game Cloud was never accepted into Soldier, Zack was, and cloud was mentally confused into believing everything Zack did he did, where in reality Cloud was just a guard type fellow (Cloud using a gun? fo-shaw!?)
I'm really not sure how many FF7 fans will enjoy playing this game as it is only based loosely on the world squaresoft created, I have no intentions with this game of connecting it 100% to the storyline of FF7 as I feel that would be impossible. What I hope to achieve is an Alternative look at Shinra. Shinra are not the bad guys, Cloud actually got into Soldier and, for the most part, Sephiroth is actually a semi-nice guy. For now, atleast.
This may not be what most people are looking for in a FF7 spin-off but I myself, as a designer, find it alot easier to base the story loosely on the world of FF7 with my own spin, taking the story where I want it to go (rather then where the FF7 storyline dictates it should). Some people will like this, others won't. You can't please everyone though.
I'm expecting the following in responses;
35% - Oh great! another FF7 spinoff *ignore*
35% - God, what did this idiot do to the storyline? Its rubbish!
20% - Not bad... could be better, alot better
10% - Not bad, infact, I kinda like it.
so yeah, I'm aiming this game at a very limited market =P
