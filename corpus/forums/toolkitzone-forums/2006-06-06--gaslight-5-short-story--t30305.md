# 'Gaslight 5' Short Story

### Skeej · 2006-06-06 15:20 · #B325301

There was once a gangster, only 22-year-old, called Gascoin. He was a bartender in a brand new pub in Chicago. Right after the ceremonial openning of the pub, he was murdered by some gangsters, trying to save the life of his boss. He died, so his boss decided to rename the pub in memoriam his bartender. The brand new pubs name became 'Gaslight', Gas from the bartenders name and light from the suny day, when the incident happened.
Now, the boss, who's 30-year-old himself, five years the tragical event, had problems again with the gangsers, so he's forced now to save his pub with any cost. Will he succeed...?!
