# Originality...Important...or overrated bandwagon?

### Zap! · 2006-06-07 01:43 · #B325467

Let me start off with an opening word of this, originality can work...and it can fail.
This topic, is entirly devouted to the reality of originality. That being, that we all know as of now, the majority of "original," idea's arn't that original, and the out of the one's that are...very few ever make it to see daylight.
So we seek the question...why is origanlity important? This is speaking in terms of games, what at this moment in time, would you rather do, play a game of poker, or play a game of chess. Take that answer, and figure this into it, which game is older? Which game is more original?
To find that, we have to delve deep into the past, and dig through many facts, but my question would be, why does it matter? You can play both, in many different ways, none of them being that original, and all of them having their own name's, weather you know it or not, is that what is going to sell?
Which game does sell better, maybe it's your taste to not have a game that is at all like Chess or Poker, so what kind of game would you like to play? Think of the name of a game that you would want to play, now tell me, how orginal is that game?
Originality, seems to have the mark of a great teller, or does it? It seems the mark of a great teller can create a Zelda spin off and have a good game. Say one of the Zelda expansions wasn't created by the people who made the original Zelda...and say it was as good as it currently is. Take any Zelda game, and tell me, if it was created by someone else would you give it a lower rating just becuase they copied the idea?
Maybe, imagine but...that can be ok I suppose, mark down a game becuase they used a direct rip of an idea, BUT...what happens when everyone seems to pick on people who make games that wern't out of the first 100 popular Hack n' Slash games, and then someone comes pu and says.."Great..pfft..This is so stupid, yet ANOTHER Hack n' Slash game based off saving some damsel in distress who is locked away in a Castle guarded by a fire-breathing dragon. Your game sucks dude, quit life. /wrists" That's the point it's getting to...and for what? The majority of the time, the people who make comments on orginality never have worked on a game, or made up all their work and posted fake results, never to release anything substantial.
So I present the question to you, if we were talking about styles of writing, does orginality become a killer? Take Harry Potter, how original was it? It wasn't that original at the core...but it sold damn well, and is a great read, true it had some originality, that I could tell, but I wouldn't have marked off it if was a Lord of the Rings spin off.
Movies...how original are movies? Generally not, but people don't seem to mind. I think the overall point of originality is to only go with that one spark of genius every now and then, and keep a classic well-going idea installed, and put you're own touchs on it as you go. That my friends, is what I call original, but so many people will bash it saying something isn't original, just becuase it might look like Final Fantasy, or becuase it smells like Zelda.

### SomeNick · 2006-06-11 15:34 · #B326569

Many people say that games don't really need a story. True, there have been many games without a story (basically) that yet struck it high.
However, I think having some story is fun. SUre, as long as it is used carefully, you don't need to bug the player every 3 seconds with some weird flash back or anything, but having a nice story is still essential for making the game original.
Being original doesn't mean finding something nobody has ever done before.
Basically, there are pretty much similar stories.
Most stories will involve one or more of the following: monsters, orcs, aliens, sorceresses, knights, snipers, gunners, robots, swordsmen, mages, creatures in general. All of those are already well known, but you can still make a terrific story based off of them. The defining factor is what your story is:
a) You could have a story in which the sorceress decides to turn the entire world into frogs.
b) Or you could have the same sorceress wanting to make an army of robots to create one mighty and powerful empire. Sounds different, huh?
Likewise, you can derive dozens and dozens more stories. However, never forget gameplay. A good story with a bland gameplay will kill it. Just as it happened in Final Fantasy 8. Good story, bad gameplay.
