# Reign of Chaos

### Internet? whats that? · 2006-06-07 22:23 · #B325662

ideas for the game kinda inspired by guild wars and runescape
Thousands of years ago, the land was a void. Nothing existed on the wasteland we call home. The gods opened a portal to this land, allowing an advanced, and very powerful, species to emerge. Using their advanced technology, they made the land habitable.
A sub species, that they usually used for grunt work, called humans, began to revolt. They demanded freedom, seeking ways to destroy their masters. They far outnumbered the more advanced people, but they only had access to pitiful weapons of iron and wood. Seeking more advanced ways to gain power, they genetically modified themselves, in the same way that the land was created. There was a great war, and the humans won. But not without consequence. Half of the race had become crazed creatures of fire, chaos, and killing. Whereas the rest were given abilities over magic, strength to fight, the gift of marksmanship, and summoning. So now, there is a civil war of sorts among the humans, they are more imprisoned than ever.
basically, you would be able to pick your class, a sub-class like with guild wars, and be able to train to raise skills and gain power. you would search for ways to end the war, and eventually wind up fighting an advanced race.
game features would include:
HUGE scrolling boards - huge as in, 2000+ pixels for exploring - tiled at first, most likely custom drawn in later versions
parties of up to 3 npc's which would follow you around the map
visible enemies on the map, but battle happen on another board
ATB battle system
all menus can be accessed and edited on-board while still moving your player via canvases
an explorable world, with variables for your exploration completion, with rewards for exploring 75%+ of the map
would be a VERY difficult game to make. but i do beleive its possible from what ive seen of the toolkit so far. not asking for a team, as i am new, i dont think anyone would follow me, but i shall try to make something work before i release anything more than this.
