# The Beginning of the End

### forensixman · 2006-06-07 01:44 · #B325468

This is the storyline for the beginning of the game. I just felt inspired, so decided to belt this one out. You can find it on my profile too.
As Zorrick dropped the heavy, red book on the table, a plume of dust erupted from in between each single page. It covered the table as it elegantly settled upon it's wooden surface. He carefully unwrapped the golden bow that held the two covers together, then opened the front cover slowly, as to not break the fragile binding. The first page was completely blank, as was the second. The third, however, contained a simple title:
The Beginning of the End
Zorrick contemplated for a second wether or not to go further, for it was beginning to disturb his thoughts and feelings. But then, he remembered the image of his father being pulled into the shadows and knew he had to continue. He turned the page, and he was met by intricate imagery, laden upon the yellowed pages of the book. It showed an old wizard groping for power, for mastery, and for domination. The next few pages showed the man travelling across different treacheries, seemingly lost but yet very determined. In the next few pages, the illustrator inscribed images that showed a small cave getting closer and closer. Upon entrance, the wizard fought many evils, knocking them down one by one to reach the ultimate prize. At last, the wizard reached the Inner Sanctum, where the glowing orbs rested upon their majestic pedastals. The architecture consisted of stones, stacked upon each other like large, rectangular arches. The center, both on the floor and ceiling, were engravings and etchings that almost certainly entailed the story of the chamber, the orbs and life itself. Each letter that was carved upon the stone was illuminated from the back, emitting a green glow and creating an eerie presence that was nonexistent in the chamber. This was the only color picture in the book, possible because it was of utmost importance.
Zorrick was drawn into the book so much that he didn't notice the hooded figure slip into the archives collection behind him. He examined the last few pages, finishing the story of Greshok the Wizard seal away a demon using powers unbeknown to man. He then was intrigued by the not proven, but suspected clues left behind for whomever was to save the kingdom next time this beast were to break free. And finally, the tomb that was dug by hand in which Greshok lied down in and never got up, for he starved to death on purpose. Now that Zorrick had absorbed this information he had to decide what goes next. Does he look for Greshok's family? Does he forget about it and hope more powerful warriors find has father? Just then, the hooded figure slipped out from behind the bookcase and threw a bag over Zorrick's head. He stunned him, picked him up and ran out the door towards the hole in the ground in which he had come. Zorrick never had a chance...
