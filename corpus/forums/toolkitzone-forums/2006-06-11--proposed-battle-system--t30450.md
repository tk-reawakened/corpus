# Proposed Battle System

### Cedrick Bryan Sagum · 2006-06-11 10:55 · #B326527

Somehow, the battle will be like Yugi-oh, but I've added some RPG elements into it.
---------------------
LEVEL UP SYSTEM
---------------------
You gain exp by defeating enemies in a duel.
You gain exp by destroying opposing enemy summons and enchantments.
At level up, player gains 1 SP
There are some stats that can be raised
MaxHP: Initial = 150; MaxHP + 10 per SP
MaxMP: Initial = 100; MaxMP + 10 per 2 SP
MP Regen (MP Acquired per turn): Initial: 5 /turn; MP Regen + 1 per 2 SP.
Deck Capacity (Allowed "strength" of deck. Each card has a particular deck cost. Sum must not exceed Deck Capacity) Initial: 1000; Deck Capacity +10 per 1 SP
Draw Plus: (Cards drawn per turn, tentative) Initial = 1 ; Draw Plus + 1 per 10 SP!!!
Speed (Determines how fast the spellcaster gains a turn). Speed + 1 per 1 SP
Still thinking of other "stats". In addition to other stats, there are some support skills that you can use
Channel - Discard a card to to gain 10 MP
Conversion - discard card to gain 10HP
--------------------------------------------------------------
Each player has 40 cards in deck. These contain creatures, enchantments and spells.
-------------
OBJECTIVE
-------------
The objective of the game is to bring your opponent's life to 0 by attacking with creatures or using spells. The opponent can only be attacked if he has no creatures. If the player is unable to draw a card, he loses the game.
--------------
CREATURES
--------------
The player summons creatures to fight for them. Maximum number of summons allowed is 5.
A creature has these stats
Name Name of Monster
Mana Cost Mana cost needed to put monster in play
Creature Type Creature Type. Some spells may affect them
Creature Element Creature Element. Affects enemies. Border color determines creature element.
Attack Attack of monster
Max HP Max HP of monster before killed.
Speed How fast these unit gain a turn
Skills Monster skills
Unlike Yugi-oh, which is turn-based, Monsters gain a turn on their own, enabling them to perform their own attacks or skills.
--------
SPELLS
--------
Spells can only be casted by players during their turn. Any number of spells can be cast. Some spells can enhance/weaken a creature, some spells give them ability, some spells destroy creatures and spells and some deal damage. Some spells also inflicts negative status or does unique effects.
-----------------
ENCHANTMENT
-----------------
Enchanments support the wizard and affects everyone throughout the field. Maximum number of enchantment allowd is 5.
Any ideas you can contribute would be welcome
