# This Morning's Sunset

### Lisha · 2006-06-11 14:28 · #B326558

Friday I sat down at the computer meaning to write more of Saphyra's story, but I found myself instead opening up a new document and typing something totally different. It turned out to be a script. From that script I began to think of an idea for a game, which is now called This Morning's Sunset. So yeah. I'll introduce it now.
First of all, in no way whatsoever does this mean that I am giving up on The Horse Smell or putting The Horse Smell on hiatus. I'll divide my time equally between the two games, with The Horse Smell on a slight favor.
Okay. That's out. Moving on.
Story: You play as Araiyan, a boy of about fifteen, sixteen, or seventeen (I can't decide which yet). He lives with his mother and his orphaned cousin Neleya, who is about his age and is very very sick. He hates his mother, because, to put things frankly, she's a complete jerk. To put it mildly. He is very close to Neleya, who is around his age. His mother refuses to pay for a doctor for Neleya, so she just gets sicker and sicker. On her last day of life, she asks Araiyan, "When it happens, will you protect me?" He has no idea what she's talking about, but he doesn't want to upset her. He also thinks that she's probably the person he loves most out of everyone else, and he would protect her from anything. Would it matter what it was? So he answers yes. She responds by saying that she will protect him, too. He tells her to get some rest and then leaves, still not knowing what she was talking about. The next morning, Neleya is dead. But even after she's dead, he keeps having these strange feelings that she's still around. He'll turn and think he sees her for a split second, swear that he hears her voice every so often. And then, two days later, he finds a letter from a dead girl....
I know it sounds kind of cliche at the beginning, but trust me, it isn't your generic "save the lovely girl" RPG. In fact....
Gameplay: ... it really isn't much of an RPG at all. There is no fighting in this game, and, like the Horse Smell, it is done in first person clickable view. The goal of the game is to try and find Neleya and figure out what the whole deal here is. You will have a party, though instead of fighting they each have certain strengths that you might need at certain parts of the game. Example: Lets just say you have a girl named Lily in your party. Maybe Lily is really charismatic and can get people to tell you stuff that no other characters could get them to tell you. Or maybe theres this guy named Henry. Maybe Henry is good at finding stuff, so the player with have little hints as to where passageways or clues (for lack of a better word) are if they choose to play as him for that part of the game. But you will also have relationships with each of these party members, so youve got to be nice to them or else they just might choose to ditch you or refuse to help you. There wont be excessive puzzles in the game, like the Myst games for example. Instead you have to read things and look at things and try to put two and two together yourself, all building up to the end of the game when all this stuff will be very useful.
Wow. Ive been typing a lot. Sorry about that. ^_^ Kudos to anyone who read all that. The profile is here: <http://www.toolkitzone.com/games.php?game=2070> Right now there isn't much there but the title screen and a more detailed, well-written version of the begining of the story, but I'm almost done with one screen and a profile pic, as welll as the mwin, so more stuff will be added most likely later today.
So there you have it.
