Now, this is a fun little competition I've thought up. And because we know it's all most people care about, I'll tell you what the prize is right off the bat. A never before released methods pack. Now it may not be the biggest thing but it has a few cool little features:
1.) Get Loop
Description: It's just like it says. It's a Get Loop, but unlike the Get() command which requires you to press the button at the exact moment, or the Wait() command which is obstructive to the game, this is a infinite Get() loop that ends when the user presses a button.
2.) Percent Finder
Description: Just as it says, it's useful if you want to find a specific percent out of a player's health, mana ect. Or just to play around with mathematical equations.
3.) Random Interval
Description: It's just like the Random() code except you get to choose an interval. It can be in any range as long as it doesn't duck bellow zero or go above the random number.
Now this may seem a small pack, but you can do anything you want with it. ( And I mean ANYTHING, I'm no longer responsible for it, it's yours, for all extensive purposes you made it, you can take all the credit for it and everything. )
Now on to the competition:
Let's see who can manipulate the market the best. You're given 50,000 imaginary American dollars, and are able to buy into any stock in any amount as long as it's within your limit. You are also allowed to go into more then one stock. Now at the end of two months ( August 9th ), whoever has earned the most money wins. All submittions must be made within 2 weeks via private message. They must include your account name, stocks, and amounts. After 2 weeks I will post the collected stocks, amounts, and competitors on a reply. If I've made a mistake you must tell me within 2 days or it's permenant.
I hope this is in the right section. Enjoy the competition, and follow the rubric or else you won't qualify for the competition.
Note: Only one submition per person.

I'm not sure what you mean, of course, by "random interval", but I suspect you mean this:

**Code:**

```
method randInt(low!, high!)

{

   returnMethod(random(high! - low!) + low!)

}
```

**Code:**

