# Rune Denver Plot

### forensixman · 2006-06-12 15:51 · #B326824

Rune Denver, a boy of 14, lives in the Kingdom of Mecka. This kingdom has had a very interesting past, but has remained peaceful for over a hundred years. In it's past, however, lies a group of high anarchists called the Blood Army. They were led by none other than a woman named Helena, who incidentally was the Queen at the time. Her husband, King Harshune, did not know that the largest anarchist group in the union was led by his own wife, for they put a lot of effort into keeping this a secret. After Helena and the Blood Army did not need the King anymore, they set up an assassination. They killed him easily, but did not do a good enough job of covering up their tracks. The Blood Army was traced and killed off, including the Queen. The new king went to great lengths to eliminate all traces of the traitor that penetrated the walls of the governmental palace. Nobody ever heard of them again...
Now, over 100 years later, the Blood Army is back with a vengeance.
They are not using just brute force and tactics, but supernatural powers that they found the source of while in their dormant period. This time, they are not just looking to control the government, but the universe as a whole...The problem - nobody knows that they have returned except for the town of Drecht, in which they attacked first. They meant to wipe out everyone, but a certain 14-year old happened to remain elusive to their radar...
Now Rune needs to find a way to overpower their ancient, supernatural device or everything he knows will be torn away - including his soul...
