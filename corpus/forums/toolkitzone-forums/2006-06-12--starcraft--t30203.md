### Darwin · 2006-06-12 14:16 · #B326811

Yes, but Red Alert still had same "mechanics" for both sides.
In StarCraft, for comparision, each race has completely unique features and ways to do things. Terrans can repair their buildings and construct them regularly, the Zerg can only built on "creep", which is spread through certain buildings, all their units regenerate and the workers transform themselves into constructions while units are grown from larva generated by their hives, while the Protoss summon buildings (and don't need to be there at construction place after the summoning), and can only build them near power sources.
Needless to say, of the above, the Zerg are certainly the most innovative race (my pal plays Zerg Wink).
Compared to this, in Red Alert, both sides always build same way, both sides use electricity, both sides train units at barracks or factories, etc.
