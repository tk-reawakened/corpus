### Spyder · 2006-06-13 22:05 · #B327314

RPGMaker essentially sacrifices freedom and power for ease of use and speed. It's scripting system is far less complicated than RPGCode, but at the same time, is not nearly as flexible and powerful. Although RPGMaker does come with some very nice looking sprites, tiles, and background images, it's limited to a very low resolution and low number of colors (particularly with RPGMakerXP) which, although allow it to run quite fast and smooth, force your game to look like something being played in DOS.
The toolkit provides a platform that allows much more freedom to create the game you want to create, and not something tailored around the engine which will eventually look like every other RPGMaker game out there. Your not limited to low resolution graphics or 256 colors. There is no limit on the number of tiles you can use per board. Board sizes are much more flexible. RPGCode allows a much easier way of creating custom menus, battle systems, and such. With RPGMaker you essentially have to trick the system to do these things. The RPGMaker engine itself does not support it, but people have found ways to work around it so that it can be done.
If you want a simple way to quickly create a generic RPG that won't take you a hell of a lot of time to produce, then RPGMaker is for you. If your interested in making something original, then the Toolkit is for you.
I once considered using RPGMaker to make a rough draft of my game and then port it to the TK, but found that because I'd already been using the TK for so long, it would take me just as long to use RPGMaker as it would to use the TK because I'd have to learn an entirely new engine Confused
