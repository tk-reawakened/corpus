### MrB · 2006-06-13 19:36 · #B327246

Yes, if you remember the first one of these topics, you are truly a tkgaming oldbie... Wink
I don't really know what happened over the last two months and so, but as of now, this is where we stand for features and games...
Game of the Week (Tuesdays)
Talk It Up! (Mondays)
Members of the Month (Last day of each month)
And that's it. If you were doing a feature before, post here. I'll include it in the list for discussion. Also, The Appreciated Man has ran its course, and it's time for a new game. So we need suggestions for a game. It cannot involve distracting people from their current projects, but it should promote playing or creating games. So a weekly competition to see who can pixel the best ____ won't work. It shouldn't involve anyone actually creating something unless it can be used directly for their game or promotes them working on their game.
So here's the list of ideas. If you want to add one, just reply to this topic. If you have an opinion on an idea, reply here, and please specify which idea you're talking about.
Features
TK All Nighter!
You can't go wrong with a TK All Nighter. You guys seemed to enjoy them, so perhaps setting a monthly date to have one would be a good idea?
If you've never heard of TKAN, it's a night where we basically get in groups in MSN Messanger conversations and work on our games during the night for six hours straight. Usually it's more fun than it sounds, hehe.
Publicity Shoutout
Each week, a topic is created where people can freely suggest game profiles that they think people should check out that they probably haven't noticed yet.
Games
Red Circle Wars (open to name change...)
This is actually a twist on an idea for the forum I had before I even created it. Basically, we'd split ourselves into two teams. Being the Amercian history freak that I tend to be, I chose the team names Founding Fathers (blue) and Rough Riders (yelllow). It's a little complicated, so here's an example week:
Myself and OBCT are on the Rough Riders team, while Bim and UltimaWizard are on the Founding Fathers team. On Wednesday, we all post a single screenshot in our game profile. As with all screenshots, the community sees them and gives them a rating of 1 to 5. One week later, we tally the points - each team receiving one point for every rating their screenshots receive. So say my screenshot received a rating of 4, OBCT's a 4, Bim a 5, and UltimaWizard a 2. Rough Riders would have a total of 8 points, while Founding Fathers only had 7 points. Rough Riders win.
As far as picking teams, people changing teams, etc., I haven't thought about it really. I was thinking about having one Captain for each team. They would pick teams just like were playing a friendly game of football.
Also, just to clarify, each person is only allowed to post one screenshot for the game and must post it in the featured topic (along in the game profile of course). No changing your mind for what screenshot's rating you want to use at the end of the week or anything. And teams aren't repicked each week. You're assigned a team and stay on it, but you don't have to post a screenshot each week. You just won't have a chance to score your team points if you don't.
Now start brainstorming!
