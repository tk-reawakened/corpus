# Idea Pitch

### Kingrussel · 2006-06-13 04:21 · #B327080

I have been working on a game plot and I want to get others opinion.
Title:Living By A Thread.
You are King a strange bandit just doing as he feels.
You are run out of your current "home town" because you stole from the richest and most powerful citizen of the town Shill and are forced to wander the desert with nothing but your staff your clothes and the gem you stole.
You barely make it to the oasis near the next town and then you head for the town for a new place to stay.
Sadly the only place to buy is very expensive.
The land lord allows you to pay a down payment being your gem.
You now are poor and have no where to go.
You read a sign that states there is a tourney at the colousem that has has a 20,0000 Rilt reward.
You quickly go to apply but are told there are no spost open.............but luckly the clerk there liked your humor and gets you a chance to talk with the boss of the arena for a chance.
You go into the room labled "L-K" and you see a big burly man who towers infront of you angrly.
You ask " L-K?" and he laughs and tells you he is just the help and leaves.
You look around the room and then as you go to the closet you are hit with your own staff and knocked out.
You wake up and a women with firey red hair and green eyes looks at you and says "So you are that King guy right?"
You say "L-K? Please don't hit me!"
She replies "Yeah but just call me leema."
You talk with her and beg and try and get into the tourney.
She says "ok..........One condiditon...........even if you win you have to beat me"
That is the main intro to the game.
I want to know what others think about it.
I am new to the whole game writing thing so be kind.
