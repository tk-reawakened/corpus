# My idea for an intro/ending

### Myth · 2006-06-13 00:39 · #B327003

I thought of something that I thought would be good for a beginning and/or ending, but I wanted to know if it seemed like I was copying other things to much.Here is the idea:
When you select new game, it plays the Mission Impossible Theme Song and shows a bottled explosion(a magical item in the game) with a long fuse on it. The fuse is lit, and one at a time, each main character comes on the screen and it shows their name and stuff.
I thought it would be kinda cool, but then I thought it might be copying stuff to much. People all ready think I got the name of my game from The Chronicles of Narnia. Well I thought of this name before I even heard of that book/movie.
