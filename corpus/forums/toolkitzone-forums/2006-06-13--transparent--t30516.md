I need that too, so I am making some kind of method that includes cursor map and setImageTransparent, but then moves the set image as the cursor is moved. If you are RPGCode savvy, this will be fairly easy.

### DrakeLord · 2006-06-13 17:04 · #B327191

When making your cursor, make the background pure red (255,0,0). Then use CursorMapHand("cursor.bmp") somewhere in the start.prg, or wherever you need it.
Make sure "cursor.bmp" is the name of your own. Wink
