# Traps...and more.

### UltimaWizard · 2006-06-13 03:44 · #B327076

In Ander's Journey, I've hit a little part that I haven't gotten to coding in any way, but I'm not sure about, and though it *is* my call, I need advice and opinions on.
First things first, I have to explain a little about what I'm up to. Right now there are two parts to AJ that have my attention: ST-Effects (like Poison, Sleep, etc.) and a character by the name of Ricardo who uses a lance...but makes traps from dropped monster parts. My Battle System sports a Duel System that whenever you hit [Attack] and choose a target, the character who it is actually goes 1-on-1 with the enemy you chose...my response to the vague sword-swinging of the Final Fantasy games.
Ricardo's 'job' is that of a Sapper. For those of you unfamiliar with military terminology, sappers were military engineers back in the OOOLLLLDDD days...they're first reported to have been used by the Assyrians, actually. He uses a spear for fighting, but has a special subcommand on his Item menu that reads 'Trap'. Basically, you choose 2 monster items and Ricardo throws it. Whoever runs across it first trips it...and gets a nasty suprise ranging from poison, minor damage, or major damage.
This brings me to the first point of contention--Status effects. Everyone I know who plays games like FFVII and FFVIII just dosen't use status-based magic. I personally only equip status materia on characters in the afore-mentioned FFVII to work towards getting the coveted Master Magic materia. Even in games without such a lush customization system, Status-Effect Attacks tend to be useless--they never work on enemies no matter what and always work on your party members. There's no point in having them unless you want a headache--just hit with attack magic or your sharp pointy weapons, and you'll carry the day a lot faster than waiting for the enemy's HP to drain .00000000000000000005% of HP at a time.
Though I'm considering granting some enemies Poison attacks (and you do want to heal my version of Poison immediately in Ander's Journey, trust me...) I've given the mages of the game and the characters no way in which to apply status effects of any sort to enemies because it's simply useless--if it works, it makes the battle too easy, but because that's so damn rare, what's the point? It's just stupid and obviously already unbalances the game.
Thus back to traps, how I'm not sure if they should cause ST-Effects...and my second point of contention--Traps and arguably a Trap Kit you can win from a special Future-Branching NPC quest that allows any non-Ricardo character to craft and set a trap in battle. Though traps are a rather unique idea that will make fighting human trap-laying opponents an iffy 'are you feeling lucky?' matter, will it unbalance the game and therefore not bother being implemented, or should it be added as an interesting reason in which to pursue FBNPC subplots? I need advice on these matters, please.
