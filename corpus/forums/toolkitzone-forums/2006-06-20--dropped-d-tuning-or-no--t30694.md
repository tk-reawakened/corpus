### Randy · 2006-06-21 05:39 · #B329365

Well, considering that Avenged Sevenfold makes heavy use of the dropped tunings, I don't think it is "nub" to play their music dropped since that is the way they wrote it.
I use a variety of different tunings, but usually stick with Standard E or half-step down. It depends on the vocal ranges of the songs usually because some songs sound better in the lower vocal ranges that a lower tuning will give you and vice versa.
On another note, dropped D offers much more than "easy power chords". I love using it to play a variety of chords utilizing open (unfretted) strings, etc.
It can also make for some interesting solos. Anyone that says dropped D is for "nubs" obviously hasn't experimented with it much.
