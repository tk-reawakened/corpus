# Blade of Champion, The

> Demo 2 500 years ago, the world was ruled by a dark spirit that had the power of the Dark Blade on his side. As a young teenage warrior, you destroy the Dark Spirit, but you are frozen for 500 years. When you are released, you learn that the ruthless Overlord Shadow Maker is planning to seek out the heir of the Dark Spirit. If he successfully finds the heir of the Dark Spirit, he will then revive the powers of the Dark Blade, and use the heir and the Dark Blade to revive the Dark Spirit himself! You attend to a warrior-training academy to learn new skills that will help you defeat Shadow Maker.
