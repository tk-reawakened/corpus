# Dark Mystic

> Demo 1.0 In the middle ages, 4 elements balanced all life. Water, Fire, Earth, and Darkness. Darkness controlled all evil, and was sealed away never to be summoned again. 3 relics were scattered throughout the land, one for fire, one for water, and one for earth. Together, they were the only way to break the seal on darkness, and unleashed evil to the world once again. The noble Dark Mystic was appointed the task of keeping the relics safe, and keeping the world from darkness. However, with his new found powers darkness soon took control of him, and slowly but effectifly, the Dark Mystic began to lose his life.
