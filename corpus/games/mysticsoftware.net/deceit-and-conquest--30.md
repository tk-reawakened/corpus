# Deceit and Conquest

> Chapter 1.1 Deceit and Conquest is considered by many to be the greatest RPGToolkit game ever created. In fact, the Toolkit itself couldn't even contain D&C in it's entirety. Cyrus has now discontinued the game in hopes that his story will be better protrayed in novel form.
