# Dragoon, The

> 1.2 The Dragoon takes place in the Davian Kingdom, the long time ruler of Davia, a region that has always appeared peaceful and honorable. With lively town villages on the outskirt of the intimidating Davian Castle, overlooking tranquil forests, quiet streams, and hundreds of acres of rolling pastures, the stable kingdom is envied by the rest of the world. The Davian Kingdom has conquered nearly a fourth of the world and steadily controls all of its territory. Most of its prospering population admires and respects its ruling kingdom - but the unfortunate few that have lived to see its unmasked ruling do not. You are Ervin, a respected officer of the Davian Force climbing his way up the ranks quickly. Your father was a successful general in the force, and you are simply following in his footsteps. Since you were a child your father had pushed you to join the force. You had never questioned if its what YOU wanted, and when you finally had to decide on a path for your profession, you simply went the road you were pressured and expected to go. Your father has been killed in battle, creating a hole in the force you are expected to eventually fill. You are currently on an important mission with your life-long companion, Jacobs, to find and rescue a Dragoon in the Tivian Caves. Jacobs joined the forces simultaneously as you, and the two of you went through training and are venturing up the rankings ladder at the same quick pace.
