# TK Land

> Graphics Demo More Screenshots: The new world is thriving on a new way of thinking, RPG Code as a science. The old world of spells and magic are nothing more than old tales of legends, but history repeats itself; the new and old world will collide and the balance will tip. Evil is never truly defeated, only suppressed. However there is always a slight chance that someone, a catalyst, will see our past mistakes and not allow us to make them again. There have been prophecies made long ago that are starting to be unraveled. The Catalyst will come to drive evil back. Spyder a master ninja who has had haunting dreams since his childhood days awaken to the realization that his dreams are becoming reality. Destined to rise against a force unseen in the new world he must conquer his own fears if he is to stand a chance. The only link he has to the old world lies in the local RPG Code shop owner, MrG. With his guidance and loyal friends they will set forth on an adventure to save the world and bring balance back to Tk Land. Player Ratings Have you played this game?
