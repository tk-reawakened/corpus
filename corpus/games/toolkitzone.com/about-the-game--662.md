# about the game

An RPG Zelda game that i'm hoping will have online play. Link reurns to Hyrule to find a new evil that wants to avenge Ganons defeat. So Link must seek out the 6 Sages to join his party and fight the evil along with villagers, monsters and anyone else who is willing to join Link. Of course, i'm ripping off music from Final Fantasy because its just too dam good. It's also gana have 3D cinomatics that I will create with a different program. I'm shure it will be a fun and addictive game.
