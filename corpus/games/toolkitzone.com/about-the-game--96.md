story...
posted on 07 October, 2004 by [Alex0Xander](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=29)
*Use to include a whole lot more, but we cant have the whole story revealed before time now can we? =]* - Me one night, long ago at around 5am, half asleep. Young man finds himself changing into a horrible being of destruction, and goes on a quest to find himself while avoiding the persecution of those who hate his kind. Vale Affray is about a young man who finds out he is a SEED. SEED is what TErrans, the people of the planet, call decendants of a man named Dunovan Hirohart who was once a great hero. Dun (for short) saved this planet from that great era of death when a meteor crashed into Terra and brought an onslaught of fiends, and along with it a demon sorceror from another universe. The story is nearly forgotten now, but one thing is certain; something horrible happened during this era, something unspeakable. Whatever it was, it caused the people of terra to loathe decendants of Dunovan, and now.. the SEED are on the run.
*"What if were not alone in this universe, or what if this is not the only universe?"*
**.Vale Affray.**

griever specifics
posted on 20 May, 2004 by [Alex0Xander](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=29)
GRiever used to be Dunovans sword, and somehow it makes its way into Alex's hands (youll see how in the game). Like Dunovan and Zane, the the blood of Ora runs through it, so now its a demon sword. This sword eats other weapons, and absorbs their stats. I will not go into great detail on the stats and stuff, cuz I like to surprise people, and if I do say so mysekf, its quite ingenious. The stats include FIRE, ICE, WIND, THUNDER, Anger, Sorrow. Anger and sorrow are special stats that determine Grievers strenght. Also if a certain Stat combination is achieved, Griever changes. Griever Also determines Alex's Perfect Demon form..oops..said too much.
