# Blood Ritual

Reach out feel & See The Evil... A tradegy has befallen a man named "Ammott". He lost his Betrothed during the night. He now must embark on a journey to save his loved one from the grip of the undead. He swore he will seek out and stop them out, but before its too late... - Equip sub-weapons such as Bible's, Crosses, Holy Water and More to give you a new edge in battle! - Equip the souls of the past Vampire Hunters on your Sub-weapons to give them more of an edge.
