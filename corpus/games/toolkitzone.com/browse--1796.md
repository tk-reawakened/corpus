# Browse

Well, I've just started on this game-creation venture of mine using toolkit 3. I can't say much about the game at this point but that it'll be pretty much a basic turn-based battle RPG with a nice origional story using alot of in-game elements from FFXI though. It'll be a long time before I'm able to release a full demo which is my main goal at the moment. I'm not that good with making battle systems or menu systems either, but that dosen't mean I'll let my game look neglected, and if anyone is ... "Finding Freedom: Between the Devil and the deep blue sea" is a story about the conflict between men and demons in the world of Feorra. A land that man was banished from and cast into the sea. Man has yet to claim it's place among the land, but has mastered the oceans in the process, with giant ship-cities faring the stormy waters and sailing past the demon-infested land.
