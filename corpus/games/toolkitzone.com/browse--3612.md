'Magna Carta' is all about the search for the ultimate battle cards, spreaded through the country. Everyone plays it and it's superpopular. The idea behind the game is that you, the newbie, defeat other players to get better cards and in the end becoming the world champion of 'Magna Carta'. Expect a story mode, exhibition mode and a challenge mode. Story mode is just running around the country defeating gyms and becoming the champion, in exhibition mode you can fight anyone you have already defeated in story mode and challenge mode is just winning 10 consecutive battles to get some good cards.
