# Cry of the Dead

You live near a haunted house and somehow find yourself trapped inside. In order to leave, you must help the ghosts of the house cross over. Each ghost has a task you must complete as an evil ghost tries to stop you. You slowly die and each task you complete replenishes some health. If you run out of health, you die and are doomed to walk the halls of the house for all eternity.
