# DC (tentative title)

Based of the disgruntled comic series, and being 99.9% custom made, this game is going to stray away from all the general aspects of the common rpg, but doing so in a comical manner. At the same time it promises to be the most humorous TK game to date. (that is, if we ever finish it...) Anyway, you start out as the main character of the comic, Blaino, a protagonistic and down to earth boxing penguin. Of course, several other characters will join you as you progress.
