# Fantasy World Era

A sin which destroyed the past world for its bad deeds towards the gods has returned for revenge...but this command wasnt demanded by the gods, just out of shure evil. Shif, a famous bounty hunter and millionare lives the life everyone wants. Even though many envy him so, theyre still happy to see normal humans can make it out of the hardest of times. One day on a normal bounty, shif finds a item that a demon requires, but little did shif know, the demon was himself!
