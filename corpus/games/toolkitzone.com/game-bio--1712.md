**Game**
Midnight 13
**Lead Developer**
Midnight 13. A young man appears suddenly on a beach in the middle of nowhere wondering who he is and where he is. As he embarks on a journey to learn the truth and destroy his alleged brother constructing a diabolical scheme far away. **Plot Summary**
November 27th, 2009 **Profile Last Updated**
Progress
Graphics - 1%
*For a while many artists were working on the team and they got a significant amount of designs drawn up before leaving for various reasons either temporarily or permanently. Not much has yet been modelled and so far im the only team member to have spent time modelling anything, All but one model are works in progress. I hope to recruit more people in this field once our next demo is complete.*
Music - 5%
* Midnight 13 already has an extensive OST of music created for it. 10 songs have been completed, Another 6 are in the process of being rewritten, several are in the process of being editted to perfection, And about another half dozen are being worked on. The 5% completion in this section is just for the 10 songs finished.
Sound Effects - 0%
*We have no one to fill in this job, And this job is of minor priority until the later stages of Midnight 13's development.*
Storyline - 5%
* The progress bar has been rolled back significantly. The plot for Midnight 13 is in the process of being completely rewritten with a large portion of early parts in the game already done, And older copies of the plot upto near the finale of the game. Currently there is not much in the way of side storylines or alternate storylines, However the games plot is extensive so 5% is quite alot in gameplay value compared to 100% on most games. 5% represents only the rewritten work.
Programming - 1%
* We are going to create our own engine from scratch but currently have no programmer to help with this sizeable task and thus programming is minimal. All of the basics required to make Midnight 13 have been prototyped without an engine however and can be easilly transferred over once an engine exists.
Documentation - 1%
*Documentation on Midnight 13's gameplay and all other aspects of the Midnight 13 that need creating or implementing.*
