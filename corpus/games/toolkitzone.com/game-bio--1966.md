"It has been rumoured...I have heard whispers...But I didn't think it was true...This is just a legend, childs play...but...it's real? It can't be real!" - Drugh Mordan **Plot Summary**
January 4th, 2007 **Profile Last Updated**
Basically, this game has been designed to run off an external server, using the winsock plugin, this way the stress of being online is handled by the server, and not by the TK, therefor TK doesn't take such a performance hit. Now, as for the actual playing of the game? I have designed quite a nice storyline to go into this game, along with the ability to go off and just have fun interacting with other players. Right now, I'm looking at...somewhere between September or October. Notice, I am leaving the year off, but shooting for '06. Of course I can do it, I have been working in WinSock alot in C++ here recently, for the main purpose of being ABLE to do this. Why that's not a very fair thing to say! I am fully prepared to finish this game. I am I can get some help though, mainly in the art department. Actually, yeah I am not too good at art design myself, so I will try to do what I can, but it's -ALOT- of work, to be going at an entire game...all by yourself. I have to worry about sound, art, coding, overall time management, and so many other things! I already know I want help in the graphics department, but not at this stage. I want a more solid, concrete base working, before I start recruiting other people to help! As of this time, no. That's not a question! I honestly, have no idea. I figure it will run a bit slower than a normal TK game will, just becuase of the overhead with this game being online. Well, I tell you what. You make an online game and tell me how smooth it runs. Yes, and I intend to stay very well mannered, despite what anyone says. I have been thinking about, and designing this project for over two months...yes. Over TWO months. Many times I have told myself, I should just quit, not tell anyone, and just not even try it. Well, that side of me sucks dick, and I'm not about to give in to something that could be so beneficial to me, and everyone else at TKZ. Well, clearly, if I have an online game that runs, I could release the server technology that runs it, along with with the game's source. This way, people take out all of the "game" and use my game as a game base for their online games. Of course not It would make me very happy to see something I did, benefit alot of people. Even if they never released their games, it's a really nice feeling to know other people are using YOUR game base. No. At this point, no! once I have a game base started, that works well, I will hold events that will allow you to get your name in the credits. ( But not for free, you still will have to do quality work to get your content into the game. ) I can't work on it 24/7, I need a break! Yes. Despite the extremely ugly GUI, I am using toolkit 3. What exact version? Probably the one that is about to come out. I don't know yet. I'm hoping I will have a job and a nice paycheck before I need to worry about that. But, if not, at the very least I will be able to run it off my own computer. I read your mind, what specs are my computer and connection? Yeah ADSL on a 2.5ghz intel 4 processor. Best server machine ever? No. Will it be better then nothing if it comes down to it? Yes. Whoever is running the server, is going to have to be VERY responsible, that is, unless I decide to make a server that you have to logon using an admin account to change anything. But, that wouldn't be very fair to the guy hosting it. At any rate, don't worry about it now. I intend to release them as as I can say they are stable. Can I give a date on this? No, I just started re-writing the server becuase I realized I had put alot of work into the game concept and design, but little into the server design, so due to some design issues it has to be re-done. This isn't too big of a problem though, it wasn't *complete* anyhow before, but it was working a bit. Yes, just drop me a PM! **Q: What is the design of CMO like?**
