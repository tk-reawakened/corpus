# Game Bio

**Game**
Talona
**Lead Developer**
The world of Talona is broken - dominated by huge volcanoes and canyons, deserts and wastelands. The surface of the only continent is cracked and chaotic with only a few civilisations struggling to cling on to existance. Talona is essentially about a futile quest: Not a quest to save the world, not a quest to save humanity, but a quest to right the wrongs of the past and gain an understanding of why things went so horribly wrong. **Plot Summary**

![Box](templates/default/images/icons/more.gif) **Main Characters**
Findec?no is an aprentice of master Valandil. A talented psionicist and philosopher, Findec?no often finds himself taking ideas to extreem levels. Findec?no has the sence of humour expected from someone who has grown up around people who enjoy philosophical musings - a fact that Valandil is constantly trying to change. With his initiation into the order of Malona and a marriage with Nienna sure to follow, Findec?no is leading a pretty stable life. One of the more mysterious characters in the game, much of the main storyline is spent finding clues about her past. She has immense psionic strength, however having suffered a huge memory loss she has not got the experience to weild this strength. Tear is one of the best fighters in the village - much to the despair of the younger men. She is the champion of the ritual of combat and has not yet been bested. Aswell as being a good fighter she is also aprenticed to a psionist, her mental abilities aid her physical prowess. Fingon has had many traumatic experiences over the past and these have effected his psionic power. He has power over emotions, and the atmosphere around him is constantly changing. Because of this there are few who can stand being around him - Tear is his greatest freind. Not exactly the nicest psionicist alive. Xarnen uses his powers to his own great advantage - always in constant competition with his rival Findec?no. Xarnen has a curious system of honour, he always repays debts - so he works hard to make sure he is NEVER in debt to anyone. He expects of everyone that which he expects from himself, and so looks down upon those without the power of psionics. The leader of the great city-state of Fortisamo. Saturan rules his city with an iron dictatorship - keeping absolute controll of his people and soldiers. He has great physical strength, which makes up for his lack of mental power. Similar to Malona, Salandrea has great power. However, his power is only matched by his desire for controll - Controll over anything and everything in existance.
