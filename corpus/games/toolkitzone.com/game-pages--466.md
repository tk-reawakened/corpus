# game pages

**about the game**
You controll four soldiers on diffrent missions to eventually rise through the ranks in the war that they are battling. But these aren't any soldiers, these are kids. And these aren't just any kids, they are nerds.
added on 13 August, 2004 by [pixelated](http://www.toolkitzone.com/forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=3781)
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/466/941.JPG)
Name - Zach
Age - 13
Assignment - Stealth Soldier
Zach is a huge nerd. He is ignored by all except his friends, except when he is being teased. He has been a nerd for 9 years, so he has really gotten used to it. But he is really good at sneaking around. During the missions, you will use Zach to sneek past guards to do things like pull alarms to draw the guards away, or grab an item that is essential to the Cool.
Age - 13
Assignment - N\A
OK, here is some science fiction for all you fans out there. Jack is a seriously screwed person. He was born with psychic powers allowing him to levitate, become invisible, go intangable, read minds, and use telekinesis. He has just recently found out about his powers, and he still has trouble controlling them. On his missions, you will have to go invisible and steak out important Cool meetings, but watch out! He can only be invisible for 30 seconds. So you will have to find a way to hide when you are visible, and a way to get up close when you are not.

Age - 12

Name - Hack
Age - 14
Assignment: Computer Engineering
Hmm... I wonder what Hack is good at. It is a real stumper. Sorry, bad joke. He has been raised on the computer, so duh!!! He is a nerd. His missions will be text-based, basicially trying to find a file in a limited amount of time before a computer virus protection program comes and knocks you off the network. Fast typing will be needed in his missions.
