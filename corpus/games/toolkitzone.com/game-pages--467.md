**A COUPLE GOOD CONTROLLABLE GUYS:**
(Human Soldier): **Runes Casandral**
A carefree, laid-back assassin that just recently joined SilverSpear, seeking training with the sword. Has a huge grudge against Malcutoiner, who murdered his brother. Weapon: Yo-Yo
(Human Mortal Fighter): **Don Hodges**
Don is a serious, powerful fighter of SilverSpear, and being a Mortal Fighter, he sure knows how to kill enemies in one blow. He has a large future, but doesn't know about it. Weapon: Scythe
(Human Metal Knight): **Idris Almquist**
A drifter who fights for revenge of her destroyed hometown. She survived the attack when she hid in a dragon's nest, then slain the dragon. Weapon: Dual Lances
(Human Siege Knight): **Erikk Hatterick**
The big boss of SilverSpear, his maces can put the hurt on heavily-armored foes. But Don is still more skilled, and no one knows why Erikk's the boss. He fights for his dead wife, Zuri. Weapon: Mace
(Human White Sorcerer): **Harold Geezer**
A strange, hunchbacked man with a weird last name and a staff. He seems like he belongs in a mental house, but he still has great skill with spells 'n such. Weapon: Ruby Staff
(Human Archer): **Betsy Regal**
Possibly the best archer in the game. Her strength is amazing, and if you get her up to the Hunter class quickly, she'll learn some moves to increase her defense by a lot. Give her a crossbow and let her do her thing! Weapon: Compound Bow
(Human Theif): **Nate Roferg**
The baddest of the Pikes. His armor may seem primitive, but his skills will serve you well. Once he reaches Nightblade class, he'll dish out damage like there's no tomorrow when he learns to use a Crystal Claw. He's terrible with his war hammer, but he's the best at using secondary weapons. Weapon: Hammer
(Human Swordsman): **Yanisin Cafwhilder**
A gentleman with perfect manners. He despises Malcutioner and lends his sword to use as much as he can. He pleases women but doesn't know it. Well, he has some pretty good Strength and some average defense, as well as tons of elemental attacks to learn. On top of that, his armor looks like a tux! Weapon: Rapier
(Human Black Sorceress): **Bronwyn**
A powerful magic user, her spells can turn around most battles. She's not with SilverSpear, but she's against Malcutioner anyway. Her past is unknown. Weapon: Staff
(Human Aero Knight): **Celia Hatterick**
The daughter of Erikk, she is quite skilled with many weapons. Sheis the only person on the good side to use the Airbound Technology. She likes Don... A lot... :)))))))))) Weapon: Tornadic Lance
(Human Axeman) **Nicolette Ferina**
The only other survivor of the fire in Idris' village. She can easily carry an axe that a grown man would struggle with. She can also handle Secondary weapons on her left hand, but she's not very good with them and can only use 1st level ones. Weapon: Axe
(Bandat Theif) **Jef Harbor**
A weird bandat that lives in the Valkren Woods. He might join the party depending on how you play through the game. Weapon: Big Knife & Small Knife
**"Grand" Kaona** (???? Hunter)
A "changed" former Malcutioner. Nobody trusts him, due to some crime that Runes never knew about. His skill with a javelin is unmatched. Weapon: Javelin
(Human Sage) **Lem Scoffer**
A smart scholar living close to Malcutioner's destruction base. He knows some great spells and can actually survive a couple of physical hits. He might join the party dpending on how you play through the game. Weapon: S. Gun
(Ghost) **Zuri Hatterick**
She only appears in flashbacks as the lovely wife of Erikk.
(Kalinder Axeman): **Rast Cohander**
The only interactive Kalinder in the game. He is mortal enemies with Usek and Crash, so you meet him during the Crash fight. He also seems to know about the fire in Idris' village. He has extremely tough defense for an Axeman, and he has some decent attack too. But he still has low HP... Weapon: Drill

**DA ENEMIES:**
(Human ???): **Daern**
The recently deceased leader of Malcutioner that died when trying to retrieve the Celestial Emerald. His greed killed millions of innocent people. He wasn't much of a leader, but nearly led Malcutioner to victory.
(Human Aero Knight): **Cardinal**
Using the prototype Airbound Technology, he fights with a charmed lance that turns into a tornado when it is used. He is the main scout of Malcutioner, but he packs a punch anyway.
(Elf Brawler): **Usek**
A dim-witted fighter who only uses brute force to battle. He is very slow, and goes down easy. He only fights for money.
(Monster): **Creamic**
A huge, six-legged beast that hides at the bottom of the Vale Dungeon execution pit. It has an unlimited supply of Demonic Ports to toss at you six times a turn. It's really dangerous, but its one weakness is the hard-to-get Puree Whirlwind that you earn by getting Runes to the Swordsman class, and that's no easy task, considering you fight him early in the game.
(Sprenta Criminal) **Ttandros**
A really, really powerful Sprenta that uses chains to take life from anything in its path. He never lets anything slow him down, because he's a Criminal with insane speed and strength.
(Monster) **Crash**
The resident bad guy that hangs around Vale. He's half-human, half-monster, with long arms and silver hair growing all over him. He attacks people with his poison-tipped claws. Weapon: Strike Claws
(Elf/Monster) **Engreen Baltsor**
A cocky little dude, he stands 2 feet tall and is made fun of. But when he gets in one of his war machines and attacks Runes, he's not so small anymore! You fight him often, like all rivalries.
(??? ???) **Kdual**
The so-called "godslayer" feared by the four gods themselves. His massive sword can split planets! But he can only live for one day at a time. Each century, he awakens from the Asteroid Mass Square and goes on a huge rampage.
**Lord Dakmana** (Dragon ????)
A guy that just kinda... hangs around... and acts mysterious. He doesn't do much at the beginning, but he will become your mortal enemy later, muahaha.

**SOME OTHER GUYS:**
(Monster) **The Conchraths**
They don't really do much, but they will do anything for their master. They have nothing personal against SilverSpear, but apparently, the master does. Weapon: Conchrath Blade
(Human Grand-Master): **Jun Kendama**
Runes' childhood friend, and a famed martial artist. His skills are feared by many. He collects the pages of Monster's Ire and hopes to one day get every page. Weapon: Katar
(Elf Druid): **Great Sage Ylandor**
The oldest man in the world, and the creator of magic. He saw Taifa being created as well as Ursula 8. His magic is beyond compare, it's too bad that you can't get him in your party.
