# One Night Project

G_Dragon, Moo Of Doom, and Talyn proudly present... the product of 7 hours of collaborative work that took place, for the most part, during the TK All Nighter, March 11, 2006.
**One Night Project**
**History**
Last Updated: March 12 2006, 7:22 am
This project started very near to the beginning of the TK All Nighter March 11, 2006. Talyn mentioned he was going to work on a half-hour or so long game to be completed within the 6-hour period of the all-nighter. G_Dragon replied "Hey, we should all work on this project," and after some (small) debate, it was decided we would try to complete a game. Thus One Night Project was born. G_Dragon started working on tiles, while Moo Of Doom composed the songs and Talyn wrote the story and programmed. After an interchange of data, and some seven hours (yes, we didn't quite make the deadline...), the project was completed, and here it is.
**Proving a Point**
Last Updated: March 12 2006, 7:27 am
As Moo rightly said in the above paragraph, this was my fault really. I wanted to prove something with it. The Toolkit has always claimed that you can have a game going in 20 minutes. I therefore came to the conclusion that you could get quite a good game done in 6 hours. Turns out it's impossible to do everything alone in 6 hours, but as a group, you can do it. This project also proves that group efforts can work! Us three had never ever worked together before, and after 7 hours, released a game together. That's something special.
