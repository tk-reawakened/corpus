# Rainbow Monsters

The world of Palletopia's magic (colors) are being erased taking with it the lives of the monsters. as a monster yourself, supporting the color White, you are able to learn all types of magic. Travel the world and restore the towns to their rightful colors and stop the Grey Emporer from setting loose demons of grey. Team up with other monsters and set forth on a great journey! pick up items and use them to restore colors to the needed places and awaken King Rainbow to aid you in your quest!
