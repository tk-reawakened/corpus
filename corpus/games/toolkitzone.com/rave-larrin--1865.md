# Rave Larrin

He once helped the king. Now the king helps him. Rave Larrin is a young boy leading a group of misfits, adopted by the king after saving His Majesty's life. They lead a simple life within the castle, secretly protecting the kingdom by night and enjoying comfort and safety by day. However, when the mysterious Lady Suna is appointed general of the king's army, sinister events begin to take place, and Rave finds that he's being placed in one lethal position after another...
