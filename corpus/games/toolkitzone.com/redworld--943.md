# Redworld

A post-apocalyptic fantasy made in the style of an interactive anime series. You play as young Alec Radikov, a cabin boy aboard a pirate ship, adopted by the captain. Once he is released by the captain's convictions of his murderous past, Alec ventures into the unknown world in search of his purpose in life, which leads him to finding out the truth about the world he lives in, and that Earth is only the planet next door. Currently in Pre-production stage as of (3/8/05)
