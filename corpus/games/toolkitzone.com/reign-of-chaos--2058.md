# Reign Of Chaos

When a powerful race from the future creates a world, there is bound to be consequences. Their "dogs", the humans, cant take it anymore. In a last ditch effort for freedom, they genetically modify themselves. After a long war, they finally win, but now, there is a civil war among themselves. Can a single hero end the reign of chaos over the land? Or will they need help from old enemies?
**Reign Of Chaos**
**Character Features**
Last Updated: June 8 2006, 1:58 pm
Ideas for this derived from the MMORPG Guild Wars Every player will be a human. you will have 3 basic classes to choose from. Warrior, Mage, and Ranger. in your class there will be various sub-classes you can choose from for added power. Warrior- Melee combat - high range defence, low magic defence Subclass: berzerker - fast attack speed, lower melee defence Subclass: Footman - average attack speed, normal defence Subclass: knight - slower attack speed, high defence Mage- magic attacks - powerful vs. warrior, low defence against melee and range Subclass: Summoner - Bring other creatures into the fight with you Subclass: Necromancer- curse spells to harm your opponent over time Subclass: Elemental- spells of the elements, fire, water, air, and earth. Ranger- long range attack, low melee defence Subclass: Trapper- hurt enemies turns later from deadly traps Subclass: Sniper- get the first shot in a battle with added damage, but take more damage next turn Subclass: Archmage- Light arrows aflame for extra damage, or small spells for multiple quick shots and added power. downside: very low defence
**Boards and Battle System**
Last Updated: June 8 2006, 2:03 pm
Boards will be huge, meaning 2000+ pixels square. In version 1: boards will most likely be made using the default tiles, or tiles downloaded from the TKZ resources. In version 2: nothing will be tiles. all boards will be hand drawn into the game. All party members will be visible on the boards following the player loosely Battle system: on the board, enemies will be as items walking around. they will move around, and if they see the player, then they will follow them. if the enemy catches the player or a party member, then the battle will start on another board. battle system will be ATB, maybe even real time zelda like battle system if ddl can program one.
**Menu System**
Last Updated: June 8 2006, 2:09 pm
The menu system will not be like the conventional RPG. the menus will be drawn to the screen via canvases, and a simple keypress will activate the buttons as clickable. meaning: menus are visible at all times as a bar down the left side of the screen. i pick up a sword and want to equip it. a keypress activates th buttons on the bar, making them clickable. i click the inventory/equip one and equip my item. i close the menu, another keypress deactivates the buttons, and im on my way again. the keypress will most likely set the buttons overtop of the previous graphics, and start the menu system, making the player unable to move. the canvases on the screen would be there to illistrate the system, but would not do anything until the activation keypress. a help key will be set, so there are not tons of questions about how to open the menu.
