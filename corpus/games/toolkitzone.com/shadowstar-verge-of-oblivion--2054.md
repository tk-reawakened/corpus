# ShadowStar: Verge of Oblivion

ShadowStar: Verge of Oblivion is a fun, fantasy filled RPG that envokes true emotion from the player with a riveting, dramatic story of a young boy who's life gets turned upside down when the world he thought he knew starts to crumble around him. He is thrown into the middle of a battle that is 800 years in the making. Though the road is going to be tough, he makes new friends, learns more about the wide world, and discovers in his heart what really matters.
