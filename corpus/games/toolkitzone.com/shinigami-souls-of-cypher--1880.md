# Shinigami: Souls of Cypher

One day in the present era, Inari's life was taken by a demon who took his body. As a wandering soul lost in the Purgatory realm, he was founded by his guardian angel named Jun. In order to get his life back, Inari must work for Heaven to become The Grim Reaper. Travel in the realms between the living and dead. Discover the forbidden secrets of Heaven. So you can obtain what was lost, but at what cost?
