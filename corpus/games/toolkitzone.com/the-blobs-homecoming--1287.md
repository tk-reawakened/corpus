# The Blobs: Homecoming

Many years ago, a probe was sent from Earth into space full of information so that aliens could find it and come and make contact with Earth. Then one fine day many years later, on the Blobs planet, it hit the ground! Upon closer inspection by the Green Prof. Blob, he finds Earths information and an invitation to make first contact!
**The Blobs: Homecoming**
**Basic Story**
Last Updated: June 20 2005, 2:29 pm
"Many years ago, a probe was sent from Earth into space full of information so that aliens could find it and come and make contact with Earth. Then one fine day many years later, on the Blobs planet, up in the sky they saw what looked like a bright shining star in the middle of the day! Suddenly it got closer and closer until it hit the ground! Upon closer inspection by the Green Professeur Blob, he finds Earths information and an invitation to make first contact! The Prof. sends out one of his faithful helpers to go and aquire a crew to help fly his ship to Earth!" Then the game starts with you playing as the Prof's helper guy, and you have to find a willing (and probably crazy) crew. Once you have all the people you need, you head back to the ship to depart for Earth! But of course, not all goes as planned when you near Earth, causing the balence of the universe to be unwittingly in your hands!
**Features**
Last Updated: June 20 2005, 3:08 pm
All these are all planned to be implemented to the game: Plus some more that I either haven't thought of, or aren't listed!
** Original Storyline Story will take place across two different planets this time! Non-linear gameplay Christmas Catastrophe style boards ~ IE; house roof comes off all buildings, and inside same size as out. Custom Mwin Custom Menu GTA style 'wanted level' - determines how easy you can buy items from shops etc. Day and Night will bring about certain missions Your actions can determine how things will be affected - eg: Police will arrest you if you are caught in places you shouldn't be Transport will be available to get you around quicker
*These are subject to change*
**Gameplay**
Last Updated: June 20 2005, 3:03 pm
The gameplay isn't going to be like a standard RPG with battles and the like, it will be more of an adventure/puzzle type of game hopefully (think of a point and click type of game.. but without the point and clicking :P) Solve puzzles/tasks/missions to get a certain objective done, that kind of thing. With recent plot devolopments (I've just re-wrote a lot of it) certain things will need to be learnt in order for you to be able to get to the next part of your quest, and those things you learnt from NPCs etc, will help you get to completing your mission quicker the next time you need to do it (certain things will need to be done a few times over.. you'll understand when you play!)
**Battles?**
Last Updated: June 20 2005, 3:05 pm
This game isn't going to have battles as such like the usual RPGs. All being well, I'm going to have life or death situations where you need to do or say the right thing to be able to get out alive, so more of a puzzle battle kind of thing ala Broken Sword and the like. With that in mind now, I'm not sure if I'll have the energy bar as planned before now. If I can get it to work I'll use it still as another little feature to have, otherwise it will be just the 'puzzle battles' to keep you amused!
**And Finally...**
Last Updated: June 20 2005, 3:12 pm
