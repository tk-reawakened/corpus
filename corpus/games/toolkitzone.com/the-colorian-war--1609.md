# The Colorian War

In an post-apocolypse, Ronny Marone and his evil minions have ruled the world...and made it Yellow? Only Team Green could restore the world to its former glory, and stop this terrible psycopath, but would that be enough?
**The Colorian War**
**Main Characters**
Last Updated: November 6 2005, 1:55 pm
Doctor Ronny Clarci Marone: This guy is pretty much the heads up in Team Yellow. He enjoys drinking LOTS of wine, seducing women, and watch poor souls being tortured. He's a psycopath, and goes ill when he sees something not Yellow. Master Yellowful: The supreme head in Team Yellow, Sammy Sosctsa, a.k.a. Master Yellowful, is an elderly man who often dislikes Ronny's actions...but don't think that makes him kind at heart. Little is known about Yellowful though... Kari: Kari leads Team Green. She is a nice, young woman who believes times should be like the used to. Rick: Rick is Kari's accomplice. He leads Team Green along with her, and is always there to help you out. Ryan: This is you. You are a Team Green Admin, administering everything that goes on. Unfortunately, Team Yellow has the upper edge over you. Are you enough to defeat them? Team Yellow Executive Colin: Ronny's best friend; Colin is usually seen with him. When he is not, Colin is efficiently protecting a TY scheme. He is a force to be reckoned with, but always underestimates you. This is an egotistical jerk you'll love to beat. Team Yellow Elite Nats: This silly, bumbling TY Elite is always standing in your way. He'd be their best member if he didn't go and blab out their plans everytime you saw him. Team Green Private Boing: On your later adventures, you'll meet this TG Private who greatly admires you. He seems to be just an annoyance, but could he maybe be your last help...? CATS: This weird guy recites the famous AYBABTU phrase often. He may be a cyborg with an odd hairdo, but he can be a quite powerful colleague!
**Missions:**
Last Updated: November 6 2005, 2:38 pm
Mission One: Ryan (the main character) is sent by Lady Kari to investigate a mysterious noise heard from outside the base. He finds Ronny Marone, who would have fought him if Master Yellowful wouldn't have come to give Ronny strict orders not to kill any Team Green agents. Heading along your way, you discover a Team Yellow roadblock up North held by TY Elite Nats. Speak to him and he may cough up some information about TY robbing The Underground Shrine. Now it's time to head there. Fight off as many guards as you can to make your way to the shrine keeper. He'll give you the secret key to open the secret safe which TY strangely robbed. Go deeper underground and defeat all the guards. You'll meet up with Master Yellowful, but now that he's defenseless, he'll run away. Mssion 2: Discovering a secret tunnel in the shrine, you take it and it leads you to Vermillion City. Everyone strangely disappeared, and you'll find Rick telling you to investigate. Nats guards the entrance to Yellow Studio, a high-tech night club, so speak to him for more information about TY's plot. Now you must head to the Team Green base. It is disquised as a regular house, so it is exactly to the left of the distinguishable Yellow Studio. TG will be manning a giant laser beam which will drill a hole into Yellow Studio, allowing you to access it. You'll find Ronny in there, with the secret safe. Once again, before you can fight him, Yellowful will call him off to head to Stroid Mountain. Strangely, he'll drop the scret safe. Open it and you'll find a Hydron Plug. This may be useful... Mission 3: Head over to the slopes of Stroid Mountain. Manuvering up, you'll find Yellown Town, with yet another Team Green base. Speak to Kari there, but you'll find she sounds a little bit like Ronny. Then you'll hear the Ronny theme song, and Nats will be there! It was a trap, but you won't relise it, so do as Kari *coughronnycough* tells you...head to the top of Stroid Mountain... Mission 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8 are coming soon! :)
**TCW Cliches**
Last Updated: November 6 2005, 2:51 pm
Many great games have cliches. The Colorian War is one of them. Here is a long list of cliches it follows: The Law of Travel: Everyone, including the flithy rich final boss, just walks around for travel. The Law of Valuables 1: Whenever the evil guys steal something important, you know they'll drop it sometime. The Law if Valuables 2: Whenever you collect something important, it is usually very big, but somehow it fits in your coat pocket without a bulge...or a tear. The Law of Music: You know you're around someone evil when you hear their theme song. To Be Deaf, Dumb, and Blind...or More So Blind and Dumb: The evil guys will pull an obvious trap and fool you dearly, but you'll have no idea what happened. The Law of Security 1: Half the TY troops you meet will either be asleep or ignoring you. The Law of Security 2: The more powerful you are, the better your guards. Master Yellowful has giant robots with laser cannons guard him, and Nats has a bunch of sleephead troops. The Law of Security 3: The higher up you are, the less efficient you are. When you're an Elite, a baby would be more efficient. The Law of Security 4: If you see a weird guy with long purple hair, you know everything's going to be okay. The Law of Creation: For some reason, once Team Yellow desides they want to go somewhere, in the next 5 minutes they cover everything in TY flashing tiles and TY bases. The Law of Ignorance 1: Even though you walk around in a Team Green uniform, no common TY troops seem to notice. The Law of Ignorance 2: Know one seems to care that TY stole their family members...they just worry about their furniture. The Law of Evilness 1: All evil masters MUST have an evil laugh or else they wouldn't be evil, would they? Heheheheheheheheh... The Law of Evilness 2: All evil masters must allow you to live so you can foil some of their plans, then gripe about you foiling the plans, so they can have a big showdown in their mountain top base guarded by giant robots! The Law of Power 1: Somehow, one man can beat 1000s of TY guards are 4 TY heads and be left without a scratch. The Law of Power 2: Somehow Team Green can set up a giant laser, blast a hole in a TY base, and set up TG carpeting, and no one seems to notice. Then you haev troops walk in and no one STILL notices! The Law of Power 3: The most pathetic, weak troops will always help you out in the end. That's my list of TCW cliches, I hope they were most enjoyable! :)
**Plot**
Last Updated: November 6 2005, 2:58 pm
In an alternate dimension of current time, where technology is greater and most of the world is Yellow, Ronny Marone rules all! He leads Team Yellow for his post-apocolyptic show! Torturing whoever stads in his way, two people couldn't stand this. Lois and Sean Belaque created the original Team Green to stand against Ronny. Though both were killed, their sole daughter Kari and her friend Rick now lead Team Green against them. Could they be enough to fend of this insane foe though...?
