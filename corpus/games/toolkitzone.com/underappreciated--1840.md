# Underappreciated

You see RPG heros and heroines fight evil all the time, and every time they get washed in a tide of glory. But who's left out of the spotlight? The Merchants, that's who! Now you get to play as a merchant, and you have to know where the darling heros are, what they need, and how to get it to them at all times. Plus you have to deal with thier oh-so-charming personalities. And you'll surely have no reconization at the end of the whole ordeal. How wonderful.
