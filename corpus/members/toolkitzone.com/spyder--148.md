**About Me:**
John was born in Seattle, WA on September 22nd 1981. His family moved back and forth between Washington and Alaska for the next five years before finally settling in the retirement/naval community of Oak Harbor, WA. John has always had an interest in computers, even as early as kindergarten he used an Apple IIGS in school and at home. In Junior High this minor interest progressed and he began learning more about software and hardware, and eventually dabbled a little in programming. By High School he started writing his own programs in Applesoft BASIC in order to complement a Pen&Paper style role-playing game he had created called Godiva. Eventually he was introduced to the RPG Toolkit through a friend, and Godiva:Alpha went into production. Eventually he discovered the small community associated with it, and became a regular on the forums, eventually being handed ownership of the resource site "Mystic Software". Both Mystic Software and his game Godiva:Alpha grew quickly, and were quite popular at the time. John graduated High School in the year 2000, and was accepted into DeVry University in Arizona. Because of the lack of time in between trying to maintain a job, as well as go to school full-time, work on both Godiva:Alpha and Mystic Software came to a near stand-still, and neither have recovered since. However, John still remains an active member and moderator of the TK community today. He currently lives on his own in Glendale, Arizona, and works for a financial printing company as a typesetter, formatting financial documents for companies using HTML as well as proprietary languages created by his employer.
