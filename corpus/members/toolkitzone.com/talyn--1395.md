**About Me:**
Alex started off as most people do as a small child with a dream. Continually writing stories as a kid, he was never happy with just imagining the stories. He wanted to walk through the worlds with the characters. Many years later a friend showed him a strange little bit of software that allowed you to make your own games. It's name? The RPG Toolkit Development System 2.18. Unfortunately, the home computer was under par to run it. However, three years later and equipped with a spanking new machine, the Toolkit landed on Alex's desktop. And he never looked back. After learning several programming languages in his own time during high school, Alex was all set to study Computer Science at University. However a last minute attack of nerves pushed him back onto one of his lifelong hobbies; Photography. Once again, he never looked back. Alex is reknowned for some bad relationship choices, however his current relationship with the gorgeous Tuey is possibly one of the best things that ever happened to him. He freely admits that he's madly in love with this girl and feels totally crap for being away at Uni for so much of the time and not being able to see her. Alex's online persona Talyn gained popularity in the ToolkitZone community and was elevated to the rank of Moderator in late 2005, a position he enjoys and intends to remain in until he leaves the community. Currently studying for a degree in Digital and Photographic Imaging at the University of Westminster in London, England, Alex's passion for imagery often spills over into his 24/7 gorgeous avatars of the lovely Keira Knightley.
