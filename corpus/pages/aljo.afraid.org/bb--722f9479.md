# Beautiful Bengals by Spencer, a family-run cattery in Carrollton, Texas.

![Image](images/cat4.gif)
We specialize exclusively in the breeding of Bengal cats.
Our cattery is home-based and we keep just two breeding females. This allows us to treat our cats as family pets and give them all the love and attention they deserve. For breeding purposes, we ensure that we breed the best from the best with mates chosen from champion bloodlines only.
Beautiful Bengals by Spencer is registered as a cattery with TICA under the name Spencer Cattery.
