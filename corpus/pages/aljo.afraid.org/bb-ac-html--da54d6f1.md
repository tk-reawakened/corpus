# Adding a Bengal To Your Family

Beautiful Bengals by Spencer breeds Bengals that are suitable for showing, breeding or for just keeping as loving pets . All of our kittens are pure-bred with traceable pedigrees to at least five generations. However, suitability of a kitten for showing or breeding can only be judged by comparing it to the TICA-accepted characteristics for the breed.
![](images/cat1.gif)
We typically only produce four litters per year, with on average five cats per litter, and because of the high demand for Bengals, it is uncommon for us to have cubs immediately available. Therefore, once youve made the decision to add this bundle of pure joy to your family, it is advisable to contact us as soon as possible to ensure that your name is put on our waiting list. This will enable you to be involved right from the birth of your new family addition.
The cost of a Bengal depends on its quality and sex, ranging from around $800 for a pet up to more than $2000 for a breeding male. Bengals sold for breeding purposes include full breeding rights which allows the pedigree to be maintained. Bengals sold as pets cannot be used for breeding, and must be spayed or neutered to prevent any accidental surprises.
To make the first step towards adding a Bengal to your family, please . [contact us](contactus.html)
