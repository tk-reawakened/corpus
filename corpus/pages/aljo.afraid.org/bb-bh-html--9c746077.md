# Bengal History

Despite their name, Bengal cats are not related to Bengal tigers at all, and despite their appearance they are not related to wild leopards either. The Bengal is actually a relatively new breed that was created by cross-breeding Asian Leopard cats (a small, wide cat found throughout east Asia) with a variety of domesticated pedigree breeds such as the Egyptian Maus, Abyssian and Ocicat. Repeating this breeding pattern for three or more generations ensured that the Bengal lost the negative traits of its wild ancestry without losing its charm. Once this stage was reached the Bengal was established as a breed in its own right and was recognized by TICA in 1985.
