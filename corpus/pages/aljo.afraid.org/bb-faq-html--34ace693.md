# Frequently Asked Questions

On this page you will find answers to some of the questions that prospective Bengal owners often ask. If you have a question that is not answered here then feel free to contact us , and we will be happy to assit you.
Is a Bengal right for me as a pet?
If you want a cat that everyone will admire, then yes.

I already have other cats and dogs. Will they accept a new Bengal?
They should do, and a Bengal is no different to any other cat in this respect. When introducing a new cat into a household with an existing cat, there is likely to be a period of adjustment lasting a few days, but after this it is not unusual for the two to become best of friends.

What do Bengal cats eats?
Most owners feed their Bengal a high quality cat food of their choice. This can be either a dry or wet food, though all cats will love the occasional wet-food treat. Most varieties of cat food have special kitten versions that should be used for cubs less than a year old. As with any other cat, Bengals cant resist the temptation to try any food they can get to, but human food treats should be kept to a minimum.

What diseases are Bengals prone to?
As with most other breeds, Bengals are susceptible to a number of feline diseases such as feline leukemia (FeLV), feline infectious pertinitus FIP, feline immunodeficiency virus (FIV), Rabies etc. Bengals are also susceptible to parasitic infestations such as heart worms, intestinal worms, flees, ticks and ear mites. Many of these conditions are very serious and potentially fatal. Luckily, almost all of them can be prevented by regular vaccination and topical anti-parasite treatments. Therefore, it is vital that your Bengal has its kitten shots and receives regular vets check ups.
