# Our Cats

![](images/cat2.gif)
Beautiful Bengals by Spencer currently has two female cats  Clarkstone Bebe Jewel in the Jungle (Bebe) and Clarkstone Coco Empress in the East (Coco). We do not keep any male Bengals and instead, we use the stud services of other Bengal catteries to ensure that we produce the highest quality cubs.
Bebe and Coco have totally distinct bloodlines going back at least five generations which means that if you wish to establish your own in-house breeding program we can supply you with an un-related breeding pair of Bengals.
