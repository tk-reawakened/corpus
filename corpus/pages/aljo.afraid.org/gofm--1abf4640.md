![](images/void.gif)
Go FM streams at 128 kbps from Manchester, NH. Go FM is mostly automated, though occasionally you'll hear us on the microphone playing requests. Go FM is heavy on 80s & 90s pop/rock with a dose of 60s & 70s and some current music all rounded out with a small touch of 50s and country. The station leans mostly to the rock edge, but some dance/disco stuff does get in.
We don't take ourselves too seriously. Some of the music mixes are train wrecks. A real radio programmer would question some of the music choices, but we really don't care. It's all about having fun.
I haven't sprung for any real liners yet, so don't laugh too much at my lame, unprofessional home-made liners and IDs. (My wife says I should continue to do them myself. She's helped on a few.)
We're always looking for input from listeners. What do you like best about Go FM? What do you like least? How can we be better? Let us know what you think.

GO FM is operated purely as a hobby. There is no on-air advertising and, of course, no cost to the listener. This is all about fun! That said, several listeners have expressed an interest in helping cover costs. Our monthly cost is $2 per listener slot (we currently have 100 slots and hope to add more soon) plus music royalties and music purchases. If you would like to make a donation to help offset these costs in addition to a future increase in available listener slots, click on the donate button.
