# Hi there & thanks for visiting!

August 1 2001 was a day that changed my life. All my life I had wanted to create games, that were enjoyable and fun to play, and last summer, when I stumbled upon the RPG Toolkit, my chance was presented.
Here's some boring information about me!
I was born on December 14 1986 in Birmingham, West Midlands, UK. My interests are football (soccer), basketball, boxing, and video games.
So far I have never completed a full game, but I vow to do so before the end of the year! Click on games to find out more.
