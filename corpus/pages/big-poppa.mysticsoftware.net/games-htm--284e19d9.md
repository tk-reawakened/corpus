# GAMES

At the moment I have one game in development, which is . I'm not going to give a release date, so I can avoid disappointment if there are any delays. All I'm saying is wait at least a couple of months, and I hope you won't be disappointed! *PIRATES*
Here is some info...
You play Reefer , a young swashbuckler, who is sick of hearing great stories about battles and treasure. He wants to be part of the action instead of a spectator. He wants the glory of battle, or sinking a ship, and of finding jewels the size of his fist!
Features in the game will include...
- 100% original graphics
- A great soundtrack which will add to the atmosphere
- Non-linear gameplay which will allow the player to do whatever he or she wants, be it rob people, sink ships, or do side quests.
- A fantastic menu system made by [DoddyUK](http://www.lotss.tk)
- And more, which haven't been decided yet!
