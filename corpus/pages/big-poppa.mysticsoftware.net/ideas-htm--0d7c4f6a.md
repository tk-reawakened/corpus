# THUG LIFE

IDEAS
Here are a collection of games I hope to make after is complete. The games are not in any specific order. The descriptions are very brief, as I don't want to give too much away. I have provided just guidelines for the plot, which of course would be developed. ***Pirates***
**THUG LIFE**
A game many will know about, you play a young street kid who has to do missions for the mob in exchange for information about his supposedly dead father. This game is non-linear.
**ATTORNEY**
You play a lawyer representing a man who who on trial for murder, and you have to find clues around the big city for him to be freed. The trouble is, local gangsters and the mob want him to get the death penalty, so they are in your way trying to stop you from getting the evidence.
