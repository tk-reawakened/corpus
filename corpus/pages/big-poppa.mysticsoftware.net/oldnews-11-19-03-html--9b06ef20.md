November 13, 2003 We're Back!
Ahh the soft glow of DreamWeaver... Good to be finally back in the community with a steady, fast internet connection. For those of you who haven't seen the thread on the forum, I'm finally back; hopefully for good. I'll be working quite a bit on the site over my vacation time, which lasts from November 20th through December 7th. I'll be in Montana and Washington states for Thanksgiving vacation visiting my family...only my brother won't be there so I'll be bored out of my mind most of the time, which is the perfect setting to get work done. The 100+ TKZ tilesets are the first on my list of things to do, as well as getting those links to the games fixed (which I might actually end up doing before the vacation). I'm also thinking of uploading new versions of the TK to the server because of a complaint on the speed of rpgtoolkit.com's new server. Mystic Software's server is quite faster, though I downloaded the TK from rpgtoolkit.com at an average of 62kb/sec...which is hardly "slow". But in comparison, I downloaded the game "6 Wizards" from Mystic Software at an average of 329kb/sec. At the very least, it'll take some bandwidth off of rpgtoolkit.com, which may or may not help Chris out.
