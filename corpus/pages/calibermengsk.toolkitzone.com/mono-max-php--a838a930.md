About the game
Mono-Max's battle system is unique to the TK. Currently, there are "Battle Areas" on the boards. Within the specified areas set up by the creator (me, Caliber Mengsk) you fight a specific group of enemies of that section. This means ONE map for the world map. I know a few people are saying, "Oh no... that will take for ever to load!" and truth is, currently it would, but with later advances in the TK, it wouldn't take much time at all. Here is a current screenshot of the battle system. It is updated from what it was. Now, you may notice that the Enemy names and health bars aren't in the bottom left box. This is something I am doing in preperations for the graphics to work. Eventually, the HP bar will only appear after you use a spell. Also, the enemy name will be replaced with the animated graphic. Then the name will be moved to the bottom left box. This is the battle system running with enemy graphics. For the most part, that system is working... I have slight issues with the TK itself at the moment as to why my BS isn't working right, and they should be fixed soon hopefully. If not, I will probably end up scrapping the project. Sucks, because I think that I could continue the idea. But, the random system works for the most part. I'll probably have to recode stuff later though. This is just a quick screenshot of the city tiles in place. Currently not much. I will add an NPC or two after I actually finish all of the programing. Here is the on board walking animation for Max. Like it? I have been working on the programs a few months. Due to advances in the Item menu system, I have been able to fix older problems in the battle system such as the enemy choosing. The bug I had there was sometimes if you held enter in, it could actually kill all 3 enemies if you kill the first one in one hit. Bad little bug. So next release that is fixed. Also, I have fully finished the item system. The same system will be used in the battle system as you would use on the map. The magic system will be a bit different. Since I will have to make a seaperate program for battle and in-game. I still haven't decided if there will be onboard graphics for magic. (this means showing the magic happen in a regular city, or anywhere not in a battle.) Though this could be neat looking, for Mono-Max, it just doesn't acomplish much. Well...
