I'm finally working on the actually RPG Code implementation of the Custom Battle System Tutorial, I have Chapter 1 completed, and as you can see on the left Chapters 2-4 are in the works, and I already have a guide for what chapters 5-7 will be, this will be an extensive series that will continue until I believe I have fully covered the topic of RPG Code battle systems.
This is the custom battle system tutorial, it is developed by (matt72186) as an instructional guide to get users started in creating a battle system for use in the . It is my goal, by the end of the series, to have covered fully many types of battle systems that can be used for your games. Along the way you will learn basic RPG Code and slowly progress to more advanced topics, I will teach good programming practices for organization of your code. This tutorial will show the simularities and differences of battle systems, and how that can help you to make any system you want, even ones not covered. Before you begin this tutorial assumes you have beginner knowledge of the RPG Toolkit.
