# Continent of Umber

There will be more continents present in the game: Orion, Sagmont, Saharia, New Ascalon, Tepec, Volcania and Meridia.
Ascalon
Government Capital: Mercedes
Church Capital: Campana
Other cities: Kodra, Northfall, Almirante
Ascalon is a powerful empire which holds most of Umber in it's grasp. It is ruled by the king Gregory XIII, said to come from Merovingian dynasty (having divine bloodage of nephilim, fallen angels which have been forgotten by the Elder God, as legends claim), based on strong beliefs and aggresive toward Ventus. The powerful city of Mercedes is a capital of Ascalon's government, while the ancient Campana is main pillar of the Church, ruled by cardinal Leo.
Ventus
Capital: Nitor
Other cities: Marcopole, Silverback, Viemont
The dark city of the necromancers, Nitor, and the surrounding cities create country of Ventus, which achieved independence from Ascalonian Empire. Necromancers have direct access to the Devil Spine mountains, and sworn to protect land from power of darkness.... though they manifest power of darkness within themselves.
Nordreich
Capital: Yrvader
Other cities: Himmelweiss
Nordreich is a country of harsh winter, to the far now. Yrvader is a fortified city, ruled by female militia who call themselves Valkyries. Nordreich have promised to help Ascalon to destroy Ventus, mainly because in ages past necromancers tried to invade north.
Nifle
Capital: Albion
Other cities: Fogbell, St Francis
Nifle is an island country, which mostly grew in power by colonialism and conquering far realms. Nifle is ruled by queen Victoria, which tries not to side in incoming conflict, but rather mind her own business.
Plutonia
Capital: Tyr
Plutonia is an isolated island, inhabited by the dwarves. It is relatively close to the Great Glacier, and contains glacial caves itself.
