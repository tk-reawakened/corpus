# Dark Ages

The glorious ages of antiquity were gone. World has gone mad, some said... The Campanian Empire has fallen, and age of prophets, messiahs and religion came. The Church was slowly assembling, different cults have been growing, and people experimented with magic and knowledge... Some dark warlocks, skilled in occultic magic, have opened gates to Hell itself. A sourge of demons have flooded the land. A realm became tomb of humanity. But there were still people, who gathered their strength to fight...
Middle Ages
The king of Ascalon have united almost whole Umber under his rule. He trained his best warriors to become paladins and led crusades against demons. The dark powers were defeated with faith and honor of the holy men. At the same time, surviving necromancers have united and formed their Guild - to prevent use of their dark forces for evil, again. The guild has claimed the eastern part of Umber, and gave birth to country known as Ventus.
Enlighted Ages
Ages have passed, and the power of Ascalon fell. Ruled by the king who claimed to come from angels' (nephilim) bloodline, country waged several wars on necromancers in the post. But now, the demons return. And some new, unknown organisation known as Gnosis comes to power. Unsure what to do, Ascalon accuses Ventus for the situation and war hangs in the air. If someone won't do something, a world will once again become shattered by conflict - bloody and senseless, which will most likely only open borders before the nether forces.
Summary (from the author)
Sounds stupid? Propably. But when (or, if) you'll see the game itself, you'll see it all in action. It takes place on alternate world, shattered by an Accident in time it was merely assembling... and now reaches age of technology, gnosticism, communism, fanaticism and existencial problems... And the adventurer, who returns to his home at Kodra after years of travel, and meets the world he knew as peaceful changed - shall soon have to observe and decide, where the true evil lies...
