# Actual in-game shots

![](DarkAge2.jpg)
Dark Age 2
I'm putting far more work into Dark Age 2 that I did its predecessor. It will be 100% original, including an original battle system, menu, and will contain plenty of other extras that I don't want to give away just yet. Dark Age 2 will not be a continuation of Dark Age's plot, but will be an entirely new, independent adventure that shares some themes with the original. My goal is to make a commercial quality game with the Toolkit. I plan on selling the final version, but of course, I will release a free demo along with it. When will it be released? Not any time soon...

***The Battle System***

**Some of the system's features include:**
-Stanima system: you get weaker the more you attack!
-Levels of attack: choose how strong your physical attack will be, as well as stanima cost
-Breaker Attacks (a.k.a. limit breaks, heh)
-Active time battle: speed bars indicate who will attack next.
-Extreme special attacks!![Fireball!!!](Fireballsmall.gif)
-Keyboard cursor selector, no cheap mouse clicking needed.
-One character with up to two enemies in battles
-Keyboard combination attacks
-Status effects (i.e. Poison, Slow, etc.)
***Increasing your strength:***
[***![](newscreenshot4small.gif)***](newscreenshot4.jpg) I'm trying something different than the generic 'level up' system that is employed in 99% of the RPGs out there. Basically, after a battle you are awarded a certain number of points. You then can choose to distibute these points to increase your stats however you want. So, you can actually mold your character into a heavy-hitting fighter, a spell happy wizard, or anything in between. You can also determine which element of spells you want to develop, if any. Will you develop your magic evenly and gain a wide assortment of elemental attacks, or will you master one element, gaining its powers more quickly? On top of that, if one element significantly dominates the others, your character will become that element type. That means your character will become strong against some elemental attacks and weak against others...
