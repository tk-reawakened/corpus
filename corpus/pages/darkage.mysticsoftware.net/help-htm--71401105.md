# Stop pounding the keys!

Help
If you can't find what you're looking for below, visit the [message board](http://disc.server.com/Indices/169157.html)e-mail me with your question. I am debating whether a few parts of the game are too obscure, so I will answer questions about game play itself (don't expect an immediate answer).
Frequently Asked Questions
-- General --
**Q:** *When I try to run the installation program, it says "Invalid Data". What should I do?*
Files occaisionally become corrupted when they are downloaded. Re-download the file. **A:**
**Q:** *Why won't the in-game help work?*
You probably didn't install the game to the default directory (C:\DarkAge). The help file will only work if the defaults are kept while installing. **A:**
-- Game Play --
**Q:** *I'm stuck in Varda Castle after the opening events. The king just told me to "get some rest". What do I do?*
Find Vance's room and press the space bar to the left of his bed (make sure to explore first!). **A:**
Getting used to the battle system
The RPG Toolkit's internal battle system takes some getting used to (Okay, it annoyed me to the point that I wrote my own battle system for my next game). It contains a few little quarks that should be addressed...
*Stop pounding the keys!*
Don't get frustrated if you make a selection and your command doesn't get carried out right away. If the enemy decides to attack while you are in the middle of choosing your commands, the command window will freeze while the enemy attacks. Don't worry, every time you press a key (you can use the mouse or keyboard, I recommend the keyboard) the action will be carried out as soon as the computer gets time. I. E. If you press down three times while the menu is frozen, the computer will finish performing the enemy's attack and move the selector down three times. This is good to keep in mind if you want to attack as quickly as possible. *then*
![](fightmenu.jpg)
When the fight menu is first availible, nothing is highlighted. Some people think you have to press the down key to make the selector highlight the first choice. In reality, you can just press the space bar (or whatever key you use) and the first choice is selected automatically. *Tip:*
*Oh no! Vance casted Fire on himself! I know I selected Cure! What's going on!?!?*
This is quite possiblly the most irritating thing in the world... In order to understand this, lets run through the steps of using a special move. When a player is first ready to make a move, we select an option from the first menu. Let's say we choose Magic, a type of special move. Then we get a list of all the spells the player knows. We then choose what spell we want. Finally, we choose who we want to use it on. Here's the catch: if the enemy uses a special move in beween the time you select your move and the time you choose who you want to use it on, the special move your character uses will be changed to what the enemy uses. How do we fix that? I know its annoying, but you have to go back and select your move again if you want to prevent this.
Confused? Here's a step-by-step on special moves:
1. Select special move type.
2. Select special move.
If enemy uses a special move any time between step 2 and 3, go back to 1 *2a.*
3. Hurry! Select who you want to use it on!
Don't let this scare you, its not so bad once you're used to it. This is why I'm letting you know now instead of making you figure it out the hard way (like I did!). Normal attacks and items are not affected by this. You can tell that an enemy uses a special move when it makes a sound before it flashes.
*Running*
If you choose to run from a battle (weakling) you loose a certain amount of GP. Don't press any button when the window pops up to tell you how much you lost, or you will be punished (some bugs I can play off as intentional... heh).
Game Play
Enough about flukes. Here's some tips on game play outside of battle.
*Controls*
Direction keys-- Move player
Direction keys + Shift-- Run
Enter-- Calls menu
Space Bar-- Activiation key (talk, etc.)
R-- Redraws screen (fixes any graphical errors you don't like, Vance also strikes a cool pose!)
H-- Calls in-game help
Occasionally, the number keys are used to make selections.
The menu doesn't always update itself if you call it more than once while standing in one place. Just take a step and then open it again to update it. *Note:*
*Secrets*
Dark Age is full of little secrets (big ones too) that are not neccessary to complete the game but make the game easier. If the going gets rough (why does he only have one special move?!) take a break from the main story and have a look around. You never know what you might find...
