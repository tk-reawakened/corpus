It has a name... Yes, after some serious contemplation, I've decided to name my current project Dark Age 2. Production is going great (at the moment...). I managed to solve some major problems I've been having. The battle system has been finished off and upgraded to support two enemies. With the basics out of the way, I can actually start working on gameplay itself, namely the story and graphics.
