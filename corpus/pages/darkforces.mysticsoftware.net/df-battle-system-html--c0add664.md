# Attack:

The Dark Forces battle system is not the default BS that comes with the Tool Kit. Instead, I created my own, with its own special features such as magic, wisdom, a special ability that differs by character, and more (these are explained in the section)! Dark Forces BS is a speed gaged system (a system that works as when a bar fills up, it's your turn!). When it becomes one of your character's turn, a menu will appear on the screen with the options: Attack, Magic, Item, and Run. At certain points in the game, one of your characters may gain an ability to use that will appear between "Attack" and "Magic". Secting this will cause an arrow to appear over the first enemy on the field. Next to this arrow will also be a small menu containing the monster's name and current HP. You can then move back and forth between other enemies on the field if they exist. You now have a choice to either select an enemy to attack it, or press the back button to go back to the main menu. When pressed, this will open up another menu containing any magical abilites the current chracter has. After choosing which attack you wish to make, you then can either select an enemy or go back as it was like when attacking. After choosing, a small menu will come out as it did with magic, but with a list of all the items your party has. Some of these items can attack the enemy, heal you, increase attack or defence, etc. If it helps your party, you will be allowed to choose someone from your party. If it effects something on the enemy's side, you will be able to select it. Options afterward carry out like attacking. Selecting this will allow you to possibly run, though this may sometimes fail if you are not fast enough. If you run, you will gain no experience, Lucure (Terrenus's currency, or money), or any items you may possibly accertain. [**Game Play**](df_game_play.html)
