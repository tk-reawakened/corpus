# Chapter 1: Opening Eyes

Fire, it gave off a foul sent of destroyed life to rise up into the wind. Kail smelled it now. It lifted into his nose, almost suffocating him from clean air. He quickly lifted up his chest in his bed, startled at the smell. He looked around to see a horror before him. Fire was quenching his room, engulfing all of his possessions. "Mother!" Kail called over the roaring flames, "Mom! Dad! Where are you?" Kail franticly ran down stairs in search of his parents. He had to crawl over blackened debris scattered in the house. Kail pulled himself to the front door, choking on the smoking fumes filling his lungs. He tried with all his might to open the door, but it wouldn't budge. Looking around for something to break it with, he saw the sword that his father had given him laying on its stand. He grabbed it, released the blade from its sheath, and raised it above his head. The sword fell to the earth as he slashed it downwards at the door. With a loud crash, the door split apart, and opened a pathway to the outside. Kail burst outside of his house into his village, but he found there, was far worse. His village was under attack by the Dark Army! They were setting flame to the houses, and destroying crops and livestock. Demons were everywhere, killing the people of the village to ensure no resistance in the future. Kail ran left towards the neighbor's house. Suddenly, there was a loud crashing sound! The entire wall of the house was blown to pieces, and flame shot out of it. A young man fell onto the floor, still inside of the building. And in front of him, stood a massive demon with a giant sword, fangs, wings, and a horned, whip like tail. "Don't hurt me! Please!" the man pleaded. "Noonemaylive," the monster said in a deep and slow voice. The monster picked up the man and threw him into the air. As he fell, the demon's tail whipped foreword and pointed up directly under him. And the man's stomach was pierced on the top horn of the tail. Blood dripped rapidly to the floor. The man coughed and gagged in his shock of the pain. Then, he lay limp, quietly, no sign of life left. The monster glared with the approval of his kill. But out of the corner of its eye, it caught sight of Kail. Slowly, it turned its head towards him. Its body began to move in the direction of Kail. Kail tried to back up, but stumbled and fell. "Anotherkill, Hewill beverypleased," the demon said in its horrifying voice. Kail tried to move back, but couldn't go fast enough on the ground. So he stood up to face the demon, and drew his sword. The demon laughed at the sight, "The boymeansto fight, then Ican granthiswishofdeath." The demon's sword that lay on its side pulled itself out of its sheath and lifted into the monster's hand. The blade seemed to glow a fiery red, and was twice as tall as Kail, and just as thick. The demon raised the sword over his head, and became ready to swing it quickly down onto Kail. But suddenly, Kail jolted forward, and ran under the creature. He came out the other side, and jabbed his sword up into the monster's tail, and ripped out to the side! The tail fell to the ground and flopped with its last bit of life. "Ahhhhhh!" the demon screamed, "You cursed human! You will die for you pathetic foolishness!" The monster turned around, and with its giant fist, struck Kail from the side, slamming him into a bolder! Kail fell to ground, but managed to catch himself on his hands and knees. Blood dripped from his mouth from the heavy blow. The wind had been knocked out of him, but he regained his breath fast, and along with it came panting. Kail spat the blood out of his mouth and stood up, sword now in hand. The demon charged forward, raised it sword into the air and slammed it down to the ground. Kail evaded it by quickly jumping to side, then brought his own sword up and slashed at the monster's hand, while jumping again to avoid any attack that might come again. There was a short pause, and blue-green blood oozed out of it's hand, and the demon dropped the massive sword, along with it's own hand. As it screamed in agony and clutched the handless arm with the other, Kail ran forward and swung out his sword for a final blow, aiming it at the demon's neck. The sword hit, embedding itself into the chasm of its throat. Blood filled the monster's lungs, and it gagged for air relentlessly. Putting it out of its misery, Kail ripped the sword to the side, tearing the flesh, instantly killing the beast. As it dropped dead to the floor, Kail turned around, running, to continue his search for his parents. The smell of fire and smoke still drifted through the air. He could hear screaming and swords clashing from all around the village. One dangerously close scream shocked him, and he spun around to see where it came from. To his horror and disgust, he found what he dreaded. His mother, lying on the ground, with what appeared to be a very young Orien, standing over her with his sword drawn, ready to mercilessly kill at any given second. No!" screamed Kail as he drew his sword once more, running towards his mother. The Orien looked up, and saw Kail running towards him. He grinned with pleasure as he jabbed the blade down, piercing the back of Kail's mother. Still screaming, Kail swung his sword once and slashed at the Orien's neck, severing it from the rest of the body. All that was left of it fell to the floor, clashing as the armor collided with the stone. "No! Mother!" Kail yelled as he dropped to the floor to level with her. "I'msorryKail" she breathed, "forgive me" A line of blood seeped from her eye to the ground, and blood slowly sifted from her mouth. She slowly went cold and limp, life leaving her body. Kail's head eased onto his mother's, tears falling from his eyes. Sorrow filled him, a grief that was to hard for him to bear. A distant noise of swords clashing raised his head to see. His father stood on the other side of the small river that ran through the village. Another figure stood with him, fighting, clashing swords in battle. Slowly moving to the side, the two embraced in battle. They went out of view behind a group of trees, and Kail stood to his feet. "Father! Come back!" he shouted to his dad. There came no response, but a distant scream of pain. Kail's head lowered again. Tears poured out of his eyes, falling to the hard ground. "What am I to do? Why have you both left me?" Kail paused, expecting no answer. But one came, one that was not from his father or mother, or any human. A low growling sounded from behind him. Slowly, he turned around unknowing of what was to come. His face met with that of a dark, shadowy Raigien, engulfed in black mist! "Raigein!" he screamed, hoping for help from anyone. But no one came to aid him. The Raigien slowly lifted its sword, pointing at Kail. Quickly it came forward, piercing it into Kail's stomach. A searing pain shot through Kail, and blood poured out of him! The Raigein withdrew the sword, and faded away into its dark chasm. Kail was left alone, blood seeping out of his stomach to the ground. His mind cleared, and his vision blurred. Then, all of his senses went numb, and all went black. Kail's eyes opened. Sweat was dripping from his forehead to his bed. A cramping feeling came to his stomach, remembering the pain. The door to his room swung open, and Mr. Narrin stepped in. "Is everything alright Kail? You were nearly screaming in your sleep!" exclaimed the pastor. "Father Narrin, it was that dream again. It keeps coming back for me, and I cannot stop it" "That was seven years agowhen you were only ten. What do you think it means?" Narrin wondered. "I don't knowbut it's painful" Kail winced as he lay back down to fall again to sleep. Kail awoke again, and lifted his head off the pillow the church had provided him. He slumped out of the bed and put some clothes on. Stepping out side of his room, he could smell a wondrous food cooking downstairs. Kail went down, and into the kitchen. He found Naura, the church caretaker, cooking food on the stove. He sat down at the table and had his small share of the food, thanked her, and left the church to go outside into Ceder. The town bustled with activities, people going from here to there, doing their daily chores. The market was especially busy today, for it was Saturday, and everyone would gather all that they needed so that they could rest on the Sabbath. The wind blew a sudden gust, and swept through the town. It came upon Kail as water broke on rock. It surged past him, rippling his blond hair, waving constantly. His kind, green eyes, felt the wind, and they watered from it's coldness. The brown cape that he wore shifted too, blowing behind him, ripping back and forth, revealing the sword that he hid underneath of it. Kail moved forward and down the church's front stairs. He walked into the center of the village, and stood next to the fountain that spewed out sparkling water from the nearby spring. Kail's head turned to the left and right as he scanned the shops for one that carried weapons and armor. He stopped upon one that had a light smoke filtering from the windows. Loud cracks of fire sounded every so often, and sparks shot past the window. He walked up to the door, and glanced up at a double-hinged sign that read, "Ceder Blacksmith." Kail opened the door and stepped into the shop, which smelled heavily of charcoal and fire. A young man stood over a piece of metal that glowed as brightly as the sun, and was pounding away at it with a hammer, turning it over every once in a while. "Hello?" Kail yelled over the explosive booms. The boy looked up from his work, and said, "Oh! Kail! You've finally come have you? Well, what can I do for you?" Kail looked at the boy, his face was black with ashes. "I need for my sword to be sharpened and upgraded to the strongest metal you have. And, I need some chain-mail, could you get all that for me?" "Yes! I'll get to it right away!" the boy said ecstatically. But then he looked puzzled, "What do you plan to do with all that? Are you really going to do it, you know, right?" "YeahI've been planning to leave for a long time" Kail responded with a tone of regret in his voice. "Then I'll get this done as fast as I can! Does noon sound good to you? "Sure, just come and find me, I'll be either in the town center or at the church." "No problem Sir! Soon as possible!" the boy said happily. Kail smiled at him, handed him the sword he carried, left the shop, and went back into the town. He walked out and headed towards and shop that carried all sorts of useful items for the journey he was going to take. When he stepped inside, a small bell jingled, and a girl, who appeared to be fifteen or sixteen, looked up at Kail and stood. She wore an apron with purple flowers, and her eyes looked kindly at Kail. "Hey, what can I get you?" she asked. "I'm in need of a pound of herbs, and a couple of those regens. Do you still have them in stock?" "Yes, I'll go get them for you!" she said, "It will total up to fifty five Lucure." Kail paid and thanked the girl, took the items and quietly left. He headed back to the church to talk with Pastor Narrin. When he arrived he found the pastor out working in the garden with his wife, Naura. He walked up to them and sat down on the bench close to where they worked. "So, I take it you plan to leave today, Kail?" Narrin questioned even though he knew the answer. Kail sighed, "Yeah, I guess I am I've already made all of the arrangements. All I'm waiting for now is for the blacksmith." "Well, I've made a small snack for your journey." Kail stood, and so did the pastor and his wife. They all headed towards the church gate. "I guess I'll be off then," Kail spoke solemnly. "May God's grace be with you on you journey," Mrs. Naura said as she handed him the snack she had prepared. "Yes, good luck too! You should try looking for information on the whereabouts of you father up in a town called Maidus," the pastor advised. The Ceder Blacksmith boy ran up to them, panting and out of breath. "Ah, I found you! I've been searching through the whole town! But here," the boy said as he handed Kail his sword and armor, "I've upgraded your sword and made the chain mail you wanted! That's one hundred twenty five Lucure Sir!" He paid him, and the boy was off after saying goodbye. Kail turned towards the town, and walked away from the church, and then exited the town. He unfolded a map that Pastor Narrin had given him. He searched for Maidus, and found it just north of the Colidaur Forest, which was north from where he stood. Kail looked up towards the north, and spotted the forest a far ways away. He started down the hill that Ceder Village was built on, and ran swiftly yet silently across the outstretched grass before him. Trees were randomly scattered across the vast plain, and only a few small streams flooded the region. He ran up to about a mile away from the wood and stopped for rest, and looked up. The sun was beginning to set over the hills, and night crept over the sky. After running further, his foot finally touched the first dead, fallen leaf of the forest's trees. It collapsed from Kail's weight and made a crunching sound, quite loud compared to the dead silence of the chilled night air. More crunching continued, but Kail wasn't moving. It was being emitted from inside the forest's outer wall of trees. A loud growl suddenly burst the dark silence of the air as a gigantic beast sprang out of the darkness of the wood! It slammed into Kail and smacked him into the ground, knocking air from his lungs! He sat up, regained his breath, and quickly spun around as he drew his sword from its sheath. To his surprise, the monster had already begun to commence a second strike by launching forward and outstretching his claws. Kail swiftly rolled to the side as he sprung his sword upwards from it dormant position. It stuck straight out as the beast flew by, right into and through the blade! Its collision caused Kail to shift back, and he had to release his grip on his sword to roll back with the force. The monster hit the ground and slid across the dirt, sliding to a halt. Kail stood up, and grabbed his sword, returning it to its sheath. He turned to the beast, and looked at its dying corps. It was a sort of lion creature, but with sharp fangs and what appeared to be elongated claws. It twitched and tried to move, but the last bit of energy was sucked put by the last blow. Kail turned around and faced the tall, enormous trees of Colidaur Forest. He stepped into the darkness, and it crept around him as he entered. After he passed through the wild brush that concealed the inner parts of the forest, he came to a more open area. Light filtered into the space trough gaps in the forest ceiling. Kail looked around at the trees and paths. On his left he noticed a large fallen tree that landed across the whole path, and it looked as if it were smashed by something trying to get through. On his right, he saw an immense gap in the brush and trees, also as if something rather big was trying to move around in the area. Kail ran forward and jumped over the fallen tree, and headed through the gap in the brush. He entered a second almost carved out area of trees. Massive spikes of rock jotted out of the ground every so often in the place, and on it smaller spikes were pierced out of the stone. Kail advanced to one of the spikes, and using it as a ladder, climbed up to the roof of the forest, and gazed out at the vastness of it. The forest seemed to spread out endlessly before him. But to the side, he saw a broken path of trees, pushed over and crushed by an enormous weight. He climbed back down a little and jumped the rest of the way down to the ground. Then, running towards the path of dead trees, Kail came to a small ledge that out jetted over the trail. Jumping off and landing on a small tree, he sprinted on to try to find the end of it. He had to push out all of the small branches and plant that had grown in his way, and more and more brush and trees began to appear in his way as he ran. Soon, Kail was back in forested land, dodging trees and bushes that were scattered around him. Slowly, he came to a stop as he neared a small opening in the forest were light was shining through the tree roof. On the ground lay an animal, and around it sat a puddle of its dark blood. The dead creature wore large gashes on its side with blood slowly spilling out. Kail brushed past the area and ran ahead to find the northern edge of the forest. Yet no matter how fast he ran, he could never find an end. He now ran out of breath fast, and he was extremely tired. Out of nowhere, large pounding and rumbling sounds blasted through the forest, shaking the soil beneath Kail. Then, a noise of trees collapsing and plants being crushed sounded in his ears. He stood up, and drew out his blade, expecting the worst from the depths of the forest. The thumping came closer and closer in Kail's direction. Suddenly, the trees in front of him snapped in half and flew to the sides, ripping through the forest! Before him, stood a tall being, roaring as it saw Kail as its pray. Large wings, lined in a golden gloss and webbed in a sickening green, spread out of its sides. The head of the dragon like creature rose from its still position to the sky, and its horns turned downwards. The dragon let out a large cry, searing through the forest, cutting into the ears of all of the beings. The head looked back at Kail, and in an instant it swung down one of its massive claws! Kail leaped to the side right as the fist slammed to the ground, and blew him further away from it! Kail stood up and held out his sword and charged at the creature. The dragon again lifted his hand and swung down for Kail! But Kail jumped forward in front of the blow, and when it hit the ground it propelled him straight at the dragon! Kail slammed the sword forward when he got close enough in the air to hit the monster, but instead the dragon kicked off the ground to its left and use its wings to spin around in the air! After it spun, it quickly brought up its tail and launched it at Kail's stomach! Kail saw the dragon let out this attack and so he fell to the ground as fast as he could. But to his surprise the dragon had shifted the tail's swing downwards and it smashed into Kail's back! The blow caused his legs and head to be thrown upwards as his abdomen was crushed under the weight of the tail. For moments, they stayed in the same position while the massive blow stunned Kail. Saliva shot out of his mouth, followed by a trail of blood from his lips. Kail regained his thought, and wiggled free of the position faster than the dragon could react. He hastily swung his sword down at the tail, and it ripped into the flesh, blood spilling out! The monster roared in its suffering, and Kail dragged the sword inside the tail, then ripping it free! The tail had been separated from the rest of the dragon, and it flopped off to the side in shock. The dragon immediately swung back around and smashed its head into Kail, blasting him to the side and into a tree! Kail hit and fell to the ground as his legs collapsed. He tried to drag himself to the side by grasping roots with his hands and pulling, but he couldn't move fast enough. The monster positioned its head over Kail, and slammed its mouth down into Kail's leg, biting into the flesh! Kail screamed in flustered pain as blood drained out of the gored wound! The dragon lay still, biting into the leg. Kail hastily picked up his sword and stabbed it into the dragon's head, releasing him from the fang's grip! The dragon brought itself upward on its feet, swaying back and forth. It then fell to the side and crashed into the ground, groaning. A sum of blood came out around the dragon's head as Kail dragged his leg and walked over to a tree. There, he lay back, and took out one of the herb packs that he had bought. He sprinkled the herbs onto the wound in his leg. A stinging pain flushed through the wound as the herb quickly healed the gash. Blood that had spilt out sucked back into the leg and the wound closed itself with the force of the herb. Kail stood up and shook the once hurt leg, making sure it worked right. He looked up from what he was doing. Nearby, where the dragon was first standing, he noticed a small hole through a heavy brush. He went over to it to take a closer look at it. Kneeling down and pressing his face on the dirt covered floor, Kail looked out the hole and saw grass and trees, possibly the way out! And the hole was just big enough for him to fit though! Kail squeezed through and popped out the other side. The grassy field looked similar to the one on the other side of Colidaur Forest, but not the same. Not too far away from where he stood, Kail saw Maidus! Excided, he ran down the small hill he was on, and off towards the town. Nearly skidding when he reached the entrance gate, Kail halted to be let through. "What is it that you need here?" a posted guard asked him. "I'm here to rest and look for someone," "Alright," the guard okayed, "let em' inside! Hey, you should try going to the restaurant down the road near the houses, lots o' food there! Gott'a nice inn too!" The gates creaked open as the gate guards pulled open the heavy doors. Kail walked in and the door closed again to their torpid position. He walked on past all of the farms and smaller gathering places into the main section of the city. Like Ceder, Maidus also buzzed with activity. People were flooding the place, trading, selling, and buying. Kail found the restaurant near a grouping of houses, and pushed his way through the crowd to the door, opened it, and went inside. There wasn't much light in the room except for the sun shining through the windows. Kail went over the counter where a man stood cleaning dishes for reuse. "What can I get ya today young sir?" the man at the counter asked. "Water's it, no food," The man reached down and brought up a cup, and then filled it with water, and then handed it to Kail, "Here ya go sir! Water's free here, no need for Lucure!" Kail took his water and sat down by himself at one of the corner tables. "Where to next? Where should I start to look?" he mumbled to himself as he fiddled with the eating utensils on the wooden table. Kail was so deep into his own thoughts while sipping the water that he didn't notice a girl who looked about his age walk up to his table. She had her hair tied into a bun with two sticks holding it up, and her eyes had a kind and friendly blue color. "Hey therewhy are you all by yourself over here?" she asked. Kail jumped, startled by her sudden question, "N-nothing" "Do you mind if I sit here?" she wanted to know even though she had already invited herself to sit. "S-sureI don't care" "I'm Fiona, I live right next to here," she said, followed by a long silence, "What's you name?" "Kail" he responded. "Well it's nice to meet you Kail!" Fiona said gleefully. An hour later, Kail stood up from his chair. Fiona had already left to go back to her home next door. He walked over to the counter where the man still stood. After renting a room for the night, he went into it to rest his mind and worries. Even though the bed wasn't too comfortable, Kail slept easily after his hard traveling and work.
