# Chapter 2: Succession

Screaming erupted throughout the town, breaking the silence of sleep. Kail leaped in his bed from the startling sound. Kail ran swiftly outside of the inn. Smoke rose in the east of the village, the place where the gate and farms were. Screams of fear and pain were exploding from all around the town. An older woman suddenly came running and screaming up the road from the area. Behind her ran a soldier of some kind, wearing green pants and armor all over his upper body except for his head and neck. When he saw Kail, he stopped chasing the woman and pointed his sword at him. "You there! Leave this town at once or face the death that awaits you here!" the soldier demanded. "I'll leave when I wish" "Ha! Those who defy the order of Raihian must die!" the man yelled as he charged at Kail, blade still drawn. Kail brought out his own sword ready to kill this strange man. When the soldier came close enough to hit him, Kail slid to the side, raising his sword, and smacked the man on the head with the backside. Kail then spun around very fast and hit the man on the other side of the head in the opposite direction. Then, he brought the blade to the soldier's neck level, and slashed it sideways, effectively cutting the throat. The man swayed back and forth, gagging, and then fell to the ground, dead. Fiona suddenly ran out of her house, and ran over to Kail. "What the heck is going on out here?" she yelled with excitement. "I don't knowbut it doesn't look good" Kail responded. Just then, two more of the same armored soldiers ran up the road with their swords drawn for battle. "You two!" the men shouted, but then when they got closer, they saw their fallen comrade, "What the hell? What have you done?! You will pay for his life you fools!" The two men charged as the first did, and with the same style. Fiona took out the two sticks she had in her hair, and when she pressed the bottom of both of them, two blades shot out! She readied the daggers in both her hands, and Kail brought up his sword. The man on the left charged at Kail, who jumped to the side evasively, and the man fell over from his failed attack. The other came up to Fiona and tried to stab at her stomach, but she hit his sword up into the air by one of her daggers. She then stabbed forward and pierced into the man's chest while Kail raised his own sword and bashed down upon the other soldier. Fiona's attacker released his sword and fell to the ground. Kail's tried to get up onto his knees but was then smacked back down onto the ground and stopped moving. Kail put his sword back into the sheath, and Fiona replaced the daggers in her hair. "We should leave, I have a feeling there will be more," Kail said while sighing. Kail and Fiona both ran down the road in the opposite direction from which the soldiers had come. They came to the back gate of the town, and found the door had already been opened, and so they ran out of Maidus. "Can we take a break yet?" Fiona whimpered with exhaustion. "Fine, we can stay here for the day, maybe the town will clear out so we can return tomorrow," Kail said hopefully, "I'm going to go get some wood," Kail left Fiona by all their stuff and ran over to a small group of trees to the north. He took out his sword and started to cut down the smaller trees, and then those into pieces. He carried what he could back to the place Fiona rested, and dropped them onto the ground. After organizing them a bit, he tried making a flame with two of the sticks. "No need!" Fiona said as she stood up and walked over to Kail. She held her hand above the wood and closed her eyes. After a minute, a small aura began to glow softly around her body. She tensed up the hand and all of the aura was concentrated into her hand. Then she relaxed it, and a spark shot out of her hand into the wood, igniting it! "You know magic!?" Kail said surprised. "Well, only a little bit. My mother taught me a long time ago, and I'm still only able to do that." There, they waited for hours with the idea that the town would be empty of the soldiers, but that didn't seem like it would be happening. "So, Kail, what brings you all the way out here? You've got to have a good reason for going through Coulidaur don't you?" Fiona asked. "I'm looking for someone who I lost a long time ago during the war a long time ago. It was my father. My mother was killed then, so now all I can to do is to search for him" "Sorry Hey! I have an idea! The Wise One, you could try asking him! If anything, he would know where your father is!" Fiona suggested with hope. "Where is this 'Wise One'?" "He lives on the Terren Summits, just west of here. It wouldn't be that long of a trip at all! And when we get back, the town might be empty by then!" Fiona said. "HmmI guess it sounds like a good idea, let's get going now then, before the sun begins to set!" Fiona led Kail westward, to the Terren Summits, in search of the Wise One. When they reached the mountains, they found steep cliffs, jagged rocks, and few places to grasp to avoid falling. Kail jumped up onto a higher cliff, about ten feet up. He reached down his hand for Fiona to grab, and he pulled her up. They began to climb up the harsh terrain. Grabbing small stones, hanging onto vines and plants to keep away from death. Kail put his hand up onto the top of the cliff they were scaling and lifted himself up, and then help Fiona to the top. They both looked ahead and saw higher mountains with snow on their peaks. "Well, at least they're not cliffs like that one!" Kail said reassuringly. The two continued on, hiking up the mountains, stopping to rest only for minute intervals. The mountains contained no life once so ever. No plants, animals, or even humans as one could see from the ground. Kail and Fiona were nearing the end of the highest, snow-tipped mountain when Kail's eyes caught sight of a small house on the top. "Is that it?" Kail yelled over the fierce winds blowing the snow around. "Yes! Just hurry in! I'm getting cold!" Fiona said dreadfully. "You don't say?" he responded as he hiked up to house, and walked in, holding the door open for Fiona. They both looked around at the interior of the hut. It was filled with shadows, and only several candles lit the room, excluding the warmth of the fire in the hearth. The place was almost littered with papers, pot and other containers, and strange objects. A man with a beard stepped out of a shadow, "What can I do for you, young ones?" "We're looking for the Sage, being the last of them all," Fiona stepped forward to say. "I am he, but the question was not who you are looking for, but what you want" "Yes, I wanted to ask you if you had any information on the whereabouts of my father do you know?" Kail spoke. "Hmm what is your name, boy?" the Sage questioned him. "Kail," "Hmmcan he be?" the Sage whispered to himself, not allowing Kail or Fiona to hear, "What is your father's name?" "Eric, but why do you ask?" Kail wondered. "Ah! You fool! You are the son of that bastard?" the Sage screamed, "I sent them to kill you! You should have died seven years ago!" "What do you mean?! You are the one who killed my mother? You sent that hoard of beasts?!" Kail yelled furiously. "Oh yes, boy, and I enjoyed every minute, watching the pain I made with that army," "But that was the army of Titus, he sent those demons, didn't he?" Fiona asked. "No, you foolish little girl, I did! I" he began to speak. "You bastard!" Kail drew his sword and flung it straight at the Sages head. But suddenly, the Sage turned into a vapor-like substance! His form changed into a beast. "What the the Sage a Venator?" Fiona said with a wavering voice. The sword hit the wall with a loud clashing sound, and stuck into it, "Yes, I have fooled you as I have everyone else!" The Sage turned back into a human, and jumped over to a wall and yanked off a long spear. He flew back to the ground and charged at the defenseless Fiona. The Sage swung up the wooden end, and smacked her jaw. Then, while she was still in the air, he swung the spear in a sideward motion and hit her ribs, sending her flying into one of the house's sides. The Sage flew forward again, but at Kail, and slammed his head into his stomach, blowing him backwards to the floor. Kail stood up, and threw a fist at the Sage's head. Instantly, the Sage turned back into his vapor form and the first flew right through it! He then stepped back and turned back into a human, then he grabbed Kail's arm and swung him around and released his grip, sending him into a bookshelf. It fell over on top of Kail as books fell onto him. He coughed roughly as his lungs tried to expand under the weight of the shelf, but they couldn't. The Sage slammed him hand onto the backside of the case and lifted it off of Kail, and threw it into another wall, smashing the wall through. He then turned back to Kail, and reached down to his neck. He picked up the beaten Kail and strangled him. "How does it feel, Deusi? Your own time to die has come, and I assure you, I will make it as painful as this body permits," the Sage murmured to Kail. Fiona stood up as fast as her weakened body would allow her. She quietly pulled the sword from the wall, and ran up to Sage's backside, where he couldn't see. She slammed the sword forward at the flesh. But right when the sword made the slightest scratch on him, he turned back to vapor. Kail dropped to the floor, through his hand, and coughed in air. But even in the vapor form, a small trickle of blood came down where the sword had lightly hit. Instantly, the Sage made a flash of light and vanished from sight. Fiona walked over to Kail and helped him up to the ground. "What did he mean, Deusi?" Fiona asked Kail. "I don't know it doesn't make sense"
