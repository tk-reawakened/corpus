# Prolouge

Long, long ago, Titus, the creator of all darkness, brought the demon Exilis to life. The land of Infiri, where the demons of the night lurk, marks the birthplace of the Exilis, and so, the origin of all shadow. Titus used Exilis to torment Terrenus, the land where the humans lived. No power seemed as if it could defeat the evil, until the Seven Sages were appointed as the leader of the humans. An alliance of the Sages and an army of men marched to Infiri where Exilis and Titus were resting in their fortress, Redgrias, and were guarded by the demons and shadow. In the last effort to bring down the horrid power, the great battle in the gloom of Infiri began. Men equipped with swords, knifes, and with the ability of the Sages, thought they had a chance. But then, the forces of Redgrias were unleashed before them. Ten thousand Oriens, the demonic elves, wielded double-sided blades cursed with the fire of Faor, who was their king for the last five hundred thousand years. Just a touch would scorch your hand and burn your soul to the core. Then there were five hundred Raigeins, the half dead souls that ever haunt even the wickedest of demons. They held the magic of the elements of fire, wind, and darkness, granted to them by Titus. At least fifty Venators floated amongst them, the beings that humans thought to be but a myth. These creatures could pass through solid objects like vapor, but they could still kill and destroy mercilessly. The most powerful of all of them, was the force of the Exilis. It held a massive fire blade that could cut through almost anything, and leave a melted path of its remnants behind. And the powers granted to him far out matched any force the humans could conjure. And there was also Titus, who only looked as a weak creature. But his strength was greater than ten thousand men, and his magic was greater than even Exilis's. And so, he led the force in the battle for reign over Terrenus, Infiri, and Redgrias. The demonic army annihilated the human forces. The human army pillaged and burned before Titus, laughing at every man's attempt to survive. But then, when all was lost, a four winged creature, crowned in gold and robed in silk, came down from the heavens. It carried a bow and only one arrow, engulfed in a flaming light. The being held up the bow and knocked the arrow, pointing it directly into the center of the demonic army. Quickly, it released the arrow, and it flew towards the quivering demons. A great explosion of fire and light came from all around. A horrid screeching sound let out, numbing the ears of all. Slowly, the light faded, and the smoke cleared, and all that was left in place of the demons, was Titus, and a dead Exilis. Afraid of the being that had left, Titus fled to the mountains of Terrenus, leaving his beloved demon behind. Only ten years later, a threat of an on going evil is rising. The Sages have all but one died, the leader of the human army missing, and the winged creature still unknown. So a hero must step forth to banish the evil once again. But that hero will realize that not all in his world is as it seems
