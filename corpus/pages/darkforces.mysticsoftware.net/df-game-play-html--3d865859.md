# Controlls

Activation key. Character/cursor movement. Menu activation (outside of battles). Holding down will allow character to run. Pauses game (works only when not in story mode or in battle waiting phase). Screen redraw. Fixes screen errors. During game play, you can also change every menu's back and confirmation keys to a key that you desire by accessing the "Options" choice in the main menu. Each character presented in Dark Forces has his/her own unique ability to use in and outside of battle. Some are magical abilities that can be used to clear objects blocking you path, or just abilities to gain items. You can assertain these powers by increesing your level, or by using special items you can find on your journey. Each time you win a battle, your characters gain a certain amount of experience. You can also gain Exp. by using rare items you might find along your way. When a character kills an enemy, they will recieve extra experience for killing it. After a battle is over, if your total Exp. exedes a point, you will gain a level. At certain levels, your strength, defence, etc. will grow to make you stronger: The power a character has that decides the amount of damage delt to the enemy. Upgradable by item or Exp. The shielding a character has that decides the amount of damage delt to the himself. Upgradable by item or Exp. The rate of which the speed gage of a character will increase by to decide when a character's battle turn takes place. Upgradable by experiece or items. Wisdom is the power to influence strength, defence, or magic to increase in power during an attack. It can also influence a salesman to lower the price of an object for you. Can be increased by a very rare item or Exp. The power given to a magical attack used during battle, and can also influence some events outside of battling. Empowerable by Exp. or items. The amount of Health Power and Mana Power inside or outside of battle. When the HP is gone, the game ends and you must restart. When MP is gone, you are unable to use magic in battle or on the world map. -The currency used in Terrenus is called Lucure.
