> Problem:
> Client wanted an Internet-based auction where bidders could submit bids on multiple items at one time. Complex filtering capabilities on available inventory and storage of the multiple defined filters for later reuse were necessary.
> ** Solution:
> Mystic Software designed and implemented a solution based on Microsoft's IIS 5.x and ASP using MS SQL Server.
> **Results:**
> Clients were able to quickly identify the inventory they were interested in and submit bids on multiple items easily and all at once.
