**Characters:**
Name: Mark
Age: 20
Class: Peasant
Home Land: Mala
Weapon: Sword
Level: 1
Mark is the hero of the game. You play as him and he must always be in your party!
Name: Jimmy
Age: 19
Class: Thief
Home Land: India
Weapon: Wip
Level: 1
Jimmy and Mark are best friends. They both got into alot of trouble until Jimmy was caught by their Mayor Bob and was made his slave. But Mark was able to free him. He Joins you very early in your quest!
Name: Suzin
Age: 25
Class: Warrior
Home Land: Rela
Weapon: Sword
Level: 2
Suzin was raised by her mother because his father died before she was born. When she was 15 her mother was killed by a giant rat/bear. She then lived on her own for then on. She joins Mark because her life is in chaos and she wanted to have fun and do something for a change
Name: Arbor
Age: Unknown
Class: Tree Man
Home Land: Tree Town
Weapon: Board
Level: 10
Name: Dragon Egg
Age: He doesn't have an age! He's not born yet!
Class: Dragon
Home Land: Pyramid
Weapon: Wing Blade
Level: 10
Dragon Egg is a dragon that is having alot of trouble inside his egg. He can't seem to get out, be he doesn't have much of a problem. His or her *we are not quit sure yet* parents were eaten by a cannibalistic dragon who is known only as The Mega Dragon. The Mega Dragon is the evil ruler of Pyramid and if there inhabits don't do want he wants them to well then...They are eaten! Dragon Egg joins you in exchange for the assistance in the assignation of the Mega Dragon.
Name: Bob
Age: 33
Class: Slaver
Home Land: Mala
Weapon: Wip
Level: 30
Bob is a slaver and is also the mayor of Mala. He is not actually such a bad man to the people in Mala. But if they brake the law then he makes them his slaves. However Mala is such a peaceful town that barely any crimes are committed. Bob will actually join you because he himself went on the quest to find The Castle of Peace. He dose however get a bit mad when he sees Jimmy in your party.
Name: Gapolie
Age: 5
Class: Animal
Home Land: Giant Tree
Weapon: Vine, Banana
Level: 30
Gapolie has to have the worst reason to join you, I had no clue what I was thinking, but I suppose he's fun to have around. Gapolie joins you if you give him a coin. He will eat it but in exchange he joins your party. He uses Banana based special moves. He can feed bananas to himself and team mates to heal them. Or throw bananas at enemies to trip them. Or force Poison Banana's down his enemies throat!
Name: Peire
Age: 30
Class: Knight
Home Land: France
Weapon: Sword
Level: 30
Peire is the commander of a French Military out post. This Military out post was made out side The Black Walkway. The French Solders believe that they can simply wipe out the daemons and blow up the Black Walkway, however it can only be done if The Magic Sword is put in the right hands. He shall join you to aid in the battle to defeat The Black Walkway
Name: Phillip
Age: 19
Class: Magician
Home Land: France
Weapon: Punch, Magic
Level: 30
Phillip is Pierre's younger brother. He has trained to do alot of Magic and is very powerful
in that section. He joins you to help battle in The Black Walkway!
