# 2002 Gallery

In the year 2002 a lot of things happened in my life. I graduated highschool. I went to college. I laughed, I cried, and I even contemplated suicide once. That stopped when I realized suicide is the coward's way out of life, and I started sketching again. Sketching to get my feelings out saved me for the next three years of life.
