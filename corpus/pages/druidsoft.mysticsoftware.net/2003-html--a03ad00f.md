# 2003 Gallery

2003 was the year of change for me. I took a few art classes at college, and suddenly my artwork started looking a hell of a lot better. I also expanded my anime collection intensely, and learned from looking at other artist's works. I discovered Renga during this year...his artwork has changed my perspective on profiles drasticly, and now my profile sketches all have a bit of his touch in them.
