# About the Author

![](images/darkaura.png)
I am known as Walker B Riley. My real name is Brenton Dinsmore. I am 18 years of age and a student at the University of Maine. I have been using the RPGToolkit for about 5 years now. I have been using it since it was in version 2.02. This makes me almost the oldest TK user on the boards, beaten only by about three other people who were around during it's 1.4 era.
There is a story behind that picture of me. My friends and I were taking a stroll and some photography students asked if they could take each of our pictures. We each took our pics at the same spot, in the same light, etc. Theirs came out bright. Mine was dark. Very dark. The photographers couldn't give a good explanation as they had checked the lighting meters and everything. So we just said, "Hey, Walker has a dark aura about him!!" This is the Dark Aura picture.
I am a jack-of-all-trades. I am able to do almost anything, but I cannot master anything. This ability is making my college carreer endangered. I enjoy making things most of all. I can make almost anything I set my mind to. I have made Cloaks, Tunics, Computers, Crystal Radio sets, etc. I am currently making a sword. I also enjoy writing and drawing. My writing usually gets turned into an RPG of some sort. My artwork can be seen splattered all over this website.
I am male, obviously. I am single. I do not plan on having this status changed any time soon. I like anime to the point where I own more series than almost anyone I know. In fact, if you ask me any question about anime, I can probably answer it.
