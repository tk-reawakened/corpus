# "What is with this guy?" and other information about the artist.

![](sketches/2003/me004.jpg)
"What is with this guy?" and other information about the artist.
Where to start....I suppose the first thing would be the standards. I'm nearly 20 years in age and in college. My major is business administration, though I think I might minor in the arts. I am male.
Now then, with that out of the way....I have been watching anime for the better portion of my short life. The first anime I ever saw was Dragon Warrior. That was back when I was maybe five or six. It's not a very well known anime in the states, and finding english translated episodes is harder than finding diamonds in your back yard. Many people will probably remember the RPG by Enix by the same name, though. I was instantly hooked, and have been watching anime since then. I have emassed a vast collection of anime dvds, vhs, manga, etc. My favorite anime at the current time, and probably forever more, is Kanon.
Let's see...if it's 2004 now, then it would have to be about eight years ago that I started trying to draw anime. Unfortunatly, I can't find anything before the year 2001, so I only have the accumilation of three years of sketches. I know for a fact that I have more sketch books hidden around my room, but finding them is going to be tough. So I only have the past three years of work here.
Other than that, there's not much to say.
