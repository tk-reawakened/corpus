# Artwork of the past

Something I do enjoy a lot is drawing. I never was a good artist, and probably never will be. But that doesn't keep me from doing something I like to do.
These gallery's hold fanart I've done, and original commissioned artwork I've done. They also have some original work I did for myself.
[![](images/fanart/D&Brough.png)](rough.htm)
This gallery holds all the rough sketches I've done. I've found in recent years that it is a good idea to keep a copy of all sketches I do. I suppose any artist could tell me that. It just took me awhile to figure it out.
[![](images/fanart/D&Bclean.png)](inked.htm)
After I finish off a rough sketch, I'll ink it. Well, only if I like it. These are usually just "primers" for when I color art, anyways.
[![](images/fanart/D&Bcolor.png)](color.htm)
A fully finished piece of artwork is a beautiful sight to behold. My colored gallery has all the artwork I colored. Most of it is fanart, some personal art, all 100% me.
