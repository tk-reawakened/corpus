# Yet another. Benj is the supportive character in the comic. He is the one that gives everyone else their strength.

Benj: Erick's roomy and best friend. He watched Erick and Amy through the years and thinks they should still be together. He keeps Amy informed on Erick's goings ons.
[![](photogallery/photo32047/benj.jpg)](images/comic/rough/benj.png)
Benj was the first character I seriously began making. Even though he can be considered a secondary character, there would be no comic without Benj.
[![](photogallery/photo32047/benjink.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/benjink.png)
Relearning how to properly ink my drawings took me some time. My earlier renditions of my characters took me a bit. Benj was fun to draw though, so I didn't mind.
[![](photogallery/photo32047/BenjAngry.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/BenjAngry.png)
Giving my characters emotion and distinction was a problem. I never ever have been able to get just the right expression. Here, however, Benj looks...great. This is also where I first started experimenting with tones.
[![](photogallery/photo32047/BenjFront.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/BenjFront.png)
Front views were also a pain in the ass for me, but I knew how those worked. Benj is not the kind of guy to get worried easily, so this would be a rare moment for him.
[![](photogallery/photo32047/BenjWorried.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/BenjWorried.png)
