# Colored Gallery

After hard work, turmoil, blood, sweat, tears, paper, and several computer crashes, I finish coloring my artwork. I use Paint Shop Pro 7.x to do all the coloring. I use several layers, even when I don't need half of them. I keep everything at a large scale so I can see what needs to be done. Sometimes I'll scale it down first, though, and then color. It all depends on how I feel at that time.
