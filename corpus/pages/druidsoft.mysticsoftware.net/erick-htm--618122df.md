# Rough pics of Erick. He's fun to draw too, and being the main character, I draw a lot of him.

Erick: a loser to almost everyone except Amy and Benj. Erick went to college on a hunch and has yet to figure out what that hunch is.
[![](photogallery/photo5235/erick.jpg)](images/comic/rough/erick.png)
Rough pics of Erick. He's fun to draw too, and being the main character, I draw a lot of him.
[![](photogallery/photo5235/ErickSketches.jpg)](images/comic/rough/ErickSketches.png)
This is where I delved heavily into how to do profiles. The bottom left is prolly the closest to my current style. The soft nose is taken from an anime series called SaiKano.
[![](photogallery/photo5235/erickink.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/erickink.png)
Erick was a pain in the ass to come up with. I've changed his hair style at least four times now. I like him though. He is fun.
[![](photogallery/photo5235/ErickCreepy.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/ErickCreepy.png)
Erick is not creepy, he never will be. This was...attempting facial expressions the characters don't normally have. And it looks wierd. And he will prolly never use this one. Ever.
[![](photogallery/photo5235/ErickWorried.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/ErickWorried.png)
Taken from my earliest of early. These inks are mostly used for practicing my toning abilities. Tones are fun things, but I need work with em.
[![](photogallery/photo5235/Erickagain.jpg)](images/comic/rough/Erickagain.png)
People don't seem to get that that is cardboard. I was bored so drew on the back of a notebook. Embarressed Erick is my favorite of the two. This is where I started shifting his hairstyle.
[![](photogallery/photo5235/inkedrandomness.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/inkedrandomness.png)
More work with profiles. This is where I finally decided on longer hair in the back. The trademark two strands of hair in the front is still there. I was also trying out body poses.
[![](photogallery/photo5235/ErickInkScene.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/ErickInkScene.png)
I needed to show that he was on a roof, so I put clouds around him. But that didn't seem right. It was too nice a day for him to be on a roof on a nice cloudy day.
[![](photogallery/photo5235/rain.jpg)](images/comic/inktone/rain.png)
So I made it rain. This is my favorite scene to date. I have been given so many nice comments on this. It still needs work. His leg is disproportionate, and the rain looks too 2d. But I'll prolly use this scene again someday.
