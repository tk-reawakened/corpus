# Inked Gallery

These are what come after inking, scanning, and cleanup. I usually ink with a .25 mm Micron archival ink pen. I might boost up to .5mm soon but for now I like the thin lines. I scan everything in at 300 dpi. It makes it easier when coloring and it looks soo much better.
Walker from an RPG I was in called the Forgotten Parish. He was a magic user who fell in with two half-demons. This ink isn't that great as it was inked from a regular lead pencil drawing.
Zero and Riley from Modern Time. This would go over on the Modern Time RPG page except that it doesn't go with the computerized version of the game. This is more along the lines of the Club-based RPG we had going.
My first cleaned fanart. Don't they look cute. I love the idea of Dave and Blue getting together, and wish desperately for it to happen.
It's inked, and that's about all I can say for it. I got a little shaky while inking Margaret's hair. Dave has had a crush on Margaret since highschool, and she had just stated that she doesn't return his love. Poor Dave. Now go to Blue, damnit!
