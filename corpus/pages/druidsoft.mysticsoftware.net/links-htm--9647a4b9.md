# Link link link, all day long, click these links while I sing this song.

This is now the biggest repository of RPGToolkit resources. It is also the nice people who are hosting this site. Spyder has been a friend of mine since about a month after I started using RPGToolkit. I predate him about that amount of time. And now he owns his own site and I'm leeching off his bandwidth. Funny how these things turn out.
[![](images/links/ms.png)](http://www.mysticsoftware.net)
RPGToolkit is the first online community I joined and stuck with. It's also what I use to create awesome games. The community has gone downhill, in my opinion, over the years, but the program is still the best you'll find out there. Try it. You can make most any kind of game with it.
Fred Gallagher is the whole reason I decided to make a webcomic of my own. That and 10 tons of caffeine at 2 o'clock in the morning. There is hardly an otaku nowadays that doesn't know what Megatokyo is. If you don't, go read. It's good.
Ah yes, RocketBox Comics. This is the hot spot for learning how to webcomic. It has many resources and a friendly community. If you want to make a webcomic, I suggest going here.
Once described as being the bastard children of Hello Kitty and Bomberman. A funny webcomic that's been around for awhile. Read it. For the ninjas if nothing else.
[![](images/links/lg_medium1.gif)](http://www.little-gamers.com)
[![](images/links/abancrfh.gif)](http://www.crfh.net/)
I read this religiously now. College Roomies from Hell is a great webcomic that I wish I had started earlier (there's a lot of comics that I see floating around but don't read for some reason). There's nothing this comic doesn't have. Except for Aliens. Those have already been taken though....
Real Life is the first webcomic I ever started reading. That's amazing, ne? I love it because it incorporates many things I enjoy doing. Give it a read. You'll like it. Trust me.
[![](images/links/cast-lg.gif)](http://www.itswalky.com/)
It's Walky! is one of the most wacked out comics out there. It has comedy, suspense, action, danger, romance, and aliens!!! A continuation of the Roomies story and one of my favorites (as is all that are listed here).
