# All the sites that have inspired me to try and be a better artist.

![](components/links.png)
All the sites that have inspired me to try and be a better artist.
AKA Links
Fred Gallagher started doing Megatokyo some four or five years ago. Since then, it's gained an insane fanbase, and he's even having Megatokyo published by Dark Horse. And he still thinks his own artwork sucks. He is an inspiration.
CRFH, as done by Maritza Campos, is what got me through my first year of college and dorm life. I hated dorm life, and this webcomic made it easier to live. I once became somewhat well known on the forums, but recently I haven't been there much. Still love it though.
Renga's page. It's all in japanese, but it's easy enough to navigate. I'm slightly disappointed in the lack of artwork on the page. Renga has a great style and it helped me immensely when I was still trying to figure out how to draw figures properly.
This is Yukirin's site. I don't know much about her or anything. I found this page by accident while looking for Kanon fanart. She has some of the most beautiful artwork I've seen, and she's even done a Kanon webcomic, which I think takes place after the events of Kanon happened. I'm still trying to translate it.
