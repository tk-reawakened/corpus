# How? How can I keep fighting?

*How when you, something dear to me,*
*said you hate me?*
***Modern Time RPG***
Modern Time started as a dream, then became a Yahoo Club based RPG, and now is my current RPGToolkit Project. Although this page is set up for it, I don't particularly like to promote my projects anymore. Every time I do, I wind up not doing them as too many people ask me when it is coming out. This causes me to rush and do half assed jobs and the end demo product is too buggy to release. So, enjoy the artwork that I put up here. It is all related in some way to Modern Time.
