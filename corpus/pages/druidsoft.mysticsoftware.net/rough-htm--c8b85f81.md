# Rough Gallery

I used to use a .5mm No.2 pencil to draw all of my work. This got messy as the pencil lead smeared and scanned in even on black and white settings. I couldn't get a clean scan to inked. Then I learned the beauty of blue lead. Now I use .5mm blue lead in a mechanical pencil. The only problem is that it doesn't erase well...but that's not too much of a problem since I'm not inking my mistakes or scanning the blue (blue lead doesn't scan in b&w)!
Aaron was suppose to look like a scientist in his 40s. At the time of this drawing, I didn't really know how to do that. One of the first characters I drew for Mike Hill. This was also the era where I knew nothing about how good copies of art was created. I was still doing everything in grey pencil.
Higato is supposedly one of the main characters in Mike Hill's TK game. Beyond that I have no clue. He just asked me to make a guy, mid 20s, scrabbly, slightly on the large side but not husky, bigboned etc. He has yet to tell me if he liked this one or not.
Mike Hill asked me to do several drawings for him. This one I did, yet it just didn't feel right to me. This is Marie. I do not like how she came out.
Yuuki. She is one of my best drawings to date. Mike Hill approached me and asked for a young, sad looking girl. This is what I came up with. He liked it, I liked it. I love her enough that I almost didn't let Mike have her.
My first attempt at fanart for something I love. College Roomies from Hell is a great webcomic by Maritza Campos. Here I have Dave and Blue, my two favorite characters. This is also, ironicly, the first sketch for someone else I did with blue lead.
This pic didn't come out the way I wanted it to, and subsequently caused me to completely destroy the colored version. You might still be able to find it on the forums somewhere over at CRFH. Soon I'll redo this image, just because it's Blue and Dave and because they're spooning (just how it should be.)
