# One day I just woke up...and I asked myself.

*What the Hell?!*
What the Hell? a webcomic.
What the Hell? a webcomic started off as a small idea in the back of one of my 3 friends and mine's brain. Then we had a night of sugar, caffeine, and staying up till four in the morning. It was at about two o'clock that one of us, after managing to stop giggling, stated "We should make a webcomic!" We do not remember who said it, but we all got down, made characters and some plot, and started on the first ever comic done by DruidSoft Productions.
It bombed. I still have the 5 or so strips we had done. Now, two years later, What the Hell? is slowly being revived under a new plot line. Why? Because I felt like it. I don't plan on having this thing ready to read any time soon. Nay, I doubt it'll even work. I don't have the time and patience to produce a webcomic anymore. But I'm still gonna try. And until I realize how futile it is, this page is created to pay homage to the images I've created.
