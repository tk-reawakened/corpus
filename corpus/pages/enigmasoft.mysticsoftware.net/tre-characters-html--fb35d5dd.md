# Kage Spencer

Characters
Humans
**Kage Spencer**
Kage is the main character in Tre. He is a Lance Corpral in the Army of Tre and one of Admiral Kishka's personal bodyguards. Although he is one of over one hundred thousand new recruits since the outbreak of the West Isle War, he was hand selected by Kishka for some unknown reason. His mother lives in the small apple farming town of Dysan South. His father's identity has been kept from him his entire life.
**Zane Trudeau**
Zane is you best friend. You've known each other since you were kids in Dysan South. Unlike you, Zane lived in poverty until he joined the army. He has advanced through the ranks especially fast, and he is currently a sergent. Zane is a few years older than you, and his militery career has given him a more accurate picture of the world. Because of this he talks very little. He never questions his orders, no matter how immoral they may be.
**Tina Neilson**
Your childhood friend from Dysan South, Tina can always cheer you up. She is very optimistic and energetic. Her father was a famous swordsman, and she naturally learned to fight with swords. She has lived all of her life in Dysan South and vies to see the world. However, her father would rather she stay and not live the life that he once did.
**Stakbar Kishka**
Kishka is the militery dictator of Tre and Admiral of the army. He lead the High Priest's holy army but once the public began to distrust the church, Kishka assassinated him. Using his superior army, he conquered the surface world's warlords and claimed power. He is extremely hateful towards the Slithen and feels that only Humans deserve to live.
Slithen
**Suro**
General Suro is known the world over as the mastermind behind the West Isle War. He controls the Slithen militery and personally trained the infamous 92nd Trooper Partion, also known as the Death Hawks. The Slithen population reveres him as a great leader, and anything he does is supported by the public. He conquered the entire West Isles in less than a year and was poised to strike on the main continents, but stunned the world when he called for peace. No one knows what his intentions are after Sherik.
**Sele**
As head of the Lilelgguren, Slithen Special Forces, Sele is a very influential Slithen. She passed the rigorous entrance exam when she was only 16, and is considered to be a prodigy. Many think that she will try to become Valukess after Lieone is gone. Although her years in the Lilelgguren have turned her into a hardened veteran of combat, she has a soft side which can make her very impulsive at times.
**Silnir**
Silnir was one of the Lilelgguren's finest until he was expelled for his sexual exploits with many of the female members. His name is synonymous with success in the eyes of most Slithen. Using his Lilelgguren training he trained an elite mercenary commando force and leased it out for enourmous sums of money. He is currently a living as a millionaire in the desert town of Cashmere.
Amals
**Khalubog**
As one of the two Amal sent to Fa'lung to try to hold off a war between the Slithen and Humans, Khalubog is very well known. He was sent to the Humans as an ambassador and militery advisor so that they may be able to hold off the Slithen in case of a war. He rarely ventures out of the embassy, and is never seen in public. A very mysterious person.
**Lieone**
Lieone was sent to Fa'lung to become the Acting Valuk of Lil Uss. The Amals were wary that if the Slithen conquered the surface, they could soon become powerful enough to invade Gre'shan. Lieone is a pacifist, who has spent the past year trying to end the war. He is considered to be a weak leader even by the Amals because of his inability to control Suro.
