# Story

Fa'lung, a war torn planet with a past shrouded in mystery. The surface was ruled by the church, headed by the High Priest. But when support of the theocracy began to dwindle and local warlords took control, Admiral Kishka of the Holy Army assassinated the High Priest and conquered the world.
Then a massive earthquake shook the planet and created several deep fissures. The Slithen, gray humanoids that live in the network of caverns beneath the surface of Fa'lung, were generally isolated from the Humans until the quake. When the Slithen began to build colonies on the surface, Kishka took it as an act of war and attacked.
The Amals, a race of highly advanced shadow people which lived on the neighboring planet of Gre'shan, grew wary of their own safety. They knew if the Slithen controlled the surface they could lauch spaceships to Gre'shan, and possibly invade. So they sent a militery advisor to Kishka and replaced Lil Uss' Valuk with an Amal, to keep the peace.
However, one man did not want peace. General Suro rallied the army, and attacked the West Isles. While Kishka slowly implemented the Amal technology into his army Suro's men swept across the isles, and in one short year the entire region was conquered. As the Slithen army waited for the order, the destruction of the Humans seemed imminent. What would happen to Fa'lung?
