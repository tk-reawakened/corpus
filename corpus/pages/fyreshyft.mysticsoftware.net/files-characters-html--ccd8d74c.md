# Ageless Characters

![Khaldore](Khaldore.jpg) Having turned 21, Khaldore was finally able to fulfill his dream: to become a soldier. He had dreamed of, and trained for, nothing else for the last ten years. His skills in the art of Kyndarus, or "Knight's Blade", were among the best in the realm. He left for the castle city of Hargon to enlist not long after coming of age. **Khaldore Nightwell**
Having lived a wizard's life for more than 50 years, Reslin cast what he thought would be the last spell of his life: one to erase every trace of arcane knowledge from his mind. Once that was finished, he set off for Thryston, a lush port city, to retire by the sea and enjoy the rest of his days.![Reslin](Reslin.jpg) **Reslin Cristal**
At 25, Eryn had been learning the healing arts of the Jexaru for just 4 years. Her father, Phystus, was the Elder of the Jexaru, and he continuously stressed the importance of knowing how to heal the sick and wounded. Lately, however, he had been brooding over something. Little did she realize at the time how disturbing his thoughts truly were...![Eryn](Eryn.jpg) **Eryn Mardin**
