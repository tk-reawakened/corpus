# Ageless Status

Future Plans & Projects
Ah, the "Projects" page... This is where I'll keep anyone who comes by updated as to my current status on , as well as news of any other projects I may have planned for the future. **Ageless**
**Ageless Status**
As it stands, a good deal of my demo is complete. My progress lately has been rather slow, since several other things have been going on in my non-PC life. But, as before, I still lack the following:
- Enemy Graphics & Animations
- A working plugin for my custom menu
- I still have some Items, NPCs, Spells/Skills, & some Coding to finish
Yeah, there's still a good bit left to do, I know. I may leave the custom menu out for this release, as it does lack a plugin, and I'm not a programmer, for the most part.
In other news: A future project of mine may well be a game called "Solstice Of Time". For that, I've only completed the inital storyline, which I'll quote below for anyone interested in reading. It'll be a good while before I consider working more on it, as I'm hoping to get more experienced with the Toolkit first. In any case, enjoy the story!
>
> *My name is Eric Parlen, and to anyone's best knowledge, I am the oldest human alive today. Yes indeed, 115 years on this godforsaken planet, and somehow, I suspect I'll see another 115 years at the rate I'm going. Anyway, this isn't about me, this is about Armageddon, time, and how the two fit together. See, 95 years ago, the worst war to ever grace our little Earth happened, and it was (and still is) called Armageddon. Almost 8 billion people died over the course of 2 days as every government around tried to wipe themselves and each other out. Those sorry ba... er, politicians, brought out everything they could get their greasy hands on. Nukes, chems, bios, you name it, they used it. Lucky they didn't blow up this entire rock we live on, though they might as well have.*
>
> *Anyhow, near as anyone can tell, there's less than a billion beings alive today, and most of them live in what's left of the big cities. You know, New York, Hong Kong, Moscow... what's that? Why did I say 'beings'? Well, with all those blasted chemicals and biological agents thay used, there've been a lot of mutations that have since become genetic templates. Do what? My language? Ah, well, I was studying to be a neurologist when the nukes fell, so I still remember a lot of them fancy words. Now hush up and listen to me, this is important. Anyway, about them templates... originally, they were just mutations caused by the chems and bios. But, since children and grandchildren have been born with the same mutations as their parents, it's been decided that they're parts of new humanoid races. Not blacks, whites, or anything like that... actual races. Some are short and muscular with loads of body hair, some are tall and thin, with pointy ears. Them's the Dwarves and Elves, we call them. Others are genetic crossbreeds, thanks again to those darn poisons. You got Cenatauris and Tigrans too. Course, those are just the 'friendly' ones... so to speak. There's a lot of messed up things out there that simply live to hunt and survive, too, but they're harmless unless you make 'em mad.*
>
> *Then, there's the Others. Yeah, that's the last group of humanoids I've gotta mention. They look just like any other humanoid, but all those chems and bios have twisted their minds, made them sadistic and downright evil. Think a serial killer times 1000, then take that one being times a million or more, and that's an idea of what I'm talking about. Their only desire is to take over and rule what's left of the World. Course, if the Others think for one second that they can't take over, they'll simply imprison, enslave, maim, or kill whoever they have to (or anyone that gets in their way, for that matter) to give them some kind of edge. Yeah, it sounds cliched, but truth *is* stranger than fiction, you know? Anyhow, seems several large groups of these evil ba... er, beings, have spread out in force and taken over some of those big cities I mentioned earlier. New York, Moscow... remember? My point is, they've managed to enslave probably half of the people still alive, and there's no signs that they're slowing down.*
>
> *Problem is, the more these 'Others' spread, the more things around them seem to be getting disjointed, perhaps out of sync with time itself. I know it doesn't make much sense, but my best guess is, as the Others grow, the mutation of their minds mingles them with the space/time continuum somehow. Almost a sort of 'chronokinesis'... the ability to alter the flow of time. Problem is, most of them don't seem to know how to control it. That may be why the more of them gather in one place, the worse the effect on time gets. Slows down, speeds up, even freezes occasionally... and since space and time are linked, there's no telling the effect that's having on the fabric of reality. If they take over, all those untrained minds could warp time so badly it'd rip that 'fabric of reality' apart, and Earth right along with it.*
>
> *That, of course, brings us to you. I despise the idea of sending my own grandchild into the fray, but you're the 'Best of the Best', as they used to say, and I think you've got a chance of restoring the flow of time, not to mention freeing those that the Others have enslaved. You'll have to get some friends to go with you (4, to be exact), since 'best of the best' or not, you're gonna need help. I don't wanna see you end up dead before I go, all right? Now, head downstairs and pick up the stuff outta my room... you're gonna need it. By the way... good luck, and Godspeed.*
>
