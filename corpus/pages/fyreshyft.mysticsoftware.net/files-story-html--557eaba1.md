# Ageless Storyline

![Kyron from space](Kyron.jpg) The planet of Kyron is older than time, some say... more ancient than the gods, others believe... but all will agree that their home is Ageless. For uncounted eons Kyron has circled its sun, watching over millions of generations as they are born, live, and die. But where do the souls of the dead go? The Jexaru, the Holy Ones of Kyron, believe a celestial vortex delivers them to the afterlife, where they reside with the spirit of Kyron itself and watch over the living.
The Jexaru, as is the case with most imperfect beings, were only half right. There is indeed a vortex, but rather than sending souls to their final rest, it devours them to feed its own existence. It thrives on death, but now its hunger grows beyond what mere souls can sate. It craves... needs... more.
For twenty years, the Vortex has slowly made its way across the face of Kyron, decimating everything in its path. Entire kingdoms have been reduced to barren land, and many of the inhabitants of the planet vanished when the Vortex found them. They were the lucky ones. The unlucky ones survived the onslaught, but were transformed into mindless beasts and nightmarish creatures.
There are four, however, that are immune to the all-consuming intensity of the Vortex. One of them possesses an unrealized secret that may save their world... if it comes to light before it's too late. Come now, and lead these heroes to their destiny.
