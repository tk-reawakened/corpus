VIII. Note from the Guild Leader
As members of the Righteous Warriors we promise to uphold justice, peace, and righteousness as we protect our fellow person from evil in every game world we partake in.
*Motto*
We promise to:
Code of Righteousness
- Be righteous in all of our actions
- Protect our fellow guild members
- Hold allegiance to our allies, second only to the guild itself
- Vanquish evil from all lands with virtue and courage
- Respect friends as well as adversaries for their individual views
- Protect new players and provide for them when we see fit
- Set aside an amount of money for a guild fund
- Not use foul language
- Be mature and respectful during the game
- Have fun above all and remember that our real lives are most important
Gameplay Rules
*All rules are subject to change and will be done in the form of amendments, which need to be passed by 3/4 council vote.
- Absolutely no cussing in game. Allowed in private IMs where the members involved consent to that language being used.
- No cheating, bug exploiting, or game enhancing.
- No auctioning items for real money.
- No spamming, harassment, discrimination, or disrespect on any chat rooms, forums, Ims, mailing lists, or IRC chats that involve the games we play in.
- Absolutely positively no divulging of guild plans to any non-member without permission of guild leader. If you are unsure, contact Romar.
- No hacking of game servers.
- No dual-guild enrollments in games we participate in. Any game we do not participate in, you may join another guild. If you wish not to be a part of our guild for a particular game your access to that game information will be blocked.
- No intentional player killing( player killing is allowed in player versus player zones, and against marked enemies)
- No sexual comments, innuendo, or sexual references during any chat rooms, forums, IMs, mailing lists, or IRC chats that involve the games we play in.
- No p2p programs, hacking programs, gambling, or adult information on forums or other guild owned web space.
Rights of Guild Members
- Have fun
- Report harassment to a admin or the guild leader
- Question the guild leader and lend advice
- Offer suggestions to improve the guild
- Access private forums and chat room
- Enjoy a game with interesting people without being harassed or annoyed by immature behavior or constrained by a total commitment to the game.
- Play as you want. Frequent participation is encouraged but our real lives are more important. Guild events can be seen as opportunities to play, do what you wish. There will only be mandatory meetings when there is an emergency or an extremely important announcement. One of the best forms of communication is a mailing list. A forum and chat room will be set up as well for communication purposes and for leisurely talk..
- To leave the guild temporarily for unspecified reasons. Please notify the Guild Leader. If you do have a reason please specify it or if you are uncomfortable doing so, then just say "for undisclosed reasons"
- To leave the guild on good terms. All members who "retire" may apply for re-admission at a later date. All positions within the guild is lost though. You may only quit and reapply once.
Guild Leader- (Romar) Leads and administrates guild. Bodyguard- Defends Leader's honor if he is defeated. Assistant Guild Leader- Handle day to day affairs and tactics. Regional Commanders- Control regional troops and have a council seat. Field Commanders- Command individual divisions. Veteran Soldier- Experienced soldiers who have shown valor in battle. Soldier- Entry rank into guild and is the most important part. Apprentice- Rank of those who are going through trial membership. NOTE: The council is made up of 7 people including the guild leader.
Guild Structure
Admission Guidelines
Prospective members- If you have read the Guild Beliefs, you believe in them, and think you would like to join this guild, please see The guild leader will then contact you through forum private message to ask you questions and get to know you. If you still want to join, then the leader will decide whether you should be an apprentice or not. If he decides you should be an apprentice, then you shall be assigned a guild member to guide you. During your apprenticeship which lasts 30 days you can get to know the guild members and spare with them in games. At anytime during this 30 period you can back out. At the end of 7 days the leader will make his decision and if there is no objection from the council you will be made a member. If there is an objection, then a simple majority vote will be needed to determine if you can become a full member. If accepted you will automatically gain the guild member rights and will take the pledge(which is the motto). If you are not accepted the guild leader will decide two things (1) If to keep you as an associate and in another 30 days you can reapply.(2) If he doesn't keep you, if you should be allowed to apply again. Punishments
- A breach of the righteous code will only warrant a reminder from the guild leader. The second time a reprimand and a third time will be dealt with on an individual basis.
- Severe breach of gameplay rules(2-5 and 9-10) will result in immediate suspension and a meeting of the council. 3/4 must consent to any ban. It must be unanimous consent to ban someone permanently.
- Less serious offenses of spamming, cursing, and immature behavior will call for a reprimand, then a discussion with the leader, then thirdly it will warrant a suspension
- Constant reprimand and warnings will result in suspension and possible expulsion.
- If expelled you will only be considered for re-admission if you were expelled due to excess reprimands.
- We are all forgiving, but constant rule breaking will not be allowed *All punishments may be based on the individual and are at the discretion of the guild leader and the council.
