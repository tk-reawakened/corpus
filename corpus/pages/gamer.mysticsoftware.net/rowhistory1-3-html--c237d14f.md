# Time

Guild History
"Sir, how much farther until we reach Bordga," asked Kaoi. "Only a few more miles, my friend and then we shall rest. Tis a journey marked by exhaustion." replied Romar. The wind whistled around the small band of soldiers as the fiery afternoon sun gave way to dusk. Horses presently came galloping over the hill in front of the Righteous Order Of Warriors. Dressed in black, these men rode atop their proud stallions. The head of the strange troupe slowed his horse to a gallop in front of Romar, as he ordered, "Halt stranger. Hand over your possessions and then turn and return where you came from. You have entered Lord Zarkan's domain and are not permitted here." Romar forcefully answered, "Thieves do not tell us what to do. Lord Dashon, ruler of this land, has sent us to confront the evil that you represent. You are under arrest." Without relenting, the thieves circled in the three members of the Righteous Order Of Warriors. Romar leaped off his horse, long sword drawn, Artemis placed an arrow in his bow, and Kaoi twirled his knives. Two robbers lunged for Romar, but he rolled to the right and caught one in the chest. He swung at the legs of the second, but missed. Artemis's first arrow met its target and he unleashed a second arrow which brought a second assailant to his knees. Kaoi deftly flung a knife into a thief's neck and danced around another until he plunged a knife into his[thief's] heart. Romar switched blows with his enemy until using his speed caught the man off balance and promptly sliced him. From over the hill came more of the thieves, seeing they were overwhelmed the Righteous Order Of Warriors stood back to back. The reinforcements rode at the Righteous Order Of Warriors, but couldn't break them. Men clad in green came running from behind the action and were soon attacking the robbers. Surprised at the assistance, Romar let his guard down for a second to catch a blow in the arm. Screaming in pain, he went into a furry and unleashed a devastating combination of blocks, weaves, and thrusts on the thieves. Having routed the thieves, the Righteous Order Of Warriors met with their saviors. The mysterious men asked, "Good sir, how are you and your men?" Romar confidently replied, "They are tired and I just have a flesh wound, we should be fine. We are eternally in your debt. Who are you?" The leader replied, "We are a part of the resistance to Lord Zarkan and we pledge allegiance to the fair Dashon." "This is good to hear for we the, Righteous Order Of Warriors are recruiting men for our ranks to protect Dashon and Codesh from the evils of Lord Zarkan." offered Artemis. Romar added, "We would be honored to have all of you in our ranks, let us find a place to discuss what must happen." The leader replied, "We may go to the second floor of the Divan Inn in Bordga where we can talk in peace." That night the Righteous Code was drawn up and the Righteous Order Of Warriors were officially formed to protect and serve Dashon, Lord of Codesh and all fair kingdoms.* In the land of Garhim the forces of good and evil clash in an epic battle as control of the powerful kingdom of Codesh is up for grabs. Dashon, the fair prince, of Codesh upheld justice throughout all of Garhim. Lord Zarkan of Tetomias envied the power of Codesh. While attacking Dashon's western flank, the sly Lord Zarkan is able to slip through. His army of mercenaries sought revenge for the justice Dashon had brought upon them. Dashon was becoming less powerful, by the day as his commanders began to question him and why they should fight this fight. Needing a valiant force of soldiers to combat the growing evil, Prince Dashon called on the young commander Romar. Romar the Righteous Paladin heeded his master's call and formed a group of men to fight the minions of evil, thus began the adventures of the Righteous Order of Warriors...
