# This game is suitable for all ages

DustBall
*This game is suitable for all ages*
![](dustball/screen2.jpg)
Game Designer: Sophia Y. Hsu
Time in Production: May 2001~ Aug 2001
Current Status: (6 MB) [Demo](http://www.godivaalpha.com/games/dustball_demo.zip)
![](dustball/screen1.jpg)
*The Story*
In this game, you play the role of DustBall... A cat who has been throw out of comfortable homes due to his ability to speak. When a peasant boy name James took DustBall in, the clever little cat is determined to somehow repay James... whether he likes it or not.
![](dustball/screen5.jpg)
*Demo Features*
-1/8 of the story
-Original Graphics
-A Maze
-Side Quests
*Awards*
DustBall (demo) is the winner of the 2001 graphic and public choice award hosted by the Toolkit Game Review.
