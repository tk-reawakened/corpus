# About Game Studio 43

I first came across the in Dec 2000 out of boredom. As an art student I was impressed with the toolkit's ability to use custom graphics and liked the idea that I could create my own game without having to study complicated programming. Soon I was hooked by the program and spent three months just learning how to do all the basic codes. In the following summer(2001), I started my first serious toolkit game: DustBall. **The Software that started it all:** [RPG toolkit](http://www.rpgtoolkit.com)
**The Birth of the Studio:** Game Studio 43 was born on the 11th of July, 2000. It all started when I dragged my brother (Nickname: ButterEvil ) into starting a website for our toolkit games, and for fun we each design a cartoon character to be put on the website as the representational image of us. Although my brother later dropped out of the toolkit game making business, I grew attached to his character (just look at all the comics I did!) and decided to keep him as a mascot.
![](history/resource.jpg)
**Site Changes:** Since its opening, Game Studio 43 went through many different site designs... and quite a few different hosts as well. The first version of Game Studio 43 never made it to see the public, because Tripod accidentally deleted my account. (they restored it two weeks later, but I was too touchy to even consider using their services again) Hypermart has too much pop-up ads and TKZone closed down after three months of wonderful service. Eventually I ended up with the current host, Mystic Software, which offers toolkit related sites ad-free web space. (Thanks Spyder)
