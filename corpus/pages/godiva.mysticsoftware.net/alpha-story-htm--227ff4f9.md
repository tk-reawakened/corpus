# Alpha

A dark shadow has spread over the land of Godiva. The ever growing Corisca Military is starting to gain empirical power. The Dark One has employed dark Clerics to create leagues of un-natural creatures to serve as his minions. The Dark Sorceress, the head of these Clerics, has personally seen to the destruction of towns adjacent to Corisca.
The Corisca Military, made up of two branches, the Green Branch, headed by Kable, and the Blue Branch, headed by Sycos, have taken control of the cities too large and valuable to destroy. Supreme Commander Rosk, who has controlled the Corisca Military since it was first formed, personally sees to the torture of the rulers who had resisted the Corisca Empire.
Your town of Eisle has been lucky enough to survive the constant attacks by the new creatures in the land, and your location is far enough away from Corisca to worry about the armies destrying it.
But people are dying. Eisle cannot hold out much longer, and eventually the armies of Corisca will destroy it, if the creatures don't first. You and your life-long friend set out to eliminate the closest source of the creatures, and perhaps find the answers to questions unknown to you at the time.
