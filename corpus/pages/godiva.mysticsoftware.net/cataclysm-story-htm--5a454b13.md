# Alpha

Ten years after the destruction of Godiva, the few remaining humans fight for their lives. As long as your village can hold out, there is always hope. Your best friends father discovers a small cavern a few miles from your village while setting traps for your enemies. In the cavern he finds a book that tells the tale of a hero who fought the Dark One in the great war 10 years earlier. Not many still remain that remember those years, but your companions father does. He also remembers the great weapons that can be used to defeat the Dark One. The book may somehow unveil the location of the famed weapons, and the book instills hope in your entire village.
