# Alpha

The Dark Clerics have been defeated, and now it is time for the final battle against The Dark One. 100 years after the Great Battle, Godiva is once again prospering, but slowly. A shadow still lingers over the entire planet. The famed weapons of Godiva have all been recovered, and the armies have taken positions around all the Towers of Darkness, Exodus awaits you...
