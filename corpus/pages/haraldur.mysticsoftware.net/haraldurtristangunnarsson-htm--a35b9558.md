Character leaves out of the city and comes across some Mauler soldiers and we have first battle of game. Halli has a first aid kit ( with 20 bandages only), a dagger, an AK47 with 7,000 bullets, anti-radiation suit, boots, helmet and bullet proof vest, all camouflaged. Battle system is turn based; the faster you are the more turns you get in comparison to enemies. The options are: Gun, Hand-to-hand, First aid kit and Run. Under Gun the options are Fight and Special. Under Fight you have a choice between Normal, Strong and Devastating. The amount of bullets used is random, but lessens at higher skill levels. At first Normal uses about 100 bullets, Strong 200 and Devastating 500. A miss uses about double than that for a hit e.g. 190-210 for a Normal miss. Each bullet goes for a random body part, a head shot being an instant kill and a leg shot being not very damaging. At higher skill levels bullet usage lessens as more bullets hit the target or hit more damaging area of the target. Under Gun under Special you get special gun attacks, such as Head Shot which can do more damage, but they use more bullets as the head is a smaller target, but you are aiming all your bullets for the head. You are more likely to miss also. It is not a good idea to use Specials early in the game as you are very likely to miss. Under Hand-to-hand you get Fight and Special. Under Fight you get the same options as Gun but the options use the same amount, but of Stamina. Under Special you get options like Behead, which is similar to headshot. A hand weapon also parries Hand-to-hand attacks from enemies so is armour against Hand-to-hand only. Armour makes attacks less likely to hit and sometimes reduce the effect of an attack. Under First aid kit you get various options, but at the beginning there is only bandage, which increases the speed of healing, and uses 1 bandage. HP regenerates at a random rate (based on the amount of turns in battle or steps on a board) which depends on where the injuries are and your healing skill, and whether you have used a bandage or some other thing from the First aid kit or not. Stamina also regenerates and regeneration is increased by the consumption of food, and the rate is also random and is abased on how much stamina you have left, if you have a lot of stamina left then you regain it quickly, at low levels you regenerate Stamina slowly. Sleeping in a Hotel or in a tent also regenerates HP and Stamina, Hotel regenerates completely and a tent the amount you normally would buy faster, but still random. After a battle you pick up everything that the enemy had which is useful, guns, bullets, armour, food and First aid equipment and, of course, money. After the battle you get a certain amount of experience points which you spend either on HP, Healing, Stamina, Resting ( basically how effectively you regenerate Stamina), Shooting, Hand-to-hand, Parrying (only used when attacked by Hand-to-hand attack) and Dodging (against all attacks).
