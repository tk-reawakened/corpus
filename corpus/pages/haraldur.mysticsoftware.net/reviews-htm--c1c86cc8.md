6 Wizards:
crappy graphics, bad Mwin, nasty menu, redeeming feature= a mini game, of a sort, default battle system, very little story build up, almost non-existent story, music OK maybe ripped, you can just get items from a castle, sudden events with no biuld up eg. asked to help people without being told what is wrong. In short a crappy game I could not be bothered to play to the end. 1/10.
