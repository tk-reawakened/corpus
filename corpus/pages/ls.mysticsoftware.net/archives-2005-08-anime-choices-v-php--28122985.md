# Anime choices vary so much

August 2005 - 21:42
It's very interesting what can get me into anime. In most cases, I'll start watching a series solely based on character design, without knowing a single thing about the story or anything (and usually, it pleases me enough). There are numerous anime that have done that to me, especially lately.
First series that I can immediately recall is Mahoraba ~Heartful Days~. While the fansubs are still incomplete, the series was good enough in an of itself to keep me going too, so I get two prizes: 1) Wonderful character design, Kozue-chan kawaii desu!!! -- 2) Nice, slice of life, stories, with the typical love interest building. -- Well, searching in retrospect, it seems that the fansubs are caught up to the end, time to download them.
Next in line is Izumo: Takeki Tsurugi no Senki. This was purely based on character design, a single character alone actually. Sakuya (again, I guess I should have my 'kawaii desu'). But watching it, I started to developing a liking for most all the characters. I've got scans, just need to make some wallpapers. The story is simple, but as it stands, once I started watching it, I've gotta finish it.
This brings me up to date. I was wandering my most common wallpaper supplier - minitokyo ( ). And with such a good content system, they have it listing most recent wallpapers. A few catch my eye, obviously, and I download them, completely ignoring the fact that I haven't seen the series. I start going back again today, caught another one, decided, in a "what the hell" situation, to do my wallpaper raid, and download the series involved. Of course, i've gotta clear up some space before I start downloading them, I'm sure I'll be quite satisfied with the series involved. Those being: Da Capo (and it's Second Season), and Iriya no Sora, UFO no Natsu. <http://www.minitokyo.net/>
Actually, thinking in retrospect after posting - I've still got three series I did this too again as well. Sakura Taisen happened through character design, from the manga first, though the story works well with me now too. Shuffle! is another character design one, I just didn't have the opportunity to start watching it too much. Then there's Kidou Shinsengumi Moeyo Ken TV. All of it's character design, and of course, it's all female character design!
Even more retrospect - Futakoi, Futakoi Alternative (Shirogane twins!), anything by Key (Air, Kanon, Clannad [though not an anime yet]), Fruits Basket (and story here), and too much more than I can think of, this worries me!
On a completely unrelated note, my collection of Otakon 2005 memories is still in processing. I'll post as soon as I finish, so until then, ja ne!
