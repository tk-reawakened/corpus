# first

So...uh...yea...I went to Otakon 2005....now let's see....uh
August 2005 - 22:13
Everybody surely has reasons for going to anime conventions...better yet, everybody has reasons for travelling 3 hours (or more as the case may be) for one. Mine are...simple and few (only 5.....though 1 should've sufficed...) 1) What can I say...I'm an anime fan... (note to self: watch the ...'s) 2) It's only 3 hours away...long but easy drive. 3) The Megatokyo Panel - Simply and to the point, it's not my time seeing Fred (refer to daily comments) 4) The Megagear Booth - Simple: Sketch + Autograph = wai~~~!! Plus - Fred = Cool Guy 5) Oh, and there's meeting people -- Of course there's meeting Fred, but I can't let this one slide out from under me. One major goal was to meet Brenton "Walker-san" Dinsmore, a friend of mine whom I've known for 6 years online.
***first***
--Now, I'm that I've forgotten something. *sigh* Oh well, on with the show. **positive**
Okay, I guess I should start at the beginning...(no, not my life!)...of the trip.
It all starts here -- Thursday, 18 August, 2005 - The day I leave! So, we ultimately left around 15:00, of course, we were kinda delayed period by the fact we decided to eat before leaving. Once we got on I-78, we hit some traffic that pulled us behind about 30-45 minutes. But once we got past it, it was clear sailing. A little traffic on I-83 outside of Harrisburg, but no big deal, managed to average a nice 75 MPH for the length of the trip, got us to Baltimore around 18:00-18:30.
I was a little surprised to see my brother's apartment when we got there, didn't seem as small as it was made out to be (but it was still a little cramped for 3 people to be staying.) We sat around for a little bit, then around 19:00, we left so I could go pick up my badge and info packet.
The line wasn't bad when I got there, and actually moved fairly fast, but those who went earlier know how bad it was (wrapping around twice....da~mn.) Halfway through, my Brenton calls me, of course, being the first time he called, he played the "can you guess who it is?" game. As the line progressed, we managed to find each other. Things were a tad surprising here, I mean, it's not often that you find somebody whom you've known online for 6 years, and actually meet them in person. I go inside, pick up my stuff, and come back out. We (Brenton, his friend Mike, and I) just hung out for about 15 minutes before I left to go have dinner with my mom and my brother.
The dinner for the day was Pizzeria Uno, and considering we had a rather large lunch earlier, I wasn't very hungry. The service was a little swamped, and the food was about par. Oh well, it topped me off, and I went back to the apartment, watched a little TV, and had one bad night of sleeping. **Remind me never to sleep on an air mattress.**
--Friday, 19 August, 2005 - First Day of Convention After mishaps galore, forgetting the badge at the apartment, then my cell phone, and making a long, roundabout trip back to the apartment to get it, I finally got to the convention center around 9:45. Roughly 15 minutes before I was to meetup with Brenton and Mike. I waited until about 10:15 and dubbed them a no-show. Started wandering, then found them about a half-hour later.
Our main goal for the day was really getting into the dealer's room, but we had time to kill. We watched some Urusei Yatsura TV that was in video (redacted: address). Following that, we found the line for the dealer's room (or at least, what we thought was the line) and waited to go in. Didn't take too long, and we managed to get in. First thing we did - get sketches from Fred. ^ ^ - The line was rather short then, so we didn't have long to wait, and it went quite smoothly. Brenton and I both got Haibane Kimiko sketches (mine is now framed), and Mike got himself a Boo sketch. Of course, we couldn't just end it there, so we went around, got ourselves some other loot. I got myself a shirt, lanyard, and a Kimiko poster, though there's more to buy when I get more money. The others got generally the same thing. .
Having settled after this, we started wandering around the dealer's room itself, and managed to lose Mike for a little bit. We found him later though, after Brenton had bought some stuff (I was waiting patiently to find something very specific.) Then we passed by a booth that had that one thing - a copy of Sakura Taisen (the game itself). The only downside is, now I gotta find a Sega Saturn, but I can live for a while.
That purchase also caused me to lose Brenton and Mike, who apparently vanished in a tightly confined booth area, lost from my site. So, around 14:00, I wandered a little more around the dealer's room, then decided to go check out the gaming room. There I find Will. I followed him around a little bit, watch him almost win a round of Super Smash Bros. Melee, not get picked for the tournament, and play some DDR. I quickly snuck out for a second to buy myself a wooden practice sword, like everybody else.
We then went off to get dinner. He was looking for seafood specifically (well, Phillip's Restaurant), but most of the places were either too crowded (the Cheesecake Factory) or too expensive (Philip's Restaurant, Buffet, etc). He managed to find a fastfood seafood place in the food court of the Light St. Pavilion. I just ate chinese. After eating, he went back to his hotel for a little bit, and I wandered around, listened to a little karaoke, then back to the apartment. My night was rounded out with some TV, and then sleep.
--Saturday, 20 August, 2005 - Second Day of Convention Saturday started out with the usual, wandering. Brenton and I were supposed to meetup around 11:00, but Mike wanted to go to the Inuyasha panel. I went into the dealer's room for a few moments, found myself a copy of Sakura Taisen 2, not that it should've been a surprise. After that purchase, I found out that they were in line for the Inuyasha panel, and went to meetup with them. Didn't take long to find them, the line wasn't long at that time, not that it got VERY long to begin with.
We sat for the half-hour or so in the panel, which was fan-run. There were two special guests there, first was Mari Morimoto, the translator for the series (first translation), and then there was Seiji Horibuchi, executive producer for the dub. Both were able to provide insights on some of the cultural and mythological aspects of Inuyasha, as well as outlining the extent of the work going into localization. So that part of it was interesting. The fan-based panel (outside of the special guests) shared their stories of how they got into Inuyasha, and their thoughts on the series. Again, interesting to some extent. Having followed the series myself, and going through fansubs and scanlations, there were a few things I disagreed with (as well as Mike, who had to bite his tongue a couple of times.) In all, it was fairly interesting, ending with Seiji's advance showing of 15 minutes from Inuyasha - Movie 3.
After the panel, it was back to the dealer's room. This time we managed not to lose each other, and wandered for quite a while, killing some time before Fred's Megatokyo Panel. Before leaving, I managed to spend some more money, resulting in a catalog from 2001 featuring Inuyasha, Fruits Basket, Sakura Taisen, and more -- good for some scanning. I also purchased the Sakura Taisen 2 Artbook, and a shitajiki (pencil-board). This all set me back about $50, which left me $23 for food each day, or maybe a little more, considering we did run out for something to eat before the Megatokyo Panel. As far as lunch is concerned - the best burgers in the area are in the Pratt St. Pavilion, upper level. Mike and I had our burgers and fries, Brenton just ate Koala cookies. Curse him.
Lunch is settled, we return to the convention, back to the dealer's room, quick wandering around, then back up to get us a nice spot in line for the panel. The line was pretty long, and got longer after we arrived. Took about a half-hour for us to start filing into the hall, but we got in, that's all that mattered. In all, after everybody got in, we filled about half the hall, maybe a little more.
The show got off to an interesting start (even before it started) with Shaggy's musical selections (which were fairly entertaining to me). Of course, people have to poke fun at Fred, so the show opened with the guys from Little Gamer's reading off a list of things to improve Megatokyo. Many were greeted with the supply of laughter and applause from the audience, including the most commonly used idea - updates. Between "more updates" and "more updates on time," there was more than enough amusement at the expense of Fred. Then there's the applause after people notice Fred walking up to guys, kinda in the "yea yea, you've had enough makin fun of me" pose.
Now the show really started here. It was simple enough, so it get's a pointed summation: - Fred covered Megatokyo's status as manga, including jokes on the OEL Manga title, which needs to be renamed. - He covered his trip to Japan back in 2004, and showed pictures related to many familiar scenes in Megatokyo, effectively showing their origins, and general accuracy (in positioning at least.) - He made everybody's favorite announcement - Boo plushies!! (That squeek!) - He had his requisite brief history. - And of course, he finished, calling the show a failure, as per the usual.
Needless to say, it wasn't a total failure, but then again, it's also Fred's nature.
After the panel, there was a group cosplay picture outside of the main hall. Being there, with my oh so shitty camera, I've got a supply of pictures that need enhancement. I also managed to get my own personal picture with Fred (as well as Brenton and Mike, two birds and one stone ring a bell, ne?) -- Out of all the pictures I have, this is one I probably, well, definitely won't post, after reading how Fred and Sarah prefer to have some privacy in regard to their "RL visages."
This is where I parted company with Brenton and Mike for the rest of the con. Gotta have dinner. Went to the Cheesecake Factory with my mom. Good food, reasonable prices. We then went to the Barnes & Noble down the street. All I picked up there was normal journal, which I should have a copy of this written in it. Went back to the apartment and slept.
--Sunday, 21 August, 2005 - Third and Final Day of Convention This was a straightforward day for me. I woke up around 10:00, showered, and went to the convention. I wandered for a few minutes, then got in line for the Kumiko Kato concert. Getting there early resulted in getting a decent seat. Once we got in and settled, she came out on stage. *Cue typical fanboy shouts.* She did a number of selections, all good in their own right. While it's not exactly my favorite kind of music, I did find one or two that I should find for my music collection. After the concert, I went to Subway for lunch, then back to the apartment. By 13:00 Brenton and Mike had left to return home, with a nice 10-11 hour drive ahead of them. Though even the night before, I knew I wouldn't be seeing them today.
After returning to the apartment, I helped out installing some blinds, packed up, and came home. Again, there was traffic, but this time on I-83 outside of Baltimore, for the same reasons as on I-78 - construction. The drive home was a little more difficult insofar as I made a one turn mistake, so we had to turn around, but manage to pick up I-83 again, then the usual I-81 and I-78 to home.
In all, it was a very good experience, and I intend to go again next year. Hopefully I'll have a working laptop so I can post from the convention. It was a little overwhelming for a first timer, but it was a good experience nonetheless. I met, in some form or another, the goals I set for myself, so I'm satisfied. Next year I'll definitely have more money to blow, but I still have no plans to buy any $500+ Anime cels, no matter how beautiful they may be!
This post is a collected summary of multiple days work, so I hope I can convince some of you readers to go to Otakon at some point. Finally posted, so, until my next post, ja ne!
