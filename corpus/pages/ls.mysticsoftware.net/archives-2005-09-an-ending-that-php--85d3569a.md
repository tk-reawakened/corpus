# barely

An ending that, sadly, fits...
September 2005 - 18:57
As stated in a previous blog, one of my "what the hell" decisions of anime to download recently was Iriya no Sora, UFO no Natsu. Also stated in that same post was my optimism that I would be satisfied with it. Now, having completed watching it, I can quite easily compare my thoughts then to my thoughts now.
1) Yes, I did find it satisfying, even if only six episodes long. Again, for numerous reasons, those being the interesting story, and the character designs. 2) I didn't quite want it to end the way it did, but nonetheless, I can live with it because it does provide closure (while still leaving available for fanfics.) That, and it wasn't like the ambiguous, bittersweet ending of Kono Minikuku mo Utsukushii Sekai - while livable, it was quite annoying.
*barely*
The progression of the series is simple, but well paced enough to make it easy to follow. The first two episodes served to introduce the characters, and keep a light atmosphere around. The second two episodes, three and four, served to advance the plot, adding in mystery, and a bit of darkness. Finally, episodes five and six concluded the story, with the usual plot twist, as well as it's share of darkness.
It all works together, and makes it one of those series that was a worthwhile download for me. To anybody, I would recommend it as a definite watch. With that, I yawn, and go off to do other things.
March 2006
