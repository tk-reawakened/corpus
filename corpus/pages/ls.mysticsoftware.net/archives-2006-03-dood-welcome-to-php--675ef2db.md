# Dood, welcome to chaos - Invitation to the Makai

March 2006 - 21:07
Comments | | Posted in: , [0](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2006/03/dood_welcome_to.php#comments) [Post a Comment](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2006/03/dood_welcome_to.php#comments) [Anime](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/anime/) [Makai Senki Disgaea](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/anime/makai_senki_disgaea/)
That should be enough, ne? Okay okay, the rest are in my extended entry.
I had originally intended to have my first official anime blog post be episode 1 of REC, but after a friend sent me this, I've gotta let the readers have a laugh! Based on the game, it looks to be good so far, but it has the extreme potential to be very sucky. So, a few images later, and a preview, let's have fun!
Our preview opens with Etna trying to wake Laharl up from his 2 year "nap." She attempts various methods, from hammers and spears, to jackhammers and stinky socks, to heavy artillery. Then it proceeds to the fight between Vye- ahem...Mid-Boss and Laharl, with ample humor. Furthering along, we get a few scenes of Laharl fighting and Etna calling her Prinny squad. It's Prinny dood!
Enter Flonne, our would-be assassin from heaven. Oh, and her bow and pink heart arrow, and I suppose the requisite mahou shoujo phrase. On we go to our visitors from Earth, and the Prinny Raid attack. Dood! More mayhem involves Laharl handgrabbing a large demon's sword, and returning the favor with an overkill attack.
Our final segment is the actual advertisement. Each character (Etna, Flonne, and Laharl) tries to squeeze their screen time claiming they're the star of the show. Little do they know, it's me!!! (Okay....maybe I can dream....)
From the preview, the series looks to be total mayhem, with a good share of action. Of course, we won't know anything until it airs this April, but it is definitely something I look forward too. Even the CG doesn't look too CG-ish as it does in a few other series. Sure, you notice it, just by the virtue of how it's moving, but it looks more in tune with the actual animation. As I said before, it does have the potential to be either really great, or really sucky. The only way it could really suck is if the story and humor actually differ from the game, hopefully, they won't.
That reminds me, I have to finish playing the game!
