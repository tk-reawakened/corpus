# Every year, 7 masters and 7 servants gather to....PARRTAAAY! (Or not...) - Fate/Stay Night 1

March 2006 - 23:01
Comments | | Posted in: , [0](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2006/03/every_year_seve.php#comments) [Post a Comment](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2006/03/every_year_seve.php#comments) [Anime](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/anime/) [Fate/Stay Night](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/anime/fatestay_night/)
Gyahh! Gomen, I had meant to do this last night, but, I threw off my schedule. Well, my personal schedule at least. One of my lovely methods that help me to organize things is my note sheet. Sadly, or brilliantly, or pathetically, I print one up for each episode. I needlessly complicate simple tasks, just my nature. So, I have note sheets for each episode, and I made one up for Fate/Stay Night (F/SN) 1 last night, but I didn't write an entry. What's it matter, I'm writing it now, so without delay, I bring you the important blow-by-blow of F/SN 1.
Disclaimer: Screenshots are not on the blow-by-blow, they are what they are.
Yea, I differed in structure a bit, and I'll continue a little more.
24 screenshots! I keep increasing as I go...I'll need my own host soon.
Onto the blow-by-blow!
Okay, so our first episode opens with a fight, that ends up destroying Fuyuki City, 10 years ago. A very intense fight with a vagueness about it. Interesting way to hook the watcher I think.
10 years later, we get a girl chanting a magic spell. She finishes, there's a crash, and there's a guy in her house. We learn that she's one of the seven masters.
And we move onto the background behind the Holy Grail War, the main event throught the series. Every few decades, seven masters are chosen to compete in a war for the Holy Grail. It is said that whomever obtains the grail shall be granted one wish. These seven masters are magicians, skilled in sorcery. They each summon a servant of a specific class. Each class has it's own specialty as they fight between each other to emerge victorious. F/SN is the story of the current Holy Grail War. Interesting enough to capture the interest of most fantasy followers.
The OP to F/SN is 'disillusion', sung by Sachi Tainaka. It is a remix of the actual game OP, 'This Illusion.' The music took a while to grow on me, but now it is clear to me that it suits the style, tone, and story of F/SN. The animation that goes with it fits quite well, and does a suitable job of giving us an overview of the series and its characters. Musically, the OP is definitely worth a look if you want to add to your music collection.
Back into the series, we return to the scene of Fuyuki City where a young boy is wandering through the rubble. He proceeds to fall down, and is saved but a moment later. The scene shifts to the boy in the hospital, with bandages around his head. All he says is that, "Everyone's dead."
10 years later, we're in a dark building where Sakura walks in to wake up Emiya [Shirou]. It is quite apparent at this point that Sakura helps to care for Shirou. As Shirou tells Sakura that he'll be ready for breakfast in moments, he reminisces about his adoptive father, Emiya Kiritsugu. All we learn at this point is what I just said, that Shirou is adopted. That, and his adoptive had died 5 years earlier.
At the breakfast table, Fujimura-sensei (Fuji-nee) tells Sakura not to spoil Shirou with meals everyday. She also makes a mention that Shirou may end up taking advantage of her. Shirou replies that Fuji-nee is the one taking advantage of the situation, only visiting at breakfast and dinner. Go Fuji-nee!! With that, Fujimura-sensei runs out, screaming she's late and still has to grade tests. Some sensei, ne?
In other news, there have been reports of gas spills in the region. Sakura started helping out Shirou when he got hurt. While Sakura and Shirou stand a crosswalk, police drive by. Interesting day so far.
Upon arriving at school, Shirou is asked to help Issei by repairing his clubs stove. Here we learn of Shirou's magical inklings as he determines the failure in the stove, and repairs it without opening it. Upon returning to the hallway, the girl from the intro walks by Issei and Shirou. Shirou, without voicing anything, gets a common suspicious vibe. Issei states that the girl is Tohsaka Rin. Basically all the male students' idol. Issei sees Shirou looking at her, and voices his dislike of her.
Somebody's late again, it must be Fuji-nee! As she rushes to class, she manages to fall upon entry. The class discusses how to wake her, and they conclude by shouting 'Tiger-sensei,' prompting an amusing scene of Fuji-nee anger. After a quick homeroom, with an announcement about school curfews, we cut to the roof and Rin. Her servant, Archer, is there with her. Rin is interested in locating her enemies, the other six masters. Her and Archer agree to go searching for them.
It is lunch time, and Issei and Shirou spend the time together. Issei mentions that there was a homicide earlier. The police concluded that the weapon used was long, either a spear or a Japanese sword. There are no suspects in the investigation. This all caught Shirou by surprise. Shirou then goes to work, zoned ouit, unaware of other people calling for him.
We now learn of Shirou's dreams to save everybody. His father had told him that by saving one person, there are others whom aren't saved. This is something that Shirou can't accept. The owners where he worked give us a few clues into Shirou's nature. He did both his work and that of another part-timer. It's also made known that he's never refused a favor asked of him. As he walks home, he continues to think of the homicide.
He passes by a girl who calls him onii-chan, and tells him to summon a servant soon, or he'll be killed. This confuses him a little, and he returns home, to eat with Fuji-nee and Sakura. Fujimura makes a few comments on Shirou's nature saying that he's too helpful. Sakura asks if he'd always been like that, to which Fuji-nee replies yes. She also says how he wrote an essay entitled "The Defender of Justice," and that he still believes in it now.
Shirou notes the posters sitting behind Fuji-nee, and asks what they were for. Fuji-nee says she got them for him, even though he doesn't want them. He attempts to hit her with one rolled up, and in a skilled style, she pulls a metal one and hits him on the head. Sakura then gets worried. (She really cares for him.) Then Fujimura and Sakura leave for the night, leaving Shirou to reminisce more on his father. This time, he recalls when his father told him that he was a magician. Shirou states how he admires his father, and sees him as a hero. Shirou lacks magical talent, save for one skill. His father had told him that sorcery breeds conflict, and that it must be hidden from the world. He also says that sorcery is a skill, and to keep practicing so that it can be used to help people. With that, he reiterates how he'll save somebody.
On top of a building stand Rin and Archer. Archer comments on how they should've started their search from there. Rin states that the best way to learn a city is to go through it first. Archer replies that while he can't see to another city, he can count the number of bolts on a bridge fairly far away, surprising Rin. Archer says Rin is a superior master and can only think that he's being tested. End Episode 1.
The ED, Anata ga Ita Mori, by Jukai is rather good. The animation is simple, but the music grabbed me quicker than 'disillusion' did. The animation suits the music well, but as far as fitting into the tone of the series, I'm not sure as yet. Come get me later.
The forecast for the next episode, action with a mix of character interaction.
A rather long entry. My longest to date. Nice to know everything fits in my template well (except for an unexplained horizontal scroll bar.....)
Fate/Stay Night was a series I had been looking forward to for a little while. I haven't played the game, but I have had some interest in it having a general interest in visual novels, and related anime. (Kanon, Air, hopefully a Clannad anime) There are a few aspects of the animation style I had to get used to, but everybody draws things a bit differently, so. Overall, I like it, continue to watch, even if I am 8 episodes behind.
