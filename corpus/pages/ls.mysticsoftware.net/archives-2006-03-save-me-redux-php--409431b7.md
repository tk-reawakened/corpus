# Save me, redux...

March 2006 - 23:50
Okay, I'm still not quite ready yet, coming off two long days of work. As far as adjustments go to this site, I'm thinking about shifting focus a little. Of course, I'll still do my share of anime, but perhaps adding some games to the mix, granted, mostly visual novels. Although...I do have two anime style games on hand at the moment, one of which is going to be an anime series next month. Disgaea, of course.
In a shift like this though, I'm going to have to make a few concessions, although they've been nagging me for a little bit now. The series I've started, but haven't finished catchup on yet, REC and Fate/Stay Night, shall be saved from these concessions. Kashimashi is going to be dropped, without a blogging start from me, so it's not a big loss. HanTsuki, being in a completed series category, I'll save for later on.
One more thing, I'm debating the possibility of changing the blog style from what it is at the moment, an episode summary with a few thoughts, to something more, worthwhile. I won't drop the episode summary, but I will condense them, maintain the screenshots, and call to important moments. I'll probably be putting more than my $0.02 worth into the mix as well. I only debate this because it occurred to me, what's different between almost all of the blogs at the moment? Only the content of the rough thoughts each blogger posts, otherwise, it's a general mold. We've got some screenshots, a good bit of summary, and some thoughts. Hmm, idea for one of those 'editorial' posts....*wanders away in thought*
March 2006
