# So much for playing catchup

March 2006 - 10:59
So, for having nearly an entire month off, I think I'll wait until the new seasons of anime starts before I start getting really into the swing. I'll play catchup in some other off time, and for series that'll last longer, like Fate/Stay Night, I'll probably start with the latest episode with a quick recap of what's happened earlier.
