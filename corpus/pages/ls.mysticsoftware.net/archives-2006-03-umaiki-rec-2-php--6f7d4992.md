# "Umai-ki!" - REC 2

March 2006 - 17:52
Comments | | Posted in: , [0](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2006/03/umaiki_rec_2.php#comments) [Post a Comment](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2006/03/umaiki_rec_2.php#comments) [Anime](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/anime/) [REC](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/anime/rec/)
A lot of screencaps for this episode. I figured I'd try something different and run it all from memory. Good episode, with barely enough Aka-chan.
I decided to take the screencaps as I rewatched the episode, and ended up with a different set of OP screens (a good thing) and a total yield of 36 screencaps. The number just keeps increasing.
Episode 2 starts with Matsumaru walking to work, remembering what had transpired the day before. Upon arriving at work, he bursts full of energy shouting, "WORK IS LIFE!" He rushes pass Tanaka-san, from accounting, only to run back, tell her he's given up, and run back to work. This leaves Tanaka-san rather annoyed. Possibly a 'hard-to-get' situation?
Upon arriving in the office, Matsumaru continues to bustle with energy, cleaning up trash, and still shouting his mantra, "Work is life!" Hatakeda stops him, telling him the section chief wants to speak with him. Matsumaru is worried he did something wrong, and promptly apologizes. The chief tells him that they are doing the commercial for their new snack, Ha, based on his proposal, and that he's to observe the auditions. We get a glimpse of the mascot for the commercial. Half-Cat/Half-Tree. Interesting...
In the reception area, Matsumaru sees many people there for the audition, and looks around for Aka-chan. She shows up a little late, wearing Matsumaru's clothing. Aka-chan states to the room that she's had a sample bag and finished it in a sitting. The rest of the room laughs at her. Matsumaru is asked to tell them about the mascot, and what kind of voice they want. His choice, Audrey Hepburn. The rest of the room laughs at him. *Aka-chan kawaii desu!*
At the audition, Hatakeda comments that Matsumaru should be more agressive. Matsumaru wasn't selected as a judge, even though it's his commercial. After one shot of another seiyuu auditioning, it's now Aka's turn. Upon hearing her audition, Matsumaru becomes a little ecstatic at how perfect her voice is for Nekoki, the mascot. Back in the reception room, Matsumaru looks for Aka-chan, but can't find her.
Matsumaru waits outside of the room where the judges were deliberating, and asks the chief if they had decided on the voice for Nekoki. The voice for Nekoki will be, Shiro.....no, wait....Aka. Matsumaru is elated to learn that. He walks home, saying how lucky he is, and quoting a line, from a Audrey Hepburn movie of course, "Don't reach for the moon child."
Matsumaru returns home to find Aka-chan waiting outside his door. She offers up food as celebration for the both of them. Aka-chan for getting a role, and Matsumaru for getting a commercial made. Aka-chan helps Matsumaru to remember the lines which he had quoted earlier, from . She also says that she needs to stay there a while longer, and that they should keep their relationship a secret from her agency and his company. Matsumaru is quick to make a move on Aka, who quick pushes him away, into a wall. She says that last night had only happened because she was in shock, and that it meant nothing. *Sabrina Fair*
For closing, we get a cute shot of Aka-chan sleeping, and Matsumaru being rather frustrated. Better luck next time. I think I made better timing on this post. REC continues to provide enjoyment, from the amount of humor, to the developing relationship between Aka-chan and Matsumaru. Next episode leaves us with a few shots of Aka-chan outside. But considering I'm catching up, we all know that there's more to the episode.
Maybe I'll continue writing the episodes this way, screencaps as I watch, no notes, write-up shortly afterwards, from memory.
