# J.C. Staff pleases me. Asatte no Houkou - Episode 1

October 2006 - 00:22
Comments | | Posted in: , [3](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2006/10/jc_staff_please.php#comments) [Post a Comment](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2006/10/jc_staff_please.php#comments) [Anime](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/anime/) [Asatte no Houkou](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/anime/asatte_no_houkou/)
I'm a little behind, but that's okay. I seem to have some weird attraction to series done by J.C. Staff. It's as though they know just what I want, for the most part. Other series that I've watched, and enjoyed, knowing full well it's J.C. Staff are Mahoraba ~Heartful Days~, Shakugan no Shana, Zero no Tsukaima, possibly others, but that's not the purpose of this post.
Asatte no Houkou has been one of my 3 definite series for this fall season, that is, one of the 3 series that I can guarantee that I'll keep watching just because of extreme interest. The story, ultimately being the age switching, holds a sort of attraction from me. My mind may be a little screwed up (hey, whose isn't?), considering that I'll go after a series that does this sort of thing. Of course, the minute I heard the basic info on the series, before it aired, I started doing research. Finding out that there is a manga that it's based off of. Of course, I purchased the two available volumes (thank you Kinokuniya Books for having it in a local store!), despite not being able to read it yet. I'm learning, though, there's more to this post.
![Asatte no Houkou - Manga - Volume 2 - Cover](/images/anime/anh-v2-cv.jpg)
Okay, this post shouldn't be about me and everything I did around this, so I'll actually write up the episode now.
This first episode ranks about a medium-high on my scale. If not for the fact that they did the age switch at the very end of the episode. I shouldn't complain though, it'll make me look even weirder. The first episode is actually quite good, and laid back. It's wonderful for me now especially, I feel like having some laid back series to watch. The opening, , is sung by Suara. You should recognize her from the opening to Utawarerumono. The opening moves well, not too fast, and not too slow. It definitely sets a summer tone, which bodes well as we're moving into autumn and winter. Quite good. *Hikari no Kisetsu*
The basic story: Our main character, Iokawa Karada, along with her brother, Iokawa Hiro, lost their parents 4 years ago. Since then, Hiro has been taking care of Karada. This isn't necessarily bad, but Karada feels she's a burden on Hiro-nii. As such, she wants to grow up fast so she's no longer such a burden. Now, let us flip sides.
Nogami Shouko is Hiro's ex-girlfriend. They had gone out for a while, but when Hiro had to step in and take care of Karada, he did not go with Shouko when she left. She never knew he had a sister, and is feeling a mix of emotions after reuniting. Nonetheless, she still wants to start over, and the best way to do that is to be a kid again.
This episode happens to get a few things out of the way. The requisite swimsuit episode is one of them, though I'd expect one later on, especially if they follow the manga more closely than it seems. Yes, there are a few changes from the manga. Though exact details elude me due to lack of understanding a gracious amount of Japanese.
Shouko-san seems to be a mix of personalities. At various moments, she's kind, abrubt, standoffish, and even rude. With later episodes, I'll be interested as to where they take her character development.
In contrast, besides being uber- for most of the episode, Karada is always nice, with a few mixes of preoccupied 'depression' and sadness brought on by Shouko's moment of rudeness. *kawaii*
The age switch occurs at the end of the episode, leaving a bit of a cliffhanger. Older Karada remains , in a sense. With how is used, it tends to lean towards the younger age. Young Shouko though, I can't really say she's extremely or not. I'll grant maybe a little cutesy-ness, but that's it. *kawaii* *kawaii* *kawaii*
The ending definitely sets a laid back, lazy summer tone. by Yuumao may, for some, take one or two times to get used to it, but with J.C. Staff's artwork, and the pacing of the ending, it fits really well. Oh, did I mention that neither the OP nor the ED really (if at all) show anything surrounding the age switch? Nope, not that I can recall. I wonder if that'll get updated now. Well, until the second episode airs, we'll be left in mystery. Ja ne! *Sweet Home Song*
