# My autumn - 2006!

October 2006 - 11:58
Time to step away from the generic anime post and go into the generic season post. Way back when (much earlier this year) when Kanon was announced as a remake by KyoAni, I, though not being the only one, was quite ecstatic. When the release date was determined, this season quickly became the most anticipated. Now, even better yet, when I saw the listing for all the series this season, I became more interested, and did some research. When posted his , I divided them into 3 groups: will not watch, will determine after 3 episodes, and will definitely watch. So, I present to you my breakdown, listing only 2 of those groups. [Omni](http://randomc.animeblogger.net/) [list of the 2006 autumn season](http://randomc.animeblogger.net/2006/09/22/fall-2006-shows/)
In the " " category, there were 3 series: **Will Definitely Watch**
- Kanon 2006
- Asatte no Houkou
- Negima!
I'll say that so far, even only seeing one episode of each, none of them disappoint.
In the 3 episode evaluation category, I have 12 series:
- La Corda D'Oro
- Tokimeki Memorial ~Only Love~
- Lovedol ~Lovely Idol~
- Death Note
- Ghost Hunt
- Busou Renkin
- Yoake Mae Yori Ruri Iro na -Crescent Love-
- Mamoru-kun ni Megami no Shukufuku wo!
- Happiness!
- Sumomomo Momomo
- Gift ~Eternal Rainbow~
- Kekkaishi
A few of these series have not been watched yet, and being that I must go through them raw, probably won't go past the 3-ep lest they get subbed. Two of them were promoted to definite watches after both the raw and subs, they are Tokimeki Memorial ~Only Love~, and Yoake Mae Yori Ruri Iro na -Crescent Love-. Despite others saying that the character designs in TokiMemo are plain, I don't care. I like a little plain every now and then. Yoake Mae Yori Ruri Iro na -Crescent Love- is a character design choice, as well as story now. I present you a that helped in the decision. [figure](/images/anime/yoake-figure.jpg)
Of course, Kekkaishi hasn't aired, and I haven't watched a couple yet, so there's more to anticipate. I can say this though, La Corda D'Oro is likely to fail this 3 episode evaluation.
Maybe I'll write up something else I've watched later, but not now though. Until then, ja ne!
