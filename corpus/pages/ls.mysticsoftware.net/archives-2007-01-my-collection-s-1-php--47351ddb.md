![Rika-chan!](/images/images_2/clock_hantsuki.jpg)
**Rika-chan!**
The last item in my collection isn't something you can easily obtain...sorta. My grandmother gave me a clock whose alarm just wouldn't turn off. So, in boredom, I decided to make a new faceplate for it. The result is as you see. The alarm does indeed turn off, but can be a pain at times. Making the plate was simple enough, scan in the original plate, superimpose the image(s) that I wanted on it, and make minor changes. The hour markers are big blue dots, with the hour written next to them using kanji. The minute markers are smaller, orange dots. Each hour has alternating blue/orange color usage. I'm proud of this one.
