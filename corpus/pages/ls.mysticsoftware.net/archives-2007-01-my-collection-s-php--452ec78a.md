Welcome to the inaugural edition of "My Collection" -- A new series of posts highlighting my merchandise purchases that drain my wallet to barely livable standards. You can expect a lot of merchandise to be highlighted here as my collection is moderately large (by my standards, though nowhere near as large as others), and it will continue to grow.
![My Collection - Shelf One](/images/images_2/my_col_shelf_1.jpg)
**My Collection - Shelf One**
More to follow, click on!
I'd like to start with my latest acquisition, Tsukimiya Ayu!
![The taiyaki thief returns!](/images/images_2/fig_ayu.jpg)
**The taiyaki thief returns!**
I had preordered this from back in November, with a release this month. Well, it just arrived today, prompting me to start this segment. This is a 1/8 scale figure of Ayu running with her bag of taiyaki, while eating. It comes with two backpacks, a regular one, and one with a little angel hanging off it, which'll make sense if you've played Kanon, seen Toei's version to the end, or decide to stick it out to KyoAni's end. This set me back nearly $60 after shipping. Well worth it. [Animaxis](http://www.animaxis.com/)
Next up, Rakka!
![Rakka, I need not say more.](/images/images_2/fig_rakka.jpg)
**Rakka, I need not say more.**
This was my next most recent purchase. I ordered this from . It set me back almost $50 after shipping. Another 1/8 scale figure. This one features Rakka from Haibane Renmei, sitting on a chair as some of her popular artwork. The image should speak for itself. [HobbyLink Japan](http://www.hlj.com)
Next up, Furukawa Nagisa.
![Furukawa Nagisa](/images/images_2/fig_nagisa.jpg)
**Furukawa Nagisa**
Finally, my first figure purchase. Furukawa Nagisa from Key's Clannad. Another 1/8 scale figure, also purchased from . I don't recall the cost at the moment, but I believe it was another $50 setback, not that I care about that too much. She happens to be featured in her Clanyad ears and tail at the moment, but they are removable for a regular appearance. The ears are held in place magnetically, specific to each side (so they will repel if on the wrong side) and the tail is just a squeeze. This is the first figure that started the long, expensive route of figure collecting. I'm very happy. [HobbyLink Japan](http://www.hlj.com)
Next time, I finish up shelf one. I'll also let you in on a little secret, I have two figures preordered, both from , Kannabi no Mikoto (from Air, aka Kanna ^^), and Misuzu (from Air). Just think... [HobbyLink Japan](http://www.hlj.com)
Ah damn you! Mine is on backorder with HLJ. So I probably won't be seeing this for a while.
