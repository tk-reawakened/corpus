# Mata ashita.

June 2007 - 13:26
Comments | | Posted in: , [0](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2007/06/mata_ashita.php#comments) [Post a Comment](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/2007/06/mata_ashita.php#comments) [Life in General](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/life_in_general/) [Site](http://ls.mysticsoftware.net/archives/site/)
I'll keep this short and simple. Obviously I haven't been keeping up with the blogging as I had originally intended, as such, I've come to a few decisions. Before I get there though, I shall mention what leads to what.
First off, I need to make money, and to make money, I need a job. I have a job, and am looking for a better job, but nonetheless, having a job means I lose time from my schedule. Next, in part of that search for a better job, I am in the process of completing a degree. Therefore, that's more time taken out of my schedule for my studies. Thirdly, I've been getting out more lately, hanging around campus and the houses of some friends. As such, there's even more time removed from my schedule, though not as much as the other two. Finally, I am finding myself not really wanting to write strictly about anime, but even more so, anime is a hobby. It is something I watch, and enjoy watching. The notion of blogging about the series, and what not, effectively removes that enjoyment if I have to force myself to write about it.
Given these situations, my decisions are as follows: 1) Effective the time of this post, I will no longer be blogging about anime in the traditional sense. When I do blog, it will most likely be articles that I have an interest in writing. 2) Given the changes in my life circumstances, I will be repurposing this website, and possibly, should I find that I can afford it, be moving off to my own webserver and domain. In repurposing, I will be writing about a broader range of topics, such as games and anime, and more. 3) I may further continue to refine this layout, and redesign the site. I may also change the engine that it runs under from MovableType to WordPress. 4) When the new site is unveiled, the existing writings will be placed under a separate installation, for archival purposes.
With this, I say, Mata Ashita. Until tomorrow.
