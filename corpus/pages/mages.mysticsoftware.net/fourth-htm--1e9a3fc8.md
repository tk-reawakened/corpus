Features
**Custom Battle System**
"The Fourth Dimension" features a in the style of Phantasy Star and RPG Maker 2000. It was designed originally for simplicity, but with possibilities to expand and become more complex. of the game makes full use of this feature! Included within it are: *Custom Battle System* [Demo 2](downloads.htm)
- Critial Hits and Misses!
- Full Special Move support!
- Item support!
- Options to Run, or face the consequences of failing!
- Options to defend and try and withstand attacks!
- Fully integrated Leveling-Up!
- Original (and fairly well-done in my opinion) Battle Sprites and Backgrounds!
- Keyboard-Based, no mouse needed!
- Turn-Based to keep your 'Final Fantasy' fingers at bay!
**Custom Menu System**
