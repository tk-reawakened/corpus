**A breath of fresh air ...**
05:50 pm, September 03, 2003
Ah ... so the new site design is up ... in all it's PHP glory. It's been a long job, and tears and sweat have been shed by the bucket-load. But it's here.
So what has happened over the last 8-9 months since the release of demo 2? Well, funnily enough, not enough. The game's progress has been seriously barred by TK3's unstableness (I need some of it's features) so I'd guess the summer 2003 release date flauntered at the end of the demo is well out of picture. Although strictly nowadays I don't deal in release dates.
So what can you expect? Well, for a while I've been flauntering around of some of the new graphics. There are more, but I'll hold onto them until I get some proper screenshots up. However, once the TK3 gets to the stable stages, then progress I imagine will continue at a fast rate and I'll be updating fairly regularly.
