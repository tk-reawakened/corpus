# Previews

THE CRYSTAL
I promised a lot when I posted in the Preview Thread of the Mystic Software Forums, but I will deliver.
The Crystal is going to be based around two characters: An Elf and a Neko (both unnamed). Obviously, one is male and the other female. You will be able to choose which character you want to play as, and their stories will be COMPLETELY different. This means that I'll have 3 different main files for the game, but who cares? Also, the other character will appear every now and then doing their own thing.
The Battle System is going to be Cursor-Based, with One-Player Two-Enemy Support. It's still not perfected, but when I get it to work 100%, I'll be posting more screenshots. Limit Breaks (or Ki Overflows) will be supported. When you go into Ki Overflow, your "ATTACK" command changes to "KI", which triggers an energy blast that attacks all enemies with an attack power twice as powerful as your normal physical attack. When Magic turns to Sorcery, you can cast 2 spells at once without using MP. Shield will give you a 3-turn barrier which prevents almost everything from hitting you. Of course, recovery spells won't work then. Run turns to teleport so that you don't have to put up with that 1-in-3 chance of escaping. There will be a lot of cool spell animations and stuff. Also, you have to equip spells and items through the menu system (3 items at a time and 2 spells at a time). This screenshot will NOT be in the final game. It was just for testing purposes.
[![Battle System Screenshot](bsscreenshot01.JPG)](bsscreenshot01.JPG)
The Menu System is still in it's infancy. I plan on having a cursor-based menu system with nothing but custom functions...but I'm still with the #InternalMenu code right now. Of course, when the MS is done, I'll either sell it to Squaresoft for one hell of a lot of money, or I'll just post screenshots.
MOST of the music will be done by me. I'll be receiving a little help from a few people (you know who you are), whether they be writing a full piece for me or giving me a throwaway piece. I'm working on the battle theme now, and that will be the first major thing in this section when it's done.
ETERNAL BLADE (Discontinued)
Since I've had a lot of negative feedback and the story kept falling apart, I've discontinued this project. I have also taken the download link off of my site, and I'll get one of the MS admin to remove the game from the server as soon as possible. I'm sorry, but I cannot allow this game to see the light of day ever again.
