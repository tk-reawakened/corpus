# verse one

Distinctly Devout There's God on the face of the water, He bathes and he watches, Us slip on the rocks. When I talk, I have noticed I gesture, You smile politely, And stare at your socks. I feel like I'm squeezing, Hope from humanity, Trying to break into you. Maybe I'm stuffy, It could be I'm candid, Or maybe I'm acting too shrewd. Still I can't rule out, the distinctly devout, Maybe I still resemble the zoo. The timekeeper glances, Up from his headlines, To murmur his orders again. The marchers they shuffle, They dangle and mutter, And the jaws of the wheel cave in. The role of the artist in civilization, Is the same as the sand, As it boils to glass. And afterwards vanity chokeholds yer smile, Am I getting through, Or's my signal gone slack? Your timepiece might fail, As you're in the middle, Of some key expedition one day. But don't chew on your pride, I'll make sure you're famous, For discovering new things to hate. But I can't rule out, the distinctly devout, It could be that we're something God baked. And I guess you're forgiven, For not understanding, The motion of hands on the waves. Or rudely protesting, A marriage of money, Just cos you're in love with the bride. Or just not accepting, For practical reasons, That art and reality twine. And the martyrs they shuffle, They chuckle and mutter, And the jaws of the wheel, Cave in.

January Soul The storm is shouting whispers, Bout the strangers that it's christened, Brought together for the worship, Of its natural religion. On the benches underneath, The overhangs, Of dripping wood. Blowing to the roof beneath, My static gray, One window room. Where I listen to the pigeons, Tell of stories where they've flown, Castles made of statues, And poles for telephones. And I stare around at walls, That I've come to call my home, I dig down through the plaster, Just to try to find a soul. This place just feels as empty, As this plaintive rock and roll. The thunder cracks the candle, And it's audible as fury. The ghosts of all my holidays, Seem to scatter in a hurry, January's fallen out, Of stranger smiles, And sidewalk cheer. Anxious crows are headed down, They murmur style, Is back to fear. And the lightning's shallow dance, Strikes a city to its roots. Urban hearts that glimmered, Now promptly follow suit. As this bitter funeral toll, As the mocking pass of time, As these blankets that're cold, As these windowpanes of grime. As this January soul (x2) Electricity beguiles, The poles for telephones, I must admit I worried, Humanity was gone. But your voice is like a chapel, Made of early morning foam. With notes that reach the rafters, And crush the cobblestones.

Highlights of Blue You turned the radio down, As we pulled out of town. You said, "I need my puppets," "And silhouette sounds." I said, "You're talking in circles," And you shook your head. As the wind was all trumpets, And vacant eyed crowds. You said, "That's just it, I'm tired of graves, And I'm tired of static, Leading processions to drown." I said, "Maybe you're not - " But you cut me off. And the sky was all highlights of blue, And the sky was all blonde. Now I'm coming up fast, After everything's gone. The carpet's made of glass, And your eyes are full of dawn. I'm falling up past, With only the storm, And its quizzical, rhythm-less song. Now the dust turns in reams, With its patented dream, Past the polished old stones, By the cemetery figs. Past the outskirts and the theater, Creaking to steel, With the telephone rows, And its rusted old beams. You said, "That's just it," And you closed your eyes, And I caught specks of gold, Through the clovers in factory steam. I said, "Maybe it's not - " But you cut me off. And the sky was all highlights of blue, And the sky was all blonde. Now the rug went electric, As the radio came on. I listened for the chords, To convince me you were gone. I never heard 'em fall, I only heard the storm, Then civilization shrug, And the windshield respond. And the sky was all highlights of blue, And the sky was all blonde.
