# : Aljo Production's Mighty G Duck

***
If It Weighs the Same as a Duck
Our latest contest attempts to explain just why no one wants to do any contests anymore. It's quite simple really. You have to make a graphical presentation, a sort of screen saver if you will, that has some kind of duck explaining to me, however subtly or directly you want, just why nobody does these contests anymore. You can make it as long or as short as you like. Of course all graphical coding has to be pure RPGCode. The presentation with the spiffiest looks and of course the most clever explanation will win the vaulted prize. And not only will it win the vaulted prize, but your explanation will be immortalized on this page for all to see what they must overcome to have the gumption to enter a contest. Have at it!**
** In Rank:
![](uberbunny.gif) *Duck.prg*** 1. :
Dont_Think_So's Disformed Duck Well, this one just had that certain something about it. The duck was good looking, not the best looking duck out of the entries, but it had a nice unique curve to it. Probably the key to this entry's appeal is that not only did it give one original idea as to why people don't enter contests, but it gave three animated ones you could cycle through at your leisure. Woo-hoo, interactive features! Not too long, not too short, perfectly paced duck zen. The only problem is that sadly it seems a bit bugged and sometimes will give you the reasons in an odd order, and you have to press x to exit. Still very cool, though. And DTS wins the vaulted prize of having the next contest be something to do with an alien attacking, thanks to one of his reasons! (Yes, I realize I changed the prize. It's better this way, trust me. Yes, I also realize I am breaking the parentheses rules).
2. Well, the premise is quite excellent: put *me,* the undispusted master of Zen into the contest entry. This little (hehe) submission used quite a unique and original apporach to original RPGCode drawing with some very clever coding. Sadly though, I thought that the Mighty G Duck could come up with something better than a pit of doom, and ultimately that's what kept this entry just below DTS's. Still something you should check out though, as you'll be very surprised to see what's coming out of #DrawLine and #ColorRGB.![](silverrod.gif) *Gduck.zip*

3. This has the disctinction of being my longest entry and possibly my only entry written by someone on acid at the time. The gfx show part of this entry has the whole trippy Jethro Tull thing going for it, but once you get past the psychadelia you'll see some really impressive RPGCode drawing talent, with wonderful tricks like lattice patterns and manual zooms. Unfortunetly, I timed it, and it takes exactly seventy five years to get to the end with the duck. Now the duck is the best looking pure RPGCode duck of the bunch, but though blaming Vampz was an inventinve and original twist, it would need Vampz's British patience to get that far. But still really, really cool graphics and a nice, silly reason. Really, any one of these top three could've won; they all did quite a spiffy job and the competition was quite close. It fades in and out and in and out and oh my poor head. But interactive features, which are cool! Has the distinction of being the one entry from a person who pointedly didn't care about contests. A very red duck with sadly only a simple #MWin explanation. The explanation had about seventeen different ways of looking at it though, which was nice.![](bronzerod.gif) *Jer1400s_duck.prg*
