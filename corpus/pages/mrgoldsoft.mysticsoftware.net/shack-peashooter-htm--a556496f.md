# : Gothic Mario

***
Pea Shooter
The newest contest spawns from one of you people's clever ideas. And since I can't leave well enough alone, two of you clever people's ideas. The first component of this mystifying challenge is to make a kind of side scroller. If you don't know what a side scroller is, rest assured it does involve the words side and scrolling thereon. Think Mario Brothers or Jazz Jackrabbit or whatever. It can be done with normal TK scrolling, but that's no fun, so do something crazy with the idea. The second part of the contest involves pea shooting. This side scroller has to involve pea shooting in some way. Now go scroll your peas. (There's a sentence one doesn't usually get to use in one's life.) **You can use any kind of gfx you want, but please don't waste all your time making gfx. Ripped gfx are fine.****
** In Rank:
![](uberbunny.gif) *Littleman.zip*** 1. :
Little Man (With A Pea Shooter) Dilligence pays off. This spunky little game impressed me in the same way Martin's Slowball managed to capture the silver award of the original Pong contest. This isn't a fast paced shoot em up side scroller, as the Aljo brothers wisely reasoned such things might not be so easy to craft out of RPGCode. What LittleMan is is a captivating little strategy game that requires thought and cunning to work your way out of the three levels. A crisp side scrolling engine that reminded me of JetMan holds the whole package together. A superb and imaginative effort, and congratulations to the Aljo brothers for their first place award; an award that may mean a bit more than the previous ones simply because of the sheer challenge of this contest! G'job!
2. Grey_Havoc seems to be committed to making all his mini-games/contest entries really hard. Again though, we have a nice pea shooting strategy element armed with a little jumping. A bit of a bogged down scrolling system and a lack of different levels and challenges like Little Man just barely eek this game out of the first place award. Be aware though, either one could've easily gotten it, and also be aware that they are both far better than you out there who didn't submit anything at all you pug nosed slackers.![](silverrod.gif) *Gothmario.prg*
