# Three in a Row

Well, here is my first contest as the new manager of the Shack. Unfortunately, I was unable to attain that certain level of MrG-ishness the previous challenges display that we've all come to love... I did manage to think up a simple yet challenging contest, though. Your task, should you choose to accept it, is to design a one-player tic-tac-toe program. As usual, no outside plugins or graphics are allowed. What's the catch? It has to be . The primary area your program will be judged on (other than if it works, of course) is how difficult the AI is. Extra features (i.e. two-player mode, AI levels) are welcome, but not required. The submission should run in TK2. Good luck! ***hard***
