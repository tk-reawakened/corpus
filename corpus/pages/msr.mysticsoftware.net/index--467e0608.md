- Saturday, May 11, 2002 - 11:48 PM Eastern TimeMichael J. Hill
***Mystic Software (PHP) New Version System***
Mystic Software (PHP) now has a version numbering system. Anything labeled M is a milestone copy. So as of right now, MS(PHP) is at v1.0 M1, Milestone 1. Anything labeled R is a release version. Anything labeled B is a beta copy. Milestones are not always betas. Betas will be few as most new features will be Milestone releases. F labels mean Final Release for that given version. This version number system will help to guide visitors through the development stages. I will commonly use this as a means to let the people know how far I am into construction. As of right now, v1.0 M1 is a very early version.
