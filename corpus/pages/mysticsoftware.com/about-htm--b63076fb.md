# We Bring Solutions to your Company’s needs !

Founded in 1984 by Philip Herzfeld, Mystic Software is a Systems Integrator, and custom software Development and Consulting Company. Although we emphasize leading edge solutions to Information Systems needs, we support many legacy platforms as well.
We have an experienced staff of developers that support Windows, Windows 95 and Windows NT, Data General’s AOS/VS and Unix. We develop, enhance, and maintain applications written in Borland’s Delphi (object Pascal), C, C++, Visual Basic, Java, and COBOL. In addition we create, and support Web Sites and Web development using HTML, Java, Java Script and Active-X for both the Internet and internal Intranets.
We develop on, manage, administrate, and support MS SQL Server, Interbase, Infos II, MS Access, Paradox, and DBASE. We can develop, maintain, and enhance SQL Triggers and Stored Procedures in addition to fine tuning the performance of those databases.
Our staff is also well versed with integrating various Office Suites, including Microsoft’s Office Suite (Word, Excel, Access, etc.) and Corel’s Office Suite (WordPerfect, Quattro Pro, Paradox, etc.)
In addition, we have several trained developers available to help integrate or extend various mid-range accounting packages, including Great Plains’ Dynamics, and Platinum Software’s SQL Platinum.
Our staff boasts extensive experience with applications for the financial industries, insurance industries, medical fields, production control and distribution.
