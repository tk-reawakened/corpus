Welcome
Advanced Pension Programs is The premier source for the high income/high net worth professional seeking income tax deferral and asset protection strategies.
**What We Do**
Design, implement, and administer tax deferred pension/profit sharing plans for wealth accumulation. We also fund the plans with investments and provide additional liquidity to one's estate with life insurance products.
**How We Do It**
Utilizing comprehensive knowledge of the complex pension and profit sharing plan rules, we creatively design plans that provide the affluent high income taxpayer with the largest & safest tax deductions possible to create added wealth and asset protection.
**Who We Serve**
The high income(over $200,000), high net worth(over $2,000,000) professional( doctor, dentist, lawyer, etc.), self employed or business owner, and executive employee.
