Purchase Property with a Plan
Real Estate is the absolute best investment for pension plans.
Real Estate is protected from liability, and all income would be deferred until age 70 1/2.
Title Over Existing Property
While Advanced Pension Programs can help you purchase new property with your pension plan that we create, we can also help you title over your existing property. This gives you great asset protection, as well allows you to defer tax on property you already own.
Advantages of Owning Real Estate Using 401(k) Plans
- All capital gain taxes from the sale of the real estate can be avoided while the real estate is owned by the plan.
- All income from all sources can escape taxes until age 70 1/2 , and even then, can be stretched out over one's lifetime.
- All mortgage payments (principal and interest) can bve paid with tax deductible dollars, substantially lowering the cost of a monthly note by as much as 40% (based on one's tax bracket).
- New parcels of realestate can be purchased and financed with tax deductible dollars.
- All real estate owned by the plan can be completely ***protected from the claims of creditors.***
- Property can be easily transferred to children without selling or gifting the property. (Simply make them participants in the 401(k) plan).
- If the plan is properly insured, it can provide enough liquidity to pay for income taxes starting at retirement age, or age 70 1/2 and also estate taxes.
