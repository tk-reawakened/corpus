# 8-Step Quote Worksheet

![](images/Software.jpg)
![](images/PlanningYourWebSite.gif)
To survive in the Network Economy businesses must embrace the World Wide Web and make the Internet, Intranets and Extranets an integral part of their e-business strategy. Mystic Software is an e-business strategic planning and consulting firm helping clients develop new strategies and improve their business processes through Web-based technologies.
Mystic Software has been a strategic partner to a diverse range of clients, from traditional corporations to entrepreneurial web-based businesses.
Our consulting team is comprised of visionary, creative and technical web experts. We will work closely with you to accelerate the process of planning, creating and deploying powerful and efficient web-centric information technology solutions.
Mystic Software has developed a proven process to plan, build, host, manage and promote your e-business strategy. Our 8-Step process will provide upper management tracking and strategic input options while carefully guiding middle management and staff with the organizational structuring, infrastructure planning, content assembly, website maintenance, interactive management, website promotion, e-commerce integration and performance analysis.
**8-Step Quote Worksheet**
We will develop a quote based on the 8-Step process, the 8-step Quote Worksheet is divided into 8 sections representing each of the 8 steps, each of the steps have line items which represent specific actions and deliverables within a specific step. A Mystic Software WWW strategic planner will produce a 8-Step Quote Worksheet based on initial discovery meetings
**Project Tracking**
Each of the individual items in the 8-step Quote worksheet represents a specific assignment or a series of assignments. The Project Manager will send these as assignments to the appropriate team member. Each member responsible for each line item completes the task, they will record notes and time spent on that task As the project progresses, the Project Manager will track the actual time required to the quoted time for each line item and provide upper management with a report and recommendations for adjustment if there are any large deviations.
