> Problem:
> Factoring/intercompany transfers of complex bulk transactions semimonthly between company databases in Epicor Platinum were a manual process requiring several man days to complete and allowing too much room for error. Existing supplied aging reports did not have the capability to age intercompany transactions.
>
> ** Solution:
> Mystic Software designed and implemented a series of custom system enhancements that reduced the factoring process to less than an hour, regardless of transaction volume. Custom aging reports that spanned multiple companies were created, reducing the manual process of combining aging reports from hours to minutes.
>
> **Results:**
> Clients experienced a significant increase in productivity and a decrease in accounting costs across the board.
