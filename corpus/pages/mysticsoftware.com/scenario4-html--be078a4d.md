> Problem:
> The client needed to generate and distribute on a monthly basis approximately 1,000 marketing and resource useage analysis reports specific to each of its customers. Each customer's reports contained tabular data as well as graphs and charts. The existing system required 40 to 60 hours to complete the report generation, after which the hard copy reports had to be postal mailed to each account representative.
>
> ** Solution:
> Mystic Software designed and implemented a report generation tool, leveraging Microsoft Excel, MS SQL Server, and a custom developed front-end 'driver' application dynamically configured using report template files and data warehousing.
>
> **Results:**
> The client was able to generate all of the required reports in less than three hours (down from 40 to 60) and have them ready for automatic electronic ditribution. The reports were based on layout templates that were easily modified to add additional reports or 'tweak' their appearance.
