Work not going bad...
**Day 3, month 8, year 2004**
I made some progress on SL. Propably I will be able to put up more "complete" demo one day, just not now yet! I got Paint Shop Pro 7.1 (it fell from the sky) and now you can expect slightly better elements of graphics in game. I already used it to make catherine's wheel in Grand Cathedral of Campana.
Current progress on SLmainly includes new in-battle item system, Nephilim TK full screen logo before game loads, upgrades to some areas (Kodra forest has new elements added) and propably I'll work on graphical BS layout and something more in closer time.
I plan on adding third "quest" to Kodra town area, of course as all "quests" there it will be pretty simple and involve "going there, slaying this and getting that". This is all, have fun. And admire the new Satarel's Legacy logo I put up above, the new lame background and site's news archive.
