Want to see your artwork here? You can [e-mail me](contact.htm) a Dark Age related artistic creation, and I'll put it up here for all to see. Here are the guidelines:
- Any image format is fine (I will most likely convert it to JPG format, though).
- Try to keep file size under 500 KB (should be easy if you don't send a BMP).
- Include the title of the work in the e-mail.
- Include your name/screen name in the e-mail as you want it to appear here, or tell me you want to be anonymous.
- Nothing offensive.
- Image must have something to do with the Dark Age series.
- If possible, please compress your image in a zip file (I seem to be having trouble saving attachments that are image files).
- Make it your own work! Don't send me a collage of images from this site!
