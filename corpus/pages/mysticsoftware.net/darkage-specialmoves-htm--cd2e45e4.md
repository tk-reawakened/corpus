Special Move Descriptions
While many special moves are self-explanatory, some of the characters' special moves require explaination. Most moves require SMP to use. Remember, not all of the moves listed here can be learned by level. Some must be acquired through different means...
Sword Techniques
Focus must be used before using a sword technique. It gives the user 50 SMP no matter who it is used on (can only be used in battles). *Focus*
Basic sword technique.* Slash
Advanced sword technique.* Falling Cut
Extreme sword technique. Hits the target with an FP of about 80 multiple times. The times it hits is equal to the user's level (bright white flash is a critical hit).* Level Strike
Magic
Basic to mid-level single enemy attacks.* Fire, Ice, Lightning, Shade, Blast
Basic to advanced attacks that hit all opponents.* Explosion, Earthquake, Thunder Rage
Extreme attack on one enemy.* Crimson
Restore HP to one character.* Cure, Cure 2, Cure 3
Restores around 150 HP to all characters (doesn't matter who the target is).* Healing Breeze
Restores around 500 HP to all characters (doesn't matter who the target is).* Healing Gust
Legendary single enemy attack.* Sacred Fire
Slows the target (enemy attacks less often)* Slow
*Haste* Makes target faster (attacks more often)
Transformations
Transformations work the same way as magic. They include both single and "all" attacks. If I told you what your character could transform into, it wouldn't be nearly as fun finding them, would it?
Science
Science is probably the most complex group of special moves. Its use is not based on SMP, but on items.
These two moves are used in conjunction with each other. Flamethrower uses a number of fire cartridges from your stockpile. The more it uses, the more powerful it is. You must define how many fire cartridges Flamethrower uses at one time by using Calibrate Flamethrower outside of battle. The maximum is 20 at a time. Each fire cartridge is worth abouth 50 FP of damage. They can be found, won from enemies, and bought at stores.* Flamethrower, Calibrate Flamethrower
These two moves work the same way as described above, only with auto shells. However, there is no limit to the number of auto shells you can use at once. Also, each auto shell's FP is equal to the user's FP. Auto Shotgun can easily be the most powerful attack in the game if you acquire a large amount of auto shells and use them all at once. Auto shells can be found and won from enemies.* Auto Shotgun, Calibrate Auto Shotgun
Shows the enemy's statistics. The only requirement for use is that you find and equip the item that lets you use it...* Scout
When using an attack that hits all enemies, choose the enemy you think has the most HP left as the target. The attack will be more likely to do more damage. *Tip:*
