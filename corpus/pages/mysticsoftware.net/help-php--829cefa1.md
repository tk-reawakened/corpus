# Mystic Software

Though the battle system tutorial and template is undergoing a major revision (the Battle System Devkit), for now you can take a look at the new HTML battle system style tutorial. You may be wondering where the old battle system template/tutorial is, butI simply cannot recommend the template in its current form. A better way to learn about battle systems would be to download a commented battle engine, like Zelda or DragBattle2, and study the code. The current template is just too outdated to be useful...
- Battle Systems - By MrG
