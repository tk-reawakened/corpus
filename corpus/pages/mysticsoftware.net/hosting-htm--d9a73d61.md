**NOTE: April 11, 2002**
**Mystic Software is temporarily not accepting any more requests for hosting until the move to the new server is complete. Once the move is done, hosting will resume, however, space will be limited to 10MB's per site (this could change). Space for hosting your game does not count against your 10MB limit of course. All hosted sites will have FTP access, a sub-domain name, and one free email ((redacted: email)).**
Mystic Software provides HTTP and FTP hosting for the Toolkit community for those who are tired of having to go through all sorts of trouble finding a good host for their site and game, with a decent amount of web space, no ads, and sill be free. Mystic Software provides a quick solution to all these problems by giving you short URL's (yourname.mysticsoftware.net), 10MB's of space (there is no limit for the size of your game, as it is stored on another account specifically set up for Toolkit games), and all of this for free.
To get your site hosted here at Mystic Software, you'll first need to ask yourself if you know how to use FTP, and an FTP client. If the answer is no, then to go find out how, and to download some popular FTP clients. [click here](http://www.cochran.com/start/guide/ftp.html)
After you've accomplished this, all you have to do is sign up for an account by emailing us at with your desired username and password (all lowercase please), input the FTP server information for Mystic Software (provided below and in the email sent after you sign up), and begin uploading your site to the (redacted: email)
