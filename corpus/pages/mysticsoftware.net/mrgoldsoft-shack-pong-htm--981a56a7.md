# : Martin's Nycatsoft Slowball Pong

***
I'd Rather Be Playing Tennis
Tennis may just be the greatest sport on the face of the Earth, and on the face of other various planets as well and even sometimes on the legs of these planets. It was certainly the sport that inspired that first sporty video game, Pong. In this challenge you must make the coolest Pong game out of pure RPGCode. Cooler than say, MrG's. That is all. Go!**
** In Rank:
![](uberbunny.gif) *Tennis.prg*** 1. :
Jer1400's Tennis When Jer first sent me Tennis, he had outside RPGCode images with it. While it was a great game, of course the rules didn't allow for outside images. So I told him this, and lo and behold, he managed to make something that looked nearly exactly the same using only RPGCode's graphical functions. Not only that, but it cleverly uses the mouse for control, has a tough but not totally impossible AI, and it runs at a decent clip (with some flicker but a lot of the entries had flicker.) Jer1400 managed to make a fairly decent little tennis game completely out of RPGCode, and for that he wins the grand prize. What is the grand prize you ask? Well, it's that beautiful pic up there, an ever enshrined monument on the intro page of my site, a mention on RPGToolkit.com and an inclusion of Jer's Tennis program alongside all my other mini-games in Zeo-K. Oh, and the ability for Jer to constantly tell you (yes you) that he is a wonderful coder. Congratulations!
2. At first glance this doesn't seem like much, I'll admit, with its spartan graphics. But graphics don't make the game, and this version of Pong had the best gameplay out of all the entries. Slowball with its unique, ploddish pace forced actual strategy into the game of Pong, and even had me noticing nuances and coining terms (the richochet point anyone?) This is the most challenging (on hard) and most rewarding game of Pong. Martin is better at it than me, sadly, as he managed to beat the computer on hard 10-4, while I could only do 10-5. This one is quite a bit of fun!![](silverrod.gif) *Slowball.prg*

3. Sadly unfinished, this version of 3D Pong does have its slight faults. For instance no scoring scheme other than an #MWin "Goal!" and some questionable boundries. But this is still 3D Pong, and for that it is quite an accomplishment. The 3D craze that recently hit the community may have been a direct result of Dissy's efforts to craft a little 3D Pong Goodness. It's just too bad he didn't manage to get out a finished version before the (ahem, extended) deadline. My most varied entry, Pogo's version included no less than 10 different versions of Pong, including a small (but for me unworkable, sadly) version of 3D Pong. Just missed the Top 3 by inches. Has the distinction of being my first entry. Simple Pong with a cool battle mode twist. Pat C's entry. Wonderful interface system, but like Dissy, not enough time to finish the game, and as such has a few critical gameplay errors. Still very good looking. Like Pogo's, a bunch of wild and crazy varied options, but the slow gameplay brings it down, unfortunetly. Still a great attempt with a lot of unique touches. Erm, well, I can't win sadly, so I have to have an honorable mention. This is a little modified version on the Original Disco Pong ™ with the best angles and smoothest gameplay of them all (modesty not one of my virtues, sadly.)![](bronzerod.gif) *Pongpractice.prg*
