**February 29 , 2004**
**Back Online**
All the files for the main part of Mystic Software are back online and all the links should work. If you have problems, make sure and . The forums won't be online until Mike Hill or one of the other staff members that knows about MySQL databases can put them back up. Hopefully that won't take too long. As far as hosted sites go, I need to wait for the server to start working to get most everyones site back in working order. Everyone that had a site should still have one, and as Xorlak mentioned, should be able to access it via http://www.mysticsoftware.net/username/. There are a couple of you that will receive emails from me asking for username/password info so that I can re-setup your account. Mostly this will only be those that signed up before January 1st, 2004. In case you missed it, only a few of you will continue with mysticsoftware.net sub-domain names. The rest will be hosted either on godivaalpha.com, or ms2.mysticsoftware.net. This will mean your address will be either username.godivaalpha.com, username.ms2.rackhost.net, or ms2.mysticsoftware.net/username/. Since all of these are relatively long, you can sign up for a free domain name at www.dot.tk to have point to your account. As far as I know they are still ad-free. Thanks to everyone for being patient with us while we aquired more space for this site. This will insure that we will be able to expand the site in the near future.contact me [godivaalpha.com](http://www.godivaalpha.com)
