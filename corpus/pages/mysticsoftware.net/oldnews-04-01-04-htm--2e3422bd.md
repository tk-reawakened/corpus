![](/images/news.png)

**April 1, 2004**
**Goodbye**
This is very hard for me to tell everyone this. For as long as MS has been in the TK community and all, it's very hard for me to say that it will be no more. This will be the last month that it is online, and then I will be removing the website and all it's files and hosted sites. The URL will be re-directed to The Toolkit Zone. Why you ask? It's pretty obvious I think. I'm just not up to updating the website consistantly, and it's not exactly friendly to my wallet either. I'm very sorry for those of you that have helped support this website and community for the last four years. This is just something I can't continue.
*April Fools!*
