Reviewer: Sophia Game Author: Craig Reeves
Brief Intro
Aspen High finally got out of jail and ready to start his life over… But his past keeps coming back and hunts him. With the help from some new friends, will he ever discover the truth about his past?
Story 18/20
The story starts out quite slow, and gets quite confusing. For people that aren't very patience, the early part of this game might turn them off quite easily. But if you stick with the game for a while, the storyline gets very intriguing and all the confusing part starts to come together. There are a lot of characters in this game that will aid the main character, and their personalities are all very well expressed.
Graphic 15/20
Usually I am against mixing original tiles with ripped graphic, because they usually make the game looks like its slapped together in a hurry. But Craig seems to be quite careful when it comes to mixing these tiles together, so the quality of the graphic is fairy balanced. Board design can still use some work, but it's very good already compare to most of the tk games I've played. Times are taken to do a lot of cut scenes that keeps the player interested, which a lot of tk game designers sometimes don't bother to do.
Sound 18/20
I was a little disappointed when most the songs I heard in Alcatraz are the ones in the music pack that VIP studio has made for the tk users. This might be because Craig is way too busy writing songs for everybody else (including me =P) that he doesn't have time when it comes to his own game. The music of course, is still excellent and helps the players enjoy the game. Sound effects are very good too.
Originality 16/20
Despite some of the ripped graphics, the game is very original indeed. That includes the storyline, characters, music, and a custom menu. The battle system is default. But since not everyone can code their own battle system (I know I can't!), it's not a big deal.
Game Play 15/20
One of the most annoying things for me, there's about 5-8 seconds pauses between battles and entering (or exiting) towns…. Not sure if this is a tk bug or just my computer =P. There are quite a few bugs, but nothing major that will stop player from following the story. Some conversations went by too fast, but Craig can easily fix that for the next version of Alcatraz. The custom menu is very cool, but I find it a bit hard needing to switch between keyboard and mouse. The battles are very balanced, not too tough on a pushover like me =). And Craig took time to make some battle animations that adds enjoyment to the game.
Over all
82/100
Additional comments:
For the players: This game is not something you can finish in a hour, expect to spend at least a whole day on it.(if you plan on playing it non-stop) The story and characters are the best part of this game, which can only be enjoyed if you play through the game instead of just checking out the beginning.
For Craig: I highly suggest you some how downsize the game a bit and upload a bug-free version. I know it's not easy because you have a slow modem, but it will be worth it to get more people to download this game that you have obviously worked very hard for. You have no idea how scary it is to see there's three patches needed to be install along with a 38 MB game.
This Review is written by Sophia Hsu, dated April 25, 2002
