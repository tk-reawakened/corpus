Gameplay/Story
13/30
What story? These two guys, like, died fighting against Chimera, won, but got killed by the guy's sword. That's Cheap! The gameplay was good though, nice, thorough, battles and warnings of strong enemies ahead. Pretty fun, if you can figure out what to do.
Graphics:
17/20
Nice looking tiles and all well placed. And good battle animations. Ripped though, but ain't no shame is his game.
Programming:
11/20
No custom anything. Maybe Hyren is planning something for the second demo, but the programming in this game was average at best.
Bugs:
20/20
No bugs I ran into.
Sound:
16/20
Nice change, he used music that came with the TK. Original, but not very original. Good FX though.
In A Nutshell:
76/100
A nice game, worth a download. Pretty fun, if you know what to do.
