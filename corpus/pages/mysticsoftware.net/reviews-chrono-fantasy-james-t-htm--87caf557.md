Chrono Fantasy
Stephen Choi
James T
50/100
Main/Start
69/100
The start up screen is good in this game. It has a 3D picture and...and (um) that's it. The thing that tells you the story at the top is to slow and you have to go through about 50 of them to start the game.
Graphics:
40/100
All the tiles in Chrono Fantasy are the default ones and for the characters and enemies are from RPG Maker tiles.
Music/Sounds:
50/100
Again all the sounds are ripped from "The jewel of destiny" apart from a meow sound that comes from a cat when you bump into it. All the music is from RPG Maker games or Jewel of destiny.
Plot:
10/100
There is no origanality at all in the plot thats why I gave it such a low score.
Characters:
30/100
At the begining you start in a dream sequence when you have loads of charecters with high HPs. When your charecter wakes he is on his own with only 20Hp and was defeted by a fox out side the house.
Overall:
50/100
Pretty dredful game all round. You'd be better playing Rpg Toolkits DEMO game.
