**A Matter of Criminal Justice**
Reviewer: Hyren Game Author: Legendary Fox Productions
**Story 6/10**
It seemed like a fairly interesting story. It's World War III and you work for the CIA. The U.S. is falling apart politically and someone sabatoged your project to save the world.
**Graphics 5.5/10**
RPG maker graphics. The reason I gave this a 5.5 is because of the way they were used. They looked like they were slapped together really fast.
**Music 5/10**
It's World War III and there's happy town music in the backround!
**Enemies 4/10**
There were two enemies, each of which I killed in one hit. Your character has a special move that has no special graphics or anything.
**Programming 5/10**
I didn't see much of it besides getting weapons and the message window.
**Fun 3.5/10**
There were only three screens. What else is there to say?
**Originality 4/10**
It reminds me of old war movies, just with the CIA.
**Intro 4/10**
It had a few pictures, and that's it.
**Items 4/10**
There was a gun and a clearance card.
**Bugs 10/10**
The game was so short I didn't notice any.
**Overall 51/100**
An average toolkit game. Nothing more, nothing less.
This Review is written by Hyren, dated July 6th, 2002
