**Story, What's it all about? (10/10)**
Dark Age by far has one of the most original and complex storylines ever created in the RPG toolkit community. The story contains all of the elements a true RPG game sure contain, from love, hate, betrayal and tragedy. Ok, so we know the story contains, but what is it about? Well I'll tell you… The kingdoms of Varda and Celena have been at war off and on for centuries. The militant Varda has built up quite an army as a result. But this is not another war story. Vance, a skilful yet passive general of Varda's army, notices an increase in violent behaviour from his king, Fredrick, and in particular, another Varda general named Mortis. With the recent peace treaty between the two sparring nations in effect, Fredrick seems to be using his military more and more for internal affairs, such as stealing from the Wizard Palace for not paying taxes. Vance doesn't mind the increased activity; it's his job after all. But his co-general, Mortis, has been becoming increasingly harder to deal with. Vance and Mortis have accepted greater responsibility since the death of the head general, Walter, and Vance is feeling the extra stress. On top of that, a deadly dragon has been spotted in the mountains near Varda Castle... The characters have really wicked names but what do they look like…

Yep, you guessed it these are all the main characters. There are many, many more but these are the main ones for now. Vance is the character you control for 90% of the game. He is a general of the Kingdom of Varda, Vance is loyal and courageous. He always tries to perform his missions to the best of his ability and follow his king's instructions exactly. Vance is trained in the magic arts and is one of the few who can use White Magic. The other characters have there own special abilities, background history and separate story. The thing that makes the story get such a high rating is the fact that each character has there own individual personality which is very hard to achieve when programming your own full game RPG game.
**Boards (9/10) and graphics (9/10)**
All of the boards through out Dark Age are very nicely done. Each board contains plenty of detail and scrolls very nicely. Most of the tiles are original but there are quite a few which are ripped from Final Fantasy 4. When you are playing through a board you actually do feel like you are in the location, what I mean by that is when you enter a castle the tiles are so well put together and placed it really does look like a proper castle, take a look for yourself…
