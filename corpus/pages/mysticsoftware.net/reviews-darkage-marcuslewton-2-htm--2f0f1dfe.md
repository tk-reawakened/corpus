**Items (10/10)**
Well, let's just say that the game Dark Age contains loads and loads of Items all with there own special ability and features. I can tell you what Items to expect when playing through this tremendous role-playing game, there is everything from Potions to magic orbs and some Items even influence how your character turns out in the final battle! There is really nothing else to say apart from the Items are brilliant and must of took absolutely ages designing and programming. Bravo! Xorlak you did it again!
**Battles (8/10)**
The battles in Dark Age are pretty impressive and have special effects in such as magic and even Limit Breaks! These are really powerful attacks like in Final Fantasy 7. Xorlak makes his Limit breaks look really effective and every battle he has nicely placed a background image, which blends in nicely with the enemies and match pretty well. See for yourself…

**Enemies (10/10)**
Well, what's there to say apart from they are all 100% original and are drawn superiorly well with perfect detail. Each enemy has it's own special characteristic and special attack. Some enemies are very tough and challenging and others are well, hard! The enemies are fair but when playing through the game don't expect to wiz through the enemies due to they are actually very difficult and I guarantee you that the more progress you make through the game the more times you will be defeated, I just hope you take losing well.

**Bugs (9/10)**
Now surprisingly enough there were quite a few bugs and they could of actually effected the game play. But wait this is Xorlak, do you really think he forgot all about bugs? Cause he didn't! Xorlak programmed a special program set as a run time activation program. This particular program is known as a force redraw, whenever the board had a funny 5 minutes and decided to make some tiles solid when they were suppose to be normal all the player had to do was simply hit the "R" key and the whole board got redraw via leaving the board bug free. Now I have seen some special programs in my time but this one has to be the cleverest. Why can't every game contain this simple command? I mean you get marked out of 10 for bugs and if you use something like this your going to get at least 8 easy marks. So in the future use something like this and your game still wont be perfect and bug free but will reduce the amount of bugs that crop up!
**There you have it, see I told so…**
