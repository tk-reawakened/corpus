**So what are Xorlak's plans for Dark Age 2…**
"I'm putting far more work into Dark Age 2 that I did its predecessor. It will be 100% original, including an original battle system, menu, and will contain plenty of other extras that I don't want to give away just yet. Dark Age 2 will not be a continuation of Dark Age's plot, but will be an entirely new, independent adventure that shares some themes with the original. My goal is to make a commercial quality game with the Toolkit. I plan on selling the final version, but of course, I will release a free demo along with it. When will it be released? Not any time soon.

**Interview with the master Xorlak**
.How long did it take to make Dark Age?
**1**
*A little over nine months.*
. Would you say you were an artist at drawing computer art? **2**
* I'd say so. I've been working on a lot of original tiles and graphics lately and I'm getting the hang of working on images pixel by pixel.
. Ever thought of going into programming for a living? **3**
* Yes, programming has been my ambition for some time now, though I'm not sure if I'm going to go into game making. I know standard c++ and am starting my second year working on a computer science degree this fall.
. What can Dark Age Fans expect from Dark Age 2? **4**
* Well, in a nutshell, a fun game! I've been paying attention to what people like and don't like in a game, and I'm trying to put something in for every type of (RPG) player.
. Will there be a Dark Age 3? **5**
*Heh, I'm not sure about that yet...*
. What do you think of Mysticsoftware.net? **6**
* I think it's a great site. Free e-mail and hosting with no advertisements!
. What's your favourite toolkit 2 game? **7**
* There are some really nice games out there and I have yet to try them all, but I think I had the most fun playing Deceit and Conquest. Although it's incomplete, the story really drew me in and there were some very nice special effects (best Toolkit moment: the exploding monk!). A close second would be the Warrior Legends demo. It has some unique features that really livened the game.
