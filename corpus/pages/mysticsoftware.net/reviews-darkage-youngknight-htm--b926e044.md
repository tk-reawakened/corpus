**Dark Age**
Reviewer: Young Knight Game Author: Xorlak
**Graphics: 19/20**
I liked the tiles, but some looked a little different from others. I don't frown on this mixing, but at times, it took away from the gameplay. Other than that, there were some good battle backgrounds and well-drawn out boards. Also, when you use an elixir, the border lights up, which I think is pretty cool.
**Sound: 20/20**
The music was great, and it always happened to be perfect for the given scene. I have not seen a better game that has such great sound integration. Also, when certain items are used, sound effects are set off, which adds to the gameplay.
**Fun Factor: 19/20**
This game is really fun, although some battles in the beginning can't be ran from. When this problem is fixed, the game can't be more fun, even for a hardcore shoot 'em up gamer.
**Gameplay: 20/20**
The gameplay of this epic is just astounding. Every little piece is just there, with optional other things, like watching two guys fight. That is much like FF7. The battles are amazing, especially the amount of detail in the backgrounds and the enemies graphics and their stats. It's hard to make a game this good, but Xorlak pulled it off.
**Overall: 20/20**
With little or no bugs, hassles, or just plain boringness, Dark Age promises to please you. Especially since its a small 5.33MB file, almost anyone can get it (Hey I got a 56K). So, if you're looking for a good game, get Dark Age, because it won't fail to impress.
**GRADE: 98/100= A+**
This Review is written by Young Knight, dated June 2, 2002
