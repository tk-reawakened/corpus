**Dustball**
Reviewer: DoddyUK Game Author: Sophia
Heralded as one of the greatest TK games ever, we shall see whether I think it lives up to the hype.
**What's it about?**
Strange one this... This story follows a puss-in-boots-type talking cat called DustBall in his quest to mach up his new friend James with the Princess.
**Is it any good?**
If you overlook the cutesyness of it all you will discover an excellent, original and always amusing story. The antics of DustBall never fail to bring a smile to your face, and the fact that the main character is a cat casts this game away from the steriotypical RPG. Genius.
**Storylines: 5/5**
**Does it look good?**
Wow. That is the only word that can describe how good these graphics are. The frames in the introduction and the cut-scenes must have take Sophia several hours if not days to make, and they look awsome. The graphics in general are also gobsmacking, the best I've seen in a TK game. Superb.
**Graphics: 5/5**
**Is it glitch-free?**
Pretty much so. There's nothing too special in this department apart from the message windows during a battle, but everything is ship-shape. No glitches to talk about here, but a few extras in later versions wouldn't go a miss.
**Programming: 4/5**
**And what's the sound department like?**
The sound department is very good, if not a tad quiet. Not having played Chrono Trigger, I am in no position to justif TKZ's comments, however I think I have heard the "enemy dead" sound effect in MD Sonic 2 (Switch box in 2 player mode). Also, the Hanabi tune from FFVII also lurks around in this game. Apart from that, again very good.
**Music: 4/5**
**In a nutshell...**
There is no word powerful enough to say how I feel about this game. It may be a tad on the cutesy side, but hell, THIS IS THE GREATEST TOOLKIT GAME EVER!!! DOWNLOAD IT NOW, AND THAT'S AN ORDER! This Review is written by DoddyUK, dated May 25, 2002
