**Dust Ball**
Reviewer: Saryentoss Game Author: Sophia Web Site:
**Review submitted: 01/08/02**
Intro: In dustball you play the part of a little cat named Dustball.You have a hard life and meet a young man named James. He (*Cough*) coincidently falls in love with the lovely princess Lillian and Dustball goes on a zany quest to help out his pal James (the Saryentoss Scale)
Scale 1-10 in each category (1 lowest,10 highest) Overall 10-100 (10 being run away and dont look back and 100 being total onwnage.) Intro(10)
Very nicely done! I love the intro and the comedy that Sophia has put into the presentation. It really made me laugh and is something i have watched over and over agian.
Tiles(10)
very nicely done and orginal. Tiles seem to merge wonderfully and they fit into the bright, happy and colorful world.
Bugs(7)
Very few bugs. but when i loaded the game after stoping it started me at a point i was not at(and yes i made sure i saved it right! So there!)thats why i have to lower this score*Sorry Sophia dont want to*
Length(5)
The reason im killing the length is that it ended way to soon. i know making a game is hard so im gonna be nice and up it to 5.Its only a demo but it should be just a tad longer.
Difficulty(3)
it is fun but fights in the begining become a bit to repetive and the grauds at the end before the ending are way to easy.I dont really want to but im lowering the score for that
Originality(10)
a very nice piece of work i hope that it will become even better in the future.
Music/Media(7)
im giving this a seven because of the fact that the animations were decent but not as great as they could be.Plus alot of the music was ripped and to dectectable (Am i playing Chrono Trigger agian? I thought i was playing Dustball) The only thing that spares this score is that it fits nicely into the story and it is great stuff.
Characters(10)
Nice characters nothing else.. that merits a ten
Replayability(10)
its so good that you have to play it over and over agian..... thats why i give a ten. its a little to predictable but still fun to do.
Cinematics (10)
Very nice! I love the anime type move secense they made.. I hope more is added. Overall 82/100 A very nice game but could use improvements. Great work by Sophia and her team and I hope to see future versions bring out the potential of the game. GO AND DOWNLOAD THIS GAME NOW
