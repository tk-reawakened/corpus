**Friends and Enemies**
Reviewer: DoddyUK Game Author: Deadalus
Well then, this is my first TK review, so bear with me if I seem to be too harsh or lean. Are you sitting comfortably? Then I will begin.
**What's it about?**
This game focuses on two friends who are training to be agents in the anti-terrorist organisation IPESCO. The game starts in a power plant where the terrorist group GODA have shut off the valves and set a bomb. Recruits Labedo and Vega with Agent Banard are sent out to foil this plan. I will not give much away about what happens inside the plant, but the mission was a success. Now all that stands between the two recruits and the job of official Agent at IPESCO is one final, top secret mission...
**Is it any good?**
Suprisingly, yes. This has a very gripping storyline that hooks you by the neck and drags you in head first. The story of spies on their most important mission yet may have been use time and time again, but the plot in this game is very fresh and is seldom boring. Storylines: 5/5
**Does it look good?**
Well, yes and no. At first I found the Terrence and Philip-type character graphics rather wierd, but after a while I got userd to them and actually started to like those graphics. The overall graphics, however, could be better. They are all drawn from scratch, but some tiles are one-tone and bland. Fairly average overall.
Graphics: 3/5
**Is it glitch-free?**
I'm afraid not. None of the glitches are serious, but you do get the occasional niggle of the message window appearing in a different place to the text. The satelite map function is very good - but it never works properly because the outdated #GetPlayerCoords command is used. Added to the fact that the menu system takes a fair while to load, this game gets annoying at times.
There are also errors with items and battles. I don't know whether it's trans2's fault because the internal shops are used, but when I buy items they don't appear in my item stash. Also, when in the sewers level trans2 screwed up when it gave me the message No enemies of skill2 found. Having looked at the enemy list I discovered that there were no enemies of skill 2. Oh dear.
There are some very good ideas, the satelite map and the combox are very good, but in the RPGCode stakes, the bad outweighs the good. Sorry.
Stability: 2/5
**And what's the sound department like?**
Pretty good, although the music is fairly short, so it tends to cut out half way through an important story sequence. However, I am certain that not all of the music is entirely original. I am deadly certain that I have heard the lift music in the Power Plant mission in Zelda: Link's Awakening. I'm also kinda sure I've heard the music in IPESCO headquarters somewhere in Final Fantasy VIII. Apart from that, the music in this game fits in fairly well.
Music: 4/5
**In a nutshell...**
This is a fairly good game with an excellent plot. It's a shame though that small mistakes are littered throughout. I'm being extremely generous here by giving this game four stars, but I'm doing so in the hope that the next demo will be better in the RPGCode department. Despite this, I have to say that this is the best TK game I have played so far. Download if you fancy playing a fairly good game.
**Overall: 4/5**
