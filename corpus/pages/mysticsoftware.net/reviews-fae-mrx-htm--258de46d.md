**FAE Mission 1**
Reviewer: MrX
At a first glance, I was impressed. The original tiles are well done. The characters are nice. The story is not your everyday RPG, but that's what makes this game good. The only turn off is the music, they are all ripped (as far as I tell). But, with what music it does use, I have to say it fit. Overall, here is what I thought:
Story- 22/25 I like the story very much. I was actually rather excited while playing it!
Character design- 22/25 Nice looking characters. Goes along with the game.
Tiles- 25/25 Once again, I have to say that this game had great tiles!
Media- 15/25 I was kinda disappointed when I heard ripped music. I am hoping that this will be changed. This game would be awesome with some nice original music.
