**Final Fantasy: the Mystery Stone**
Reviewer: Hyren Game Author: Daniel Halloway
**Story 3/10**
I'm sorry, but the only story I saw was a guy's mom making dinner.
**Graphics 9/10**
The bitmaps were pretty good and the tiles were awsome. I think they were original too. Boards were done well and they fit together.
**Music 4/10**
There were four songs. I swear, I heard one in Dustball. One was default in the RPG Toolkit. The battle music was from Final Fantasy IX. The intro was original, I think.
**Enemies 6/10**
Nothing special. Just two different kinds of slime that always killed me.
**Items 5/10**
There were potions, ethers, and antidotes. I don't know why there were antidotes if no enemies could poison you though. No equipment at all.
**Bugs 10/10**
I found no bugs whatsoever. That's because the game's so frikin' short!
**Gameplay 4/10**
Too short! There's 2 people in the game besides your character! And I saw no "Mystery Stone"!
**Programming 6/10**
It used the default battle system and menu, but that's not why I counted off. You can't save! You can't save on the boards and there aren't any save points! The reason this even got a 6 is because I liked the intro.
**Intro 7/10**
The words growing and shrinking was a nice effect. The buttons were drawn well.
**Fun 4/10**
The enemies were too hard and I always got killed.
**Originality 6/10**
It was nothing like Final Fantasy which kind of made it original.
**Overall 64/100**
Everything looks rushed except for the tiles which were good. Nothing like Final Fantasy.
This Review is written by Hyren, dated June 18, 2002
