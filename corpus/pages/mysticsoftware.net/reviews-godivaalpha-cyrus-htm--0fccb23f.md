OVERALL SCORE: 90 *Highly reccomended*
**Godiva: Alpha 1.1b**
Reviewer: (redacted: name)
First off, let me say that I have played several TK games over the years and I am evaluating this game in comparison to those games. So, the scale is based on What I've seen so far in Tk games.
The Scale of measurement is 1-100 (100 being the highest)
Godiva: Alpha has been in production for a long time and it shows. Spyder has gone alot of work on the various areas of the game and has reached a level of quality few other games have been able to. When playing through GA I paid particular to some of the specific design areas and the following is my impressions of each...
**GRAPHICS:**
The Graphics in GA are very well put together... The tiles seem to mesh well together and maintain the overall look of the game consistently. Spyder chose to use ripped tiles from other RPGs and even though they are not original (yet) it is obvious that he put a great deal of thought into their placement on boards and avoids using the same tiles too often. His player graphics are very nice and animate well. They also seem to fit the boards well, keeping the sense that these tiles are meant to be together. His enemies are nicely done although it might be said that some of them fit better in the game than others. An example of this is the stark difference between his choices like mechanical looking enemies and fantasy style ones. They seem to keep the same drawing style though which makes that only a small concern. His intro is very nice also and gives a definite sense of fantasy to the game.
Tiles: 80 - deduction for using ripped tiles so this should raise later Boards: 95 - Very nice layout and perspective is maintained consistently (except for the starting board) Characters: 80 - same deduction for ripped tiles Enemies: 78 - same deduction plus slight deduction for unexplained enemy style mixing) Cinematics: 85 - nicely done but not enough of them
**MUSIC:**
Very nice. The music is one of the best parts of the game. Its all original works by Chris and Craig Reeves. It all seems to fit well with the exception of a few areas. All in all, its fantastically orchestrated.
Placement: 90 - Some areas it is a little too upbeat...but that's just an opinion. Style: 98 Quality: 99 Amount: 95 - Good stuff... I want to hear more
**GAMEPLAY:**
One of the best things about this game is that its fun. Its kept at sort of a medium difficulty range and if you actually play it how it is intended to be played (meaning you buy things and do a little leveling) its a blast. The addition of the cave area where Spyder has created a semi-maze that you have to navigate only adds to the enjoyment and length of the demo. Although, the game is a bit short at this point...its just a demo... and for a demo its really a pretty good length. I was hoping to do more after I left the cave but thats what future releases are for. The are also enough enemies and items in the game to keep it fresh throughout the playing experience. Its clear Spyder wanted to keep throwing new things at you. There are 2 towns that you can visit and the first is done nicely. There are several people to talk to and places to visit. the second town is pretty much deserted and you have to venture a little off the beaten path to get there...so a little disappointing.
Fun Level: 90 Difficulty: 85 - maybe a little work could be done...but its pretty balanced Variety: 75 - that second town dropped this a little Length: 80 - not bad for a demo
**FEATURES:**
There are alot of cool things in this demo such as a custom Menu and the ability to choose a player class. This is the first game I've seen attempt to do custom characters. Its pretty impressive. The only thing lacking is some sort of distinction between the classes besides their levels and how their graphics look...such as starting in different places..or anything really. Still, very cool. The menu is the best one I've seen thus far...well thought out and effective. It looks very nice and doesn't take too long to load up. Another nice addition is the ability to name your character. Its more of a style thing...but it works well here. I also give Spyder credit for implementing the options to skip the intro and to see the new info about the game at the start.
Quality: 95 Quantity: 95 Originality: 85 Usefulness: 99
**STORYLINE:**
Well here is where the game falls short. There is very little story at all...confining itself to the brief intro and the dialog with the townspeople. There is also no real explanation for what's going on or why the enemies are present. I think this can improve with time and in fact, it may prove to be a good idea to not tell the players too much about the game at the start...only to reveal it later. So anyway, this is an area that certainly needs improvement but I am confident that this will happen in future releases. The plot isn't bad though...and not overly corny. ...so its not gonna be like trying to save a sinking ship or anything.
Presentation: 60 - Sorry...just not much there Cinematics: 0 - None that affect the plot Dialog: 70 - need more content and information Plot Depth: 40 - Obviously needs work Plot growth potential: 100 - It may be lacking but there are infinite possibilities.
**OVERALL OPINION:**
The game is alot of fun. Its got a lot of nice features, great music and a good amount of gameplay. Its plot isn't exactly Les Miseribles but its got potential. It has tons of features and it looks nice. If you want to play one of the highest quality TK games out there, then I would have no problems recommending this one. ...and its only a demo...
