**KINGYO II Demo**
Reviewer: Ares Game Author: Blair
After I managed to find the right main file of the game, the game looked rather impressive. The design of the title screen was good, and the in-game graphics were also good.....
Story (2\25) All I can say is... what story? All that happens is, Blair fights a gangster, talks to someone, goes to his house, meets a robot, and talks to someone else. The story was almost non- existent. I was all spelt incorrectly and it made no sense. I did like the use of coarse language though... heh.
Tiles (18\25) The tiles look good with the game, although I believe they were ripped. They were placed well in the game.
Audio (15\25) The music was good, although I don't remember hearing any sound effects.
Difficulty (0\25) All you do is walk around a bit, but you do fight one enemy... who you can kill in one hit.
Overall = 35\100 This game was rather dissapointing. Sure the graphics and music were nice, but the game is too short, and the story is poor. Hopefully, Blair will fix these problems by the final game.
~Ares-Chan PS. No offence to Blair.
