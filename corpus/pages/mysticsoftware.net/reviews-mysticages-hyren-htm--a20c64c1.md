**Mystic Ages**
Reviewer: Hyren Game Author: Chris K.
**Story 1/10**
I'm a little lost here. The light gets morphed or something?
**Graphics 4/10**
Default RPGTK character graphics. There were 5 different non-character tiles.
**Music 0/10**
Absolutely no music or sound FX.
**Enemies 0/10**
There were no enemies! There was one tiny scrolling board!
**Programming 1/10**
It actually got a point in this section because there were two message windows.
**Fun 1/10**
One board!!!! Are people TRYING to be worse than Mini-Cars!?!? It used 26 Mega bites! For one board!!!!!!!!!
**Originality 1/10**
An excact copy of Mini-Cars!!! Mini-Cars was the first! Why must people copy the worst!? Mini-Cars got 8 originality for being the first! Now this!!!!
**Intro 5/10**
A squashed Mystic Ages logo that hurt my eyes and some fading words.
**Items 0/10**
No items. At all.
**Bugs 3/10**
The game was one board!!!! I consider that a bug!!!!
**Overall 16/100**
Sniff...In all my days as a reviewer I never thought I'd play a game worse than Mini-Cars...
This Review is written by Hyren, dated July 17, 2002
