**Mystical Quest**
Reviewer: Marcus Lewton Game Author: Mike D.
**Story, What's it all about? (7/10)**
I have played and completed the game a total of three times and have to say the story isn't all that great, however it has that certain 'Hook' in it, which is only found in special games. It makes you want to see the game, well the demo through to the very end. The impression I got from the story was that two Kingdoms were working together nicely until one of the Kingdoms decides to get greedy and plans to crush the other Kingdom with a bunch of powerful killer machines, which are controlled by the children of your hometown. Your Quest is to stop this, before it's to late. That's basically the impression I got from the main plot.
**Boards (6/10)**
Well, unfortunately this is probably one of the flaws, which kind of let the game down. The boards are actually quite boring and all of the tiles are ripped from the RPG 95 collection (I think it's from that set) the boards do actually scroll very nicely, but the scenery just stays the same. However the game does use light effects and reflections very well probably this is one of the best features in the game and shows all of the other RPG toolkit games how light effects should be used. The game also contains a well-set out brilliant World map; this is a classic example of a carefully planned, well thought out map.
**Graphics (6/10)**
Nothing much to say, apart from all the graphics are ripped from an RPG tileset collection (as I've mentioned above) and are actually put together very nicely but unfortunately you can tell they are ripped.
**Items (2/10)**
Well, where can I start? There are no Items! There are a few weapon shops but they are only selling about three weapons. I explored basically every where and I couldn't find any items…. But maybe I am wrong there may be some I just haven't found. Either way there is not enough items.
**Battles (8/10)**
Now this is also a really good feature in the game the character you control who is known as Sky uses Magic and powerful attacks, unfortunately the battle system is default (the one we all hate) but the battle animation and sound effects are really good and fit in well.
**Enemies (7/10)**
Very good graphics and I don't think they were ripped (They might have been) some of the enemies use special attacks which is very good, but the enemies are very, very weak and easy to defeat (I didn't die once!) There is no real challenge here but maybe the Game designer did this purposely so you could see all aspects of his game.
**Programming (6/10)**
Nothing really which stands out but there is one bit when you get to go into a boat and control it down the river, this is pretty good fun. The message window is basic but it's pretty clever how the writing scrolls and the dialog is actually pretty good, but there is only one bitmap image and no special effects.
**Bugs (9/10)**
Lets just say that this whole demo is bug free, I found one bug in the message window and that was it, but everything else was perfect
**Overall**
A very good attempt and worth a look. The story makes you want to play on to the very end and you get straight to the main plot. The game just lacks in hardly if not any items and all of the graphics are ripped. But worth a look and hopefully the full version will be even better, I did enjoy playing this game:
**Final score 51/80 (64%)**
This Review is written by Marcus Lewton, dated June 14, 2002
