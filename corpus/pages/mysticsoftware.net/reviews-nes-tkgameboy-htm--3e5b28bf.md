**Never Ending Scroll**
Reviewer: TK Game Boy Game Author: White Divine Web Site:
<http://ChicoGames.cjb.net/>
Never Ending Scroll is a cool game with original music and graphics. The graphics seem to originate from the original NES system (except for the enemies) but the game play is SNES quality (according to me).
**Graphics-**
The graphics are excellent. They are drawn greatly. They are bright yet not colorful. The enemies are greatly drawn (by mouse I think). The animations are also greatly drawn and set.
**Media-**
The music is original. They go excellent with each board but is low in volume. The sound effects are great too. They go along with what is happening.
**Plot-**
The story is original. Your mission is to get 3 scrolls back from an evil wizard. Then the story suddenly changes to another plot. The ending will surprise you.
**Programming-**
This game is greatly programmed. The menu and battle systems are custom programmed. The bag and equipment are text based and specified (which means that they are certain items you can get). Also the setting of images great also (i.e. the dragon on the castle).
**Game Play-**
Excellent game play. The character moves at a decent speed. The enemies popping out are significantly timed at a civilized moment. Also the part of transforming into a dragon is well written.
**Fun Factor-**
This is a really fun game. The map is large (especially by boat) though the world itself is rather small. Though you may get stuck on some places, it gets easy when you find the answer to the problem.
**Bugs-**
There are a couple of bugs. When you reach a certain level, you start to level up faster in every battle turning you invincible. Also when you play for a long time, the graphics in the menu start to get messed up. Saving and quitting and running the game again can fix this bug.
**CONCLUSION-**
Awesome game. A definitely must play when you are feeling bored or what to know how to make you’re own battle system.
**Rating: 8/10**
This Review is written by TK Game Boy, dated June 4, 2002
