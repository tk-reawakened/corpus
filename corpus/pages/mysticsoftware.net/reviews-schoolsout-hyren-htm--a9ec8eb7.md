**Schools Out**
Reviewer: Hyren Game Author: Phred
**Story 4/10**
From what I could tell, you start out as Super Randy and then have to go save the school. But you can't because when you try to leave, you get stuck on the stairs!
**Graphics 3/10**
Let's just say that original graphics aren't always the best choice. Board drawing wasn't very good: the tileswere scattered recklessly over the area of all 3 boards.
**Music 5/10**
The music was okay, but not original.
**Enemies 4/10**
Uh, he used the enemy from the Jewel of Destiny and even named it the same: Evil Thing. There were also digimon and scorpions with a mario character next to them.
**Programming 4/10**
There wasn't much of it besides the message window.
**Fun 1/10**
Boooooooooooriiing. I don't consider 3 boards fun. It ranked right up there with Mini-Cars.
**Originality 8/10**
Hmmmmm. This game was original alright.
**Intro 6/10**
It looked like there was an intro of some sort with the tower...
**Items 0/10**
There were none.
**Bugs 3/10**
I couldn't get off the stairs! That's where my game ended.
**Overall 38/100**
Hey, it was better than Mini-Cars. Just not much.
This Review is written by Hyren, dated June 21, 2002
