**SkateWorld**
Reviewer: Marcus Lewton Game Author: Zepher (SSJGohan)
**Story, What's it all about? (2/10)**
Story? There is no story, basically you go around a few boards and well, Skate.
**Boards (2/10)**
Basically there are no boards; each board looks exactly the same there is a selection of different concrete floors, made out of the default tileset.
**Graphics (3/10)**
Nothing special at all, the colours of the tiles are mixed well though. The only good graphic in the whole game is probably the one shown below:
This is one of the games best bitmap image. In fact it's the only bitmap image the game has.![](../images/skate.gif)
**Items (1/10)**
Lets just say there are only two items with the names: Item 1 and Item 2
**Battles (0/10)**
There are none…
**Enemies (0/10)**
Huh… again there are none.
**Programming (4/10)**
Well, nothing special but it is different so I got to give some credit
**Bugs (9/10)**
Finally! Something positive about the game.
**Overall**
It's different… and is pretty addictive but not in the "fun" game play way. Nevertheless it is very original and makes a change from the final fantasy want to be games. Download this only if your looking for some thing very different…
**Final score 21/80 (26%)**
This Review is written by Marcus Lewton, dated June 21, 2002
