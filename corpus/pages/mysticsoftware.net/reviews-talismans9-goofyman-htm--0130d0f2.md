**Talismans of the Nine**
Reviewer: Goofyman Game Author: Tyrant Trenchcoat
**--Intro--**
The storyline isn't quite clear yet. All that is known is that something evil is happening, and a certain kingdom might be to blame. Their over-surplus through the past time has been incredible, to the extent that something evil MUST be somewhere in the group.
This storyline is a semi-original one. I would say, that I have never seen a game where 'you' must help 'hope' and 'hope' will help 'you'.
**--Ratings--**
**Music: 4/5**
The music was good, but it just got annoying after a while. I liked the music in the other life santuary though.
**Programming: 3/5**
This game had 3 bugs from the part I reached. The first one is in the second part of the castle, when you pick up the backpack, the program never dissapears, and you can continue eternally to collect potions (I stopped at 86). Also, while fighting the ninja, for some reason, when the Message Window was open, the enemy's fighting stopped. No attacking was done, and I was allowed 9 turns before I closed the message window and continued the fight. The third was after I died in the battle against the Death Spirit. For some reason, after my defeat, the screen went crazy and restarted and went back, repeating this process for another minute.
Other than that, the game was quite nice program wise, but I didn't see much use of the name you put in at the start. I might not have been looking hard enough though...
**Overall 'look': 4/5**
The tiles were brilliant, the character was good, but some tiles seemed out of place. The black that is the edge of the bridge should be changed to something more 'texture-ee'. What I like about the look is that everything goes together. The flow isn't 100%, but I would say that it isabout 79-82% flow-worthy.
In conclusion...
TotN got a on my scale. **12 out of 15**
This Review is written by Goffyman, dated June 27, 2002
