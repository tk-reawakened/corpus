Andromeda Sky
demo 1
20 July 2004
plot
> Great beggining for this game. The story looks exciting and dialogs are perfect. Actually many should play this game to get an exemple of a well-introduced story ! Music is very well chosen and just add something to the atmosphere. However the very classic ripped tiles look quite boring and I hope they will be replaced sometime later. Boards are ok but not perfectly drawn. There was an annoying bug at the beggining of the game that made you restart a sequence when you walk near the tree. Besides, it's ok but i couldn't find the menu key. Battles really need work...Sort of long and boring. I know it's only the beggining so we can't really judge but I suggest reducing ennemies' HPs down. Interseting game to test, because with some minor and major improvements it could become one of the best TK game. A good example of what you can do with the Toolkit and a good story, without having great artistic or programming skills. If some of you have, then you should give a hand to this exciting game.
> A quite long and surprising game that everyone should play. The story isn't great so far but still ok, it looks quite promising. Dialogs aren't always great, but there are many surprises in the plot and that makes the game quite fun to play.Fights are ok. About graphics, it depends. Sometimes quite poor but several boards are very nicely done. Talking animals are amusing.

> Overall a pretty good game.
