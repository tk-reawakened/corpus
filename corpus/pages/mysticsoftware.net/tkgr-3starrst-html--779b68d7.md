Silver Sky
demo 1
November 2002
story
> With a strong story and nice plot, the game begins on an introduction which explains a little what's happening...But the player doesn't know much about the world he plays in. The tiles are original, and very good besides some stuff. Other graphics aren't always great, text is too big, etc. But overall the design of the game is better than usual. The large boards make the game quite slow, and there are several RPGCode bugs and mistakes. Still, the game is very fun and got great potential. Needs work and longer time of play =)
> In this game everything fits together, a bit like Dustball, but in a different mood. Tiles are original, nice (but not perfect) and the music is very good. The best thing in this game will probably be the story, as it seems to be *very* good, with excellent dialogs. The players gets progressively the plot, which makes the game look very profesionnal. Too bad the demo was a bit too short...

> This game is very original, at least. There are some bugs in the demo and almost no story, but it introduces us to the game. First, graphics were just great, some aren't very good but most of them are perfect. The game is cool, you're a thug and you wander in the street, well that's the game's concept and it's original. Dialogs are in the mood of the game, and that's a good thing =) So the game has a good potential, but isn't perfect.
3 stars
