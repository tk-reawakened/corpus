# - u p d a t e s - last update : 13rd of August 2004

![](tkgrb.jpg)
Welcome to the Toolkit Game Reviews website ! Here is THE place to read tons of quick reviews about RPGToolkit-made games, as I'm trying, with the great help of MysticSoftware, to review every single game in our beloved community ! The purpose is to give readers a quick preview of what they're going to play, and to the game makers it is a way for them to see that their game has been played by at least 1 player !
I try to make constructive critics, but if you're offended by any of my reviews or have something to say about one of them, please post a message on the forum, I'll always be there to answer any question. One last thing, for the readers as for the game-makers : i'm still only giving my personal opinion, and although I sometimes give suggestions and things that could be improved, it must be taken in a positive way : all I want is to help TK users to improve their games, and to congratulate or thank for their hard work ! Finnally, please apologize for my english which could be sometimes of a bad quality, I try my best :).
