Now that this board has become a bit popular, it is time to lay down some ground rules. This is to help everyone have an enjoyable experience.
1. Adult images = NO. Spyder has requested that this board remain PG13. Rackhost, the host for MysticSoftware, does not allow adult and explicit content. Thus, I've disabled all the adult flags. Failure to follow this first and most important rule will result in a ban. Spyder has been more than generous in lending the space for the board, let's try to be mature and respect his decision in this matter.
2. Constructive criticisim. There is a line that many people cross when commenting on artwork. It goes from helpful comments to flaming. *I* will not tolerate it. Flaming will result in a ban. Helpful comments, or just friendly comments only please.
3. Drawings only. We do have a limited amount of space and bandwidth. Therefore simple scribbles that don't make any sense or take any shape or form will be removed. Multiple offenses will result in a ban. I want to keep this board, and I don't want Spy angry that I used up all his bandwidth for the month. If I can't tell what you drew, it will be removed. Sorry, but that's how it goes.
4. Have fun. This is a sketch board for people who want to sketch or draw and have someone else give them good constructive criticisim.
I'm an easy going guy, so long as you follow the rules. I appreciate the effort in following them. Happy sketching! ~Walker-san
