# Government of the Off Topic Republic

Document prepared by Colin on August 2nd, 2005
The Off Topic Republic is governed as a Constitutional Monarchy, based on the principles of Republicanism and separation of powers, with Emperor Diddums as its reigning Head of State. The organization of Government is illustrated hierarchically . [here](files/otr_gov.PNG)
Emperor Diddums
Emperor Diddums is an embodiment of the Off Topic Republic, capturing the spirit of the country and showing it to the outside world. His Excellency possesses the power to:
- swear new members into His Privy Council;
- dismiss members from His Privy Council;
- create new committees of His Privy Council;
- remove existing committees of His Privy Council;
- appoint counsellors to and dismiss counsellors from committees of His Privy Council (counsellors dismissed from a committee retain their position in the Council, unless the Emperor dismisses them from the Council too);
- appoint new members to the National Assembly; and
- dissolve Government.
In practice, however, His Excellency will execute these powers only by and with the Advice of the Premier. These powers should be used unilaterally only to stop such a blatantly stupid move as appointing a repeated spammer to the Privy Council. When in doubt, the Emperor will accept the Advice. Although He acts as part of a system of checks and balances, He will not ignore Advice when He merely disagrees with it.
A bill passed by National Assembly does not become law until it receives Royal Assent from Emperor Diddums. His Excellency will decide whether to grant Assent by and with Advice from His Cabinet. The Cabinet must agree unanimously; whether it is agreed is decided by the Premier.
Because His Excellency is immortal, succession to the throne is not a concern. A bill could be passed to change the Emperor or modify how the Emperor is chosen or how long His or Her term is, however. As with any other bill, it would need to be granted Assent by the Emperor before coming into effect. Such a bill should generally be viewed with suspicion, and it is the Emperor's prerogative whether to grant Assent.
National Assembly
The National Assembly of the Off Topic Republic is the legislative branch of Government. All moderators as of August 2nd, 2005 are entitled to one seat per person in the National Assembly. But future members of the Moderation Committee need not be granted a seat in the National Assembly, and future MLAs (Members of the Legislative Assembly) shall not be granted moderating powers. Members of the National Assembly are appointed by and with the Advice of the Premier.
Seats in the Assembly are for life. A member can only be removed with the agreement of two thirds plus one of the members of the Assembly. There are fifteen seats in the House, but this can be changed with an Act. Members have the right to the title “MLA” after their names (e.g., Colin, MLA).
The job of the National Assembly is to create laws that are “needed” for the Republic—that is, laws must not be arbitrary. If bills central to a Government's programme fail to pass, the Government is dissolved. A law is enacted in several steps.
First, a bill is introduced to the Assembly. There are two kinds of bills: Government Bills, which are introduced by the Cabinet as a whole (whether the cabinet is agreed is decided by the Premier); and Private Members' Bills, which are introduced by individual MLAs. Bills should be written with an optional preamble, and the enacting formula, “His Excellency The Emperor Diddums, by and with the Advice and Consent of the National Assembly of the Off Topic Republic, enacts as follows:” followed by what the bill will do should it be enacted.
All members are free to vote by their conscience on Private Members' Bills, but any Ministers who are also MLAs (Ministers do not need to be MLAs, but they can be) must either vote in favour of Government Bills, or resign their Cabinet post.
The bill is then read a first time. After the reading, members may propose amendments to the text. Amendments will be made if fifty percent plus one of the members agree. The possibly amended bill is read again, and a vote takes place. If fifty percent plus one of the members vote in favour of the bill, it is sent to Emperor Diddums for Royal Assent. Otherwise, the bill is defeated. If the Emperor grants Assent, the bill is enacted and thereafter styled as an Act.
There is nothing about Government that cannot be changed with a bill.
Privy Council
Emperor Diddums' Privy Council is the executive branch of Government. The Privy Council is collectively responsible for enforcing the laws of the Off Topic Republic, and managing the Republic's day-to-day affairs. The Council is an administrative body designed to provide the Emperor with Advice. Members of the Council are called Privy Counsellors, and they have the right to a “PC” after their names (e.g., Colin, PC).
Every member of the council is permitted to modify pages of the Republic and use the files tool for uploading content without prior discussion, but the other duties of the Council are delegated to its committees.
Because the Privy Council is integral to Government, it is responsible to the National Assembly. If an MLA thinks that the Privy Council is doing an inadequate job of enforcing the laws and otherwise administrating the Republic, he or she may attempt to pass a Motion of No Confidence. Such a motion is worded, “Do you have confidence in His Excellency The Emperor Diddums' Government?” If half the MLAs plus one do not have confidence, Government is dissolved.
Moderation Committee
The Moderation Committee handles the moderation of the Republic's forums. At present, this consists exclusively of deleting inappropriate posts, but moderation abilities may be extended in the future.
All moderators as of August 2nd, 2005 are entitled to a position on the Moderation Committee, and will be sworn into the Privy Council, but they may lose their positions if the National Assembly has no confidence in Government.
Cabinet
Members of the Cabinet (Ministers) are responsible for ministries of the Republic (e.g., Ministry of Webmastery). The Premier is head of the Cabinet. Thus there is only one fixed ministry, that of the Premier, and the Premier may advise Emperor Diddums to create any number of other ministries. Ministers are entitled to the style The Honourable, and the Premier is entitled to The Right Honourable (e.g., The [Right] Honourable Colin).
The Cabinet collectively introduces Government legislation, and it must unanimously agree on legislation that it is introducing. Whether the Cabinet has agreed will be decided by the Premier. Any Ministers who are also MLAs (though Ministers do not need to be MLAs) are required either to vote for Government Bills or resign their Cabinet post.
Dissolution of Government
Privy Counsellors do not serve for a set term. Dissolution of Government causes Privy Counsellors to lose their status and an election for Premier to be called. Government is dissolved when:
- legislation central to Government's programme fails to pass;
- an MLA moves that there is no confidence in Government, worded, “Do you have confidence in His Excellency The Emperor Diddums' Government?” and half the MLAs plus one do not have confidence; or
- the Premier requests a dissolution of Government from Emperor Diddums and it is granted.
In the first two cases, an election is held immediately to appoint a Premier. If the Premier requests a dissolution, however, he or she may set that date of the election, allowing for a campaign.
