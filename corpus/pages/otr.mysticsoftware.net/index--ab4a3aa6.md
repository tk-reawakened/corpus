1998-2003: The Rpgtoolkit once had an off-topic forum, which was maintained by an Ex-Rpgtoolkit member named, “Kaptan”. A well standing leader, who often visited the off-topic forum himself. At this time, all Rpgtoolkit forums were in threaded format. For the duration of this time, the rpgtoolkit.com site underwent changes from the black and red style to its sky-blue and white. Despite the layout’s change, the Official Rpgtoolkit off-topic forum remained black, and red. During this time, CBM (Christopher Matthews) owned the Rpgtoolkit, and maintained the rpgtoolkit.com website once in awhile.
CBM announced his resignation, and gave Vampz along with TKZ (a resource-site at that time) full entitlement of being the new official home to the Rpgtoolkit. At that time, CBM departed, giving Vampz full control, and authority of the Rpgtoolkit Community. **2003:**

Only a few of us remained, and have decided to pledge loyalty to the once former official Rpgtoolkit off-topic forum.
Over time, Vampz progressively eliminated contact with this forum entirely. At first, he had links which linked to the threaded forums of the Rpgtoolkit. Then one day, he removed the links that linked to the former official Rpgtoolkit off-topic forum.
We were banished from the Rpgtoolkit community.

**Experimental Project 4:** *The Number Game.*
An Rpgtoolkit off-topic long-standing ritual where a person posts one number, and the person, responding to that post posts a number that is one more than the previous post.
Ie. Bob posts number “5”. The person responding to Bob’s posts a post with a subject heading, “6”. Then the person responding to “6”, responds with a “7”. And so on.
