Five Pillars of TK

- Acceptance of ignorance as the path to enlightenment.
- Refusal of Cherry Coke and Goldfish Crackers as sustenance.

Four Gooey Truths
- No project is ever finished.
- Projects are not finished because they are too ambitious.
- Ambition can be crushed by humiliation.
- The only projects that are completed are those that humiliate their creators.
Ten Commandments of Goo
- I am the Lord your Goo.
- You shall have no other Goos before Me.
- You shall not make wrongful use of the name of the Lord your Goo.
- Remember the sabbath day, and keep it gooey. Six days you shall procrastinate all your work. But the seventh day is a sabbath to the Lord your Goo; you shall continue not to do any work. For in six days the Lord made RPG Toolkit, and all that is in it, but tested the seventh day; therefore the Lord blessed the sabbath day and consecrated it.

- You shall not murder, except to stab Marz.
- You shall not commit adultery, except with an enemy's mother.
- You shall not steal, except in the case of music, movies, and video games.
