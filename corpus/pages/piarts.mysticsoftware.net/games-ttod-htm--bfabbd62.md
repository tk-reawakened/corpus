# -Affiliates-

The Trio of Destruction (A Heraldry Sidestory) Title:
PC Platform:
RPG Genre:
: 1 Player/s
On a bright sunny day, we find our Trio in the western part of Skyra traversing a hanging bridge. Brought about by a comically unfortunate accident, the bridge fell down leaving our heroes at the mercy of the raging current below. They eventually come across a remote village which they shall later learn to be known as Irolan; a place that would open up a whole series of interesting adventures for them. Learn more about the forgotten Mystic Cave to the west, where powerful monsters lurk. Venture the Ta Ku Gei ruins to the south, home to the ancient Dragoons of the past. And finally, marvel at the Grenam Pron Dungeon, Irolan's pride tourist attraction. Built by the mad wizard Carty, our heroes shall take up the challenge of conquering this 10-storey tower in search of the elusive treasure found at the top.
This game is made with the RPGToolkit v3.0.6. The game features custom menu system, custom turn-based (Phantasy Star style) battle system, custom shop system, and a custom save/load system. The graphics features colorful tile-based boards and gives the appeal of a styled SNES RPG. The musical score is of orchestral in nature that aims to help immerse the player in the experience.
