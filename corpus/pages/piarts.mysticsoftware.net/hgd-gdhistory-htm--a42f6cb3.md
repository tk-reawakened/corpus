# Welcome to the Online Roleplaying world of Heraldry:Great Divide.

![Picture Here](sign.gif)
Welcome to the Online Roleplaying world of Heraldry:Great Divide.
The time is 3246 A.D.---a new 'living' planet is found in the galaxy Andromedae. Discovered by the Great Explorer Mackenzie Powell of the space fleet Ambition, the planet was proven hospitable to human existence. As Earth is massively overpopulated, the elites decided to migrate from Earth to Nahcram (the name of the planet, given by Powell, of course). They (the Elites) landed on a savannah-like terrain, and there decided to make their city--Lucero. Not a week has passed when they spotted four more aliencrafts on landing course to Nahcram--the Erthkeepers, the Jinnees, the Ruecians |ru-shans|, and the Croissans |kro-yans|. alien lifeforms have already been confirmed way back in 3000 A.D., so this was not a surprise. Following the rules of planet conquest, the aliens agreed that the planet be called Nahcram, but since the Humans, have not yet established full authority over the planet (and full authority is given after at least 1 month), a territorial treaty is set: the humans have the savannah and the desert, the Erthkeepers have the dense vegetation down south, and the Croissans have the floating Earth masses--this is the left continent or what was called Feather side (as its shape resembles that of a feather); the ruecians decided to take the mountainous continent (nobody else wanted it anyways), and it was called the Lion side (the continent resmbles a lion's head). The jinnees held dominion over the ocean between the two continents. On this setting will rise the epic story of truth and power
THE HUMANS
Having 3246 years of technology and civilization, the humans have mastered on the following :
- Laser technology
- Robotics
- Nanotech
- Space travel
They live in sectors and are methodically arranged in a circular manner, on which is surrounded by a sphere of shield. This is Lucero. The head of this race is Xavier Lucero.
Rewards:
- +5 for every experience gain
- -2 to research duration
Penalties:
- No HP/MP regeneration
- +10 dymnos for MP healing price
To be a member:
- Name
- Occupation
- Machinist- Mechanical experts who are able to pilot any given craft. Can be used for both combat and engineering.
- Medic- Masters of the art of healing. Increases the curative power of all medicines. Lowers the appraisal value of medicines. Can also engage in combats.
- Artillery- Main offensive unit. Can engage in meelee and ranged combat.
- Research- Intelligence and economic unit. Cannot engage in combat. Shortens the time it takes for researches to complete, and lowers the research cost.
- Specific skill (in one of the domains above)
- Sector No. (the first member will be the Mayor of the said sector) 1 to 9 only
THe ERTHKEEPERS
The guardians of life, Erthkeepers hold dominion over beasts and flora. They have mastered the following:
- Beast manipulation
- Plant manipulation
- Regeneration
They are born from seeds from a 'Garden'. This is Kojima. Headed by GaiNu Cadmia.
Rewards:
- +2 for every experience gain
- -10 dymnos for HP healing price
- HP/MP regeneration (+1 per day)
Penalties:
- N/A
To be a member:
- Name
- Occupation
- Ai--Beast Tamers. Offensive purpose. Has the ability to perform medical enhancements, such as increasing the curative power of a medicine, and lowering the price of a medicine.
- Nai--Plant Manipulators. Offensive purpose. Hasthe ability to perform medical enhancements, such as increasing the curative power of a medicine, and lowering the price of a medicine.
- Sai--Bot knowledgable of beast and plant technology. For research purposes. Cannot engage in combat. Enhances both engineering and medicine fields.
- Pack Name (for beast tamers only) up to 4 pack names only. The first to declare a pack name will be the leader of his/her pack
- Garden name (for plant tamers only) up to 4 garden names only. The first to declare a garden name will be the leader of his/her garden
THE CROISSANS
Winged creatures inhabiting the floating lands. Harnessing the ancient Crest Technology, they are experts of the following:
- Transfiguration (any magical beast)
- Crest Magick
- Flight (Croissan dragon taming)
Set in a monarchaic government of Skyra. The queen is Increstia.
Rewards:
- +5 HP modifier
- HP regeneration (+2 per day)
Penalties:
- No MP regeneration
To be a member:
- Name
- Occupation
- Herald--Magick users. Offensive purpose. Enhances the field of medicine.
- Horns-- Transfigurators. Built for both combat and engineering purposes.
- Tyds--Research personnel. Cannot engage in combat. Enhances research works.
- Specific skill (in one of the domains above)
- Croissan Dragon name
- Dragon Herd name (up to 6 names only) The first to declare a herd name will be the leader of his/her herd
THE JINNEES
Entities of pure energy, they have lived since time immemorial. Unfortunately, they haven't progressed much. They mastered the following:
- Energy
- Levitation
- Teleportation
Living in the center of the ocean, they built a floating city called Atlantius. The queen is Sanne Cresencia.
Rewards:
- +10 MP modifier
- HP/MP regeneration (+1 HP, +3 MP per day)
- -10 dymnos for MP healing price
Penalties:
- +3 to research duration
To be a member:
- Name
- Occupation
- Djinn--Progenitors. Magick users. Offensive unit. Enhances the field of medicine.
- Levant--Warriors. Experts in both melee and ranged combat. Offensive unit.
- Aspis--Research unit. For economic and engineering purposes only. Cannot engage in combat.
- Specific skill (in one of the domains above)
- Temple name (up to 6 names only) The first to declare a herd name will be the leader of his/her temple
THE RUECIANS
Born of gems, these creatures live at a very old age, or until their birth gems are destroyed. They mastered the following:
- Earth labyrinth (underground siege)
- Gem Lore
- Twin Gem (creature familiar)
Living in the mountain ranges, they established their city at the heart of Mt. Julius, where gems are rich. They called it Ohollah. The ruler is Queen Diamond.
Rewards:
- +10 for every dymnos gain(rewards not included) per head per week
- Doubles dymnos acquisition, max HP/MP, -2 to research duration(If inside the camp perimiter or at most one block away from the town center, and base is situated within mountain ranges).
- MP regeneration (+1 per day)
Penalties:
- No HP regeneration
To be a member:
- Name
- Occupation
- Miner--Miners. Built for both engineering and combat. Gives a +5 dymnos bonus per miner per day.
- Mage--Mages. Offensive unit. No meelee or ranged physical combat ability. Enhances the field of medicine.
- Alchemes--Rresearch unit. No combat ability.
- Specific skill (in one of the domains above)
- Birth Gem
- Gem class (up to 6 classes only) The first to declare a herd name will be the leader of his/her class
