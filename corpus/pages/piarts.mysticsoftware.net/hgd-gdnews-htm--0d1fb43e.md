# Activities

All times in GMT+8
Week Reset
March 14(3:36AM)
- Game reset.
- All clans have been reset, all characters are Level 1 again, all clans have no research. Each clan has recieved 500D and 20 of each resource.
- Destruction of the Inactive Clans
- Earlier today Xavier Lucero, the Leader of the Human settlement on Nahcram confirmed that Sector's 1-4 have been destroyed in a violent explosion. The cause of this explosion is unknown and currently being investigated. Lucero has decided that it is not in his best interests to rebuild these colonies at this time, and has renamed Sector 9 to Sector 1, Sector 7 to Sector 2, Sector 8 to Sector 3, and Sector 6 to Sector 4.
- Coincidently, the Prime Minister of Croissia has confirmed the loss of Shadowwing, Tyrant Skies, and Coronus clans. He was pleased to inform the public, however, that a new clan has emerged. Xavin Warriors has recently been formed and it's location shall be made public sometime later this month.
- GadMu Kadia has announced that she has lost her only pack, Gru, and has no replacement ready at this point in time. She also regrets that Taknu has gone missing and if anyone sees him to please contact the Bleeding Gardens clan immediately.
- Rumors of Boaz's disappearence have been confirmed as well. The young Reucian leader told his younger brother Alpha that he was leaving to seek enlightenment. When Alpha followed him to see where he was going, he witnessed Boaz ascend into the heaven on a beam of light. Alpha says he will return someday when the other world rulers return from their long absense.
- Several underwater explosions have been detected as well. The cause is also unknown, but after these explosions Me-Ow-Z and Zapham are nowhere to be found.
- Finally, a Human explorer team has discovered a new location. Ahrid Kahn, the infamous temple of Martial Arts mastery, has been located after Kahn concealing it from the public since it's building. It is located on the northwest continent for those interested.
November 9(10:00AM)
- Spawning Monsters
- News has broken out that monsters started spawning at random areas and at a terrible unrest. Not one week pass that there's no village being plundered and mercilessly destroyed. Although they are not known to attack fortresses. Chances are they only take advantage of smaller communities, mostly of 10 people maximum. Due to their experience in pillageing they have acquired numerous treasures. Although they specifically have no use for them, they still take the items and keep them.
Treasure Hunting October 26(10:03 AM)
- The first item is the Wisdom Ore. This artifact grants the Hunter instant completion of any research. Even if the research is newly proposed, when a Wisdom Ore is used, it is completed instantly. Normally, Items are open for hunting only one at a time....but as it seems, the races have not yet progressed to move on all land types...so there will be 4 Wisdom Ores!
