# Membership

GAME RULES : - - - - - - - -MembershipResearchEngineeringCharacter GrowthBattleCamp PlacementMovementModeratingElement Chart
Membership
- 5 people per sector/pack/temple/herd/gem.
- Each clan has its own set of money bags. Currency is in dymnos.
- To be a leader, one must declare his/her own clan. Unless the clan limit for a particular race has been reached, the new clan will be recognized. Members who wish to join a clan may simply declare the clan of their choice.
- In the event that the clan leader is no longer efficient, clan members may autonomously dethrone the new one, whether by force, by democracy, etc. Subject to final approval by the moderator.
- Members may change their clan dynamically. They simply declare their clan of choice, however, the clan to which this member would group into will suffer a penalty.
- Internal disputes are out of the moderator's responsibility. Each clan leader will be held responsible for his/her clan.

Research
- Clans with researcher get a -1 bonus to research duration(per researcher). However, there is a minimum of 1 day.
- Researchers cannot fight on tactical battle events.
- Research activities must be declared(posted). Research is subject to the approval of the clan leader and finally the game moderator.
- The moderators will reply along with sanctions (approve, revise or deny). Once the specifics are done, moderators will post an official statement that allows the researchers to begin their work. The research, once completed will be posted at the Forum, and the Great Divide Home Page.
- Shared research is also allowed. As long as the clans are of the same race, they may declare a shared research. This will substantially shorten their time, but research price will not go down. Instead, it shall be divided into the number of requesting researchers.
- Technology buying is also allowed. This is available for economic research only, and for every clan/race. The owner of the technology shall be the one to declare how much their technology costs. The buyer will have to negotiate. Basic technologies for offense and defense can also be bought(e.g. Laser Plasma technology is only for HUMANS and is impossible for a JINEE to buy or harness. Improved mining, however, can be harnessed by ALL races and therefore can be bought).
- Research will not begin until there is sufficient funds.
- Researcher can research technologies for any type of race. If the technology is not compatible with their clan/race, they cannot use it, but it can be sold.

Engineering
- Building establishments do not require engineers. However, construction suffers a +5 days to duration in the absence of an engineer.
- The number of engineers do not affect construction.
Character Growth
- Levels are important to characters in GD if they want to access better skills. A level determines the strength of skill a character can use. If the level requirement is met, the character can now use the skill. To level up, a character must accomplish certain tasks, such as researching, skirmishing, attacking,defending, healing, or even movement to gain EXP. EXP determines when a character can level up. A character gains a level after acquiring 50 EXP. This required EXP is constant for all levels. RESEARCH can gain a character EXP by completing the research proposal. EXP gained is based on the level of research. For example, a research that has a prerequsite of lvl1, once completed, rewards the character with 1 EXP. If he researched a lvl2 skill, he will receive 2 EXP, and so on. Remember, a character only gains research EXP when the Research is completed. EXP is awarded to researchers only. In the event that there is no researcher in a clan, the EXP shall be awarded to the person who declared the research. SKIRMISH can give characters EXP by battling wild and hostile creatures in Nahcram. Skirmish EXP is based on how the character performed during skirmish. Skirmish EXP is gained by PM'ing the WIN CODE (see skirmish rules) to the mod (specif. khin). The mod will then reply with the experience gained by the character. ATTACK, DEFEND and MOVEMENT EXP are gained during Battle Scenarios only. ATTACK EXP is determined by the {level of attacked - level of attacker = (cannot be negative. if reached negative, 1 is given)*2 = total EXP}. For example lv1 attacked lvl3: that means lvl3-lv1=2*2=4 EXP. But say lvl2 attacked lvl1: lvl1-lvl=-1(this is negative, so we'll give)-->1*2=2 EXP. In short, the lowest EXP one can gain by attacking is 2 EXP. MOVEMENT EXP is gained every to blocks. When one reaches two blocks away in one turn, he'll receive 1 EXP. Remember:2 blocks in one turn--not every after 2 blocks. You cannot gain MOVE EXP when you moved 2 turns with 1 block each. DEFEND EXP is gained when you defend and get attacked. You get 1 EXP per attack received. HEALING EXP is gained both on Battle Scenarios or in Field. The EXP formula with HEAL EXP is the same with ATTACK EXP: <{lvl(attacked)-lvl(attacker}if negative, will be equal to 1>*2 = EXP. When one Heals inside SKIRMISH, he/she will not gain EXP.
- Experience(EXP) is gained for every research completion, puzzle-solving, technology bonus, mission completion, and skirmishes.
- EXP's are also given by moderators to those seen worthy.
- For every 50 EXP's the level of the player goes up.
- For every level up, HP and MP of a character will increase by 5.
- Players will recieve a 3 pts bonus to be distributed among the character status.
- The character status are explained by the following:
- STR- Strength. Raw attack power.
- STA- Stamina. Determines the strength of physical and spritual defense.
- WIS- Wisdom. Base attribute for magical offense and defense.
- MOV- Movement in the battlefield. Determines the maximum number of blocks one can move to during tactical battles.
- The compound status are affected by status effects, equipments, items, etc.
- ATK- The attack power. The amount of damage one character can inflict onto another.
- DEF- Physical defense. Subtract the attack value from this value to get the damage received by the attack target. Negative result means no effect.
- MAG- Magical/spiritual power. Enhances the effect of a certain spell. Add this value to the spell's base value to get the total possible damage inflicted.
- MDEF- Magic defense. This attribute is derived by getting the average between a player's STA and WIS. Subtract the magic value from this value to get the damage received by the speell target. Negative result means no effect.

Battle
- Fighting will be done in given scenarios.
- 2 Types of battles:
- Tactical Battles: Historical battles that occur between players only. Soon to be expanded.
- Skirmish Battles: Battles that occur between the player and non-players. May range from simple text-based battle( player vs mod) to interactive programs where you fight CPU-controlled opponents.
- 2 Types of Skirmish Battles:
- Scenario Battle Battle event that gets triggered from branching decision-making by the player. Occurs on scenarios and has no definite occurence limit. Does not consume a turn.
- Skirmish Battle Occurs when a player who steps at the same block as a skirmish icon(red monster icon) declares a skirmish battle. Consumes one turn.

Camp Placement
- Clans are to choose their placement in the land of Nahcram to be able to participate in various Great Divide activities. Even if the clan is registered, they will not be able to participate if they still haven't chosen a location to build their base. To place the base, declare the coordinates of the chosen block {horizontal,vertical}. Make a 16x16 flag. The background is your race color (look into the GD site for the race colors). NOTE: Only Leaders can choose. We will not entertain non-Leaders, unless they are given permission. Please PM your Leaders about this.
- A clan base initially takes up one (1) block in the map. This expands as the clan researches technologies that require usage of other blocks (e.g. marketplace, research towers, etc). Land requirements must first be met (e.g. must be in water, forest, etc.). For expansion buildings, a total of 4 structures are allowed per block. HUMANS can place their base on LAND. When research is complete, they can place their base ounder WATER and on MAGICK CONTINENT. They can never place their base on FOREST. ERTHKEEPERS can place their base on FOREST. When research is complete, they can also place their base on LAND with WATER. This gives AIs and NAIs access to Water-borne plants and creatures. When Erthkeepers place a base on Mountains, they gain access to Air-borne creatures. RUECIANS can place their base on LAND. When research is complete, they can place their base under WATER. When Ruecians place their base on Mountain, their mining skills are greatly increased (money gained is doubled, research time is reduced by 2, HP and MP is doubled). When at least one member leaves the base, their stats are normalized. Ruecians can never place their base on FOREST and MAGICK CONTINENT. JINNEES can only place their base on and under WATER. CROISSANS can only place their base on MAGICK CONTINENT. When research is complete, they can conjure more MAGICK CONTINENTs to live in. Unlike other races, CROISSANS do not need extra blocks for infrastructural add-ons.

- When a clan decides to transfer his/her base, A fee of 500 dymnos is required. The transfer target must be within the clan's movement range. If a clan declared a transfer without the required 500 dymnos, his/her base will be forfeited and the clan will be unable to participate until the 500 dymnos is met.
- A castle is built once the race completes the research. This takes up four (4) blocks. land requirements should still be met. Only one castle can be built per race. The castle gives the race additional rewards. It can also accomodate the whole population of the race at any given time.
- The base of the clan who chooses to reside in the castle will not be forfeited and can still function, this time as an encampment for traveling clans.
- If the block has more than 1/2 of the needed land-type (e.g. more than 1/2 is water, forest, etc.), the land requirement is met.
- Once a camp has been placed, clan members may begin Wandering enables players to work and earn income through agricultural or industrial means. To wander, simply declare your action into one of the following: *wandering.*
- Mining(+10 dymnos,+2 EXP,+1 Mineral Stock)
- Fishing(+7 dymnos,+3 EXP)
- Hunting(+5 dymnos,+5 EXP)
- Woodcutting(+3 dymnos,+2 EXP,+5 Wood Stock)
- Quarrying(+2 dymnos,+2 EXP,+5 Stones Stock)
- Wandering action should be logical to your location . Those in forested areas can either hunt or cut woods, those at sea shores or at the sea itself can fish, those in mountains can mine, and those in lowlands(drylands) can stone-quarry. One turn is exchanged for this, and will take effect on the next game update.

Movement
- Initially, a character can move 1 block per turn, and 1 block away is reached within 1 day. So if a character in {0,0} decides to move to {0,1}, he consumes 1 turn, and he'll reach {0,1} after 1 day.
- Intially, a character can move a maximum of three blocks in one week. This consumes all 3 turns alloted for him.
- There are researches that expand one's movement range. When such researches are completed, one may move 2,3 or more blocks per turn, or one may move to the block in less than a day (this skill is specifically useful in battle scenarios).
- In the event that "transport" researches are proposed and completed, masses of people can be moved in a single turn. THe turn consumed will be that of the one who proposed the move, or let us say "the Driver". All the people in the transport can be the driver. So for a transport with 5 people, a maximum of 15 blocks can be reached. This is also under the rule 1 block per day (unless of course a research compensates for this), which means the said transport will reach the block 15 untis away in 15 days.
- Even when the people inside the transport have movement enhancements (like additional blocks per turn or shortened duration of travel), the speed of the transport will be unnafected. To make transports faster, separate researches must be made.
- The map of Nahcram is, unfortunately, flat. It will require so much tweaking should we allow a spherical map, so if you want to go to the other side of the world, you'll have to cross it.
- Please be noted that we, the moderators, cannot watch over this 24/7. Certain skills, such as shortened travel duration, may be nullified in some cases when we cannot update right away (as said, this shortened travel duration is useful in Battle Scenarios, when the effect is readily updated). To be clear, the moderators are on from 10am-6pm from Mondays to Saturdays(GMT+8, Hongkong/Philippines time). No action updates shall be made on Sundays..that's when the official site is being updated. You can still make turns on Sundays, but they will not take effect until Monday.
- The world of Nachram is littered with treasures. Treasures are announced and most of the time accompanying clues are given. Players must make wild guesses or solve puzzles to find these treasures.
- If a player lands on a block with a treasure, the player shall be PMed saying that a treasure has been found. It shall be then given to the respective owner. The treasure on that said spot shall be nullified prior to the discovery.
- At given times instead of treasures, scenarios shall be encountered. Scenarios gives players a more immerse activity on a particular block. Scenarios are most likely roleplaying the character through given situations.

Skirmish
- Skirmish happens whenever a battle occurs between players and map characters. Player vs Player (PvP) is called , otherwise a . Skirmishes may be participated by everyone. Skirmish blocks are areas in the map with red icons on them. Some skirmishes are accessed through a scenario. **Tactical Battle** **Skirmish Battle**
- Normally, a character can Skirmish only once per week. For example, even if a character is 1 turn away from the Skirmish block, he needs 1 turn (2 more) to get there. He'll need 1 more (1 left) to access the SKIRMISH sim. After that, he'll only have 1 turn left. even if the other Skirmish block's 1 turn away, he'll get there, but will not be able to access the SKIRMISH sim. He'll have to wait next week.
- The only time a character can SKIRMISH 2 times per week is when he's already on the Skirmish block at the start of the week. He'll access the Skirmish Sim (1 turn used), go to the other Skirmish block 1 turn away (another 1 turn), and SKIRMISH again (last turn).

Element Chart
![](Heraldry_element_chart.jpg)
- Players(except humans) have elemental innates. Elemental innates take effect on battle events. The damage of magical attacks depend on the element of the spell and the innate of the receiver.
- Ruecians may choose Fire, Earth, Wind, Ice, Metal, Lightning, Time or Space as innate.
- Jinnees may choose Spirit, Wind, Ice, Holy or Dark as innate.
- Croissans may choose Earth, Fire, Wind, Ice, Dark, Holy or Ankh as innate.
- Erthkeepers may choose Earth, Wind or Lightning as innate.
- Humans cannot choose magic.
- There are some spells in an element that are only accessible to certain races. For example, Some Earth, Wind, Ice and Lightning Elements can only be acquired by Erthkeepers.
- When an arrow points to an element, it means the pointing element will make 2x damage to the pointed element.
- Moreover, the pointed element can only do 1/2 damage to the pointing element.
- Elements with no arrows pointing at each other will incur normal damage to each other.
- Every character has a spellbook allotment of 15 spells. He may fill this spellbook as he wishes (e.g. 15 basic magic or; 10 basic+5 intermediate or; 4 basic+7 intermediate+4 advanced, and so on), but he may not exceed the 15 spellbook allotment. Ultima and Summon spells are excluded.
- A character may learn other elements if he/she wishes to, Provided that it is within his/her limits. If his spellbook is full, then he must UNLEARN a chosen spell to make room for the desired one.
- A character may not learn spells from the element pointing at his innate.
- Advanced, Ultima and Summon Spells may only come from a character' innate.
- Advanced spells are included in the spellbook allotment.
- Ruecians may learn 2 Ultima, and 1 Summon. (The 2nd Ultima can be chosen from Fire, Earth, Wind, Ice, Metal, Lightning, Time or Space. A character may not learn Ultima from the element pointing at his innate.)
- Jinnees may learn 1 Ultima and no Summon.
- Croissans may learn 1 Ultima and 1 Summon.
- Erthkeepers may learn 1 Ultima and no Summon.

Moderating
- Moderators shall update the game weekly. Among the weekly updates will be money in stocks, technology researched, population, recent activities approved and disapproved.
- Moderators are present form Monday to Saturday at a regular time from 10 AM to 6 PM.
- Time Zone being implemented is in GMT+8(Hongkong/Philippines time).
- No turns are considered on Sunday(GMT+8).
- The amount of money will increase weekly depending on the population of the clan.
- 10 dymnos per head in a clan.
- Subject to rewards and penalties.
- A penalty is exercised by halving the dymnos earning of the player per day.
- Rules are subject to expansion as seen fit. However, there is a very low chance that a declared rule will be nullified, unless proven inefficient.
