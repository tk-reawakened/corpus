# A Place For My Head

Thursday, July 22, 2004
Mothers...
My friend Venus just had a baby girl last month, she's 21. I just found out today that my friend Stacy is now pregnant, she's 23. Neither of them wanted to be moms, yet both were having sex with no form of birth control whatsoever. Neither of the fathers want anything to do with the mother or the child. What the hell is wrong with people today? At this rate, by the time my self-esteem reaches a point where I'm able to talk to girls I have feelings for, it won't matter anymore, because everyone's going to be a parent already... Stacy doesn't know that I know she's pregnant, and I'm supposed to act surprised/shocked when she eventually tells me. Not that it should be hard...as I really don't know what to say.
