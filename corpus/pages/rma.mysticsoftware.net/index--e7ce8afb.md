# Story:

Just a fun game, your plane crashes on some weird location, monsters are everywhere, you need to kill them to get out, or run away but you can't hide... You are traveling home from vacation when in a lightning storm your plane crashes into the sea, you wash up on shore of a deserted island with no idea of where you are, but when you search the island you discover that the natives are hostile, the animals untame, and now all that you want is to leave it, but can you escape alive?
