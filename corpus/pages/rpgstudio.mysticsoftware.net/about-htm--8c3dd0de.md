# A picture of me:

have My name is Bjorn Johansson and I live in Sweden. I been using toolkit for about two years soon. I like to create RPG games and that is way RPG Toolkit is a perfect program.

![](ansikte.bmp)
But how did I find Toolkit from the beginning?
As a kid, I used to play the Zelda games. My favorite was, Zelda; A link to the past. I loved that game. And I hoped I could create my own games one day. So one day, after hours of Zelda playing. I started to search on the Internet after a game maker program. I did not know anything about any programs, so didn't think that I would find a program. First after searching on , I had the first result. It was "Game Maker". So I started. I reed throw a text of 30 pages and after that I start creating my game. It was a game like "Raptor", I was a airplane who destroyed other airplanes in the air. It was pretty fun but not what I wanted. I wanted a real Zelda maker. So I stopped using Game Maker, and started searching again. And I found RPG Toolkit. [www.download.com](http://www.download.com)
