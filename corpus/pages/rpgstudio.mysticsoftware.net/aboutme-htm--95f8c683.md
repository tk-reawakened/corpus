But how did I find Toolkit from the beginning?
s ed As a kid, I used to play the Zelda games. My favorite was, Zelda; A link to the past. I loved that game. And I hoped I could create my own game one day. So one day, after hour of Zelda playing. I started to search on the Internet after a game maker program. I did not know anything about any programs, so I didn't think that I would find a program. First after searching on , I had the first result. It was "Game Maker". So I started. I reed throw a text of 30 pages and after that I start [www.download.com](http://www.download.com) creating my game. It was a game like "Raptor", I was a airplane who destroyed other airplanes in the air. It was pretty fun but not what I wanted. I wanted a real Zelda maker. So I stopped using Game Maker, and started searching again. And I found RPG Toolkit.
A picture of RPG Toolkit:
I started to read about this program, in the help files. And I find it interesting and pretty fun. But I was not very good. A funny thing is this: When I finally started with Toolkit, I opened the "Tile Editor" and I made a sword and a title. Because I thought it was the title editor! But I know a lot more now. And I like to thank Christopher B Matthews for the features of RPG Games.
By The Way: RPG Toolkit changed my life 4-ever.
