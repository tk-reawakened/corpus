# Card Game

This is in the beginning of the card game. You can choose between six buttons, but you can only press three. So if you pressed three buttons, and if you find one sword, one arrow and a star. Then you have two bad and one good. But then you lose, because you need two good, two stars. This is all the buttons:
Bad buttons: Axe, Arrow and Sword.
Good buttons: Stars.
Other buttons: If you press on this button "?". Then you see if it have a good or a bad button in it. And after that the "pressed" button shows up on the button position.
![CardGame](cardgame2.bmp)
This is when you pressed two buttons and when you found one star and one bad button.
![CardGame](cardgame1.bmp)
You can play this game some times in the game. With NPC's and other characters.
