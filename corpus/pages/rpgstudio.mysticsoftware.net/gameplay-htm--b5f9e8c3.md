# Game Play

This game wont be like the rest of all RPG games, even if they're not created with I will create a game that will capture your heart. And take you to a adventure that no other games can do. This game will have the feeling from Zelda, the power from Diablo (but without the perfect real time battle system), and the adventure form me. [rpgtoolkit.](http://www.rpgtoolkit.com)
My game starts with a character that you can choose, you can be a boy or a girl, and they're both around the age of 20. So he starts in his little cottage were he lives with his family, his mother, father and his little sister. And he will enter the castle and get his biggest quest ever, to save the world from Ice Dremen.
The whole game will be custom. You will use the arrow keys to walk around, and you will also use the arrow keys in the menus because I don't like buttons any mroe.
There will be several keys that you can use during game play:
-
Arrows - To move the character
-
Enter - To enter the menu system
-
R - To refresh the screen form any buggs
-
Esc - For a fast ending, a menu shows up and you can choose between ending the game or return to the game
-
Space - A message window shows up and tell you what time it is and what name of the board it is
-
Shift - Will show you a map over the planet
I'm not sure if I should use a activation key yet. Because it just fit in to a few games, so I will see what I will do. And if so, then I put it in the space key and move the old key to m (=map).
The game is effected to day and night in the real world because it gives a nice feeling if you play on the night, it's to bad that it's not effected to months so if it is december it would be more white, like snow or something. Maybe in toolkit 4.
