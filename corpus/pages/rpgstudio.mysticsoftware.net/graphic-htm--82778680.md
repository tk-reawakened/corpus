Everything in my menu will be custom. This is the start graphic, that shows up when the player press "Enter".
![Start Menu](menu1.bmp)
This is the character menu, with a level up system in it:
![Character Menu](menu2.bmp)
You can see that the player have 6 stat points. The player gain 6 stat points when he grow a level so he is level 2 now. And if the player press the button "Level". Then you'll see the window on the right side. And the "Strength" text will become white. And if the player press the down arrow key, then the "Defense" text will become white. And if he then press "Enter". Then he will put one point on "Defence". But the player doesn't grow one on "Defense". But the number on the right, under "To Grow", will be 4 instead of 5, and when it's 0, then he grow on Defense so he have 11.
This is pretty nice, don't you think so.
