# Suggestions:

Ideas and Suggestions
Its not easy to create a game, or even know what the game shall be about. So I have some suggestion. So what shall your game be about?
- Year 100  Theres not many games that been created in this age, so this is a good age for you. This is before the -Vikings-. And the house should look very old, and they shall be of hand picked stone or three. And the character shall have simple armors or dirty cloths. This age is not that funny to play in. Because most of it is so old. But the nature is great.
- Year 800  There is a lot more game in this year. About 75 % I think. This is the perfect age if you want to have a warrior and fight against evil powers, like black dragons and evil wizards. But I suggest, if you use this age, that you dont have a evil wizard to fight against. Take a archer or a axe man. I dont know a game with a evil archer man so that would be great. The house is pretty easy to create for this age. Just make some tiles and put some lines in it. They shall look better (newer) then the houses in year 100. And also the NPCs and characters shall look newer. They would have more color on there cloths.
- Year 1500  I know a few games that are created with this age. Maybe 30 %. The houses could be made of -tegel-. And they have pretty nice windows. And there could be some fancy horse driven cars in the town. That would look really nice. And use high houses. Made of wood. And some white houses, that was popular on that time. And the NPCs and characters shall have a lot of colors on there cloths and long hair. Maybe you seen a movie, then you can take ideas from the graphic. I suggest you fight against a Dark Angel that came down from the heaven to rule the world. Thats new I think.
- Year 2000  This is the time right now. You know how it shall look. Large houses with large gardens. Football fields and sport centers. Food shops, and many other buildings. Maybe some industries. The forest should look pretty used. It shall have a color like this:![](idesuggest_files/image002.jpg) And the houses could be like they are now. Make a lot of different cars, that would look professional. And use sword, use guns. And maybe a body armor. There should be a gun shop, but you have to do a mission first so you can get permission to buy there. That would be fun to play. dont
- Year 3000  The feature can look however you want to. Because now body knows. It can be destroyed by a meteor. Or it can be great, with perfect nature. Or it can be totally different. People are flying around in small air plains. And the houses is of metal with a great security system. Use you imagination. This could also be different. You can fight against a terrorist force or maybe you are the chosen one. Or maybe you shall stop a meteor from hitting the planet.
This is some of many ideas. You can do your game however you want to, maybe you want to create a car game, thats not impossible. Its very simple. Or a air plan game. You dont even need to fight against anybody. Maybe you dont like battle. You can use different card games, and secret ways to pas the game. You can have some fancy home made systems. Like the character has to find five keys to open a door.
- What about the NPCs?
You shall have about 30 % childrens, 27 % men, 23 % women and 20 % of old people. Dont use two childrens and ten old guys. That would be crazy. This is an important thing.
- How shall the graphic be?
I suppose you draw you own graphic, thats the best way to create a game. And you will enjoy it much more. Its not funny to play a game with some others graphic. So use MS Paint or Adobe Photoshop, they are both good programs for creating tiles. You can also use the default tile editor. A tile have the size 32*32 pixel. You shall have about 2-4 different trees and about 5-7 different houses. If you have one tree and one house, that would look pretty boring. I think you can see it in the front of you. So create some nice small trees. That would be great. The character can have the same form. But change the cloths, from a jacket to a t-shirt, and a skirt And change the hair.

***Suggestions:***
- Dont use others story, create your own and make it good.
- You can use others ideas, but your game shall be yours so create your own.
- Draw your own graphic. Its easier to use others but its better to create your own.
- Try to make everything custom, its much funnier to play then.
- Create a game with a lot of large adventure.
- Dont create a lot of empty space in buildings and in the forest.
- Some NPC shall talk about the adventure, other about the weather.
- NPC shall move around, but some shall not move.
- Create a nice title of the game, maybe the world that they live in.
- Dont use the same music all the time.
- If you can, try to create your own music (midi files).
- The character speed shall not be to high. He shall walk pretty slow.
- Let the player choose name in the beginning of the game.
- Create the game after the story.
***Ideas:***
- Play games created by other toolkit users. And take ideas from good things and skip the boring and bad things.
- Try to play the Zelda games, they have a lot of great ideas.
- Its important to have a lot of secret areas, so make some in your game.
I know its not easy to find out good things to a game. Because there are thousands of games created. But you have to think a lot. Its very important.
