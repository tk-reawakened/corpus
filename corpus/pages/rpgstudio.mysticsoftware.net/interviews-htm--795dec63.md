Were do you get your ideas from?
-
T The best way to find ideas is to play other games. hat way you can write down the good things and what's bad. And then you can make your game with the funny things. But don't take everything from others game. It shall be your own game. And do not copy the story from anybody, make your own.
How do you draw your graphic?
-
I use Adobe Photoshop 7.0 and it's a very good and easy program to use. But it's not easy to find it if you don't have it. If you don't have Photoshop and still want to create your own graphic, then I would tell you to use the "tile editor", because it's a good and easy way to draw nice tiles.

T , Mysticsoftware or I don't think there is an answer on this question, but it shall have your own ideas, and things that you invented. It shall be a big adventure and it shall be funny to play.
