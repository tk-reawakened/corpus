# This game is not finished. I have just started. It will take me a time to finish it.

But you can download it here when it's finished.
Designer: Bjorn Johansson
Started: 1 January
Finished: --------
Story:
It was in the ancient ages of 750. There was a peaceful land called, Marovia. That land was divided into five territories, they had different names and different locations. Two was in north, they were called, Bevrias and Acrotia. And there was one in west, one in east and one in south. The one in south was called, Marviol. Master Evricos owned the two territories in north. He was a powerful and strong king who wanted to rule the world of Marovia. The territories in south, Marviol, was a great kingdom with loyalty and strength. Their king was Artimus. He had won battles in all ages, and he was tired. He did not know how long he could rule Marviol. He had to find a new king, a new fighter who was strong enough to take the place as a king. Marovia had never fight against a territory in Marovia, only territories in other lands like Levingard. Marviol had always been a peaceful place. It had green forests and it had blue water. The castle in Marviol was large, nearly the largest in Marovia, but the territory in north, Bevrias. Had a castle that was two times larger but it was hard for they to have guard because it was so big. So Marovia had the most safe castle. It was built my free peoples not by slaves.
At the same time, Master Evricos gathered his evil forces in Bevrias and they grew stronger for every day. The forces marched day and night and they knew that a war will start soon. He had decided to defeat the three other territories so he could rule the world.
Marviol, and the two other territories, in west and east, had no good contact. They didnt think that the northern territories would start a war against them. So as fast as they could, they start training warriors, archers, axmen and they send away the best fighters, to defeat the northern master. It went to Relamore a dangerous war, with power that no mankind could fight against. Master.Evricos, had found the magical armour, it made him immortal to sword, axe and arrows. But a arrow that been down in the deep ocean of . Had a special power. It could fly through metal, stone and even the hardest armour that was built in Marovia. But after one year of war. The northern army won. There was a few soldiers from Marviol that survived. They took their horses and went back home. They told the king about the war and that they did not have any chance to defeat them. The three territories, west, east and south felt helpless.
The rumours about the northern army spread through the world of Marovia and reached the forest, just outside Marviol. A warrior called William, heard about this. William had been a warrior all his life, but he was also very good at bows and axes. So he was a great fighter. But William had one secret to carry, one heavy secret that he had since he was born. He was a king, he was the true king of Marovia. Artimus was Williams father. But Artimus thought that his son died in a battle some years ago. But William ran away from the kingdom. He wanted to be on his own, he wanted to take one day at the time. Every day in the forest, William been training to be a better fighter. He was better then the best. And he decided to help the southern kingdom and the mankind, from Master.Evricos. He ran to the kingdom Marviol, and entered the great doors...
