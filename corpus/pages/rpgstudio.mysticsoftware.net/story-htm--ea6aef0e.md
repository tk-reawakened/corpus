# Marovia

*Ice Dremen*
This story begins far away from the earth and its mankind. All the way to the other side of the sun. And the planet is 10 times smaller then the earth and its called Marovia. The people on this planet are almost the same as the humans on earth. Except that the people are called Hambers, and they have different skills. Like some have water or fire knowledge. And some know about black magic. And there are also some normal Hambers that live exactly like the people on the earth. Without skills and without fear.
Marovia is in the ago of 600, it was created when a huge meteor crashed into another planet and it divided the planet into two pieces. One of the pieces is lost but one kept it life and they started all over again. But its not that peaceful and quite as many can think, because there is a wizard with a lot of power, who became really mad because the people didnt choose him to rule the world. So he ran away from the town. And up to the top of the dark mountain of Marovia. He stood there and reed a spell for 50 years. But nobody knew about this. So they lived in happiness until he was finished.
The year is now 650 and there are a lot of mystical things that appear on the planet. People disappear, towns are destroyed and many other things have attacked the planet. But nobody ever had seen anything, whats causing the damage. But there are a lot of roomers. Many people believe that the wizard resurrected the Ice Dremen. It was a creature who had a really powerful attack; he could freeze people in a distance of 100 meters. But the people forced him into the mountain in the age of 250. They had so much luck when they attacked him because a small meteor hits him, so he fainted and the people carried him deep down in the mountain. And they wont have that luck again. So there were a lot of people who was afraid and the land was in chaos.
So it needed its hero, but there were not many people that took the place on the competition field. There were four people, and there could only be one winner. There was one man who many people doubted on, because he was only seventeen years old, and the other guys was over 25. But strange enough, there was a girl. She thought she can compare with the guys, so we will she what happens to her.
So they had a hard mission in front of them and the one that passes throw the forest and throw the temple first wins. They are aloud one weapon. And they can choose between a sword, a bow, an axe or two knifes. And they can fight against each other, so it wont be easy. So we will now see if the young man can show if he is good enough or not, or maybe the girl will save the planet from the evil powers, nobody know yet
