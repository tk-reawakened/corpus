# World

![](maroviamap.bmp)
The map of Marovia.
Marovia is like many other countries. But the land is divided into five territories. And there are five kings who rule the land of Marovia. Marovia have two large oceans: Deep Gardio and Relamore. Both of the oceans have their different story. There is a mountain in the north, and there are five castles.
This was the ocean of power. Some people believe that a person who drink from the ocean, will die at once. Nobody have ever drink from the ocean. But there are one small village. East of the ocean, who have the ocean as a religion. They believe the ocean is the life power of Marovia. But nobody know if the story about Deep Gardio is true. The only way to know, is to try... Deep Gardio:
Nobody know if it's true. Nobody have the courage to dive in. Nobody have ever think of dive down to the bottom. The ocean is hundreds of meters deep. And the rumors is telling that there are a great dark power on the bottom. But once, for about 5000 years ago. there was a wizard who created a arrow.
