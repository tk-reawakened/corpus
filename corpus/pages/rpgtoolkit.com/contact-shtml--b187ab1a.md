# Your email address:

You're probably here because you want to email me (Chris Matthews), the creator of the RPG Toolkit. No problem! I think it's important that the autthors of all software be available for contact by email. I'm always here and I'll help you if I can. Try to keep this stuff in mind:
- I'm always welcome to suggestions.
- I get loads and loads of mail. So if I don't reply, don't think it's because I'm ignoring you. I try to read all of the mail I get, even if I don't reply to it all.
- Submit bugs on the bugreport form.
- I will not debug your RPGCode programs for you.
- I generally will not answer questions if the answers can be easily found in the help files for the Toolkit.
