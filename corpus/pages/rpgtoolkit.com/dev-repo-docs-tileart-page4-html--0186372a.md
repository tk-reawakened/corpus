# -Adopting a style-

**-Adopting a style-**
Adopting a style is very important if you're a beginer. If you pick a style, the best thing to do is to stick to it. Mastering both realiztic and cartoonish styles is a very hard and labourous task. You should adopt a style that you think that will be most useful to you. If you like modern or futuristic themes, then chances are, you'll adopt a more realiztic and dark style. If you like more of the middle age, then you might you'd most likely adopt a more cartoonish style.
**-The Cartoonish style-**
The Cartoonish style tends to have heads that are bigger than the body, or at least reasonablely large in comparison to the body. So, there's lots of room to fit big eyes, or other details on the face. So, the best thing to do at first is to get down the porportion of the characters in your game. The most important two porportions being, the head and body. Keep in mind however that the head must not be too exagerated, or it will not only make the character look bad, but will also make it extremely difficult to add the rest of the characters body exagerations (such as the feet or hands).
