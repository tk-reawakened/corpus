# Discussion

There is a very active community surrounding the Toolkit. It is the people who use it that make the Toolkit so fun! Here you can get in touch with other people in the community-- ask for help, advertise your game, and browse the current news in the community...
**rpgtoolkit.com Forums**
- Place for general RPG discussion. The main place for talking about the Toolkit. [General Discussion Forum](cgi-bin/acforum.cgi?forum=1000)
- Just learning the Toolkit, or want to give others a hand? This board is for you. [Help Board](cgi-bin/acforum.cgi?forum=1001)
- Most of the Toolkit's features started as user suggestions. [Suggestion Board](cgi-bin/acforum.cgi?forum=1002)
