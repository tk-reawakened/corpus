# Home of the Free RPG Toolkit Development System for the Creation and Play of your own RPG Games

**August 11, 2001 - get a couple more of these babies**
Well, I've just released the 2.18 bug-fixed version. Most of the issues people have been having have been resolved. If your items got messed over the last time you installed 2.18, copy your *old* item files back into your game, and when you run the new version of 2.18, it will convert them properly (you need the old item files because they have the images embedded within them).
Also, issues with players and items leaving trails behind them has been resolved, as well as some refresh issues with different commands in RPGCode.
I would suggest that most people will be using Parallax type 1 (that is, the Sprite Layer disabled, but parallaxing still enabled). This should provide enough speed on most systems. However, if you want to disable parallaxing entirely and turn it on mid-game, you can use the New command #Parallax(type!) and give it a number (0=parallaxing on, 1=sprite layer disabled, 2=parallaxing off).
I hope this version will be a lot more stable than the last one. I think that in the future I'll release betas before finalizing the release to catch the bugs. Enjoy!
Oh, and just as a matter of interest-- the Toolkit has reached 100,000 downloads over at . That, coupled with the high approval rating it's getting over there, leads me to believe that there's a lot of people out there who use it but never visit the boards.
