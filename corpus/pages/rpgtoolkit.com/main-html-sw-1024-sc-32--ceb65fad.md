official website moved
posted on 2004-02-14 20:04:12 by [cbm](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=51)
Hi, everyone. If you haven't noticed, this is cbm (Chris) talking. If you haven't gotten here through the rpgtoolkit.com domain, you will in the next few days. A few weeks ago I contacted Vampz to ask him if we could use the ToolkitZone as the new official community site. As most of you know, since I started working last year and now that I'm engaged, I don't have very much time for the Toolkit anymore. And I was never very good at maintaining a site :) Anyway, I intend to maintain the open source section of the site. My goal in open-sourcing the Toolkit was to become more of an administrator of the code to maintain the official distribution. For those of you who have made improvements to TK3, I thank you very much, and encourage you to submit your changes to me for inclusion in the official distribution for all to enjoy. The old website will remain up for a bit. In time I hope to use that webspace as more of a personal site. If you'd like to get there, the address is http://yukon.sytes.net A special thanks should go out to the entire ToolkitZone staff for stepping up to the plate to maintain the official online presence of the Toolkit!
