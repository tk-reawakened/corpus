Here are the answers to some questions you might have about registration... . The Standard and Community Editions of the Toolkit are and always will be free. But devoting time, money and bandwidth to the Toolkit is very very expensive for me. If you can help me, please do! . I accept donations through . If you can't afford to register, or if you just want to help me, please consider leaving me a small donation: . You can download free upgrades both for version 2 and version 3. . But there is a discount for previous version 2 customers who are upgrading. I ship by air mail or better to anywehere in the world. You will receive a labelled CD. If you ordered a manual, you will also receive a bound manual (celerox binding). . I have shipped the Toolkit all over the world, including the UK, France, Spain, Germany, Brazil and Australia. If you order through PayPal in US dollars, the amount will automatically be converted to your local currency by your credit card company. . All registrations are personal and I keep a record of who registered. You cannot share your registration with others. . I have it on record. If I recieve more money than I'm supposed to for an order, I will write you a cheque for the difference (in Canadian dollars) and send it with your order.
