# System Requirements

The RPG Toolkit is a Role Playing Game Editor and Engine. Version 2 is very powerful. Under development for 4 years, Toolkit 2 became one of the most popular RPG Engines available on the Internet, and was featured in GamePro magazine. It's still used by many people today, and has a huge library of supporting games, plugins media, etc etc etc... The Toolkit's philosophy is that the ability to create games should be accessable to us all. It's therefore easy to use, but it has multiple layers of complexity. You can get deeper and deeper into the inner workings of your game and customize it as much as you wish. The Toolkit does not shape your game. Your game shapes the Toolkit... Lots of screenshots! System Requirements
- Pentium 133 MHz or higher
- 64MB or more RAM
- Windows 95, 98, ME, NT 4, 2000, XP or higher
- About 12 MB of free harddrive space.
- An active imagination!
Features
- Fully integrated development environment-- all the tools you need are right there!
- Over 4 years in development.
- Complete compatability with version 1
- True color (24 bit) graphics. Never be bothered by color palettes!
- Parallaxing backgrounds.
- Huge boards- from 19x11x8 layers up to 2048x2048x4 layers. And no tile restriction.
- Built-in lighting effects for board drawing.
- Plugin support-- use existing plugins, or create your own with C++.
- Complete flexibility in game design with the powerful RPGCode built-in language.
- Most things are entirely automated, such as player stats, fight systems, menu systems and lots more.
- Two modes of fighting and an option to create your own fighting engine.
- Internal menu (skinnable), or the option to create your own menu system.
- game worlds. The size of your game is only limited by your available harddrive space. Huge
- Awesome multimedia capabilities! Natively supports wav, midi, mod, it, s3m, xm, avi, gif, jpeg, bmp. Can gain access to any file format through OLE automation (including mp3)
- Design your game in style. Includes an mp3 player that lets you listen to music while you work on your games.
- Extremely simple interface. It's easy to get things done, and you can optionally dig deeper into the engine to customize it even further.
- Excellent default media to help you start a game right away-- or create your own.
- Fantastic community support (over 700,000 downloads!)
- Much much more. more! Much
