# Software

**RPG Toolkit Development System, Version 2**
The RPG Toolkit is an RPG Editor and Engine. It's powerful. One of the most, if not most, powerful RPG Engines on the internet today. With the Toolkit, a beginner can have a simple game up and running within 15 mintues of installation (don't fool yourself-- an RPG is still a lot of work, but youcan get a simple one started right away). *the*
The Toolkit's philosophy is that the ability to create games should be accessable to us all. It's therefore easy to use, but it has multiple layers of complexity. You can get deeper and deeper into the inner workings of your game and customize it as much as you wish. The Toolkit does not shape your game. Your game shapes the Toolkit...
**Features and Specs**
- System requirements: Pentium class computer, Windows 98, ME, 2000, XP, NT 4.0 or equivalents. 64 MB of RAM, approx 10 MB of free harddrive space, a brain.
- Fully integrated development environment-- all the tools you need are right there!
- Over 4 years in development.
- Complete compatability with version 1
- True color (24 bit) graphics. Never be bothered by color palettes!
- Parallaxing backgrounds.
- Huge boards- from 19x11x8 layers up to 2048x2048x4 layers. And no tile restriction.
- Built-in lighting effects for board drawing.
- Plugin support-- use existing plugins, or create your own with C++.
- Complete flexibility in game design with the powerful RPGCode built-in language.
- Most things are entirely automated, such as player stats, fight systems, menu systems and lots more.
- Two modes of fighting and an option to create your own fighting engine.
- Internal menu (skinnable), or the option to create your own menu system.
- game worlds. The size of your game is only limited by your available harddrive space. **Huge**
- Awesome multimedia capabilities! Natively supports wav, midi, mod, it, s3m, xm, avi, gif, jpeg, bmp. Can gain access to file format through OLE automation (including mp3) *any*
- Design your game in style. Includes an mp3 player that lets you listen to music while you work on your games.
- Extremely simple interface. It's easy to get things done, and you can optionally dig deeper into the engine to customize it even further.
- Excellent default media to help you start a game right away-- or create your own.
- Fantastic community support (over 300,000 downloads!)
- Much much more. more! **Much**
The Toolkit's features are always expanding. Since it's release, it has undergone 19 major revisions (that's at the time of this writing-- those figures are always going up!) I am a devoted programmer and I try to listen to the suggestions that people give me. Most of the coolest features in the Toolkit were originally suggestions offered to me by Toolkit users.
**Screenshots**
The Toolkit has a wide range of styles. Due to the wide availablitly of different tilesets on the Internet, you can make your game look pretty well any way you want.
