***RPG Toolkit Development System, 2.0***
The RPG Toolkit is the famous RPG Editor and Engine. It allows you to create your own Role Playing Games from scratch or from existing templates. It's powerful. One of the most, if not most, powerful RPG Engines on the internet today. The Toolkit originally started as a personal project when I wanted to make my own RPG games, but it has since exploded into what it is today. With the Toolkit, a beginner can have a simple game up and running within 15 mintues of installation (don't fool yourself-- an RPG is still a lot of work, but youcan get a simple one started right away). *the*
The Toolkit's philosophy is that the ability to create games should be accessable to us all. It's therefore easy to use, but it has multiple layers of complexity. You can get deeper and deeper into the inner workings of your game and customize it as much as you wish. The Toolkit does not shape your game. Your game shapes the Toolkit...
![](images/featspecs.jpg)
- System requirements: Pentium class computer, Windows 95, 98, 2000, NT 3.5, NT 4.0 or equivalents. 32 MB of RAM (may run on as little as 8 MB), approx 10 MB of free harddrive space,a brain.
- Complete compatability with version 1
- True color (24 bit) graphics. Never be bothered by color palettes!
- Huge boards- from 19x11x1 layers up to 50x50x8 layers. And no tile restriction.
- Complete flexibility in game design with the powerful RPGCode built-in language.
- Most things are entirely automated, such as player stats, fight systems, menu systems and lots more.
- Two modes of fighting and an option to create your own fighting engine.
- Internal menu (skinnable), or the option to create your own menu system.
- game worlds. The size of your game is only limited by your available harddrive space. **Huge**
- Awesome multimedia capabilities! Natively supports wav, midi, mod, it, s3m, xm, avi, gif, jpeg, bmp. Can gain access to file format through OLE automation (including mp3) *any*
- Design your game in style. Includes an mp3 player that lets you listen to music while you work on your games.
- Extremely simple interface. It's easy to get things done, and you can optionally dig deeper into the engine to customize it even further.
- Much much more. more! **Much**
The Toolkit's features are always expanding. SInce it's release, it has undergone 7 major revisions and 9 minor ones (that's at the time of this writing-- those figures are always going up!) I am a devoted programmer and I try to listen to the suggestions that people give me. Most of the coolest features in the Toolkit were originally suggestions offered to me by Toolkit users.
***Quick Info***
If you're new to the Toolkit, here are some answers to some common questions...
What is the RPG Toolkit?
It's a game editor that allows you to create RPG-style games.
How much does it cost?
Nothing. If you want to create games for free distribution, the Toolkit is absolutely free. If you want to sell your games, you will have to purchase a license. See the section for more info. [registration](registration.html)
Who are you?
I'm Chris Matthews, the creator of the RPG Toolkit. You can read about the histroy of the Toolkit . [here](background.html)
