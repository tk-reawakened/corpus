# there are currently online two members

[
![Hephaestus16](thumb/80/vault/images/254_thumb.PNG)
Role-Playing 2008 Antquest
In this game one of the most important factors determining the amount of exp how get from a battle is the number of time you've fought the enemy before. Therefore your character will get more experience from shrinking down to microscopic size and fighting cancer for the first time then for having the 12th duel with a giant octopus that drags oiltankers that are just about to enter their port back into the middle of the ocean. As the amount of experience always goes down to 1 it is always better to boldly go where you haven't gone before and seek out new ass to kick.
Red streak's Rpg character power comes at the expense of irresistible desire to use that power, therefore during the rare moments where red streak isn't killing something the urge to do so rises, eventually his mind will break and the power will take over unless this urge is decreased.
As well as saving your game the save points in this game, are the place the character returns to if they die, Stat losses are possible when this occurs
The ants in the game use a base 6 number system. The 6 numbers are, in order, Ju So Ki Ku Ha Fo Place value is told by Stomping, Each Stomp Making the Following number 6 times Larger Stomp Stomp So: 72 in Decimal numbers
