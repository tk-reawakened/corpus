? During the old times, at around 2 A.E. the people of Nachram has become most interested in the study of spiritual arts. Mainly the knowledge has come from humans who mastered Martial Arts, and the Jinees, as beings of pure energy has managed to develop skills similar to human monks. The temple of Ahrid Khan was established by Ahrid Tien, renowed for his martial arts ability by both humans and Croissans. For some years Ahrid Tien has been the Master of every monk who wished to learn the way of the Fist and Spirit. Ahrid Khan has become the center for combat studies by those of pure spirit. It is situated in the mountain ranges of the savannah region to the north, right after the Ggoe Desert.After three years of establishment, Ahrid Khan was forced to closure by the Prime Minister of Croiss, Eiegann. The Croissan Minister saw the growing numbers of monks in Ahrid Khan, and therefore has decided to stop its further growth before the number of monks would outnumber the Croissan army. Initially, he proposed an alliance with Ahrid Tien. Tien, being a wise man that he is, courteously refused the offer. Ahrid Khan does not belong to any nation and will continue to be a place welcome to all races. Eiegann felt threatened with this growing population and forced the temple to surrender.The temple was closed, all monks were sent home. Because most monks were homeless to begin with, they became wanderers thus the population of vagabonds has grown, causing a boon in mercenaries that spread all over the land.When the Great Divide broke, Tien Ahrid joined the human race in their effort for victory. Unfortunately he became one of the casualties. After the Great Divide, Ahrid Khan was left without a Master. Eventually, Tien's bloodline continued what he started. Since Minister Eiegann no longer poses a threat to the temple, they wanted to reopen Ahrid Khan to the public. Ahrid Xiu inherited the throne to Khanship but he refused to reopen the temple.Days before the Great Divide, Tien entrusted his eldest son a book containing all the combat arts of Ahrid Khan. Xiu was entrusted with it to master the art and thus that's what Xiu have done right after the war. It seemed like his father knew about the war breaking out and arranged it so that the temple and the people in it would be safe.Xiu was asked for a reason why he refused to reopen the temple. It turns out that his own father, the great Tien Ahrid was the one who wanted to close the temple. He feared that the ways of Ahrid Khan is to be used for the war, which did happen. Croiss hired thousands of mercenaries who were former monks of the temple to side with them during the Great Divide. the wise Master foresaw all these and since then decided to never reopen the temple again. His last student would be his own sons who shall master the art, but were not allowed and will never be allowed to pass the knowledge to anyone else except for their own children. The martial arts was made exclusive to the Ahrid family.the temple was still open to the public, but no longer as a school, but a place of rest. The depressed souls of the land, tired and haggard from the remains of the destructive war, found peace and solitude within the walls of the temple. Some even decided to stay and live there.

Defensive player will declare major card of choice, plus minor card for action (normal attack or magic). Normal attacks can only be countered with normal attacks, magic can only be countered with magic. Normal attacks are calculated by cards' properties. In calculating, elemental circle is followed. in case the elements are unrelated or the same, follow the card damage.ex. 1 of cups > 10 of wands, 10 of wands > 2 of wands, 7 of coins > 4 of wand. Regardless of who wins or losses in an encounter must suffer the appropriate damage dealt. Defeated major cards will go to the graveyard. defeated minor cards remain on the battle ground. Magic actions do not follow the elemental circle. Replacing defeated cards does not cost a turn. Offensive player may use his turn to change a major card and a minor card in one turn. this is considered defense.S Second player will now make a turn. process same as first player. If offensive player does not use an offense, defensive player need not to defend. offensive player's turn ends. ---------El Genesis------------- --Earth, 5000 AD The Account of Humans-----It's been a millenium since the discovery of new lifeforms in outer space, and treaties have already been in pact for better interspace relations. Earth has advanced its technology far more than any human could have perceived 3000 years back. Unfortunately, Earth has become too populated and birth control couldn't remedy the problem as effectively as it used to be; therefore a call for new habitat has been recognized. Mars has been proven inhabitable by the humans since 3000 AD and is now densed with human population, nearing its critical capacity, as Mars can only sustain life half as many as Earth. Thus started the Discovery Race in 4995 AD, where explorers search the universe for new inhabitable planets. Enter Mackenzie Powell: Explorer Level 7, has 149 tomb raids, ruin finds, and lost land explorations under her belt. She was the leader of the explorer team who spotted a very unique solar system having 1 sun, a lone planet with 2 satellites in 5000 AD. The sun was traced to be Altair, the principal star of the constellation Aquila. She named the planet Nahcram and the two satellites Gemini. Once every 10 years the faster satellite named Castor overtakes the slower one named Pollux, resulting to a Lunar-lunar eclipse which later will be known as the Black Crescent. Being a very far flung planet, only the extremely affluent people were able to migrate to Nahcram. They landed on a desert and found a city named Lucero. The Account of Erthkeepers-----Unnoticed just 500 lightyears away from Nahcram was the planet of Chress, ultra densed with wildlife and flora. This was an abode of the Erthkeepers, fountains of life. The planet, due to unchecked proliferation of lifeforms, now threatens to explode. This was a normal thing to Erthkeepers. As a form of population, An Erthkeeper populates a barren planet to its limit, until it explodes. Prior to the explosion, however, Erthkeepers make vessels resembling pods, which will ensure their safety and survival in space in the moment of explosion. They travel on in different directions, until they land on another barren planet and thus repeat the cycle. Such was the case with the explosion of Chress in 4760 AD. GaiNu Cadmia was an Erthkeeper from Chress who chanced upon Nahcram on 3997 AD, then bare of lifeforms, and made her Garden. After founding her main colony Cadmia proceeded to populate the planet with vegetation as well as wildlife. The Account of Ruecians-----An "uninhabitable" planet was also spotted 346 lightyears away from Nahcram. Unbeknownst to humans is the existence of a glorious race called Ruecians, and their planet is called Ballion. No vegetaion was detected, thus the planet was deemed uninhabitable. But of vegetation the Ruecians need none. All they needed were the stones and gems for reproduction, energy source, shelter and food. This the planet Ballion is abundant of. But, as time predicts, Ballion is now low on gem resource and if things do not improve Ruecians will meet their end. A plan to search the space of new mineral-rich planet was initiated. They made meteor cannons from volcanoes, and fired meteors out into space. These meteorites contain several gems, enough to sustain a city. One of the gems, a diamond, is to be automatically transfigured into Ruecian form in its moment of contact with a new planet. Fromm her the other gems will be called to life and thus start a new kingdom in that newfound land. Queen Karthia II was the diamond ruecian of the meteor Karthia. It fell on Nahcram on 3988 AD, on the mountainous section, which proved to be abundant with gems and stones. Thus the ruecian Kingdom of Ohola was found. The Account of Jinnees-----Ikalii was a star about 5000 lightyears away from Altair. But inside this star actually exists a race of pure energy, known as the jinnees. they have a high level of technology, harnessing an extremely lightweight and unique metal known as the Evantine. Having a rigid culture, jinnees detest inferiority. Jinees who are rendered unfit and useless are banished into the universe. Some of these jinnees reached Earth, and what we know as the Genies imprisoned in magical lamps. Once a jinnee has fulfilled the wishes and has served his or her master, the jinnee is freed and allowed to go back to Ikalii. From Earth a banished jinnee named Ju-On returned, bearing great news: that water holds much greater energy than fire. The jinnees thus decided to leave Ikalii and go to Earth. Along the way, they noticed this planet with abundant and pure water, and decided to stay there instead. They settled quickly and in no time the kingdom of Jinea was found. The Account of Croissans-----Contrary to popular belief, there actually was a race native to Nahcram: the Croissans. They are winged creatures living on the floating land masses called "magic continents" of Nahcram, and prey on the grotesque and giant beasts living below. These monsters became the general source of things the Croissans needed: stone from golems, metal from Haggars, and food and clothing from beasts of the water and air known as the Hairulls. They use crests as forms of writing and enchantment. When the aliens came to land, the Croissans paid little attention to them, and as no one of the aliens bothered them, the Croissans continued to live their lives as before.

They fled from the lowlands because it was littered with wild and savage creatures and flew to the floating lands. Unfortunately, these floating lands are barren so occasionally they had to conduct dangerous hunting and agricultural expeditions just to get through the day. It was the ErthKeepers who brought life to the desolate planet thrived only by wild creatures by bringing domestic animals and growing more plants. Jinees are beings of pure energy. They are born of the stars and materialized into intelligent beings. They harness vast technology that humans are still longing to grasp. Humans also came to thrive onto the planet. Earth and Mars are getting quite crowded and the discovery of this planet has paved a way for a new habitat. The humans who were able to live in this planets are those powerful ones only; the richest of the rich, and their slaves of course. Inevitably, the thirst for conquest would overcome them and as tension heats up, it ended up in a war called Great Divide. This great war resulted in the actual erruption of Nachram, creating a two-halved planet. Races became extinct with others fleeing and others dieing. Croissans were wiped out, leaving only those in sacred and protected places alive; ErthKeepers left the planet, while others decided to stay behind and continue their duty; Ruecians were driven to extinction, although their ability to harness time and space made them able to send young ones either into the past or future, or even in another dimension; Jinees were also wiped, leaving their main city, Jinea sinking into the ocean with only a handful of survivors; Humans died, leaving the preserved/hidden children and women underground, where they are bound to wake up and rebuild the city. The number of wild creatures have decreased greatly, so they were able to settle on lowlands. They bolted the floating mass, preserved the castle, and established their town underneath it. Other Increstians expanded southward, and established their towns: Kadmia, Kadmia Port, Durham, Aurea and many others. Kadmia was located near the origial Cadmia city, where the Erthkeepers have lived. They named their town after the wise ErthKeeper leader. The eastern continent became populated by Human-Croissan-Ruecian breeds. The kingdom of the Black Moon was established, along with a subcity of the old Ruecian City(Ohollah), Karthagi. Increstians started exploring their own land, chartering every town and special locations, as well as creatures that inhibit certain places.
