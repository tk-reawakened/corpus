Role-Playing 2004 Heraldry: Freija
The first from the the four-volume HERALDRY series. The game promises to raise the standards of RPG TOOLKIT games--visually, and gameplay-wise. You play the role of Freija Moonault, a highschool kickout and daughter of the great Moonault cartographers. As Freija, your goal is to rediscover the world and disprove of the historical lies that your government has fed to the people.
