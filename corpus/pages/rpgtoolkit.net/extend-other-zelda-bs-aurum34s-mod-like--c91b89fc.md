Zelda BS (Aurum34's Mod)
**A mod of Grindalf's battle system to give it a more fullly-fledged Zelda experience, such as working bombs and arrows.**
Aurum34 has managed to take Grindalf's excellent battle system, and turn it into something more akin to a full-fledged Zelda experience. The system is complete with working bombs, arrows, a boomerang, and triple-slash action. Also provided is an extensive readme with a guide on how to easily customize and integrate the system into any project.
