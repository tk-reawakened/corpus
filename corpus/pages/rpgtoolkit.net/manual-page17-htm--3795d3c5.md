# RPGCode

Whether it's fighting in your battle system, equipping for the route ahead on a menu, or witnessing a climactic story sequence, programmed events are integral to any role-playing game. In order to enable you to setup your own such events, the toolkit features an internal scripting language known as RPGCode. The language has multiple levels of complexity so that it can be equally enjoyed by those who have never programmed before, alongside veterans in the field. The primer covers basic syntax and concepts, the function list explores the ensemble of ways in which you can interact with the toolkit, and the final section, object oriented coding, details a subset of advanced programming also available for your use.
