# features

[![feature](vault/official/1-small.jpg)](vault/official/1.jpg)
Complete suite of the tools you need to put together your game, from creating characters and items, to building your game world with tiles and animations. **Editor**
[![feature](vault/official/2-small.jpg)](vault/official/2.jpg)
Native tile-based graphics and animations, both standard and isometric, and support for programmable image drawing and manipulation to create interfaces and custom graphical effects. **Graphics**
[![feature](vault/official/3-small.jpg)](vault/official/3.jpg)
Comes with its own simple coding language for creating your games and also with advanced features such as objects and threading for those with a little programming experience. **Coding**
[![feature](vault/official/4-small.jpg)](vault/official/4.jpg)
With support for plugins developed by the community, there are a wide array of extensions you can use to add exciting new features to your game. **Plugins**
[![feature](vault/official/5-small.jpg)](vault/official/5.jpg)
Compile your game as an executable so people can download and get playing straight away, or deploy as part of an installation package. **Package & Distribute**
[![feature](vault/official/6-small.jpg)](vault/official/6.jpg)
The Toolkit is made by game makers, for game makers. It will always remain free and open for talented programmers to improve upon even further. **Open-Source**
