** Basic Guidelines for Anatomy
by TKGB ************************************************************************************
*** Firstly I want to say I'm no artist. So why am I doing a tutorial about drawing humans? Because I'm somewhat good at it now. I used all the references and tutorials from many anime and anime tutorials includin buying a beginers book. That was like 2 years ago and now I realized that it doesn't matter what kind of style your drawing or from what country it originates. I realized that drawings that are aimed at looking more humanly, from anime to comic books, have something in common. The lines used for both styles are somewhat similar. Heck even cartoony style like The Simpsons have some similarities as well even though the art style is very different. Well whether your drawing cartoony looking drawings or near to life comic pages, there are some similarities between them. This cannot be said to surreal cartoons like Ren & Stimpy where everything is exagerated but to more real life drawings or at least mimmicking real life. This tutorial is to try to see if I can get you to understand how to draw people that decently look human to those of you who can only draw them using the rectangle and circle tool. ************************************************************************************ *** Firstly, let use take alook at some cool stuff from other works and see what similarities they have. In this section, just like the rest of the tutorial, we will be looking only at the lines that make up the char and not the colors or textures. First up is Captain America from Marvel comics created by Joe Simon and Jack Kirby. The Captain is shown all buff and ready for action so with that his physique has to be made like he works out. His biceps are drawn with what seems like circular motions. Look at the left bicep. It looks like two circles were used for it. Albeit be modified to give the bicep look, it could've started out as circles in the first stae. This may be well the same for his chest but they have more of a squarish shape to it. In fact, most of his body could of used circles as a starting stage. Well for a big guy, he sure does have small hands and feet and yet it makes him look normal. Just because someone is huge and buff doesn't mean everything about him has to be but at the same time you can't make em too small or he'll look silly. The feet and hands are almost the same shape as his head so in a way, the head would work best as a guide since you start with the head first. Next let's look at an anime char. This is Van Fanel from "The Vision of Escaflowne". Unlike Captain A., Van isn't buff and big and doesn't tower over america's enemies but similarities are apparent. Firstly Van's feet and hands, if you can envision his hands, are the same size as his head, just like Captain's hands and feet. Also his body, upper and lower, are at almost the same ratio to his body as Captain's body. Even though Van is skinny and Captain is buffed up, the proportions and sizes of their bodies fit them very well and are balanced. The arms of Van's and Captains are obviously incomparable but look at their shoulders. A circle must've of been used as a starting point. If Van needed muscles, the artists could've used the same method. So this teaches us whether your art style is western or easter, one can learn from one another. Now let's look at something much simpler, Beavis and Butthead; two morons who prob couldn't recite the alphabet if their life depended on it. Unlike Van and Captain, Beavis and Butthead have been drawn with minimun lines. Out of the three, Beavis and Butthead are easiest to draw but some principles apply. Besides their overgrown heads, BV and BH have a good balance on their bodies. The upper body is no bigger than their lower body. Their hands and feet cannot be compared with the head but it fits the body very well. Had the hands and feet have been made bigger, it would look kinda foolish and their arms and legs would have to be bigger and if you were to do that then you'd need to enlarge their upper and lower bodies and after doing that, the head wouldn't look right so you'd have to enlarge that too. So in a way, you cannot improve on this drawing cuz it's the way it's suppose to look like, telling us that sometimes when you need a guide, you usually have to go with what you know and sometimes come up with some rules of your own. Of course I'm going to show you some of Pigement Art's stuff. When it comes to great art, Khin, ashe, Frei, and Angelis is our communities highly respected artists. Here is a pic of the Erthkeeprs as called. Look at the guys bicep. Look familiar? The same two circle deal used for Captain's arms. Though this gentleman isn't a complete beef cake like Captain, you can tell he's not one to mess with. The woman next to him to shows she is as skilled but she doesn't show any muscles like the Captain does or even Fanel. What makes her too a worthy opponent? Well sometimes being skinny doesn't mean your weak. The woman is indeed thin but well built. Just like Fanel, she looks like she's fit to fight because the way her body was drawn. So just because she doesn't have a hunk of muscles, doesn't mean she can't beat up Captain America, though her weapon also can be of use to her aid. ;) After seeing the four pics shown, it is shown that no matter what style your going for, you can also look at some other peoples work and see that some features are the same. ************************************************************************************ *** Whether your char is original or based on someone, there is no better reference than photographs since that's the aim most ppl look for but there is difficulty in using photographs because with so many features and textures, you really can't get a grip on how exactly to redraw a photo. I myself do not use photographs as reference but I believe it would be useful to this tutorial to make this segment. Firstly we shall google a photograph. Now let's say we want to redraw him in black and white but you don't know where exactly to start. Should we start with the face? How does one draw the hair? How do you get the right look to make him look the same as the photo? Well there is no one answer for these questions so you must find ways to slim down your options. One of these is to lower the color value to figure out what stands out. The bitmap was lowered to 256 colors. He still looks the same but the colors have been decreased, making our job easier. Now we can see what stands out and work from there. Here's the drawing I made. I never drew a person based on a photo and this is my first time but I think I did very well. Though not as good, you can prob tell it's the same person. Even if you cannot, you can atleast say he's based on somebody. So what makes him recognizable? Well you have to decide what stands out the most of the photo and only use that for the drawing. In this case the face is a must but you must configure it in a way that it matches the photo. Having the eyes too far apart or the mouth too low will not do. The hair need not be drawn by every single hair. Just looking at the shape and redrawing that is good enough. Also any notable wrinkles or other features can add char such as the lines underneath the temples needed to be there to show of his aging. The important thing to know about using photographs is looking for what stands out. What stands out of a person is what makes him unique and it's usually all that is really needed to make a simple outline of a person. ************************************************************************************ *** Alright. Now were in the part where my tutorial really gets in depth; the body parts themselves. I will start from the top of the body and work my way down. The following segments are going to concentrate on adult bodies of male and female. These are rough drawings I drew in 15 minutes but show the basic simplicity of drawing people's outlines. I will concentrate on making it as simple as possible to draw each part. ************************************************************************************ ****** The head of a persons body is the most recognizable part because it's where the person's emotion are carried out most of the time. I will only teach you the basic design for a head. The face I will cover later. Ladies first. Here is an outline of a woman's head. You can instatly tell it's a woman's head no? What makes it feminine is the fact that I used alot of hair since the majority of women have long hair and sometimes with volume (the raising of the hair). Also the shape of the head helps too. This shape is no good. It looks too odd to be taken seriously. It's too distracting and ugly. The first one looks very nice and feminine not just because of the hair but because the shape of the head is rounded, giving it a soft look and softness adds to the femenine charm of all persons, not just females. Male's too can have this but with that, they are sometimes mistaken for women. So for women, you use a soft touch by using roundness for the face. Long hair is also more prefer. You can have short hair too for women but for that, a face must be the right kind so as to not mistake them for men. Here are four more examples. The first one displays long, black, straight hair. The second one is the opposite and can be seen as a blond with long, lively hair. The third one is short but with a little flair on it and the last one looks like she needs a comb. Guy's heads are designed differently. While women have soft faces, a man's face is more chistled in. The faces of the female figure took me like 2 strokes but for a mans face required 4-5 strokes. I do not know why exactly but this is more acceptable for a man. The man's temples and chin stand out more than a womans, whose are sometimes not visible. The hair is a no brainer. Make it short or long depending on how your char is. So long as the face is chistled well, the hair doesn't really matter. In these examples, the first one shows a typical male with the chainsaw looking hairstyle. The second one shows one with long hair. Even though it's long, you can tell it's a male. The third one shows us a guy with a pompadore. The last one has no hair at all but you are still able to tell that it's male thanks to the design. One thing you must be wary about drawing hair is it's position. Whether it's short, long, or whatever, it must fit like a hat. Something is terrible wrong with the hair in this pic. Can you guess? Of course you can. It looks like it's melting. The reason why is because it's ill prepared. It was just slapped on. By adding more black to the hair, this has been remedied. Always look out for abnormalities you may encounter when doing the hair and if you do find anything wrong with it, try fixing by giving it more volume or giving it less volume in case you make it really high. ************************************************************************************ ****** Whenever I draw a body, my second step is the arms. Why not the body? Because the arms can actually guide me to how long the body will be but if you feel more comfortable doing the body first, then you can but for now, we'll work on the arms. To those who always drew ppl's arms with two straight lines (4 if it's bending), you're half right to draw it but of course that isn't anatomycally correct. If you notice by the example, the arm is straight but it's a bit rounded in both parts of the arm and the elbow, which a small bump at the joint is suffice. If you want to confirm this, look at your own arm (assuming you haven't pumped some weights into it). If we were to split the arms... ...they would both look like 4 fish bones. So in a way, they are both straight and not straight. HOLY MOLY! Will you look at that? Is that an arm or a big hunk of meat? It's both I guess and that's what it's suppose to mimmick. Big, hunky arms that look like they can crush a guys face like a beer can are big and strong so to draw it you must keep those two things in mind. You must draw it big and you must use quick strokes too. It helps. Though the lines don't have to be thick like I did here but it sometimes helps to add more aggresiveness. Also look at the shape of each bicep. They are round but have edges too. I did this easily but for beginers, you may want to first try this out. Cartoons usually draw them like this for comic purposes. When you have this, simply sharpen some parts like this. This may take some practice since muscle bound arms are kinda hard to get right. Also the first image showed four additional lines within the arm. This is to show the arm bulging with muscle. This also takes practice. You have to place these lines to be congruent with the arm. One suggestion I have is to make it flow with the arm, preferably along the lines of the outline. The big bicep designs also apply to those who have small biceps. It is not a monstrous arm but it does show that the person who has it can kick anyones butt. If you are capable of drawing a normal arm and a muscle-with-muscles arm, then this type of arm should be natural but don't think we're done yet. We still got the hand to worry about. No doubt the hand is the hardest part of the body to draw. Chances are if you are not experienced in drawing, you usually remedy this by using a mitten hand which omitts the fingers and leaves a big stub with a thumb and it's no surprise either since we all do it when we first begin to draw. So why is a hand so hard to draw? Let's see. Well the obvious reason is the way it's shaped. It's very odd. Not even feet look like this. There various joints and the ones on the on the fingers are the most prominent. Also the hand is capable of looking very different most of the time depending on how you rotate it, move the fingers, bend the wrist, etc. The best way to figure this out is use a model and what is the best model than your own hand. Try moving it around and look at how differently each movement makes the hand look. Even clenching it in a fist changes the prospective of the hand. To draw the hand will require practice to make it look decent. So how do we start? Well first try making a psuedo hand. If you are using a paint program on the computer, you can try drawing a circle for the palm and use the line tool for the fingers and thumb. Compare it to your own hand to see if it fits. Then using your hand as a model, try modeling it after it. Though this requires cleanup and the lines for the joints, you must admit, it's way better than a mitten hand. In all honesty, I do not know if there is a sure way to draw a hand. I guess this is a case where the style best suits the method. But what I can recommend is using your own hand as a model. With that, you'll be able to draw it in many differet poses. ************************************************************************************ ****** What are two arms when you have no body? Well here we come to the next part. The upper body is important in showing the physical stature of a char. Firstly well work with a male body. A guy's bod can be made simply though if you don't know how to start, you can always use two rectangles like so. A male's body is very rectangular and straight to show that he is tough. Then again, there are those who shows us they have hunger. Women's bodies are the opposite. They are not straight but curvy. They are not rectangular but more of a "S" shape. The female body should be easier to draw. Though this is for regular women's shapes. What about weightlifter's? If you are gonna do a weightlifters body some of the male's body will have to be implied, mainly in the upper part in the chest. ************************************************************************************ ****** Now we come down to the legs and feet. I wont do the hips and waist cuz once you learn how to draw the body and legs, it shouldn't be too hard to figure out. The legs are almost the same as the arm but don't count on the "two fish bones" theory to work. This is different. The leg is made up the same way as an arm but with different kinds of curves. The upper half of the leg may be drawn the same as one another but the bottom part of the leg should have the back part of the shin have the majority of the curve on the upper to represent the calf. To get it right, you must practice it alot and use reference pic. The one I drew isn't that impressive. Prob cuz I don't draw them alot. Feet, unlike the hand, is more easier to draw because there isn't much to it. 5 toes and foot is all there is. If you still feel you don't know how exactly to start on one, then just like the hand, make a psuedo foot and look at your own foot. ************************************************************************************ ****** The last part is to get the face for the head. The face is the first thing ppl look at when a new char shows him/herself. The face is made up of two eyes, nose, and a mouth. Sounds easy right? Well let's see. The most important part of a face is the eyes. They show more emotion in them alone than the entire body can all together. To get the right eyes is a task you must figure out for your char but regardless, you gotta know how to draw them first. This woman's eye was draw in mere seconds. Beginners can do so to but with the basic cartoon eye which is a circle and a dot. They probably don't know how to begin drawing such an eye. Well actually, it only takes 4 strokes to make a basic eye outline. This is it. Almost every eye I have ever seen in drawings have this basic outline. One line on top, one at the bottom, and two inside in between. Notice how the side lines aren't on the edge but actually inside, sandwhiched between the top and bottom line. With these four lines, you can set the design and mood for the eye thats perfect for your char. Also remember this: a females eyes are most of the time more rounded and a males eyes are mostly more squarish. The second eye would be nice for a girl while the fourth is more suited for a guy. Though if you can pull it off, you can prob reverse this. Anyways, we got the outline, now we need to add the pupil. This is a method I use. Don't think of it as a 100% guarantee everyone does it like this. Firstly, add another line between the two side lines. Not directly in the center but near another line, depending on where the char is looking. Fill in the middle of it, leaving a little bit of white on the side. Add two sploshes of white on the pupil to give the illusion of the eye being glossy. The last step is adding eyelashes. I also learned this from a book. Add two lines on top and the bottom to make it look like the eye is bulging. Will you look at that? You drew an eye. Now let's move on to the nose and mouth. There are two things I don't like to draw: ears and noses but unlike the ears, I have to draw the nose. I usually just go with the anime nose where its pointy but you can try drawing a real nose. The most simplest way is to use two strokes. Though if you need even better inspiration, consider this. Draw a 4 but not plain. Try making it fancy. Now remove the long line on the right side. TADAH! You drew the side of the nose. With that, you can modify it easily. But what if the guy is looking straight at you? No problem. You can use the same nose. If it looks good enough, you can pull it off. So we got the nose down for a male but what about a female nose? Well it's no differnt except it's smaller. Try drawing a smaller nose and it'll work out. Lastly for the face, the mouth. The mouth is easy. Usually people just draw a line to represent it. Ppl do it all the time and it's no different so a mouth is nothing to sweat about. ************************************************************************************ *** Well we done all the parts for the human body on a adult but what about kids? Most tutorials will only give you guides for adults and you assume that kids are just smaller but there are some things to consider: What do I mean by body balance? Well consider an adult. If you compare the upper body (arms and body) with the lower body (legs and waist), it would be the same. Though for a child, it's age needs to be thought out first. While the mid section for an adult may be below the waist at any age, a child's mid portion may be its abdomen. The older the child, the lower the mid section is. The two boys are of different ages. The one on the right is very young and his mid section is between the chest while the boy at the left has a mid section on his abdomen. Also for kids, alot of work isn't neccesary. If you notice, the arms and legs I drew here aren't as detailed as the ones of the adult. Maybe if they are upclose you should draw them but from this distance, you can draw them willingly and won't make a difference. ************************************************************************************ **Part 1: Introduction to my tutorial**

Also, with this and every other tutorial, you shouldn't depend on this tutorial too much. Don't think of this tutorial as a 100% guide to being perfect cuz I don't know what exactly I did on this tutorial and just because you read a tutorial by a proffesional doesn't mean that's also a 100% way as well. This tutorial is to differnt itself from any style and to actually bring many styles together. So as a way, this tutorial should open your mind to create your own style that includes aspects of your favorites. Who knows, you may even learn to love your own style more than others.
