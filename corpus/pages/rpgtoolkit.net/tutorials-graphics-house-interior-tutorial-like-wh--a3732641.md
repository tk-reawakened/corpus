House Interior Tutorial
**A step-by-step tutorial for creating a house interior in 7LegionArmy's flagship style.**
First this is not an isometric tutorial. Now that, that is clarified, lets get to the tutorial.
![](vault/images/34_1.PNG)
1. Draw the basline of the house first. Use the southwest and southeast lines to create the nothwest and northeast lines.
![](vault/images/34_2.PNG)
2. Copy the northwest and east lines, raise them to a desired height. Then connect those lines with the baselines.
![](vault/images/34_3.PNG)
3. Next create the door. A character can't exit an interior board if there is no indication to where the door is.
![](vault/images/34_4.PNG)
4. Next create the window, Use the directions in step two. The window can be anysize of your choice.
![](vault/images/34_5.PNG)
5. Now add the frames, for both the walls and the window.
![](vault/images/34_6.PNG)
6. Now add the illusion of depth to the window.
![](vault/images/34_7.PNG)
7. Now draw in the fireplace. The fireplace is simple just draw the basic outline then add depth.
![](vault/images/34_8.PNG)
8. Adding color would seem difficult but really it isn't. First choose your pallete. Once you have choosen your colors decide where your light source is. Once you've determined the light sourse. Variate your color pallete, as seen on the left side. The points where the light hits is obviously darker then the points with less contact of the light source.
![](vault/images/34_9.PNG)
9. Finally detail. Once you have choosen the base colors for the board and applied them add detail. For instance use the variating colors of brown on various planks on the wall. Also use gray lines to indicate morter between the bricks.
