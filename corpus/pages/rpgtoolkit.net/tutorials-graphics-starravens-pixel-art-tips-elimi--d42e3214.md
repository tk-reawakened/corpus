StarRaven's Pixel Art Tips - Eliminating The Grid
**Short step-by-step tutorial on getting rid of the grid effect when using the same tile over a large area.**
Eliminating The Grid
A common problem when people create their own grass tiles, or any repeated floor tile for that matter, is the appearance of a "grid". This short tutorial shows you one approach of how to solve that issue.
![](vault/images/36_1.PNG)
First, you will need to make your tile. You can make it in any style you'd like and don't worry about matching up the edges for now. For this example, I've used two colors, but you can use more if you want. **STEP 1:**
![](vault/images/36_2.PNG)
Now, make a 2x2 tile (64 by 64 pixels) square of your tiles. (a) Here you can see how obvious the grid is. To fix it, draw over those pieces in the middle where the grid is most obvious. **STEP 2:**
I've circled the biggest problem area in red. (b) There are some other problems, circled in blue, but ignore them. Focus on the parts in the center area.
Draw until you have something similar to (c), with the grid in the center edited out, and then...
![](vault/images/36_3.PNG)
...Select a 32x32 square out of the middle where you did most of your work (a)... **STEP 3:**
...remove it - this (b) is your new tile...
...and make a 2x2 tile square out of it. (c) Now you can see if there are some other problems with it. Fix those the same way you did in STEP 2: Find the place where the problems are most obvious, fix them there, and the cut a new 32x32 tile out of them.
![](vault/images/36_4.PNG)
You may need to make a square bigger than 2x2 - here I used a 3x3 square as well. The dark line (outlined in red) was not very obvious in a 2x2 square. It is very hard to see many problems when you are zoomed in (even at only 200%) so if you are using MS Paint, you can go to View -> Zoom -> Show Thumbnail to open a new window at only 100% size. **TIP:**
![](vault/images/36_5.PNG)
After a bit of poking around, you should have a tile that tiles quite seamlessly. Although you will always be able to tell that the pattern is repeating itself, you should be able to make it very hard to tell where one tile ends and the next begins.
