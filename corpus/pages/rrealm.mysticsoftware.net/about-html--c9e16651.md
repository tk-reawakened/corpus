# (c) 2002 Reverie Realm.

Reverie Realm
Last year as I was "strolling" through the internet, I came across a cool little program called the RPGTK. I thought it’d be pretty cool to mess around with it, just to see how making an RPG would be like. Since I’m very much into RPGs and gaming (or basically, anything that I can incorporate anime into). Then as I explored the official RPGTK site, I found that the community was actually very much alive and active. Eventually I downloaded games that others have created. The graphics weren’t all that amazing, but the story and creativity was there. And this sparked me to make my own, therefore I created Reverie Realm. Where the best dreams are never forgotten! Bwa ha ha ha ha haaaa…. .. Erm, anyway, this is a one girl company, sort of, type thingie. (Yes, you read right, I’m a girl.) I like to do everything myself, but there are times when I do go for help. I just think it’s a lot more fun to figure things out for yourself until you’re completely stumped/stupified. ^_^
