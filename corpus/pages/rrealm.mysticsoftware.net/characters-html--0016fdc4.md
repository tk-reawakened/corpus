# Characters

Last modified: June 15 2002
***********
![](chara/Iris.gif)
Name: (redacted: name)
Age: 20
Height: 57"
Weapon/Element: Sword
Occupation: Demon Slayer
Iris is the daughter of Chancellor Machivel of the Lasine Kingdom. She dislikes the attention she gets from her fathers public status so she became a Demon Slayer for a small town. Although, she still gets plenty of attention from men where ever she goes.
She doesnt show much emotions around people she doesnt trust. Which is not many, so she is often times viewed as shady or uncompassionate towards others.
Quote: "Without darkness, there are no dreams."
![](chara/Ray.gif)
Name:Sir Ray Bethlehem Garofalo
Age: 21
Height: 6
Weapon/Element: Sword, Wind
Occupation: Elemental Knight of Lasine
Ray has excellent relations with the King of Lasine and Iriss father. Therefore Chancellor Machivel constantly urges Iris to marry him. However, she always refuses, until one day when
Ray is excellent with a sword but has a habit of slacking off when his duties are not completed in a short period of time. Knowing this, King Lasine constantly sends him off on side quests to help the people.
Quote: "Where there is sadness, hate, death and lies; happiness, love, life and truth will be there as well if you just open your eyes."
![](chara/Mace.gif)
Name: Mace Shokun
Age: 21
Height: 511"
Weapon/Element: Dagger
Occupation:Adventurer/Mercenary(?)
Mace is Rays closest friend and rival, but at the age of sixteen he suddenly took off to explore the world. Leaving everyone stunned, especially his childhood sweetheart, Iris.
He has a care free spirit, setting aside disappointments easily and often times acting before thinking. (And he wonders why he has such bad luck) Whats worse is that there is a 10,000GP reward on his head if he is brought back to the Kingdom of Divina alive.
Quote: "Its all right to let yourself go, as long as you can get yourself backright?"
![](chara/Ileena.gif)
Name: Ilena Impreza
Age: 17
Height: 54"
Weapon/Element: Bow and Arrow, Water
Occupation: Water Goddess Priestess
Ilena has dedicated her life to helping people with the blessing Water Goddess Thacia gave her. She is very well known and loved by people all over the world because of her dedication. Recently though, she has gotten the crazy idea to leave the shrine
Ilena is the opposite of Iris, yet somehow they manage to get along. From time to time Iris will come to the Water Shrine to heal her wounds and to tell Ileena stories of the world outside.
Quote: "Be kind to everyone that you meet, for there is always someone who is fighting a harder battle than you."

![](chara/Kerri.gif)
Name:Kerri Yeis Souna
Age:18
Height:55"
Weapon/Element:Scythe
Occupation:Mercenary
Recently Kerri has met a wealthy young archeologist in Wind City who was searching for a way to decipher some scripts. She was asked to go to the Dark City Shinucrya and obtain information from the Dark High Priestess
Kerri is from Wind City, which is very well known for overlooking the Ruins of Heavenz. She is tomboy-ish in her ways and loves playing the "hero."
Quote:"Nothing in this world can hold me back!"
![](chara/Victoria.gif)
Name:Victoria Emi Reinhart
Age:24
Height:59"
Weapon/Element:Spear, Fire
Occupation:Royal Body Guard for Princess Tiara and Yuri
Victoria prides herself on being the strongest woman in the Kingdom of Lasine. She loves competition and enjoys living her life in luxury and wealth. Although the King of Lasine knows Victoria is very capable of becoming the first Female Elemental Knight, he doesnt allow her to become one for fear of harsh criticism to her.
Quote:"Women are jusd as sdrong as menmaybe even sdronger!"
![](chara/Aliyah.gif)
Name:Aliyah
Age:Unknown
Height:56"
Weapon/Element:Claws, White
Occupation:Message Carrier
Aliyah works as a message carrier traveling from village to village all over Moaru. She has had contact with all types of species.
Aliyah is always cheerful, very out going and loves taking care of others. But if shes pressured to do something she doesnt want to do, shell run away. She also loves amusing others with her tricks and stories.
Quote:"Why cant we all just get along?"
![](chara/Tyrs.gif)
Name:Tyreese Riachnik
Age:22
Height:56"
Weapon/Element:Whip
Occupation:Archeologist, Historian, Scientist, Scholar, Teacher
Tyreise is from a wealthy family in the Kingdom of Jurai. She emphasizes her life on beauty, intelligence and discovery. Her recent travel to Wind City has sparked her newfound interest in the ancient Ruins of Heavenz.
She doesnt enjoy lazy-ing around, dirty objects or bad jokes. Instead she must always be thinking or teaching others.
Quote:"Silence challenges the mind to think..."
