# Story

Sophia
Thousands of years ago a comet crashed into the planet Altia. Suddenly demons emerge destroying everything in their path. Those that were brave enough slayed demons, but in the end were slain by demons themselves. Then from a "miracle" in the dark, rose the 6 Elemental Goddesses. They fought bravely to rid the demons for decades. However when they headed to the source of the demons, the continent of Moaru, where the comet crashed. Their attempt to eliminate the source failed. With the last of their powers, the goddesses cast a holy barrier around Moaru to prevent the stronger demons from terrorizing…
Recently, the minor demons have been increasing and it’s rumored that the Holy Barrier is weakening. The demand for Demon Slayers increases, but every year there are fewer and fewer peoples who take up the profession. As a result towns have become isolated and travelling is discouraged. Only people who are brave and strong enough travel the road and risk their lives…
