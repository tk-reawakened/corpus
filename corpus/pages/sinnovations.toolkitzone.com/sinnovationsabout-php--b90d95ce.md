**About the name Sinnovations, Inc.**
Sinnovations, Inc. was created by Ben Switzer in early May of 2004. Ben's original company name was Switzer Innovations, Inc. Ben wanted to have a name that was easy to say, and unique as well. He then decided to merge the name by adding the 'S' to innovations. Thus the Sinnovations, Inc. name was born.
**Sinnovations, Inc.'s misson**
Sinnovations, Inc. mission is to bring innovative, and fun games to the toolkit.
