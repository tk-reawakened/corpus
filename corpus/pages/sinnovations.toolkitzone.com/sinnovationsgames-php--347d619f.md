**Of Darkness and Light**
**Intro**
I first started thinking of an idea for a RPG about 5 years ago. I had just bought a RPG, the name of which escapes me. After reading the manual for the game, I started a new game and it wasn't long before I was able to start predicting many of the upcoming events from the game. One reason for this was that the game used too many cliches for an RPG, another reason for my predictions was due to the manual giving too many things away. I know that the pictures just want to get people anxious, but showing bosses and future fights is really unneccessary. This inspired me to start my own RPG that would hopefully pull away from some of the cliches in many RPGs today.
**The game**
Of Darkness and Light will be a game that will test your affinity towards good/evil. You will have options during the game that will push the main character towards one side or another. Although there are two clear sides, there will be an option to not choose a side at all.
**The main character**
The main character, Damien, is a thief. His recent scores, have made many people in his home town suspicious. His father and brother are both warriors for the king. Damien's parents constantly try to convince him to find another occupation. Damien's brother Simon doesn't like Damien's choice, but tries to support him as best he can. Recently agents of Darkness have been watching Damien.

**Stix and the Stones**
**Intro**
I started thinking of what might be a funny game, and be fun at the same time. This idea of a "stick man" stuck in a 3D world, trying to become normal came to me. At first, I wasn't sure about it, but I ran the idea across several people and it was generally well received, so I decided to go ahead with it.
**The game**
Stix and the Stones will be about a 1 or 2 dimensional stick figure searching throughout his world for a way to become 3 dimensional. Stix will come across a witch that tells him about the 6 stones which have the power to turn a peice of his body to 3D. THis means that at any time Stix may have a 1D head and a 3D Left leg. Many of the peices have special abilities that will be neccesary to acquiring other peices. Im not quite sure how this would work out in the TK, but I may give it a try. Stix will have some unique abilites and enemies as a stick figure as well. Some of his abilities may be something like turning into a broom and sweeping the enemy, turning into a baseball bat and hitting a baseball at the enemy, orplaying fetch with dogs. Some of the unique enemies will be dogs that want to use him as a pissing post, cats that want to use him as a scratching post, etc.
**The main character**
The main character, Stix, is well a stick figure. He's tired of being the only person in his world that isnt 3D.
**The Generals' Sons**
**Intro**
I thought about this game after the war in Iraq started. I wasn't very happy about the reasons that the president used for going to war. I decided to make a game that would have some message about war. Well I dont know how the message will go yet.
**The game**
The Generals' Sons is about two nations that have been fighting for many centuries. The two generals of the leading armies both have a son that are about the same age. The sons are both just coming of age to where they are expected to enter their respective armies and fight beside their fathers. At the start of the game you will be able to choose which son you want to be the main character.
**The main characters**
Well basically, the main characters are the two sons. I'll put more information about them as I get closer to starting on this game.
