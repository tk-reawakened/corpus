# Home

The Story of a Spirit Walker![kalen closeup portrait.jpg (9140 bytes)](kalen_closeup_portrait.jpg)
In a distant future, mankind discovers a Creator, an all-powerful being worshipped as God in ancient times that created the galaxy and populated it with habitable worlds for man to grow and expand over the ages. But these worlds and the space between them are not without a price - for the galaxy is at war, and has been since its creation. This war is not one that many people are aware of, for it takes place in another dimension hidden from physical sight - the spiritual realm. There angels and demons battle, with mankind as their tools, in a war for supremacy between Good and Evil, between the forces of the Creator and his nemises, the Corrupter.
Around two thousand years ago, people began to arise from amongst the citizens of the galaxy, calling themselves the Prophets. They believed that they could see and interact with a spiritual realm that lay behind all of the physical universe. Most people thought they were crazy, though others followed their teachings. These Prophets began a war which spread amongst the worlds of the galaxy, a war they claimed was against a darkness that could destroy all mankind. At some point, the fighting dwindled into obscurity, and the Prophets disappeared from the history books.
In recent years, however, an Order of spiritual warriors, calling themselves the Spirit Walkers, has arisen. They travelled the galaxy doing good, and trying to turn people from their wicked ways to stabalize the galaxy and bring peace to all. Then came Zaranos, a powerful Spirit Walker who became their leader. His teachings were slightly different to the others, and slowly he corrupted and tainted those around him until the Spirit Walkers were feared by all, using their powers for personal gain and selfish ambition.
Zaranos's power grew, until the very Galactic Council that goverened known space was subverted and under his control. He set about a purge of all those Spirit Walkers who followed the old ways, decreeing their deaths with no mercy. Kalen Ondassa was one who followed the old ways, guided by the teachings of Honour, Justice and Truth. Seeing the deaths of his friends, his soul burned, and he longed to fight back against those who had fallen into darkness, but his aging teacher and mentor Malavek urged him to flee instead. Malavek believed that at that time, there was no hope for the few untainted Spirit Walkers to win a fight against Zaranos. Instead, they should hide and wait, building up their strength and gathering others to their cause.
Young and headstrong, Kalen refused to flee, until after killing some of the new Shadow Walkers, he changed. Their deaths at his vengeful hand weighed heavily on his soul, and he realised that he had not fought in defence, but had murdered for revenge. He was becoming no better than the new enemy he faced. With such thoughts, he finally fled the Core Worlds, and hid out on rimworld system known as Vantaire.
There, on Vantaire's second world, he worked in a mining camp, taking up hard labour to forget about his past. It is there, on Vantaire Beta, that Kalen's story will begin...
