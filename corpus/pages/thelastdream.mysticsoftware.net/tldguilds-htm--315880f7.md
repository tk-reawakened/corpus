# The LastDream: Guilds

**In The LastDream you will come across Guilds and guild houses they will be in most towns if not all. What you can do is find a guild for example Knights Guild, You join this guild and you can do quests for them and after each quest they will give you an item. If you become a Knight in one town you will be a knight in all the towns on that continent.**
**Knights Guild (nothing to do with Rpyal Knights)**
**Mages Guild**
**Thieves Guild**
**You will get different items after different quests depending on what guild you choose.**
**Also Once you finsih all quests for that certain guild you can then join the next guild up heres a list of what they will be**
**If you joined Knights guild you will be able to join Palladins guild**
**If you join Theieves guild you can join muggers guild**
**If you join Mage's guild you can join Wizards guild**
