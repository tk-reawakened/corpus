# The LastDream: Magic

**In The LastDream There are many magical spells to find and use. Magic Spells are not that rare you see fighters and explorers and also wizards useing them, But you dont see Townspeople use them.**
**Magic spells all start at level 1 then as they are used more they gain EXP and the magic spell increases level The highest level is 3. You Can find magic and you can also buy magic. Magic is written on scrolls and to learn the magic spell you must read the scroll.**
**Spells are the basic Elemental magics but you also have different types like healing spells**
**There are also Summon spells these are called "Spirit Guides" They are rarer then magic spells but alot of fighters do use them. There are 3 ways in The LastDream to get Spirit Guides one way is to find the Spirit Guide and do a quest for him so it knows if it wants you to be its master. The other way is to buy them at Magic shops BUT the Spirit Guides that you can buy are not the storngest infact they are the weakest. The last way is to get some of the Spirit Guides and go to a Spirit Shop and the shop keeper will meld the Spirit Guides together into another Stornger one.**
