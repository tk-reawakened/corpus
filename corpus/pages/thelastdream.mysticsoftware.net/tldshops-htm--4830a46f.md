# The LastDream: Shops

**In The LastDream there are many different types of shops here is a basic veiw of some of them and what they look like in the game.**
**- This is the Inn, Here you can rest and get some need relaxation. [![](test.jpg)](/users/thelastdream/test.jpg)**
**- Here is the item shop, you will come here to buy and sell items that you have fount. [![](item.jpg)](/users/thelastdream/item.jpg)**
**- Here is the bank. You can come in here to save your money or take your money out. [![](bank.jpg)](/users/thelastdream/bank.jpg)**
**- Magic Shop, Come to buy and sell Magic scrolls that you have or need. [![](magic.jpg)](/users/thelastdream/magic.jpg)**
**- Weapon shop, This is where you can buy and sell weapons. [![](weapon.jpg)](/users/thelastdream/weapon.jpg)**
