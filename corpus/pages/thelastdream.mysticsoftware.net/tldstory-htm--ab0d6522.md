# The LastDream: Story

**Here is a basic summary of the plot. I have done this so i dont spoil to much.**
**In the world of Zidas, A World full of Magics, weapons and Creatures.In a Town called Meblum. You are a 17 year old boy/girl. You and Your Rival are students of the same teacher who is teaching you the wonderful art of Sword fighting and Magics. One sad day your teacher passes away, you r summoned to see the king he tells you and your Rival to explore the world together to become strong and experianced so you can become a member of The Royal Knights (a Group of Knights that guards the king and is the most respected Knights). You and Your rival leave together but unfortunatly something happens to him/her. On your Travels you learn about 8 Evil forces coming to destory the planet. And you have to stop them .......But you dont understand why you have to do it. You also learn that the only way to defeat them is to collect the 8 holy stones and 8 holy weapons. But this task becomes hard as the 8 evil forces have the aid of a mysterious evil boy/girl helping them to destory the world.**
**But are you really what you think you are?...........Or are you something else?.................If so what are you?**
