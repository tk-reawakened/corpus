# Development

Unlike its predecessors, Version 3 of the RPGToolkit is an open source project; the whole community is involved in its development. Not all tasks require programming knowledge; we invite you to join in! See our for more information. [general principles](?page=gen)
Developers
The developers work to fix bugs, add new features, optimize, and make further development simpler.
Alternatively, for simple fixes or additions, anyone can . [submit a patch](?page=submit_patch)
Testers
Each release, testers work to confirm the functionality of all purported fixes. They try out their games; they look for problems; they are central to delivering a successful product.
