# Submit Patch

For simple features or fixes, anyone can submit a patch for inclusion in the official release. Code in submitted patches needs to be in accord with the or patches will be rejected; patches should be based off the latest , or they also may be rejected. Please fill out this form if you would like to submit a patch. [coding guidelines](?page=code) [source](/source)
Contact Information
TKZ user name
Your Patch
Give your patch a brief name for our records.
Please explain how we should implement your patch. Be very explicit: mention projects, file names, line numbers, and everything possible to make including the patch into the official repository as simple as possible.
We highly recommend you attach an archive with any files you have changed to make your explanation easier to follow.
Agreement
I agree to release the contents of my patch under the or its where applicable. [AC Open Licence](?page=acol) [derivative](?page=acold)
