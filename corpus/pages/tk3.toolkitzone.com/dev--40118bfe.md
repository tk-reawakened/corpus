# Testing Principles

These are requirements for potential testers.
Experienced use of the Toolkit and RPGCode.
Knowledge of the structure of Toolkit games and RPGCode; editor layout and features.
Will have time to properly and thoroughly test any builds and bugs.
Testers must be able to devote a reasonable amount of time to being in the team for both testing and discussing.
Preferably be working on a Toolkit game with which to test.
Developers will use games to test the builds themselves, but testers making games will be in the best position to discover any bugs that may occur.
Current team members will assess applicants based on these criteria and vote on admittance of the user, requiring a majority for entry.
