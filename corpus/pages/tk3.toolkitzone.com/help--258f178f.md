# Troubleshooting

No matter your skill level, at one point or another, you will experience some sort of trouble. Maybe your characters won't level up, maybe your battle system shows only a black screen, maybe your villagers just won't wander, or maybe the toolkit doesn't even open. In this section of the manual, we'll attempt to shoot down many of the frequently occurring problems realized by users like you. And if this manual cannot be if assistance, there's a community of hundreds waiting with open arms. We hope your problem is short-lived.
Please choose the topic that you would like to learn more about.
