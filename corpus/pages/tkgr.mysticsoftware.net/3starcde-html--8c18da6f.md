Dragon Ball Z : The Cell Game

> This is the first DBZ game and although some would disagree with the 3 stars, I think DBZ fans will be quite pleased with this game. Tiles, certainly ripped, are quite good and it's easy to recognize the characters from the series. Special Moves are greatly animated, it just adds something to the fights. Sound is the worst part of the game. Most of the time I found music and sounds to be very annoying. The story isn't introduced at all. Very hard to understand for those who didn't watch it on TV. Game worths 2 stars for them, or for those who don't really like DBZ.
