# And the winner for the AWARD OF GRAPHICS is...

> The second award is the Award of . The RPGToolkit allows 16 millions of colors, and bitmap-based boards...And some tile artists used this feature very well. **Graphics**
> Graphics are very important in a game, because they help the player to immerge in the created world. Just look at Dustball's colorful tiles, they have a cartoonish style and help you to immerge in the humour and the world created by Sophia. Same thing for Deceit and Conquest, for which the dark tiles are a good support for the ambiance.
> Another important is the game design. Your tiles must match together, or it'd look strange to the player. Composition Software's games have always the same style of graphics. The character graphics match with the board tiles, so it's easier to "believe" in the worlds of these games.
> This awards is also the award of board drawing. Your tiles may be beautiful, if you can't use them, the game won't be beautiful...Just look at Emporia's perfect drawn boards, or Xorlak's work for the boards of Dark Age. Both games use ripped tiles, but they used them perfectly, and the result is convinving and professionnal.
> So with all those criterions, you may understand it wasn't very easy to choose 1 winner...However, the winner is the best tile artist, the best board drawer, the best game designer to me.
And the winner for the AWARD OF GRAPHICS is...
