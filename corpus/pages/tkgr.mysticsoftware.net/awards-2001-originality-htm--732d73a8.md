# > Those ideas are the main engine to the creation of original game creation.

> So here's the first award... . A very important factor. I think that a homemade games are interesting by their originality. The winner of this first award will be the game with the most originality. The most surprising game. I've chosen not to reward a mini-game (like or ) because the interesting point is to make a game that is original and fun to play. **Originality** **mini-cars** **chocorun** **complete**
> It wasn't an easy selection. There were several games that were very original and interesting, that could've won this award easily -Dustball, Godiva:Alpha, Friends & Enemies, Voyage Eternal...
> Those games are a result of great imagination and there are lots of nice ideas in them. I'm sure almost every TK user is a gamer before all, and has thought about "That'd be cool if they'd implement that in a game"...
> Those ideas are the main engine to the creation of original game creation.
> And the winner is the game maker who created, according to me, the most surprising game, with the most pleasant ideas...
