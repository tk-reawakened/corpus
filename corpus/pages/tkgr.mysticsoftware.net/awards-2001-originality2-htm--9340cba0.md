# JOHNNY TRIGGER by Wasteland

Winner of the TKGR AWARD **ORIGINALITY**
![](originality2001.gif)
> As I said, it wasn't an easy choice. But I'm sure many will agree that Johnny Trigger is a great example of using imagination to create an unique game.
> In this one, Wasteland created a great atmosphere, unique to the RPGToolkit world, and even in the whole gaming world, there are very few games (if not none) having an as great western feel.
> ![](JTss1.gif)
> Tiles just match perectly to that western feel. Sounds are the same as old western movies. Dialogs are so great, using the right vocabulary.
> This game isn't perfect, but the atmosphere is so great that when you play it you forget the bad points, and personnally I found it great to travel in the West, visiting ranches, saloons, deserts, and other typical places.
> ![](JTss2.gif)
> Moreover, ennemies and items are just perfect, and there's been a lot of work on those.
> ![](JTss3.gif)
> Finnally, the originality of the "GAME OVER" screen beats all the other ones, it's so cool that i'd worth an award just for it ! Great job Wasteland !
