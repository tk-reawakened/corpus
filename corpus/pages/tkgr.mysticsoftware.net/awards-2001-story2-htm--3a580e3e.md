# DECEIT AND CONQUEST by Cyrus

Winner of the TKGR AWARD **STORY**
![](story2001.gif)
> Deceit and Conquest...This award won't be a surprise for a lot. This old game -old, by the date of its first release- has been much appreciated in the community, since its first demo.
> Cyrus is a master of the game plot, and an excellent story writer. The ambiance begins before the game is released : with great ads and a very good site that gives plenty of information about the game and its world, its conflicts, with detailed info about the main characters involved in this war.
> ![](dc1.jpg)
> This game is theatralic, with excellent plot and instrumental music by Chris Chiu - that should receive an award too for the best music score.
> The story is about conflict between people and characters, but also about friendship, and the background is a terrible war - but the story goes ever deeper and deeper, and gives the game an aspect of a great adventure, with the action, the mystical plot, and I bet everything will be at the end linked together.
> ![](dc2.jpg)
> This game remains the favourite tk game of many players, that have been addicted by the story and don't mind downloading about 50 Mo to know what's next in Seor.
