# Toolkit Game Reviews Award | | G A M E P L A Y ________________________________________________________________

> > Toolkit Game Reviews Award **| | G A M E P L A Y** ________________________________________________________________
> > To me the "gameplay" covers many things... A game with good gameplay should be very easy and fun to play. That means, firstly, no bugs. Also, boards shouldn't be confusing and fights well balanced - not too hard, not too easy. Balanced fights are certainly the "final touch" in a game, and few TK gamers really care about it.
> > So far I don't think there's a TK game that could bring a "prefect" gameplay, but I still think there are very good ones. Games that could make you forget the RPGToolkit 2 flaws !...
