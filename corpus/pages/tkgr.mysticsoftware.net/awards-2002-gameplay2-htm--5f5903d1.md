# The Fourth Dimension by Rueben

> > The Fourth Dimension by Rueben
> > ________________________________________________________________
> > >
> > > ![](2002_tKgR_Gameplay_Award.gif)
> > >
> > > Rueben released an amazing game, and I really look forward 2003's release. (or just next release) Actually it could have received many award, because of its great story, music and graphics. A great adventure seems to begin in this game...
> > >
> > > [![](gpawardSS1.png)](gpawardSS1.png)
> > >
> > > But there was one thing that, above everything esle, deserve an award : gameplay. Boards were greatly designed, even very big ones, which is a quite hard thing. The game wasn't too much confusing, but you still had to search for places to continue the story.
> > >
> > > Characters were nice too, each of them having their own techniques.
> > >
> > > [![](gpawardSS2.png)](gpawardSS2.png)
> > >
> > > And, finnally, fights were perfect. The characters' special moves really helped, the first boss was a real challenge (I lost 2 times against him) and the enemies were perfectly done...It was very balanced, but not too hard. Moreover, Rueben made his own Battle System - I must say it didn't bring a lot to the game, but I don't like TK's default one, and his worked great.
> > >
> > > [![](gpawardSS3.png)](gpawardSS3.png)
> > >
> > > Overall, "The Fourth Dimension"'s gameplay made the game very enjoyable, and that's why the game is amongst the best TK ones.
> > >
> > > Congratulations Rueben !
