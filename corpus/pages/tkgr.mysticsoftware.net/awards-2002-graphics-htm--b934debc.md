# Toolkit Game Reviews Award | | G R A P H I C S ________________________________________________________________

> > Toolkit Game Reviews Award **| | G R A P H I C S** ________________________________________________________________
> > Tiles and board drawing are - according to me - the most time-consuming thing when creating a game. And the hardest thing to do if you want good results.
> > But I think it gives a much more "artistic" feel to your game. It gives more personality and originality. I think the top games of 2003 will be able to use their art with TK3's new features.
> > Besides just creating great tiles, game-makers also have to use them to create great looking places. Dark Age is a good example of well using unoriginal tiles... Think about the lovely places of Dustball, the great Neo Sandal, the deserts of Johnny Trigger, Mini-Cars's awesome track...(err), Deceit and Conquest's world map and monastere...
> > Game graphics don't stop there. If menus and other windows fit with the tiles, the game feels much better visually.
> > I've given this award to a game that could bring excellent visuals - visuals that fits together and with the ambience of the game.
