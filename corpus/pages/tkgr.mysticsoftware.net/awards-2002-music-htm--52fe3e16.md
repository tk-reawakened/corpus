# Toolkit Game Reviews Award | | M U S I C ________________________________________________________________

> > Toolkit Game Reviews Award **| | M U S I C** ________________________________________________________________
> > Yes, this is the only new award this year. Music is an important part of game creation, even if very few game makers take the time for it.
> > But this year I thought many people were actually going to make their own music - because they wanted to get the feeling that they had made a complete artistic creation. A real "home-made" game. I think it's very important, because at first when you download the RPGToolkit you don't want to make music. You want to make games, because either you would've downloaded a music creation program. So making your own music really means "I want to CREATE something".
> > As it is a very important and hard thing to do, this award really have its place amongst the other TKGR awards.
