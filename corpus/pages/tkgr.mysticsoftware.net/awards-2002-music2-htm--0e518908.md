# The Fourth Dimension by Rueben

> > The Fourth Dimension by Rueben
> > ________________________________________________________________
> > >
> > > ![](2002_tKgR_Music_Award.gif)
> > >
> > > That was what surprised me the most in The Fourth Dimension, because I knew that Rueben was doing the music himself and I know it is very hard to do when you have to make the whole game yourself. Music making is not an easy thing ( I gave it a try - so I know what I'm talking about ^ ^ ) and it takes a lot of time if you want to fully understand programs for it - then again it takes a lot of time and needs talent if you want to get something good for your game.
> > >
> > > But Rueben did it, and for the first time in a toolkit game I found myself whistling the melodies long after playing the game.
> > >
> > > The great thing in his music is that it really adds to the game - without its themes and cool battle midis, the game would've been very different...A bit like Star Wars without John Williams' music, if you understand what I mean.
