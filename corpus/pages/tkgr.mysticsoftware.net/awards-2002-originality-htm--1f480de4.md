# ...Amazing, isn't it ?

> > Toolkit Game Reviews Award **| | O R I G I N A L I T Y** ________________________________________________________________
> > This was certainly the last year for games created with the second version of RPGToolkit. Now, when we look back at all the games that were created with it, most of them didn't use the full potential of the program in term of originality.
> > They have heroes, towns, start screen, fights, menus where you can save/load/use items, people you can speak with, etc... Of course some of them were original because set in unique worlds or having unique features. Now just take some minutes to imagine what could've been done with this awesome program. Imagine games without heroes, without fights, without towns...
> > ...
> > ...
> > ...Amazing, isn't it ?
> > The next version of the RPGToolkit will allow even more...
> > I decided to give this award to a game that has, according to me, showed the awesome potential of RPGToolkit 2.
