# Rudolph's Adventure by Sophia

> > Rudolph's Adventure by Sophia
> > ________________________________________________________________
> > >
> > > ![](2002_tKgR_Originality_Award.gif)
> > >
> > > This wasn't a really surprising award ! Once again Sophia released a lovely game, showing her talent and imagination. Unlike Dustball, I was not impressed by how imaginative the world was, but how Sophia used the RPGToolkit to create an unique Christmas game.
> > >
> > > [![](orawardSS1.png)](orawardSS1.png)
> > >
> > > This second game is the favorite of the year for many, which also proves how important it is to create an original game.
> > >
> > > I understand that when people download the Toolkit, the first thing they want to do is to tell their story, because they've imagined a world, heroes, they've written the plot or even drawn maps. So they often forget to care about creating an unique game - THEIR unique game - not a clone of another RPG they love. Because there are many stories, many good ones, but if yours isn't really different from the other ones, you have to tell your story differently so that players take care about it.
> > >
> > > [![](orawardSS2.png)](orawardSS2.png)
> > >
> > > Sophia's Rudolph Adventure hasn't got any interesting story, but by its lovely graphics and amusing quests - with an unique BattleSystem (designed by MrG, hehe) - it's one of the best TK game this year.
> > >
> > > [![](orawardSS3.png)](orawardSS3.png)
> > >
> > > I hope the new features of RPGToolkit 3 - with Sophia's games - will make game creators also think about originality.
> > >
> > > Congratulations Sophia...And congratulations MrG, for this unique BattleSystem =) ! Your great help to the community alone deserve 10 awards, and I'll be pleased if a bit of this award becomes yours...(you may, well, just cut a piece of the Award Pic and display it on your site...Oh well...)
