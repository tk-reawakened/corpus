# Warrior Legends by EB Productions

> > Warrior Legends by EB Productions
> > ________________________________________________________________
> > >
> > > ![](2002_tKgR_Story_Award.gif)
> > >
> > > As said in my review, Warrior Legends' story had a great "Star Ocean" feel I liked a lot. But what made this game really excellent is how professionally it was made. The introduction is perfect, it really tells you enough about the story - not too much though.
> > >
> > > [![](stawardSS1.png)](stawardSS1.png)
> > >
> > > And again, dialogues are perfect. The player always feel something very important is going on, but it is happening to real characters, who talk with their own way of talking - which is something we don't see often in non-professional games.
> > >
> > > [![](stawardSS2.png)](stawardSS2.png)
> > >
> > > So in this game the way the story is told is the best thing, the plot is great but anyways the story in itself seems really good. I actually can't wait before future releases, because I want to know what's going on next !
> > >
> > > [![](stawardSS3.png)](stawardSS3.png)
> > >
> > > Moreover, the many customizable things make the game very fun, which keeps the player interested in the story. And there is one more thing, very important : the two different storylines. At the beggining of the game, the player chooses between 2 heroes (male or female character). So far I don't know what EB has planned about it, but I think it could become something very interesting...
> > >
> > > Congratulations E.B. !
