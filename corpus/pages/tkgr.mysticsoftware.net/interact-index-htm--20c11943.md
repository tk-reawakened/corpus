# This feature allows any visitor to submit his own review, based on several rules, to the website.

These rules are very simple :
- **1]** I will read your review before adding it to this section. (I will contact you if I refuse to do it)
- Points are given by categories. These points go from 1 to 10. You may choose your categories, although I suggest you to use the same as in the website reviews (story, originality, tiles, music, gameplay, fun). You must give an overall score on /100. **2]**
- **3]** You must add a comment to these points. Of course offensive words aren't tolerated.
To submit your review :
- Contact me by email
