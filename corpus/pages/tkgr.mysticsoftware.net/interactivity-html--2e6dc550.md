# This feature allows any visitor to his own review, based on several rules, to the website. submit

> | N E A T V T |
This feature allows any visitor to his own review, based on several rules, to the website. submit
These rules are very simple :
- I will read your review before adding it to this section. (I will contact you if I refuse to do it)
- Points are given by categories. These points go from 1 to 10. You may choose your categories, although I suggest you to use the same as in the website reviews (story, originality,features, tiles, music, gameplay, fun). . **You must give an overall score on /100**
- You must add a comment to these points. Of course offensive words aren't tolerated.
To submit your review,
- send it bye-mail
- click "add reply" in and write your review. [this thread](http://forums.mysticsoftware.net/viewtopic.php?t=813)
