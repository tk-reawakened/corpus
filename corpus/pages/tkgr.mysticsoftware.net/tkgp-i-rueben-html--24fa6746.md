# I N T E R V I E W - Rueben by Martin

> What are your current and future projects ?
> Currently, I am working on 'The Fourth Dimension' which is coming on very nicely. It is aimed to be the first game of three, each being one part of a trilogy named 'The Mages Trilogy.' Similar to Deceit and Conquest when it was still in production. Future games, therefore, will be 'Only the Strong' (part 2) and 'Soul of Rueben' (part 3). I have intentions to work on an adventure game using the toolkit, but that will not be for a while.
> Will the heroes of the first part be in the next ones, or will this be 3 separate stories ?
> It is planned that the main hero, 'Braxus Chandler' will be in all three parts, with various heroes accompanying him in each part. It is a very story-driven game in this respect. The main difference is time, the second part taking place approximately 10 years after the first and the third taking place a further year on.
> Is there any release date for the next demo of "4th Dimension" ?
> Release dates don't work for me to be quite frank. I learnt that with demo 1 and the proposed 'March/April' demo 2 release date, heh! I've therefore tried to be quite tight-lipped about exact dates, but a pretty safe bet would be August for demo 2 and the full game by the end of the year.
> Will there be any improvement between the versions, like a new battle system or something like that ? Or will you try to keep a stable game system ?
> A battle system was on the agenda, and quite a lot of progress was made. However, a long break was made and I decided on a complete overhaul of the system. New features will include improved graphics, a new custom mwin, an improved menu system, quick save/load options, a new comm-shades system and some top quality music, among others.
> Looks great. Will the other two parts be on a different system or use the same Toolkit 2 at all cost ?
> The RPG Toolkit will definately be used, whether it be version 2 or the upcoming version 3. I've already got many ideas brimming, including a custom battle system and shop, in fact, a similar concept to Dark Age 2! I plan to make the sequels as console style as possible. I'm hoping they will be big games for the previews once part 1 is released fully!
> Ok. One last question, will you think about "hiring" new members to help with music, or just some art ?
> Absolutely not. This is a project I'm working totally on my own steam. My plan is to do all my own art and write my own music. Fortunately I'm not too bad at either, and programming is coming OK. It would be nice to think that in the future people would offer their services, but the sense of satisfaction obtained by working on my own is too great.
> I understand that - that's why we're using the Toolkit after all. Well, seems like you really want to create a strong trilogy, that looks very promising. Thanks for this moment of deep thinking ! :)
> Thank you Martin
