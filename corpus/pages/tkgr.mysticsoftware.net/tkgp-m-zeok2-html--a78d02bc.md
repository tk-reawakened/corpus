# Special Preview Coverage

**[![](zeok/banner_b.gif)](zeok/banner.gif)**
(note: lots of infos found here can also be found in the of MrG) [interview](http://tkgr.mysticsoftware.net/tkgp/i/mrg.html)
> **General Game Info**
- Name : **Zeo-K**
- RPGCode, Game Director : **MrG**
- Tiles : **Sophia**
- Release Dates : **Winter 2002 (demo)**
[![](zeok/prettyboat_b.gif)](zeok/prettyboat.png)
[click to enlarge](zeok/prettyboat.png)
> **Features**
> That's when it gets interesting ;)
- Advanced Character Creation
> You'll be able to build an extremly unique character with many varities on skills, stats, appearance, etc.
- Real-Time Battle System
> MrG is certainly the master of custom Battle-Systems in the community, so his last bad joke would've been to release a game without its own unique BS, better than any other that has ever been. Well, I'm not sure about the last part. Anyways, it's here ! The BS for Zeo-K will be a "realtime" one, that means no turned based (like most of the RPGs, the "FF-type"). You'll be able to interact with the environment (destroy/use object from the field), and there will be a slo-mo mode (cf. The Matrix) which is very cool.
> [![](zeok/uglybattlesystem2_b.gif)](zeok/uglybattlesystem2.png)
> [click to enlarge](zeok/uglybattlesystem2.png)
- Non Linear Gameplay
> There will be 3 different actions to help you getting out of serious trouble (or just avoid getting lost) : shooting, sneaking, and hacking. These actions will allow the player to choose how he will proceed in the game, adding a little more interactivity to the
- Custom Menu System / Shops / Message Window
> Everything in the game will be cutsom, which is a nice way to make a professional looking game.
> [![](zeok/uglymenu2_b..gif)](zeok/uglymenu2.png)
> [click to enlarge](zeok/uglymenu2.png)
- Mini-Games
> There will be tons of mini-games to spend all your days, don't worry ;) (including pool, drag race, gamble...)
- NPC A.I.
> NPC will interact just as in professional games.
> **Impressions**
> Mmmhhh....
> Mmmmhhhhh.....
> Well, this game looks great. Tiles ARE great. RPGCode WILL be great. Story WILL be great.And hopefully X-Skyodad will make the music. (no, that's just a rumor...hehe).
> Anyways that was the "general" impression I have of this upcoming game. You get the picture... Now, let's see if MrG can really make it to the top of the TK games...
> This was to expect, but RPGCode stuff looks amazing, showing off all what the Toolkit can do, and giving the game a little more fun. Battle-System and NPC AI look creative and unique.
> MrG wants to create a strong game, that differs from classic RPGs in many ways, and of course RPGCode comes perfectly to help to create that game. Features like character actions and customization are attracting, and I think G is making a very special TK game, with a lot of "wow, that'd be cool if I'd put that in my game" stuff.=)
> One question remains : will MrG's world fit with Sophia's mind and Art ? Looks like it works, as seen on the screenshots - but we can only tell that when playing the game (or before seing more screens), because Sophia's style might not be appropriated with G's story...But of course I'm getting deep in the critic, as it's a TK game (and no one here can hire a whole team of different artists to work on their games...). But in a perfect game, stuff like that is noticed easier -for reviewers as well as players...So even if tiles are great and story is great, both might not get well together.
> About the story, I think we get something that looks very well constructed and that will probably have a lot of action, with a deep story in the background. Let's see if MrG can make a game that includes well together both aspects of the plot.
> Of course, you can say : "very nice, looks promising, but I can do better, my game will have tons more features...etc". But this game is *really* coming. Most of the features explained above are already programmed and working. And a demo - that will still take you hours to complete - is coming this Winter !
> While MrG's story looks as deep as Cyrus' one, MrG's programming and Sophia's Art will work together to bring a new experience to the TK users, certainly the best one.
> Now the other makers have just to wake up and get to work, if they want to keep a chance for 2002's Game Of The Year Award :)
