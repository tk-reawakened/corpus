# TK Ranting

What is this about?
Well I'll tell ya. I started making these in January of 2004 but enjoyed drawing comics most of my life. When I was in mid school, I would draw a small series called "Bird and Worm" on any blank paper. I started drawing about 8 huge cells but then got to a point to draw about 30 cells on both sides. Though the images were horrible and almost every aspect of the place was copywritten, it was fun to do. I even made some plans for a game back when I had my Sega genesis (will I still do it? Maybe with the blobs). The cartoon is now dead. As to say, I don't work on them anymore. I have made 97 and then I got bored of it. But I still continued to draw comics. I then started a new series called Jimmy, and if I knew that I could upload them, I would of saved them. This is the third series of comics I have: TK Rants. *** How I draw them is simple. Here is a pic of Chris Reeves: Not really complex. What I do is zoom in X10 (yes it is possible) on the bitmap. I pixelate the hair and then add the forehead. Add the eye, nose, mouth, then neck. And tadah. May not be anime but atleast they look like something. So do they look like that? Nope. Take a look at this: Hopefully the Aljo bros will let me use their pics. Thats right. Their the Aljo bros (I forgot who's who). Their twins and yet I drew them like two different ppl. That's because one, I found out they were twins after I drew them and two, I prefer to draw them differently. I base the char on how the act on the community and also ask them sometimes if I don't know them. *** I would like to thank the community on behalf of them enhjoying the comics and I will alwasy love creating them. Also like to thank Vampz for helping with the hosting.
