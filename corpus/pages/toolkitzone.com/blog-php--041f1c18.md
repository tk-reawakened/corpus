18 December, 2004
No, this isn't some pathetic sob story, but a thought that came to me the other day in one of my frequent day-dreaming sessions. Surely all love can eventually bring is pain. By this I mean emotional pain, not physical. Before you all think I'm a horrendously depressed person, think about it. If you fall in love with someone but they don't like/love you then you're hurt - therfore pain. If you love somebody and end up dating or even marrying them, then there are really only two possible outcomes. Either you split up, causing more pain, or you're relationship lasts until one of you dies - causing pain for the other partner. Ok,i know there's a lot of pleasure to be had in a relationship but is there any way (apart from dying) that it can end in happyness?
