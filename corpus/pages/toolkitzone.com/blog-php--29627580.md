26 August, 2005
Well, not just yet anyway. At any rate, my sister-in-law is going to finally be having her baby! She's been almost ten days overdue. Well, they're at the hospital now just waiting. Little Chloe (I think that's how they spell it) should coming along sometime tonight, maybe tomorrow early in the morning. This means that I'm an uncle....again. Heh, now I have a niece and a nephew! And, of course, like any good uncle I'm going to teach them about TKZ when they're old enough. :) As I sit here, eating a peach and drinking my third cup of tea, I have to wonder how much longer it'll be before Chloe decides to come out into the world. Me mum said it'll probably be a long night but we'll just have to wait and see! As I side note I'd like to mention that I broke my finger for the third time in under two years whilst in football practice (soccer). :)Anyway, with school starting again, football, and the fact that I'll probably get my job back I might not be on as much. I know that I will definitely not be about to work on my game as much, which makes me sad. But that's life anyway, right?
24 August, 2005
Well, I finally downloaded TK3. I must admit that I like programming in TK3 better than in TK2. However, I think I liked the layout of TK2 a little bit more. Anyway, it appears that TK3 doesn't have a CallPlayerSwap code. So, I decided to start coding my own CPS to be used in my new game, "The Livordrian Saga." Well, no one told me that it would be a bit more difficult than I first preceived. Anyway, I have about a quarter of it done with about an hour's worth of programming. I wouldn't even bother with it except I'd like to use in the game. It'll be one of the regular menu items to choose from, probably because I've always enjoyed games where you had the freedom to swap players at any given opportunity. Ah, steak, potatoes, and tea with a peach and a doughnut for dessert...now that's the meal of a true champion. This is what I had for dinner whilst I coded the beginning to my CPS program. I think food helps me with coding, of course, that could just be some weird mental problem and I very may need to see a specialist. Actually, I eat all most every time I'm on the computer anyway. In fact, I just finished my doughnut! So I think everyone who programs should be eating when they do so, trust me, you'll do better. Then again, it could just be that mental issue I talked about earlier. Either way, I'm going to continue to eat and program.
