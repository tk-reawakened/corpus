Yes, I know that my -graphy ain't that cali- and that I'm getting weird, but anyway: Arabic is the most complex, advanced and beautiful script ever made. The thing that mainly differs it from other scripts is that it's composed of hand-drawn linear caligraphy rather than singular joined symbols. Inspired by such linear handwriting, attempts to create more phonetic english and reduce the digraphs, as well as hebrew method of marking vowels as diactritics rather than letters, I have forged the english caligraphic system! Lo, and behold: The text in the upper-right is my simplified "Mess with the best, die like the rest" (the 1337 haxxxor quote) (the rectangles on the pic above basicaly mean "any letter") In case you didn't get it, it reduces all the unnecessary letter combinations like "-tion" or "-que" as well as most digraphs like "th", "ch", "ng" etc. Another thing is getting rid of double consonants and most popular long double vowels ("ee", "oo") - they're now just simply marked instead of being repeated. Short vowels "e" and "i" are gotten rid of as well, but there are simple rules: sometimes a "tall letter" prevents adding a mark for these (like q or g for e and l or t for i), in which case "e" or "i" has to be written regularly. Also, "e" mark can sometimes join with letter or not, depending on it's looks (ie. it doesn't join with r, but it does with b) And yes, dammit, I know my handwriting style sucks! :P
