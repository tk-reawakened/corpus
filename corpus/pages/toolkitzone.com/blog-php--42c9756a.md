I had a nice dream 2 days ago, that I remember amost completely. It was about the Smurves (or Smurfs). Of course, it was a complete alteration of the child tale. At the smurf village, there was a well-like openable thing, where the Black Hole was said to appear. And there came a time that the Black Hole appeared. The Black Hole was something that appeared after Papa Smurf opened the well. It was like a black hexagon that looked like "pasted" over the well when one opened it, and its position was absolute, ie. didn't change when camera view changed. The appearing of Black Hole has signified a great time for the Smurfs, it was in a way reference to the Jews and the Messianic Age. Unlike the cartoon, there were two bad guys. One was Gargamel (or maybe was it that dragon from one lame Polish cartoon?), I could not really tell, the one other was a cunning Bishop in Red, who I remember I kept refering to "bishop" the whole time. He looked like first form of Ballos, at least from the face (he had a red robe, but without the eye symbols that Ballos had). He was making wierd facial expressions. Anyway, there was a giant fest at night at the smurf village (which was a giantic place with some sort of dam, looked pretty spectacular). Papa Smurf was going to say what waits the Smurfs with the appearing of Black Hole now, and he didn't really know, but he said in a clear voice, in Polish, what he predicted (I remember the quote perfectly): "It will be nothing else, but a Wonder of the World." Wonder of the World was not a building, or a place, but an event, a new Messianic Age of the Smurfs. Anyway, Gargamel and the Bishop tried spoling the celebration, but they got caught, and tied to some chair. But they freed themselves by running around in circular motion. Then the Bishop said something, made a weird expression and broke the glass of the window of the room they were held in. It came out they were in a sort of, hmm, waste dumb factory, with green ooze rivers running. The whole place looked like from Duke Nukem 3d. Around that I woke up.
