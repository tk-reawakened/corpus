Damn that tablet! What ever happened to it!? Eversince it went wacko on my PC I had to resort to using the mouse...now I can't make background images as I used to.. :( I just keep looking at the bright side: eversince i started using the mouse, I started practicing pixel art, which i never really do. To me, Pixel Art is so old school, less appealing than 3D or even CG. Sure they're cute, but you gotta admit, a CG can make you say wow...or maybe that's just me. Anyway, since I had no choice but to go pixelling(or..I could stop drawing until I can afford a new tablet), I went on and started making sprites... My early pixel art attempt. I got a decent set of comments from the guys at and told me that i gotta fix jagged edges and put some AA around it. They voted against the outline... I fixed it and still got some good comments. Pixel Art has taught me discipline, patience, quality, and logic. Don't use colors that you don't really need. A piece of art can be a stunning work even with just 15 colors. Every pixel counts, don't use auto AA brushes coz' they tend to mess up your work. Be patient, pixel art doesn't have to be done in a couple of minutes. You'll likely get the hang of it and soon will be able to finish the same works in less time. Be very concious about each color, where they should go, and how many should be in the pic. Truly it has taught me discipline that I have always overlooked on my previous artworks. Later on I pursued my pixel art practice and opened up MSpaint. Within an hour I have managed to whip up this sprite: After I did this in paint, I realized I did pure pixel art! Whoa, I couldn't believe myself. I had no assistance from layering and all that, yet the result is so cute. I am satisfied by it. The next step I had to take was to get the metal texture to work, which I'm sure I will get in no time. I also devised some of my own techniques to pixel art, you see, even if i can do it and all, I still have to conquer speed. Doing a sprite in an hour is way slow for me, who's used to doing an entire set of walking sprites in an hour. I had to cut the time...so I resorted to layering. the disadvantage, however, is the loss of conciousness of the color count. With layering one could carelessly paint lines all around and adjust opacity, resulting in a lot of colors. While this is fast, this isn't acceptable as pure pixel art. But I'm not after the essence of pixel art, rather I am concerned with the filesize..that's why I have to learn to use layering and reduce the color count for smaller file size. Very high color count..256. For a small piece, it doesn't deserve this. This could happily go with 32 colors...I failed at this one. Don't get me wrong, I appreciate the art that I did..it's just that the color count isn't how I wanted it to be... This one's pixelled also, and I took into consideration with the color count..still it has 96 colors(Ewwww..). I am learning to not go beyond the 32 color mark for small pictures such as this. Well I'm proud of this one... One of my most recent works use fewer colors, but I observed some Capcom and SNK sprites and they appear to only have 16 colors in them! Sheesh! I have a lot of catching up to do. I really really have to deviate from my old style of multi-shading. I get discontented with two shades, because I am used to shading for a minimum of 4 layers...which isn't the point of Capcom-esque pixel art. Next time...I'll be more careful. Latest pixel working...i think I am improving though...just gotta learn more. At this point of my looooong blog, I haven't pointed out what the title is about....I am getting impatient because I can't make huge backgrounds with the mouse...I tried and tried, and failed each time.
