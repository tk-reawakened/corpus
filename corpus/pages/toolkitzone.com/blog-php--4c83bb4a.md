I can't help but draw the comparison. These blogs seem to be about attention. That's fine. We all need attention, I just thought it would be amusing to myself to post here about it. I have worked with a lot of different actors and a couple of different singers and there seems to be an inherent desire to be the center of attention. I know that personally I like some attention, but I would rather spend the majority of my time being left alone. At least that's what I tell myself. When it comes to writing though I seem to be the complete opposite. I wonder how many other people here feel the same way.. ya know? The internet is sort of a comfortable place to free up your thoughts and express your ideas. You might get flamed or insulted but it's a very safe way to open up. ...safe because there are minimal risks to ones ego. ...and thats what this is all really about. Ego. The image we have of who we are is reflective in the real world... however the image we have of who we are inside seems to be embraced on the internet. People even make up fake names and identies... whole new personalities when they are online. I know that a few years back I was sort of like that. Before I ever came to the TK community I used to vist other places and waste tons of time there. I even had a fake name... 2 of them actually. ...but we learn and we grow. ...and even still here I am... feeding the insatiable desire for attention... a thirst for personal indulgence that can never be fulfilled. I guess it all comes back to ego... to the desire for affection and attention... the hope for acceptance and adoration. No matter the circumstance or the age, I am still a slave to my own selfishness. Now I'll go and let someone elsestand here. This light is starting to get a little too bright... but I'll be back again... as always.... -Cy
