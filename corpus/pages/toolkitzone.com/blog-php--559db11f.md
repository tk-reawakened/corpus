Hi there. Well, if any of you have been reading the most recent new area, you'll know that I happen to be doing a game called Life in the City with my friends, captainalan, bobsabob, and Evan (doesn't have a screen name yet). The game happens to be dedicated to a friend called Taylor, who's moving from our good old rural and urban city to a completely urban city. So, some of the features of the game are exaggerated as a parody of his new lifestyle to come. I mean, puddles of water for a bathtub and sink? The bedroom and bathroom being the same room? That's got to be exaggerated. By the way, I did not start this game to copy City Life (or whatever it's called), which is being made by, uh, I forgot. Sorry. But what I mean is that I started this game before I was even aware that there was another TK game called City Life. Never fear though, I'm sure mine is different, and I've found proof for it: Okay, fine. Our games are both kind of based on the Sims, but the similarity stops there. First off, my game does not have a storyline. It's just another one of those get-a-job-and-buy-new-houses-and-furniture simulation games. My game's split up into five sections: "Downtown", "The Suburbs", "Utopia", "The Docks" (has to be unlocked), and "Lake Summer" (has to be unlocked). As you can see, our games are very different. Mine is simply a simulation, while his is an RPG. I think I've said enough for today. Please give me some feedback for my game, too.
