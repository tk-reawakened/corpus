23 April, 2006
Howl's Moving Castle was awesome. Confusing, but still awesome. Anyway, I feel like starting my blog over again. Why not? It's more fun than just updating my profile; here I can ramble on about me, too. Hmm... I guess I'll review from a bit before Easter, fill you in on the mundane events of my tepid life, but first I'll describe myself a bit. (I like to be very organized.) A Bit About Myself Well, I don't know how often my dad flits in and out of this place, so I'll refrain from giving up my name. (He seems to visit the site every once in a while to check up on what I'm up to.) I've got a huge chest, I'm tall, broad-shouldered with sideburns. Sound fun? Well, it's not. I feel like this giant...erm...giant. It doesn't help that seventy-five percent of my friends are tiny, either. And I'm thirteen. It sucks. The worst part is when I hear people say how happy I must be about it and everything, too. Hmm...not much else to say about myself, except for the fact that I love to draw, pixel, watch anime (particularly anything Studio Ghibli), and read Harry Potter. Oh, and I'm a blonde. No jokes, please. It detracts from the fact that I look sexier than you. My Life (For the Past Week) As I write, my mom's parents are going to church. They came over for Easter, but they're leaving on Tuesday. I recall liking them more. Still, I swear that I'm an exact duplicate of my dad; I can relate to mom fine, but relating to my mom's relations is unrelatable. That's probably why conversations with them are a bit awkward. Is it normal to wonder deeply about your genes once you hit teenage years? Easter was awesome. We got more candy than I can count, plus Narnia on DVD. It's fullscreen, which sucks, but I'd rather have it than not have it. After pigging out on that for I while, I spent the rest of the day playing Tales of Symphonia. Best 'Cube game ever. The next few days were mostly pixelling and crap. I snuck off to my dad's parent's house to watch Harry Potter for a while, which was fun, and then I mostly...I dunno...played Tales. It's been too rainy to do anything else, really, aside from going to the dump to drop off some leaves. Oh well, more pixelling. Farodin No, it's not dead. Want proof? Check the profile. I just updated it yesterday. Most of my time has been devoted to Rave, but developement isn't at a standstill yet. Just don't expect regular updates. Rave Larrin Okay, so I couldn't resist drawing more grass, but It was just a tiny bit. Still, I've got to be more careful.
