10 October, 2005
Lately (past week or two) I've been having flashbacks to... things... from before I was depressed. Nothing important, just random memories that I've lost over time. Before this started, I could hardly remember anything, and I couldn't care less.. Now I'm struggling to hold on to them, as though they were the only way I can escape from my depression. Also... I've started to notice that no one else is maturing anymore... friends, even my lover, are all acting like little kids all the time. Why? Is it them who are becomming less important in the world, or am I becomming more so? ... Valie was right, some people just on different levels. And yet, I can't help but feel envious of them, knowing that they have little worries or cares anymore. Knowing that everything I want, they have. ... Yet they have nothing. I don't know. Don't mind me, I'm just rambling. I figured I should write this somewhere before I decided it's not important.
