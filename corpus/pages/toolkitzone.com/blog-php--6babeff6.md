# User Entries (2)

09 March, 2006
Today was really weird for me. First off, most of my days are pretty boring and I don't really enjoy school that much. I get a 3.9 ish gpa but I'm always just...tired or something during the day. But not today. Today was cool. I didn't get any more sleep than I usually do but I was upbeat. I was socializing and having a great time with my friends at school. I was actually on fire in discussions in all my classes, especially French. Usually in French, if madame asks me a question in French, I have to ask another person what it means and then ask them how I should answer. But today, I was the one telling everyone all the answers and stuff. It felt pretty cool. We also had pick-a-game day in phy ed. We had the choice of playing floor hockey, ultimate frizbee, or exercising with a Tibo (sp?) tape. I picked floor hockey and I was just burning up the floor and nets. It was truely awesomeness. And finally, I had the stride going everywhere I walked. You know, the stride that inherit when you feel like you're on top of the world. The stride that makes all the gals notice you. Ya, I'd have to say that I felt pretty invincable. I know for sure I'm not gonna get another one of these days for a while so I'm trying to saver the day while it lasts.
14 February, 2006
I became bored one day and thought up a list of the top 10 things I hate. So I thought what better than make it into my first blog. And so without further adew...drumroll please! When people point out, "You can run, but you can't hide." I'll do whatever the hell I want!! Who the hell are you to tell me what I can and can't do?? I hate when spreads on sports scores are something-and-a-half. You can't score a half a freaking point. When people say, "life is short." What the hell?? Life is the longest freaking thing anyone ever does!! What can you do that's longer? I hate when something is, "new and improved!" Which is it? If it's new, then there has never been anything before it. If it's an improvement, then there must have been something before it, it couldn't be new. I hate people who ask, "Can I ask you a question?".... Didn't really give me a choice there, did ya pal? When people say while watching a film, "Did you see that?" No Loser, I paid $12 to come to the cinema and stare at the damn floor. When people say, "It's always the last place you look." Of course it is. Why the hell would you keep looking after you've found it? When people say, "Oh you just want to have your cake and eat it too." Damn right! What good is cake if you can't eat it? I hate people who are willing to get off their ass to search the entire room for the T.V. remote because they refuse to walk to the T.V. and change the channel manually. I hate people who point at their wrist while asking for the time...I know where my watch is pal, where the hell is yours? Do I point at my crotch when I ask where the toilet is?
