Well finally I start my new job after weeks (maybe months) of waiting! I'm not sure if I've even mentioned it here before, so you probably don't know what I'm talking about! So here's a little background info: My dad wrote a musical about 10 years ago and it was performed in places etc, then not alot happened with it. He got a job with some american TV company and put him incharge of channels and stuff. He showed his musical to the people incharge and they liked it and are going to fund us to turn it into a film! It's going to have live-action with some 3D animation mix in the film.
