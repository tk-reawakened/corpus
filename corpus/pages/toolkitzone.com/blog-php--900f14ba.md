12 August, 2005
Well, I just dumped about 60% percent of the data for my game, leaving only the boards, programs and about half of the bitmaps (well, they're all PNG's, but the folder is named Bitmaps, regardless.) Also, the whole weapon switch theory has been tested, and works better than I hoped. I'll only need about 25 or so programs (one for each item) total, as opposed to the worst possible scenario, with 12 programs per character.. As such, when I was testing, no enemies had sprites (thus meaning the screen looked like glitch-land), so I'm not entirely certain it even truly worked properly. Anyways, now that I think about it, my game files have been changed 100% by now since the switch to TK3, as the progarms needed re-writing, and the boards have been modified numerous times. I suppose the plot is mostly the same, but in reality, that's about all. I guess that's a good thing, as when I first made my game profile, I was not far after switching from TK2. And looking back, that stuff was pretty much garbage. Yeah, I know that this is entirely about my game but, whatever. I thought I oughta get more blogs. Hey, it beats some people's three sentence blogs. And nobody bothers reading the TK Game news, either.
