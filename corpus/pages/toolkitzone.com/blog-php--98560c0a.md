# User Entries (2)

16 July, 2005
I just came back from the 3 Doors Down Seventeen Days Tour stop in Burgettstown, PA. It so kicked ass. The funny was that Staind actually played more songs the 3 Doors Down did, which is fine because they are mainly the reason I went :P. No Address and Breaking Benjamin also performed. I was a bit skeptic with No Address as they are a fairly new band, but they were ok. Breaking Benjamin rocked out loud though. If the Seventeen Days Tour is coming near you, I highly suggest you go. ;)
06 May, 2005
Finally, the ugly tests are finished. Aced my first one with a 96% and my final grade was a 94% (in physical science). I'm not sure about my others because my professors are lazy bastards :p. Something rather interesting happened the other day (tuesday I believe). As I was walking to my car with friends from class, we saw a small car accident. Some girl rear-ended some guy, doing the slightest bit of damage. This guy comes out of the car, screaming like an idiot. My friends and I pause for a bit, standing next to my car. This idiot starts knocking on the window of the girl's car and is still screaming like an idiot. The girl hesitantly exits the car and is visibly upset. Amazingly this guy to scream except in her face. So, one of my friends felt obligated to intervene. Which means we all went over :p. We really didn't know how upset the girl was until we got over there, and well ... wow. Her face was beat red and her eyes were basically pruned with tears. It's funny too, her car was more damaged than his. And after seeing this, one of my friends started to push the guy away from the girl. The idiot then started to scream at my friend which started to scare me a bit because this particular friend has no inhibitions at all, and would quickly act on his instincts. So, I hovered around him so I could intervene if I needed to. My other friend (there were 3 of us) seemed to be comforting the girl and such. Not long after the idiot started screaming at my friend, he started to finger point in his face. Now, I'm almost certain my friend will do something, so I get a bit closer :p. I was actually amazed he didn't do anything yet, but well ... it wasn't long before my friend started to scream back, with assorted finger points :p. Campus police made their way to the accident. But most of the damage had already been done. After my friend started to walk away from the idiot, he actually started to charge my friend in which I intervened. I explained to him that it wouldn't be a good idea. The Campus Police got the girl's insurance info and gave it to the idiot. He got into his 'damaged' car and drove off, as did the girl. I really hope the idiot doesn't escalate this into something it's not because I'm a witness to the accident and don't feel like going to court. :p So yeah... not bad for my second blog. ;)
