# User Entries (2)

14 May, 2004
Ah.. as I sit here writing my script I start to remember the good ole days. I was 14 and my favorite game was Phantasy Star II. This was an EPIC RPG for its day. And my favorite character you ask. Nei of course. She was different. The first character I immediately got to use both hands to fight with. She wasnt the average RPG chick ( White mage or a summoner). She was a fighet that kicked butt. Then they go and kill her. "NEI, no NEI why did you have to die?" I screamed. "What are you yelling about," my mom asked. Anyways, yes I knew of the way to revive her, but her death just futher fueled my anger to kick NeiFirst's ass. And and ass whooping I did lay down on her. With this I kiled NeiFirst taking revenge for my love. Ahh, its hard for me to have feelings for video game characters anymore. Anyone have a love for a rpg character that died tragically. Please dont say Aerith, I saw that a mile away.
