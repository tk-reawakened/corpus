28 July, 2004
First of all, political advertising must be outlawed, and the same with contributing money to a political party. Why? Well, big corporation give money to their favourite (or all) candidates, to make sure they will do what the corporations want, ignoring the needs of the country. In the current system, corporations have real power, because they make it so you can only win with good advertising, and so with corporate money. That means the public will vote for the most convincing adverts, voting on the policies, so democracy is destroyed. Also, in the UK 16 year olds should get the vote. Why? Because if they work and get a certain amount of income, they pay tax the same as older people, but they have no say how it is spent, and they should do. They should either get the vote, or not be taxed. Was it not the American revolutionaries who said: "No taxation without representation"? The campaigners for women to get the vote in the UK used that same argument also. And one of the main arguments against 16 year olds voting is: "16 year olds would not vote responsibly." Really? Are you saying that people who already vote, vote respomnsibly? Is that seriously what you are saying? What about people who vote for the BNP (the British National Party, a racist political group)? Are these people responsible? I think not! For those who say: "Most 16 year olds do not bother to vote, so it would be pointless." Well? Some would. Also, that would mean fewer "irresponsible" 16 year olds would vote. Also, there are huge numbers of people aalready with the right to vote that do not. For example, in the recent European Elections, turnout in the UK was less than 40%. Why don't we take the voting rights of these 60% of non-voters away? No, it would cause chaos, and would be undemocratic.
