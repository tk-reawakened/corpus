04 October, 2005
Or CFE. Ryan and I have agreed to start writing progress reports to be blogged to the community. Seeing as its nearning compleation, we would have some time to relax and share some of our...uhm...experiances with the community. Our experiances majorly include major obsticles we had to go through. Those will, supposedly be shared in Ryans blog. But I can say, our obsticles included choice of program language, arguments and many other disagreements. Currently, ChaosForce is designed to make Tekken style video games. There will (Hopefully) be 3 versions, standard, Enterpise and pro editions. All three will be the same, but diffrent yet again. standard will be the simplest of all. It will contain IDE tools. The engine portion of it, will be pretty basic, font depth, font size and error logging basically. The enterprise edition is the mother of all, we will be going all out in the editor and in the engine. The engine will come with a 'compiler' that will encrypt the text. Plugin support, free patches and other related fixes for a good amount of time (Currently 3 months) along with other misc stuff.. The CFE IDE itself will be able to encrypt/decrypt also. Possiably a plugin manager. The pro edition will include plugin functions, free updates and other misc stuff. The CFE IDE itself will be little to no diffrent from the standard edition. As of right now, we're not sure if we're legaly allowed to sell CFE, thus the reasoning behind the 3 types. If we're allowed to sell, legaly, then we may include a license to sell in the enterprise edition. Now for a bit more about the CFE. ChaosForce Chaos Code: ChaosForce's Programming language is possiably more like the TKs. Mainly for simplicity on both the engine ends and the user ends. Simple commands include: texts("Text",x,y) Which is like the TKs "text" command. We also have simple, easy to remember ones such as 'Alert' and 'bitmap_Full_screen'. Currently, we do not have much. But after the alpha has been released, Ryan and myself have full intentions on filling up the library itself. Imagery: Currently, Chaosforce's imagery is limeted to bitmaps, which really bites. I myself am looking into a few image librarys that we can use with allegro for allowing use of Jpgs, pngs and gifs. Sound/Music: Currently, along with out image area, we have limeted use of sounds, mainly midi's. Again, we are searching for sound librarys that will play .Wav .Mp3 and other popular sound/music files. We would use the windows API, but we're trying to keep this crossplatform. BattleSystem: Chaosforces battle system is currently, of the tekken style. Although it in itself is not fineshed, Ryan has made drastic changes to that, in which, it looks better. The movement system is also in progress. Status bars, player information is now visiable. AI: The artificial Intelligence has been programmed by me. By the by, I did not have as much time as I wanted to compleat it, and it will get my attention later. Parser: Ahh. Yes. What can I say here? Out parser is a piece of work, enterly coded, by me, its a piece of shit. I'ts giving me more trouble then its worth, it is. Parts of it do not work at all, and I have been fiddeling with it over and over again. This is the MAIN hangup in our program. If I could get this parser to work perfectly, or 'releasable' we would be done with the engine, and move on to the IDE itself..
