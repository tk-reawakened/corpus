As I have later learned, she was taken, north, gassed, and turned into soap."). Other thing is the "jargon" or "slang" utilised at the concentration camps. People's level was based on their "number" (corresponding to how long they survived - "low numbers" who were in the camp for long were of greater respect), the sector where prisoners unloaded dead bodies for cremation was known as "Canada", long-time prisoners who were totally devastated mentally and physically were known as "muslims", etc. Another fun thing is realism. People aren't shwon as some innocent victims, the camp life makes them struggle for survival at all cost. Fun. "Is that your father on the photo?" "Yes." "It's alright." "It's not. I gassed him."
