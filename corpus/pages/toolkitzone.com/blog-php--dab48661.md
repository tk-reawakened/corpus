# User Entries (1)

01 September, 2004
Thought I'd have a little go at one of these blog fellas incase anyone cares - which more than likely they don't. Not a lot to say - I've finished uni - that was cool, but now I have a job and stuff, so I'm pretty busy. Still want to be a pilot what-ho chocks away spiff spiff. Anyway, for now, Im an engineer, and I will pass on my vast wealth of knowledge to anyone fortunate enough to be reading: 1) Uni rules - whatever you do, go to uni - even if you take a year out before starting and dont want to go back - believe me, its worth it. In my first year, I was on a fairly gross corridor of guys, but it was dead good. One day we saw a program on tv saying that if you stood an egg in a certain position, it could withstand the weight of a person - this is a lie as we found out- 12 eggs and a dirty kitchen floor later. What was more funny was that we hoovered up the broken eggs with the vacuum cleaner, which we also used for spilt milk, soup, and other food - after about a month, the corridor really stank - turns out that when we opened the hoover to clean it, it was totally crawling with flies. Nice. 2) Get a car - you should learn to drive before you go to uni - otherwise you'll be too skint, and driving is another very important thing to be able to do. You need a car (whether your own or your mums) as you will all find out, for the transport of large amounts of beer. This beer can then be used for medical reaserch - on experiment my collegues and I have carried out several times is; How much beer can a human drink in one night? Reasearch is ongoing... 3) Don't crash your car - its a really bad idea - not that Ive ever done it, but its just as well, otherwise I'd be bankrupt. Also I forgot to mention above - motorbikes are cool too. In fact the 'cool' factor is even greater than that of a car, but lesson 3 applies even more on a motorbike. Now you are wise and intelligent. By Doc Wellsy. The end
