**Dishwashing Tactics** [Gnarf](community.php?blogs&user=374)comments
Well, I've been trying to get it right for quite sometime, but everytime I wash the dishes the cups end up making everything taste like soap. I use hot water, I clean every single part of the piece, and I rinse as fast as possible. I'm told I'm slow at washing dishes, so I try to speed it up by rinsing quickly. The problem is that the dishes start tasting soapy. Recently discovered another awesome game, Sanrio World Smash Ball! (SNES) Don't let the cute name fool you or the Comic Sans MS font... Hello Kitty and her cast of friends are ready to smash, each to their own physical abilities. Every character is different and as seen in the screenshot, they range from balanced to something great with flaws... The object of the game is a lot like air/speed hockey, to score a goal on the opposing side by stricking the puck into it, although in this case, barriers must be broken and power ups may be used. There are a variety of levels each with or without their own gimmicks. In this one, the fans in the middle may spin the puck and shoot it in a random direction. Now, despite the awkward cuteness, this game is pretty addicting and much more than expected for Hello Kitty. It's also a lot like Firestriker (SNES) but without the overworld map and RPG elements. Both very enjoyable.
