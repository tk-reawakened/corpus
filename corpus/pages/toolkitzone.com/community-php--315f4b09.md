# Blogs

by (3 , 28 views) Friday 5.16am, February 16th 2007
Well, this is the first time I blogged in a long while. But, I want to get some stuff off my chest. Anyways, here's the story. At around September, I started working for this temp agency. And there was this company who'd call me back a lot, and seemed to want to hire me. I wasn't working full time for the company. Maybe 3 days and working at other warehouses to fill up the week. They said they were waiting for the time to pass before they could hire me. (They had to wait 3 months, or pay the temp agency to "buy me".) At around mid January, I came onto their payroll (Going from $10 to $11! Yay!). Anyways, I suggested my brother join the company to move from his $9.50 an hour to $11 plus (Since he has his forklift ticket and experience). Anyways, pretty soon after, things started to dawn on a few of my co-workers and me. We were being screwed. Basically, instead of working a full 40 hour work week, we're working 20 hours. Essentially, I've lost $120 a week. But things get better. Fairly recently I just learned how cheap the company is. For example, my brother gets $13 an hour... when he uses the forklift. So, that means that he'd probably be using the forklift a lot right? Wrong. He's supposed to use the forklift as little as possible. Basically meaning he may be using the forklift... maybe an hour a month. There's also a few other things, but I want to end the blog on a happy note. Recently Mike and Indy asked me to work on a project for TKLand. I've been given the task of designing part of the game. And from the looks of it, you guys will have fun playing Also, I've been spending the last month or so experimenting with Blender to produce games for the tk. If anyone wants to find out what I've learned, then reply, email, pm, etc. All I can say is it should all work. Now, all the toolkit needs is true alpha transparency Lastly, I've been working on my drawings, 3d modeling, and all that jazz. So, I may have something posted sooner or later. Chow
