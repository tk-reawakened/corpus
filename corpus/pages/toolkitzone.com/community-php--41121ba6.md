I get the craziest ideas every few seconds that would be cool in a movie or a book. Mostly though, they are just crazy or outlandish ideas that inspire me in other areas. Anyhow, her are a few of my latest ideas: 1. A person is on a boat with hundreds of clones of themself running frantically around the deck. It is very stormy and very dark. Then a wave crashes into the side of the boat, causing it to tip, and all the clones start pouring of the side. The actuall person is in the big swarm of clones and goes of backwards, and as she looks up at the boat, it begins to melt, and they wake up. 2. A kid who gets kicked from his home for attracting strange shadow beings goes out, walking with no aim. Every day he meets someone demented, crazy, or otherwise diffrent from the rest. One day, he sees an old lady staring at a poster depicting a child pasted to the inside of a bank window. She is smiling an unnaturally twist grin, all teeth showing wide, eyes bulging. When he stops and looks, she turns her head toward him, ceasing her smile, but in an unnatural way, like it deformed to that face. "I know what you must do," she states in a voice on the verge of maniacal laughter. Then her eyes move back to the poster, and she resumes her grin. These are only a few, but for now I'm done...
