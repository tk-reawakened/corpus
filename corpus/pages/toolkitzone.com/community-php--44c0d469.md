**mi essay de colegio (yay for college being near)** [Gnarf](community.php?blogs&user=374)comment
Welcome, I am Elie. This is my college essay para el Art Institute de San Francisco. Feel free to give feedback. Seriously though, I feel proud I was able to write this so comfortably, and I think that's because it's something I'm so interested in. Feel free to print and burn it if you hate it, but give me credit beforehand. La palabra: List three of your strengths. Choose two and explain how those strengths have contributed to your success in school, in art or in your life. ?I like nonsense; it wakes up the brain cells. Fantasy is a necessary ingredient in living; it?s a way of looking at life through the wrong end of a telescope. Which is what I do, and that enables you to laugh at life's realities.? I?d like to think I was as great as Dr. Seuss, but until then, his fine words seem to sum up my strengths very well. Up until this point, I didn?t find having a random or spontaneous mind to be much of a strength. It may be awkward that I stare at an object or person for a long while, realizing the hues, shadows, details, lights, reflections, and diversities that others may disregard or allow to go unnoticed, but I find this eye to be an essential strength for any artist. Simplicity could easily describe art and definitely looks great; however, most of my art has a trend of complexity which could be a strength or weakness in several cases. I like applying art to anything I see or do, and most of the time, it?s unnecessary; it still adds a whole new perspective to things. Usually, this involves me glaring at an object until it notices, even if it?s inanimate and should eventually feel uncomfortable. If you are this object, feel free to imagine my eyes being off center and really staring at something else. It?s logical, because the longer I see something, the more details become apparent and faces become less general and more diverse. That?s not to state that I don?t see details at a first glance, but it?s definitely more rewarding to allow my eyes to apply these details to art. How this has become an aid should be obvious; to restate, it allows me to see what makes something diverse and rather than drawing a general face, for example, with basic eyes, ears, and so on, I?m able to revisit these details in my mind while free hand drawing and break away from these generalizations. This becomes a clear example to me, that fantasy is indeed based off of reality. I?ve received feedback that I?m weird or oddly silent, but when I do bring something up, or maybe even laugh at it, it?s usually something random. As stated by Dr. Seuss, ?that enables you to laugh at life?s realities.? Indeed, it does; I would rather specialize in distorting reality once I?ve learned to recreate it vividly. I love that the mind is capable of such things, and this has definitely been a great aid in my art. It?s difficult for someone to look through my sketchbook and not laugh or question the oddity of an image when I?ve purposely added my own touches. Of course, I?ll have standard studies of reality that I usually keep to myself, but with those studies drilled in mind, I?m able humor myself, and other occasionally, with my random illustrations. And when a spur of spontaneity comes to mind and I lack a fresh piece of paper, my homework becomes my canvas. My strengths haven?t earned me a hug, but they?ve certainly been amusing when applied to my life and art. I can assure myself, however, that they haven?t lent me much success in school, save for creative writing assignments, so it?ll be great to know I will have chance to bring my strengths to life outside of home sometime in this near future that awaits me so nearly. When I look to my right, and see this bottle of ?Johnson?s baby oil? on my desk, the word MILDNESS becomes apparent, something I hope to go beyond; this is my motivation for anything I may have discussed here and now.
