# hell

Blogs
by (5 , 1 view) Saturday 1.12am, December 2nd 2006
Yesterday was the first day I have ever heard that name out of my girlfriend all throughout our 3+ month relationship. It went from a walk to get some air and exercise to a afternoon with some guy named "Carl". That was what I heard today. Who the is Carl and where did this guy come from all of a sudden?! Why have I never heard of him? Most of all I want to know what the fuck is going on. Why is this guy taking my girl out to dinner? She claims he's married but how do I really know that? This isn't the first time that this has happened though... Maybe a month or two ago there was a "Jeff" but I got so upset because he was stealing her away from me everyday that I eventually just said how I felt about the situation. Funny how after that there was no more Jeff because she hasn't said a thing about him since. I should trust her a little more over these kinds of things, I know... But you can't help but to feel intimidated having known there is a guy named Carl taking your girl out to dinner soon. I mean, come on. But I mean... I was talking to my friend about it today and the first thing he said was "He's not real." What if Carl isn't real and my girlfriend is just trying to make me jealous? Maybe this is some little way to make me want her even more or something? I let my jealousy out when Jeff was around and I never heard that name again. He went from best friend to not even existing. I just don't even know anymore... I don't know what to think of this. I feel like something is being hidden from me and I it. ***"I'm finally back! I stopped at the library and went with Carl to the store"***
