**I'm Burning Sunshine** [Bagel](community.php?blogs&user=4798)comments
Well, I started playing rugby this spring and it's been a pretty fun experience so far. I decided to play in part to help stay in shape for football in the fall and also in part to peer pressure. I've never played rugby, or knew the rules of it, until this year so I've been learning on the fly. For those of you who do know a little bit about rugby, I play one of the wing positions. Anyways, this past Saturday we took 3rd in an 8-team tournament. We played 3 matches that day and we were all very tired and beaten by the third match. Granted, we only played 30-minute halves but I ended up playing all three matches in their entirety. I'm still quite sore. We won our first match which was a bit of an upset as that team supposedly crushed us last year. The second match was terrible. We all played very poorly and it was kind of embarrassing. Though, I believe that the team we lost to went on to win the tournament. In our third match, everyone was pretty dead tired. We had injuries that limited us to about 16 guys. You need 15 on the field. I had a swollen left knee but since we don't have depth at the back positions to begin with and our other wing was injured from the second match, I pretty much had to play. And, so I did and ironically, it was my best game of the tournament. I didn't score any tries but I had some long runs in which I'd pass the ball to a teammate who would then score. I also made a couple of other key plays that secured the victory and 3rd place in the tournament. Also, I got my first bloody nose from rugby. Unfortunately, there's no picture of it.
