# July 3rd:

Blogs
by (1 , 20 views) Sunday 8.13pm, July 5th 2009
**"I found a way to steal the sun from the sky"** [Bagel](community.php?blogs&user=4798)comment
Shinedown - "Fly from the Inside" There's a little event that occurs at the lakefront of Lake Michigan in Milwaukee every summer. Maybe you've heard of it - maybe not. It's called Summerfest and it's the largest music festival in the world. During 11 consecutive days the event hosts over 700 bands live on the ground's 11 stages. I decided to attend the event the past two days (July 3rd and 4th). The two headlining bands, both playing at 10pm, I wanted to see those days were Shinedown and Theory of a Deadman, respectively. Not only does the festival feature tons of great music and food, but also allows for a great view of the Milwaukee fireworks that annually occur on July 3rd. I made the trip down with a couple of hometown friends and as expected there were a ton of people down there. Not only was the crowd packed for the fireworks, but also for the Shinedown concert. We arrived at the stage they would be playing at a little over an hour before they were suppose to be on stage. There were so many people there that our standing spot made it difficult to see the stage. Fortunately, seeing the stage and hearing the music are two different stories. Shinedown performed an excellent concert. One of the most impressive things I found was that interludes to each song were epic. Although Brent Smith tended to talk a good amount in between each song, his deep voice and messages were epic enough to pass and the band behind him were supporting it with great riffs and beats. If those interludes become parts of future songs, Shinedown will be rocking for a long time. Here is the set list they played: Cry For Help Sound of Madness Save Me I Dare You 45 Heroes Fly from the Inside Sin with a Grin If You Only Knew Call Me Left Out Crow and the Butterfly Cyanide Sweet Tooth Suicide Second Chance Devour On this day, I went down by myself to meet up with a few college buddies of mine. We debated going to see the Fray play at 7:30pm but we ultimately decided not to. Instead we went to the stage where Theory of a Deadman would be playing later. We found great seats as there weren't as many people there as the previous night. At 8pm, Soil took the stage and were pretty good. I don't know many of there songs but they ended their show with two that I did know which were "Redefine Me" and "Halo". The latter song really was a great end to the show. We stayed in our place for the headlining show knowing that people would be filling in for that. Theory of a Deadman took the stage and was awesome. I was surprised at how well the singer's voice was live. Although he didn't need it for the song "Hate My Life" since the crowd literally sang the whole song with the band only playing the background music. It was a great show. I can't remember exactly what order they played their songs but here is their set list, more or less: Better Off By the Way Little Smirk So Happy Not Meant To Be No Surprise Got It Made Make Up Your Mind All Or Nothing Say Goodbye Hate My Life Santa Monica Bad Girlfriend I had a great time. **Music:**
