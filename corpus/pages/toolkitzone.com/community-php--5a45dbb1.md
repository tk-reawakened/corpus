# Blogs

by (0 , 10 views) Thursday 4.27am, February 22nd 2007
**Blog entry number 1.** [riryoku](community.php?blogs&user=15092)comments
I pretty much quit writing dedicated blogs quite a while ago, so I'm having another go now. Not that anyone will pay any sort of attention, but that's not the point. Today was OK. It was Ash Wednesday today, the start of Lent in the Catholic Church. I had a bag of beef jerky today, because I forgot that I wasn't supposed to have it. : Also, I played the bass in the worship choir dealeo. It was probably my worst performance yet as a musician. Just...abysmal. eeehhh. Yesterday, I took a Chemistry quiz on stoichiometry. I didn't make any mistakes on the calculations, but I got almost every question on the first page wrong because, well, I didn't study stoichiometry the way the book did it. :p so, basically, I knew how to work through stoichiometry calculations logically, but I paid no attention to the methods. I probably should have studied the methods, but I hate being methodical. I mean, who cares how many conversion factors it takes to complete a mass-mass calculation? Not everyone does stuff like that the same way. After a week of not studying or paying attention in class, I attempted a cramming session for the quiz, but I gave up, because I thought the way the book did calculations was stupid. I'm not cut out for chemistry, I know. :p I got an 86 on the quiz, though. Heh. In actual toolkit related news, I have actually done nothing productive. Due to the upcoming release of the next version of RPGTK, I have found myself questioning whether or not I should try to learn a good amount of 3.0.6 RPGcode just to get a good start, or hold off learning code in favor of working on graphics. I'm leaning toward the latter, since I just can't seem to grasp this programming shiz. Speaking of graphics, I'm trying to improve. The problem is that I think every single piece I do is shameful, and don't release anything I do to the public. This probably applies to anything I do, including writing and music. I'm not very prolific, because I just can't stand showing off stuff that even I don't like. Sometimes, I don't even care what other people think; if I don't like it, it gets wiped off my hard drive, period. There are always endless streams of ideas in my world, all destined to flow into the gutter. I hate that about me. Anyway, done. Maybe I'll try a daily blog and see how that works out for me. Out.
