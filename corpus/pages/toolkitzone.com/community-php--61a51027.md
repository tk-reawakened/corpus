I reply "NOT WHEN YOU CALL ME OUT OF THE DAMN SHOWER!!!!". Guess what he says? "Oh, that was what I was going to ask you". MGWTF?!!? I stump back to the shower muttering "STUPID STUPID STUPID" and thoroughly soaking the damn carpet as I get back to scrubbing my ass. *My house has two stories, and outside of the door to my bathroom there is a glassless window that overlooks the "family room". It is the most commonly used method of communication between dimensions. *rush*
