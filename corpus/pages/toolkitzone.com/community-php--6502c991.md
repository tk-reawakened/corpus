Everything blows right now... I ended up breaking up with my girlfriend because she simply doesn't give a fuck anymore. All she cares about is her games all day long... So I broke up her and now I feel down and lonely... for some reason I've lost all energy to even get out there and try to get a new girlfriend. I just don't feel like it nor do I wanna have to put up with that at the moment... I think I need a break from dating and relationships. I feel the need to play a online game right now so I returned to an old RO private server where I had a big name and then ended up making a fool out of myself and can't return in order to spare myself the humiliation. After that I didn't feel like playing Ragnarok anymore.. And well... School sucks. Everyone has moved and there's nothing exciting going on at school anymore like there used to be.... it's just... school. So there's like nobody but a whole group of uninteresting people there that have absolutely nothing to say. Also our Christmas sucked... The family is like slowly tearing apart and we did absolutely nothing Christmas eve and Christmas.... It didn't even feel like Christmas when it got here. Just felt like any other day. I was also recently showed up on the guitar and didn't feel like even plucking a string since... Since those heavy metal goth dudes mocked me. On the good side though... I did somehow manage to find hidden talent at the drums... I picked up on playing drums by myself, no lessons or anything and ended up replacing a bands drummer. Now I have a drum lust that some time during the day I need to play some kick ass beat. Though life sucks at the moment I can only hope things get better soon... I'm starting to even feel depressed. This isn't that bad =P... nothing I'd go and be all emo about, threatening to kill myself over. I do miss my girlfriend because she was perfect in my eyes but I guess it was too good to be true... Well, anyways. For Christmas I didn't get a Wii but I did manage to get some Cloths, a new watch and 3 kick ass video games >:O. Which are .hack//G.U., Zelda twilight princess for GC, and FFXII. Zelda Twilight Priness, I love... I'm on the third temple at the moment. Just getting into it anyways. I would be further but I've been addicted to .hack//G.U. for the past 2-3 days and proud of it... I love the wide environment of TP... Lake Hylia's my favorite place to run around in at the moment =P. FFXII I rented before. Good game, but I haven't played it since i last rented it... So it's still rapped in it's plastic store cover thing. .Hack//G.U. is my favorite game i got though. I absolutely LOVE this game. Even though the environments never change like in the old ones, no matter how many times you change up the area keyword... This game has a awesome story and some cool characters. I would love it if "The world" was a actual online game minus the coma stuff =D...As well as Atoli. I love her cute personality in the game... Maybe I should find my own Atoli in real life ;o... Thanks to .hack//G.U. now I wanna play some mmorpg like that... but there is no mmo like that =Y I don't think so anyways... Maybe I should go on a mmo hunt sometime tonight or tomorrow. Well... That's all for a blog. Now that I don't have "her" anymore I do sorta feel the least bit free.. I feel like I can get out and do stuff again and get to flirt with girls again and what not... So I guess that's a up...? = I'm out.
