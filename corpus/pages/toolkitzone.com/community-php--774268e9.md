**Ranting - Game Engine Woes** [rpg gamer 17](community.php?blogs&user=12066)comments
I've been trying to create a game engine but each system I create for it I get caught in; Like a spider in a ruined web. I've edited and edited my system too many times that it is a pain to work with. My failure, simplicity. I don't want to disect my masterpiece but I don't want to use it's complex nature. Even after I realized the complexity of this system, I still edited and edited. Now my system is wounded be on repair. I've warped the fragle barrier that held it together. I've tortured the foundation that I've stood upon. In my misery I reminber what an old bird once told me: before the dark comes the dawn. I felt as if there was no dawn for this darkness. There was just the crushed light of defeat. I was that light. I was crushed by my ambition to build my masterpiece. Now we both lie in a pit of despair. I am saddened but not dead. I have not been destroyed by simplicity. I will build a new. I will weave myself a new web. I will build the ground I shall stand on! I will not fear the falls that I may encounter. I dare say they will not happen again but when misfortune smiles at me; I shall smile back!
