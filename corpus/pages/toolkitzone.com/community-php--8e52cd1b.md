and I haven't slept for about 36 hours now. I feel like crap. I have no idea why I can't sleep. My eyelids have a weight hanging on them, but everytime I get in bed to go to sleep I end up tossing and turning for hours only to look at the clock and realize school's a few minutes away. I'm starting to see things moving in the corner of my eye. I have random pulsing head aches. I feel depressed as hell right now. I save my homework until about 3:00 am and decide I won't do it. I have two tests tomorrow, and again, I've done nothing to prepare. I just got off a week's vacation with family members, and back home, I feel so lonely, save for playing backgammon with my dad occasionally. I only see my friends outside of school on the weekends. I feel so depressed. I'm sick of school. I'll probably not regret saying this. Life really feels dull right now...anything I do doesn't feel productive or worth my time. Time goes by way too fast. I enjoy myself for what feels like a few minutes, only to notice that hours have passed. I lay on my bed and stare at the cieling thinking of how much I'm going to hate the next day. You must be thinking, "well, what a lazy ass." "he doesn't care about school, so he must be an idiot." "he won't succeed in life." Hmmmm...same old one a-em crap.
