Hes pretty good for taking a step out of rock and straight to rap.But in some songs it feels like he needs that rock beat to it.What do you think?
[![TBD](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/17714193054429fc3a811c7.png&w=60&h=60&iar=false&bw=1)](forum/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=3890)
Rock rarely has a definitive beat, in comparison to other genres such as rap. In my opinion anyways, in rap it's just one dumbass talking over a few drums and cymbals whereas in rock its three+ dumbasses yelling over drums and cymbals. And I honestly thought the guy in Fort Minor (Shinoda or whatever) rapped mostly on Linkin Park's albums anyways.
[![TBD](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/158838120745a6a00ea9569.jpg&w=60&h=60&iar=false&bw=1)](forum/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=148)
Mike Shinoda (aka Fort Minor) did mostly rap for Linkin Park. That said, as a big Linkin Park fan, I had high hopes for Mike Shinoda's "solo" effort, but frankly, was pretty dissappointed. There are only two or three tracks on the album that I could stand playing more than just once in a while, and some of the tracks are just plain horrible, particularly the one or two that sound like nSync got their hands on the mic. I also despise the fact that rappers today have the need to gather all their buddies and put them on their record as well, and unfortunately, the same applied to the Fort Minor album. I think it would've been much better as a truely solo album, with maybe one or two tracks featuring another artist as a guest, but "The Rising Tied" seemed to have other people rapping more than Mike was at times... The style of rapping was also decidedly different from what you hear on the LP album. Mike trys to be more of a thug, when he's just... not. For his next album, I hope he ditches Jay-Z as a producer. In all honesty, Mike could probably produce his own music and do just fine. He's got tons of talent, not only with writing, but with instruments, and music in general. Anyways, I can't wait for the next LP album to come out and let me forget this album.
