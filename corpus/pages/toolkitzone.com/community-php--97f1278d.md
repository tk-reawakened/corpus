**Windows Longhorn** [Dan](community.php?blogs&user=7)comments
A pretty new topic for the RPG Toolkit Community, but meh, I figured you may be interested. I've been following the development of Longhorn for a while now, through sites like longhorn blogs and Windows Supersite, its looking pretty sexy, I must say... However, I just saw this, and thought you might be interested to know what sort of system you may be expected to have... Source: http://www.microsoft-watch.com/article2/0,1995,1581842,00.asp Microsoft is expected to recommend that the "average" Longhorn PC feature a dual-core CPU running at 4 to 6GHz; a minimum of 2 gigs of RAM; up to a terabyte of storage; a 1 Gbit, built-in, Ethernet-wired port and an 802.11g wireless link; and a graphics processor that runs three times faster than those on the market today. Sounds a lot, doesn't it? Will be a nice system to play with, though.
[![TBD](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/121206969847e371eb6b8e3.jpg&w=60&h=60&iar=false&bw=1)](forum/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=24)
God. Who, other than rich people, is going to have that kind of a computer!?!
[![TBD](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/2662577440e35677ecd35.png&w=60&h=60&iar=false&bw=1)](forum/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=7)
Everyone with an up to date computer, I would have thought. My laptop is nearly 3ghz already, with 512mb RAM and I have an 802.11g wireless network in my house... So it's not *that* far to go, really.
There's no way in hell I'm going to have a system like that in two years. My family's main computer right now is only 850mHZ with 30 GB of hard drive space and 256 MB of RAM. We do really well, and there's no reason for us to upgrade to anything faster. If Microsoft expects systems like those to be average in just a few years (and they're not even viable systems to build right now!), then Microsoft can probably kiss all but two sales of Longhorn goodbye...

IIf Longhorn has all that DRM stuff in it when it comes out in 2006 then I'm probably gonna migrate to Linux completely. Hopefully WINE will be better by then. If I could run all my stuff on Linux then I'd never use Windows again.
