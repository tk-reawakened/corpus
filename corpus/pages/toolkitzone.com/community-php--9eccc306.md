# Blogs

by (0 , 16 views) Thursday 9.06pm, February 8th 2007
**Friggin Ignorance** [Juice](community.php?blogs&user=7931)comments
So, last Friday I got to school, got my shit together, and headed to class; same as always. Got to Bio first period and did some lab work. The teacher began the lab by instructing our groups to take out one sheet of paper per group, which we did. Got close to the end of class, so we began to clean up, etc. I asked my partner for the sheet to copy it so I can type up the official lab report over the weekend. But the bell rang, "I'll bring it home, type it, and IM you the file on AIM tonight." She says, so I'm like aight, whatever. I log on only to find out that she is going out with some people, and that she couldn't do it tonight. So once again: aight, whatever. She doesn't even sign on Sunday, which if I had school the next day I would have been screwed. But I didn't have school. It canceled due to being cold as hell. She signs on Monday night, and says she just started typing it up (which I now know was a bunch of bullshit). Then she just signs off. Thankfully, got off Tuesday as well for the same reason. She signs on Tuesday afternoon and tells me she lost the paper. -_- So, got a 2 hour delay the next day, which didn't make sense to me (its not like its going to miraculously raise 20 degrees; cancel school, or have a normal day. I don't enjoy 2 hour delays. I like the sleep, but once I'm in school, we just sit there anyway). Get into school, once again, first period Bio. "Ok, when I call your name, come back to my desk and show me your lab." She calls my name a few minutes later. I simply said "I don't have it." Same with about 4 other people who didn't do it. She goes through the list, gets up and starts rambling. She goes on and mentions me saying shit like "Its not my fault you decided to just not do it." and "You can't just decide to not do homework, but I'm not upset with you, I'm just disappointed with this crap you people try to pull." I took much offense to that, considering it wasn't even my god damn fault. I would have done the damn lab if my partner didn't lose the info. Its not like I ENJOY getting flamed by that teacher, so why would I just NOT do an assignment, especially a lab, which is like 30% of my grade. She could tell I was getting pissed. I was about ready to say what I just told you all; prepared for a detention at that point. But, I didn't say anything. Still pissed, she gives us a whole chapter to read, which didn't bother me, since I can read pretty fast. the bell rang, so we all left to get on with the day. I had nothing to do after I finished eating at lunch, which I knew would happen. So, I read that chapter from Bio. By the end of the day I had one more homework assignment due in Math, so I did it (gasp). Got a haircut that night. *fast-forward* Woke up the next day (today), no 2 hour delay, so I had less sleep that the day before. Got all my books I needed, and headed for Bio (oh joy). As soon as I walk in the door, shes already yelling at me (not shittin' you, either). She even yelled at me for the color of me pen. (It was green, which was odd. But still, fuck that, leave me alone. Its first period; I'm basically still sleeping.) Got attendance done, etc etc. Then, she's like: -"Your labs were horrible." *Blah Blah Blah* -"And Matt, where's your lab?" -"I didn't have it, remember?" -"Why didn't you do it last night?" (In that angry tone teachers have) -"I didn't know I was suppose to." -She sighs. I thought to myself, and almost started laughing because the day before I told my mom and brother about the whole situation and commented that "I hope she doesn't expect me to do that lab tonight." My brother goes "She already gave you an F, why would she." (or something to that affect). She did indeed say she gave me an F, along with all the others (who, by the way, didn't do theres last night, either. But it seemed I got the heat of her frustration, as usual.) I just basically ignored her from then till the end of the period because she is def when it comes to reason. Shes a teacher so I know: she's heard every excuse in the book. But don't you think there is a slight chance that an excuse is true? And that I didn't just decide to not do her damn lab two nights in a row? Not to mention, she NEVER told any of us we had to do the lab that night. And judging by the fact she already gave us F's, its not its implied that I should do it for fun. That chapter she assigned: no one read it, or people just forgot a lot (which I did). And by asking questions, she could tell. So now tonight for homework we have to outline the whole chapter. And in this outline, we have to give 2 facts per paragraph...per paragraph. ;___________; I love it how some teachers can be so ignorant. I also love how when a teacher forgets something for her lesson, the students suffer. And when a student forgets to bring something to class...the students suffer. We never do win. Its not worth complaining about most things, since its 'life', but some things I just don't take. But you really can't stick up for yourself much, cus if you do, chances are it leads to a detention. Even if you say nothing 'bad', they'll give it to you just for talking back or something. When I move, I hope I can keep myself (on the last day at that school) from flipping my bio teacher the bird. Friggin Ignorance. -_-
