**Run, Forest, Run!** [Bagel](community.php?blogs&user=4798)comment
Wow. This has to be the shortest time span in between blogs I've written. Wait, nope. Upon further review, May of 2006 holds that record for me. Oh well. Anyhow, this afternoon, I helped in a fundraiser for the local volunteer fire department. They are raising money for a new training tower. The closest training tower is around 2-3 hours away so this is something they really need. I did this with a group of around 25 friends (read: my fellow fraternity brothers) and collectively we ran a little more than 200 laps around a residence hall area driveway at my college. Each of us ran 8 laps though some people decided to run a couple extra. We received donations for each lap we ran. Also, we held a cookout for people to grab food and a couple individual contests for people to get involved in. Unfortunately, the weather was overcast and rainy and a little cool so the turnout was not as good as it has been in the past. However, we weren't going to let a little rain stop us. In terms of the running of laps, we ran in groups of 4 or 5 with each person donning full firefighter gear. The tank itself weighed around 65 lbs (30 kg for you metric folks). I only weigh 160 lbs (73 kg) myself so I was carrying almost half my body weight. And I must say that it was damn tiring. I guess in the end, it was a good thing that the weather wasn't hot and sunny. That would have been brutal. Hooray for PICTURES!
