**Thrilling winter expo news** [RPGLand2000](community.php?blogs&user=557)comment
Today, I signed up for the winter expo. What do I have? A plot, a tenative features list and some board designs. Actually, many board designs. Problem is, I drew the board designs and I don't have a scanner. Nor a good program to re-create them in. So, I'm using MS Paint. I'll try to exactly reproduce my drawings, and if not, I'll make some random crappy artwork. So what will the features be in my game? -You will be a stick figure. -The game's objective is to cross the board and touch a square. -Touch the square, and you win! -Once you win, you play a 3d remake of Mini-Cars. This remake is also known as "Big Rigs". -No programming. Well, no programming without bugs in it, at least. -All dialouge is in l33t speak. -And, finally, you spend the last board fighting a copy of Heraldy: Freija, deriding it as a uninspired piece of crap before you actually see the battle system or anything beyond a tech demo.
