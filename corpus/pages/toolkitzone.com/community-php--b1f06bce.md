Today was fun...had to hang out at my mom's work all day and help her out, since I'm not cleared to go back to school till tomorrow. Of course, I did have almost all day to surf the net and chat with one of my senior friends (and finish up some of the hw that I neglected while I was in the hospital, lol.) I also took advantage of some of my spare time to get some more storylines written down. I'll tell ya, the antagonist that I have planned for "Light in the Dark" is coming out to be even more of a bastard than I originally pictured. Let's just hope that Trent can do his voice right. I start back on martial arts at 7 pm tonight, so yeah. I'm personally hoping that Master Kevin won't ask too many questions, since my mom told him that I had been cutting myself and all...but if he asks, I'll have no choice but to answer. I mean, he's cool and all, but I'd be afraid to talk to him about it...I just dont feel it's his business, really, sorry to say. Jesus, if Im talking about lunchmeats and all, it really must have been a while since I made lunch for school...like 3 years, in fact! 3 years I've been going without eating lunch in school. Thank god that 3 year period is over. I haven't seen Amy online recently, but then again, she's not on much. I heard from a friend of mine that she's really worried about me because of my absence from school and all, but I'm sure she'll understand when I tell her tomorrow morning. I mean, she's not the kind of person who would hate me for the rest of my life for going to the hospital, so yeah. Besides, after this pic was taken: I highly doubt that she'd suddenly change her opinion of me and all because of a hospital stay. Of course, if she does decide to hate me, then I guess it's for the best. That will leave me open for some of the other girls at River Hill, heh.
