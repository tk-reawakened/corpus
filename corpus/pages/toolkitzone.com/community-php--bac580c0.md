# Blogs

by (5 , 8 views) Thursday 6.44am, September 1st 2005
So, I'm taking this Latin course where I go to school, and there's this hot girl in there. While waiting for the previous class to finish so we could all go in, me and my classmates were all sitting in the hall against the walls. That hot girl came, and sat down over by me at a blank spot on the wall. We didn't say anything, but man, I could so totally tell she was into me! Classes have been decent this week so far. Nothing too terribly difficult to cope with, but that'll change once we get past the Labor Day weekend. I'm already enjoying myself far more than I was last year. "He is extremely weird, but the class is SUPER easy." - RateMyProfessor.com note on professor Philosophy is an interesting class. The professor is pretty good at explaining the subjects, and has the energy to teach the subject well. It's a lot of fun to be in the class, and I'm really looking forward to more of it. There's a lot of reading involved, but once you get past the initial shock value of things, the reading isn't too bad. "This guy is a nut, but his class is super easy." Psychology will be my least favorite class, I think, just because it's the only class of mine in a huge lecture hall. I hate the lecture hall classes, since they're all filled with noisy freshmen and you can never hear the professor unless you sit in the first three rows or so. While I do like the subject of psychology, being in lecture halls is just too distracting for me, especially since I'm the type of guy who gets sidetracked by a squirrel collecting nuts or a girl pouring coffee at a restaurant. "He is crazy but completely worth it." Latin is a neat class. The professor is, well, strange. He likes to ramble a bit, and say strange things, or throw chalk, but he's a nice guy. He likes the subject, and knows it too. We won't be required to actually listen and understand spoken Latin, which is a real plus, since that was my problem area in French, listening comprehension. "He is ill-tempered and likes to "shake the tree" of the department... looking for the bad apples to fall." The assembly language and computer architecture class is going to be hard. The material will be hard, and the professor will be hard; I can't understand the guy. He's an old Indian man with a thick accent that tends to mumble. The first day of class, I was only picking up around 80% of what he was saying. I'm really wondering how many CPS people will drop out of his class, since it's pretty much required for a degree. I'm not going to drop, though; I must stick through it. I'll just find a way. "Awesome dude. He likes having fun during lectures, which lightens the mood." Finally, data structures will be a great class. The professor digresses quite a bit (reminds me of me), but knows his stuff. It's going to be a lot of theory in the class, which will be fine with me; I like to learn why something is the way it is rather than being given a piece of code to copy and memorise. I'm looking forward to learning something.
