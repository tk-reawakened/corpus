**Met a skeptic** [Dave the Hamster](community.php?blogs&user=16496)comments
Yesterday I was finishing up one of my finals. I was transfering my traditional animation file to the school computer for the teacher to collect when one of the people in class starts talking to me about my toolkit project. He then stated to me that I would not finish it. I know that the toolkit is littered with unfinished projects, mine being among them, but I though it was rather rude and whatnot to be assuming that because he never finished his game that he was working on in whatever program he was using that the same fate is with me. Seemed a little bit on the biggoted side to me. I think it's up to me as to whether or not "Legend of Exile" gets finished or not. Heck, thats one of the reasons that I'm trying to do most of it myself, so that I don't have to worry about any kind of time constraints or unrelaible people, which have both sunk personal projects in the past. I mean, "Legend of Exile" has been in development for over 2 years now, and I'll admit that it's not that far along. Thats what I get for being a student and for having a short attention span. But I say this now: "Legend of Exile" WILL be finished. It may be YEARS from now. But someday I'll have it up on here for all of you wonderful toolkitzone people to download for free for helping me to make it happen! This guy obviously didn't have such a wonderful community filled with happy little helpers who jump at the chance to give you coding advice, act as testers, or critiquer for your work. Take that you skeptical little man you! And Good night!
