After reading TKGB's new TK Rant about me, I realized that I don't remember the first half of the summer. It's not a good thing when chunks (in this case the month of June) of your life are missing. Anyways, so I started pixeling again, only to notice, I have lost a lot of my skill (if there was any to begin with). I don't at all contribute this to the summer I've had, but only to the lack of practice as a result of the summer I've had. Well, I guess my life is getting better, but I'm not quite sure. Found a girl...kinda. Technically she is going out with the bassist in my band, but that's just a minor technicality. Also she's never technically cheated on him with me, they were on a break when everything happened. But she's leaving for college this Saturday. We agreed that we would hang out Wednesday, and I have one thing to say. I don't give a rat's ass about the fact she is going out with my bassist. She does not like him, he is bound on fixing it, it's not gonna happen. She's planning on breaking up with him when she gets to Marist anyways. So that's good. But I'm gonna miss her a lot. It's gonna be hard. Also for those who haven't already heard (which is just about all of you). I have a job. Yup. I work at the Dunkin' Donuts across from the police station. For all those who do not Dunkin' Donuts in their area/country, it's a doughnut/coffee shop. So I come home smelling like coffee. The hours are sometimes ridiculous though. Especially morning shift. School starts Thursday. I'm gonna be a senior in high school which is exciting. My school (along with many in the US) offer some classes for college credit. I am going to be taking 3 this year. Two of them (English and Physics) are UConn credits and one of them (Calculus) is accepted as a college credit if you get a certain score on the AP Exam. For the college I am aspiring to go to (Rochester Institute of Technology) I have to get a 4 for them to accept it as college credit. Because Math is my favorite subject, I think I will have no problem doing that. But I could be wrong. So that's all. Just wanted to talk about my life, which I do quite a lot. I like to write it down/type it because it allows me to reflect upon my life. For now, I'm going to bed. I need a fucking cigarette(Hooray lung cancer)... -Zack
