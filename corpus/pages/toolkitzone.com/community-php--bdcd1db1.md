You're searching for intelligent life in a cemetery? The last one is my favorite as well. Why is it that so many black and white photos are actually brown and white?
Well, for the work I like to do, I prefer a little bit warmer tones; straight black and white always looks a little bit too cold for me. The people I work with seem to prefer warmer tones, too, so that may have had some impact on how I view my work. I've played around with some other colors for toning, but I've never really liked the effect (but, I do have a book on night photography where the author has been pretty successful with other colors). I think there's a little bit of a nostalgia factor, too, since so many photographs used to be sepia toned, or at least have aged and have become brown.
