I'm a self-proclaimed FF8 Guru. =P It really depends on the type of game you're playing. Most people prefer to keep their levels low and abuse the junction system. You can junction the right stuff and have characters at base levels with 9999 HP, for example - and because low-leveled, the enemies will be, too - but because you junction to your stats to raise them up, you're basically level sixty or so if you want to compare, and you can beat everyone easily. Most people don't take the time to figure out the junction system and ways it can be abused, it seems. And I couldn't tell you exactly how to do this - you can probably find a FAQ on GameFAQs that tells you how - but refine magic to get the Aura spell. I forget exactly what items you'll need to refine... but trust me. You can dominate this game easily with the Aura spell. And if you don't know, Aura gives characters their limit breaks, even at full HP, over and over and over again... so you can use the already overpowerd limit breaks to easily beat the game. If you're lucky with Squall, you can chain Lion Heats.... O_O If you've yet to see the damage this mug causes... you'll piss yourself. I'm not lying. Imagine just casting Aura on Squall, hitting his limit break, him using Lion Heart after the Renzokuken, and killing the final boss. The most powerful GF would have to be Eden. At level 35, if I'm not mistaken, his HP maxes out. That's why teaching him the GFHP*% abilities is pointless (though you should teach him the GFDam+% abilities). His attacks on average, when you first get him (with me, at any rate) is around 135000. No, I'm not kidding. He breaks the damage barrier... one of the few attacks that can do that (Cactuar's 1000 Needles does slightly at level 100, as does Kamikaze, and one other that I'm forgetting). This, however, introduces a new damage barrier that caps at around 17000 HP, if I'm not mistaken, and the only way to top that is with hacking, only possible with cheat devices like a GameShark or on the PC. It is powerful enough to kill almost all normal monsters, if not all, all early bosses, and some of the end bosses. While this may seem like a lot, Squall's limit break Lion Heart breaks that barrier. You can get this if you're willing to put about seven hours (as the reported average) of work into it as early as disc 1, but its easier to be got after the Ragnarok on Disc 3. Top out his strength stat at 255 (not that hard), pull a Lion Heart out yer ass... 9999 chained. I forget what it ads up to, but its enough to kill everything except the final boss and the optional bosses Ultima Weapon and Omega Weapon at high levels (though at low levels, it can still accomplish this). So your stats HP-wise and what I assume your stats would be from your level and an educated guess at your junctions, I would have to say they probably suck. But what sucks more is that your overleveled.... If you want to make the game easier. It gets boring working with the Junction system, but if you're willing to spend at least an estimated three hours at the least over the course of the game, it'll make the game a LOT easier.
