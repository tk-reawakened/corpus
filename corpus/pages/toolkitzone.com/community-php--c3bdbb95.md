# Monday- 6/19/06

Blogs
by (0 , 1 view) Tuesday 4.13am, June 20th 2006
**Got my camera working right.** [Icbim](community.php?blogs&user=3770)comments
Turns out it was infected...It's working now and now I just need 3 AAA batteries. When I have some, i'm gonna start a photoblog occasionally on some places in NC. Just to get you seeing what i've lived around for 16 long years. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Well, today was nothing that special. It started off with me waking up and going for a walk around the neighborhood...I do this around 8am everyday...Just because it's quite outside, no neighbors are out and it's beautiful out. So I walk around for 30-40 minutes in loops and the annoying little chiwawa dog follows me and yipes. After all that I just hung around the house for awhile...My mom brought home some news that we were gonna go look at this house this afternoon...I was kinda excited because I had nothing planned this afternoon besides head over to showmaker guitars. So the day passed by and we finally got to the house looking part. We got there was nice and big...had a nice, big yard. I was excited...We got in and looked at the inside of the kitchen. It was horrible, but nothing we couldn't fix. Mainly the old lady wall paper was bugging me out. It pissed me off that we didn't have a dishwasher in there either...and the appliances were kinda old. Next was the living room. It was a good looking living room. Nice window and all else. Had a cool front door. It led off into the stairs that lead up to two rooms as well as led off into the master bedroom. We checked out the bedroom first which was nothing more than a big room with a big room and two walk-in closets as well as a big bathroom. Up the stairs were two rooms, two closets, a bathroom and a attic. The two rooms were nice with windows...One could be my room and the other a work area for the computer and the guitars and stuff. The bathroom was tiny, but why the hell do I care? I'm a dude, I can cope . The backyard was the last stop. It was . Alot of trees to provide shade, a fenced in area for a dog, a huge deck, shed, beautiful garden and a huge area that we plan to turn into a in-ground pool if we decide to buy the house. I'm praying that we get the house, cause I simply love the feel. I'm doubting it now 'cause my mom says it is too tiny. She wants something she can get lost in. Looks like the tables have turned and i'm just gonna have to cope again. We may find something better...I'll post some picture nect time, though. I had no batteries today . That was about it. We stopped by Panera Bread for a frappicino thing...I love those things, Even though I hate coffee. Another day...I have yet to plan anything for the rest of this week with any friends....I'm just that lazy. I guess I could use this time to haul ass on my game. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Yeah, yeah...Quit trying to be so spiritual..I know. I'm serious, though. I don't think i've realized yet what I can truly do. I mean, I can play alot of songs that require some pretty fast movements and what not... I just need to realize it and when I do I should have that one hit song that i'll fall in love with. None of this tiddy baby stuff, either. I need something with pure soul...something I can bop my head to in a pop-acoustic/rock way. When I do...You guys'll be the first to know . ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Well, i'm outta here. You guys enjoy the rest of your day. -Icbim
