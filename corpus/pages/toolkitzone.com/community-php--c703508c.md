**Drama Monologues** [Gurgan Dellik](community.php?blogs&user=3463)comment
Well as very few of you know, I'm in a beggining drama class. At the start of the spring semester I had no third period. So I went to my counsellor to get one and she told me that the only open classes were dance, choir, or drama. I was horrified... I took the third choice, knowing the teacher. Anyways we are currently doing monologues in the class. I performed a couple of days ago, wednesday actually. The goal was to do a caricature and I chose to do mine as a Philosopher giving a lecture at a university. Well here it is... "What is life? Or more specifically; what is the purpose of life? Is there an afterlife? And If so, is this life some sort of proving ground, some sort of test, by through our actions our ultimate fate is decided? Then if life is in fact a test, is it a fair one; with everyone having different trials set before them? These are just some of the questions which answers I strive for. Where would one find these answers? The only logical place would be the ?afterlife? itself. Which would in turn void the reason of knowing. So instead of the logical we must look to the illogical, to intuition and insight. It?s the only thing we can go by. Through rationalizing we will accomplish nothing. Nothing but an endless cycle of unanswered questions. You must simply know the answer. Know it as you would? say? an emotion. Love for example. Now love is something completely divided from rational thought. You don?t use logic and reasoning to determine how you feel. No, you just simply know. It?s just there. Your pulse quickens in their presence. You blush at the mention of their name. All you want to do is take their hand in yours, look into their eyes, and tell them how you feel. Irrational? Yes, but it exhibits so much more truth than most of what is so called ?rational.? How could you rationalize that you love someone? How many reasons would it take to officially declare it? It can?t be done. You just have to know. You just have to know. This all brings me back to my original questions. What is the purpose of life? Is there an afterlife? Is this life some sort of test? And; is it a fair one? And my conclusion is? My conclusion is that I don?t know. Simply three words often given by today?s youth, I don?t know. So, for now, all I can do is live my life the very best I can. [sigh] Perhaps embrace some irrationality and maybe, just maybe, find a little bit of truth in it? Thank you for listening; you have been a wonderful audience. Farewell and good-luck on your journeys." Imagine me doing wild hand gestures through out and being overly dramatic during well... everything. I thought it was crap, which it is. However everyone loved it. One of the teacher assistants asked if they could use it in an upcoming audition... crazy bastard. Anywho, at least I remembered my lines... unlike most of the rest of the class.
