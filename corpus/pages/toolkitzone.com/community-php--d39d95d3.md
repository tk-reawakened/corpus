**Gourmet Nirvana Sentai** [Gnarf](community.php?blogs&user=374)comments
"Smells Like Teen Spirit" What's wrong with me!? It took me this long to realize how great Nirvana is and was. I've heard of them for so long but never bothered to listen. I'm in love with most of their songs since a few hours ago. It's a shame, though; after reading up on Kurt Cobain I really feel sympathy for the guy. He had an awesome voice, nonetheless, and that's really why their songs seem to sound so darn good. Well, I've listened to "Smells Like Teen Spirit" most of all the songs I've gotten, and it never gets old. Just sounds so fecking awesome! Also, I heard "It's My Life" by Bon Jovi at school today and I liked the first minute, so I decided to listen to it all. It's not bad, in fact another one of my favorites now. I'd probably get trashed by hardcore fans since my preferences seem to be all over the place...except for rap. Aside, I've been bored as usual. This mostly ends up in me playing random SNES games I've never heard of before. I discovered Gourmet Sentai, recently, and it's really an entertaining beat 'em up. Rather than just smashing the attack button (which there's plenty of) you can also do some neat combos and collect a small variety of food from each enemy you defeat. This is great, because aside from near impossible bosses (further into the game, saving states becomes your friend), at the end of each level you're allowed to make a combination dish using two ingredients that were found during any previous levels. Such combinations as tofu and beef add up to equal a salad...yum! You've now gained 1/20 of the health that you lost, making you completely ready for the next mission. It's not too bad though, since you're given the ability to continue from the last checkpoint in the level (with full health...). Now, eye candy: Each character is given a wide variety of erotic poses that serve to be completely useless and open yourself for an attack. The names are great, though. Gotta admit, that's a nice ass. Anyway, one boss was a giant cat with huge mechanical balls, too explicit for TKZ... This game...truly does rock.
