Mood: Listening To: every song on my computer So it's like this: I don't think I have the best voice, and I'm only a decent dancer (thank you DDR!). However, my drama teacher REALLY wants me to audition for the spring musical ("Once on This Island"), so I did. I started the vocal auditions as a hit, giving a great answer to the director's question. As I was doing scales I noticed the director nodding a few times, which I've learned to be a positive thing. However, I was not confident in my audition, and thusly nervous as all hell to do the dancing auditions tonight. However, after ten minutes of practice I was ordered to join my group in the dance auditions (in which EVERYONE trying out got to watch me). Surely enough, I was the best one in my group, and one of the best males auditioning. I guess I did well overall, but I won't find out until January 3rd.TBD
