Got off from school today, since it was like -20 outside. But theres always a catch, isn't there? Began getting sick last evening, and by the time it was time to go to bed, I was miserable. Woke up at 9, which anyone who actually knows me, that happens on days off. I'm usually out cold until about 12 or 1. Made some midis on Saturday for Montai, and hopefully I'll get on today to edit the midis, then make them awesome mp3s. Started work on my new site, which is good. Not positive, but I settled on the name Obscur'd. Got a banner completed *cough*Indy*cough*, so look out for that. And G_D offered to do a special little something as well. We'll see what happens this month. I hope I get better soon, since I'm sure I'll have school tomorrow, unless is friggin cold again. Hopefully we eat some soup or something tonight, which always makes me feel awesome. Time to get back to work, but first, The Price is Right seems to be on. I'll watch that for a bit. *sniff sniff* Damn sicknesses. -_- *never*
