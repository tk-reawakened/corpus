You may know that I am one of the new staff, and I review Audio. Some of you may like that, others may not. I am quite glad I got the position and I hope I get everyone's approval. If you'd like to, I welcome, in fact, members(perhaps former reviewers) to suggest ideas, methods, etc, on how you would like me to review. I have started my reviews with some simple text. Basically, a quick description and I'll tell what the pack (set) will contain. I tried to keep it simple and to the point, because I know some people see a huge paragraph and say 'screw it'. But others may not agree. So thats why I'm posting this. I didn't really think it would be appropriate as a thread, so I just made a blog. So, ideas, methods, suggestions, problems, crits, etc?
