Long time, no see toolkitzone! I see new users around as well as some new games and so on. We need to do some catching up ... For those of you who are pretty new and don't know me, i'm Icbim as it says as the username... And some of you may already know me... Maybe as I_Cant_Believe_Its_Me or as Icbim since you guys did give me the name after all =P. I think i'm going a little over-board with the catching up because I was only gone for a couple of months... 4-5 months, maybe less. Last thing i did was snag the reward as runner-up in the Summer Expos of 2006 Best Story award for my game "Antique" and then after that I ran off to do my own thing with my music and playing some online games as well as to the side I've been writing new stories and getting back on life stuff. I've also been working on some art on the computer trying to grow out of the basic cartoon style to a much better version of my work. Well, game-wise I've done nothing for either of my two games in my profile... Fantasy World Era is currently moved to display due to i'm waiting on 3.07(Octember, eh? =P) and Antique... Well the runner-up award made me slack heheh. Well, as for my return i'm doing alot of thinking over what I should do game-wise... I won't be attending the winter expos since I have nothing new to ride on and... Well as much as I change my game-making team I've decided to start fresh with a new name, new style, new everything including a new Icbim. Things happening with me over these past couple of months... - We never did move, as one of my old blogs said I was bound to do. - Last blog I lost 13 pounds... This blog I lost 47 pounds over the time and happy because I look good and not big. - Last time you heard from me I was the joker that had no true sense in what a good girl is who dated the ditsy blonds ... So I've been in a relationship with a down to earth, smart and very funny girl for 3 months now and she's all I could ever ask for. - I'm a Junior. I was scared I wasn't going to pass but i did. - I went from the chubby class-clown kid to the laid back guy that looks good now because he lost tons of weight... Which had everyone impressed and now everyone takes me seriously rather than the kid that does nothing but crack jokes. - I never did get that private driving instructor and i'm still without my license... - My mom hasn't talked to that guy that I hate so much since I left here. - My guitar playing has improved miraculously as well as my vocals with it... I'm still song-less, but I play better than ever now and I could write a song at any moment... I just don't know what i'm waiting for. - Speaking of music I joined a band with my best friend Geoff in it. I've changed in so many ways over the couple months... It's crazy but it's the truth. Speaking more about the band i'm in... "Opinion of the Affair".... We've been together for about 3 months now. We have tons of music but the only songs recorded are the really emotional piano songs. Yeah, our band reeks of emotional music and I hate it but it's a band full of my best friends so I can't pass that up. I play lead guitar in the band, Robert is the lead singer and plays piano, Geoff (my best friend) plays back up guitar and back up vocals, and Josh plays the crazy Jeep-Wrangler loving drummer. We've shared some great experiences together... band trips and what not. In future blogs I can fill you guys in with it... Right now we're just not practicing as much due to Robert going through a break up and it hit him hard because him and his girl had been together for like two years now and that's a damn long time. I guess I've done enough blogging for today. I missed you guys... and Lisha's =P... Not unless we've gotten more girl members over the time I've been gone. Meh, who knows? It's great to be back~! -ICBIMTBD
