**Tic-Tac-Tesseract** [Colin](community.php?blogs&user=226)comments
Imagine a two-dimensional tic-tac-toe board, if you will. Now, from our two-dimensional plane, imagine two more two-dimensional planets coming off the side of it, to get this: Now we have a three-dimensional space. We are going to let two more three-dimensional spaces come off the side of this, which I illustrate like this: And this is the brilliant game which I call Tic-Tac-Tesseract. It half-rhymes! Note that if you think it would be possible to draw a line connecting more than three spaces here, you are not appreciating what I am trying to illustrate. The tesseract is impossible to imagine because the brain has been evolved to model only three dimensional images, but we won't let that stop us from playing tic-tac-tesseract once I have an online version ready. It's quite fun; I have tried it on paper. I think it works better if we play for points (total number of lines) rather than for the first line because the former opens up a lot more room for strategy. As I said, I played a couple games of this (I also played tic-tac-5- and 6- polytope but that was a little too crazy!) and it is quite intriguing. It's also a good exercise in imagining tesseracts.
