# Blogs

by (3 , 23 views) Sunday 4.59pm, February 18th 2007
I feel today's a perfect day for a blog. I haven't been around lately... Mainly because i'm either waiting for 3.07 or it's music or something else. I've been up to alot lately, though. Music-wise, my name's starting to get out around the area and people are starting to ask me for help in writing songs. I guess you could say it's starting to be a job for me, helping bands with writing their music. I'm still in the same band that I joined back in September though... which we changed the name to "The War is at 2" which I don't really like but I honestly don't care. I'm still working on my own music seperatly from the band though because i'm more into acoustic stuff than the band's emo rockish crap. And away from all that me and my best friend Geoff are going to start a side project because we know a guy named eric who wanted to sing for our band but the lead singer of our band doesn't like Eric [I'd say even jealous of Eric] so me and Geoff are starting our own band to let Eric sing in it since he really wants to be in a band. So yeah, i'm getting into alot with music. Still playing guitar, starting to play drums, getting my harmonica skills back and I still suck at the piano. And believe it or not i'm starting to get into acting to. I tried out for a TV show and made it as a permanent cast member [i'm surprised I did though because I was nervous and shaky at the try-outs]. It's not a big tv show or anything, it's just more of a local show that comes on around here... But TV is TV. I wouldn't pass that up. It's more of a show about teens and for teens... It's really fun to go to and just hang out with the rest of the cast, especially since Geoff's on there too. I also started up my myspace again... I deleted my old one awhile back and now it's back up. The url: http://www.myspace.com/icbim1 If you have a myspace and wanna add me, knock yourself out. That's pretty much all the big stuff. The rest is just everyday life stuff like girls and school and what not. I'm out.TBD
