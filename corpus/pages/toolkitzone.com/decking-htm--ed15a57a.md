# Deck Building and Repair

[![Toolkitzone](http://www.toolkitzone.com/images/Toolkitzone.png)](http://www.toolkitzone.com)

Few things can add as much value and interest to a home as a beautiful deck. This is especially true with homes that have a pool or perhaps a great view to marvel at from the deck's surface. If you feel like you could get something great out of a new deck, then you have never had more options than you do today. All you need to do is decide on the type of deck you need and the materials you want to use. Once these decisions are made, the rest of the process is very easy to get through by comparison.
Deck Materials
There are many different materials that decks are made out of these days and the material you select will depend mostly on your climate. Composite materials are very popular in some parts of the country while metal is popular in others. Wood is still widely used throughout the country on deck builds, but the specific type of wood used depends heavily on the area. Since selecting a deck material is the first and most important part of having a deck built, this is not a decision that should be made lightly.
Composite Materials
Composite materials are commonly used on decks that are found in very cold or very wet climates around the country. To put it simply, composite deck materials are made of a mixture of moisture and weather-resistant materials. ABS plastic is a very common material used in composite decks due to its high level of weather resistance and ability to withstand shock. Since it is a naturally resistant material, you don't need to regularly treat it like you would with a wood deck. This can end up saving you a lot of money, time and effort throughout the life of your deck.
Metal Materials
Metal decks are popular in very specific areas for a few unique factors. In some areas, the weather gets so cold that other materials simply can't stand up to it. Wood decks in areas where temperatures drop to extreme lows get dried out very quickly and become brittle. This means that their lifespan is significantly decreased in very dry areas, which makes metal or composite almost a necessity. Many people opt for metal deck materials over composite materials because of the cost involved. Metal deck supplies are often much less expensive than their composite analogues.
Wood Deck Materials
Wood decks are still the most popular decks you'll find attached to homes all around the country. Many people cite the "classic" look and feel that you can really only get with a genuine wood deck. These decks are great for use in coastal ares and you will find many wooden decks outside of homes along coasts. Metal decking materials corrode very quickly on the coasts due to the salt in the air. By contrast, wood decks stand up to this environment very well. This is especially true with cedar decks since cedar is naturally resistant to rotting and corroding from salt air.
Selecting a Design
There are many different types of decks you can choose from when you are setting a deck up at your home. The exact shape and style of your deck will depend almost entirely on the look and style of your home.. You want a deck that will blend in naturally with your home and accentuate its beauty.
