**Luminus**
by Shadio **Starting up the story Luminus**
May 5 2006, 3:02 pm
'Sup?...Im starting on my new and first rpg...Luminus...of course as you can see on the description the game is fully based on "dreams" and "nightmares". Of course I cant tell you much about the gameplay and all...cuz... I havent even started in making the main characters and all...but I've already made part of the story...the story is about a kingdom called Luminus, named after the great god of dreams that gave his life to save his kingdom and all the children from the nightmares...He used all of his powers to create a giant crystal emerald which trapped all the nightmares inside...and he made 10 small crystals to protect it...he was completely desintagrated in dust...the giant emerald was called Luminus and so was the kingdom for the sacrifice of the god of dreams...Lumina, Luminus's wife, felt very lonely after her husband's death, so she created her first daughter which she called Luz...16 years after that horrible incident...a weird man wearing a black robe appeared on the suburbs of the kingdom...now...there is a new nightmare on it's way to bring chaos to the world of dreams...
