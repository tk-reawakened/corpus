# Labrinth 13

July 21, 2005, 10:48 am
Development
A superhero rpg in which a group of teenagers are kidnapped and supjected to experiments which give them various superpowers. Escaping, they join a secret government strike force to stop their former captors.
**Labrinth 13**
by catwhowalksbyhimself **Game Features**
January 3 2005, 4:46 pm
Game flow Features.
>
> After the first part of the game, there will be a custom mission system. There will you will be able to send your team to one of several sites and will investigate it to find a Labrinth. Some will have none, others will have abandoned Labs as well as fully equiped ones. Depending on which one you choose, it will effect the next set of available missions, causing some bases to be abandoned, and allowing your team to discover more possible locations as well as eliminating others. Each mission may have different characters to recruit as well as different points of view of the main storyline. As a result, the game should have good replay value.
>
> There will be a large number of recruitable characters. The last two missions, however, will require you to split the into two teams of up to seven characters, so you must use different characters and have at least fourteen leveled up character ready, or you will be in trouble! Also, because of the mission selection system as well as other choices made in the game, you will not be able to get all, or even close to all, of the possible characters in any one game, although the main seven plus the three Argonaut members will always be present.
>
> Most missions will have one character whom you must have on your team. The other slot will be fully selectable. That character will have something to do with the story in that mission.
>
