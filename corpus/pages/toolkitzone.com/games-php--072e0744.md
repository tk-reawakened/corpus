Quintessence is an role-play game set in a fantasy world. The story revolves around Ilya, a knight of the Kingdom of Starafel who leads a small band of elite warriors on a mission to retrieve the Quintessence, an ancient object which holds celestial knowledge and exerts control over armies and empires. A war is raging north of the mountains, which moves ever further south. It is feared the Quintessence, thought to be mythical artefact, has been discovered and wielded by the clans of the north to raise a war upon the world. With its power they would not lose. As Ilya, you are the only hope for your kingdom on the brink of destruction. Only a handful believe the enemy possess the Quintessence, and its whereabouts is unknown - the armies do not carry it. You must battle and travel over great distances through deadly lands in your search for this ultimate device before there is nothing left to fight for...![Thumbnail](http://www.qtk.ip3.co.uk/img/square1.t.jpg)

- Original graphics

- Puzzles and more...
