# The Eudima

October 5, 2006, 8:59 pm
Design
Welcome to a world made of nostalgic 16x16 tiles in a single 32-color pallette used for the whole game. Follow the journey of the man who will carve out a whole new world through the use of a mystical artifact called the Eudima. Featuring: Homemade 8-bit(ish) graphics, an original orchestral score (in midi format -.-) composed by moi, a custom battle system, menu system, item system, and the works.
