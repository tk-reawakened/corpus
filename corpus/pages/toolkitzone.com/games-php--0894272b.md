The Battle System, as can be seen, is still far from done, but this is a graphical and interface update. I have added some slick gradients and the like graphically, but the programming is the major difference. There are three tabs at the bottom (one is popped up). When it is your turn, you will click on one of these tabs (or press the corresponding hot key) to make it rise up into a full menu (fight is already risen up). You will then be able to select different commands from each category. I have a lot of techniques and special features, so I had to divide it up this way. And the characters/enemies/tiles are not permanent. They are just for testing. ;) ~
