# Illicit Soul

January 31, 2006, 4:32 pm
Development
A young woman awakens in the untamed forest world of Kalahan, unsure of her origin or purpose. Along with a band of fast friends, she'll embark on a mission of self-discovery, and indeed, discover the very nature of the cosmos.
**Illicit Soul**
by venstor **What's Coming in the Demo**
June 15 2004, 3:34 pm
I've been making RPGs on paper for years, so I've got complete games sitting around in my head, but once you realize how much work it is to draw tilesets and program, you have to set realistic goals. That said, here's what I plan to include in the first demo:
Five areas:
Ursprung Forest: The first "dungeon" you'll have to escape from right off the bat.
Rorick and Kaleen's House: A place of rest where you'll form your final party.
The World Map: Self-explanatory
Part of Woodtown: The huge town in the center of the world map. I probably won't be able to build it all for the demo, so I'll just include the business district so that players can buy better weapons.
A forest temple: An ancient temple that will serve as the demo's final dungeon. In the real game, it'd still be an introductory area.
Three Playable Characters
Was originally going to be five, with players able to form their own parties of three, but for the demo I'll just go with Cerna, Rorick, and Kaleen. I still plan to let players choose their own party order. I drew these sprites, and darn it if people are going to see them all. It'd be nice to make a custom menu and have an "Order" button, but since that's not realistic, there will probably be a key item you can use to change order.
Full Monster Set
Since I already designed and pixeled all my monsters months ago, they're all ready to be placed in the game. I plan to use as many of them as possible. All I have to do is determine their stats.
Default Battle System (sort of...)
Some day, when I'm more experienced with programming, I'll write my own. For now, I just plan to cherry out the default thing. I've got a program written that randomly selects from predetermined parties of monsters instead of facing off against one or four of whatever enemy the game deems fit. That means I can make players fight four weak enemies or one very strong one and not have to worry about the game dishing out impossible battles of four strong enemies. I'm eliminating levels. Players will always stay at level one. Stat boosts will come from new equipment. This allows me to more easily balance the game and give it a correct difficulty level. The incentive to battle will be merely to gain gold. I'll try to keep new weapons fairly cheap so that players don't have to do too many random battles, which annoy the crap out of me. I'm also getting rid of the SMP system. I've always hated how useless mages become once they're out of MP. Spells are now innate and can be used as many times as the player wants. I'm using the SMP and MaxSMP stats as Magic Attack and Defense now, and writing that into the coding of spells. This way, a warrior can have high defense but be incredibly weak to enemy spells, mages can be used to attack enemies with high defense, etc. Much better system, in my opinion.
Default Shop System
The plan is to eventually replace this with a synthesis system, where players have to forge new weapons and armor from raw materials they find or win from enemies. But that'll probably be more coding than I want to initially handle, so I'll go with the default for now.
Default Menu
I tried coding a custom menu, I did, but it always freezes up my game so I'll have to stick with the original. There's a little problem of the spacer characters with the default menu, though. I'm using blank spacer characters with no graphics and no HP to spread my three man party out in battle. If you have 64x64 sprites and they're all bunched up on top of each other at the top of the screen, you get way more graphical glitches than normal. So the spacers solve that. They just look bad in the menu when you've got blank pictures and all their stats between players. I'll see if I can cover them with a background image or something...
A Revised Script
I'm going to include as many final story sequences as possible, but I think I'll try to write some sort of conclusion to the story so that, in case I don't ever finish the final game, the demo will at least be somewhat satisfying. I'll have to alter the game script somewhat to make it self-contained, while still hinting at the larger story I want to tell.
Ripped Music
I'm sort of able to hum out melodies, but I've yet to find a MIDI program that is simple enough to let me turn those melodies into finished music. Since I can't make my own, I'm trying to use lesser known ripped stuff. Hearing Final Fantasy or Chrono music in a toolkit game always just grates on me, so I'm using stuff from Japanese RPGs like Terranigma and Bahamut Lagoon. Hopefully those will be less familiar to players and won't take them out of the moment.
As far as sound effects go, I'm filling in all the slots for players and enemies in the default system. They have all their defend, fight, and dying sounds in place. I'm using royalty free sound effects from around the internet and editing them together to give each character a unique voice.
Completely Original Graphics
This is most important to me. I will not release a game with any ripped graphics. I'm trying to build my own game world with its own look. Besides, I'm using 32 x 64 characters, and I've yet to see a ripped tileset that works with larger characters. All sprites are hand drawn, and the animation I've got for them is pretty sweet looking. I'm having more trouble with world tiles than with sprites, because they've got to tile properly. I have a forest tileset entirely done, a world map mostly done, and starts on town and interior tiles. (Nature's a lot easier to draw than architecture) My three main character sprites are mostly done, but I haven't really started on NPCs. All NPCs will be stationary, so I don't need walkcycles, so they shouldn't take long to draw. As I said before, enemies are done. They won't have any animation for the demo. (Maybe for a future release) For battle backdrops, I've got two drawn (forest and world map) and I need two more. They're digital paintings which I can do in a couple of hours after I get my tilesets done, so I'm not too worried about them.
Release Date
I can't make any promises on this. If I keep working on this as hard as I am now, it might be ready within a month. I'm working as fast as I can to get this released. I genuinely have fun playing it and think it looks great, but time will tell if anyone agrees with me.
