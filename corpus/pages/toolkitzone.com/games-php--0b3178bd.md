**Sin Eternal**
by Montai **Progress and Name change.**
May 10 2006, 4:03 pm
First I would like to report that "Final Fante:Sin" is now going to be called "Sin Eternal". I changed the name to this because I think it sounds better, and it fits what happens in the game better. Progress on this game is going very well. So well that I am going to release an Alpha version soon. I'm not sure if I can have it out by the time of the expo, but I'll try. Battle music- Instead of listening to one battle theme troungh whole game, you'll be able to choose from your favorites. Or you can choose to not have battle music at all. Summons - Summonings in Sin Eternal are almost like the old final fantasy games, where a beast will pop in and use it's signature move then leaves. Grand metamorphosis - Is a form of summoning that summons the spirit of the godbeast which shoots in to a character's body then changes he/she into the godbeast. The art can only be performed by summoners. - Will be like Super Mario World's world map. Of course I'll add my own flavor to it. Thats how I am ;P Thats all the news I'll give out for right now. I hope that you all are excited :P **Name change:**
