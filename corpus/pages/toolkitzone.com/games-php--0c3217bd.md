# Browse

Posted on by (162 views, 5 ) Continents are big, ya know?
**Tuesday 5.50pm, July 14th 2009** **[UltimaWizard](community.php?user=5278)**comments
I've got about a third of the High Continent done thus far - the Kyrana Plains and the Armouth Region being the main time-eaters. I'm also working on reworking all the architecture for Ander's Journey. Having drawn out some more careful, conservative concepts that fit more with my "right-angle" graphic style, I should be able to begin on major cities (again) soon. Another thing me and Necromancer13 are currently working on: sprites. Necro13's battle sprites are coming along, as are my NPCs. The best piece of advice I can give other developers, though - not all NPCs need to move. The important ones should stand still. Makes life easier, and players always know where to find the NPC should they want the quest they have. We're still aiming for September 1st as the release date of Ander's Journey!
