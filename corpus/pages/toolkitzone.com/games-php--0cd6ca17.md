# Browse

Posted on by (400 views, 2 ) Whoa, it's a sci-fi game
**Tuesday 4.04am, September 2nd 2008** **[Brad](community.php?user=1384)**comments
I'm going to give a sci-fi RPG a try. FDOM is a game I worked a good bit on this summer and am just getting back into now that college has settled down and I've at least partially recovered from the typical stupidity that unfolds after a guy turns 21. FDOM is going to try and take what was appealing in Three Guys No Beer, give it a new and fresh setting, and add some tried-and-true satisfying RPG gameplay. But the humor will return in full force with some dark twists thrown in for good measure. The game tells the story of the first Mars space station in the year 2022 experiencing a mysterious crisis and its panic-struck crew. There will be some irresponsible sexual relationships, brief nudity, and desperate hostage situations as the station's population self-destructs. Hopefully FDOM will have a little more meat to it than 3GNB also and clock in at around two and a half hours. A modified version of 3GNB's battle system will be used.
