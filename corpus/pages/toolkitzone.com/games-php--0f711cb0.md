# Illicit Soul

January 31, 2006, 4:32 pm
Development
A young woman awakens in the untamed forest world of Kalahan, unsure of her origin or purpose. Along with a band of fast friends, she'll embark on a mission of self-discovery, and indeed, discover the very nature of the cosmos.
**Illicit Soul**
by venstor **Monsters**
August 14 2004, 1:00 am
For the monsters of Illicit Soul, I'm taking my cue from Pokémon. Each monster is an individual with its own personality. I've put a lot of thought into making each one unique, so players won't be fighting generic "bats," "slimes," or "wolves."
**Monsters of Ursprung Forest**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/527.gif)
- The weakest foe in the game. Most characters can swat them down in one blow. They can use their horns to inflict a bit of damage if they're not taken care of right away, but they're more of a pest than a real threat. **Cornflir**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/526.gif)
- A tiny flying snake that's as at home in the air as it is in the water. They're timid and generally avoid humans. They're fairly weak, but their bites are mildly poisonous, so caution is advised. **Serpara**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/528.gif)
- These tiny birds hunt in packs using boomerangs. They think of themselves as a tribe of elves instead of as birds, and they have their own society and language. In great numbers, they can take down much larger prey, but alone, they're not very dangerous. **Brownee Sparrow**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/525.gif)
- These turkey-sized birds are fairly rare. They're fascinated by shiny objects and will peck up and swallow any gems or metals they see. Although they're not intelligent in the least, their sharp talons and large beaks do make them the most dangerous creatures in Ursprung Forest. **Dankbird**
**Monsters on the world map**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/598.gif)
- Dandelions are fierce animal/plant hybrids that take root along well-travelled pathways and wait for prey to pass within striking distance. They're absolutely ravenous and will attempt to eat anything that comes near. Being rooted to the ground gives them an obvious disadvantage in battle, but with their tough, fibrous hides and horrible bites, they should not be taken lightly. **Dandelion**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/599.gif)
- Teeth, claws, and a deadly sting make these wild cats among most dangerous predators roaming the open lands of Kalahan. Although typically thought of as irrational beasts, the first creature Cerna encounters is a sentient, friendly arakat by the name of Katikal. **Arakat**
**Monsters of the Forest Ruins** Updated 8/14
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/836.gif)
- A brightly-colored bat with a venomous bite. It lives in the dark rooms inside of the ruins. A fairly dangerous creature, as in addition to the danger of poisonings, it has a high resistance to magic attacks and is able to replenish its HP by draining its victim's lifeforce. **Batidote**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/992.gif)
- Dire tree-like creatures that serve as the ancient guardians of the Forest Ruins. Set in place long ago by the ruins' builders, they dispatch of intruders with deadly efficiency. Their very breath is a poisonous fume, their flexible limbs deliver deadly blows, and their bark-like skin repels the strongest of physical and magical attacks. **Entinal**
