This is the final puzzle in the demo. You have to make the beams of light that come from the crystals when you push the light switch to hit the black dot by refracting through the prism. the graphics on this were done quickly for the demo and will be changed. Yes, that grey thing at the top is a prism. the mirror mover button moves each mirror in that row or column along one space.
