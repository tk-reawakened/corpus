# The Comet of Mortalitas

March 5, 2006, 1:53 pm
Design
10 years to the day that the mysterious comet fell upon Natio, you decide to leave home and see the world. The world is inhabited by mutated creatures that mutated from a cosmic dust from the comet. With the power of crystals that keep these creatures at bay, you set out on an unplanned adventure to save the Natio world. Many wonders, and dangers, lie in your path, but with the aid of the mysterious Arin, you may be able to survive. **The Main Plot:**
