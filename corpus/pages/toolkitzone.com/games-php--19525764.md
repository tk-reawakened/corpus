# Finding Freedom

February 17, 2006, 8:18 pm
Design
"Finding Freedom: Between the Devil and the deep blue sea" is a story about the conflict between men and demons in the world of Feorra. A land that man was banished from and cast into the sea. Man has yet to claim it's place among the land, but has mastered the oceans in the process, with giant ship-cities faring the stormy waters and sailing past the demon-infested land.
**Finding Freedom**
by Con Safos **Just started**
February 14 2006, 3:56 am
Well, I've just started on this game-creation venture of mine using toolkit 3. I can't say much about the game at this point but that it'll be pretty much a basic turn-based battle RPG with a nice origional story using alot of in-game elements from FFXI though. It'll be a long time before I'm able to release a full demo which is my main goal at the moment. I'm not that good with making battle systems or menu systems either, but that dosen't mean I'll let my game look neglected, and if anyone is real good with custom battle systems and menu systems PM me if you'd like to help. Other that that, I'll try to bring a nice, fun RPG to the lineup but thats about it for now.
