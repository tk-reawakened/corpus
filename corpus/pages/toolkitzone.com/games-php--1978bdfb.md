# Forgotten Legacy

April 26, 2006, 11:53 pm
Development
Peace has been obtained by the kingdoms of Tsubane, Berk, and Celare. In this some-what medieval world, people have found a reason for living finally after centuries of fighting. Unfortunately things are never so easy. In the kingdom of Tsubane a commander stands alone in his army, opposing the mages that poison his countrymen's mentality. Along the way he will gain a few allies to help fight in this cause, slowly undoing the damage inflicted by the tainted hearts of man.
