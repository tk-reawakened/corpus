10 years to the day that the mysterious comet fell upon Natio, you decide to leave home and see the world. The world is inhabited by mutated creatures that mutated from a cosmic dust from the comet. With the power of crystals that keep these creatures at bay, you set out on an unplanned adventure to save the Natio world.
