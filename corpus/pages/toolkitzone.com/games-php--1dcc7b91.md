# Apocalypsis of Time

May 10, 2006, 7:00 pm
Development
Chris is getting ready for the year's best tournament ever. But he has some strange illusions about someone, who looks like his friend, Kalyn, asking for help. Everytime he needs strengh, he feels a power surge from a strange necklace and gives him strengh. After he uses all his energy, he gets weak and realizes that the balance of the past, present and the future depends on him . He must travel to the past and the future, and return to the present and save the world.
