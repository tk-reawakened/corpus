Set in the future, on an alien world protected by an intergalactic treaty, an old veteran tires of fighting, seeking to live out his days amidst the primitive lifeforms.
**Star Cross**
by Angel-Of-Osmond **Back in the saddle**
June 16 2006, 12:24 am
Well, I thought i'd wait for the next toolkit to come out, but darn...now I have nothing to do. So I guess I'll just work on another project (that'll make three so far) for now. Star cross showed me how hard it was to code a custom battle system, not that I couldn't do it, but that I set my goals far too high for a fisrt try. I think the killer was the complexity of the skills characters could choose from, which were all hard coded special moves. For a first time battle system, I'm going to set my sights a bit lower... at least for the demo.
