# Sythia: Curse Of The Inca

February 15, 2006, 11:58 am
Demo
An adventure game based on myst, using puzzles and problems instead of battles. Following the disappearence of an old archeologist, you and your companion have been sent to find him, and uncover the lost relic that he was searching for.
**Sythia: Curse Of The Inca**
by jpsmtm **A Copy of Hagans Notes**
February 12 2006, 2:30 pm
**The Legend of the Relic of Sythia**
/\ / \ / \ --------- / \ -------------- / \ -------------------------------- | | / \ | | | | / \ | | \ \ / / \ \ / / \ \ / / \ \ / / \ \ / / \ \ / / \ \ / / / \ / \ | \/ | | | | \ | / \ | / \ | / \ | / \ |/ \/
The legend tells of a relic of ancient magic, One that if touched by a person whose heart is pure, will grant that person limitless wealth and power. Held on an island that has never been found, it awaits the day that the chosen one will come and free it from its prison. If one not worthy should try to free it, they will die, caught forever in the moment of despair, produced by the fact that the relic will never be theirs.
Inhabited be relatives on the Inca tribe, the island of Sythia has been shrouded in mist for centurys, keeping it caught in eternal darkness. It will never be day on the island, until the curse is broken and the relic is freed.
**The Island of Sythia**
As far as i can tell, the island is no longer inhabited, when the Incas left it, they set a multitude of traps and puzzles, making sure that no one would ever ne able to obtain the relic, except for the chosen one.
I have no map of the island, only a set of co-ordinates that i decoded from an ancient script drawn on the wall of an Inca pyramid in peru. I have decoded their language, their ancient symbols. All i know about this island is that on it lies the temple of the Inca's, and in this temple the relic of Sythia lies, on a pedastal of fire, under the watchful gaze of the angel goddess.
**My Plan**
I have stole away under cover of night, my beloved curators, if you are reading this then know that i will return, before the year is out with the relic of Sythia. I have worked where the island lies and of some of the trials that await me their, i leave you in charge of the museam until i return. This will be a glorious day, the legend will at last be proved true, and all of us will be blessed with limitless wealth and power!
Micheal Hagan
