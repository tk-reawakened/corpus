# Star Cross

July 12, 2006, 10:34 pm
Development
Set in the future, on an alien world protected by an intergalactic treaty, an old veteran tires of fighting, seeking to live out his days amidst the primitive lifeforms.
**Star Cross**
by Angel-Of-Osmond **CBS: On and On**
July 12 2006, 10:34 pm
My battle syetem is coming along nicely. I have decided to separate it into two major parts: The first sets up the players, monsters, and other major permanents. The second provides interactivity between the player and the interface, which will then end the battle with a program dependent on what happens during the battle, such as all players being defeated and such. I'm done with the first portion and with player turns in the second portion. So basically, the only thing in my way is getting the enemies to take their own actions and ending the battle. Both of which are about halfway into being made. I'm not saying a battle system is going to get done in a matter of days or even weeks...well, maybe weeks...but I'm definitely making progress.
