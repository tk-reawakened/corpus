Audio
Awful
same as the graphics. it seems a shame to penalize the game on this point but the truth is there is no sound.
Story
Decent
well there wasent really a story but the game progressed nicely and played perfectly like a game show.
Fun
Perfect
I was torn between 9 and 10 for this one, but this is really one of the most fun games Ive ever played on the TK
Stability
Perfect
no slow down, no noticable rpgcode errors, no trans3 errors, everything ran perfect so it deserves perfect
Length
Perfect
considering it was for the monoprog contest it can be quite long if you play all the games, and yet can also be short if you only play the games you are supposed to. perfect length really.
Thoughts
An excelent game that really shows some amazing rpgcode programing, im still tring to figure out how he did some of it, and I really think this should be a nominee for best programing of 2009. the games graphics are a little crap if you dont know the reason for it, that they are all done in rpgcode, which then makes them great, the lack of music and sounds sucks but again that is the fault of the monoprog contest, but of course the game has to score a zero on that point the games were great fun to play(well some of them) and I recomend that anyone making a game should download and play them all as it gives some great minigame ideas.
