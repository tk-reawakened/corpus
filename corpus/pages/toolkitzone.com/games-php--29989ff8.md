Thoughts
I'll tell you right now, due to my ineficiency with graphics and sound, that I dont have very high standards at all, lol (not to mention Im a believer of positive encouragement)...what put the graphics above average for me was the opening of the title screen, very well done, IMO. Sound, well, everything seemed to just fit.... nice bkg music and sounds. Again introduces something I find awsome: Being able to change/shut off music(although should be in a menu imo). For that implementation, i say this was quite decently put together. The Story is what usually makes a game for me, a story is usually needed to keep interest/suspense.......I say "usually" because though this game lacks a good/origonal storyline, it has other features which make you want to keep playing. ok, I had said that the storyline is "usually" what makes games fun for me, well, what this game lacks in Story it makes up for in playability.... getting the "hang" of the game is quite easy, and so are the controls also the way it was coded allows you a few ways to use some strategy ( like jumping bullets ). This game is a great "time-passer" trying to stay alive and upgrading wepons and the like. The upgradings however need better descriptions on how they'll help progression imo. well, nothing I can see wrong with the stability except that when game starts up it says that a plugin is missing..... The reason I'll give an average length rating is due to the type of game it is, a "side scrolling shooter"......these types of games do well at shorter lengths of levels and LOTS of levels to go through, IMO of course
