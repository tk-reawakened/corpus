Ratings
Graphics
Decent
Very colorful and of decent quality.
Audio
Decent
Some more fun sound effects! The music was not ripped from Zelda or FF, which is a plus. Fit the atmosphere well.
Story
Poor
Well at least there was a reason to go on your trip. Saving your girlfriend is a worthy story.
Fun
Excellent
Really nice side scrolling gameplay, although it did feel a bit slow to me.
Stability
Good
Ran very well, no slow-downs. Some bugs and spelling mistakes though.
Length
Very good
Grindalf has really nailed making games feel the right length.
Thoughts
At it again, Grindalf is the man at making fun games that run smoothly and have lots of character and style. There were some bugs transitioning from one screen to another. Some places you could go over, others you could not. In addition, there was not a lot of time to read the note about your girlfriend's abduction (it had to have only been a second). The puzzles and challenges were fresh and interesting, I especially enjoyed the falling ceiling in the first cavern area. Not the same old same old level design, but remains changing. Again, Grindalf prevails. A couple of things for future games: Use spellcheck! Almost every game I have played has had glaring spelling errors or even grammar errors. Have you thought of bringing on a composer?
