I would suggest takeing a full day or two and work on tiles alone. Look at someone elses that are rather well, look at shadeing, detail, lightling, consistancy, and looping that doesn't look tiled. (my isotown tutorial goes into grass and dirt paths as well as bricks. It was made for Iso tiles, but it will work as well for regular tiles) If you can't or won't use someone elses tiles as an example, look outside. Look at grass in the distance rather than close up. Look at your walls for textures, look at brick buildings, anything that you can incorporate into your works to make them better looking, and then you'll get better. The reason I say take a full day and devote it is because once you start going into how to do it well with one type, keep going to others. Don't be afraid to use the layer system on the boards either. Anything I show normally is 2-3 layers worth of tiles. Hope this helps you some. I don't mean to be balling you out or anything, just trying to help you get better at tiles. (I know I used to suck.)
