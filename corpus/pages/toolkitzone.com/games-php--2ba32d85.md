# Talona

January 19, 2006, 5:27 pm
Demo
The world of Talona is broken - dominated by huge volcanoes and canyons, deserts and wastelands. The surface of the only continent is cracked and chaotic with only a few civilisations struggling to cling on to existance. Talona is essentially about a futile quest: Not a quest to save the world, not a quest to save humanity, but a quest to right the wrongs of the past and gain an understanding of why things went so horribly wrong.
