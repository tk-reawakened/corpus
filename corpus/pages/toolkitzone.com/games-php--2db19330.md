# Heraldry: Freija

December 28, 2005, 1:01 am
Demo
The first from the the four-volume HERALDRY series. The game promises to raise the standards of RPG TOOLKIT games--graphically, and gameplay-wise. You play the role of Freija Moonault, a highschool kickout and daughter of the great Moonault carthographers. As Freija, your goal is to rediscover the world and disprove of the historical lies that your government has fed to the people.
**Heraldry: Freija**
> Heraldry:Freija mini Demo [Demo Folder](games.php?mode=files&view=1&game=69)
April 18 2005, 6:25 am
We called this a mini-demo because it's a minor upgrade from the first demo. Aside from the fully updated graphics, time system, minigame, and npcs, there's nothing else still. No storyline revealed; no menu system; no battle system; no treasures; no shop. The purpose of this release is to show the interested parties about the progress of the game so far. If you find any bugs, report them. Although it is expected to have bugs, especially on collision and character movement. You can show/hide the clock with 'C'. You can quit the game anytime with 'Q' You can interact with npcs with 'SPACE' Made with TK 3.0.6, using non-isometric grid. None is finalized yet. More are still to come. Watch out on the EXPO... Happy playing :)
