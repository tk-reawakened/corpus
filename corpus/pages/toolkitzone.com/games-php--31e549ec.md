# Lode Story

June 19, 2006, 12:12 pm
Design
In an age gone past , Great Beasts of chaotic origin roamed the world of Legalia. Together the beasts wreaked havoc and destruction upon the world. But, a band of heroes, with the help of the 8 Great sages, sealed the monsters with the power of the Lode weapons. Now, many years have past, and The Beasts have returned. Now new Heroes must take up the lost Lode weapons, restore their power, and defeat the beasts or Legalia will face utter destruction...
