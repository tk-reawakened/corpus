The first from the the four-volume HERALDRY series. The game promises to raise the standards of RPG TOOLKIT games--graphically, and gameplay-wise. You play the role of Freija Moonault, a highschool kickout and daughter of the great Moonault carthographers. As Freija, your goal is to rediscover the world and disprove of the historical lies that your government has fed to the people.

The shop system is now complete. A fully functional and customized shop has been implemented that has it's unique features against other RPG shop systems. Each shop is called a which only buy/sell items according to their classifications. This means that a will only offer weapons of any type, and likewise buy from the player. This makes for more challenge when a player needs something, as he/she must find the proper shop to suit his/her needs. Aside from the shop system, we are also implementing a system, where the trader will accept item from the player in exchange for the items in its inventory.
