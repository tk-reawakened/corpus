2 - Controls/How To Play
i - Navigating
Use the navi-pad to move around. The forward arrow walks forward, the left and right arrows turn you 90 degrees, the back arrow spins you 180 on the spot. An eye next to an arrow indicates that you look that way instead of move that direction. Any thing that confuses you when your moving please feed back.
ii- The Menu
Open the menu with the menu button, (duh!), when your click an item, (apart from the journal), USE, and DROP will appear. Drop lets you drop that item, you can get items again from the place you got them originaly. The USE command is needed when you, suprisingly want to use something. For the second demo version, i will have your hands as a use item, for when you want to examine an item or use a lever.
iii- Save/Load/Quit
Saving is done by selecting the journal from the menu, as is Quit. To Load a game, you must reboot the game. For now the only way to load is from the start screen.
-----------------------------
3 - The Puzzles
i - the Slide Puzzle
Use the arrow keys to slide the panels into the blank space, try to make the picture whole.
ii - The Crystal Puzzle
Ooh thoses crystals, stuck in their cages, Use your logic and puzzling solving skills to help you release them, HINT, examine everything thouroghly.
iii - The Mirror Puzzle
The mirror puzzle is currently full of imperfections but it works and it is solvable. The small buttons around the perimeter move the mirrors in that row or coloumn along one space. The large blank button turns on the light beams from the crystals, (no other buttons work until you turn off the lights again, same button).
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1777/5617.PNG)
Try to rebound the beams off the mirrors into the black dot. HINT-- The grey thing at the top is supposed to be a prism, use the laws of reflection and refraction to make the beams hit. By the way, this puzzle is not finished and so when the lights should refract they dont, but if they are in the right place it will still say you've won. The Reset button resets the mirrors to their original position, The quit button is self explanatory.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1777/5618.PNG)
When the light switch is pressed, the lights turn on rebounding off any mirrors in their path.
