# The Blobs: Annihilation

February 2, 2006, 5:14 am
Full Game
The red prof.blob has gone slightly insane and has made plans to create a super blob. The orange prof. blob finds out about this and, as you are the closest person to him at the time, asks you to go and destroy the plans before they are put to use!
**The Blobs: Annihilation**
by Blob Dude **Review!**
November 25 2004, 8:19 am
Here is a review by Aljo for this game: ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Blobs, The Review by Aljo The Blobs Reviewer: Aljo Game Author: Blob -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Story 15/20 The story line is a simple and very unoriginal. Basically the bad guy has found out something mean awful and rude and the good guy is supposed to stop him. In The Blobs, the mean Red Prof. Blob has found a way to make a SUPER blob. In the brief history of blobs that Blob Dude displays on his site, all races of blobs are rivals. Red blobs fight Green and Orange blobs and visa versa. The game opens up with You in the Orange Prof. Blobs house. You play as You in the game. By some stange coinsidence the Orange Prof. Blob has found out the Red Prof. Blob has this plan up his sleaves and leaves it to You to find it. Blob Dude makes a humorous display out of this. You will find that the story is easy to follow through the story but there is always new things poping up such as the Office Blob. Graphics 15/20 The graphics on this game is plain and simple but goes with the flow of the game. Blobs are plain as well. There is some ripped graphics but Blob Dude has assured he has gotten rid of a lot of it. All the bitmaps are excellent. I must give him credit for that, and the flash back is wonderful. Those were handy work. Sound 15/20 The music and sounds are ok but most are the defaults that come with the RPG Toolkit. I do like though the noises and how Blob Dude uses these interactivly and very talently through out the game. For example in one of the towns there is a couple of constructer blobs and in the background you can always hear one of them sawing, blowing up something, or hammering. Originality 17/20 It is very original because it doesn't deal with people or animals. The Blobs is based on an idea that Blob Dude had for a time now and has been the only one to have had it. Besides the idea and some of the story the originality is very poor, but I think the Idea gives about 75% of anyones originality. There is a custom menu by the way. Game Play 17/20 As in most RPG Toolkit games there is bugs. People disappering, popping up or just plain out be screwy. For instance in the Office where you get the Office Blob to join your team, down stairs there is a bug were no matter what the security says something along the lines of "You may not enter" even though you can. The controls are a bit screwy because he has his characters on the top square of the walking graphics in the editor. That presents a problem on judging where you must walk. The battles are simple and there aren't too many of them. Blob Dude deserves some credit on his work though. Over All 79/100 This game is very addicting. I played the demo Blob Dude sent me and when I got to the ending part of the demo where the story was getting really interesting it cut me off. :( That is what demos are good for I guess. If you can hold out through the begining, you are sure to enjoy this. It gets better as you go. At 34.8 MB I think it's a bit steep. He could stick to other file formats other than bitmaps but they look good.
