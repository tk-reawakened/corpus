Thoughts
Looks like 2009 truly is the year of non-RPG games. Now we got a gameshow style game; Pancake Stack. So what's it about? Well just like any gameshow, it starts out with the host introducing the game and then the contestants; you and two AI players. You can customize yourself and the intelligence of the AI. After the introduction, you are presented to the screen where 9 challenges are randomly selected. Each challenge is unique from throwing balls into buckets to shooting targets with the mouse. After you have done a challenge, the other 2 CPU opponents take their turns. With 9 challenges, each contestant get's 3 challenges they must compete for prizes. Prizes range from a classic iPod, to a Nintendo Wii, and free movie tickets. You can choose to keep this or wager them for an even bigger prize. After the 9 challenges are done, the final challenge is presented: eating pancakes. But hold on a minute, there's more to it than just a pancake eating contest. You are presented with 5 pancakes at the start unless you pooled in a prize which lowers the pancakes you must eat. The host will then start giving trivia questions and you must answer them correctly. When you do, you get to eat a pancake by typing NOM over and over until the pancake is eaten. When you have finished one, you answer another question to have a chance to eat another pancake. You must keep on doing this until your plate is empty and win your prizes. Programming wise, this is excellent work. Everything is flawless and runs so smooth, I can't believe this was done with RPGCode. The one where you throw balls into buckets is my favorite. You gotta set the angle and set the strength of the ball and then throw it to 5 buckets that circle around in an orbit. The graphics are very impressive. It's a psuedo-3D thing, not just 2D. Very impressive. The only complaint I got of this game is no music or SFX. Gameshows have zany music and crowds applauding and shouting out answers. But this game doesn't have that. It's quiet throughout. This game would've gotten an even higher score if it had them. Regardless, the game is fantastic. It plays so well. Good job. Maybe next time you can make an option to have 3 other human players.
