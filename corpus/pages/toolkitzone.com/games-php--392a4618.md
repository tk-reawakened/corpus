# Reign Of Chaos

June 11, 2006, 3:06 pm
Design
When a powerful race from the future creates a world, there is bound to be consequences. Their "dogs", the humans, cant take it anymore. In a last ditch effort for freedom, they genetically modify themselves. After a long war, they finally win, but now, there is a civil war among themselves. Can a single hero end the reign of chaos over the land? Or will they need help from old enemies?
