The final version is here, but to say the least, this is just getting my feet wet for the game I and my team of friends are working on. Added extras include... Added story line One extra main character More monsters A secret boss More music Sound Effects Animations Brand new, smoother character pictures And finally, a custom message window Instructions are the same as for the demo, And I found no major errors playing the game myself. Gameplay lasts anywhere from a 25 minutes to 55 minutes, depending on how explorative you are, and how much you read.
