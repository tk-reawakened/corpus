**Final Say**
**Said About**
January 31st, 2010 **Said On**
Ratings
Graphics
Good
Graphics were pretty good, especially given the context. Not the best, but quite solid.
Audio
Very good
Music was very fitting, and finally some much needed sound effects! Thank you Lotusware!
Story
Poor
Story was briefly outlined in the beginning. Having to do with a cat and cheese.
Fun
Excellent
I loved Lemmings as a kid. Great strategy/puzzle game. This emulated that experience well.
Stability
Excellent
Stability was excellent, though one beef that wasn't related to the fault of Grindalf.
Length
Good
25 levels seemed like an adequate length. Password system emulates Lemmings system (the era before saved games!)
Thoughts
I remember when my brother rented Lemmings on the SNES. I was like 6 or 7. Such a great game. I loved playing it, but I had more fun watching my brother play the more advanced levels and trying to figure them out. Eek copies that experience. And when I say copy, I mean copy. This game doesn't exactly feel original - unfortunately the scoring system doesn't represent originality very well. Its like Lemmings re-skinned with mice. Graphically pleasing mice I must say. Aside from that, this game rocks. I'd say I enjoyed this game more than Fishbate the Worm. But Galaxy Defender is still my favorite of Grindalf's. Now, my only beef is with the massive slowdowns when you get more than 3-4 mice on the screen. I know, its not Grindalf's fault. That's why I couldn't justify docking the stability score, but I had to give a 9 instead of a 10 because it was annoying. I look forward to Euix's new release!
