# The Blobs: Chickit Hunt

May 24, 2005, 11:33 am
Full Game
The Chickit has been kidnapped by someone to try and ruin Easter! Play as a police Blob and follow clues to track down the Chickit and save Easter.
**The Blobs: Chickit Hunt**
by Blob Dude **Chickit Hunt Walkthrough**
February 12 2005, 4:18 pm
** The Blobs: Chickit Hunt Walkthrough
You start the game in the Fnarg Police Station.
You can watch the security cameras or you can go to the cells and play a mini game. To play the mini game, talk to the crack in the wall and when you are asked for a password type Chickit. Its case sensitive so make sure you use a capital C.
To continue with the main game you first need to watch the security camera on the right twice, then take your gun from the table. Your gun will automatically be equipped.
Now you can leave the police station, but before you do so open the two lockers at the left of the police station. The left locker has 5 medikits and the right locker has body armour. The body armour doesnt automatically equip like the gun, so go to the menu and equip it yourself.
Once outside you need to go right. On the way is a blob looking for the hotel. The correct answer is option 1, but you dont get any reward for it, so choose option 3 and get him lost ;) To the right are the teleporters. The only one you can try to use is the one to Naej, and you need security clearance for that. Go into the building and talk to the receptionist. You will find out that you need clearance from someone higher ranking than yourself, so go right again and you will find a police sergeant. He gives you a police radio and tells you to find a retired police blob. (You can talk to the sergeant again to get another radio, but you dont need it and you cant sell it, so there isnt any point)
To get to the retirement home go all the way left until youre above the police station, then go into the upper left corner and you will be taken to the map. From the map there are a few places you can go: the place for shopping, Orange Prof. Blobs house, the retirement home, or some burnt buildings. You need to go to the retirement home, but first there is a secret to find. Move around the town of Fnarg and enter it again from the south-east (the tile with the canyon on it) From here you can get a case, which you might have noticed when you talked to the sergeant. In the case is 20 medikits, and not the same type of medikit as you got before, these are better.
Now you can look around the other places on the map (theres nothing useful though) or you can go straight to the retirement home. The retirement home is to the south-west. In the home, the retired police blob is on the second floor (third floor if youre not in the UK) He tells you to look in the attic. The code is in the middle left box (shown in picture) Now you can go back to the teleporters and step into the fourth one to go to Naej.
![](http://impossibleprod.mysticsoftware.net/Other/ChickitWalkthruPic.gif)
At Naej there are two paths to take. I suggest you take the right path (youll see why) but ill give help for both.
To take the right path, go to the right of the screen and step on the chickit footprints. You need to find a clue before going to the next screen; the clue is on the stick in the ground. On the next screen, if you look at the left you will notice two trees which are leaning the wrong way and have stones behind them. This is a secret passage, out the other side is a supply crate with a shotgun and body armour (they both auto equip) Do not open the crate more than once or the game will go into an infinite loop.
Now go back a screen. There are three rocks at the bottom, you can push the top one out of the way. In the next screen is the chickits burrow. After looking at it, go right, through the trees. Then go down the beach and left and you will find a boat.
If you want to take the lower path, and miss out on the supply crate, then when you teleport to Naej, go south and look at the feathers. There are a couple of eggs you can look at in the next screen, but you dont have to look at either of them. There is a secret passage in the trees here too, look for backwards trees to the left again. On the next screen you need to find the balaclava before you can continue. The balaclava is behind the rocks to the right. In the next screen you will see the scene of the chicknapping. Go right after seeing this and you will find the boat.
Whichever path you took, get in the boat. When the chicknapper runs off, go north until you see him again, then go east, then north again, then north and west.
Once you are at the beach call the sergeant on the radio and go into the house. When you fight the chicknapper dont even try to win, hes too strong. When hes beaten you once the backup will arrive, and then you can win. Just keep attacking with everyone and you should have plenty of medikits if you need to use them (although you shouldnt need them, the boss is quite easy now) Once this battle is over, thats it, you can just sit back and enjoy the ending. =D
