# Problem of Life and Death

September 30, 2006, 3:16 am
Design
Based of the Japanese board game Go, I have been wanting to make this for a while and now that I have a little bit of time while waiting for some key components in my other game I will work on this one. If you have never played Go before there will of corse be a tutorial option to learn all the aspects of the game, along with joseki problems (life and death problems) to help your game. I am having high expectations for this game so I hope you love it!
