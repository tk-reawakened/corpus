# The Blobs: Chickit Hunt

May 24, 2005, 11:33 am
Full Game
The Chickit has been kidnapped by someone to try and ruin Easter! Play as a police Blob and follow clues to track down the Chickit and save Easter.
**The Blobs: Chickit Hunt**
by Blob Dude **Review!**
November 25 2004, 8:17 am
Here is a review by TKGB for this game: ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Blobs: Chicket Hunt, The Review by TKGB One of the TK's classic. Chicket Hunt is still an admireble game. With its suspensful music and story line. A good game to play. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Plot (1) - The story goes that you are a police blob on patrol the day before easter. You are in charge of making sure the chicket is safe. But while on duty, someone steals the chicket. Now you must find the clues to reclaim the chicket. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - Err...well it is originally drawn but there are some photographs which kinda butcher it. But it still looks good. Nothing is out of place and what not. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - Now the music is awesome. BD has used great police tunes probably from movies of the 80's. Well they make the scenes better. Also the SFX are nice. I dunno where he got that voice saying "We got the place surrounded" but it is awesome. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - Wow. You can get lost if you don't know where to go. Most of the scene takes place in F'narg, which is a desert city that BD has put. Alot stuff to see here, the great moon Ap, canyons, and the teleporters. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (1) - Not alot of fancy smhancy stuff. It is very minimal. Though the game turns out well. No bugs and stuff. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - The only character I like is the police blob. He happen to have a friend who died in raid. Or so it seems. I don't wish to spoil it but somewhere along the line he hasnt yet disapeared. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (1) - Just the regular menu disguised but it looks nice. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - The default system. Its pretty anoying so I wont give it a point. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - The blob moves well but the battles occur to quickly. Thats a disapointment but the battles end when you get to Bbhos (or wherever that place is). -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - I love it. The game is fun even though some things are crummy. They way the NPC's talk and interact are funny and greatly said. Also when you get to Bbhos and find the clues are a nice touch. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (9/10) - A great game to play and an original. Play it and play it well.
No comments.
