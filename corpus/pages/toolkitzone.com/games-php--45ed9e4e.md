Thanks to the hardcore coders Shao and Jackyboy, I've figured out how to point to objects in RPGCode which has made my menu system as well as my Battle system near completely possible. While the Menu System is all but done, the Battle System still only has it's basic framework set up. ...And here I was thinking I was done with icons. Not so. Really, from playing WoW a little bit more, as well as just using my own menu system and deciding I've found inadequacies, I'm going to wind up making *each item* a 64x64 icon. Fun, huh? Even better than that, to get my characters all prepped for the battle system, I'm going to be pixeling 64x64 templates for male and female characters. I'm going to make the templates for standard position, low stance, mid stance, and high stance. I'm using an old attack sprite that Ryukenshen made for Ander as a villager as my graphic inspiration for how it's going to animate. I'm ahead of myself on music. "Battle" is already composed and in good shape. I still desperately need a sound library that will fit the feel of Ander's Journey. I'm probably going to wind up hitting Google hard in the next few days (or as soon as I have enough of the BS coded that I wind up needing it...either or.) **[Programming]:**
