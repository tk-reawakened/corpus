# Forgotten Memories

February 20, 2006, 10:49 am
Design
Anar is a boy that lost memories of his childhood. He is currently 17 and is searching for a new life. He moved to the little town of Kamara, where he may live in peace and quiet. He becomes restless and is eager to regain his memories. But how? He recalls one thing of his childhood. Memory crystals. He was obsessed with them. He concludes that he must go find 12 of them, to regain the six years worth of memories.
