Name: Skill Build System
Battle System(After Level Gained) or Status Menu Implemented In:
Description: Inspired from Ragnarok, anyone who has played it, after your character has gained a job level you can level a move of your choice. Well with this little number (if all goes well), you can go to a trainer at a town and purchase moves to be taught and you are allowed to level a skill up after a level up. At the beggining of the game, which i'm still planning out, you are taught how to do this and then rewarded 3 moves of choice as well as 3 skill points rewarded. It's still in planning though.

Description: The one and only original pet made by me. The devious little foxes and their home will appear for you to visit and eventually end out owning one somehow. More will be added to this later.
