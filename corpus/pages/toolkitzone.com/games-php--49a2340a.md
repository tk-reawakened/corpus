**Illicit Soul**
October 13 2005, 7:18 pm
In the past, with these pieces of character art, I've been faily lazy with the actual coloring and just relied on Photoshop's dodge and burn tools to do things for me. For this piece, I wanted to actually mix every color myself, like I do when I pixel, and I think it turned out a lot better. It takes more time, but I think this is how I'm going to colorize my character drawings from now on.
