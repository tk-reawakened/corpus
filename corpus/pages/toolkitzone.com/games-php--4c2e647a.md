**Born Unto Gods**
June 12 2005, 12:25 pm
Zedus had been raised as a mortal, yet little did he know he was born a god, hidden on the earth in desperation as an evil force sought to overthrow the gods. Now as the evil searched for him, the final key in their plans, he must undertake a quest to discover himself and the destiny that is his. This is a short demo for born unto gods. There is a sidequest, the inn and shop, and a few enemies and a few boards. No music yet, really just the bare essentials. Realy I am releasing this just to get some feedback on things. Currently I am farther now, took three days to figure out how to upload *lol* got alot done in that time. But so far this is how its looking. So tell me what you think. I will release a much fuller demo later, after I get some feedback and I finish a true teaser with storyline to wet some appetites
