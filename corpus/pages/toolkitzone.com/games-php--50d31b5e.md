June 12 2006, 5:41 pm
Each time you complete a quest in The Dragoon, both main and side quests, you'll be able to choose a reward for completing the quest. Each quest has different special items and amounts of gold and experience to choose from. You are allowed to choose between obtaining gold, experience (each member gets that amount), or one of five different special items. The special items will not be the same that the enemies drop in the quests, they'll be unique ones that can only be obtained by choosing them as a reward. When playing the game, you choose what quests to play and when. You can also choose to play a quest as many times as you wish, and each time you complete it, you'll be able to choose a reward from that quest again. And just to clarify, you won't really be expected to play quests multiple times just to get strong enough to go to the next quests. One play through should be fine. However, the game will have different difficulties (Normal, Heroic, Epic) that are laid out similiar to hack-'n-slash commercial games. Each difficulty features the same areas and enemies, but their stats are higher and drop different, stronger items. In the two higher difficulties, playing quests two or three times may be helpful to obtain the different rewards. But not on Normal. I don't expect the majority of people to play past Normal, but I'm going to include the other difficulties for the people that love the game. ;)
