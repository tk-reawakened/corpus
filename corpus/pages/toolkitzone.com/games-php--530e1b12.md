Another, non glitch, ORAW gripe (for me at least), was the way that movement was drawn, specifically that it just went all over the characters. If felt messy, as well as this, the pathfinding buggered up around other characters. Both of these problems have been fixed to what I feel is a high degree of reliability. There'll be a closed demo of the movement system demo-ed in a few days to see if there are any particully pressing flaws in it. Edit>> The collision on the ship isn't handled yet as it's essentially treated like a large sprite.
