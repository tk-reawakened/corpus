**Andromeda Sky - Angel Tears**
by Talyn **The Battle System**
February 20 2005, 6:03 pm
The battle system started out life as a shoddy little number key pressing system with no cursor input at all. Oh how things have changed. The new system is fully cursor based with almost every area of the system Dynamic in some way. The features are as follows:
- Fully graphical system
- Individual enemy AI
- Dynamic menu's and status boxes
- New "Combo" system allows players to team up against tough opponents.
- New "Emotion" rating will either help or hinder you depending on how you use it.
- Random Character placement at the start of the battle. No two fights will ever look the same.
- Slick interface which matches the other systems in the game.
- Special Moves for both Players and Enemies
- Enemies also have Emotion, allowing them to become more of a threat.
