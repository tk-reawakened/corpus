An explanation on how The Horse Smell?s gameplay will work
The Horse Smell?s gameplay will be unique, that?s for sure. It will be shown in first-person view, and you click in the area you want to go. No boards will be used. Instead, think of the game as one huge clickable menu. For example, look at the screenshot below. (yes I know its huge, I was just too lazy to resize it...)
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1782/5723.jpg)
If you were to click on the blue door, then you would find yourself in the tack room, viewing the tack menu. If you were to click on the big sliding door, you?d find yourself viewing another clickable screen of the first screen in the town. Now, if you?d like to back up or turn around, click near the bottom of the screen. In this partular case, you?d back up, but, depending on where you are, you could turn around as well.
There will also be a certain amount of menus in the game. Things like horses? info and care actions, the tack room, the arena, shops, and showing will be done through menus. I know this sounds, as my sister put it, ?really stupid?, but, unlike most horse games, you never actually see yourself or control yourself riding the horse. Again, I realize that that might seem boring to some of you; but, for reality?s sake, I feel that I shouldn?t make that sort of ?showing?. I absolutely hate the concept of simply pressing a key and having the horse go a certain way, even if it is just a game. I think it gives the idea that when you ride a horse, you kick him to go, pull him to stop, and jerk him any which was to turn. It is NOT SO IN THE SLIGHTEST. If you?ve ever ridden a horse seriously, meaning more than a little trail ride, you probably know this. So, I?d much rather have none of that at all than portray horseback riding the way it would. And besides, I mean for The Horse Smell to be more strategic anyway.
Well there you have it. A short little blurge on how The Horse Smell?s gameplay will work. Hope you enjoyed it!
