# Contradictory

October 2, 2006, 12:12 am
Development
This game is about a boy who has a dream that tells him that he has 3 weeks to morally prepair himself for his judgment day. You spend your three weeks going around town and participating in events. All the events that you play a role in affect your moral, which will affect how you are judged on judgement day. So remember, every action has a reaction. If you cant tell, its in black and white to elaborate on Good and Evil, Light and Dark.
