# Illicit Soul

January 31, 2006, 4:32 pm
Development
A young woman awakens in the untamed forest world of Kalahan, unsure of her origin or purpose. Along with a band of fast friends, she'll embark on a mission of self-discovery, and indeed, discover the very nature of the cosmos.
**Illicit Soul**
by venstor **About Illicit Soul**
May 24 2004, 6:51 pm
, once completed, will hopefully be one of the prettier Toolkit games around. I'm drawing my inspiration from the old SNES greats like Secret of Mana and Chrono Trigger. All sprites, enemies, and terrain will be hand pixeled, and battle backgrounds will be reduced color digital paintings. **Illicit soul**
The game will take place in a forested valley on the world of Kalahan. Eventually, players will find ways to travel to other worlds, but on each world, I want to keep a sense of isolation by confining players to a smaller region of the world as a whole. There will be no full world maps. Each region will still have plenty of areas to visit. (My inspiration comes from the El Nido archipelago of ) **Chrono Cross**
Hopefully, the story will be another of the game's strong points. I'm trying to avoid clichés wherever possible, so it should at least be original. Instead of an overt battle against evil, it should play as more of a quest for knowledge, with the player unraveling mysteries as the game world unfolds.
Battles will not be the game's focus, but I hope to someday complete a strategic battle system in which elemental strengths and weaknesses will come into play. Coding's not my strong point, so that will probably be a ways off.
