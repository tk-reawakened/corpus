Here's the elemntal chart that Heraldry uses. The arrows you can see deternmines how the two elements affect each other. The element pointing to another deals double damage to it, while in return it receives halved damage to the said element. In case both elements point at each other, the effect is nullified and will therefore receive double damage and deals double damage from and to the other one. EX: Use a Fire elemental on an Ice Innate creature will deal double damage to the creature.
