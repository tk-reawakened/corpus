"Life in the City" is a game that's kind of like "Coke Music" or "The Sims", but still unique. You live in a city. You start the game in an apartment in the downtown area with a box for a toilet. You have to step out and find a job! The only thing in this game is that you can't build your own house or edit your house. But, you can choose curtains! Eventually, you can move out. Another important factor in this game will be to build relationships, and eventually get married.
