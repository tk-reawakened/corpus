Set in the future, on an alien world protected by an intergalactic treaty, an old veteran tires of fighting, seeking to live out his days amidst the primitive lifeforms.
**Star Cross**
by Angel-Of-Osmond **Alignment Ideas**
June 17 2006, 12:33 am
Okay, I played star wars knights of the old repulic, and I thought to myself how cool alignment would play in my game, like having alternate endings, or maybe even being able to recruit or talk to specific people, but this idea just flashed in my head that I couldn't ignore. Alignment of the main character is good in its own right, and based on that alignment, he might have say, alternate graphics associated with him, just as in knights of the old republic. However, What if I took it one step further, making the alignments of other party members change as well, causing them to look good or bad based on what the main alignment is. After all, why would good people be led by a bad person. This sort of thing is easy enough for me to do, so I do plan on implementing it on star cross, among other things.
