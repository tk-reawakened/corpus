# Dark Age 2

November 24, 2005, 11:48 am
Completed
Dark Age 2 is a medieval fantasy role playing game filled with wizards, dragons, kings, castles, ogres, and the like. Unlike its predecessor, Dark Age 2 does not contain elements ripped from professional games, but rather is a completely original work including graphics, music, and programming. All of the game's internal systems are custom, including the battle system, menu, and shops.

Dark Age 2 is a medieval fantasy role playing game filled with wizards, dragons, kings, castles, ogres, and the like. Unlike its predecessor, Dark Age 2 does not contain elements ripped from professional games, but rather is a completely original work including graphics, music, and programming. All of the game's internal systems are custom, including the battle system, menu, and shops.
