I decided i wasnt satisfied with 3 characters ... so im going to make more! I felt that having only 3 characters wasnt very interesting so i needed to add more, i have added a total of 5 more character breifs to my design notes, I have also re-designed the primary 3 characters and decided who dies and who dissapears etc. So i now have 4 characters with key roles and 4 with additional roles so it makes the game slightly easier. Cleon owns a branch of Inns throughout Kipel and is brought to Link because 2 Bogal Knights (Shelters Infantry) pillage his town. Xabi and Phog join Link when Gail dies (yes i decided to make it sad hehehe ...) I will type up character briefs when i have the time! Anyway, bye for now!
