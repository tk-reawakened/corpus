This is the demo game with the most basic features: 3 color triangles, the ability to form them into squares, and the ability to destroy them.
**Technical**
In the game, you may encounter the background turning white. This is not a bug. This is merely me using the SetImage command to draw a white square onto a canvas in order to make them disappear. I'm trying to find a new way of doing this. Also sometimes a triangle may disappear when you place it down. I still don't know what causes this but it's pretty rare but plz do tell me if it happens.
