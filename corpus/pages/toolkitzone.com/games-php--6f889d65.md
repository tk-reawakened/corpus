# Lazarus Mist : Frozen Sun

March 12, 2006, 8:59 am
Development
Action packed isometric / 3d RPG following the adventures of a normal person with a mysterious past who's work as an assassin and spy gets them involved in an inter-dimensional conspiracy, and then into an apocalyptic war for the survival of humanity. The first part of the Lazarus Mist trilogy. Overall progress,I've written most of the game, and have finished the maps for most of the demo. Another note, the game will be FULL of plot twists. So trust absolutely nothing :-)
