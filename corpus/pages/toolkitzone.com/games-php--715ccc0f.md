Okay here's a quick lesson on how to use it. In a single round, a character may: Attack (+1 attack bonus), Move and Attack, Reload and Attack, Move and Use Inventory, Dash, or just Use Inventory. To attack an enemy, click on attack, then select an enemy to attack (the orange squares). The character with the rifle can either single shot or rapid shot (3 shots at -4 accuracy). Move makes you move 4 squares. Dash makes you move 10 squares (controlled by the arrow keys). Inventory opens up your inventory. If you haven't moved that round, you can reload your gun with the reload button. Or you can click on one of the boxes in the menu to use that item. The only usable item in this demo is the Concussion Grenade. Click anywhere on the map. If its out of throwing range, it'll tell you so. If not, it'll go boom in that square, and cause splash damage.
