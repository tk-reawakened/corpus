# about the game

A superhero rpg in which a group of teenagers are kidnapped and supjected to experiments which give them various superpowers. Escaping, they join a secret government strike force to stop their former captors.

For several years, a secret government group code named Argonaut has been hunting a misterious and secret group of scientists tied to a great many coporations, governments, and terrorist organizations. This group, code named Minotaur, has been doing illegal experments on animals and humans in a number of hidden underground facilities code-named labrinths. The leadership of Minotaur is believed to be hiding in the heavily-armed Labrinth 13, but its location is still unknown. The experiments are aimed at creating super-soldiers with fantastic powers, to be sold to the highest bidder. A group of teenage experiments escape and join Argonaut, freeing other experiments along the way, in an attempt to stop Minotaur once and for all.
