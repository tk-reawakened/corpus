# Browse

Posted on by (9 views, 1 ) New ideas!
**Wednesday 12.10am, July 26th 2006** **[Lisha](community.php?user=10048)**comment
I was trying to work on my training system, but it wasn?t working. Why? Because I still didn?t have a good idea of how things would work in the game. I didn?t know how much one training session should increase stats, or what sort of things would play a factor in the discipline, or anything. It just wasn?t working. So? I?m taking a break from coding and stepping back to plan, one step at a time. I think this is what caused the issues with all of my codes for the previous version - I didn?t know how things in the whole game would work, so therefore a lot of my codes were useless/buggy in the game. This turned out to be a really good idea. I?ve come up with quite a few new ideas even without meaning to. To name a few: *Upgrading the barn - I?m changing it so that Aimee isn?t renting out the barn to you; you bought it instead. It can hold 6 horses when you first start out, then you?ll be able to upgrade and expand it so it can hold more. I?m thinking the max will be something like 35 or 40, maybe more. Probably sounds like a lot now, but in real life, large scale stables have like, 500 horses. But there?s no way a player could have that many and have it work. >. < At least, not without?. *The Hired Help! Okay, so you?ve got 40 horses, and all of them wake up bright and early calling in their little (adorable) horse neighs, ?Feed me! I?m hungry!? and ?Groom me! I?m filthy!? or ?My stall smells like crap! Come clean it out!? My god, how are you supposed to feed, groom, and muck out the stalls of 40 horses without the game becoming a dull click-fest? Well, you hire some help! Not sure exactly how this?ll work yet, but I?m thinking you?ll either: A) Be able to get some of the villagers to work for you Or B) Hire some other unknown NPC, but not really have much interaction with them other than a ?report? at the end of the day. Uh? there was more, but I guess I should have written them down because I sort of lost them. ^_^U Ah, well, they?ll come back. They always do! =P
