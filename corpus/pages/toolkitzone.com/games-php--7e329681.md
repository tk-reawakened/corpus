# Omen of Oblivion

July 14, 2006, 4:45 pm
Development
After two brothers are appointed the new leaders of their guild faction, they must travel the country of Ferok doing jobs to make a living for them and their employees. While on a normal routine mission, they uncover a phrophecy of Oblivion... yet it was supposed to have already happened. What does this mean? As an unknown menace is terrorizing the country of Ferok and Iona, the picture becomes more clear. Over 50 playable chars and large locations!
