# Final Say

Browse
[![Thumb](thumb.php?src=forum/images/avatars/98829289145cff1c50db35.png&iar=1&aoe=1&bw=1&w=100)](community.php?user=5400)
**Final Say**
**Said About**
February 14th, 2007 **Said On**
Ratings
Graphics
Bad
The graphics are generic RPGXP fare, with the backgrounds being ripped from someone (TKGB didn't even remove where he ripped them from.) The only graphics that are his own are the profiles, and the special attacks.
Audio
Very bad
MIDIs (that are not of TKGB's own creation) at their worst. Also, no sound effects.
Story
Very bad
Plot is more or less non-existant, other than 'There is a tournament. Enter it or something.'
Fun
Awful
Is choosing the same action 70 times fun? No?
Stability
Poor
Quite a few bugs abound (though none are fatal), with the most blatant being near the ending. Also, the lack of an MWin causes annoying flashes and glitchy stuff.
Length
Average
It's 9 battles, No boards, nothing else. Though there are quite a few teams, each of which you can choose from, but they don't have a serious effect on story (not that there is any) or gameplay, thus no real reason to re-play as the other 7 teams.
Thoughts
The game consists of a grand total of three things: Choosing your team, fighting, and shopping in between fights. So, you'd expect a game totally focused on battling to have a nice, solid, battle system? Nope, the plain old TK3 standard battle system. You're fully healed after every single battle, so there's no need to hold back on SMP, worry if someone died, etc. If you happen to have a team with spells, sling them without hesitation. If not, the character's attack is probably high enough to finish off the enemy in two or three hits. There seems to be a bit of a balance problem, as the first couple of battles are difficult, but after that, I seemed to one-hit almost everything. AI is stupid, and heals you quite frequently. Also, sometimes attacks simply wouldn't register. There's no ability to save, but that really doesn't matter, as it takes about 15 minutes to play though the whole thing. It feels as though the game's duty was to show off its battle system, and at that it would succeed, but it is just the basic TK3 battle system, making the whole thing's existance feel largely pointless.
