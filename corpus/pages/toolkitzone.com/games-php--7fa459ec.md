I've decided to cancel the Updated Demo release that was planned for next month. I'm just going straight to the full game. If you've ever experienced releasing a major demo, there's actually a lot of miscellaneous work involved if you want to do it right. Good news? Well, not much for you. For me, this makes the development process much easier and smoother since I don't have to try and throw another demo out and base my plans on that. You'll probably get the full game a week or two earlier though. Of course, the bad news is there's no Updated demo, so you'll have to just put up with the Expo demo for now. Or more so me putting up with knowing people are playing the Expo demo when the game is now so much better...
