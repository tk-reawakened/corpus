# Heraldry: Freija

GAMES
March 14, 2005, 12:43 am
Demo
The first from the the four-volume HERALDRY series. The game promises to raise the standards of RPG TOOLKIT games--graphically, and gameplay-wise. You play the role of Freija Moonault, a highschool kickout and daughter of the great Moonault carthographers. As Freija, your goal is to rediscover the world and disprove of the historical lies that your government has fed to the people.

*Heraldry: Freija - Demo Officially Released*
The waiting is over. But before downloading it, we strongly suggest that you read the status report regarding the game.![](stuff/files/157/hf_titlePage.gif) **The demo is out now!**
The main purpose of the demo is to:
- to the community the game entitled Heraldry:Freija **Introduce**
- out the game's realtime system. No battles, an incomplete(but useable) menu system, no story, no plot. You will be served with a total of eight (8) boards/locations for you to explore. Some may be empty, while others may be filled with some irrelevant npcs. No shop system, though there will be merchants at the town market. Please bear in mind that this is a non-story related demo. Play it and test the time system and give us feedback about it. **Test**
- The intro is complete, but is not included in the package. Why? Because screw it up. When you issue the commad, the avi file plays, but afterwhich the screen will turn gray and evetually will not refresh. You will still be able to move around, but you won't be able to see anything at all. So for the time being, bye bye intro...anyway it's 5 mb alone, so we really have to consider removing it. You can always dowload it and view it anyway, just click . **trans3** ***#playavi()*** [here](http://www.mysticsoftware.net/piarts/)
- As stated prior to the release, the game features a realtime world, so there's a clock. Each day is divided into quarters: morning, day, afternoon, and evening. Transition shall manifest by dynamic background changing, meaning the background will change right before your eyes while you're moving around. However, it has been discovered that there's a glitch on this system: when you move in from oe board to another at ay give time besides daytime, the loaded location shall show a daytime background(as the default setting in the board editor). You'll have to wait for half a second to a full second to allow the background to refresh and display the appropriate background for the time
- It's quite noticeable that npc are either walking or static. This has been a disappointment in our part, as we would have loved seeing npcs, but we're afraid the game engine would not permit us to access the animations available for an item. If we try using a command, the game becomes jerky. *"breathing"* ***#animation()***
- The background music isn't finalized. Except for the title screen music, the rest are subject to change. -The menu system is not yet complete. You can only view the status screen of the main character, and view some items given to you.
- No treasures yet; no mini-games. Only a minimal set of boards for you to wander around until you get tired. Please test the time as long as you could.
- Progress can be saved. Just head to the inn and ask to save your game.
- Installing and running the game has been tricky in our part. First, run the compressed file after dowloading it. Initially we imagined the game would automatically be included in the shortcut. Unfortunately it wasn't, so we had to browse and look for it. After finding it, we ran trans3.exe. After specifying the .gam file, we were able to play it. We just hope the results with you would be better. *Programs*
