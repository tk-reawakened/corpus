# Heraldry: Freija

GAMES
March 14, 2005, 12:43 am
Demo
The first from the the four-volume HERALDRY series. The game promises to raise the standards of RPG TOOLKIT games--graphically, and gameplay-wise. You play the role of Freija Moonault, a highschool kickout and daughter of the great Moonault carthographers. As Freija, your goal is to rediscover the world and disprove of the historical lies that your government has fed to the people.
**Heraldry: Freija**
by khin **Just a minor setback**
January 23 2005, 11:28 pm
Bad news. My hard disk was formatted by accident. Fortunately I have a backup of the demo, so development will not be halted--it will only be slowed down. I'm redoing some additional boards, sprites, images. I am also programming the same system. It might have brought some advantages as now I get to recode some stuff that has been very messy. At any rate, we'll still try our best to release another demo on my birthday (April 12). Ashe is still on menu programming. The menu system will now be on RPGCode(previously via C++). Frei is creating the storyboards for the cutscenes. We're planning on releasing a short 30-minute film(anime) of the game. Hope we could make it work.
