**Outsets**
by Tao-Kun **City board near completion**
October 16 2005, 9:17 pm
Why is it that I always have a bloody city named Lexicon in all my stories, game ideas, and story fantasys? Oh well. The City of Lexicon is almost complete. All the buildings have been created and placed on the board. To complete the board, I will have to add more trees, flowers, and other little crap you see around you. I won't show a snap shot of it though because I do not want to dish out the chance for people to learn where everything is before the game even come out. (Side Note: The board saved in PSD format is current 256mb, so it might turn out to be 10mb when saved in GIF) Although the city board is almost complete, that doesn't mean the game is. The game is far from completion, but having this board completed is a big step to completing it. Having the city board done, I can now focus more on the story line so that I make acutally have a demo out before the expo. Current game size: 28mb Expected size when completed: Around 100mb.
