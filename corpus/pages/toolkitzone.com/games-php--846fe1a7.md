January 28 2006, 4:03 pm
This is the quest log, which gives you the basic info on your current quest. Each quest has several objectives that you accomplish in order, so you can see "Find Grandfather Watch" is the first one. Once that objective is completed, the next one will be introduced and unlocked. The "Special Items of Quest" is a list of the special items that enemies in that quest randomly drop. It's similar to finding a "Rare" or "Unique" item in games like Diablo or World of Warcraft.
