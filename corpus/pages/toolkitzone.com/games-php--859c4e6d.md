Thoughts
EHHHH....A fighting game for the TK. Oh boy. Grindalf has made many games of the TK that it's not suppose to do but this one is my personal favorite. Sprite Wars is a Smash Bros. style game that uses characters from different game series like Sonic, Zelda, Final Fantasy, and even Crash Bandicoot makes a appearance. The game starts with a Star Wars type intro with the prolouge scrolling by. The story is not so engaging but this game was done for a contest so the point is not the story but the gameplay itself. After the intro, you select one of 10 characters, all from different games. When you select a character, the battle begins (as soon as you press ENTER that is). The controls are simple. LEFT and RIGHT moves the character, UP is for jump, DOWN is for block,. SPACE is for attack, and press DOWN+SPACE to do a special move. Each character has his or her own ways to fight such as Sonic can dash across the screen to ram you and jump to attack the opponent in mid air while Link can use his sword or shoot an arrow. It is very impressive for a TK game and even with simple controls it is very fun but there is not much else to do after finishing the game. Once you defeat all characters you get the credits. No special ending. Like I said this was for a contest so the point was to make the game play as such. So unless you like kicking the same guy over and over again, not much else. Regardless, try this game out. It is very fun and you will be pleased and maybe amazed at waht the TK can do.
