**Avirus**
by Daymor **The Beginning**
February 12 2006, 7:08 pm
Finally, I have made a profile. The first thing to do was to introduce. My point in making this game was to make a decent 3d rpg, without the common rpg cliches. I am working on this as often as possible, on my father's computer, as I save up for my own. I have a good 3d editing program, I am looking for something to make my music with. I have some written already, and I have a good enough keyboard to the point that I can play it, I just do not have anything to mix it with. If anyone knows of a decent download program, let me know please and thank you. I will try to keep everyone updated, please comment on any ideas you get. John.
