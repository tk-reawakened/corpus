Any info on the project? Am I right in assuming this will be much like Godiva?
June 18 2006, 2:26 pm
yeah, it will be very similar to Godiva (it uses the same graphics, after all). There are a few differences though. This will be based more off of Rogue based games then Godiva. Also, after the initial release is released, I will write some articles on how to add content (like classes, weapons, spells, etc.) and anybody can add to this game. Every so often I will release an update with everything that people have included. I do have the right to tweak and to playbalance anything, or not to include anything at all (if its really dumb). RIght now, I'm working on releasing a small demo: 4 races, 1 class, 1 4-level dungeon (amazing how those numbers worked out). From here I can hopefully assemble a team together, because it'll take a lot of manpower to assemble huge dungeons in this game, and I do want huge dungeons, cause huge dungeons are fun 8)
