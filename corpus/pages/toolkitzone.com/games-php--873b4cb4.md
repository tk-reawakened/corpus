The second game in the popular Andromeda Sky series, Gateway follows the story of a group of characters on the Andromeda Sky world's Eastern Continent.
**Andromeda Sky - Gateway**
by Talyn **Progress so far...**
May 12 2005, 12:16 pm
What's New?
Well, not a lot if I'm honest. Gateway is still very much in design, with no actual game files even created yet. I don't intend to start Gateway until Angel Tears is finished, however I am doing a lot of concept art and systems designs towards the project as we speak.
I intend to get the systems working first before anything else is done on the game. That was the major mistake with Angel Tears, I never had enough time to create a solid system. All the systems will be custom and hopefully a break from the norm. I'm not saying anything else for the moment, all I'm saying is that the game will revolve heavily around battles and the tactics for battles (something which was heavily overlooked in AT.)
Be prepared for a sequel to Angel Tears which will shape the face of TK Games.
