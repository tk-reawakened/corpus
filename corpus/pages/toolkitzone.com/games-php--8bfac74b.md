# The Rising

May 13, 2005, 2:19 pm
Development
Jellyphant's first proper game. Based in a medieval period. A saboteur called Faller was sent into a rebellious faction and was betrayed, and forced to flee. He must return to the king, and kill the traitor.
**The Rising**
by Night_Elf **Characters and Stuff**
August 11 2004, 10:37 am
*The Rising- Characters and Stuff*
***The Rising***
***The Rising are a rebellious faction, fighting against The King. They are constantly growing, and The King is concerned about the threat they represent. They all wear green and red uniforms which they use to identify each-other by. They always attack in groups of at least 20 men, and hide-out in various areas of the land. Because of the way they are split, it is almost impossible for The King to simply invade their hideout.***
***Sir Faller II***
***A knight, serving his King loyally. His father and mother were both killed by The Rising, and his brother captured. The Rising is posing a growing threat to The King's reign, and the King has sent you, Sir Faller, to join the Rising, and sabotage it from the inside. Only Faller, the King, and the King's Advisor knew of this plan. Faller's personal grudge against the Rising makes him all too keen to take on this job, as does his loyalty to the King, so he accepts. All seems to be going according to plan, until The King's Advisor arranges a secret meeting with the leader of The Rising, and The Rising's Leader pays the King's Advisor to tell him who the sabatour is. When Faller discovers he has been betrayed, he had to flee immediatley. Faller has to now kill the traitor, and return to the King. The only thing standing in his way...The Rising...The wild animals which roam the kingdom...and worse yet...The King's army, who think Faller is part of the Rising, because he is still in his disguise.***
***Threllia***
***Threllia is a deceptive merchant. He has no real interest in The King, The Rising or Faller. He is only interested in making as much money as he can, even if it means breaking a few laws. He encountered Faller while selling (over-priced, low quality) weapons. When Faller refused to buy anything from him, Threllia persisted, saying,***
***"Sir Faller, I assure you my merchandise-"***
***Faller never found out what Threllia's merchandise was. He at that moment drew his sword and demanded to know how Threllia knew who he was. Threllia, not wanting his throat cut, told him that he had met a man with some "information." This man (little did Threllia know) was in fact the King's Advisor, who was spreading information about Faller's mission, in the hopes that more of The Rising would find him quickly. Faller demanded that Threllia take him to the place he met he king's advisor, selling his information.***
***Haru (Character Story by Lauren M)***
***On the run, Faller takes shelter (hiding) within the Farm at which Haru is working. Haru knowing of his presence helps him until the danger has past. Harus knowledge of the group and their dealings strikes an interest. Harus large awareness of the world brings a conclusion that he may know more to the dealings of The Rising and the Advisors plot than what he appears to let on in the beginning.***
***Slea***
*** Who was this woman? Faller found her meditating on top of a hill, where he had went while being chased by The Rising. Completely unbothered by the fact that the hill was surronded by a mob of red-and-green branishing swords, axes and flaming sticks, she continued to meditate. Realising she wasn't going to be much help, Faller drew his sword and prepared for the battle, which would likely result in his death. Just before taking the first swipe at the nearest Rising member, the sky darkened, and there was a flash of light, which dispersed the whole army of The Rising. She never explained how she did it, but Faller thought such a person could be a useful person to have on their jorney, although she was likely to be hung upon arriving at the castle.
***D. Faller***
***David Faller, the little brother of Sir Faller II, and the son of Sir Faller I. His title, in that era, was a very insignificant one, and when The Rising became strong, D. Faller joined it, after an attack on his village. He had at first been captured by the Rising, but saw that he could have much more power here than The Kingdom's fudal system would allow within the law. He soon became the Leader of The Rising, shortly before Sir Faller II was to begin his mission to sabotage the Rising.***
