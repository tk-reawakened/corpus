# Cyber World

July 11, 2006, 12:25 am
Development
Zak is a game psyche, one of the many MEDNO players around the world. His real name is unknown, but one thing is for sure,... HIM AND ALL MEDNO PLAYERS ARE IN GRAVE DANGER. Things worsen when he tries to find out the true meaning of the game, and puts him and his other friends in grave danger. A game of wits and skill, World of MEDNO will leave you amazed and with a great curiosity of knowing more of the game and is sure to leave you guessing the unexpected.
**Cyber World**
by kiochi_2006 **How did this game came into being.**
July 10 2006, 3:24 pm
First, I used to be a player in the .hack series. I like the game so much, I thought of making a similar game, but with a different story. I had a hard time creating characters, so i made a small trivia and gave it to some of my friends. So, according to their answers, I managed to create their characters and my own one, Nightgale. I started it in a comic book, not sure of how the story was going to turn out. With time, I made a few more characters, added items, weapons, and went completing and planning the story in another notebook. After a while, my friend told me about toolkit, and I thought, " Hey, why not make it a game?" So i started the profile slowly. I figured it was best if I continued it in Toolkit rather than as a comic, otherwise, it could take me ages to finish drawing and planning. After getting frustrated from all the hard work of looking for teammates, I decided to start another game and abandon this one. But I gave it some thought after other players asked me not to quit the game and kept updating and changing.
