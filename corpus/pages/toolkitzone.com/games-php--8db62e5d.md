Background information on main characters.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1601/4823.PNG)
**Absalon De'Adluss**
Born in 1374 to Balvus De'Adluss and Leia Rheims in the small town of Ar'el. Balvus was a highest ranking Knight in the Realm, a Divine Knight and guardian of the Emperial Family, Delaine. Balvus was killed in an assasination attempt of young William Delanie and his mother the Empress Atikisha in 1377. Leia later grew very ill then died a year later. Absalon's godfather Geofrey Holmes Head Monk of Monmonoth Monestary in the Dylkildre Countryside took custody of him in 1378 and began his schooling. The story begins 15 years later.
Class: Page, Knight, Temple Knight, Holy Knight, Divine Knight
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1601/4825.PNG)
**Caliberus Trumpf**
Born in 1372 to Herald Trumpf and Gordellia Trumpf. Both Caliberus parents were killed in the Pagan Riots of 1389. Caliberus was left with nothing. He began peicing his life back together by picking pockets, his potential was instantly recognized by the Leader of the Thief Guild in Lebanon, Master Thief Benon. He worked with the Guild till 1399 until he left the Guild against Benon's wishes.
Class: Thief, Master Thief
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1601/4826.PNG)
**Hellena Belfuedamont**
Born in 1373 to Balthizar and Willamina Belfuedamont. Hellena's father was a well respected Alchemist in the scientific community. Her father had great expectations for her to be the same. Hellena began her studies at a young age and excelled at them. Willamina and Hellena were forced to move in 1384 when her father began working on a secret project for an anonymous group. Willamina died several years later. Hellena then began her search for her father.
Class: Alchemist
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1601/4827.PNG)
**Malacod Tuscelles**
Malacod doesn't remember much of his past. He never knew his parents or where he was from. At a fairly young age he began work as a bounty hunter. He is currently under the employment of several royal feuding houses.
Class: Bounty Hunter
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1601/4828.PNG)
**Sir Garius Beowulf**
Sir Beowulf was born in the Slums of Londumburg in 1342. His parents denounced him, called him a mistake. Though Beowulf lived with his parents he was basically on his own. He got in brawls daily, he was well known as a strong fighter amougnst the many gangs in the slums. In 1367 he was taken under the wing by a young vasal to the King of Londumburg named Leopold Renald of Guisborne. He then began his training as a Temple Knight. Some years past he became a well respected knight. In battle he was nick named Knight of Death for his ferocity in battle and later recognized as the leader of the Temple Knight Order.
Class: Temple Knight
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1601/4829.PNG)
**Joan of Arc'e**
Born in 1374 in the small Town of Arc'e. Daughter and only child of Sir Beowulf. Sir Beowulf raised her as a son, taught her to be a Temple Knight. In 1407 she was excepted into the Temple Knight Order despite some objection. She like her father soon became a respected knight, rivaled by many other knights. In 1408 she was sent by the Temple Knight Order to be a Emissary to the Lord Chancellor, she was reported missing soon after.
Class: Temple Knight
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1601/4830.PNG)
**Autumn Foilles**
Born in 1370 to parents unknown. Very little is known of Autumn expect that she is the last of an ancient race known as the Aszure. Magic wielders capable of summoning horrific creatures to aid them in battle. She was employed by the Merlincroft, but then left and joined a young man who she believed was her soul mate. She now works with a rebel group called the Free Mages Front.
Class: Summoner
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1601/4831.PNG)
**Jarin Trolusse (Green Thumb)**
Born in 1365 in the Royal House of Trolusse to Henry and Kilitia Trolusse. When his parets discovered his deep interest in magic they disowned him. He then joined the Merlincroft. There he expanded his knowledge of Geomancery. He soon was nick named Green Thumb. It was with the Merlincroft that he met Autumn Foilles, who he believed was his soul mate. Together they left the Merlincroft and started the Free Mages Front.
Class: Summoner
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1601/4833.PNG)
**Artimus Lea**
Artimus was born in 1364 to Galileo and Geena Lea. In 1372 Artimus was sent to Monte Fiaso University in Rheins. Three years later Artimus Father was burned at the stake for Herasy, his mother became a total reclouse. Artimus now works as personal advisor to Duke Leo of Melbourne.
Class" Astrologer
