A look at a few of the villagers you'll meet in The Horse Smell.
Whoever named Lexington ?Horse Capital USA? hasn?t seen Seawater Falls. Practically everyone who lives there has one or more horses. It rests alongside saltwater river, which falls over a massive waterfall into a lake. Hence the town name. It?s a very tiny town, consisting of only about ten villagers (as of now), but it?s a pleasant little place nonetheless. - Aimee used to be your best friend through your childhood, until she moved away at eighteen to go to college. Now, eight years later, she owns a fairly large, successful stable. When you decide you?re ready to get into the horse business seriously, you give her a call. After explaining yourself to her, she tells you that she has a small, unused barn she?d be willing to rent out to you for a small price. She will probably be hard to make an enemy, unless you purposely try to. Right now, I?m thinking it will be possible to have a romance with her. - This shady guy isn?t a villager, really. He?s pretty much just someone you tell cheat codes to. You can?t have any relationships with him, friend or enemy. He?s just kind of?there. A young man a little younger than you. He lives here with his uncle, Devon, while he takes classes at a nearby college. He?s not as into horses as most of the other people around, but now and then you might catch him being a little affectionate towards Liberty Bell, a little gelding at Devon?s farm. He also is ?affectionate? towards Stacy, though if that affection is returned is yet to be seen? ;-) He?s a little harder to be friends with, but it can be done. Devon owns Patriot Farms, is Andrew?s uncle, and is about the most patriotic man you?ll ever meet in your life. A few of his horses include Liberty Bell, Lady Liberty, Honest Abe, and a pair of little Shetlands named Stars and Stripes, just to give you a taste of his patriotic spirit. He?s a friendly guy, though, and is easy to be friends with. - A young woman Andrew?s age. She?s isn?t college bound, and never intends to be. Very peppy, perky, pink, and prosperous, she?s spoiled and used to having everything she wants. Her current hobby is horses, and she owns one beautiful Dutch Warmblood, named Benevolence. Of course, Benevolence is the best of the best, and he?s being wasted being trained by a newbie like Gina. Nonetheless, he still poses a threat if you decide to train in dressage. Behind Gina?s sticky-sweet exterior, though, lies a jealous nasty creature. - The owner of Lithe Stables, John, along with his wife Catherine, is the most successful horseman for miles. He is married to Catherine and the father of Randy. He has a practically unbeatable horse, named Veni Vidi Vici, who is competing in western events, particularly barrels and poles. He raises mostly western horses with a few jumpers and racers here and there. Most of his horses are dynamite (not literally), so watch out. - John?s wife, Randy?s mother, and also owner of Lithe Stables. She?s pretty quiet and shy, but friendly enough. Um?. Can?t think of much more to say about her?. - An eight year old kid. He?s the son of Catherine and John. He has a pony, Quiz, who training with his parents? help to compete in small-time jumping shows. REALLY easy to befriend. He starts out practically adoring you. - Despite the name, Kiki is about as tough as tough gets. She?s a few years older than you, and VERY competitive. Very easy to make an enemy out of her. She believes in ?tough love? training for horses, who are all huge, husky drafters. She looks after her grandfather, Henry, and he?s practically the only one she?s nice to. Sometimes. - Kiki?s grandfather. He?s very old, very confused, and it?s very hard to hold a conversation with him. Your relationship with him will fluctuate a lot because he?s so unpredictable and?.well, insane. - A middle-ages guy who trains racers. Keeps mostly to himself. I haven?t figured out much about him yet.**
