It's often extremely helpful, for complex programs like battle systems, to plan everything out very carefully, to draw all of the designs and figure out at what pixel locations graphics will be located, and to divide the different functions of the system into sub-systems. You'll do much better if you already know where the different sections of the program end and begin; for instance, any AI should be programmed separately and fit into the main battle system, probably by giving the same commands the player does. You'll want to separate out attack and defense systems, the on-screen menu should be separate from the functions it controls... etc. ^___^ Anyway. It just helps--it both makes it easier and makes a better battle system--to plan everything out carefully before you even begin to code. o.o
