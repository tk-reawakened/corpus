# Life's Desire

October 5, 2006, 9:31 pm
Design
'Life's Desire :: Paths to Immortality' takes place in relatively modern times. Roen (Row-en) is a poor resident, among many others, of the town of Tuckerson Cove. Your main objective in the game is to get revenge on the army of Govilton Empire. After the raid on your town, your family is nowhere to be found. Find them, bring them to safety...if you can. Find and destroy the emperor which you have so longed to do! But many choices lie ahead. It is up to you to choose your path to immortality.
