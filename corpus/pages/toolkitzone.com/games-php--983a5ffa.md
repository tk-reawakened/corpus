Mortalis Demo Re-Release
*Click to enlarge*
**Date Added**
1.0 Sunday 5.02am, April 10th 2005
This is actually a re-release of the original demo, but it will be the official one until the actual full release of Mortalis. Most importantly, this demo is to show off the sweet original soundtrack Dev has been working on for the last while.
**Technical**
However, this demo includes a number of other additions/improvements: -2 new enemies to fight -Can use a bow, and 2 new bow techniques -New stats increase the damage done by whichever weapon you use the most (same applies to magic damage, also) -Now battle system shows if you use an axe/bow in the character's hand -Actual credits animation to watch available from the main menu -Fixed bugs and minor tweaks to gameplay -Is in version 3.0.6 of the toolkit, which is bound to be more stable There is no new storyline, and the download is approximately 15 megabytes. So, if you haven't played Mortalis, now is the perfect time to check it out. If you played the original demo, however, you may want to download this one just to check out the great music, but I won't cry if you don't Wink
