This game is actually a sequel to an rpg that I made a long, long time ago. I don't think I can salvage those files, but I will say that it was made with Rpg Maker 1995. I do think that this game can stand on it's own, even though it's a sequel; there really is very little of the original story in it. An intersting concept of the game is Jinkojin, which is the center of the story and a main element of the battle system. Jinkojin is basically crystalized magic. Think materia from FF7.
