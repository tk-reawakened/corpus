**Outsets**
by Tao-Kun **Taking a nice, long break**
December 18 2005, 8:14 pm
Lately I ahve been composes images for many things, and it hit me. Everysince I begun acutally drawing back in 2004, I have spent well over $300 on art supplies and learning items so that I may learn how to draw; pretty much, my results of my drawing skills can both be seen in Outsets and Advent Adventure. Although I love to draw, and I love to create games, I decided that I won't be able to do either unless I learn how to draw better and learn how to program; therefore, I am taking a nice little break from both projects. Outsets will not continue for toolkit, but the story will live on in the Outsets community (comming soon) and in the future when It get turned into 3D. Anyone wishing to work on Outsets themselves, just drop me a PM; I'd be more then happy to let you have all the files containing to the game (Everything in the game is around 1 gig, everything circling the game is about 2 gig) If you decide to work on Outsets yourself, you are more then welcome to work on Outsets under two conditions. 1. You may not use any of the characters listed Miko, Su, Naota, Osaka 2. If you use my files, my name and Studio name must be in it. I apologize, but I really need to study programming and learn beter drawing techs. Until then, good bye for now Outsets.
