***Cheats? Why Not?***
**An overview of The Horse Smell's cheats**
I simply couldn't make The Horse Smell without putting a few cheats in there here or there. I just couldn't resist the notion of putting my favorite lesson horse in as an unlockable, or an ultimate horse, or even a new NPC. I mean, really, who resist? So here you have it. Just a simple little overview of how the cheats will work, and an example of some things you can unlock. *can*
So, you have your cheat code. How do you input it? Well, you go seek out The Cheat Dealer, of course! The Cheat Dealer is a shady character, complete with brimmed hat pulled low over his eyes, high-collared trenchcoat, and one thin eye staring suspiciously at you through the shadow covering the rest of his face. Talk to him, and he'll offer you the chance to enter a cheat code. If you get it right, he'll give you what the cheat offers. If not, he'll turn you away. Which leads us to the next section: what kinds of cheats will there be?
Well, let me answer that with a list of a few of the things that, as of now, will most likely be accessable through cheats:
-
A new horse, Bailey. I ride Bailey in riding lessons a lot, and he's a lazy but very cute little guy.
-
Make Aimee fall in love with you. I couldn't resist adding this one. ;-)
-
Probably a mini game or two will also be unlockable
-
The ultimate horse. This will be a massive three-part code, as this horse will be mega-maxed in EVERYTHING and is a one way ticket to fame and fortune. I may not even add this one... because I'm afraid it would make the game to easy if someone figured it out. =/
-
Of course some special items
-
A special cutscene or two
Those are only a few of what could very well be dozens. I'm a cheat code fanatic, so there should be quite a few in The Horse Smell. ;-)

For the ultimate horse, you could make it so you have to do a specific thing (such as beat the game or gain a certain amount of money?) before being able to unlock it. If the game can be beat, and you make that the condition, then you can have a data file with a simple value of "no". If the player ever beats the game, change the data file to "yes". When the cheat is entered, open the data file and check if it's "yes", then give the horse, otherwise tell them they haven't unlocked the cheat or something. Just a quick little thought on the ultimate horse cheat, as it would be a good way to play through the game a second time.
