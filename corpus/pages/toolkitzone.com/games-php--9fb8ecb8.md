# Phantom Warriors

June 12, 2006, 4:10 pm
Development
You play as the bodyguard of the king of Grolands. After being sent one year to the past by a misterious being from an invading force, you struggle to figure out where are you and trying to get to your homeworld within one year to avoid the same thing to happen again, and save your king. More description on the news.
**Phantom Warriors**
by Gooseboy **The story begins.**
June 8 2006, 11:42 pm
The story of the game goes as follows: You play as the bodyguard of the king of Grolands. As the civilisation from the closest planet, Paradoxx, misteruously began to invade your planet and thus causing a blind war between the two planets. The most powerfull being from the invaders, known as the paradoxx, (same name as their homeworld) used his incredible powers to send you one year to the past, eliminating you from the current existance and thus, killing his target, the king Caldar. When you wake up, you find yourself in a misterious cave trying to figure out "Where am I?" and begin worring about your planet and your people. When you remembered you were sent one year to the past, you suddenly try to escape from where ever you are and return to your home planet within one year to avoiding the same war to happen again, and thus, avoiding the death of your king and loosing everything you once loved, including your homeworld Grolands. As Kraddor Sleming (the name of the main character) tries to figure out how to escape from where he is, he suddenly finds a fainted girl on a very dark forrest. Because he has a great heart, he decides to take care of her untill she wakes up, and asks if you are his "daddy". "No, but dont worry, you are safe now." you said to the little girl. When he suddenly finds a hidden village, he decides to ask for help and if the father of the little girl lives there, wich it does. When you are taken to the father's house, her father, Barto, ran towards her confused daughter and hugs her, and he said he coud do anything to repay you. What do you ask for? "To get the hell out of here... my people will be in danger and ive been sent one year to the past, and im stuck in this unknown world..." What happens next? Find out! "Phantom Warriors" will promise to keep you hooked up to its interesting story, wich ive been developing since 2001, wich is one of the best storylines seen in toolkitzone.com
