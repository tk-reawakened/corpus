# Little Man Classic

October 23, 2005, 8:49 am

Little Man Classic is the Original Little Man With a Pea Shooter that was featured in MrG's Shack Challenge with a few modifications to appeal to the general TK User. This includes a small variety of original graphics, musical score, and game play.The object of the game is to effectivly manuver Little Man, locate the Pea, and shoot it across the level without Little Man or the Pea being lost down a bottomless pit.
