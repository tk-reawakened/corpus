# Illicit Soul

January 31, 2006, 4:32 pm
Development
A young woman awakens in the untamed forest world of Kalahan, unsure of her origin or purpose. Along with a band of fast friends, she'll embark on a mission of self-discovery, and indeed, discover the very nature of the cosmos.
**Illicit Soul**
by venstor **Characters**
April 13 2005, 9:30 am
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/469.gif)
- Cerna is the game's main protagonist. She suddenly blinks into existence in the middle of Ursprung Forest, fully grown, able to speak and think coherently. She has no memories, however. It's not amnesia, her mind is simply a blank slate. Absolutely everything is new to her. Sunlight, trees, the act of breathing itself, these things are all utterly new and fascinating to her. The world of Kalahan can be a dangerous place, however, and someone as naive as Cerna isn't going to last long on her own... **CERNA REISENDER**
Cerna is very weak physically. She takes damage like mad and takes two hits to kill the game's weakest enemy with slaps. Where she really shines is in her magic. She's able to summon spheres of dark matter that rend enemies to shreds, and she's equally adept as a healer. Keeping Cerna alive will be vital to winning many battles.
Her name is Czech for "black" (to denote her elemental affiliation with shadows) and is pronounced "SAIR-nuh."
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/470.gif)
- Rorick is the son of a Woodtown guard. For as long as he can remember, he's wanted to be a great swordsman. He wears a rusty old breastplate and carries a wooden sword with him where ever he goes. He loves roaming the forests around his home and hunting any monsters he can find. During one expedition, he finds Cerna surrounded by monsters and rescues her. He instantly falls in lust with her and takes her back to his house. Rorick serves as Cerna's and the player's guide to the world of Kalahan. He's arrogant and he constantly hits on Cerna, but he's invaluable as a warrior. **RORICK PADRAIG**
Once Rorick gets some better equipment, he becomes the heavy hitter of the party, able to slash down beasts with his sword and withstand all kinds of blows with his armor. His magic ability is nill.
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/58/472.gif)
- Kaleen is Rorick's adopted sister. She's a member of the all-but-extinct Elvish race, but the only family she's ever known has been humans. She's very sensitive about the fact that she's different, and tries desperately to fit in. Kaleen has a reputation around Woodtown for being promiscuous. She's instantly distrustful when Rorick brings Kaleen home, and though she agrees to accompany them on their adventure, she makes no attempt to hide her contempt for the new girl. **KALEEN** **ELANESSË**
Kaleen is a deadly archer who can take out foes with stunning efficiency. Her defense isn't great, but she holds a high resistance to magic.
The name "Kaleen" comes from reversing the syllables of "Link," since she was originally supposed to be female version of the guy from Zelda.
