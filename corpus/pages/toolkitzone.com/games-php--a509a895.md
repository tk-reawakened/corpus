# Illicit Soul

January 31, 2006, 4:32 pm
Development
A young woman awakens in the untamed forest world of Kalahan, unsure of her origin or purpose. Along with a band of fast friends, she'll embark on a mission of self-discovery, and indeed, discover the very nature of the cosmos.
**Illicit Soul**
June 26 2004, 1:22 am
Here's a piece of Star Ocean music I've modified to fit my game by converting an spc file into a MIDI. The section with the tuba and woodblock is my favorite part, I think. To match the forest environment of my game, I'm generally trying to avoid brass and stick with woodwinds and other wooden instruments. But the tuba works here. This music will be playing when Cerna first visits the farm where Rorick and Kaleen live.
