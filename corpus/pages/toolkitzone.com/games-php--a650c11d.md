**Andromeda Sky - Angel Tears**
by Talyn **Andromeda Sky - Enter the End**
August 28 2004, 11:06 am
Enter the End is still quite a new idea in my head. Meaning that it has little storyline plan, no graphics or programming to speak of yet and hasn't even been seriously considered yet. However, it is an idea that I want to persue!
Plot - What little plot has been decided is that all the characters from both games come together to face a threat to the entire planet(s) [Hehe, the plural on Planet will all come clear in Gateway...]. It has not been decided whether you run around as Raie or Skie, and I'm actually toying with the idea of having a female lead in this one... Any feminists out there?
Characters - As I said before, the characters of both games come together to face a threat to both their ways of life, (This threat HAS been decided).
Setting - This game will be set in the entire Andromeda Sky world, meaning that you will visit locations from both games. However, the entire world will not be accessible as this would be too big a game.
Ending - The end to this game hasn't been decided yet. Meaning that it's still in the balance of whether you succeed or not. I'm even thinking about doing a FFX-2 sort of thing and making it so that the player decides on the ending through their actions in the game.
Release Date: Probably not for at least a year!
