Bio-Chips:Bio-chips are mainly used as identification perposes from from ranging from Credit cards, Driver's Id's and the such.
Nano-Chips: Nano-Chips are the most detailed creation of all time. The chip is placed usually in hand or wrists. Nano-chips can help people or give people new strengths. Nano-chips have been found to heal disease's and wounds quickly. Nano-chips made it possible so lung cancer, diabetes and various other illness' are no more. Why? Well special Nanites run through the people's body who have nano-chips healing whats wrong and replacing it with what is good. All Nano-chips run off of a main computer which tells the nanites certian task's to do. These chips even can enhance humans into killing machine but the goverment doesnt release those to the public, but thus a killing machine is born by the Govt. means more Cyborgs gone.
Main computer: IT give's signals to the nano-chips across the world to do certain tasks.
Cyborgs: Cybernetic-Organisms who were once humans. But why did they transform themselves? Power, able to kill, or defense. Armies used to be made up of these until the nano-chips were released thus creating holding plants of cyborgs in cryo-sleep. The Govt. Basically used the for war during the cyborg wars and threw them out.
Religon: Religon is still a big part in the future society, but is against the laws to the Govt. People still gather around in hidden area's to have holy cermonies. MAny who are religous didnt take any nano-chips since many say its the mark of the beast. Some even said that about Bio-Chips and now many are homeless since they have no identity,
Plasma Blades: They are Different melee weapons to an ordinary house knife. When they cut something, it then cuts to through the molecular level, this is one way of killing anything micro-scoptic.
The Cyborg Wars: they ended 30 years ago and started 30 before that. some of the vetrens live today still, with ages around 50 or so. THe war was basically held because of of Robots and machine's went haywire. Thats when the Govt. either took people off their streets or soldiers and turned them into Cyborgs, many are depressed of loved one's lost and society tries to forget the war.
