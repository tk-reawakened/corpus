# Browse

Posted on by (38 views, 2 ) Story
**Wednesday 3.13am, May 12th 2004** **[Trent](community.php?user=351)**comments
The actual story of Fighter's Fury, and not the poopy little 250 character one.
An evil android in a remote dimension is plotting the destruction of all other dimensions, so that he may expand his empire. He cannot do this on his own, even though he's massively powerful. He learns of a stone called the Fighter's Fury, a powerful stone that is hidden in each dimension. It holds the power of all emotions. The android sends his 3 most trusted minions to 3 of the dimensions to retrieve the stone. Dark Evil was sent to the dimension this game takes place in. He arrives at a city with his 2 minions, Demon and Zephyr. He needs 3 special coins to access the Fighter's Fury, so he sends the minions out to get them. Yen Powers, the protagonist, lives in Spring City, OH (In the USA). He lives a nice life. But when Demon shows up and destroys the city, Yen and his friends set out to get revenge. But the thing that none of them know... The weight of the world is on their shoulders.
