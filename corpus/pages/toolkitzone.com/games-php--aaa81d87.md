# Sauce Packet Conspiracy

Evil Vegetarian Zombies are trying to take over the world...
**Sauce Packet Conspiracy**
by Hamster **Working on Characters**
August 5 2005, 10:26 pm
Ive started doing characters for the game. Im Hand Drawing Them and then using gimp to outline and color in them on a second layer. So far here's a pic of one of the characters ive finished. As for other poses and walking animations, im only drawing a pic of the character front view back view and r l views. This is just my first pic so its weird, with the character looking around something sort of far away from them(a pose i want to put in the game). I sort of like drawing people, im not very good at it but i think im getting better. As for the fact that person is sort of plain without detail, i think that keeping things simple will help in the long run. As for the size of the characters, They are 83* 210, and my game resolution is now 1024*768. The introduction clip which i have posted pics of-you know, the nightime rain ones- are sized for when you are in a vehicle, the boards with characters will be different. As for the other pics ive posted, im getting rid of all of them except the light rail board(which got a lot of nice comments and is too cool to get rid of) because they are not the same quality and are not to scale. Even though you wouldnt think it, drawing and using bitmaps is sort of easier than making tiles look real nice.
