# Eternal Saga: Renaissance

June 30, 2006, 7:17 pm
Development
First in Series; After an Evil Tyrant brought war to the world of Typira, it's countries were devastated, but only 5 years later countries have began to prosper again. In the war, Rex Oxide lost his Brother, Gus. But due to the determination of Rex and his best mate; Kizza, they are on a journey to see if Gus is still alive but more terrible things lay ahead.......
**Eternal Saga: Renaissance**
by The Iron Bulldog **Renaissance: Prologue**
June 30 2006, 7:17 pm
Well I'm putting Eternal Saga: Renaissance on temporary hold for a few weeks to fine tune the story, which is almost done. But I then had a thought. I decided to start creating a little non-playing game to showcase the events that happened when Gus was killed. This will be a short "movie" that I hope will help portray events more clearly as they have an impact on the main game. I should finish it soon, maybe a week or two. After that I will start Renaissance back up again and aim to release a demo that will reach the end of the first sub-plot. And Renaissance will be finished before I turn grey, I promise. Thank You.
