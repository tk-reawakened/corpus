# Heraldry: Freija

Demo
The first from the the four-volume HERALDRY series. The game promises to raise the standards of RPG TOOLKIT games--graphically, and gameplay-wise. You play the role of Freija Moonault, a highschool kickout and daughter of the great Moonault carthographers. As Freija, your goal is to rediscover the world and disprove of the historical lies that your government has fed to the people.

After the war, Skyra was thought of the as only inhabitable continent in new Nachram. The survivors of the Great Divide recognized Skyra as the new name of their planet. Among Skyra are the cities of Imperia, Lucero, Kojima, Aurea, and villages like Kojima Port, Ahrid Khan, and Burhamh. The main city of Skyra is called Imperia. This is where Aryx Academy is located, where Freija studies.
