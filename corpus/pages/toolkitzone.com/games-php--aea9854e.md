**Date Added**
Wraith Kingdom Demo Tuesday 12.18am, February 6th 2007
This is the very first Wraith Kingdom demo available for public download. Once unzipped, just hit "Setup," don't try unzipping the other folder or you may have an unwelcome 80MB taking up your hard drive. I know it's not all that great because the characters are floating, there's not yet an opening AVI, and it only goes to Level 7. The full game will definitely have addressed these problems.
**Technical**
If you experience any graphical errors, try lowering your hardware acceleration (Right-click on desktop, Properties, Settings tab, Advanced, Troubleshoot, Disable Direct 3d), but I'm sure you've already figured it out if you own TK.
