February 26 2006, 11:43 am
Yes, there is a custom battle system also, which you can view by going into the gallery. As for the tiles, yes - they are ripped. I say the same thing to everyone, and it may be a rather feeble excuse, but I just don't have the time to produce the tiles needed for my project. Ripping tiles allows me to spend more time on the programming, design and gameplay aspects of the game. I try my best to use the ripped tiles as best as I can though, so I just need people to look past the fact that they are ripped and focus on the design when commenting. I have never really noticed the clash between the weapon sets and other tile set, I guess they don't match perfectly but I don't really see it as a major problem, these things can be changed easily in the future anyway.
