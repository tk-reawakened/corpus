# Labrinth 13

July 21, 2005, 10:48 am
Development
A superhero rpg in which a group of teenagers are kidnapped and supjected to experiments which give them various superpowers. Escaping, they join a secret government strike force to stop their former captors.
**Labrinth 13**
by catwhowalksbyhimself **Characters**
December 4 2004, 6:39 pm
Default Cast: The characters that the game begins with:
> Psycho: Age 17
>
> Somewhat of an introvert, Bill Mentole all the more valued the friends he had. He became known and valued for his advice, becoming something of a leader. His life changed when he was kidnapped by Minotaur on the way home from his friend Jeff\'s home. Subjected to the latest in a series of experiment to give soldiers super-human powers, Bill found himself able to read the minds of his captors and put his own thoughts in their heads. He quickly learned to control his new abilities, tricking Minotaur into thinking he only had a range of one foot from his body. He could actually touch the the minds of his fellow captives, contacting and befriending all them and encouraging them to hide the extent of their powers as well. His mental powers allow him to influence human minds and plant suggestions. With practice, his powers may give him a form of mind control and a method of attack.
>
> Havock: Age 18
>
> Star highschool football player and athlete Jim Hudson was not the stereotypical jock. He was a thinker, prefering to plan before acting, a habit that he had to learn to control as an athlete. His thinking skill served him well as a quarterback. Although he seemed to have the idea life to his fellow students, his home life was somewhat lacking. His father was constantly away from home on what he called buisiness trips, although Jim\'s mother suspected otherwise. When he dissappeared altogether, it only seemed to confirm her suspicion. The truth was the he was a the leader of the secret government task force known only as the argonauts and was tracking Minotaur. He was captured and tortured, become the first human experiment. Nearly a year later, Minotaur kidnapped Jim, his son, as a means of revenge. When Minotaur experimented on him, his physical abilities were incredibly enhanced, but his body was also greatly mutated. Planning to escape, but having no good means of doing so, he hid the full extent of his strength. Although his cell was reinforced, he believes he could still fight his way past it. Used to being a leader himself, he resents Psycho\'s attempts at leading the new group of teen heros.
>
> Circus: Age 15
>
> Susana Snortright was one of Franklin High School\'s best known nerds. She was known for spending all her free time in the biology lab, or at the zoo, studying the animals there and questioning busy officials. Minotaur\'s experiments have only increased her interest in biology, allowing her to touch the minds of animals, but strangly, not people. She can directly tap the senses of nearby animals and even control them as extensions of her own body. Never known for her common sense, Circus did not hide her powers and was locked in a cell away from the other experiments where no guard dogs were allowed to patrol. Because of her distance away from the others, she is the one teen experiment that Psycho has not yet contacted.
>
> Peregrin: Age 17
>
> Sam Falcane was a good-natured boy who was also quite overweight, even from a young age. His sophomore year in high school, he weighed 320 pounds. It was then he decided he needed to change. After a very determined change of diet and lifestyle, he lost nearly 50 pounds, but he soon realized that he would never be light weight enough to fulfil his dream of being a runner. His quick reflexes did, however earn his a place as a goalie in his local hockey team. His life changed when became lost during an away game and stumbled into a front operation owned by Minotaur. Their experiments radically altered his metabolism, speeding all of his body functions. He lost even more weight and gained a measure of speed and dexerity completely unknown to humans. As his abilities are still growing, it is unknown just how fast he will become.
>
> Flashback: Age 16
>
> Sara Sanders seemed to have it all. She came from a wealthy political family, has good looks, and was a science wiz at her private high school. She had no way of knowing that some of her family\'s wealth came from bribes given to her senator grandfather from Minotaur. When her grandfather began to have second thoughts, she soon found herself a captive, then the subject of Minotaur\'s latest experiments. The mutations have given her the ability to partially alter time inself, cause certain objects, or even parts of a human body, to revert to an earlier state, or trasform into a future possible state. In this way she can heal injuries, teleport herself where she was in will, or reoppen an old injury. Although she can now only alter time to the extent of about a minute, the time range is increasing very rapidly. Minotaur does not yet understand the nature of her seemingly random powers, and keeps her under closer surveilence.
>
> Game Over: Age 15
>
> Peter Anderson never had very many friends. It wasn\'t because he was not likeable, but Peter was a computer addict. When his mother and teachers began expressing their concern for him, Peter decided to turn his problem into in asset. Soon he was know as the \"Computer Wizard\" and assisted the computer teachers in his school. One day, as he was searching the internet, he found some seemingly innocent and unrelated sites that had a strange connection which no one without is level of expertise could have noticed. Intriqued, he tried to hack into the sites. Unknown to him, he had stumbled on Minotaur\'s hidden network. Minotaur agents kidnapped him that night. Their experiments seemed to tap into his infinity for computers, and now he can read computer programs and processes with his mind, almost a computer telepathy. Although the range of his powers is known to Mintaur, Psycho has been able to use his powers to extend Game Over\'s range, allowing him to keep a close watch on his captors and to learn the layout of his prison.
>
>
> Blink: Age 17
>
> A seemingly average student in a seemingly average high-school, Jonathan Smith could not know that the family clinic in his town was actually a front for a Minotaur Labrinth, Labrinth 8. One day, on a dare, he snuck into the clinic at night and stumbled upon the Labrinth entrance. He was quickly captured and pressed into service as an experiment. The expermints in his case have resulted in turning his body into an unstable state, able to transform in a beam of pure energy and back into matter again. His control over his powers is shaky at best, and even if he had complete control over them, the walls of his cell have specially formulated mirrors which keep his energy form from escaping through the cell walls.
>
The Triple Duet--Three sets of two characters, only one set of which the player will get, based on a decision made early on in the game:
> Symbiote and Queen: Ages 16 and 17
>
> Unknown the rest of the Labrinth 8 experiments, there were two very special experiments being stored nearby. When they joined Argonaut, Psycho was able to detect these two experiments before Minotaur could remove them. The were both attempts as creating a master super-soldier who would be able to control other super-powered soldiers in order to create a docial and controlable super army that any evil dictator would pay dearly to possess. Symbiote has become completely dependent on others for his powers and abilities. When he is first rescued, he is weak and powerless, but as he is around other super-humans, he gradually absorbs their powers, making him the potentially most powerful of them all, if he can survive combat long enough. Queen,lacking Symbiote\'s peculiar vulnerabilities, is able to alter and control the powers of othe super-humans, and can even absorb some powers and permanently bestow them on her allies.
>
> Sarge and Needles: Ages 35 and 63
>
> Police Sergeant Jake Franses knew the moment he saw those eight strange teenagers that they were in some kind of trouble. He just didn\'t know how much in trouble they really were. Taking under his protection, he introduced them to retired doctor George \"Needles\" McClair. The two soon found themselves caught in the middle of the Minotaur conspiracy and were forced to join Argonaut along with the eight super-powered teens.
>
> Spike and Doll: Ages 15 and 13
>
> Life is tough as a teenager, but Jim and Jesse Arton had it tougher than usual. After their parents died, they were tossed around from foster home to foster home, sometimes together, but usually apart. When Jesse\'s last foster father tried to rape her, Jim had enough. The two of them escaped together from their respective foster families and took a bus to a random town, the small city of Farmton, secret base of Labrinth 8. The two found and abandoned building and were living there and surviving off the streets when they met the super-powered fugitives escaping from Labrinth 8, with government agents and police seemingly right behind them. Their group of two quickly became ten as the street-smart siblings joined the super-powered refugees.
>
Argonauts: Members of the elite strike force Argonaut which teams up with the Lab survivors
> Snarky: Age 32
>
> Jonathan Snarky was one of the CIA\'s top agents before he was secretly approached and asked to help for the secret task force later code-named Argonaut. He has been investigating Minotaur for two years now and became the leader of Argonaut a year before when Jack Hudson, the original leader, was captured by the Minotaur. A trained agent and spy, Snarky is well-trained in stealth and is good at marksmanship, first aid, and hand-to-hand combat.
>
> Goggles: Age 25
>
> A strangely detatched individual, Greg \"Goggles\" Handerson is one of the best snipers ever to work for the Navy SEALS. As a member of Minotaur, his marksmanship is invaluable, and the team would greatly miss him on the field if he was gone--even if they would never miss him in the barracks.
>
> Terror: Age 28
>
> Brandon Terror is known by his friend to be the \"Terror\" on the battlefield. This is not because he a great fighter, but simply because his \"bedside manner\" as a field medic is somewhat lacking. In spite of their complaints, he is well-loved by his teammates and has saved their lived with his guns as often as with his needles and surgical kit.
>
Other characters, which you will be able to get on certain missions:
>
> Energy Man: Age 17
>
> A freak mutation gave Edward Worpondo a strange affinity for energy. Objects floated in air around him, electrical surges often went through the room he was in, and the temperature around him often rose or fell for no particular reason. His family learned to live with these supposed poltergeist phenomena, but when his parents died in a car accident, he was sent to live with his aunt. She quickly decided that he was haunted, and threw him onto the streets. Minotaur found him wandering the streets and took him as one of their experiments. Their careless disreguard for who they take as subjects has created a very unique power in Blink. The power mutation process has both greatly enhanced his natural mutations and given him the ability to control them. Edward quickly found that he had a great deal of control over all forms of energy, including motion, light, heat, and electricity. In an overwelming burst of power which suprised even him, he burst free from Labrinth 22 where he was held and escaped into the nearby town. There he became the super-powered vigilante known only as Energy Man. In his arrogance, he thought Minotaur would never be able to stop him. He was wrong. Fortunately, Argonaut had detected the enormous energy flux caused by his escape and arived just in time to rescue him.
>
> Medicine Man: Age 21
>
> Derek Eagle-Eye was a police officer in the small town of Indian Creek, Michigan, when he became suspicious about the chemical plant which employed many of the townspeople. Unfortunately, Derek got all too close to the truth and Minotaur felt the need to capture him. Subjected to their mutation process, he gained the strange ability to give life to ghost-like "spirit-beings" made out of pure energy assembled by his mind to mock intelligent behavior. His spirit warriors are powerful in battle, although they never last for very long. He also can create energy blasts and some limited illussions. Freed by the Argonauts, he quickly joined them to continue his quest to free his home town from the Minotaur threat.
