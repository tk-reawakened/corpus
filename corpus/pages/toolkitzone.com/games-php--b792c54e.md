February 2 2005, 12:48 pm
This is the first World Map board. It shows your house (directly above the character) as well as the town of Eisle (to the left of the character). Depending on which side of the town you enter from, determines where you start at on the Town of Eisle board (ie if you enter from the north, you are place in the north part of town). The board is currently 100x100 tiles in size, and depending on speed improvements of the Toolkit, and how fast the game is developed, may increase in size.
