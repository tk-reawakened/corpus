# NOTE:

February 18, 2005, 1:50 am
Demo
Godiva: Alpha is the first in a trilogy of games based on popular RPG's like Diablo, AD&D, Final Fantasy, and many more. It has a very expansive class and magic system, as well as a extremely large amount of weapons, armor, and other items to customize your character(s) with. Images and information of the game may or may not represent the current, or future demo, but were/are works in progress unless stated otherwise.
