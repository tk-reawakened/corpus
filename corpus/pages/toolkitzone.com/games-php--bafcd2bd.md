# Stealth High

February 19, 2006, 3:02 pm
Design
A university of fighters of all kinds has just opened, and is now taking in its first regestrators. This is the university of stealth fighting; Stealth High. You play as one of the first regestrators to the new university named Keijiro Fuunharo.As you play through Stealth High you will experience plenty to do, from game related quests, to quests of your own. From ICBIM(I_Cant_Believe_Its_Me) comes the return of Stealth High!
