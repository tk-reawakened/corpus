# Donkey Kong RPG

April 23, 2006, 10:09 pm
Design
When several mishaps occur at the Kong family reunion, Donkey Kong sets out on a role-playing adventure to seek vengeance for the accident that wiped out most of the Kong family. Play as DK, as well as Diddy, Dixie, Kiddie, Funky, Candy, and a suprise guest. Each character has his/her own special moves, both on and off the battlefield.
**Donkey Kong RPG**
by hehheh, allllrite **Custom Systems**

So I've made some modifications to the custom systems I made back in January, thus pretty much eliminating this tedious task. Back then, I created systems such as an item system, a save system, a menu system, and a battle system. I'm pretty much redoing the battle system, but I'm keeping several key elements from my old one. As of now, I'm beginning on the battle system. If I work at it, I should be finished in three weeks tops.
