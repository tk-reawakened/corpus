# The Horse Smell: Mini Horse

October 5, 2006, 5:08 pm
Development
The full name is actually "The Horse Smell: Miniature Horse", but I couldn't fit it in the profile, so I shortened it. This is a one day, one person game. It is almost completely text-based, and rather small (hence the name "Miniature Horse"). It's something like the original The Horse Smell was going to be like, where you own a horse and care for him/her. However, a lot more stress is put on riding, and molding your training to the horse's personality.
