# Goomba

March 12, 2005, 2:41 am
Development
This will be a game that is sorta like a tamagotchi, Neopet mix that is interactive online. You will be able to see other people's goombas and play around on a 2d platform environment. It will be based on the Little Man gaming engine. It will be fun and fullfill your curiousity for a bit. What started as a fun filled comic will be an interactive game.
