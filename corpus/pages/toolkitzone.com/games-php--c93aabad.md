# Heraldry: Freija

March 22, 2005, 5:36 pm
Demo
The first from the the four-volume HERALDRY series. The game promises to raise the standards of RPG TOOLKIT games--graphically, and gameplay-wise. You play the role of Freija Moonault, a highschool kickout and daughter of the great Moonault carthographers. As Freija, your goal is to rediscover the world and disprove of the historical lies that your government has fed to the people.
**Heraldry: Freija**
by khin **Patch 1.0-1 Released**
May 10 2004, 10:04 pm
After the reportedly buggy first release, it has been our initiative to investigate and re-run more tests. Though the game proved itself to be stable on our testing centers, there are still a number of dissatisfied gamers complaining about many bugs that hinder them from enjoying it to the fullest. Many thanks to one of our contacts, Delano, for he has come up with an idea regarding the plugin. Apparently tkpluginHeraldry was causing all the problems. It wasn't compiled for Release mode, so I had to go back to codes and recompile it. Now it will no longer look for system-dependent files(specifically Visual Srudio runtime files). Tests have been run once again on those who had problems before. Good news! It works now. Download the patch . In case you haven't got the whole game, . To install, copy/paste the unzipped file(tkpluginHeraldry.dll) to the plugins folder of the game.
[here](http://www.mysticsoftware.net/piarts/tkpluginHeraldry.zip) [download it here](http://msgames.rackhost.net/games/Heraldry1.zip)
