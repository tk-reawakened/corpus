September 13 2005, 7:11 am
The water tile consists of only 2 colors. I just filled a 32x32 picture with a dark turquoise color tone from the standart 256 color palette of windows (color tone R 27 G 55 B 75) then selected a purple color tone from the same palette, and after selecting made it brighter with the color sliders of the drawing tool(purple tone R 48 G 44 B 99), and then made some hatching over the picture with the purple color. About the water on the cliffs, this is no semitransparency effect. I've drawn a line along the height of the water and re-pixeled the rocks below by hand, using different colortones than for the above part, making it look like semitransparency. There's nothing special about the drawing tool (using "The Games Factory" from europress for drawing), I had to set pixel by pixel with a pen tool.
