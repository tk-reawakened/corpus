# Antarah: Rise to Djin

April 27, 2006, 3:44 pm
Development
Outcast to the Sahhara, Antar searches for the legendary kingdom of Egypt. He is worn to the point of near-death when he sees a beautiful gazelle take flight across the sands, chased by a Fire Bird. He destroys the bird with two arrows, and the gazelle returns to him. It is transformed into Pri Gul Nazar. She is greatfull for his courage and tells him he shall reacieve the three things all great warriors desire... Ultimate vengence, Unlimited power, and the joy of love. .
