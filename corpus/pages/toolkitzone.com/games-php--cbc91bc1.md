Heh. Might try fixing the nose. Elves in my world have sort of a distinctive nose shape to differentiate them from humans, but I probably didn't render that in the best way. For lineart, I do a pencil drawing, then ink over the top of that, erase the pencil lines, and then scan it in and clean it up a little by messing with levels and brightness/contrast in Photoshop. It's less work in the long run to ink stuff than it is to try to clean up pencil lines, I find.
