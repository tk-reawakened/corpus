3D sprites is the same as making 3d objects. Make a sphere for the head, except elongated at the bottom, and two round shapes on the sides, 4 layers (i use) depth backwards at the top goin to two at the bottom. Next make a shape that looks like a body of course, i usually make the arms and legs 12 to 15 layers thick, and round it off at the end to make hands and fingers. fingers and shoes should be easy. Hair is a pain. If you want crazy hair, in completely 3d, im sorry. But basic hair, you just increse the size of the top of the head by a tad, outline the shape of the hair, then do color and shading. etc. Dont forget to do clothes, be sure to increase the height of the sides of shoulders if you are including armor. hope this could help. Refer to the library for more info if this waws no help.
