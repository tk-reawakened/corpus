Thoughts
Reasons for the scores not bieng tens - Graphics - the characters could have used a little bit more animation. for example like when a ninja turtle threw a shuriken it could have been spinning a little bit. Audio - the songs were good, but the quality lacked a little. Story - There was a story but it was something like - if you win, your the best, hooray.short and sweet but too short. Fun - It kept my attention and i played all the way through but it was too easy, it would have been better if you can unlock character with each win of the tournament or something like that. Stability - There werent any bugs, but there was a glitch that made it far too easy to win. if you corner your oponent and keep hitting them right before they touch the ground(you gotta time it right) you can keep hitting them until K.O, also the credits at the beggining were too slow. Length - well, real short. but the only reason this score is'nt like a 2 or lower is becuase the type of game this is, its not suppose to ne long, and also if it were longer that means you would have to fight the same player more then once and it would have lowered the fun score. When i was playing this game i just chose a random character not paying attention becuase i like waiting for the fights to see who is a choosable character. When i saw snake i almost crapped my pants. that guy is awesome! and the toughest characters in the game would probobly be mega man and sonic/shadow but thats just me.
