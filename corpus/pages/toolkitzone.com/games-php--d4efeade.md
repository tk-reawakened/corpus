# The Horse Smell

June 9, 2006, 6:54 am
Development
The Horse Smell got its name because I'm sure every horseperson alive must love that smell when you walk into a barn. Well, I could think of no more worthy name for my new Horse Simulation game. In The Horse Smell, you can own and care for your own horse, as well as show it, exercise it, and more.
