This is an in-game shot. Obviously, when the game is polished, it will be much nicer than this, featuring a fairly professional interface laden with icons and such. For now, however, I have basic block letters and such, plus a black background. The black will usually be an organ or a body, though. Red = Current Vitals (heartbeat) Little S with blue streaks = Selected scalpal Blue = Time left in operation In the middle it shows me making an excision. You must hit all of the yellow dots to make it work. Right now it draws straight lines between each hit point, but soon it will follow the cursor pixel by pixel for the trail.
