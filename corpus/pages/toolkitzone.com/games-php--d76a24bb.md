# G.O.E.: From Petals to Dust

July 6, 2006, 2:24 pm
Design
Gears of Eternity: From Petals to Dust is my first project with RPG Toolkit. It follows the story of a young man named Omae, and delves into his personal feelings toward others, toward love, toward inner peace, and toward himself above all else. G.O.E. will pose the question: "Is it better to become a machine or to risk suffering loss through love?"
**G.O.E.: From Petals to Dust**
by Avedis **Script and Other News...**
July 6 2006, 1:24 pm
I've begun work on the script for the game, and it begins with a memory of Omae's, of losing someone dear to him, which triggers his fear of losing the lives of those around him.
