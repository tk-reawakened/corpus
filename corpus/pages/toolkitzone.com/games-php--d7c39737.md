# Destination Unknown (working)

June 15, 2006, 11:01 am
Development
Take control of Brian "Zeppo" Sydney, Zack Sydney and their crew of misfits as you try to uncover the truth about their childhood, defeat rival gangs and clans, take control of the mid-west, and become king of the seas. Using an art style similar to games such as Shining Force, Destination Unkown should be inviting to the older population of gamers. With major plot twists, romantic relations and some goofy personalities it is bound to be a joy ride for all RPG lovers.
