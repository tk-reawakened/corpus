# Treasure of Atmagia

September 13, 2005, 9:59 pm
Development
A midiEvil Studios production that heads off the Atmagian Tales, a series where each game features a different ancient Atmagian lore. Treasure of Atmagia is about an Elf and a Werewolf's quest to find the Legendary Treasure and protect the island country of Atmagia's Golden Age from entering darker times. Along the way they meet many memorable characters, good and bad, including a dragon that becomes the third main character.
