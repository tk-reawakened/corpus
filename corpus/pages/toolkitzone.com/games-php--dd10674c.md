# The Rising

May 13, 2005, 2:19 pm
Development
Jellyphant's first proper game. Based in a medieval period. A saboteur called Faller was sent into a rebellious faction and was betrayed, and forced to flee. He must return to the king, and kill the traitor.
**The Rising**
by Night_Elf **Magic - The Rising**
September 13 2004, 6:10 am
As some people know, getting the special attacks on The Rising to work has been quite a task! We couldn't equip magic from the menu (its a custom menu make on TK2) but we didn't want something lame like getting magic when you reached a level. We wanted it to be more flexible (or complex, make your mind up). So heres what we've decided. You collect power stones, but they are useless unless you draw power into them. There will be points in the game where you can put power into these stones, which will give you a new attack - but you can only hold so much power at once. Thats it in a nutshell, theres a few more bits and pieces, which I'll post when its ready.
