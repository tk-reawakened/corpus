July 7 2006, 3:49 am
Here is a shot of the battle system I am working on. So far, players can only do a basic attack and enemies have simple AI. However, it currently supports up to 9 enemies (or less with larger sizes) and it supports 4 players. It runs pretty smooth with minimal flickering, using canvases, and I have most of the important methods done, so the rest should be a piece of cake (except maybe the item and magic systems, because they have to be integrated with stuff that doesn't exist yet).
