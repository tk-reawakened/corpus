# NOTE:

February 18, 2005, 1:50 am
Demo
Godiva: Alpha is the first in a trilogy of games based on popular RPG's like Diablo, AD&D, Final Fantasy, and many more. It has a very expansive class and magic system, as well as a extremely large amount of weapons, armor, and other items to customize your character(s) with. Images and information of the game may or may not represent the current, or future demo, but were/are works in progress unless stated otherwise.
**NOTE:**
**Godiva: Alpha**
May 9 2004, 10:39 am
This is the 800x600 version of the in-game menu. It currently includes support for up to five player characters. The map with the green crosshairs is a functioning GPS map that shows your location on the current board. I may include a feature later on that shows your position in relation to the full world map as well; when you click a button. The layout of the map is temporary, and will probably be changed before the release of Demo 2. It will eventually support resolutions of 640x480, 800x600, as well as 1024x768 without having to stretch the images and reposition text. Higher resolutions may also support additional information displayed on the screen.
