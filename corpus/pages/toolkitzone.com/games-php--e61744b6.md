**Final Say**
**Said About**
June 8th, 2008 **Said On**
Ratings
Graphics
Average
The graphics were simple yet stylistic, a good attempt in my opinon.
Audio
Very good
I loved the sound effects, no music to speak of but some of the sound effects had me rolling!
Story
Decent
A decent altough simple story, it was well enough to keep me semi - interested till the end.
Fun
Very good
I had a lot of fun playing this game, the humor and all the little touches really added to the fun factor to the game.
Stability
Very good
Only miner glitches here and there, nothing serious.
Length
Decent
A decent length 20 min or so.
Thoughts
I really had a lot of fun with the game, there was a lot of little touches that really made it stand out. The actual game it self is very simple but it was the little things, the humor and the blob style, that made this one of the few toolkit games that I actually played long enough to beat. Oh and though 20 min really isn't that long, the story was very simple, along with the combat, I feel it it woulda lasted much longer I woulda started to get bored, but all in all I think it's a great game that every TK member should try. Sincerely, Data 1
