Ripped graphics and the inclusion of sound effects! So exciting! Generally okay.
Story
Bad
At least there was an introduction screen, but more could have been said. I would consider this to be Grindalf's weak point.
Fun
Very good
Wow, this was fun, and I actually beat it all the way through! Some balancing issues, and there are traps that the AI falls into, more later.
Stability
Very good
Occasion drawing glitches for the sprites and some flickering, but otherwise, killer!
Length
Average
It was rather short, but enjoyable. It ran its course quite well, given its simplicity.
Thoughts
Wow, after reading Ultima's say I was coming into this expecting mediocrity. Contrary to him, this ran VERY smooth and nice on my computer, considering its a 3 year old single core CPU. Very fun overall package, and I like the concept, being able to fight characters from different franchises. However, as far as gameplay goes, there needs to be some playbalancing. I played as Cecil, and most characters I put down REALLY fast, except for MegaMan (who was my first challenge), DK, and I think the TMNT character (Leo I think), and of course fighting myself in the end. All those characters had high HP ratings....you might want to work on balancing some more and release a patch. Also, if I get the AI cornered onto the side, I can pretty much obliterate them without taking damage, they jump right into my character and I attack before they have a chance to, then jump back and right back into my again.
