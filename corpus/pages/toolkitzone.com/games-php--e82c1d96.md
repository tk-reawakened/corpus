In an age gone past , Great Beasts of chaotic origin roamed the world of Legalia. Together the beasts wreaked havoc and destruction upon the world. Now new Heroes must take up the lost Lode weapons, restore their power, and defeat the beasts or Legalia will face utter destruction...
