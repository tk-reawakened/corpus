Development
A young woman awakens in the untamed forest world of Kalahan, unsure of her origin or purpose. Along with a band of fast friends, she'll embark on a mission of self-discovery, and indeed, discover the very nature of the cosmos.
**Illicit Soul**
by venstor **Rethinking Story Elements**
September 27 2005, 7:38 pm
Sometimes I get so bogged down in the little details of animating sprites and working terrain tiles that I fail to look at the big picture. I recently realized that there were a lot of contradictions and inconsistencies in the backstory of the Illicit Soul universe. Since I haven't released much information on the game world anyway, this isn't going to affect people's perception of the game, but it is something that needs to be worked out before I go much further writing the story. Cerna's origin and purpose have been the same since I started this project and will remain so, but other elements need rethinking. I'm talking the general relationships between the various races of human, the monsters, the spiritual entities, and the planets. (I"m not even sure how many planets are in the game universe yet. I'm waffling between six and three) Changing these things are going to have profound effects on the characters of Kaleen, Reinhart, and Katikal. I'm trying to decide whether to make a game page here explaining the game universe, or whether to keep the details to myself and reveal them through the events of the game.
