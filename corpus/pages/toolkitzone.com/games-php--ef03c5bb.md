I played through the demo more. Basically, I agree with your description, you need to test it (example: the first two chests in the first room are buggy). Also, the battle system really will be great in tk3, I'm seriously looking forward to see a little work done on it, and then to see it in action with true multi-tasking. What you have now is cool (although buggy, and tk2 is a bugger, it kept popping up an error 8 window), but in tk3 it could be awesome, and fun. The boards could use for a little sprucing up. Right now they're just big, confusing open areas (me talking about this is a little ironic), and the player just gets overwhelmed. A map would be nice, from the menu perhaps. And the menu is pretty cool, I took a look at the character one and I like how you can distribute your points like that, that's a great way of doing it. But I do think you should include some help files or have someone tell the player in-game a little bit about the menu. Also tell the player what keys to use (like m for menu, a lot of people wouldnt guess that, and the space bar for conversations). Just do more testing, and if you have time read through my article on tk gaming (tkgaming.net) about delivering polished products, because it could use for some polish. I'll definitely play your next release in tk3.
December 18 2004, 6:52 pm
Actually, I've already enacted most of your suggestions. Maps will be available when the inventory system is complete, the battle system and chests will be fixed in TK3, and I've already written up a help file, which will be released either with the TK3 version or with an upcoming patch.
