I'm still learning. The coloring looks bad when I'm lazy and just run over the flat colors with dodge and burn tools instead of taking the time to manually pick shades. (That's what I did on this piece) I do find that I'm elongating my characters more the more I draw them. In the beginning, I was basing the artwork off of the sprites, but the more I work in this style, the more I'm taking it away from that. Maybe I should just experiment with using full-on anime proportions, but I wanted to maintain at least a little bit of Western cartooniness.
