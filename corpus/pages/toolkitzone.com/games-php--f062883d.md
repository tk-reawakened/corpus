This is the first screenshot of the battle system in action. It's a little different than your normal RPG, and you can tell through the UI. Your character can switch weapons at any time during the battle (with real-time graphic changes) to best tackle the present threat. The mouse cursor disappeared when I took the screenshot, but that "67% 42" window pops up when the player cursors over an enemy to attack it. "67%" is the chance to hit the enemy, and "42" is the damage that will be done if the shot lands.

@Mars: Well, obviously the game is still in very early stages as far as balancing numbers like those go. But since you do seem interested, I will explain that a weapon's accuracy changes depending on the distance of the enemy to the player. So you can see that the player has a sniper rifle equipped - with that, accuracy increases drastically the further back the enemy is (for the shotgun, it's the exact opposite), so the accuracy for the two enemies in the back would be 80-90%, and the one way in the front would be 10-20%. So that's a big aspect of the gameplay - deciding on which enemies to kill with which guns and in what order.
