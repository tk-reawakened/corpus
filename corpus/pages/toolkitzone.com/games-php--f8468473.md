**The Dragoon**
by MrB **The latest**
January 29 2006, 3:50 pm
Just to keep anyone who wants to be updated, here's the latest on what's going on with Goon: The big change recently was that the game will be broken into quests. Before the game was planned to be basically like your typical traditional RPG where you follow a linear storyline. For a few reasons I decided to try something new, an idea I had kept changing my mind about since the end of last year. But I finally made a decision, so the game will in fact be broken into quests. Check out the 'Quest Log' screenshot if you haven't already, but these quests will be about an hour long on average. Each quests is in a different environment and has around 4 objectives that you'll have to achieve. There are also 'Special Items' that enemies randomly drop or are in some hard-to-find chests that are unique to each quests. In your quest long, there's a list of all the special items, and their names are exposed once you find them. 'Special Item' are similar to rare/unique/epic items found in online RPGs. Quests are also replayable if you want to go back and find any special items you didn't the first time or you want to run it again for quest rewards and experiences. One of the more unique aspects of Goon that is made possible by quests is that you can actually play any quest in the game at any time. Of course, if you're just beginning the game, you'll be easy targets for the later quests, but the option is there for different methods of playing as you progress through the game. As of now, I plan on the demo only featuring one quest, Tivian Forest. I may change my mind, but we'll see. I'm hoping for the demo to be done in a couple of weeks, so make sure you check back here to get your hands on the demo when it's out. :)
