Design
The Covenant was once not a great intergalactic empire, but just a group of religious zealots seeking the advanced technology of a disappeared race known simply as 'Forerunners'. This is the tale of a Gus'nak--one who would later be known as an Elite--who fought to create what is now known as the Covenant.* Second Age of Reclamation Covenant Holy City 'High Piety'/Step of Wisdom Sanctum of the Priests

> Old Jackal Energy Shield [Other Gallery](games.php?mode=images&view=5&game=1642)
December 11 2005, 11:45 pm
This is a pre-Covenant-Era Chiiang Energy shield (before they were known as 'Jackals' in the Covenant caste hierarchy.) These shields were very valuable to Chiiang forces in their struggle against the Covenant, as they manipulated plasma to be reflective via a polarization system. Sadly, they weren't very efficient, could only take 6 shots from even a Plasma Pistol, and more powerful Covenant weaponry could damage or destroy the protective gear.
