**Fuzzball 1 - Tales of Taint**
by Fuzzman **Charachters**
December 13 2005, 4:03 pm
So far, there are only two important charachters. With my advancment into the snowplain, however, this will soon change.
**Fuzzball**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1377/4039.JPG)
The hero of the game. Fuzz is a small, yellow rabbit thing that came from an attempt at drawing Kirby. He is able to survive with little or no water perpetually. Though, if he goes to long without water, he will shrivel.
Fuzz was roped into the quest because he could go without the water that others needed.
Fuzzball, is a laid back smart aleck. He occasionaly calls Redemption 'Red', wich annoys Redemption severely. When the quest begins, he doesn't know the magnitude of his quest, or what it could cost. He is forced to come to grips with this terrible reality midway through the quest, when the tainted water begins to take its toll...
**Redemption Seed**
![](http://www.toolkitzone.com/mygames/images/1377/4040.JPG)
Redemption was once a fuzz-creature, but he had been transformed into an evil beast that Fuzzball then slew. Redemption's spirit felt strongly the need to make up for his attack, and he was allowed to resurect in the form of a seed to aid Fuzzball on his quest.
Redemption and Fuzz struggle to get along throught the game. Their personalities just bounce off each other. Redemtion comments early in the game that he lived up north, but never says much about his past. He has a certain drive that Fuzz lacks, but just what causes that drive Fuzz has yet to learn.
