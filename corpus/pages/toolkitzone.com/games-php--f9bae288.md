Design
Set in the world of Eph Landra is the story of a 19 year old peasent who lost his father at a young age, due to a war. Out of despair Dash joins a mysterious group and embarks on an adventure of the ages.

There are a lot of good things about this board and a lot of bad. PROS: Nice choice of pastel-based colors. A realistically large fountain, something most RPGs nowadays neglect. Quaint-looking houses. CONS: Noticably tiled in some places while not in others; most prominent on tall grass and roofs. Ripped character - probably temporary but still far too out-of-place; should be changed quickly. Colored lines are good but these are too light; the statue is particularly badly done. Sort of chunky-looking shadows. They'd look better hand-drawn and smoothed out.
