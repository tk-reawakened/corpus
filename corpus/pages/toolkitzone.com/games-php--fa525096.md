**Final Say**
**Said About**
October 25th, 2008 **Said On**
Ratings
Graphics
Decent
Good style and variety in the graphics, but not too original or inspiring.
Audio
Decent
The music was good, though the sudden starts, stops, and repeats were annoying (should have used FMod). Also the lack of sfx is apparent.
Story
Average
From what I could tell, average story (read below for why I didn't finish it)
Fun
Decent
The different quests and open-ended nature was pretty cool, but running into the same bug (and the constricting nature of the save system) was annoying
Stability
Decent
Many drawing glitches in the battle system, and the collision bug made me quit the game after three times of running into it.
Length
Good
Hard to gauge as I did not finish it, but judging from the amount of quests people were talking about, it seemed of decent length.
Thoughts
This is a potentially great game marred by stupid little bugs (and I'm not talking about Colony RPG, though apparently both these games are held to different standards from the attitudes relating to unfixable engine bugs). The intro was lengthy but interesting, and the town seemed typical but full of content. There were several quests available that seemed lengthy and interesting. My first complaint is the saving. You can only save at the inn. I hate this aspect of JRPGs, it annoys me to no end. It probably wouldn't annoy me as much as if I'm deep into the forest hunting for hides and I run into that damn collision bug that forces me to start all over on my hunt. After 3 times I just gave up. Khin, you need to update this to 3.1.0. The battle system offered an interesting new perspective, though was reminiscent of the original Final Fantasy where you selected all your attacks at the same time, and if the enemy died before you can make your move, oh well, too late. There were lots of drawing bugs and even some programming bugs. If one of the characters were knocked out, the enemy stopped attacking completely. Also, all the canvasing drawing bugs seemed avoidable. The battle system also seemed weirdly balanced. Characters would most deal 1 damage but would randomly deal an absurd amount of damage. This was most apparent with Serge, the apparent wizard, who whenever made an attack that dealt a hefty damage value, would be way ahead of Ross or the dwarf. I am very critical of this game because it has professionalism to it, it has the potential to be a great game, not just a great Toolkit game. An update to 3.1.0 could more than likely repeal the collision bug, and a content/balance update for the battle system would improve the score even more.
