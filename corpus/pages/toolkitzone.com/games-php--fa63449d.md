H armfull monsters threaten that of the world of Aganna. Human kind has built boundaries to that with the army of Antique-Aggana. No matter the number of soldiers that are put into the army though, they can only hold the creatures back from taking control over Aganna. The only ones the army of Aganna can really look towards and pray for though, is the ancient race of a being made through ritual called the "Red Momento" ritual. These being created were named the "Antiques"...
T he Antiques were beings created through a ritual which required a sample of a male and a female's blood and momento charished by the male. Once the parents both pass away, the Antique was then born, along as the fathers momento and it's powers. The Antiques of the past were nothing but human weapons though, used to conquer land. The Antiques were born with amazing abilities, a fine example was being able to control their powers and emotions at all times. Antiques were always the firsts to advance in anything at an outstanding time to that of a normal being. With this and more, they became used as weapons...Eventually Human kind realized what the antiques could do to them and their lands, so killed them off and banned the ritual. There still those who still performed the ritual though. Even after the war. Their actions against the law would soon be all worth it though, and answer Aganna's prayers soon enough... *were*
