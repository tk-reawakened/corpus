Thoughts
This is not a bad game to download if your a fan of puzzle games. It took me a little while to actually figure out how to play. Even with the instructions before the game. There was some Stability problems. Sometimes when I would be moving my triangle it would get stuck moving in the direction that I pressed the key. It got very annoying after awhile. But it didn't do this the entire game just seemed to happen to me randomly. But like I said before this isn't a bad little demo to download and play. It really shows off some things that the TK can do outside of the RPG area.
