A 'Best of' compilation of tracks from the first two specially chosen to fit best into games, mainly as Dungeon or battle music. Quite nice, but a little lazy - not worth the download if you have the other two packs, but for others its pretty good

Quite a large collection of metal midis here (31) - some notably better than others - most of them dungeon/battle inspiring tunes. The midi format give the metal guitar a wierd sound in some of them and a few arent that great, hence the score - really I want to be able to give this a 3.7 rating - as its still well worth a download
