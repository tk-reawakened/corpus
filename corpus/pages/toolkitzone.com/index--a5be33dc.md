# filters

submitted by on 20 July, 2004 [TKGB](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=2451)
TKGB adds to his alternative font methods with this tileset concept, allowing for gradients, borders and other little extras. Limitations are obvious; you're stuck with the size of tiles, however it's still a useful package and could be put to good use. As with Gifont, which uses bitmaps for fonts, this comes will all commands fully explained in the help document.
submitted by on 20 July, 2004 [TKGB](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=2451)
Simple but impressive idea TKGB came up with here - bitmap fonts. Useful for lots of things, and he makes sure it's simple to use with detailed explanations. It just needs a nice little speed boost. The updated version comes with a small bug fix.
submitted by on 16 July, 2004 [TKGB](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=2451)
These are enemies and bosses taken from Willow, ripped by TKGB from the test menu. Not the greatest quality due to 16x16 format - but have a cool look to them. Also included are the enemy files for bosses. The tiles are actually still in bitmap form, to convert them as you wish using the Toolkit's tile grabber.
submitted by on 08 July, 2004 [TKGB](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=2451)
Set from Capcom's 1980s game Willow, based off the film. Ripped by TKGB, this is a nice retro-16x16 tiles - but probably best suited to a side-scroller or a game that makes good use of paralaxing. Includes waterfalls and forests, the screenshot provided is an example of the tiles in the actual game.
