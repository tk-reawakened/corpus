# About Us

Creation
The website was created in May 2000 by Xavier, then called Vampz, as a home primarily for RPG Toolkit resources. It fast became the most popular resource site and benefited immensely from its only other staff member being Triuken, a prolific tileset ripper. As the months went by new staff were brought in to help manage the growing content, most notably the now almost legendary MrG for anything rpg code related and Seafroggys for game reviews.
Closure
In April of 2002 the site abruptly closed down when Xavier decided he wanted to leave the RPG Toolkit community. MrG went on to create a new RPG Code resource site called the Code Shack using the wide array of files he had archived during his time with TKZ.
Rebirth
The site made a comeback in December 2003 but this time was developed with PHP, allowing for more extensive and flexible features. Again, Xavier was at the helm and was joined by staff members such as Dan and Hablethorn. It quickly re-established itself as the leading resource site once the then long departed Triuken was track down and able to re-submit his old TKZ tilesets.
Evolution
In February 2004 the Toolkit Zone was announced as the new official home of the RPG Toolkit by developer CBM. The move caused some initial unrest within the community as the PHPBB forums used by TKZ were a far cry from the comfortable and long-standing threaded forums on the original site. Over the course of several months the site began to expand with the addition of a games section, blogs and a library.
Re-Code
After nearly two years of constantly tweaking the site, the design and code was messy and brittle so the decision was taken by Xavier to completely re-code the website. The process took 13 months to complete as it was developed as a side project and never made public as to not disappoint should it not come to fruition. On November 30th 2006 the site launched sporting a radical new layout and many new features.
