# About Sound Effects

submitted by on 10 November, 2004 [TK Game Boy](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=304)
Set of 22 sound effects from Capcom's Carrier Air Wing game, first pack of two. Not suprisingly all have a somewhat militeristic/computer feel to them.
Very 'beepy' - BBC-esque synthesised sounds - maybe useful for a cheesy space style game - they sound very retro
submitted by on 02 April, 2004 [JM](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=385)
Very small pack of effects, most of which would be suited for battles when fighting some slime ball of a monster.
submitted by on 16 November, 2004 [TK Game Boy](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=304)
Rather odd set of voice clips consisting of the word "Question" and then different clips for each number. Not the most relevant set of sound effects ever, but if you're making a quiz game or mini-game - perfect! TKGB has also provided a little example program to play the relevant sound file after user input of a number.
submitted by on 22 May, 2004 [ViperCat](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=203)
Some fairly average effects here - only 8, two of which are midis. Theres the usual level up effect, as well as the more obscure Inn effect. If any of you have a pet bat, I hope it doesnt sound like the one in here!!
submitted by on 02 December, 2004 [jcb020790](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=4435)
I moved this to the effects section as there aren't really many songs in here - more sound effects - they are however all MIDIs. Whilst there are a few sounds that may be useful, there are some where it sound like someone has had a midi keyboard, got cross with it and thrown it down the stairs, whilst recording from it. When the saints go marching in is particularly badly played. A strange pack - the mario doorbell tune is interesting, thats about it
