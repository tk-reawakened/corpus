# About Us

Christopher B. Matthews
The original mind behind the Toolkit and was the sole developer from 1997 through to 2003 when the software went open source, you can read Chris' personal account of those six years and the progression of the Toolkit [here](index.php?about&mode=toolkit) . Commonly referred to as CBM, in recent years he has taken a back seat to development and allows the new open source contributors to do what they feel is best for the software.
