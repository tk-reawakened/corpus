# About Utilities

submitted by on 02 August, 2004 [Andreaz](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=3346)
Robust tileset editor, allowing you to manage your sets. Main features include auto-resizing to fit window, tile movement/deletion, export/batch export, merge multiple sets and other nice things. Definately the most complete external tileset editor out there - and getting better every release! Good work, Andreas!
submitted by on 13 October, 2004 [Shao](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=4177)
For ripping AutoTiles, which will be supported in the next TK3 release. AutoTiles are tilesets with tiles arranged in a certain preset, meaning when you apply them to your board it automatically draws them in a certain manner. Converts AutoTiles from RPG Maker XP, RPG Maker 2000/2003, Sim RPG Maker, and prearranged AutoTileset images, including some RPG Maker 95 tilesets, to tk3 compatible AutoTilesets.
submitted by on 26 October, 2004 [matt72186](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=31)
Script that will convert a specified Toolkit tileset into an image for your website. You can specify width of the images as well as start and end tiles. Image file types supported are gif, jpeg and png.
Great tileset editor that comes with features such as the ability to convert a tileset to bitmap, merge two tilesets and has its own editor to delete/duplicate tiles and various other functions. Also comes with Visual Basic 6 source code.
