# About Utilities

submitted by on 26 April, 2005 [Renegade](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=5471)
A midi creation tool with a slightly different edge- its more based around the theory of music composition than most others. Its well worth the download particularly for people new to music as it has some good help files to explain a bit about music theory, which should help no end. As for the tool itself, I didn't like the interface at all its a graphical interface using the mouse and i didnt like it at all. That said, its not a run of the mill piece of crap, and I recommend the download.
submitted by on 04 August, 2004 [Colosseus](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=718)
Another improved release, this updated music player plays mp3s, wavs and midis (and possibly other things), has repeat and random play features and a nice looking interface. Improvements include bug fixes and a new playlist feature - This version wants a 3.5 rating, but Im not yet giving it a 4 - keep your eyes peeled for 1.4, that sounds very promising
submitted by on 21 March, 2004 [Colosseus](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=718)
It's a simple enough player with the obvious play, pause, stop buttons. Also allows you to edit ID3 tags and eject the current audio CD. Unfortunately, you can only play a track at a time as opposed to a playlist or a selection of tracks.
