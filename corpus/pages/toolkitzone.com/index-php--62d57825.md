submitted by on 15 February, 2006 [Oyerth](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=37)
Plugin from Oyerth that introduces a whole array of fantastic new audio capabilities for the Toolkit. Supporting a total of 22 file types, this plugin provides 24 audio related commands allowing you to, in short, play multiple sounds at the same time, group sounds, apply audio effects, fade outs, individual volumes and pausing. It also comes complete with documentation providing summaries of all commands to get you started. However melodramatic I may sound, this really does revolutionise the audio prowess of the Toolkit. : Two new commands for pitch and tempo, fixed bug with fading a channel and reduced plugin size.
