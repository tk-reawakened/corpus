# About Collections

submitted by on 04 January, 2004 [Xavier](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=6)
16x16 TileSet from ASCII's RPGMaker 2000. As with all 16x16 TileSets, its not very impressive visually.
submitted by on 08 July, 2004 [TKGB](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=2451)
Set from Capcom's 1980s game Willow, based off the film. Ripped by TKGB, this is a nice retro-16x16 tiles - but probably best suited to a side-scroller or a game that makes good use of paralaxing. Includes waterfalls and forests, the screenshot provided is an example of the tiles in the actual game.
submitted by on 13 June, 2004 [Symphonia](forums/profile.php?mode=viewprofile&u=1989)
Collection of enemy files with their tilesets from Meowy's unique looking monsters, as well as some objects and outdoor tiles - although I have no idea what the file title stands for. Might be hard to fit into your games graphical style, but nonetheless it's a nice ready-to-go package for those who want it.
