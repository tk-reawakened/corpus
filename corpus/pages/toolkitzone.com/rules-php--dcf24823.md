# password

![](img/folder_open.gif) zone account
**password**
These are the TKZ rules. If you don't agree with them, you can leave or refuse to register an account. Upon breaking a rule you might receive a warning, but this is not mandatory. You can be banned for your first offense of breaking these rules. When the term "ban" is used we do not mean forever! Bans may range from a single day to two weeks, and is up to the discretion of the moderators. If you receive a ban, take is maturely and learn from the lesson. There are two huge rules that you simply must follow. Breaking these rules is an automatic ban. No warning, nothing. You will most likely get the maximum ban (currently two weeks) for breaking these simple rules. a) Do NOT post pornographic material or any "questionable" material on the TKZ forums; if you would be uncomfortable or get into trouble for viewing it at work or at school, do not post it here. Posting hand-drawn art on the forum which you fear may fall into this category will be down to the discretion of the moderator. If you are unsure, contact a mod first. b) No hateful or racist remarks. Using trickery or any other shenanigans to get around this is bannable. Over-use of the word gay or any other homosexual put down is also prohibited. Before making a topic, make sure you are going to be posting in the correct forum. Although moderators have tools to move you topics, it eases our job if you check yourself. It should be pretty clear where to post, each forum has a description. When making a topic in the general forum, try to keep it somewhat Toolkit related, too. We don't need to know what you ate for breakfast, etc. Give your topic a good title! This one is often overlooked, and it's amazing how many more replies you'll get with a relevant title. Before posting a topic, please ask yourself the following question: "Am I making a post which is either funny, informative, or interesting on any level?" If you can answer "yes" to this, then please post. If you cannot, then refrain from posting. Before replying, please ask yourself the following question: "Does my reply offer any significant advice or help contribute to the conversation in any fashion?" If you can answer "yes" to this, then please reply. If you cannot, then refrain from replying. Your post or reply may be deleted and you might get banned. If you feel a topic is spam, DO NOT REPLY to it stating so. Simply ignore it or maybe PM a moderator about it. By replying, you only help keep the spam active. If a thread of yours has been locked or deleted, it has been locked or deleted for a reason. Complaining about this on the forums will probably get you banned. If you have a problem, contact an admin or a mod via PM. Don't reply to your own reply if you forget to add something, ie: post two consecutive posts. Instead, edit the initial reply. In such cases, the second post may be deleted. Gimmick posters will be banned, and if you have some joke to tell or funny story keep it to the off-topic forum only. Do not repeatedly advertise! If TKZ users didn't like your site the first time you posted it, chances are they'll like it even less the second time around! If you've made great improvements, at least reply to your original topic! Don't go starting a whole new topic. Sharing of piracy information is NOT allowed and will result in a ban. If you are new to the forums, always lurk before posting. This will give you a good idea of what is acceptable and unacceptable posting practice. It will also give you the chance to observe how you can avoid annoying off individual moderators, since some mods find certain infractions more offensive than others. If you complain about what you feel are shortcomings of the current moderation team, the topic will most likely be deleted. Keep it to PMs or e-mails with the moderators. Sometimes flame wars are fun to watch, but usually it's just annoying. Try to keep fighting to a minimum. Don't follow another user around from thread to thread saying "you suck." We will ban anybody attempting to circumvent any rule in a way that they obviously feel is remarkably clever and witty. CAPS: Most people are getting fed up with all-cap posts. It's not allowed in subjects or topics. Impersonating TKZ staff members or any other regulars is prohibited. This means don't create another account and use a capital I instead of a lowercase l or anything like that. We'll ban the imp account and your main one as well. Make sure your posts add to the discussion. Don't just go into a topic and post something like "What?" or "I don't care." Try to make your posts longer than one or two words. If the moderators deem that you are posting just to increase your post-count, you will have your posts deleted and even receive a ban. The Toolkit community is a friendly and warm place, but it still needs rules. We just want people to use common sense. If you don't have something interesting or helpful to discuss, don't post a topic! General Overview
