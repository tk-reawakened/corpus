# About RPG Toolkit

The RPG Toolkit is an open source role-playing game maker, although more than just RPGs have been produced with it due to it's robust nature. Sporting its own scripting language with both simple and advanced functionality to suit users of all coding abilities and easy to use editor, you can create a variety of styles not just limited to simple 2D.
The Toolkit has been under constant development since 1997. From that time, it has undergone three massive versions, and changed from one man's hobby to an open source project. Many sites are devoted the system, and a tightly-knit community has been born.
: Toolkit 3.x
Latest version of the Toolkit, now developed as an open source project by community members. Major enhancements include isometrics, canvases and object-orientated support to name but a few.
