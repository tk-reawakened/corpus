# Staff Members

The site relies on volunteer help from members of the community to help manage resources, games, documents and the general day to day running. Below are the good people involved in doing just that.
>
> [sound resources] **Doc Wellsy**
>
> Long standing member of the Toolkit community, the well respected doctor reviews and manages all sound resources sent into the site.
>
> [game moderator] **Oblivious_Casualty**
>
> Was part of the moderating team when in charge of the now dead isometrics board, and rejoined the team to help keep track of the game section. Actively works on the popular game Bounty.
>
> [code resources] **Oyerth**
>
> Member of InDream Studios, Oyerth handles all coding resources and also heads up the popular Expo event for Toolkit games.
>
> [graphics resources] **RDemusX**
>
> Working on one of the most aniticipated projects around, Mansions of the Moon, RDX casts his knowing eye over the graphic related files submitted by the community.
>
> [webmaster] **Xavier**
>
> First created and designed the initial ToolkitZone way back in April 2000 and has been webmaster ever since. Currently a Computer Science student at University and supposedly head of the multi-million GDP empire. May actually make a TK game some day...maybe not...
