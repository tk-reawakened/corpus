# About ToolkitZone.com

The site was first created in May 2000 as a home for Toolkit resources as well as game reviews. Initially it had a staff of two, Xavier (then Vampz) and Triuken. Triuken was a prolific tileset ripper and added a dozens upon dozens to our collection. As the months went by new staff were brough in to help manage the growing content, most notably MrG for anything rpg code and Seafroggys for his game reviews. The site closed just short of its two year anniversary in April 2002. The site made a comeback in December 2003, and this time was far more extensive than ever before after being coded in PHP. Again Xavier was at the helm, and soon he brought in new staff once more. In February 2004, the site was announced as the new official Toolkit home by creator CBM and the transition from mere resource site to full blown community site began.
