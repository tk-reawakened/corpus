The sprite concept is "Superhero", you know the drill... a human with some form of super power, saving the world one bad guy at a time. Remember to be unique - it's your superhero! The look and feel of the sprite is completely down to the user but people will be voting not just on how good it looks but relevance to the concept. Remember, the sprite must be 32x64 (that's 32 pixels wide, 64 pixels high), be a full body sprite (head to toes) and must be front-on. The use of in-hand objects is allowed.
