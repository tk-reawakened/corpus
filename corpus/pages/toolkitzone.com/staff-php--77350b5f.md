**toolkitzone.com staff**
Vampz - [webmaster]
The site was first born in May 2000 by Vampz, who has been at the helm ever since. Although very keen on the community, has never released a game but vows to one day finish one of his many ideas...honest!
Hablethorn - [graphics manager]
Hablethorn was the first staff member to join the site after TKZ was re-launched and has made an instant impact. A great knowledge of both web development has meant the site has another enthusiast behind it to make it even better.
John@Aljo - [rpg code manager]
John came back to the community about the same time TKZ came back, and was immediately snapped up to help manage the rpg code section. Has released various plugins and methods to improve our Toolkit experience.
Doc_Wellsy - [sounds manager]
The Doc has been around for a while now and is a well rounded and respected individual within the community. He's our newest member, replacing Randy as our sounds manager.
Dan - [games manager]
Danny boy will be heading up the forthcoming games section. Dan has been around for a while and ran TK-X, one of the many resource sites that attempted to fill the void TKZ left when it closed in April 2002.
Seafroggys - [writer]
Other than Vampz, Seafroggys is the only staff member to return from the original team. A much respect game reviewer, he will be heading up various articles and features for the site.
