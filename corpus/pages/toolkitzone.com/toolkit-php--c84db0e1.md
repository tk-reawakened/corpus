# Documentation

4. Playing
After you've created your game—and hopefully, many, many times during the process—you'll want to try it out on the toolkit's game engine. When your product is completely polished, you'll need to deliver it to its audience in an effective, professional manner. This section of the manual covers the trans3 engine, as well as how you can distribute your game.
Please choose what you would like to learn more about.
