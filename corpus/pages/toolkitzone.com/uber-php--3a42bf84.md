# über membership

**Welcome!**
Hello Community Member, Firstly, let me thank you for visiting this section to find out more about über membership, regardless of whether or not you end up becoming part of the scheme. This website has grown immeasurably over the months since becoming the new home of the Toolkit, and as a result the costs for keeping it online, functioning and expanding have also increased. To help keep this site online, über membership offers a variety of rewards for those who donate to the site since the Toolkit does not make a profit of any sort to help pay. At time of writing, seven members have helped keep this community going by becoming über members. Their money has helped pay for an extra domain name for users to choose from, extending the ToolkitZone.com domain by another two years and of course contributed to the monthly costs for the site. Within these few pages you can find all the details of what this special user status is all about, what you get in return and the information you need to ask for an upgrade. Even if you decide against über membership or simply cannot afford it, I still appreciate you taking the time out of your online day to explore the possibility. Thank you, I'll see you on the forums!
