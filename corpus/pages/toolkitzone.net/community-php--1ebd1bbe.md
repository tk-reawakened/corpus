# Contests

Current Contest
Every year the community is invited to write their own short story that revolves around three specified plot points. The stories are then voted upon by the community to form a shortlist for an invited judge to decide a winner from. April 1st - May 14th

The titans of pixel art clash heads in the first ever knockout competition; Sprite Off. Contestants are randomly drawn against eachother and given a sprite concept, the contestant with the most votes advances to the next round. [Sprite Off](community.php?contest=7)

A unique team contest whereby users work together to complete an audio, a coding and a graphics task. The entries are voted on and the eventual winner is the team which is the most successful over all three categories. [Toolkit Triathlon](community.php?contest=6)
