A idea diary
If your like me, you have many wonderful game ideas in your head that you want to make a game out of but in reality, you can't cuz you don't have enough time to do them all. Even if you do manage to start it, it might not get finished because even though it sounds god on your head, it doesn't translate the way you wanted it to. That's why I thought of this: Log every idea you have in your head. That way when you get around to starting it, you'll have a good better idea how it goes and therefore judge whether it is a good idea or not. And even if you never get around to it, at least you have it written down somewhere so the idea is getting out at least. If your thinking that this is the same as planning a game, no, do not mean that. You could call it that but what I have in mind is to just write anything you have thought of for the game you have in your mind whenever it pops in so as to log it for either one day not forgetting it when you start your project or just so it could not be forgotten in general. I don't know if you get what I mean but the overall concept is to write down ideas somewhere to either pick it up later or just to have it written. Does this sound like a good idea?
