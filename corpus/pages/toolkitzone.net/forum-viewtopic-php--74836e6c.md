# - Jeffrey Sinclar

Fishing for Ideas
So Brad has inspired me to make a small 2-3 hour game. I definitally want to do some sort of tactical turn based game. I've already developed the basis for such a game mechanic (having developed it for Colony RPG), I just need to figure out the actual game. I think a cool little game would be your squad boards an enemy ship and you have to capture it. You could be pirates, enemy organization, mercenaries, whatever. Capturing certain ship components can yield different results (for example, having access to the security network will allow you to lock and unlock doors). Any other thoughts or ideas? Btw Brad I'll start working on the boards tomorrow
