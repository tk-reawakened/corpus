1 say @ 23%, game not ranked as it requires at least five says.
Game developed by and released on September 12th 2010 [brenthenry](community.php?user=18678)
And here I thought we were pass the era of bad gaming. Well, we kinda are but sometimes a game comes along and suprises us that people are still making awful games. Worst off, this isn't from some n00b. Nope. It's from brenthenry. WTF? First let's start with the graphics: They're awful! You are bamboozled by the title screen, which looks decent, but once the game starts, it vomits into your face. Everything is n00bish MS Paint. The tiles are just blocks of colors with a few dots and strangely enough, diamonds are placed in a pattern. Kinda like a kitchen counter. But that's not even the worst part. It's isometric. You can tell these tiles weren't meant to be isometric. They're just awful. The other graphics are the battle scenes. Sloppy MS Paint with the thickest brush used. Not good at all. Next, let's talk about the programming: The only programming that is decent is the battle system. Just click the enemy until it dies. If you don't click fast enough, the enemy will kill you. Easy as that. It's just like RPGADVETUUUUUUUUURE or whatever it was called where you had to tap the A and S key fast enough. Other than that, just Mwin's. As for the story: Whatever it is, it's just convoluted. When you start the game, you're just on the field (if you can even call it a field), then you talk to this old guy who just munches (or are you the one that's munching) and are sent to some strange place. Then after trying to figure out how to get to your next destination thanks to the awful controls (I'll explain that next), you see a king, but the king is being attacked by some kind of terror teddy. Well after killing the monstrous teddy bear, you found out he is Prince Bob and he has been expecting you, so you gotta meet him at Fort Larking for some strange reason. After walking a bit more--AAAAAAAAHHHHH screw this. This game's story just drones on and on for no reason at all. There's no reason why you are doing this. In fact, who are you in this game. This game sucks in he story department. The only reason why it doesn't get a 1 is because at least there is a story, as crappy as it may be. Music: No. Problems: Besides the awful graphics, yeah. It's the controls. I can't control the character right because it's on a isometric view. This is the first TK game where the controls are an issue, which is a mystery because the TK has also given you the controls, default as they may be, but no one ever complained. How the heck do you ruin the controls from something that doesn't require you to input? The controls in this game are awful. Going down actually sends you south-east, up sends you north-west, left takes you north-east, and and right takes you south-east. You may think that you'll get used to them, but no, you don't, because they're just so stiff. Everytime you press the down button, you're gonna hope that it goes down but it will only go down if it hits a wall because of the TK's smart walk feature. Speaking of walls, what's up with the layout? It's hard to tell what's a wall or not. You may look at it and see it clearly what's a wall or not, but no, sometimes you can pass a specific area, even though it looks like you can't. But then again, the controls are just so awful, you won't be able to move about freely as you wish to figure out how to get from point A to point B. OI! I couldn't finish this game mainly because the controls are just so terrible. Not to mention, when you die, you start all the way from the begining. No save feature, not even menus. Though the battle system seems simple enough but I was careless and placed the cursor away from the game screen because I hate seeing the cursor in the middle of the game, but you gotta have it there because as soon as the battle appears, you're gonna have to click it as fast as you can, because if you are not fast enough, it's game over. My verdict: Don't download this game. You're better off playing RPGADVENTUUUUUUUUUUUUUURE.
