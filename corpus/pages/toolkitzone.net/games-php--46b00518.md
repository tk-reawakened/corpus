Thoughts
First, I must say that this game demo is one of the best I have played on TKZ. I started smiling before I ever began a new game, and didn't stop smiling until it was over with. (except for a few moments that I almost jumped out of my computer chair!) PROS: It was very embracing, and captured your attention like no other game on TKZ. The music entrances you, and lulls you into a sense of complacency, then dumps a barrage of images and scary sound on top of you. Certainly not a game I would want to play with the lights off! It is very involved, with the little puzzle with the lights. The information given isn't enough to overload the player, but just enough to keep them interested in what comes next. The superb graphics make the walk through this awesome game even more enjoyable. Programming wise, this is a feat, with the custom systems, and visual effects. CONS: The length is a bit short for my taste. Maybe that was what 7e was attempting to do... to not expose too much upfront. Either way, I would have enjoyed it more if it was longer. That also brings me to the story, which leaves much to be desired. You know almost nothing more, by the end of the game, then you knew at the start screen. But once again, that may be work out to be an advantage of 7e to flesh the story or change it as much as he deems necessary, until the full release. The stability of the game is excellent on XP, but on Vista it is a spotty installation. That is probably a combination of the programming and the TK Engine. CONCLUSION: Really a phenomenal demo, living up to all my expectations, and more. Other than a couple random complaints, most related to my own preferences, this demo deserves top honors! If you haven't downloaded it yet, you need to.TBD
