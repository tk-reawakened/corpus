A second shot of the equipment menu. This shows the process of selecting an item that you want to replace with a newly equipped item. Or just to check out your party's equipment. You scroll the grid using the arrow keys, and if you sit still on an item for about a second, the dark bubble will come filled with wonderful stats on that item. Press an arrow key, and that bubble disappears and you're off running again. Press Enter, and some items pop up down at the bottom that you can equip to replace to selected item (illustrated better in the Equipment 1 screenshot).
