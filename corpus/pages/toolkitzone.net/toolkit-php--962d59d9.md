# Browse

What is the RPG Toolkit?
The RPG Toolkit is a free, open source project delivering a simple, flexible and powerful tool for the creation of role-playing games on your personal computer. The Toolkit has various easy to use tools that allow you to put together enemies, special moves and much more with a few clicks of the button. With its own programming language, RPG Code, you can build unique and personalised game features that make your game stand out.
