# Daemon Calibre is created and maintained by TsunamiRPG. Made with use of the RPGtoolkit™

**9th August 2002 - Whats the story?**
- (whats the story?) Moring glory. Again a new site. New layout, new design, new game...the works! Sorry for the lack of updates recently, but work on the rpg has taken priority as this is the holiday.
**30th July 2002 - Don't go changing.....**
- Lots of changes occur in the life cycle of a RPG. New concepts and designs are a weekly occurrence. One of the changes happening in Daemon Calibre is the fatigue system. After an amount of time, the player will become tired and unresponsive. This can be a fatal problem in certain situations.
**26th July 2002 - Fearful Symmetry**
- A far to frequent situation - An internal error resulted in my hardrive's memory being erased. Unfortunately, stored within that memory was my RPG. Using this as a chance of a fresh start work has commenced on a new game with a new genre. A new survival horror RPG. Daemon Calibre
