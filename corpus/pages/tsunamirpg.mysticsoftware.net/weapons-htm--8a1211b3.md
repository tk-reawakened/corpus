**Ingra 9mm Custom handgun**
The Ingra Custom handguns are legendary. Their efficiency in killing daemons is unsurpassed by any other weapon of this calibre.
Established 2 years after the Namess meteor collided with the Earth, the Ingra weaponry company has produced guns of the highest quality. Guns of a quality so high in fact that the Ingra 9mm custom handgun is now standard issue for all members of the I.D.E.C ( International Daemon Elimination and cleansing Corporation ).
Fitted with a suppressor and laser-scope as standard, the Ingra 9mm custom handgun is one of the most powerful weapons available. The clips are specially calibrated to hold the Tironium / Carbonic alloy bullets, yet are adaptable to hold the standard titanium bullets. However, in doing so the air speed velocity of the bullets fired drops from 16 times the speed of sound to 8 times. This means that stronger daemons may take more hits to go down.
**Ingra-Magneto Pulse Rifle**
The Ingra-Mgneto pulse rifle is one of the most feared weapons in the world. Their stealth and efficiency makes them the optimal weapon to use in a multi-daemon confrontation
The Namess meteor crash in the Middle East introduced the world to a new element, Tironium. This element is now the standard material for use in the construction of firearms. It was found that when combined with copper, Tironium could be converted into an electrical conductor that had virtually no resistance, regardless of the size of the piece of material.
The Ingra-Magneto Pulse rifle was the first production born of a revolution in the way weaponry is constructed. The barrel is lined with two strips of Copper / Tironium alloy. By passing an electric current through these strips, an electromagnetic field can be induced. This means that a magnetic bullet could travel along the length of the barrel of the gun at the same speed as the current passing through the strips. By this means, a single bullet could be fired at just under the speed of light.
Combined with the technology used in the common machine gun, the Ingra-Magneto Pulse rifle has become the worlds first rapid fire, electromagnetic weapon. However, although the weapon has got a rapid fire capability, very often one hit is enough to kill most Daemons.
**Ingra Light Cannon**
The Ingra Light Cannon was first produced in the year 2031, four years after the Namess Meteor crash.
When heated, Tironium emits light of an incredibly high frequency. The Ingra light cannon focuses the light into a single beam, less then one molecule in size. When fired at an object or being it passes straight through it, severing parts of the body. The beam is so small that a being can remain alive even after their body has been severed several times. However, the slightest movement can cause the severed parts of their body to slide apart, causing instant death.
Initially created to ward daemons away from the cities, congress meetings between the highest ranking military officers has meant that Sub-Light Cannons are now issued to I.D.E.C soldiers for use in desert reconnaissance and daemon cleansing missions.
**Ingra ZX290**
The Ingra ZX290 is the first semi-automatic explosive rifle to use Tironium in it's design.
The Ingra ZX290 is a pneumatic, semi-automatic rifle that fires explosive shells at an extremely high velocity. Designed to embed into the object or being they are fired at, the shells explode causing great damage to the target.
In it's natural state, Tironium is combined with many other minerals to form Tirosite. When subjected to high pressures, Tirosite morphs into Nonosite. A highly explosive material which is a liquid at room temperature. An electrical charge passed through the Nonosite will cause combustion and eventual explosion of the material.
The shells fired by the Ingra ZX290 are actually a switch like mechanism. When fired at a target, the frontal, inner shell is forced backwards, receding into the outer shell. This completes a circuit within the shell causing a current to flow through the Nonosite, and thus explosion of the shell and often death to the target.
**Twin Katana Blades**
A Tironium Katana blade is a devastatingly powerful and useful ally in a daemon attack.
For years, guns and projectile weapons seemed the strongest defense against Daemons. However, during the fifth daemon cleansing mission in 2034, an entire squad was annihilated.
An investigation in to this travesty was conducted. It appeared that every member of the 2-O-5 squadron had emptied an entire clip firing at the daemons, yet not a single daemon body was found.
A badly injured, surviving daemon was found, hunched in the corner of the room. Out of pure hatred, private John Mathews began to fire at the daemon. With every bullet that pierced it's flesh the daemon seemed to become stronger. It soon had enough energy to stand and launch an attack on the investigators. John Mathews' soul was ripped from his body, and he fell to his knees in absolute agony before he died.
Bullets began to rain from the skies at the daemon creature, yet it just became stronger and stronger.
It wiped out all but one of the investigators. The lone survivor, Private Candalaria Sandriguez, reached out and clasped her hands on the one thing that would prove to be her salvation.
Swinging with all her might, the axe that she held in her hands severed the head of the daemon. It fell to the ground and it's soul fled the body, only to dissipate shortly after.
After this event, Private Candalaria Sandriguez was promoted to Sergeant.
Since this event, Katana blades have been used by I.D.E.C soldiers, should this tragedy ever be repeated.
