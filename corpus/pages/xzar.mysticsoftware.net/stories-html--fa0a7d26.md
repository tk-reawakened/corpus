STORIES
These are the stories from the games that I am creating. On a world not much different than our own a huge war begins. Only months earlier the demon king decided to invade the planet but the king of Astaria, the main continent, discovered is plan and rallied a large army to him. A peaceful forest, trees that are as old as the ages stood there in front of him as he aimed his bow steadily at the nearby prey. Decra, a twenty year old of the Mundi tribe, who were hunters, was rather down. Just hours before he had been banished for hunting the sacred orange beast. The Wudja, a beast eight foot high and three foot wide, it's mouth was as big as a mans head and could take it off in just one bite. Then the shrill noise of a battle loomed closer, Decra heard it and wondered how far off it was. The cries were getting closer. They were heading for the forest! Decra ran as fast as he could to the edge of the forest... It isn't very long at the moment but I will expand it when I get the time to do so.
