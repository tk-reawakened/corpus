A Future looms uncertainly...
World governments squabble, the UN lies in dissarray. Militia groups have finally unionized, inspired by a charismatic leader and a mystifying cult religion. United under common purposes, they push boldly onward through international choke points and into seats of incredible power. Armies mobilize and find themselves dismally ill fit to counter the ruthless cunning and black market technology of terrorist forces.
A Wasteland is born...
With things on the verge of boiling over, someone, somewhere strikes a match. In a large region in the southern English countryside, missiles launched from northern African countries land squarely on the rolling hills. Radiation levels skyrocket and the world holds a hand to its collective mouth and utters "nuclear" in disbelief.
A Pawn enters the game...
Under pressure, UN santctioned scientists develop a device to cleanse the Wasteland of radioactive contamination. It is rushed from sterile laboratories and white collared scientists to the beaches of Britain, where NATO again stumbles as it fails the delivery to the local outpost. Splintered wasteland gangs steal and disassemble the device, and the drums of anarchy roar. ... Trying to avoide the hawk-eyed, liberal world media, a new brand of world protection, the IDI, dreams up a project for a small band of government-less mercs to quietly enter the Wasteland and recover the device. Money and desperation fuel this uneasy partnership...

A Sky darkens....
In Zeo-K you play the role of one unnamed, anonymous mercenery employed for a desperate government. But the sky has shifted, and the tides of power ebb with violent ferocity.. Can you triumph in a desolate Wasteland and uncover the dark secrets of its past?
