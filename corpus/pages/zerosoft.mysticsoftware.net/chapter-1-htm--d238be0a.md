# Vocies: Story of the Toushi - Chapter 1, Part 1

"The vocies will reach you..."
A boy turns around, one of his friends calls out, "Hey stop day dreamin' JD!"
JD sighs, "Yeh yeh...hold on will ya? I thought I herd somethin' geez" JD shrugs, "Must be hearin' things...", he returns to playing baseball with his friends.
"...The vocies of a distant star."
JD does a double take and he drops his baseball bat. One of his friends threw a hard pitch and pegged him right in the back of the head. JD starts rubbing the back of his head and start calling out, "Hey...that hurt you little..." JD starts toward his friend. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"You are the one..."
JD turns back around, "What the hell is goin' on here..." he mumbles. He starts backing away, and before he knew it he ran into someone. He was about 5'8, 240 lbs, white T-shirt and jeans, with finger less gloves. JD was only around 4'9 and 110 lbs, he wasn't wearing a shirt because it was so hot outside. He wore simple blue jeaned shorts, he also has a neckalce made of crystalis and a bracelet on his left hand.
"You wanna start somethin' you little bitch!?" he big burly guy yells. JD taps his necklace and conentrates his KI energy, it then turns into a crystalis sword, about 40 inches in lenght and curved, with spikes pertruding from the hand guard. He holds it forward at the burly guy, then his bracelet forms and turns into a crystalis gauntlet, with the ability to change shape. The burly guy starts backing away, "Hold on man, I didn't want a fight...geez dude..."
"Too late...John...asshole" he mumbles.
John started backing away even further. "Hey hey...I really didn' mean it god...you is so picky..." JD started forward after John, sword forward and gauntlet-hand steady, Toushi-E earing jambling (Toushi-E means E rank, there are 5: A, B, C, D, E, A being the highest and E being the lowest), JD started a low sounding laugh. "Haha...why do you run? We are at the Toushi school of anything goes Matrial Arts! Aren' we supposed to fight? Your a C rank Toushi...why should you care? Im only an E rank...I'd lose at your superb prowlessness...haha...!"
John was angered by JD's mocking. "So...thats how you want it!? Come on then!" John charged at JD directly, JD flipped his sword around so it faced behind him and ran at John. John's fist met JD's gauntlet, JD flipped his sword around and slashed at his outstreched hand, John dodged backward, but not without suffering injury, the left side of his face was gashed. John was on his knees, screaming, "Oh my FUCKIN' god WHAT THE HELL DID YOU DO YOU LITTLE BITCH!" John pulled out his switch-blade and stabbed at JD.
JD used his Ki energy and formed the guantlet into a shield and the blade bounced off the shield and flew toward John, leaving another scar on the right side of his cheek. John was in pain, so much, he was about to faint. JD held the sword above John's head and mumbled "Goodbye...bitch." JD came down swiftly, John didn't have time to react, within seconds of the blow, he was dead. JD sighed. "Why does it have to come to this..." his sword returned to a crystal necklace and his gauntlet to a simple bracelet. "Glad I didn' have to use my revolver, someone else mighta gottn hurt."
JD sighed and he walked away from the bloody stage that was set. He mumbled something to himself "Once a Toushi kills his first prey, he must leave his town of birth in search of stronger opponents..." At that time his earring shone and changed to a more gloosy blue D rank color, instead of a green E rank.
