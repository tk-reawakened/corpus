# 100 Guns

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by TKGB*
> I didn't knew what to expect from this game but I did not get an ordinary TK Game. Yes some of the stuff from the TK is here, including that awful default BS but this game still has good gaming going on. Plot (1) - In 100 Guns, Double Gun was in prison, doing his rounds when Killer, a noutorous...err...killer, broke out of jail. He and his minions all clone themselves to form armies of bad people throughout New York city. Killer is out to get a briefcase filled with some ooze that will turn him into the most powerful in the world. Double Gun must overcome these clones and take down everyone one of Killer's thugs one by one to get to Killer before it's too late. Oi. Doesn't this story remind you of "Gungrave" or what? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - The graphics suck but I like em a little. Double Gun looks like a stiff like that. Everyone else looks a bit better but DG could use some work. The backgrounds are quick, 1 minute tile sets but they look kinda good though not super great. Well the artwork can use alot of work, particularly on the chars. They only have one tile to be used as char graphics. What I mean is whether you push LEFT, RIGHT, DOWN, UP, it doesn't matter, the char will still face south. That needs work. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - This game features spy music; music from spy movies. Most, if not all, are from James Bond. They sound good but the one for the boss battle isn't. Its the intro to the James Bond movies. Eck. Doesn't fit here at all. If you are to rip off music then use it well please. You might want to check out some of the music from Gungrave. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - Well the way this game goes, it has levels like an action game. Each level consist of a large board where you must go from point A to point B while kicking clone butt. Well this is find and dandy but the only obstacle is the clones themselves. Without them, you can just wisk yourself to the end of the level by holding down one direction button. It could use more than that. Also the logic of the boards don't work on some boards. The first level seems like a freeway but no other cars are visible. Well this is understanble as some highways are empty at night but second level needs work. For one thing, if level one took place at night, why is level two at daylight. Also level two takes place in the sky and your riding an airplane. So why is there a plane waiting for you at the end? There should be a small cut scene here where you catch up to it. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - Good try but not good enough. In fact this whole game needs to have a serious programming act. The menu system is only good saving and healing. It should just have those two then so programming a custom menu system will help ALOT! Not to mention this game should only save after defeating a boss. I can save anytime I want which makes things easier. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - Two main peoples. Double Gun, a cop (or whatever he is) who is after Killer, the bad guy. Not to mention an array of henchemen for you to find. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - Completely useless. Like I said, it needs to be remodeled for this game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - This game needs to throw away this awful BS and make it custom to the game itself. It's a nice idea but this game is not RPG. What it needs its a new style of battling out thugs that seem to be everywhere. Its nicely balance and all but still the default BS does not belong in this game. Its gotta find a way to have battles but not show that its not TKish. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - This game might have something here. The big-board-and-kick-clone-butt-till-you-get-to-the-boss idea is pretty neat. Would make for a good action game but this is the TK, so let's improvise. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - I like this game because it's original for the TK. Reminds me alot of Gungrave except you don't the protagonist coming back from the dead. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (6/10) - Well this game does have it's faults but it's also so good. I love it. I want to see this thing finish and complete with custom everthing. And will it kill ya to make a title screen?
