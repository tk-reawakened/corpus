# 6 Wizards

> *Review by zzz*
> Booo...:D I played this game awhile back, and just recently did as well. A word of warning: This game hurts. Programming (10/25) Not much here, but I did kinda like the card game. Art (14/25) The sprites were decent, but nothing special, the terrain tiles all looked flat to me. Story (15/25) It had an ok story, but it did have some flaws. Errors (23/25) All games have there bugs, and this one did. Sometime my character would dissapear for whole boards, but that normal for TK. Overall (62/100) A decent attempt at a game. If you have some spare time (and a fast connection) go ahead and give it a go.
