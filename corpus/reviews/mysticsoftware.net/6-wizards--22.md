# 6 Wizards

> *Review by Bradseguin*
> Graphics 4/20 They were really bad, that is all I can say. The ones that were custom were really badly done, and the rest were default. He used some ripped tiles all of a sudden for enemies in the middle of the game. Game Play 7/20 Although it was really bad gameplay overall, I did enjoy the custom card game he created, even if it was very simple. Programming 5/20 The only reason that I gave it a 5 here is because you can name your characters, and the card game he made. The rest was nothing special. Story 1/20 MADE NO SENSE AT ALL!!!! Music/sound 12/10 The music was obviosly not original, but there were some cool tunes to listen to there. Overall 29/100 Well this was one of the worst games I have ever played personally. Exept for elmos revenge. That was pointless. Worth the download? NOOOOOOOOOOO
