# 6 Wizards

> *Review by Akira*
> 6 Wizards - Review For a 4.18mb download, Six Wizards is definitely not worth the download (especially on a 56k modem) the game is badly made in almost all respects. The story is simple and full of spelling mistakes once ther was 2 wizards one lived in one world the other lived in another one of the wizards is unknown", the graphics are either ripped or done in paint using only one or two colors, the custom features are non-existent other than the menu (which I think uses DoddyMenu) and the boards arent even layered. There is a card game feature (with a whopping four cards!!) where you basically bet on who has the higher card. At the beginning of the game there was a glitch that stopped me from carrying on after the first Boss. Im not sure if the programming, my machine or the game engine caused this, but I cant seem to get passed it anyway. The sounds are all ripped like most TK games (it even uses the demo game fight sounds) but flow they nicely together anyway. The game play is poor and boring (probably because of the lack of plot) with normally only one place to go per map. Six Wizards is supposed have about 48 enemies but I only saw around 6 (an eighth) as the glitch stopped me from going on. Overall Six Wizards was very disappointing and seems like a sad attempt at making a Final Fantasy game. If you still want to prove me wrong I suggest you get working on it right away. I WONT be looking forward to the final. Gameplay: 3/10 Story: 0/10 Graphics: 3/10 Sound: 5/10 Programming: 3/10 Overall: 14/50 Akira
