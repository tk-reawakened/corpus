# 6 Wizards

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by TKGB*
> Another crummy game. But the attempt of it was pretty good though. Regardless of typos and bad graphics, this game could of made it if they had put more effort to it. Plot (1) - Taken from the game at it's exact words. Oi! And what's with the "..."? That means you are thinking so why use it here? So from what I got in the game is you take control of one of the wizards and with your partner you go on a quest to find them. Not a bad story. Too bad it didn't go so well. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (0) - The graphics are not the greatest in this game but at least they are drawn...err...originally. Yes they look like a quick 1 minute bitmap but still not bad looking. Though I wouldn't want to stare at that all day. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - Ripped music but I like it how it was used here. Nice flow with everything. Yes it is ripped from Final Fantasy games but they aren't awfully spilled onto this game. They are placed brick by brick so that makes it great. I give it a point. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - The enviroment is good in this game. Lots of places to go. And the sky gives it a touch of transportation to another continent when you get on the dragon. I will give it a point to this area because it atleast resembles a world. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (1) - Everything is default here. But what does it have to give it a point? A card game. You go and fight off monsters and if you are lucky, you get a card that you can play but the game aint really challenging. You just gotta make sure you have the highest value in your card than the other person. But it is impressive. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - You play the role of the 6th Wizard that you name along with a pal who can be a knight or a ninja. There is also a dragon who joins your quest as a mode of transportation and big brutish fighter. For some reason there is a monk who joins you for no reason at all. That is confusing. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - DoddyUK's menu system. The creator expected it to do the job. Little did that person know is that you also gotta add some effects cuz it aint gonna cut it with just Doddy's menu system. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - Yeesh. More abuse of the TK Default system. The enemies are alright. There attacks arent. The only exception is the ??? boss. Nice animation he has were he turns to a dragon mage thing and blows fire at you. Everything else is just whack. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - I don't like it. You move too quickly and some fights you cannot escape and they come all the time. It is annoying. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - You will lose interest in this game pretty fast. There is no real adventure here and everything is rushed so you wont get the benifit of an actual adventure game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (5/10) - Well this game didn't live up to its title. Not really a good looking TK game to play so just skip it.
