# A Matter Of Criminal Justice

> *Review by TKGB*
> My first impression of this game was some kind of Grand Theft Auto kinda thing but nope it aint like that. AMOCJ is not about a cop either. It's about a CIA agent retrieving a project. Nothing to do with criminals or putting them in jail. Well first impressions shouldn't be taken seriously especially with a 3 minute demo. Plot (1) - Like I said, nothing to do with criminals. The setting takes place in 2029. The US is in the process of creating an exoskeleton (for what is not told) but the prototypes were stolen by UAP's enemy countries. They call for Ryan Maxwell to find out who sabotaged the project and stole the prototypes. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - The tiles are ripped but aren't terrible. As far as this demo goes, the graphics are all simple. The enemies are the only edits are very nicely edited. No menu graphics which kinda makes it dull. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (0) - The music only has 3 songs and two of them are from the VIP fo sho pack and one of them doesn't go along with the game. One song is original but very plain and simple. Not very fashionable here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - The main boards are your room and the hallway along with the weapon room. Not very widely expandable from here. Also one thing that bothers me is the hallway of the CIA where you encounter gangsters and thieves. What the heck are they doing in a government office? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - The only good programming is that you need to show a pass to the gaurds to get to the weapon room. Thats it. Not alot of programming here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (0) - You take the roll of Ryan Maxwell, the best in the buisness. Who is he? Who knows. Who's his boss? Don't know. But this is a small demo, we might get more in depth later one. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - Black is the main color here. No profile pic and no special effects when you use items. Frankly it could of done without it but seeing as at least it could of gotten a point here, the game should of at least tried. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - You fight gangsters in the hallway of the CIA? What's that about? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - Nothing to do. The creator says you can continue to kill enemies if you want but who wants to do that with the default TK battle system? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - This demo is more of a show-and-tell kinda thing so there is no fun factorasation here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (2/10) - If you want a TK game with government plots, espionage, and a great feel of life as an agent, play F&E which is much closer to what this game is trying to do.
