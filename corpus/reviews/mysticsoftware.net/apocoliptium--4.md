# Apocoliptium

> *Review by Hyren*
> First off, it's got a stupid name. I mean, Apocliptiwhat!? Graphics: 6/10 All ripped, but well used. Except for the mysterious lack of walls and some profile images... Music: 2/10 A word of advice for programmers. No matter how bad you are at making music, DO NO USE FINAL FANTASY! Every one knows it and it gets annoying! The only reason it got 2 is because they played my favorite FFIX song. Sound: 0/10 In the time I played I heard NO sound FX. Originality: 4/10 Why does everyone make Final Fantasy-style games? Why? Let SquareSoft make em! Bugs: 5/10 Infested. From minor things like dissappearing tiles to EVIL PRISON BOARDS! I walk into this town and, no matter how hard I try, I CAN'T ESCAPE! "Maybe we should look around some more..." was my character's excuse for not leaving the town! The towns only got 4 houses! 2 of which you can't enter! 2 of which, you can walk on! Programming: 6/10 Nothing special here... Enemies: 6/10 I was only able to fight one enemy before the mysterious prison town captured me. It was a normal battle system with normal stats. Fun: 5/10 Get it if your bored, but AVOID THE PRISON TOWN AT ALL COSTS! Intro: 7/10 I like it how the words fade in and out. Items 4/10 Not many. Very few weapons and items. Overall: 45/100 Just your average Toolkit game. Get if your board. This review was written by Hyren, dated June 2, 2002
