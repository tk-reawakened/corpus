# BeastFire

> *Review by TKGB*
> BLEGH! Another one of those TK games that has a good title but HORRIBLY gameplay. Plot (0) - Uhh...WHAT PLOT!!!????? I don't know what I'm doing. I don't what I'm fighting for. I DON'T KNOW WHO I AM!!!!!!!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (0) - The graphics are not the worse I seen but they don't deserve an award for greatest graphics. At least Juno looks alright but that's as far as it goes. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - Okay one good point and the only reason to download this game so you can take the music and use it in your games. Features some from FF and others from misc. sources. No SFX either. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - Big dissapointment. In the .zip file there is a map that is pretty huge and yet your stuck in a small area with just your house, a small forest, and a giant tree with two rooms. Oi. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - Super sucks. There is nothing out of the ordinary. Just #Mwin's and #Fight's. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (0) - I don't know who this Juno is and the reason why is that there is no background to him. What's he up to? No one knows. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - Nothing good here either. Items do no special things. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - Blegh. Boring. As you can tell it's the default TK BS system. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - Okay not bad. The character don't walk through walls and stuff and got's a decent speed. No multitasking either. Hmm. But that wouldn't make a difference to the game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - Nope. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (2/10) - When you finish the demo (if you can cuz I sure haven't) it says the following: "Well that is the end of the demo. I am now working on the full version so get ready i'ts going to be great" BAH! Well you can forget about that. This game ain't never gonna be created so it's not worth you time downloading it.
