# Black Walkway, The

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by dreammakermaster*
> What up, members of the TK Community. Here wit me, I have a review of the first chapter of Dude Man's old game series: The Black Walkway! Well, here goes nothing... Graphics: 5/10 Most of the game's graphics were deafults, yet some were original, or ripped from other games. Although some of the graphics were ripped, I actually didn't care, because I thought some of them were well used. :) Music: 7/10 Same with graphics, basically. Some were ripped, others deafult, and some were remixes. Music was used wisely. And if fitted the mood of the game perfectly! Programming: 3/10 Sadly, I hate to admit it, but this game didn't really have that impressive progamming in it. Battle System: Default. Menu System: Remixed Default Font: Taken from The Jewel of Destiny. Sorry, Dude Man, but this game didn't have the best programming in it. But hey, what can you expect from a first game? Story: 5/10 Because I haven''t been able to play the whole game, I shall tell you all what I know. These two guys, Mark and Jimmy(I think that's his name.) pointlessly go on a journey, and save a kid from a Dark Captain. Then they need to go around, and kill these 3 evil gansters called the Big Daddys. Not that bad in my opinion. Overall Fun: 6/10 To tell the truth, I accually found the parts of the game I played somewhat enjoyable. It's probably not the best game ever, yet it's still worth a try. 26 out of 50 Good job, Dude Man! Pretty decent for a first game!
