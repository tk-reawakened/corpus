# Black Walkway, The

- Articles
- Interviews
> *Review by TKGB*
> The Black Walkway is like a burrito. The tortilla makes it look nice but open it up and you see a bunch of ingredients all smushed up together. Black Walkway is a nice game but it's all messed up. It looks like it was created halfway throughly and halfway by a 5 year old kid. This game isn't the ultimate, suckiest game but not one that Nintendo would want to franchise. Plot (1) - As of what I understand from the description on MS: As of what I understand from playing the game: Err not very convincing here. The plot is good but it don't go with the gameplay. Oi. Well i'll still give it a point. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (0) - Err no. Why? Cuz it's pretty much crazy. You got tiles from the Jewel of Destiny, RPGMaker, some hand drawn stuff and misc. Also not all of it is organized. In fact, this does not help to give the illusion of midieval europe. Hell it don't give the visualization of earth. Another point is the editing of the chars. The char's are from the Jewel of Destiny game (Mark has Tano's .tst OI!) and the editing don't look like anything from the imagination. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (0) - Ripped music and not placed very well. I mean there is a castle you go in and the music is a joyous castle theme yet it's dark and there are enemies. The game uses the VIP music pack which is getting pretty old. They are good songs but not to be abused. Some of the songs are ripped from various games like Final Fantasy, Sonic, Xenogears, Mario, and who knows what else. They are good but hard to find a fit. Yes some of them are placed neatly such as fight music but others are just oddly placed. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - You're kidding me right? The enviroment is very messed up. Nothing is good looking here. Just invision your roof outside in the street floating at the same level as your house. That's how it is here. In fact just picture half of the roof of your house floating in the middle of the street. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (1) - The programming isn't so bad; there are alot typos though. Not to mention bugs. Nothing fancy here. Just your average toolkit programming. #Mwin, #Callshop, and #Fight. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (0) - No. I don't believe they are heroes of any kind. In fact they are just two characters who decided to do this for no apparent reason. The game doesn't explain why they are actually searching for the castle of peace. The characters are Mike who is a nobody and Johnny who is a slave. We also go the Big Daddys who are the gang who went straight to hell and given magic powers. So what do I think? Well just envision yourself on a quest to take down Donald Trump out of buisness. Now you see what I mean? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (1) - The default menu is present but with a mask on. The game has a nice gradient effect for the profile backgrounds. The buttons are nice touch as well. The effects...what effects? You heal yourself and thats it. No flashing lights and stuff. The characters bio pics...err...let's move on. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - BORING! The fights aren't to my taste. Simple attacks with the default .wavs. Special moves aren't special. Just like the items, no flashes or animations. I see animation files on the game but they don't show up. Probably my comp but still I see nothing. The game probably depended on the Special Move Editor to do all the work but sometimes that is not the case. The Special Move Editor does makes Special Moves but sometimes its best to program some things like animations and HP loss. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - The gameplay aint all that bad. The character doesn't jerk alot. Runs really smoothly. NPC are noticibly taking turns to runtime but we don't really care about that just as long as they do what they do. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - Quiet strange. I find alot of things in this game wrong yet I am determine to finish the game. Probably cuz it's a final realese (but hopefully it ain't final). -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (5/10) - This game needs a million hours more put into it. It's great, the story is original but some elements suck like our heroes who don't look like they are doing this for any particular reason. This game seems to lack a sense of realism. I don't feel like I am Mike or Johnny. OI.
