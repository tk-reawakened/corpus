# Blobs:Christmas Catastrophe, The

> *Review by Rueben*
> Blob Dude (aka Luke Wilson) holds the dubious pleasure of being possibly one of only two people to have completed more than one game with the RPG Toolkit. But while the other one goes off to win critical acclaim and awards for his work, The Blobs series has always been "put to one side" by the community. Which is a shame, because they are actually very good. The Christmas Catastrophe is the latest offering from Blob Dude, released last year (2003). It follows the "Ninja Clan" of Blobs who undertake a mission to save Christmas from the evil Blue Blob who has possessed Santa Clause (Claws) into putting bombs into everyone's Christmas Sack. In fact, the whole idea of Role-Playing I virtually put aside in this game until the very end, concentrating more on adventure elements. This includes mostly disarming bombs in people's houses (the bomb disarm code will briefly flash up before you actually attempt to save the house) ... which is an interesting and occasionally challenging aspect of the game. The graphics are good, if occasionally a little inconsistant, with remarkable detail paid to the interiors of buildings. The still-frame cut-scenes however, allow the game to really shine in its excellent use of humour. Sound-wise, the music is pretty much entirely remixed Christmas tracks which really bring the game to life, and the sound effects are very well used (Ho ho ho - Santa). The game is pleasantly bug-free for the most part, although it would have been nice to have dialogue not skippable by pressing the arrow keys to avoid accidentally missing dialogue! Other than that, apart from internal toolkit problems, it's fairly clean. Overall, the game is short but sweet, and satisfying to play. Blob Dude has produced another great toolkit game, and when it comes to humour, he is leading the way. Excellent work. Pros - Good graphics - Good use of humour - Well thought-out mini-games Cons - Short - Use of internal battle system (out of place) Overall: 80% *full*
