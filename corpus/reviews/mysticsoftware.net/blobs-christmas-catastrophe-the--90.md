# Blobs:Christmas Catastrophe, The

> *Review by TKGB*
> Blob Dude is at it again and this time it is a xmas game. This game original was submited on xmas during the 03 winter expo but play it anytime cause it's a good game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Plot (1) - The evil blue blob is up to no good. On chrismas eve he decided to turn Santa Blob evil by hypotising him with his ray gun. Upon this he ordered Santa Blob to put bombs on every house. Now it's up to you to stop the evil blue blob. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - All original Blob Dude graphics. They may not be arcade style but they are good enough to make you smile. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - What is an xmas game without xmas music. Jingle Bells, the Christmas Carol, Silent night. Its all here. Some of them with remixes. Really good music that goes along with the whole game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - The enviroment is greatly planed although not much. But it does not disapoint alot. There is maze of tree's that you can get lost to and stuff so there may not be much but the enviroment is well planned. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (1) - I like the programming here. Although it aint no MrG style, it works. First thing you have is the bomb disarm panel. I think that is an awesome idea that BD did. Though for as far as advance programming that is probably it. The rest are good but the bomb is by far the best. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - I love the characters. You have yourself play as a ninja blob, the evil Santa Blob, The evil Blue Blob, and some sailors who are a bunch of fools. Its all good and fun. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (1) - Custom design but that's as far as it goes. There is a status panel of your mission as well as your health. A count that checks how many houses have been saved and lost. The rest is typical TK implements. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - One fight with the default system. No real challange their. Though I don't think the game is about the fights so it shouldn't count. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - Gah. Here is the anoying part. The char moves WAY too slow. Ack. I had to open the start program and edit the speed to make it nice. BD should fix this. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - With bomb defusions and funny comics, this game is a blast to play and not only that but it has additional endings with one of them having a surprise in the end. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (8/10) - This is a must play for everyone. A great game with some custom features. Although its pretty short, it still can become an inspiration for newbs of the community.
