# Bounty

> *Review by TKGB*
> Bounty is a game by oblivious_casualties. The game is good looking and impresive in the artistic side but not alot in the gaming side. Everything looks great and thought out but it lacks gaming. Of course this is a demo but if it's like this during the course of the full version, it may not be as big as a hit that it should be. It should have more interaction with other characters and there should be more detail to the story line but then again I am reminded that this here is a demo so we must wait. Plot (1) - The plot is different from other TK games. The game takes place in a civil war in England. As the title says, you take the role of a bounty hunter who does the dirty work of an informant. Very good but it's more of an action game thing than a RPG'ish game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - The art get's an original score. The entire city looks abondoned and broken like a slum, just like war should be. The sewers is very nicely drawn as well and the alleys are dark and dingy. This game looks impressive with it's art. The characters in the game look cool but did England had green haired punk looking guys during the civil war? Whatever. I don't know anything about english history. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - You got mainly two tunes in this game and for the battle they are altered to a higher tempo. The music is mostly, if not always, a single piano tune. The sound of it is a sad theme. It fits the game cause this isn't a time to sing and dance on the streets. I give it a point but some situations may need a little more than a piano theme to it. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - The enviroment is kinda small but this is a neighborhood you are at. Also the way you go up to the other side get's confusing. When you go to the other side of the block you are in, it flips it so it's facing you and you might still press the same button that is leading you towards the place and mistakenly go back (yeah I know, it sounds confusing). But that is the only thing that is annoying. I like the fact that shops, inns, and whatnot are in alleys trying to keep off from the warzone. It's dark and it's dirty so it's pretty great. Also going from place to place requires a little knowing where to go like in your first assignment to get a man named Exile, you have to go through the sewer. Getting there is the easy part, getting out of it might take some searching about. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - The game's programming is low here. It uses the basic stuff and some aid from the default of the demo game. Throughout the demo, you mostly get message windows. Then again, this game doesn't feature much. I don't really know what could programming help here since a battle system couldn't do any good since you don't fight alot but it would be nice though. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - The game features three characters you get to play as. At the moment you only get Axel. There is also another dude and a girl you can play as but not available. So Axel happens to be a bounty hunter to survive. He takes his orders from Asimir, the informant he has known a long time. Pretty good. If it is as good as this for the others, the game will be great. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - No real use. The menu is mostly empty for the moment. In fact saving is not even an option. I wish it was cause my comp seems to keep my game from playing smoothly. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - There is only one special effect effect in the game and its the yellow border that sorrounds the screen when you do a special. Other than that it's the repetitive battle of many TK games that don't have a custom BS. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - The character is kinda slow moving, even with the press and hold of the SHIFT key. But after playing many TK games, that is no prob for me but it could use a little speed up. The battle system is boring in my opinion. Not really great and seems forced to be there. What I mean by that is the game has put in a battle system just to keep in with the RPG style of it. The game could of used some other means of fighting but that would take some programming to do. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - Not really. This game features original artwork and a nice storyline about the english civil war but I can't seem to find any fun factorization in this game. This game is more fitted to be of some other genre like shooting. It will take alot of work to make this an RPG game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (5/10) - In one hand the game gots great graphics and a original plot to it with good music and nicely done characters. But the other hand says it doesn't got the drive to be an RPG. It's kind slow and not much to do. This game needs to have more programming and step outside of the RPG genre to be a success. I'm not saying not to do it outside the TK but I say make the TK do the impossible and go for a different genre of the game with the TK.
