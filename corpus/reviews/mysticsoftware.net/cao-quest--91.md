# Cao Quest

> *Review by TKGB*
> I remember when I was new to the toolkit, I used to hang around Moonews alot. I really thought he was cool back then and I still think he is cool now. I refer to him as my sensei since I try to learned from him but he didn't do much so I just went on to learn the TK on my own. When Moonews comes to mind, I think of Cao Quest cause that's his game. Though I don't think he will finish it but it's quite a facinating idea. Though the game ain't really good but I love the idea of it. Moonews must like furry games cuz that's his main aim as in Cao Quest. Plot (1) - I like this plot. You play the role of a cao named Cao. He is a curious fellow and when an earthquake errupts, Cao set's out to find out why. He speaks with the smartest person in the entire country, who happens to be human by the way, to find out that he aint very smart cause he doesn't know either. From that point the plot stops. I guess the general idea was to find out what cause the earthquake by yourself. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - You know what i'm about to say. That's right, ripped tiles but the tiles are beutifully been put here. Everything is lush green here. Grass and trees and green all around this little world. It's just so beutiful and I just love it. The characters, or at least some, were drawn by Sensei...err I mean Moonews. Not bad in my opinion but the profile pics scare me. I don't even like opening the menu cause I really don't like it at all. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - What goes great with the greenery is the music. It's a classical violin theme that goes really nice with the scenery. But that's about it for the music. There are only three songs and that's my only favorite one. The one in the battle is nice but I prefer the town music. Sound effects are minimal too. Not too much here. In fact this department is practically empty. Not alot of audio from this game. Really shame too, the game was turning out good in the audio factor. I give it a point but some people might get bored with the song as they so often don't like hearing the same song over and over again. I do like this song. In fact while writting this review, I was listening to it. But of course in general no one really likes listening to a one instrument song all the time. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - Like I said, the world is practically a green paradise kinda look. It's really nice and Sensei really brought out the beuty of the default RM2K tileset. The world practically looks smushed in my opinon. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - There is no real programming here like always. The only effort was the Mwin, which is a oval bubble. Not bad but that does not help it to wash away all the other defaults. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - Characters are furry people named Cao. You play as a cao who is named Cao. Your freind is Koji who seems real stuck up, and he works for the king. A note on the king, he is human and so is the wiseman. Uhh...why are there humans in a world with furry animals? Makes no sense. Well maybe it's not the world that is filled with Cao's but just a small village since you begin in a small village filled with Cao. This is making confused. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - Another boring BS. I didn't even look at it cause Cao's profile pic is scary looking to me so I don't want to give it a point and i'll stop here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - Boring and repititive. The special moves don't look good, the fight is the same as always, and I think the enemie's are ripped. Also I didn't encounter any fights in the overworld. Only when the fight is summoned by the #Fight command. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - Cao moves slow and if you hold down the direction button to much, he will walk by himself depending on how long you held the button. Not good. And like I said, no fights so that don't help it to gain a point. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - Naw. No real fun in this game. It's the same token as other TK games. No real effort put into it. It's just plopped into the TK and depends on the TK to make it work. It don't work like that people. You gotta do more than just boards and enemies. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (5/10) - A nice humble game this is. It lacks many important factors but it still remains one of my favorites because it's so beutifully done graphically. It's so pretty i'm going to cry now.
