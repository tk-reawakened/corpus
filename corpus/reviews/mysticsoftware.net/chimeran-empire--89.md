# Chimeran Empire

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by TKGB*
> I remember seeing Chimera Empire in the list but never really bother to download it. I have downloaded it recently and must say it is a good game. Never expected it to be this good. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Plot (1) - A man named Giar decided to take it on to himself to destroy the evil tyrant Chimera. Along with his friend Singe, they went to the planet Gage and destroyed the Chimera. Though they did not survive for the power of Chimera's sword, Juggernaut, destroyed them as well. Six years later, their son's Ifrin and Greg are about to discover the secret of Juggernaut. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - The tiles are ripped but that doesn't mean it isn't nice to look at. The graphics are well put. They are not put in any weird order. Everything looks nice in this game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (0) - The music is for the VIP pack that comes with the TK. In my opinion it sucks. But on the plus, some of the music is from other sources but not enough. The sound effects are decent. You can tell the status of an action by the sounds it emits so its well put. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - The eviroment I like. There is no overworld but the path from one town to the other was designed well. The signs are also a nice touch too. The towns are a little bit off. I see no boundries between them on some of them but this does not mean its not crummy. You can easily tell if you are leaving a town or not. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (1) - No fancy stuff but I still give it a 1. Everything is the default of what the TK uses but the person took time to perfect the message window text and the conversations. The chest and wells are nicely programmed and so is the arena. There is no real bugs that are terrible. Though one thing that is impressive is the sleep in the inn. Sometimes you would dream that a monster is attacking you and wake up from it. A great touch of originality. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - Characters are great. You got the two sons of the two fallen heroes, a stranger who is looking for the Juggernaut, and two of Chimeras henche men. Im sure there is more but we will have to wait for the next version. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (1) - Default menu but the creator didn't give the job to the TK itself. He went about to make sure it worked the way he wanted it too. The items respond with a sound. The equipment is also great. The design isnt half bad so its not really crummy from what we expect from the default TK menu. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (1) - Defaul fight but in this case its fun to use which I found rarely in TK games. The fight is well balanced. No enemy is to hard and your controls aren't hard to use either. The powers aren't random number of decreas SMP so I love it. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - The character runs great. The battle's appear a little to much but not as much as 12 steps. The BS isn't anoying and its fun to use. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - The game is fun even though everything is default. The way the townspeople talk, the way the story evolves. With some minor programming skills, the game could be a masterpiece. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (9/10) - Well I dunno about previous versions of the game but this game is a gem. A wonderful piece of the TK library.
