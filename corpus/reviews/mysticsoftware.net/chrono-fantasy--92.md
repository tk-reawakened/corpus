# Chrono Fantasy

> *Review by TKGB*
> Talk about old here. This is the first TK game I have ever played back when I was a newb. At that time I was looking for more inspiration. This game did gave me inspiration but it isn't the greatest to download. In fact it's pretty much a waste of diskspace. Plot (1) - -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - The tiles are ripped but they are meshed together nicely. I don't see a single misplaced tile. It's quite fascinating but of course these are the same tiresome tilesets that are in every TK demo game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - The music is also rip but it's just as good cuz it's got some of my fav's including one I really like. The SFX are decent in the game. It features wooshes and zip's and other great sounds. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - BOO! The enviroment sucks. You are mainly in a small town that you can't get out because the creator didn't made a link to the outterworld. The design's of the boards were really great and I expected alot but what cut it off was the fact that you couldn't leave the town. No point. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - Not alot here. There is the level up thing that is nice but a flag aint going to save this game. Not to mention the bug that wont let you leave the town. The requirement to leave the town was to talk to the king and save the girl in the dungeon but not even that can get you out of the village. Xupor suX. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (0) - Now I like the cast in the game but you don't get to see alot of them. Caspian is the great knight of the kingdom, and you got 4 more that you know nothing about. Oi, I wish you could keep em to get a good feeling of them but you only keep em in your dream. Also Caspian doesn't acknowledge the fact that the girl he saves was in the dream he had about her. I believe that could of been of good use. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - The only thing good is the char pics. Everything else is pretty boring. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0.5) - The battle is mediocre. It has good SFX for options and the special moves are kinda nice but they don't have special effects. The stats of enemies are sometimes too strong outside of the dream and your supposedly the greatest knight. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - Caspian walks fine but battles are up the ying yang. The village is being raded by thieves and creatures but they appear like every 5th step. This means they are crawling from everywhere and that doesn't seem right. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - It's really exciting till you wake up then it gets frustriating. Caspian should of kept sleeping ^_^. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (3/10) - Bah. This game is below 5 so it don't deserve to be downloaded.
> *In the world of Cron, there is an evil witch named Kaos. She want's to capture the time bird Xenon which is supposedly a magic bird but this demo doesn't feature Xenon. Kaos has manage to make people who are resistent to her power resisntant no more. When she comes to take Xenon, the world will be plunged to chaos. Only you, Caspian, the greatest knight in the kingdom, can take down Kaos.*
