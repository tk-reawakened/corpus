# Danger the Dingo

> *Review by Zerzic*
> Title: Danger the Dingo Author: Moonews Reviewed by Zerzic Danger the Dingo came out a few years ago and was greeted with warmness around the tk community. Being a friend of Moonews and having him create tilesets for my old game Genesis Eve I thought it suiting that I review his second Danger the Dingo game which is his first rpg. GRAPHICS (16/20): Moonews graphics style is very unique and detailed and adds great depth to the game. The boards are very interesting (I especially enjoyed the cliff area and statue)and dont get repetitive. One thing I must say about the graphics is that they are grainy which is one small downfall. Gameplay (14/20): The gameplay is entertaining but is a bit buggy and the battle system isnt the best, although there are many parts of the gameplay that are worth your time. Features like picture expressions deepen the gameplay. Music (13/20): The music is suitable for the most part but is ripped so I cant give it a high score. Storyline (16/20): The story is imaginative and unique due to the fact its centered around dingo's, its only a demo so it could use some improvement but for the most part DTD has an exceptional story. Replayability (15/20): I have played this game many times over although it does get boring after a few times. OVERALL (74)
Order it Today!
