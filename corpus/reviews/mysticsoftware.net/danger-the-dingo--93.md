# Danger the Dingo

> *Review by TKGB*
> Another game by Sensei Moonews and this game features, you guessed it, furry animals. Dingo's to be exact. I remember back like 1 or 2 years ago when MS announced Danger the Dingo but back then I was more concerned about my own TK games. Well I downloaded it. Wow, it's really great in the graphics but blegh in the gameplay. Read on. Plot (0) - I don't know what's this game about. You start out coming out of your house and this homeless guy is getting robbed by two thieves. You show them a big knife and they run like scared cats. Then you walk around for the rest of the game. I don't know what you are suppose to do. The NPC's keep mentioning this is a demo so I guess it's just to look around. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - Oh. The graphics in this game are very beutiful. Unlike Cao Quest, the graphics are drawn by sensei himself. They are very wonderful to look at yet they aren't enough to keep you playing over and over again. I must give real credit to sensei for drawing such wonderful tiles that look like paintbrushed. Maybe thats what it is. The title screen is the only thing that sucks. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - I don't know how sensei decides on the music but he always manages to get great music that's in the mood of the game. The music in this game isn't his but they are nice and give real atmosphere to the game. The outside of the house, the enterior of the houses, the shops, the title screen, they are all magnificent to listen to. I'm just falling in love with the songs here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - It's small and I can't seem to get out of it. I'm not sure if there is an overworld but if it's like this then it's not really good. But I am reminded this is a demo game and the purpose is to show what it's all about. One thing that is also bad is the way the boards connect. This game features a "Loading" banner for every board crossing and it is utilized by putting a .prg on the tile where you will go next so he didn't use the links on the board editor but some of them do link and supposedly they need to use the .prg to go the next board. What Moonews didn't count on was probably that some boards do link when they are not suppose to. I.E. when you walk off a town, you may end up in the mid of the cliff of the next board when really you should of taken the road. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (1) - Programming is not bad but not impressive. The Mwin has a profile pic that shows emotions but only for Danger. The menu is probably downloaded from MS. That's pretty much it. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - "Mommy, mommy, look at the funny doggy." Danger looks funny with his big legs. That's what I like about him. I read in a manga book that the upper half of the body must be the same size of the legs to make it look normal but this is just weird. He seems like a really funny guy but the game doesn't go throughly within this. So Danger remains an unknown character. I still like him though. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (1) - The menu looks nice but of course it's the default TK menu implements. The mask makes it look nice so I give it a point. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (1) - Default BS and there is only one fight and it's against one of the crooks. So far it is impressive. First off it is not the side view like every TK battle but its a first-person view. So you are facing the enemy. I like the way he looks when you wack him. Makes me laugh just a bit. But then he gets you with his stick. Not nice. The special effects are the same for every move but not bad. One point it gets. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - Danger walks a tad bit slow but not really slow. So far from what I got the gameplay isn't bad. One particular part is the NPC's who acknowledge that they are in a game, they are NPC's, this is a demo, etc. It was fine in the Jewel of Destiny but in this game it don't go right. Still it's pretty fun to see what they think of themselves as NPC's. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - Well it's all linear really, not really fun but pretty good to watch. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (7/10) - And in conclusion, here is the official Danger the Dingo walkthrough: Congratulations. Not only did you beat the game but killed 10 minutes.
> * "Us npcs are so mistreaded! "In the full game we'll have our" "own side quests!" "It's gonna be so cool!"
> *Start the game. You will stop the thieves from robbing the homeless man. Go to the other part of the town. Go to the upper-left side house but get the flower first. Give it to the girl inside the house. She will compliment you. Leave the house and meet with the crooks again. A conversation will take place then ask if you are gonna give em your money. If you say yes then Moonews will call you a wimp. If you say no you will fight one of the thieves. Defeat him.*
