# Dark Age

> *Review by Hyren*
> Game: Dark Age Author: Xorlak Reviewer: Hyren Score:70/100 This is the best game I've ever played! I still haven't beat it though... Story: 8/8 The story rules! You're one of the canidates for the new head general of Varda. Mortis is the other. The story is slowly revealed, piece by piece, until you're in so much suspense you can't stand it! Graphics: 6/8 Unfotunattly, the graphics are ripped, but he used them so well it more than makes up for it. Music: 7/8 The music is also ripped but it fits in very well with the mood of the game. Bugs: 5/8 There were quite a few small bugs with the the game but nothing huge. Programming: 8/8 Xorlak really knows how to program. He can probably program better than Chris Matthews and he proved it in Dark Age! Battles: 5/8 Unfortunattly, it used the same battle system we all hate. But he did at least make cool enemies and magic attacks. Fun: 8/8 I would give it 10 but it only goes up to 8. I can't describe how addicting this game is! Download it and find out for yourself! Originality: 6/8 As we all know, there are no truly original RPGs anymore (except Mini Cars *Shivering with terror*), but Xorlak put in enough secrets and side quests to make it seem more original. Final: 45/64 And that's good! You must get this game! It's free! It doesn't use much memory! Get it! This review was written by Hyren, dated May 22, 2002
