# Dark Age

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by RPGLand2000*
> Plot The plot of this game is, well, there is a evil guy you must kill to save the world. The plot was so unoriginal that I knew everything that would happen before it happened. 1 out of 5. Characters Most of the characters are mere one-dimensonal stereotypes. There are nearly no exceptions to this. These characters are forgettable as well; I cannot recall a single character name besides Dr.Science. 1 out of 5. Graphics The greatest part of the game. The graphics are all either new or ripped well (I'm sure a few are ripped, but I could be wrong). Graphical glitches are a problem sometimes, however. 4 out of 5. Gameplay This game uses the normal combat system. There could have been a custom one, but most of the time I have no problem with the default combat system; this is no exception. The bosses and enemies, however, are extremely hard if your level is too low, which it almost always will be. In this game, you will frequently die to normal enemies. And the bosses....well, they're even worse. I didn't even manage to beat the next-to-last boss. 1 out of 5. Music As I did not listen to the music, I cannot judge this. N/A. Conclusion This game is frustarating and has a plot that was trite back in SNES days with none of the character development abd extra subplots that make, say, the PS1 Final Fantasy games, which had these types of plots, worth it. Due to the excellent graphics, I will add a point, making this game a 2 out of 5.
