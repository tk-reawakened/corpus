# Dark Age

> *Review by TKGB*
> In my opinion, the greatest TK game ever developed. The original "Dark Age" is the most fun game I have played. I enjoyed the way this game is developed and how the story is really worth going for. This game is truly a great game to play because it just has more stuff to do than any other classic. Of course I'm speaking on behalf of other 2nd gen games. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Plot (1) - The story is about a war but not revolving about it. There are two kingdoms: Varda and Celena. The kingdom of Varda has two generals: Vance and Mortis. Vance is a loyal subject while Mortis seems very different. The story unfolds to go into different paths. A great plot. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - Why of course the art in this game is great but some of the tiles are ripped from FF games. The caves, townspeople, the castle all seemed rip. But not all is ripped. The main characters are original and so are the monsters. Even though there are ripped tiles, Xorlax made sure they clash together. They blend in nicely. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - RIPPED! The music is from various games: Final Fantasy, Zelda, hell even one from Mega Man X. Why did I gave it a 1? Cause it sounds nice and put approprietly. Sure it would of been nicer to have original music but what can you do? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - The enviroment is nice and juicy. There are some neat towns that are pretty big. Not to mention the Arena, a place to gamble, which is really fun. And of course, the black castle. Crazy place to be in. There is alot of places to visit and lots of monsters to face. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (1) - Most of the features are the default of the TK but that doesn't mean that there is no programming done. A good example is the arena. There are great casino games all custom by Xorlax such as slots and a blackjack table. There is also a somewhat maze of rooms in the black castle where you got to follow a certain direction in order to get passed (like in 'Legend of Zelda'). -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - There are unique characters within the game. You got the good guy Vance who is the humble kind, Mortis the power hungry one, three knights that accompany you in the begining of the game and other great characters. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (1) - The menu is default of the TK but not at fault. The items do certain things when used like chime and light up the borders. Also there is a nice background which is a spectacle. So I give it a 1 because it looks nice unlike other TK games where it's just slapped together. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (1) - The battle system is default. The default TK does sucks but Xorlax made sure it was fun. First, lots of animations. For every attack, Xorlax made an animation to make it look a lot nicer than just the number of HP that the opponent lost. The enemies look really great. They have been drawn greatly and are just facinating. The programming of special moves are also facinating. There is one by the red dragon where he countsdown to make an attack. So even though the defailt battle system sucks, it can be made better. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - The game runs really smooth for a TK game. The battles go don't delay and make things worse for you. Though there is the initial bug were if you press 'run' twice, you lose more money (Xorlax said it was punishment but we know its just the TK responding to the run command). -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - Yes! Fun! This is what it's all about. The game is indeed fun. With side quest to do and the Arena to earn more cash and compete in a fight. The most amazing thing of all though is how the characters interact. It is amazing. It is as if they are talking to each and wasn't even programmed. That is what makes it a spectacle. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (10/10) - One of my all time favorite games. This is what a home made game should be made of. If you haven't tried it, go and download it. It's free and it isn't huge so it's win win.
