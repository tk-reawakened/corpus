# Dark Age

> *Review by Marcus*
> Here is what you've all been waiting for; it's the first ever-full picture review for one of the most popular toolkit games ever created. In this review I will cover all aspects of the game from graphics to originality. If you would like to know more information about this game and would like to know where to download it simply type in on your web browser http://darkage.mysticsoftware.net. Don't forget to check out the first ever preview for Dark Age 2 with the latest up to date screenshots. If your wondering "What's the point of reading this review when I can just go and visit the official website?" I'll tell you, basically the official website is more of an advertisement feature and gives you details and pictures of the actual game but the question you really want to know is "Is this game worth downloading?" well I'll tell you, if you read on Anyway enough of the chitchat let's get on with the first ever project for the game Dark Age. Story, What's it all about? 10/10 Dark Age by far has one of the most original and complex storylines ever created in the RPG toolkit community. The story contains all of the elements a true RPG game sure contain, from love, hate, betrayal and tragedy. Ok, so we know the story contains, but what is it about? Well I'll tell you The kingdoms of Varda and Celena have been at war off and on for centuries. The militant Varda has built up quite an army as a result. But this is not another war story. Vance, a skilful yet passive general of Varda's army, notices an increase in violent behaviour from his king, Fredrick, and in particular, another Varda general named Mortis. With the recent peace treaty between the two sparring nations in effect, Fredrick seems to be using his military more and more for internal affairs, such as stealing from the Wizard Palace for not paying taxes. Vance doesn't mind the increased activity; it's his job after all. But his co-general, Mortis, has been becoming increasingly harder to deal with. Vance and Mortis have accepted greater responsibility since the death of the head general, Walter, and Vance is feeling the extra stress. On top of that, a deadly dragon has been spotted in the mountains near Varda Castle... The characters have really wicked names: Vance, Mortis, Darius, Erik, Gredrat and Dr. Science.There are many, many more but these are the main ones for now. Vance is the character you control for 90% of the game. He is a general of the Kingdom of Varda, Vance is loyal and courageous. He always tries to perform his missions to the best of his ability and follow his king's instructions exactly. Vance is trained in the magic arts and is one of the few who can use White Magic. The other characters have there own special abilities, background history and separate story. The thing that makes the story get such a high rating is the fact that each character has there own individual personality which is very hard to achieve when programming your own full game RPG game. Boards: 9/10 Graphics: 9/10 All of the boards through out Dark Age are very nicely done. Each board contains plenty of detail and scrolls very nicely. Most of the tiles are original but there are quite a few which are ripped from Final Fantasy 4. When you are playing through a board you actually do feel like you are in the location, what I mean by that is when you enter a castle the tiles are so well put together and placed it really does look like a proper castle. Items: 10/10 Well, let's just say that the game Dark Age contains loads and loads of Items all with there own special ability and features. I can tell you what Items to expect when playing through this tremendous role-playing game, there is everything from Potions to magic orbs and some Items even influence how your character turns out in the final battle! There is really nothing else to say apart from the Items are brilliant and must of took absolutely ages designing and programming. Bravo! Xorlak you did it again! Battles: 8/10 The battles in Dark Age are pretty impressive and have special effects in such as magic and even Limit Breaks! These are really powerful attacks like in Final Fantasy 7. Xorlak makes his Limit breaks look really effective and every battle he has nicely placed a background image, which blends in nicely with the enemies and match pretty well.Regrettably Xorlak uses the default battle system but if any of you have actually tried programming your own battles system you know how frustrating and difficult it actually is! So unfortunately despite Xorlak's excellent effort to produce the highest quality battle system, I can't give full marks but I can give a total score of 8/10 Enemies: 10/10 Well, what's there to say apart from they are all 100% original and are drawn superiorly well with perfect detail. Each enemy has it's own special characteristic and special attack. Some enemies are very tough and challenging and others are well, hard! The enemies are fair but when playing through the game don't expect to wiz through the enemies due to they are actually very difficult and I guarantee you that the more progress you make through the game the more times you will be defeated, I just hope you take losing well. Bugs: 9/10 Now surprisingly enough there were quite a few bugs and they could of actually effected the game play. But wait this is Xorlak, do you really think he forgot all about bugs? Cause he didn't! Xorlak programmed a special program set as a run time activation program. This particular program is known as a force redraw, whenever the board had a funny 5 minutes and decided to make some tiles solid when they were suppose to be normal all the player had to do was simply hit the "R" key and the whole board got redraw via leaving the board bug free. Now I have seen some special programs in my time but this one has to be the cleverest. Why can't every game contain this simple command? I mean you get marked out of 10 for bugs and if you use something like this your going to get at least 8 easy marks. So in the future use something like this and your game still wont be perfect and bug free but will reduce the amount of bugs that crop up! Programming: 10/10 Well now come on lets do the math so far this game is brilliant this guy basically programmed his own bug solution so what do you think I'm going to say about the programming? Dark Age basically uses nearly every basic Rpg code command from the basic Mwin to the colour transparently. The code, which is probably used the most, would have to be the push and push item command it's used constantly and fits in well with the story and story sequence. Xorlak uses many more programs and uh, ummm Oh screw it he can probably program better than Chris Matthews himself he is using some of the most advance programs ever! He has got every thing! he has even programmed his own gambling machines and slot machines, I said it before and I'll say it again, you do the math! Sounds: 8/10 Sounds? Ok now look I know how to write reviews and show off graphics and boards but to show you sounds that's virtually impossible! So if I can't show them to you you might as well listen to them First I'd just like to say that all the sounds fit in extremely well and suit the atmosphere some are ripped but hey, this guy uses sound effects as well so let's give him credit. Final: 83/90 92% So there you have I have covered all the aspects of the game Dark Age. You may have noticed I have missed out the category "Originality" that's essentially because it is blatancy obvious that the game is 90% original all you have to do is look at the boards, characters and story and straight away you can tell that it's original so if I did decide to do the category "Originality" it would have been pointless because I would of just been repeating myself
