# Dark Age

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by The Slasher 07*
> First Review ever, thank you very much ^_^ DarkAge...named by many as the best RPGToolkit game ever...I guess. Well, even though this game is about as Cliche as you can make it for an RPG, I had actually enjoyed it and even finished it, which, as far as TK games go, is a rare sight indeed ^_^ Let's go to the specs, for those of you who like to see numbers instead of words... 17/20 Story didn't blow me away. You get the typical Medieval area, and it leads up to a huge climax to save the world or something, like most RPGs. It kept me interested enough to keep going on with the game...but those of you into intricate stories with extreme plot twists everywhere won't be too happy here. It does well enough, though. 20/20 AWESOME. Keeps the ultimate RPG Tradition of wandering around from place to place, but I've always had a strange liking for that thing. Plus, Minigames were added in, which kept me addicted for awhile ^_^ Mind you, I'm covering everything BUT the Battle System here, so scroll on.... 10/20 Ugh. Someone say "ugh" with me. Great. Now everyone together. Perfect. The default battle system is used here, and even though I'm thankful for the effort of the creator, it's extremely crude, slow, and sometimes unresponsive. Original tactics and animations were made here, which kick it up a few points, but this is definately not a saving grace. Once you get used to it, though, it's not that annoying, and keeps up the standard RPG fare of Turn-Based. FF1 fans will easily adapt. 19/20 Numbers can speak for me here, but since I like speaking, I'll explain. When it comes down to TK-created games, people automatically look at the tilesets, and how well they fit together. Well, it's been done perfectly here. Original and ripped tiles have been used, but they fit so seamlessly that it's hard to tell which is which sometimes. This would've been perfect had a *tad* more tiles been original, but for TK, this is first-class. 19/20 Well, same as Graphics. The music seems to have been carefully chosen depending on where you are, so that it really "sets the mood" for that place. I heard a few ripped tunes, but I couldn't tell for some of them, so I assume there were some original tracks in there. Normally, DarkAge would've earned an 85. Well, this is a special section where we dole out some extra points needed. This is, afterall, a TK-made game, and you can't expect Final Fantasy work here. As such, the game will be awarded points based on how well the TK was used, and in this case, it scores a Perfect. 5/5 That means 5 extra points will be added to DarkAge's score. Hrrm. Well, that jumps it up to a 90. DOWNLOAD. Definately. Hardly a TK game to pass up.
