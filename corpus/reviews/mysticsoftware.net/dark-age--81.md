# Dark Age

> *Review by Unknown Author*
> Probably one of the best Toolkit Games of all time! Story: 13 out of 15. The main character is Vance, a general of Varda competing against the evil Mortis, to see who will be head general. After that, you must go hunt down a big dragon who is spotted in the mountain. Bassiccley that's all the major stuff I discovered so far. Graphics: 11 out of 15. Some of them were original,(a couple sprites for example.)others ( maybe some board tiles for example.)were ripped from other games, like Final Fantasy. But still, they were used very well! Music: 13 out of 15. All of the midis( except maybe some small sound effects.) were ripped from Final Fantasy, or something else. But since a couple were remixes, some of them were kinda original. Good choices! Programming: 15 out of 15!!!!!!! THAT'S RIGHT! Xorlak may as well be one of greatest Toolkit progrrammers, if not the best. Well, okay, maybe he left behind a couple bugs, or left some small sections of the game out, but aside from that..... PERFECT!!!!!!!! Congrats, Xorlak! Final Score: Well I haven't completed it yet, but for now, my score is................. 92 out of 100!!! Probobly not just one of my favorites RPG Toolkit games, but also one of my favorite RPG games of all time!
