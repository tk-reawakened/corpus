# Dark Age 2

> *Review by DarkElite*
> Now this review is based on the Dark Age 2 demo . Differences may exist between the demo version, and the full version of the game. -Graphics- 22/25 The DA2 graphics are very well made and fit every bit of the story. They appear to be a Final Fantasy styled tiles, but still have that original feel! -Story- 21/25 Though the story appears to be going in little direction at points, suspensive parts are throughout the game, and it still has a very original and wonderful feel. -System- 25/25 The battle and menu systems in DA2 are amazing! Smooth and well played out, they are the best I've seen in any game! -Character Developement- 6/10 This was a bit of a disappointment for me, personally. There were no sort of connections I could feel with the main character. There was not any sort of love story in the game, which I usually enjoy. But, still, a lot of it has some to do with the background of Brash. -Enjoyment- 15/15 This game is fun to play, unfortunately it does run a bit slow at times, but thats not something wrong with the game! -Overall- 90/100 B+, great work Xorlak!! only
