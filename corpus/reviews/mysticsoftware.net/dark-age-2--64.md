# Dark Age 2

> *Review by Seveneternal*
> This review is based on the TK game Dark Age 2 (Demo) Dark Age 2... What can we say about the one of the greatest TK games in existence? Graphics, music, and coding were all decent. However, the general gameplay wasn't what I expected. Let us review... Graphics 14/20 A major aspect of the game are graphics. I cannot say that these graphics were the best. However, they were good enough that I could generally tell what it was. But the problem with this graphic is, that it seems a bit too flat. It doesn't use much shading, and sometimes lacks texture. Music 18/20 Another important part of the game. Personally, I think the musics were done nicely. It has been chosen carefully to fit the surrounding. Not much to say about this one. Story 15/20 I didn't get too much of the story, seeing that it is a demo. The story is good, but lacks originality. But I would have to play the full version to give a true rating. Programming 18/20 I found very few flaws and everything worked smoothly enough. Again, not much to say about this. Gameplay 14/20 Again, it just had to use random encounters. I met a enemy about every 10 steps I took. It really took me away from the current situation in the game. One other thing is the walking. Maybe its the TK2's fault, but the slow reaction and the cunky movement really took the feel out of the game. I have not counted this however, mainly because... its the TK2. Overall 75/100
