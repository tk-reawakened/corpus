# Dark Mystic

> *Review by TKGB*
> Mystic Software in the Dark? No. Dark Mystic. A game with a good title but is it cool to play? Plot (1) - In the mid ages, 4 elements controlled life: Water, Fire, Earth, Dark. Dark was the most unstable and evil (obviously), so they gave a dark mystic the task of keeping it away from the world. The dark mystic was to keep on to the evil within himself and some day it might break out. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - More ripped tiles (im getting sick of these). Not alot of original artwork here. The logo and the titles are the only original artwork I see but that can be created by almost any available editing software. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (0) - Ripped music from Chrono Trigger, Golden Sun, and F&E? The game has gone so far as to rip from ANOTHER TK GAME!? The music sounds all great but since they have been heard from before, they don't make the game any better because it doesn't sound new. It does go along with the scene but since these songs are from other well known games, the scene isn't original. No point in my account. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - Not really big. You got out side and go into your neighbors house. That's mostly it. Nothing inspirational here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - No fancy mancy stuff here either. Everything is simply made. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - Here's something original in a TK game: silent protagonist. You name the char and you take the roll of that char. Really great but since this demo is really short, you don't get to fit into this persons shoes. So you are a young man who is ordered by your parents to go to your neighbors house. Thats as far as "you" go. It may be bleak but originality always gets a point. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - See my review on "A Matter of Criminal Justice" and you'll see. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - The fight isn't really neccessary here. Even though your mom says that you must be careful, the fight just doesn't fit well. The house you must go to is just across so why must you fight your way to it. It's like grabbing your gun and battling off birds and rabid dogs just to get to your corner store. Really dumb in my opinion. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - Gameplay doesn't really matter sinse this demo is so short, it wouldn't matter if you went fast or slow. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - Just like Criminal Justice, this demo is just a show-and-tell. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (3/10) - This game is best to be waited for the real release. It's better just to see the screenshots rather than downloading this game.
