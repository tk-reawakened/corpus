# Dark Mystic

- Articles
- Interviews
> *Review by Colosseus*
> You start off by reading the background story on a black background. Then, you are in a house, and your parents are talking. The house is nicely tiled, and although the tiles and character graphics are ripped, they're still nice to look at. When you move outside, there are a few flaws (like houses way too small to contain their actual inside volume). The areas can seem empty at times, but anyway - nice to look at. The gameplay is where it slips. Even though the story is great, there's way too much storytelling and way too little gameplay. The actual time you play the game before the demo ends if you just move straight for your objective, is hardly 30 seconds. The resting 10 minutes are used for reading the story, or maybe battling some bats and blobs here and there. Needs lots of polish to raise this gauge. And here's the nicest thing about the game - its pearl of a story. Alright, maybe I blazon it abroad a tad too much, but I'm serious - one of the better pieces of storywriting I've ever seen in a TK game. I really enjoyed reading it, and if the actual gameplay is able to keep up with the story, this will be one heck of a game. The music is a bit loud, and there aren't many sound effects to listen to - not at its best. However, it's not bad either, so it's worth listening to. The battle theme can get annoying though... Nice graphics, above-average sounds, great story, below-average gameplay - lots of variety here, but the value of this game still has a long way to before reaching the redline. The game is way too short, but it has a great story. Not much work done with the sounds or graphics, but they're nicely placed and fit the game. DBS and DMS, so there's nothing special programming-wise, but I'll still say that this game is well worth a try
