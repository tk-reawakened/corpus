# DarkEdge1

> *Review by TKGB*
> Well can't say this game sucks but can't say its the best either. It is even at that. Average gameplay and average storyline. Good music but bad graphics. This game is inbetween the good and the bad aspects. Plot (1) - The plot is good. The game is about 4 airbase soldiers on their mission to stop an evil monster trying to recovere the Dark Edge sword, which is a powerful weapon. The game starts as Konos, Totoro, and Ghost on their airship to a destination when aliens have invaded the ship. You fall to the ground and hit the ice and Vyredi finds you and goes for help. From there you continue to your original quest on foot. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (0) - BLEGH! The art is original but horribly created. Everything is too simple. The buildings are just squares and the shape doesn't go with its volume. The characters look like deformed stick figured legos that are horribly colored. Bitmaps are made within minutes. The attempt was great but unfortunately it didn't come out excellent. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - The music is pretty decently made. It's simple tunes but some are them are catchy..at least to me. Some people may find it kinda annoying. Most of the songs are simple melodies. One song is a great song. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - The area is great but kinda confusing. Everything looks like kinda...err...well I can't explain it, but at least there are places to go and people to see. Thought its got its problems, especially one board where the walls are invisible and other walls aren't there. Maybe an optical illusion, I don't know. But since you can get around everywhere, it isn't so bad. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (1) - Meh. The programming isn't all time high here. #Mwin sucks. Could of been better left all black. Well some good programming are the password where the password must be typed. It's on the floor and it must be on a certain order. Pretty good. Though there is one feature I like. The menu has a stat bar for MP and SMP. Whether it was downloaded from MS or home made, it don't matter, I like it alot. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - The characters are facinating. You got Vyredi who is the luitenant of the redbase, Konos the novice soldier as well as his pals Totoro and Ghost. The evil bad guy Yusoi, the one searching for the Dark Edge. All these characters are great. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (1) - Menu is from Rueben's Spiffy menu. It's pretty good but still uses the same item window, same status window, etc. from the default TK system. Well it aint so bad. Items are still dull and boring. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (1) - The fight sucks with only two people but really fun with all four characters. Enemies look stupid. Your stats are WAY up but enemies are tough. Somekind of balance there. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - The walk speed is alright. Things don't twitch or get laggy so it's fine. Conversations and cut scenes are next to perfect. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - This game is fun even if it has crummy graphics. A real nice game put together and would of been nice with better tiles and better conversations. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (9/10) - How I gave it a 9? I don't have an idea but it seems that looks can be deciving. Thought it still looks kinda dumb in my opinion. If you wish to try this game, look at some screens before deciding to try it out.
