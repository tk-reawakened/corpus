# DarkEdge1

> *Review by Dude Man*
> Dark Edge 1 Review By: Dude Man Game By: Kahn Graphics 13/20 Well the graphics didn't really look too good. They had shading, and it does look like he put a bit of time making them. Or he just isn't skilled at making tiles. Nothing too wrong with that, I'm not myself. If I was him, I would have just downloaded tiles. Game Play 13/20 The game was pretty fun, but not the funnest TK game I have played. It did use the default battle system, and Mwin, I can't make a BS myself *well at least a good one* so it's probably for the best that he used it. However I don't really like that default Mwin, it's too...boring. The music wasn't really that good, it seemed a bit to mono and empty, like the midis I've made, however the final battle music was actually good, and I give him a sticker for that! *puts a sticker that looks like a music note on Kahn's forehead* And there's plenty more where that came from! Programming 13/20 The programming, well it did have quite a few bugs in it, I believe at story lines and such, and this odd thing kept happening, the character, kept moving downward all the time, but I think I'll ignore that, because I think it's happened before with other games, because I remember that happening in Dark Age. Story 18/20 Story was very good! Best part of the game. It made sense why all of the characters join you, (and that is lacking in a lot of even professional games, like Fallout)The ending however was a sad ending, the bad guy won....and that sucks...he kills everyone but the main character at the end...:'( it's sad...And I kinda hate it how Ghost sacrificed himself, for nothing, but he didn't have much point of being alive really... Music/sound 14/20 The sounds were, okay. Not alot of them however, it had some cool gunfire sounds, and some odd magic sounds, but that was about it. Overall 71/100 I say it was a good game. It kind of puzzles me, how it could have taken 2 years to make this game. Like that's longer then I've even had Toolkit and I have finished a game. But don't get me wrong here, it was a good game. Keep up with those awesome story lines, and make sure you finish Dark Edge 2, because I want to know what happens and the game is basically the beginning story for Dark Edge 2. THE END!
