# Dawn of the Dead

> *Review by TKGB*
> I never seen Dawn of the Dead because I'm not a big fan of horror flicks. Well I may never has seen it but I can tell that this game doesn't reflect the movie because it looks too unreal to the comparison of the movie. Read on. Plot (0) - The whole planet has been zombified. Zombies are running the show. Roger's friend Stephen found a helicopter ready to get the heck out of there but it needs gas. You must go and find gas to get the helicopter to work. To get gas should get you problems with zombies everywhere but in this game, you can easily acquire it assuming you can kill the zombies. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (0) - Rip tiles from the Jewel of Destiny (Powerhouse edition) and some original art that is anything but original (as in other TK games have crappy drawn enemies). This place doesn't look like its being invaded by dead people. Its gots green trees and grass and im pretty sure the movie takes place in a city, not some mountain park place. Aich! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (0) - CHRONO TRIGGER MUSIC DOESN'T BELONG HERE!!!!!!!!!!!!! OI! This game uses songs from Chrono and Final Fantasy. Some of the FF music fits but CT music is a big no no. I don't like it. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - Pathetic. It doesn't really resemble anything like a zombiefied world. It all looks too clean. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - If attempt to make good programming was put forth, this game would come out worse. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - I believe they are based on the movie, but since I never seen it, I got to assume they are. You got Roger and Stephen whom you play here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - The profile pics are photographs possibly from the movie. They look good but other than that, this is useless. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - VERY ANNOYING! The zombies are too damn strong, especially the cops and the boss is impossible to defeat. Not good. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - You walk fine in this game. Thats all I got to say since this game lacks any real "game" in it. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - What do you think? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (2/10) - A very bad attempt to make a game based on a movie. I rather watch the movie than attempt to finish this demo, that is if you can finish it..
