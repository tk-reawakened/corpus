# Dawn of the Dead

> *Review by Marcus*
> Story, What's it all about?: 6/10 First of all, I'd like to say I downloaded and decided to review this game because I am a horror game fanatic. Also I do enjoy watching horror movies and one of my all time favourites was indeed Dawn Of The Dead. I was quite disappointed with this game because it didn't live up or even compare in the slightest ways to Dawn Of The Dead the movie. The story wasn't the best it was very basic and simple; You take control of Rodger and your objective is to find a can of Gasoline and give it to your friend Stephen who had hijacked a helicopter so you and Stephens wife can escape. I couldn't complete this game because it had one of the worst bugs ever! See battles for detail. Boards: 5/10 They weren't that bad some tiles were original but the rest were all mainly default. There were quite a few buildings and houses but half of them you couldn't enter, the buildings you could enter lacked in a lot of detail. Graphics: 4/10 Nothing special here just the basic default graphics. Some were original but most of them were default. Items: 4/10 Well, it was only a demo and the only items you could get were basically grenades and first aid kits, oh yes and of course the gasoline can. Remember this game is based on a horror film so there were obviously no magic attacks or special moves. Battles: 1/10 Well let's just say if you go into a battle it's game over, the enemy carries on attacking you but when it gets to your turn the characters just stare into space and does thing. Basically it will not let you have a turn, this is a major bug I restarted the game a number of times and it just keeps happening. I tried the same game on my other computer and again the annoying bug kicked in and when it got to my go this time the system locked up. I give the battles a score of one due to it did actually have battles. Enemies: 2/10 Well I did get a glimpse of them, and they are actually very well drawn but all the enemies looked exactly the same and had some stupid names like; Zombie A, Zombie B, Zombie cop etc. Visibly I couldn't tell how much HP they had. Programming: 4/10 Nothing special what so ever just a message window with scrolling font and a few bitmap images. Some programs were not conditional active so I could just run them over and over again. Bugs: 3/10 Need I say more? Overall: Not the worst game ever programmed but I really don't think it was wise programming a game token of from the movie. After all though it was original and makes a change from having a final fantasy want to be game. Download it, have a bit of fun for 10mins and then get rid of it. I suppose it's worth a lookjust try not to fall into any battles. Final: 29/80 or 36% This review was written by Marcus Lewton, dated June 16, 2002
