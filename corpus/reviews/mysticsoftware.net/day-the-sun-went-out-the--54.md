# Day the Sun Went Out, The

> *Review by Marcus Lewton*
> The Day the Sun Went Out Reviewer: Marcus Lewton Game Author: N/A -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Story, What's it all about? (7/10) I can tell you a bit about the story but not much due I couldn't complete the whole game because a lot of programmes were constantly being run over and over again From what I got, the story was about 3 professional soldiers who kept the peace of their quite little town. Until one day an enemy attacks the town and runs away all three soldiers chase after it and end up in this really weird place where they discover about 10 members from there soldier Legion all dead and cut up on the floor. That's when you meet your second boss Dominic (think that's his name) he is responsible for all the deaths. After the fight you get some type of "Sun Power" which you then use to your advantage and on your quest. Carter Lauren Nick These are all drawn from scratch And are the main characters in the whole game Boards (6/10) For once most of the tiles were original but poorly drawn though, but I admire and give credit for his hard work. Graphics (7/10) Nothing special here just the basic default graphics. There were a lot of original tiles though and all the characters had been drawn from scratch Items (8/10) Very good there were loads of items with some really wicked names and each item had it's own special power, but unfortunately there was no graphics for any of the items Battles (4/10) Pretty poor the special moves were basically nothing there was no flashing light or well drawn out bitmap images just the original battle system. All of the characters were nicely drawn but sadly all the characters were facing the wrong way and in some battles only one of the characters showed up. Enemies (7/10) Not that bad they were all-original but were poorly drawn nevertheless it must of took a lot of time drawing them. One of the major mistakes in the whole enemy department was the actual enemies they had pretty stupid names like Sausage and Mouldy Sausage the enemies didn't fit that well into the game. The enemies HP were pretty fair not to easy and not to hard. Programming (3/10) All I can basically say about the programming is very poor! There were no special effects what so ever all it was was Mwin this Mwin that there was no line of coding what so ever about pushing items and push character. When an event occurred on a board such as a the main character punching someone all that happened was the message window appeared saying something like: "*Nick punched Xorlak*" (No Xorlak isn't in it I was just using it as an example) Also another major let down in the programming section was that the most important programs kept running over and over again none of them were set on conditional active! Bugs (4/10) There weren't any serious bugs but there were some quite annoying ones, such as character disappearance. Overall Not the worst game ever, but there is definitely something missing. The game doesn't make you want to play on, you play it once and that's it you will not go on it again It's a shame really a lot of hard work went into the game but I think with a little more checking through the programs and perhaps designing better enemies and using more advance code I think this game could go quite far. Final score 46/80 (58%)
