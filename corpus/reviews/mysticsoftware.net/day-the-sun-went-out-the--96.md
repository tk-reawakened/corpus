# Day the Sun Went Out, The

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by TKGB*
> The name caught my attention one day so I decided to download it but then I saw just how...blegh it was. Not what I expected. REALLY not what I expected. Plot (1) - This plot is a keeper. A gigantic wolf wanted to eat the sun, and since we all like sunshine in our lives, the heroes of the land, known as the viking warriors, decided to get him. The battle was bloody but the battle was won by the warriors. They vanished the wolf to the ice caps. This battle was now known throughout the ages as Ragnarok. Throughout my quest to learn as much beastier as I can, I learned that this story is probably based on Fenrir. Fenrir was a very strong wolf that not even chains can keep him in his place. The gods offered him a challange: to break a magical ribbon to test his strength. At first Fenrir wasn't really eager but he eventually agreed only if one of the gods would place his hand within his jaws. Tyr agreed on this. Fenrir tried as much as he could be could not break the ribbon and ripped Tyr's hand off. Fenrir was then locked away. On ragnarok, he would break free, kill and devour Odin. Vidkar would then kill him. Err...sorry for that mythology lesson so lets get back to the review. Probably this version of the game was to feature Fenrir after he broke away from his prison and probably was to use the sun as a weapon to kill Odin. Pretty good storyline. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (0) - LOL! This is a joke right? Not even a really good intent to draw decent. This artwork looks like it was drawn by a 6 year old with crayons or something. Originality is always good but MAN this is just awful. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - Ripped music from Final Fantasy games and Lufia. I never played either games so they sound good to my ear but it might get repitive to those who have. Though these songs don't seem repetitive and might just be one of those songs you can listen to all day and all night. I give it a point. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - The outer world is just as bad looking as the town. Not really attractive. Looks huge but you learn that it isn't. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - The programmer was so lazy, he didn't even put a variable activation on the conversations. When you talk to Sir Guy, a battle insues with a giant. After the battle, he is suppose to say something else but he says the same thing and the same battle goes one. Really weak. Also they programmer didn't guarantee a conformation about joining with your teamates. You can talk to Sir Guy without having your partners and he will still talk to you as if you have them. Weird and dumb. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - You got Nick who looks like a swordsman, Lauren who is a fencer extrodinaire, and Carter who is a lancer and summoner as well. Other cast members is your boss, who is like a father to them, Sir Guy. Ronin and Frieda who happen to be your trainers, and Domino who is the bad guy of this story. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - Err the profile pics don't look good here. They are goofy looking. Items are boring and repititive. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - Moldy sausage as enemies? Umm...weird. Anyways, the battle is upfront to the enemy but the chars don't make it look so. They face you instead of the enemy and when they attack, they don't even look back. What is that about? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - Char's move really fast so you are always avoiding fighting most of the time. Not that fighting is really good here anyways. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - Not. How?
