# DBZ: The Cell Game

> *Review by RPGLand2000*
> Plot: Well, it's...the DBZ plot. Anyway, this game follows the plot well...which means it's a bad plot. Anyway, at least the plot is told semi-well...2 out of 5 Characters: ....See above. 2 out of 5. Graphics Merely okay. The opening cinema, though ripped from a SNES Dragon Ball Zeta game, was good. Original graphics for everything. 4 out of 5. Gameplay There a few fights in this; though it uses the default system, these fights are still fun. 4 out of 5. Music N/A. Conclusin Dragon Ball Z: The Cell game is a fun, short demo. The only faults are really with the source material; though the original source has a poor plot, the plot is at least told well. 3 out of 5.
