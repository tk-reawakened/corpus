# Deceit and Conquest

> *Review by TK Game Boy*
> The best the Ive seen in the toolkit. This games shows what the toolkit can accomplish. A excellent game. Graphics: The graphics are perfect. Some of the scene shots are greatly shown. The characters have been drawn with many postures and scenes making more enjoyable to watch. Media: The music in this game rocks (especially when you enter a battle). The creator used MP3s instead of MIDI files. You have to hear it to believe it. Plot: The creator of the game must of thought of the story for sometime. The game is about a war in a world and you play the role of Hal Geran. A plot so good, its becoming a book. Programming: Let me start of by the battle system. Highly programmed unlike anything I have ever seen in the toolkit. More complex than the Never Ending Scrolls battle system. Though the creator used some of the default menus from the toolkit but it doesnt matter cause its the battle system from the toolkit that annoys us. Game Play: The conversations in the game seem long and it take sometime to load to what the other guy is going to say. If it where more faster then it would be great. Besides that, it has splendid game play. Fun Factor: I enjoy the battle system a lot. Its easy to use when you know what to do. There is a lot of reading in this game so for those of you who dont like reading then you may not enjoy it a lot. Bugs: I have found no bugs in this game. There is the occasional green background from the enemies attacking where its suppose to be transparent but it doesnt matter to most people. Conclusion: The game is great. The story is good and the graphics are at its best. If you have the time to download it, download it. Rating: 9/10 This review was written by TK Game Boy, dated June 5, 2002
