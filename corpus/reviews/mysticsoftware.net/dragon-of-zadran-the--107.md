# Dragon of Zadran, The

> *Review by TKGB*
> Worst 20 MB I ever spent. Plot (1) - One legend speaks about a band of adventurers out to destroy a dragon and a mage. Their story has been told for many generations. The story is not told to you cause you play it. So this game is about the legend and the adventurers. To know the story, you must play. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (0) - AAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! And I thought Black Walkway was terribly tiled and 6 Wizards terribly drawn. This takes the cake. OH GOD! It's a nightmare! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (0) - BOO! The music is the Fo Sho music pack and one song that was recording without editing. BOO! Oi but there is something interesting. Home made SFX. They are suppose to be death sounding, magic fluttering sound effects but what can you get from a 12 year old kid? Blegh. There more funny than serious, especially the part where a certain enemies death cry goes "oh my god you killed me you bastard" in a kids voice and in monotone. Everything is is crap and can make you def. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - YEESH! Terrible. This doesn't look like a planet to me. Everything is everywhere. Nothing looks like anything. Nothing resembles anything. Not to mention the battles and the area. I mean you got ppl standing idly by and yet monsters attack you. You'd think these ppl would get attacked too. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - Small attempt at programming but it's horrible. The programmer wanted to do fishing and mining by just stepping on the rock but the programming is so terrible, you can mine a million times by one step and it wont stop till you get something. That is pretty much cheating your way to ore or fish. The conversations are worse. Some conversations dont end with #Wait() so you miss a part. WHAT!? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (0) - NO NO NNNNNNNOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!! Frap and Tano's identity had been violated by crappy editing. OI! and is Lupe a girl or boy cause he's got Tano's char. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - NO MORE NO MORE!!!!!!! The profile pics are from Golden Sun!!!!!!!!!!!! And Lupe has Issac's profile. The third member looks REALLY crappyly drawn. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - Fights are terrible. The enemies are stupid and you can easily get so strong, no one can defeat you. OI! One particular enemy is the Programmer who happens to be *GASP* the creator of the game. I dont care if he is 12 years old, I like kicking his ass >:-). -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - The characters walk sloppily. The conversations go too fast. The battle is not worth it. Do you wish for me to go on? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - This game is more frustriating than fun. BlEGH! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (1/10) - OI! This game is not worth the 20 MB. Don't downloaded it cause it aint worth it.
