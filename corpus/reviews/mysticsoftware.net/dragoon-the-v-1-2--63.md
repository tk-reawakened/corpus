# Dragoon, The (v.1.2)

> *Review by oblivious_casualty*
> Ripped graphics. You'd think that with as large a company as Revival Gaming, they'd have one artist to help with The Dragoon. At least he used them correctly. Although not the greatest music ever found in a TK game, it is original. However, all of the music is ridiculously short. MrB could have avoided this by forcing the music to loop a few times when he made the music. The music is fitting for their respective parts of the game, but near the end of the demo, I had to turn off the games music and listen to some CDs. It's cliche. I'm not going to pretend like it isn't. But I've seen MUCH much worse than this. Pretty decent. I'll probably play the next demo to see what happens. Not bad. The battles are actually compelling at times. But after the first 5 minutes of the same enemies, battles become a chore. At least there's plenty of variety in enemies. Ugh. The code for the custom systems is #branch heaven. This leads to a few errors in the battle system and menu system. Nothing too disrupting, though. At least it has custom systems to begin with. Surprisingly, I was satisfied with the length of this demo. It felt complete. The board design was superb. I'm still wondering how Jacobs and Ervin got into that cave to begin with. There was only one exit. I would've liked to be able to enter all of the houses in the village. Ah, the bugs. Buggy bug bugs. No transparent background for cursor and other graphics. Some programs, like the healing spring, ran twice. Sometimes the text will run outside the message window. The play time clock reset at one hour. Only the Slash skill showed up in the Dragoon menu. In the town, the 3 buildings in the back gave me an error when i tried to enter them. Method not found. At least disable debugging when you release a demo. Replay value? No, I wouldn't bother to play this demo again. Once is enough for me. This demo was fun. Despite the ripped graphics and bugs abound, I had fun. Not bad for a demo. **Graphics**
