# Dust Ball

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by RPGLand2000*
> Dust Ball is a odd TK game. Graphics: These are the best graphics in any TK game besides Sophia's other games and the game Friends and Enemies. There is nothing at all to fault here. 5 out of 5. Story/Writing: The writing in this game is great. Nothing else to say here. 5 out of 5. Gameplay: The lacking part of this game. Dust Ball uses the original battle system, and has too little gameplay at times (it can seem). 3 out of 5. Conclusion: Dust Ball, despite the somewhat lacking combat, is still a excellent TK game you must play. The graphics are the best, besides FaE (which is the greatest TK game, in my opinion). It is still a fun game. 4 out of 5.
