# Dust Ball

> *Review by BlackDragon*
> Dustball Reviewer: BlackDragon Game Author: Sophia -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Brief Intro As a small cat, DustBall used to live with a person. Being DustBall's first word, "Sausage!!" got the cat kicked out, for being a talkign cat. Everywhere DustBall couldn't seem to fit in, and got booted out. One day DustBall got booted off a ship and was found by a boy, who eventually knocks over the princess accidently and gets a noble mad... In an attempt to win the boy love from the princess, DustBall begins a couragous adventure dressed as a person.......... Atmosphere 10/10 The atmosphere in this game is outstanding. Its consistant comic-style cartoon atmosphere is extremly well done and very involving, unique and exciting.. Story 7/10 The story to DustBall is well placed and extremely humorous, especially during cut-scenes and charactor conversations. However DustBall is loosing points because of the princess idea, only because of originallity on that part, but a high rating is maintained by the idea of a talking cat, and by the sub-stories met within the game. I absolutely love the concept of DustBall with its extremely funny comic style role play. Graphics 9/10 Very consistant graphics. The comic/cartoon style graphics fit very well with every other aspect of the game, although there are a few areas that are a little too consistant. All graphics are original (i think) and the only dissapointment i had with graphics is some enemy graphics. One of the best things about DustBall is the main charactor emotion pictures within the message window, well done indeed. Also loading screens are a favourite. Sound 9/10 The sound backs up the atmosphere very well. Sound effects are very kind to the atmosphere and are original. Most of the music is original, but I think I've heard some songs before, but not sure.. Battle music is a favourite. Originality 9/10 As I have said, the game is extremely original, a lot of hard work has been put into this area. Haha, I think I have already said too much about originality in the otehr areas. Reliability 10/10 I found absolutely no bugs what so ever within the game, and found in most cases, realism was kept in place as much as possible. This is a good effort and reveives full marks. The game runs very smoothly with very little lag, and the only bug I found really wasn't to do with demo 1 and will no doubt be fixed for demo 2. Game Play 9/10 This game was very, very fun and so interesting, I didn't really leave my seat untill I finished demo 1. The only thing I have to say is "more, more and more please!!". Cut scenes, loading graphics, message window emotion pictures, smooth gameplay, consistant atmosphere etc.... The only thing I was a little annoyed at was going back and forth through the maze between the boy's cabin and the town. But the games humor makes up for it. Gamers will find the word "Sausage" very funny constantly throughout the game. ( - and i can't forget the idea of climbing trees ;) - ) Over all 90/100 Additional comments: For the players: The start may seem a little strange, killing worms and all, but the story gets quite interesting. Don't get put off by constantly walking from cabin to maze and make sure you complete the demo before making comment. For Sophia: That bug I mentioned on your message board needs to be fixed. Otherwise, great game and I think a classic of tk cause its unique, more ppl have to play it. I can't wait for demo 2, and i hope its out soon. Oh by the way, cool comics.. BlackDragon
