# Dust Ball

> *Review by Seveneternal*
> This review is based on the TK game DustBall (Demo) Dustball. Beautiful game. It's really nice to see a game that doesn't revolve around some super-serious storyline with swords clashing all the time. If this was a full version game with decent costom BS and Menu, I would have gave this game at least 80/100. There were only a few points of dissapointment. Allow me to explain... Graphics 17/20 One of the strongest points of the game. A bit of repetition, and more defined shading would have helped, but besides, its really good for its style. Music 13/20 I noticed most of the music from other games. It was ripped, but most of it really fit the mood right. Story 15/20 It may not be one of those super-complicated and serious storylines, but for some reason, I was attracted by the basic concept. The style and the music set it off just right. It also explained the cat's perspective and view really well. Programming 14/20 I didn't really see much as of programming. But I found no flaws and the game was very smooth. The only thing that prevent this from getting a high score was its all-default systems. Gameplay 14/20 Great gameplay. There were no extremely long waits, and it ran really smoothly. Also, I loved the intro. However, I didn't really like the forest between the house and the overworld. The battles made going back and out a tedious work. Overall 73/100
