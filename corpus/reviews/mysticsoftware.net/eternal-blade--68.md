# Eternal Blade

> *Review by Seveneternal*
> This review is based on the Eternal Blade (Demo) Eternal Blade... As overused as it sounds, the quality of the game itself shouldn't be judged by its title. In this case however, it can. Graphics 10/20 This is your average beginners graphics. It is not too bad, while it does not have anything too fantastic about it. A little shading and definitive texture would have helped. It looked a bit too flat. Music 12/20 Not much music on this one. However, it was used carefully and it didn't sound too bad either. The thing was, there was only about 3 or 4 songs used. Story 9/20 Now we look at the story. Im basing this off of what was told so far. The main character goes out, fights some underling of the evil boss, and he has to find this "eternal blade"... That's about far as it gets. This is very simple and often used. I remember a story generator program making things like this. This absolutely needs work. Programming 13/20 Default everything. Dialogues and short cutscenes. However, the costom Mwin was a plus. Gameplay 10/20 Default. The mere presence of this word makes the developers tremble in fear. Everything changed too quickly. The basic idea is going into the squary overworld, fighting the evil thing, watch a nice little cutscene and then you go to the end of the overworld board where the game stops. It's not too exciting if you ask me. Overall 54/100
