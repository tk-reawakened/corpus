# Final Fantasy: Sage of the Seals

> *Review by catwhowalksbyhimself*
> The graphis are mostly rm95, which is not a bad thing, but it is hardly worth more then seven. The moster graphics are also not original. The graphics are, however well-used and the fighting graphics are indistiguishable from the original set. Not much to say here. I barely notice it, in fact, which means that there was nothing outstandingly bad and that everything fit in pretty well. The game's strong point. It is not terribly original, mind you, but it very compellingly written. Some things could be tweaked, or made easier to understand, but it is definitely is good start. The cutscenes are well programed and do catch your interest. The beginning is a little too tough, as there is no way to heal your one character, and there are lots of imps. Adding a healing pool or potion or something would fix this. The rest is pretty well designed. No real problems here. I did not see any bugs or problems in any of the programming, although there is not much here yet. The character introduction and custom name dialog are nice touches copied from FF6. Very short. It only took a few minutes to finish. Of course, this is just the first demo. You are left completely hanging. The programmer could have chosen a better place to leave off the demo. There are a LOT of spelling and grammar errors here. The message box is also hard to read. Could have been better done. Not much here yet, so not real reason to replay. This game is fun, in spite of its flaws. A little more work and it could be a very good Toolkit game. A word to the author: don't blow it now. Be careful with your characters and make them unique. Keep the story moving and don\'t let it become too cliche. And please keep working on it, I want to play the next demo! **Graphics: 7**
