# Forgotten Legends

> *Review by TKGB*
> R# made a game? No way. But he did. Forgotten Legends. Well not just him. He had help from alot of people and the thing that goes through my mind is if a team made this game, why does it look like it lacks many factors? Everything still looks kinda crummy. Well whatever the case, it doesn't spoil it. Plot (1) - One day Roc was going to the market as usually when all of a sudden a spell was cast upon him that made him fall asleep. He awoke only to find his town ashambles. Everyone blamed Roc because the thing that attacked the village was after him and they were all sure that Roc was killed by it, even his own mother believed this. Roc, along with his friend D'jom, decided to go and hunt down this monster. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - Umm...uhh...uhh...okay...wait give me a minute to think of something...well...err...I can't explain this. It's good but not "good". You got RM2K tile sets mixed with others as well along with some custom drawn tiles. Everything looks alright but at the same time, umm, not very appealing. Well whatever the case, they were used nicely. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - most of the music are MP3's. Though they aren't D&C quality, they do the job. You got suspenseful tunes as well as the royalty type and some orchestrated songs as well. MIDI's are also in this game. They are nice and go with this game. I don't believe R# had the ability to create the MP3's but who knows maybe he did. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - The enviroments are plentyful. There are alot of boards in this game. I couldn't get to the rest cause my stats sucks but the world is big I can tell you. There are some 1000 more boards here to keep you pleased and they do not suck. They look wonderous. Some of the programming put into these look weird but thats not really a matter. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - Shotty work. The programming in the conversations can use alot of work because it gets kinda confusing. But thats not all. The inn don't don't work, some of the graphics don't get display, methods aren't activated at times. Overall, this game could of used more programming and effort into it. Now who was in charge of this area, hmm? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - Well I could only get 3 chars because the inn wouldn't let me heal and the battles are too tough and boring to get stats. Roc is the protagonist who lost his home town, his friend, D'jom, travels along and a woman named Lami seems to able to help you guys. Im sure there are other chars you can have but ACK, with such low stats and hard enemies, how the heck can you make it? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - Blegh. Boring, as always. The profile pic is kinda good but naw. Not to mention only Roc has a profile pic. No black background which is nice. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - YIKES! The enemies are too tough and experience don't increase anything. Even though their HP and other stats are around the 10's area, your as well in that area, including the FP and DP. There is no balance here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - Well this game does go well. Roc seems to walk fast but so do the battles appear at that speed. Everthing seems to run accordingly. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - Err...hard to say. This game is frustruating but at the same time, it does have its good times. The story evolves well and all but of course the gameplay seems to want to step away from this. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (7/10) - Well the team is long gone now, but I hardly notice that this game was created by a team. R#'s would of had a better bet doing this by himself. Err no offense to anyone in the team.
