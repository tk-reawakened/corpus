# Friends and Enemies

> *Review by RPGLand2000*
> This game is one of the best games to ever be made for the RPGToolkit, and it is a pity it was never finished. Graphics FAE has a unique graphics style. These graphics are excellent as well as unique. 5. Sound Never listened to it (need to start doing that). N/A Story The story is told extremely well, better than the majority of TK games. If you want to learn how to incorperate a plot into your game, play this as a example. 5. Gameplay The combat in this game is the most fun in a TK game since Godiva: Alpha. 5. Programming No real flaws. 5. Conclusion: My feelings on this game are obvious. If not obvious by this review, you could tell them based on the fact I planned to finish it on my own once. It is a pity Deadalus left the community. 5 out of 5.
