# Gendin's Quest 2

> *Review by Hyren*
> Gendin's Quest 2 Reviewer: Hyren Game Author: Doc Wellsy -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Story 8/10 Fairly original story. These three amulets were stolen and the prince tries to recover them so he runs away. Then you have to go find him and then the amulets. Graphics 8.5/10 The tiles were all original and were very good. There was a nice variety and they fit together. Music 8/10 The music was nearly perfect for the mood of the game. Plus, I think it's original! Enemies 5/10 There were only two enemies and both were very hard. I died at the first boss. The enemies were way to strong for the beginning of the game. Programming 9/10 A custom menu plus a custom BS! Both were done very well and in battles, you could move around to try and avoid attacks and stuff. Fun 7.5/10 An extremely fun game-very addicting. And it's a small download! What are you waiting for!? Get it! Originality 6/10 It seemed to share a few themes with the Legend of Zelda series. Intro 8/10 Very nice programming in the intro. Items 3/10 The game's one weak point. Only red and green potions that were way overpriced. Bugs 9/10 Very good in this department. Only a few very minor problems. Overall 72/100 Very enjoyable but difficult. A small download.
