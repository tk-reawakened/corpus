# Godiva: Alpha 1.1

> *Review by BlackDragon*
> Graphics : If there's one thing I like about this game, its the graphics. All the tiles and all the charactors are perfectly fitting and give a true RPG feel. The enemies were ok, but in battle, my charactors were always invisible. I give the graphics 8/10. Music : The music was, if enthusiastic, a little out of place. The music I enjoyed was the battle and the cave-maze. The inside music suited quite well. But overall the music was pretty poor. I give the music 5/10. Sounds : I was definately dissapointed with the lack of realistic sounds, especially in battle. I won't even bother to comment further on the sounds... I give the sounds 2/10 Intro and Start: The intro was ok, however a little short and boring. The part when the King (Or whoever he is) talks to the others is ok, but too short. I love the fact that you can choose your charactor class and rename them. Then a random charactor from those classes starts off being your friend. I was impressed by the charactor selection etc... I give the intro and start 7/10. Items and Weapons: Although I didn't buy much, I noticed that each item/weapon, could be used only by specific charactor classes, that was good. But when I went to the pub, I noticed something. Only a fool would buy coconut milk or bread, because they give less HP and SMP than the Milk, which is cheaper!... Anyway, there were a lot of items and I was pretty much impressed. I also noticed that some charactors had SMP, oviously for Talismans, but only the Red Mage could use them in the demo 1.1. I give the items and weapons 6/10 Battle and Enemies: The battles were dissapointing. I couldn't see my charactors, and all the enemies could be ran away from, so I completed the cave-maze by running away from the battles. The boss took a while, but I could get in at least 3 shots each before he could strike, then after I beat him, it crashed... The normal enemies were good-looking, but not much else. I was extremely amused by the fact that you fight bums on the world map. I give the battle and enemies 4/10 Menues: The menues were good. The start was a bit buggy, but the options menu was a good-looking custom one. The interface was relitively smooth and user friendly, except for the start. I give the menues 6/10 Boards and Worlds: The world interface was poor. It had very... unsuited music and the boards were empty. (Apart from the main one). The castle (Or whatever) was a board filled with my charactor... ok then. I give the maps 3/10 OverAll: Overall, I was dissapointed. But, if your curious, play it anyway. Overall, I give Godiva : Alpha 1.1 (65%)
