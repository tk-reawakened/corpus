# Godiva: Alpha 1.1

> *Review by R040382*
> Description: Godiva: Alpha has many new features, and innovative style. When starting off, you get to choose your characters and practically play them through the story, and plot. There are some secrets in the game where you dont normally see. However, I cannot reveal them due to the request of the game creator. Simply, you move through the game, and eventually meeting a boss at a very complex cave. This game also provides a score rating if you manage to successfully complete the first demo. This is rarely seen, due to the lack of people managing to complete the demo due to its difficulty. A word of advice, train a lot against weaker foes and progress through the game a step at a time. You can easily download this from the very resourceful Mystic Software site. Strong Point: This game presents new and very innovative ideas, and the main overall of it is very well presented. Weak Point: Even though the game itself is very good, the game lacks story, plot and non-playable characters. It could use more speech to enhance the interest of the game, and to recognize what the game's objectivity is all about. Rating: Story/Plot: 3/5 The game lacks plot, and story. However, there is a nice intro at the starting which gives us a starting idea of the games story. Hopefully in the near future, the next demo will improve on this part to enhance the overall plot. Game Play: 4.5/5 An interesting game, that is catchy to play. It shows new ideas that are not presented from other various toolkit games, and is known to differentiate from any average rpgtoolkit game. Graphics: 4/5 The games graphics is visually appealing, but however they are unoriginal. I am not obsessed over this little issue, so I will be basing its rating by bypassing this issue. Graphics for enemies, characters also pretty much blends with the game itself. Sound/Sound Effects: 5/5 Godiva: Alpha shows us various sounds effects even when selecting an option in battling. Little un-noticeable things like that are added bonuses in game play experience. It also shows us that the creator is using his time to work on this game. Even the originality of the music is quite pleasing, and up to beat. Originality: 3.5/5 The games graphics are not original, but nevertheless the new features that are in this game backs it up, giving it a satisfactory score of 3.5/5. Overall Score: 20/25 Percentage: 80% Reviewers Comments: I recommend those people to download and try this game out. This isnt a waste of time, and I guarantee that you wont regret it.
