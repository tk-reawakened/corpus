# Godiva: Alpha 1.1b

> *Review by Hyren*
> Wow. Godiva Alpha's a really good game, but...... Bugs 3/10 OK. The game was awsome but BUG INFESTED! Whenever I try to equip my Red Mage with a Fire Talisman, the game locks up my computer! There's a few minor bugs with the town tiles, but nothing serious. The castle is completely messed up, though. Originality 8/10 The very first (and possibly last) class chosing RPG for the Toolkit. Programming 8/10 He made an awsome custom menu! There's even a global map! Tiles 7/10 Hey, they're ripped, but he made em look good. Especially the characters. Fun 10\10 BY FAR THE MOST FUN RPG EVER! It's addicting! Even with the bugs, it makes you want to keep playing! You must get this game! Music 10/10 It fits in perfectly with the game's mood. AND IT'S ALL ORIGINAL! Sound 10/10 Sound FX for EVERYTHING! Almost every enemy had a different attacking or blocking sound! Enemies 8/10 I love the variety in the enemies (especially the bum, he, he, he...), except WHY THE HECK DO YOU FIGHT CATS AND DOGS!? THOSE ARE NOT EVIL MONSTERS COMING FROM SOME DARK PASSAGE! Intro 6/10 The music was cool, but it lacked pictures of people and stuff. Items 10/10 There's so many different items in one shop you have to scroll down to see them all! The items are awsome! Overall: 80% This is an awsome and addicting game! You have to download it to believe it! Can't wait for the next demo. This Review is written by Hyren, dated June 1, 2002
