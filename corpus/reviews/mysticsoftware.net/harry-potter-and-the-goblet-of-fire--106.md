# Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

> *Review by TKGB*
> Well I'm not a big, huge fan of promo games. Promo games are games based on movies and TV shows but thats just what I call them, but when I saw a Harry Potter TK game, why my curiosity just had to see it. What I expected, I got. Just another TK demo. Being Harry Potter, you expect something better, but it aint. Plot (0) - The game starts off as Harry, Ron, and Hermoine wake up from a disturbance outside. They disturbance was being cause by people in black coats and hoods. Harry also lost his wand and is looking for it. They reach a submit and were approached by 3 men, one of them being Mr. Weasley. The rest I didn't get. Thats as much as you are gonna get. The rest is trivial. There is no point in knowing that Harry went to a quidditch game if it has nothing to do with the story. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (0) - The tiles? You guessed it. Ripped! Everything is ripped from the RM2K set and I'm pretty sure Harry and the other chars are ripped too but if they aint, then its a good job of drawing them. The profile pics are photographs from the Harry Potter movies and they don't look good. There facial expressions are not profile pic material. A profile pic should be still and giving some sort of facial expression but they are expressing too much. Not good. As for the tiles, they are being used so much they are boring. I don't like it. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - One good thing is the music which is from the Harry Potter movies. The same brand music goes well in this game. It is just great but not exactly rememberable. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (0) - Too small. There is like only 6 boards then the demo is over. There are a couple of people outside like that Draco kid. Other than fighting, this place is dull. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - No real programming here. Simple stuff like talking and all that is there and thats the only thing there is. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - The characters are based on the books which is good. You got Harry, Ron, and Hermoine, Mr. Weasley, Draco, and a couple of others that appear in the game. I wish there was more to this game but there isn't but atleast the game is getting the right track. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - Useless. The profile pics do nothing to it since they are wrongly used. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - The same old boring default TK battle systems and whats worse is that the magic Xuper suX! You get one spell only: Flipendo, whatever that is, and everyone has that same move. I would think that Hermoine would have more than this. Also they have low SMP! I thought they were wizards. If they been in the school for like 3 years, they would have more SMP that this. And the graphics for the spell sucks as well. Its just a small, drop like thing hitting them. I would expect some kind of comet move or something. BLEGH! The battle is really stupid. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - Despise dumb battles, the gameplay aint so bad. Mainly the conversations are really nicely written. The words sounds like its coming from the book. It's great read. Everything else is dull but the conversations aren't so bad. Expect for the last part which I didn't get. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (0) - Naw. This game lacks graphics for alot of things. Really dull demo game. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (3/10) - Well as I expected, just another TK demo. This game is going on the right track but looks like its getting lazy. The programming needs work and graphics need to be emphized and make it look more...I dunno...magical!
