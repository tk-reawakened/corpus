# Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by dreammakermaster*
> Yesh, it is I, Dream Maker Master, and I have come to give all of you a review of the game based on the book/future movie: "Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire." Graphics: 3/10 Yes, graphics. This game probobly had some of the crappiest graphics a game could get. Some were original, some were ripped, and the character bitmaps were from the movie. Interesting, but still wasn't too appealing. Music: 2/10 Ahh, music...sadly, this game had terrible music, exepet the title theme: a piano version of the Harry Potter theme. So yeah, I didn't really like the music too much. Programming: 1/10 You guessed right. This game had terrible programming. It used all the deafults, which is boring unless edited abit. Only a couple programes were made in it, such as the opening, item pick ups, and the final event in the game. Right, programming was terrible! Story: 2/10 This demo is based on the beginning of the book. While the story of Harry Potter is decent, the parts of the story in it were sadly not very interesting. So yeah. I'm giving this story a 2, only because I sorta used to like the Harry Potter series. lol Overall Fun: 1/10 Yep, when I played this demo, from beginning to end, it was boring. Sorry to whoever made this game, but the truth is, I didn't really enjoy your game that much. But it might be interesting if a full version comes out, and it's more better made.
