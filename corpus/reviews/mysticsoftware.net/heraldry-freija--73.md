# Heraldry: Freija

> *Review by Seveneternal*
> This review is based on the TK game Heraldry:Freija (demo) The game that just may be the greatest new-age TK game. But my competitiveness will never allow this to happen. Focusing on this game anyhow... I cannot deny that this game has great potential once it's reached its full stage. But not all games are perfect. And this isn't excluded. I'm surpirsed no one did a review on this... because I have no life, I decided to do one. Graphics 18/20 High scores on graphics. Finally a game that does not use tiring old snes graphics... This gives a new look to the TK itself. But one flaw about this is that the types of tiles are not well defined. The background was slightly messy too. These factors keep it from earning a 19/20... or maybe even a perfect score. Music 11/20 Looking at the level of the graphics, I was expecting far better in music. Most of them are simple Midi and not really much to get excited about. Story 13/20 Finally, a unique storyline. Not all overwhelmingly interesting, but diffrent. I can't get much from the demo however. Programming 13/20 From what I see, I can expect superior programming with this. Not much was in the demo however. Gameplay 12/20 Its a demo. Of course there isn't a lot of gameplay. It was a matter of walking around and talking. Overall 67/100
