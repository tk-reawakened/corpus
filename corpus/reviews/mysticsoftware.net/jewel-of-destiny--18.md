# Jewel of Destiny

> *Review by Dude Man*
> Jewel Of Destiny (Review for the Non-Registerd Version) Review By: Dude Man Game By: CBM Graphics 18/20 Well let's see, they were the default graphics, but CBM made RPGToolkit and the graphics, therefor they are original and I say the graphics are very good! Game Play 17/20 The game was very fun, it had a very good story. The game however being a demo, it was very short. The feel of playing the game felt like I wasn't playing an RPGToolkit game for some reason, it gave me that old fasioned cool RPG one, so I found it very fun to play! Programming 18/20 I don't quite remember any bugs, however I do believe there were a few. It used all the default stuff, however didn't CBM make that? Story 18/20 The story was very good. However I didn't get all of it, it seemed more like one of those see what happens at the end stories. Therefor it is a good story, because ones like that, kick @$$! But, the part of Tano joining you, I guess it was okay, because they were friends before, but I suppose he could have done a bit more, but it was still good. Music/sound 17/10 Sounds, they were good. But there weren't very many. It uses the defualt music, that was made by VIP Studios, so I am unsure if it would count as original music, but it dosen't matter to much to me, because the music went GREAT with the boards. Overall 88/100 Excellent work CBM! You get an A! I'm very proud of you....HAVE A COOKIE! :) THE END!
