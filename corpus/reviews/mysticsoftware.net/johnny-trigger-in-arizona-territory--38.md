# Johnny Trigger in Arizona Territory

> *Review by Craig Reeves*
> Johhny Trigger and the Arizona Territory: Gold Edition Reviewer: Craig Reeves Game Author: Wasteland -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Johnny Trigger Gold Edition, is basically a rendition of the first Johnny Trigger to hit the net, however with improved graphics, and new menu systems. When I first turned this game on, I noticed something that I didn't like, the game was in default resolution! AHHH!!! I hated that. Anyway, I assumed that the resolution that it would play best in was 640*480, nope! Everything was out of place and garbled. So I tried 800*600, and everything looked clear to me. After I started the game, I noticed that the intro program was quite slow, and very boring and annoying, but I sucked it up, and started gameplay. As soon as I hit that "New Game" button, I was disappointed that the same ugly message window was used in this version. I read the nicely laid out story, and I was down at Simpson ranch. Once I started actually playing, my spirits went up as I saw a 3d rendered Johhny walking around, the graphcis were sharp and everything was pretty nice, albeit very slow for a TK game, even on my 930 Mhz Pentium III, it still looked very pretty. Once I got inside the house, Jessica needed help and obviously was in danger, so Big Tom ("I think that's his name") and I headed north. Well, I was strolling around the pretty scenery, until I got into a fight. Once I got into my first fight, my eyes said "Yeah!" as I saw my 32*64 players's animations dazzle the screen, probably the best player animations I've ever seen in a homemade RPG. However, one thing bothered me, and that's some of the lengthy sound effects, such as when the javelina attacks you, and when the javelina dies, the sound effects are too long, which slows down battle. Also, another thing that annoyed me about the batles, was that the sounds that play when moving up and down to select options slowed down things as well. When I got out of battle, I was strolling north, ran into more battles, and eventually ended up in Huachua (or something like that). I eventually got tired, and went to bed, but Angela got through the whole game in 6 hours and 30 minutes, this is without running away from battles, or rushing through dialog. OVERALL RATING: 5/10 PROS: -Very pretty graphics -Good music -Beautiful battle graphics -Lots of custom menus -Very Original CONS: -Battles are slow because of some sound effects -Terrible Intro -Generally Slow -Some enemy graphics need improvement.
