# Just Another Day

> *Review by catwhowalksbyhimself*
> When I saw that this game has now been released as a full game, I thought I would try it. The author warned me on his toolkitzone project page that it was very short, and that it was basically a learning project. As a learning project, it is not really much of a game, but shows great promise for the author, especially if he teams up with the right people for his upcoming projects. Graphics--5/10 The graphics are original and brightly colored. Not the worst thing I have ever seen, but definitely not great. Sound--7/10 Not bad. All the music seems to fit the feeling of the game, but then again, there isn't much feeling to this game. Some seems to be original, but much is not. Story--1/10 There isn't really any. Gameplay--1/10 There isn't much of that, either. You just walk around through a couple of mazes and explore a city where you can play several mini-games. That's it. No fighting, no explorations, nothing else. Programming--9/10 Here is where the author shows promise. His in car section has potential, even if not really used. His mini-games are not exactly original, but they are well designed and programed. They are pointless as far as the game is concerned, but hey, they do show potential. Length--0/10 The project page says it takes 30 minutes to finish. It takes closer to 10, even if you do explore the town and try the mini-games. Completeness--8/10 Well, I suppose it is complete, as far as there was any plot to complete in the first place. Flawlessness--10/10 Maybe I just wasn't paying attention, but I didn't see any bugs or problems. Replay value--2/10 The only reason I rated it that hight is that some people might enjoy replaying the mini-games. That is the only reason anyone would do so. Overall fun--2/10 Same as above. Only the mini-games are fun.
