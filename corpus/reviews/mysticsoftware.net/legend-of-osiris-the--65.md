# Legend of Osiris, The

> *Review by oblivious_casualty*
> whatever... Riiipped. Not mcuh else I can say. There may have been a few original tiles, but they never caught my attention if they were there. Riiiped. Straight from the default music selection. At least get some different music from a TK website. Although not cliched as most, it's nowhere near original. What's with the darkness motif? Nothing was explained. Things just happened. Ugh. I had trouble paying attention while playing the game. I could've been doing two other things at the same time, and never miss anything. No equipment? And fighting Goblins? What's wrong here? Default. Default. Default. Ten minutes. No joke. That makes me mad, seeing how large the download is. Complete? No. It has trouble trying to explain the backstory, much less answer anything else. What's the character's name? Osiris or Osirus? People just randomly joined your 'quest' and random stuff like that. And the conversations such as: "YOU BASTARDS!" or "OH MY GOD!!!" or even "SHUT THE HELL UP AND LEAVE!!!" as completely uncalled for. Amazingly, the game didn't have many flaws. Maybe it's because you had hardly no freedom. The game was more linear than a timeline. People said everything you needed to do. I will probably never play this demo ever again. EVER. This game seemed like a chore, almost. I wouldn't call it fun. And the swearing really killed any fun the game had a chance of giving you. Anything IQ Software changes will be an improvement, I think. I don't know what went wrong with this game. But my gut instinct is everything. **DARKNESS?**
