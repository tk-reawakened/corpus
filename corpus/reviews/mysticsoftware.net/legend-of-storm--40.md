# Legend of Storm

> *Review by Mandy*
> Legend of Storm Reviewer: Mandy Game Author: Grey Fox -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Legend of Storm is like most rpg's, based back in the time when thinking was illegal. It begins with an attack of rabid demons and their only defender are the mercenaries and the hero, Storm. Now what's unique about this game is it's main character. It's not a mean man with a muscly bod or a cute maiden with a shining smile. It's about a fuzzy ball of joy. Half-wolf,half-man,all-Storm. So if Grey was good at creating a main character, lets see how he did on everything else. Graphics: The character graphics are good *I'm not sure if they were his or not*. Except for his friend Steve who is a black square when you first meet him. The landscape was ok, but sometimes not detailed enough*I include buildings as landscape*. The landscape was decent most of the time. I give the graphics a 6 out of a possible 10. *1 being the worst* StoryLine: Well, he didn't get very far in the story. I can't yet rate this in a fair manner. Characters: Like I said, he made an interesting main character. Who has a few friends. Another thing I like is Storm moves very smoothly. In battle his animations are smooth*and I like that in a game*. I give the characters a 7 out of 10. Enemies: Most of the enemies are the same thing over and over only a different color. That's sorta bad. At first the enemies are too tough, but Storm gains a level almost every battle, than they become too easy,too quick. The one boss that you fight, I killed in 2 hits, but I'm sure he's working on it. I give enemies 4 out of 10 *ouch, sorry Grey*. GamePlay: Now this was interesting. You had to press the activation key to go inside somewhere.*that was good*The one dungeon like area that you go through was also good because it took some time to get through. It had a challenge to it. I give gameplay for Legend of Storm 7 out of 10. Overall: Legend of Storm is a fairly decent game. It downloads fairly quick too. It's a great game to try out if your looking for a character who's grown more hair than your aunt and uncles beards combined. Overall, I give Legend of Storm,6 out of a possible 10. Best Quality of the Game: A Unique Main Character
