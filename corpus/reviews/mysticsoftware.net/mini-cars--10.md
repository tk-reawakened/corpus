# Mini Cars

> *Review by Zepher*
> Hmmn, I downloaded this game expecting some gameplay. Sadly enough, I didn't get any of it. Heres why: Graphics: 1/10 Uses the default graphics with a few exceptions such as the cars and stands. Music: 5/10 I must say, this area of the game was reasonably good. It seemed to fit well with the track. Tracks: 1/10 Absolutely crap. Im sorry to say it, but I have to. There is one track, with only 2 boards in it. Playability: 1/10 I did 1 lap of the circuit and got bored. Very lacking in depth. Fun Factor: 1/10 Ahh, the all important fun factor. It doesn't look like the creator looked at that very much while making this game. Overall: 9/50 Avoid it like the plague. Does not justify the 3mb download. This review was written by Zepher, dated July 12, 2002
