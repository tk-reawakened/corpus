# Mini Cars

> *Review by Hyren*
> Mini Cars Reviewer: Hyren Game Author: Dean -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- I was looking through the games section in Mystic Software and saw a game called Mini Cars. Now, everyone says this game sucks. And I say, "YES IT DOES! IT WASTED 3000 KB ON MY C DRIVE FOR THIS!?" Now, let me tell you why I think this. 2 screens. Default graphics. Do the math. It STINKS! Story 0/8 There is none! What else is there to say?! I mean, you're a car in a race with no other racers! Graphics 3/8 All default except the ONE RACER, the stands, and the camera guys. The graphics aren't the worst I've ever seen but they're no where close to the best. Music 5/8 There's one song and, supprisingly, it actually fits in with the game. Bugs ?/? Obviously, there can't be bugs if there's no programming so I technically can't say if it has bugs. Programming 0/8 There is none. Need I say more? Battle System 0/8 No enemies. No battles. Fun 1/8 If your about to die of boredom, you can still get the game and have the fun of making fun of it in a review like this. Originality 8/8 Hey, what other game lets you be a little orange Beetle and drive around a race course? Final Score: 17/80. Get it, play it, delete it, make fun of it in a review. Not worth the KB.
