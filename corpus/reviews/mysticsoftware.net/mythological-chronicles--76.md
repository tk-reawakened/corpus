# Mythological Chronicles

> *Review by Dendow*
> Hiya, Dendow here. I'm writing a review about the demo Mythological Chronicles. It's pretty much aimed at the game designer, though. Here's the different areas you need to work on. Story: The story is a bit weak. When I went to talk to the old man in his mansion, the explanation wasn't very thorough. All I got out of it was a "you were on my doorstep and you're some sort of special elf." It doesn't really flow together, and how does this man know this? Is he some sort of lorekeeper? And why does the man suggest he go with Triven? You have to think of a reason that he'd want Triven to go with him. Perhaps Triven knows something for you to get further? Or maybe he is a hired bodyguard for you? My advice on the story: Think it out! Characters: The characters are kind of messed up, to tell you the truth. You have to think of it in terms of the real world: Are all people going to talk the same? My answer to you is: Uh-uh. You need to think up of different ways to make these people act! I know you are only one person, but they certainly aren't! They also need a sort of background, only for you, to tell you how you should make them act. Graphics & Boards: Fine really, except for the big white chunks at the ends of boards. That's gotta go. Also, you should try to make your own graphics so you can go beyond creative. If you can't draw, hire someone for your game, and offer to put them in the credits. Music & Sound: Hmm...The music sound ripped to me. You really shouldn't rip music. You should compose your own with a Anvil Studio-esque program. I suppose ripping sound effects is fine though, because they really are quite hard to make. Bugs: I found quite a few bugs in the demo itself. For one, you could not enter the Inn in Peasantpeep Village. That has to be fixed quickly. Also, you should set smartstep to on in the start program so that if the character runs into a solid object, he stops, and doesn't go to the left or the right. Also, program R to do a force redraw to get any extra bugs out during gameplay. That is very important. Those were the worst bugs, and I assure you, there's more. Just do what I told you, and you'll be fine. Overall Advice for the Game Designer: Try to be more original with what you put in the game, no rips except for sound effects. Hire people to do the work you can't do. Try to put more backbone in the characters, or they'll all end up like the same person: you. Try to fix up the bugs and things and be sure to have a force redraw and smartstep. TTFN, Dendow
