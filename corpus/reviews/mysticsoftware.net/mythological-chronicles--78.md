# Mythological Chronicles

> *Review by Cesque*
> Custom ones, except characters. Not that bad, but seems a bit senseless, flowers are larger than characters and the road design is pretty weird. Other than that, good. I especially like building tiles. Also, even after a long contemplation, I was unable to tell what the "save point" is. A bench? A phone? A bed? Title screen is piece of crap. Default. There's music, so one point, is used not worse, so second point. This is it. All right... Infinite apples, and ever funnier, author claims it's not a bug. Catching a bird is not working, also a bird seems to look like one of villagers. Going north makes you forever pass village/wilderness combination, but going south makes you end up at the first building. What a strange laws of geography! Story is explained, or better, unexplained, by first character. A typical cliche of character of mysterious past, blah blah. But there is a story and is explained a bit, so 5 points. It's a demo, so we can't expect all to be revealed. Gameplay sucks, truly. Areas you go through don't make lot of sense. Battle system is default. One of rooms doesn't even scroll, but you have to go to it's border to appear at another side. Smart step combines with having to press space before door to enter sucks. 4 points hard earned by custom save points and "catch the bird" minigame, which doesn't work anyway. What this game has so special to offer what no other games can? I have no idea. Design is pretty bad, first character after explaining all he can suddenly goes all silent and another character appears without even explaining his function to join you, woman says quote over and over again - how many games (count in hundreds) with this crappy thing have we seen before? Boards go north into infinity as I said previously, road design doesn't make lot of sense. Game should use a bit more work to become a bit better... I see no reason why you should ever return to this game. It sounds crappy, but most likely it simply needs more work. I don't highly recommend playing this, though. **Graphics - 5/10**
