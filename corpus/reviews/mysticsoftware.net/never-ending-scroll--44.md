> *Review by R043482*
> Never Ending Scroll Reviewer: R043482 Game Author: White Divine Web Site: http://ChicoGames.cjb.net/ Reviewed Based on the standards of TK games at: Friday December 14th, 2001 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Description: "Prepare to take on the role of Granden, A Great knight from the Adamor Kingdom. Your challenge is to find the truth and explore the world, without Kragon getting in your way. The story will unfold and become clearer as you gain the knowledge you need." Strong Point: -Original Graphics and Music -Random Generated Dungeons -New Menu System -New Battle Engine -New Shops This game has a lot of neat features, which what some other games lack and Very original artistic like-ness to it. Saving, and loading screens are also very well carefully planned out, which shows the precise details put into this game. Weak Point: Weak points? I guess the only thing that keeps this game from absolute perfection as based on other toolkit games are the more in-depth introduction of the story and plot, and crisper, upbeat music? The Story, and music are near perfection to what I like, and it's excellent overall. Rating: Story/Plot: 4.5/5 The game supplies a very interesting introduction, which catches the eye, and is very surprising. What else could I say? You got to play it to see it Game Play: 5/5 It is a very addictive, with a lot of random possibilities with dungeons. It isn't the same when you play the dungeon twice, giving you an endless possibility when exploring dungeons, never allowing you to easily pass through the dungeons. There is even an added bonus of changing the walking speed of the hero. The work of art, music, and effort put into this is an additional plus too with a refreshing new battle system making the game plays extraordinary. Graphics: 5/5 The game's graphics are original, and very artistic. A very good artistic effort put into this, giving the player an enlighten experience, and an excellent quality to the game. Sound/Sound Effects: 4.5/5 Music for the Never Ending Scroll is original. There is a great variety of music supplying limitless entertainment. Sound effects are additionally added for enhance game play experience. Originality: 5/5 Perfect Originality! Everything is original, from the battle system to the stories and plot to the graphics and music. Every single aspect in this game is original, even the menu system and game play, with additional features to boot! Overall Score: 24/25 Percentage: 96% Reviewers Comments: This game breathes new life to the next generation of Rpg-Toolkit games! The battle system is unique, and all work is original with a crisp story, and randomized options for dungeons. A very unique game, which can only be played the same once, which keeps this game an interesting experience for future players to come. Robert Dang (R043482)
