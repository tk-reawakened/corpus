# Phenomenal

> *Review by Seveneternal*
> This review is based on the TK game Phenomenal (demo) This game is a disgrace to all RPG's out there. At least spell things right... Since this game sucks, and I love mocking games, I decided to do a review. Here we go: Graphics 6/20 Beautiful default graphics, amazing ripped tiles, marvelous stolen characters. The author doesn't deserve any credit, but since it wasn't slapped on in random orders, I guess it's saved from a zero... Music 5/20 I heard, two or three music. They were all crappy Mimis and didn't really fit accordingly. Story 7/20 The story is about going after some dude and killing him over this power. I think my IQ dropped playing this game. Programming 10/20 Wow. The word "default" is all over this game. Costomize! Costomize! Comstomize! Don't upload a crappy game full of defaultness. Only uses of simple commands. Gameplay 11/20 Some gameplay... At least... Too bad it was again, random encounters. But how much can you do with a default BS? Overall 39/100
