# Platinum Blade

> *Review by David Solomon*
> Platinum Blade Reviewer: David Solomon Game Author: Composition Software -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Graphics: 17/20 Good tiles, mostly original (It is very rare to find good original tiles...or even original tiles at all.) Character sprites are also very well done and are in a good original style. Story: 18/20 Original, and complex. There are many interesting characters with individual personalities that are expressed very well. The story revolves around the search for the legendary, and perhaps mythical Platinum Blade, said to so powerful the who ever possessed it could not be defeated in battle. But it is not just some boring, "Hey, lets look for the Platinum Blade!" thing. You begin the game as a great general, but through an intriguing turn of events you are framed by another general, who is possibly quite insane, for opening the gate standing between your country and cruel and evil Imps. You are imprisoned and escape only to learn that the man who framed you is after the Platinum Blade. It is up to you, and a few friends, to stop the power hungry mad man from possessing unimaginable, and un match-able power. Bugs 15/20 Unfortunately this one of the games weak points. As a tip, do not use bread from the item menu while not in a fight, the game will freeze. None of the bugs are devastating to the game play, so it's not so bad. Although there are some points where after a video an exit is blocked by an invisible item, and the only way to get by them is to save the game and load it back, and the exit will not be blocked. Opening 18/20 The opening has some very good bitmaps, and when you start the game there is an interesting video. Fun factor 16/20 The only reason points were removed from this was because there are a lot of videos and a lot of reading that can become boring. There are also a few times when the random battles are too close together. These flaws are not, however, so bad that it makes you dislike the game Total rating 84/100 This is a very good game, I even prefer it over Deceit And Conquest. You should definitely download it. Also, the full version is out, so you don't have to worry beating a short little demo, and wondering when the next release is.
