# Platinum Blade

> *Review by Young Knight*
> Platinum Blade Reviewer: Young Knight Game Author: Composition Software -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Graphics: 20/20 Wow! The graphics in Platinum Blade are absolutely astounding! The original tiles are great, but they are the norm coming from Compsition Sofware. The dialogue took a lot of work, because it is color coded for each character. Gameplay: 20/20 The gameplay is great, all the maps are nicely made, all the programs are where they are supposed to be and are functioning, and nothing seems out of place. All the enemy's stats are just enough to be challenging but not hard. Composition Software really outdid themselves with Platinum Blade. Sound: 18/20 Nothing special, just FF7 music. Although it is played at appropriate times in the game, which adds to the fun factor and gameplay. Fun Factor: 19/20 It is a fun game, but it cuts off at a part that leaves you hanging ( but oh well, that's how demos go). The battles are great, the graphics are AWESOME, the story is riveting, man you need to get this game! But, the replay value is kindalow. Overall: 19/20 You can't go wrong with this game.
