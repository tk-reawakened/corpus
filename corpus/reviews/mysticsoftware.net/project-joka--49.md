# Project Joka

> *Review by Marcus Lewton*
> Project Joka Reviewer: Marcus Lewton Game Author: Tomoso -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Story, What's it all about? (5/10) It's one of those war stories, just put it like that. I don't really want to go into lots of detail on what the game play is like and what are the main characters like. Basically just see for yourself. : Overall I would say a half decent attempt for a storyline Boards (5/10) Umm, Mmmm now this is going to be hard to describe. Very, very original all the characters are original and so are the tiles. However this may not be everyone's cup of tea, a lot of people say originality is the most important thing in a game but if the drawings aren't satisfactory the game gets criticised which is remotely unfair. This was tough deciding on a mark due to most of the boards are drawn like this therefore if you think originality is everything then I give this 7 but on the other hand if you think originality is good but only good if the drawings are very well drawn and look good then I give this 4 out of 10. At the end of the day the bottom line is "Does it look good?" well I would say not in particular but it is a damn good effort so. Graphics (7/10) All very original but they don't really come across as outstanding graphics. It must of taken ages drawing the graphics and tiles, which go to show far much more effort was put into making this game. The introduction to the game is excellent probably one of the best introductions I have see everything again was original and the way the writing was drawn was classic! Bugs (1/10) Damn! No offence to the producer but it was like infested with bugs! Every time I received an item it didn't show up on the menu system! Also another major bug was (Well I think it was a bug) I could never fall into a battle! Also when the characters walked around they left a black square behind! Sorry but the bugs were very poor Before you get into the game a message does come up explaining that "this is the early version of the game and there me some bugs" MAY BE some bugs it was infested but at least you could move Items (2/10) There were a lot but because of the "Item Bug" I didn't have a clue what they did! Battles (0/10) Again I never even fell into one Enemies (0/10) Need I say more? Programming (8/10) Excellent this was good probably the best feature of the game. The Mwin command was colour coded and the producer used a lot of variables input boxes. However there was a default menu system. (They are very hard to program, so I'm not going to take off marks) Overall pretty good. Overall Well if the producer sorts out all his bugs and redraws the tiles, which look pretty rough around the edges. He's on his way to producing a top quality game but then again I haven't seen his enemies or battle system. With time and if puts more effort into it he will go far Final score 28/80 (35%)
