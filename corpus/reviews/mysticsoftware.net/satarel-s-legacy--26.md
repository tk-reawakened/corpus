# Satarel's Legacy

> *Review by Haraldur*
> Satarel's Legacy Beta Demo 1: Graphics: The graphics in the start menu were very good and nice to look at. The tiles and sprite are nice and the board design is quite good. Overall: 9/10 Music and Sound: The music was quite good and fit in well, especially the music in the tavern was good. There were no noticeable battle sound affects, so battles were not as good as they might have been. Overall: 7/10 Story: The story, while interesting, did not play a big role at all. There was also a "welcome to the demo" part, where you were told what to do without any story build up at all. Hopefully this will be improved in the next version. Overall: 4/10 Gameplay and Programming: To fix sprites going wrong when they are two tiles high, Cesque has copied the concept of using a refresh image (r) key to fix the graphics when they go wrong. That is good. However, the game as a whole not the most coherent and is quite confusing sometimes. The battle system is quite good and is nice and simple to understand. There is, however, no working menu system at all. But he gets points because of the custom BS. Overall: 7/10 Bugs: There quite a few small bugs in this game. It seemed that the temple was a prison board. Some of the commands using numbers would not work at all. After a while music and background sound stops until "r" or "space" is pressed. By pressing "backspace" you get a completely useless default menu. In the battle system the character graphic disa\ppears. Overall: 3/10 Overall score: 60% which is a very respectable score for a beta demo. Improvements wanted, please: Iron out all the bugs, make it longer (obviously), get a menu system, get some sound affects in the battle system and give more story build up to explain the player's actions. This game looks promising, keep it up!
