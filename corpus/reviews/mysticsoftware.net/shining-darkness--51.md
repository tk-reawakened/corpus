# Shining Darkness

> *Review by MrX*
> Shining Darkness Reviewer: MrX Game Author: Hyren -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- // MrX Says: Hello kiddies! It's time for another review by the one and only X-Man! (Not to be confused with the great Zen Bunny, MrG!) This week, we shall be discussing the game By Hyren, named Shining Darkness. First Glance After firing the program up, There was no intro and it just went straight to the title screen, which only consisted of the Toolkit's Demo game buttons and a black screen. Not much effort there I must say. The game starts you off in a cave type place with one exit to the north. In the next room the is a guy standing in the middle. When you talk to him, he tells you that he brought you from you home world to this one, for you are the one to help them in their hour of need. Oh, and you also get to choose your class from six choices. After talking to him he joins you for your training. You make your way through the forest while fighting enemies. At the end he leaves you and tells you to go to a temple. From there on you can visit the town which consists of nothing but merchants selling you weapons, depending on what class you chose, find the temple, or gain some levels. Then I got lost. I found the temple, but couldn't figure out where to go from there. Bummer... Rating the Game: Graphics - 10/25 I think the standard in TK games for graphics is the RPGMaker95/2k tilesets. I can't complain. They're better than the default graphics (No offense to the Big Cheese...). Heck, I used 'em in my projects, they were simply better than anything I could ever draw up. Overall, they we're laid down nicely, not as good as some games I've seen. Also, on boards that didn't scroll, there was this white space on the top and bottom. Very annoying. No menu graphics, and uses the default menu. Pretty ugly just seeing all black and white text. Reminds me of DOS. Character graphics are RM95/2k sets, ripped but they go with the set. Battle graphics where simple and special moves where simple. Being a very basic looking game kept this score low. Story - 10/25 The story was very vague, with only one major spot that there was any hint of what it was about, and that was at the beginning. There was one other spot that help it, but other than that, it seemed like a Dragon Warrior-ish type of story... almost not there! Though the character class' are cool, which is starting to get used more and more. Needs more story to keep the gamer going. Media - 18/25 Nice use of the default music that comes with the TK. Seeing as it is not ripped, which is a plus, I gave this a higher score. The Reeve's brothers are very talented composers. General Effect - 8/25 Not an visually impressive game. Very basic. Much room for improvement, which I hope the author with do. Don't rush things. The games fighting wasn't too diffucult though it was overly easy. Overall Effect - 46/100 (46%) Pretty basic game, with a few perks. Needs more graphical enhancement, along with bug fixes, and storyline!
