# Shining Darkness

> *Review by Young Knight*
> Shining Darkness Reviewer: Young Knight Game Author: Hyren -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Story/Gameplay: 16/20 This time, you meet some guy and he tells you to become a class. Then, you go to some city, and they are (reasonably) scared that an unknown force is trapping them into one corner of the continent so they can be conquered. I think. Graphics: 16/20 Ripped tiles, but good looking and well placed. Sound: 16/20 Default TK music. Nice change. Programming: 15/20 OMG, sorry Hyren, but the Start program, frankly, sucked. Hard. But, you got points for the cool class/character choosing programs. Bugs:13/20 Not a lot of bugs, but one BIG one. One, you could get as many characters as you want from the temple. The big one is at the bridge. I visited the temple, but the bridge wasn't built yet, so the game basically was over. Overall: 76/100 A game with a lot of potential.
