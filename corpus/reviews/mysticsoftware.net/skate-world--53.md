# Skate World

> *Review by Marcus Lewton*
> SkateWorld Reviewer: Marcus Lewton Game Author: Zepher (SSJGohan) -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Story, What's it all about? (2/10) Story? There is no story, basically you go around a few boards and well, Skate. Boards (2/10) Basically there are no boards; each board looks exactly the same there is a selection of different concrete floors, made out of the default tileset. Graphics (3/10) Nothing special at all, the colours of the tiles are mixed well though. There is only one good graphic in the game. Items (1/10) Lets just say there are only two items with the names: Item 1 and Item 2 Battles (0/10) There are none Enemies (0/10) Huh again there are none. Programming (4/10) Well, nothing special but it is different so I got to give some credit Bugs (9/10) Finally! Something positive about the game. Overall It's different and is pretty addictive but not in the "fun" game play way. Nevertheless it is very original and makes a change from the final fantasy want to be games. Download this only if your looking for some thing very different Final score 21/80 (26%)
