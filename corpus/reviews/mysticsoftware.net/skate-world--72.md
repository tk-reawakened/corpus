# Skate World

> *Review by Seveneternal*
> This review is based on the TK game Skate World Skate World was not a very good title for this game. Skate Prison would have described it better. This is just one of those games... You know? Think Minicars... then you will know what I mean. Fortunately, you got someone like me to endure the horrors of this game to warn you about how much it sucks, so you do not have to. Don't you love me? Onto the game: Graphics 5/20 Unbelivable... A cement graphic with a few triangles and bars... Music 0/20 There is like... no music. Story 0/20 The story is: Go skate. Programming 2/20 At least it had some... Gameplay 2/20 You move around in a board, and if you step on a tile, a Mwin comes up telling you the name of the trick you just did. Overall 9/100
