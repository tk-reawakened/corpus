# Talismans of the Nine

> *Review by DoddyUK*
> Talismans of the Nine Reviewer: DoddyUK Game Author: Tyrant Trenchcoat -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- What's it about? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Take up the role of a man who has escaped from a overthrown castle in his quest to find and bring vengance the asassins responsible. Is the plot any good? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Not too sure what to make on this one. The actual storyline isn't that clear, and although there are prophecies, myths and legends to assist you, you may feel quite bewildered by it all. Good effort though Storylines: 3/5 Does it look good? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hell yeah! The graphics in this game could easily compete with the big guns of DustBall and Friends And Enemies. They are rich in colour and are "in your face", and the enemy animations in the battle are superb. Words are not enough to say how good this looks. Can you say "Eye Candy"? Graphics: 5/5 Is it glitch-free? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Yep. Absolutely nothing wrong in the rpgcode stakes here, but unfortunately there is nothing special to comment about. The coding may be spot on, there's nothing to comment on apart from the message windows. No custom menu either. A few extras for next time please. Programming: 3/5 And what's the sound department like? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The biggest plus point about the sound department in this game is that all pieces are completely original. The battle music provides a fairly good atmosphere, althougth the castle attack music is fairly annoying at first. Still, the fact that all music is original is a fact that is extremely rare in first demos. Hats off to Ice Nine. Music: 5/5 In a nutshell... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- This is an extremely promicing game by Ice Nine. Good graphics, original music plus the fact that the download is only 3 and a half megs equals one game you must seriously concider playing. The only downside is the lack of any interesting programs. Not quite as good as Dustball in my opinion, but this is an awesome demo. Check it out. Overall: 5/5* *Editors Note: Again someone over-rates the game Talismans of the Nine. Is this going to become some kind of trend? The scores above clearly add up to 16/20, or 80%, or 4/5; NOT the 5/5 that was given by the reviewer. Again I must stress that you can not give an imperfect game a perfect score, regardless of who made it.
