# Talismans of the Nine

> *Review by Young Knight*
> Talismans of the Nine Reviewer: YoungKnight Game Author: Tyrant Trenchcoat -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Graphics 23/20 Tyrant got bonus points in this area. All the graphics in this game were original. And Tyrant in a Trechcoat did a GREAT job. I mean dang, these tiles were utterly amazing. The walking tiles were great, with much more movement than I've ever seen. Also, the artwork was absolutely astounding. Sound 17/20 The music was OK, but I don't remember any sound effects. Although, the music on the first board was all beepy and weird. Fun Factor 20/20 Talismans of the Nine has a deep, involving sotryline, complete with prophecies of your guy. I could just play the demo over and over because it is so great! Gameplay 19/20 There's nothing wrong with this game, except a little bug, nothing to really worry about. Overall 20/20 This game is great, and when the custom menu is done, it'll be even better. With only one little bug, a small 3MB file, and great artwork and story, Talismans of the Nine is a sure fire great TK game. TOTAL: 100/100= A+!!* PS: Tyrant, you HAVE to teach me how to make tiles that good!! This Review is written by YoungKnight, dated June 20, 2002 *Editors Note: Unfortunately you can't give an imperfect game a perfect score. Tyrants game therefore should've rated at 76/80, or 95%
