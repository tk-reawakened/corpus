# Talismans of the Nine

> *Review by Seveneternal*
> This review is based on the TK game Talismans of the Nine (demo) This game received a perfect score from someone... But in my opinion, it is nowhere near perfect. However, not so bad either. Why? Thats why this review is here for. Graphics 14/20 At last! A game without ripped graphics! This is definitely a plus. However, the graphics could have used improvement and better defined. It looked too tile-bound. Also, variations between tiles would have helped as well. Music 12/20 Sounds were ok. Definitely fit the mood of the situation. But I was disgusted by the quality and how it sounded. It could use better MIDIs or MP3. I was glad however, that it was not a overused FF song. Story 14/20 The story seems ok so far... Nothing too special about this. Programming 14/20 Not much bugs in the programming. But it used the default BS, and there wasn't much except dialogues and the story itself. Gameplay 14/20 I was relieved to not find battles every ten seconds. The walk was balanced too. But could use improvement. Really no costom or new features... so thats rather on the downside. This is a usual TK game. Overall 58/100
