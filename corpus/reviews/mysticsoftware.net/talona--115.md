# Talona

> *Review by TKGB*
> This game was featured in the TK winter expo 2004. In my opinion, it is one of the best demos in the expo. I love the way this game was developed. I love the characters, the towns, this game is very, very, good. Plot (1) - In this planet psionics live. These are the people who created the world. A long discussion was dealt in the creation of himanity, the ones who would pass on the legends from time to time. One psionics used a weapon against another psionics causing the world to become disatrous with volcanoes and magma. The psionics who was the cause of the fallen world was imprisoned for her crimes. Two children survived and they would be the ones to pass down the tales and powers to the future of this world to be prepared from the psionics who caused the world to be created like this to be freed one day. Err...the story goes something like that. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - Ripped tiles from RM2K, man are we getting lazy lately? Well anyways its not bad looking. Everything looks okay. The towns to the outside all look neat but not a big green world you'd expect. More like a small wasteland, the way it is explained in the prolouge. Not bad. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - AH! The music is beutiful. I love it. It's great. We got good MIDI's that aren't the highest standards but not crummy sounding. Oh boy how they sound so good together. My personal favorite is the "Village of Talona". That one I really like listening to. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - So far this demo only features the town of Talona and a couple of others. I can't say there is no outterworld cuz I couldn't pass the first boss. Ack. Well from what i've seen, it's not bad. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - This department could of been WAY better. The cutscenes with the descriptions are a nice touch but not alot to go from here. Eh. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - The characters are great. They each have they're own background bio. Findecáno is an apprentice learning to become a psionic. Valandil is the wise psionic who tends to joke alot about life even though somethings are considered sacred. There are plenty of others with great personality you must see. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - Bland. Not even a profile pic. It's boring. That's all I can say. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (1) - It's okay. The animations are nice and it seems perfectly balanced. I died in the second battle cuz the first is a boss battle. This battle tends to make you really weak and you have to immediately fight again but you get killed easily. Well if you can win the second fight, good for you. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (0) - BLEGH! There is a lag here. Walking sucks. The game waits to load when you change direction. What the heck is that about? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - Well this game is sure to please some people. Although the actuall gameplay kinda sucks but the story and the characters are all cool. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (7/10) - A fantastic game in the making. I wish to see this game evolve with custom BS and other goodies. I would expect nothing less from InDreams.
