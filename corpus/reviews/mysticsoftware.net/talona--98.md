# Talona

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by oblivious_casualty*
> Talona Demo 1 Review by oblivious_casualty The first demo released by Chaos, Talona Demo 1 features the prologue of the game's story. Graphics: All of the graphics in the game were RPGMakerXP tiles ripped by Spyder. Chaos used these tiles to make some great boards, with lots of detail. All of the tiles work well together. However, the tiles are still ripped. 8/10 Sound: Most of the music in Talona (maybe all of it) was made by Chaos. The musical style flows beautifully, and the music fits the mood of each scene. 9/10 Story: The storyline is one of the best I have ever played through in a Toolkit game. The characters react accordingly to the situation, and the characters actually have brains; they make decisions and think like normal people. The prologue displays a great deal of the backstory and I'm looking forward to learning more about this storyline. 9/10 Gameplay: The gameplay in Talona is one of the weakest aspects of the game, however the game focuses on its storyline. There are two boss battles, and one other type of enemy that you fight multiple times. Not much variety. 5/10 Programming: There is no custom systems in the game, but the cut-scenes are programmed very well. The menu in the game is almost useless; there is no equipment, and only one type of item. I only opened the menu twice. The default battle system proves frustrating, with the simple ENTER, DOWN, ENTER routine of attacking. 4/10 Length: For a prologue, the game is considerably long. It should satisfy most players. 6/10 Completeness: The demo feels somewhat complete, but a bit rushed. 7/10 Flawlessness: After I debugged the demo, I found only one minor typo. 9/10 Replay Value: The game doesn't have much replay value. Once you've played through the cut-scenes once, you don't need to go through them again. And the demo is comprised of mostly cut-scenes. 5/10 Overall Fun: The first time through the game, it was very fun to play. I was curious about psionics, and the cut-scenes provided some information about this power, but not too much as to avoid spoiling the storyline. A good play if you want to play a great demo. 9/10 Total: 71/100
