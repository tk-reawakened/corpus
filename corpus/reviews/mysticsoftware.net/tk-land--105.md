# TK Land

> *Review by TKGB*
> TK Land is one of the most anticipating games of our time (the other being mine :-P). The game is to feature TK community members as the protagonist. A crazy idea that many attempted and failed miserably (like myself). But this time the InDreams team has the right formula and its ready to roll. Oh no wait. This is just a graphics demo. Blegh. Plot (0) - This demo does not feature a story. In the begining, Spyder laments and then wakes up in a cold sweat. He goes out side and meets MrG to search in Thanian Nation where they find R# who wants to says to destroy. Thats it. Nothing else really. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - Wow! The art ~*~SHINES~*~ in this game. The art is pretty. I love the way things look. Everything is kinda cartonish but not exactly Sophia cartoonish (as you can tell by the GameStudio 43 logo as well). The characters have their own "persona"l style, as I call it. Each looks different from the other. Just look at khin and compare him to MrG or look at Noadi and compare her to...well, anyone. PERFECT! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (0) - This demo has no music within it but it does come with the music. It's alright but not really super fantastic crazy good. Not even Xorlax's 5 themes sound super cool but they are all good and organized and dont sound like a amish person doing MIDI's. But it wasn't used in the game so I can't give it a point. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - WOW! This place is !!!! There is so many places to go and places you may not think you can go but can. But there is a restriction. There are "Under Construction" signs almost everywhere. This is a border meaning that the demo only happens within the walls. But even with these, you might have a good time walking around and searching for every TK member you can possibly find. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (0) - Nothing in particular. Just you and MrG walking together is the best programming i've seen. Everything else is mostly basic. No point here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (1) - What kind of characters does TK Land have? Why, TK members of course. Throughout the demo, you play as Spyder and meet, MrG, khin, Outlawz, Dan, Noadi, RetroGamer, Matt#, Kobold, and many, MANY others. Oi and this is just the demo. The full version is going to be CRAWLING with these guys. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (1) - A custom menu system but its just there to show. You can't do anything. The programming of it sucks in my opinion but the graphics are awesimir. Really cool and super great. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (0) - There is no fights. Bummer. I wanted to kick Cesque's ass >:-)). Yes I'm evil. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - Spyder moves at a decent speed that is just perfect. This may be the only TK game that has such perfect walking. The menu controls are kinda rough but you wont look at the menu for long. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - To walk around and meet the TK community is really cool. Is the next best thing to doing the real thing but sadly in real life, we cant go up face-to-face with each other. Then again we wouldn't really want that so its better to look at the cartoon you :-)). -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (6/10) - WOO! This may be just a graphics demo but it is so good, you must play it or I will slap you silly if you don't. Come on, it doesn't take that long to download. Go for it.
