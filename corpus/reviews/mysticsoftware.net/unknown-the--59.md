# Unknown, The

> *Review by Marcus Lewton*
> The Unknown Reviewer: Marcus Lewton Game Author: Jordaan Mylonas -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Story, What's it all about? (1/10) Well, umm this is going to sound pretty pathetic but frankly I'm not quite sure. The font size is set as default, which is so small; I couldn't read it at all! I tried to change it but it was made using the Powerhouse Edition. The dialog was also very long and some of the words went of the screen! I was about to give up at this point but the game was dedicated to someone who had died so for respect I thought I play the game straight through despite the horrible font size. So for a mark out of 10, I'm not really sure Some people may disagree with this comment but if I can't read the writing how can I say anything about the story so I'm afraid I give it 1/10 Boards (6/10) Not that bad some tiles are actually made up which takes a lot of time and effort so I give the producer some extra credit for that. The boards scroll nicely and nearly every house you see, you can enter. Unfortunately some tiles are just slapped together and Most houses are pretty poor. Graphics (5/10) Nothing special here just the basic default graphics. Some are original but mostly default. Items (8/10) Well I think there were quite a lot of items to get through out the game but I couldn't really see what I was collecting but it must have took a long time so it deserves above the average score. Battles (4/10) Well not that bad because there were a few special effects such as magic but it's the default battle system and some of the fight backgrounds are really, really poor and are really rushed. When you are fighting your not even facing the enemy, the characters are facing the screen, there are a lot of signs that the producer didn't spend any time checking his battle system and he has rushed it dreadfully and it really does show. Enemies (5/10) Not that bad, but very frustrating and difficult at times. Some enemies have special attacks and others don't, but unfortunately the enemies are facing in different directions and this is a sign that the enemies were rushed. There is also hardly any battle animation. Programming (7/10) Nothing special but it must of took ages and ages to have so much dialog every character you see says something. There is even one bit where you go up to a phone and chose who you phone, each person has it's only dialog I bet that took ages I do admire hard work. Bugs (8/10) Not bad a few now again but I didn't explore every aspect of the game so there could be more, but from what I saw it was pretty good. Overall Well, this game must have taken absolutely ages to make due to so much dialog and programs but the font size is so small you will not be able to read it. The battles are rushed and the characters on the board have that horrible white stuff around them, the producer hasn't even taken the time to set the characters as items. It's not the worst game I have ever played and I like the idea how that the game was dedicated to someone who had died, I respect that. But the game doesn't even come close to being one of the best. Final score 44/80 (55%)
