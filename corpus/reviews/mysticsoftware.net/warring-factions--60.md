# Warring Factions

> *Review by Jer1400*
> Warring Factions (beta) Reviewer: Jer1400 Game Author: Zepher -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- STORYLINE: Although very short it seemed to be off to a great start. In the 7 or 8 minutes of gameplay the player is already into the game and starting to understand whats going on. Rating: 74% Reasons: Short but sweet, if the game was to expand it could get a better score. TILES: All original tiles including original enemy graphics, sadly they weren't that great. Rating: 60% Reasons: The art wasn't that good, but i think if Zepher was to spend more time drawing the tiles, then they WILL be better. GAMEPLAY: ? Rating: ?% Reasons: This beta wasn't big enough for me to write anything about the gameplay. On the other hand it does utilize the rarely seen front-side-view battle system. Originality: This game has original tiles, and an original storyline, and although not seen in this beta, I think Zepher has the capability of an original menu.(He has the capability, don't know if he will use it.) Rating: 85% Reasons: Most of the things in this game are original, but some are not. Examples include the menu system and the battle system. NOTES: Has the potentional to become a really interesting and great game. Overall Score 73%
