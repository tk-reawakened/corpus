# Warrior Legends

> *Review by DoddyUK*
> Warrior Legends Reviewer: DoddyUK Game Author: EB Productions -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- What's it about? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- You play as either James Wilson or Taria Lloyd in a quest to recover the Koshi crystal in an attempt to stop the gate of time from being re-opened and all hell breaking loose. Is the plot any good? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hmm... I don't quite know what to say. The plot is fairly good, although it does fall into the steriotypical "Character has strange dream and goes on a quest to save the Universe" genre of RPG. There are fairly good subplots, and there are moments that put a smile on your face, but I have to say that for the most part the plot seems like deja vouz to me. Also, where else have we heard of the Vermillion Dragon, the Zelda crystal and Karakiko Village? I thought so. Plus, to quote a TKZ review, "since when can kids smell danger?" Storylines: 3/5 Does it look good? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- This is pretty much the opposite from FaE. Most of the scenery tiles are very good, but the character graphics have been ripped from RPGMaker 95. However, saying this, what it lacks in good character graphics it makes up with enemy graphics. No enemy battle animations though... The internal menu is used for the character stats, but the quest status menu and map look extremely good. Overall, a fairly good effort by EB. Graphics: 3/5 Is it glitch-free? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Pretty much so. EB has included a fairly good amount of options and extras - including a background music selecter - that are very original and work perfectly. I did find a glitch where after defeating the Vermillion Dragon, the battle program kept looping. I found nothing wrong with the code, so I'm blaming CBM's internal fight menu and not taking any marks off the score. Probably THE finest point of the demo. Programming: 5/5 And what's the sound department like? -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- As far as the game is concerned, four words: Zelda - Ocarina of Time. Most maps have a Zelda tune as their background music. I know it's very hard to compose a good piece of music (I should know - I'm currently trying to compose the title music for Silver Sky), but why use music from one of the most famous RPGs of all time? Despite this, the original pieces are pretty good (well, maybe apart from the silghtly annoying main theme...). If you want the best from the music in the game, you'd better open Windows Media Player to listen to some of the most famous gaming songs of all time from the likes of the Final Fantasy series, Chrono Trigger, Super Mario 64 and Zelda: Ocarina of time as well as other gems such as the themes from Mission Impossible, Goldeneye and Star Wars. Basically, download this game not for the in-game music but for these hidden tresures Music: 4/5 In a nutshell... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- I felt slightly disappointed the first time I played this demo. There's no deniying that its a big demo (with a big download size to match), but I was left fairly underwhelmed considering that I am very much looking forward to this game. It's a fairly good game with its good and bad points equaling out, but if I had to choose between this and Friends and Enemies, it would be an extremely close call, but I would have to pick FaE because of its slightly more original storyline. Sorry. Overall: 4/5
