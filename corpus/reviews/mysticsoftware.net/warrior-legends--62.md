# Warrior Legends

> *Review by Seafroggys*
> Warrior Legends (demo 2) Reviewer: Seafroggys Game Author: EB Productions -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Opening I have never played the original Warrior Legends demo, so I have nothing to compare this interactive application with, but from what I have seen, this game has the potential to be one of the greatest Toolkit games ever made! Right now, it is excellent, opposing other 'classics' such as Never-Ending Scroll and Godiva: Alpha. Read on to see if this game is worth the 26 meg download. Graphics: 3/5 The tiles of this game are the same old same old that one would see in many Toolkit games. Not only are they ripped, they are overused. Bitmaps and such used for the menu, message window, trading posts, etc., are quite well done, and saved this score from a measly two. If another decent ripped tileset was used, or even decent original graphics, expect a score of 4 or even 5 in this category. Design: 5/5 One interjection: WOW! I am blown away by the systematic of this game. Not only are the tiles and cities well designed and well placed (and lots of variety too), theres no open spaces! Finally someone has listened to my complaints (or someone is a genius and decided to do it without any input from myself). In addition to well designed arenas, a fine custom menu, several styles of shops, multiple versions of currency.....its all too much! This beats any other Toolkit game to the ground. One problem, though: the intro and ending credits went by soooooooooo slow. I have a 500 mhz btw, so judge for yourself if it is slow. Music/Sound: 3.5/5 This was a rather difficult area to score. Ripped music and sounds, yet it all seemed to fit quite nicely into the overall structure. A real song is even included (as well as a song from the original Final Fantasy! Yes!). If it was regular old ripped crap, this would be a three, but it is implemented well, so naturally I'd give it a 4 or 5, so I comprimised. Story: 3/5 Not a lot to judge, as are most demos. It seems kinda too cliché-ish, some guy trying to save the world right off the bat. His (I've only played the he character, there's a she character too I have not tried out yet) story and history slowly unfolds in a nice and mysterious way, giving the story some positive feel. Gameplay: 5/5 There is quite a bit to do in this game. Several side quests I have not attempted, a few towns that have really no point (but are great for exploring), decent combat with the internal battle system, and lots of special moves and characters. My main guy only lost one hitpoint everytime he was attacked, and the bosses seemed a bit easy, but other then that, the battles were well balanced, especially for being so early in the game. I have not even tried out talismans, skills, and all that other goodie stuff that is included in the game, for I lacked the funds. Debugging: 4/5 Warrior Legends was relatively bug free. The only bugs I encountered were numerous typos in characters' speech, as well as some engine bugs with the battle system. In a Nutshell: 78% Good
