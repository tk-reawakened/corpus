# Warrior Legends

> *Review by Seveneternal*
> This review is based on the TK game Warrior Legends (Demo) Warrior Legends. A game that could have been great with a ittle more effort. Sadly, it looks like there are no future plans for releases. I am however, looking forward to Eternal Sphere. As for this game, I decided to do a review. Check it out: Graphics 13/20 Graphics. I think some or all of these tiles were ripped. But I can't really tell. Just by judging the graphics it self, it certainly needs more work. this style has been overused and abused for me too appreciate it anymore. A diffrent style is always better... Almost always... Music 13/20 Not so great. But not so bad either. Just your average TK music. Ripped. Story 14/20 There are two storylines to choose from... Which I was neat. However, the beginning seemed like a rip-off from Chrono Trigger. The rest was ok however. Nothing special. Programming 16/20 So close to the perfect ending... I loved the costom menu and Message Window. I especially liked the music player. Too bad it didn't play any music. What ticked me off was the costom battle. Gameplay 13/20 Random encounters! They are pure evil! It was fine on the girl version of the story, but the other version had too many encounters in that cave. Man, did I mention how much I hate the default BS? Overall 69/100
