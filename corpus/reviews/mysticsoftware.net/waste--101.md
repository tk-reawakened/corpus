# Waste

These reviews do not reflect the opinions of Mystic Software and/or it's staff, and we will not be held responsible for the content of the reviews.
> *Review by TKGB*
> Waste is a game that was featured on the Winter expo of '04 by Invisible Software and Revival Gaming. The game is your basic TK game but the programming given in the game is grand in my opinion, even though some things crash my comp but I believe this only happens in my computer, I don't believe it is a bug of sorts. Plot (1) - In this time era, the world has suffered a severe drought of sorts and the entire bottom hemisphere has become a wasteland. The northern part regain and continue to live on but the bottom struggles to survive the harsh climate. One day, the northern part of the world decided that it is time to end the desert people once and for all. Very nice. Better than the usual entire-world-gone-dry-and-anarchy-rules plots like. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Art (1) - AH! The graphics are bright and highlighted. That is until you get into a cave were it is dark and gloomy. The lightings in the game are perfect but one thing is wrong. The torchlight makes the outside dark if it runs out. Err what is this about? That must be a mistake because I don't believe it was meant on purpose to have the torch the source of all light, even for a "deylun". The color scheme used in the game are mostly bright orangy colors. Since this is a desert, what other colors would there be? Pink? Unless it was a pink desert which is not. The graphics are also awesome. Look at the NPC's and the deylun himself. They are neatly drawn. The houses are also great and the detail on the sand is nice. One problem though. The soft ground that is depicted which is usually used for the farming part of the game. It don't look right. It makes it look like a walk. It should be a bit isometric. Other than that the graphics are great. Super point there. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Music (1) - The music ain't so bad. You mostly got a desert theme going one which is the use of string instruments that you probably find in those eastern countries like India or Indonesia. There is one FF song but I like it alot so I give it a point for even that. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Enviroment (1) - The enviroment reminds me alot like Johnny Trigger except much better. Unlike Trigger, it is not top view with the character being at 45 degree view. Everything is 45 degree view with road lines. The enviroment is pretty small but great exploring. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Programming (1) - The programming on this game uses plugins and utilizes some neat tricks like a custom Mwin that is alright. Also there is a help box that helps you when you reach a new area and a skill must be used. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Characters (0) - Right now there is one character. His name is Deylun Avisahd. Whatever he is, not much is spoken in the game about him. There is also a weird guy on the begining of the game that speaks to you. Who is this weirdo? I dunno. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Menu (0) - Custom as well as default menu. The custom one is kinda bland and the default one is well...default. Not much here. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fight (1) - A new kind of fighting for the TK. A zelda style battle system where you can attack monsters while being on the board. It's a shame it didn't work for me. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Game Play (1) - Well you can select how the character walks but once you do, you cannot change again so if he is going to fast, you can't change it to something slower. Hopefully this wont be happening in the full version. So anyways, everything else is good. The menus don't interrupt, and if I could try the BS, then I could probably comment on that. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Fun Factor (1) - Everything was great but I didn't really have fun with this game. Probably because I couldn't use the BS. But I did enjoy myself looking around. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Conclusion (8/10) - Great game here. It's good. If you can use the BS it will be even better. This is one of the games I am hoping for a full release.
