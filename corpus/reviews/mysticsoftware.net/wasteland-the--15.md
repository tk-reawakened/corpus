# Wasteland, The

> *Review by Dude Man*
> The Wasteland Review By: Dude Man Game By: Psychopathic Graphics 13/20 The graphics of this game werent the best, however, they were original and I have seen worse, but they were okay I suppose, they could have been better. Some of the graphics I dont believe were original at all, such as the truck and the wolf boss, but I dont recognize them, probably because of my lack of playing RPGs other then Fallout Game Play 17/20 Speaking of Fallout, this game was much like it, probably because of the NPC talking and the fact it took place after a nuclear war. The story was very good, the beginning was cool, and being sent on the big mission on the warehouse was good wholesome fun! However it did have that not as cool mission after it, that kinda ruined the mood for me, but hey, its probably just me, and its even been in the most provisional commercial games, so Ill let that go. Programming 14/20 Not a lot of bugs I found, but however it lacked custom stuff, however it did have a rather cool default MS background. It did however have a very cool policeman program, when he says a random thing every time, basic, but lacking in almost all TK games, Psychopathic was the only other person I saw other then me do that in a game. It had good story sequences of characters moving around, no characters moving but disappearing or having a white background, like Ive seen in other TK games, so he did a great job on that, I could tell he went threw the game and made sure he had no bugs! However he did miss one! Once you try and leave the town and then you are forced to drive your truck back, the game just sort of stops and your truck is just frozen there, it got be pain in the butt Story 15/20 A yes the story was okay, I didnt really get much of what Sanjuro (sp?) was trying to do, exactly, I believe he was trying to find his military base or something. I liked the missions you were sent on, however the characters joining you werent that good, well having the robber join you was okay, but hes a bit stronger then you at the start so why doesnt he just kill you and run off and I thought it was odd why the mechanic stayed with you, when he had no reason to. Music/sound 16/10 The sounds were great! Not all original but still very good, some doom ones and some from other games that I dont remember, but I think he did have some original ones in there. The music was good and set a good mood for the game, went very well with game! Overall 75/100 Good job! You get a B and thats a good mark! Unless your picky about your marks or your sad because you didnt get perfect and if you are, then your odd because you have a good game! But not Dark Age 2 good, more likeThe Wasteland goodwhat? OH! This game IS The Wasteland! So itsuhhh THE END!
